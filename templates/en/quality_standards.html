Our quality standards in Quran teaching

Teaching the Quran is a great responsibility. These are the standards we commit to in front of every student and parent, for a safe, respectful and excellent learning experience.

  • The teacher's charter
  • Lesson standards
  • Progress tracking
  • Working with children
  • Respect and privacy
  • Quality guarantee
  • Student rights

The teacher's charter

  • Patience and kindness with every student, young and old, in a calm and motivating environment.
  • A model recitation based on the highest standards of Tajweed and authentic readings.
  • Modest dress and Islamic etiquette in video classes, for dignity and professionalism.
  • Respect for the student's privacy: no part of a class is recorded or shared without prior permission.
  • Punctuality: no lateness except for genuine emergencies, reported to the management in advance.
  • Teaching step by step at each student's level, without unnecessary complexity or pressure.
  • Constant encouragement, using positive words that build the student's confidence.
  • Courteous communication with parents through the platform.
  • Continuous professional development through training workshops and regular review of teaching methods.
  • Fairness between students, with no favouritism.

Lesson standards

Every class follows this order:

  1. A short recitation to consolidate memorisation or skills.
  2. Careful recitation correction, explaining mistakes simply.
  3. Practical training on the articulation points and qualities of the letters.
  4. A part of the Tajweed rules, simplified to the student's level.
  5. A quick check that the student has understood.
  6. A suitable daily or weekly homework task.
  7. A short plan of what to revise before the next class.

Progress tracking

  • An initial assessment to place the student accurately.
  • A personal learning plan matching age, ability and level.
  • Written monthly reports on the student's level, strengths, weaknesses and next steps.
  • Updating the plan when needed to get the best results.

Working with children

  • No intimidation or psychological pressure on a child, ever.
  • Encouragement and age-appropriate educational play.
  • Respect for differences in children's abilities, patience and focus.
  • Weekly contact with the parent to explain progress.
  • A calm, safe environment during lessons.
  • No comparing a child with other children.

Respect and privacy

  • No class is recorded without permission.
  • No data is shared with any outside party.
  • Students' photos or voices are never used for any purpose without consent.
  • Communication between the teacher and the student or parent happens only through the platform, supervised by the management.

Quality guarantee

  • A free or discounted trial class for new students.
  • Re-explaining anything not yet mastered at no extra cost.
  • A monthly internal review of teachers' performance.
  • Regular development plans using modern teaching methods.
  • Working towards a mastery level of at least 85% in recitation and memorisation across the learning stages.

Student rights

  • To learn with respect and in safety.
  • To change teacher when needed.
  • To a clear learning plan and ongoing follow-up.
  • To a partial refund of fees where there is a valid reason.
  • To a certificate at the end of each level.

Our quality standards are a promise to our students and their parents: Quran teaching that is refined, professional and safe, worthy of the words of Allah and of those who seek them.
