At Al-Agmi Quran Academy (alagme.com) we respect the privacy of our visitors, students and their parents. This page explains exactly what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

1. Data we collect

  • Data you enter yourself: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, country, and the student's age and level, when you book a trial class or send a question or comment.
  • Student portal account data: username, class times, monthly reports, chat messages with the teacher, and voice recordings of homework.
  • Automatic technical data: browser and device type, IP address, the pages you visited and when, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

2. How we use the data

  • To contact you to arrange the trial class and choose the right programme.
  • To deliver classes, follow the student's progress and send reports.
  • To answer questions published in our Q&A section.
  • To improve the website and learn which pages help visitors.
  • To send notifications about new articles or chat messages, only if you allow them in your browser.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

3. Protecting students and children

  • All communication between teacher and student happens inside the platform under the supervision of our management, and neither side's phone number is shown to the other.
  • Messages and voice recordings are seen only by the student and their parent, the teacher, the supervisor and the management.
  • Children's accounts are created by the parent in coordination with the management, and we never ask a child for data directly.

4. Cookies

We use cookies that are necessary for the website to work (such as logging in and protecting forms), and analytics cookies from Google Analytics to understand visitor numbers and the most visited pages. You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings, but some features such as logging in may then stop working.

5. Advertising (Google AdSense)

  • Ads from Google may appear on some public pages of the website. They never appear inside the student portal or the teacher portal.
  • Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party ads at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

6. Third parties we work with

  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager: visit analytics.
  • Google AdSense: ads on public pages.
  • Firebase by Google: sending notifications to those who allowed them.
  • Cloudinary: storing images and files.
  • Railway: hosting the website and database.
  • YouTube and EveryAyah: playing videos and Quran recitations on the Quran pages.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

7. How long we keep data

We keep a student's data for as long as they are subscribed, and for a reasonable period afterwards to refer back to reports and accounts. Trial class requests that did not go ahead may be deleted after one year.

8. Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or cancel any notifications. Send your request through the contact page and we will reply as soon as possible.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page if our services or the law change, and the date of the last update is shown at the top.
