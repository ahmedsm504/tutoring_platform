Our goal is for every article and answer on this website to be useful, accurate and something a parent or student can actually apply. This is how we work.

Who writes the content

Our content is written by the Al-Agmi teaching team, drawing on the experience of our certified teachers in teaching children and adults, and on real questions we receive from parents.

The sources we rely on

  • The Quran text: in the Uthmani script, according to the narration of Hafs from Asim.
  • Tajweed rules: as set out in Tuhfat al-Atfal and Al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah and their recognised explanations, via the Shatibiyyah route.
  • Hadith: we only cite authentic, established hadith, naming who narrated them.
  • Upbringing and memorisation methods: from our practical classroom experience, without exaggerated promises.

Review before publishing

Any article containing Tajweed rules, verses or hadith is reviewed before publishing to check the text and the ruling. We do not issue fatwas on personal fiqh matters, and we always advise referring to qualified scholars for them.

If you find a mistake

If you notice a mistake in a verse, a ruling or any information, tell us through the contact page. We will review it, correct it and update the article's date.
