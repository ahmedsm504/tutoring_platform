Surah Al-Qamar begins with a miracle that the people of Makkah saw with their own eyes: "The Hour has come near, and the moon has split." Then it moves at great speed through the stories of nations who denied their messengers: Nuh, 'Ad, Thamud, Lut and Pharaoh, each told in a few vivid strokes. After each story comes a refrain: "And We have certainly made the Quran easy for remembrance, so is there any who will remember?" The Quran is easy to memorise, easy to understand and easy to take lessons from. The only question is whether anyone will take the lesson. The Prophet ﷺ recited this surah in the Eid prayers.

This guide explains Surah Al-Qamar section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Qamar
- **Number of verses**: 55.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 54, in the twenty-seventh juz.
- **Name**: Al-Qamar means "the moon", from the first verse.
- **Recited in Eid**: in Sahih Muslim, Abu Waqid al-Laythi said the Prophet ﷺ recited Qaf and Iqtarabat as-Sa'ah (Al-Qamar) in the Eid prayers.
- **Main themes**: the nearness of the Hour, the fate of deniers, the ease of the Quran, and divine decree.

## Verses 1–8: The Moon Split
﴿ٱقۡتَرَبَتِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ وَٱنشَقَّ ٱلۡقَمَرُ﴾ ﴿وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ ءَايَةٗ يُعۡرِضُواْ وَيَقُولُواْ سِحۡرࣱ مُّسۡتَمِرࣱّ﴾ ﴿وَكَذَّبُواْ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓاْ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡۚ وَكُلُّ أَمۡرٖ مُّسۡتَقِرࣱّ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَنۢبَآءِ مَا فِيهِ مُزۡدَجَرٌ﴾ ﴿حِكۡمَةُۢ بَٰلِغَةࣱۖ فَمَا تُغۡنِ ٱلنُّذُرُ﴾ (Quran 54:1-5)

**"The Hour has come near, and the moon has split."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud said: "The moon split in two while we were with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ at Mina, and he said: Bear witness." Anas also reported that the people of Makkah asked the Prophet ﷺ to show them a sign, so he showed them the moon split in two.

**Their reaction**: **"If they see a sign, they turn away and say: Passing magic."** They denied and followed their desires. **"And every matter will be settled"**: everything reaches its final end.

﴿فَتَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡۘ يَوۡمَ يَدۡعُ ٱلدَّاعِ إِلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ نُّكُرٍ﴾ ﴿خُشَّعًا أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ يَخۡرُجُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ كَأَنَّهُمۡ جَرَادࣱ مُّنتَشِرࣱ﴾ ﴿مُّهۡطِعِينَ إِلَى ٱلدَّاعِۖ يَقُولُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا يَوۡمٌ عَسِرࣱ﴾ (Quran 54:6-8)

**The Day of the call**: they will come out of the graves with eyes humbled, **"as if they were locusts spreading"**, hastening towards the caller. The disbelievers will say: **"This is a difficult Day."**

## Verses 9–17: The People of Nuh
﴿۞كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ فَكَذَّبُواْ عَبۡدَنَا وَقَالُواْ مَجۡنُونࣱ وَٱزۡدُجِرَ﴾ ﴿فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَغۡلُوبࣱ فَٱنتَصِرۡ﴾ ﴿فَفَتَحۡنَآ أَبۡوَٰبَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَآءٖ مُّنۡهَمِرٖ﴾ ﴿وَفَجَّرۡنَا ٱلۡأَرۡضَ عُيُونٗا فَٱلۡتَقَى ٱلۡمَآءُ عَلَىٰٓ أَمۡرٖ قَدۡ قُدِرَ﴾ ﴿وَحَمَلۡنَٰهُ عَلَىٰ ذَاتِ أَلۡوَٰحٖ وَدُسُرٖ﴾ ﴿تَجۡرِي بِأَعۡيُنِنَا جَزَآءٗ لِّمَن كَانَ كُفِرَ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَد تَّرَكۡنَٰهَآ ءَايَةٗ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ﴾ ﴿فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ﴾ (Quran 54:9-17)

**Nuh** was denied and called mad, and driven back. He called his Lord: **"Indeed, I am overpowered, so help."** Allah **opened the gates of the heaven with rain pouring down** and **caused the earth to burst with springs**, and the waters met for a matter predestined. **"And We carried him on a construction of planks and nails, sailing under Our eyes."** **"And We left it as a sign, so is there any who will remember?"**

**The refrain appears for the first time**: **"And We have certainly made the Quran easy for remembrance, so is there any who will remember?"**

## Verses 18–22: 'Ad
﴿كَذَّبَتۡ عَادࣱ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ رِيحٗا صَرۡصَرٗا فِي يَوۡمِ نَحۡسٖ مُّسۡتَمِرّٖ﴾ ﴿تَنزِعُ ٱلنَّاسَ كَأَنَّهُمۡ أَعۡجَازُ نَخۡلٖ مُّنقَعِرٖ﴾ ﴿فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ﴾ (Quran 54:18-22)

**'Ad** denied. **"We sent upon them a screaming wind on a day of continuous misfortune, extracting the people as if they were trunks of palm trees uprooted."** Then the refrain again.

## Verses 23–32: Thamud
﴿كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ بِٱلنُّذُرِ﴾ ﴿فَقَالُوٓاْ أَبَشَرٗا مِّنَّا وَٰحِدٗا نَّتَّبِعُهُۥٓ إِنَّآ إِذٗا لَّفِي ضَلَٰلٖ وَسُعُرٍ﴾ ﴿أَءُلۡقِيَ ٱلذِّكۡرُ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۢ بَيۡنِنَا بَلۡ هُوَ كَذَّابٌ أَشِرࣱ﴾ ﴿سَيَعۡلَمُونَ غَدٗا مَّنِ ٱلۡكَذَّابُ ٱلۡأَشِرُ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا مُرۡسِلُواْ ٱلنَّاقَةِ فِتۡنَةٗ لَّهُمۡ فَٱرۡتَقِبۡهُمۡ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ﴾ ﴿وَنَبِّئۡهُمۡ أَنَّ ٱلۡمَآءَ قِسۡمَةُۢ بَيۡنَهُمۡۖ كُلُّ شِرۡبٖ مُّحۡتَضَرࣱ﴾ ﴿فَنَادَوۡاْ صَاحِبَهُمۡ فَتَعَاطَىٰ فَعَقَرَ﴾ ﴿فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ صَيۡحَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ فَكَانُواْ كَهَشِيمِ ٱلۡمُحۡتَظِرِ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ﴾ (Quran 54:23-32)

**Thamud** said: **"Is it a human being from among us that we should follow? Then we would be in error and madness."** They called Salih "an insolent liar". Allah sent **the she-camel as a trial**, with water shared between them. **"But they called their companion, and he dared and hamstrung her."** **"We sent upon them one blast, and they became like the dry twig fragments of a fence-builder."**

## Verses 33–40: The People of Lut
﴿كَذَّبَتۡ قَوۡمُ لُوطِۭ بِٱلنُّذُرِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَاصِبًا إِلَّآ ءَالَ لُوطٖۖ نَّجَّيۡنَٰهُم بِسَحَرٖ﴾ ﴿نِّعۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي مَن شَكَرَ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَنذَرَهُم بَطۡشَتَنَا فَتَمَارَوۡاْ بِٱلنُّذُرِ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ رَٰوَدُوهُ عَن ضَيۡفِهِۦ فَطَمَسۡنَآ أَعۡيُنَهُمۡ فَذُوقُواْ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ صَبَّحَهُم بُكۡرَةً عَذَابࣱ مُّسۡتَقِرࣱّ﴾ ﴿فَذُوقُواْ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ﴾ (Quran 54:33-40)

**The people of Lut** denied the warnings. Allah sent upon them **a storm of stones, except the family of Lut, whom We saved before dawn**, as a favour. They demanded his guests, **"so We blinded their eyes"**. **"And there came upon them by morning an abiding punishment."**

## Verses 41–42: Pharaoh
﴿وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَ ءَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ ٱلنُّذُرُ﴾ ﴿كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا كُلِّهَا فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُمۡ أَخۡذَ عَزِيزٖ مُّقۡتَدِرٍ﴾ (Quran 54:41-42)

**The people of Pharaoh** received the warnings, denied all Our signs, **"so We seized them with a seizure of one Exalted in Might and Perfect in Ability"**.

## Verses 43–48: A Prophecy of Badr
﴿أَكُفَّارُكُمۡ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكُمۡ أَمۡ لَكُم بَرَآءَةࣱ فِي ٱلزُّبُرِ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ نَحۡنُ جَمِيعࣱ مُّنتَصِرࣱ﴾ ﴿سَيُهۡزَمُ ٱلۡجَمۡعُ وَيُوَلُّونَ ٱلدُّبُرَ﴾ ﴿بَلِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ مَوۡعِدُهُمۡ وَٱلسَّاعَةُ أَدۡهَىٰ وَأَمَرُّ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ وَسُعُرٖ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يُسۡحَبُونَ فِي ٱلنَّارِ عَلَىٰ وُجُوهِهِمۡ ذُوقُواْ مَسَّ سَقَرَ﴾ (Quran 54:43-48)

**"Are your disbelievers better than those?"** Or do you have immunity in the scriptures? **"Or do they say: We are an assembly supporting each other? Their assembly will be defeated, and they will turn their backs in retreat."** This was revealed in Makkah when the Muslims were weak. In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas reported that on the Day of Badr, the Prophet ﷺ came out of his tent saying: "Their assembly will be defeated and they will turn their backs in retreat." A prophecy fulfilled years later.

**"But the Hour is their appointment, and the Hour is more disastrous and more bitter."** The criminals will be dragged on their faces into the Fire: **"Taste the touch of Saqar."**

## Verses 49–50: Everything by Measure
﴿إِنَّا كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَٰهُ بِقَدَرٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَمۡرُنَآ إِلَّا وَٰحِدَةࣱ كَلَمۡحِۭ بِٱلۡبَصَرِ﴾ (Quran 54:49-50)

**"Indeed, all things We created with predestination."** In Sahih Muslim, Abu Hurayrah said: "The idolaters of Quraysh came disputing with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ about destiny, and this verse was revealed." Belief in *qadar* is one of the six pillars of faith. **"And Our command is but one, like a glance of the eye."**

## Verses 51–55: The Seat of Honour
﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَآ أَشۡيَاعَكُمۡ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ﴾ ﴿وَكُلُّ شَيۡءٖ فَعَلُوهُ فِي ٱلزُّبُرِ﴾ ﴿وَكُلُّ صَغِيرٖ وَكَبِيرٖ مُّسۡتَطَرٌ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ وَنَهَرٖ﴾ ﴿فِي مَقۡعَدِ صِدۡقٍ عِندَ مَلِيكٖ مُّقۡتَدِرِۭ﴾ (Quran 54:51-55)

**"Everything they did is in written records, and every small and great thing is inscribed."** Nothing is forgotten.

**"Indeed, the righteous will be among gardens and rivers, in a seat of honour near a Sovereign, Perfect in Ability."** *Maq'ad sidq*, a seat of truth and honour, near *Malik Muqtadir*, the Sovereign who has all power. The surah that began with the moon splitting ends with the highest place of honour.

## "Easy for Remembrance": What Does It Mean?
The refrain is repeated four times, after the stories of Nuh, 'Ad, Thamud and Lut. Scholars explained "easy for remembrance" in three ways:
1. **Easy to memorise**: millions of people, including children and non-Arabs, have memorised the whole Quran. No other book in history is memorised like it.
2. **Easy to understand**: its lessons are clear for those who reflect.
3. **Easy to take lessons from**: its stories speak to every generation.

**"So is there any who will remember?"**: the ease is on Allah's side; the effort of remembering is on ours.

## Word Meanings
- **Iqtarabat**: has come near.
- **Inshaqqa**: split.
- **Mustamirr**: passing, continuing.
- **Muhti'in**: hastening.
- **Munhamir**: pouring down.
- **Dusur**: nails.
- **Muddakir**: one who remembers, takes heed.
- **Sarsar**: screaming, freezing.
- **Munqa'ir**: uprooted.
- **Ashir**: insolent.
- **Hashim al-muhtazir**: dry twig fragments of a fence-builder.
- **Hasib**: storm of stones.
- **Zubur**: written records.
- **Qadar**: measure, predestination.
- **Maq'ad sidq**: seat of honour.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The opening miracle**: two short sentences, one about the future and one about a sign already seen.
2. **"Like locusts spreading"**: a picture of countless people rushing.
3. **The refrain**: four times, like a recurring call.
4. **"How was My punishment and My warning?"**: a question after each story.
5. **"Like a glance of the eye"**: the speed of Allah's command.
6. **"A seat of honour near a Sovereign"**: the most dignified ending.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 11).
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *bi-ma'in munhamir* (verse 11), the tanween merges into the meem; and in *sayhatan wahidatan* (verse 31), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *muqtadir* (verses 42 and 55), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna*, on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah An-Najm before it ended with "The Approaching Day has approached", and Al-Qamar opens with "The Hour has come near". Surah Ar-Rahman after it begins with "He taught the Quran", matching Al-Qamar's "We have made the Quran easy".

## Making the Quran Easy in Your Life
1. **Memorise a little daily**: the Quran is made easy.
2. **Read a translation and a short tafsir** to understand.
3. **Take one lesson from each story.**
4. **Teach children early**: their memory is strong.
5. **Revise regularly**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said the Quran escapes the heart faster than a camel from its rope.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Believe in the miracles of the Prophet ﷺ.**
2. **Learn from nations who were destroyed.**
3. **Do not dismiss signs as "magic".**
4. **Believe in qadar**: everything is by measure.
5. **Remember that every deed is recorded.**
6. **Aim for the seat of honour.**
7. **Be among those who remember.**

## Teaching Children
- **Look at the moon together** and tell them about its splitting.
- **Teach them the refrain** and let them say it each time it appears.
- **Tell them the story of Nuh's ark** made of planks and nails.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Did the moon really split?
Yes. Ibn Mas'ud and Anas reported it in al-Bukhari and Muslim, and the Prophet ﷺ told the people to bear witness.

### What does "We have made the Quran easy for remembrance" mean?
Allah made the Quran easy to memorise, understand and take lessons from.

### How many times is the refrain repeated?
Four times, after the stories of Nuh, 'Ad, Thamud and Lut.

### What prophecy does the surah contain?
"Their assembly will be defeated and they will turn their backs", fulfilled at Badr, as the Prophet ﷺ recited on that day according to Sahih al-Bukhari.

### What does "all things We created with predestination" mean?
Everything is created by Allah's measure and decree; it was revealed when Quraysh disputed about destiny, as in Sahih Muslim.

### When was Surah Al-Qamar recited?
The Prophet ﷺ recited it with Qaf in the Eid prayers, as in Sahih Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Qamar?
55 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Qamar shows the moon split before people's eyes, and nation after nation who saw signs and still turned away. Four times it asks: the Quran is made easy, so is there anyone who will remember? Be one who remembers, believe that everything is by measure, and work for the seat of honour near the Sovereign, Perfect in Ability.
