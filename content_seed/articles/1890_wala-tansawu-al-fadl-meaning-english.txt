﴿وَأَن تَعۡفُوٓاْ أَقۡرَبُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۚ وَلَا تَنسَوُاْ ٱلۡفَضۡلَ بَيۡنَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:237]

## The context
- The verse discusses divorce **before consummation** after a dowry was set: half of the dowry is due, unless one side pardons the rest.

## Pardon is nearer to taqwa
- Giving up part of your right voluntarily is closer to piety than insisting on every detail.

## "Do not forget graciousness"
- *Fadl* is kindness beyond what is owed. Even when a relationship ends, do not forget the good that was between you.

## A model for all separations
| Situation | Application |
|---|---|
| Divorce | No humiliation, no exposing secrets |
| Ending a partnership | Fair settlement and kind words |
| Leaving a job | Gratitude and honesty |

## Allah sees
- *Allah sees what you do*: how you treat people at the end shows your character.

## Lessons
- **End things gracefully.**
- **Be generous** with your rights when you can.
