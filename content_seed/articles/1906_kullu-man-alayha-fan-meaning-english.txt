## "Everyone on Earth Will Perish, and the Face of Your Lord Remains": Quran 55:26-27 Explained

﴿كُلُّ مَنۡ عَلَيۡهَا فَانٖ﴾ ﴿وَيَبۡقَىٰ وَجۡهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ﴾ [Ar-Rahman 55:26-27]

### Everything passes
- Everyone on earth, every king, building and empire, **will perish**.

### The One who remains
- *The Face of your Lord remains*: Allah alone is eternal. Ahl al-Sunnah affirm the Face of Allah as befits His majesty, without likening it to creation.

### *Dhul-Jalali wal-Ikram*
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Jalal* | Majesty, greatness |
| *Ikram* | Honour and generosity to His servants |

- The Prophet ﷺ encouraged using this name in duʿa: *Ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### Why among the blessings?
- After these verses comes the refrain *Which of the favours of your Lord will you deny?* Death leads to recompense and justice, and the eternity of Allah means His promise remains.

### Lessons
- **Attach your heart** to the One who remains.
- **Use this name** in your duʿa.

## Surah Ar-Rahman Meaning: "Which of the Favours of Your Lord Will You Deny?"

**Surah Ar-Rahman** (78 ayat) opens with one of Allah's names and then lists His gifts, again and again asking humans and jinn a single question.

### The refrain
﴿فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ﴾ — *So which of the favours of your Lord will you both deny?*
- Repeated **31 times**.
- **"You both"**: humans and jinn.

### The structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | The Most Merciful taught the Quran, created man and taught him speech |
| 5–13 | The sun and moon, the stars and trees, the balance, the earth and its fruits |
| 14–25 | Creation of man and jinn, the two easts and two wests, the two seas, ships |
| 26–30 | Everything perishes except the Face of your Lord |
| 31–45 | The Day of Judgement and the fate of the criminals |
| 46–61 | Two gardens for those who feared standing before their Lord |
| 62–78 | Two more gardens, and the blessing of His name |

### Key ayat
﴿كُلُّ مَنۡ عَلَيۡهَا فَانٖ﴾ ﴿وَيَبۡقَىٰ وَجۡهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ﴾ [Ar-Rahman: 26–27]
﴿هَلۡ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ إِلَّا ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنُ﴾ [Ar-Rahman: 60]

### The jinn's answer
- The Prophet ﷺ recited this surah to his companions and said the jinn had answered better: each time he recited the refrain they said, "None of Your favours, our Lord, do we deny; to You belongs all praise" [At-Tirmidhi].

### Lessons
- **Count your blessings** daily.
- **Answer the refrain** in your heart when you hear it.

## Surah Al-Waqi'ah Meaning: The Three Groups on the Day of Judgement

**Surah Al-Waqi'ah** (The Event, 96 ayat) describes the Day of Judgement and divides people into **three groups**.

﴿إِذَا وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ﴾ ﴿لَيۡسَ لِوَقۡعَتِهَا كَاذِبَةٌ﴾ ﴿خَافِضَةࣱ رَّافِعَةٌ﴾
- **Lowering, raising**: it brings down the arrogant and raises the humble believers.

### The three groups
| Group | Ayat | Description |
|---|---|---|
| The foremost (as-sābiqūn) | 10–26 | Nearest to Allah, in gardens of bliss |
| Companions of the right | 27–40 | Shade, water and fruits |
| Companions of the left | 41–56 | Scorching wind and boiling water |

### Signs of creation
| Ayat | Sign | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 57–62 | Your own creation | Who created it? |
| 63–67 | The seeds you plant | Who makes them grow? |
| 68–70 | The water you drink | Who sends it down? |
| 71–74 | The fire you kindle | Who made its tree? |

### The noble Quran
﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَقُرۡءَانࣱ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فِي كِتَٰبٖ مَّكۡنُونٖ﴾ ﴿لَّا يَمَسُّهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُطَهَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلࣱ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ [Al-Waqi'ah: 77–80]
- Many commentators said "the purified" here are the **angels**, and scholars also took from it respect for the Mushaf.

### A note on its virtue
- A hadith that reading Al-Waqi'ah every night protects from poverty is **weak** according to many hadith scholars.
- Reading it with reflection remains a great act of worship.

## Surah Al-Hadid Meaning: Has the Time Not Come for Hearts to Humble?

**Surah Al-Hadid** (29 ayat) is the first of the **musabbiḥāt**, the surahs that begin with glorification of Allah.

### Who is Allah?
﴿هُوَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُ وَٱلۡأٓخِرُ وَٱلظَّـٰهِرُ وَٱلۡبَاطِنُۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٌ﴾
- *He is with you wherever you are* [Al-Hadid: 4].

### The question
﴿۞أَلَمۡ يَأۡنِ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَن تَخۡشَعَ قُلُوبُهُمۡ لِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا نَزَلَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّ﴾
- *Has the time not come for the believers that their hearts humble for the remembrance of Allah?*
- **A warning**: don't be like those whose hearts hardened over a long time.

### The reality of this world
﴿ٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لَعِبࣱ وَلَهۡوࣱ وَزِينَةࣱ وَتَفَاخُرُۢ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَتَكَاثُرࣱ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَوۡلَٰدِۖ﴾
| Stage | Like |
|---|---|
| Play and amusement | Childhood |
| Adornment | Youth |
| Boasting and competing | Adulthood |
- Then it withers like crops after rain.

### Iron
﴿وَأَنزَلۡنَا ٱلۡحَدِيدَ فِيهِ بَأۡسࣱ شَدِيدࣱ وَمَنَٰفِعُ لِلنَّاسِ﴾
- **Iron**: strength and benefits, alongside the Book and the balance for justice.

### Lessons
- **Soften your heart** with regular remembrance.
- **Spend** before it's too late.

## Quran 57:16 Explained: Before the Heart Grows Hard

﴿۞أَلَمۡ يَأۡنِ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَن تَخۡشَعَ قُلُوبُهُمۡ لِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا نَزَلَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا يَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَطَالَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡأَمَدُ فَقَسَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡۖ وَكَثِيرࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ﴾ [Al-Hadid 57:16]

### A gentle question
- *Has the time not come?* It is addressed to **believers**, a call to wake up, not a condemnation.

### Two things that should humble hearts
| What | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The remembrance of Allah | His names, His blessings, His warnings |
| What came down of the truth | The Quran |

### The danger of time
- *The period became long for them, and their hearts hardened.* Long gaps from the Quran and from repentance make hearts hard.

### Fudayl ibn ʿIyad
- It is reported that Fudayl was on his way to commit a sin when he heard someone recite this verse. He said: *Yes, my Lord, the time has come*, and he repented, later becoming a famous worshipper and scholar.

### The next verse
- *Know that Allah gives life to the earth after its death.* Hard hearts can be revived, like dry earth after rain.

### Lessons
- **Do not wait** for "later" to change.
- **Do not let distance from the Quran grow long.**

## "So You Do Not Grieve over What You Missed": Quran 57:22-23 Explained

﴿مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن نَّبۡرَأَهَآۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿لِّكَيۡلَا تَأۡسَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَفۡرَحُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالٖ فَخُورٍ﴾ [Al-Hadid 57:22-23]

### Written before
- Every calamity on earth or in ourselves is recorded **before** creation.

### Why tell us?
| Purpose | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Do not despair over loss | It was not meant for you |
| Do not exult over gain | It was given, not earned alone |

### What kind of joy is forbidden?
- Scholars clarify: the forbidden joy is **arrogant pride**, not gratitude. Natural sadness is fine; despair is not.

### The ending
- *Allah does not love the arrogant and boastful*: pride in gains is the danger.

### Lessons
- **Hold blessings lightly** and losses calmly.
- **Thank Allah** for gains without pride.

## "Allah Has Heard the Speech of the Woman Who Argued with You": Quran 58:1 Explained

﴿قَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّتِي تُجَٰدِلُكَ فِي زَوۡجِهَا وَتَشۡتَكِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَٱللَّهُ يَسۡمَعُ تَحَاوُرَكُمَآۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعُۢ بَصِيرٌ﴾ [Al-Mujadila 58:1]

### The story
- Aws ibn as-Samit declared *zihar* on his wife Khawlah bint Thaʿlabah, saying she was to him like his mother's back, which in pre-Islamic times meant divorce.
- She came to the Prophet ﷺ to complain, and the opening of Al-Mujadila was revealed with the ruling and expiation of zihar.

### Allah's hearing
- ʿAishah said: *Praise be to Allah whose hearing encompasses all voices. The woman who argued came to the Prophet ﷺ and spoke in a corner of the house, and I could not hear what she said, then Allah revealed: "Allah has heard..."* (reported by al-Bukhari in a muʿallaq form, and by al-Nasaʾi and Ibn Majah).

### "She complains to Allah"
- She complained to Allah about her situation and her children, and He answered.

### Protecting the family
- Islam cancelled zihar as divorce and set an expiation, protecting women and families.

### A gem of the surah
- The name *Allah* appears in every one of its twenty-two verses.

### Lessons
- **Complain to Allah**: He hears you.
- **Speak up** for justice in your family.

## Surah Al-Mujadilah Meaning: Allah Heard the Woman Who Pleaded

**Surah Al-Mujadilah** (22 ayat) begins Juz 28 with a woman's complaint that Allah answered from above the seven heavens.

﴿قَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّتِي تُجَٰدِلُكَ فِي زَوۡجِهَا وَتَشۡتَكِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَٱللَّهُ يَسۡمَعُ تَحَاوُرَكُمَآۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعُۢ بَصِيرٌ﴾

### The story
- **Khawlah bint Thaʿlabah**'s husband told her "you are to me like my mother's back", a pre-Islamic form of divorce called *ẓihār*.
- She came to the Prophet ﷺ and pleaded. ʿAishah said: "Blessed is the One whose hearing encompasses all voices", as she had been in the room yet could not hear all of it.

### The ruling on zihar
| Expiation, in order | |
|---|---|
| 1 | Freeing a slave |
| 2 | Fasting two consecutive months |
| 3 | Feeding sixty poor people |

### Etiquette in gatherings
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمۡ تَفَسَّحُواْ فِي ٱلۡمَجَٰلِسِ فَٱفۡسَحُواْ يَفۡسَحِ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ﴾
- **Make room**, and Allah will make room for you.
- *Allah raises those who believe among you and those given knowledge by degrees.*
- **Private talk**: not in sin and aggression, but in righteousness and piety.

### Lessons
- **Take your complaints to Allah**: He hears.
- **Knowledge raises** its people.

## "Allah Raises Those Who Believe and Those Given Knowledge by Degrees": Quran 58:11 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمۡ تَفَسَّحُواْ فِي ٱلۡمَجَٰلِسِ فَٱفۡسَحُواْ يَفۡسَحِ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ وَإِذَا قِيلَ ٱنشُزُواْ فَٱنشُزُواْ يَرۡفَعِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ دَرَجَٰتٖۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ [Al-Mujadila 58:11]

### Etiquette of gatherings
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Make room | Widen space for your brothers |
| Rise | Stand when asked |

### Reward like the deed
- Make room for others, and **Allah makes room** for you. Rise humbly, and **Allah raises** you.

### Two elevations
- Faith raises a person; knowledge with faith raises him **degrees higher**.

### The path of knowledge
- *Whoever travels a path seeking knowledge, Allah makes easy for him a path to Paradise* (narrated by Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Make room** in the mosque and the class.
- **Seek knowledge**: it is the way to elevation.

## Surah Al-Hashr Meaning: The Generous Ansar and the Beautiful Names of Allah

**Surah Al-Hashr** (24 ayat) moves from history to the heart, ending with some of the most majestic ayat about Allah.

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | The expulsion of Banu an-Nadir |
| 6–10 | How wealth is distributed; Muhajirun, Ansar and those after them |
| 11–17 | The hypocrites and their false promises |
| 18–20 | Look at what you have sent ahead |
| 21–24 | The Quran on a mountain, and Allah's names |

### The Ansar
﴿وَيُؤۡثِرُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَلَوۡ كَانَ بِهِمۡ خَصَاصَةࣱۚ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفۡسِهِۦ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾
- *They prefer others over themselves, even when in need.*

### The Quran on a mountain
﴿لَوۡ أَنزَلۡنَا هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ عَلَىٰ جَبَلٖ لَّرَأَيۡتَهُۥ خَٰشِعٗا مُّتَصَدِّعٗا مِّنۡ خَشۡيَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾

### The names of Allah

## "They Prefer Others over Themselves, Even Though They Are in Need": Quran 59:9 Explained

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ تَبَوَّءُو ٱلدَّارَ وَٱلۡإِيمَٰنَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ يُحِبُّونَ مَنۡ هَاجَرَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا يَجِدُونَ فِي صُدُورِهِمۡ حَاجَةٗ مِّمَّآ أُوتُواْ وَيُؤۡثِرُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَلَوۡ كَانَ بِهِمۡ خَصَاصَةࣱۚ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفۡسِهِۦ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ [Al-Hashr 59:9]

### The Ansar's qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| They love those who emigrated | Genuine welcome |
| No envy for what others received | Clean hearts |
| They prefer others over themselves | Ithar |
| Even when in need | Sacrifice, not surplus |

### The guest story
- A man from the Ansar hosted a guest of the Prophet ﷺ with only enough food for his children. He and his wife put the children to sleep, dimmed the lamp, and pretended to eat so the guest could eat. Allah praised them, and this verse is mentioned with the story (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Protection from greed
- *Whoever is protected from the stinginess of his soul, those are the successful.*

### Lessons
- **Give when it costs you.**
- **Clean your heart** from envy.

## "Our Lord, Forgive Us and Our Brothers Who Preceded Us in Faith": Quran 59:10 Explained

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَلِإِخۡوَٰنِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ فِي قُلُوبِنَا غِلّٗا لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ رَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمٌ﴾ [Al-Hashr 59:10]

### Three groups
| Verse | Group |
|---|---|
| 59:8 | The Emigrants |
| 59:9 | The Helpers |
| 59:10 | Those who came after them |

- The third group includes every believer until the Day of Judgment who carries this quality.

### Their qualities
- They seek forgiveness for those who preceded them in faith, and ask Allah to clean their hearts of **ghill** (rancour).

### Love for the Companions
- Scholars note that the verse shows loving and praying for the Companions is part of complete faith.

### Lessons
- **Pray for those before you**: parents, teachers, the Companions.
- **Check your heart** for resentment toward any believer.

## "Let Every Soul Look to What It Has Sent Forth for Tomorrow": Quran 59:18 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَلۡتَنظُرۡ نَفۡسࣱ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ لِغَدٖۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ [Al-Hashr 59:18]

### Taqwa twice
- Scholars explain: the first taqwa is to **do** what is commanded and leave what is forbidden; the second is to keep watch and correct what slipped.

### "Tomorrow"
- *Ghad* refers to the Day of Judgment. It is called *tomorrow* to show **how near** it is.

### Self-accountability
- ʿUmar ibn al-Khattab is reported to have said: *Hold yourselves to account before you are held to account.*

### How to practise
| Time | Question |
|---|---|
| Daily, before sleep | What good did I do? What should I repent from? |
| Weekly | Did I keep my Quran and prayer routine? |
| Monthly | Am I closer to Allah than last month? |

### The next verse
- *And do not be like those who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves.*

### Lessons
- **Review your day** before sleeping.
- **Send something forward** every day.

## "Had We Sent This Quran upon a Mountain": Quran 59:21 Explained

﴿لَوۡ أَنزَلۡنَا هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ عَلَىٰ جَبَلٖ لَّرَأَيۡتَهُۥ خَٰشِعٗا مُّتَصَدِّعٗا مِّنۡ خَشۡيَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلُ نَضۡرِبُهَا لِلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ [Al-Hashr 59:21]

### The words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Khashiʿan* | Humbled |
| *Mutasaddiʿan* | Split apart |
| *Min khashyati Allah* | Out of awe of Allah |

### The parable
- If a mountain could understand like a human and the Quran were revealed to it, it would humble itself and split apart. The point: **why do human hearts stay hard?**

### Strength and softness
- The mountain is the symbol of firmness, yet it would crack. What about the human heart?

### The names that follow
- The surah ends with the names of Allah: *He is Allah, there is no god but Him* (59:22-24). Knowing Allah through His names softens hearts.

### Lessons
- **Recite with a present heart.**
- **Ask Allah** to soften your heart.
