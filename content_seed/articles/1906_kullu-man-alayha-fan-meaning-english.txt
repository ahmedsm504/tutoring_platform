﴿كُلُّ مَنۡ عَلَيۡهَا فَانٖ﴾ ﴿وَيَبۡقَىٰ وَجۡهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ﴾ [Ar-Rahman 55:26-27]

## Everything passes
- Everyone on earth, every king, building and empire, **will perish**.

## The One who remains
- *The Face of your Lord remains*: Allah alone is eternal. Ahl al-Sunnah affirm the Face of Allah as befits His majesty, without likening it to creation.

## *Dhul-Jalali wal-Ikram*
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Jalal* | Majesty, greatness |
| *Ikram* | Honour and generosity to His servants |

- The Prophet ﷺ encouraged using this name in duʿa: *Ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

## Why among the blessings?
- After these verses comes the refrain *Which of the favours of your Lord will you deny?* Death leads to recompense and justice, and the eternity of Allah means His promise remains.

## Lessons
- **Attach your heart** to the One who remains.
- **Use this name** in your duʿa.
