﴿وَقُولُواْ لِلنَّاسِ حُسۡنٗا﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:83]

## The covenant
- The verse mentions a covenant taken from the Children of Israel with eight items:
| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Worship only Allah | Tawhid |
| Kindness to parents | Right after tawhid |
| Relatives | Keeping ties |
| Orphans | Care for the vulnerable |
| The poor | Support |
| **Speak good to people** | Gentle words for everyone |
| Prayer | Connection to Allah |
| Zakah | Purifying wealth |

## "To people"
- The verse says **people**, not only believers. Good speech is owed to everyone: Muslim and non-Muslim, friend and stranger.

## What is *husn*?
- Scholars explain it as truthful, gentle words, enjoining good and forbidding evil kindly, and avoiding harshness and insults.

## A lesson for us
- The covenant was broken by many: *then you turned away, except a few*. The Quran tells us so we do not repeat it.

## Lessons
- **Your words are part of your religion.**
- **Be kind in speech**, even in disagreement.
