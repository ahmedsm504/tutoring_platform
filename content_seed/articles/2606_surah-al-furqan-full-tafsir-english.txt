Surah Al-Furqan ends with one of the most beloved portraits in the Quran: the servants of the Most Merciful (*'Ibad ar-Rahman*). They walk on the earth humbly, answer the ignorant with peace, spend their nights in prostration, are balanced in spending, avoid shirk, murder and adultery, repent so sincerely that Allah turns their bad deeds into good ones, and pray for spouses and children who will be a comfort to their eyes. Before reaching them, the surah defends the Quran as *al-Furqan*, the Criterion between truth and falsehood, answers the objections of the deniers, shows a wrongdoer biting his hands in regret over a friend who misled him, and records the Prophet's ﷺ painful complaint: "My Lord, indeed my people have taken this Quran as a thing abandoned."

This guide explains Surah Al-Furqan section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Furqan
- **Number of verses**: 77.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 25, in the eighteenth and nineteenth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Furqan means "the Criterion", a name of the Quran that separates truth from falsehood.
- **Prostration of recitation**: at verse 60.
- **A famous hadith**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'Umar heard Hisham ibn Hakim recite Al-Furqan differently from how he had learned it and brought him to the Prophet ﷺ, who approved both readings and said: "This Quran was revealed in seven modes, so recite what is easy of it."

## Verses 1–3: The Criterion
﴿تَبَارَكَ ٱلَّذِي نَزَّلَ ٱلۡفُرۡقَانَ عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِۦ لِيَكُونَ لِلۡعَٰلَمِينَ نَذِيرًا﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَمۡ يَتَّخِذۡ وَلَدٗا وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ شَرِيكࣱ فِي ٱلۡمُلۡكِ وَخَلَقَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖ فَقَدَّرَهُۥ تَقۡدِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ءَالِهَةٗ لَّا يَخۡلُقُونَ شَيۡـٔٗا وَهُمۡ يُخۡلَقُونَ وَلَا يَمۡلِكُونَ لِأَنفُسِهِمۡ ضَرّٗا وَلَا نَفۡعٗا وَلَا يَمۡلِكُونَ مَوۡتٗا وَلَا حَيَوٰةٗ وَلَا نُشُورٗا﴾ (Quran 25:1-3)

**"Blessed is He who sent down the Criterion upon His servant that he may be to the worlds a warner."** A message for all humanity. **"He to whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and earth, who has not taken a son, and has not had a partner in dominion, and has created each thing and determined it with precise determination."** In contrast, false gods **"create nothing, while they are created"**.

## Verses 4–20: The Objections of the Deniers
﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّآ إِفۡكٌ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُ وَأَعَانَهُۥ عَلَيۡهِ قَوۡمٌ ءَاخَرُونَۖ فَقَدۡ جَآءُو ظُلۡمٗا وَزُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَقَالُوٓاْ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ ٱكۡتَتَبَهَا فَهِيَ تُمۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ بُكۡرَةٗ وَأَصِيلٗا﴾ ﴿قُلۡ أَنزَلَهُ ٱلَّذِي يَعۡلَمُ ٱلسِّرَّ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ مَالِ هَٰذَا ٱلرَّسُولِ يَأۡكُلُ ٱلطَّعَامَ وَيَمۡشِي فِي ٱلۡأَسۡوَاقِ لَوۡلَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِ مَلَكࣱ فَيَكُونَ مَعَهُۥ نَذِيرًا﴾ ﴿أَوۡ يُلۡقَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِ كَنزٌ أَوۡ تَكُونُ لَهُۥ جَنَّةࣱ يَأۡكُلُ مِنۡهَاۚ وَقَالَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَ إِن تَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا رَجُلٗا مَّسۡحُورًا﴾ ﴿ٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ ضَرَبُواْ لَكَ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلَ فَضَلُّواْ فَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ سَبِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 25:4-9)

They said the Quran was invented with help from others, or legends of former peoples dictated to him. And: **"What is this messenger that eats food and walks in the markets? Why was there not sent down to him an angel, or a treasure, or a garden?"** **"Look how they strike for you comparisons; they have strayed."**

﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ إِلَّآ إِنَّهُمۡ لَيَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ وَيَمۡشُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَسۡوَاقِۗ وَجَعَلۡنَا بَعۡضَكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٖ فِتۡنَةً أَتَصۡبِرُونَۗ وَكَانَ رَبُّكَ بَصِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 25:20)

**The answer**: **"And We did not send before you any of the messengers except that they ate food and walked in the markets."** Messengers are human, so they can be followed. **"And We have made some of you as trial for others: will you have patience?"**

## Verses 21–29: The Regret of the Wrongdoer
﴿وَيَوۡمَ يَعَضُّ ٱلظَّالِمُ عَلَىٰ يَدَيۡهِ يَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي ٱتَّخَذۡتُ مَعَ ٱلرَّسُولِ سَبِيلٗا﴾ ﴿يَٰوَيۡلَتَىٰ لَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أَتَّخِذۡ فُلَانًا خَلِيلٗا﴾ ﴿لَّقَدۡ أَضَلَّنِي عَنِ ٱلذِّكۡرِ بَعۡدَ إِذۡ جَآءَنِيۗ وَكَانَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ خَذُولٗا﴾ (Quran 25:27-29)

**"And the Day the wrongdoer will bite on his hands, he will say: Oh, I wish I had taken with the Messenger a way. Oh, woe to me! I wish I had not taken that one as a friend. He led me away from the remembrance after it had come to me."**

**The lesson about friends**: a bad friend can lead you away from the Quran and the Messenger. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ compared a good companion to a seller of musk and a bad companion to a blacksmith's bellows: from the first you get a pleasant scent, from the second you either get burnt or get a bad smell.

## Verses 30–34: Deserting the Quran
﴿وَقَالَ ٱلرَّسُولُ يَٰرَبِّ إِنَّ قَوۡمِي ٱتَّخَذُواْ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ مَهۡجُورٗا﴾ (Quran 25:30)

**"And the Messenger has said: O my Lord, indeed my people have taken this Quran as a thing abandoned."** Scholars listed forms of abandoning the Quran: not listening to it, not reciting it, not reflecting on it, not acting on it, not seeking healing through it, and not judging by it. Every Muslim should ask himself which of these he has fallen into.

﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡلَا نُزِّلَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ جُمۡلَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗۚ كَذَٰلِكَ لِنُثَبِّتَ بِهِۦ فُؤَادَكَۖ وَرَتَّلۡنَٰهُ تَرۡتِيلٗا﴾ ﴿وَلَا يَأۡتُونَكَ بِمَثَلٍ إِلَّا جِئۡنَٰكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَحۡسَنَ تَفۡسِيرًا﴾ (Quran 25:32-33)

**"Why was the Quran not revealed to him all at once?"** **"Thus it is, that We may strengthen thereby your heart. And We have spaced it distinctly."** Revelation over twenty-three years answered events as they happened and strengthened the Prophet ﷺ step by step.

## Verses 35–44: Nations and the Worship of Desire
﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَجَعَلۡنَا مَعَهُۥٓ أَخَاهُ هَٰرُونَ وَزِيرٗا﴾ ﴿فَقُلۡنَا ٱذۡهَبَآ إِلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا فَدَمَّرۡنَٰهُمۡ تَدۡمِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَقَوۡمَ نُوحٖ لَّمَّا كَذَّبُواْ ٱلرُّسُلَ أَغۡرَقۡنَٰهُمۡ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ لِلنَّاسِ ءَايَةٗۖ وَأَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلظَّـٰلِمِينَ عَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا﴾ ﴿وَعَادٗا وَثَمُودَاْ وَأَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلرَّسِّ وَقُرُونَۢا بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ كَثِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَكُلّٗا ضَرَبۡنَا لَهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلَۖ وَكُلّٗا تَبَّرۡنَا تَتۡبِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَتَوۡاْ عَلَى ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ ٱلَّتِيٓ أُمۡطِرَتۡ مَطَرَ ٱلسَّوۡءِۚ أَفَلَمۡ يَكُونُواْ يَرَوۡنَهَاۚ بَلۡ كَانُواْ لَا يَرۡجُونَ نُشُورٗا﴾ (Quran 25:35-40)

Musa and Harun, the people of Nuh, 'Ad, Thamud, the companions of the well, and many generations: **"each We destroyed with total destruction"**.

﴿أَرَءَيۡتَ مَنِ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَٰهَهُۥ هَوَىٰهُ أَفَأَنتَ تَكُونُ عَلَيۡهِ وَكِيلًا﴾ ﴿أَمۡ تَحۡسَبُ أَنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ يَسۡمَعُونَ أَوۡ يَعۡقِلُونَۚ إِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا كَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ بَلۡ هُمۡ أَضَلُّ سَبِيلًا﴾ (Quran 25:43-44)

**"Have you seen the one who takes as his god his own desire?"** Whatever a person obeys over Allah has become his god. **"Or do you think that most of them hear or reason? They are not except like livestock; rather, they are further astray in way."**

## Verses 45–62: Signs of Creation
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَيۡفَ مَدَّ ٱلظِّلَّ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ لَجَعَلَهُۥ سَاكِنٗا ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَا ٱلشَّمۡسَ عَلَيۡهِ دَلِيلٗا﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ قَبَضۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡنَا قَبۡضٗا يَسِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ لِبَاسٗا وَٱلنَّوۡمَ سُبَاتٗا وَجَعَلَ ٱلنَّهَارَ نُشُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَرۡسَلَ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ بُشۡرَۢا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ طَهُورٗا﴾ ﴿لِّنُحۡـِۧيَ بِهِۦ بَلۡدَةٗ مَّيۡتٗا وَنُسۡقِيَهُۥ مِمَّا خَلَقۡنَآ أَنۡعَٰمٗا وَأَنَاسِيَّ كَثِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ صَرَّفۡنَٰهُ بَيۡنَهُمۡ لِيَذَّكَّرُواْ فَأَبَىٰٓ أَكۡثَرُ ٱلنَّاسِ إِلَّا كُفُورٗا﴾ (Quran 25:45-50)

**The shadow** extended and withdrawn; **the night as clothing**, sleep as rest, the day as resurrection; **winds as good tidings** before His mercy; **pure water from the sky** to give life to a dead land and drink to animals and people.

﴿۞وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي مَرَجَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ هَٰذَا عَذۡبࣱ فُرَاتࣱ وَهَٰذَا مِلۡحٌ أُجَاجࣱ وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَهُمَا بَرۡزَخٗا وَحِجۡرٗا مَّحۡجُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ مِنَ ٱلۡمَآءِ بَشَرٗا فَجَعَلَهُۥ نَسَبٗا وَصِهۡرٗاۗ وَكَانَ رَبُّكَ قَدِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 25:53-54)

**Two seas**, one fresh and sweet and one salty and bitter, with **a barrier and prohibiting partition** between them. **Humans created from water**, made into relations of lineage and marriage.

﴿وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ قَالُواْ وَمَا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ أَنَسۡجُدُ لِمَا تَأۡمُرُنَا وَزَادَهُمۡ نُفُورٗا۩﴾ (Quran 25:60)

**"When it is said to them: Prostrate to the Most Merciful, they say: And what is the Most Merciful?"** Here is the prostration.

﴿تَبَارَكَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ بُرُوجٗا وَجَعَلَ فِيهَا سِرَٰجٗا وَقَمَرٗا مُّنِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ خِلۡفَةٗ لِّمَنۡ أَرَادَ أَن يَذَّكَّرَ أَوۡ أَرَادَ شُكُورٗا﴾ (Quran 25:61-62)

**"Blessed is He who has placed in the sky great stars and placed therein a burning lamp and luminous moon. And it is He who has made the night and the day in succession for whoever desires to remember or desires gratitude."** If you missed a good deed at night, make it up in the day, and vice versa.

## Verses 63–77: The Servants of the Most Merciful
﴿وَعِبَادُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡشُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ هَوۡنٗا وَإِذَا خَاطَبَهُمُ ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ قَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَبِيتُونَ لِرَبِّهِمۡ سُجَّدٗا وَقِيَٰمٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا ٱصۡرِفۡ عَنَّا عَذَابَ جَهَنَّمَۖ إِنَّ عَذَابَهَا كَانَ غَرَامًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّهَا سَآءَتۡ مُسۡتَقَرّٗا وَمُقَامٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَنفَقُواْ لَمۡ يُسۡرِفُواْ وَلَمۡ يَقۡتُرُواْ وَكَانَ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ قَوَامٗا﴾ (Quran 25:63-67)

1. **"Those who walk upon the earth easily"** (*hawnan*): with humility and calm, not arrogance or weakness.
2. **"And when the ignorant address them, they say: Peace."** They do not answer foolishness with foolishness.
3. **"Those who spend the night to their Lord prostrating and standing."**
4. **"Those who say: Our Lord, avert from us the punishment of Hell."** Worship at night with fear by day.
5. **"Those who, when they spend, do so not excessively or sparingly, but are ever between that, justly moderate."**

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَدۡعُونَ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ وَلَا يَقۡتُلُونَ ٱلنَّفۡسَ ٱلَّتِي حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا يَزۡنُونَۚ وَمَن يَفۡعَلۡ ذَٰلِكَ يَلۡقَ أَثَامٗا﴾ ﴿يُضَٰعَفۡ لَهُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَيَخۡلُدۡ فِيهِۦ مُهَانًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يُبَدِّلُ ٱللَّهُ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ حَسَنَٰتٖۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا﴾ ﴿وَمَن تَابَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَإِنَّهُۥ يَتُوبُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَتَابٗا﴾ (Quran 25:68-71)

6. **"Those who do not invoke with Allah another deity,**
7. **nor kill the soul which Allah has forbidden, except by right,**
8. **nor commit unlawful sexual intercourse."**

In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud asked the Prophet ﷺ which sin is greatest. He said: "That you set up a rival to Allah while He created you." Then: "That you kill your child fearing he will eat with you." Then: "That you commit adultery with your neighbour's wife." And this verse was revealed confirming it.

**The door of repentance**: **"Except for those who repent, believe and do righteous work. For them Allah will replace their evil deeds with good."** In Sahih Muslim, Abu Dharr reported a hadith in which a man's minor sins are shown to him and replaced with good deeds, until he asks about his major sins, and the Prophet ﷺ laughed. **"And whoever repents and does righteousness does indeed turn to Allah with accepted repentance."**

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَشۡهَدُونَ ٱلزُّورَ وَإِذَا مَرُّواْ بِٱللَّغۡوِ مَرُّواْ كِرَامٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ لَمۡ يَخِرُّواْ عَلَيۡهَا صُمّٗا وَعُمۡيَانٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعۡيُنٖ وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا﴾ (Quran 25:72-74)

9. **"Those who do not testify to falsehood, and when they pass near ill speech, they pass by with dignity."**
10. **"When reminded of the verses of their Lord, they do not fall upon them deaf and blind."** They listen with understanding.
11. **"Those who say: Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous."** A du'a for the family and for leadership in goodness.

﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يُجۡزَوۡنَ ٱلۡغُرۡفَةَ بِمَا صَبَرُواْ وَيُلَقَّوۡنَ فِيهَا تَحِيَّةٗ وَسَلَٰمًا﴾ ﴿خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ حَسُنَتۡ مُسۡتَقَرّٗا وَمُقَامٗا﴾ ﴿قُلۡ مَا يَعۡبَؤُاْ بِكُمۡ رَبِّي لَوۡلَا دُعَآؤُكُمۡۖ فَقَدۡ كَذَّبۡتُمۡ فَسَوۡفَ يَكُونُ لِزَامَۢا﴾ (Quran 25:75-77)

**Their reward**: **"They will be awarded the chamber for what they patiently endured, and they will be received therein with greetings and peace."** Patience is the key that holds all these qualities together.

**"Say: What would my Lord care for you if not for your supplication?"** Du'a is what gives a person value before Allah.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Blessed is He who sent down the Criterion"**: a majestic opening.
2. **"Bite on his hands"**: regret made physical.
3. **"Taken this Quran as a thing abandoned"**: a complaint that echoes to every age.
4. **"His own desire as his god"**: a precise diagnosis.
5. **"They say: Peace"**: the dignity of not responding to foolishness.
6. **"Evil deeds replaced with good"**: the generosity of forgiveness.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *hawnan wa idha* (verse 63) and *sujjadan wa qiyama* (verse 64), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ma'an tahura* (verse 48).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yaqturu* (verse 67), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Prostration**: at verse 60.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah An-Nur before it ended with "to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and earth", and Al-Furqan opens with "to whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and earth". Surah Ash-Shu'ara' after it tells the stories of messengers rejected by their peoples, continuing Al-Furqan's mention of destroyed nations.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does al-Furqan mean?
The Criterion, a name of the Quran because it separates truth from falsehood.

### Who are 'Ibad ar-Rahman?
The servants of the Most Merciful described in verses 63–77 by their humility, night prayer, moderation, avoidance of major sins, repentance and du'a.

### What does "My people have taken this Quran as a thing abandoned" mean?
The Prophet's ﷺ complaint about people who neglect listening to, reciting, reflecting on and acting on the Quran.

### What does "takes his desire as his god" mean?
Obeying one's desires over Allah's commands, as if worshipping them.

### Can bad deeds really be turned into good?
Yes. Verse 70 says Allah will replace the evil deeds of those who repent, believe and do righteous work with good.

### What is the du'a in verse 74?
"Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous."

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Furqan?
77 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Furqan defends the Quran as the Criterion, warns against the friend who leads you away from it and against abandoning it, and then paints the people every believer should want to resemble: the servants of the Most Merciful. Walk humbly, answer with peace, stand at night, repent sincerely, and pray for your family to be the comfort of your eyes.
