Surah Muhammad is the only surah named after the Prophet ﷺ, and it is also known as Surat al-Qital, the surah of fighting, because it was revealed in Madinah when the young Muslim community faced armed enemies. But its lessons go far beyond the battlefield. It contrasts deeds that Allah wastes and deeds He accepts, promises "If you support Allah, He will support you and plant firmly your feet", describes the four rivers of Paradise, commands "Know that there is no god but Allah, and ask forgiveness for your sin", warns that turning away leads to corruption and cutting family ties, and asks a question every reader of the Quran must face: "Then do they not reflect upon the Quran, or are there locks upon their hearts?"

This guide explains Surah Muhammad section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Muhammad
- **Number of verses**: 38.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 47, in the twenty-sixth juz.
- **Names**: Muhammad, from verse 2, and Al-Qital (fighting), from verse 20.
- **Main themes**: the two paths, supporting Allah's cause, the hypocrites, reflection on the Quran and steadfastness.

## Verses 1–3: Two Paths, Two Outcomes
﴿ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَضَلَّ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَءَامَنُواْ بِمَا نُزِّلَ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡ كَفَّرَ عَنۡهُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ وَأَصۡلَحَ بَالَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱتَّبَعُواْ ٱلۡبَٰطِلَ وَأَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّبَعُواْ ٱلۡحَقَّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ أَمۡثَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:1-3)

**Those who disbelieve and avert people from Allah's way**: **"He will waste their deeds."** Even their apparent good deeds will be lost.

**Those who believe, do righteous deeds and believe in what was revealed to Muhammad**: **"He will remove from them their misdeeds and amend their condition"** (*aslaha balahum*): settle their hearts and set their affairs right.

**The reason**: one group followed falsehood, the other followed the truth from their Lord.

## Verses 4–6: Rulings of Battle
﴿فَإِذَا لَقِيتُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَضَرۡبَ ٱلرِّقَابِ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَثۡخَنتُمُوهُمۡ فَشُدُّواْ ٱلۡوَثَاقَ فَإِمَّا مَنَّۢا بَعۡدُ وَإِمَّا فِدَآءً حَتَّىٰ تَضَعَ ٱلۡحَرۡبُ أَوۡزَارَهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَۖ وَلَوۡ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ لَٱنتَصَرَ مِنۡهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن لِّيَبۡلُوَاْ بَعۡضَكُم بِبَعۡضٖۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ قُتِلُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَلَن يُضِلَّ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿سَيَهۡدِيهِمۡ وَيُصۡلِحُ بَالَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿وَيُدۡخِلُهُمُ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ عَرَّفَهَا لَهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:4-6)

**In battle**, believers are commanded to fight decisively. After victory, captives may be freed as a favour or ransomed, **"until the war lays down its burdens"**. Allah could defeat the enemy without fighting, **"but to test some of you by means of others"**. Those killed in Allah's cause: **"He will never waste their deeds. He will guide them and amend their condition, and admit them to Paradise, which He has made known to them."**

## Verses 7–11: If You Support Allah
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن تَنصُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَنصُرۡكُمۡ وَيُثَبِّتۡ أَقۡدَامَكُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:7)

**"O you who believe, if you support Allah, He will support you and plant firmly your feet."** Supporting Allah means supporting His religion: obeying Him, calling to Him, standing for justice. The reward is victory and steadfastness. This verse applies in every field: the student, the parent, the worker who supports Allah's way in his life finds Allah's help.

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَتَعۡسٗا لَّهُمۡ وَأَضَلَّ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ كَرِهُواْ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ فَأَحۡبَطَ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿۞أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۖ دَمَّرَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ وَلِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ أَمۡثَٰلُهَا﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَوۡلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَأَنَّ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ لَا مَوۡلَىٰ لَهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:8-11)

The disbelievers face misery and lost deeds, **"because they disliked what Allah revealed"**. **"Have they not travelled through the land and seen how was the end of those before them?"** **"That is because Allah is the protector of those who believe, and the disbelievers have no protector."**

## Verses 12–15: The Rivers of Paradise
﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدۡخِلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ جَنَّـٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يَتَمَتَّعُونَ وَيَأۡكُلُونَ كَمَا تَأۡكُلُ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمُ وَٱلنَّارُ مَثۡوٗى لَّهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:12)

**"Those who disbelieve enjoy themselves and eat as cattle eat."** A life of consumption without purpose. **"And the Fire will be a residence for them."**

﴿مَّثَلُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِي وُعِدَ ٱلۡمُتَّقُونَۖ فِيهَآ أَنۡهَٰرࣱ مِّن مَّآءٍ غَيۡرِ ءَاسِنٖ وَأَنۡهَٰرࣱ مِّن لَّبَنٖ لَّمۡ يَتَغَيَّرۡ طَعۡمُهُۥ وَأَنۡهَٰرࣱ مِّنۡ خَمۡرٖ لَّذَّةٖ لِّلشَّـٰرِبِينَ وَأَنۡهَٰرࣱ مِّنۡ عَسَلٖ مُّصَفّٗىۖ وَلَهُمۡ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ وَمَغۡفِرَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡۖ كَمَنۡ هُوَ خَٰلِدࣱ فِي ٱلنَّارِ وَسُقُواْ مَآءً حَمِيمٗا فَقَطَّعَ أَمۡعَآءَهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:15)

**The four rivers of Paradise**:
1. **Rivers of water unaltered**: never stagnant or changed.
2. **Rivers of milk the taste of which never changes.**
3. **Rivers of wine delicious to the drinkers**: free of intoxication and harm.
4. **Rivers of purified honey.**

**"And for them therein is every kind of fruit and forgiveness from their Lord."** Compare them with those **"given to drink scalding water that will sever their intestines"**.

## Verses 16–17: The Hypocrites in the Gatherings
﴿وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَسۡتَمِعُ إِلَيۡكَ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا خَرَجُواْ مِنۡ عِندِكَ قَالُواْ لِلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ مَاذَا قَالَ ءَانِفًاۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ طَبَعَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓاْ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱهۡتَدَوۡاْ زَادَهُمۡ هُدٗى وَءَاتَىٰهُمۡ تَقۡوَىٰهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:16-17)

The hypocrites sat with the Prophet ﷺ, then asked the people of knowledge: "What did he just say?" They were present in body and absent in heart. **"Those are the ones whose hearts Allah has sealed and who have followed their desires."** By contrast, **"those who are guided, He increases them in guidance and gives them their righteousness"**.

## Verses 18–19: Know That There Is No God But Allah
﴿فَهَلۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا ٱلسَّاعَةَ أَن تَأۡتِيَهُم بَغۡتَةٗۖ فَقَدۡ جَآءَ أَشۡرَاطُهَاۚ فَأَنَّىٰ لَهُمۡ إِذَا جَآءَتۡهُمۡ ذِكۡرَىٰهُمۡ﴾ ﴿فَٱعۡلَمۡ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مُتَقَلَّبَكُمۡ وَمَثۡوَىٰكُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:18-19)

**"So know that there is no deity except Allah and ask forgiveness for your sin and for the believing men and believing women."** Imam al-Bukhari titled a chapter with this verse: "Knowledge comes before speech and action", because Allah began with "know". Tawhid begins with knowledge, then comes action: seeking forgiveness for yourself and all believers.

**"And Allah knows your movement and your resting place."**

## Verses 20–24: Cutting Family Ties and Locked Hearts
﴿وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَوۡلَا نُزِّلَتۡ سُورَةࣱۖ فَإِذَآ أُنزِلَتۡ سُورَةࣱ مُّحۡكَمَةࣱ وَذُكِرَ فِيهَا ٱلۡقِتَالُ رَأَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضࣱ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَيۡكَ نَظَرَ ٱلۡمَغۡشِيِّ عَلَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۖ فَأَوۡلَىٰ لَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿طَاعَةࣱ وَقَوۡلࣱ مَّعۡرُوفࣱۚ فَإِذَا عَزَمَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ فَلَوۡ صَدَقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:20-21)

When a surah commanding fighting was revealed, the hypocrites looked at the Prophet ﷺ **"with a look of one overcome by death"**. Obedience and good words would have been better for them.

﴿فَهَلۡ عَسَيۡتُمۡ إِن تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ أَن تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَتُقَطِّعُوٓاْ أَرۡحَامَكُمۡ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَعَنَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ فَأَصَمَّهُمۡ وَأَعۡمَىٰٓ أَبۡصَٰرَهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:22-23)

**"So would you perhaps, if you turned away, cause corruption on earth and sever your ties of relationship?"** Turning away from Allah leads to two corruptions: spreading mischief and cutting family ties. **"Those are the ones that Allah has cursed."**

**The hadith of kinship**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah created the creation, and when He finished, the womb (kinship) stood up and said: This is the place of one who seeks refuge in You from being cut off. He said: Yes. Are you not pleased that I will keep ties with whoever keeps ties with you, and cut off whoever cuts you off? It said: Yes, my Lord. He said: Then that is for you." Then the Prophet ﷺ said: "Recite if you wish: *So would you perhaps, if you turned away, cause corruption on earth and sever your ties of relationship?*"

﴿أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ أَمۡ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبٍ أَقۡفَالُهَآ﴾ (Quran 47:24)

**"Then do they not reflect upon the Quran, or are there locks upon their hearts?"** The heart is either open through reflection or locked by sins and heedlessness. Reflection (*tadabbur*) is the key.

## Verses 25–32: Apostasy and Hidden Hatred
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱرۡتَدُّواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَدۡبَٰرِهِم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡهُدَى ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ سَوَّلَ لَهُمۡ وَأَمۡلَىٰ لَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَالُواْ لِلَّذِينَ كَرِهُواْ مَا نَزَّلَ ٱللَّهُ سَنُطِيعُكُمۡ فِي بَعۡضِ ٱلۡأَمۡرِۖ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ إِسۡرَارَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿فَكَيۡفَ إِذَا تَوَفَّتۡهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ يَضۡرِبُونَ وُجُوهَهُمۡ وَأَدۡبَٰرَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمُ ٱتَّبَعُواْ مَآ أَسۡخَطَ ٱللَّهَ وَكَرِهُواْ رِضۡوَٰنَهُۥ فَأَحۡبَطَ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ حَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌ أَن لَّن يُخۡرِجَ ٱللَّهُ أَضۡغَٰنَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَأَرَيۡنَٰكَهُمۡ فَلَعَرَفۡتَهُم بِسِيمَٰهُمۡۚ وَلَتَعۡرِفَنَّهُمۡ فِي لَحۡنِ ٱلۡقَوۡلِۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ﴾ ﴿وَلَنَبۡلُوَنَّكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ نَعۡلَمَ ٱلۡمُجَٰهِدِينَ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ وَنَبۡلُوَاْ أَخۡبَارَكُمۡ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَشَآقُّواْ ٱلرَّسُولَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡهُدَىٰ لَن يَضُرُّواْ ٱللَّهَ شَيۡـٔٗا وَسَيُحۡبِطُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:25-32)

**Those who turned back after guidance became clear**: Satan enticed them and prolonged their hopes. **"And you will surely know them by the tone of their speech."** Hypocrisy shows in the way people speak. **"And We will surely test you until We make evident those who strive among you and the patient."**

## Verses 33–38: Do Not Invalidate Your Deeds
﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَلَا تُبۡطِلُوٓاْ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:33)

**"O you who believe, obey Allah and obey the Messenger and do not invalidate your deeds."** Deeds can be ruined by showing off, by reminding people of favours, by disobedience, and above all by disbelief. Guard your good deeds after you do them.

﴿فَلَا تَهِنُواْ وَتَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلسَّلۡمِ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَوۡنَ وَٱللَّهُ مَعَكُمۡ وَلَن يَتِرَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لَعِبࣱ وَلَهۡوࣱۚ وَإِن تُؤۡمِنُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ يُؤۡتِكُمۡ أُجُورَكُمۡ وَلَا يَسۡـَٔلۡكُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلَكُمۡ﴾ (Quran 47:35-36)

**"So do not weaken and call for peace while you are superior"**: do not surrender out of weakness when you have the upper hand. **"And Allah is with you and will never deprive you of the reward of your deeds."** **"This worldly life is only amusement and diversion."**

﴿هَـٰٓأَنتُمۡ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ تُدۡعَوۡنَ لِتُنفِقُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَمِنكُم مَّن يَبۡخَلُۖ وَمَن يَبۡخَلۡ فَإِنَّمَا يَبۡخَلُ عَن نَّفۡسِهِۦۚ وَٱللَّهُ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُۚ وَإِن تَتَوَلَّوۡاْ يَسۡتَبۡدِلۡ قَوۡمًا غَيۡرَكُمۡ ثُمَّ لَا يَكُونُوٓاْ أَمۡثَٰلَكُم﴾ (Quran 47:38)

**"Here you are, those invited to spend in the cause of Allah, but among you are those who withhold. And whoever withholds only withholds from himself."** Stinginess harms the stingy, not Allah. **"Allah is the Free of need, while you are the needy. And if you turn away, He will replace you with another people; then they will not be the likes of you."**

## Word Meanings
- **Adalla**: wasted, made lost.
- **Balahum**: their condition, their hearts.
- **Al-Qital**: fighting.
- **Mannan**: as a favour.
- **Fida'**: ransom.
- **Tansuru**: support.
- **Yuthabbit aqdamakum**: plant firmly your feet.
- **Asin**: altered, stagnant.
- **Musaffa**: purified.
- **Tadabbur**: reflection.
- **Aqfal**: locks.
- **Lahn al-qawl**: tone of speech.
- **Tubtilu**: invalidate.
- **As-Salm**: peace, surrender.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Waste their deeds" vs. "amend their condition"**: two outcomes from two paths.
2. **"Eat as cattle eat"**: life without purpose described sharply.
3. **Four rivers**: water, milk, wine, honey, each unspoiled.
4. **"Locks upon their hearts"**: a picture of a heart closed to guidance.
5. **"Whoever withholds only withholds from himself"**: stinginess turned back on its owner.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Ikhfa'**: in *in tansuru* (verse 7), the noon sakinah is hidden before the ta'.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *aqdamakum* (verse 7), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *anharun min ma'in* (verse 15), the tanween merges into the meem.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ma'in* (verse 15).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Ahqaf before it ended with "Is anyone destroyed except the defiantly disobedient people?", and Surah Muhammad opens by describing those whose deeds are wasted. Surah Al-Fath after it brings the "clear victory" promised to those who support Allah.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is Surah Muhammad named after the Prophet ﷺ?
Because his name appears in verse 2; it is the only surah named after him. It is also called Surat al-Qital.

### What does "if you support Allah, He will support you" mean?
Whoever supports Allah's religion through obedience and calling to it will be supported by Allah with victory and steadfastness.

### What are the four rivers of Paradise?
Rivers of unaltered water, milk whose taste never changes, wine delicious to drinkers, and purified honey.

### What does "know that there is no god but Allah" teach?
That knowledge of tawhid comes before speech and action, as al-Bukhari noted.

### What does the surah say about cutting family ties?
That turning away from Allah leads to corruption and cutting family ties, and those who do so are cursed.

### What does "are there locks upon their hearts" mean?
Hearts that do not reflect on the Quran are as if locked, closed to its guidance.

### How many verses are in Surah Muhammad?
38 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Muhammad presents two paths: deeds wasted and deeds accepted. It promises that whoever supports Allah will be supported and made firm, describes the rivers awaiting the believers, and warns against cutting family ties and against hearts locked to the Quran. Open your heart with reflection, guard your deeds, and remember: Allah is the Free of need, and you are the needy.
