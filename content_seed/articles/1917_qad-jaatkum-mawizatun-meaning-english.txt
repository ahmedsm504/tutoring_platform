﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءࣱ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ بِفَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَبِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَفۡرَحُواْ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ﴾ [Yunus 10:57-58]

## Four descriptions
| Description | For |
|---|---|
| Admonition | Hearts that need reminding |
| Healing for what is in the chests | Doubts, envy, sorrow |
| Guidance | The path |
| Mercy | Believers who act on it |

## Rejoice!
- Islam does not forbid joy; it **directs** it. The best joy is in Allah's bounty and mercy.
- Many scholars explained *bounty* as Islam and *mercy* as the Quran, or the reverse.

## Better than what they amass
- Money, property and status cannot compare with guidance.

## Lessons
- **Celebrate** finishing a surah or a khatmah.
- **Value guidance** above wealth.
