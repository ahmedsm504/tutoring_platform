﴿ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيۡبَۛ فِيهِۛ هُدٗى لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَيُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:2-3]

## Why "that"?
- *Dhalika* points to something distant, though the Quran is in our hands. Scholars explain: it shows its **high rank**.

## No doubt
- A complete denial: no doubt it is from Allah, in its news or in the justice of its rulings.

## The embracing stop
- The three dots over *rayba* and *fihi* mean: stop on **one** of them, not both.
| Stop on | Reading |
|---|---|
| *la rayba* | No doubt. In it is guidance... |
| *fihi* | No doubt in it. Guidance for the pious |

## Guidance for the pious
- The Quran guides all people by showing the way, but those who truly benefit are the pious.

## The first qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Belief in the unseen | Allah, angels, the Last Day, what senses cannot reach |
| Establishing prayer | Complete and on time |
| Spending | *From what We provided*: part of what Allah gave |

## Lessons
- **Read with certainty** that it is the truth.
- **Be among the pious** to benefit from its guidance.
