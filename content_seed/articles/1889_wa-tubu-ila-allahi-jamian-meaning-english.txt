﴿وَتُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعًا أَيُّهَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ [An-Nur 24:31]

## After the rulings of modesty
- The verse comes at the end of the commands to lower the gaze and guard modesty. Since everyone may fall short, the call is: **repent, all of you**.

## "All of you, O believers"
- Repentance is not only for sinners in the eyes of people; it is for **every believer**. The Prophet ﷺ said he sought forgiveness and repented more than seventy times a day (narrated by al-Bukhari).

## Conditions of tawbah
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stop | Leave the sin now |
| Regret | Feel genuine remorse |
| Resolve | Intend not to return |
| Restore rights | If others were wronged |

## Success
- *So that you may succeed*: repentance is a path to falah, not a mark of failure.

## Lessons
- **Repent daily**, not only after big sins.
- **Repent together**: families and communities.
