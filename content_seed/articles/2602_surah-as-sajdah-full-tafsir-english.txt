Surah As-Sajdah was recited by the Prophet ﷺ in the first rak'ah of Fajr every Friday, and reported to be among the surahs he recited before sleeping. It describes believers in a way that has inspired night worshippers for centuries: "Their sides part from their beds; they call upon their Lord in fear and hope, and they spend from what We have provided them." And it promises them a reward so great it is hidden: "No soul knows what has been hidden for them of comfort for eyes." The surah contains a verse of prostration, tells of man's creation from clay and the breath of the spirit, describes the criminals hanging their heads before their Lord, and gives the formula for leadership in religion: patience and certainty.

This guide explains Surah As-Sajdah section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah As-Sajdah
- **Number of verses**: 30.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 32, in the twenty-first juz.
- **Name**: As-Sajdah means "the prostration", from the verse of prostration (verse 15).
- **Friday Fajr**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah said the Prophet ﷺ used to recite *Alif Lam Mim Tanzil* (As-Sajdah) and *Hal ata 'ala al-insan* (Al-Insan) in Fajr on Friday.
- **Before sleep**: at-Tirmidhi reported from Jabir that the Prophet ﷺ would not sleep until he had recited *Alif Lam Mim Tanzil* and *Tabarak* (Al-Mulk); al-Albani graded it authentic.

## Verses 1–3: A Book Without Doubt
﴿الٓمٓ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِ مِن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُۚ بَلۡ هُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكَ لِتُنذِرَ قَوۡمٗا مَّآ أَتَىٰهُم مِّن نَّذِيرٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:1-3)

**"The revelation of the Book, about which there is no doubt, is from the Lord of the worlds."** The idolaters said he invented it. **"Rather, it is the truth from your Lord, that you may warn a people to whom no warner has come before you, that perhaps they will be guided."**

## Verses 4–9: Creation and the Breath of the Spirit
﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ مَا لَكُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا شَفِيعٍۚ أَفَلَا تَتَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿يُدَبِّرُ ٱلۡأَمۡرَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ثُمَّ يَعۡرُجُ إِلَيۡهِ فِي يَوۡمٖ كَانَ مِقۡدَارُهُۥٓ أَلۡفَ سَنَةٖ مِّمَّا تَعُدُّونَ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 32:4-6)

**"It is Allah who created the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them in six days; then He established Himself above the Throne."** **"He arranges each matter from the heaven to the earth; then it will ascend to Him in a Day, the extent of which is a thousand years of those which you count."**

﴿ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحۡسَنَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقَهُۥۖ وَبَدَأَ خَلۡقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ مِن طِينٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ جَعَلَ نَسۡلَهُۥ مِن سُلَٰلَةٖ مِّن مَّآءٖ مَّهِينٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ سَوَّىٰهُ وَنَفَخَ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِهِۦۖ وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةَۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:7-9)

**"Who perfected everything which He created."** Look at anything Allah made: it is excellent in its form and function. **"And He began the creation of man from clay. Then He made his posterity out of the extract of a liquid disdained. Then He proportioned him and breathed into him from His spirit"**: a spirit created by Allah and honoured by being attributed to Him. **"And made for you hearing and vision and hearts; little are you grateful."**

## Verses 10–14: The Angel of Death and the Criminals
﴿وَقَالُوٓاْ أَءِذَا ضَلَلۡنَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَءِنَّا لَفِي خَلۡقٖ جَدِيدِۭۚ بَلۡ هُم بِلِقَآءِ رَبِّهِمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿۞قُلۡ يَتَوَفَّىٰكُم مَّلَكُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ ٱلَّذِي وُكِّلَ بِكُمۡ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمۡ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:10-11)

They said: "When we are lost in the earth, will we indeed be in a new creation?" **"Say: The angel of death will take you, who has been entrusted with you. Then to your Lord you will be returned."**

﴿وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذِ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ نَاكِسُواْ رُءُوسِهِمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ رَبَّنَآ أَبۡصَرۡنَا وَسَمِعۡنَا فَٱرۡجِعۡنَا نَعۡمَلۡ صَٰلِحًا إِنَّا مُوقِنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَوۡ شِئۡنَا لَأٓتَيۡنَا كُلَّ نَفۡسٍ هُدَىٰهَا وَلَٰكِنۡ حَقَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ مِنِّي لَأَمۡلَأَنَّ جَهَنَّمَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ أَجۡمَعِينَ﴾ ﴿فَذُوقُواْ بِمَا نَسِيتُمۡ لِقَآءَ يَوۡمِكُمۡ هَٰذَآ إِنَّا نَسِينَٰكُمۡۖ وَذُوقُواْ عَذَابَ ٱلۡخُلۡدِ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:12-14)

**"If you could but see when the criminals are hanging their heads before their Lord: Our Lord, we have seen and heard, so return us; we will work righteousness."** But it is too late. **"Taste, because you forgot the meeting of this Day of yours."**

## Verses 15–17: The Believers Who Prostrate
﴿إِنَّمَا يُؤۡمِنُ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهَا خَرُّواْۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ وَسَبَّحُواْ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ۩﴾ (Quran 32:15)

**"Only those believe in Our verses who, when they are reminded by them, fall down in prostration and exalt with praise of their Lord, and they are not arrogant."** This is the verse of prostration. When you reach it, prostrate.

﴿تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمَضَاجِعِ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُمۡ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:16)

**"Their sides part from their beds; they supplicate their Lord in fear and aspiration, and from what We have provided them, they spend."** Three qualities: night prayer, du'a with fear and hope, and spending.

**Night prayer**: at-Tirmidhi reported in the hadith of Mu'adh that the Prophet ﷺ listed the gates of good: "Fasting is a shield, charity extinguishes sin as water extinguishes fire, and the prayer of a man in the depth of the night." Then he recited this verse. At-Tirmidhi graded it *hasan sahih*.

﴿فَلَا تَعۡلَمُ نَفۡسࣱ مَّآ أُخۡفِيَ لَهُم مِّن قُرَّةِ أَعۡيُنٖ جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:17)

**"And no soul knows what has been hidden for them of comfort for eyes as reward for what they used to do."** They hid their worship in the night, so Allah hid their reward. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah says: "I have prepared for My righteous servants what no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no human heart has imagined." Then he said: "Recite if you wish: *And no soul knows what has been hidden for them of comfort for eyes*."

## Verses 18–22: Not Equal
﴿أَفَمَن كَانَ مُؤۡمِنٗا كَمَن كَانَ فَاسِقٗاۚ لَّا يَسۡتَوُۥنَ﴾ ﴿أَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمۡ جَنَّـٰتُ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ نُزُلَۢا بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ فَسَقُواْ فَمَأۡوَىٰهُمُ ٱلنَّارُۖ كُلَّمَآ أَرَادُوٓاْ أَن يَخۡرُجُواْ مِنۡهَآ أُعِيدُواْ فِيهَا وَقِيلَ لَهُمۡ ذُوقُواْ عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:18-20)

**"Then is one who was a believer like one who was defiantly disobedient? They are not equal."** Believers have gardens of refuge; the disobedient have the Fire, and whenever they try to leave, they are returned.

﴿وَلَنُذِيقَنَّهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَدۡنَىٰ دُونَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:21)

**"And We will surely let them taste the nearer punishment short of the greater punishment, that perhaps they will return."** Hardships in this world can be a wake-up call, a mercy to bring a person back before the greater punishment.

## Verses 23–25: Leadership Through Patience and Certainty
﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ فَلَا تَكُن فِي مِرۡيَةٖ مِّن لِّقَآئِهِۦۖ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ هُدٗى لِّبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنۡهُمۡ أَئِمَّةٗ يَهۡدُونَ بِأَمۡرِنَا لَمَّا صَبَرُواْۖ وَكَانُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا يُوقِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:23-24)

**"And We made from among them leaders guiding by Our command when they were patient and were certain of Our signs."** Two conditions for leadership in religion: **patience** and **certainty**. Scholars summarised it: "Through patience and certainty, leadership in religion is attained." Patience protects from desires; certainty protects from doubts.

## Verses 26–30: Signs and the Victory
﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَهۡدِ لَهُمۡ كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ يَمۡشُونَ فِي مَسَٰكِنِهِمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٍۚ أَفَلَا يَسۡمَعُونَ﴾ ﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّا نَسُوقُ ٱلۡمَآءَ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلۡجُرُزِ فَنُخۡرِجُ بِهِۦ زَرۡعٗا تَأۡكُلُ مِنۡهُ أَنۡعَٰمُهُمۡ وَأَنفُسُهُمۡۚ أَفَلَا يُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:26-27)

**"Have they not seen that We drive the water to the barren land and bring forth thereby crops from which their livestock eat and they themselves?"** Dead land brought to life: a sign of resurrection.

﴿وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡفَتۡحُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَتۡحِ لَا يَنفَعُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنُهُمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنظَرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنۡهُمۡ وَٱنتَظِرۡ إِنَّهُم مُّنتَظِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:28-30)

They ask: **"When will be this conclusion, if you should be truthful?"** **"On the Day of Conclusion, the belief of those who had disbelieved will not benefit them."** **"So turn away from them and wait; indeed, they are waiting."**

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Their sides part from their beds"**: a vivid picture of rising for night prayer.
2. **"Fear and aspiration"**: the two wings of worship.
3. **"What has been hidden"**: hidden deeds rewarded with a hidden reward.
4. **"Hanging their heads"**: the posture of shame.
5. **"When they were patient and were certain"**: leadership earned, not inherited.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Alif Lam Mim*.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 5).
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *khawfan wa tama'an* (verse 16), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *al-adna* (verse 21), the dal sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Prostration**: at verse 15.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Luqman before it ended with the five keys of the unseen, and As-Sajdah continues with "no soul knows what has been hidden for them". Surah Al-Ahzab after it addresses the Prophet ﷺ directly with commands of taqwa and reliance.

## Building a Night Prayer Habit
1. **Start with two rak'ahs** before Witr.
2. **Sleep with the intention to wake.**
3. **Set an alarm** before Fajr.
4. **Recite As-Sajdah and Al-Mulk** before sleeping.
5. **Make du'a in fear and hope.**
6. **Keep it secret** where possible.
7. **Be consistent** rather than intense.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Recite As-Sajdah in Friday Fajr** if you lead the prayer.
2. **Prostrate at verse 15.**
3. **Thank Allah for hearing, sight and heart.**
4. **Prepare for the angel of death.**
5. **Rise at night, even a little.**
6. **Hide some of your good deeds.**
7. **See hardships as wake-up calls.**
8. **Build patience and certainty.**

## Teaching Children
- **Teach them the prostration of recitation** at verse 15.
- **Wake them once in a while for a short night prayer.**
- **Explain the hidden reward**: what no eye has seen.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### When did the Prophet ﷺ recite Surah As-Sajdah?
In Fajr prayer on Friday with Al-Insan, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim, and before sleeping with Al-Mulk, as reported by at-Tirmidhi.

### Where is the prostration in Surah As-Sajdah?
At verse 15.

### What does "their sides part from their beds" mean?
They leave their beds at night to pray and call on their Lord.

### What is the hidden "comfort for eyes"?
A reward in Paradise that no eye has seen, no ear has heard and no heart has imagined, as in the hadith qudsi in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the "nearer punishment"?
Hardships in this world that may cause people to return to Allah before the greater punishment.

### How is leadership in religion attained according to this surah?
Through patience and certainty, as verse 24 says.

### How many verses are in Surah As-Sajdah?
30 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

### What does "He perfected everything which He created" mean?
Everything Allah created is excellent and suited to its purpose, a sign of His wisdom.

## Conclusion
Surah As-Sajdah draws a portrait you can step into: someone whose side leaves the bed at night, calling on his Lord in fear and hope, giving from what Allah gave him, and falling in prostration when reminded. For such people, Allah has hidden a comfort for the eyes beyond imagination. Begin tonight with two rak'ahs, and let patience and certainty make you a guide for others.
