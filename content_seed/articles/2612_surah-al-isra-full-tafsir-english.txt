Surah Al-Isra' opens with a miracle: "Exalted is He who took His servant by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa." It goes on to set out one of the most complete moral codes in the Quran, beginning with "Your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and to parents, good treatment", and continuing with commands about relatives, spending, children, chastity, life, orphans, promises, honest measures, knowledge and humility. It teaches the famous du'a for parents, "My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small", declares that everything in creation glorifies Allah, honours the children of Adam, and gives the Prophet ﷺ the words he recited while breaking the idols around the Ka'bah: "Truth has come, and falsehood has departed."

This guide explains Surah Al-Isra' section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Isra'
- **Number of verses**: 111.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 17, the first surah of the fifteenth juz.
- **Names**: Al-Isra' (the Night Journey) and Bani Isra'il (the Children of Israel).
- **A nightly recitation**: at-Tirmidhi reported that 'A'ishah said the Prophet ﷺ would not sleep until he had recited Bani Isra'il and Az-Zumar.
- **Main themes**: the Night Journey, the Quran as guidance, moral commandments, the honour of man and the truth of revelation.

## Verse 1: The Night Journey
﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَسۡرَىٰ بِعَبۡدِهِۦ لَيۡلٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ إِلَى ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡأَقۡصَا ٱلَّذِي بَٰرَكۡنَا حَوۡلَهُۥ لِنُرِيَهُۥ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَآۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 17:1)

**"Exalted is He who took His servant by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa, whose surroundings We have blessed, to show him of Our signs."** The journey was by body and soul, in a single night. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ described being brought al-Buraq, travelling to Jerusalem, leading the prophets in prayer, and then ascending through the heavens, where the five daily prayers were prescribed.

**Notice "His servant"**: in the moment of his greatest honour, the Prophet ﷺ is called by the title of servanthood. And al-Aqsa is described as blessed, linking the two holy mosques forever.

## Verses 2–8: The Children of Israel
﴿وَءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ هُدٗى لِّبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ أَلَّا تَتَّخِذُواْ مِن دُونِي وَكِيلٗا﴾ ﴿ذُرِّيَّةَ مَنۡ حَمَلۡنَا مَعَ نُوحٍۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ عَبۡدٗا شَكُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَقَضَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَتُفۡسِدُنَّ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَّتَيۡنِ وَلَتَعۡلُنَّ عُلُوّٗا كَبِيرٗا﴾ ﴿فَإِذَا جَآءَ وَعۡدُ أُولَىٰهُمَا بَعَثۡنَا عَلَيۡكُمۡ عِبَادٗا لَّنَآ أُوْلِي بَأۡسٖ شَدِيدٖ فَجَاسُواْ خِلَٰلَ ٱلدِّيَارِۚ وَكَانَ وَعۡدٗا مَّفۡعُولٗا﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ رَدَدۡنَا لَكُمُ ٱلۡكَرَّةَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَأَمۡدَدۡنَٰكُم بِأَمۡوَٰلٖ وَبَنِينَ وَجَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ أَكۡثَرَ نَفِيرًا﴾ ﴿إِنۡ أَحۡسَنتُمۡ أَحۡسَنتُمۡ لِأَنفُسِكُمۡۖ وَإِنۡ أَسَأۡتُمۡ فَلَهَاۚ فَإِذَا جَآءَ وَعۡدُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لِيَسُـُٔواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ وَلِيَدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدَ كَمَا دَخَلُوهُ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖ وَلِيُتَبِّرُواْ مَا عَلَوۡاْ تَتۡبِيرًا﴾ ﴿عَسَىٰ رَبُّكُمۡ أَن يَرۡحَمَكُمۡۚ وَإِنۡ عُدتُّمۡ عُدۡنَاۚ وَجَعَلۡنَا جَهَنَّمَ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ حَصِيرًا﴾ (Quran 17:2-8)

Allah gave Musa the Scripture as guidance. He decreed for the Children of Israel that they would cause corruption on earth twice and be punished each time. **"If you do good, you do good for yourselves; and if you do evil, it is against yourselves."** **"But if you return to sin, We will return to punishment."**

## Verses 9–22: The Upright Guidance and Personal Records
﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ يَهۡدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقۡوَمُ وَيُبَشِّرُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٗا كَبِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 17:9)

**"Indeed, this Quran guides to that which is most suitable (upright)."** In belief, worship, character, family and society.

﴿وَيَدۡعُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ بِٱلشَّرِّ دُعَآءَهُۥ بِٱلۡخَيۡرِۖ وَكَانَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ عَجُولٗا﴾ (Quran 17:11)

**"And man supplicates for evil as he supplicates for good, and man is ever hasty."** In anger, people pray against themselves and their families.

﴿وَكُلَّ إِنسَٰنٍ أَلۡزَمۡنَٰهُ طَـٰٓئِرَهُۥ فِي عُنُقِهِۦۖ وَنُخۡرِجُ لَهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ كِتَٰبٗا يَلۡقَىٰهُ مَنشُورًا﴾ ﴿ٱقۡرَأۡ كِتَٰبَكَ كَفَىٰ بِنَفۡسِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ عَلَيۡكَ حَسِيبٗا﴾ ﴿مَّنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَهۡتَدِي لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن ضَلَّ فَإِنَّمَا يَضِلُّ عَلَيۡهَاۚ وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةࣱ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۗ وَمَا كُنَّا مُعَذِّبِينَ حَتَّىٰ نَبۡعَثَ رَسُولٗا﴾ (Quran 17:13-15)

**"And every person We have imposed his fate upon his neck, and We will produce for him on the Day of Resurrection a record which he will encounter spread open. Read your record; sufficient is yourself against you this Day as an accountant."** **"No bearer of burdens will bear the burden of another. And never would We punish until We sent a messenger."**

﴿مَّن كَانَ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡعَاجِلَةَ عَجَّلۡنَا لَهُۥ فِيهَا مَا نَشَآءُ لِمَن نُّرِيدُ ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَا لَهُۥ جَهَنَّمَ يَصۡلَىٰهَا مَذۡمُومٗا مَّدۡحُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَمَنۡ أَرَادَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَسَعَىٰ لَهَا سَعۡيَهَا وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنࣱ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ كَانَ سَعۡيُهُم مَّشۡكُورٗا﴾ ﴿كُلّٗا نُّمِدُّ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ وَهَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ مِنۡ عَطَآءِ رَبِّكَۚ وَمَا كَانَ عَطَآءُ رَبِّكَ مَحۡظُورًا﴾ ﴿ٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ فَضَّلۡنَا بَعۡضَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۚ وَلَلۡأٓخِرَةُ أَكۡبَرُ دَرَجَٰتٖ وَأَكۡبَرُ تَفۡضِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 17:18-21)

Whoever wants only the immediate world is given what Allah wills, then has Hell; **"but whoever desires the hereafter and exerts the effort due to it while he is a believer, it is those whose effort is ever appreciated."**

## Verses 23–39: The Great Commandments
﴿۞وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنًاۚ إِمَّا يَبۡلُغَنَّ عِندَكَ ٱلۡكِبَرَ أَحَدُهُمَآ أَوۡ كِلَاهُمَا فَلَا تَقُل لَّهُمَآ أُفّٖ وَلَا تَنۡهَرۡهُمَا وَقُل لَّهُمَا قَوۡلٗا كَرِيمٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱخۡفِضۡ لَهُمَا جَنَاحَ ٱلذُّلِّ مِنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَةِ وَقُل رَّبِّ ٱرۡحَمۡهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 17:23-24)

**1. Tawhid and parents**: **"Your Lord has decreed that you not worship except Him, and to parents, good treatment."** When they reach old age: **"Say not to them 'uff', and do not repel them, but speak to them a noble word. And lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy, and say: My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small."**

Even "uff", the smallest sound of annoyance, is forbidden.

﴿وَءَاتِ ذَا ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ حَقَّهُۥ وَٱلۡمِسۡكِينَ وَٱبۡنَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَلَا تُبَذِّرۡ تَبۡذِيرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُبَذِّرِينَ كَانُوٓاْ إِخۡوَٰنَ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِۖ وَكَانَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ لِرَبِّهِۦ كَفُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَإِمَّا تُعۡرِضَنَّ عَنۡهُمُ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ رَحۡمَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكَ تَرۡجُوهَا فَقُل لَّهُمۡ قَوۡلٗا مَّيۡسُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ يَدَكَ مَغۡلُولَةً إِلَىٰ عُنُقِكَ وَلَا تَبۡسُطۡهَا كُلَّ ٱلۡبَسۡطِ فَتَقۡعُدَ مَلُومٗا مَّحۡسُورًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقۡدِرُۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ بِعِبَادِهِۦ خَبِيرَۢا بَصِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 17:26-30)

**2. Relatives, the poor and travellers**: give them their rights. **3. No wastefulness**: **"Indeed, the wasteful are brothers of the devils."** **4. Balance**: **"Do not make your hand chained to your neck or extend it completely"**, neither stingy nor reckless.

﴿وَلَا تَقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَوۡلَٰدَكُمۡ خَشۡيَةَ إِمۡلَٰقٖۖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُهُمۡ وَإِيَّاكُمۡۚ إِنَّ قَتۡلَهُمۡ كَانَ خِطۡـٔٗا كَبِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ ٱلزِّنَىٰٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فَٰحِشَةٗ وَسَآءَ سَبِيلٗا﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَقۡتُلُواْ ٱلنَّفۡسَ ٱلَّتِي حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّۗ وَمَن قُتِلَ مَظۡلُومٗا فَقَدۡ جَعَلۡنَا لِوَلِيِّهِۦ سُلۡطَٰنٗا فَلَا يُسۡرِف فِّي ٱلۡقَتۡلِۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ مَنصُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ مَالَ ٱلۡيَتِيمِ إِلَّا بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ حَتَّىٰ يَبۡلُغَ أَشُدَّهُۥۚ وَأَوۡفُواْ بِٱلۡعَهۡدِۖ إِنَّ ٱلۡعَهۡدَ كَانَ مَسۡـُٔولٗا﴾ ﴿وَأَوۡفُواْ ٱلۡكَيۡلَ إِذَا كِلۡتُمۡ وَزِنُواْ بِٱلۡقِسۡطَاسِ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمِۚ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَحۡسَنُ تَأۡوِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 17:31-35)

**5. Do not kill your children for fear of poverty**: **"We provide for them and for you."**
**6. Do not approach unlawful sexual intercourse**: **"Indeed, it is ever an immorality and an evil way."** Notice "do not approach": avoid everything that leads to it.
**7. Do not kill a soul Allah has forbidden**, except by right.
**8. Do not approach the orphan's property**, except in the best way.
**9. Fulfil every commitment**: **"Indeed, the commitment is ever that about which one will be questioned."**
**10. Give full measure and weigh with an even balance.**

﴿وَلَا تَقۡفُ مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمٌۚ إِنَّ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡبَصَرَ وَٱلۡفُؤَادَ كُلُّ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ كَانَ عَنۡهُ مَسۡـُٔولٗا﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَمۡشِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَحًاۖ إِنَّكَ لَن تَخۡرِقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَن تَبۡلُغَ ٱلۡجِبَالَ طُولٗا﴾ (Quran 17:36-37)

**11. Do not pursue that of which you have no knowledge**: **"Indeed, the hearing, the sight and the heart, about all those one will be questioned."** Do not speak, judge or share without knowledge.
**12. Humility**: **"Do not walk upon the earth exultantly. Indeed, you will never tear the earth apart, and you will never reach the mountains in height."**

## Verses 40–60: Glorification and Good Speech
﴿تُسَبِّحُ لَهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ ٱلسَّبۡعُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَن فِيهِنَّۚ وَإِن مِّن شَيۡءٍ إِلَّا يُسَبِّحُ بِحَمۡدِهِۦ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تَفۡقَهُونَ تَسۡبِيحَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ حَلِيمًا غَفُورٗا﴾ (Quran 17:44)

**"The seven heavens and the earth and whatever is in them exalt Him. And there is not a thing except that it exalts with His praise, but you do not understand their way of exalting."**

﴿وَقُل لِّعِبَادِي يَقُولُواْ ٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ يَنزَغُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ كَانَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ عَدُوّٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾ (Quran 17:53)

**"And tell My servants to say that which is best. Indeed, Satan induces dissension among them."** Many conflicts begin with careless words.

## Verses 61–72: The Honour of Man
﴿وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِأٓدَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ قَالَ ءَأَسۡجُدُ لِمَنۡ خَلَقۡتَ طِينٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَ أَرَءَيۡتَكَ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كَرَّمۡتَ عَلَيَّ لَئِنۡ أَخَّرۡتَنِ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ لَأَحۡتَنِكَنَّ ذُرِّيَّتَهُۥٓ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَ ٱذۡهَبۡ فَمَن تَبِعَكَ مِنۡهُمۡ فَإِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ جَزَآؤُكُمۡ جَزَآءٗ مَّوۡفُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱسۡتَفۡزِزۡ مَنِ ٱسۡتَطَعۡتَ مِنۡهُم بِصَوۡتِكَ وَأَجۡلِبۡ عَلَيۡهِم بِخَيۡلِكَ وَرَجِلِكَ وَشَارِكۡهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَوۡلَٰدِ وَعِدۡهُمۡۚ وَمَا يَعِدُهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ إِلَّا غُرُورًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ عِبَادِي لَيۡسَ لَكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سُلۡطَٰنࣱۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِرَبِّكَ وَكِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 17:61-65)

The story of Iblis refusing to prostrate to Adam, and his vow to mislead. **"Indeed, over My servants there is for you no authority. And sufficient is your Lord as Disposer of affairs."**

﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ كَرَّمۡنَا بَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ وَحَمَلۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَرَزَقۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَفَضَّلۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٖ مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقۡنَا تَفۡضِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 17:70)

**"And We have certainly honoured the children of Adam and carried them on land and sea and provided for them of the good things and preferred them over much of what We have created."** Human dignity is a gift from Allah.

## Verses 73–84: Prayer and Victory
﴿أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِدُلُوكِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ إِلَىٰ غَسَقِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَقُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِۖ إِنَّ قُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ كَانَ مَشۡهُودٗا﴾ ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَتَهَجَّدۡ بِهِۦ نَافِلَةٗ لَّكَ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَبۡعَثَكَ رَبُّكَ مَقَامٗا مَّحۡمُودٗا﴾ (Quran 17:78-79)

**"Establish prayer at the decline of the sun until the darkness of the night, and the recitation of dawn. Indeed, the recitation of dawn is ever witnessed."** The verse covers the five prayers. The Fajr recitation is witnessed by the angels: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said the angels of the night and the day gather at the Fajr prayer.

**"And from part of the night, pray with it as additional worship for you; it is expected that your Lord will resurrect you to a praised station."** *Al-maqam al-mahmud* is the station of the great intercession on the Day of Judgement.

﴿وَقُل رَّبِّ أَدۡخِلۡنِي مُدۡخَلَ صِدۡقٖ وَأَخۡرِجۡنِي مُخۡرَجَ صِدۡقٖ وَٱجۡعَل لِّي مِن لَّدُنكَ سُلۡطَٰنٗا نَّصِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَقُلۡ جَآءَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَزَهَقَ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡبَٰطِلَ كَانَ زَهُوقٗا﴾ ﴿وَنُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَا هُوَ شِفَآءࣱ وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَلَا يَزِيدُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا خَسَارٗا﴾ (Quran 17:80-82)

**"And say: My Lord, cause me to enter a sound entrance and to exit a sound exit and grant me from Yourself a supporting authority."** **"And say: Truth has come, and falsehood has departed. Indeed, falsehood is ever bound to depart."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud said that on the day of the conquest of Makkah the Prophet ﷺ entered while there were 360 idols around the Ka'bah, and he struck them with a stick in his hand, saying: "Truth has come, and falsehood has departed."

**"And We send down of the Quran that which is healing and mercy for the believers."**

## Verses 85–100: The Spirit and the Challenge
﴿وَيَسۡـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلرُّوحِۖ قُلِ ٱلرُّوحُ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّي وَمَآ أُوتِيتُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 17:85)

**"And they ask you about the soul. Say: The soul is of the affair of my Lord. And mankind have not been given of knowledge except a little."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud reported that some Jews asked the Prophet ﷺ about the spirit, and this verse was revealed. However much humanity learns, its knowledge remains little.

﴿قُل لَّئِنِ ٱجۡتَمَعَتِ ٱلۡإِنسُ وَٱلۡجِنُّ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَأۡتُواْ بِمِثۡلِ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ لَا يَأۡتُونَ بِمِثۡلِهِۦ وَلَوۡ كَانَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ لِبَعۡضٖ ظَهِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 17:88)

**"If mankind and the jinn gathered in order to produce the like of this Quran, they could not produce the like of it, even if they were to each other assistants."**

## Verses 101–111: Musa, the Quran and the Closing Praise
﴿وَبِٱلۡحَقِّ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ وَبِٱلۡحَقِّ نَزَلَۗ وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا مُبَشِّرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَقُرۡءَانٗا فَرَقۡنَٰهُ لِتَقۡرَأَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ عَلَىٰ مُكۡثٖ وَنَزَّلۡنَٰهُ تَنزِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 17:105-106)

**"With the truth We have sent it down, and with the truth it has descended."** **"And it is a Quran which We have separated (by intervals) that you might recite it to the people over a prolonged period."**

﴿قُلۡ ءَامِنُواْ بِهِۦٓ أَوۡ لَا تُؤۡمِنُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦٓ إِذَا يُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ يَخِرُّونَۤ لِلۡأَذۡقَانِۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ﴾ ﴿وَيَقُولُونَ سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّنَآ إِن كَانَ وَعۡدُ رَبِّنَا لَمَفۡعُولٗا﴾ ﴿وَيَخِرُّونَ لِلۡأَذۡقَانِ يَبۡكُونَ وَيَزِيدُهُمۡ خُشُوعٗا۩﴾ (Quran 17:107-109)

Those given knowledge before it, when it is recited to them, **fall upon their faces in prostration**, and **"they fall upon their faces weeping, and it increases them in humble submission"**. Here is the prostration of recitation.

﴿قُلِ ٱدۡعُواْ ٱللَّهَ أَوِ ٱدۡعُواْ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَۖ أَيّٗا مَّا تَدۡعُواْ فَلَهُ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ وَلَا تَجۡهَرۡ بِصَلَاتِكَ وَلَا تُخَافِتۡ بِهَا وَٱبۡتَغِ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ سَبِيلٗا﴾ ﴿وَقُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَمۡ يَتَّخِذۡ وَلَدٗا وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ شَرِيكࣱ فِي ٱلۡمُلۡكِ وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ وَلِيࣱّ مِّنَ ٱلذُّلِّۖ وَكَبِّرۡهُ تَكۡبِيرَۢا﴾ (Quran 17:110-111)

**"Call upon Allah or call upon the Most Merciful; whichever name you call, to Him belong the best names."** **"And do not recite your prayer too loudly or too quietly, but seek between that a way."** **"And say: Praise to Allah, who has not taken a son and has had no partner in His dominion... And glorify Him with great glorification."**

## Word Meanings
- **Asra**: took on a night journey.
- **Al-Masjid al-Aqsa**: the Farthest Mosque, in Jerusalem.
- **Aqwam**: most upright.
- **Ta'irahu**: his fate, his record.
- **Uff**: an expression of annoyance.
- **Janah adh-dhull**: the wing of humility.
- **Tabdhir**: wastefulness.
- **Maghlulah**: chained.
- **Imlaq**: poverty.
- **La taqfu**: do not pursue.
- **Marahan**: exultantly.
- **Duluk ash-shams**: the decline of the sun.
- **Ghasaq al-layl**: the darkness of the night.
- **Mashhudan**: witnessed.
- **Al-Maqam al-Mahmud**: the praised station.
- **Zahaqa**: departed, perished.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Exalted is He"**: the miracle begins with glorification.
2. **"His servant"**: honour through servanthood.
3. **"Do not say uff"**: forbidding the smallest disrespect.
4. **"As they brought me up when I was small"**: the du'a recalls the parents' care.
5. **"You will never tear the earth apart"**: humbling arrogance with a picture.
6. **"Truth has come, falsehood has departed"**: a declaration of victory.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *laylan mina* (verse 1), the tanween merges into the meem.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *al-aqsa* (verse 1) and *aqwam* (verse 9), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna hadha al-qur'an* (verse 9), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *qawlan karima* (verse 23), the tanween is hidden before the kaf.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ja'a al-haqq* (verse 81).
- **Prostration**: at verse 109.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah An-Nahl before it ended with "Indeed, Allah is with those who fear Him and those who are doers of good", and Al-Isra' opens with the highest honour of Allah's servant. Surah Al-Kahf after it begins with praise of Allah who sent down the Book, as Al-Isra' ends with praise.

## Living the Commandments of Al-Isra'
1. **Worship Allah alone.**
2. **Honour your parents**, especially in old age, and pray for them daily.
3. **Give relatives and the poor their rights.**
4. **Avoid waste and stinginess.**
5. **Stay far from what leads to zina.**
6. **Keep promises and give full measure.**
7. **Do not speak without knowledge.**
8. **Walk with humility.**
9. **Say what is best.**
10. **Pray Fajr**: it is witnessed.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Love al-Aqsa** and remember the Night Journey.
2. **Make the du'a for parents** part of every day.
3. **Verify before sharing**: your hearing, sight and heart will be questioned.
4. **Pray at night** hoping for the praised station.
5. **Use the Quran as healing.**
6. **Glorify Allah** as the surah ends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the Night Journey (al-Isra')?
The Prophet's ﷺ journey by night from al-Masjid al-Haram in Makkah to al-Masjid al-Aqsa in Jerusalem, followed by the Ascension through the heavens.

### What is the du'a for parents in Surah Al-Isra'?
"My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small."

### What does "Do not say uff to them" mean?
Do not show even the smallest sign of annoyance towards your parents.

### Why is the Fajr recitation "witnessed"?
Because the angels of the night and the day are present at Fajr, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the "praised station"?
The station of the great intercession that the Prophet ﷺ will be given on the Day of Judgement.

### What does the surah say about the spirit?
"The soul is of the affair of my Lord, and you have not been given of knowledge except a little."

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Isra'?
111 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Isra' takes you from Makkah to al-Aqsa in a single night, and then gives you a map for every day of your life: worship Allah alone, honour your parents with humility and du'a, be balanced, keep your promises, speak with knowledge and walk with humility. Pray Fajr with the angels, rise at night when you can, and say with confidence: truth has come, and falsehood is bound to depart.
