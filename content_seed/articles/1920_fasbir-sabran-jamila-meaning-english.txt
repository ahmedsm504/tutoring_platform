﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ صَبۡرٗا جَمِيلًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُمۡ يَرَوۡنَهُۥ بَعِيدٗا﴾ ﴿وَنَرَىٰهُ قَرِيبٗا﴾ [Al-Maarij 70:5-7]

## Beautiful patience
- Scholars describe *sabr jamil* as patience **without complaining to people**. Complaining to Allah, as Yaʿqub did, does not break it.

## Far or near?
| They | Allah |
|---|---|
| See the Day as far | Sees it as near |

- Everything that is coming is near.

## Perspective
- The hardship that feels endless is short compared to eternity.

## Three "beautiful" acts in the Quran
- Beautiful patience, beautiful forgiveness (15:85), and beautiful distancing (73:10).

## Lessons
- **Complain to Allah**, not to everyone.
- **Remember the end** to endure the present.
