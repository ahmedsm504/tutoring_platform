﴿وَقُل رَّبِّ أَدۡخِلۡنِي مُدۡخَلَ صِدۡقٖ وَأَخۡرِجۡنِي مُخۡرَجَ صِدۡقٖ وَٱجۡعَل لِّي مِن لَّدُنكَ سُلۡطَٰنٗا نَّصِيرٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:80]

## Three requests
| Request | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A truthful entrance | Begin with sincerity and correctness |
| A truthful exit | End well, with honour |
| Supporting authority from You | Strength and proof to uphold truth |

## The Hijrah
- Many scholars mention that it relates to leaving Makkah and entering Madinah.

## For every new chapter
- A new job, a move, marriage, a school or a project: ask that both the start and the end be **with truth**.

## "From Yourself"
- *Min ladunka*: support directly from Allah, not dependent on people.

## Lessons
- **Say this duʿa** before beginnings and endings.
- **Seek honest paths** in and out.
