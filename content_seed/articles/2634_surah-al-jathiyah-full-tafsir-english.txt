Surah Al-Jathiyah takes its name from one of the most powerful images of the Day of Judgement: every nation on its knees, each called to its own record. Before reaching that scene, the surah walks through the signs of Allah in the heavens, the earth, the alternation of night and day, the winds and the rain, and reminds you that everything in the heavens and the earth has been subjected to you. It then exposes the root of disbelief: not lack of evidence but desire. "Have you seen he who has taken as his god his own desire?" And it answers those who say that only time destroys them.

This guide explains Surah Al-Jathiyah section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Jathiyah
- **Number of verses**: 37.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 45, in the twenty-fifth juz, one of the seven surahs that open with Ha Mim.
- **Name**: Al-Jathiyah means "the kneeling", from verse 28.
- **Main themes**: the signs of Allah, arrogance toward revelation, the shari'ah, desire as an idol, the hereafter and the records.

## Verses 1–6: Signs for the Believers
﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ (Quran 45:1-2)

**"Ha Mim. The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Wise."**

﴿إِنَّ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَفِي خَلۡقِكُمۡ وَمَا يَبُثُّ مِن دَآبَّةٍ ءَايَٰتࣱ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُوقِنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مِن رِّزۡقٖ فَأَحۡيَا بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَا وَتَصۡرِيفِ ٱلرِّيَٰحِ ءَايَٰتࣱ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:3-5)

**Signs in the heavens and the earth** for the believers, **in your creation and the creatures He disperses** for people of certainty, and **in the alternation of night and day, the rain that revives the dead earth, and the directing of the winds** for people who reason. Three levels: faith, certainty and reason.

﴿تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱللَّهِ نَتۡلُوهَا عَلَيۡكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثِۭ بَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ وَءَايَٰتِهِۦ يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:6)

**"These are the verses of Allah which We recite to you in truth. Then in what statement after Allah and His verses will they believe?"**

## Verses 7–11: Woe to Every Sinful Liar
﴿وَيۡلࣱ لِّكُلِّ أَفَّاكٍ أَثِيمٖ﴾ ﴿يَسۡمَعُ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ثُمَّ يُصِرُّ مُسۡتَكۡبِرٗا كَأَن لَّمۡ يَسۡمَعۡهَاۖ فَبَشِّرۡهُ بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 45:7-8)

**"Woe to every sinful liar who hears the verses of Allah recited to him, then persists arrogantly as if he had not heard them. So give him tidings of a painful punishment."**

﴿وَإِذَا عَلِمَ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا شَيۡـًٔا ٱتَّخَذَهَا هُزُوًاۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ مُّهِينࣱ﴾ (Quran 45:9)

**"And when he knows anything of Our verses, he takes them in ridicule."**

## Verses 12–15: Everything Subjected to You
﴿۞ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ لِتَجۡرِيَ ٱلۡفُلۡكُ فِيهِ بِأَمۡرِهِۦ وَلِتَبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:12)

**"It is Allah who subjected to you the sea so that ships may sail upon it by His command and that you may seek of His bounty; and perhaps you will be grateful."**

﴿وَسَخَّرَ لَكُم مَّا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا مِّنۡهُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:13)

**"And He has subjected to you whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, all from Him. Indeed in that are signs for a people who give thought."** The whole universe is made to serve mankind: a call to discovery, gratitude and responsibility.

﴿قُل لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ يَغۡفِرُواْ لِلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ أَيَّامَ ٱللَّهِ لِيَجۡزِيَ قَوۡمَۢا بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:14)

**"Say to those who have believed that they forgive those who expect not the days of Allah."**

﴿مَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَنۡ أَسَآءَ فَعَلَيۡهَاۖ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمۡ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:15)

**"Whoever does a good deed, it is for himself; and whoever does evil, it is against himself. Then to your Lord you will be returned."**

## Verses 16–20: The Shari'ah
﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحُكۡمَ وَٱلنُّبُوَّةَ وَرَزَقۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَفَضَّلۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُم بَيِّنَٰتٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِۖ فَمَا ٱخۡتَلَفُوٓاْ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡعِلۡمُ بَغۡيَۢا بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَقۡضِي بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فِيمَا كَانُواْ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:16-17)

The Children of Israel were given the Book, judgement and prophethood, and clear proofs, **"and they did not differ except after knowledge had come to them, out of jealous animosity between themselves"**.

﴿ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَٰكَ عَلَىٰ شَرِيعَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ فَٱتَّبِعۡهَا وَلَا تَتَّبِعۡ أَهۡوَآءَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:18)

**"Then We put you on an ordained way (shari'ah) concerning the matter; so follow it and do not follow the inclinations of those who do not know."**

﴿هَٰذَا بَصَـٰٓئِرُ لِلنَّاسِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُوقِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:20)

**"This is enlightenment for mankind and guidance and mercy for a people who are certain."**

## Verses 21–23: Not Equal, and Desire as a God
﴿أَمۡ حَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱجۡتَرَحُواْ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ أَن نَّجۡعَلَهُمۡ كَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ سَوَآءٗ مَّحۡيَاهُمۡ وَمَمَاتُهُمۡۚ سَآءَ مَا يَحۡكُمُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:21)

**"Or do those who commit evils think that We will make them like those who have believed and done righteous deeds, equal in their life and their death? Evil is that which they judge."**

﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتَ مَنِ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَٰهَهُۥ هَوَىٰهُ وَأَضَلَّهُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٖ وَخَتَمَ عَلَىٰ سَمۡعِهِۦ وَقَلۡبِهِۦ وَجَعَلَ عَلَىٰ بَصَرِهِۦ غِشَٰوَةٗ فَمَن يَهۡدِيهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱللَّهِۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:23)

**"Have you seen he who has taken as his god his own desire, and Allah has sent him astray due to knowledge and has set a seal upon his hearing and his heart and put over his vision a veil?"** The most widespread idol is not stone but desire: whatever it commands, he obeys.

## Verses 24–27: "Nothing Destroys Us But Time"
﴿وَقَالُواْ مَا هِيَ إِلَّا حَيَاتُنَا ٱلدُّنۡيَا نَمُوتُ وَنَحۡيَا وَمَا يُهۡلِكُنَآ إِلَّا ٱلدَّهۡرُۚ وَمَا لَهُم بِذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عِلۡمٍۖ إِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا يَظُنُّونَ﴾ (Quran 45:24)

**"And they say: There is not but our worldly life; we die and live, and nothing destroys us except time. And they have of that no knowledge; they are only assuming."**

**Do not curse time**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah said: "The son of Adam wrongs Me; he curses time, while I am time. In My hand is the command; I alternate the night and the day." Time does nothing by itself; Allah is the One who controls events, so cursing time is really aimed at its Controller.

﴿وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ مَّا كَانَ حُجَّتَهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُواْ ٱئۡتُواْ بِـَٔابَآئِنَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلِ ٱللَّهُ يُحۡيِيكُمۡ ثُمَّ يُمِيتُكُمۡ ثُمَّ يَجۡمَعُكُمۡ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:25-26)

When the verses are recited, their only argument is: **"Bring our forefathers, if you should be truthful."** **"Say: Allah causes you to live, then causes you to die; then He will assemble you for the Day of Resurrection, about which there is no doubt."**

## Verses 28–35: Every Nation Kneeling
﴿وَتَرَىٰ كُلَّ أُمَّةٖ جَاثِيَةٗۚ كُلُّ أُمَّةٖ تُدۡعَىٰٓ إِلَىٰ كِتَٰبِهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:28)

**"And you will see every nation kneeling. Every nation will be called to its record: Today you will be recompensed for what you used to do."**

﴿هَٰذَا كِتَٰبُنَا يَنطِقُ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا نَسۡتَنسِخُ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:29)

**"This, Our record, speaks about you in truth. Indeed, We were having transcribed whatever you used to do."**

﴿فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ فَيُدۡخِلُهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ فِي رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾ (Quran 45:30)

**The believers**: **"Their Lord will admit them into His mercy. That is the clear attainment."**

﴿وَقِيلَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ نَنسَىٰكُمۡ كَمَا نَسِيتُمۡ لِقَآءَ يَوۡمِكُمۡ هَٰذَا وَمَأۡوَىٰكُمُ ٱلنَّارُ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن نَّـٰصِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 45:34)

**The deniers**: **"Today We will forget you as you forgot the meeting of this Day of yours."**

﴿ذَٰلِكُم بِأَنَّكُمُ ٱتَّخَذۡتُمۡ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ هُزُوٗا وَغَرَّتۡكُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَا يُخۡرَجُونَ مِنۡهَا وَلَا هُمۡ يُسۡتَعۡتَبُونَ﴾ (Quran 45:35)

**"That is because you took the verses of Allah in ridicule, and the worldly life deluded you."**

## Verses 36–37: To Him Belongs All Praise and Grandeur
﴿فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ رَبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَرَبِّ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَهُ ٱلۡكِبۡرِيَآءُ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ (Quran 45:36-37)

**"Then to Allah belongs all praise, Lord of the heavens and Lord of the earth, Lord of the worlds. And to Him belongs grandeur within the heavens and the earth, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise."** In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah said: "Might is My garment and grandeur is My cloak; whoever competes with Me in either of them, I will punish him."

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Jathiyah**: kneeling.
- **Affak**: a habitual liar.
- **Athim**: sinful.
- **Sakhkhara**: subjected.
- **Ayyam Allah**: the days of Allah (His punishments and favours).
- **Shari'ah**: an ordained way.
- **Ijtarahu**: committed.
- **Hawa**: desire.
- **Ghishawah**: veil.
- **Ad-Dahr**: time.
- **Nastansikh**: We transcribe.
- **Al-Kibriya'**: grandeur.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Three levels of signs**: for believers, for the certain, and for people of reason.
2. **"All from Him"**: everything subjected, all from His grace.
3. **Desire as a god**: the subtlest idolatry.
4. **The kneeling nations**: a scene of universal humility.
5. **"We will forget you as you forgot"**: recompense of the same kind.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi mukhaffaf**: in *Ha Mim* (verse 1), the mim is stretched six counts, while the ha is stretched two.
- **Idgham without ghunnah**: in *waylun li-kulli* (verse 7), the tanween merges into the lam.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *min as-sama'i* (verse 5).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *ijtarahu* (verse 21), the jim sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *ummatin jathiyah* (verse 28), the tanween is hidden before the jim.

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Ad-Dukhan before it ends with "So watch; indeed, they are watching", and Al-Jathiyah opens with the revelation of the Book. Both belong to the Ha Mim family, and Surah Al-Ahqaf after it opens with the same words.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Read the signs** around you with faith, certainty and reason.
2. **Never be arrogant toward a verse** you hear.
3. **Use creation responsibly**, as something subjected to you by Allah.
4. **Forgive those who wrong you** when it serves the call.
5. **Follow the shari'ah**, not the whims of those without knowledge.
6. **Watch your desire**, so it does not become your god.
7. **Do not curse time**; turn to its Controller.
8. **Prepare your record**, for every nation will kneel before its own.

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Jathiyah
- **Week 1**: verses 1–20.
- **Week 2**: verses 21–37, with full review.

## Teaching Children
- **Make a list** of things Allah subjected to us: the sun, the sea, animals.
- **Explain why we do not curse time** with the hadith qudsi.
- **Talk about desire**: when do we say no to what we want?

## Desire in the Light of the Sunnah
The warning of verse 23 is echoed throughout the Sunnah. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Paradise is surrounded by hardships, and the Fire is surrounded by desires." The path to Paradise passes through things the self dislikes: waking for Fajr, controlling anger, giving charity when wealth feels short. The path to the Fire is lined with what the self craves. This does not mean every desire is evil; Islam permits and honours lawful pleasures. The danger is when desire becomes the one who commands, so that the question "Does Allah love this?" is replaced by "Do I want this?"

Scholars described three signs that desire has taken the place of a god: a person follows it even against clear knowledge (hence "due to knowledge" in the verse), he stops listening to advice (the seal on the hearing), and he stops seeing the consequences (the veil over the vision). The cure is the reverse: keep learning, stay near people who advise you sincerely, and reflect regularly on where your choices are leading.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is it called Al-Jathiyah?
Because of verse 28: "And you will see every nation kneeling."

### What does "taken his desire as his god" mean?
Obeying one's desires in everything as one would obey a god.

### Is it forbidden to curse time?
Yes; the Prophet ﷺ reported that Allah said the son of Adam wrongs Him by cursing time, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the shari'ah in verse 18?
The ordained way given to the Prophet ﷺ, which he was commanded to follow.

### What does "everything subjected to you" mean?
That Allah made the heavens and the earth serve humanity, all of it from His grace.

### What is the record in verse 29?
The book of deeds that Allah had transcribed, speaking the truth about each person.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Jathiyah?
37 verses.

### Is every desire blameworthy?
No. Lawful desires are permitted; the blame is for making desire the master that overrides Allah's command.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Jathiyah places a single scene before your eyes: you, kneeling among your nation, handed a record that leaves out nothing. The way to prepare is in the rest of the surah: read the signs, accept the verses humbly, follow the shari'ah, and dethrone the idol of desire. To Allah belongs all praise and all grandeur; give Him your praise and keep your humility.
