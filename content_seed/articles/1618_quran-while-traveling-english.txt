Travel breaks routines, but it can also give you **extra time** for the Quran.

## The Prophet's example
- ʿAbdullah ibn Mughaffal said he saw the Prophet ﷺ on the day of the Conquest of Makkah, on his camel, reciting Surah Al-Fath in a beautiful voice [Al-Bukhari].

## Use the waiting time
| Moment | What to do |
|---|---|
| Airport or station | Read your daily portion |
| Plane or train | Revise from memory, quietly |
| Car | Listen to a reciter and repeat |

## Mushaf or app?
- A **small Mushaf** is ideal.
- If not, use a **trusted app** with the same Madinah page layout as your Mushaf.

## Adjust, don't stop
- **Halve** your portion if needed, but keep something every day.
- **Revise** instead of memorizing new pages on travel days.

## Revise from memory
- Long journeys are perfect for **reciting a juz** you know, from start to end.
