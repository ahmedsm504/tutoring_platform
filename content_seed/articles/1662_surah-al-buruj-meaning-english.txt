**Surah Al-Buruj** (22 ayat) tells of believers who stayed firm even in the face of fire. It was revealed in Makkah when Muslims were being persecuted.

## The people of the ditch
﴿قُتِلَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأُخۡدُودِ﴾ ﴿ٱلنَّارِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡوَقُودِ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ هُمۡ عَلَيۡهَا قُعُودࣱ﴾ ﴿وَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفۡعَلُونَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ شُهُودࣱ﴾
- Tyrants dug a ditch, lit a fire, and threw believers into it while watching.
﴿وَمَا نَقَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ﴾ — *Their only fault was believing in Allah.*
- Sahih Muslim has the story of the boy, the king and the monk, which many scholars linked to this surah.

## Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Oaths: the sky with great stars, the promised Day, the witness |
| 4–9 | The people of the ditch |
| 10–11 | Punishment for persecutors who don't repent; gardens for believers |
| 12–16 | Allah's power and names |
| 17–22 | Pharaoh and Thamud; the Quran preserved |

## Allah's names
﴿وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلۡوَدُودُ﴾ ﴿ذُو ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡمَجِيدُ﴾ ﴿فَعَّالࣱ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ﴾
- **Even for persecutors** the door of repentance was mentioned: *those who then do not repent*.

## Lessons
- **Faith is worth any sacrifice.**
- **Persecutors never win** in the end.
