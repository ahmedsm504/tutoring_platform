﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَدۡخُلُواْ بُيُوتًا غَيۡرَ بُيُوتِكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ تَسۡتَأۡنِسُواْ وَتُسَلِّمُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهَاۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ [An-Nur 24:27]

## *Tastaʾnisu*
- Many scholars: ask permission. The word *uns* hints that permission should come with comfort and welcome.

## Etiquette of permission
| Etiquette | Source |
|---|---|
| Greet and ask | This verse |
| Three times | *Asking permission is three times; if you are permitted, enter, otherwise return* (al-Bukhari and Muslim) |
| Stand to the side | Not facing the doorway |
| Name yourself | Not just "me" |

## If told to return
- *If you are told to go back, then go back; it is purer for you* (24:28), without anger.

## The wisdom
- Protecting privacy and the sanctity of homes.

## Lessons
- **Teach children** to ask permission, even at home at certain times.
- **Respect privacy** in visits and messages.
