﴿وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱلۡحَيِّ ٱلَّذِي لَا يَمُوتُ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِهِۦۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِهِۦ بِذُنُوبِ عِبَادِهِۦ خَبِيرًا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:58]

## Why "the Ever-Living who does not die"?
- Anyone you rely on among people will die, change or fail. **Only Allah** remains forever.

## Tawakkul and tasbih
- Reliance is followed by *glorify with His praise*: the heart that trusts Allah also praises Him.

## "Sufficient is He as Aware of His servants' sins"
- He knows everything, so He is the best One to judge and to forgive.

## Practical tawakkul
| Do | Then |
|---|---|
| Plan and work | Trust the result to Allah |
| Ask for help from people | Know that help comes from Allah |

## Lessons
- **Do not hang your heart** on mortals.
- **Pair trust with praise.**
