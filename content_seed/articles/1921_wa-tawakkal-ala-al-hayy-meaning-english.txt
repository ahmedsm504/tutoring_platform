## "Rely upon the Ever-Living Who Does Not Die": Quran 25:58 Explained

﴿وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱلۡحَيِّ ٱلَّذِي لَا يَمُوتُ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِهِۦۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِهِۦ بِذُنُوبِ عِبَادِهِۦ خَبِيرًا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:58]

### Why "the Ever-Living who does not die"?
- Anyone you rely on among people will die, change or fail. **Only Allah** remains forever.

### Tawakkul and tasbih
- Reliance is followed by *glorify with His praise*: the heart that trusts Allah also praises Him.

### "Sufficient is He as Aware of His servants' sins"
- He knows everything, so He is the best One to judge and to forgive.

### Practical tawakkul
| Do | Then |
|---|---|
| Plan and work | Trust the result to Allah |
| Ask for help from people | Know that help comes from Allah |

### Lessons
- **Do not hang your heart** on mortals.
- **Pair trust with praise.**

## Surah Al-Furqan Meaning: The Criterion and the Servants of the Most Merciful

**Surah Al-Furqan** (The Criterion, 77 ayat) is named after one of the names of the Quran.

### The Criterion
- It opens by blessing Allah, who sent down **the Criterion** that separates truth from falsehood, to be a warner to the worlds.

### Objections and answers
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Why does this messenger eat food and walk in markets?" | All previous messengers did the same |
| "Why was the Quran not revealed all at once?" | To strengthen the Prophet's heart, and it was recited in measured stages |
| "These are tales of the ancients" | It was sent by the One who knows the secret of the heavens and earth |

### The servants of the Most Merciful
﴿وَعِبَادُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡشُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ هَوۡنٗا وَإِذَا خَاطَبَهُمُ ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ قَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗا﴾
Their qualities include:
- They spend the night in prayer.
- They ask to be saved from Hell.
- They are balanced in spending, neither wasteful nor stingy.
- They do not commit shirk, murder or zina.
- They do not witness falsehood and pass by idle talk with dignity.
- They pray for righteous spouses and children, *a comfort to our eyes*.

### Hope for those who repent
- After listing major sins, Allah promises that those who repent, believe and do good will have their bad deeds **replaced with good**.

### Lessons
- **Character is the proof of faith**: humility, patience and balance.
- **Repentance opens a new page.**

## "The Servants of the Most Merciful Walk Humbly on the Earth": Quran 25:63 Explained

﴿وَعِبَادُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡشُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ هَوۡنٗا وَإِذَا خَاطَبَهُمُ ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ قَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:63]

### An honoured title
- Allah attributes them to His name **ar-Rahman**, right after the polytheists asked: *And what is ar-Rahman?* (25:60).

### *Hawnan*
- With calm, dignity and humility, without arrogance and without affected weakness. The Prophet ﷺ walked briskly, as if descending a slope.

### Replying with peace
- *They say: peace*: words that keep them safe from sin; they do not answer foolishness with foolishness.

### Manners first
- This is the **first** quality listed for the servants of the Most Merciful, before night prayer and the rest.

### Lessons
- **Walk with humility.**
- **Do not argue with the foolish**: say peace and move on.

## "When They Spend, They Are Neither Wasteful Nor Stingy": Quran 25:67 Explained

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَنفَقُواْ لَمۡ يُسۡرِفُواْ وَلَمۡ يَقۡتُرُواْ وَكَانَ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ قَوَامٗا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:67]

### The words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Israf* | Going beyond limits, or spending on sin |
| *Qatr* | Tightness and stinginess in what is due |
| *Qawam* | The balanced middle |

### A similar verse
- *Do not chain your hand to your neck, nor extend it completely* (Al-Isra 17:29).

### Applying it
| Area | Balance |
|---|---|
| Home | Needs met without waste on luxuries |
| Children | Reasonable giving and teaching them to save |
| Charity | Regular giving without neglecting family rights |

### Lessons
- **Make a budget** with needs, charity and savings.
- **Moderation** is loved by Allah in money and beyond.

## "Allah Will Replace Their Evil Deeds with Good": Quran 25:70 Explained

﴿إِلَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يُبَدِّلُ ٱللَّهُ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ حَسَنَٰتٖۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:70]

### The context
- The previous verses mention shirk, murder and zina and their punishment. Then comes **the exception**.

### Three conditions
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Repentance | Turning back sincerely |
| Faith | Correct belief |
| Righteous deeds | Proof of change |

### How are sins replaced?
- Scholars give two explanations:
  1. Allah replaces sinful **habits** with good ones in this life.
  2. On the Day of Judgment, the sins themselves are turned into good deeds, as in a hadith narrated by Muslim.

### Generosity beyond forgiveness
- Forgiveness wipes the record clean; replacement **fills** it with good.

### Lessons
- **No past is too dark** for repentance.
- **Follow repentance with action.**

## "Grant Us from Our Spouses and Children Comfort to Our Eyes": Quran 25:74 Explained

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعۡيُنٖ وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:74]

### Among the servants of the Most Merciful
- This duʿa is one of the final qualities of *ʿIbad ar-Rahman* in Surah Al-Furqan.

### "Comfort of the eyes"
- *Qurrat aʿyun* describes an eye that is calm and cool with joy. Scholars explain that what truly comforts a believer's eye is seeing his family **obeying Allah**.

### Two requests
| Request | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Spouses and children as comfort | A righteous, peaceful family |
| *Make us leaders for the pious* | Models others follow in good |

### "Grant us" (*hab*)
- *Hab* means give as a gift. A righteous family is a **gift from Allah**, so ask for it constantly.

### Ambition in good
- Asking to be a leader of the pious shows that seeking a high rank in religion is praiseworthy.

### Lessons
- **Make this a daily duʿa** for your family.
- **Be the example** you want your children to follow.

## "When I Am Ill, It Is He Who Cures Me": Ibrahim Describes His Lord in Quran 26:78-82

﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَنِي فَهُوَ يَهۡدِينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي هُوَ يُطۡعِمُنِي وَيَسۡقِينِ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا مَرِضۡتُ فَهُوَ يَشۡفِينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي يُمِيتُنِي ثُمَّ يُحۡيِينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡمَعُ أَن يَغۡفِرَ لِي خَطِيٓـَٔتِي يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ [Ash-Shuara 26:78-82]

### Six descriptions
| Description | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Created me | From nothing |
| Guides me | In this life and the religion |
| Feeds me and gives me drink | All provision |
| Cures me | The true Healer; medicine is a means |
| Gives death and life | Death and resurrection |
| Forgives | Hope on the Day of Judgment |

### Beautiful manners
- *When I am ill*: he attributed illness to **himself** and cure to Allah. Scholars note this as etiquette with Allah.

### "I hope"
- The Friend of the Most Merciful said *I hope*, not *I am sure*: humility.

### Taking medicine
- *Seek treatment, O servants of Allah* (narrated by Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi): treatment does not contradict trust.

### Lessons
- **Ask Allah for cure** while taking medicine.
- **Speak with respect** about Allah's decrees.

## "Except One Who Comes to Allah with a Sound Heart": Quran 26:87-89 Explained

﴿وَلَا تُخۡزِنِي يَوۡمَ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ لَا يَنفَعُ مَالࣱ وَلَا بَنُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَنۡ أَتَى ٱللَّهَ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٖ﴾ [Ash-Shuara 26:87-89]

### Ibrahim's request
- *Do not disgrace me on the Day they are resurrected*: from Allah's close friend, a lesson in humility.

### What does not benefit
- Wealth and children: the two things people rely on most in this world.

### The sound heart
- Scholars explain it as a heart **free of shirk**, doubt, hypocrisy, envy, hatred and arrogance.
| Free from | Filled with |
|---|---|
| Shirk | Tawhid |
| Envy | Love of good for others |
| Hatred | Forgiveness |
| Arrogance | Humility |

### How to purify it
- Duʿa, the Quran, sincerity, avoiding sins, and wishing good for people.

### Lessons
- **Work on your heart** as much as your deeds.
- **Ask Allah** for a sound heart daily.

## Surah Ash-Shuara Meaning: Seven Prophets and a Sound Heart

**Surah Ash-Shuara** (The Poets, 227 ayat) is one of the longest Makkan surahs by number of verses.

### Comfort for the Prophet ﷺ
- It opens by consoling the Prophet ﷺ, who grieved that his people would not believe.

### Seven stories, one pattern
| Prophet | People |
|---|---|
| Musa | Pharaoh |
| Ibrahim | His father and people |
| Nuh | His people |
| Hud | ʿAd |
| Salih | Thamud |
| Lut | His people |
| Shuʿayb | The companions of the thicket |

- Each story ends with the same refrain: *Indeed in that is a sign, but most of them were not believers. And indeed your Lord is the Mighty, the Merciful.*
- Most of the prophets say the same words: *I do not ask you for any payment; my reward is only from the Lord of the worlds.*

### Ibrahim's prayer and the sound heart
﴿يَوۡمَ لَا يَنفَعُ مَالࣱ وَلَا بَنُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَنۡ أَتَى ٱللَّهَ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٖ﴾

### The poets
- The surah distinguishes poets who follow every fancy from believing poets who do righteous deeds and remember Allah much.

### Lessons
- **Truth repeats across history**: the message of every prophet is the same.
- **Prepare your heart**: wealth and children will not help on that Day.

## "My Lord, Enable Me to Be Grateful for Your Favour": Quran 27:19 and 46:15 Explained

﴿فَتَبَسَّمَ ضَاحِكٗا مِّن قَوۡلِهَا وَقَالَ رَبِّ أَوۡزِعۡنِيٓ أَنۡ أَشۡكُرَ نِعۡمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَيَّ وَأَنۡ أَعۡمَلَ صَٰلِحٗا تَرۡضَىٰهُ وَأَدۡخِلۡنِي بِرَحۡمَتِكَ فِي عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ [An-Naml 27:19]

### *Awziʿni*
- Inspire me, enable me and keep me devoted to gratitude. Even thanking Allah needs **His help**.

### Two settings
| Place | Who | Moment |
|---|---|---|
| An-Naml 27:19 | Sulayman | After hearing the ant |
| Al-Ahqaf 46:15 | The believer | On reaching forty |

### The parents' share
- *Your favour on me and on my parents*: blessings on your parents are blessings on you.

### At forty
- Al-Ahqaf adds: *and make my offspring righteous; I have repented to You and I am of the Muslims.* Forty is a point of maturity to renew gratitude and repentance.

### Lessons
- **Ask Allah** to help you thank Him.
- **Include your parents and children** in your duʿa.

## Surah An-Naml Meaning: The Ant, the Hoopoe and the Queen of Sheba

**Surah An-Naml** (The Ant, 93 ayat) is named after a tiny creature whose words the Quran records.

### The ant's warning
- As Sulayman's army approached, an ant told its colony to enter their dwellings so they would not be crushed, adding that the army **would not even notice**.
- Sulayman smiled and asked Allah to help him be grateful.

### The hoopoe and Sheba
- The hoopoe returned with news of a queen ruling Sheba whose people worshipped the sun.
- Sulayman sent a letter beginning with *Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim*.

### The throne brought in a blink
﴿قَالَ ٱلَّذِي عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَنَا۠ ءَاتِيكَ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن يَرۡتَدَّ إِلَيۡكَ طَرۡفُكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهُ مُسۡتَقِرًّا عِندَهُۥ قَالَ هَٰذَا مِن فَضۡلِ رَبِّي لِيَبۡلُوَنِيٓ ءَأَشۡكُرُ أَمۡ أَكۡفُرُۖ وَمَن شَكَرَ فَإِنَّمَا يَشۡكُرُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ رَبِّي غَنِيࣱّ كَرِيمࣱ﴾
- Sulayman's first reaction to a miracle was **gratitude**, not pride.
- The Queen eventually submitted *with Sulayman to Allah, Lord of the worlds*.

### Questions that leave no answer but Allah
﴿أَمَّن يُجِيبُ ٱلۡمُضۡطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكۡشِفُ ٱلسُّوٓءَ وَيَجۡعَلُكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾

### Lessons
- **Care for others**, like the ant.
- **Every blessing is a test** of gratitude.
- **Call on Allah when desperate**: He answers the one in need.

## "Who Answers the Distressed When He Calls?": Quran 27:62 Explained

﴿أَمَّن يُجِيبُ ٱلۡمُضۡطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكۡشِفُ ٱلسُّوٓءَ وَيَجۡعَلُكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ [An-Naml 27:62]

### A series of questions
- This verse is part of a chain in An-Naml (27:60-64), each ending with *Is there a god with Allah?*

### Who is the *mudtarr*?
- The one with **no other door left**: every human means has failed, and he turns to Allah with complete need.

### Why answered?
- Scholars explain: desperation produces full sincerity and total reliance, and Allah loves this from His servant.

### Three acts of Allah
| Act | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Answers the distressed | Responds to duʿa |
| Removes harm | Lifts the trouble |
| Makes you successors | Generations inherit the earth |

### Even disbelievers
- The Quran mentions that people at sea call Allah sincerely in storms (Yunus 10:22). Desperation reveals the truth of tawhid within.

### Lessons
- **Call Allah with the heart of the desperate**, even in normal times.
- **No situation is hopeless** with Allah.
