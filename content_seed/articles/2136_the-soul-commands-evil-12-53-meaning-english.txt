﴿۞وَمَآ أُبَرِّئُ نَفۡسِيٓۚ إِنَّ ٱلنَّفۡسَ لَأَمَّارَةُۢ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ إِلَّا مَا رَحِمَ رَبِّيٓۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ [Yusuf 12:53]

## Who said it?
| View | Basis |
|---|---|
| The wife of al-ʿAziz | It continues her words *Now the truth has become clear*, a view favoured by a number of researchers |
| Yusuf | Said out of humility, a view of a group of commentators |

## *Ammarah*
- An intensive form: constantly urging evil.

## Three souls in the Quran
| Soul | Verse |
|---|---|
| Commanding evil | This verse |
| Self-reproaching | *I swear by the self-reproaching soul* (Al-Qiyamah 75:2) |
| Tranquil | *O tranquil soul* (Al-Fajr 89:27) |

## "Except whom my Lord has mercy on"
- Protection from the soul's evil is by **Allah's mercy**, not human strength alone.

## Lessons
- **Do not praise yourself** or feel safe from your soul.
- **Seek Allah's mercy** to be saved from it.
