Surah Az-Zumar contains what many scholars call the most hopeful verse in the Quran: "Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins." It was revealed for people who had committed the gravest sins and wondered whether there was any way back. The surah is built around sincerity: "Unquestionably, for Allah is the pure religion." It contrasts the one who prays at night, fearing the hereafter and hoping for mercy, with the one who forgets Allah when his hardship ends. It describes the Quran as the best of speech, at which the skins of believers shiver and then soften. And it ends with a scene that gives the surah its name: people driven in groups (*zumar*), some to Hell and some to Paradise, where the gates are opened and the keepers greet them: "Peace be upon you; you have become pure."

This guide explains Surah Az-Zumar section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Az-Zumar
- **Number of verses**: 75.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 39, in the twenty-third and twenty-fourth juz.
- **Name**: Az-Zumar means "the groups", from verses 71 and 73.
- **A nightly recitation**: at-Tirmidhi reported that 'A'ishah said the Prophet ﷺ would not sleep until he had recited Bani Isra'il (Al-Isra') and Az-Zumar.
- **Main themes**: sincerity, the signs of creation, the Quran, hope and repentance, and the final destinations.

## Verses 1–4: The Pure Religion
﴿تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ فَٱعۡبُدِ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصٗا لَّهُ ٱلدِّينَ﴾ ﴿أَلَا لِلَّهِ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡخَالِصُۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مَا نَعۡبُدُهُمۡ إِلَّا لِيُقَرِّبُونَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ زُلۡفَىٰٓ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَهُمۡ فِي مَا هُمۡ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي مَنۡ هُوَ كَٰذِبࣱ كَفَّارࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّوۡ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ أَن يَتَّخِذَ وَلَدٗا لَّٱصۡطَفَىٰ مِمَّا يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥۖ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡوَٰحِدُ ٱلۡقَهَّارُ﴾ (Quran 39:1-4)

**"So worship Allah, being sincere to Him in religion. Unquestionably, for Allah is the pure religion."** Worship mixed with anything else is not accepted.

**The idolaters' excuse**: **"We only worship them that they may bring us nearer to Allah in position."** This is the root of every form of intercession-based shirk: taking intermediaries in worship. Allah rejects it and calls them liars and disbelievers.

## Verses 5–8: Creation in Three Darknesses
﴿خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ يُكَوِّرُ ٱلَّيۡلَ عَلَى ٱلنَّهَارِ وَيُكَوِّرُ ٱلنَّهَارَ عَلَى ٱلَّيۡلِۖ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلࣱّ يَجۡرِي لِأَجَلٖ مُّسَمًّىۗ أَلَا هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡغَفَّـٰرُ﴾ ﴿خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفۡسٖ وَٰحِدَةٖ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنۡهَا زَوۡجَهَا وَأَنزَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ ثَمَٰنِيَةَ أَزۡوَٰجٖۚ يَخۡلُقُكُمۡ فِي بُطُونِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡ خَلۡقٗا مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ خَلۡقٖ فِي ظُلُمَٰتٖ ثَلَٰثٖۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡ لَهُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُصۡرَفُونَ﴾ (Quran 39:5-6)

**"He creates you in the wombs of your mothers, creation after creation, within three darknesses."** Commentators explained the three darknesses as the belly, the womb and the membrane around the foetus.

﴿۞وَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ ضُرࣱّ دَعَا رَبَّهُۥ مُنِيبًا إِلَيۡهِ ثُمَّ إِذَا خَوَّلَهُۥ نِعۡمَةٗ مِّنۡهُ نَسِيَ مَا كَانَ يَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ مِن قَبۡلُ وَجَعَلَ لِلَّهِ أَندَادٗا لِّيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۚ قُلۡ تَمَتَّعۡ بِكُفۡرِكَ قَلِيلًا إِنَّكَ مِنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ (Quran 39:8)

**"And when adversity touches man, he calls upon his Lord, turning to Him. Then when He bestows on him a favour from Himself, he forgets Him whom he called upon before."** A human pattern the surah warns against.

## Verses 9–10: Those Who Know and Those Who Do Not
﴿أَمَّنۡ هُوَ قَٰنِتٌ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيۡلِ سَاجِدٗا وَقَآئِمٗا يَحۡذَرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَيَرۡجُواْ رَحۡمَةَ رَبِّهِۦۗ قُلۡ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡلَمُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَۗ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ (Quran 39:9)

**"Is one who is devoutly obedient during periods of the night, prostrating and standing, fearing the hereafter and hoping for the mercy of his Lord, like one who does not?"** The night worshipper combines fear and hope. **"Say: Are those who know equal to those who do not know?"** True knowledge produces this worship.

﴿قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡۚ لِلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةࣱۗ وَأَرۡضُ ٱللَّهِ وَٰسِعَةٌۗ إِنَّمَا يُوَفَّى ٱلصَّـٰبِرُونَ أَجۡرَهُم بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ﴾ (Quran 39:10)

**"Indeed, the patient will be given their reward without account."** Every good deed has a known multiple, except patience, whose reward is beyond measure.

## Verses 11–20: Sincerity and Those Who Follow the Best
﴿قُلۡ إِنِّيٓ أُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَعۡبُدَ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصٗا لَّهُ ٱلدِّينَ﴾ ﴿وَأُمِرۡتُ لِأَنۡ أَكُونَ أَوَّلَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ إِنۡ عَصَيۡتُ رَبِّي عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾ ﴿قُلِ ٱللَّهَ أَعۡبُدُ مُخۡلِصٗا لَّهُۥ دِينِي﴾ ﴿فَٱعۡبُدُواْ مَا شِئۡتُم مِّن دُونِهِۦۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَأَهۡلِيهِمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۗ أَلَا ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡخُسۡرَانُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾ (Quran 39:11-15)

The Prophet ﷺ was commanded: **"I have been commanded to worship Allah, being sincere to Him in religion, and to be the first of the Muslims."** The true losers are **"those who will lose themselves and their families on the Day of Resurrection"**.

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱجۡتَنَبُواْ ٱلطَّـٰغُوتَ أَن يَعۡبُدُوهَا وَأَنَابُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ لَهُمُ ٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰۚ فَبَشِّرۡ عِبَادِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ فَيَتَّبِعُونَ أَحۡسَنَهُۥٓۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَدَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمۡ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ (Quran 39:17-18)

**"Give good tidings to My servants who listen to speech and follow the best of it."** Those who hear many words and choose the best. **"Those are the ones Allah has guided, and those are people of understanding."**

## Verses 21–23: The Opened Chest and the Best Speech
﴿أَفَمَن شَرَحَ ٱللَّهُ صَدۡرَهُۥ لِلۡإِسۡلَٰمِ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ نُورٖ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡقَٰسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مِّن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ﴾ (Quran 39:22)

**"Is one whose breast Allah has expanded to Islam so he is upon a light from his Lord like one whose heart rejects it?"** **"Woe to those whose hearts are hardened against the remembrance of Allah."**

﴿ٱللَّهُ نَزَّلَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡحَدِيثِ كِتَٰبٗا مُّتَشَٰبِهٗا مَّثَانِيَ تَقۡشَعِرُّ مِنۡهُ جُلُودُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُمۡ ثُمَّ تَلِينُ جُلُودُهُمۡ وَقُلُوبُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِي بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡ هَادٍ﴾ (Quran 39:23)

**"Allah has sent down the best statement: a consistent Book wherein is reiteration. The skins shiver therefrom of those who fear their Lord; then their skins and their hearts soften at the remembrance of Allah."** The Quran first shakes the believer with awe, then calms him with mercy. *Mathani*: themes repeated in pairs: Paradise and Hell, the believers and the disbelievers, promise and warning.

## Verses 24–35: Parables and Death
﴿ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا رَّجُلٗا فِيهِ شُرَكَآءُ مُتَشَٰكِسُونَ وَرَجُلٗا سَلَمٗا لِّرَجُلٍ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِيَانِ مَثَلًاۚ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 39:29)

**The parable of two slaves**: one owned by several quarrelling partners, each giving contradictory orders, and one belonging entirely to one man. **"Are they equal in comparison?"** The idolater is torn between many masters; the monotheist serves one Lord and finds peace.

﴿إِنَّكَ مَيِّتࣱ وَإِنَّهُم مَّيِّتُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ عِندَ رَبِّكُمۡ تَخۡتَصِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 39:30-31)

**"Indeed, you are to die, and indeed, they are to die. Then indeed you, on the Day of Resurrection, before your Lord, will dispute."** When the Prophet ﷺ died, Abu Bakr reminded the stunned Companions of this reality with the verses of the Quran, as reported in Sahih al-Bukhari.

## Verses 36–41: Is Not Allah Sufficient?
﴿أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِكَافٍ عَبۡدَهُۥۖ وَيُخَوِّفُونَكَ بِٱلَّذِينَ مِن دُونِهِۦۚ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡ هَادٖ﴾ (Quran 39:36)

**"Is not Allah sufficient for His servant? And they threaten you with those other than Him."** A verse of strength for anyone who is threatened or afraid.

﴿وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ لَيَقُولُنَّ ٱللَّهُۚ قُلۡ أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ إِنۡ أَرَادَنِيَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرٍّ هَلۡ هُنَّ كَٰشِفَٰتُ ضُرِّهِۦٓ أَوۡ أَرَادَنِي بِرَحۡمَةٍ هَلۡ هُنَّ مُمۡسِكَٰتُ رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ قُلۡ حَسۡبِيَ ٱللَّهُۖ عَلَيۡهِ يَتَوَكَّلُ ٱلۡمُتَوَكِّلُونَ﴾ (Quran 39:38)

If you ask them who created the heavens and earth, they will say Allah. **"Say: Then have you considered what you invoke besides Allah? If Allah intended me harm, are they removers of His harm? Or if He intended me mercy, are they withholders of His mercy? Say: Sufficient for me is Allah; upon Him relies whoever would rely."**

## Verses 42–52: Sleep and the Taking of Souls
﴿ٱللَّهُ يَتَوَفَّى ٱلۡأَنفُسَ حِينَ مَوۡتِهَا وَٱلَّتِي لَمۡ تَمُتۡ فِي مَنَامِهَاۖ فَيُمۡسِكُ ٱلَّتِي قَضَىٰ عَلَيۡهَا ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَيُرۡسِلُ ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰٓ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمًّىۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 39:42)

**"Allah takes the souls at the time of their death, and those that do not die during their sleep. Then He keeps those for which He has decreed death and releases the others for a specified term."** Every night is a small death and every morning a small resurrection. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ taught that when going to bed one should say: "In Your name, my Lord, I lay down my side, and by You I raise it. If You take my soul, have mercy on it, and if You release it, protect it as You protect Your righteous servants."

﴿وَإِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَحۡدَهُ ٱشۡمَأَزَّتۡ قُلُوبُ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَإِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ إِذَا هُمۡ يَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 39:45)

**"And when Allah is mentioned alone, the hearts of those who do not believe in the hereafter shrink with aversion, but when those other than Him are mentioned, immediately they rejoice."**

## Verses 53–61: Do Not Despair of Allah's Mercy
﴿۞قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 39:53)

**"Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful."**

**The reason for revelation**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas said that some people from among the idolaters had killed a great deal and committed fornication a great deal. They came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: "What you say and call to is good; if only you would tell us whether there is expiation for what we have done." So this verse was revealed.

**Notice "My servants"**: even those who have transgressed are called His servants, with tenderness. And **"all sins"**: no sin is too great for repentance.

﴿وَأَنِيبُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمۡ وَأَسۡلِمُواْ لَهُۥ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَكُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ ثُمَّ لَا تُنصَرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱتَّبِعُوٓاْ أَحۡسَنَ مَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكُم مِّن رَّبِّكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَكُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ بَغۡتَةٗ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَشۡعُرُونَ﴾ ﴿أَن تَقُولَ نَفۡسࣱ يَٰحَسۡرَتَىٰ عَلَىٰ مَا فَرَّطتُ فِي جَنۢبِ ٱللَّهِ وَإِن كُنتُ لَمِنَ ٱلسَّـٰخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿أَوۡ تَقُولَ لَوۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هَدَىٰنِي لَكُنتُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ ﴿أَوۡ تَقُولَ حِينَ تَرَى ٱلۡعَذَابَ لَوۡ أَنَّ لِي كَرَّةٗ فَأَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 39:54-58)

**The condition**: **"And return to your Lord and submit to Him before the punishment comes upon you."** Hope must lead to action: return, submit, follow the best of what has been revealed, before a soul says: **"Oh, how great is my regret over what I neglected in regard to Allah."**

## Verses 62–70: The Earth in His Grip
﴿وَمَا قَدَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ حَقَّ قَدۡرِهِۦ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ جَمِيعٗا قَبۡضَتُهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ مَطۡوِيَّـٰتُۢ بِيَمِينِهِۦۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ (Quran 39:67)

**"They have not appraised Allah with true appraisal, while the earth entirely will be within His grip on the Day of Resurrection, and the heavens will be folded in His right hand."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud reported that a rabbi came to the Prophet ﷺ and described how Allah will hold the heavens and the earth on one finger, and the Prophet ﷺ laughed in confirmation and recited this verse.

﴿وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَصَعِقَ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا مَن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۖ ثُمَّ نُفِخَ فِيهِ أُخۡرَىٰ فَإِذَا هُمۡ قِيَامࣱ يَنظُرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَأَشۡرَقَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ بِنُورِ رَبِّهَا وَوُضِعَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ وَجِاْيٓءَ بِٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَ وَٱلشُّهَدَآءِ وَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَوُفِّيَتۡ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّا عَمِلَتۡ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 39:68-70)

**The Trumpet is blown**, and all in the heavens and earth fall dead except whom Allah wills; then it is blown again and they stand, looking on. **"And the earth will shine with the light of its Lord."** The records are placed, the prophets and witnesses are brought, and judgement is made with truth.

## Verses 71–75: Driven in Groups
﴿وَسِيقَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ جَهَنَّمَ زُمَرًاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءُوهَا فُتِحَتۡ أَبۡوَٰبُهَا وَقَالَ لَهُمۡ خَزَنَتُهَآ أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِكُمۡ رُسُلࣱ مِّنكُمۡ يَتۡلُونَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّكُمۡ وَيُنذِرُونَكُمۡ لِقَآءَ يَوۡمِكُمۡ هَٰذَاۚ قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ وَلَٰكِنۡ حَقَّتۡ كَلِمَةُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ ﴿قِيلَ ٱدۡخُلُوٓاْ أَبۡوَٰبَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۖ فَبِئۡسَ مَثۡوَى ٱلۡمُتَكَبِّرِينَ﴾ (Quran 39:71-72)

**The disbelievers are driven to Hell in groups.** **When they arrive, its gates are opened**, and its keepers ask: **"Did there not come to you messengers from yourselves?"**

﴿وَسِيقَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ رَبَّهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡجَنَّةِ زُمَرًاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءُوهَا وَفُتِحَتۡ أَبۡوَٰبُهَا وَقَالَ لَهُمۡ خَزَنَتُهَا سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡ طِبۡتُمۡ فَٱدۡخُلُوهَا خَٰلِدِينَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي صَدَقَنَا وَعۡدَهُۥ وَأَوۡرَثَنَا ٱلۡأَرۡضَ نَتَبَوَّأُ مِنَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ حَيۡثُ نَشَآءُۖ فَنِعۡمَ أَجۡرُ ٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَتَرَى ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ حَآفِّينَ مِنۡ حَوۡلِ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡۚ وَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ وَقِيلَ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 39:73-75)

**The righteous are driven to Paradise in groups.** **"When they arrive there and its gates have been opened"**: notice the extra "and" in Arabic (*wa futihat*), which commentators said shows the gates were already open, waiting for them, like a house prepared for honoured guests. Its keepers say: **"Peace be upon you; you have become pure; so enter it to abide eternally."**

The surah ends with the angels surrounding the Throne, glorifying their Lord, and the words: **"All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds."**

## Word Meanings
- **Ad-Din al-Khalis**: the pure religion.
- **Zulfa**: nearness.
- **Zulumat thalath**: three darknesses.
- **Qanit**: devoutly obedient.
- **Bi-ghayri hisab**: without account, beyond measure.
- **Mathani**: reiteration, paired themes.
- **Taqsha'irru**: shiver.
- **Mutashakisun**: quarrelling partners.
- **Ishma'azzat**: shrank with aversion.
- **Asrafu**: transgressed.
- **Taqnatu**: despair.
- **Aniibu**: return.
- **Qabdatuhu**: His grip.
- **Matwiyyat**: folded.
- **Zumar**: groups.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Fearing the hereafter and hoping for mercy"**: the balance of worship in one line.
2. **"Skins shiver, then soften"**: the double effect of the Quran.
3. **The slave of many masters**: an unforgettable image of shirk.
4. **"O My servants who have transgressed"**: tenderness to the sinner.
5. **"And its gates have been opened"**: one letter that shows the honour of the welcome.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Ikhfa'**: in *zulumatin thalath* (verse 6), the tanween is hidden before the tha'.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *taqsha'irru* (verse 23), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna Allaha yaghfiru* (verse 53), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ja'uha* (verses 71 and 73).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Sad before it ended with "It is not but a reminder to the worlds", and Az-Zumar opens with "The revelation of the Book is from Allah". Surah Ghafir after it opens with Allah as "Forgiver of sin and Acceptor of repentance", continuing the hope of verse 53.

## A Programme of Hope and Repentance
1. **Never think your sin is too big.**
2. **Repent immediately** and do not delay.
3. **Follow repentance with good deeds.**
4. **Combine fear and hope.**
5. **Stand in prayer at night**, even a little.
6. **Be patient**: its reward is beyond measure.
7. **Say "Sufficient for me is Allah"** when afraid.

1. **Make your worship sincere.**
2. **Remember Allah in ease, not only in hardship.**
3. **Listen to many views and follow the best.**
4. **Let the Quran move your heart.**
5. **Say the bedtime du'a.**
6. **Never despair of Allah's mercy.**
7. **Prepare to be among the groups welcomed into Paradise.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does az-Zumar mean?
"The groups", referring to people driven to Hell and to Paradise in groups.

### What is the most hopeful verse in Surah Az-Zumar?
Verse 53: "O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins."

### Why was verse 53 revealed?
People who had committed killing and fornication asked if there was expiation for them, as Ibn 'Abbas reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does "the patient will be given their reward without account" mean?
The reward of patience is beyond measure.

### What are the "three darknesses"?
Commentators explained them as the belly, the womb and the membrane around the foetus.

### What does the surah say about sleep?
That Allah takes souls during sleep and returns them to those whose time has not come.

### How many verses are in Surah Az-Zumar?
75 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Az-Zumar calls you to sincere worship, warns you against forgetting Allah after He relieves you, and makes the Quran shake and soften your heart. Above all, it calls to every sinner with tenderness: do not despair of Allah's mercy, for He forgives all sins. Return to Him now, so that one day you may be among the groups who find the gates of Paradise already open and hear: "Peace be upon you; you have become pure."
