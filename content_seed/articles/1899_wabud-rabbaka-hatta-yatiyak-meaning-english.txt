﴿وَلَقَدۡ نَعۡلَمُ أَنَّكَ يَضِيقُ صَدۡرُكَ بِمَا يَقُولُونَ﴾ ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَكُن مِّنَ ٱلسَّـٰجِدِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱعۡبُدۡ رَبَّكَ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَكَ ٱلۡيَقِينُ﴾ [Al-Hijr 15:97-99]

## Allah knows your pain
- *We know that your chest is tightened by what they say.* Allah acknowledges the Prophet's distress at people's mockery.

## The remedy
| Remedy | Practice |
|---|---|
| Glorify with praise | *SubhanAllahi wa bihamdih* |
| Be among those who prostrate | Prayer and long sujud |
| Worship continuously | Never stop |

## What is *al-yaqin*?
- The Companions and scholars explained it as **death**: worship has no retirement. Al-Bukhari reported this explanation from Salim ibn ʿAbd Allah.

## No end date
- Some claim that after reaching a certain level, worship is no longer required. This verse refutes that: even the Prophet ﷺ worshipped until death.

## Lessons
- **When hurt by words, turn to prayer.**
- **Keep going** until the last breath.
