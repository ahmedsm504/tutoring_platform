Have you noticed that the moon changes shape every night? At the start of the month it is a thin, curved crescent. Night after night it grows until it becomes a full, round moon, then it shrinks again until it returns to a thin curve, like an old, dried stalk from a date-palm bunch. Allah describes this exact picture in Surah Yasin, comparing the moon at the end of the month to "the old date stalk", al-'urjun al-qadim. When that stalk dries out, it becomes yellow and curved, just like a crescent!

Surah Yasin is loved by Muslims everywhere, and many people memorize it. It is full of verses about the universe that amaze children: the sun that runs, the moon and its phases, night and day that never overtake each other, and ships that carry people across the sea. It also tells the story of a brave man who came running from the far end of the city to stand up for Allah's messengers. In this guide, we explain Surah Yasin to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Yasin

- **Position**: surah 36, in juz 22 and 23.
- **Number of verses**: 83.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: it begins with the two letters Ya Sin, among the "disconnected letters" whose meaning Allah knows best.

### A note on its virtues
Many hadiths about special virtues of Surah Yasin are widely shared, but most of them are weak according to hadith scholars, such as the one calling it "the heart of the Quran". We read and memorize it because it is the word of Allah, and every letter of the Quran brings a reward multiplied ten times, as in the hadith reported by at-Tirmidhi, who graded it authentic.

## Lesson 1: Allah Records Everything

﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَنَكۡتُبُ مَا قَدَّمُواْ وَءَاثَٰرَهُمۡۚ وَكُلَّ شَيۡءٍ أَحۡصَيۡنَٰهُ فِيٓ إِمَامٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 36:12)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah will bring the dead back to life on the Day of Judgement, and He records everything people did, and also their athar, their footprints. Footprints are what a person leaves behind: knowledge they taught, a tree they planted, or a good child who makes du'a for them. Everything is written in a clear record."

### From the Sunnah
In Sahih Muslim, the tribe of Banu Salimah wanted to move closer to the mosque, and the Prophet ﷺ told them: "Stay in your homes, your footsteps are recorded." Every step to the masjid counts!

### Activity: leave a good footprint
Ask your child: "What good footprint would you like to leave?" Plant something at school, teach a younger sibling a surah, or donate a book to the library. Then do one of them this week.

## Lesson 2: The Man Who Came Running

﴿وَجَآءَ مِنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ رَجُلࣱ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ (Quran 36:20)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah sent messengers to a town, but its people rejected and threatened them. At the far end of the city lived a believing man. When he heard what was happening, he came running and said: My people, follow the messengers! Follow those who ask you for nothing and are rightly guided. He defended them with beautiful words, and his people killed him. He was told: Enter Paradise. And he said: I wish my people knew how my Lord forgave me and honoured me."

### Lesson for kids: run towards good
"This man did not wait. He ran to say the truth. And even after his people hurt him, he still cared about them and wished they knew how Allah had honoured him, so they would believe too. A believer wants good for everyone."

### Activity: the good-deed runner
Challenge your child to be "the good-deed runner" this week: run to help grandma when she calls, hurry to pray when the adhan sounds, or rush to share a snack with a friend.

## Lesson 3: The Dead Earth Comes Alive

﴿وَءَايَةࣱ لَّهُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ ٱلۡمَيۡتَةُ أَحۡيَيۡنَٰهَا وَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مِنۡهَا حَبّٗا فَمِنۡهُ يَأۡكُلُونَ﴾ (Quran 36:33)

### Explaining it to your child
"One of Allah's signs is the dry, dead earth. Rain falls, and Allah brings it to life and grows grains that we eat, like wheat, rice and corn. The One who brings dead earth to life can bring people back to life too."

### Experiment: two pots of soil
Fill two pots with dry soil and plant seeds in both. Water only one. After a week, one pot is green and the other is not. Then read the verse together.

## Lesson 4: Everything in Pairs

﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَزۡوَٰجَ كُلَّهَا مِمَّا تُنۢبِتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمِنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَمِمَّا لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 36:36)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah created everything in pairs: male and female in people and animals, in plants too, and in things we don't even know about! Scientists have discovered pairs in many things people never knew about before, like positive and negative charges in electricity."

### Activity: the pairs game
Take turns naming pairs: night and day, sun and moon, hot and cold, sky and earth. After each pair, say "SubhanAllah".

## Lesson 5: Night and Day

﴿وَءَايَةࣱ لَّهُمُ ٱلَّيۡلُ نَسۡلَخُ مِنۡهُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فَإِذَا هُم مُّظۡلِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 36:37)

### Explaining it to your child
"Night is a sign of Allah. He peels the daylight away from it, like taking off a garment, and suddenly people are in darkness. Watch how the light disappears little by little at Maghrib!"

### Activity: watch the sunset
Sit with your child at sunset and watch the sky change from blue to orange to red to dark. Then pray Maghrib together and read the verse.

## Lesson 6: The Sun Runs

﴿وَٱلشَّمۡسُ تَجۡرِي لِمُسۡتَقَرّٖ لَّهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقۡدِيرُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ﴾ (Quran 36:38)

### Explaining it to your child
"The sun is not standing still. It runs on a course Allah has set for it, towards a resting place He decided. This is the decree of the Almighty, the All-Knowing, who plans everything precisely."

### A fun science fact
"Scientists have discovered that the sun moves through space at an incredible speed, carrying the earth and all the planets with it. The Quran said 'the sun runs' centuries before people knew this."

## Lesson 7: The Moon and Its Phases

﴿وَٱلۡقَمَرَ قَدَّرۡنَٰهُ مَنَازِلَ حَتَّىٰ عَادَ كَٱلۡعُرۡجُونِ ٱلۡقَدِيمِ﴾ (Quran 36:39)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah set phases for the moon, shapes it passes through every month: crescent, growing, full moon, shrinking, until it looks like the old curved stalk from a bunch of dates."

### Activity: moon diary
Make a "moon diary" with your child. Every night for a month, they look at the moon and draw its shape. At the end of the month, they will have seen all its phases for themselves! Show them a picture of a dried date stalk and compare it with the crescent.

## Lesson 8: Each Swimming in an Orbit

﴿لَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ يَنۢبَغِي لَهَآ أَن تُدۡرِكَ ٱلۡقَمَرَ وَلَا ٱلَّيۡلُ سَابِقُ ٱلنَّهَارِۚ وَكُلࣱّ فِي فَلَكٖ يَسۡبَحُونَ﴾ (Quran 36:40)

### Explaining it to your child
"The sun never catches up with the moon, and the night never overtakes the day. Each one has its own orbit and system, gliding along calmly, the way fish swim in water. They never crash! This is the precise system Allah created."

### Activity: the swimming planets
Let the children act it out: one is the sun in the middle, another is the earth moving around it, and a third is the moon moving around the earth. They move gently without bumping into each other. Then say together: "Each is swimming in an orbit."

## Lesson 9: Ships on the Sea

﴿وَءَايَةࣱ لَّهُمۡ أَنَّا حَمَلۡنَا ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ ٱلۡمَشۡحُونِ﴾ (Quran 36:41)

### Explaining it to your child
"One of Allah's signs is that He carries people in loaded ships across the sea. A ship is extremely heavy, made of steel and wood, full of people and cargo, and yet it floats! Who taught people to build ships? And who made water able to carry them? Allah."

### Experiment: float or sink?
Fill a tub with water and test different objects: a piece of wood, a stone, a paper boat, a plastic ball. Which ones float, and which sink? Then explain: "Allah made laws that let enormous ships float."

## Lesson 10: "Be, and It Is"

﴿وَضَرَبَ لَنَا مَثَلٗا وَنَسِيَ خَلۡقَهُۥۖ قَالَ مَن يُحۡيِ ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ وَهِيَ رَمِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ يُحۡيِيهَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَهَآ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ خَلۡقٍ عَلِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 36:78-79)

### Explaining it to your child
"A man came to the Prophet ﷺ holding an old, crumbling bone, crushed it in his hand and said: Who will bring bones back to life when they have turned to dust? Allah answered: Say, the One who created them the first time will bring them back to life. The One who created you from nothing can surely create you again."

﴿إِنَّمَآ أَمۡرُهُۥٓ إِذَآ أَرَادَ شَيۡـًٔا أَن يَقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾ ﴿فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 36:82-83)

### Explaining it to your child
"When Allah wants something, He simply says to it: Be, and it is, immediately! He needs no time, no tools and no help. So glory be to the One in whose hand is the kingdom of everything, and to Him we will all return."

### Lesson for kids
"We need tools and time to make things: a year to build a house, an hour to paint a picture. But Allah says: Kun, and it is. SubhanAllah!"

## Let's Read Together: Hands and Feet Will Speak

﴿ٱلۡيَوۡمَ نَخۡتِمُ عَلَىٰٓ أَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَتُكَلِّمُنَآ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَتَشۡهَدُ أَرۡجُلُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾ (Quran 36:65)

### Explaining it to your child
"On the Day of Judgement, Allah will seal people's mouths, and their hands will speak and their feet will testify about what they did. Your hands might say: He prayed with us and helped people. Your feet might say: She walked us to the masjid. So use your hands and feet for good, so they speak well of you."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Moon Diary"

At the start of the Islamic month, Tariq's mother gave him a small notebook with "Moon Diary" written on the cover. "Every night, go out on the balcony and draw the moon exactly as you see it," she said.

On the first night, Tariq searched the sky for a long time, then spotted a thin silver line just after Maghrib. He drew it and wrote: "Night 1: tiny crescent." Night after night he saw it grow, until it was half a circle, then more.

On the fourteenth night, Tariq stepped outside and saw a perfect, glowing circle, like a big lamp in the sky. "Mum, it's a full moon!" he shouted. Then it began shrinking night after night.

At the end of the month, Tariq flipped through his diary with his mother and saw the whole cycle: growing, then shrinking. His mother read the verse about the moon and its phases, then showed him a photo of an old date-palm stalk, yellow and curved. "It looks exactly like the crescent!" Tariq said. "How did people know this so long ago?" His mother answered: "Allah created the moon, and He is the One who described it in the Quran."

The next month, Tariq took his diary to school, showed his classmates, and read them the verse. His teacher said: "Tariq, you're a little astronomer!"

Ask your child: "How does the moon change during the month? What is the old date stalk?"

## What We Learn From Surah Yasin

- **Allah records** our deeds and our footprints.
- **Run towards good** without delay.
- **Want good for everyone**, even those who hurt us.
- **Allah brings dead earth to life**, and the dead too.
- **Everything is created in pairs**.
- **The sun and moon** follow a precise system.
- **Ships** are among Allah's signs.
- **"Be, and it is"**: Allah's power has no limits.
- **Our hands and feet** will speak on the Day of Judgement.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Is it a sunnah to read Yasin for the dead?
The hadith "Read Yasin over your dead" has weakness according to many hadith scholars. The whole Quran is good to read, and we teach children to read Yasin like any other surah, without fixed numbers or rituals.

### Is Yasin "the heart of the Quran"?
The hadith with this wording is weak according to hadith scholars. The surah is great because it is the word of Allah.

### Who was the man who came running?
The Quran does not name him; some commentators mention a name. What matters is his courage and his love for his people.

### When should a child memorize Yasin?
Usually after Juz 'Amma and Surah Al-Mulk, since its verses are longer. Before that, children can memorize the universe passage, verses 33 to 40, which is easy and fun to understand.
