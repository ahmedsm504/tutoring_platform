Surah An-Najm is the surah at which everyone prostrated. In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas said: "The Prophet ﷺ prostrated at An-Najm, and the Muslims, the idolaters, the jinn and mankind prostrated with him." Its verses are so powerful that even those who did not believe could not stay standing. It begins by defending the truthfulness of the Prophet ﷺ: "Your companion has not strayed, nor has he erred, nor does he speak from his own desire." It describes his sight of Jibril at the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary, exposes the emptiness of the idols, lays down principles of justice, and ends with a command that the whole gathering obeyed: "So prostrate to Allah and worship."

This guide explains Surah An-Najm section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah An-Najm
- **Number of verses**: 62.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 53, in the twenty-seventh juz.
- **Name**: An-Najm means "the star", from the first verse.
- **Prostration of recitation**: at the final verse.
- **A first**: in Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn Mas'ud said it was the first surah containing a prostration to be revealed, and that the Prophet ﷺ prostrated and everyone with him prostrated except one old man, who took a handful of dust and raised it to his forehead, saying "This is enough for me". Ibn Mas'ud said: "I later saw him killed as a disbeliever."

## Verses 1–4: Not From Desire
﴿وَٱلنَّجۡمِ إِذَا هَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿مَا ضَلَّ صَاحِبُكُمۡ وَمَا غَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحۡيࣱ يُوحَىٰ﴾ (Quran 53:1-4)

**"By the star when it descends."** Allah swears by the star as it sets or falls.

**The answer to the oath**:
- **"Your companion has not strayed"** (from the path through ignorance),
- **"nor has he erred"** (deliberately),
- **"nor does he speak from his own desire."**
- **"It is not but a revelation revealed."**

**Notice "your companion"**: Quraysh knew him. He lived among them forty years before prophethood, and they called him *al-Amin*, the trustworthy. Why would he lie about Allah now?

**The status of the Sunnah**: scholars took from these verses that what the Prophet ﷺ conveyed of religion is revelation, whether in the Quran or the Sunnah.

## Verses 5–18: The Two Sightings of Jibril
﴿عَلَّمَهُۥ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡقُوَىٰ﴾ ﴿ذُو مِرَّةٖ فَٱسۡتَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ بِٱلۡأُفُقِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ دَنَا فَتَدَلَّىٰ﴾ ﴿فَكَانَ قَابَ قَوۡسَيۡنِ أَوۡ أَدۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَأَوۡحَىٰٓ إِلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِۦ مَآ أَوۡحَىٰ﴾ ﴿مَا كَذَبَ ٱلۡفُؤَادُ مَا رَأَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَفَتُمَٰرُونَهُۥ عَلَىٰ مَا يَرَىٰ﴾ (Quran 53:5-12)

**"Taught to him by one intense in strength"**: Jibril ﷺ. He appeared in his true form on the high horizon, then **"approached and descended, and was at a distance of two bow lengths or nearer"**, and revealed to Allah's servant what He revealed. **"The heart did not lie about what it saw."**

﴿وَلَقَدۡ رَءَاهُ نَزۡلَةً أُخۡرَىٰ﴾ ﴿عِندَ سِدۡرَةِ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ﴾ ﴿عِندَهَا جَنَّةُ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ يَغۡشَى ٱلسِّدۡرَةَ مَا يَغۡشَىٰ﴾ ﴿مَا زَاغَ ٱلۡبَصَرُ وَمَا طَغَىٰ﴾ ﴿لَقَدۡ رَأَىٰ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّهِ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰٓ﴾ (Quran 53:13-18)

**The second sighting**: **"And he certainly saw him in another descent, at the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary, near which is the Garden of Refuge."** This was during the Night Journey and Ascension. **"When there covered the Lote Tree that which covered it"**: colours and light whose beauty cannot be described. **"The sight did not swerve, nor did it transgress"**: he looked with complete composure and discipline. **"He certainly saw of the greatest signs of his Lord."**

**Whom did he see?** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Masruq asked 'A'ishah, and she said that whoever claims that Muhammad saw his Lord has made a grave lie; rather, he saw Jibril in his true form twice. In Sahih Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud said: "He saw Jibril with six hundred wings."

## Verses 19–23: Al-Lat, Al-'Uzza and Manat
﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱللَّـٰتَ وَٱلۡعُزَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَمَنَوٰةَ ٱلثَّالِثَةَ ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَلَكُمُ ٱلذَّكَرُ وَلَهُ ٱلۡأُنثَىٰ﴾ ﴿تِلۡكَ إِذٗا قِسۡمَةࣱ ضِيزَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّآ أَسۡمَآءࣱ سَمَّيۡتُمُوهَآ أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُم مَّآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَا مِن سُلۡطَٰنٍۚ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّ وَمَا تَهۡوَى ٱلۡأَنفُسُۖ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُم مِّن رَّبِّهِمُ ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ﴾ (Quran 53:19-23)

**"Have you considered al-Lat and al-'Uzza, and Manat, the third, the other one?"** The great idols of the Arabs. They called them daughters of Allah, while they themselves disliked having daughters: **"Is the male for you and for Him the female? That then is an unjust division."**

**"They are not but names you have named them, you and your forefathers, for which Allah has sent down no authority."** Idols are names without reality. **"They follow not except assumption and what their souls desire, while there has already come to them from their Lord guidance."**

**A note on a story about these verses**: a story circulates claiming that Shaytan inserted words praising these idols into the Prophet's ﷺ recitation. Many leading scholars rejected it as inauthentic, and it contradicts the opening of this very surah: "nor does he speak from his own desire".

## Verses 24–30: Intercession and Assumption
﴿أَمۡ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ مَا تَمَنَّىٰ﴾ ﴿فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰ﴾ ﴿۞وَكَم مِّن مَّلَكٖ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ لَا تُغۡنِي شَفَٰعَتُهُمۡ شَيۡـًٔا إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ أَن يَأۡذَنَ ٱللَّهُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَرۡضَىٰٓ﴾ (Quran 53:24-26)

**"Or is there for man whatever he wishes?"** No. **"To Allah belongs the hereafter and the first life."** Even the angels' intercession benefits no one except after Allah permits it for whom He wills and approves.

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لَيُسَمُّونَ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ تَسۡمِيَةَ ٱلۡأُنثَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَمَا لَهُم بِهِۦ مِنۡ عِلۡمٍۖ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّۖ وَإِنَّ ٱلظَّنَّ لَا يُغۡنِي مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّ شَيۡـٔٗا﴾ ﴿فَأَعۡرِضۡ عَن مَّن تَوَلَّىٰ عَن ذِكۡرِنَا وَلَمۡ يُرِدۡ إِلَّا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ مَبۡلَغُهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ﴾ (Quran 53:27-30)

They named the angels with female names without knowledge, following assumption, and **"assumption avails not against the truth at all"**. **"So turn away from whoever turns his back on Our message and desires not except the worldly life. That is their sum of knowledge."** A striking verse: some people's entire knowledge stops at this world.

## Verses 31–32: Avoiding Major Sins
﴿وَلِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لِيَجۡزِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسَـٰٓـُٔواْ بِمَا عَمِلُواْ وَيَجۡزِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَى﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَجۡتَنِبُونَ كَبَـٰٓئِرَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ إِلَّا ٱللَّمَمَۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ وَٰسِعُ ٱلۡمَغۡفِرَةِۚ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِكُمۡ إِذۡ أَنشَأَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِذۡ أَنتُمۡ أَجِنَّةࣱ فِي بُطُونِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡۖ فَلَا تُزَكُّوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡۖ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَنِ ٱتَّقَىٰٓ﴾ (Quran 53:31-32)

**"Those who avoid the major sins and immoralities, only committing slight ones"** (*al-lamam*): small faults that a person falls into and then repents from. **"Indeed, your Lord is vast in forgiveness."**

**"He was most knowing of you when He produced you from the earth and when you were foetuses in your mothers' wombs. So do not claim yourselves to be pure; He is most knowing of who fears Him."** Do not praise yourself. Allah knows you better than you know yourself.

## Verses 33–41: Only What He Strove For
﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي تَوَلَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَعۡطَىٰ قَلِيلٗا وَأَكۡدَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَعِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ فَهُوَ يَرَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ لَمۡ يُنَبَّأۡ بِمَا فِي صُحُفِ مُوسَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ ٱلَّذِي وَفَّىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَلَّا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةࣱ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَن لَّيۡسَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّ سَعۡيَهُۥ سَوۡفَ يُرَىٰ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ يُجۡزَىٰهُ ٱلۡجَزَآءَ ٱلۡأَوۡفَىٰ﴾ (Quran 53:33-41)

**The man who turned away**: he gave a little, then held back. **Does he have knowledge of the unseen?**

**Principles from the scriptures of Musa and Ibrahim**, "who fulfilled his obligations":
1. **"No bearer of burdens will bear the burden of another."**
2. **"There is not for man except that for which he strives."**
3. **"His effort is going to be seen."**
4. **"Then he will be recompensed with the fullest recompense."**

**An important clarification**: "There is not for man except that for which he strives" establishes that a person is judged by his own effort. At the same time, authentic texts show that a person benefits from others' du'a for him, charity given on his behalf, and the ongoing charity he left behind. Scholars reconciled these: the verse describes what a person *owns* by right, while the other texts describe what reaches him through Allah's bounty.

## Verses 42–55: To Your Lord Is the Finality
﴿وَأَنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ أَضۡحَكَ وَأَبۡكَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ أَمَاتَ وَأَحۡيَا﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّهُۥ خَلَقَ ٱلزَّوۡجَيۡنِ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰ﴾ ﴿مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ إِذَا تُمۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلنَّشۡأَةَ ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ أَغۡنَىٰ وَأَقۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلشِّعۡرَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّهُۥٓ أَهۡلَكَ عَادًا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَثَمُودَاْ فَمَآ أَبۡقَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَقَوۡمَ نُوحٖ مِّن قَبۡلُۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ هُمۡ أَظۡلَمَ وَأَطۡغَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡمُؤۡتَفِكَةَ أَهۡوَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَغَشَّىٰهَا مَا غَشَّىٰ﴾ ﴿فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكَ تَتَمَارَىٰ﴾ (Quran 53:42-55)

**"And that to your Lord is the finality."** Then a beautiful list of His actions:
- **He makes laugh and makes weep.**
- **He causes death and gives life.**
- **He created the pairs, male and female, from a drop.**
- **Upon Him is the next creation.**
- **He enriches and suffices.**
- **He is the Lord of Sirius**, the star some Arabs worshipped.
- **He destroyed the first 'Ad, and Thamud, and the people of Nuh, and the overturned towns.**

**"Then which of the favours of your Lord do you doubt?"**

## Verses 56–62: Prostrate to Allah
﴿هَٰذَا نَذِيرࣱ مِّنَ ٱلنُّذُرِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَزِفَتِ ٱلۡأٓزِفَةُ﴾ ﴿لَيۡسَ لَهَا مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ كَاشِفَةٌ﴾ ﴿أَفَمِنۡ هَٰذَا ٱلۡحَدِيثِ تَعۡجَبُونَ﴾ ﴿وَتَضۡحَكُونَ وَلَا تَبۡكُونَ﴾ ﴿وَأَنتُمۡ سَٰمِدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ۩﴾ (Quran 53:56-62)

**"This is a warner like the former warners. The Approaching Day has approached. Of it, other than Allah, there is no remover."**

**"Then at this statement do you wonder? And you laugh and do not weep, while you are proudly sporting?"** Three wrong responses to the Quran: wonder, laughter and heedlessness, while weeping would be the right one.

**"So prostrate to Allah and worship."** And at this, everyone present prostrated.

## Word Meanings
- **Hawa**: descends, sets; and in verse 3, desire.
- **Dalla**: strayed.
- **Ghawa**: erred.
- **Shadid al-quwa**: intense in strength, Jibril.
- **Qaba qawsayn**: two bow lengths.
- **Sidrat al-Muntaha**: the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary.
- **Jannat al-Ma'wa**: the Garden of Refuge.
- **Dizah**: unjust.
- **Al-Lamam**: small faults.
- **Ajinnah**: foetuses.
- **Akda**: held back.
- **Ash-Shi'ra**: Sirius.
- **Al-Azifah**: the Approaching Day.
- **Samidun**: proudly sporting, heedless.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Hawa" twice**: the star descending and the desire denied, the same root with two meanings.
2. **"The sight did not swerve, nor did it transgress"**: perfect composure in the greatest moment.
3. **"Names you have named"**: exposing idols as empty words.
4. **"That is their sum of knowledge"**: a cutting description.
5. **The list of His actions**: laughter and weeping, death and life.
6. **"You laugh and do not weep"**: a reproach that still stings.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *wan-najmi* (verse 1), the jim sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *wahyun yuha* (verse 4), the tanween merges into the ya'.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna rabbaka* (verse 30), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Prostration**: at the end of verse 62.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah At-Tur before it ended with "at the retreat of the stars", and An-Najm begins "By the star when it descends". Surah Al-Qamar after it begins "The Hour has come near", continuing "The Approaching Day has approached".

## Practical Lessons
1. **Trust the Prophet ﷺ**: he does not speak from desire.
2. **Follow the Sunnah**: it is revelation.
3. **Reject superstition and idols** in every form.
4. **Avoid major sins** and repent quickly from small ones.
5. **Do not praise yourself.**
6. **Know that your effort will be seen.**
7. **Weep, do not laugh, when you hear the Quran's warnings.**
8. **Prostrate at the end of the surah.**

## How to Memorise Surah An-Najm
- **Week 1**: verses 1–18.
- **Week 2**: verses 19–32.
- **Week 3**: verses 33–62.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them about Jibril** and how the Prophet ﷺ saw him with six hundred wings.
- **Teach them that only Allah gives laughter and tears, life and death.**
- **Prostrate together** when you reach the end of the surah.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "nor does he speak from his own desire" mean?
The Prophet ﷺ conveys only revelation from Allah, not personal whims.

### What is the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary?
A tree in the seventh heaven near the Garden of Refuge, where the Prophet ﷺ saw Jibril in his true form during the Ascension.

### Did the Prophet ﷺ see Allah during the Ascension?
'A'ishah said he saw Jibril in his true form, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim, and that whoever claims he saw his Lord has said something grave.

### What is al-lamam?
Small faults that a person falls into and then repents from.

### What does "There is not for man except that for which he strives" mean?
A person is judged by his own efforts; others' du'a and charity on his behalf reach him through Allah's bounty, as other authentic texts show.

### Is there a prostration in Surah An-Najm?
Yes, at the last verse, and everyone present prostrated when the Prophet ﷺ recited it, as in Sahih al-Bukhari.

### How many verses are in Surah An-Najm?
62 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah An-Najm assures you that the Prophet ﷺ did not speak from desire, takes you to the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary, empties the idols of meaning, and reminds you that only your effort will be seen and recompensed. Its last word is the right response to everything you have heard: prostrate to Allah and worship.
