Listening is one of the strongest tools for hifz, but the reciter you choose matters.

## What to look for
| Quality | Why |
|---|---|
| Slow, clear pace (murattal) | You hear every letter and rule |
| Accurate tajweed | You copy what you hear |
| Consistent style | Easier to remember |
| Riwayah Hafs (if you read Hafs) | Matches your Mushaf |

## Teaching vs performance
- **Teaching (muʿallim) recordings** repeat each ayah, sometimes with children repeating after the sheikh. Excellent for beginners.
- **Murattal** is steady recitation, ideal for daily memorization.
- **Mujawwad** is slower and melodic, beautiful to listen to but less practical for hifz.

## Reciters many memorizers use
- **Sheikh Mahmoud Khalil al-Husary**: very clear murattal and a teaching recording.
- **Sheikh Muhammad Siddiq al-Minshawi**: murattal and a teaching recording with children.
- Choose any **trusted reciter** whose style helps you; these are examples, not the only options.

## Stick to one
- Switching reciters often confuses your memory of the melody and the stops.

## Don't depend only on audio
- **Read from the Mushaf** while listening, so you memorize the page as well.
- **Recite to a teacher**: listening does not correct your own mistakes.
