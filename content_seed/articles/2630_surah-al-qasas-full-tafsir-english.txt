Surah Al-Qasas, "The Stories", tells the life of Musa in more detail than anywhere else in the Quran: his birth under a tyrant who was killing baby boys, his mother casting him into the river, his upbringing in the palace of the very man who sought to kill him, his flight to Madyan, his marriage, his call at the burning bush, and his return to confront Pharaoh. Then it turns to Qarun, a man destroyed by his wealth. Through it all runs one promise: Allah favours the oppressed and makes them inheritors. It was revealed in Makkah when the believers were weak, and it ends with a promise to the Prophet ﷺ that he would return.

This guide explains Surah Al-Qasas section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Qasas
- **Number of verses**: 88.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 28, in the twentieth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Qasas means "the stories", from verse 25.
- **Main themes**: Allah's plan for the oppressed, trust in Allah, the story of Musa, wealth and arrogance, and the hereafter.

## Verses 1–6: Pharaoh's Tyranny and Allah's Will
﴿إِنَّ فِرۡعَوۡنَ عَلَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَجَعَلَ أَهۡلَهَا شِيَعٗا يَسۡتَضۡعِفُ طَآئِفَةٗ مِّنۡهُمۡ يُذَبِّحُ أَبۡنَآءَهُمۡ وَيَسۡتَحۡيِۦ نِسَآءَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:4)

**"Indeed, Pharaoh exalted himself in the land and made its people into factions, oppressing a sector among them, slaughtering their sons and keeping their females alive."**

﴿وَنُرِيدُ أَن نَّمُنَّ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتُضۡعِفُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَنَجۡعَلَهُمۡ أَئِمَّةٗ وَنَجۡعَلَهُمُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثِينَ﴾ ﴿وَنُمَكِّنَ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَنُرِيَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَهَٰمَٰنَ وَجُنُودَهُمَا مِنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَحۡذَرُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:5-6)

**"And We wanted to confer favour upon those who were oppressed in the land and make them leaders and make them inheritors."** Pharaoh had his plan; Allah had His will.

## Verses 7–13: The Mother of Musa
﴿وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنۡ أَرۡضِعِيهِۖ فَإِذَا خِفۡتِ عَلَيۡهِ فَأَلۡقِيهِ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ وَلَا تَخَافِي وَلَا تَحۡزَنِيٓۖ إِنَّا رَآدُّوهُ إِلَيۡكِ وَجَاعِلُوهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:7)

**"And We inspired the mother of Musa: Suckle him; but when you fear for him, cast him into the river and do not fear and do not grieve. Indeed, We will return him to you and will make him one of the messengers."** Two commands, two prohibitions, and two promises in one verse.

﴿فَٱلۡتَقَطَهُۥٓ ءَالُ فِرۡعَوۡنَ لِيَكُونَ لَهُمۡ عَدُوّٗا وَحَزَنًاۗ إِنَّ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَهَٰمَٰنَ وَجُنُودَهُمَا كَانُواْ خَٰطِـِٔينَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتُ فِرۡعَوۡنَ قُرَّتُ عَيۡنٖ لِّي وَلَكَۖ لَا تَقۡتُلُوهُ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَنفَعَنَآ أَوۡ نَتَّخِذَهُۥ وَلَدٗا وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:8-9)

The family of Pharaoh picked him up, **"so that he would become to them an enemy and a grief"**. Pharaoh's wife said: **"A comfort of the eye for me and for you. Do not kill him."**

﴿وَأَصۡبَحَ فُؤَادُ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰ فَٰرِغًاۖ إِن كَادَتۡ لَتُبۡدِي بِهِۦ لَوۡلَآ أَن رَّبَطۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِهَا لِتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:10)

**"And the heart of Musa's mother became empty."** She nearly revealed him, **"if We had not bound fast her heart"**.

﴿۞وَحَرَّمۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَرَاضِعَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَقَالَتۡ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِ بَيۡتٖ يَكۡفُلُونَهُۥ لَكُمۡ وَهُمۡ لَهُۥ نَٰصِحُونَ﴾ ﴿فَرَدَدۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّهِۦ كَيۡ تَقَرَّ عَيۡنُهَا وَلَا تَحۡزَنَ وَلِتَعۡلَمَ أَنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:12-13)

Allah forbade him every wet nurse until his sister said: **"Shall I direct you to a household that will be responsible for him?"** **"So We restored him to his mother that she might be content and not grieve and that she would know that the promise of Allah is true."**

## Verses 14–21: The Killing and the Flight
﴿وَدَخَلَ ٱلۡمَدِينَةَ عَلَىٰ حِينِ غَفۡلَةٖ مِّنۡ أَهۡلِهَا فَوَجَدَ فِيهَا رَجُلَيۡنِ يَقۡتَتِلَانِ هَٰذَا مِن شِيعَتِهِۦ وَهَٰذَا مِنۡ عَدُوِّهِۦۖ فَٱسۡتَغَٰثَهُ ٱلَّذِي مِن شِيعَتِهِۦ عَلَى ٱلَّذِي مِنۡ عَدُوِّهِۦ فَوَكَزَهُۥ مُوسَىٰ فَقَضَىٰ عَلَيۡهِۖ قَالَ هَٰذَا مِنۡ عَمَلِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۖ إِنَّهُۥ عَدُوࣱّ مُّضِلࣱّ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ ﴿قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي ظَلَمۡتُ نَفۡسِي فَٱغۡفِرۡ لِي فَغَفَرَ لَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 28:15-16)

As a grown man, Musa saw two men fighting, one of his people and one of his enemies. He struck the enemy and killed him unintentionally. He immediately said: **"This is from the work of Satan."** And: **"My Lord, indeed I have wronged myself, so forgive me," and He forgave him.**

﴿وَجَآءَ رَجُلࣱ مِّنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّ ٱلۡمَلَأَ يَأۡتَمِرُونَ بِكَ لِيَقۡتُلُوكَ فَٱخۡرُجۡ إِنِّي لَكَ مِنَ ٱلنَّـٰصِحِينَ﴾ ﴿فَخَرَجَ مِنۡهَا خَآئِفٗا يَتَرَقَّبُۖ قَالَ رَبِّ نَجِّنِي مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:20-21)

A man came running from the far end of the city to warn him that the chiefs were planning to kill him. **"So he left it, fearful and anticipating. He said: My Lord, save me from the wrongdoing people."**

## Verses 22–28: Madyan and the Prayer of Need
﴿وَلَمَّا تَوَجَّهَ تِلۡقَآءَ مَدۡيَنَ قَالَ عَسَىٰ رَبِّيٓ أَن يَهۡدِيَنِي سَوَآءَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ﴾ (Quran 28:22)

Heading toward Madyan, he said: **"Perhaps my Lord will guide me to the sound way."**

﴿وَلَمَّا وَرَدَ مَآءَ مَدۡيَنَ وَجَدَ عَلَيۡهِ أُمَّةٗ مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ يَسۡقُونَ وَوَجَدَ مِن دُونِهِمُ ٱمۡرَأَتَيۡنِ تَذُودَانِۖ قَالَ مَا خَطۡبُكُمَاۖ قَالَتَا لَا نَسۡقِي حَتَّىٰ يُصۡدِرَ ٱلرِّعَآءُۖ وَأَبُونَا شَيۡخࣱ كَبِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿فَسَقَىٰ لَهُمَا ثُمَّ تَوَلَّىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلظِّلِّ فَقَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَآ أَنزَلۡتَ إِلَيَّ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ فَقِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 28:23-24)

At the well, he found shepherds watering, and two women holding back their flock. Their father was a very old man. Musa watered for them, then turned to the shade and said: **"My Lord, indeed I am, for whatever good You would send down to me, in need."** A fugitive, alone, hungry and without shelter, he asked for nothing specific; he simply declared his need.

﴿فَجَآءَتۡهُ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا تَمۡشِي عَلَى ٱسۡتِحۡيَآءٖ قَالَتۡ إِنَّ أَبِي يَدۡعُوكَ لِيَجۡزِيَكَ أَجۡرَ مَا سَقَيۡتَ لَنَاۚ فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُۥ وَقَصَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡقَصَصَ قَالَ لَا تَخَفۡۖ نَجَوۡتَ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:25)

**The answer came immediately**: **"Then one of the two women came to him, walking with shyness."** Her father invited him to reward him.

﴿قَالَتۡ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا يَـٰٓأَبَتِ ٱسۡتَـٔۡجِرۡهُۖ إِنَّ خَيۡرَ مَنِ ٱسۡتَـٔۡجَرۡتَ ٱلۡقَوِيُّ ٱلۡأَمِينُ﴾ (Quran 28:26)

**"One of the women said: O my father, hire him. Indeed, the best one you can hire is the strong and the trustworthy."** Two qualities for any role: competence and honesty.

﴿قَالَ إِنِّيٓ أُرِيدُ أَنۡ أُنكِحَكَ إِحۡدَى ٱبۡنَتَيَّ هَٰتَيۡنِ عَلَىٰٓ أَن تَأۡجُرَنِي ثَمَٰنِيَ حِجَجٖۖ فَإِنۡ أَتۡمَمۡتَ عَشۡرٗا فَمِنۡ عِندِكَۖ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَنۡ أَشُقَّ عَلَيۡكَۚ سَتَجِدُنِيٓ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ ذَٰلِكَ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكَۖ أَيَّمَا ٱلۡأَجَلَيۡنِ قَضَيۡتُ فَلَا عُدۡوَٰنَ عَلَيَّۖ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ مَا نَقُولُ وَكِيلࣱ﴾ (Quran 28:27-28)

The father offered him one of his daughters in marriage for eight years of work, or ten if he chose. Musa agreed: **"And Allah, over what we say, is Witness."**

## Verses 29–35: The Fire, the Staff and the Hand
﴿۞فَلَمَّا قَضَىٰ مُوسَى ٱلۡأَجَلَ وَسَارَ بِأَهۡلِهِۦٓ ءَانَسَ مِن جَانِبِ ٱلطُّورِ نَارٗاۖ قَالَ لِأَهۡلِهِ ٱمۡكُثُوٓاْ إِنِّيٓ ءَانَسۡتُ نَارٗا لَّعَلِّيٓ ءَاتِيكُم مِّنۡهَا بِخَبَرٍ أَوۡ جَذۡوَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَصۡطَلُونَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّآ أَتَىٰهَا نُودِيَ مِن شَٰطِيِٕ ٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡأَيۡمَنِ فِي ٱلۡبُقۡعَةِ ٱلۡمُبَٰرَكَةِ مِنَ ٱلشَّجَرَةِ أَن يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنِّيٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:29-30)

After completing the term, travelling with his family, he saw a fire. At the blessed spot he was called: **"O Musa, indeed I am Allah, Lord of the worlds."**

﴿وَأَنۡ أَلۡقِ عَصَاكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهَا تَهۡتَزُّ كَأَنَّهَا جَآنࣱّ وَلَّىٰ مُدۡبِرٗا وَلَمۡ يُعَقِّبۡۚ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ أَقۡبِلۡ وَلَا تَخَفۡۖ إِنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأٓمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱسۡلُكۡ يَدَكَ فِي جَيۡبِكَ تَخۡرُجۡ بَيۡضَآءَ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ سُوٓءٖ وَٱضۡمُمۡ إِلَيۡكَ جَنَاحَكَ مِنَ ٱلرَّهۡبِۖ فَذَٰنِكَ بُرۡهَٰنَانِ مِن رَّبِّكَ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَإِيْهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:31-32)

His staff became a moving snake, his hand came out white without disease, and Allah told him to draw his arm to himself against fear.

﴿وَأَخِي هَٰرُونُ هُوَ أَفۡصَحُ مِنِّي لِسَانٗا فَأَرۡسِلۡهُ مَعِيَ رِدۡءٗا يُصَدِّقُنِيٓۖ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ أَن يُكَذِّبُونِ﴾ ﴿قَالَ سَنَشُدُّ عَضُدَكَ بِأَخِيكَ وَنَجۡعَلُ لَكُمَا سُلۡطَٰنٗا فَلَا يَصِلُونَ إِلَيۡكُمَا بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآۚ أَنتُمَا وَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَكُمَا ٱلۡغَٰلِبُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:34-35)

Musa asked for his brother Harun as a helper, **"for he is more fluent than me in tongue"**. Allah said: **"We will strengthen your arm through your brother."**

## Verses 36–42: Pharaoh's Arrogance and End
﴿وَقَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَأُ مَا عَلِمۡتُ لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرِي فَأَوۡقِدۡ لِي يَٰهَٰمَٰنُ عَلَى ٱلطِّينِ فَٱجۡعَل لِّي صَرۡحٗا لَّعَلِّيٓ أَطَّلِعُ إِلَىٰٓ إِلَٰهِ مُوسَىٰ وَإِنِّي لَأَظُنُّهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:38)

**"And Pharaoh said: O eminent ones, I have not known you to have a god other than me."** He ordered Haman to build a tower to look for the God of Musa.

﴿فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُ وَجُنُودَهُۥ فَنَبَذۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّۖ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:40)

**"So We seized him and his soldiers and threw them into the sea. So see how was the end of the wrongdoers."**

## Verses 43–75: Guidance Is From Allah
﴿إِنَّكَ لَا تَهۡدِي مَنۡ أَحۡبَبۡتَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:56)

**"Indeed, you do not guide whom you like, but Allah guides whom He wills."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, this was revealed about the Prophet's uncle Abu Talib, whom he loved and urged to say the testimony at his death.

﴿وَرَبُّكَ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُ وَيَخۡتَارُۗ مَا كَانَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡخِيَرَةُۚ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:68)

**"And your Lord creates what He wills and chooses."**

﴿قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ سَرۡمَدًا إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ مَنۡ إِلَٰهٌ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِ يَأۡتِيكُم بِضِيَآءٍۚ أَفَلَا تَسۡمَعُونَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلنَّهَارَ سَرۡمَدًا إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ مَنۡ إِلَٰهٌ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِ يَأۡتِيكُم بِلَيۡلٖ تَسۡكُنُونَ فِيهِۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:71-72)

**"Say: Have you considered: if Allah should make for you the night continuous until the Day of Resurrection, what deity other than Allah could bring you light?"** And the same for endless day.

## Verses 76–82: Qarun and His Treasures
﴿۞إِنَّ قَٰرُونَ كَانَ مِن قَوۡمِ مُوسَىٰ فَبَغَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡكُنُوزِ مَآ إِنَّ مَفَاتِحَهُۥ لَتَنُوٓأُ بِٱلۡعُصۡبَةِ أُوْلِي ٱلۡقُوَّةِ إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُۥ قَوۡمُهُۥ لَا تَفۡرَحۡۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡفَرِحِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:76)

**"Indeed, Qarun was from the people of Musa, but he tyrannised them."** The keys of his treasures were a heavy burden for a strong group of men. His people advised him: **"Do not exult. Indeed, Allah does not like the exultant."**

﴿وَٱبۡتَغِ فِيمَآ ءَاتَىٰكَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلدَّارَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۖ وَلَا تَنسَ نَصِيبَكَ مِنَ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَأَحۡسِن كَمَآ أَحۡسَنَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَيۡكَۖ وَلَا تَبۡغِ ٱلۡفَسَادَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:77)

**"But seek, through that which Allah has given you, the home of the hereafter; and do not forget your share of the world. And do good as Allah has done good to you. And desire not corruption in the land."** The balanced Islamic view of wealth in one verse.

﴿قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أُوتِيتُهُۥ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٍ عِندِيٓۚ أَوَلَمۡ يَعۡلَمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَدۡ أَهۡلَكَ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ مَنۡ هُوَ أَشَدُّ مِنۡهُ قُوَّةٗ وَأَكۡثَرُ جَمۡعٗاۚ وَلَا يُسۡـَٔلُ عَن ذُنُوبِهِمُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:78)

**"He said: I was only given it because of knowledge I have."**

﴿فَخَرَجَ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ فِي زِينَتِهِۦۖ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُرِيدُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا يَٰلَيۡتَ لَنَا مِثۡلَ مَآ أُوتِيَ قَٰرُونُ إِنَّهُۥ لَذُو حَظٍّ عَظِيمٖ﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ وَيۡلَكُمۡ ثَوَابُ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرࣱ لِّمَنۡ ءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗاۚ وَلَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلصَّـٰبِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:79-80)

He went out in his adornment. Those who wanted this world said: **"Oh, would that we had like what was given to Qarun."** Those given knowledge said: **"Woe to you! The reward of Allah is better for he who believes and does righteousness."**

﴿فَخَسَفۡنَا بِهِۦ وَبِدَارِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فَمَا كَانَ لَهُۥ مِن فِئَةٖ يَنصُرُونَهُۥ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنتَصِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَأَصۡبَحَ ٱلَّذِينَ تَمَنَّوۡاْ مَكَانَهُۥ بِٱلۡأَمۡسِ يَقُولُونَ وَيۡكَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَقۡدِرُۖ لَوۡلَآ أَن مَّنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَا لَخَسَفَ بِنَاۖ وَيۡكَأَنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:81-82)

**"And We caused the earth to swallow him and his home."** By morning, those who had envied him said: **"Oh, how Allah extends provision to whom He wills."**

## Verses 83–88: The Home of the Hereafter
﴿تِلۡكَ ٱلدَّارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ نَجۡعَلُهَا لِلَّذِينَ لَا يُرِيدُونَ عُلُوّٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فَسَادٗاۚ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:83)

**"That home of the hereafter We assign to those who do not desire exaltedness upon the earth or corruption. And the best outcome is for the righteous."**

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِي فَرَضَ عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لَرَآدُّكَ إِلَىٰ مَعَادٖۚ قُل رَّبِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ مَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَمَنۡ هُوَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 28:85)

**"Indeed, He who imposed upon you the Quran will take you back to a place of return."** In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas explained it as a return to Makkah.

﴿وَلَا تَدۡعُ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَۘ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ كُلُّ شَيۡءٍ هَالِكٌ إِلَّا وَجۡهَهُۥۚ لَهُ ٱلۡحُكۡمُ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:88)

**"Everything will be destroyed except His face. His is the judgement, and to Him you will be returned."**

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Qasas**: the stories.
- **'Ala**: exalted himself.
- **Shiya'**: factions.
- **Al-Yamm**: the river.
- **Faragh**: empty.
- **Faqir**: in need.
- **Al-Qawiyy al-Amin**: the strong and trustworthy.
- **Hijaj**: years.
- **Jadhwah**: a burning brand.
- **Al-Kunuz**: treasures.
- **Al-Farihin**: the exultant.
- **Ma'ad**: place of return.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Enemy and grief"**: Pharaoh raised the one who would end him.
2. **Two commands, two prohibitions, two promises**: verse 7.
3. **"In need"** without naming the need: perfect humility in du'a.
4. **"Walking with shyness"**: an image of modesty.
5. **Envy turned to gratitude overnight**: the onlookers of Qarun.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Izhar**: in *an ard'ihi* (verse 7), the noon sakinah before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *man yasha'* (verse 56), the noon merges into the ya'.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *fa ja'athu* (verse 25).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *wabtaghi* (verse 77), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah An-Naml before it tells part of Musa's story briefly, and Al-Qasas gives it in full. Al-Qasas ends with "everything will perish except His face", and Surah Al-'Ankabut after it opens with trials that test faith.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Trust Allah's plan** when the oppressor seems strong.
2. **Obey Allah even when it seems frightening**, as Musa's mother did.
3. **Repent immediately** after a mistake.
4. **Make the du'a of need**: "My Lord, I am in need of whatever good You send me."
5. **Help others** even when you are struggling yourself.
6. **Choose the strong and trustworthy** for any responsibility.
7. **Seek the hereafter** without neglecting your share of this world.
8. **Never attribute wealth to your own knowledge alone.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Qasas
- **Week 1**: verses 1–28.
- **Week 2**: verses 29–60.
- **Week 3**: verses 61–88, with full review.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell Musa's story** as a series: the basket, the palace, the well, the fire.
- **Teach them the du'a of Musa** at Madyan.
- **Discuss Qarun**: what is the right way to use what Allah gives us?

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is it called Surah Al-Qasas?
Because of the word *al-qasas* (the story) in verse 25, and because it contains the fullest story of Musa.

### What is the du'a of Musa in Al-Qasas?
"My Lord, indeed I am, for whatever good You would send down to me, in need" (verse 24).

### Who was Qarun?
A rich man from the people of Musa whose arrogance led to the earth swallowing him.

### What does "the strong and trustworthy" mean?
The two qualities of the best person to hire: competence and honesty.

### Who was verse 56 revealed about?
Abu Talib, the Prophet's uncle, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the "place of return" in verse 85?
Ibn 'Abbas explained it as Makkah, as in Sahih al-Bukhari.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Qasas?
88 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Qasas is the story of how Allah protects the weak and humbles the arrogant: a baby in a basket survives a tyrant, a fugitive becomes a prophet, and a billionaire is swallowed by the earth. When you feel weak, remember Musa's mother; when you feel alone, say his prayer at Madyan; and when you are given wealth, remember Qarun. Everything perishes except His face.
