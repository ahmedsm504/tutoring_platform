Juz 10 begins with ﴿۞وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا غَنِمۡتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَأَنَّ لِلَّهِ خُمُسَهُۥ وَلِلرَّسُولِ﴾, "And know that whatever you take as spoils, one fifth belongs to Allah and the Messenger", so it is called **Juz Wa'lamu**. It runs from **Al-Anfal 8:41 to At-Tawbah 9:92**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 182 to 200**.

Almost all of this juz concerns **the believing community on the field of confrontation**. It closes **Surah Al-Anfal** with the rules of victory, peace and alliance, then opens **Surah At-Tawbah**, the only surah that does not begin with the basmalah. It contains the disavowal of idolaters who broke their treaties, the battle of Hunayn, the Hijrah, and then the Quran's longest passage on **the Tabuk expedition** and the hypocrites who stayed behind.

## The big idea

The juz can be summed up as **sincerity in loyalty to Allah when tested**. In Al-Anfal the community learns how to stand firm, how to make peace and how to form alliances. In At-Tawbah comes the greatest test: marching to Tabuk in intense heat and hardship, which separates the sincere from the hypocrites. That is why At-Tawbah is also called **al-Fadihah**, "the Exposer", for it uncovered the hypocrites' secrets.

The message: **faith is proven when the call to go out comes, not by claims.**

## Part one: the close of Surah Al-Anfal (41-75)

### 1. The fifth and the Day of the Criterion (41-44)
The juz begins by dividing the spoils: one fifth for Allah, the Messenger, relatives, orphans, the poor and the traveller. Allah describes Badr as ﴿يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفُرۡقَانِ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡتَقَى ٱلۡجَمۡعَانِۗ﴾, "the Day of the Criterion, the day the two armies met", and pictures the positions: the Muslims on the near slope, the idolaters on the far slope, and the caravan below them. Allah made each side appear few in the other's eyes so that a matter already decreed would come to pass.

### 2. Six rules of steadfastness (45-47)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا لَقِيتُمۡ فِئَةٗ فَٱثۡبُتُواْ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾, "O you who believe, when you meet a force, stand firm and remember Allah much, so that you may succeed", then ﴿وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَا تَنَٰزَعُواْ فَتَفۡشَلُواْ وَتَذۡهَبَ رِيحُكُمۡۖ وَٱصۡبِرُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾, "Obey Allah and His Messenger, and do not dispute, lest you lose courage and your strength departs; and be patient, for Allah is with the patient", then ﴿وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ خَرَجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِم بَطَرٗا وَرِئَآءَ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾, "and do not be like those who left their homes boastfully and to be seen by people."

The rules are: **standing firm**, **much remembrance**, **obedience**, **avoiding disputes**, **patience**, and **sincerity without arrogance or showing off**. They apply to every collective effort, not only to battle. Note "your strength departs" (literally "your wind goes"): disputes destroy strength before the enemy even arrives.

### 3. Shaytan's deception and the law of change (48-54)
﴿وَإِذۡ زَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ وَقَالَ لَا غَالِبَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَإِنِّي جَارࣱ لَّكُمۡۖ﴾, "And when Shaytan made their deeds seem pleasing to them and said: no one can overcome you today, and I am your protector." When the two armies saw each other, he turned on his heels. That is Shaytan: he tempts, then abandons. Then the scene of angels striking the disbelievers' faces and backs at death.

Then one of the great laws of history: ﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَمۡ يَكُ مُغَيِّرٗا نِّعۡمَةً أَنۡعَمَهَا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡ﴾, "That is because Allah would never change a favour He had bestowed on a people until they change what is within themselves." Blessings do not disappear unless people change themselves. It is close in meaning to the verse in Ar-Ra'd. Read [Allah Does Not Change a People's Condition](/blog/la-yughayyiru-ma-biqawm-meaning-english/#Allah-Does-Not-Change-a-People-Until-They-Change-Themselves-).

### 4. Treaties, preparation and peace (55-64)
- Those who break their treaty every time, and the command to openly end a treaty when betrayal is feared: no treachery, even with enemies.
- ﴿وَأَعِدُّواْ لَهُم مَّا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُم مِّن قُوَّةٖ وَمِن رِّبَاطِ ٱلۡخَيۡلِ تُرۡهِبُونَ بِهِۦ عَدُوَّ ٱللَّهِ وَعَدُوَّكُمۡ﴾, "Prepare against them whatever strength you can, including steeds of war, to deter the enemy of Allah and your enemy." Preparation is for deterrence, not aggression.
- ﴿۞وَإِن جَنَحُواْ لِلسَّلۡمِ فَٱجۡنَحۡ لَهَا وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "And if they incline to peace, then incline to it and rely upon Allah." Strength and peace go together. Read [If They Incline to Peace: 8:61](/blog/rabbana-zalamna-anfusana-meaning-english/#If-They-Incline-to-Peace-Then-Incline-to-It-Quran-8-61-Expla).
- ﴿وَأَلَّفَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِهِمۡۚ لَوۡ أَنفَقۡتَ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا مَّآ أَلَّفۡتَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِهِمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَلَّفَ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ﴾, "And He brought their hearts together. Had you spent all that is on earth, you could not have united their hearts, but Allah united them." Unity of hearts is a divine gift that money cannot buy.

### 5. Patience, numbers and captives (65-71)
﴿إِن يَكُن مِّنكُمۡ عِشۡرُونَ صَٰبِرُونَ يَغۡلِبُواْ مِاْئَتَيۡنِۚ﴾, "If there are twenty patient ones among you, they will overcome two hundred", then Allah lightened it: ﴿ٱلۡـَٰٔنَ خَفَّفَ ٱللَّهُ عَنكُمۡ وَعَلِمَ أَنَّ فِيكُمۡ ضَعۡفٗاۚ﴾, "Now Allah has lightened your burden, knowing there is weakness in you", making one equal to two. Then a reproach about the captives of Badr: ﴿مَا كَانَ لِنَبِيٍّ أَن يَكُونَ لَهُۥٓ أَسۡرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ يُثۡخِنَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ﴾, "It is not for a prophet to take captives until he has subdued the land." In Sahih Muslim, 'Umar narrates how the Prophet ﷺ consulted Abu Bakr and 'Umar about the captives, accepted Abu Bakr's view of ransom, and then these verses came. The spoils were then made lawful, and the captives were promised: if Allah knows any good in your hearts, He will give you better than what was taken from you.

### 6. Alliance among the believers (72-75)
The surah ends by organising relationships: the Muhajirun and Ansar are allies of one another, and those who did not migrate have no claim of alliance until they migrate. Then: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَهَاجَرُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاوَواْ وَّنَصَرُوٓاْ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ حَقّٗاۚ﴾, "Those who believed, migrated and strove in the cause of Allah, and those who gave shelter and helped: they are the true believers." The last verse: ﴿وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلَىٰ بِبَعۡضٖ فِي كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "And blood relatives are more entitled to one another in the decree of Allah", returning inheritance to kinship.

**Notice the start and end of Al-Anfal**: it began with "they are the true believers" describing those whose hearts tremble, and it ends with the same phrase describing the Muhajirun and Ansar. True faith is a humble heart, then action that migrates and supports.

## Part two: the opening of Surah At-Tawbah (1-92)

At-Tawbah is a Madani surah of 129 verses and among the last revealed. Its other names include **Bara'ah** (Disavowal) and **al-Fadihah** (the Exposer). It is the only surah in the Mushaf that does not begin with the basmalah. Scholars differed on the reason; among the best-known explanations is that it came with a declaration of disavowal and ending treaties, and that the Companions did not write the basmalah between Al-Anfal and At-Tawbah because their subjects were so close. Most of it was revealed around the Tabuk expedition in the ninth year. For the whole surah see [Surah At-Tawbah Meaning in English](/blog/la-tahzan-inna-allaha-maana-meaning-english/#Surah-At-Tawbah-Meaning-Sincerity-Tested-and-the-Door-of-Rep).

### 1. Disavowal of the treaty-breakers (1-16)
﴿بَرَآءَةࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦٓ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ عَٰهَدتُّم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾, "A disavowal from Allah and His Messenger to the idolaters with whom you made treaties", with a grace period of four months and an exception for those who kept their word: ﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ عَٰهَدتُّم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَنقُصُوكُمۡ شَيۡـٔٗا وَلَمۡ يُظَٰهِرُواْ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَحَدٗا فَأَتِمُّوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡ عَهۡدَهُمۡ إِلَىٰ مُدَّتِهِمۡۚ﴾, "except those idolaters with whom you made a treaty who have not failed you in anything nor supported anyone against you; fulfil their treaty until its term." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, during the Hajj of the ninth year Abu Bakr sent people to announce that no idolater would perform Hajj after that year and no one would circle the House naked.

In the middle of these verses is a verse of great mercy: ﴿وَإِنۡ أَحَدࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ ٱسۡتَجَارَكَ فَأَجِرۡهُ حَتَّىٰ يَسۡمَعَ كَلَٰمَ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ أَبۡلِغۡهُ مَأۡمَنَهُۥۚ﴾, "If any of the idolaters seeks your protection, grant it so he may hear the words of Allah, then escort him to his place of safety." Even in the harshest context of war, the door to hearing and safety stays open.

**Lesson**: the disavowal was from those who broke treaties and supported attacks on the Muslims; keeping faith with those who kept faith remains a fixed rule.

### 2. Maintaining mosques, and the true measure (17-24)
﴿إِنَّمَا يَعۡمُرُ مَسَٰجِدَ ٱللَّهِ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَأَقَامَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَى ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَلَمۡ يَخۡشَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۖ﴾, "The mosques of Allah are maintained only by those who believe in Allah and the Last Day, establish prayer, give zakah and fear none but Allah." Giving water to pilgrims and maintaining the Sacred Mosque are not equal to faith and striving. Then the decisive measure: ﴿قُلۡ إِن كَانَ ءَابَآؤُكُمۡ وَأَبۡنَآؤُكُمۡ وَإِخۡوَٰنُكُمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجُكُمۡ وَعَشِيرَتُكُمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلٌ ٱقۡتَرَفۡتُمُوهَا وَتِجَٰرَةࣱ تَخۡشَوۡنَ كَسَادَهَا وَمَسَٰكِنُ تَرۡضَوۡنَهَآ أَحَبَّ إِلَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَجِهَادٖ فِي سَبِيلِهِۦ فَتَرَبَّصُواْ﴾, "Say: if your fathers, sons, brothers, spouses, relatives, wealth you have acquired, trade you fear will decline, and homes you are pleased with are more beloved to you than Allah, His Messenger and striving in His cause, then wait..."

Eight loved things on one side, and Allah, His Messenger and striving on the other. The verse does not forbid loving family and wealth; it forbids putting them before the love of Allah.

### 3. The lesson of Hunayn (25-27)
﴿لَقَدۡ نَصَرَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ فِي مَوَاطِنَ كَثِيرَةٖ وَيَوۡمَ حُنَيۡنٍ إِذۡ أَعۡجَبَتۡكُمۡ كَثۡرَتُكُمۡ فَلَمۡ تُغۡنِ عَنكُمۡ شَيۡـٔٗا﴾, "Allah has given you victory in many places, and on the day of Hunayn, when your great numbers pleased you but did not help you at all." At Badr they were few and were helped; at Hunayn their numbers impressed them and they were routed at first, until Allah sent down His tranquillity on His Messenger and the believers.

**Lesson**: being impressed with numbers and equipment leads to defeat; victory is from Allah alone.

### 4. The People of the Book and the light of Allah (28-35)
Rulings on idolaters entering the Sacred Mosque, then fighting those of the People of the Book who do not believe until they pay the jizyah. Then their deviations: some said 'Uzayr is the son of Allah, the Christians said the Messiah is the son of Allah, and ﴿ٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ أَحۡبَارَهُمۡ وَرُهۡبَٰنَهُمۡ أَرۡبَابٗا مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ﴾, "they took their scholars and monks as lords besides Allah", meaning they obeyed them in permitting the forbidden and forbidding the permitted.

Then the promise: ﴿يُرِيدُونَ أَن يُطۡفِـُٔواْ نُورَ ٱللَّهِ بِأَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَيَأۡبَى ٱللَّهُ إِلَّآ أَن يُتِمَّ نُورَهُۥ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾, "They want to extinguish Allah's light with their mouths, but Allah refuses except to complete His light, even if the disbelievers dislike it", and ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَرۡسَلَ رَسُولَهُۥ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَدِينِ ٱلۡحَقِّ لِيُظۡهِرَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلدِّينِ كُلِّهِۦ﴾, "It is He who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth to make it prevail over all religion."

Hizb 20 then begins with the warning against hoarding: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَكۡنِزُونَ ٱلذَّهَبَ وَٱلۡفِضَّةَ وَلَا يُنفِقُونَهَا فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَبَشِّرۡهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمࣲ﴾, "Those who hoard gold and silver and do not spend it in the way of Allah, give them news of a painful punishment."

### 5. The sacred months and *nasi'* (36-37)
﴿إِنَّ عِدَّةَ ٱلشُّهُورِ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱثۡنَا عَشَرَ شَهۡرٗا فِي كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِ يَوۡمَ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِنۡهَآ أَرۡبَعَةٌ حُرُمࣱۚ﴾, "The number of months with Allah is twelve in Allah's decree on the day He created the heavens and earth; four of them are sacred." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim they are Dhul-Qa'dah, Dhul-Hijjah, Muharram and Rajab. Then: ﴿فَلَا تَظۡلِمُواْ فِيهِنَّ أَنفُسَكُمۡۚ﴾, "so do not wrong yourselves during them." Then *nasi'* is cancelled: the pre-Islamic practice of postponing a sacred month's sanctity to another month.

### 6. The call to Tabuk and the cave (38-41)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَا لَكُمۡ إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمُ ٱنفِرُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ٱثَّاقَلۡتُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ أَرَضِيتُم بِٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا مِنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ﴾, "O you who believe, what is wrong with you that when you are told to go out in the cause of Allah, you cling heavily to the earth? Are you satisfied with this world instead of the Hereafter?" Allah reminds them that He helped His Messenger when only one man was with him: ﴿إِلَّا تَنصُرُوهُ فَقَدۡ نَصَرَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِذۡ أَخۡرَجَهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ثَانِيَ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ إِذۡ هُمَا فِي ٱلۡغَارِ إِذۡ يَقُولُ لِصَٰحِبِهِۦ لَا تَحۡزَنۡ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَنَاۖ﴾, "If you do not help him, Allah already helped him when the disbelievers drove him out, the second of two, when they were in the cave and he said to his companion: do not grieve, Allah is with us." His companion was Abu Bakr as-Siddiq; in Al-Bukhari and Muslim Abu Bakr said: if one of them looked under his feet he would see us, and the Prophet ﷺ said: "What do you think, Abu Bakr, of two whose third is Allah?" Read [Do Not Grieve, Allah Is with Us: 9:40](/blog/la-tahzan-inna-allaha-maana-meaning-english/#Do-Not-Grieve-Indeed-Allah-Is-With-Us-Quran-9-40-Explained).

Then the general command: ﴿ٱنفِرُواْ خِفَافٗا وَثِقَالٗا وَجَٰهِدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنفُسِكُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "Go out, light or heavy, and strive with your wealth and yourselves in the cause of Allah."

### 7. The hypocrites' excuses (42-59)
The surah exposes the hypocrites one by one:
- Had it been a quick gain and an easy journey, they would have followed you.
- A gentle reproach to the Prophet ﷺ: ﴿عَفَا ٱللَّهُ عَنكَ لِمَ أَذِنتَ لَهُمۡ﴾, "May Allah pardon you; why did you give them permission?" Notice how the reproach begins with pardon.
- Had they wanted to go out, they would have prepared for it.
- ﴿وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَقُولُ ٱئۡذَن لِّي وَلَا تَفۡتِنِّيٓۚ أَلَا فِي ٱلۡفِتۡنَةِ سَقَطُواْۗ﴾, "Among them is one who says: give me leave and do not put me to trial. Surely they have already fallen into trial." He fled an imagined trial and fell into a real one.
- If good reaches you it upsets them; the reply: ﴿قُل لَّن يُصِيبَنَآ إِلَّا مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَنَا هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰنَاۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾, "Say: nothing will befall us except what Allah has decreed for us; He is our protector, and upon Allah let the believers rely." Read [Nothing Befalls Us Except What Allah Decreed](/blog/la-tahzan-inna-allaha-maana-meaning-english/#Nothing-Will-Befall-Us-Except-What-Allah-Has-Decreed-Quran-9).
- Their spending is not accepted, since they come to prayer only lazily.
- ﴿فَلَا تُعۡجِبۡكَ أَمۡوَٰلُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُهُمۡۚ﴾, "So let not their wealth or children impress you."
- They criticise how charity is distributed.

### 8. The recipients of zakah (60)
﴿۞إِنَّمَا ٱلصَّدَقَٰتُ لِلۡفُقَرَآءِ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ عَلَيۡهَا وَٱلۡمُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَفِي ٱلرِّقَابِ وَٱلۡغَٰرِمِينَ وَفِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱبۡنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِۖ فَرِيضَةࣰ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ﴾, "Zakah is only for the poor, the needy, those who administer it, those whose hearts are to be reconciled, freeing captives, those in debt, the cause of Allah, and the traveller: an obligation from Allah." Eight categories set by Allah Himself, in reply to the hypocrites' criticism.

### 9. The hypocrites' mockery, and the two groups compared (61-72)
- They hurt the Prophet ﷺ and say, "He is all ears."
- They swear to please the believers, though Allah and His Messenger are more worthy to be pleased.
- ﴿وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُمۡ لَيَقُولُنَّ إِنَّمَا كُنَّا نَخُوضُ وَنَلۡعَبُۚ قُلۡ أَبِٱللَّهِ وَءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَرَسُولِهِۦ كُنتُمۡ تَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ﴾, "If you ask them, they will say: we were only chatting and joking. Say: was it Allah, His verses and His Messenger that you were mocking?", then ﴿لَا تَعۡتَذِرُواْ قَدۡ كَفَرۡتُم بَعۡدَ إِيمَٰنِكُمۡۚ﴾, "Make no excuse; you have disbelieved after your belief." Mocking the religion is no joke.
- Then a precise comparison:

| Hypocrites (67) | Believers (71) |
|---|---|
| Of one another | Allies of one another |
| Enjoin wrong | Enjoin right |
| Forbid right | Forbid wrong |
| Close their hands | Give zakah |
| Forgot Allah, so He forgot them | Obey Allah and His Messenger; Allah will have mercy on them |

Then the promise of gardens and pleasant homes for the believers, ﴿وَرِضۡوَٰنࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ أَكۡبَرُۚ﴾, "and the pleasure of Allah is greater."

### 10. Breaking a pledge to Allah, and seeking forgiveness for them (73-80)
- Some pledged to Allah: if He gives us of His bounty we will give charity. When He did, they were stingy, ﴿فَأَعۡقَبَهُمۡ نِفَاقٗا فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يَلۡقَوۡنَهُۥ﴾, "so He put hypocrisy in their hearts until the Day they meet Him."
- They ridiculed believers who gave voluntarily, mocking those who could give only their effort.
- ﴿ٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ أَوۡ لَا تَسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ إِن تَسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ سَبۡعِينَ مَرَّةٗ فَلَن يَغۡفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمۡۚ﴾, "Ask forgiveness for them or do not; even if you ask seventy times, Allah will not forgive them."

### 11. Those who stayed behind, and those excused (81-92)
- ﴿فَرِحَ ٱلۡمُخَلَّفُونَ بِمَقۡعَدِهِمۡ خِلَٰفَ رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ﴾, "Those who stayed behind rejoiced in remaining behind the Messenger of Allah", saying "Do not go out in the heat." ﴿قُلۡ نَارُ جَهَنَّمَ أَشَدُّ حَرّٗاۚ لَّوۡ كَانُواْ يَفۡقَهُونَ﴾, "Say: the fire of Hell is more intense in heat, if only they understood."
- A ban on praying the funeral prayer over any of them; in Al-Bukhari and Muslim it came after the Prophet ﷺ prayed over 'Abdullah ibn Ubayy.
- In contrast, the Messenger and those who believed with him strove with their wealth and lives.
- Then **those with valid excuses**: ﴿لَّيۡسَ عَلَى ٱلضُّعَفَآءِ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلۡمَرۡضَىٰ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَجِدُونَ مَا يُنفِقُونَ حَرَجٌ إِذَا نَصَحُواْ لِلَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۚ﴾, "There is no blame on the weak, the sick, or those who find nothing to spend, if they are sincere to Allah and His Messenger."
- Then the juz's final verse, one of the most tender in the Quran: ﴿وَلَا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا مَآ أَتَوۡكَ لِتَحۡمِلَهُمۡ قُلۡتَ لَآ أَجِدُ مَآ أَحۡمِلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَلَّواْ وَّأَعۡيُنُهُمۡ تَفِيضُ مِنَ ٱلدَّمۡعِ حَزَنًا أَلَّا يَجِدُواْ مَا يُنفِقُونَ﴾, "Nor on those who, when they came to you to be given mounts and you said, 'I find nothing to carry you', turned away with their eyes overflowing with tears, grieving that they had nothing to spend."

**Reflect on the ending**: the juz closes with people who wept because they could not go, in contrast to people who rejoiced at staying. The difference is not in staying behind itself, but in the heart: one weeps at being denied good, the other rejoices at escaping it. In Sahih Al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ said at Tabuk: "There are people in Madinah who were with you on every journey you made and every valley you crossed; only an excuse held them back."

## Tajweed points in Juz 10

### Between Al-Anfal and At-Tawbah
There is no basmalah at the start of At-Tawbah. When joining the end of Al-Anfal to the start of At-Tawbah, Hafs has three options: joining, a brief pause (*sakt*), or a full stop, all without the basmalah.

### ﴿عَٰهَدتُّم﴾
The sakin dal merges completely into the ta.

### ﴿وَلَا تَفۡتِنِّيٓۚ﴾
The doubled noon carries a two-count ghunnah, followed by a madd ya.

### Iqlab and ikhfa'
Iqlab in phrases like ﴿كَثِيرَةَۢ بِإِذۡنِ﴾ and ikhfa' in ﴿أَنفُسِهِمۡ﴾ recur throughout the juz; watch them in revision.

### ﴿مِاْئَتَيۡنِۚ﴾
Written with an extra alif that is not pronounced: read *mi'atayn*. For a structured study path see [A Roadmap for Learning Tajweed](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## How to memorize Juz 10 with understanding

1. **Learn the six rules of steadfastness** in Al-Anfal 45-47 as a list; they are the key to the passage.
2. **The opening of At-Tawbah is about treaties**: distinguish between those who broke them and those who kept them.
3. **Write out the eight loves** of verse 24 in order.
4. **Number the hypocrites' excuses**: a quick gain, "may Allah pardon you", "give me leave", "if good reaches you", their spending, criticising charity.
5. **Memorize the comparison of hypocrites and believers** (67 and 71) from a written table.
6. **End the juz with verse 92** and link it to verse 81: the rejoicing of those who stayed and the tears of those excused.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 10

- **Stand firm and remember Allah** in every confrontation.
- **Do not quarrel with your brothers**, or your strength will go.
- **Blessings remain as long as people stay upright within.**
- **Prepare strength, and accept peace** when the other side inclines to it.
- **Never betray**, even someone whose betrayal you fear.
- **Put love of Allah and His Messenger** above every other love.
- **Do not be impressed with your numbers.**
- **Beware hoarding without spending.**
- **Do not cling to the earth** when called to good.
- **Trust that Allah is with you** in the hardest moments.
- **Never mock anything of the religion.**
- **If you are kept from a good deed, grieve over it**; that is a sign of sincerity.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 10 called "Wa'lamu"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا غَنِمۡتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ﴾.

### Why does At-Tawbah not begin with the basmalah?
Scholars differed on the reason. Among the best-known views are that it came with a declaration of disavowal and the ending of treaties, and that the Companions did not write the basmalah between it and Al-Anfal because of their closeness in subject. What is established is that the Mushaf was written without a basmalah at its start.

### Where does Juz 10 end?
At At-Tawbah 9:92. Juz 11 begins with ﴿۞إِنَّمَا ٱلسَّبِيلُ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَـٔۡذِنُونَكَ وَهُمۡ أَغۡنِيَآءُۚ﴾, "The blame is only on those who ask your permission while they are rich."

### Who are the eight recipients of zakah?
The poor, the needy, those who administer it, those whose hearts are to be reconciled, freeing captives, those in debt, the cause of Allah, and the traveller (9:60).

### Who was with the Prophet ﷺ in the cave?
Abu Bakr as-Siddiq, as reported in Al-Bukhari and Muslim.

## Conclusion

Juz 10 is a test of sincerity. In Al-Anfal the believers learn the rules of steadfastness and victory, discover that united hearts are Allah's gift, and see that strength and peace belong together. In At-Tawbah the great call to march reveals who loves Allah above all, who makes excuses and stays behind, and who weeps because he has nothing to go out with.
