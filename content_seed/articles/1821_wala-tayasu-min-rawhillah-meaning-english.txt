﴿يَٰبَنِيَّ ٱذۡهَبُواْ فَتَحَسَّسُواْ مِن يُوسُفَ وَأَخِيهِ وَلَا تَاْيۡـَٔسُواْ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يَاْيۡـَٔسُ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ [Yusuf 12:87]

## The context
- Yaʿqub had lost Yusuf many years earlier, then his son Binyamin was held in Egypt. His eyes had turned white from grief. Yet he told his sons: **go and search**.

## Hope with action
- *Go and search*: hope is not sitting and waiting. It is **effort with trust**.

## What is *rawh*?
- *Rawh* means relief, ease and the breath of mercy that comes after distress.

## Why is despair so serious?
- Despair means thinking Allah cannot or will not help, which is a wrong belief about His power and mercy. That is why the verse links it with disbelief.

## The result
- Shortly after, the brothers returned, Yusuf revealed himself, the shirt restored Yaʿqub's sight, and the family was reunited.

## Lessons
- **Keep hope alive** after years of waiting.
- **Combine duʿa with effort.**
