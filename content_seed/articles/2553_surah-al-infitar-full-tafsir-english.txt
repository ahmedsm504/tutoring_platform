Surah Al-Infitar is short, but it contains one of the most piercing questions in the Quran: "O man, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Most Generous?" It begins with the universe falling apart on the Last Day: the sky splitting open, the stars scattering, the seas bursting and the graves being turned over. Then it turns to the human being and asks him directly why he disobeys the Lord who created him, shaped him and proportioned him. It reminds him that noble angels are writing down everything he does, and it ends with a Day on which no soul will be able to do anything for another.

This guide explains Surah Al-Infitar verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Infitar
- **Number of verses**: 19.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 82, in the thirtieth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Infitar means "the splitting apart", from the first verse "When the sky splits apart".
- **Main themes**: the end of the universe, human ingratitude, the recording angels and the final judgement.

## A Surah That Lets You See the Day of Judgement
At-Tirmidhi reported from Ibn 'Umar that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever would like to look at the Day of Resurrection as if he were seeing it with his own eyes, let him read: *When the sun is wrapped up* (At-Takwir), *When the sky splits apart* (Al-Infitar), and *When the sky is torn open* (Al-Inshiqaq)." At-Tirmidhi graded it *hasan*, and al-Albani graded it authentic. These three surahs paint the Last Day so vividly that reading them with reflection is like watching it.

## Verses 1–5: When the Universe Falls Apart
﴿إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنفَطَرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡكَوَاكِبُ ٱنتَثَرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡقُبُورُ بُعۡثِرَتۡ﴾ ﴿عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسࣱ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ وَأَخَّرَتۡ﴾ (Quran 82:1-5)

**Four scenes**:
1. **The sky splits apart**: the sky that seems so solid and perfectly built cracks open.
2. **The stars scatter**: the ordered stars fall and are dispersed.
3. **The seas burst forth**: they overflow and merge into one another, salt and fresh water mixing.
4. **The graves are overturned**: their earth is turned over and the dead come out.

**Notice the order**: from the sky, to the stars, to the seas, to the graves. The camera moves from the highest point down to the human being in his grave. The whole universe is the background; the real subject is you.

**"Every soul will know what it has put forward and what it has left behind"**: what you did in your life, and what you left behind after your death, good or bad. In Sahih Muslim, Jarir ibn 'Abdullah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever starts a good practice in Islam will have its reward and the reward of those who act on it after him, without anything being reduced from their rewards. And whoever starts an evil practice in Islam will bear its burden and the burden of those who act on it after him, without anything being reduced from their burdens."

## Verses 6–8: What Has Deceived You?
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّىٰكَ فَعَدَلَكَ﴾ ﴿فِيٓ أَيِّ صُورَةٖ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ﴾ (Quran 82:6-8)

**"O man"**: a call addressed to every human being, including you.

**"What has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Most Generous?"** What made you so bold as to disobey Him? **Notice the choice of the name "the Most Generous"** (*al-Karim*). The verse does not say "your Lord, the Severe in punishment". It reminds you of His generosity, which makes the reproach heavier: He has been nothing but generous to you, so how could you answer His generosity with disobedience? His generosity should make you shy before Him, not bold against Him.

**The favours of creation**:
1. **"Who created you"**: brought you into existence from nothing.
2. **"Then proportioned you"**: made your limbs complete and sound.
3. **"Then balanced you"**: gave you an upright, well-proportioned form.
4. **"In whatever form He willed, He assembled you"**: your height, your colour, your features, all by His choice.

**What deceives people?** Shaytan, long hopes, ignorance of Allah, mistaking His patience for neglect, and being absorbed in this world. The cure is to know Allah, to remember death and to take account of yourself.

## Verses 9–12: The Noble Scribes
﴿كَلَّا بَلۡ تُكَذِّبُونَ بِٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَحَٰفِظِينَ﴾ ﴿كِرَامٗا كَٰتِبِينَ﴾ ﴿يَعۡلَمُونَ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 82:9-12)

**"No! But you deny the Recompense"**: the real reason behind this boldness is that, deep down, people do not take the Day of Judgement seriously.

**"And indeed, over you are guardians, noble and recording, who know whatever you do."** Their qualities:
- **Guardians** (*hafizin*): they preserve and count your deeds.
- **Noble** (*kiram*): honoured by Allah and honourable in character.
- **Recording** (*katibin*): they write everything down.
- **They know whatever you do**: nothing escapes them.

**The effect of believing this**: whoever remembers that noble angels are writing will feel shy to make them write something shameful. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Angels take turns among you by night and by day, and they meet at the Fajr and 'Asr prayers. Then those who spent the night with you ascend, and their Lord asks them, though He knows best: How did you leave My servants? They say: We left them while they were praying, and we came to them while they were praying."

**And from Allah's mercy in the recording**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas reported that the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah has written the good and bad deeds: whoever intends a good deed and does not do it, Allah records it as one complete good deed; if he intends it and does it, Allah records it as ten good deeds up to seven hundred times or more; and whoever intends a bad deed and does not do it, Allah records it as one complete good deed; and if he does it, Allah records it as one bad deed.

## Verses 13–16: The Righteous and the Wicked
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ ٱلۡفُجَّارَ لَفِي جَحِيمٖ﴾ ﴿يَصۡلَوۡنَهَا يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿وَمَا هُمۡ عَنۡهَا بِغَآئِبِينَ﴾ (Quran 82:13-16)

**Two groups**:
- **The righteous** (*al-abrar*): the people of obedience and truthfulness are in bliss.
- **The wicked** (*al-fujjar*): the people of disbelief and sin are in Hellfire, **"burning in it on the Day of Recompense"**, and **"they will never be absent from it"**.

Two verses for each group, and the final destination of every human being is settled.

## Verses 17–19: The Day of Recompense
﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ مَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ لَا تَمۡلِكُ نَفۡسࣱ لِّنَفۡسٖ شَيۡـٔٗاۖ وَٱلۡأَمۡرُ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلَّهِ﴾ (Quran 82:17-19)

**"And what will make you know what the Day of Recompense is? Then what will make you know what the Day of Recompense is?"** The question is repeated to show that the human mind cannot grasp how great that Day is.

**"The Day when no soul will have power to do anything for another soul"**: no father for his son, no son for his father. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that when the verse "And warn your nearest relatives" was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ called out: "O people of Quraysh, buy yourselves (from Allah); I cannot avail you anything against Allah... O Fatimah, daughter of Muhammad, ask me for whatever you want of my wealth; I cannot avail you anything against Allah."

**"And the command that Day is entirely Allah's"**: the command is always His, but on that Day nobody will even pretend to have any power.

## Word Meanings
- **Infatarat**: split apart, cracked open.
- **Intatharat**: scattered, fell apart.
- **Fujjirat**: burst forth, overflowed.
- **Bu'thirat**: overturned, their contents brought out.
- **Gharraka**: deceived you, made you bold.
- **Al-Karim**: the Most Generous.
- **'Adalaka**: balanced you, gave you proportion.
- **Hafizin**: guardians, recorders.
- **Al-Abrar**: the righteous.
- **Al-Fujjar**: the wicked.
- **Yawm ad-Din**: the Day of Recompense.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The repeated "when"**: four conditions before the answer, building suspense.
2. **"What has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Most Generous?"**: a rhetorical question carrying a gentle but piercing reproach.
3. **"Created you, proportioned you, balanced you"**: favours listed one after another to deepen the reproach.
4. **"Noble and recording"**: praise for the angels that makes us feel shy before them.
5. **The righteous and the wicked, bliss and Hellfire**: a clear contrast.
6. **The repeated question about the Day of Recompense**: to magnify its terror.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'u* (verse 1), the hamzah follows the long vowel in the same word.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *adraka* (verse 17), the dal sakinah bounces lightly, and in *al-abrar* (verse 13), the ba' sakinah.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *la-hafizina kiraman* (verses 10–11), the tanween is hidden before the kaf.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna al-abrara* (verse 13), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah At-Takwir before it describes twelve scenes of the Last Day and ends its first part with "every soul will know what it has brought". Al-Infitar continues with "every soul will know what it has put forward and left behind". Surah Al-Mutaffifin after it begins with "Woe to those who give less", a specific sin that the recording angels write down, so the warning becomes concrete.

## The Three Surahs of the Day of Judgement
The Prophet ﷺ grouped At-Takwir, Al-Infitar and Al-Inshiqaq together. Each has its own flavour:
- **At-Takwir** lists twelve scenes, from the sun being wrapped up to Paradise being brought near.
- **Al-Infitar** shortens the scenes to four and then turns to reproach man: "What has deceived you?"
- **Al-Inshiqaq** describes the sky obeying its Lord, then tells man: "You are labouring towards your Lord, and you will meet Him."

All three move from the universe to the individual. The point is not only to describe the events but to wake you up so that you prepare.

## Shyness Before Allah
Al-Infitar builds a beautiful quality in the heart: **shyness before Allah** (*haya'*). It reminds you of His generosity: He created you, shaped you and balanced you. Then it reminds you that noble witnesses are writing. Shyness grows from two things together: seeing Allah's favours and seeing your own shortcomings. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Shyness is part of faith." When you are about to do something wrong, remember that Allah sees you, the angels are writing, and the very ground you stand on will testify.

## What You Leave Behind
"What it has put forward and what it has left behind" opens a door for planning. In Sahih Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "When a person dies, his deeds come to an end except for three: ongoing charity, knowledge that is benefited from, or a righteous child who prays for him." Ask yourself: what will I leave behind? A copy of the Quran in a mosque, a well, a child taught to recite, a good word that keeps spreading. Start one small project today.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Do not let Allah's generosity make you careless**: let it make you grateful.
2. **Thank Allah for your body**: and use it in His obedience.
3. **Guard your tongue**: the angels are writing.
4. **Start a good practice**: at home, at work or in your community.
5. **Be among the righteous**: truthful and obedient.
6. **Do not rely on family ties**: nobody can help anybody on that Day without Allah's permission.
7. **Read the three surahs of the Last Day**: At-Takwir, Al-Infitar and Al-Inshiqaq.

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Infitar
- **Day 1**: verses 1–8.
- **Day 2**: verses 9–16.
- **Day 3**: verses 17–19, then revise the whole surah.

## Teaching Children
- **Talk about the noble scribes** in a gentle way that encourages shyness, not fear.
- **Let them reflect on their own bodies**: "He created you, proportioned you and balanced you."
- **Teach them that Allah is the Most Generous**, and that the generous are thanked, not disobeyed.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "When the sky splits apart" mean?
That on the Day of Resurrection the sky will crack open and break apart.

### What does "every soul will know what it has put forward and left behind" mean?
Every person will know what he did in his life and the good or bad effects he left behind after death.

### Why does the verse say "your Lord, the Most Generous" and not another name?
Because mentioning His generosity makes the reproach stronger: how can you disobey the One who has been so generous to you?

### Who are the "noble recording" angels?
The angels assigned to write down the deeds of every human being.

### What is the virtue of Surah Al-Infitar?
The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever wants to see the Day of Resurrection as if with his own eyes should read At-Takwir, Al-Infitar and Al-Inshiqaq, as reported by at-Tirmidhi, who graded it hasan.

### Do the angels record an intention to do good?
Yes. Whoever intends a good deed and does not do it has it recorded as a complete good deed, as in the hadith of Ibn 'Abbas in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Infitar?
19 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Infitar asks you a question you cannot escape: what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Most Generous? He created you, shaped you and balanced you, and He placed noble angels with you to record everything. On the Day the sky splits apart, nobody will be able to help you. So feel shy before your Generous Lord, be among the righteous, and leave behind something you will be glad to see.
