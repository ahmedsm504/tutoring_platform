**Surah Luqman** (34 ayat) contains a short parenting curriculum: the advice of a wise father to his son.

## The first advice
﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ لُقۡمَٰنُ لِٱبۡنِهِۦ وَهُوَ يَعِظُهُۥ يَٰبُنَيَّ لَا تُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّ ٱلشِّرۡكَ لَظُلۡمٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾
- **"Yā bunayya"**: *my dear son*, a tender way to begin.

## Luqman's advice
| Advice | Area |
|---|---|
| Don't associate partners with Allah | Belief |
| Allah sees even a mustard seed in a rock | Allah's knowledge |
| Establish prayer | Worship |
| Enjoin good, forbid evil | Society |
| Be patient over what befalls you | Character |
| Don't turn your cheek in arrogance | Humility |
| Be moderate in walking, lower your voice | Manners |
﴿يَٰبُنَيَّ أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَٱنۡهَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَصَابَكَۖ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾

## Parents
- In the middle of Luqman's advice, Allah commands **kindness to parents**, mentioning the mother's hardship, while making clear that parents are not obeyed in shirk.

## The five keys of the unseen
- The knowledge of the Hour, the rain, what is in the wombs, what a soul will earn tomorrow, and where it will die.

## Lessons
- **Parenting with love**: start with "my dear son".
