The last surah of the Quran is a shield against an enemy you cannot see: **the whisperer**.

## Three descriptions of Allah
﴿قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾ ﴿مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾ ﴿إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾
| Description | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Rabb (Lord) | He creates and takes care of you |
| Malik (King) | He rules over everything |
| Ilah (God) | He alone deserves worship |
- **Three descriptions** for one evil, showing how serious the whispers are.

## The whisperer
﴿مِن شَرِّ ٱلۡوَسۡوَاسِ ٱلۡخَنَّاسِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي يُوَسۡوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾ ﴿مِنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ﴾
- **Al-Waswās**: the one who whispers again and again.
- **Al-Khannās**: the one who withdraws; scholars explained that he retreats when Allah is remembered and returns with heedlessness.
- **From jinn and men**: bad suggestions can come from both.

## Resisting whispers
| Step | How |
|---|---|
| Remember Allah | Dhikr makes the whisperer withdraw |
| Seek refuge | Recite Al-Falaq and An-Nas |
| Don't engage | Ignore and move on |
| Keep good company | Avoid people who push you to wrong |

## When to recite it
- After every prayer, before sleep, and morning and evening, together with Al-Ikhlas and Al-Falaq.
