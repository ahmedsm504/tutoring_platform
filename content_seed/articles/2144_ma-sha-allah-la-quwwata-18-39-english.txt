﴿وَلَوۡلَآ إِذۡ دَخَلۡتَ جَنَّتَكَ قُلۡتَ مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ إِن تَرَنِ أَنَا۠ أَقَلَّ مِنكَ مَالٗا وَوَلَدٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:39]

## The story
- A man was given two gardens and became proud, saying: *I do not think this will ever perish.* His believing companion advised him with these words.

## *Ma sha Allah*
- This is what Allah willed and gave, not by my strength or intelligence.

## *La quwwata illa billah*
- Admitting that the strength to keep and grow a blessing is from Allah.
- *La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah is a treasure among the treasures of Paradise* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## The end
- *His fruits were destroyed, and he began turning his hands over in regret* (18:42).

## Lessons
- **Say ma sha Allah** when you see a blessing, yours or others'.
- **Do not be deceived** by what you own.
