﴿قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَشۡكُواْ بَثِّي وَحُزۡنِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَأَعۡلَمُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ [Yusuf 12:86]

## The context
- His sons blamed Yaʿqub for constantly remembering Yusuf until his eyes turned white from grief.

## Two words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Bath* | Intense worry too heavy to hide |
| *Huzn* | Sorrow over what was lost |

## Complaining to Allah alone
- *Innama* restricts: I complain to Allah, not to you.
- This does not contradict his *beautiful patience*: that patience means not complaining to people.

## "I know from Allah what you do not know"
- He knew of Allah's mercy and the truth of Yusuf's dream, so he kept hope.

## Lessons
- **Grief is not weak faith**; what matters is where you take it.
- **Cry before Allah** in prostration.
