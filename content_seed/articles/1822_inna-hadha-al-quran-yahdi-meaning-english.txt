﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ يَهۡدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقۡوَمُ وَيُبَشِّرُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٗا كَبِيرٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:9]

## "Most upright"
- *Aqwam* is a superlative: not just upright, but **the most upright**. And the thing it guides to is left unnamed, *to that which is*, so it covers every path, way and state.

## Where it guides
| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| Belief | Pure tawhid |
| Worship | Balanced, without extremism or neglect |
| Character | Kindness to parents, justice, humility |
| Family | Rights of spouses and children |
| Economy | Honest trade, no riba, charity |

## The good news
- *A great reward* for those who **believe and do good**. Belief and action always come together.

## Context
- The verse comes soon after the Night Journey and the history of the Children of Israel, showing that the final guidance is now in the Quran.

## Lessons
- **Make the Quran your daily guide**, not just a book of blessing.
- **Ask: what does the Quran say about this?**
