﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ إِخۡوَةࣱ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَ أَخَوَيۡكُمۡۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ﴾ [Al-Hujurat 49:10]

## *Innama*
- Restriction: the lasting brotherhood is the brotherhood of **faith**.

## The context
- The previous verse speaks of two groups of believers fighting, and commands making peace. Even then, they remain **believers and brothers**.

## "Your two brothers"
- The dual form shows that reconciling is needed even when only **two** people quarrel.

## Rights of brotherhood
| Right | Source |
|---|---|
| Do not wrong or abandon him | *A Muslim is the brother of a Muslim; he does not wrong him or abandon him* (al-Bukhari and Muslim) |
| Love for him what you love for yourself | (al-Bukhari and Muslim) |
| Reconcile between him and others | This verse |

## Lessons
- **Be a peacemaker** in your family and friendships.
- **Taqwa brings mercy.**
