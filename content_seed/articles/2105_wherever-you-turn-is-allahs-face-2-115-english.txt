﴿وَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقُ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبُۚ فَأَيۡنَمَا تُوَلُّواْ فَثَمَّ وَجۡهُ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:115]

## East and west
- All directions belong to Allah; no direction limits His greatness.

## Occasions mentioned
| View | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Voluntary prayer while riding | The Prophet ﷺ prayed on his mount wherever it faced while travelling; Ibn ʿUmar mentioned this verse (narrated by Muslim) |
| The one who errs in qiblah | After genuine effort in darkness or travel |
| Before the qiblah change | A preparation for turning to the Kaʿbah |

## "There is the Face of Allah"
- Scholars explained: there is the qiblah Allah permitted, or Allah sees you there, while affirming the Face of Allah as befits His majesty.

## "All-Encompassing, All-Knowing"
- Vast in generosity and ease, knowing your intentions.

## Lessons
- **Face the qiblah** in obligatory prayers; the concession is for voluntary prayer on a mount.
- **Allah sees you** wherever you are.
