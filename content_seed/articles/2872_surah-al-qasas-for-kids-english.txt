A mother places her baby in a box, then puts the box in the river and lets it float away! Who would do that? Musa's mother did, because Allah inspired her and made her a great promise: "We will return him to you and make him one of the messengers." Allah kept His promise. Musa returned to his mother's arms, grew up, and became a great prophet.

Surah Al-Qasas is one of the best surahs for children, because it tells Musa's story from his birth: his brave mother, his clever sister who followed him from a distance, and then young Musa, strong and kind, helping two women water their sheep without asking for anything in return. It also tells of Qarun, a rich man who was arrogant about his treasures. In this guide, we explain Surah Al-Qasas to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Qasas

- **Position**: surah 28, in juz 20 and 21.
- **Number of verses**: 88.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: al-Qasas, "The Stories", from the verse where Musa tells his story to the righteous old man in Madyan.

## Lesson 1: Allah Inspires Musa's Mother

﴿وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنۡ أَرۡضِعِيهِۖ فَإِذَا خِفۡتِ عَلَيۡهِ فَأَلۡقِيهِ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ وَلَا تَخَافِي وَلَا تَحۡزَنِيٓۖ إِنَّا رَآدُّوهُ إِلَيۡكِ وَجَاعِلُوهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:7)

### Explaining it to your child
"Pharaoh was killing the baby boys of the Children of Israel. When Musa was born, his mother was afraid for him. Allah inspired her: Nurse him, and when you fear for him, place him in the river. Don't be afraid and don't be sad. We will return him to you, and We will make him a messenger. Allah gave her two promises: he will come back to you, and he will be a prophet."

### Lesson for kids: trusting Allah's promise
"Musa's mother did something very hard, but she trusted Allah's promise. And Allah never breaks His promise."

## Lesson 2: A Mother's Heart and a Clever Sister

﴿وَأَصۡبَحَ فُؤَادُ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰ فَٰرِغًاۖ إِن كَادَتۡ لَتُبۡدِي بِهِۦ لَوۡلَآ أَن رَّبَطۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِهَا لِتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالَتۡ لِأُخۡتِهِۦ قُصِّيهِۖ فَبَصُرَتۡ بِهِۦ عَن جُنُبٖ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:10-11)

### Explaining it to your child
"After she put her son in the river, Musa's mother could think of nothing but Musa. She almost cried out: That's my son! But Allah made her heart steady. She told Musa's sister: Follow him. So his sister watched him from a distance, and nobody realised she was his sister."

### Lesson for kids: a brave, clever sister
"Musa's sister was a young girl, but she was brave, walking along the riverbank following the box, and clever, making sure nobody knew who she was. Brothers and sisters look out for each other."

### Activity: the follow game
Hide a small toy and ask your child to follow you from a distance without you noticing, just as Musa's sister followed the box. Then explain that she did it to protect her brother.

## Lesson 3: Back to His Mother

﴿۞وَحَرَّمۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَرَاضِعَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَقَالَتۡ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِ بَيۡتٖ يَكۡفُلُونَهُۥ لَكُمۡ وَهُمۡ لَهُۥ نَٰصِحُونَ﴾ ﴿فَرَدَدۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّهِۦ كَيۡ تَقَرَّ عَيۡنُهَا وَلَا تَحۡزَنَ وَلِتَعۡلَمَ أَنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:12-13)

### Explaining it to your child
"The box reached Pharaoh's palace, and Pharaoh's wife loved the baby. They brought many women to nurse him, but Musa refused all of them! That was Allah's plan. His sister said cleverly: Shall I show you a family who will look after him and care for him? They took him to his mother, and he fed straight away! So Musa returned to his mother so she would be happy and not sad, and know that Allah's promise is true."

### Activity: the map
Draw a simple map with your child: Musa's home, the river, Pharaoh's palace, and an arrow back to his mother's home. Say: "Look how Musa returned to his mother, just as Allah promised!"

## Lesson 4: On the Road to Madyan

﴿وَلَمَّا تَوَجَّهَ تِلۡقَآءَ مَدۡيَنَ قَالَ عَسَىٰ رَبِّيٓ أَن يَهۡدِيَنِي سَوَآءَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ﴾ (Quran 28:22)

### Explaining it to your child
"Musa grew into a strong young man. He left Egypt in fear and walked through the desert for days towards a land called Madyan. He didn't know the way, so he said: Perhaps my Lord will guide me to the right path."

### Lesson for kids
"When we don't know what to do, we say: 'Asa Rabbi an yahdiyani sawa'as-sabil. It's a beautiful du'a for when we feel unsure."

## Lesson 5: Musa Helps the Two Women

﴿وَلَمَّا وَرَدَ مَآءَ مَدۡيَنَ وَجَدَ عَلَيۡهِ أُمَّةٗ مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ يَسۡقُونَ وَوَجَدَ مِن دُونِهِمُ ٱمۡرَأَتَيۡنِ تَذُودَانِۖ قَالَ مَا خَطۡبُكُمَاۖ قَالَتَا لَا نَسۡقِي حَتَّىٰ يُصۡدِرَ ٱلرِّعَآءُۖ وَأَبُونَا شَيۡخࣱ كَبِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿فَسَقَىٰ لَهُمَا ثُمَّ تَوَلَّىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلظِّلِّ فَقَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَآ أَنزَلۡتَ إِلَيَّ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ فَقِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 28:23-24)

### Explaining it to your child
"Musa reached a well in Madyan and found many men watering their animals, and two women standing back, holding their sheep away from the water. He asked: What's the matter? They said: We can't water our sheep until the shepherds leave, and our father is a very old man. So Musa watered their sheep for them and asked for nothing in return. Then he went to the shade, sat down, and prayed: My Lord, I am truly in need of whatever good You send down to me."

### Notice Musa's character
- **He helped without being asked**: he saw someone in need and helped.
- **He didn't ask for payment**: he did good for Allah's sake.
- **He went to the shade**: he didn't linger with the women, out of good manners.
- **He made du'a**: he was tired, hungry and a stranger, so he asked Allah.

### Activity: help without being asked
Set your child a challenge: this week, help three people without being asked, like Musa: carry grandma's bags, hold the door for a neighbour, and tidy a sibling's shoes.

## Lesson 6: The Strong and Trustworthy

﴿فَجَآءَتۡهُ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا تَمۡشِي عَلَى ٱسۡتِحۡيَآءٖ قَالَتۡ إِنَّ أَبِي يَدۡعُوكَ لِيَجۡزِيَكَ أَجۡرَ مَا سَقَيۡتَ لَنَاۚ فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُۥ وَقَصَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡقَصَصَ قَالَ لَا تَخَفۡۖ نَجَوۡتَ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَتۡ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا يَـٰٓأَبَتِ ٱسۡتَـٔۡجِرۡهُۖ إِنَّ خَيۡرَ مَنِ ٱسۡتَـٔۡجَرۡتَ ٱلۡقَوِيُّ ٱلۡأَمِينُ﴾ (Quran 28:25-26)

### Explaining it to your child
"The two women went back and told their father what Musa did. The father sent one of them to invite him. She came walking shyly and politely and said: My father invites you to reward you for watering for us. When Musa came and told his story, the old man said: Don't be afraid, you are safe from the wrongdoers. Then one of the women said: Father, hire him. The best person to hire is someone strong and trustworthy."

### Lesson for kids: strength and trustworthiness
"The young woman saw two qualities in Musa: strength, because he watered the sheep alone, and trustworthiness, because he was polite and respectful. Any successful work needs both: a successful student works hard and never cheats."

## Lesson 7: You Cannot Guide Whom You Love

﴿إِنَّكَ لَا تَهۡدِي مَنۡ أَحۡبَبۡتَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:56)

### Explaining it to your child
"The Prophet ﷺ loved his uncle Abu Talib very much and wanted him to become Muslim, but he didn't. Allah told His Prophet that guidance is in Allah's hands. We invite people to good and speak kindly, and guidance comes from Allah."

### Lesson for kids
"If you advise a friend and they don't listen, don't be too sad or angry with them. Make du'a for them and keep speaking kindly."

## Lesson 8: Qarun and His Treasures

﴿۞إِنَّ قَٰرُونَ كَانَ مِن قَوۡمِ مُوسَىٰ فَبَغَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡكُنُوزِ مَآ إِنَّ مَفَاتِحَهُۥ لَتَنُوٓأُ بِٱلۡعُصۡبَةِ أُوْلِي ٱلۡقُوَّةِ إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُۥ قَوۡمُهُۥ لَا تَفۡرَحۡۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡفَرِحِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:76)

### Explaining it to your child
"Qarun was from Musa's people, and Allah gave him so much treasure that the keys to his storehouses were too heavy for a group of strong men to carry! His people told him: Don't be arrogantly proud, Allah does not love the arrogant."

﴿وَٱبۡتَغِ فِيمَآ ءَاتَىٰكَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلدَّارَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۖ وَلَا تَنسَ نَصِيبَكَ مِنَ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَأَحۡسِن كَمَآ أَحۡسَنَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَيۡكَۖ وَلَا تَبۡغِ ٱلۡفَسَادَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:77)

### Explaining it to your child
"His people gave him four pieces of advice: use your wealth to earn Paradise, don't forget to enjoy what is halal in this world, be good to people as Allah has been good to you, and don't spread corruption on earth. But Qarun said: I only got this because of my own knowledge and cleverness! He forgot that wealth comes from Allah. So Allah made the earth swallow him and his house."

### Lesson for kids
"Everything you have is from Allah: your toys, your clothes and your intelligence. So we say: This is from the favour of my Lord, and we share with others."

### Activity: the sharing box
Keep a box at home where your child puts a toy or clothes they no longer use, to be given to a child in need. Say: "This is how we do good, as Allah has been good to us."

## Let's Read Together: Paradise for the Humble

﴿تِلۡكَ ٱلدَّارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ نَجۡعَلُهَا لِلَّذِينَ لَا يُرِيدُونَ عُلُوّٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فَسَادٗاۚ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ (Quran 28:83)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah made the home of the Hereafter for those who don't want to be arrogant over people or cause corruption. The humble, kind person is the real winner in the end."

﴿وَلَا تَدۡعُ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَۘ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ كُلُّ شَيۡءٍ هَالِكٌ إِلَّا وَجۡهَهُۥۚ لَهُ ٱلۡحُكۡمُ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 28:88)

### Explaining it to your child
"The last verse: there is no god but Allah, and everything in this world will perish except Him. Qarun's treasures are gone and Pharaoh's kingdom is gone, and Allah remains. Musa, who watered the sheep for free, is still remembered with goodness today."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Hero at the Water Fountain"

On a school trip to a big park, the children lined up at the water fountain on a very hot day. The older pupils were pushing to drink first, and two little ones from Year 1 were standing back, waiting, unable to reach.

Yusuf, from Year 4, noticed them. He remembered the story of Musa that his Quran teacher had told: the two women at the well, and Musa who watered their sheep without being asked. So he filled two cups with water and gave them to the little ones.

"Thank you! We were so thirsty," one of them said. Yusuf smiled, then went and sat under the shade of a tree, just like Musa.

His teacher saw what he did, came over and said: "Yusuf, you're like Prophet Musa at the well of Madyan! You helped someone who needed it without being asked." Yusuf said: "Miss, and Musa made du'a afterwards: My Lord, I am in need of whatever good You send me." His teacher said: "How wonderful that you know the du'a and understand it!"

Ask your child: "What did Yusuf do like Musa? Who needs your help at school?"

## What We Learn From Surah Al-Qasas

- **Allah's promise** is always true.
- **Siblings** look out for each other.
- **Allah's plan** beats every human plan.
- **Help people in need** without being asked.
- **Make du'a** when you need something.
- **Modesty and good manners** in walking and speaking.
- **Strength and trustworthiness** are the secret of success.
- **Humility and gratitude**, not pride like Qarun.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### How do I explain to my child that a mother put her baby in a river?
"Allah told her to, and promised to protect him and bring him back, so she did it because she trusted Allah, and Allah watched over him the whole journey." Focus on trust, not fear.

### Did Musa marry one of the two women?
Yes. The old man offered to marry him to one of his daughters in exchange for working for him for some years, so Musa worked and married.

### Why does this surah suit young children?
The box on the river, the clever sister and the scene at the well are vivid and easy to imagine and act out.
