﴿ٱلۡمَالُ وَٱلۡبَنُونَ زِينَةُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَٱلۡبَٰقِيَٰتُ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتُ خَيۡرٌ عِندَ رَبِّكَ ثَوَابٗا وَخَيۡرٌ أَمَلٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:46]

## Adornment, not essence
- *Zinah* is decoration: beautiful, enjoyable, but on the surface and temporary.

## The context
- Just before it, the parable of this life as plants that flourish after rain, then become dry stalks scattered by the wind (18:45).

## The lasting good deeds
- Many Companions and scholars explained *al-baqiyat as-salihat* as: *SubhanAllah, alhamdulillah, la ilaha illa Allah, Allahu akbar*, and some added *la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah*. Others said it includes every good deed.

## Two comparisons
| | Adornment | Lasting good deeds |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | Temporary | Remains |
| Reward | In this life | With your Lord |
| Hope | May disappoint | Better hope |

## Children as lasting deeds
- A righteous child who prays for you is among the deeds that continue after death (narrated by Muslim). Raising them well turns adornment into lasting reward.

## Lessons
- **Enjoy wealth and family**, but invest in what lasts.
- **Fill free moments with dhikr.**
