﴿إِنَّا كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَٰهُ بِقَدَرٖ﴾ [Al-Qamar 54:49]

## *Qadar*
- Measure: everything is created with a precise amount and wisdom, in its time, form and quantity.

## Four levels of belief in decree
| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Knowledge | Allah knew everything before it happened |
| Writing | He wrote it in the Preserved Tablet |
| Will | Nothing happens except by His will |
| Creation | He creates everything |

## A comprehensive hadith
- *Everything is by decree, even inability and ability* (narrated by Muslim).

## Decree and effort
- Belief in decree does not mean leaving effort; effort is part of decree. *Act, for each is eased to what he was created for* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Its place
- Near the end of Al-Qamar after the destruction of past nations: nothing happens by chance.

## Lessons
- **Be at peace**: what reached you was never going to miss you.
- **Work** and take the means.
