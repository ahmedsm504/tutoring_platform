**Juz Amma** is the 30th and final part of the Quran. It contains **37 surahs**, from [An-Naba (78)](/quran/78/) to [An-Nas (114)](/quran/114/), with a total of **564 verses**. Most children start their memorization journey here, because the surahs are short and every finished surah feels like a victory.

This plan is designed for a child aged **7 to 10**, memorizing **five days a week**. For younger children (4–6), simply double the time to a full year.

## Why start from An-Nas and go backwards?
- The last surahs are the shortest, so the child finishes a whole surah in just a few days.
- They are also the surahs children hear most in prayer and daily remembrance.
- As the surahs get longer, the child's memorization skill grows with them.

**Before starting:** if your child doesn't know [Surah Al-Fatiha](/quran/1/) yet, start there.

## The month-by-month plan
| Month | Surahs | Verses | Daily target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An-Nas → Al-Kawthar | 32 | 1–2 verses |
| 2 | Al-Maun → Al-Qariah | 47 | 2–3 verses |
| 3 | Al-Adiyat → Ash-Sharh | 67 | 3–4 verses |
| 4 | Ad-Duha → Al-Ghashiyah | 123 | 5–6 short verses |
| 5 | Al-Ala → Al-Infitar | 138 | 6–7 short verses |
| 6 | At-Takwir → An-Naba | 157 | 7–8 short verses |
| **Total** | **37 surahs** | **564** | |

### Month 1 in detail
An-Nas, Al-Falaq, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Masad, An-Nasr, Al-Kafirun, Al-Kawthar. The goal this month is to **build the habit**, not to rush.

### Month 3 note
Surah Al-Bayyinah (98) has longer verses than the surahs around it. Give it extra days if needed.

## A model week
| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Days 1–5 | New verses + yesterday's verses + one older surah |
| Day 6 | Review everything memorized this week |
| Day 7 | Rest, or a fun family recitation |

## The revision system
- **Every day:** recite yesterday's verses before starting new ones.
- **Every week:** review the whole week's work.
- **Every month:** in the last week, cut new memorization in half and review everything from the start.

## If your child falls behind
That's completely fine. The plan is a guide, not a rule. If a longer surah needs an extra week, give it. What matters is that your child finishes Juz Amma **loving it and knowing it well**.

## Tools that help
- **Daily listening:** play the surah recited by Sheikh Al-Husary (the *Mu'allim* teaching recitation is slow and clear, ideal for kids) on our [Quran pages](/quran/), with verse repetition.
- **Understanding:** tell your child the story behind surahs like Al-Fil (the army of elephants) before memorizing it.
- **Motivation:** a star chart, and a small celebration after every surah.
