## "You Are the Best Nation Brought Forth for Mankind": Quran 3:110 Explained

﴿كُنتُمۡ خَيۡرَ أُمَّةٍ أُخۡرِجَتۡ لِلنَّاسِ تَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَتَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَتُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِۗ وَلَوۡ ءَامَنَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۚ مِّنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:110]

### "For mankind"
- *Brought forth for people*: the Ummah exists **to benefit others**, not only itself.

### Three qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Enjoining good | Encouraging what is right |
| Forbidding evil | Preventing harm with wisdom |
| Believing in Allah | The foundation of the other two |

### A responsibility
- The title is linked to the qualities. ʿUmar ibn al-Khattab is reported to have said that whoever wants to be of this nation should fulfil its condition (reported by al-Tabari).

### Fairness to others
- The verse ends by acknowledging that **among the People of the Book are believers**, a lesson in justice even when speaking about others.

### Wisdom in correction
- Enjoining good is done with knowledge, gentleness and the best method, as in An-Nahl 16:125.

### Lessons
- **Be a source of good** wherever you are.
- **Earn the title** through action.

## "Hasten to Forgiveness from Your Lord": Quran 3:133-134 Explained

﴿۞وَسَارِعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:133]

### A race worth running
- The command is not just to *go* but to **hasten**. Forgiveness is mentioned before Paradise because it is the door to it.

### How wide is Paradise?
- Its **width** is the heavens and the earth. If that is its width, its full extent is beyond imagination.

### The qualities of the pious
﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ فِي ٱلسَّرَّآءِ وَٱلضَّرَّآءِ وَٱلۡكَٰظِمِينَ ٱلۡغَيۡظَ وَٱلۡعَافِينَ عَنِ ٱلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Spending in ease and hardship | Generosity that does not wait for wealth |
| Restraining anger | Holding it in, not acting on it |
| Pardoning people | Letting go of the right to retaliate |
| Excellence (*ihsan*) | Going further: doing good to those who wronged you |

### Three levels with anger
- Scholars note a progression: first you **hold** your anger, then you **forgive**, then you **do good**. Each is higher than the one before.

### The following verse
- The pious are also those who, when they commit a sin or wrong themselves, **remember Allah and seek forgiveness**, and do not persist.

### Lessons
- **Do not delay repentance.**
- **Practise one step**: next time you are angry, stay silent.

## "When They Commit an Immorality, They Remember Allah and Seek Forgiveness": Quran 3:135 Explained

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا فَعَلُواْ فَٰحِشَةً أَوۡ ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ ذَكَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَٱسۡتَغۡفَرُواْ لِذُنُوبِهِمۡ وَمَن يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَلَمۡ يُصِرُّواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلُواْ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:135]

### Among the pious
- This is listed among the qualities of the pious for whom Paradise is prepared (3:133). The pious may slip, but they **do not persist**.

### Two kinds of wrong
| Phrase | Explained by some scholars as |
|---|---|
| *Fahishah* | A grave, shameful sin |
| Wronging oneself | Lesser sins |

### "They remember Allah"
- They remember His greatness, warning and mercy, and that remembrance leads to repentance.

### A merciful interjection
- *And who forgives sins except Allah?* It opens the door of hope: there is no refuge except Him.

### Not persisting
- Persistence means continuing in sin without regret or resolve to stop.

### Lessons
- **Remember Allah right after a mistake.**
- **Never let a sin become a habit.**

## "Do Not Weaken and Do Not Grieve, You Will Be Superior": Quran 3:139 Explained

﴿وَلَا تَهِنُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَوۡنَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:139]

### The context
- After Uhud, the Muslims were wounded and grieving the loss of many Companions. This verse came to **lift them up**.

### Two prohibitions
| Prohibition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Do not weaken* | Do not lose resolve or give up |
| *Do not grieve* | Do not let sorrow over the past paralyse you |

### The source of superiority
- *You are the superior*, with a condition: *if you are believers*. Elevation is tied to faith, not numbers or wealth.

### The following verse
- *If a wound has touched you, a similar wound has touched the other side. And these days We alternate among people.* Victory and loss rotate; faith is what lasts.

### For personal setbacks
- A failed exam, a lost opportunity, or criticism: the verse speaks to every believer who falls and must rise.

### Lessons
- **Get up after falling.**
- **Measure yourself by faith**, not by others' opinions.

## "These Days We Alternate Among People": Quran 3:140 Explained

﴿إِن يَمۡسَسۡكُمۡ قَرۡحࣱ فَقَدۡ مَسَّ ٱلۡقَوۡمَ قَرۡحࣱ مِّثۡلُهُۥۚ وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡأَيَّامُ نُدَاوِلُهَا بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلِيَعۡلَمَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَيَتَّخِذَ مِنكُمۡ شُهَدَآءَۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:140]

### Wounds on both sides
- After Uhud the Muslims were wounded; the verse reminds them the other side was wounded at Badr.

### The rotation
- Victory and defeat, wealth and poverty, health and illness: **no state lasts** in this world.

### Wisdom in the trial
| Wisdom | Meaning |
|---|---|
| To make the believers evident | The sincere become distinct |
| To take martyrs | Honouring some with martyrdom |
| Allah does not love wrongdoers | Temporary success is not a sign of His love |

### The next verse
- *And that Allah may purify the believers and destroy the disbelievers* (3:141).

### Lessons
- **Do not be deceived by success**, nor broken by failure.
- **Look for the wisdom** in every change.

## "How Many a Prophet Fought with Many Devoted Followers": Quran 3:146 Explained

﴿وَكَأَيِّن مِّن نَّبِيّٖ قَٰتَلَ مَعَهُۥ رِبِّيُّونَ كَثِيرࣱ فَمَا وَهَنُواْ لِمَآ أَصَابَهُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا ضَعُفُواْ وَمَا ٱسۡتَكَانُواْۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:146]

### The context
- At Uhud a rumour spread that the Prophet ﷺ had been killed, and some lost heart. Allah reminded them of the followers of earlier prophets.

### *Ribbiyyun*
- Explained as large groups, or devoted scholars of the Lord.

### Three things they did not do
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Wahanu* | Lose heart |
| *Daʿufu* | Weaken in body and resolve |
| *Istakanu* | Submit to the enemy |

### Their duʿa
- *Our Lord, forgive us our sins and our excesses, make our feet firm, and grant us victory* (3:147). They began with **forgiveness**, knowing sins are a cause of defeat.

### Lessons
- **Firmness is tied to Allah**, not to any person.
- **Start duʿa in hardship** with istighfar.

## "By Mercy from Allah You Were Gentle with Them": Quran 3:159 Explained

﴿فَبِمَا رَحۡمَةٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ لِنتَ لَهُمۡۖ وَلَوۡ كُنتَ فَظًّا غَلِيظَ ٱلۡقَلۡبِ لَٱنفَضُّواْ مِنۡ حَوۡلِكَۖ فَٱعۡفُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ وَشَاوِرۡهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِۖ فَإِذَا عَزَمۡتَ فَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَوَكِّلِينَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:159]

### The context
- Revealed after the Battle of Uhud, when some Companions left their positions and the Muslims suffered. Instead of harshness, the verse commands **gentleness**.

### Gentleness is a gift
- The Prophet's soft manner is described as **a mercy from Allah**. Gentleness is not weakness; it is what keeps hearts together.

### Harshness drives people away
- *Had you been harsh and hard-hearted, they would have scattered from around you*, and this is said about the best of people. How much more for us.

### A method in four steps
| Step | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pardon them | Forgive what concerns your own right |
| Seek forgiveness for them | Pray for them before Allah |
| Consult them | Involve them in the decision |
| Rely on Allah | After deciding, trust Him and act |

### Consultation after a mistake
- Remarkably, the command to consult came right after a consultation whose outcome (going out to Uhud) was painful. The principle stayed.

### Lessons
- **Lead with gentleness**: as a parent, teacher or manager.
- **Plan, consult, then trust Allah.**

## "Do Not Think of Those Killed in Allah's Cause as Dead": Quran 3:169-170 Explained

﴿وَلَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قُتِلُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَمۡوَٰتَۢاۚ بَلۡ أَحۡيَآءٌ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ يُرۡزَقُونَ﴾ ﴿فَرِحِينَ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَيَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ بِٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يَلۡحَقُواْ بِهِم مِّنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ أَلَّا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:169-170]

### The context
- Revealed after Uhud, to console the believers about their martyrs.

### A special life
- A real life of the barzakh with Allah, whose nature we do not know, unlike this worldly life.

### In the Sunnah
- Ibn Masʿud was asked about the verse and said they had asked the Prophet ﷺ, who said: *Their souls are in the bellies of green birds, with lamps hung from the Throne, roaming in Paradise wherever they wish* (narrated by Muslim).

### Their states
| State | In the verse |
|---|---|
| Provided for | *They are given provision* |
| Joyful | In what Allah gave them |
| Rejoicing | For those yet to join them |

### Lessons
- **Death in Allah's cause** is honour, not an end.
- **Comfort for families**: your loved ones are alive with their Lord.

## "Allah Is Sufficient for Us, and He Is the Best Disposer of Affairs": Quran 3:173 Explained

﴿ٱلَّذِينَ قَالَ لَهُمُ ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ قَدۡ جَمَعُواْ لَكُمۡ فَٱخۡشَوۡهُمۡ فَزَادَهُمۡ إِيمَٰنٗا وَقَالُواْ حَسۡبُنَا ٱللَّهُ وَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡوَكِيلُ﴾ [Al Imran 3:173]

### The context
- After Uhud, the wounded Companions went out again with the Prophet ﷺ. People warned them that a huge army had gathered against them. Instead of fear, **their faith increased**.

### The words
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Hasbunallah* | Allah is enough for us |
| *Wa niʿmal wakil* | And He is the best One to rely on |

### Ibrahim's words too
- Ibn ʿAbbas said: *Hasbunallahu wa niʿmal wakil* was said by Ibrahim when he was thrown into the fire, and by Muhammad ﷺ when people said to him what is in this verse (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### The result
- The next verse: *So they returned with favour from Allah and bounty; no harm touched them, and they followed the pleasure of Allah.*

### When to say it
- When afraid, threatened, overwhelmed by debt, or facing an uncertain future.

### Lessons
- **Let pressure increase your faith**, not your fear.
- **Say it with certainty**, then do your part.

## "Every Soul Will Taste Death": Quran 3:185 Explained

﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۗ وَإِنَّمَا تُوَفَّوۡنَ أُجُورَكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۖ فَمَن زُحۡزِحَ عَنِ ٱلنَّارِ وَأُدۡخِلَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ فَقَدۡ فَازَۗ وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡغُرُورِ﴾ [Al Imran 3:185]

### Four truths
| Truth | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Every soul tastes death | No exceptions |
| Full reward on the Day of Judgment | Some reward comes in this life, but the **full** reward later |
| Real success | Being moved away from the Fire and admitted into Paradise |
| This world | Enjoyment that deceives |

### "Tastes"
- Death is described as something **tasted**: a moment, not an end. After it comes the real life.

### "Moved away" from the Fire
- *Zuhziha* suggests being pulled away with effort, as if the Fire were very close. Salvation is a great mercy.

### Deceptive enjoyment
- This world is not evil, but it **deceives** when it becomes the goal.

### A remedy for grief
- The verse is often recited at funerals and in times of loss: death is the shared path; the question is the destination.

### Lessons
- **Prepare for death** with good deeds.
- **Redefine success** by the Hereafter.

## "You Will Surely Be Tested in Your Wealth and Yourselves": Quran 3:186 Explained

﴿۞لَتُبۡلَوُنَّ فِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنفُسِكُمۡ وَلَتَسۡمَعُنَّ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ وَمِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُوٓاْ أَذٗى كَثِيرٗاۚ وَإِن تَصۡبِرُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ فَإِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾ [Al Imran 3:186]

### Certainty
- The emphatic forms *latublawunna* and *latasmaʿunna* mean the test **will** come.

### Three kinds
| Kind | Example |
|---|---|
| Wealth | Loss, decrease, spending |
| Selves | Illness, death, fatigue |
| Words | Mockery, criticism, insults |

### The response
- **Patience**: holding back from panic and retaliation in kind.
- **Taqwa**: not letting harm push you into injustice or sin.

### "Matters of determination"
- Matters requiring strong resolve, among the highest morals.

### Lessons
- **Expect harm** on the path of good, and do not quit.
- **Do not answer** harm with harm.

## "In the Creation of the Heavens and the Earth Are Signs": Quran 3:190-191 Explained

﴿إِنَّ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ [Al Imran 3:190]

### Verses the Prophet ﷺ recited at night
- Ibn ʿAbbas narrated that the Prophet ﷺ woke at night, looked at the sky and recited these verses from the end of Al Imran (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### Who are the people of understanding?
﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَذۡكُرُونَ ٱللَّهَ قِيَٰمٗا وَقُعُودٗا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِهِمۡ وَيَتَفَكَّرُونَ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقۡتَ هَٰذَا بَٰطِلٗا سُبۡحَٰنَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ﴾
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Remembrance in every posture | Allah is on their minds all day |
| Reflection | They think about the universe |
| Conclusion | *Our Lord, You did not create this aimlessly* |
| Duʿa | *Protect us from the punishment of the Fire* |

### Reflection leads to worship
- The verse connects **thinking** with **remembering**. Science that increases awe of the Creator is part of this reflection.

### The duʿas that follow
- The passage continues with a series of prayers: for forgiveness, a good death with the righteous, and fulfilment of Allah's promise.

### Lessons
- **Look up at the sky** and remember these verses.
- **Purpose**: nothing in creation, including you, was made in vain.

## "Be Patient, Outlast in Patience, and Stand Guard": Quran 3:200 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱصۡبِرُواْ وَصَابِرُواْ وَرَابِطُواْ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:200]

### Four commands
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Isbiru* | Be patient: in obedience, away from sin, and in trials |
| *Sabiru* | Outlast others in patience, especially the enemy |
| *Rabitu* | Stand guard: at the frontiers, and in the mosque waiting for prayer |
| *Ittaqu Allah* | Be mindful of Allah in all of it |

### Ribat in daily life
- The Prophet ﷺ described performing wudu well, walking to mosques often and waiting for the next prayer after the previous one, then said: **"That is ribat"** (narrated by Muslim).

### A formula for success
- The verse ends: *so that you may succeed*. Success is the result of **patience plus taqwa**.

### A fitting ending
- Al Imran discusses Uhud, struggles and setbacks. Its final verse gives the summary: endure, outlast, stay firm.

### Lessons
- **Patience is active**, not passive waiting.
- **Stay connected to the mosque.**
