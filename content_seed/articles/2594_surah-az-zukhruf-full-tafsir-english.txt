Surah Az-Zukhruf takes its name from the gold ornaments of this world, and its central message is that these ornaments mean nothing in Allah's sight. If it were not that all people would become one nation of disbelievers, Allah would have given the disbelievers houses with roofs of silver, stairways, doors and couches, and gold ornaments. The surah answers people who judged truth by wealth: "Why was this Quran not sent down upon a great man from one of the two cities?" It exposes blind imitation of forefathers, gives us the du'a we say when riding, tells of Pharaoh who boasted "Is not mine the kingdom of Egypt?", and ends with a warning that every friendship not built on taqwa will turn to enmity.

This guide explains Surah Az-Zukhruf section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Az-Zukhruf
- **Number of verses**: 89.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 43, in the twenty-fifth juz.
- **Name**: Az-Zukhruf means "gold ornaments", from verse 35.
- **One of the *Hawamim*.**
- **Main themes**: the honour of the Quran, tawhid, the worthlessness of worldly glitter, blind imitation, and companionship.

## Verses 1–8: The Quran in the Mother of the Book
﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰهُ قُرۡءَٰنًا عَرَبِيّٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ فِيٓ أُمِّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَدَيۡنَا لَعَلِيٌّ حَكِيمٌ﴾ ﴿أَفَنَضۡرِبُ عَنكُمُ ٱلذِّكۡرَ صَفۡحًا أَن كُنتُمۡ قَوۡمٗا مُّسۡرِفِينَ﴾ ﴿وَكَمۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن نَّبِيّٖ فِي ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَمَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن نَّبِيٍّ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَهۡلَكۡنَآ أَشَدَّ مِنۡهُم بَطۡشٗا وَمَضَىٰ مَثَلُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ (Quran 43:1-8)

**"Indeed, We have made it an Arabic Quran that you might understand. And indeed it is, in the Mother of the Book with Us, exalted and full of wisdom."** The Quran is recorded in the Preserved Tablet, high in rank.

**"Then should We turn the message away from you, disregarding you, because you are a transgressing people?"** Allah's mercy keeps sending the reminder even to those who turn away.

## Verses 9–14: The Du'a of Riding
﴿وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ لَيَقُولُنَّ خَلَقَهُنَّ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَهۡدٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُمۡ فِيهَا سُبُلٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي نَزَّلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءَۢ بِقَدَرٖ فَأَنشَرۡنَا بِهِۦ بَلۡدَةٗ مَّيۡتٗاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ تُخۡرَجُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَزۡوَٰجَ كُلَّهَا وَجَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡفُلۡكِ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ مَا تَرۡكَبُونَ﴾ ﴿لِتَسۡتَوُۥاْ عَلَىٰ ظُهُورِهِۦ ثُمَّ تَذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَةَ رَبِّكُمۡ إِذَا ٱسۡتَوَيۡتُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَتَقُولُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُۥ مُقۡرِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:9-14)

The idolaters admitted that Allah created the heavens and earth, yet worshipped others. The surah lists His favours: the earth as a cradle, roads, rain, pairs, and **ships and animals on which you ride**.

**The du'a when riding**: **"Exalted is He who has subjected this to us, and we could not have otherwise subdued it. And indeed we, to our Lord, will surely return."** In Sahih Muslim, Ibn 'Umar reported that when the Prophet ﷺ mounted his camel to travel, he said *Allahu akbar* three times, then recited this verse, then continued with the travel du'a. Muslims today say it in cars, planes and trains.

**"And indeed we, to our Lord, will surely return"**: every journey reminds you of the final journey.

## Verses 15–25: False Claims and Blind Imitation
﴿وَجَعَلُواْ لَهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ جُزۡءًاۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَكَفُورࣱ مُّبِينٌ﴾ ﴿أَمِ ٱتَّخَذَ مِمَّا يَخۡلُقُ بَنَاتٖ وَأَصۡفَىٰكُم بِٱلۡبَنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا بُشِّرَ أَحَدُهُم بِمَا ضَرَبَ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مَثَلٗا ظَلَّ وَجۡهُهُۥ مُسۡوَدّٗا وَهُوَ كَظِيمٌ﴾ ﴿أَوَمَن يُنَشَّؤُاْ فِي ٱلۡحِلۡيَةِ وَهُوَ فِي ٱلۡخِصَامِ غَيۡرُ مُبِينٖ﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلُواْ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عِبَٰدُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ إِنَٰثًاۚ أَشَهِدُواْ خَلۡقَهُمۡۚ سَتُكۡتَبُ شَهَٰدَتُهُمۡ وَيُسۡـَٔلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ لَوۡ شَآءَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ مَا عَبَدۡنَٰهُمۗ مَّا لَهُم بِذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عِلۡمٍۖ إِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا يَخۡرُصُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:15-20)

They attributed daughters to Allah while disliking daughters for themselves: **"when one of them is given good news of a girl, his face becomes dark"**. They called the angels females. **"Did they witness their creation? Their testimony will be recorded and they will be questioned."**

﴿أَمۡ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمۡ كِتَٰبٗا مِّن قَبۡلِهِۦ فَهُم بِهِۦ مُسۡتَمۡسِكُونَ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا وَجَدۡنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا عَلَىٰٓ أُمَّةٖ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِم مُّهۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ مَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ فِي قَرۡيَةٖ مِّن نَّذِيرٍ إِلَّا قَالَ مُتۡرَفُوهَآ إِنَّا وَجَدۡنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا عَلَىٰٓ أُمَّةٖ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِم مُّقۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿۞قَٰلَ أَوَلَوۡ جِئۡتُكُم بِأَهۡدَىٰ مِمَّا وَجَدتُّمۡ عَلَيۡهِ ءَابَآءَكُمۡۖ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا بِمَآ أُرۡسِلۡتُم بِهِۦ كَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَٱنتَقَمۡنَا مِنۡهُمۡۖ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ (Quran 43:21-25)

**Their only argument**: **"We found our fathers upon a religion, and we are in their footsteps following."** Every messenger heard the same excuse. The messenger asked: **"Even if I brought you better guidance than that upon which you found your fathers?"**

**Blind imitation** (*taqlid*) is following without evidence. Respecting parents does not mean following them in error.

## Verses 26–30: Ibrahim's Lasting Word
﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنَّنِي بَرَآءࣱ مِّمَّا تَعۡبُدُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنِي فَإِنَّهُۥ سَيَهۡدِينِ﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلَهَا كَلِمَةَۢ بَاقِيَةٗ فِي عَقِبِهِۦ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:26-28)

**Ibrahim** said to his father and people: **"I am disassociated from that which you worship, except for He who created me; and indeed, He will guide me."** **"And he made it a word remaining among his descendants that they might return."** The word of tawhid, *la ilaha illa Allah*, has remained in Ibrahim's descendants until today.

## Verses 31–35: Wealth Is Not a Measure
﴿وَقَالُواْ لَوۡلَا نُزِّلَ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ عَلَىٰ رَجُلٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡقَرۡيَتَيۡنِ عَظِيمٍ﴾ ﴿أَهُمۡ يَقۡسِمُونَ رَحۡمَتَ رَبِّكَۚ نَحۡنُ قَسَمۡنَا بَيۡنَهُم مَّعِيشَتَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَرَفَعۡنَا بَعۡضَهُمۡ فَوۡقَ بَعۡضٖ دَرَجَٰتٖ لِّيَتَّخِذَ بَعۡضُهُم بَعۡضٗا سُخۡرِيّٗاۗ وَرَحۡمَتُ رَبِّكَ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:31-32)

**"Why was this Quran not sent down upon a great man from one of the two cities?"** (Makkah and Ta'if). They judged prophethood by wealth and status. **"Do they distribute the mercy of your Lord? It is We who have apportioned among them their livelihood in the life of this world and have raised some of them above others in degrees, that they may make use of one another for service."** Differences in wealth exist so people can serve one another's needs, not as a measure of worth. **"But the mercy of your Lord is better than whatever they accumulate."**

﴿وَلَوۡلَآ أَن يَكُونَ ٱلنَّاسُ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ لَّجَعَلۡنَا لِمَن يَكۡفُرُ بِٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ لِبُيُوتِهِمۡ سُقُفٗا مِّن فِضَّةٖ وَمَعَارِجَ عَلَيۡهَا يَظۡهَرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلِبُيُوتِهِمۡ أَبۡوَٰبٗا وَسُرُرًا عَلَيۡهَا يَتَّكِـُٔونَ﴾ ﴿وَزُخۡرُفٗاۚ وَإِن كُلُّ ذَٰلِكَ لَمَّا مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ عِندَ رَبِّكَ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ (Quran 43:33-35)

**Roofs of silver and gold ornaments**: if it were not that people would all be tempted into disbelief, Allah would give the disbelievers houses with silver roofs, stairways, doors and couches, and gold. **"But all that is not but the enjoyment of worldly life. And the hereafter with your Lord is for the righteous."**

At-Tirmidhi reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "If this world were worth to Allah the wing of a mosquito, He would not give a disbeliever a sip of water from it." At-Tirmidhi graded it *sahih*.

## Verses 36–45: The Companion Devil
﴿وَمَن يَعۡشُ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ نُقَيِّضۡ لَهُۥ شَيۡطَٰنٗا فَهُوَ لَهُۥ قَرِينࣱ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَيَصُدُّونَهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَيَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَنَا قَالَ يَٰلَيۡتَ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكَ بُعۡدَ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقَيۡنِ فَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡقَرِينُ﴾ ﴿وَلَن يَنفَعَكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ إِذ ظَّلَمۡتُمۡ أَنَّكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ مُشۡتَرِكُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:36-39)

**"Whoever is blinded from the remembrance of the Most Merciful, We appoint for him a devil, and he is to him a companion."** Neglecting remembrance opens the door to Shaytan. These devils turn people from the path while they think they are guided. On the Day of Judgement, the person will say: **"Oh, I wish there was between me and you the distance between the east and west."**

﴿فَٱسۡتَمۡسِكۡ بِٱلَّذِيٓ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡكَۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَذِكۡرࣱ لَّكَ وَلِقَوۡمِكَۖ وَسَوۡفَ تُسۡـَٔلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَسۡـَٔلۡ مَنۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ مِن رُّسُلِنَآ أَجَعَلۡنَا مِن دُونِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ءَالِهَةٗ يُعۡبَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:43-45)

**"So adhere to that which is revealed to you. Indeed, you are on a straight path. And indeed, it is a remembrance for you and your people, and you are going to be questioned."**

## Verses 46–56: Pharaoh's Boasting
﴿وَنَادَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنُ فِي قَوۡمِهِۦ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَلَيۡسَ لِي مُلۡكُ مِصۡرَ وَهَٰذِهِ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِيٓۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ أَنَا۠ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي هُوَ مَهِينࣱ وَلَا يَكَادُ يُبِينُ﴾ ﴿فَلَوۡلَآ أُلۡقِيَ عَلَيۡهِ أَسۡوِرَةࣱ مِّن ذَهَبٍ أَوۡ جَآءَ مَعَهُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ مُقۡتَرِنِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱسۡتَخَفَّ قَوۡمَهُۥ فَأَطَاعُوهُۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 43:51-54)

**Pharaoh called out to his people: "O my people, does not the kingdom of Egypt belong to me, and these rivers flowing beneath me? Then do you not see? Or am I not better than this one who is insignificant and hardly makes himself clear?"** He measured worth by kingdom and rivers. **"Then why have there not been placed on him bracelets of gold or come with him the angels in conjunction?"**

**"So he bluffed his people, and they obeyed him. Indeed, they were a defiantly disobedient people."** Tyranny needs people willing to be fooled. **"So when they angered Us, We took retribution from them and drowned them all."**

## Verses 57–65: 'Isa, Son of Maryam
﴿۞وَلَمَّا ضُرِبَ ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ مَثَلًا إِذَا قَوۡمُكَ مِنۡهُ يَصِدُّونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُوٓاْ ءَأَٰلِهَتُنَا خَيۡرٌ أَمۡ هُوَۚ مَا ضَرَبُوهُ لَكَ إِلَّا جَدَلَۢاۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمٌ خَصِمُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا عَبۡدٌ أَنۡعَمۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ مَثَلٗا لِّبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ﴾ ﴿وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَجَعَلۡنَا مِنكُم مَّلَـٰٓئِكَةٗ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَخۡلُفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَعِلۡمࣱ لِّلسَّاعَةِ فَلَا تَمۡتَرُنَّ بِهَا وَٱتَّبِعُونِۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطࣱ مُّسۡتَقِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 43:57-61)

When 'Isa was mentioned, the idolaters argued: if the Christians' object of worship is in the Fire with ours, then let ours be. **"They did not present the comparison except for argument. But they are a people prone to dispute."**

**"He was not but a servant upon whom We bestowed favour"**: 'Isa is a servant of Allah, not divine. **"And indeed, he will be a sign for the Hour"**: his descent before the Day of Judgement is one of its major signs, confirmed in authentic hadith.

## Verses 66–73: Friends on That Day
﴿ٱلۡأَخِلَّآءُ يَوۡمَئِذِۭ بَعۡضُهُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ عَدُوٌّ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ (Quran 43:67)

**"Close friends, that Day, will be enemies to each other, except the righteous."** Every friendship built on sin, or on mere worldly interest, turns to enmity. Only friendships built on taqwa last.

﴿يَٰعِبَادِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ وَلَآ أَنتُمۡ تَحۡزَنُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا وَكَانُواْ مُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ أَنتُمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجُكُمۡ تُحۡبَرُونَ﴾ ﴿يُطَافُ عَلَيۡهِم بِصِحَافٖ مِّن ذَهَبٖ وَأَكۡوَابٖۖ وَفِيهَا مَا تَشۡتَهِيهِ ٱلۡأَنفُسُ وَتَلَذُّ ٱلۡأَعۡيُنُۖ وَأَنتُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡجَنَّةُ ٱلَّتِيٓ أُورِثۡتُمُوهَا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿لَكُمۡ فِيهَا فَٰكِهَةࣱ كَثِيرَةࣱ مِّنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:68-73)

**"O My servants, no fear will there be concerning you this Day, nor will you grieve."** **"Therein is whatever the souls desire and what delights the eyes, and you will abide therein eternally. And that is Paradise which you are made to inherit for what you used to do."**

## Verses 74–89: The People of the Fire and the Closing
﴿وَنَادَوۡاْ يَٰمَٰلِكُ لِيَقۡضِ عَلَيۡنَا رَبُّكَۖ قَالَ إِنَّكُم مَّـٰكِثُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:77)

**"They will call: O Malik, let your Lord put an end to us! He will say: Indeed, you will remain."** Malik is the keeper of Hell.

﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ إِلَٰهࣱ وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَٰهࣱۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ ﴿وَتَبَارَكَ ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا وَعِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:84-85)

**"It is He who is God in the heaven and God on the earth."** Worshipped in both.

﴿وَقِيلِهِۦ يَٰرَبِّ إِنَّ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ قَوۡمࣱ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿فَٱصۡفَحۡ عَنۡهُمۡ وَقُلۡ سَلَٰمࣱۚ فَسَوۡفَ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 43:88-89)

The Prophet ﷺ said: **"O my Lord, indeed these are a people who do not believe."** **"So turn aside from them and say: Peace. But they are going to know."**

## Word Meanings
- **Zukhruf**: gold ornaments.
- **Umm al-Kitab**: the Mother of the Book, the Preserved Tablet.

- **Munqalibun**: returning.
- **Ummah**: religion, way.
- **Muqtadun**: following.
- **Bari'**: disassociated.
- **Sukhriyya**: service.

- **Mahin**: insignificant.
- **Istakhaffa**: bluffed, made light of.
- **Al-Akhilla'**: close friends.
- **Makithun**: remaining.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The du'a of riding**: a daily act turned into remembrance of the final journey.
2. **"A great man from one of the two cities"**: the logic of status exposed.
3. **Roofs of silver**: the worthlessness of wealth shown by imagining it in excess.
4. **"Then do you not see?"**: Pharaoh's appeal to the eyes, while Allah appeals to the heart.
5. **"Friends will be enemies, except the righteous"**: a single exception that changes everything.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Izhar**: in *qur'anan 'arabiyyan* (verse 3), the tanween before the throat letter 'ayn is pronounced clearly.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *muqrinin* (verse 13), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *aba'ana* (verse 22) and *as-sama'i* (verse 84).
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna*, on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Ash-Shura before it ended with "Unquestionably, to Allah do all matters evolve", and Az-Zukhruf begins with the Quran in the Mother of the Book. Surah Ad-Dukhan after it opens with the Quran revealed in a blessed night.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does az-Zukhruf mean?
Gold ornaments; the surah shows that worldly glitter is worthless in Allah's sight.

### What is the du'a for riding in Surah Az-Zukhruf?
"Exalted is He who has subjected this to us, and we could not have otherwise subdued it. And indeed we, to our Lord, will surely return." The Prophet ﷺ said it when travelling, as in Sahih Muslim.

### What does the surah say about following forefathers?
It criticises blind imitation without evidence, even of parents.

### Why is wealth not a sign of Allah's love?
Because Allah would give disbelievers silver roofs and gold if not for the temptation to people; the world is worthless to Him.

### Who is the companion devil?
A devil assigned to whoever turns away from the remembrance of the Most Merciful.

### What does verse 67 teach about friendship?
Friends will be enemies on the Day of Judgement except those whose friendship was built on taqwa.

### How many verses are in Surah Az-Zukhruf?
89 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Az-Zukhruf strips the gold from this world and shows what lies beneath: a passing enjoyment that Allah would give even to disbelievers. It warns against blind imitation, against friendships without taqwa, and against leaders who measure worth by kingdoms and rivers. Remember Allah, hold fast to the revelation, and choose friends who will still be your friends on that Day.
