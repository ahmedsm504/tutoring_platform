Juz 15 begins with ﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَسۡرَىٰ بِعَبۡدِهِۦ لَيۡلٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ إِلَى ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡأَقۡصَا ٱلَّذِي بَٰرَكۡنَا حَوۡلَهُۥ﴾, "Glory be to the One who took His servant by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa, whose surroundings We have blessed", so it is called **Juz Subhan**, and it marks roughly the start of the second half of the Quran. It contains all of **Surah Al-Isra'** (111 verses) and **Surah Al-Kahf** from its start to verse **74**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 282 to 301**.

A lovely detail of the order: Al-Isra' begins with glorification (*subhan*) and Al-Kahf begins with praise (*al-hamd*), so glorification and praise meet in this juz. Both surahs are Makki and speak about the Quran and its guidance, and about the human being between honour and trial.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **the honoured human being and how he keeps his honour**. Al-Isra' announces ﴿وَلَقَدۡ كَرَّمۡنَا بَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ﴾, "We have honoured the children of Adam", then gives commands that preserve this honour: monotheism, devotion to parents, chastity, justice, knowledge and humility. Al-Kahf shows the trials that threaten it: the trial of faith in the youths of the cave, the trial of wealth in the owner of two gardens, and the trial of knowledge in the story of Musa and al-Khidr. The Quran is the way through: ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ يَهۡدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقۡوَمُ﴾, "This Quran guides to what is most upright."

## Part one: Surah Al-Isra' (1-111)

Al-Isra' is Makki and is also called **Surah Bani Isra'il** because it speaks of the Children of Israel at its start. In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Ibn Mas'ud said of Bani Isra'il, Al-Kahf, Maryam, Ta-Ha and Al-Anbiya': "They are among the earliest and finest, and they are among my oldest possessions", meaning among the first he learned. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Isra' Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-isra-meaning-english/).

### 1. The Night Journey and the corruption of the Children of Israel (1-8)
﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَسۡرَىٰ بِعَبۡدِهِۦ لَيۡلٗا﴾: describing the Prophet ﷺ as "His servant" at this most honoured moment shows that servanthood is his highest title. The Journey went from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa, followed by the Ascension through the heavens, as reported in Al-Bukhari and Muslim, and during it the five daily prayers were made obligatory.

The surah then turns to the Children of Israel: ﴿لَتُفۡسِدُنَّ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَّتَيۡنِ وَلَتَعۡلُنَّ عُلُوࣰّا كَبِيرࣰا﴾, "You will surely cause corruption on earth twice, and rise to great arrogance", then Allah's law: ﴿إِنۡ أَحۡسَنتُمۡ أَحۡسَنتُمۡ لِأَنفُسِكُمۡۖ وَإِنۡ أَسَأۡتُمۡ فَلَهَاۚ﴾, "If you do good, you do good for yourselves; and if you do evil, it is against yourselves", and ﴿وَإِنۡ عُدتُّمۡ عُدۡنَاۚ﴾, "and if you return, We will return."

**The link**: mentioning al-Masjid al-Aqsa at the start, then the corruption of those who had been there, signals that the trust of the message has passed to this nation.

### 2. The most upright guidance and human responsibility (9-21)
﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ يَهۡدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقۡوَمُ﴾: to the most upright way in everything. Then human nature: ﴿وَكَانَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ عَجُولٗا﴾, "and man is ever hasty." Then night and day as two signs. Then the record of deeds: ﴿وَكُلَّ إِنسَٰنٍ أَلۡزَمۡنَٰهُ طَـٰٓئِرَهُۥ فِي عُنُقِهِۦۖ وَنُخۡرِجُ لَهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ كِتَٰبٗا يَلۡقَىٰهُ مَنشُورًا﴾, "For every person We have fastened his fate to his neck, and on the Day of Resurrection We will bring out for him a record he will find spread open", ﴿ٱقۡرَأۡ كِتَٰبَكَ كَفَىٰ بِنَفۡسِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ عَلَيۡكَ حَسِيبٗا﴾, "Read your record; sufficient is yourself against you today as accountant."

Then two rules of divine justice: ﴿وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةࣱ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۗ﴾, "no bearer of burdens bears another's burden", and ﴿وَمَا كُنَّا مُعَذِّبِينَ حَتَّىٰ نَبۡعَثَ رَسُولٗا﴾, "We never punish until We have sent a messenger." Then the difference between those who want only the immediate and those who want the Hereafter and strive for it as believers.

### 3. The commandments of Al-Isra' (22-39)
This is the surah's greatest passage, a complete moral charter that begins and ends with monotheism:
1. **Monotheism**: "Do not set up another god with Allah."
2. **Devotion to parents**: ﴿۞وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنًاۚ﴾, "Your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and that you be good to parents", then: do not say "uff" to them or rebuke them, speak to them nobly, and ﴿وَٱخۡفِضۡ لَهُمَا جَنَاحَ ٱلذُّلِّ مِنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَةِ وَقُل رَّبِّ ٱرۡحَمۡهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرٗا﴾, "Lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy and say: My Lord, have mercy on them as they raised me when I was small."
3. **The rights of relatives, the poor and the traveller**, with a ban on waste: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُبَذِّرِينَ كَانُوٓاْ إِخۡوَٰنَ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِۖ﴾, "the wasteful are brothers of the devils."
4. **A gentle word** for someone you cannot give to.
5. **Moderation in spending**: ﴿وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ يَدَكَ مَغۡلُولَةً إِلَىٰ عُنُقِكَ وَلَا تَبۡسُطۡهَا كُلَّ ٱلۡبَسۡطِ﴾, "Do not chain your hand to your neck, nor extend it completely."
6. **Protecting children**: ﴿وَلَا تَقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَوۡلَٰدَكُمۡ خَشۡيَةَ إِمۡلَٰقٖۖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُهُمۡ وَإِيَّاكُمۡۚ﴾, "Do not kill your children for fear of poverty; We provide for them and for you."
7. **Chastity**: ﴿وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ ٱلزِّنَىٰٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فَٰحِشَةٗ وَسَآءَ سَبِيلٗا﴾, "Do not approach adultery; it is an indecency and an evil way." Forbidding even approaching it is stronger than forbidding the act.
8. **The sanctity of life.**
9. **The orphan's wealth and keeping promises.**
10. **Full measure and weight.**
11. **Certainty in knowledge**: ﴿وَلَا تَقۡفُ مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمٌۚ إِنَّ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡبَصَرَ وَٱلۡفُؤَادَ كُلُّ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ كَانَ عَنۡهُ مَسۡـُٔولٗا﴾, "Do not pursue what you have no knowledge of; hearing, sight and heart will all be questioned."
12. **Humility**: ﴿وَلَا تَمۡشِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَحًاۖ إِنَّكَ لَن تَخۡرِقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَن تَبۡلُغَ ٱلۡجِبَالَ طُولٗا﴾, "Do not walk on the earth arrogantly; you will never tear the earth apart or reach the mountains in height."

The close: "That is part of the wisdom your Lord has revealed to you; and do not set up another god with Allah."

**Notice**: the commandments begin and end with monotheism; all the character in between grows from it.

### 4. Cosmic glorification and resurrection (40-52)
﴿تُسَبِّحُ لَهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ ٱلسَّبۡعُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَن فِيهِنَّۚ وَإِن مِّن شَيۡءٍ إِلَّا يُسَبِّحُ بِحَمۡدِهِۦ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تَفۡقَهُونَ تَسۡبِيحَهُمۡۗ﴾, "The seven heavens, the earth and all within them glorify Him; there is nothing that does not glorify Him with praise, but you do not understand their glorification." Then the veil between the deniers and the Quran, and their denial of resurrection: "When we are bones and dust?" The answer: be stone or iron; the One who created you the first time.

### 5. The etiquette of speech and Shaytan's enmity (53-65)
﴿وَقُل لِّعِبَادِي يَقُولُواْ ٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ يَنزَغُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ﴾, "Tell My servants to say what is best; Shaytan sows discord among them." A good word shuts Shaytan's door between people. Then Iblis again: ﴿ءَأَسۡجُدُ لِمَنۡ خَلَقۡتَ طِينٗا﴾, "Shall I prostrate to one You created from clay?", then ﴿وَٱسۡتَفۡزِزۡ مَنِ ٱسۡتَطَعۡتَ مِنۡهُم بِصَوۡتِكَ وَأَجۡلِبۡ عَلَيۡهِم بِخَيۡلِكَ وَرَجِلِكَ﴾, "Incite whomever you can with your voice, and assault them with your cavalry and infantry", then ﴿إِنَّ عِبَادِي لَيۡسَ لَكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سُلۡطَٰنࣱۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِرَبِّكَ وَكِيلࣰا﴾, "Over My servants you have no authority, and sufficient is your Lord as Guardian."

### 6. The honour of the children of Adam (66-77)
The scene of ships at sea and how false gods vanish from people's minds in danger. Then: ﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ كَرَّمۡنَا بَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ وَحَمَلۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَرَزَقۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَفَضَّلۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ كَثِيرࣲ مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقۡنَا تَفۡضِيلࣰا﴾, "We have honoured the children of Adam, carried them on land and sea, provided them with good things, and preferred them over much of what We created." This honour is for every human being. Read [We Have Honoured the Children of Adam: 17:70](/blog/we-have-honoured-the-children-of-adam-english/). Then: the Day We call every people by their leader. Then the idolaters' attempts to draw the Prophet ﷺ away from some of the revelation, and Allah keeping him firm.

### 7. Prayer, and the Quran as healing (78-89)
﴿أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِدُلُوكِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ إِلَىٰ غَسَقِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَقُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِۖ إِنَّ قُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ كَانَ مَشۡهُودٗا﴾, "Establish prayer from the sun's decline to the darkness of night, and the recitation at dawn; the dawn recitation is ever witnessed." The verse gathers the times of the five prayers: from midday (Zuhr and 'Asr) to the dark of night (Maghrib and 'Isha'), then Fajr. In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, the angels of the night and of the day gather at the Fajr prayer. Then: ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَتَهَجَّدۡ بِهِۦ نَافِلَةٗ لَّكَ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَبۡعَثَكَ رَبُّكَ مَقَامٗا مَّحۡمُودٗا﴾, "And in part of the night, pray with it as an extra act for you; perhaps your Lord will raise you to a praised station." The praised station is the great intercession, as in Sahih Al-Bukhari.

Then the prayer of the Hijrah: ﴿وَقُل رَّبِّ أَدۡخِلۡنِي مُدۡخَلَ صِدۡقٖ وَأَخۡرِجۡنِي مُخۡرَجَ صِدۡقٖ وَٱجۡعَل لِّي مِن لَّدُنكَ سُلۡطَٰنٗا نَّصِيرٗا﴾, "Say: My Lord, let me enter a sound entrance and exit a sound exit, and grant me from Yourself a supporting authority", then ﴿وَقُلۡ جَآءَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَزَهَقَ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡبَٰطِلَ كَانَ زَهُوقٗا﴾, "And say: truth has come and falsehood has vanished; falsehood is bound to vanish." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ entered Makkah on the day of the conquest with three hundred and sixty idols around the House, and struck them while reciting this verse.

Then: ﴿وَنُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَا هُوَ شِفَآءࣱ وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَلَا يَزِيدُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا خَسَارٗا﴾, "We send down in the Quran what is healing and mercy for the believers, and it only increases the wrongdoers in loss." Read [The Quran Is Healing and Mercy: 17:82](/blog/ma-huwa-shifaun-wa-rahma-meaning-english/).

Then the question about the spirit: ﴿وَيَسۡـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلرُّوحِۖ قُلِ ٱلرُّوحُ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّي وَمَآ أُوتِيتُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا﴾, "They ask you about the spirit. Say: the spirit is from the affair of my Lord, and you have been given only a little knowledge." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, Jews asked the Prophet ﷺ about it and this was revealed. Then the total challenge: ﴿قُل لَّئِنِ ٱجۡتَمَعَتِ ٱلۡإِنسُ وَٱلۡجِنُّ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَأۡتُواْ بِمِثۡلِ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ لَا يَأۡتُونَ بِمِثۡلِهِۦ﴾, "Say: if mankind and jinn gathered to produce the like of this Quran, they could not produce its like."

### 8. The idolaters' demands and Musa's nine signs (90-104)
They demanded a spring from the earth, a garden, the sky falling, a house of gold, or climbing into the sky, and the answer came: ﴿قُلۡ سُبۡحَانَ رَبِّي هَلۡ كُنتُ إِلَّا بَشَرࣰا رَّسُولࣰا﴾, "Say: Glory be to my Lord! Am I anything but a human messenger?" Hizb 30 then begins with Allah's power to resurrect. Then the nine signs of Musa and the drowning of Pharaoh.

### 9. The humility of the learned, and the close (105-111)
﴿وَقُرۡءَانࣰا فَرَقۡنَٰهُ لِتَقۡرَأَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ عَلَىٰ مُكۡثࣲ﴾, "And a Quran which We divided so you might recite it to people at intervals": revealed in parts so it can be recited slowly. Then those given knowledge fall on their faces in prostration when it is recited: ﴿وَيَخِرُّونَ لِلۡأَذۡقَانِ يَبۡكُونَ وَيَزِيدُهُمۡ خُشُوعٗا﴾, "They fall on their faces weeping, and it increases them in humility": a place of prostration. Then: ﴿قُلِ ٱدۡعُواْ ٱللَّهَ أَوِ ٱدۡعُواْ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَۖ أَيّٗا مَّا تَدۡعُواْ فَلَهُ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ﴾, "Say: call upon Allah or call upon the Most Merciful; whichever you call, to Him belong the best names", and moderation in the loudness of prayer. The surah ends with praise: ﴿وَقُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَمۡ يَتَّخِذۡ وَلَدٗا﴾, "And say: praise be to Allah, who has not taken a son", and magnifying Him greatly.

**Reflect**: the surah began with "Glory be" and ends with "Praise be to Allah" and "magnify Him greatly".

## Part two: Surah Al-Kahf (1-74)

Al-Kahf is Makki, with 110 verses. In Sahih Muslim: "Whoever memorizes ten verses from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjal." Many scholars see the surah as revolving around four trials: of faith (the people of the cave), of wealth (the owner of two gardens), of knowledge (Musa and al-Khidr) and of power (Dhul-Qarnayn). This juz contains the first three; the fourth comes in Juz 16. For lessons from the whole surah see [Lessons from Surah Al-Kahf](/blog/surah-al-kahf-lessons-english/).

### 1. Praise for the upright Book (1-8)
﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَلَمۡ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ عِوَجَاۜ﴾, "Praise be to Allah who sent down the Book to His servant and placed no crookedness in it", straight, to warn of severe punishment and give good news to the believers. Then a warning against saying Allah has taken a son. Then comfort for the Prophet ﷺ: "Perhaps you would destroy yourself with grief over them." Then: ﴿إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَا مَا عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ زِينَةࣰ لَّهَا لِنَبۡلُوَهُمۡ أَيُّهُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلࣰا﴾, "We made what is on earth an adornment for it, to test which of them is best in deeds." This verse is the key to the surah: this world is adornment and test.

### 2. The people of the cave: the trial of faith (9-26)
﴿إِذۡ أَوَى ٱلۡفِتۡيَةُ إِلَى ٱلۡكَهۡفِ فَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةٗ وَهَيِّئۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَمۡرِنَا رَشَدٗا﴾, "When the youths retreated to the cave and said: Our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and prepare for us right guidance in our affair." Then: ﴿إِنَّهُمۡ فِتۡيَةٌ ءَامَنُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡ وَزِدۡنَٰهُمۡ هُدٗى﴾, "They were youths who believed in their Lord, and We increased them in guidance." The scenes:
- **Withdrawal**: they fled with their faith from people who took gods besides Allah.
- **The long sleep**: the sun inclined away from their cave on the right and passed them on the left, and their dog stretched its forelegs at the entrance.
- **The awakening**: they asked how long they had stayed, and sent one with their silver coins to buy food, telling him ﴿وَلۡيَتَلَطَّفۡ﴾, "and let him be gentle"; many scholars count this word as the midpoint of the Quran's letters.
- **The lesson**: Allah let people find them so they would know Allah's promise is true and the Hour is certain.
- **Their number**: three, five or seven; ﴿قُل رَّبِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ بِعِدَّتِهِم﴾, "Say: my Lord knows best their number." The Quran discourages arguing about what brings no benefit.
- **Their stay**: three hundred years, plus nine.

In the middle of the story comes a great etiquette: ﴿وَلَا تَقُولَنَّ لِشَاْيۡءٍ إِنِّي فَاعِلࣱ ذَٰلِكَ غَدًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ﴾, "Never say of anything: I will do that tomorrow, without adding: if Allah wills."

**Lesson**: believing youth may need to withdraw from a corrupt environment to protect their faith, and Allah takes care of those who flee to Him.

### 3. Keep yourself with the righteous (27-31)
﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ نَفۡسَكَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَدَوٰةِ وَٱلۡعَشِيِّ يُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَهُۥۖ وَلَا تَعۡدُ عَيۡنَاكَ عَنۡهُمۡ تُرِيدُ زِينَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ﴾, "Keep yourself patiently with those who call on their Lord morning and evening seeking His face, and let not your eyes pass beyond them, desiring the adornment of worldly life." Then: ﴿وَقُلِ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ فَمَن شَآءَ فَلۡيُؤۡمِن وَمَن شَآءَ فَلۡيَكۡفُرۡۚ﴾, "Say: the truth is from your Lord; whoever wills, let him believe, and whoever wills, let him disbelieve", then the two destinies.

**The link**: this verse connects the youths' story with that of the two gardens: righteous company protects against both the trial of faith and the trial of wealth.

### 4. The owner of two gardens: the trial of wealth (32-44)
Two men: one was given two gardens of grapes surrounded by palms with crops between them. He told his companion: I have more wealth than you and a stronger following, and he entered his garden wronging himself: I do not think this will ever perish, and I do not think the Hour will come. His believing companion replied: do you disbelieve in the One who created you from dust? Then the saving words: ﴿وَلَوۡلَآ إِذۡ دَخَلۡتَ جَنَّتَكَ قُلۡتَ مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "Why, when you entered your garden, did you not say: What Allah wills; there is no power except with Allah?" Then his fruit was destroyed, and he began wringing his hands, saying: ﴿يَٰلَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أُشۡرِكۡ بِرَبِّيٓ أَحَدٗا﴾, "I wish I had not associated anyone with my Lord."

**Lesson**: wealth is a blessing when attributed to Allah, and a trial when attributed to oneself. Say at every blessing: *ma sha' Allah, la quwwata illa billah*.

### 5. The parable of this world and the lasting good deeds (45-49)
﴿وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا كَمَآءٍ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَٱخۡتَلَطَ بِهِۦ نَبَاتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَأَصۡبَحَ هَشِيمࣰا تَذۡرُوهُ ٱلرِّيَٰحُۗ﴾, "Present to them the example of worldly life: like rain We send down from the sky, with which the plants of the earth mingle, then it becomes dry stalks scattered by the winds." Then: ﴿ٱلۡمَالُ وَٱلۡبَنُونَ زِينَةُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَٱلۡبَٰقِيَٰتُ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتُ خَيۡرٌ عِندَ رَبِّكَ ثَوَابٗا وَخَيۡرٌ أَمَلٗا﴾, "Wealth and children are the adornment of worldly life, but lasting good deeds are better with your Lord in reward and better in hope." Read [Wealth and Children Are Adornment: 18:46](/blog/al-malu-wal-banuna-zina-meaning-english/). Then the scene of the record: ﴿وَوُضِعَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ فَتَرَى ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ مُشۡفِقِينَ مِمَّا فِيهِ وَيَقُولُونَ يَٰوَيۡلَتَنَا مَالِ هَٰذَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا يُغَادِرُ صَغِيرَةࣰ وَلَا كَبِيرَةً إِلَّآ أَحۡصَىٰهَاۚ﴾, "The record will be placed, and you will see the criminals fearful of what is in it, saying: Woe to us! What is this record that leaves nothing small or great unrecorded?"

### 6. Iblis and human argumentativeness (50-59)
﴿وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِأٓدَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَفَسَقَ عَنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّهِۦٓۗ﴾, "When We told the angels to prostrate to Adam, they prostrated, except Iblis. He was of the jinn and departed from his Lord's command." This verse settles that Iblis was of the jinn, not the angels. Then: will you take him and his offspring as allies instead of Me, when they are your enemies? Then: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ صَرَّفۡنَا فِي هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ لِلنَّاسِ مِن كُلِّ مَثَلٖۚ وَكَانَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَكۡثَرَ شَيۡءٖ جَدَلٗا﴾, "We have set out in this Quran every kind of example for people, but man is the most argumentative of things."

### 7. Musa and al-Khidr: the trial of knowledge (60-74)
The story of Musa and the righteous servant begins. Its background is in Al-Bukhari and Muslim: Musa was asked who was most knowledgeable and said "I", so Allah reproached him for not attributing knowledge to Him and told him of a servant at the meeting of the two seas who knew more.
- **The journey**: ﴿لَآ أَبۡرَحُ حَتَّىٰٓ أَبۡلُغَ مَجۡمَعَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ أَوۡ أَمۡضِيَ حُقُبٗا﴾, "I will not stop until I reach the meeting of the two seas, even if I travel for ages." A seeker of knowledge endures hardship.
- **The fish**: it was the sign of the place, and the young man forgot it.
- **The meeting**: ﴿فَوَجَدَا عَبۡدٗا مِّنۡ عِبَادِنَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَا وَعَلَّمۡنَٰهُ مِن لَّدُنَّا عِلۡمٗا﴾, "They found a servant of Ours to whom We had given mercy from Us and taught knowledge from Our presence."
- **The student's manners**: ﴿هَلۡ أَتَّبِعُكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن تُعَلِّمَنِ مِمَّا عُلِّمۡتَ رُشۡدٗا﴾, "May I follow you so that you teach me some of the right guidance you have been taught?" A great prophet asks permission to follow.
- **The condition**: do not ask me about anything until I mention it to you.
- **The ship**: he made a hole in it, and Musa said: ﴿لَقَدۡ جِئۡتَ شَيۡـًٔا إِمۡرٗا﴾, "You have done a grave thing." He reminded him: ﴿أَلَمۡ أَقُلۡ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرٗا﴾, "Did I not say you would never be able to be patient with me?"
- **The boy**: he killed him, and Musa said: ﴿أَقَتَلۡتَ نَفۡسٗا زَكِيَّةَۢ بِغَيۡرِ نَفۡسٖ لَّقَدۡ جِئۡتَ شَيۡـٔٗا نُّكۡرٗا﴾, "Have you killed a pure soul who had killed no one? You have done a terrible thing." Here the juz ends.

Juz 16 completes the story with the wall and the explanation of the three acts.

**Lesson**: knowledge has no limit; above every knower is one more knowing; humility is a condition of learning; and what looks like harm may hide wisdom.

## Tajweed points in Juz 15

### The pause on ﴿عِوَجَاۜ﴾ in Al-Kahf 1
The first of Hafs's four places of *sakt*: a brief pause without breath on the alif, then ﴿قَيِّمٗا﴾. The small "s" sign above marks it.

### ﴿وَلۡيَتَلَطَّفۡ﴾
The doubled ta' is heavy, and the final sakin fa is pronounced clearly without echo, since it is not a qalqalah letter.

### ﴿لِشَاْيۡءٍ﴾ in Al-Kahf 23
Written with an extra alif after the sheen that is not pronounced.

### ﴿لَّٰكِنَّا۠ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ رَبِّي﴾ in Al-Kahf 38
The alif after the noon is dropped when continuing and pronounced when stopping.

### The sajdah of Al-Isra'
At ﴿وَيَزِيدُهُمۡ خُشُوعٗا﴾, verse 109. For more on special signs see [A Roadmap for Learning Tajweed](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. "We have diversified in this Quran"
- Al-Isra' 41: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ صَرَّفۡنَا فِي هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ لِيَذَّكَّرُواْ﴾.
- Al-Isra' 89: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ صَرَّفۡنَا لِلنَّاسِ فِي هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مِن كُلِّ مَثَلٖ﴾, with "for people" before "in this Quran".
- Al-Kahf 54: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ صَرَّفۡنَا فِي هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ لِلنَّاسِ مِن كُلِّ مَثَلٖۚ﴾, with "for people" after.

### 2. "Did I not say that you..."
- Al-Kahf 72: ﴿قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُلۡ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرٗا﴾.
- Al-Kahf 75 (start of Juz 16): ﴿قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكَ إِنَّكَ﴾, adding "to you" because the objection was repeated, so the emphasis grew.

### 3. *Imra* and *nukra*
- The ship: ﴿شَيۡـًٔا إِمۡرٗا﴾, "a grave thing."
- The boy: ﴿شَيۡـٔٗا نُّكۡرٗا﴾, "a terrible thing", stronger, since killing is worse than damaging a ship.

### 4. Iblis in Al-Isra' and Al-Kahf
- Al-Isra' 61: ﴿ءَأَسۡجُدُ لِمَنۡ خَلَقۡتَ طِينٗا﴾.
- Al-Kahf 50: ﴿كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَفَسَقَ عَنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّهِۦٓۗ﴾.

## How to memorize Juz 15 with understanding

1. **Al-Isra' is five blocks**: the Journey, the commandments, Shaytan, honour and prayer, the demands and the close.
2. **Count the commandments** on your fingers: twelve.
3. **Al-Kahf is stories**: memorize each story as a unit: the youths, the gardens, Musa and al-Khidr.
4. **Begin with the first ten verses of Al-Kahf** for their virtue, then continue.
5. **Read Al-Kahf on Fridays** once you have memorized it; it strengthens retention.

For a dedicated plan see [How to Memorize Surah Al-Kahf](/blog/memorize-surah-al-kahf-english/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 15

- **Take pride in being Allah's servant**; it is the noblest title.
- **Read your record before you are told to read it.**
- **Never say "uff" to your parents**, and pray for them.
- **Be moderate in spending** and never wasteful.
- **Do not approach adultery** or its paths.
- **Do not speak without knowledge**; hearing, sight and heart will be questioned.
- **Be humble in walk and speech.**
- **Say what is best** to shut Shaytan's door.
- **Guard the Fajr prayer**; it is witnessed.
- **Pray at night**, even a little.
- **Let the Quran be your healing.**
- **Say "in sha' Allah"** for whatever you intend.
- **Keep yourself with the righteous.**
- **Say "ma sha' Allah, la quwwata illa billah"** at every blessing.
- **Be humble in seeking knowledge** and patient with your teacher.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 15 called "Subhan"?
Because it begins with ﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَسۡرَىٰ بِعَبۡدِهِۦ﴾.

### What is the virtue of the start of Al-Kahf?
In Sahih Muslim: "Whoever memorizes ten verses from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjal."

### Where does Juz 15 end?
At Al-Kahf 18:74. Juz 16 begins with ﴿۞قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكَ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرٗا﴾.

### Was Iblis an angel?
No. The Quran states in Al-Kahf 50: ﴿كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ﴾, "he was of the jinn."

### How many prostrations are in this juz?
One, in Surah Al-Isra', verse 109.

## Conclusion

Juz 15 speaks to you about your honour and how to keep it. Al-Isra' announces the honour of the children of Adam and lays down commands that protect it, making the Quran a healing and prayer a bond. Al-Kahf shows the trials of faith, wealth and knowledge and points to the way out: righteous company, attributing blessings to Allah, and humility in learning.
