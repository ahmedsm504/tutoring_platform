﴿أَوۡ زِدۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا﴾ [Al-Muzzammil 73:4]

## The context
- It comes at the start of Al-Muzzammil, in the command to pray at night: half of it, a little less, or more.

## What is tartil?
- In language: arranging something in a measured, orderly way. In recitation: reading slowly while giving each letter its right.

## A famous definition
- It is reported from ʿAli that tartil is **perfecting the letters and knowing the stops**.
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Perfecting the letters | Articulation, qualities and rules |
| Knowing the stops | Where to pause and where to begin |

## The Prophet's recitation
- Anas was asked about it and said it was drawn out; he recited *Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim*, lengthening each word (narrated by al-Bukhari).

## Lessons
- **Do not rush** your recitation.
- **Perfect articulation and stops** with a teacher.
