## "He Created You from a Single Soul": Quran 4:1 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفۡسٖ وَٰحِدَةٖ وَخَلَقَ مِنۡهَا زَوۡجَهَا وَبَثَّ مِنۡهُمَا رِجَالٗا كَثِيرٗا وَنِسَآءٗۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي تَسَآءَلُونَ بِهِۦ وَٱلۡأَرۡحَامَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ رَقِيبٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:1]

### Addressed to all people
- *O mankind*: the surah about rights begins by reminding everyone of their **shared origin**.

### One origin, one family
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *A single soul* | Adam |
| *Its mate* | Hawwa |
| *Spread from them many men and women* | All humanity descends from them |

- No race, tribe or class is superior by origin.

### Taqwa twice
- The verse commands taqwa **twice**: first of *your Lord who created you*, then of *Allah by whom you ask one another*. People used to say "I ask you by Allah" when requesting something.

### The wombs
- *And the wombs*: fear Allah regarding family ties. Cutting relatives is a serious sin; keeping ties is a cause of blessing.

### "Ever-Watchful"
- *Raqib*: He sees how you treat your spouse, parents, children and relatives.

### Why at the start of An-Nisa?
- The surah goes on to orphans, women, inheritance and family. This verse is the foundation: **all are family under one Lord**.

### Lessons
- **Treat every human with dignity.**
- **Call a relative today.**

## Surah An-Nisa Meaning: Rights, Justice and Care for the Vulnerable

**Surah An-Nisa** (The Women, 176 ayat) builds a society on justice and care for the weak.

### One origin
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفۡسٖ وَٰحِدَةٖ وَخَلَقَ مِنۡهَا زَوۡجَهَا وَبَثَّ مِنۡهُمَا رِجَالٗا كَثِيرٗا وَنِسَآءٗۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي تَسَآءَلُونَ بِهِۦ وَٱلۡأَرۡحَامَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ رَقِيبٗا﴾

### Rights protected
| Group | Protection |
|---|---|
| Orphans | Their wealth guarded; consuming it unjustly is like eating fire |
| Women | The mahr as their right, and kind treatment |
| Heirs | Detailed inheritance shares set by Allah |
| Relatives, neighbours and travellers | Kindness commanded alongside worship |

### Standing for justice
﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُواْ قَوَّـٰمِينَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ شُهَدَآءَ لِلَّهِ وَلَوۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَوِ ٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ وَٱلۡأَقۡرَبِينَۚ إِن يَكُنۡ غَنِيًّا أَوۡ فَقِيرٗا فَٱللَّهُ أَوۡلَىٰ بِهِمَاۖ فَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡهَوَىٰٓ أَن تَعۡدِلُواْۚ وَإِن تَلۡوُۥٓاْ أَوۡ تُعۡرِضُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٗا﴾

### Hope for sinners
- Allah does not forgive associating partners with Him, but forgives what is less than that for whom He wills.

### ʿIsa
- The surah states that ʿIsa was neither killed nor crucified, but it was made to appear so, and Allah raised him to Himself.

### Lessons
- **Justice starts with yourself and your family.**
- **The weak have rights** that Allah Himself guards.

## "Live with Them in Kindness": Quran 4:19 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا يَحِلُّ لَكُمۡ أَن تَرِثُواْ ٱلنِّسَآءَ كَرۡهٗاۖ وَلَا تَعۡضُلُوهُنَّ لِتَذۡهَبُواْ بِبَعۡضِ مَآ ءَاتَيۡتُمُوهُنَّ إِلَّآ أَن يَأۡتِينَ بِفَٰحِشَةٖ مُّبَيِّنَةٖۚ وَعَاشِرُوهُنَّ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۚ فَإِن كَرِهۡتُمُوهُنَّ فَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تَكۡرَهُواْ شَيۡـٔٗا وَيَجۡعَلَ ٱللَّهُ فِيهِ خَيۡرٗا كَثِيرٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:19]

### What it abolished
| Pre-Islamic practice | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Inheriting a widow as property | Forbidden (narrated by al-Bukhari from Ibn ʿAbbas) |
| Pressuring a wife to give up her dowry | Forbidden |

### Kindness (*maʿruf*)
- What the Shariʿah and decent custom recognise as good: kind words, provision, fairness and good character.

### If you dislike something
- *Perhaps you dislike a thing and Allah places much good in it*: a righteous child, or affection later.
- *A believing man should not hate a believing woman; if he dislikes one of her traits, he will be pleased with another* (narrated by Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Look at the good**, not only the flaws.
- **Kindness** is the standard at home.

## "Allah Wants to Lighten Your Burden, and Man Was Created Weak": Quran 4:28 Explained

﴿يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ أَن يُخَفِّفَ عَنكُمۡۚ وَخُلِقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ ضَعِيفٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:28]

### Allah wants ease
- The verse comes after rulings about marriage, where Allah allowed options for those unable to afford certain marriages. Then: *Allah wants to lighten for you.*

### Created weak
| Weakness | Mercy in response |
|---|---|
| Physical | Concessions in illness and travel |
| Desires | Lawful marriage as an outlet |
| Patience | Forgiveness and repentance always open |

### A balanced self-view
- Knowing you are weak protects you from arrogance, and knowing Allah wants ease protects you from despair.

### Three wills of Allah
- The surrounding verses mention three things Allah wants: to **make clear** to you, to **accept your repentance**, and to **lighten** your burden (4:26-28).

### Lessons
- **Take the concessions** Allah gave.
- **Ask Allah for strength**, admitting your weakness.

## "Do Not Consume One Another's Wealth Unjustly": Quran 4:29 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَأۡكُلُوٓاْ أَمۡوَٰلَكُم بَيۡنَكُم بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ إِلَّآ أَن تَكُونَ تِجَٰرَةً عَن تَرَاضٖ مِّنكُمۡۚ وَلَا تَقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِكُمۡ رَحِيمٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:29]

### "Your wealth"
- It says *your wealth*, not others': your brother's wealth should be guarded like your own.

### Forms of falsehood
| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| Cheating | Hiding a defect |
| Riba | Forbidden interest |
| Bribery | Paying to cancel a right |
| Gambling | Gain without real exchange |

### Mutual consent
- Lawful trade rests on the consent of both sides, without force or deception.

### "Do not kill yourselves"
- Scholars explain: do not kill one another, do not take your own life, and do not destroy yourselves with sins.

### Allah's mercy
- *Indeed Allah is ever Merciful to you*: these rules are mercy.

### Lessons
- **Do not sell** with a hidden defect.
- **Be content and make others content** in every deal.

## "Worship Allah and Be Good to Parents, Relatives and Neighbours": Quran 4:36 Explained

﴿۞وَٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَلَا تُشۡرِكُواْ بِهِۦ شَيۡـٔٗاۖ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنٗا وَبِذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱلۡجَارِ ذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡجَارِ ٱلۡجُنُبِ وَٱلصَّاحِبِ بِٱلۡجَنۢبِ وَٱبۡنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَمَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ مَن كَانَ مُخۡتَالٗا فَخُورًا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:36]

### The ten rights
| # | Right |
|---|---|
| 1 | Allah: worship without partners |
| 2 | Parents |
| 3 | Relatives |
| 4 | Orphans |
| 5 | The poor |
| 6 | The near neighbour |
| 7 | The distant neighbour |
| 8 | The companion at your side |
| 9 | The traveller |
| 10 | Those under your authority |

### Neighbours
- Scholars explain the *near neighbour* as the one close in kinship or location, and the *distant neighbour* as the stranger or the one farther away.
- Jibril kept advising the Prophet ﷺ about the neighbour until he thought he would make him an heir (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### The companion at your side
- Explained as a travel companion, a colleague, a spouse, or anyone who sits beside you.

### The ending
- *Allah does not love the arrogant and boastful*: arrogance destroys these rights.

### Lessons
- **Check your list**: which right have you neglected?
- **Start with your neighbour today.**

## "Allah Does Not Forgive Association with Him, But Forgives Less Than That": Quran 4:48 Explained

﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَغۡفِرُ أَن يُشۡرَكَ بِهِۦ وَيَغۡفِرُ مَا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَن يُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰٓ إِثۡمًا عَظِيمًا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:48]

### The meaning
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Does not forgive shirk | For the one who dies upon it |
| Forgives what is less | Other sins are under His will |
| A great sin invented | Shirk is the greatest lie against Allah |

### With Az-Zumar 39:53
- *Allah forgives all sins* (39:53) is about **those who repent**, even from shirk. Al-Nisa 4:48 is about the one who **dies** without repentance.

### Hope for the believer
- A believer in tawhid who dies with sins unrepented is under Allah's will: He may forgive or punish, but he will not remain in the Fire forever.

### Why is shirk the gravest?
- It gives Allah's exclusive right to others and equates creation with the Creator.

### Lessons
- **Guard tawhid** above everything.
- **Repent now**: repentance erases even shirk.

## "Render Trusts to Those Entitled to Them": Quran 4:58 Explained

﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُكُمۡ أَن تُؤَدُّواْ ٱلۡأَمَٰنَٰتِ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهَا وَإِذَا حَكَمۡتُم بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ أَن تَحۡكُمُواْ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ نِعِمَّا يَعِظُكُم بِهِۦٓۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ سَمِيعَۢا بَصِيرٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:58]

### Two commands
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Return trusts | Give back what was entrusted to you, fully |
| Judge with justice | Decide between people fairly |

### The key of the Kaʿbah
- Many scholars mention that after the conquest of Makkah, the Prophet ﷺ took the key of the Kaʿbah, then returned it to ʿUthman ibn Talhah, its keeper, reciting this verse.

### Forms of amanah
| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| Money | Deposits, loans, inheritance |
| Words | Secrets, advice, testimony |
| Work | Doing the job you are paid for |
| Positions | Giving roles to the qualified |
| Family | Raising children well |

### Amanah and faith
- The Prophet ﷺ said: *There is no faith for the one who has no trustworthiness* (narrated by Ahmad).

### "Excellent is what He instructs you"
- Allah praises His own advice, encouraging people to accept it, and closes with *All-Hearing, All-Seeing*: He hears your words and sees your actions.

### Lessons
- **Return what is not yours**, even small things.
- **Give positions to the qualified**, not to friends.

## "Obey Allah and Obey the Messenger": Quran 4:59 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِ مِنكُمۡۖ فَإِن تَنَٰزَعۡتُمۡ فِي شَيۡءٖ فَرُدُّوهُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَٱلرَّسُولِ إِن كُنتُمۡ تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۚ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَحۡسَنُ تَأۡوِيلًا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:59]

### A careful wording
| Phrase | What scholars note |
|---|---|
| *Obey Allah* | Absolute obedience |
| *And obey the Messenger* | The word "obey" is **repeated**: the Sunnah is followed independently |
| *And those in authority among you* | "Obey" is **not repeated**: their obedience follows obedience to Allah and His Messenger |

### The limit
- The Prophet ﷺ said: **"There is no obedience in disobedience to Allah; obedience is only in what is right"** (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Who are "those in authority"?
- Scholars mention rulers and also **scholars**, each in their field.

### When people disagree
- *Refer it to Allah and the Messenger*: to the Quran, and to the Sunnah after his death. This is a condition of faith: *if you believe in Allah and the Last Day*.

### Lessons
- **The Sunnah is a source of guidance** alongside the Quran.
- **Resolve disputes with evidence**, not personal opinion.

## "With Those Allah Has Blessed: Prophets, the Truthful, Martyrs and the Righteous": Quran 4:69 Explained

﴿وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلرَّسُولَ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَ وَٱلصِّدِّيقِينَ وَٱلشُّهَدَآءِ وَٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَۚ وَحَسُنَ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ رَفِيقٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:69]

### Four ranks
| Rank | Description |
|---|---|
| Prophets | The highest |
| The truthful (*siddiqin*) | Complete in faith and truth |
| Martyrs | Those killed in Allah's cause and those who bear witness to truth |
| The righteous | Those whose deeds are sound |

### Link to Al-Fatihah
- These are the people we ask to follow in every rakʿah: *the path of those You have blessed* (1:7).

### Excellent companions
- *And excellent are they as companions.*

### A person is with whom he loves
- A man asked about the Hour; he had not prepared much, but loved Allah and His Messenger. The Prophet ﷺ said: *You will be with those you love* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Obedience** is the road to their company.
- **Love the righteous** and follow them.

## "When You Are Greeted, Greet with Better or Return It": Quran 4:86 Explained

﴿وَإِذَا حُيِّيتُم بِتَحِيَّةٖ فَحَيُّواْ بِأَحۡسَنَ مِنۡهَآ أَوۡ رُدُّوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ حَسِيبًا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:86]

### Two levels
| Level | Example |
|---|---|
| Better | *As-salamu ʿalaykum* → *Wa ʿalaykum as-salam wa rahmatullah* |
| Equal | *Wa ʿalaykum as-salam* |

### The ruling
- Scholars: starting salam is Sunnah; returning it is obligatory.

### Levels of reward
- A man said *as-salamu ʿalaykum* and the Prophet ﷺ said *ten*; another added *wa rahmatullah* and he said *twenty*; another added *wa barakatuh* and he said *thirty* (narrated by Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi).

### "Ever an Accountant"
- Allah takes account of everything, even greetings.

### Spreading salam
- *Shall I not tell you of something that, if you do it, you will love one another? Spread salam among you* (narrated by Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Start with salam** and add when replying.
- **Teach children** the complete greeting.

## "Prayer Is Prescribed for the Believers at Fixed Times": Quran 4:103 Explained

﴿فَإِذَا قَضَيۡتُمُ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ فَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ قِيَٰمٗا وَقُعُودٗا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِكُمۡۚ فَإِذَا ٱطۡمَأۡنَنتُمۡ فَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۚ إِنَّ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ كَانَتۡ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ كِتَٰبٗا مَّوۡقُوتٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:103]

### The context
- It comes right after the verse on **the prayer of fear** during battle: even in war, prayer was not dropped.

### *Kitaban mawquta*
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Kitaban* | A binding obligation |
| *Mawquta* | With specified times |

### Dhikr after prayer
- When prayer ends, remembrance continues, standing, sitting and lying down.

### Praying on time
- The Prophet ﷺ was asked which deed is most beloved to Allah. He said: *Prayer at its time* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Prayer is never dropped** because of busyness.
- **Plan your day** around prayer times.

## "Whoever Does Wrong Then Seeks Forgiveness Will Find Allah Forgiving": Quran 4:110 Explained

﴿وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ سُوٓءًا أَوۡ يَظۡلِمۡ نَفۡسَهُۥ ثُمَّ يَسۡتَغۡفِرِ ٱللَّهَ يَجِدِ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:110]

### Two kinds of wrong
| Phrase | Explained by some scholars as |
|---|---|
| *Does evil* | Wrong that harms others |
| *Wrongs himself* | Sin that harms only oneself |

### "Then seeks forgiveness"
- Whatever the sin, the next step is the same: **istighfar**.

### "Will find Allah"
- The verse does not just say Allah forgives; it says he **will find** Him forgiving: present, waiting, near.

### The context
- It comes in a passage about a theft and a false accusation, showing that even after such wrongs, the door of forgiveness is open.

### Forgiveness and rights
- If others were wronged, repentance includes returning their rights.

### Lessons
- **Seek forgiveness immediately** after a mistake.
- **Expect to find Allah forgiving.**

## "No Good in Much of Their Private Talk, Except...": Quran 4:114 Explained

﴿۞لَّا خَيۡرَ فِي كَثِيرٖ مِّن نَّجۡوَىٰهُمۡ إِلَّا مَنۡ أَمَرَ بِصَدَقَةٍ أَوۡ مَعۡرُوفٍ أَوۡ إِصۡلَٰحِۭ بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِۚ وَمَن يَفۡعَلۡ ذَٰلِكَ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ مَرۡضَاتِ ٱللَّهِ فَسَوۡفَ نُؤۡتِيهِ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:114]

### *Najwa*
- Secret conversation between two or more.

### The rule
- Most private talk has no good: gossip, idle talk, or plotting.

### Three exceptions
| Exception | Example |
|---|---|
| Encouraging charity | Urging someone to help a needy person |
| Encouraging good | Private sincere advice |
| Reconciling people | Bringing disputing people together |

### Reconciliation
- *Shall I not tell you of something better in rank than fasting, prayer and charity? Reconciling between people* (narrated by Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi).

### Sincerity
- *Seeking the pleasure of Allah*: the great reward is for the sincere.

### Lessons
- **Review your private chats**: is there good in them?
- **Be a peacemaker** in private before public.

## "Stand Firm for Justice, Even Against Yourselves": Quran 4:135 Explained

﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُواْ قَوَّـٰمِينَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ شُهَدَآءَ لِلَّهِ وَلَوۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَوِ ٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ وَٱلۡأَقۡرَبِينَۚ إِن يَكُنۡ غَنِيًّا أَوۡ فَقِيرٗا فَٱللَّهُ أَوۡلَىٰ بِهِمَاۖ فَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡهَوَىٰٓ أَن تَعۡدِلُواْۚ وَإِن تَلۡوُۥٓاْ أَوۡ تُعۡرِضُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:135]

### "Persistently" just
- The word *qawwamin* is an intensive form: not just occasionally fair, but **constantly** upholding justice.

### Against whom?
| Case | The challenge |
|---|---|
| Yourself | Admitting your own fault |
| Parents | Love and respect pull you toward bias |
| Relatives | Family loyalty |
| Rich | Fear or hope of benefit |
| Poor | Pity that tempts you to favour them |

- *Allah is more worthy of both*, so neither wealth nor poverty changes the truth.

### Three warnings
1. **Do not follow desire** so that you are unjust.
2. **Do not distort** testimony.
3. **Do not withhold** it when needed.

### Compare with Al-Maidah 5:8
- There the command is to be just even with **enemies**. Together the verses close every door to bias: family on one side, enemies on the other.

### Lessons
- **Justice is worship**: you testify *for Allah*, not for people.
- **Start with yourself.**

## "What Would Allah Do with Your Punishment If You Are Grateful and Believe?": Quran 4:147 Explained

﴿مَّا يَفۡعَلُ ٱللَّهُ بِعَذَابِكُمۡ إِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ وَءَامَنتُمۡۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ شَاكِرًا عَلِيمٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:147]

### A question full of mercy
- Allah gains nothing from punishing anyone. If you are grateful and believe, why would He punish you?

### Why gratitude first?
- Some scholars explained: a person first sees blessings and thanks the Giver, and gratitude leads him to faith. Others said the order does not indicate sequence.

### Two names
| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Ash-Shakir* | Rewards a small deed with great reward |
| *Al-ʿAlim* | Knows every deed and its value |

### The context
- It comes after describing the hypocrites, and the exception for those who repent, reform and are sincere: **the door stays open**.

### Lessons
- **Gratitude and faith** are protection.
- **Allah appreciates** your small deeds, so never belittle them.
