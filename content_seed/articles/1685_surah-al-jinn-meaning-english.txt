**Surah Al-Jinn** (28 ayat) tells us how another kind of creation reacted to the Quran.

﴿قُلۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ أَنَّهُ ٱسۡتَمَعَ نَفَرࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا سَمِعۡنَا قُرۡءَانًا عَجَبٗا﴾ ﴿يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلرُّشۡدِ فَـَٔامَنَّا بِهِۦۖ وَلَن نُّشۡرِكَ بِرَبِّنَآ أَحَدٗا﴾
- *We have heard an amazing Quran. It guides to the right way, so we believed in it.*

## What the jinn said
| Their words | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Our Lord has no wife or child | Pure monotheism |
| Some fools among us spoke lies about Allah | Admitting past error |
| We used to sit in the heavens to listen; now we find guards | The sky was guarded at the coming of the Prophet ﷺ |
| Among us are Muslims and others | Jinn are accountable like humans |

## The mosques belong to Allah
﴿وَأَنَّ ٱلۡمَسَٰجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدۡعُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدٗا﴾ — *The mosques are for Allah, so do not invoke anyone along with Allah.*

## The unseen
- Allah alone is the Knower of the unseen, and He reveals of it only what He wills to a messenger He chooses.
- **So**: don't believe fortune-tellers or anyone claiming to know the future.

## Lessons
- **Listen to the Quran with your heart**, as the jinn did.
- **No need to fear** the jinn excessively: the morning and evening remembrances protect you.
