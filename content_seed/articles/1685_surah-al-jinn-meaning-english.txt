## Surah Al-Jinn Meaning: When the Jinn Heard the Quran

**Surah Al-Jinn** (28 ayat) tells us how another kind of creation reacted to the Quran.

﴿قُلۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ أَنَّهُ ٱسۡتَمَعَ نَفَرࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا سَمِعۡنَا قُرۡءَانًا عَجَبٗا﴾ ﴿يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلرُّشۡدِ فَـَٔامَنَّا بِهِۦۖ وَلَن نُّشۡرِكَ بِرَبِّنَآ أَحَدٗا﴾
- *We have heard an amazing Quran. It guides to the right way, so we believed in it.*

### What the jinn said
| Their words | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Our Lord has no wife or child | Pure monotheism |
| Some fools among us spoke lies about Allah | Admitting past error |
| We used to sit in the heavens to listen; now we find guards | The sky was guarded at the coming of the Prophet ﷺ |
| Among us are Muslims and others | Jinn are accountable like humans |

### The mosques belong to Allah
﴿وَأَنَّ ٱلۡمَسَٰجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدۡعُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدٗا﴾ — *The mosques are for Allah, so do not invoke anyone along with Allah.*

### The unseen
- Allah alone is the Knower of the unseen, and He reveals of it only what He wills to a messenger He chooses.
- **So**: don't believe fortune-tellers or anyone claiming to know the future.

### Lessons
- **Listen to the Quran with your heart**, as the jinn did.
- **No need to fear** the jinn excessively: the morning and evening remembrances protect you.

## Surah Al-Muzzammil Meaning: Stand at Night and Recite with Tarteel

**Surah Al-Muzzammil** (20 ayat) is the surah of **night prayer and tarteel**.

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُزَّمِّلُ﴾ ﴿قُمِ ٱلَّيۡلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا﴾ ﴿نِّصۡفَهُۥٓ أَوِ ٱنقُصۡ مِنۡهُ قَلِيلًا﴾ ﴿أَوۡ زِدۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا﴾
- *O you wrapped in garments, stand at night except a little, half of it or a little less, or a little more, and recite the Quran with tarteel.*

### Why the night?
| Reason | From the surah |
|---|---|
| Preparation | *We will cast upon you a heavy word* |
| Focus | The night is stronger in impact and clearer in speech |
| Balance | In the day you have long work |

### Patience
- *Be patient over what they say, and leave them gracefully.*

### The ayah of ease
﴿فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ﴾ [Al-Muzzammil: 20]
- Revealed later, it recognised that people would be **ill, travelling for work, or striving in Allah's cause**, and made night prayer easier.

### Lessons
- **A little night prayer** is better than none.
- **Tarteel**: recite slowly, clearly, with reflection.

## "And Recite the Quran with Measured Recitation": Quran 73:4 Explained

﴿أَوۡ زِدۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا﴾ [Al-Muzzammil 73:4]

### The context
- It comes at the start of Al-Muzzammil, in the command to pray at night: half of it, a little less, or more.

### What is tartil?
- In language: arranging something in a measured, orderly way. In recitation: reading slowly while giving each letter its right.

### A famous definition
- It is reported from ʿAli that tartil is **perfecting the letters and knowing the stops**.
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Perfecting the letters | Articulation, qualities and rules |
| Knowing the stops | Where to pause and where to begin |

### The Prophet's recitation
- Anas was asked about it and said it was drawn out; he recited *Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim*, lengthening each word (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### Lessons
- **Do not rush** your recitation.
- **Perfect articulation and stops** with a teacher.

## "So Recite What Is Easy of the Quran": Quran 73:20 Explained

﴿فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ﴾ [Al-Muzzammil 73:20]

### The easing
- The beginning of the surah commanded long night prayer. Its end brought ease: *He knew you would not be able to keep count of it, so He turned to you in mercy.*

### Three excuses
| Excuse | In the verse |
|---|---|
| Illness | *Some of you will be ill* |
| Earning a living | *Others travel the land seeking Allah's bounty* |
| Striving | *Others fight in Allah's cause* |

- Note: earning a lawful living is placed **beside** striving in Allah's cause.

### Repeated twice
- *Recite what is easy* appears twice in the verse: never cut off from the Quran, even with a little.

### The ending
- Prayer, zakah, a goodly loan to Allah, and seeking forgiveness.

### Lessons
- **Keep a fixed portion**, even one page.
- **Small and lasting** beats big and broken.

## Surah Al-Muddaththir Meaning: Arise and Warn

**Surah Al-Muddaththir** (56 ayat) contains some of the first commands given to the Prophet ﷺ when revelation resumed after a pause [Al-Bukhari].

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُدَّثِّرُ﴾ ﴿قُمۡ فَأَنذِرۡ﴾ ﴿وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرۡ﴾ ﴿وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرۡ﴾ ﴿وَٱلرُّجۡزَ فَٱهۡجُرۡ﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَمۡنُن تَسۡتَكۡثِرُ﴾ ﴿وَلِرَبِّكَ فَٱصۡبِرۡ﴾

### Six commands
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Arise and warn | Begin the mission |
| Magnify your Lord | Allāhu akbar in heart and action |
| Purify your garments | Physical and moral purity |
| Avoid idols | Shun all impurity |
| Don't give to gain more | Give without expecting return |
| Be patient for your Lord | The road will be hard |

### The man who called it magic
- The surah describes a man who thought, measured, frowned, then said: *This is nothing but magic imitated*. Many commentators named him al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah.

### Saqar and the nineteen
- *Over it are nineteen*: the keepers of Hell, a test for those who doubt.

### Every soul is held
﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسِۭ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ رَهِينَةٌ﴾ — *Every soul, for what it has earned, is held in pledge.*

### Lessons
- **Start with yourself**: purify, magnify Allah, then call others.

## Surah Al-Qiyamah Meaning: The Self-Reproaching Soul and the Promise to Preserve the Quran

**Surah Al-Qiyamah** (40 ayat) speaks to the conscience and to every student of the Quran.

﴿لَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ﴾ ﴿وَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلنَّفۡسِ ٱللَّوَّامَةِ﴾

### The self-reproaching soul
- **An-nafs al-lawwāmah**: the soul that **blames itself** after a mistake. A sign of a living conscience.

### Even the fingertips
- *Does man think We will not assemble his bones? Yes, We are able to proportion his fingertips.*

### Don't rush with the Quran
﴿لَا تُحَرِّكۡ بِهِۦ لِسَانَكَ لِتَعۡجَلَ بِهِۦٓ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا جَمۡعَهُۥ وَقُرۡءَانَهُۥ﴾ ﴿فَإِذَا قَرَأۡنَٰهُ فَٱتَّبِعۡ قُرۡءَانَهُۥ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا بَيَانَهُۥ﴾
- The Prophet ﷺ used to move his lips quickly with Jibril, fearing he would forget; Allah promised to preserve it [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].
- **For memorizers**: listen first, then repeat.

### Faces on that Day
﴿وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ نَّاضِرَةٌ﴾ ﴿إِلَىٰ رَبِّهَا نَاظِرَةࣱ﴾ — *Some faces that Day will be radiant, looking at their Lord.*

### The moment of death
- *When the soul reaches the collarbones, and it is said: Who will cure him?*

### Lessons
- **Listen to your inner blame**; it is a gift.

## Surah Al-Insan Meaning: Feeding Others for the Face of Allah

**Surah Al-Insan** (31 ayat), also called **Ad-Dahr**, was recited by the Prophet ﷺ with As-Sajdah in the Fajr prayer on Fridays [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

﴿هَلۡ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ حِينࣱ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهۡرِ لَمۡ يَكُن شَيۡـٔٗا مَّذۡكُورًا﴾ — *Has there come upon man a period of time when he was nothing to be mentioned?*

### The test
- Allah created man, gave him hearing and sight, and showed him the way: **either grateful or ungrateful**.

### The righteous
﴿وَيُطۡعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسۡكِينٗا وَيَتِيمٗا وَأَسِيرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا نُطۡعِمُكُمۡ لِوَجۡهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمۡ جَزَآءٗ وَلَا شُكُورًا﴾
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Fulfil vows | Keep their promises to Allah |
| Fear a Day of widespread evil | Awareness of the Hereafter |
| Feed despite loving the food | Generosity when it is hard |
| For Allah's face | Expecting no reward or thanks |

### Paradise described
- Couches, gardens, no burning sun or bitter cold, silver vessels, a drink mixed with ginger from a spring called *Salsabil*, garments of green silk, *and their Lord will give them a pure drink*.

### Lessons
- **Feed others** with a pure intention.
- **Remember your beginning**: you were once nothing.

## Surah An-Naba Meaning: The Great News of the Day of Judgement

**Surah An-Naba** (40 ayat) opens Juz ʿAmma. Its name comes from its second ayah: *the great news*, meaning the Resurrection.

﴿عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾ ﴿عَنِ ٱلنَّبَإِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي هُمۡ فِيهِ مُخۡتَلِفُونَ﴾ — *About what are they asking one another? About the great news, over which they disagree.*

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | The question about the great news |
| 6–16 | Signs of Allah's power and blessings |
| 17–20 | The Day of Sorting and the Trumpet |
| 21–30 | Hell, waiting for the transgressors |
| 31–36 | Success for the righteous |
| 37–40 | The Day the Spirit and angels stand in rows |

### Signs all around you
﴿أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَلِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِهَٰدٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡجِبَالَ أَوۡتَادٗا﴾ — *Did We not make the earth a resting place, and the mountains as pegs?*
- The surah lists the earth, mountains, pairs, sleep, night and day, the heavens, the sun, and rain.
- **The argument**: the One who made all this can bring you back to life.

### The final scene
﴿ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ مَـَٔابًا﴾ — *That is the True Day; so whoever wills may take a way back to his Lord.*
﴿وَيَقُولُ ٱلۡكَافِرُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَٰبَۢا﴾ — *And the disbeliever will say: I wish I were dust.*

### Lessons
- **Reflect on daily blessings**, such as sleep, as signs.
- **Choose your way back** to Allah now, while there is time.

## Surah An-Naziat Meaning: Musa, Pharaoh and the Great Overwhelming Event

**Surah An-Naziat** (46 ayat) begins with oaths by angels, then tells a short story with a big lesson: the arrogance of Pharaoh.

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Oaths by the angels who take out souls and carry out commands |
| 6–14 | The Day of the quake and the deniers' surprise |
| 15–26 | Musa sent to Pharaoh |
| 27–33 | Creation of the heavens and earth as proof |
| 34–41 | The great overwhelming event and the two fates |
| 42–46 | When is the Hour? Only Allah knows |

### Musa and Pharaoh
- Musa was told to go to Pharaoh and invite him gently to purify himself and know his Lord.
- Pharaoh denied and proclaimed: *"I am your lord, most high."*
- **Lesson**: arrogance ends in punishment, *a lesson for whoever fears*.

### The two fates
﴿فَأَمَّا مَن طَغَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَءَاثَرَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ﴾
﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ وَنَهَى ٱلنَّفۡسَ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ﴾
| Path | Result |
|---|---|
| Transgression and preferring this life | Hellfire |
| Fearing the standing before Allah and restraining desire | Paradise |

### Lessons
- **Desire is not evil**, but it must be restrained.
- **Gentle words**, even with the arrogant, as Musa was commanded.

## Surah Abasa Meaning: The Blind Man Who Came Seeking Guidance

**Surah Abasa** (42 ayat) begins with a scene that teaches a timeless lesson: **every sincere seeker matters**.

﴿عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَن جَآءَهُ ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ﴾ — *He frowned and turned away, because the blind man came to him.*

### The story
- The Prophet ﷺ was busy inviting leaders of Quraysh, hoping they would accept Islam.
- ʿAbdullah ibn Umm Maktum, a blind companion, came asking to be taught, as reported in At-Tirmidhi.
- Allah gently corrected: ﴿وَمَا يُدۡرِيكَ لَعَلَّهُۥ يَزَّكَّىٰٓ﴾ — *And what would make you know? Perhaps he would be purified.*

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–16 | The blind man and the honoured pages of the Quran |
| 17–23 | Man's ingratitude |
| 24–32 | Look at your food: rain, crops, fruits |
| 33–42 | The Day of the deafening blast |

### Look at your food
﴿فَلۡيَنظُرِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِلَىٰ طَعَامِهِۦٓ﴾ — *Let man look at his food.*
- Water poured down, the earth split open, grain, grapes, olives, palms, gardens and fruits.

### The Day of fleeing
﴿يَوۡمَ يَفِرُّ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مِنۡ أَخِيهِ﴾ ﴿وَأُمِّهِۦ وَأَبِيهِ﴾ ﴿وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَبَنِيهِ﴾ ﴿لِكُلِّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ شَأۡنࣱ يُغۡنِيهِ﴾

### Lessons
- **Value people by their sincerity**, not their status.
- **Gratitude** for every meal.
