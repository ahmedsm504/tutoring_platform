﴿مَّن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يُقۡرِضُ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا فَيُضَٰعِفَهُۥ لَهُۥٓ أَضۡعَافٗا كَثِيرَةٗۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَقۡبِضُ وَيَبۡصُۜطُ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:245]

## Why a "loan"?
- Allah needs nothing, yet He calls charity a **loan** so the giver knows it is safe, returned and multiplied.

## Abu al-Dahdah
- Scholars mention that when it was revealed, Abu al-Dahdah asked: *Does Allah want a loan from us?* The Prophet ﷺ said yes, and he gave his garden of six hundred palms (mentioned by Ibn Kathir and others).

## Conditions
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sincerity | For Allah |
| Lawful | From good earnings |
| Willingly | Without reminders or harm |

## "Allah withholds and extends"
- Provision is in His hand: giving does not impoverish, holding back does not enrich.
- Tajweed note: in Hafs, *yabsut* here is read with a *sin*, indicated by the small sin over the sad.

## Lessons
- **Give**, confident it is a loan with Allah.
- **Do not fear poverty** because of charity.
