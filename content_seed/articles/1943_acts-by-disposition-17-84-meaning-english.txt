## What is *shakilah*?
| Explanation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Way | Each acts according to his path |
| Intention | According to his intention |
| Nature | According to his character |

## The context
- Just before it: the Quran is healing for believers but increases wrongdoers in loss. The same Quran affects people according to their **shakilah**.

## Judgement belongs to Allah
- *Your Lord knows best who is better guided*: not people's opinions.

## Reform the inside
- *There is a piece of flesh in the body; if it is sound, the whole body is sound... it is the heart* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Lessons
- **Watch your intentions.**
- **Leave judgement of others to Allah.**
