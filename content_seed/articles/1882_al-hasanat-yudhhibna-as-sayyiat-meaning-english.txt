## Prayer times in the verse
| Phrase | Explained by scholars as |
|---|---|
| The two ends of the day | Fajr, and Dhuhr and ʿAsr (or Maghrib) |
| Parts of the night | Maghrib and ʿIshaʾ |

## The occasion
- A man kissed a woman who was not permitted to him, then came to the Prophet ﷺ regretful. This verse was revealed. He asked: *Is this for me alone?* The Prophet ﷺ said: **"For all of my nation"** (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Good erases bad
- The five prayers, wudu, charity and dhikr wipe away minor sins. Major sins require sincere repentance.

## A Prophetic instruction
- *Fear Allah wherever you are, follow a bad deed with a good one and it will erase it, and treat people with good character* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

## A reminder
- *That is a reminder for those who remember*: those who remember Allah benefit most.

## Lessons
- **Do not despair after a slip**: pray and do good.
- **Keep the five prayers**, they are the daily cleansing.
