## What is *silm*?
- Ibn ʿAbbas and others explained it here as **Islam**.

## *Kaffah*
| Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Describing the religion | Enter all the laws of Islam |
| Describing the believers | Enter all together |

- Both meanings go together: everyone takes the whole religion.

## Satan's footsteps
- Satan rarely invites to a major sin at once; he leads **step by step**. Beware the small beginnings.

## No picking and choosing
- A Muslim does not take what suits his desires and leave what does not.

## Lessons
- **Review your life**: is any area outside Islam's guidance?
- **Close the door** at the first step.
