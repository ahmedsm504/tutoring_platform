Juz 3 begins with ﴿تِلۡكَ ٱلرُّسُلُ فَضَّلۡنَا بَعۡضَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۘ﴾, "Those messengers, We preferred some of them over others", so it is called **Juz Tilka ar-Rusul**. It runs from **Al-Baqarah 2:253 to Al Imran 3:92**, ending with ﴿لَن تَنَالُواْ ٱلۡبِرَّ حَتَّىٰ تُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ﴾, "You will never attain righteousness until you spend from what you love." In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 42 to 61**.

This is one of the richest juz of the Quran. It contains **the greatest verse in the Book of Allah**, **the longest verse** in the Mushaf, and **the closing verses of Al-Baqarah**, and then it opens **Surah Al Imran**, the second of "the two radiant ones".

## The big idea

Juz 3 joins **the greatness of Allah** with **human responsibility in wealth and belief**. It starts with the messengers and their ranks, introduces Allah in Ayat al-Kursi, shows His power to give life in three scenes, and then turns to money: spending sincerely, the prohibition of riba, and recording debts. Al-Baqarah closes with the faith and prayer of the Messenger and the believers. Then Al Imran opens by establishing correct belief about Allah and about 'Isa (Jesus), peace be upon him, and engages the People of the Book with proof and gentleness.

The thread: **whoever truly knows Allah handles wealth well and stands firm on the truth when doubts arise.**

## Part one: the close of Surah Al-Baqarah (2:253-286)

### 1. The ranks of the messengers (253-254)
Allah preferred some messengers over others: to some He spoke directly, some He raised in rank, and to 'Isa He gave clear proofs and supported him with the Holy Spirit. The verses warn against division after them and urge believers to spend before a Day when there is no trade, no friendship and no intercession.

### 2. Ayat al-Kursi (255)
﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُۚ لَا تَأۡخُذُهُۥ سِنَةࣱ وَلَا نَوۡمࣱۚ﴾, "Allah: there is no god but Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all. Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him." It is the greatest verse in the Book of Allah. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ asked Ubayy ibn Ka'b which verse of Allah's Book was greatest; he answered with this verse, and the Prophet ﷺ struck his chest and said: "May knowledge be pleasing to you, Abu al-Mundhir." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, in the story of Abu Hurayrah and the one who kept taking from the charity, whoever recites it when going to bed has a guardian from Allah, and no devil comes near him until morning.

The verse has ten sentences, all about Allah: His divinity, life, self-sufficiency, freedom from drowsiness and sleep, ownership, intercession only by His permission, all-encompassing knowledge, the vastness of His Kursi, the ease with which He preserves heavens and earth, and His highness and greatness. Read [Ayat al-Kursi Meaning in English](/blog/remember-allah-as-you-remember-fathers-english/#Ayat-al-Kursi-Meaning-The-Greatest-Ayah-in-the-Quran-Explain).

### 3. No compulsion in religion (256-257)
﴿لَآ إِكۡرَاهَ فِي ٱلدِّينِۖ قَد تَّبَيَّنَ ٱلرُّشۡدُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَيِّۚ﴾, "There is no compulsion in religion; right guidance has become clear from error." After Allah introduced Himself in Ayat al-Kursi, the truth is plain, so there is no need for force. Whoever rejects false gods and believes in Allah has grasped the firmest handhold. Allah is the ally of the believers, bringing them out of darkness into light.

### 4. Three scenes of giving life (258-260)
These scenes prove in practice the attribute ﴿ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُۚ﴾:
- **Ibrahim and the king who argued about his Lord.** Ibrahim said: ﴿رَبِّيَ ٱلَّذِي يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُ﴾, "My Lord is the one who gives life and causes death." When the king twisted the point, Ibrahim moved to an argument he could not answer: Allah brings the sun from the east, so bring it from the west. ﴿فَبُهِتَ ٱلَّذِي كَفَرَۗ﴾, "So the disbeliever was dumbfounded."
- **The man who passed a ruined town.** ﴿فَأَمَاتَهُ ٱللَّهُ مِاْئَةَ عَامٖ ثُمَّ بَعَثَهُۥۖ﴾, "So Allah caused him to die for a hundred years, then revived him." His food had not spoiled, and his donkey was brought back to life before his eyes.
- **Ibrahim and the birds.** ﴿رَبِّ أَرِنِي كَيۡفَ تُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰۖ قَالَ أَوَلَمۡ تُؤۡمِنۖ قَالَ بَلَىٰ وَلَٰكِن لِّيَطۡمَئِنَّ قَلۡبِيۖ﴾, "My Lord, show me how You give life to the dead. He said: Do you not believe? He said: Yes, but so that my heart may be at rest."

### 5. Parables of spending (261-274)
The focus turns to wealth, beginning with the most beautiful image of multiplication: ﴿مَّثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ حَبَّةٍ أَنۢبَتَتۡ سَبۡعَ سَنَابِلَ فِي كُلِّ سُنۢبُلَةٖ مِّاْئَةُ حَبَّةٖۗ﴾, "The example of those who spend their wealth in the way of Allah is like a seed that grows seven ears, in each ear a hundred grains." A chain of parables then explains what makes charity accepted and what ruins it:
- **Reminders of favour and hurt** cancel charity: "they do not follow what they spend with reminders of generosity or with hurt", and ﴿۞قَوۡلࣱ مَّعۡرُوفࣱ وَمَغۡفِرَةٌ خَيۡرࣱ مِّن صَدَقَةࣲ يَتۡبَعُهَآ أَذٗىۗ﴾, "Kind words and forgiveness are better than charity followed by hurt." See [Kind Words Are Better Than Charity](/blog/surah-al-baqarah-meaning-english/#A-Kind-Word-and-Forgiveness-Are-Better-Than-Charity-Followed).
- **The smooth rock** with a little soil on it, washed bare by heavy rain: the parable of one who gives to be seen.
- **The garden on high ground** that, when heavy rain falls, yields double: the parable of the sincere.
- **The garden destroyed** when its owner was old with weak children: the one who ruins his deeds at the end of life.
- **Give from good things**, not the worthless; **hidden charity** is better; and the **dignified poor**, whom the unaware think rich because of their restraint.
- The close: ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ﴾, "Those who spend their wealth by night and by day, secretly and publicly, will have their reward with their Lord." See [Spending by Night and Day: 2:274](/blog/surah-al-baqarah-meaning-english/#Those-Who-Spend-Their-Wealth-by-Night-and-Day-Secretly-and-O).

### 6. The prohibition of riba (275-281)
After giving comes its opposite: taking increase without right. ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْ لَا يَقُومُونَ إِلَّا كَمَا يَقُومُ ٱلَّذِي يَتَخَبَّطُهُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ مِنَ ٱلۡمَسِّۚ﴾, "Those who consume interest will not stand except as one stands who is driven to madness by Shaytan's touch." Then the reply to their claim that trade is like riba: ﴿وَأَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡبَيۡعَ وَحَرَّمَ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْۚ﴾, "Allah has permitted trade and forbidden riba." Then the rule: ﴿يَمۡحَقُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْ وَيُرۡبِي ٱلصَّدَقَٰتِۗ﴾, "Allah destroys riba and makes charity grow." Then the decisive command: ﴿وَذَرُواْ مَا بَقِيَ مِنَ ٱلرِّبَوٰٓاْ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "and give up what remains of riba if you are believers", with a declaration of war on those who do not stop.

Then mercy for the debtor: ﴿وَإِن كَانَ ذُو عُسۡرَةٖ فَنَظِرَةٌ إِلَىٰ مَيۡسَرَةٖۚ وَأَن تَصَدَّقُواْ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡ﴾, "If the debtor is in hardship, then grant him time until ease; and if you give it as charity, it is better for you." Then a verse many commentators name among the very last revealed: ﴿وَٱتَّقُواْ يَوۡمٗا تُرۡجَعُونَ فِيهِ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ﴾, "And fear a Day when you will be returned to Allah." Read [Allah Destroys Riba: 2:276](/blog/surah-al-baqarah-meaning-english/#Allah-Destroys-Interest-and-Increases-Charity-Quran-2-276-Ex) and [Give Time to a Debtor in Hardship](/blog/surah-al-baqarah-meaning-english/#If-the-Debtor-Is-in-Hardship-Then-Grant-Him-Time-Quran-2-280).

**Notice the order**: charity, then riba, then giving time to the debtor. Charity is giving without return, riba is taking without return, and granting time protects the right while showing mercy to the weak.

### 7. The debt verse: the longest verse in the Quran (282-283)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا تَدَايَنتُم بِدَيۡنٍ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى فَٱكۡتُبُوهُۚ﴾, "O you who believe, when you contract a debt for a fixed term, write it down." It is the longest verse in the Mushaf, with precise rulings: writing the debt, a just scribe, the debtor dictating, a guardian for one unable, witnesses (two men, or a man and two women), not tiring of writing small or large amounts, witnesses for sales, and not harming the scribe or witness. Then pledges held as security when travelling.

**Why is the longest verse about money?** Because protecting financial rights prevents disputes and keeps hearts clean. Islam does not settle for preaching honesty; it gives practical tools to protect it.

### 8. The closing verses of Al-Baqarah (284-286)
The surah ends by affirming Allah's ownership and knowledge, then: ﴿ءَامَنَ ٱلرَّسُولُ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِ مِن رَّبِّهِۦ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَۚ كُلٌّ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتِهِۦ وَكُتُبِهِۦ وَرُسُلِهِۦ﴾, "The Messenger has believed in what was revealed to him from his Lord, and so have the believers. All have believed in Allah, His angels, His books and His messengers", then ﴿لَا يُكَلِّفُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ﴾, "Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity", and the comprehensive prayer that ends ﴿فَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾.

In Al-Bukhari and Muslim: "Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah at night, they will suffice him." In Sahih Muslim, Allah replied to each request in it: "I have done so." The ending also returns to the beginning: the surah opened with those who believe in the unseen and in what was revealed, and it closes with the Messenger and the believers believing in what was revealed. Read [The Last Two Verses of Al-Baqarah](/blog/surah-al-baqarah-meaning-english/#The-Last-Two-Ayat-of-Surah-Al-Baqarah-Meaning-and-How-to-Mem).

## Part two: the opening of Surah Al Imran (3:1-92)

Al Imran is a Madani surah of 200 verses. With Al-Baqarah it forms **az-Zahrawan**, "the two radiant ones". In Sahih Muslim: "Recite the two radiant ones, Al-Baqarah and Al Imran, for they will come on the Day of Resurrection like two clouds, or two shades, or two flocks of birds in ranks, pleading for their companions." Many commentators mention that its opening came in the context of the delegation of Christians from Najran visiting Madinah, which is why this part focuses on the reality of 'Isa, peace be upon him. For the whole surah see [Surah Al Imran Meaning in English](/blog/rabbana-la-tuzigh-qulubana-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Imran-Meaning-Firmness-of-Heart-and-Lessons-from-Uh).

### 1. The Ever-Living who sent down the books (1-6)
It opens with the same letters as Al-Baqarah, ﴿الٓمٓ﴾, then ﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُ﴾, the same attributes as Ayat al-Kursi. It affirms that Allah revealed the Torah and the Gospel before, and the Quran confirming what came before it, and that He shapes people in the wombs as He wills.

### 2. Clear and ambiguous verses (7-9)
﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِنۡهُ ءَايَٰتࣱ مُّحۡكَمَٰتٌ هُنَّ أُمُّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَأُخَرُ مُتَشَٰبِهَٰتࣱۖ﴾, "It is He who sent down the Book to you; in it are clear verses, which are the foundation of the Book, and others that are ambiguous." Those with deviation in their hearts chase the ambiguous seeking discord, while those firm in knowledge say: we believe in it, all is from our Lord. Their prayer: ﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾, "Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself; You are the Bestower." Read ["Our Lord, Let Not Our Hearts Deviate": Quran 3:8 Explained](/blog/rabbana-la-tuzigh-qulubana-meaning-english/#Our-Lord-Let-Not-Our-Hearts-Deviate-Quran-3-8-Explained).

**Why here?** Because the dialogue with the People of the Book would touch on phrases such as "a word from Allah" and "a spirit from Him", so Allah first set the method: interpret the ambiguous in light of the clear.

### 3. The lesson of Badr and the attractions of this world (10-17)
Wealth and children will not help the disbelievers; then a sign in the two armies that met at Badr. Then: ﴿زُيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ حُبُّ ٱلشَّهَوَٰتِ مِنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ وَٱلۡبَنِينَ وَٱلۡقَنَٰطِيرِ ٱلۡمُقَنطَرَةِ مِنَ ٱلذَّهَبِ وَٱلۡفِضَّةِ﴾, "Made attractive to people is the love of desires: women, sons, heaped-up treasures of gold and silver..." followed by what is better: gardens and Allah's pleasure for the God-conscious, who are patient, truthful, devout, generous and seek forgiveness before dawn.

### 4. The testimony of oneness (18-25)
﴿شَهِدَ ٱللَّهُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ قَآئِمَۢا بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۚ﴾, "Allah bears witness that there is no god but Him, and so do the angels and those of knowledge, maintaining justice." Allah joined the testimony of the people of knowledge to His own and the angels', a great honour for scholars. Then: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمُۗ﴾, "Indeed the religion with Allah is Islam."

### 5. Owner of sovereignty, and loving Allah (26-32)
﴿قُلِ ٱللَّهُمَّ مَٰلِكَ ٱلۡمُلۡكِ تُؤۡتِي ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتَنزِعُ ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مِمَّن تَشَآءُ﴾, "Say: O Allah, Owner of sovereignty, You give sovereignty to whom You will and take it from whom You will." Then the measure of true love: ﴿قُلۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تُحِبُّونَ ٱللَّهَ فَٱتَّبِعُونِي يُحۡبِبۡكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ ذُنُوبَكُمۡۚ﴾, "Say: if you love Allah, follow me, and Allah will love you and forgive your sins."

### 6. The family of Imran (33-63)
﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱصۡطَفَىٰٓ ءَادَمَ وَنُوحٗا وَءَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَءَالَ عِمۡرَٰنَ عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "Allah chose Adam, Nuh, the family of Ibrahim and the family of Imran over the worlds." The surah takes its name from here, and the story is a chain of miracles:
1. **The wife of Imran** vows her unborn child to the service of Allah's house, gives birth to a girl, names her Maryam, and seeks Allah's protection for her and her offspring from Shaytan.
2. **Maryam in Zakariya's care**: ﴿كُلَّمَا دَخَلَ عَلَيۡهَا زَكَرِيَّا ٱلۡمِحۡرَابَ وَجَدَ عِندَهَا رِزۡقٗاۖ﴾, "Whenever Zakariya entered upon her in the prayer chamber, he found provision with her", and she said: ﴿هُوَ مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۖ﴾, "It is from Allah."
3. **Zakariya prays** right there and is given Yahya despite his old age and his wife's barrenness.
4. **The good news to Maryam** of 'Isa, a word from Allah, named the Messiah, 'Isa son of Maryam, honoured in this world and the Hereafter.
5. **The miracles of 'Isa**: a bird from clay by Allah's permission, healing the blind and the leper, reviving the dead by Allah's permission, and telling people what they ate and stored.
6. **The disciples**: ﴿نَحۡنُ أَنصَارُ ٱللَّهِ﴾, "We are the helpers of Allah."
7. **'Isa raised up** and purified from those who disbelieved.
8. **The decisive argument**: ﴿إِنَّ مَثَلَ عِيسَىٰ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ ءَادَمَۖ خَلَقَهُۥ مِن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ قَالَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾, "The example of 'Isa with Allah is like that of Adam: He created him from dust, then said to him, Be, and he was." If being born without a father meant divinity, Adam had neither father nor mother.
9. **The mubahala**, a mutual invocation, offered to those who kept arguing after knowledge came.

**Notice the progression**: a birth from an old barren mother (Yahya), then a birth with no father ('Isa). Whoever accepts the first must accept the second; both come through the word "Be".

### 7. A common word, and Ibrahim (64-68)
﴿قُلۡ يَـٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ تَعَالَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ كَلِمَةٖ سَوَآءِۭ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَكُمۡ أَلَّا نَعۡبُدَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَ وَلَا نُشۡرِكَ بِهِۦ شَيۡـٔٗا﴾, "Say: O People of the Book, come to a word common between us and you: that we worship none but Allah and associate nothing with Him." The Prophet ﷺ quoted it in his letter to Heraclius, as reported in Sahih Al-Bukhari. Then the dispute over Ibrahim is settled: ﴿مَا كَانَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ يَهُودِيّٗا وَلَا نَصۡرَانِيّٗا وَلَٰكِن كَانَ حَنِيفٗا مُّسۡلِمٗا﴾, "Ibrahim was neither a Jew nor a Christian, but he was upright, a Muslim." The Torah and Gospel were revealed after him.

### 8. Tactics of obstruction, trust, and the covenant of the prophets (69-91)
- A group of the People of the Book wanted to lead believers astray; some said: believe at the start of the day and disbelieve at its end, so that believers might turn back.
- **Trust**: some of them, entrusted with a heap of gold, return it; others, entrusted with a single coin, do not.
- **Those who sell Allah's covenant for a small price**, and those who twist their tongues with scripture.
- **The covenant of the prophets**: Allah took a covenant from every prophet that if a messenger came confirming what they had, they would believe in him and support him.
- **Whoever seeks a religion other than Islam**, it will not be accepted from him.

### 9. The close of the juz: you will never attain righteousness (92)
﴿لَن تَنَالُواْ ٱلۡبِرَّ حَتَّىٰ تُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ﴾. In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, when Abu Talhah heard this verse he gave away his most beloved property, the garden of Bayruha'. The juz that began with spending at the end of Al-Baqarah closes with its highest level: giving what you love. ## Tajweed points in Juz 3

### ﴿الٓمٓ﴾ followed by ﴿ٱللَّهُ﴾ at the start of Al Imran
When reading continuously, the meem of "Meem" may be stretched **six counts** (keeping the original) or **two counts** (because of the vowel added to join), and the meem takes a fatha to meet the next sakin. When stopping on ﴿الٓمٓ﴾, stretch six.

### Clear noon before 'ayn in ﴿مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۖ﴾
Sakin noon before a throat letter is pronounced clearly (izhar), with no extra ghunnah.

### Iqlab in ﴿أَنۢبَتَتۡ﴾, ﴿سُنۢبُلَةٖ﴾ and ﴿وَيُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ﴾
Noon before ba becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah; it appears often in the parables of spending.

### Stopping well in Ayat al-Kursi
There is no compulsory pause for Hafs in this juz, but observe the permissible stop signs in Ayat al-Kursi, such as after ﴿ٱلۡقَيُّومُۚ﴾ and ﴿وَلَا نَوۡمࣱۚ﴾. For a detailed walk-through see [Ayat al-Kursi Tajweed](/blog/remember-allah-as-you-remember-fathers-english/#Ayat-al-Kursi-with-Tajweed-How-to-Memorize-and-Recite-It-Cor).

### Connected madd in ﴿ٱلسَّمَآءِ﴾ and ﴿ٱلنِّسَآءِ﴾
Four or five counts for Hafs by way of Ash-Shatibiyyah, keeping one length throughout.

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. "Their reward" with and without *fa*
- 2:262: ﴿لَّهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ﴾, without *fa*.
- 2:274: ﴿فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ﴾, with *fa*.
**Key**: the first, at the start of the spending section, has no *fa*; the last, at its end, has it.

### 2. Zakariya and Maryam
- Zakariya (40): ﴿قَالَ كَذَٰلِكَ ٱللَّهُ يَفۡعَلُ مَا يَشَآءُ﴾, "Allah **does** what He wills."
- Maryam (47): ﴿قَالَ كَذَٰلِكِ ٱللَّهُ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ﴾, "Allah **creates** what He wills."
**Key**: Yahya was born of two parents, which suits "does"; 'Isa was born without a father, an unusual creation, which suits "creates".

### 3. "A boy" and "a child"
Zakariya said ﴿أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي غُلَٰمࣱ﴾ (*ghulam*, a boy); Maryam said ﴿أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي وَلَدࣱ﴾ (*walad*, a child).

### 4. ﴿الٓمٓ﴾ in the two surahs
In Al-Baqarah it is followed by ﴿ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ﴾; in Al Imran by ﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ﴾. Verse 2 of Al Imran is the opening of Ayat al-Kursi without the rest.

## How to memorize Juz 3 with understanding

1. **Split it in two**: the end of Al-Baqarah (about 9 pages) and the start of Al Imran (about 11 pages).
2. **Start from what you may already know**: many people know Ayat al-Kursi and the last verses of Al-Baqarah; use them as anchors.
3. **Memorize the spending parables as a chain of images**: seed, rock, hill garden, burnt garden.
4. **Give the debt verse two or three days** and break it into phrases: writing, scribe, dictation, witnesses, sales.
5. **Memorize the family of Imran in time order**: Imran's wife, Maryam, Zakariya, the good news, the miracles, the raising.
6. **Revise this juz with Juz 2**, because the end of Al-Baqarah continues the themes of spending and fighting before it.

For a fuller method see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 3

- **Recite Ayat al-Kursi** after each prayer and before sleep, reflecting on Allah's attributes in it.
- **Do not ruin your charity** with reminders of favour or hurt; a kind word beats a hurtful gift.
- **Write down your debts**, however small, as the Quran commands.
- **Give time to a debtor in hardship**, and forgiving the debt is better still.
- **Interpret the ambiguous through the clear**, and do not chase doubts.
- **Pray the prayer of the firmly grounded**: our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate.
- **True love is following**: "If you love Allah, follow me."
- **Give from what you love** to attain righteousness.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 3 called "Tilka ar-Rusul"?
Because it begins with ﴿تِلۡكَ ٱلرُّسُلُ فَضَّلۡنَا بَعۡضَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۘ﴾.

### What is the longest verse in the Quran?
The debt verse, Al-Baqarah 2:282, which is in this juz.

### Where does Juz 3 end?
At Al Imran 3:92, ﴿لَن تَنَالُواْ ٱلۡبِرَّ حَتَّىٰ تُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ﴾. Juz 4 begins with ﴿۞كُلُّ ٱلطَّعَامِ كَانَ حِلّٗا لِّبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ﴾, "All food was lawful to the Children of Israel."

### What is the virtue of the last verses of Al-Baqarah?
In Al-Bukhari and Muslim: whoever recites the last two verses of Al-Baqarah at night, they will suffice him.

### Why does the opening of Al Imran speak so much about 'Isa?
Because it came in the context of a dialogue with Christians, so it explains the reality of 'Isa as a servant and messenger of Allah, and that his creation without a father is like Adam's creation without father or mother.

## Conclusion

Juz 3 takes you from the greatest verse about who Allah is, to the most detailed verse on protecting rights, to the truest account of 'Isa, peace be upon him. One thread runs between these stations: faith in the Ever-Living, the Sustainer, shows in how you spend, how you deal with people and how firmly you hold to the truth.
