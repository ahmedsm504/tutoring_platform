**Surah Al-Humazah** (9 ayat) links two sins that often go together: **mocking people** and **worshipping wealth**.

﴿وَيۡلࣱ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٖ لُّمَزَةٍ﴾ — *Woe to every scorner and mocker.*
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Humazah | One who mocks or slanders, often with gestures |
| Lumazah | One who finds fault with words |

## The wealth illusion
﴿ٱلَّذِي جَمَعَ مَالٗا وَعَدَّدَهُۥ﴾ ﴿يَحۡسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُۥٓ أَخۡلَدَهُۥ﴾
- **He counts his money** and thinks it will keep him forever.

## The crushing fire
﴿كَلَّاۖ لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ فِي ٱلۡحُطَمَةِ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحُطَمَةُ﴾ ﴿نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُوقَدَةُ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّتِي تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةِ﴾
- **It reaches the hearts**: the very place where arrogance and contempt lived.

## Why these two together?
- Many who look down on others do so **because of their wealth**.

## Lessons
| Sin | Remedy |
|---|---|
| Mocking people | Remember the Quran forbids ridicule, and you don't know who is better |
| Counting wealth | Spend in charity |
