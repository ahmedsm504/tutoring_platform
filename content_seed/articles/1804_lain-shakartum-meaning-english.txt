﴿وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمۡ لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدࣱ﴾ [Ibrahim 14:7]

## A public announcement
- *Taʾadhdhana* means Allah **declared and proclaimed** it, a law, not a possibility.

## The promise
- The verse uses strong emphasis: *I will surely increase you*. Increase in what? It is left open: wealth, health, faith, peace and more.

## The warning
- Notice it does not say "I will punish you", but *My punishment is severe*. Scholars note the gentle phrasing even in warning.

## Three pillars of gratitude
| Pillar | Practice |
|---|---|
| Heart | Recognising that the blessing is from Allah |
| Tongue | Saying *alhamdulillah* and speaking of His favours |
| Limbs | Using the blessing in obedience, not sin |

## Context
- Musa reminded his people of their rescue from Pharaoh and conveyed this law to them.

## Lessons
- **Count three blessings daily.**
- **Use blessings for good**: that is real gratitude.
