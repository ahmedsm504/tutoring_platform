﴿۞وَعِندَهُۥ مَفَاتِحُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ لَا يَعۡلَمُهَآ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۚ وَمَا تَسۡقُطُ مِن وَرَقَةٍ إِلَّا يَعۡلَمُهَا وَلَا حَبَّةٖ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا رَطۡبٖ وَلَا يَابِسٍ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ [Al-Anam 6:59]

## The keys of the unseen
- The Prophet ﷺ explained the keys of the unseen as five, citing the last verse of Surah Luqman: knowledge of the Hour, the coming of rain, what is in the wombs, what a soul will earn tomorrow, and in which land it will die (narrated by al-Bukhari).

## Knowledge in detail
| Scene | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Land and sea | The visible and the hidden worlds |
| A falling leaf | Even the smallest movement |
| A seed in the darkness of the earth | What no eye can see |
| Wet and dry | Everything, without exception |

## A clear book
- All of this is written in *a clear record*, showing that Allah's knowledge precedes and records events.

## Tawhid in practice
- Since the unseen belongs only to Allah, **visiting fortune-tellers, horoscopes and claims of knowing the future** contradict this verse.

## Comfort for the believer
- If He knows every leaf that falls, He knows your worry, your effort and your duʿa.

## Lessons
- **Trust the One who knows** what you cannot see.
- **Reject superstition.**
