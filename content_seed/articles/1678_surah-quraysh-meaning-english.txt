**Surah Quraysh** (4 ayat) continues the message of Surah Al-Fil: Allah protected His House, so worship Him.

﴿لِإِيلَٰفِ قُرَيۡشٍ﴾ ﴿إِۦلَٰفِهِمۡ رِحۡلَةَ ٱلشِّتَآءِ وَٱلصَّيۡفِ﴾ ﴿فَلۡيَعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَيۡتِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٖ وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنۡ خَوۡفِۭ﴾

## The journeys
| Season | Destination (as commentators mentioned) |
|---|---|
| Winter | Yemen |
| Summer | Ash-Shaam |
- Because they lived by the Kaʿbah, the Arabs respected their caravans.

## The command
- ﴿فَلۡيَعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَيۡتِ﴾: the natural response to blessing is **worship**.

## Two blessings
| Blessing | Ayah |
|---|---|
| Food | ﴿أَطۡعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٖ﴾ |
| Security | ﴿وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنۡ خَوۡفِۭ﴾ |
- These are the foundations of any stable life.

## Lessons
- **Your daily meal and safety** are gifts that call for worship.
