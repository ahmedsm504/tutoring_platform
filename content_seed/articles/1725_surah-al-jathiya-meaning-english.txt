**Surah Al-Jathiyah** (37 ayat) takes its name from a powerful scene: **every nation kneeling** on the Day of Judgement.

## Signs for different people
| Signs in | For people who |
|---|---|
| The heavens and earth | Believe |
| The creation of animals | Are certain |
| Night, day, rain and winds | Reason |

## Not equal
﴿أَمۡ حَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱجۡتَرَحُواْ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ أَن نَّجۡعَلَهُمۡ كَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ سَوَآءٗ مَّحۡيَاهُمۡ وَمَمَاتُهُمۡۚ سَآءَ مَا يَحۡكُمُونَ﴾
- **Justice demands** a Day of Judgement.

## Desire as a god
- *Have you seen the one who takes his desire as his god?*
- **A modern idol**: following every desire without limit.

## Every nation kneeling
- *You will see every nation kneeling. Every nation will be called to its record.*
- *This Book of Ours speaks about you in truth. We used to record what you did.*

## Lessons
- **Live as if your record is being written**, because it is.
