## Surah Al-Jathiyah Meaning: Every Nation Kneeling

**Surah Al-Jathiyah** (37 ayat) takes its name from a powerful scene: **every nation kneeling** on the Day of Judgement.

### Signs for different people
| Signs in | For people who |
|---|---|
| The heavens and earth | Believe |
| The creation of animals | Are certain |
| Night, day, rain and winds | Reason |

### Not equal
﴿أَمۡ حَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱجۡتَرَحُواْ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ أَن نَّجۡعَلَهُمۡ كَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ سَوَآءٗ مَّحۡيَاهُمۡ وَمَمَاتُهُمۡۚ سَآءَ مَا يَحۡكُمُونَ﴾
- **Justice demands** a Day of Judgement.

### Desire as a god
- *Have you seen the one who takes his desire as his god?*
- **A modern idol**: following every desire without limit.

### Every nation kneeling
- *You will see every nation kneeling. Every nation will be called to its record.*
- *This Book of Ours speaks about you in truth. We used to record what you did.*

### Lessons
- **Live as if your record is being written**, because it is.

## Surah Al-Ahqaf Meaning: Kindness to Parents and the Jinn Who Listened

**Surah Al-Ahqaf** (35 ayat) is the last of the seven surahs that begin with *Ḥā Mīm*.

### Kindness to parents
- *We have enjoined upon man kindness to his parents. His mother carried him with hardship and delivered him with hardship.*
- **At forty**, the believer says: *My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour upon me and my parents…*

### Hud and the sand dunes
- **Al-Aḥqāf**: the sand dunes where ʿAd lived.
- When they saw a cloud, they said: *This is a cloud bringing us rain*. It was a wind carrying painful punishment.

### The jinn who listened
﴿وَإِذۡ صَرَفۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ نَفَرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ فَلَمَّا حَضَرُوهُ قَالُوٓاْ أَنصِتُواْۖ فَلَمَّا قُضِيَ وَلَّوۡاْ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِم مُّنذِرِينَ﴾
- **Their etiquette**: *Listen quietly!* Then they went back to warn their people.

### Patience
- *So be patient, as were those of determination among the messengers.*

### Lessons
- **Honour your parents** at every age.
- **Listen to the Quran in silence.**

## Quran 47:7 Explained: Supporting Allah's Religion and the Gift of Firm Feet

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن تَنصُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَنصُرۡكُمۡ وَيُثَبِّتۡ أَقۡدَامَكُمۡ﴾ [Muhammad 47:7]

### Supporting Allah
- Allah needs no support. The meaning is supporting **His religion, law and Messenger** through obedience, calling to good and action.

### Two promises
| Condition | Reward |
|---|---|
| Support Allah | He supports you |
| | And makes your feet firm |

### Reward like the deed
- Like *Remember Me, I will remember you* (2:152) and *Guard Allah and He will guard you* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### Firm feet
- Firmness is its own blessing: in battle, in trials, at death and on the Bridge.

### Lessons
- **Support Allah in yourself first** through obedience.
- **Ask for firmness** with duʿa and action.

## Surah Muhammad Meaning: If You Support Allah, He Will Support You

**Surah Muhammad** (38 ayat), also known as **Al-Qital**, was revealed in Madinah during the years of struggle.

### Two groups
| Believers | Disbelievers |
|---|---|
| Their deeds are accepted | Their deeds are lost |
| Their condition is set right | They follow falsehood |
| They follow the truth from their Lord | They are like cattle in their enjoyment |

### The promise
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن تَنصُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَنصُرۡكُمۡ وَيُثَبِّتۡ أَقۡدَامَكُمۡ﴾
- **Supporting Allah** means supporting His religion: obeying Him and spreading good.

### The four rivers
| River | Description |
|---|---|
| Water | Never stale |
| Milk | Its taste never changes |
| Wine | Delicious, without intoxication |
| Honey | Purified |

### Reflect
﴿أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ أَمۡ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبٍ أَقۡفَالُهَآ﴾ — *Do they not reflect upon the Quran, or are there locks upon their hearts?*

### Knowledge before action
- *So know that there is no god but Allah, and ask forgiveness for your sin* [Muhammad: 19].

### Lessons
- **Reflection unlocks the heart.**

## "Know That There Is No God But Allah, and Seek Forgiveness": Quran 47:19 Explained

﴿فَٱعۡلَمۡ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مُتَقَلَّبَكُمۡ وَمَثۡوَىٰكُمۡ﴾ [Muhammad 47:19]

### Knowledge first
- Al-Bukhari titled a chapter *Knowledge comes before speech and action*, citing this verse: Allah began with *know*, then *seek forgiveness*.

### *La ilaha illa Allah*
- The verse says *know* it, not merely say it: understand its meaning, conditions and implications.

### Forgiveness for all
| For | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Your sin | Personal repentance |
| Believing men and women | Praying for the whole Ummah |

- Praying for believers in their absence is answered, and an angel says: *And for you the same* (narrated by Muslim).

### Movement and rest
- *Allah knows your movement and your resting place*: by day and night, in this life and the next.

### Lessons
- **Learn tawhid** with understanding.
- **Include all believers** in your istighfar.

## "Do They Not Reflect Upon the Quran?": Quran 47:24 Explained

﴿أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ أَمۡ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبٍ أَقۡفَالُهَآ﴾ [Muhammad 47:24]

### What is tadabbur?
- From *dubur*, the end of something: looking at what the words lead to, their meanings, lessons and application.

### Locks on hearts
- *Or are there locks upon hearts?* The word *qulub* is indefinite, and *locks* are attributed to them: **their own** locks, made by their own sins and heedlessness.

### What blocks reflection
| Lock | Key |
|---|---|
| Persistent sin | Repentance |
| Rushing through recitation | Slower reading |
| Not understanding words | Reading a simple tafsir |
| Distracted heart | Quiet time, phone away |

### A similar verse
- *A blessed Book We revealed to you, that they may reflect upon its verses* (Sad 38:29). Reflection is **the purpose** of revelation.

### Simple steps
1. Read one verse with its translation.
2. Ask: what does Allah want from me here?
3. Write one action and do it today.

### Lessons
- **Quality over quantity** sometimes.
- **Ask Allah to open your heart** to the Quran.

## Surah Al-Fath Meaning: The Clear Victory of Hudaybiyah

**Surah Al-Fath** (29 ayat) was revealed on the way back from Hudaybiyah. The Prophet ﷺ said: «A surah has been revealed to me tonight that is more beloved to me than everything the sun rises upon» [Al-Bukhari].

﴿إِنَّا فَتَحۡنَا لَكَ فَتۡحٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾ — *Indeed, We have given you a clear victory.*

### A victory that looked like a setback
| What it seemed | What it was |
|---|---|
| Turned back from ʿUmrah | Peace that allowed Islam to spread |
| Terms seemed unfair | Recognition of the Muslims as a power |
| Disappointment | Two years later, Makkah was opened |

### The pledge under the tree
- Allah was **pleased** with the believers when they pledged allegiance under the tree, *and He knew what was in their hearts, so He sent down tranquility upon them*.

### The true dream
- The Prophet ﷺ had seen that they would enter the Sacred Mosque in safety; the surah confirms it would happen, and it did the following year.

### The companions described
﴿مُّحَمَّدࣱ رَّسُولُ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥٓ أَشِدَّآءُ عَلَى ٱلۡكُفَّارِ رُحَمَآءُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۖ﴾
- **Firm** against aggressors, **merciful** among themselves, often bowing and prostrating, with the mark of prostration on their faces.

### Lessons
- **Don't judge events by first appearances.**
- **Tranquility** is a gift Allah places in hearts.

## "If a Wrongdoer Brings You News, Verify It": Quran 49:6 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن جَآءَكُمۡ فَاسِقُۢ بِنَبَإٖ فَتَبَيَّنُوٓاْ أَن تُصِيبُواْ قَوۡمَۢا بِجَهَٰلَةٖ فَتُصۡبِحُواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلۡتُمۡ نَٰدِمِينَ﴾ [Al-Hujurat 49:6]

### The command: *fatabayyanu*
- **Verify**: check the source, look for confirmation, and do not act until the truth is clear.

### Why "a fasiq"?
- News from an untrustworthy source needs checking. Today, most unverified content online comes from unknown sources.

### The danger
| Without verification | Result |
|---|---|
| Harming innocent people | *Lest you harm a people in ignorance* |
| Regret | *Then become regretful over what you did* |

### The context
- Many scholars mention that it was revealed about a report brought concerning the tribe of Banu al-Mustaliq, which turned out to be untrue.

### A Prophetic warning
- *It is enough of a lie for a person to narrate everything he hears* (narrated by Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Do not forward** before checking.
- **Ask: who said this, and how do they know?**

## "The Believers Are But Brothers": Quran 49:10 Explained

﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ إِخۡوَةࣱ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَ أَخَوَيۡكُمۡۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ﴾ [Al-Hujurat 49:10]

### *Innama*
- Restriction: the lasting brotherhood is the brotherhood of **faith**.

### The context
- The previous verse speaks of two groups of believers fighting, and commands making peace. Even then, they remain **believers and brothers**.

### "Your two brothers"
- The dual form shows that reconciling is needed even when only **two** people quarrel.

### Rights of brotherhood
| Right | Source |
|---|---|
| Do not wrong or abandon him | *A Muslim is the brother of a Muslim; he does not wrong him or abandon him* (al-Bukhari and Muslim) |
| Love for him what you love for yourself | (al-Bukhari and Muslim) |
| Reconcile between him and others | This verse |

### Lessons
- **Be a peacemaker** in your family and friendships.
- **Taqwa brings mercy.**

## Surah Al-Hujurat Meaning: The Manners of a Believing Society

**Surah Al-Hujurat** (18 ayat) is often called the surah of **manners**: a short guide to building a healthy community.

### The manners in order
| Ayat | Manner |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Respect for Allah and His Messenger ﷺ; lowering voices |
| 6 | Verify news from an unreliable source |
| 9–10 | Reconcile between fighting believers |
| 11 | No mockery, no fault-finding, no insulting nicknames |
| 12 | Avoid suspicion, spying and backbiting |
| 13 | All people are equal; nobility is by taqwa |
| 14–18 | The difference between Islam and iman |

### Brotherhood
﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ إِخۡوَةࣱ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَ أَخَوَيۡكُمۡۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ﴾

### Backbiting
- The Quran compares backbiting to **eating the flesh of a dead brother**: something everyone hates.

### Equality
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن ذَكَرٖ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ شُعُوبٗا وَقَبَآئِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ أَكۡرَمَكُمۡ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَتۡقَىٰكُمۡۚ﴾
- Nations and tribes are **for knowing one another**, not for boasting.

### Lessons for social media
- **Verify before sharing.**
- **No mocking comments.**
- **No spreading private matters.**

## "Let Not a People Ridicule Another People": Quran 49:11 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا يَسۡخَرۡ قَوۡمࣱ مِّن قَوۡمٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونُواْ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ وَلَا نِسَآءࣱ مِّن نِّسَآءٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُنَّ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُنَّۖ وَلَا تَلۡمِزُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَلَا تَنَابَزُواْ بِٱلۡأَلۡقَٰبِۖ بِئۡسَ ٱلِٱسۡمُ ٱلۡفُسُوقُ بَعۡدَ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنِۚ وَمَن لَّمۡ يَتُبۡ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَ﴾ [Al-Hujurat 49:11]

### Three prohibitions
| Prohibition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mockery (*sukhriyah*) | Laughing at others to belittle them |
| Defaming (*lamz*) | Criticising and finding faults |
| Offensive nicknames | Calling people by names they dislike |

### "Perhaps they are better"
- The one mocked may be better before Allah. Outward appearance does not show the heart.

### "Do not defame yourselves"
- The verse says *yourselves*: the believers are like one body, and insulting a brother is insulting yourself.

### A bad name
- *Wretched is the name of disobedience after faith*: whoever does these things earns the label of *fusuq*.

### Today's application
- Bullying at school, mocking accents, humiliating comments online, and hurtful family nicknames all fall under this verse.

### Repentance
- *Whoever does not repent, those are the wrongdoers*: the door is open, but it must be entered.

### Lessons
- **Joke without hurting.**
- **Call people by names they love.**

## "The Most Noble of You in Allah's Sight Is the Most Pious": Quran 49:13 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن ذَكَرٖ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ شُعُوبٗا وَقَبَآئِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ أَكۡرَمَكُمۡ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَتۡقَىٰكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ [Al-Hujurat 49:13]

### To all people
- *O mankind*: a universal message, not only to Muslims.

### Three facts
| Fact | Meaning |
|---|---|
| From a male and a female | Same origin, same worth |
| Nations and tribes | Diversity is from Allah |
| *To know one another* | Diversity is for acquaintance, not pride |

### The only measure
- *The most noble of you before Allah is the most pious.* Not lineage, colour, wealth or nationality.

### In the Farewell Sermon
- The Prophet ﷺ said there is no superiority of an Arab over a non-Arab, nor of a non-Arab over an Arab, nor of white over black, nor black over white, except by taqwa (narrated by Ahmad).

### "All-Knowing, All-Aware"
- Taqwa is in the heart; only Allah knows its true level. So no one can claim it over others.

### Lessons
- **Respect every person.**
- **Compete in taqwa**, not status.
