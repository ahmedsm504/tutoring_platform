﴿فَأَمَّا ٱلۡيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقۡهَرۡ﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّآئِلَ فَلَا تَنۡهَرۡ﴾ [Ad-Duha 93:9-10]

## Mirroring the blessings
| Blessing | Command |
|---|---|
| *Did He not find you an orphan and shelter you?* | Do not oppress the orphan |
| *And find you lost and guide you?* | Do not repel the one who asks |
| *And find you in need and enrich you?* | Proclaim the favour of your Lord |

- As Allah honoured you, honour those in your former state.

## *Qahr* and *nahr*
- *Qahr*: humiliating and dominating, or taking their rights.
- *Nahr*: scolding and shouting. Some scholars include the one asking for **knowledge**.

## The orphan's guardian
- *I and the guardian of an orphan will be in Paradise like this*, and he joined his index and middle fingers (narrated by al-Bukhari).

## Lessons
- **Never shout at someone who asks**, even if you cannot give.
- **Answer your children's and students' questions** patiently.
