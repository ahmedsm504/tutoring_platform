"Fa-bi-ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban": "So which of the favours of your Lord will you deny?" This single sentence is repeated thirty-one times in Surah Ar-Rahman! It is as if, after every blessing, Allah asks us: this is one of My favours, so which of them will you deny? Ala' means favours or blessings, and the question is addressed to both jinn and humans.

At-Tirmidhi reported from Jabir that the Prophet ﷺ recited Surah Ar-Rahman to his companions and they stayed silent. He said: "I recited it to the jinn on the night of the jinn, and they responded better than you. Every time I reached: So which of the favours of your Lord will you deny, they said: None of Your favours, our Lord, do we deny, and all praise is Yours" (graded hasan by al-Albani).

Surah Ar-Rahman is the surah of blessings. It begins with Allah's name "Ar-Rahman", the Most Merciful, and then lists His blessings one by one: the Quran, the human being, speech, the sun and moon, the stars and trees, the sky, fruits and palms, the seas with their pearls and coral, and the great ships. Its rhythm is beautiful and children love repeating it. In this guide, we explain Surah Ar-Rahman to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ar-Rahman

- **Position**: surah 55, in juz 27.
- **Number of verses**: 78.
- **Type**: Meccan according to many scholars.
- **Name**: Ar-Rahman, "The Most Merciful". It is the only surah that begins with one of Allah's names.

### Activity: count the repeated verse
Listen to the surah with your child and give them a sheet of paper. Every time they hear "Fa-bi-ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban", they make a mark. At the end, they count: thirty-one!

## Lesson 1: The Most Merciful Taught the Quran

﴿ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ﴾ ﴿عَلَّمَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ﴾ ﴿خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ﴾ ﴿عَلَّمَهُ ٱلۡبَيَانَ﴾ (Quran 55:1-4)

### Explaining it to your child
"The very first blessing Allah mentions is: He taught the Quran! Even before mentioning that He created the human being. That is because the Quran is the greatest blessing. Then He created the human being, then taught him al-bayan, clear speech and expression. You can talk, read, write and say what is in your heart, and all of this is from Allah's teaching."

### Lesson for kids: the gift of speech
"Imagine if you couldn't speak! How would you ask for water? How would you tell your mum you love her? Speech is a huge blessing, so we use it for good: Quran, dhikr, and kind words."

### Activity: silent half hour
Play the "silent half hour" game: your child communicates only with gestures for thirty minutes. They will find it hard! Afterwards, say together: "Alhamdulillah for the gift of speech."

## Lesson 2: The Sun and Moon by Precise Calculation

﴿ٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُ بِحُسۡبَانٖ﴾ (Quran 55:5)

### Explaining it to your child
"The sun and moon move by a very precise calculation. That is why we know exactly what minute the sun will rise tomorrow, and when the moon will be full. They help us count days, months and years, and know our prayer times."

### Activity: the prayer timetable
Print the prayer timetable for this month and look at it with your child. Notice how Fajr and Maghrib shift by a minute or two every day in a perfect pattern. Then say: "The sun and moon by precise calculation."

## Lesson 3: The Balance

﴿وَٱلسَّمَآءَ رَفَعَهَا وَوَضَعَ ٱلۡمِيزَانَ﴾ ﴿أَلَّا تَطۡغَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡمِيزَانِ﴾ ﴿وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلۡوَزۡنَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ وَلَا تُخۡسِرُواْ ٱلۡمِيزَانَ﴾ (Quran 55:7-9)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah raised the sky high and set up the mizan, the balance, which means justice and fairness. He told us not to cheat in the balance: weigh fairly and never give less. A shopkeeper doesn't cheat on the scale, a student doesn't cheat in the exam, and a brother doesn't cheat his brother when sharing."

### Activity: fair sharing
Give your child ten dates or sweets and ask them to share fairly between themselves and a sibling. Did they share equally? Then say: "That is the balance Allah commanded."

## Lesson 4: The Earth and Its Gifts

﴿وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَضَعَهَا لِلۡأَنَامِ﴾ ﴿فِيهَا فَٰكِهَةࣱ وَٱلنَّخۡلُ ذَاتُ ٱلۡأَكۡمَامِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡحَبُّ ذُو ٱلۡعَصۡفِ وَٱلرَّيۡحَانُ﴾ (Quran 55:10-12)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah laid out the earth for all living beings. It has many fruits, palm trees with their date bunches wrapped in sheaths, grains with their husks and leaves, and sweet-smelling plants."

### Activity: the blessings market
Visit a fruit and vegetable market with your child and ask them to count the different fruits. With each one, say "Fa-bi-ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban". Then buy a fruit they have never tried and taste it together, thanking Allah.

## Lesson 5: Which of the Favours Will You Deny?

﴿فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ﴾ (Quran 55:13)

### Explaining it to your child
"After listing blessings, Allah asks: So which of the favours of your Lord will you deny, O jinn and humans? And the answer we say is: None of Your favours, our Lord, do we deny, and all praise is Yours."

### Activity: the beautiful reply
Teach your child to reply, when they hear the verse outside of obligatory prayer: "La bi-shay'in min ni'amika Rabbana nukadhdhib, fa-lakal-hamd", just as the jinn replied to the Prophet ﷺ.

## Lesson 6: The Two Seas, Pearls and Coral

﴿مَرَجَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ يَلۡتَقِيَانِ﴾ ﴿بَيۡنَهُمَا بَرۡزَخࣱ لَّا يَبۡغِيَانِ﴾ ﴿فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ﴾ ﴿يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهُمَا ٱللُّؤۡلُؤُ وَٱلۡمَرۡجَانُ﴾ (Quran 55:19-22)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah released two seas that meet, a fresh one and a salty one, or two different seas. Even though they meet, there is a barrier between them that people cannot see, so they don't completely mix. And from them come shining white pearls and beautiful red coral."

### A fun science fact
"Scientists found that where fresh and salty water meet, there is a zone between them where each keeps its own properties. And a pearl forms inside an oyster when a tiny grain gets inside it. The oyster covers it with shiny layers, year after year, until it becomes a pearl."

### Activity: make a pearl
Explain how pearls form, then make a "pearl" by wrapping white thread around a small bead, layer after layer. Say: "The oyster does this for years, by Allah's command."

## Lesson 7: Ships Like Mountains

﴿وَلَهُ ٱلۡجَوَارِ ٱلۡمُنشَـَٔاتُ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ كَٱلۡأَعۡلَٰمِ﴾ (Quran 55:24)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah owns the ships sailing in the sea, huge like mountains! Who taught people to build them? And who made the sea able to carry them? Allah."

## Lesson 8: Everything Will Pass Except Allah

﴿كُلُّ مَنۡ عَلَيۡهَا فَانٖ﴾ ﴿وَيَبۡقَىٰ وَجۡهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ﴾ (Quran 55:26-27)

### Explaining it to your child
"Everyone on earth will pass away one day, and only Allah remains, the Owner of Majesty and Honour. This world does not last, and what remains are the good deeds we did for Allah."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Hold fast to: Ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram" (at-Tirmidhi; graded authentic by al-Albani). In other words, call on Allah often with this name.

### Activity
Teach your child to include "Ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram" in their du'a, especially in sujud.

## Lesson 9: Every Day He Is Busy

﴿يَسۡـَٔلُهُۥ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ كُلَّ يَوۡمٍ هُوَ فِي شَأۡنٖ﴾ (Quran 55:29)

### Explaining it to your child
"Everyone in the heavens and the earth asks Allah for what they need: people, birds, animals and angels. And every day Allah is attending to matters: providing for one, healing another, relieving another, answering someone's du'a. Nothing distracts Him from anything else."

## Let's Read Together: The Reward for Good

﴿وَلِمَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ جَنَّتَانِ﴾ (Quran 55:46)

﴿هَلۡ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ إِلَّا ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنُ﴾ (Quran 55:60)

### Explaining it to your child
"Whoever fears standing before their Lord and obeys Him will have two gardens in Paradise! Then Allah asks: Is the reward for good anything but good? Whoever does good in this life, Allah will be good to them in the next. And whoever is kind to people, Allah is kind to them."

### Activity: a chain of kindness
Encourage your child to do one kind thing for someone today and notice how people return kindness. Then ask: "If people reward kindness with kindness, how much greater is Allah's reward?"

## Blessings We Can't See

Some blessings in the surah we can see with our eyes: the sun, the moon, fruit and the sea. But there are blessings we cannot see, and we need to notice them:

- **Air**: we breathe it every second without thinking.
- **Health**: we only notice it when we get sick.
- **Safety**: sleeping peacefully in our homes.
- **Family**: parents and siblings who love us.
- **Our minds**: we can think, learn and understand.

### Activity: hidden blessings
Ask your child to close their eyes for one minute, breathe slowly, and think of a blessing they cannot see. Then they open their eyes, tell you, and say: "Fa-bi-ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Blessings Jar"

Maryam complained a lot: "I don't have toys like my friend", "I hate vegetables", "It's too hot", "Why don't we travel like other families?" So her mother decided to teach her a beautiful lesson.

She brought an empty glass jar and wrote on it: "The Blessings Jar". She said: "Maryam, we'll read Surah Ar-Rahman together, and every time we hear: So which of the favours of your Lord will you deny, you'll write down one blessing you have and put it in the jar."

They began. "Ar-Rahman, taught the Quran": Maryam wrote, "I know some surahs by heart." "Created man, taught him speech": she wrote, "I can talk and read." "The sun and moon by precise calculation": she wrote, "I see the sun every day." "In it are fruits": she wrote, "I love mangoes and strawberries."

They continued to the end of the surah. By then the jar held thirty-one papers! Maryam stared at it. "Mum, I have so many blessings! I never noticed them."

Her mother said: "And those are only the ones we remembered in one hour. The blessings we don't even know about are far more." From that day, whenever Maryam started complaining, she looked at the jar, smiled and said: "None of Your favours, our Lord, do we deny, and all praise is Yours."

Ask your child: "What did Maryam do before the jar? What changed after? What blessings will you write?"

## What We Learn From Surah Ar-Rahman

- **The Quran** is the greatest blessing.
- **Speech** is a gift we use for good.
- **The universe** runs on precise calculation.
- **Be fair** in the balance and in everything.
- **The earth's gifts** are from Allah.
- **Thank Allah** after every blessing.
- **The wonders of the sea** are signs of Allah.
- **This world passes**, and Allah remains.
- **Good is rewarded with good**.

## Two-Week Memorization Plan

- **Day 1**: verses 1–4.
- **Day 2**: verses 5–9.
- **Day 3**: verses 10–13.
- **Day 4**: verses 14–18.
- **Day 5**: verses 19–23.
- **Day 6**: verses 24–28.
- **Day 7**: review verses 1–28.
- **Days 8–13**: the rest of the surah, about five verses a day.
- **Day 14**: recite the whole surah and celebrate!

2. What is the first blessing mentioned in the surah?

7. What is the reward for good?

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Why is the verse repeated so many times?
To remind jinn and humans of a blessing after each blessing and to reinforce gratitude. It is a beautiful style that settles the meaning in the heart, and it also makes the surah easy for children to join in.

### Should we reply to the verse during prayer?
The reply comes in the hadith about listening to the recitation, and some scholars encourage it outside obligatory prayer. In the obligatory prayer behind an imam, it is better to listen silently.

### Who is meant by "your Lord" in the dual form?
The address is to the jinn and humans, the two groups responsible for worshipping Allah.

### How can I make my child love the sound of Surah Ar-Rahman?
Play a calm recitation by a skilled reciter in the car or at bedtime. Children usually memorize the repeated verse first, then the rest follows easily.
