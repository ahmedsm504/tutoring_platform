﴿وَعِبَادُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡشُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ هَوۡنٗا وَإِذَا خَاطَبَهُمُ ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ قَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:63]

## An honoured title
- Allah attributes them to His name **ar-Rahman**, right after the polytheists asked: *And what is ar-Rahman?* (25:60).

## *Hawnan*
- With calm, dignity and humility, without arrogance and without affected weakness. The Prophet ﷺ walked briskly, as if descending a slope.

## Replying with peace
- *They say: peace*: words that keep them safe from sin; they do not answer foolishness with foolishness.

## Manners first
- This is the **first** quality listed for the servants of the Most Merciful, before night prayer and the rest.

## Lessons
- **Walk with humility.**
- **Do not argue with the foolish**: say peace and move on.
