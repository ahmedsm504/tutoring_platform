Surah Al 'Imran is one of the "two bright ones" (*az-zahrawan*). In Sahih Muslim, Abu Umamah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Recite the Quran, for it will come on the Day of Resurrection as an intercessor for its companions. Recite the two bright ones, Al-Baqarah and Al 'Imran, for they will come on the Day of Resurrection like two clouds or two canopies, pleading for their companions." The surah addresses the believers after the painful defeat at Uhud and teaches them how to stand firm after trials. It begins with the foundations of faith and the danger of following ambiguous verses, tells the story of the family of 'Imran, Maryam and 'Isa, calls the People of the Book to a common word, and gives the Muslim community its essential instructions: hold fast to the rope of Allah together, be the best nation that enjoins good, and race to forgiveness. It ends with ten verses the Prophet ﷺ would recite on waking at night.

This guide explains Surah Al 'Imran section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al 'Imran
- **Number of verses**: 200.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 3, in the third and fourth juz.
- **Name**: Al 'Imran means "the family of 'Imran", the family of Maryam.
- **Main themes**: firmness in faith, the story of Maryam and 'Isa, dialogue with the People of the Book, unity, the lessons of Uhud, and reflection on creation.

## Verses 1–9: The Living, the Sustainer and the Clear Verses
﴿الٓمٓ﴾ ﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُ﴾ ﴿نَزَّلَ عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ وَأَنزَلَ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلَ﴾ ﴿مِن قَبۡلُ هُدٗى لِّلنَّاسِ وَأَنزَلَ ٱلۡفُرۡقَانَۗ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ شَدِيدࣱۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزࣱ ذُو ٱنتِقَامٍ﴾ (Quran 3:1-4)

**"Alif Lam Mim. Allah, there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of existence."** He sent down the Book confirming what came before it, as He sent the Torah and the Gospel.

﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِنۡهُ ءَايَٰتࣱ مُّحۡكَمَٰتٌ هُنَّ أُمُّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَأُخَرُ مُتَشَٰبِهَٰتࣱۖ فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡ زَيۡغࣱ فَيَتَّبِعُونَ مَا تَشَٰبَهَ مِنۡهُ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ ٱلۡفِتۡنَةِ وَٱبۡتِغَآءَ تَأۡوِيلِهِۦۖ وَمَا يَعۡلَمُ تَأۡوِيلَهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُۗ وَٱلرَّـٰسِخُونَ فِي ٱلۡعِلۡمِ يَقُولُونَ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ كُلࣱّ مِّنۡ عِندِ رَبِّنَاۗ وَمَا يَذَّكَّرُ إِلَّآ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ (Quran 3:7)

**Clear and ambiguous verses**: **"It is He who has sent down to you the Book; in it are verses that are precise, they are the foundation of the Book, and others ambiguous. As for those in whose hearts is deviation, they will follow that of it which is ambiguous, seeking discord and seeking an interpretation suitable to them."** Those firm in knowledge say: **"We believe in it. All of it is from our Lord."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'A'ishah reported that the Prophet ﷺ recited this verse and said: "When you see those who follow what is ambiguous of it, they are the ones Allah named, so beware of them."

﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾ (Quran 3:8)

**"Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate after You have guided us and grant us from Yourself mercy. Indeed, You are the Bestower."** A du'a every believer needs.

## Verses 10–32: Desires, Tawhid and Love of Allah
﴿زُيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ حُبُّ ٱلشَّهَوَٰتِ مِنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ وَٱلۡبَنِينَ وَٱلۡقَنَٰطِيرِ ٱلۡمُقَنطَرَةِ مِنَ ٱلذَّهَبِ وَٱلۡفِضَّةِ وَٱلۡخَيۡلِ ٱلۡمُسَوَّمَةِ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ وَٱلۡحَرۡثِۗ ذَٰلِكَ مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَٱللَّهُ عِندَهُۥ حُسۡنُ ٱلۡمَـَٔابِ﴾ (Quran 3:14)

**"Beautified for people is the love of that which they desire: women, sons, heaped-up sums of gold and silver, fine branded horses, cattle and tilled land. That is the enjoyment of worldly life, but Allah has with Him the best return."**

﴿شَهِدَ ٱللَّهُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ قَآئِمَۢا بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۚ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمُۗ وَمَا ٱخۡتَلَفَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡعِلۡمُ بَغۡيَۢا بَيۡنَهُمۡۗ وَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ﴾ (Quran 3:18-19)

**"Allah witnesses that there is no deity except Him, and so do the angels and those of knowledge."** **"Indeed, the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam."**

﴿قُلِ ٱللَّهُمَّ مَٰلِكَ ٱلۡمُلۡكِ تُؤۡتِي ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتَنزِعُ ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مِمَّن تَشَآءُ وَتُعِزُّ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتُذِلُّ مَن تَشَآءُۖ بِيَدِكَ ٱلۡخَيۡرُۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿تُولِجُ ٱلَّيۡلَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ وَتُولِجُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فِي ٱلَّيۡلِۖ وَتُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ وَتُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَيِّتَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَيِّۖ وَتَرۡزُقُ مَن تَشَآءُ بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ﴾ (Quran 3:26-27)

**"Say: O Allah, Owner of Sovereignty, You give sovereignty to whom You will and You take sovereignty away from whom You will. You honour whom You will and You humble whom You will. In Your hand is all good."**

﴿قُلۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تُحِبُّونَ ٱللَّهَ فَٱتَّبِعُونِي يُحۡبِبۡكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ ذُنُوبَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 3:31)

**"Say: If you should love Allah, then follow me, so Allah will love you and forgive you your sins."** Love of Allah is proven by following the Prophet ﷺ.

## Verses 33–63: The Family of 'Imran, Maryam and 'Isa
﴿إِذۡ قَالَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتُ عِمۡرَٰنَ رَبِّ إِنِّي نَذَرۡتُ لَكَ مَا فِي بَطۡنِي مُحَرَّرٗا فَتَقَبَّلۡ مِنِّيٓۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا وَضَعَتۡهَا قَالَتۡ رَبِّ إِنِّي وَضَعۡتُهَآ أُنثَىٰ وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا وَضَعَتۡ وَلَيۡسَ ٱلذَّكَرُ كَٱلۡأُنثَىٰۖ وَإِنِّي سَمَّيۡتُهَا مَرۡيَمَ وَإِنِّيٓ أُعِيذُهَا بِكَ وَذُرِّيَّتَهَا مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ ٱلرَّجِيمِ﴾ ﴿فَتَقَبَّلَهَا رَبُّهَا بِقَبُولٍ حَسَنٖ وَأَنۢبَتَهَا نَبَاتًا حَسَنٗا وَكَفَّلَهَا زَكَرِيَّاۖ كُلَّمَا دَخَلَ عَلَيۡهَا زَكَرِيَّا ٱلۡمِحۡرَابَ وَجَدَ عِندَهَا رِزۡقٗاۖ قَالَ يَٰمَرۡيَمُ أَنَّىٰ لَكِ هَٰذَاۖ قَالَتۡ هُوَ مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرۡزُقُ مَن يَشَآءُ بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٍ﴾ (Quran 3:35-37)

**The wife of 'Imran** vowed her unborn child to Allah's service. When she delivered a girl, she said: **"I have named her Maryam, and I seek refuge for her in You and for her descendants from Satan."** **"So her Lord accepted her with good acceptance and caused her to grow in a good manner."** Whenever Zakariyya entered her prayer chamber, he found provision with her. **"She said: It is from Allah. Indeed, Allah provides for whom He wills without account."**

﴿هُنَالِكَ دَعَا زَكَرِيَّا رَبَّهُۥۖ قَالَ رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي مِن لَّدُنكَ ذُرِّيَّةٗ طَيِّبَةًۖ إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ ٱلدُّعَآءِ﴾ (Quran 3:38)

**"At that, Zakariyya called upon his Lord, saying: My Lord, grant me from Yourself a good offspring."** Seeing Allah's provision to Maryam inspired him to ask for a child in old age.

﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَتِ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ يَٰمَرۡيَمُ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱصۡطَفَىٰكِ وَطَهَّرَكِ وَٱصۡطَفَىٰكِ عَلَىٰ نِسَآءِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿يَٰمَرۡيَمُ ٱقۡنُتِي لِرَبِّكِ وَٱسۡجُدِي وَٱرۡكَعِي مَعَ ٱلرَّـٰكِعِينَ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ أَنۢبَآءِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ نُوحِيهِ إِلَيۡكَۚ وَمَا كُنتَ لَدَيۡهِمۡ إِذۡ يُلۡقُونَ أَقۡلَٰمَهُمۡ أَيُّهُمۡ يَكۡفُلُ مَرۡيَمَ وَمَا كُنتَ لَدَيۡهِمۡ إِذۡ يَخۡتَصِمُونَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ قَالَتِ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ يَٰمَرۡيَمُ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُبَشِّرُكِ بِكَلِمَةٖ مِّنۡهُ ٱسۡمُهُ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ وَجِيهٗا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَمِنَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبِينَ﴾ ﴿وَيُكَلِّمُ ٱلنَّاسَ فِي ٱلۡمَهۡدِ وَكَهۡلٗا وَمِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَتۡ رَبِّ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي وَلَدࣱ وَلَمۡ يَمۡسَسۡنِي بَشَرࣱۖ قَالَ كَذَٰلِكِ ٱللَّهُ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ إِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمۡرٗا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾ (Quran 3:42-47)

**The angels said: "O Maryam, indeed Allah has chosen you and purified you and chosen you above the women of the worlds."** Then the glad tidings of a son, **'Isa the Messiah**, without a father. **"Thus Allah creates what He wills. When He decrees a matter, He only says to it: Be, and it is."**

﴿إِنَّ مَثَلَ عِيسَىٰ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ ءَادَمَۖ خَلَقَهُۥ مِن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ قَالَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾ (Quran 3:59)

**"Indeed, the example of 'Isa to Allah is like that of Adam. He created him from dust; then He said to him: Be, and he was."** If Adam was created without father or mother, 'Isa's birth without a father is no reason to call him divine.

## Verses 64–91: A Common Word
﴿قُلۡ يَـٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ تَعَالَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ كَلِمَةٖ سَوَآءِۭ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَكُمۡ أَلَّا نَعۡبُدَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَ وَلَا نُشۡرِكَ بِهِۦ شَيۡـٔٗا وَلَا يَتَّخِذَ بَعۡضُنَا بَعۡضًا أَرۡبَابٗا مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۚ فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَقُولُواْ ٱشۡهَدُواْ بِأَنَّا مُسۡلِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 3:64)

**"Say: O People of the Scripture, come to a word that is equitable between us and you: that we will not worship except Allah and not associate anything with Him and not take one another as lords instead of Allah."** In Sahih al-Bukhari, the Prophet's ﷺ letter to Heraclius, the Byzantine emperor, quoted this verse.

## Verses 92–101: Spending What You Love
﴿لَن تَنَالُواْ ٱلۡبِرَّ حَتَّىٰ تُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِن شَيۡءٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِهِۦ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 3:92)

**"Never will you attain righteousness until you spend from that which you love."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Anas reported that when this verse was revealed, Abu Talhah gave away Bayruha', his most beloved garden near the Prophet's ﷺ mosque. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Excellent! That is profitable wealth."

﴿إِنَّ أَوَّلَ بَيۡتٖ وُضِعَ لِلنَّاسِ لَلَّذِي بِبَكَّةَ مُبَارَكٗا وَهُدٗى لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿فِيهِ ءَايَٰتُۢ بَيِّنَٰتࣱ مَّقَامُ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَۖ وَمَن دَخَلَهُۥ كَانَ ءَامِنٗاۗ وَلِلَّهِ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ حِجُّ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ مَنِ ٱسۡتَطَاعَ إِلَيۡهِ سَبِيلٗاۚ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِيٌّ عَنِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 3:96-97)

**"Indeed, the first House established for mankind was that at Bakkah, blessed and a guidance for the worlds."** **"And due to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House, for whoever is able to find thereto a way."**

## Verses 102–120: Unity and the Best Nation
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ حَقَّ تُقَاتِهِۦ وَلَا تَمُوتُنَّ إِلَّا وَأَنتُم مُّسۡلِمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱعۡتَصِمُواْ بِحَبۡلِ ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعٗا وَلَا تَفَرَّقُواْۚ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ كُنتُمۡ أَعۡدَآءٗ فَأَلَّفَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِكُمۡ فَأَصۡبَحۡتُم بِنِعۡمَتِهِۦٓ إِخۡوَٰنٗا وَكُنتُمۡ عَلَىٰ شَفَا حُفۡرَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ فَأَنقَذَكُم مِّنۡهَاۗ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 3:102-103)

**"O you who believe, fear Allah as He should be feared and do not die except as Muslims. And hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not become divided."** Remember how you were enemies and Allah brought your hearts together, **"and you became, by His favour, brothers"**.

﴿وَلۡتَكُن مِّنكُمۡ أُمَّةࣱ يَدۡعُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡخَيۡرِ وَيَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِۚ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 3:104)

**"And let there be from you a nation inviting to good, enjoining what is right and forbidding what is wrong."**

﴿كُنتُمۡ خَيۡرَ أُمَّةٍ أُخۡرِجَتۡ لِلنَّاسِ تَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَتَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَتُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِۗ وَلَوۡ ءَامَنَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۚ مِّنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 3:110)

**"You are the best nation produced for mankind. You enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong and believe in Allah."** The honour is tied to the role.

## Verses 121–175: The Lessons of Uhud
﴿۞وَسَارِعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ فِي ٱلسَّرَّآءِ وَٱلضَّرَّآءِ وَٱلۡكَٰظِمِينَ ٱلۡغَيۡظَ وَٱلۡعَافِينَ عَنِ ٱلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا فَعَلُواْ فَٰحِشَةً أَوۡ ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ ذَكَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَٱسۡتَغۡفَرُواْ لِذُنُوبِهِمۡ وَمَن يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَلَمۡ يُصِرُّواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلُواْ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ جَزَآؤُهُم مَّغۡفِرَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡ وَجَنَّـٰتࣱ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ وَنِعۡمَ أَجۡرُ ٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ﴾ (Quran 3:133-136)

**"And hasten to forgiveness from your Lord and a garden as wide as the heavens and earth, prepared for the righteous: who spend during ease and hardship, who restrain anger and who pardon people; and Allah loves the doers of good. And those who, when they commit an immorality or wrong themselves, remember Allah and seek forgiveness for their sins."** The righteous are not sinless; they repent quickly.

﴿وَلَا تَهِنُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَوۡنَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِن يَمۡسَسۡكُمۡ قَرۡحࣱ فَقَدۡ مَسَّ ٱلۡقَوۡمَ قَرۡحࣱ مِّثۡلُهُۥۚ وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡأَيَّامُ نُدَاوِلُهَا بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلِيَعۡلَمَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَيَتَّخِذَ مِنكُمۡ شُهَدَآءَۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 3:139-140)

**"So do not weaken and do not grieve, and you will be superior if you are true believers."** **"If a wound should touch you, there has already touched the opposing people a wound similar to it. And these days We alternate among the people."**

﴿وَمَا مُحَمَّدٌ إِلَّا رَسُولࣱ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهِ ٱلرُّسُلُۚ أَفَإِيْن مَّاتَ أَوۡ قُتِلَ ٱنقَلَبۡتُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡقَٰبِكُمۡۚ وَمَن يَنقَلِبۡ عَلَىٰ عَقِبَيۡهِ فَلَن يَضُرَّ ٱللَّهَ شَيۡـٔٗاۚ وَسَيَجۡزِي ٱللَّهُ ٱلشَّـٰكِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 3:144)

**"Muhammad is not but a messenger. Other messengers have passed on before him. So if he was to die or be killed, would you turn back on your heels?"** In Sahih al-Bukhari, when the Prophet ﷺ died, Abu Bakr recited this verse to the stunned people, and it was as if they had never heard it before.

﴿وَلَقَدۡ صَدَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَعۡدَهُۥٓ إِذۡ تَحُسُّونَهُم بِإِذۡنِهِۦۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فَشِلۡتُمۡ وَتَنَٰزَعۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِ وَعَصَيۡتُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ مَآ أَرَىٰكُم مَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ مِنكُم مَّن يُرِيدُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمِنكُم مَّن يُرِيدُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۚ ثُمَّ صَرَفَكُمۡ عَنۡهُمۡ لِيَبۡتَلِيَكُمۡۖ وَلَقَدۡ عَفَا عَنكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو فَضۡلٍ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿۞إِذۡ تُصۡعِدُونَ وَلَا تَلۡوُۥنَ عَلَىٰٓ أَحَدٖ وَٱلرَّسُولُ يَدۡعُوكُمۡ فِيٓ أُخۡرَىٰكُمۡ فَأَثَٰبَكُمۡ غَمَّۢا بِغَمّٖ لِّكَيۡلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمۡ وَلَا مَآ أَصَٰبَكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ أَنزَلَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱلۡغَمِّ أَمَنَةٗ نُّعَاسٗا يَغۡشَىٰ طَآئِفَةٗ مِّنكُمۡۖ وَطَآئِفَةࣱ قَدۡ أَهَمَّتۡهُمۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ يَظُنُّونَ بِٱللَّهِ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡحَقِّ ظَنَّ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِۖ يَقُولُونَ هَل لَّنَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ مِن شَيۡءٖۗ قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡأَمۡرَ كُلَّهُۥ لِلَّهِۗ يُخۡفُونَ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِم مَّا لَا يُبۡدُونَ لَكَۖ يَقُولُونَ لَوۡ كَانَ لَنَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ شَيۡءࣱ مَّا قُتِلۡنَا هَٰهُنَاۗ قُل لَّوۡ كُنتُمۡ فِي بُيُوتِكُمۡ لَبَرَزَ ٱلَّذِينَ كُتِبَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَتۡلُ إِلَىٰ مَضَاجِعِهِمۡۖ وَلِيَبۡتَلِيَ ٱللَّهُ مَا فِي صُدُورِكُمۡ وَلِيُمَحِّصَ مَا فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ تَوَلَّوۡاْ مِنكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡتَقَى ٱلۡجَمۡعَانِ إِنَّمَا ٱسۡتَزَلَّهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ بِبَعۡضِ مَا كَسَبُواْۖ وَلَقَدۡ عَفَا ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٌ حَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 3:152-155)

**The archers' mistake**: at Uhud, some archers left their positions on the hill when they saw the enemy fleeing, and the Muslims were attacked from behind. **"Among you are some who desire this world, and among you are some who desire the hereafter."** Yet **"Allah has already forgiven them"**.

﴿فَبِمَا رَحۡمَةٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ لِنتَ لَهُمۡۖ وَلَوۡ كُنتَ فَظًّا غَلِيظَ ٱلۡقَلۡبِ لَٱنفَضُّواْ مِنۡ حَوۡلِكَۖ فَٱعۡفُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ وَشَاوِرۡهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِۖ فَإِذَا عَزَمۡتَ فَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَوَكِّلِينَ﴾ (Quran 3:159)

**"So by mercy from Allah, you were lenient with them. And if you had been rude and harsh in heart, they would have dispersed from around you. So pardon them and ask forgiveness for them and consult them in the matter. And when you have decided, then rely upon Allah."** After the very people whose mistake caused the defeat, the Prophet ﷺ was told to pardon, pray for and consult them.

﴿وَلَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قُتِلُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَمۡوَٰتَۢاۚ بَلۡ أَحۡيَآءٌ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ يُرۡزَقُونَ﴾ ﴿فَرِحِينَ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَيَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ بِٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يَلۡحَقُواْ بِهِم مِّنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ أَلَّا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾ (Quran 3:169-170)

**"And never think of those who have been killed in the cause of Allah as dead. Rather, they are alive with their Lord, receiving provision, rejoicing in what Allah has bestowed upon them."**

﴿ٱلَّذِينَ قَالَ لَهُمُ ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ قَدۡ جَمَعُواْ لَكُمۡ فَٱخۡشَوۡهُمۡ فَزَادَهُمۡ إِيمَٰنٗا وَقَالُواْ حَسۡبُنَا ٱللَّهُ وَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡوَكِيلُ﴾ (Quran 3:173)

**"Those to whom people said: Indeed, the people have gathered against you, so fear them. But it merely increased them in faith, and they said: Sufficient for us is Allah, and He is the best Disposer of affairs."** In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas said these words were said by Ibrahim when thrown into the fire and by Muhammad ﷺ when told the enemy had gathered.

## Verses 176–189: Tests and Death
﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۗ وَإِنَّمَا تُوَفَّوۡنَ أُجُورَكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۖ فَمَن زُحۡزِحَ عَنِ ٱلنَّارِ وَأُدۡخِلَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ فَقَدۡ فَازَۗ وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡغُرُورِ﴾ (Quran 3:185)

**"Every soul will taste death, and you will only be given your full compensation on the Day of Resurrection. So he who is drawn away from the Fire and admitted to Paradise has attained success. And what is the life of this world except the enjoyment of delusion."**

﴿۞لَتُبۡلَوُنَّ فِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنفُسِكُمۡ وَلَتَسۡمَعُنَّ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ وَمِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُوٓاْ أَذٗى كَثِيرٗاۚ وَإِن تَصۡبِرُواْ وَتَتَّقُواْ فَإِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾ (Quran 3:186)

**"You will surely be tested in your possessions and in yourselves."**

## Verses 190–200: The Last Ten Verses
﴿إِنَّ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَذۡكُرُونَ ٱللَّهَ قِيَٰمٗا وَقُعُودٗا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِهِمۡ وَيَتَفَكَّرُونَ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقۡتَ هَٰذَا بَٰطِلٗا سُبۡحَٰنَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ ﴿رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ مَن تُدۡخِلِ ٱلنَّارَ فَقَدۡ أَخۡزَيۡتَهُۥۖ وَمَا لِلظَّـٰلِمِينَ مِنۡ أَنصَارٖ﴾ ﴿رَّبَّنَآ إِنَّنَا سَمِعۡنَا مُنَادِيٗا يُنَادِي لِلۡإِيمَٰنِ أَنۡ ءَامِنُواْ بِرَبِّكُمۡ فَـَٔامَنَّاۚ رَبَّنَا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَكَفِّرۡ عَنَّا سَيِّـَٔاتِنَا وَتَوَفَّنَا مَعَ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارِ﴾ ﴿رَبَّنَا وَءَاتِنَا مَا وَعَدتَّنَا عَلَىٰ رُسُلِكَ وَلَا تُخۡزِنَا يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۖ إِنَّكَ لَا تُخۡلِفُ ٱلۡمِيعَادَ﴾ (Quran 3:190-194)

**"Indeed, in the creation of the heavens and the earth and the alternation of the night and the day are signs for those of understanding, who remember Allah while standing or sitting or lying on their sides and give thought to the creation of the heavens and the earth, saying: Our Lord, You did not create this aimlessly; exalted are You; then protect us from the punishment of the Fire."**

In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas described spending the night at the house of his aunt Maymunah. The Prophet ﷺ woke in the night, wiped sleep from his face and recited the last ten verses of Al 'Imran, then made wudu' and prayed.

﴿فَٱسۡتَجَابَ لَهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ أَنِّي لَآ أُضِيعُ عَمَلَ عَٰمِلٖ مِّنكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰۖ بَعۡضُكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡضٖۖ فَٱلَّذِينَ هَاجَرُواْ وَأُخۡرِجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِمۡ وَأُوذُواْ فِي سَبِيلِي وَقَٰتَلُواْ وَقُتِلُواْ لَأُكَفِّرَنَّ عَنۡهُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ وَلَأُدۡخِلَنَّهُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ ثَوَابٗا مِّنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱللَّهُ عِندَهُۥ حُسۡنُ ٱلثَّوَابِ﴾ (Quran 3:195)

**"And their Lord responded to them: Never will I allow to be lost the work of any worker among you, whether male or female; you are of one another."**

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱصۡبِرُواْ وَصَابِرُواْ وَرَابِطُواْ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 3:200)

**"O you who believe, persevere and endure and remain stationed and fear Allah that you may be successful."** Patience, out-patience, steadfastness and taqwa: the final instruction after a long surah of trials.

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Hayy al-Qayyum**: the Ever-Living, the Sustainer.
- **Muhkamat**: precise verses.
- **Mutashabihat**: ambiguous verses.
- **Zaygh**: deviation.
- **Ar-Rasikhun fil-'ilm**: those firm in knowledge.
- **Mihrab**: prayer chamber.
- **Kalimah sawa'**: a common, equitable word.
- **Al-Birr**: righteousness.
- **Bakkah**: Makkah.
- **Habl Allah**: the rope of Allah.
- **Al-Kazimin al-ghayz**: those who restrain anger.
- **Nudawiluha**: We alternate them.
- **Fazzan**: rude.
- **Ghalizh al-qalb**: harsh in heart.
- **Rabitu**: remain stationed, steadfast.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Let not our hearts deviate after You have guided us"**: guidance can be lost; we ask to keep it.
2. **"In Your hand is all good"**: complete reliance.
3. **"Like Adam"**: a simple argument that settles a great question.
4. **"Hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together"**: unity as a rope.
5. **"Had you been harsh in heart, they would have dispersed"**: leadership through mercy.
6. **"Alive with their Lord"**: the martyrs' true state.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Alif Lam Mim*; when joined to *Allah*, the meem is read with a fathah and may be stretched six counts or shortened to two.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *linta lahum* (verse 159), the noon sakinah is hidden before the ta'.
- **Izhar**: in *fazzan ghaliza* (verse 159), the tanween before the throat letter ghayn is pronounced clearly.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *qiyaman wa qu'udan* (verse 191), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna ad-dina* (verse 19), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Baqarah before it ended with "Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us if we forget", and Al 'Imran begins with "Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate". They are the "two bright ones". Surah An-Nisa' after it opens with "O mankind, fear your Lord", continuing the closing command of Al 'Imran: "fear Allah that you may succeed".

## Lessons From Uhud
1. **Obedience to leadership** protects the group.
2. **Love of this world** can turn victory into defeat.
3. **Days alternate**: victory and loss are tests.
4. **Do not grieve or weaken.**
5. **Leaders must be gentle**, forgive and consult.
6. **Martyrs are alive**: loss in Allah's path is not loss.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Pray "Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate".**
2. **Follow the Prophet ﷺ to prove love of Allah.**
3. **Give from what you love.**
4. **Hold to the community and avoid division.**
5. **Enjoin good and forbid evil.**
6. **Restrain anger and pardon people.**
7. **Repent quickly after mistakes.**
8. **Recite the last ten verses at night.**
9. **Be patient and steadfast.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al 'Imran
- **Weeks 1–2**: verses 1–63.
- **Weeks 3–4**: verses 64–120.
- **Weeks 5–6**: verses 121–175.
- **Week 7**: verses 176–200.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them the story of Maryam** and her provision in the prayer chamber.
- **Tell them about Abu Talhah's garden** and giving what we love.
- **Recite the last ten verses together** at night.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why are Al-Baqarah and Al 'Imran called the "two bright ones"?
The Prophet ﷺ called them *az-zahrawan* and said they will come on the Day of Resurrection like two clouds pleading for their companions, as in Sahih Muslim.

### What are the clear and ambiguous verses?
Clear verses are precise and form the foundation; ambiguous verses carry more than one possible meaning and must be understood in light of the clear ones.

### What does "the example of 'Isa is like that of Adam" mean?
Allah created Adam without father or mother, so creating 'Isa without a father is no reason to consider him divine.

### What is the "rope of Allah"?
The Quran and the religion of Islam, which the believers are commanded to hold together.

### What lessons does the surah draw from Uhud?
Obedience, the danger of loving this world, patience, the alternation of days, and leading with mercy and consultation.

### What are the last ten verses of Al 'Imran?
Verses 191–200, which the Prophet ﷺ recited when waking at night, as Ibn 'Abbas reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al 'Imran?
200 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al 'Imran teaches a community how to stand firm after a painful defeat: hold to the rope of Allah together, keep your hearts from deviating, spend from what you love, restrain your anger, repent quickly, and lead with mercy. It shows Maryam's devotion and the truth about 'Isa, and it ends with the night verses of those who reflect on creation and say: Our Lord, You did not create this in vain.
