## 10 Screen-Free Games to Help Kids Revise the Quran at Home

Children love memorizing something new, but revision often feels like boring repetition to them. The solution is to make revision a **game**. Here are ten games that need nothing more than simple items from around the house.

### 1. Finish the Verse
Read the first two words of a verse and let your child finish it. Give a star for each correct answer, then swap roles so they test you too.

### 2. Pass the Ball
Sit in a circle. Whoever holds the ball recites a verse, then throws it to someone else, who recites **the next verse**. Great for siblings and for linking verses together.

### 3. The Surah Box
Write the names of memorized surahs on folded slips of paper in a box. Your child draws one and recites that surah. Add some fun slips: "Recite it in a whisper" or "Recite it standing on one foot."

### 4. The Verse Ladder
Draw a ladder on a large sheet of paper. For every correct verse, your child climbs one step. A small reward waits at the top.

### 5. Which Surah?
Recite a verse from the middle of a surah and let your child guess its name. For older kids, make it harder: they guess the verse number too.

### 6. The Missing Verse
Recite a surah and deliberately skip a verse or a word. Your child spots it and says it. Kids love "catching" grown-ups making mistakes!

### 7. Card Sorting
Write the verses of a short surah on separate cards, shuffle them, and ask your child to put them in order. For little ones, use the words of a single verse instead.

### 8. The Little Broadcaster
Your child stands like a reciter on the radio while you record them on your phone (without them looking at the screen). Then listen together, celebrate it, and send it to their teacher.

### 9. Treasure Hunt
Hide slips of paper around the house, each with a surah name. Every time your child finds a slip and recites the surah, they get a clue to the next one, until they reach the "treasure".

### 10. The Weekly Family Quiz
Pick a fixed day each week for a mix of "finish the verse", "which surah?" and "what do you know about this surah's story?". Make the prize shared: everyone wins if the family reaches a common goal, so nobody feels like a loser.

### Tips for making games work
- **One game per session**, no longer than 15 minutes.
- **Focus on older surahs** that need revision, not just the new ones.
- **Correct gently** without stopping the game.
- **Rotate games** every week so it stays fresh.

### For days when screens are fine
Purposeful digital games are fine on some days. The [student portal](/student-portal/games/) has games like "Order the Verse" and "Listen and Guess the Surah", built on your child's own surahs, where they earn stars.

## Keeping Your Quran Routine While Traveling

Travel breaks routines, but it can also give you **extra time** for the Quran.

### The Prophet's example
- ʿAbdullah ibn Mughaffal said he saw the Prophet ﷺ on the day of the Conquest of Makkah, on his camel, reciting Surah Al-Fath in a beautiful voice [Al-Bukhari].

### Use the waiting time
| Moment | What to do |
|---|---|
| Airport or station | Read your daily portion |
| Plane or train | Revise from memory, quietly |
| Car | Listen to a reciter and repeat |

### Mushaf or app?
- A **small Mushaf** is ideal.
- If not, use a **trusted app** with the same Madinah page layout as your Mushaf.

### Adjust, don't stop
- **Halve** your portion if needed, but keep something every day.
- **Revise** instead of memorizing new pages on travel days.

### Revise from memory
- Long journeys are perfect for **reciting a juz** you know, from start to end.

## Rabbana Du'as in the Quran: 10 Supplications Beginning with "Our Lord"

Many of the most powerful du'as in the Quran begin with **Rabbanā** (Our Lord). Learning them gives you words that Allah Himself taught.

| # | Du'a | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ﴿رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٗ وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ حَسَنَةٗ وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ [Al-Baqarah: 201] | Good in this world and the next |
| 2 | ﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾ [Al-Imran: 8] | Keep our hearts firm |
| 3 | ﴿رَبَّنَا ظَلَمۡنَآ أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمۡ تَغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَتَرۡحَمۡنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ﴾ [Al-A'raf: 23] | The du'a of Adam and Hawwa |
| 4 | ﴿رَبَّنَا هَبۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعۡيُنٖ وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا﴾ [Al-Furqan: 74] | Righteous spouses and children |
| 5 | ﴿رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلۡحِسَابُ﴾ [Ibrahim: 41] | Forgiveness for parents and believers |
| 6 | ﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذۡنَآ إِن نَّسِينَآ أَوۡ أَخۡطَأۡنَاۚ﴾ [Al-Baqarah: 286] | Don't hold us to our mistakes |
| 7 | ﴿رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةٗ وَهَيِّئۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَمۡرِنَا رَشَدٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf: 10] | Mercy and guidance in our affairs |
| 8 | ﴿رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلۡ دُعَآءِ﴾ [Ibrahim: 40] | Accept our du'a |

### Two more du'as without "Rabbana"
- ﴿رَّبِّ زِدۡنِي عِلۡمٗا﴾ [Ta-Ha: 114]: for knowledge.
- ﴿لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ [Al-Anbiya: 87]: the du'a of Yunus in distress.

### Using them
- **In sujood** and at the end of prayer before the salam.
- **Learn one a week** with its meaning.
- **Teach your children** a short one like Al-Baqarah 201.

## Revising Your Quran in Night Prayer (Qiyam): A Practical Method

Allah says: ﴿إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ ٱلَّيۡلِ هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطۡـٔٗا وَأَقۡوَمُ قِيلًا﴾ [Al-Muzzammil: 6]. The night is the best time for the Quran to settle in the heart.

### Why revise in prayer?
- **No Mushaf** in front of you, so you discover what you really know.
- **Presence of heart**: you recite slowly and with reflection.
- **Double reward**: prayer and revision together.

### How much?
| Your level | Per night |
|---|---|
| Beginner | Two rak'ahs with a quarter-page each |
| Intermediate | Two to four rak'ahs with half a juz |
| Advanced | A full juz across your rak'ahs |

### Start small
- **Two rak'ahs before sleep** if waking up is hard.
- Increase slowly as it becomes a habit.

### A mistakes notebook
- After prayer, **write down** where you hesitated.
- **Revise those places** the next day from the Mushaf.

### A weekly rotation
| Night | Revise |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | One part of your memorization each night |
| 6 | Your weakest pages |
| 7 | Rest or light recitation |

## Ruqyah with the Quran: What Is Authentic and How to Do It Yourself

**Ruqyah** is reciting Quran and authentic supplications for protection and healing. It was practised by the Prophet ﷺ himself.

### What is authentic
| What | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Al-Fatihah | A companion recited it over a man stung by a scorpion and he recovered. The Prophet ﷺ said: «How did you know it is a ruqyah?» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim] |
| The muʿawwidhat | When ill, the Prophet ﷺ recited them over himself and blew lightly [Al-Bukhari and Muslim] |
| Ayat al-Kursi | Protection for whoever recites it before sleep [Al-Bukhari] |

### Authentic du'as
- «Adh-hibil-baʾs, Rabban-nās, ishfi antash-shāfī, lā shifāʾa illā shifāʾuk, shifāʾan lā yughādiru saqamā» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].
- Place your hand on the pain, say *Bismillāh* three times, then seven times: «Aʿūdhu billāhi wa qudratihi min sharri mā ajidu wa uḥādhir» [Muslim].

### Conditions scholars set
1. It is with the **words of Allah**, His names and attributes, or authentic du'as.
2. It is in **Arabic** or in words whose meaning is understood.
3. You believe it works **only by Allah's will**, not by itself.
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «There is no harm in ruqyah as long as it contains no shirk» [Muslim].

### Doing it yourself
- **No intermediary needed**: recite over yourself and your children, as the Prophet ﷺ did.
- **Recite** Al-Fatihah, Ayat al-Kursi and the muʿawwidhat, blow lightly into your hands and wipe over your body.
- **Repeat** with certainty and presence of heart.

### Be careful
- Avoid anyone who asks for your mother's name, uses strange words, or tells you to stop seeing a doctor.
- **Ruqyah and medical treatment go together.**

## Spaced Repetition for Quran Memorization: Remember More, Forget Less

The Prophet ﷺ said the Quran escapes the memory faster than camels escape their ropes [Al-Bukhari and Muslim]. **Spaced repetition** is a simple way to fight that.

### The idea
- After you memorize something, you start to forget it.
- If you **review it just before you forget**, it lasts longer each time.
- So the gaps between reviews **grow**: one day, three days, a week, a month.

### A simple schedule
| Review | When |
|---|---|
| 1 | Same day, a few hours later |
| 2 | Next day |
| 3 | After 3 days |
| 4 | After 7 days |
| 5 | After 30 days |
| Then | Monthly in your regular revision |

### Tracking it
- **A notebook**: write the page number and the next review date.
- **A spreadsheet or flashcard app**: let it remind you.

### Combining with the classic method
| Classic part | Spaced repetition role |
|---|---|
| New lesson | Day 0 |
| Recent revision (last week) | Reviews 2–4 |
| Old revision (the rest) | Monthly cycle |

### Tips
- **Weak pages** get shorter gaps.
- **Strong pages** can have longer gaps, but never stop revising them.

## Summer Quran Program for Kids at Home: An 8-Week Plan

Summer gives kids lots of free time, and much of it often disappears into screens. With a little planning, these weeks can become a real leap in your child's relationship with the Quran, **without feeling like school**.

### Principles
- **One hour a day at most**, split into short blocks.
- **Five days a week**, two days off.
- **New memorization + revision + something fun** every day.
- **A celebration at the end.**

### Daily schedule
| When | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---|
| After breakfast | New memorization | 15–20 min |
| Afternoon | Revision game | 10–15 min |
| Late afternoon / evening | A Quran story or craft | 15 min |
| Bedtime | Listen to the new surah | 5–10 min |

### The 8-week plan (for a child working through Juz Amma)
| Week | Memorization | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revise everything memorized so far | Make a "summer star chart" |
| 2 | One new short surah | Draw the surah's story |
| 3 | One new surah | Family quiz night |
| 4 | Revise weeks 2–3 + a short surah | Visit a local mosque |
| 5 | One new surah | Arrange-the-verse cards |
| 6 | One new surah | Record a "radio show" of their surahs |
| 7 | One new surah | Stories of the Prophets mentioned |
| 8 | Full revision | End-of-summer celebration |

Pick surahs based on where your child is. For little ones, one surah every two weeks is plenty.

### Fun activity ideas
- **Stories of the Prophets:** Yunus and the whale, Nuh and the ark, the army of elephants.
- **Drawing:** your child illustrates a scene from the surah they memorized.
- **Family quizzes:** "finish the verse", "which surah is this?". See [screen-free Quran revision games](/blog/quran-revision-games-kids-english/#10-Screen-Free-Games-to-Help-Kids-Revise-the-Quran-at-Home).
- **Acts of kindness:** link verses like ﴿فَأَمَّا ٱلۡيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقۡهَرۡ﴾ to a real good deed.

### End-of-summer celebration
- Your child recites what they memorized for the family.
- A "Summer Quran Champion" certificate.
- A small gift and a family outing.
