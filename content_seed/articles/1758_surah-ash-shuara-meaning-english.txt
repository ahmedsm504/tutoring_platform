**Surah Ash-Shuara** (The Poets, 227 ayat) is one of the longest Makkan surahs by number of verses.

## Comfort for the Prophet ﷺ
- It opens by consoling the Prophet ﷺ, who grieved that his people would not believe.

## Seven stories, one pattern
| Prophet | People |
|---|---|
| Musa | Pharaoh |
| Ibrahim | His father and people |
| Nuh | His people |
| Hud | ʿAd |
| Salih | Thamud |
| Lut | His people |
| Shuʿayb | The companions of the thicket |

- Each story ends with the same refrain: *Indeed in that is a sign, but most of them were not believers. And indeed your Lord is the Mighty, the Merciful.*
- Most of the prophets say the same words: *I do not ask you for any payment; my reward is only from the Lord of the worlds.*

## Ibrahim's prayer and the sound heart
﴿يَوۡمَ لَا يَنفَعُ مَالࣱ وَلَا بَنُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَنۡ أَتَى ٱللَّهَ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٖ﴾

## The poets
- The surah distinguishes poets who follow every fancy from believing poets who do righteous deeds and remember Allah much.

## Lessons
- **Truth repeats across history**: the message of every prophet is the same.
- **Prepare your heart**: wealth and children will not help on that Day.
