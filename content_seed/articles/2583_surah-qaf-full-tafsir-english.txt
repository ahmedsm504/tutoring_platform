Surah Qaf was a surah the Prophet ﷺ loved to recite to people. A woman of the Companions said she memorised it from his mouth because he recited it from the pulpit every Friday. It contains one of the most intimate verses in the Quran: "And We have already created man and know what his soul whispers to him, and We are closer to him than his jugular vein." It follows the human being from the whisper in his heart, to the two angels recording every word, to the stupor of death, to the moment the covering is removed and his sight becomes sharp, to Hell asking "Are there any more?" and Paradise being brought near to the righteous.

This guide explains Surah Qaf section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Qaf
- **Number of verses**: 45.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 50, in the twenty-sixth juz.
- **Name**: Qaf, the separated letter at its opening.
- **Recited on the pulpit every Friday**: in Sahih Muslim, Umm Hisham bint Harithah said: "I only learned *Qaf, by the Glorious Quran* from the tongue of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ; he used to recite it every Friday on the pulpit when he addressed the people."
- **Recited in Eid**: in Sahih Muslim, Abu Waqid al-Laythi said the Prophet ﷺ recited Qaf and Al-Qamar in the Eid prayers.

## Why Did the Prophet ﷺ Recite It Every Friday?
Scholars noted that Surah Qaf gathers the most important reminders a sermon needs: creation, resurrection, death, the reckoning, Paradise and Hell, and Allah's closeness and knowledge. It is a complete sermon in itself. Reading it regularly keeps these realities alive in the heart.

## Verses 1–5: Amazement at a Warner
﴿قٓۚ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡمَجِيدِ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ عَجِبُوٓاْ أَن جَآءَهُم مُّنذِرࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَقَالَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا شَيۡءٌ عَجِيبٌ﴾ ﴿أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابٗاۖ ذَٰلِكَ رَجۡعُۢ بَعِيدࣱ﴾ ﴿قَدۡ عَلِمۡنَا مَا تَنقُصُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ مِنۡهُمۡۖ وَعِندَنَا كِتَٰبٌ حَفِيظُۢ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ كَذَّبُواْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ فَهُمۡ فِيٓ أَمۡرٖ مَّرِيجٍ﴾ (Quran 50:1-5)

**"Qaf. By the Glorious Quran."** The answer to the oath is implied: you will surely be resurrected.

**"They wonder that there has come to them a warner from among themselves."** And they said: **"When we have died and become dust, that is a distant return."** Allah answers: **"We know what the earth diminishes of them, and with Us is a retaining record."** Every particle of their bodies scattered in the earth is known to Allah.

## Verses 6–11: Look at the Sky and the Earth
﴿أَفَلَمۡ يَنظُرُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَوۡقَهُمۡ كَيۡفَ بَنَيۡنَٰهَا وَزَيَّنَّـٰهَا وَمَا لَهَا مِن فُرُوجٖ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَدَدۡنَٰهَا وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجِۭ بَهِيجٖ﴾ ﴿تَبۡصِرَةٗ وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِكُلِّ عَبۡدٖ مُّنِيبٖ﴾ ﴿وَنَزَّلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ مُّبَٰرَكٗا فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا بِهِۦ جَنَّـٰتٖ وَحَبَّ ٱلۡحَصِيدِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلنَّخۡلَ بَاسِقَٰتٖ لَّهَا طَلۡعࣱ نَّضِيدࣱ﴾ ﴿رِّزۡقٗا لِّلۡعِبَادِۖ وَأَحۡيَيۡنَا بِهِۦ بَلۡدَةٗ مَّيۡتٗاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡخُرُوجُ﴾ (Quran 50:6-11)

**"Have they not looked at the heaven above them, how We structured it and adorned it, and it has no rifts?"** **The earth**: spread out, with firm mountains, and every beautiful kind of plant. **"Giving insight and a reminder for every servant who turns back to Allah."**

**The rain and the palm trees**: blessed water from the sky, gardens, grain for harvest, and **"lofty palm trees having fruit arranged in layers"**. **"And We give life thereby to a dead land. Thus is the resurrection."** The rain bringing dead land to life is a picture of resurrection repeated before our eyes.

## Verses 12–15: Nations Who Denied
﴿كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلرَّسِّ وَثَمُودُ﴾ ﴿وَعَادࣱ وَفِرۡعَوۡنُ وَإِخۡوَٰنُ لُوطٖ﴾ ﴿وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأَيۡكَةِ وَقَوۡمُ تُبَّعٖۚ كُلࣱّ كَذَّبَ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَحَقَّ وَعِيدِ﴾ ﴿أَفَعَيِينَا بِٱلۡخَلۡقِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ فِي لَبۡسٖ مِّنۡ خَلۡقٖ جَدِيدٖ﴾ (Quran 50:12-15)

The people of Nuh, the companions of the well, Thamud, 'Ad, Pharaoh, the brothers of Lut, the companions of the thicket and the people of Tubba': **all denied the messengers, so My threat was justified**.

**"Did We fail in the first creation? But they are in confusion over a new creation."** The One who created the first time did not tire; why should the second time be harder?

## Verses 16–18: Closer Than the Jugular Vein
﴿وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ وَنَعۡلَمُ مَا تُوَسۡوِسُ بِهِۦ نَفۡسُهُۥۖ وَنَحۡنُ أَقۡرَبُ إِلَيۡهِ مِنۡ حَبۡلِ ٱلۡوَرِيدِ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ يَتَلَقَّى ٱلۡمُتَلَقِّيَانِ عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ قَعِيدࣱ﴾ ﴿مَّا يَلۡفِظُ مِن قَوۡلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيۡهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 50:16-18)

**"And We have already created man and know what his soul whispers to him, and We are closer to him than his jugular vein."** Allah knows even the passing thoughts of the heart. Many commentators explained "We are closer" as Allah's closeness through His knowledge and His angels.

**"When the two receivers receive, seated on the right and on the left. Man does not utter any word except that with him is an observer prepared."** Two angels record every word. In al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ said: "A servant may speak a word that pleases Allah, without giving it much thought, and Allah raises him by degrees because of it; and a servant may speak a word that angers Allah, without giving it much thought, and he falls into Hell because of it."

## Verses 19–22: Death and the Removed Covering
﴿وَجَآءَتۡ سَكۡرَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ ذَٰلِكَ مَا كُنتَ مِنۡهُ تَحِيدُ﴾ ﴿وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡوَعِيدِ﴾ ﴿وَجَآءَتۡ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّعَهَا سَآئِقࣱ وَشَهِيدࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّقَدۡ كُنتَ فِي غَفۡلَةٖ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا فَكَشَفۡنَا عَنكَ غِطَآءَكَ فَبَصَرُكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ حَدِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 50:19-22)

**"And the stupor of death will bring the truth. That is what you were trying to avoid."** Everyone tries to avoid thinking of death, but it comes. In Sahih al-Bukhari, 'A'ishah described that during the Prophet's ﷺ final illness he would put his hands in water and wipe his face, saying: "There is no god but Allah. Indeed, death has its agonies."

**"And the Trumpet will be blown. That is the Day of the threat."** Every soul comes **"with a driver and a witness"**: an angel driving it and an angel testifying.

**"You were certainly in unmindfulness of this, and We have removed from you your cover, so your sight, this Day, is sharp."** On that Day the veil of heedlessness is lifted and everything is seen clearly, but too late to act.

## Verses 23–29: The Companion and the Throwing Into Hell
﴿وَقَالَ قَرِينُهُۥ هَٰذَا مَا لَدَيَّ عَتِيدٌ﴾ ﴿أَلۡقِيَا فِي جَهَنَّمَ كُلَّ كَفَّارٍ عَنِيدٖ﴾ ﴿مَّنَّاعٖ لِّلۡخَيۡرِ مُعۡتَدٖ مُّرِيبٍ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ فَأَلۡقِيَاهُ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلشَّدِيدِ﴾ ﴿۞قَالَ قَرِينُهُۥ رَبَّنَا مَآ أَطۡغَيۡتُهُۥ وَلَٰكِن كَانَ فِي ضَلَٰلِۭ بَعِيدٖ﴾ ﴿قَالَ لَا تَخۡتَصِمُواْ لَدَيَّ وَقَدۡ قَدَّمۡتُ إِلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡوَعِيدِ﴾ ﴿مَا يُبَدَّلُ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ لَدَيَّ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ بِظَلَّـٰمٖ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ﴾ (Quran 50:23-29)

**The companion angel says: "This is what is with me, prepared."** Then the command: **"Throw into Hell every obstinate disbeliever, preventer of good, aggressor, doubter."** **The devil companion** disowns him: "Our Lord, I did not make him transgress, but he was himself in extreme error." Allah says: **"Do not dispute before Me, while I had already presented to you the warning. The word will not be changed with Me, and never will I be unjust to the servants."**

## Verse 30: "Are There Any More?"
﴿يَوۡمَ نَقُولُ لِجَهَنَّمَ هَلِ ٱمۡتَلَأۡتِ وَتَقُولُ هَلۡ مِن مَّزِيدٖ﴾ (Quran 50:30)

**"On the Day We will say to Hell: Have you been filled? And it will say: Are there any more?"** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Anas reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Hell will keep saying: Are there any more? until the Lord of Might places His foot in it, and it will say: Enough, enough, by Your might, and its parts will draw together."

## Verses 31–35: Paradise Brought Near
﴿وَأُزۡلِفَتِ ٱلۡجَنَّةُ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ غَيۡرَ بَعِيدٍ﴾ ﴿هَٰذَا مَا تُوعَدُونَ لِكُلِّ أَوَّابٍ حَفِيظٖ﴾ ﴿مَّنۡ خَشِيَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَجَآءَ بِقَلۡبٖ مُّنِيبٍ﴾ ﴿ٱدۡخُلُوهَا بِسَلَٰمٖۖ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡخُلُودِ﴾ ﴿لَهُم مَّا يَشَآءُونَ فِيهَا وَلَدَيۡنَا مَزِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 50:31-35)

**"And Paradise will be brought near to the righteous, not far."** Its people are described: **"for every returner and keeper"** (*awwab hafiz*): one who returns to Allah often and guards His limits. **"Who feared the Most Merciful unseen and came with a heart returning."** Notice: they feared *ar-Rahman*, the Most Merciful. Their fear was mixed with love and hope.

**"Enter it in peace. This is the Day of eternity. They will have whatever they wish therein, and with Us is more."** "More" (*mazid*) was explained by many scholars as looking at the Face of Allah.

## Verses 36–40: Patience and Glorification
﴿وَكَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّن قَرۡنٍ هُمۡ أَشَدُّ مِنۡهُم بَطۡشٗا فَنَقَّبُواْ فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ هَلۡ مِن مَّحِيصٍ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَذِكۡرَىٰ لِمَن كَانَ لَهُۥ قَلۡبٌ أَوۡ أَلۡقَى ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَهُوَ شَهِيدࣱ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ وَمَا مَسَّنَا مِن لُّغُوبٖ﴾ (Quran 50:36-38)

**"We created the heavens and earth and what is between them in six days, and there touched Us no weariness."**

﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ قَبۡلَ طُلُوعِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ وَقَبۡلَ ٱلۡغُرُوبِ﴾ ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡهُ وَأَدۡبَٰرَ ٱلسُّجُودِ﴾ (Quran 50:39-40)

**"So be patient over what they say and exalt with praise of your Lord before the rising of the sun and before its setting."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Jarir reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "You will see your Lord as you see this moon... So if you are able not to be overcome in a prayer before the rising of the sun and before its setting, then do so," and he recited this verse. The Fajr and 'Asr prayers are tied to the greatest reward: seeing Allah.

## Verses 41–45: The Call and the Reminder
﴿وَٱسۡتَمِعۡ يَوۡمَ يُنَادِ ٱلۡمُنَادِ مِن مَّكَانٖ قَرِيبٖ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يَسۡمَعُونَ ٱلصَّيۡحَةَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡخُرُوجِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نُحۡيِۦ وَنُمِيتُ وَإِلَيۡنَا ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ تَشَقَّقُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ عَنۡهُمۡ سِرَاعٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ حَشۡرٌ عَلَيۡنَا يَسِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿نَّحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَقُولُونَۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِجَبَّارٖۖ فَذَكِّرۡ بِٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَن يَخَافُ وَعِيدِ﴾ (Quran 50:41-45)

**"And listen on the Day the caller will call from a place that is near."** The Day of emerging from the graves. **"We know best what they say, and you are not over them a tyrant. But remind with the Quran whoever fears My threat."**

## Word Meanings
- **Majid**: glorious.
- **Raj' ba'id**: a distant return.
- **Furuj**: rifts, cracks.
- **Munib**: one who turns back to Allah.
- **Basiqat**: lofty, tall.
- **Tal' nadid**: fruit arranged in layers.
- **Habl al-warid**: the jugular vein.
- **Al-Mutalaqqiyan**: the two receivers.
- **Raqib 'atid**: an observer prepared.
- **Sakrat al-mawt**: the stupor of death.
- **Tahid**: avoid, flee.
- **Ghita'**: covering.
- **Hadid**: sharp.
- **'Anid**: obstinate.
- **Awwab**: one who returns often.
- **Lughub**: weariness.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Closer than the jugular vein"**: closeness pictured in the body's own lifeline.
2. **"Not a word except that an observer is present"**: total accountability of speech.
3. **"Your sight this Day is sharp"**: clarity that comes too late.
4. **"Are there any more?"**: Hell personified and insatiable.
5. **"Feared the Most Merciful unseen"**: fear joined to mercy.
6. **"And with Us is more"**: an open promise beyond imagination.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in the letter *Qaf* at the opening, stretched six counts.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 6).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *aqrabu* (verse 16), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Izhar**: in *raqibun 'atid* (verse 18), the tanween before the throat letter 'ayn is pronounced clearly.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *bi-qalbin munib* (verse 33), the tanween merges into the meem.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *makanin qarib* (verse 41), the tanween is hidden before the qaf.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Hujurat before it ended with "Allah knows the unseen of the heavens and the earth", and Qaf opens with Allah knowing what the soul whispers. Surah Adh-Dhariyat after it swears that "what you are promised is true", continuing the theme of resurrection.

## Guarding the Tongue With Surah Qaf
1. **Remember the two angels** before you speak.
2. **Pause before posting**: written words are recorded too.
3. **Fill your tongue with dhikr** so there is less room for harm.
4. **Avoid backbiting, lying and mockery.**
5. **Speak good or remain silent.**

## Practical Lessons
1. **Recite Surah Qaf often**, especially on Fridays.
2. **Remember Allah's closeness** in private.
3. **Prepare for the stupor of death.**
4. **Wake up before the covering is removed.**
5. **Be an awwab**: return to Allah often.
6. **Guard Fajr and 'Asr.**
7. **Remind with the Quran.**

## How to Memorise Surah Qaf
- **Week 1**: verses 1–15.
- **Week 2**: verses 16–30.
- **Week 3**: verses 31–45.

## Teaching Children
- **Explain the two angels** who write good and bad words.
- **Look at palm trees and rain**, and read verses 9–11.
- **Encourage Fajr and 'Asr** with the hadith about seeing Allah.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why did the Prophet ﷺ recite Surah Qaf on Fridays?
It gathers the core reminders of creation, death, resurrection and the hereafter; Umm Hisham said she memorised it from his recitation on the pulpit every Friday, as in Sahih Muslim.

### What does "closer than the jugular vein" mean?
Allah knows a person's innermost thoughts; many commentators explained it as closeness through His knowledge and His angels.

### Who are the two receivers?
The two angels on the right and left who record a person's words and deeds.

### What is the stupor of death?
The agonies and intensity of the moment of death.

### What does Hell say in verse 30?
"Are there any more?" and it continues until the Lord of Might places His foot in it, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the "more" with Allah?
Many scholars explained it as looking at the Face of Allah in Paradise.

### How many verses are in Surah Qaf?
45 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Qaf reminds you that Allah is closer to you than your jugular vein, that two angels record every word, and that one day the covering will be lifted and your sight will be sharp. Be among those who feared the Most Merciful unseen and came with a returning heart, and for whom Paradise will be brought near.
