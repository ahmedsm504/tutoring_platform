﴿وَلَا تُخۡزِنِي يَوۡمَ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ لَا يَنفَعُ مَالࣱ وَلَا بَنُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَنۡ أَتَى ٱللَّهَ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٖ﴾ [Ash-Shuara 26:87-89]

## Ibrahim's request
- *Do not disgrace me on the Day they are resurrected*: from Allah's close friend, a lesson in humility.

## What does not benefit
- Wealth and children: the two things people rely on most in this world.

## The sound heart
- Scholars explain it as a heart **free of shirk**, doubt, hypocrisy, envy, hatred and arrogance.
| Free from | Filled with |
|---|---|
| Shirk | Tawhid |
| Envy | Love of good for others |
| Hatred | Forgiveness |
| Arrogance | Humility |

## How to purify it
- Duʿa, the Quran, sincerity, avoiding sins, and wishing good for people.

## Lessons
- **Work on your heart** as much as your deeds.
- **Ask Allah** for a sound heart daily.
