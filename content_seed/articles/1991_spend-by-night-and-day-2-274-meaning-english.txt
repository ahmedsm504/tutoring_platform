﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ وَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:274]

## All times, all states
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| By night and day | At every time |
| Secretly and openly | In every state |

- Charity becomes a **habit**, not a season.

## Secret or open?
- Al-Baqarah 2:271: *If you show your charity, it is good; but if you hide it and give it to the poor, it is better for you.*
- Hiding is usually better, as it is farther from showing off and protects the dignity of the poor. Showing is good when it sets an example.

## Three glad tidings
- Their reward is with their Lord, no fear for what is ahead, no grief for what is behind.

## Its place
- This verse closes the charity passage in Al-Baqarah, and right after it come the verses forbidding riba.

## Lessons
- **Keep a secret charity** no one knows about.
- **Give regularly**, even small amounts.
