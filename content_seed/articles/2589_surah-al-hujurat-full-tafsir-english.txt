Surah Al-Hujurat is often called the surah of manners. In just eighteen verses it builds a complete code of conduct: how to behave with Allah and His Messenger, how to handle news, how to settle disputes, and how to protect people's dignity from mockery, suspicion, spying and backbiting. It contains one of the most important verses for human equality: "O mankind, We created you from male and female and made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another. Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you." If a community lived by this surah alone, most of its social problems would disappear.

This guide explains Surah Al-Hujurat verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons for families, friendships, workplaces and social media.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Hujurat
- **Number of verses**: 18.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 49, in the twenty-sixth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Hujurat means "the private rooms", the apartments of the Prophet's ﷺ wives, from verse 4.
- **Main themes**: manners with Allah and the Messenger, verifying news, brotherhood, protecting dignity and true nobility.

## Verse 1: Do Not Put Yourselves Before Allah and His Messenger
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُقَدِّمُواْ بَيۡنَ يَدَيِ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 49:1)

**"O you who believe, do not put yourselves before Allah and His Messenger."** Do not decide or speak on a matter before Allah and His Messenger have spoken. The first manner: accept the guidance before giving your opinion.

## Verses 2–3: Lowering the Voice
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَرۡفَعُوٓاْ أَصۡوَٰتَكُمۡ فَوۡقَ صَوۡتِ ٱلنَّبِيِّ وَلَا تَجۡهَرُواْ لَهُۥ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ كَجَهۡرِ بَعۡضِكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ أَن تَحۡبَطَ أَعۡمَٰلُكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَشۡعُرُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَغُضُّونَ أَصۡوَٰتَهُمۡ عِندَ رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱمۡتَحَنَ ٱللَّهُ قُلُوبَهُمۡ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۚ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةࣱ وَأَجۡرٌ عَظِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 49:2-3)

**"Do not raise your voices above the voice of the Prophet."** Lest your deeds become worthless while you do not perceive.

**Thabit ibn Qays**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Anas reported that when this verse was revealed, Thabit ibn Qays, who had a naturally loud voice, stayed home saying: "I am one of the people of the Fire; my deeds have become worthless." The Prophet ﷺ missed him and asked about him, then sent a message: "Rather, he is one of the people of Paradise."

**Abu Bakr and 'Umar**: in Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn az-Zubayr reported that Abu Bakr and 'Umar raised their voices in front of the Prophet ﷺ in a disagreement, and these verses were revealed. After that, 'Umar would speak to the Prophet ﷺ so softly that he had to ask him to repeat.

**"Those who lower their voices before the Messenger of Allah, they are the ones whose hearts Allah has tested for righteousness."** Scholars said the same manners apply to the Prophet's ﷺ hadith after his death: do not raise your opinion above his words.

## Verses 4–5: Calling From Behind the Rooms
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُنَادُونَكَ مِن وَرَآءِ ٱلۡحُجُرَٰتِ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَوۡ أَنَّهُمۡ صَبَرُواْ حَتَّىٰ تَخۡرُجَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 49:4-5)

Some Bedouins came and called out from behind the Prophet's ﷺ rooms: "O Muhammad, come out to us!" **"Most of them do not use reason. And if they had been patient until you came out to them, it would have been better for them."** Patience and respect in seeking someone's attention.

## Verses 6–8: Verify the News
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن جَآءَكُمۡ فَاسِقُۢ بِنَبَإٖ فَتَبَيَّنُوٓاْ أَن تُصِيبُواْ قَوۡمَۢا بِجَهَٰلَةٖ فَتُصۡبِحُواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلۡتُمۡ نَٰدِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 49:6)

**"O you who believe, if there comes to you a disobedient one with information, investigate, lest you harm a people out of ignorance and become, over what you have done, regretful."** Commentators mention that it was revealed when a man sent to collect zakah from a tribe returned with a false report that they had refused and wanted to kill him.

**A principle for every age**: before you believe, share, or act on news, verify it. This applies directly to messages, forwarded posts and rumours on social media. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "It is enough lying for a person to narrate everything he hears."

﴿وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ فِيكُمۡ رَسُولَ ٱللَّهِۚ لَوۡ يُطِيعُكُمۡ فِي كَثِيرٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ لَعَنِتُّمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ حَبَّبَ إِلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنَ وَزَيَّنَهُۥ فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡ وَكَرَّهَ إِلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡكُفۡرَ وَٱلۡفُسُوقَ وَٱلۡعِصۡيَانَۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلرَّـٰشِدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَضۡلٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَنِعۡمَةٗۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 49:7-8)

**"And know that among you is the Messenger of Allah. If he were to obey you in much of the matter, you would be in difficulty. But Allah has endeared to you the faith and made it pleasing in your hearts, and has made hateful to you disbelief, defiance and disobedience."** Loving faith and hating sin is itself a gift from Allah.

## Verses 9–10: Reconciliation and Brotherhood
﴿وَإِن طَآئِفَتَانِ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱقۡتَتَلُواْ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَهُمَاۖ فَإِنۢ بَغَتۡ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا عَلَى ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰ فَقَٰتِلُواْ ٱلَّتِي تَبۡغِي حَتَّىٰ تَفِيٓءَ إِلَىٰٓ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ فَإِن فَآءَتۡ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَهُمَا بِٱلۡعَدۡلِ وَأَقۡسِطُوٓاْۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُقۡسِطِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ إِخۡوَةࣱ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَ أَخَوَيۡكُمۡۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 49:9-10)

**"If two factions among the believers should fight, then make settlement between the two."** If one transgresses, stop it until it returns to Allah's command; then reconcile with justice. **"Indeed, Allah loves those who act justly."**

**"The believers are but brothers, so make settlement between your brothers."** Brotherhood in faith comes before tribe, nation and race. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "A Muslim is the brother of a Muslim; he does not wrong him nor hand him over. Whoever fulfils his brother's need, Allah fulfils his need."

## Verse 11: No Mockery, No Insults, No Nicknames
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا يَسۡخَرۡ قَوۡمࣱ مِّن قَوۡمٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونُواْ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ وَلَا نِسَآءࣱ مِّن نِّسَآءٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُنَّ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُنَّۖ وَلَا تَلۡمِزُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَلَا تَنَابَزُواْ بِٱلۡأَلۡقَٰبِۖ بِئۡسَ ٱلِٱسۡمُ ٱلۡفُسُوقُ بَعۡدَ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنِۚ وَمَن لَّمۡ يَتُبۡ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 49:11)

**Three prohibitions**:
1. **"Let not a people ridicule another people; perhaps they may be better than them."** Applies to men and women alike.
2. **"Do not insult one another"**: do not find fault with each other; your brother is like yourself.
3. **"Do not call each other by offensive nicknames."**

**"Wretched is the name of disobedience after faith"**: whoever does this has earned the label of disobedience. **"And whoever does not repent, then it is those who are the wrongdoers."**

## Verse 12: Suspicion, Spying and Backbiting
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱجۡتَنِبُواْ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلظَّنِّ إِنَّ بَعۡضَ ٱلظَّنِّ إِثۡمࣱۖ وَلَا تَجَسَّسُواْ وَلَا يَغۡتَب بَّعۡضُكُم بَعۡضًاۚ أَيُحِبُّ أَحَدُكُمۡ أَن يَأۡكُلَ لَحۡمَ أَخِيهِ مَيۡتٗا فَكَرِهۡتُمُوهُۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ تَوَّابࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 49:12)

**Three more prohibitions**:
1. **"Avoid much assumption. Indeed, some assumption is sin."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Beware of suspicion, for suspicion is the most false of speech. Do not spy, do not seek out faults..."
2. **"Do not spy"**: do not search for people's hidden faults.
3. **"Do not backbite each other."**

**The image**: **"Would one of you like to eat the flesh of his brother when dead? You would detest it."** Backbiting is like eating the flesh of a dead brother who cannot defend himself. **"And fear Allah; indeed, Allah is Accepting of repentance and Merciful."**

**What is backbiting?** In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Mentioning your brother with what he dislikes." If it is true, it is backbiting; if false, it is slander.

## Verse 13: The Most Noble Is the Most Righteous
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن ذَكَرٖ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ شُعُوبٗا وَقَبَآئِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ أَكۡرَمَكُمۡ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَتۡقَىٰكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 49:13)

**"O mankind"**: not only believers; all humanity. **"We created you from male and female"**: one origin. **"And made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another"**: diversity is for getting to know each other, not for boasting. **"Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you."**

In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah does not look at your forms or your wealth, but He looks at your hearts and your deeds." This verse destroys racism, tribalism and class pride at the root.

## Verses 14–18: Islam and Iman
﴿۞قَالَتِ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابُ ءَامَنَّاۖ قُل لَّمۡ تُؤۡمِنُواْ وَلَٰكِن قُولُوٓاْ أَسۡلَمۡنَا وَلَمَّا يَدۡخُلِ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنُ فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡۖ وَإِن تُطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ لَا يَلِتۡكُم مِّنۡ أَعۡمَٰلِكُمۡ شَيۡـًٔاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمٌ﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَرۡتَابُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلصَّـٰدِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 49:14-15)

**"The Bedouins say: We have believed. Say: You have not believed; but say: We have submitted, for faith has not yet entered your hearts."** Scholars took from this the distinction between *islam* (outward submission) and *iman* (faith settled in the heart). Every believer is a Muslim, but not every Muslim has reached the level of iman.

**True believers**: **"those who have believed in Allah and His Messenger and then doubt not, and strive with their properties and their lives in the cause of Allah. It is those who are the truthful."**

﴿قُلۡ أَتُعَلِّمُونَ ٱللَّهَ بِدِينِكُمۡ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿يَمُنُّونَ عَلَيۡكَ أَنۡ أَسۡلَمُواْۖ قُل لَّا تَمُنُّواْ عَلَيَّ إِسۡلَٰمَكُمۖ بَلِ ٱللَّهُ يَمُنُّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَنۡ هَدَىٰكُمۡ لِلۡإِيمَٰنِ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ غَيۡبَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَٱللَّهُ بَصِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 49:16-18)

**"They consider it a favour to you that they have accepted Islam. Say: Do not consider your Islam a favour to me. Rather, Allah has conferred favour upon you that He has guided you to faith."** Guidance is Allah's gift; nobody does Allah a favour by being Muslim.

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Hujurat**: the private rooms.
- **La tuqaddimu**: do not put yourselves before.
- **Tahbata**: become worthless.
- **Yaghudduna**: lower.
- **Imtahana**: tested.
- **Fasiq**: disobedient person.
- **Naba'**: news, information.
- **Tabayyanu**: verify, investigate.
- **Baghat**: transgresses.
- **Yaskhar**: ridicule.
- **Talmizu**: insult, find fault.
- **Tanabazu bil-alqab**: call by offensive nicknames.
- **Tajassasu**: spy.
- **Yaghtab**: backbite.
- **Shu'ub**: peoples.
- **Ta'arafu**: know one another.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The repeated call "O you who believe"**: five times, each with a manner.
2. **"Lest your deeds become worthless"**: shows how serious manners are.
3. **"Perhaps they may be better than them"**: humility as the cure for mockery.
4. **Eating a dead brother's flesh**: an image that makes backbiting repulsive.
5. **"O mankind"**: the address widens to all humanity for the verse of equality.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Iqlab**: in *fasiqun bi-naba'* (verse 6) and *qawman bi-jahalah* (verse 6), the tanween before the ba' becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ja'akum* (verse 6).
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *shu'uban wa qaba'il* (verse 13), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna akramakum* (verse 13), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *wa la yaghtab ba'dukum* (verse 12), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Fath before it ended by describing the Companions as "merciful among themselves", and Al-Hujurat teaches the manners that make that mercy real. Surah Qaf after it begins the section of shorter surahs (*al-mufassal*) according to many scholars, and returns to themes of resurrection.

2. **Lower your voice**, especially with parents and scholars.

5. **Treat every believer as a brother.**
6. **Guard people's dignity.**
7. **Measure people by righteousness.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is it called the surah of manners?
Because it gathers manners with Allah, the Messenger, and people: lowering the voice, verifying news, reconciliation and protecting dignity.

### What does "if a disobedient one brings you news, verify it" mean?
Do not act on or spread news from an unreliable source before checking it.

### What does "the believers are but brothers" mean?
Faith creates a brotherhood that obliges mutual care and reconciliation.

### What is backbiting according to the Prophet ﷺ?
Mentioning your brother with what he dislikes, as in Sahih Muslim.

### What does "the most noble of you is the most righteous" mean?
Honour with Allah is by taqwa, not race, tribe or wealth.

### What is the difference between islam and iman in this surah?
Islam is outward submission; iman is faith that has entered the heart.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Hujurat?
18 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Hujurat is a complete school of manners. It teaches you to put Allah and His Messenger first, to lower your voice, to verify before you act, to reconcile, and to protect the dignity of every brother and sister. And it reminds all of humanity that the only nobility that counts with Allah is righteousness.
