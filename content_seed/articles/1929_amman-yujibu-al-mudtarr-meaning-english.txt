﴿أَمَّن يُجِيبُ ٱلۡمُضۡطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكۡشِفُ ٱلسُّوٓءَ وَيَجۡعَلُكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ [An-Naml 27:62]

## A series of questions
- This verse is part of a chain in An-Naml (27:60-64), each ending with *Is there a god with Allah?*

## Who is the *mudtarr*?
- The one with **no other door left**: every human means has failed, and he turns to Allah with complete need.

## Why answered?
- Scholars explain: desperation produces full sincerity and total reliance, and Allah loves this from His servant.

## Three acts of Allah
| Act | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Answers the distressed | Responds to duʿa |
| Removes harm | Lifts the trouble |
| Makes you successors | Generations inherit the earth |

## Even disbelievers
- The Quran mentions that people at sea call Allah sincerely in storms (Yunus 10:22). Desperation reveals the truth of tawhid within.

## Lessons
- **Call Allah with the heart of the desperate**, even in normal times.
- **No situation is hopeless** with Allah.
