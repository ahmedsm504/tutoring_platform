Whether you lead your family at home or a congregation at the mosque, your recitation matters. Here are the most common mistakes.

## Mistakes in Al-Fatihah
| Mistake | Correct | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| ﴿أَنۡعَمۡتَ﴾ with ḍammah on the taa | *anʿamta* | With ḍammah it would mean "I favoured" |
| Light ḥaa in ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ﴾ said as haa | Clear ḥaa | A different letter |
| Dropping the shaddah in ﴿إِيَّاكَ﴾ | *iyyāka* | Without it the meaning changes |
| Short madd in ﴿ٱلضَّآلِّينَ﴾ | Six counts | Madd lāzim |
- Read [Surah Al-Fatiha Common Mistakes](/blog/surah-al-fatiha-common-mistakes/).

## Other common mistakes
1. **Rushing** the ghunnah and madd.
2. **Stopping** in the middle of a meaning.
3. **Thin heavy letters**, like ṣaad sounding like sīn.
4. **Mixing up similar verses** from different surahs.

## Preparation tips
| Tip | How |
|---|---|
| Revise before leading | Read your portion that day |
| Choose what you know well | Not your newest memorization |
| Recite at a steady pace | Not too fast, not too slow |
| Know where to stop | Use the waqf signs |
| If you forget | Repeat the previous ayah or move to another passage you know |

## If someone corrects you
- Accept the correction calmly and continue. The Prophet ﷺ was once reminded of a verse he skipped in prayer [Abu Dawud].
