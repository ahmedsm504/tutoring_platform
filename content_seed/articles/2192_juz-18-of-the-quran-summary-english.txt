Juz 18 begins with ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾, "The believers have succeeded", so it is called **Juz Qad Aflaha**. It contains all of **Surah Al-Mu'minun** (118 verses), all of **Surah An-Nur** (64 verses) and the start of **Surah Al-Furqan** up to verse 20. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 342 to 361**.

This is the juz of **building the believer and the believing society**. It starts with the traits of the successful individual in Al-Mu'minun, then moves in An-Nur to the rulings that protect the family and society: the penalty for zina, false accusation, mutual cursing (li'an), the slander, lowering the gaze, covering, asking permission and marriage.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **success (*falah*)**: individual success through faith and deeds, and social success through purity and chastity. Al-Mu'minun opens with "The believers have succeeded" and closes with ﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾, "The disbelievers will not succeed." In An-Nur: ﴿وَتُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعًا أَيُّهَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾, "Turn to Allah in repentance all together, O believers, so that you may succeed." Between them is a light to which Allah guides whom He wills: ﴿ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ﴾, "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth."

The message: **success is faith in the heart, chastity in conduct, and a light that illuminates homes and society.**

## Part one: Surah Al-Mu'minun (1-118)

Al-Mu'minun is Makki, with 118 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Mu'minun Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-muminun-meaning-english/).

### 1. The traits of the successful (1-11)
﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي صَلَاتِهِمۡ خَٰشِعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَنِ ٱللَّغۡوِ مُعۡرِضُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِلزَّكَوٰةِ فَٰعِلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِفُرُوجِهِمۡ حَٰفِظُونَ﴾, "The believers have succeeded: those who are humble in their prayer, who turn away from idle talk, who give zakah, and who guard their chastity", then ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِأَمَٰنَٰتِهِمۡ وَعَهۡدِهِمۡ رَٰعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَوَٰتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ﴾, "who honour their trusts and their promises, and who guard their prayers." The reward: ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَرِثُونَ ٱلۡفِرۡدَوۡسَ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ﴾, "They are the inheritors who will inherit Al-Firdaws, abiding in it forever."

**The seven traits**:
1. Humility in prayer.
2. Turning away from idle talk.
3. Giving zakah.
4. Guarding chastity.
5. Honouring trusts.
6. Keeping promises.
7. Guarding the prayers.

**Reflect**: the traits begin and end with prayer: humility within it at the start, and guarding it at the end. Prayer is the fence around all the other traits.

### 2. The stages of creation and Allah's favours (12-22)
﴿وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن سُلَٰلَةٖ مِّن طِينٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ نُطۡفَةٗ فِي قَرَارٖ مَّكِينٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلنُّطۡفَةَ عَلَقَةٗ فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡعَلَقَةَ مُضۡغَةٗ فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡمُضۡغَةَ عِظَٰمٗا فَكَسَوۡنَا ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ لَحۡمٗا ثُمَّ أَنشَأۡنَٰهُ خَلۡقًا ءَاخَرَۚ فَتَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ أَحۡسَنُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقِينَ﴾, "We created man from an extract of clay, then placed him as a drop in a firm resting place, then made the drop a clinging clot, the clot a lump of flesh, the lump bones, then clothed the bones with flesh, then produced him as another creation. So blessed is Allah, the best of creators." Then death and resurrection, then ﴿وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمۡ سَبۡعَ طَرَآئِقَ وَمَا كُنَّا عَنِ ٱلۡخَلۡقِ غَٰفِلِينَ﴾, "We created above you seven layered paths, and We are never unmindful of creation", then water, gardens, the olive tree, cattle and ships.

**The link**: the One who created man through these precise stages did not leave him without purpose, which is why the messengers are mentioned next.

### 3. The procession of messengers (23-50)
- **Nuh**: ﴿يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥٓۚ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ﴾, "My people, worship Allah; you have no god other than Him. Will you not fear Him?" The chiefs called him mad; he prayed, "My Lord, help me, for they have denied me", and was told to build the ark.
- **An unnamed messenger after him**: the affluent said ﴿۞هَيۡهَاتَ هَيۡهَاتَ لِمَا تُوعَدُونَ﴾, "Far, far away is what you are promised!" The blast seized them in truth, and they became like debris.
- **Messengers in succession**: every time a messenger came to a nation, they denied him, so We made them follow one another and turned them into tales.
- **Musa and Harun** to Pharaoh and his chiefs, who were arrogant and were destroyed.
- **'Isa and his mother**: ﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ وَأُمَّهُۥٓ ءَايَةٗ وَءَاوَيۡنَٰهُمَآ إِلَىٰ رَبۡوَةٖ ذَاتِ قَرَارٖ وَمَعِينٖ﴾, "We made the son of Maryam and his mother a sign, and sheltered them on high ground, a place of rest with flowing water."

**Lesson**: the deniers' objection is the same in every age: "This is only a man like you."

### 4. Eating the good and the one community (51-62)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلرُّسُلُ كُلُواْ مِنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَٱعۡمَلُواْ صَٰلِحًاۖ إِنِّي بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ عَلِيمࣱ﴾, "O messengers, eat from the good things and do righteous deeds; I know well what you do." In Sahih Muslim the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah is good and accepts only what is good, and Allah has commanded the believers with what He commanded the messengers", and recited this verse. He then mentioned a man on a long journey, dishevelled and dusty, raising his hands to the sky, "O Lord, O Lord", while his food is unlawful: how can he be answered? Then: ﴿وَإِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أُمَّتُكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱتَّقُونِ﴾, "This community of yours is one community, and I am your Lord, so fear Me."

Then the traits of those who hasten to good: they fear their Lord, believe in His signs and associate no one with Him, and ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡتُونَ مَآ ءَاتَواْ وَّقُلُوبُهُمۡ وَجِلَةٌ أَنَّهُمۡ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ رَٰجِعُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَهُمۡ لَهَا سَٰبِقُونَ﴾, "those who give what they give while their hearts tremble that they will return to their Lord: they hasten to good deeds and are foremost in them." In Sunan At-Tirmidhi, 'A'ishah asked the Prophet ﷺ whether these were people who drink wine and steal. He said: "No, daughter of As-Siddiq. They are those who fast, pray and give charity, while fearing it will not be accepted from them." Then: ﴿وَلَا نُكَلِّفُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ﴾, "We do not burden a soul beyond its capacity."

**Lesson**: the believer combines action with fear of non-acceptance and is never deluded by his deeds.

### 5. The deniers' excuses and questions of monotheism (63-92)
A new quarter: ﴿۞وَلَوۡ رَحِمۡنَٰهُمۡ وَكَشَفۡنَا مَا بِهِم مِّن ضُرّٖ لَّلَجُّواْ فِي طُغۡيَٰنِهِمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ﴾, "Even if We had mercy on them and removed their harm, they would persist in their transgression, wandering blindly." Then questions the idolaters admit: to whom belong the earth and whoever is in it? Who is Lord of the seven heavens and the Great Throne? In whose hand is the dominion of everything? Each time: "They will say: Allah." Admitting His lordship requires worshipping Him alone.

### 6. "My Lord, send me back" (93-118)
Seeking refuge: ﴿وَقُل رَّبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنۡ هَمَزَٰتِ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ﴾ ﴿وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ رَبِّ أَن يَحۡضُرُونِ﴾, "Say: my Lord, I seek refuge in You from the promptings of the devils, and I seek refuge in You, my Lord, lest they come near me." Then the deathbed scene: ﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَ أَحَدَهُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ قَالَ رَبِّ ٱرۡجِعُونِ﴾ ﴿لَعَلِّيٓ أَعۡمَلُ صَٰلِحٗا فِيمَا تَرَكۡتُۚ كَلَّآۚ إِنَّهَا كَلِمَةٌ هُوَ قَآئِلُهَاۖ وَمِن وَرَآئِهِم بَرۡزَخٌ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾, "When death comes to one of them, he says: my Lord, send me back, so I may do good in what I left behind. No! It is only a word he says, and behind them is a barrier until the Day they are resurrected." There is no return; the barzakh lasts until the Resurrection.

Then: ﴿فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَلَآ أَنسَابَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ وَلَا يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾ ﴿فَمَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾, "When the Horn is blown, there will be no ties of kinship between them that Day, nor will they ask about one another. Those whose scales are heavy are the successful." Then the question that shakes the heedless: ﴿أَفَحَسِبۡتُمۡ أَنَّمَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ عَبَثٗا وَأَنَّكُمۡ إِلَيۡنَا لَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ ﴿فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ﴾, "Did you think We created you in vain and that you would not be returned to Us? Exalted is Allah, the True King; there is no god but Him, Lord of the Noble Throne." Then the closing prayer: ﴿وَقُل رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ وَٱرۡحَمۡ وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾, "Say: my Lord, forgive and have mercy, and You are the best of the merciful."

**Reflect**: the surah opens with the believers' success, closes with the disbelievers' failure, and then with seeking forgiveness.

## Part two: Surah An-Nur (1-64)

An-Nur is Madani, with 64 verses, opening with ﴿سُورَةٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهَا وَفَرَضۡنَٰهَا وَأَنزَلۡنَا فِيهَآ ءَايَٰتِۭ بَيِّنَٰتٖ لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾, "A surah We have sent down and made obligatory, and in it We have sent down clear verses so that you may take heed." It is a surah of binding rulings, named after the Verse of Light within it. For the whole surah see [Surah An-Nur Meaning in English](/blog/surah-an-nur-meaning-english/).

### 1. The penalties for zina and false accusation, and li'an (2-10)
- **The penalty for zina**: ﴿ٱلزَّانِيَةُ وَٱلزَّانِي فَٱجۡلِدُواْ كُلَّ وَٰحِدٖ مِّنۡهُمَا مِاْئَةَ جَلۡدَةٖۖ وَلَا تَأۡخُذۡكُم بِهِمَا رَأۡفَةࣱ فِي دِينِ ٱللَّهِ﴾, "The woman and the man guilty of zina, lash each of them a hundred lashes, and let no pity for them keep you from Allah's law." The Sunnah clarified that this applies to the unmarried.
- **The penalty for false accusation**: those who accuse chaste women and do not bring four witnesses receive eighty lashes, and their testimony is never accepted, except for those who repent.
- **Li'an**: if a husband accuses his wife without witnesses, he testifies four times by Allah that he is truthful, and a fifth time invoking Allah's curse on himself if he is lying; she averts the punishment by testifying likewise.

**The wisdom**: the Shari'ah made proving zina very difficult (four witnesses) and made false accusation severely punishable, protecting honour from both indecency and slander.

### 2. The Ifk incident (11-26)
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو بِٱلۡإِفۡكِ عُصۡبَةࣱ مِّنكُمۡۚ لَا تَحۡسَبُوهُ شَرّٗا لَّكُمۖ بَلۡ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡۚ﴾, "Those who brought the slander are a group among you. Do not think it bad for you; rather it is good for you." It was a lie spread against the Mother of the Believers 'A'ishah, may Allah be pleased with her, and Allah declared her innocence from above the seven heavens in verses that are recited, as reported in Al-Bukhari and Muslim.

**The Quran's method for facing rumours**:
1. **Think well of others**: ﴿لَّوۡلَآ إِذۡ سَمِعۡتُمُوهُ ظَنَّ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ بِأَنفُسِهِمۡ خَيۡرٗا وَقَالُواْ هَٰذَآ إِفۡكࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾, "Why, when you heard it, did the believing men and women not think well of their own people and say: this is an obvious lie?"
2. **Demand proof**: why did they not bring four witnesses?
3. **Do not pass it on**: you received it with your tongues and said with your mouths what you had no knowledge of, thinking it trivial while it was grave with Allah.
4. **Do not spread indecency**: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُحِبُّونَ أَن تَشِيعَ ٱلۡفَٰحِشَةُ فِي ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ﴾, "Those who love that indecency spread among the believers will have a painful punishment in this world and the Hereafter."

Then Hizb 36 begins: ﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَتَّبِعُواْ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ﴾, "O you who believe, do not follow the footsteps of Shaytan." Then the verse of pardon: ﴿وَلَا يَأۡتَلِ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡفَضۡلِ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلسَّعَةِ أَن يُؤۡتُوٓاْ أُوْلِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينَ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَلۡيَعۡفُواْ وَلۡيَصۡفَحُوٓاْۗ أَلَا تُحِبُّونَ أَن يَغۡفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۚ﴾, "Let not those of virtue and means among you swear off giving to relatives, the poor and emigrants for Allah's cause. Let them pardon and overlook. Do you not love that Allah should forgive you?" In Al-Bukhari and Muslim it was revealed about Abu Bakr, who swore to stop supporting Mistah ibn Uthathah for taking part in the slander. When it came down he said: "Yes, by Allah, I love that Allah should forgive me", and restored the support.

**Lesson**: whoever wants Allah's pardon should pardon people, even someone who wronged him in his honour.

### 3. The etiquette of homes and lowering the gaze (27-34)
- **Asking permission**: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَدۡخُلُواْ بُيُوتًا غَيۡرَ بُيُوتِكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ تَسۡتَأۡنِسُواْ وَتُسَلِّمُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهَاۚ﴾, "O you who believe, do not enter houses other than your own until you have asked permission and greeted their people." If told to go back, go back: that is purer for you. Read [Ask Permission Before Entering: 24:27](/blog/ask-permission-before-entering-24-27-english/).
- **Lowering the gaze for men**: ﴿قُل لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يَغُضُّواْ مِنۡ أَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡ وَيَحۡفَظُواْ فُرُوجَهُمۡۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَزۡكَىٰ لَهُمۡۚ﴾, "Tell the believing men to lower their gaze and guard their chastity; that is purer for them."
- **Lowering the gaze and covering for women**: ﴿وَقُل لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ يَغۡضُضۡنَ مِنۡ أَبۡصَٰرِهِنَّ وَيَحۡفَظۡنَ فُرُوجَهُنَّ وَلَا يُبۡدِينَ زِينَتَهُنَّ إِلَّا مَا ظَهَرَ مِنۡهَاۖ وَلۡيَضۡرِبۡنَ بِخُمُرِهِنَّ عَلَىٰ جُيُوبِهِنَّۖ﴾, "Tell the believing women to lower their gaze, guard their chastity, not display their adornment except what ordinarily appears, and draw their head-coverings over their chests", followed by the list of mahrams.
- **Collective repentance**: "Turn to Allah in repentance all together, O believers, so that you may succeed."
- **Making marriage easy**: ﴿وَأَنكِحُواْ ٱلۡأَيَٰمَىٰ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ مِنۡ عِبَادِكُمۡ وَإِمَآئِكُمۡۚ إِن يَكُونُواْ فُقَرَآءَ يُغۡنِهِمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦۗ﴾, "Marry off the single among you and the righteous of your servants. If they are poor, Allah will enrich them from His bounty." Those who cannot find the means to marry should keep chaste.

**Reflect**: lowering the gaze comes before guarding chastity, because the look is the messenger of indecency; then marriage is made easy so the lawful alternative is available.

### 4. The Verse of Light (35-40)
A new quarter opens with the greatest parable of the surah: ﴿۞ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِۦ كَمِشۡكَوٰةٖ فِيهَا مِصۡبَاحٌۖ ٱلۡمِصۡبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍۖ ٱلزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوۡكَبࣱ دُرِّيࣱّ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٖ زَيۡتُونَةٖ لَّا شَرۡقِيَّةٖ وَلَا غَرۡبِيَّةٖ يَكَادُ زَيۡتُهَا يُضِيٓءُ وَلَوۡ لَمۡ تَمۡسَسۡهُ نَارࣱۚ نُّورٌ عَلَىٰ نُورٖۚ يَهۡدِي ٱللَّهُ لِنُورِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ﴾, "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth. The example of His light is like a niche within which is a lamp; the lamp is in glass, the glass as if it were a brilliant star, lit from a blessed olive tree, neither of the east nor the west, whose oil would almost glow even if untouched by fire. Light upon light. Allah guides to His light whom He wills."

Many commentators explained the parable as the light of faith in the believer's heart: the niche is his chest, the lamp is faith, the glass is his pure heart, and the oil is the sound natural disposition that almost shines before revelation reaches it; when the light of revelation comes, it becomes "light upon light". Read [The Verse of Light: 24:35](/blog/the-verse-of-light-24-35-meaning-english/).

Then where this light is found: ﴿فِي بُيُوتٍ أَذِنَ ٱللَّهُ أَن تُرۡفَعَ وَيُذۡكَرَ فِيهَا ٱسۡمُهُۥ﴾, "in houses Allah has allowed to be raised and His name mentioned in them", ﴿رِجَالࣱ لَّا تُلۡهِيهِمۡ تِجَٰرَةࣱ وَلَا بَيۡعٌ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَإِقَامِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَإِيتَآءِ ٱلزَّكَوٰةِ يَخَافُونَ يَوۡمٗا تَتَقَلَّبُ فِيهِ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ﴾, "men whom neither trade nor sale distracts from the remembrance of Allah, establishing prayer and giving zakah, fearing a Day when hearts and eyes will turn about."

Then two parables for the disbeliever:
- **The mirage**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ كَسَرَابِۭ بِقِيعَةٖ يَحۡسَبُهُ ٱلظَّمۡـَٔانُ مَآءً حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَهُۥ لَمۡ يَجِدۡهُ شَيۡـٔٗا﴾, "The deeds of those who disbelieve are like a mirage in a lowland, which the thirsty think is water, until he comes to it and finds nothing."
- **The darknesses**: ﴿أَوۡ كَظُلُمَٰتٖ فِي بَحۡرٖ لُّجِّيّٖ يَغۡشَىٰهُ مَوۡجࣱ مِّن فَوۡقِهِۦ مَوۡجࣱ مِّن فَوۡقِهِۦ سَحَابࣱۚ ظُلُمَٰتُۢ بَعۡضُهَا فَوۡقَ بَعۡضٍ﴾, "Or like darknesses in a deep sea, covered by waves upon waves, above which are clouds: darknesses one above another", then ﴿وَمَن لَّمۡ يَجۡعَلِ ٱللَّهُ لَهُۥ نُورٗا فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن نُّورٍ﴾, "Whoever Allah has not granted light has no light."

**The contrast**: light upon light for the believer, darkness upon darkness for the disbeliever.

### 5. Cosmic glorification and obedience (41-52)
Do you not see that whoever is in the heavens and earth glorifies Allah, and the birds with outspread wings, each knowing its prayer and glorification? Then clouds, hail and lightning, and every creature made from water. Then the hypocrites who turn away from the judgement of Allah and His Messenger, and the believers' response: we hear and we obey.

### 6. The promise of succession (53-57)
A new quarter: ﴿۞وَأَقۡسَمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ جَهۡدَ أَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ لَئِنۡ أَمَرۡتَهُمۡ لَيَخۡرُجُنَّۖ قُل لَّا تُقۡسِمُواْۖ طَاعَةࣱ مَّعۡرُوفَةٌۚ﴾, "They swore by Allah their strongest oaths that if you ordered them, they would go out. Say: do not swear; what is expected is known obedience." Then ﴿وَإِن تُطِيعُوهُ تَهۡتَدُواْۚ﴾, "If you obey him, you will be guided." Then the great promise: ﴿وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ لَيَسۡتَخۡلِفَنَّهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَمَا ٱسۡتَخۡلَفَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ وَلَيُمَكِّنَنَّ لَهُمۡ دِينَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي ٱرۡتَضَىٰ لَهُمۡ وَلَيُبَدِّلَنَّهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ خَوۡفِهِمۡ أَمۡنٗاۚ يَعۡبُدُونَنِي لَا يُشۡرِكُونَ بِي شَيۡـٔٗاۚ﴾, "Allah has promised those of you who believe and do good that He will make them successors on earth as He did those before them, establish for them their religion which He has approved, and replace their fear with security. They worship Me, associating nothing with Me."

**Conditions of the promise**: faith, good deeds, and sincere worship free of shirk. **Its results**: succession, the religion established, and security after fear.

### 7. Permission inside the home and the etiquette of gatherings (58-64)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لِيَسۡتَـٔۡذِنكُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يَبۡلُغُواْ ٱلۡحُلُمَ مِنكُمۡ ثَلَٰثَ مَرَّـٰتٖۚ مِّن قَبۡلِ صَلَوٰةِ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ وَحِينَ تَضَعُونَ ثِيَابَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظَّهِيرَةِ وَمِنۢ بَعۡدِ صَلَوٰةِ ٱلۡعِشَآءِۚ ثَلَٰثُ عَوۡرَٰتٖ لَّكُمۡۚ﴾, "O you who believe, let those your right hands possess and those of you who have not reached puberty ask permission at three times: before the dawn prayer, when you take off your clothes at noon, and after the night prayer: three times of privacy for you." Even children learn to ask permission at the three times of rest; once they reach puberty, they ask at all times.

Then eating in homes, and greeting on entry: greet one another with a greeting from Allah, blessed and good. Then the etiquette of the community: ﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَإِذَا كَانُواْ مَعَهُۥ عَلَىٰٓ أَمۡرٖ جَامِعٖ لَّمۡ يَذۡهَبُواْ حَتَّىٰ يَسۡتَـٔۡذِنُوهُۚ﴾, "The believers are only those who believe in Allah and His Messenger, and when they are with him on a collective matter, do not leave until they ask his permission." Then the warning: ﴿فَلۡيَحۡذَرِ ٱلَّذِينَ يُخَالِفُونَ عَنۡ أَمۡرِهِۦٓ أَن تُصِيبَهُمۡ فِتۡنَةٌ أَوۡ يُصِيبَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ﴾, "Let those who oppose his command beware lest a trial strike them or a painful punishment."

## Part three: the opening of Surah Al-Furqan (1-20)

Al-Furqan is Makki, with 77 verses; its first 20 verses fall in this juz. It begins: ﴿تَبَارَكَ ٱلَّذِي نَزَّلَ ٱلۡفُرۡقَانَ عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِۦ لِيَكُونَ لِلۡعَٰلَمِينَ نَذِيرًا﴾, "Blessed is He who sent down the Criterion upon His servant, to be a warner to the worlds." For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Furqan Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-furqan-meaning-english/).

### 1. Monotheism in four attributes (1-3)
﴿ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَمۡ يَتَّخِذۡ وَلَدٗا وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ شَرِيكࣱ فِي ٱلۡمُلۡكِ وَخَلَقَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖ فَقَدَّرَهُۥ تَقۡدِيرٗا﴾, "He to whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and earth, who has taken no son, has no partner in dominion, and created everything and determined it precisely." In contrast: ﴿وَٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ءَالِهَةٗ لَّا يَخۡلُقُونَ شَيۡـٔٗا وَهُمۡ يُخۡلَقُونَ﴾, "Yet they have taken besides Him gods who create nothing and are themselves created", having no power over harm, benefit, death, life or resurrection.

### 2. The idolaters' objections and the reply (4-20)
They said: this is only a lie he invented, tales of the ancients he had written down. Then: ﴿وَقَالُواْ مَالِ هَٰذَا ٱلرَّسُولِ يَأۡكُلُ ٱلطَّعَامَ وَيَمۡشِي فِي ٱلۡأَسۡوَاقِ لَوۡلَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِ مَلَكࣱ فَيَكُونَ مَعَهُۥ نَذِيرًا﴾, "They said: what is this messenger who eats food and walks in the markets? Why was an angel not sent down with him as a warner?" Then a scene of the Fire seeing them from afar, its fury and roaring heard, and the Garden of eternity. The juz ends here with: ﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ إِلَّآ إِنَّهُمۡ لَيَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ وَيَمۡشُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَسۡوَاقِۗ وَجَعَلۡنَا بَعۡضَكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٖ فِتۡنَةً أَتَصۡبِرُونَۗ وَكَانَ رَبُّكَ بَصِيرٗا﴾, "We sent no messengers before you except that they ate food and walked in the markets. And We made some of you a trial for others: will you be patient? And your Lord is ever Seeing."

**Lesson**: the messenger's humanity is not a flaw; it is what makes him a model that can be followed.

## Tajweed points in Juz 18

### ﴿مِاْئَةَ﴾ in An-Nur 2
The alif after the meem is written but not read: *mi'ah*.

### ﴿ءَاتَواْ وَّقُلُوبُهُمۡ﴾ in Al-Mu'minun 60
The sakin waw after a fathah (a *leen* letter) merges into the following waw.

### ﴿أَيُّهَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ in An-Nur 31
Written without an alif after the ha, so it is read *ayyuha-l-mu'minun* when continuing; Hafs stops on it with a sakin ha.

### ﴿يَسۡتَـٔۡذِنكُمُ﴾
A sakin hamzah after the ta: pronounce it clearly. For the basics see [Learn Tajweed: A Roadmap](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. "So worship Me" and "So fear Me"
- Al-Anbiya' 92: ﴿وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ﴾.
- Al-Mu'minun 52: ﴿وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱتَّقُونِ﴾.
**Key**: Al-Mu'minun is a surah of taqwa and awe ("their hearts tremble"), which suits "so fear Me".

### 2. The stages of creation in Al-Hajj and Al-Mu'minun
- Al-Hajj 5: ﴿فَإِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ مِنۡ عَلَقَةٖ ثُمَّ مِن مُّضۡغَةٖ﴾, in the context of proving resurrection.
- Al-Mu'minun 14: ﴿فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡعَلَقَةَ مُضۡغَةٗ فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡمُضۡغَةَ عِظَٰمٗا فَكَسَوۡنَا ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ لَحۡمٗا﴾, detailing bones and flesh.

### 3. Guarding the prayer
- Al-Mu'minun 9: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَوَٰتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ﴾, plural.
- Al-Ma'arij 34: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ﴾, singular.

### 4. "We sent before you"
- Al-Anbiya' 7: ﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا﴾.
- Al-Furqan 20: ﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ إِلَّآ إِنَّهُمۡ لَيَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ﴾.

## How to memorize Juz 18 with understanding

1. **Learn the seven traits in order**: prayer, idle talk, zakah, chastity, trusts, promises, prayers.
2. **Al-Mu'minun's verse endings are similar** (*-un*, *-in*), so rely on meaning, not rhythm alone.
3. **An-Nur is a surah of rulings**: order them in your mind: penalties, slander, permission, the gaze, marriage, light, succession, permission inside the home.
4. **Memorize the Verse of Light by its imagery**: niche, lamp, glass, star, tree, oil.
5. **Link the opening of Al-Furqan** through the "eats food" objection at verse 7 and the end of the juz at verse 20.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 18

- **Be humble in your prayer** and guard it.
- **Turn away from idle talk**: your time is precious.
- **Honour trusts and promises.**
- **Eat what is good**: the unlawful blocks supplication.
- **Act while your heart fears** non-acceptance.
- **Remember you were not created in vain.**
- **Think well of believers** and do not spread rumours.
- **Pardon and overlook**, as you love Allah to forgive you.
- **Ask permission before entering.**
- **Lower your gaze** and guard your chastity.
- **Make marriage easy** for those around you.
- **Seek Allah's light** in the houses of Allah.
- **Obey Allah and His Messenger** and you will be guided.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 18 called "Qad Aflaha"?
Because it begins with ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾.

### What traits are listed at the start of Al-Mu'minun?
Humility in prayer, turning away from idle talk, giving zakah, guarding chastity, honouring trusts and promises, and guarding the prayers.

### About whom were the Ifk verses revealed?
About the innocence of the Mother of the Believers 'A'ishah, as reported in Al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Where does Juz 18 end?
At Al-Furqan 20. Juz 19 begins with ﴿۞وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا﴾, "And those who do not expect to meet Us said..."

### Is there a prostration in Juz 18?
No. The prostration of Al-Furqan is at verse 60, in Juz 19.

## Conclusion

Juz 18 draws the full path to success: an individual humble in prayer, faithful to trusts and fearing his deeds may not be accepted; a home where permission is asked and gazes are lowered; a society that thinks well of others, pardons and conceals faults; and a light from Allah filling hearts and mosques. Whoever takes these means has been promised succession and security.
