﴿وَنَفۡسٖ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿فَأَلۡهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقۡوَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا﴾ [Ash-Shams 91:7-10]

## After eleven oaths
- Surah Ash-Shams contains many oaths, and their answer is here: **success depends on the soul**.

## Inspired with both
- Allah made the soul able to recognise wickedness and righteousness. The choice is ours.

## Two paths
| Path | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Zakkaha* | Purified and grew it with obedience |
| *Dassaha* | Buried and hid it under sins |

## The Prophet's duʿa
- *O Allah, grant my soul its taqwa and purify it, You are the best to purify it; You are its Guardian and Master* (narrated by Muslim).

## How to purify
- Prayer, dhikr, charity, good company, and leaving sins.

## Lessons
- **Your soul is your project.**
- **Do not bury it** under small sins.
