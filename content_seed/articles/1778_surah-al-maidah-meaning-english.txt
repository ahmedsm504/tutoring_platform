**Surah Al-Maidah** (The Table Spread, 120 ayat) is among the last surahs revealed.

## Fulfil your contracts
- It opens: *O you who believe, fulfil your contracts*, covering promises with Allah and with people.

## The completion of the religion
- Within the verse on forbidden foods comes a historic statement:
*Today I have perfected for you your religion, completed My favour upon you, and approved Islam as your religion.*
- Revealed at ʿArafah during the Farewell Hajj, as reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

## Justice even with those you dislike
- *Do not let hatred of a people prevent you from being just. Be just; that is nearer to piety.*

## The two sons of Adam
- One son's offering was accepted and the other's was not; the second killed his brother out of envy, then learned from a raven how to bury him.
﴿مِنۡ أَجۡلِ ذَٰلِكَ كَتَبۡنَا عَلَىٰ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ أَنَّهُۥ مَن قَتَلَ نَفۡسَۢا بِغَيۡرِ نَفۡسٍ أَوۡ فَسَادٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَكَأَنَّمَا قَتَلَ ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗا وَمَنۡ أَحۡيَاهَا فَكَأَنَّمَآ أَحۡيَا ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗاۚ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُنَا بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ ثُمَّ إِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنۡهُم بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَمُسۡرِفُونَ﴾

## The table from heaven
- The disciples asked ʿIsa for a table of food from the sky. He prayed, and Allah answered with a warning to those who would disbelieve afterwards.

## ʿIsa on the Day of Judgment
- Allah will ask ʿIsa whether he told people to worship him and his mother; he will deny it and say: *You know what is within me.*

## Lessons
- **Keep promises**: they are part of faith.
- **Every life is sacred.**
