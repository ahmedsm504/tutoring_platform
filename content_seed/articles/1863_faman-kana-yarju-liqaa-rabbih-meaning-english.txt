﴿قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ أَنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱۖ فَمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ لِقَآءَ رَبِّهِۦ فَلۡيَعۡمَلۡ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا وَلَا يُشۡرِكۡ بِعِبَادَةِ رَبِّهِۦٓ أَحَدَۢا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:110]

## A human with revelation
- The Prophet ﷺ is commanded to say: *I am only a human like you*, so no one exaggerates his status, yet *revelation comes to me*, so no one belittles it.

## The core message
- *Your God is one God*: the summary of every prophet's call.

## Two conditions of accepted deeds
| Condition | From the verse | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Righteous deed | *Let him do righteous work* | In line with the Sunnah |
| Sincerity | *Not associate anyone in the worship of his Lord* | For Allah alone, not for show |

- Scholars say a deed without either condition is not accepted.

## Hidden shirk
- Showing off (*riyaʾ*) is a form of minor shirk. The Prophet ﷺ feared it for his nation and called it the hidden shirk (narrated by Ahmad and Ibn Majah).

## A perfect ending
- Al-Kahf discusses the trials of faith, wealth, knowledge and power. Its last verse gives the protection: **sincere, correct deeds**.

## Lessons
- **Check intention** before each act of worship.
- **Learn the Sunnah** to do deeds correctly.
