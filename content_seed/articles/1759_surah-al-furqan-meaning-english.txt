**Surah Al-Furqan** (The Criterion, 77 ayat) is named after one of the names of the Quran.

## The Criterion
- It opens by blessing Allah, who sent down **the Criterion** that separates truth from falsehood, to be a warner to the worlds.

## Objections and answers
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Why does this messenger eat food and walk in markets?" | All previous messengers did the same |
| "Why was the Quran not revealed all at once?" | To strengthen the Prophet's heart, and it was recited in measured stages |
| "These are tales of the ancients" | It was sent by the One who knows the secret of the heavens and earth |

## The servants of the Most Merciful
﴿وَعِبَادُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡشُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ هَوۡنٗا وَإِذَا خَاطَبَهُمُ ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ قَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗا﴾
Their qualities include:
- They spend the night in prayer.
- They ask to be saved from Hell.
- They are balanced in spending, neither wasteful nor stingy.
- They do not commit shirk, murder or zina.
- They do not witness falsehood and pass by idle talk with dignity.
- They pray for righteous spouses and children, *a comfort to our eyes*.

## Hope for those who repent
- After listing major sins, Allah promises that those who repent, believe and do good will have their bad deeds **replaced with good**.

## Lessons
- **Character is the proof of faith**: humility, patience and balance.
- **Repentance opens a new page.**
