## Surah Al-Fajr Meaning: O Reassured Soul, Return to Your Lord

**Surah Al-Fajr** (30 ayat) ends with one of the most beloved passages in the Quran: the call to the reassured soul.

### The oaths
- By the dawn, and **ten nights**, which many commentators explained as the first ten nights of Dhul-Hijjah.

### Destroyed nations
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِعَادٍ﴾ ﴿إِرَمَ ذَاتِ ٱلۡعِمَادِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّتِي لَمۡ يُخۡلَقۡ مِثۡلُهَا فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ﴾
| Nation | What they had |
|---|---|
| ʿAd (Iram) | Lofty pillars unmatched in the lands |
| Thamud | Carved homes in rock |
| Pharaoh | Power and stakes |

### A common misjudgement
﴿فَأَمَّا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِذَا مَا ٱبۡتَلَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَأَكۡرَمَهُۥ وَنَعَّمَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّيٓ أَكۡرَمَنِ﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّآ إِذَا مَا ٱبۡتَلَىٰهُ فَقَدَرَ عَلَيۡهِ رِزۡقَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّيٓ أَهَٰنَنِ﴾
- **Wealth and poverty are both tests**, not signs of honour or humiliation.
- The real problem: *you do not honour the orphan, nor encourage feeding the poor*.

### The reassured soul
﴿يَـٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفۡسُ ٱلۡمُطۡمَئِنَّةُ﴾ ﴿ٱرۡجِعِيٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةٗ مَّرۡضِيَّةٗ﴾ ﴿فَٱدۡخُلِي فِي عِبَٰدِي﴾ ﴿وَٱدۡخُلِي جَنَّتِي﴾

### Lessons
- **Don't measure your worth** by your bank balance.
- **Care for orphans and the poor.**

## "We Have Created Man in Hardship": Quran 90:4 Explained

﴿لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِي كَبَدٍ﴾ [Al-Balad 90:4]

### What is *kabad*?
- Toil, hardship and struggle. From birth to death, a person moves from one effort to another.

### An oath before it
- Allah swears by the city of Makkah and by *a father and what he begot* before stating this truth.

### Realism
- Expecting a life without difficulty leads to frustration. This verse sets the right expectation: **effort is normal**.

### The steep path
- Later in the surah, Allah describes *al-ʿaqabah*, the steep path worth climbing:
| Deed | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Freeing a slave | Liberating people |
| Feeding in a day of hunger | Generosity when it is hard |
| An orphan relative or a poor person in the dust | Care for the most vulnerable |
| Faith, patience and mercy | Encouraging one another |

### Hardship with purpose
- The believer's toil is rewarded; the same struggle without faith is wasted.

### Lessons
- **Accept effort** as part of life.
- **Climb the steep path** of good deeds.

## Surah Al-Balad Meaning: The Steep Path

**Surah Al-Balad** (20 ayat) describes the hard climb that leads to success.

### The oath and the truth
- Allah swears by **this city**, Makkah.
﴿لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِي كَبَدٍ﴾ — *We have certainly created man into hardship.*

### Gifts and two paths
- Allah gave man **two eyes, a tongue and two lips**.
﴿وَهَدَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلنَّجۡدَيۡنِ﴾ — *And We showed him the two ways*: good and evil.

### The steep path
﴿فَلَا ٱقۡتَحَمَ ٱلۡعَقَبَةَ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡعَقَبَةُ﴾ ﴿فَكُّ رَقَبَةٍ﴾ ﴿أَوۡ إِطۡعَٰمࣱ فِي يَوۡمٖ ذِي مَسۡغَبَةٖ﴾ ﴿يَتِيمٗا ذَا مَقۡرَبَةٍ﴾ ﴿أَوۡ مِسۡكِينٗا ذَا مَتۡرَبَةٖ﴾
| Step | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Freeing a slave | Releasing someone from bondage |
| Feeding in a day of hunger | Giving when it is hardest |
| An orphan relative | Double right: orphanhood and kinship |
| A poor person in the dust | The most needy |
- Then: being among those who **believe** and **advise one another to patience and mercy**.

### Lessons
- **The road to Paradise is uphill**: generosity in hard times.
- **Mercy** is a quality to encourage in others.

## Quran 91:7-10 Explained: The Soul Inspired with Good and Evil

﴿وَنَفۡسٖ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿فَأَلۡهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقۡوَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا﴾ [Ash-Shams 91:7-10]

### After eleven oaths
- Surah Ash-Shams contains many oaths, and their answer is here: **success depends on the soul**.

### Inspired with both
- Allah made the soul able to recognise wickedness and righteousness. The choice is ours.

### Two paths
| Path | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Zakkaha* | Purified and grew it with obedience |
| *Dassaha* | Buried and hid it under sins |

### The Prophet's duʿa
- *O Allah, grant my soul its taqwa and purify it, You are the best to purify it; You are its Guardian and Master* (narrated by Muslim).

### How to purify
- Prayer, dhikr, charity, good company, and leaving sins.

### Lessons
- **Your soul is your project.**
- **Do not bury it** under small sins.

## Surah Ash-Shams Meaning: Successful Is the One Who Purifies the Soul

**Surah Ash-Shams** (15 ayat) contains the longest chain of oaths in the Quran, all leading to one statement about the soul.

### The oaths
| Oath | Pair |
|---|---|
| The sun and its brightness | The moon when it follows it |
| The day when it reveals it | The night when it covers it |
| The sky and how He built it | The earth and how He spread it |
| The soul and how He proportioned it | |

### The answer
﴿وَنَفۡسٖ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿فَأَلۡهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقۡوَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا﴾
- *He inspired it with its wickedness and its righteousness. Successful is the one who purifies it, and failed is the one who corrupts it.*
- The Prophet ﷺ used to say: «O Allah, give my soul its piety and purify it, You are the best to purify it» [Muslim].

### The story of Thamud
- Thamud denied their prophet Salih. The most wretched of them killed the **she-camel** that was a sign from Allah.
- Allah destroyed them, *and He does not fear the consequence*.

### Lessons
- **Your soul is your project**: purify it daily.
- **Arrogance** of one person can destroy a whole people who approve of it.

## Surah Al-Layl Meaning: Your Efforts Are Diverse

**Surah Al-Layl** (21 ayat) shows how a person's choices about wealth shape their path.

﴿وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا تَجَلَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ سَعۡيَكُمۡ لَشَتَّىٰ﴾
- *Indeed, your efforts are diverse.*

### Two paths
| The giver | The miser |
|---|---|
| ﴿فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أَعۡطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ﴾ | ﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۢ بَخِلَ وَٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَكَذَّبَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡعُسۡرَىٰ﴾ |
| Gives, fears Allah, believes in the best reward | Withholds, feels self-sufficient, denies the best reward |
| Eased toward ease | Eased toward hardship |

### The one who spends for Allah alone
- The surah ends by describing the righteous one who gives his wealth **to purify himself**, owing no one a favour, *seeking only the face of his Lord, the Most High*, and he will be satisfied.
- Many commentators said these final ayat refer to **Abu Bakr as-Siddiq**, who freed believers who were being tortured.

### Lessons
- **Generosity opens doors** of ease in your life.
- **Pure intention**: give expecting nothing from people.

## Surah Ad-Duha Meaning: Your Lord Has Not Forsaken You

**Surah Ad-Duha** is a surah of comfort. It came after revelation paused for a while and the Prophet ﷺ was saddened [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

### The oath
﴿وَٱلضُّحَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا سَجَىٰ﴾ — *By the morning brightness, and by the night when it is still.*
- Light after darkness: **ease after difficulty**.

### The reassurance
﴿مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ﴾ — *Your Lord has not forsaken you, nor is He displeased.*
﴿وَلَلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ﴾

### Three blessings remembered
| Ayah | Blessing |
|---|---|
| ﴿أَلَمۡ يَجِدۡكَ يَتِيمٗا فَـَٔاوَىٰ﴾ | An orphan given shelter |
| ﴿وَوَجَدَكَ ضَآلّٗا فَهَدَىٰ﴾ | Unaware of the Book, then guided |
| ﴿وَوَجَدَكَ عَآئِلٗا فَأَغۡنَىٰ﴾ | In need, then made sufficient |

### Three instructions
| Ayah | Instruction |
|---|---|
| ﴿فَأَمَّا ٱلۡيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقۡهَرۡ﴾ | Don't be harsh with the orphan |
| ﴿وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّآئِلَ فَلَا تَنۡهَرۡ﴾ | Don't repel the one who asks |
| ﴿وَأَمَّا بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثۡ﴾ | Speak of your Lord's blessings |
- **Notice the match**: he was an orphan, so be kind to orphans; he was in need, so don't repel the asker; he was guided, so share the blessing.

### For difficult days
- Recite it when you feel alone: **Allah has not left you**.

## "Your Lord Will Give You, and You Will Be Satisfied": Quran 93:5 Explained

﴿وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ﴾ [Ad-Duha 93:5]

### The context
- After a pause in revelation, some mocked that his Lord had left him. Surah Ad-Duha answered: *Your Lord has not abandoned you, nor is He displeased*, then: *the Hereafter is better for you than the first*, then this promise.

### The promise
| In this world | In the Hereafter |
|---|---|
| Victory, the spread of Islam | The praised station, intercession, al-Kawthar |

### His concern for his nation
- In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ raised his hands and wept: *O Allah, my nation, my nation.* Allah sent Jibril: *We will please you regarding your nation and will not grieve you.*

### "Will" (*la-sawfa*)
- Emphasis with delay: the gift is certain, even if it comes later.

### Lessons
- **Trust Allah's promise** when it seems delayed.
- **Love the Prophet ﷺ**, whose concern was his nation.

## "As for the Orphan, Do Not Oppress; As for the One Who Asks, Do Not Repel": Quran 93:9-10 Explained

﴿فَأَمَّا ٱلۡيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقۡهَرۡ﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّآئِلَ فَلَا تَنۡهَرۡ﴾ [Ad-Duha 93:9-10]

### Mirroring the blessings
| Blessing | Command |
|---|---|
| *Did He not find you an orphan and shelter you?* | Do not oppress the orphan |
| *And find you lost and guide you?* | Do not repel the one who asks |
| *And find you in need and enrich you?* | Proclaim the favour of your Lord |

- As Allah honoured you, honour those in your former state.

### *Qahr* and *nahr*
- *Qahr*: humiliating and dominating, or taking their rights.
- *Nahr*: scolding and shouting. Some scholars include the one asking for **knowledge**.

### The orphan's guardian
- *I and the guardian of an orphan will be in Paradise like this*, and he joined his index and middle fingers (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### Lessons
- **Never shout at someone who asks**, even if you cannot give.
- **Answer your children's and students' questions** patiently.

## "And as for the Favour of Your Lord, Proclaim It": Quran 93:11 Explained

﴿وَأَمَّا بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثۡ﴾ [Ad-Duha 93:11]

### The context
- Surah Ad-Duha reminds the Prophet ﷺ: He found you an orphan and sheltered you, found you lost and guided you, found you in need and enriched you. Then three commands: do not oppress the orphan, do not repel the one who asks, and **speak of your Lord's favour**.

### What to proclaim
| Blessing | How |
|---|---|
| Guidance | Teach others and call to good |
| Wealth | Show its effect with modesty and generosity |
| Health and family | Say *alhamdulillah* openly |

### Gratitude, not boasting
- The aim is to attribute the blessing **to Allah**, not to oneself. Scholars add: be wise where speaking may cause envy or hurt someone in need.

### A Prophetic teaching
- *Allah loves to see the trace of His blessing on His servant* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### The Quran is the greatest blessing
- Some early scholars explained the *favour* as prophethood and the Quran: proclaim it by teaching it.

### Lessons
- **Speak of blessings** more than complaints.
- **Teach** what Allah taught you.
