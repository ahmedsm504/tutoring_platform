Surah Al-An'am is the great Meccan surah of tawhid. From its first word, "Praise", to its final verses, it builds the case for one God through creation, reason, history and the human heart. It describes Allah's knowledge in a way that makes the reader hold his breath: "With Him are the keys of the unseen; none knows them except Him. And He knows what is on the land and in the sea. Not a leaf falls but that He knows it." It tells the story of Ibrahim looking at a star, the moon and the sun, and turning away from everything that sets. It lists ten commandments that every person should know. And it ends with a declaration that sums up the believer's whole life: "Indeed, my prayer, my rites of sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds."

This guide explains Surah Al-An'am section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-An'am
- **Number of verses**: 165.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 6, in the seventh and eighth juz.
- **Name**: Al-An'am means "the cattle", from the verses refuting pagan superstitions about livestock.
- **A note on narrations**: reports that the whole surah descended at once escorted by seventy thousand angels are found in some books, but their chains have weakness according to hadith scholars.
- **Main themes**: tawhid, Allah's knowledge and power, arguments against idolatry, the prophets, the commandments and total devotion.

## Verses 1–11: Praise and Mercy
﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَجَعَلَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ وَٱلنُّورَۖ ثُمَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡ يَعۡدِلُونَ﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن طِينٖ ثُمَّ قَضَىٰٓ أَجَلٗاۖ وَأَجَلࣱ مُّسَمًّى عِندَهُۥۖ ثُمَّ أَنتُمۡ تَمۡتَرُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:1-2)

**"All praise is due to Allah, who created the heavens and the earth and made the darknesses and the light."** **"It is He who created you from clay and then decreed a term."**

﴿قُل لِّمَن مَّا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ قُل لِّلَّهِۚ كَتَبَ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَةَۚ لَيَجۡمَعَنَّكُمۡ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِۚ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:12)

**"Say: To whom belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth? Say: To Allah. He has decreed upon Himself mercy."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said that when Allah completed creation, He wrote in a Book with Him above the Throne: "My mercy prevails over My anger."

## Verses 12–32: Allah Alone Removes Harm
﴿وَإِن يَمۡسَسۡكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرّٖ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَۖ وَإِن يَمۡسَسۡكَ بِخَيۡرٖ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلۡقَاهِرُ فَوۡقَ عِبَادِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ﴾ (Quran 6:17-18)

**"And if Allah should touch you with adversity, there is no remover of it except Him. And if He touches you with good, then He is over all things competent."** **"And He is the Subjugator over His servants."**

﴿قُلۡ أَيُّ شَيۡءٍ أَكۡبَرُ شَهَٰدَةٗۖ قُلِ ٱللَّهُۖ شَهِيدُۢ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡۚ وَأُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ لِأُنذِرَكُم بِهِۦ وَمَنۢ بَلَغَۚ أَئِنَّكُمۡ لَتَشۡهَدُونَ أَنَّ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ ءَالِهَةً أُخۡرَىٰۚ قُل لَّآ أَشۡهَدُۚ قُلۡ إِنَّمَا هُوَ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱ وَإِنَّنِي بَرِيٓءࣱ مِّمَّا تُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:19)

**"Say: What thing is greatest in testimony? Say: Allah is witness between me and you. And this Quran was revealed to me that I may warn you thereby and whomever it reaches."**

﴿وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا لَعِبࣱ وَلَهۡوࣱۖ وَلَلدَّارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ لِّلَّذِينَ يَتَّقُونَۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:32)

**"And the worldly life is not but amusement and diversion; but the home of the hereafter is best for those who fear Allah."**

## Verses 33–58: Communities Like You
﴿وَمَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا طَـٰٓئِرٖ يَطِيرُ بِجَنَاحَيۡهِ إِلَّآ أُمَمٌ أَمۡثَالُكُمۚ مَّا فَرَّطۡنَا فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِن شَيۡءٖۚ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يُحۡشَرُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:38)

**"And there is no creature on the earth or bird that flies with its wings except that they are communities like you. We have not neglected in the Register a thing."** Every species has its own order, life and community.

﴿وَإِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا فَقُلۡ سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ كَتَبَ رَبُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَةَ أَنَّهُۥ مَنۡ عَمِلَ مِنكُمۡ سُوٓءَۢا بِجَهَٰلَةٖ ثُمَّ تَابَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ وَأَصۡلَحَ فَأَنَّهُۥ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 6:54)

**"Your Lord has decreed upon Himself mercy: that any of you who does wrong out of ignorance and then repents after that and corrects himself, indeed, He is Forgiving and Merciful."**

## Verses 59–73: The Keys of the Unseen
﴿۞وَعِندَهُۥ مَفَاتِحُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ لَا يَعۡلَمُهَآ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۚ وَمَا تَسۡقُطُ مِن وَرَقَةٍ إِلَّا يَعۡلَمُهَا وَلَا حَبَّةٖ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا رَطۡبٖ وَلَا يَابِسٍ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 6:59)

**"And with Him are the keys of the unseen; none knows them except Him. And He knows what is on the land and in the sea. Not a leaf falls but that He knows it. And no grain is there within the darknesses of the earth and no moist or dry thing but that it is in a clear record."** Every falling leaf, every seed in the dark soil.

﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يَتَوَفَّىٰكُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا جَرَحۡتُم بِٱلنَّهَارِ ثُمَّ يَبۡعَثُكُمۡ فِيهِ لِيُقۡضَىٰٓ أَجَلࣱ مُّسَمّٗىۖ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡهِ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ ثُمَّ يُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلۡقَاهِرُ فَوۡقَ عِبَادِهِۦۖ وَيُرۡسِلُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ حَفَظَةً حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَ أَحَدَكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ تَوَفَّتۡهُ رُسُلُنَا وَهُمۡ لَا يُفَرِّطُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:60-61)

**"It is He who takes your souls by night and knows what you have committed by day."** He sends guardians over you, and when death comes, **"Our messengers take him, and they do not fail"**.

## Verses 74–90: Ibrahim and the Heavens
﴿۞وَإِذۡ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ لِأَبِيهِ ءَازَرَ أَتَتَّخِذُ أَصۡنَامًا ءَالِهَةً إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰكَ وَقَوۡمَكَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ ﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُرِيٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ مَلَكُوتَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلِيَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُوقِنِينَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا جَنَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلَّيۡلُ رَءَا كَوۡكَبٗاۖ قَالَ هَٰذَا رَبِّيۖ فَلَمَّآ أَفَلَ قَالَ لَآ أُحِبُّ ٱلۡأٓفِلِينَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا رَءَا ٱلۡقَمَرَ بَازِغٗا قَالَ هَٰذَا رَبِّيۖ فَلَمَّآ أَفَلَ قَالَ لَئِن لَّمۡ يَهۡدِنِي رَبِّي لَأَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا رَءَا ٱلشَّمۡسَ بَازِغَةٗ قَالَ هَٰذَا رَبِّي هَٰذَآ أَكۡبَرُۖ فَلَمَّآ أَفَلَتۡ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنِّي بَرِيٓءࣱ مِّمَّا تُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنِّي وَجَّهۡتُ وَجۡهِيَ لِلَّذِي فَطَرَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ حَنِيفٗاۖ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾ (Quran 6:74-79)

Ibrahim said to his father Azar: **"Do you take idols as deities? Indeed, I see you and your people in manifest error."**

**The argument from the sky**: when night covered him, he saw a star and said, "This is my lord" (to engage his people's thinking). When it set, he said: **"I like not those that disappear."** Then the moon, then the sun, "This is greater". When it set: **"O my people, indeed I am free from what you associate with Allah. Indeed, I have turned my face toward He who created the heavens and the earth, inclining toward truth, and I am not of those who associate others with Allah."**

**The lesson**: anything that rises and sets, appears and disappears, cannot be God.

﴿وَحَآجَّهُۥ قَوۡمُهُۥۚ قَالَ أَتُحَـٰٓجُّوٓنِّي فِي ٱللَّهِ وَقَدۡ هَدَىٰنِۚ وَلَآ أَخَافُ مَا تُشۡرِكُونَ بِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ رَبِّي شَيۡـٔٗاۚ وَسِعَ رَبِّي كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ عِلۡمًاۚ أَفَلَا تَتَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَكَيۡفَ أَخَافُ مَآ أَشۡرَكۡتُمۡ وَلَا تَخَافُونَ أَنَّكُمۡ أَشۡرَكۡتُم بِٱللَّهِ مَا لَمۡ يُنَزِّلۡ بِهِۦ عَلَيۡكُمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٗاۚ فَأَيُّ ٱلۡفَرِيقَيۡنِ أَحَقُّ بِٱلۡأَمۡنِۖ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَمۡ يَلۡبِسُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنَهُم بِظُلۡمٍ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡأَمۡنُ وَهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:80-82)

**"Those who believe and do not mix their belief with injustice, those will have security, and they are rightly guided."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud said the Companions were troubled by this verse, asking: "Which of us does not wrong himself?" The Prophet ﷺ explained that the injustice meant is shirk, citing Luqman: "Indeed, association is a great injustice."

﴿وَتِلۡكَ حُجَّتُنَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦۚ نَرۡفَعُ دَرَجَٰتٖ مَّن نَّشَآءُۗ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ حَكِيمٌ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ إِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَۚ كُلًّا هَدَيۡنَاۚ وَنُوحًا هَدَيۡنَا مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِهِۦ دَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ وَأَيُّوبَ وَيُوسُفَ وَمُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَزَكَرِيَّا وَيَحۡيَىٰ وَعِيسَىٰ وَإِلۡيَاسَۖ كُلࣱّ مِّنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَٱلۡيَسَعَ وَيُونُسَ وَلُوطٗاۚ وَكُلّٗا فَضَّلۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَمِنۡ ءَابَآئِهِمۡ وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِهِمۡ وَإِخۡوَٰنِهِمۡۖ وَٱجۡتَبَيۡنَٰهُمۡ وَهَدَيۡنَٰهُمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِي بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦۚ وَلَوۡ أَشۡرَكُواْ لَحَبِطَ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحُكۡمَ وَٱلنُّبُوَّةَۚ فَإِن يَكۡفُرۡ بِهَا هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ فَقَدۡ وَكَّلۡنَا بِهَا قَوۡمٗا لَّيۡسُواْ بِهَا بِكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَدَى ٱللَّهُۖ فَبِهُدَىٰهُمُ ٱقۡتَدِهۡۗ قُل لَّآ أَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ أَجۡرًاۖ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 6:83-90)

**Eighteen prophets** are named in verses 83–86: Ibrahim, Ishaq, Ya'qub, Nuh, Dawud, Sulayman, Ayyub, Yusuf, Musa, Harun, Zakariyya, Yahya, 'Isa, Ilyas, Isma'il, al-Yasa', Yunus and Lut. **"Those are the ones whom Allah has guided, so from their guidance take an example."**

## Verses 91–117: Creation and Manners in Debate
﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ فَالِقُ ٱلۡحَبِّ وَٱلنَّوَىٰۖ يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ وَمُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ مِنَ ٱلۡحَيِّۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُؤۡفَكُونَ﴾ ﴿فَالِقُ ٱلۡإِصۡبَاحِ وَجَعَلَ ٱلَّيۡلَ سَكَنٗا وَٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ حُسۡبَانٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقۡدِيرُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلنُّجُومَ لِتَهۡتَدُواْ بِهَا فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۗ قَدۡ فَصَّلۡنَا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَكُم مِّن نَّفۡسٖ وَٰحِدَةٖ فَمُسۡتَقَرࣱّ وَمُسۡتَوۡدَعࣱۗ قَدۡ فَصَّلۡنَا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَفۡقَهُونَ﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا بِهِۦ نَبَاتَ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مِنۡهُ خَضِرٗا نُّخۡرِجُ مِنۡهُ حَبّٗا مُّتَرَاكِبٗا وَمِنَ ٱلنَّخۡلِ مِن طَلۡعِهَا قِنۡوَانࣱ دَانِيَةࣱ وَجَنَّـٰتٖ مِّنۡ أَعۡنَابٖ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونَ وَٱلرُّمَّانَ مُشۡتَبِهٗا وَغَيۡرَ مُتَشَٰبِهٍۗ ٱنظُرُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ ثَمَرِهِۦٓ إِذَآ أَثۡمَرَ وَيَنۡعِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكُمۡ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:95-99)

**"Indeed, Allah is the cleaver of grain and date seeds."** He brings the living from the dead. **"Cleaver of the daybreak."** He made the night for rest, the sun and moon for calculation, and the stars for guidance. From rain He brings out green plants, grains in layers, palm trees, gardens of grapes, olives and pomegranates. **"Look at its fruit when it yields and its ripening."**

﴿لَّا تُدۡرِكُهُ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَهُوَ يُدۡرِكُ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَۖ وَهُوَ ٱللَّطِيفُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ﴾ (Quran 6:103)

**"Vision perceives Him not, but He perceives all vision; and He is the Subtle, the Acquainted."** Eyes cannot encompass Him in this world, while the believers will see Him in the hereafter, as authentic hadith confirm.

﴿وَلَا تَسُبُّواْ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَسُبُّواْ ٱللَّهَ عَدۡوَۢا بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖۗ كَذَٰلِكَ زَيَّنَّا لِكُلِّ أُمَّةٍ عَمَلَهُمۡ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِم مَّرۡجِعُهُمۡ فَيُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:108)

**"And do not insult those they invoke other than Allah, lest they insult Allah in enmity without knowledge."** Respect in debate, and awareness of consequences.

## Verses 118–135: Life and Light
﴿أَوَمَن كَانَ مَيۡتٗا فَأَحۡيَيۡنَٰهُ وَجَعَلۡنَا لَهُۥ نُورٗا يَمۡشِي بِهِۦ فِي ٱلنَّاسِ كَمَن مَّثَلُهُۥ فِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ لَيۡسَ بِخَارِجٖ مِّنۡهَاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ زُيِّنَ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:122)

**"And is one who was dead and We gave him life and made for him light by which to walk among the people like one who is in darknesses, never to emerge therefrom?"** Faith is life; guidance is light.

﴿فَمَن يُرِدِ ٱللَّهُ أَن يَهۡدِيَهُۥ يَشۡرَحۡ صَدۡرَهُۥ لِلۡإِسۡلَٰمِۖ وَمَن يُرِدۡ أَن يُضِلَّهُۥ يَجۡعَلۡ صَدۡرَهُۥ ضَيِّقًا حَرَجٗا كَأَنَّمَا يَصَّعَّدُ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَجۡعَلُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرِّجۡسَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:125)

**"So whoever Allah wants to guide, He expands his breast to Islam; and whoever He wants to misguide, He makes his breast tight and constricted as though he were climbing into the sky."**

## Verses 136–150: Superstitions About Cattle and Crops
The idolaters assigned shares of their crops and cattle to Allah and to their idols, forbade some animals to some people, and killed their children out of foolishness. **"They have lost who killed their children in foolishness without knowledge."** Allah alone decides what is lawful.

﴿۞وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَ جَنَّـٰتٖ مَّعۡرُوشَٰتٖ وَغَيۡرَ مَعۡرُوشَٰتٖ وَٱلنَّخۡلَ وَٱلزَّرۡعَ مُخۡتَلِفًا أُكُلُهُۥ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونَ وَٱلرُّمَّانَ مُتَشَٰبِهٗا وَغَيۡرَ مُتَشَٰبِهٖۚ كُلُواْ مِن ثَمَرِهِۦٓ إِذَآ أَثۡمَرَ وَءَاتُواْ حَقَّهُۥ يَوۡمَ حَصَادِهِۦۖ وَلَا تُسۡرِفُوٓاْۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ﴾ (Quran 6:141)

**"And give its due on the day of its harvest. And be not excessive. Indeed, He does not like those who commit excess."**

## Verses 151–153: The Ten Commandments
﴿۞قُلۡ تَعَالَوۡاْ أَتۡلُ مَا حَرَّمَ رَبُّكُمۡ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ أَلَّا تُشۡرِكُواْ بِهِۦ شَيۡـٔٗاۖ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنٗاۖ وَلَا تَقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَوۡلَٰدَكُم مِّنۡ إِمۡلَٰقٖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُكُمۡ وَإِيَّاهُمۡۖ وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ ٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ مَا ظَهَرَ مِنۡهَا وَمَا بَطَنَۖ وَلَا تَقۡتُلُواْ ٱلنَّفۡسَ ٱلَّتِي حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ وَصَّىٰكُم بِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ مَالَ ٱلۡيَتِيمِ إِلَّا بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ حَتَّىٰ يَبۡلُغَ أَشُدَّهُۥۚ وَأَوۡفُواْ ٱلۡكَيۡلَ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ لَا نُكَلِّفُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۖ وَإِذَا قُلۡتُمۡ فَٱعۡدِلُواْ وَلَوۡ كَانَ ذَا قُرۡبَىٰۖ وَبِعَهۡدِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡفُواْۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ وَصَّىٰكُم بِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطِي مُسۡتَقِيمٗا فَٱتَّبِعُوهُۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ ٱلسُّبُلَ فَتَفَرَّقَ بِكُمۡ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ وَصَّىٰكُم بِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:151-153)

**"Say: Come, I will recite what your Lord has prohibited to you":**
1. **Do not associate anything with Him.**
2. **To parents, good treatment.**
3. **Do not kill your children out of poverty**: We provide for you and for them.
4. **Do not approach immoralities, what is apparent of them and what is concealed.**
5. **Do not kill the soul which Allah has forbidden, except by right.**
6. **Do not approach the orphan's property except in the best way.**
7. **Give full measure and weight in justice.**
8. **When you speak, be just, even if it concerns a near relative.**
9. **Fulfil the covenant of Allah.**
10. **"This is My path, which is straight, so follow it; and do not follow other ways, for they will separate you from His way."**

Ahmad and an-Nasa'i reported that Ibn Mas'ud said the Prophet ﷺ drew a straight line and said, "This is the path of Allah," then drew lines to its right and left and said, "These are paths, on each of which is a devil calling to it," and recited this verse; al-Albani graded it *hasan*.

## Verses 154–165: Ten for One and Total Devotion
﴿مَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُۥ عَشۡرُ أَمۡثَالِهَاۖ وَمَن جَآءَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ فَلَا يُجۡزَىٰٓ إِلَّا مِثۡلَهَا وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 6:160)

**"Whoever comes with a good deed will have ten times the like thereof, and whoever comes with an evil deed will not be recompensed except the like thereof."**

﴿قُلۡ إِنَّنِي هَدَىٰنِي رَبِّيٓ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ دِينٗا قِيَمٗا مِّلَّةَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ حَنِيفٗاۚ وَمَا كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحۡيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُۥۖ وَبِذَٰلِكَ أُمِرۡتُ وَأَنَا۠ أَوَّلُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 6:161-163)

**"Say: Indeed, my Lord has guided me to a straight path, a correct religion, the way of Ibrahim, inclining toward truth."** **"Say: Indeed, my prayer, my rites of sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds. No partner has He. And this I have been commanded, and I am the first of the Muslims."** This is the believer's whole life in one sentence.

﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَكُمۡ خَلَـٰٓئِفَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَرَفَعَ بَعۡضَكُمۡ فَوۡقَ بَعۡضٖ دَرَجَٰتٖ لِّيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ فِي مَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۗ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ وَإِنَّهُۥ لَغَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمُۢ﴾ (Quran 6:165)

**"And it is He who has made you successors upon the earth and has raised some of you above others in degrees that He may test you through what He has given you."**

## Word Meanings
- **Ja'ala az-zulumat wan-nur**: made the darknesses and the light.
- **Kataba 'ala nafsihi ar-rahmah**: decreed upon Himself mercy.
- **Al-Qahir**: the Subjugator.
- **Umam**: communities.
- **Mafatih al-ghayb**: the keys of the unseen.
- **Al-Afilin**: those that set, disappear.
- **Hanifan**: inclining to truth.
- **Yalbisu**: mix.
- **Faliq**: cleaver, splitter.
- **An-Nawa**: date seeds.
- **Ad-dhurriyyah**: descendants.
- **Haraj**: tight, constricted.
- **Hasad**: harvest.
- **Imlaq**: poverty.
- **Nusuki**: my rites of sacrifice.
- **Khala'if**: successors.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Not a leaf falls but that He knows it"**: infinite knowledge in a single image.
2. **"I like not those that disappear"**: a whole theology in four words.
3. **"Communities like you"**: dignity for animals and birds.
4. **"Dead and We gave him life"**: faith as resurrection.
5. **"As though climbing into the sky"**: the tightness of the misguided chest.
6. **"My living and my dying are for Allah"**: total devotion.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 6).
- **Izhar**: in *waraqatin illa* (verse 59), the tanween before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *tudrikuhu* (verse 103), the dal sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Iqlab**: in *'adwan bi-ghayri 'ilm* (verse 108), the tanween before the ba' becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna salati* (verse 162), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Ma'idah before it ended with "To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth", and Al-An'am opens with "All praise is due to Allah, who created the heavens and the earth". Surah Al-A'raf after it continues with the story of Adam and the prophets.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Begin with praise of Allah.**
2. **Trust that Allah alone removes harm.**
3. **Remember that Allah knows the falling leaf** and your hidden deeds.
4. **Reflect on creation** like Ibrahim.
5. **Keep your faith free from shirk.**
6. **Do not insult what others worship.**
7. **Ask Allah to expand your chest to Islam.**
8. **Memorise the ten commandments.**
9. **Follow the one straight path.**
10. **Make your whole life for Allah.**

- **Weeks 1–2**: verses 1–58.
- **Weeks 3–4**: verses 59–110.

- **Week 7**: verses 151–165.

## Teaching Children
- **Watch a leaf fall** and read verse 59.
- **Tell them Ibrahim's story** with the star, moon and sun.
- **Teach them verse 162** as a statement of who they are.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is it called Surah Al-An'am?
Because it refutes the pagan superstitions about cattle (an'am) and crops in verses 136–150.

### What are the keys of the unseen in verse 59?
Knowledge only Allah has; He knows everything on land and sea, every falling leaf and every hidden seed.

### What did Ibrahim say about the star, moon and sun?
"I like not those that disappear", meaning anything that sets cannot be God.

### What is the "injustice" in verse 82?
Shirk, as the Prophet ﷺ explained, citing Luqman.

### What are the ten commandments in Al-An'am?
The prohibitions and commands in verses 151–153, beginning with not associating anything with Allah and ending with following His straight path.

### What does "my prayer, my sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah" mean?
That a believer's entire life and death are devoted to Allah alone.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-An'am?
165 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-An'am fills the heart with the greatness of Allah: the One who knows the falling leaf, who brings the living from the dead, and whom no vision can encompass. It shows Ibrahim turning away from everything that sets, lists the commandments of the straight path, and ends with the sentence every believer can make his life's motto: my prayer, my sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds.
