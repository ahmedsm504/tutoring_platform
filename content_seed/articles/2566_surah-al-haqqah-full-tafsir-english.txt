Surah Al-Haqqah begins with a word repeated three times as if to shake the listener: "The Inevitable Reality. What is the Inevitable Reality? And what will make you know what the Inevitable Reality is?" It then reminds us of nations who denied that Day and were wiped out: Thamud by a deafening blast, 'Ad by a furious wind for seven nights and eight days. Then it moves to the Day itself, when the Trumpet is blown once, the mountains are crushed and the sky splits. It shows two people receiving their records: one so happy he shows it to everyone, "Here, read my record!", and one who wishes he had never been given his. It ends by proving that the Quran is not poetry or soothsaying, but "the word of a noble messenger".

This guide explains Surah Al-Haqqah section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Haqqah
- **Number of verses**: 52.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 69, in the twenty-ninth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Haqqah means "the Inevitable Reality", a name for the Day of Resurrection, because it will certainly happen and everything will become clear in it.
- **Main themes**: the certainty of the Last Day, the fate of the deniers, the two records and the truth of the Quran.

## Verses 1–3: The Inevitable Reality
﴿ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ﴾ ﴿مَا ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ﴾ (Quran 69:1-3)

**The repetition** is deliberate. A single word, then a question about it, then a second question that says: you cannot grasp it. It prepares the heart to listen to everything that follows.

## Verses 4–12: Nations That Denied
﴿كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ وَعَادُۢ بِٱلۡقَارِعَةِ﴾ ﴿فَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَأُهۡلِكُواْ بِٱلطَّاغِيَةِ﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّا عَادࣱ فَأُهۡلِكُواْ بِرِيحٖ صَرۡصَرٍ عَاتِيَةٖ﴾ ﴿سَخَّرَهَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَبۡعَ لَيَالٖ وَثَمَٰنِيَةَ أَيَّامٍ حُسُومٗاۖ فَتَرَى ٱلۡقَوۡمَ فِيهَا صَرۡعَىٰ كَأَنَّهُمۡ أَعۡجَازُ نَخۡلٍ خَاوِيَةٖ﴾ ﴿فَهَلۡ تَرَىٰ لَهُم مِّنۢ بَاقِيَةٖ﴾ (Quran 69:4-8)

**Thamud and 'Ad denied "the Striking Calamity"** (*al-Qari'ah*), another name for the Day.
- **Thamud** were destroyed **"by the overpowering blast"**.
- **'Ad** were destroyed **"by a screaming, violent wind"**, which Allah imposed on them **"for seven nights and eight days in succession"**. **"You would see the people therein fallen as if they were hollow trunks of palm trees."** **"Then do you see of them any remains?"**

﴿وَجَآءَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ وَمَن قَبۡلَهُۥ وَٱلۡمُؤۡتَفِكَٰتُ بِٱلۡخَاطِئَةِ﴾ ﴿فَعَصَوۡاْ رَسُولَ رَبِّهِمۡ فَأَخَذَهُمۡ أَخۡذَةٗ رَّابِيَةً﴾ ﴿إِنَّا لَمَّا طَغَا ٱلۡمَآءُ حَمَلۡنَٰكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡجَارِيَةِ﴾ ﴿لِنَجۡعَلَهَا لَكُمۡ تَذۡكِرَةٗ وَتَعِيَهَآ أُذُنࣱ وَٰعِيَةࣱ﴾ (Quran 69:9-12)

**Pharaoh, those before him and the overturned cities** (the cities of Lut) also sinned and disobeyed the messenger of their Lord, **"so He seized them with a seizing exceeding"**.

**The ark of Nuh**: **"When the water overflowed, We carried you in the sailing ship, that We might make it for you a reminder, and that a conscious ear would be conscious of it."** We are all descendants of those who were carried in the ark, so the story is a favour to us and a reminder. **"A conscious ear"**: an ear that hears, understands and keeps.

## Verses 13–18: The Single Blast
﴿فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ نَفۡخَةࣱ وَٰحِدَةࣱ﴾ ﴿وَحُمِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَٱلۡجِبَالُ فَدُكَّتَا دَكَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ﴾ ﴿فَيَوۡمَئِذٖ وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ﴾ ﴿وَٱنشَقَّتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَهِيَ يَوۡمَئِذٖ وَاهِيَةࣱ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡمَلَكُ عَلَىٰٓ أَرۡجَآئِهَاۚ وَيَحۡمِلُ عَرۡشَ رَبِّكَ فَوۡقَهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ ثَمَٰنِيَةࣱ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَئِذٖ تُعۡرَضُونَ لَا تَخۡفَىٰ مِنكُمۡ خَافِيَةࣱ﴾ (Quran 69:13-18)

**"When the Trumpet is blown with one blast, and the earth and the mountains are lifted and crushed with a single crush"**: on that Day the Event occurs. **The sky splits** and becomes frail. **The angels are at its edges.** **"And there will bear the Throne of your Lord above them, that Day, eight."**

**"That Day you will be exhibited; not hidden among you is anything concealed."** Nothing at all will be hidden: no deed, no intention, no secret.

## Verses 19–24: "Here, Read My Record!"
﴿فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ هَآؤُمُ ٱقۡرَءُواْ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ﴾ ﴿إِنِّي ظَنَنتُ أَنِّي مُلَٰقٍ حِسَابِيَهۡ﴾ ﴿فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةٖ رَّاضِيَةٖ﴾ ﴿فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٖ﴾ ﴿قُطُوفُهَا دَانِيَةࣱ﴾ ﴿كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓـَٔۢا بِمَآ أَسۡلَفۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَيَّامِ ٱلۡخَالِيَةِ﴾ (Quran 69:19-24)

**The one given his record in his right hand** is so happy he calls out to everyone: **"Here, read my record!"** like a student who passed with honours. **"Indeed, I was certain that I would be meeting my account."** That is his secret: in this world he lived with certainty that he would be held to account, and that certainty made him prepare.

**How Allah deals with the believer**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Umar reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah will bring the believer close, place His cover over him and screen him, and say: Do you recognise such-and-such sin? Do you recognise such-and-such sin? He will say: Yes, my Lord. Until, when He has made him admit his sins and he thinks he is ruined, Allah will say: I covered them for you in the world, and I forgive them for you today. Then he will be given the record of his good deeds."

**His reward**: **"a pleasing life, in an elevated garden, its fruit to be picked hanging near"**. And he is told: **"Eat and drink in satisfaction for what you put forth in the days past."** Those "days past" are our days now.

## Verses 25–37: "I Wish I Had Not Been Given My Record"
﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِشِمَالِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أُوتَ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ﴾ ﴿وَلَمۡ أَدۡرِ مَا حِسَابِيَهۡ﴾ ﴿يَٰلَيۡتَهَا كَانَتِ ٱلۡقَاضِيَةَ﴾ ﴿مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنِّي مَالِيَهۡۜ﴾ ﴿هَلَكَ عَنِّي سُلۡطَٰنِيَهۡ﴾ (Quran 69:25-29)

**The one given his record in his left hand** says: **"I wish I had not been given my record, and had not known what is my account. I wish my death had been the decisive one."** Then the two great regrets: **"My wealth has not availed me. Gone from me is my authority."** The two things he trusted in this world, money and power, are useless.

﴿خُذُوهُ فَغُلُّوهُ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ صَلُّوهُ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ فِي سِلۡسِلَةٖ ذَرۡعُهَا سَبۡعُونَ ذِرَاعٗا فَٱسۡلُكُوهُ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ﴾ ﴿فَلَيۡسَ لَهُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ هَٰهُنَا حَمِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَلَا طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِنۡ غِسۡلِينٖ﴾ ﴿لَّا يَأۡكُلُهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡخَٰطِـُٔونَ﴾ (Quran 69:30-37)

**"Seize him and shackle him. Then into Hellfire drive him. Then into a chain whose length is seventy cubits insert him."**

**Why?** **"Indeed, he did not use to believe in Allah, the Most Great, nor did he encourage the feeding of the poor."** Notice that failing to *encourage* feeding the poor is mentioned beside disbelief. Neglecting the poor is a grave matter in the sight of Allah. **"So there is not for him here this Day any devoted friend, nor any food except from the discharge of wounds."**

## Verses 38–52: The Word of a Noble Messenger
﴿فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِمَا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَمَا لَا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولٖ كَرِيمٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوۡلِ شَاعِرٖۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَا بِقَوۡلِ كَاهِنٖۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلࣱ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 69:38-43)

**"So I swear by what you see and what you do not see"**: an oath covering everything, seen and unseen. **"Indeed, it is the word of a noble messenger"**: the Quran is the word of Allah conveyed by a noble messenger. **"It is not the word of a poet; little do you believe. Nor the word of a soothsayer; little do you remember."** **"It is a revelation from the Lord of the worlds."**

﴿وَلَوۡ تَقَوَّلَ عَلَيۡنَا بَعۡضَ ٱلۡأَقَاوِيلِ﴾ ﴿لَأَخَذۡنَا مِنۡهُ بِٱلۡيَمِينِ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ لَقَطَعۡنَا مِنۡهُ ٱلۡوَتِينَ﴾ ﴿فَمَا مِنكُم مِّنۡ أَحَدٍ عَنۡهُ حَٰجِزِينَ﴾ (Quran 69:44-47)

**The strongest proof of truthfulness**: if the Prophet ﷺ had invented even some sayings and attributed them to Allah, **"We would have seized him by the right hand, then We would have cut from him the aorta"**, and none of you could have protected him. He lived and his message triumphed, which shows he spoke the truth.

﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَذۡكِرَةࣱ لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّا لَنَعۡلَمُ أَنَّ مِنكُم مُّكَذِّبِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَسۡرَةٌ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَقُّ ٱلۡيَقِينِ﴾ ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ (Quran 69:48-52)

**"And indeed, it is a reminder for the righteous... And indeed, it is the certain truth"** (*haqq al-yaqin*). **"So exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great."**

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Haqqah**: the Inevitable Reality.
- **Al-Qari'ah**: the Striking Calamity.
- **At-Taghiyah**: the overpowering blast.
- **Sarsar**: screaming, freezing wind.
- **Husuman**: in succession, uninterrupted.
- **A'jaz nakhl khawiyah**: hollow trunks of palm trees.
- **Al-Mu'tafikat**: the overturned cities.
- **Al-Jariyah**: the sailing ship.
- **Wa'iyah**: conscious, retaining.
- **Ha'umu**: here, take.
- **Qutufuha daniyah**: its fruit hanging near.
- **Sultaniyah**: my authority.
- **Ghislin**: the discharge of wounds.
- **Al-Watin**: the aorta.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The threefold opening**: a word, a question, a deeper question.
2. **"Hollow trunks of palm trees"**: a haunting image of fallen bodies.
3. **"Here, read my record!"**: the joy of success in a single cry.
4. **The *-iyah* endings**: *kitabiyah*, *hisabiyah*, *maliyah*, *sultaniyah*: a rhythm of grief and joy.
5. **"Little do you believe... little do you remember"**: matching the denial of poetry with faith and soothsaying with remembrance.
6. **"Seized him by the right hand... cut from him the aorta"**: a vivid picture of what would have happened to a liar.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim kalimi muthaqqal**: in *al-haqqah* (verse 1), the alif followed by a doubled qaf is stretched six counts.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *bi-rihin sarsarin* (verse 6), the tanween is hidden before the sad.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *sab'a* (verse 7), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *nafkhatun wahidah* (verse 13), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Ha' as-sakt**: in *kitabiyah*, *hisabiyah*, *maliyah* and *sultaniyah*, the final ha' is pronounced with sukun whether stopping or continuing.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Qalam before it ended with the deniers calling the Prophet ﷺ mad and the Quran "a reminder to the worlds", and Al-Haqqah proves it is "the word of a noble messenger", not poetry or soothsaying. Surah Al-Ma'arij after it begins with a questioner asking about a punishment bound to happen, continuing the description of the Day.

## Living With Certainty of the Reckoning
"Indeed, I was certain that I would be meeting my account": this one sentence is the secret of the happy person. Practical ways to build this certainty:
1. **Account for yourself daily** before you are accounted for.
2. **Remember death** often.
3. **Read about the Day of Resurrection** in the Quran with reflection.
4. **Think of every deed as a page in your record.**
5. **Ask yourself: would I be happy to show this to everyone?**

## Feeding the Poor in Surah Al-Haqqah
The surah mentions not encouraging the feeding of the poor as a reason for punishment. Even if you cannot feed people yourself, you can encourage others, support charities, share appeals and remind your family. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ was asked which Islam is best, and he said: "That you feed people and give salam to those you know and those you do not know."

## Practical Lessons
1. **Learn from the destroyed nations**: power did not save them.
2. **Be "a conscious ear"** that listens and retains.
3. **Live with certainty of the reckoning.**
4. **Do not rely on wealth or status.**
5. **Feed the poor and encourage others to do so.**
6. **Treat the Quran as the word of Allah**, not as poetry or entertainment.
7. **Exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great.**

- **Tell them the story of the ark** and how Allah saved the believers.
- **Explain receiving the record in the right hand** like getting a good report card.
- **Teach them to share food with the poor.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is al-Haqqah?
A name for the Day of Resurrection, meaning the Inevitable Reality that will certainly happen.

### How was 'Ad destroyed?
By a screaming, violent wind for seven nights and eight days in succession.

### Who carries the Throne on the Day of Resurrection?
The surah says "eight" will carry the Throne of your Lord above them that Day.

### What does "Here, read my record!" mean?
It is the joyful cry of the person given his record in his right hand, inviting others to read it.

### Why is the one given his record in his left hand punished?
Because he did not believe in Allah, the Most Great, and did not encourage feeding the poor.

### What does "it is the word of a noble messenger" mean?
The Quran is the word of Allah conveyed by a noble messenger, not poetry or soothsaying.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Haqqah?
52 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Haqqah sets two scenes before you: one person proudly holding up his record, and another wishing he had never seen his. The difference was certainty: one lived knowing he would meet his account, the other trusted his money and power. Live with certainty, feed the poor, take the Quran as the truth it is, and exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great.
