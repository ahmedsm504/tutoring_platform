**Surah At-Takathur** (8 ayat) describes a disease of every age: **endless competition for more**.

﴿أَلۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ﴾ ﴿حَتَّىٰ زُرۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَقَابِرَ﴾ — *Competition in increase distracted you until you visited the graves.*

## The hadith
- The Prophet ﷺ recited this surah and said: «The son of Adam says: My wealth, my wealth! But do you have of your wealth anything except what you ate and used up, what you wore and wore out, or what you gave in charity and sent ahead?» [Muslim].

## The repeated warning
﴿كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾
- **Repetition** for emphasis: you will surely know the truth.

## Levels of certainty
| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿عِلۡمَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ﴾ | Certain knowledge |
| ﴿عَيۡنَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ﴾ | Seeing it with your eyes |

## The question
﴿ثُمَّ لَتُسۡـَٔلُنَّ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾ — *Then you will surely be asked that Day about pleasure.*
- **Every blessing**: health, time, food, water, security.

## Lessons
- **Enough is a blessing**; more is a test.
- **Ask yourself**: how am I using what Allah gave me?
