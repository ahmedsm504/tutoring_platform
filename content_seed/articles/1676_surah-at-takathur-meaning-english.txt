## Surah At-Takathur Meaning: Distracted by Piling Up

**Surah At-Takathur** (8 ayat) describes a disease of every age: **endless competition for more**.

﴿أَلۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ﴾ ﴿حَتَّىٰ زُرۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَقَابِرَ﴾ — *Competition in increase distracted you until you visited the graves.*

### The hadith
- The Prophet ﷺ recited this surah and said: «The son of Adam says: My wealth, my wealth! But do you have of your wealth anything except what you ate and used up, what you wore and wore out, or what you gave in charity and sent ahead?» [Muslim].

### The repeated warning
﴿كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾
- **Repetition** for emphasis: you will surely know the truth.

### Levels of certainty
| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿عِلۡمَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ﴾ | Certain knowledge |
| ﴿عَيۡنَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ﴾ | Seeing it with your eyes |

### The question
﴿ثُمَّ لَتُسۡـَٔلُنَّ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾ — *Then you will surely be asked that Day about pleasure.*
- **Every blessing**: health, time, food, water, security.

### Lessons
- **Enough is a blessing**; more is a test.
- **Ask yourself**: how am I using what Allah gave me?

## Surah Al-'Asr Meaning: Four Ways Out of Loss

Three short ayat that summarize the path to success.

﴿وَٱلۡعَصۡرِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَفِي خُسۡرٍ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ﴾

### The oath by time
- **Al-ʿAṣr**: time, or the late afternoon, as commentators explained.
- Allah swears by time to show its **value**: it is your life.

### Everyone is in loss…
- ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَفِي خُسۡرٍ﴾: every human, with emphasis.

### …except those with four qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Faith | Belief in Allah and what He revealed |
| Good deeds | Acting on that belief |
| Advising to truth | Encouraging one another to what is right |
| Advising to patience | Supporting one another through difficulty |
- The first two **fix yourself**; the last two **fix your community**.

### Ash-Shafi'i's comment
- It is reported that Imam ash-Shafi'i said: if people reflected on this surah alone, it would be enough for them.

### Lessons
- **Audit your day**: how much went to the four qualities?
- **Find good company** that reminds you of truth and patience.

## Surah Al-Humazah Meaning: Woe to Every Backbiter and Mocker

**Surah Al-Humazah** (9 ayat) links two sins that often go together: **mocking people** and **worshipping wealth**.

﴿وَيۡلࣱ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٖ لُّمَزَةٍ﴾ — *Woe to every scorner and mocker.*
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Humazah | One who mocks or slanders, often with gestures |
| Lumazah | One who finds fault with words |

### The wealth illusion
﴿ٱلَّذِي جَمَعَ مَالٗا وَعَدَّدَهُۥ﴾ ﴿يَحۡسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُۥٓ أَخۡلَدَهُۥ﴾
- **He counts his money** and thinks it will keep him forever.

### The crushing fire
﴿كَلَّاۖ لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ فِي ٱلۡحُطَمَةِ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحُطَمَةُ﴾ ﴿نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُوقَدَةُ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّتِي تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةِ﴾
- **It reaches the hearts**: the very place where arrogance and contempt lived.

### Why these two together?
- Many who look down on others do so **because of their wealth**.

### Lessons
| Sin | Remedy |
|---|---|
| Mocking people | Remember the Quran forbids ridicule, and you don't know who is better |
| Counting wealth | Spend in charity |

## Surah Al-Fil Meaning: The Army of the Elephant

**Surah Al-Fil** (The Elephant) tells a story the Arabs knew well, because it happened in the year the Prophet ﷺ was born, known as the **Year of the Elephant**.

### The story in brief
- A ruler from Yemen, named in the history books as **Abrahah**, marched to Makkah with an army and an elephant to destroy the Kaʿbah.
- Allah protected His House.

### Ayah by ayah
| Ayah | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡفِيلِ﴾ | Have you not seen what your Lord did to the people of the elephant? |
| ﴿أَلَمۡ يَجۡعَلۡ كَيۡدَهُمۡ فِي تَضۡلِيلٖ﴾ | He made their plan go astray |
| ﴿وَأَرۡسَلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ طَيۡرًا أَبَابِيلَ﴾ | He sent birds in flocks |
| ﴿تَرۡمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٖ مِّن سِجِّيلٖ﴾ | Striking them with stones of baked clay |
| ﴿فَجَعَلَهُمۡ كَعَصۡفٖ مَّأۡكُولِۭ﴾ | And made them like eaten straw |

### "Have you not seen?"
- The Prophet ﷺ did not see it with his eyes, but it was **so well known** that it was as if everyone had seen it.

### The link with Quraysh
- The next surah begins: ﴿لِإِيلَٰفِ قُرَيۡشٍ﴾. Allah protected the House so Quraysh could live in safety, so **they should worship the Lord of this House**.

### Lessons
- **No power** can defeat Allah's plan.
- **Allah protects** what He wills.

## Surah Quraysh Meaning: Food and Security from the Lord of This House

**Surah Quraysh** (4 ayat) continues the message of Surah Al-Fil: Allah protected His House, so worship Him.

﴿لِإِيلَٰفِ قُرَيۡشٍ﴾ ﴿إِۦلَٰفِهِمۡ رِحۡلَةَ ٱلشِّتَآءِ وَٱلصَّيۡفِ﴾ ﴿فَلۡيَعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَيۡتِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٖ وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنۡ خَوۡفِۭ﴾

### The journeys
| Season | Destination (as commentators mentioned) |
|---|---|
| Winter | Yemen |
| Summer | Ash-Shaam |
- Because they lived by the Kaʿbah, the Arabs respected their caravans.

### The command
- ﴿فَلۡيَعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَيۡتِ﴾: the natural response to blessing is **worship**.

### Two blessings
| Blessing | Ayah |
|---|---|
| Food | ﴿أَطۡعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٖ﴾ |
| Security | ﴿وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنۡ خَوۡفِۭ﴾ |
- These are the foundations of any stable life.

### Lessons
- **Your daily meal and safety** are gifts that call for worship.

## Surah Al-Ma'un Meaning: Faith That Shows in Kindness

**Surah Al-Ma'un** (7 ayat) shows that true faith appears in **how you treat people** and **how you pray**.

### Denial in action
﴿أَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿فَذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِي يَدُعُّ ٱلۡيَتِيمَ﴾ ﴿وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ﴾
- **Denying the Recompense** is not only a belief; it shows in pushing away orphans and ignoring the poor.

### Warning to those who pray
﴿فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُصَلِّينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَن صَلَاتِهِمۡ سَاهُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ يُرَآءُونَ﴾ ﴿وَيَمۡنَعُونَ ٱلۡمَاعُونَ﴾
| Fault | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Heedless of their prayer | Delaying it carelessly or neglecting it |
| Showing off | Praying to be seen |
| Withholding *al-māʿūn* | Refusing small help, like lending household items |
- **Note**: it says *ʿan* ṣalātihim (heedless **of** their prayer), not *fī* (in it). Scholars noted that occasional lapses of focus **in** prayer are not meant here.
- **Recitation tip**: do not stop on ﴿فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُصَلِّينَ﴾, but join it with what follows.

### Lessons
- **Prayer and kindness** go together.
- **Small help** matters to Allah.

## Surah Al-Kawthar Meaning: The Shortest Surah with a Great Promise

**Surah Al-Kawthar** is the shortest surah in the Quran, only three ayat, but it carries a great comfort for the Prophet ﷺ and his followers.

### Ayah 1
﴿إِنَّآ أَعۡطَيۡنَٰكَ ٱلۡكَوۡثَرَ﴾ — *Indeed, We have granted you Al-Kawthar.*
- **Al-Kawthar** means abundant good.
- The Prophet ﷺ described it as **a river in Paradise** that his Lord promised him, with much good in it [Muslim].

### Ayah 2
﴿فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنۡحَرۡ﴾ — *So pray to your Lord and sacrifice.*
- **Gratitude in action**: prayer and sacrifice for Allah alone.

### Ayah 3
﴿إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلۡأَبۡتَرُ﴾ — *Indeed, the one who hates you is the one cut off.*
- Some enemies mocked the Prophet ﷺ after his sons died, calling him "cut off".
- Allah answered: **his enemy** is the one cut off, while the Prophet's mention ﷺ fills the world.

### Lessons
| Lesson | Practice |
|---|---|
| Allah's gifts are greater than what people take | Don't despair at mockery |
| Gratitude is worship | Pray and give |
| Truth lasts | Stay firm |

### Recitation tips
- ﴿إِنَّآ﴾: full ghunnah on the doubled noon, then a madd of 4–5 counts.
- ﴿فَصَلِّ﴾: heavy ṣaad.
- ﴿وَٱنۡحَرۡ﴾: clear noon before ḥaa, and the raa is heavy when stopping.

## Surah Al-Kafirun Meaning: To You Your Religion, and to Me Mine

**Surah Al-Kafirun** (6 ayat) is a declaration of **pure worship**. The Prophet ﷺ recited it with Al-Ikhlas in the two units before Fajr [Muslim].

﴿قُلۡ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿لَآ أَعۡبُدُ مَا تَعۡبُدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَآ أَنتُمۡ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعۡبُدُ﴾ ﴿وَلَآ أَنَا۠ عَابِدࣱ مَّا عَبَدتُّمۡ﴾ ﴿وَلَآ أَنتُمۡ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعۡبُدُ﴾ ﴿لَكُمۡ دِينُكُمۡ وَلِيَ دِينِ﴾

### The context
- Commentators reported that some leaders of Quraysh proposed a compromise: worship their gods for a year and they would worship Allah for a year. This surah was the answer.

### Why the repetition?
| Ayat | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| 2–3 | Now: I do not worship what you worship |
| 4–5 | Past and future: I never have and never will |
- **Repetition closes every door** to compromise in worship.

### "To you your religion"
- This is **not approval** of their religion, but a **clear separation** in worship.
- At the same time, Islam forbids compulsion and calls for justice with all people.

### Lessons
- **Tolerance in dealings, clarity in belief.**

## Surah An-Nasr Meaning: Victory, Praise and Seeking Forgiveness

**Surah An-Nasr** (Victory) is short, but it carries a deep message: success should lead to **humility**, not pride.

### Ayah by ayah
| Ayah | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿إِذَا جَآءَ نَصۡرُ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡفَتۡحُ﴾ | When the help of Allah and the victory come |
| ﴿وَرَأَيۡتَ ٱلنَّاسَ يَدۡخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ ٱللَّهِ أَفۡوَاجٗا﴾ | And you see people entering Allah's religion in crowds |
| ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ تَوَّابَۢا﴾ | Glorify with praise of your Lord and seek His forgiveness; He is ever accepting of repentance |

### Ibn ʿAbbas's understanding
- ʿUmar asked the senior companions about this surah, and Ibn ʿAbbas said it was a sign that **the Prophet's life ﷺ was nearing its end**. ʿUmar said: I know of it only what you know [Al-Bukhari].

### The Prophet's practice
- ʿAishah said that after it was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ often said in his bowing and prostration: «Subḥānakallāhumma Rabbanā wa biḥamdik, Allāhummaghfir lī» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

### Lessons
- **After every success**: praise Allah and seek forgiveness.
- **Finishing a task** is a time for istighfār, as after prayer and Hajj.
- **Victory belongs to Allah**: *naṣrullāh*.

## Surah Al-Masad Meaning: The Fate of Abu Lahab

**Surah Al-Masad** (5 ayat) is the only surah that names an enemy of the Prophet ﷺ: his own uncle, **Abu Lahab**.

### Why it was revealed
- The Prophet ﷺ climbed Mount Safa and called his people to warn them. Abu Lahab said: "May you perish! Is this why you gathered us?" Then this surah was revealed [Al-Bukhari].

﴿تَبَّتۡ يَدَآ أَبِي لَهَبٖ وَتَبَّ﴾ ﴿مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُ مَالُهُۥ وَمَا كَسَبَ﴾ ﴿سَيَصۡلَىٰ نَارٗا ذَاتَ لَهَبٖ﴾ ﴿وَٱمۡرَأَتُهُۥ حَمَّالَةَ ٱلۡحَطَبِ﴾ ﴿فِي جِيدِهَا حَبۡلࣱ مِّن مَّسَدِۭ﴾

### Meaning
| Ayah | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | His efforts are ruined, and he is ruined |
| 2 | His wealth and gains did not help him |
| 3 | He will burn in a blazing fire |
| 4 | His wife, the carrier of firewood |
| 5 | Around her neck a rope of palm fibre |
- **"Carrier of firewood"**: commentators said she spread thorns in the Prophet's path ﷺ, or spread gossip that kindles enmity.

### A prophecy fulfilled
- Abu Lahab lived for years after this surah, and he **never accepted Islam**, even outwardly, confirming the Quran's words.

### Lessons
- **Family ties do not save** without faith.
- **Wealth does not protect** from Allah's decree.

## Surah Al-Ikhlas Meaning: Why It Equals a Third of the Quran

Four short ayat that describe Allah more clearly than many books. **Surah Al-Ikhlas** (Sincerity) is the surah of pure belief in One God.

### Ayah 1
﴿قُلۡ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ﴾ — *Say: He is Allah, One.*
- **Aḥad** means One, unique, with no partner and nothing like Him.

### Ayah 2
﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ﴾ — *Allah, As-Samad.*
- **As-Samad**: the Master whom all creation turns to in their needs, while He needs no one.

### Ayah 3
﴿لَمۡ يَلِدۡ وَلَمۡ يُولَدۡ﴾ — *He neither begets nor is begotten.*
- He has no child and no parent.

### Ayah 4
﴿وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدُۢ﴾ — *And there is none comparable to Him.*

### A third of the Quran
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «By the One in whose hand is my soul, it equals a third of the Quran» [Al-Bukhari].
- Scholars explained that the Quran covers **belief, law and stories**, and Al-Ikhlas is pure **belief in Allah**.
- This is about **reward**, not that it replaces reading the whole Quran.

### The companion who loved it
- A companion used to end every recitation in prayer with Al-Ikhlas. When asked, he said: "It is the description of the Most Merciful, and I love to recite it."
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Tell him that Allah loves him» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

### Recitation tips
| Word | Tip |
|---|---|
| ﴿أَحَدٌ﴾ | Clear ḥaa, not haa; qalqalah on daal when stopping |
| ﴿ٱلصَّمَدُ﴾ | Heavy ṣaad |
| ﴿كُفُوًا أَحَدُۢ﴾ | Clear tanween before the hamza |

## Surah Al-Falaq Meaning: Seeking Refuge from Four Evils

**Surah Al-Falaq** and **Surah An-Nas** are called *al-muʿawwidhatān*: the two surahs of seeking refuge.

### Ayah 1
﴿قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلۡفَلَقِ﴾ — *Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak.*
- **Al-Falaq**: the breaking of dawn. The One who brings light out of darkness can protect you from every darkness.

### The four evils
| Ayah | Evil |
|---|---|
| ﴿مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ﴾ | The evil of everything He created that has harm in it |
| ﴿وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ﴾ | The night when its darkness settles |
| ﴿وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّـٰثَٰتِ فِي ٱلۡعُقَدِ﴾ | Those who blow on knots: magic |
| ﴿وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ﴾ | The envier when he acts on his envy |

### When to recite it
- **After every prayer**: the Prophet ﷺ told ʿUqbah ibn ʿĀmir to recite the muʿawwidhāt after each prayer [Abu Dawud].
- **Before sleep**: the Prophet ﷺ would blow into his hands, recite Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas, and wipe over his body [Al-Bukhari].
- **Morning and evening**: three times each [Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi].

### Recitation tips
- ﴿ٱلۡفَلَقِ﴾: major qalqalah on the qaf when stopping.
- ﴿شَرِّ﴾: don't over-roll the raa.
- ﴿حَاسِدٍ إِذَا﴾: clear tanween before the hamza.

## Surah An-Nas Meaning: Refuge from the Whisperer

The last surah of the Quran is a shield against an enemy you cannot see: **the whisperer**.

### Three descriptions of Allah
﴿قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾ ﴿مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾ ﴿إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾
| Description | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Rabb (Lord) | He creates and takes care of you |
| Malik (King) | He rules over everything |
| Ilah (God) | He alone deserves worship |
- **Three descriptions** for one evil, showing how serious the whispers are.

### The whisperer
﴿مِن شَرِّ ٱلۡوَسۡوَاسِ ٱلۡخَنَّاسِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي يُوَسۡوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾ ﴿مِنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ﴾
- **Al-Waswās**: the one who whispers again and again.
- **Al-Khannās**: the one who withdraws; scholars explained that he retreats when Allah is remembered and returns with heedlessness.
- **From jinn and men**: bad suggestions can come from both.

### Resisting whispers
| Step | How |
|---|---|
| Remember Allah | Dhikr makes the whisperer withdraw |
| Seek refuge | Recite Al-Falaq and An-Nas |
| Don't engage | Ignore and move on |
| Keep good company | Avoid people who push you to wrong |

### When to recite it
- After every prayer, before sleep, and morning and evening, together with Al-Ikhlas and Al-Falaq.
