**Surah Ibrahim** (52 ayat) speaks of bringing people out of darkness into light.

## Messengers in their own language
- Every messenger was sent in the language of his people, **to make the message clear** to them.

## The law of gratitude
﴿وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمۡ لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدࣱ﴾

## The good word and the bad word
| Word | Parable |
|---|---|
| The good word (*la ilaha illa Allah*, as many scholars explain) | A good tree: firm roots, branches in the sky, fruit in every season |
| The bad word | A tree uprooted from the earth, with no stability |

## Satan's speech
- On the Day of Judgment Satan will say: *I had no authority over you except that I invited you and you responded. So do not blame me; blame yourselves.*

## Ibrahim's prayers
- He prayed for Makkah to be a place of security, to be kept away from idol worship, for hearts to incline toward his family settled in a barren valley, and for himself and his descendants to establish prayer.
- *Our Lord, forgive me, my parents and the believers on the Day the account is established.*

## Lessons
- **Gratitude multiplies blessings.**
- **Responsibility is personal**: no one can be blamed for our choices.
