**Surah Al-Inshiqaq** (25 ayat) contains one of the most personal addresses in the Quran: *O man, you are toiling toward your Lord.*

## The sky obeys
﴿إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنشَقَّتۡ﴾ ﴿وَأَذِنَتۡ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتۡ﴾ — *When the sky splits open and listens to its Lord, as it must.*
- **Even the sky** obeys its Lord. What about you?

## Toiling toward your Lord
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدۡحٗا فَمُلَٰقِيهِ﴾
- **Every effort** you make, good or bad, is a step toward meeting Allah.

## The two records
﴿فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ﴾ ﴿فَسَوۡفَ يُحَاسَبُ حِسَابٗا يَسِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ مَسۡرُورٗا﴾
| Record | Result |
|---|---|
| In the right hand | An easy account and returning to family in joy |
| Behind the back | Calling for destruction |
- The Prophet ﷺ explained to ʿAishah that the "easy account" is only the presentation of deeds [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

## The prostration
- At ayah 21 there is a **prostration of recitation**. Abu Hurayrah prostrated there and said he had seen the Prophet ﷺ do so [Muslim].

## Lessons
- **Make your toil worthwhile**: it will be shown to you.
