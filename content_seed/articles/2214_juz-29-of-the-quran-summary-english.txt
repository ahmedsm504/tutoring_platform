Juz 29 begins with ﴿تَبَٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ﴾, "Blessed is He in whose hand is the dominion, and He has power over all things", so it is called **Juz Tabarak**. It contains eleven complete surahs: **Al-Mulk**, **Al-Qalam**, **Al-Haqqah**, **Al-Ma'arij**, **Nuh**, **Al-Jinn**, **Al-Muzzammil**, **Al-Muddaththir**, **Al-Qiyamah**, **Al-Insan** and **Al-Mursalat**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 562 to 581**.

It is **the juz of beginnings and endings**: it contains some of the earliest revelation (Al-Muzzammil and Al-Muddaththir), some of the most powerful scenes of the Day of Resurrection (Al-Haqqah, Al-Qiyamah and Al-Mursalat), and the training of the caller to Allah in night prayer, patience and character. Many people memorize it after Juz 'Amma.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **testing and preparing for the Day of Decision**. Its opening declares the purpose: ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ أَيُّكُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلٗاۚ﴾, "who created death and life to test which of you is best in deeds." Al-Haqqah, Al-Ma'arij, Al-Qiyamah and Al-Mursalat show scenes of that Day; Al-Qalam, Al-Muzzammil and Al-Muddaththir draw the picture of the caller of fine character who is patient and stands at night; and Al-Insan closes with the righteous who feed others for the sake of Allah.

The message: **life is a test of the best deeds and the Resurrection is real, so prepare through night prayer, good character and kindness to people.**

## Surah one: Al-Mulk (1-30)

Al-Mulk is Makki, with 30 verses. In Sunan At-Tirmidhi and Abu Dawud, the Prophet ﷺ said: "A surah of the Quran of thirty verses interceded for a man until he was forgiven: Tabarak alladhi biyadihi al-mulk." For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Mulk Meaning in English](/blog/khalaqa-al-mawta-wal-hayat-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Mulk-Meaning-The-Surah-That-Intercedes).

### 1. Dominion and testing (1-5)
﴿تَبَٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ أَيُّكُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلٗاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡغَفُورُ﴾, "Blessed is He in whose hand is the dominion, and He has power over all things; who created death and life to test which of you is best in deeds, and He is the Almighty, the Forgiving." Then: ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٖ طِبَاقٗاۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مِن تَفَٰوُتٖۖ فَٱرۡجِعِ ٱلۡبَصَرَ هَلۡ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٖ﴾, "who created seven heavens in layers. You see no flaw in the creation of the Most Merciful. Look again: do you see any rifts?"

**Reflect**: He said "best in deeds", not "most in deeds": what counts is excellence and sincerity, not quantity.

### 2. The regret of the people of the Fire and Allah's knowledge (6-14)
Whenever a group is thrown in, its keepers ask them: did no warner come to you? Then: ﴿وَقَالُواْ لَوۡ كُنَّا نَسۡمَعُ أَوۡ نَعۡقِلُ مَا كُنَّا فِيٓ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ﴾, "They will say: had we listened or reasoned, we would not be among the companions of the Blaze." Then: ﴿وَأَسِرُّواْ قَوۡلَكُمۡ أَوِ ٱجۡهَرُواْ بِهِۦٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾ ﴿أَلَا يَعۡلَمُ مَنۡ خَلَقَ وَهُوَ ٱللَّطِيفُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ﴾, "Conceal your speech or proclaim it; He knows what is in the chests. Does He not know, who created? He is the Subtle, the Aware."

### 3. Favours of earth and sky (15-30)
﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ ذَلُولٗا فَٱمۡشُواْ فِي مَنَاكِبِهَا وَكُلُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِهِۦۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ ٱلنُّشُورُ﴾, "It is He who made the earth tame for you, so walk among its slopes and eat of His provision; and to Him is the resurrection." Then the birds spreading and folding their wings, held up only by the Most Merciful. The close: ﴿قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَصۡبَحَ مَآؤُكُمۡ غَوۡرٗا فَمَن يَأۡتِيكُم بِمَآءٖ مَّعِينِۭ﴾, "Say: have you considered, if your water were to sink away, who could bring you flowing water?"

**Lesson**: working on earth is worship when joined with remembering the resurrection.

## Surah two: Al-Qalam (1-52)

Al-Qalam is Makki, with 52 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Qalam Meaning in English](/blog/khalaqa-al-mawta-wal-hayat-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Qalam-Meaning-By-the-Pen-and-the-Prophet-s-Great-Ch).

### 1. Great character (1-16)
﴿نٓۚ وَٱلۡقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسۡطُرُونَ﴾, "Nun. By the pen and what they write": by your Lord's favour you are not mad. Then the divine testimony: ﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾, "And you are truly of a great character." In Sahih Muslim, 'A'ishah was asked about the Prophet's character ﷺ and said: "His character was the Quran." Then the command not to obey the habitual swearer, the slanderer who goes about with gossip.

### 2. The owners of the garden (17-33)
﴿إِنَّا بَلَوۡنَٰهُمۡ كَمَا بَلَوۡنَآ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ إِذۡ أَقۡسَمُواْ لَيَصۡرِمُنَّهَا مُصۡبِحِينَ﴾, "We have tested them as We tested the owners of the garden, when they swore to harvest it in the morning", without saying "if Allah wills". A visitation from your Lord came upon it while they slept, and it became like a harvested field. When they saw it they said: we have lost our way; no, we have been deprived. Then: glory be to our Lord; we were wrongdoers.

**Lesson**: the intention to deny the poor deprived the owners of everything. Stinginess begins as an intention and then destroys the harvest.

### 3. Divine justice and the man of the whale (34-52)
﴿أَفَنَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ كَٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ﴾, "Shall We treat the Muslims like the criminals?" Then the Day the shin is uncovered and they are called to prostrate but cannot. Then: ﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تَكُن كَصَاحِبِ ٱلۡحُوتِ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ وَهُوَ مَكۡظُومࣱ﴾, "So be patient for your Lord's decision, and do not be like the man of the whale when he called out in distress." The close: ﴿وَإِن يَكَادُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَيُزۡلِقُونَكَ بِأَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡ لَمَّا سَمِعُواْ ٱلذِّكۡرَ﴾, "Those who disbelieve would almost strike you down with their eyes when they hear the reminder."

## Surah three: Al-Haqqah (1-52)

Al-Haqqah is Makki, with 52 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Haqqa Meaning in English](/blog/khalaqa-al-mawta-wal-hayat-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Haqqah-Meaning-The-Inevitable-Reality).

### 1. The fates of the deniers (1-12)
﴿ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ﴾, "The Inevitable Reality": what is the Inevitable Reality, and what will make you know what it is? Thamud and 'Ad denied the Striking Hour: Thamud were destroyed by the overwhelming blast, and 'Ad by a screaming, violent wind imposed on them for seven nights and eight days in succession. Then Pharaoh, the overturned towns, and the people of Nuh.

### 2. The Day of display and the records (13-37)
﴿فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ نَفۡخَةࣱ وَٰحِدَةࣱ﴾, "When the Horn is blown with a single blast", and the earth and mountains are lifted and crushed with a single blow. Then:
- **The one given his record in his right hand**: ﴿فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ هَآؤُمُ ٱقۡرَءُواْ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ﴾, "As for one given his record in his right hand, he will say: here, read my record!" I was certain I would meet my account. He is told: ﴿كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓـَٔۢا بِمَآ أَسۡلَفۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَيَّامِ ٱلۡخَالِيَةِ﴾, "Eat and drink in satisfaction for what you put forward in days past."
- **The one given his record in his left hand**: ﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِشِمَالِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أُوتَ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ﴾, "As for one given his record in his left hand, he will say: I wish I had not been given my record", then ﴿مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنِّي مَالِيَهۡۜ﴾ ﴿هَلَكَ عَنِّي سُلۡطَٰنِيَهۡ﴾, "My wealth has not availed me; my authority has gone from me." Then the cause of his ruin: he did not believe in Allah, the Most Great, nor encourage feeding the poor.

**Reflect**: the secret of the first man's salvation was "I was certain I would meet my account". Certainty of reckoning sets deeds right.

### 3. The truth of the Quran (38-52)
I swear by what you see and what you do not see, ﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولٖ كَرِيمٖ﴾, "it is the word of a noble messenger", not the word of a poet or a soothsayer. The close: ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾, "So glorify the name of your Lord, the Most Great."

## Surah four: Al-Ma'arij (1-44)

Al-Ma'arij is Makki, with 44 verses. For the whole surah see ["Man Was Created Anxious, Except Those Who Pray": Quran 70:19-22 Explained](/blog/khalaqa-al-mawta-wal-hayat-meaning-english/#Man-Was-Created-Anxious-Except-Those-Who-Pray-Quran-70-19-22).

### 1. A Day of fifty thousand years (1-18)
A questioner asked about a punishment bound to happen; the angels and the Spirit ascend to Him in a Day whose length is fifty thousand years, so be patient with a beautiful patience. Then the Day the sky is like molten metal and the mountains like wool, no friend asks about a friend, and the criminal wishes he could ransom himself with his children.

### 2. Anxious man and the exception (19-35)
A new quarter: ﴿۞إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ خُلِقَ هَلُوعًا﴾ ﴿إِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ جَزُوعٗا﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلۡخَيۡرُ مَنُوعًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱلۡمُصَلِّينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ دَآئِمُونَ﴾, "Man was created anxious: when evil touches him, impatient; when good touches him, withholding; except those who pray, who are constant in their prayer." Then their traits: in their wealth is a known right for the one who asks and the deprived; they believe in the Day of Judgement; they fear their Lord's punishment; they guard their chastity; they honour their trusts and promises; they stand firm in their testimonies; and they guard their prayers.

**Lesson**: the cure for anxiety, impatience and stinginess is constant prayer. As in Al-Mu'minun, the traits begin and end with prayer.

### 3. The close (36-44)
What is with the disbelievers, hastening towards you? Does each of them hope to enter a garden of bliss? Then the Day they emerge from the graves rushing as if racing to a goal.

## Surah five: Nuh (1-28)

Nuh is Makki, with 28 verses, entirely about the story of Nuh, peace be upon him. For the whole surah see [Surah Nuh Meaning in English](/blog/khalaqa-al-mawta-wal-hayat-meaning-english/#Surah-Nuh-Meaning-950-Years-of-Patient-Calling).

### 1. Methods of calling (1-9)
We sent Nuh to his people: warn your people. Then Nuh's complaint: my Lord, I called my people night and day, but my call only increased them in flight; whenever I called them, they put their fingers in their ears; then I called them openly, then I announced to them publicly and confided to them privately.

**Reflect**: Nuh varied the times (night and day) and the methods (openly, publicly, privately), and was patient with it for centuries.

### 2. The fruits of seeking forgiveness (10-20)
﴿فَقُلۡتُ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفَّارٗا﴾ ﴿يُرۡسِلِ ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّدۡرَارٗا﴾ ﴿وَيُمۡدِدۡكُم بِأَمۡوَٰلٖ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ أَنۡهَٰرٗا﴾, "I said: seek your Lord's forgiveness; He is Perpetually Forgiving. He will send rain from the sky upon you in abundance, give you wealth and children, and provide for you gardens and rivers." Then: ﴿مَّا لَكُمۡ لَا تَرۡجُونَ لِلَّهِ وَقَارٗا﴾, "What is wrong with you that you do not attribute to Allah due grandeur?"

**Lesson**: seeking forgiveness is a key to rain, wealth, children and gardens; whoever finds his provision tight should increase it.

### 3. The idols and the final prayer (21-28)
He names their idols: Wadd, Suwa', Yaghuth, Ya'uq and Nasr. Then the comprehensive prayer: ﴿رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيۡتِيَ مُؤۡمِنٗا وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۖ﴾, "My Lord, forgive me, my parents, whoever enters my house as a believer, and the believing men and women." Read [The Dua of Nuh for Parents: 71:28](/blog/khalaqa-al-mawta-wal-hayat-meaning-english/#My-Lord-Forgive-Me-and-My-Parents-The-Duʿa-of-Nuh-in-Quran-7).

## Surah six: Al-Jinn (1-28)

Al-Jinn is Makki, with 28 verses, and Hizb 58 begins with it. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Jinn Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-jinn-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Jinn-Meaning-When-the-Jinn-Heard-the-Quran).

﴿قُلۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ أَنَّهُ ٱسۡتَمَعَ نَفَرࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا سَمِعۡنَا قُرۡءَانًا عَجَبٗا﴾ ﴿يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلرُّشۡدِ فَـَٔامَنَّا بِهِۦۖ وَلَن نُّشۡرِكَ بِرَبِّنَآ أَحَدٗا﴾, "Say: it has been revealed to me that a group of jinn listened and said: we have heard an amazing Quran, guiding to right conduct, so we believed in it, and we will never associate anyone with our Lord." Then the jinn's admissions: exalted is the majesty of our Lord, who has taken neither wife nor son; we reached the heaven and found it filled with stern guards and flames; some of us are righteous and some otherwise.

Then: ﴿وَأَنَّ ٱلۡمَسَٰجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدۡعُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدٗا﴾, "The places of prostration are for Allah, so do not call on anyone with Allah." The close: ﴿عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ فَلَا يُظۡهِرُ عَلَىٰ غَيۡبِهِۦٓ أَحَدًا﴾, "Knower of the unseen, He does not disclose His unseen to anyone", except a messenger He approves.

**Lesson**: the jinn themselves do not know the unseen, so anyone claiming knowledge of the unseen through them is lying.

## Surah seven: Al-Muzzammil (1-20)

Al-Muzzammil is Makki, with 20 verses, among the earliest revelations. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Muzzammil Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-jinn-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Muzzammil-Meaning-Stand-at-Night-and-Recite-with-Ta).

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُزَّمِّلُ﴾ ﴿قُمِ ٱلَّيۡلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا﴾, "O you wrapped in garments, stand in prayer at night except a little", half of it or a little less, ﴿أَوۡ زِدۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّا سَنُلۡقِي عَلَيۡكَ قَوۡلٗا ثَقِيلًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ ٱلَّيۡلِ هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطۡـٔٗا وَأَقۡوَمُ قِيلًا﴾, "or add to it, and recite the Quran with measured recitation. We will cast upon you a heavy word. The hours of the night are more effective and more suitable for words." Then: ﴿وَٱذۡكُرِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ وَتَبَتَّلۡ إِلَيۡهِ تَبۡتِيلٗا﴾, "Remember the name of your Lord and devote yourself to Him completely." Read [Recite the Quran with Tartil: 73:4](/blog/surah-al-jinn-meaning-english/#And-Recite-the-Quran-with-Measured-Recitation-Quran-73-4-Exp).

Then the last and longest verse of the surah, opening a new quarter: ﴿۞إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَعۡلَمُ أَنَّكَ تَقُومُ أَدۡنَىٰ مِن ثُلُثَيِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَنِصۡفَهُۥ وَثُلُثَهُۥ وَطَآئِفَةࣱ مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ مَعَكَۚ﴾, "Your Lord knows that you stand nearly two-thirds of the night, or half or a third of it, and so does a group of those with you"; so recite what is easy of the Quran; He knows some of you will be ill and others travelling through the land.

**A lesson for the carrier of the Quran**: night prayer is the school for carrying "the heavy word", and tartil is the manner of recitation. Then came the lightening as mercy: "recite what is easy". See [Revision in Night Prayer](/blog/quran-revision-games-kids-english/#Revising-Your-Quran-in-Night-Prayer-Qiyam-A-Practical-Method).

## Surah eight: Al-Muddaththir (1-56)

Al-Muddaththir is Makki, with 56 verses; its opening was among the first revealed after the pause in revelation, as in Sahih Al-Bukhari. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Muddaththir Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-jinn-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Muddaththir-Meaning-Arise-and-Warn).

### 1. The first commands (1-7)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُدَّثِّرُ﴾ ﴿قُمۡ فَأَنذِرۡ﴾ ﴿وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرۡ﴾ ﴿وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرۡ﴾ ﴿وَٱلرُّجۡزَ فَٱهۡجُرۡ﴾, "O you covered in a cloak, arise and warn, and magnify your Lord, purify your garments, and shun idolatry", and do not give expecting more in return, and be patient for your Lord.

**Six commands for the caller**: warning, magnifying Allah, purity, shunning idols, giving without reminding, and patience.

### 2. The schemer and Saqar (8-31)
When the trumpet is sounded; then the story of a man who thought and deliberated, may he be destroyed how he deliberated, then said: this is only magic handed down. His recompense: I will drive him into Saqar; over it are nineteen.

### 3. Every soul held in pledge (32-56)
﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسِۭ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ رَهِينَةٌ﴾, "Every soul is held in pledge for what it has earned", except the companions of the right, who will ask the criminals: what brought you into Saqar? They will say: we were not of those who prayed, we did not feed the poor, we used to indulge with those who indulged, and we denied the Day of Judgement. The close: ﴿وَمَا يَذۡكُرُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ هُوَ أَهۡلُ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ وَأَهۡلُ ٱلۡمَغۡفِرَةِ﴾, "They will not remember unless Allah wills. He is worthy of being feared and worthy of forgiving."

**Lesson**: the four causes of entering Saqar: abandoning prayer, not feeding the poor, indulging in falsehood, and denying the Day of Judgement.

## Surah nine: Al-Qiyamah (1-40)

Al-Qiyamah is Makki, with 40 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Qiyama Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-jinn-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Qiyamah-Meaning-The-Self-Reproaching-Soul-and-the-P).

﴿لَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ﴾ ﴿وَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلنَّفۡسِ ٱللَّوَّامَةِ﴾, "I swear by the Day of Resurrection, and I swear by the self-reproaching soul." A soul that reproaches its owner for falling short is a sign of a living heart. Then: does man think We will not assemble his bones? Yes, We are able to restore even his fingertips.

Then the etiquette of receiving revelation: ﴿لَا تُحَرِّكۡ بِهِۦ لِسَانَكَ لِتَعۡجَلَ بِهِۦٓ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا جَمۡعَهُۥ وَقُرۡءَانَهُۥ﴾, "Do not move your tongue with it to hasten it. Upon Us is its collection and its recitation." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas reported that the Prophet ﷺ found revelation intense and would move his lips, so this was revealed.

Then the two scenes: ﴿وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ نَّاضِرَةٌ﴾ ﴿إِلَىٰ رَبِّهَا نَاظِرَةࣱ﴾, "Some faces that Day will be radiant, looking at their Lord", and some faces that Day will be gloomy. Then the deathbed: no! When the soul reaches the collarbones, ﴿وَقِيلَ مَنۡۜ رَاقٖ﴾, "and it is said: who can cure him?", and shin is wrapped with shin. Then: does man think he will be left neglected?

**Reflect, memorizer of the Quran**: do not rush your memorization; Allah guaranteed to gather it in His Prophet's heart. Being firm comes before speed.

## Surah ten: Al-Insan (1-31)

Al-Insan is labelled Madani in the Madinah Mushaf, with 31 verses, also called Surah Ad-Dahr. The Prophet ﷺ recited it in Fajr on Friday along with As-Sajdah. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Insan Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-jinn-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Insan-Meaning-Feeding-Others-for-the-Face-of-Allah).

﴿هَلۡ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ حِينࣱ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهۡرِ لَمۡ يَكُن شَيۡـٔٗا مَّذۡكُورًا﴾, "Has there come upon man a period of time when he was nothing worth mentioning?" Then: ﴿إِنَّا هَدَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلسَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرٗا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا﴾, "We guided him to the way, whether he is grateful or ungrateful."

Then the traits of the righteous: they fulfil vows and fear a Day whose evil is widespread, ﴿وَيُطۡعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسۡكِينٗا وَيَتِيمٗا وَأَسِيرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا نُطۡعِمُكُمۡ لِوَجۡهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمۡ جَزَآءٗ وَلَا شُكُورًا﴾, "and they give food, despite their love for it, to the poor, the orphan and the captive: we feed you only for the face of Allah; we want from you neither reward nor thanks."

Then their detailed bliss: a garden and silk, reclining on couches, seeing neither burning sun nor bitter cold, with vessels of silver and crystal cups passed among them. The close: this is a reminder; whoever wills may take a way to his Lord.

**Lesson**: "despite their love for it" means while they themselves loved and needed the food; then "we want neither reward nor thanks". Sincerity prevents even waiting for a word of thanks.

## Surah eleven: Al-Mursalat (1-50)

Al-Mursalat is Makki, with 50 verses. In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Ibn Mas'ud reported that it was revealed to the Prophet ﷺ while they were in a cave at Mina. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Mursalat Meaning in English](/blog/sun-and-moon-letters-english/#Surah-Al-Mursalat-Meaning-Woe-That-Day-to-the-Deniers).

﴿وَٱلۡمُرۡسَلَٰتِ عُرۡفٗا﴾, "By those sent forth in succession", and the violent storms: what you are promised will occur. Then scenes: when the stars are obliterated, the sky is split open and the mountains are blown away. Then: did We not create you from a humble fluid? Did We not make the earth a container?

A refrain recurs in the surah: ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾, "Woe that Day to the deniers", **ten times**. The close: ﴿فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثِۭ بَعۡدَهُۥ يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾, "In what discourse after this will they believe?"

**Lesson**: after every cosmic proof or scene of the Hereafter comes the warning to deniers; there is no excuse for one who hears these proofs and then denies.

## Tajweed points in Juz 29

### ﴿نٓۚ وَٱلۡقَلَمِ﴾ at the start of Al-Qalam
Read *Nun* with a six-count necessary madd; in Hafs via Ash-Shatibiyyah the noon is pronounced clearly (izhar) before the waw, as in *Ya Sin wal-Qur'an*.

### The pauses in ﴿مَالِيَهۡۜ﴾ and ﴿مَنۡۜ رَاقٖ﴾
- Al-Haqqah 28: ﴿مَالِيَهۡۜ﴾ may take a brief pause without breath, or be merged into "halaka".
- Al-Qiyamah 27: ﴿مَنۡۜ رَاقٖ﴾ takes an obligatory brief pause in Hafs, one of the four *saktah* places.

### The ha of pause in Al-Haqqah
﴿كِتَٰبِيَهۡ﴾, ﴿حِسَابِيَهۡ﴾ and ﴿سُلۡطَٰنِيَهۡ﴾: the sakin ha is pronounced both when continuing and when stopping.

### ﴿قَوَارِيرَا۠﴾ in Al-Insan 15
The alif is written but not read when continuing, and is pronounced when stopping; in the second occurrence, ﴿قَوَارِيرَاْ﴾ (16), Hafs drops it both when continuing and when stopping. For the basics see [Learn Tajweed: A Roadmap](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. "So glorify the name of your Lord, the Most Great"
- Al-Waqi'ah 96 and Al-Haqqah 52: ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾, identical wording.

### 2. Guarding the prayer
- Al-Ma'arij 23: ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ دَآئِمُونَ﴾, "constant".
- Al-Ma'arij 34: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ﴾, "guarding".
- Al-Mu'minun 9: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَوَٰتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ﴾, plural "prayers".

### 3. The refrains of Al-Mursalat and Ar-Rahman
- Al-Mursalat: ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾, ten times.
- Ar-Rahman: ﴿فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ﴾, thirty-one times.

### 4. The calls of Al-Muzzammil and Al-Muddaththir
- Al-Muzzammil 1-2: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُزَّمِّلُ﴾ ﴿قُمِ ٱلَّيۡلَ﴾, "stand at night".
- Al-Muddaththir 1-2: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُدَّثِّرُ﴾ ﴿قُمۡ فَأَنذِرۡ﴾, "arise and warn".

## How to memorize Juz 29 with understanding

1. **Learn the eleven surah names in order**: Al-Mulk, Al-Qalam, Al-Haqqah, Al-Ma'arij, Nuh, Al-Jinn, Al-Muzzammil, Al-Muddaththir, Al-Qiyamah, Al-Insan, Al-Mursalat.
2. **Single-rhyme surahs**: many keep one verse-ending (Al-Jinn and Nuh in *-a*, Al-Muzzammil in *-la*), so use the rhythm.
3. **Memorize Al-Mulk first** and recite it every night.
4. **Al-Ma'arij**: learn the traits of those who pray and compare them with the start of Al-Mu'minun.
5. **Al-Qiyamah**: note the pause in "man raq".

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 29

- **Make your deeds excellent**, not merely many.
- **Work on earth** and remember the resurrection.
- **Follow the Prophet's character ﷺ**: his character was the Quran.
- **Do not withhold the poor's right**, like the owners of the garden.
- **Be certain of the reckoning**, and your deeds will improve.
- **Be constant in prayer**, and be healed of anxiety.
- **Seek forgiveness**, and receive rain, wealth and children.
- **Do not believe those who claim the unseen.**
- **Stand at night** and recite with tartil.
- **Purify your garments** and shun idolatry.
- **Feed the poor and guard the prayer** to be saved from Saqar.
- **Listen to your self-reproaching soul.**
- **Be firm in memorization**; do not rush.
- **Give food for Allah's face** and expect no thanks.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 29 called Juz Tabarak?
Because it begins with Surah Al-Mulk: ﴿تَبَٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ﴾.

### How many surahs are in Juz Tabarak?
Eleven.

### Is there a prostration in Juz 29?
No, there is no place of prostration in this juz.

### How many times does "Woe that Day to the deniers" occur in Al-Mursalat?
Ten times.

### Where does Juz 29 end?
At the end of Al-Mursalat. Juz 30 begins with Surah An-Naba': ﴿عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾, "About what are they asking one another?"

## Conclusion

Juz Tabarak tells you that life is a test of the best deeds, shows you a great character to follow, displays records taken in the right and left hands, heals your anxiety with prayer and your tight provision with seeking forgiveness, wakes you at night to recite the Quran with tartil, and ends with the righteous who feed others for Allah's face.
