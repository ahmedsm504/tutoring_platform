Many sisters prefer to learn the Quran with a **female teacher**: they feel more comfortable reciting, asking questions and making mistakes. With online one-to-one classes, that's now easy to arrange from home, wherever you live.

## Why many women choose a female teacher
- **Comfort:** reciting aloud, especially at the beginning, can feel exposing. A sister teacher often makes this much easier.
- **Flexibility around family life:** female teachers understand the reality of balancing children, work and home.
- **Privacy:** you can learn from home, with your camera on or off as you prefer.

## What can you study?
- **Reading from zero:** the Arabic alphabet through to fluent reading. See [learning to read the Quran as an adult](/blog/learn-quran-as-an-adult-from-zero/).
- **Tajweed:** correcting your recitation, starting with [Surah Al-Fatiha](/blog/surah-al-fatiha-common-mistakes/).
- **Memorization (Hifz):** with a plan and regular revision. See [how to memorize the Quran step by step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).
- **Ijazah preparation:** for advanced students. See [what an Ijazah is](/blog/what-is-ijazah-quran-english/).

## How online classes work
1. You choose a time that suits you, in your own time zone.
2. You join a video call from your phone, tablet or laptop.
3. Your teacher listens, corrects, and explains in a simple way.
4. Between classes, you send **audio homework** and receive feedback, so you keep progressing even in busy weeks.

## Fitting classes around your life
- **30-minute classes** two or three times a week are enough for steady progress.
- Many sisters choose times when the children are asleep or at school.
- In difficult weeks, keep a minimum: listening and short revision.

## Learning alongside your children
A beautiful option: learn while your children learn with their own teacher. You can revise Juz Amma together, and they see you as a role model. Read [how to teach your child the Quran online](/blog/learn-quran-online-for-kids/).

## What to look for in a teacher
- Proficiency in Tajweed, ideally with an Ijazah.
- Patience and a gentle teaching style.
- A free trial class, so you can see if you feel comfortable.
- A clear plan and regular feedback.
- Safe communication inside a platform, supervised by the academy.
