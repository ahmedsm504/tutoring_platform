**Surah Al-Waqi'ah** (The Event, 96 ayat) describes the Day of Judgement and divides people into **three groups**.

﴿إِذَا وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ﴾ ﴿لَيۡسَ لِوَقۡعَتِهَا كَاذِبَةٌ﴾ ﴿خَافِضَةࣱ رَّافِعَةٌ﴾
- **Lowering, raising**: it brings down the arrogant and raises the humble believers.

## The three groups
| Group | Ayat | Description |
|---|---|---|
| The foremost (as-sābiqūn) | 10–26 | Nearest to Allah, in gardens of bliss |
| Companions of the right | 27–40 | Shade, water and fruits |
| Companions of the left | 41–56 | Scorching wind and boiling water |

## Signs of creation
| Ayat | Sign | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 57–62 | Your own creation | Who created it? |
| 63–67 | The seeds you plant | Who makes them grow? |
| 68–70 | The water you drink | Who sends it down? |
| 71–74 | The fire you kindle | Who made its tree? |

## The noble Quran
﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَقُرۡءَانࣱ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فِي كِتَٰبٖ مَّكۡنُونٖ﴾ ﴿لَّا يَمَسُّهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُطَهَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلࣱ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ [Al-Waqi'ah: 77–80]
- Many commentators said "the purified" here are the **angels**, and scholars also took from it respect for the Mushaf.

## A note on its virtue
- A hadith that reading Al-Waqi'ah every night protects from poverty is **weak** according to many hadith scholars.
- Reading it with reflection remains a great act of worship.
