﴿وَذَا ٱلنُّونِ إِذ ذَّهَبَ مُغَٰضِبٗا فَظَنَّ أَن لَّن نَّقۡدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ فَنَادَىٰ فِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ أَن لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ [Al-Anbiya 21:87]

## Who is Dhun-Nun?
- *Dhun-Nun* means "the one of the whale", a title of Yunus, peace be upon him.

## The darknesses
- Scholars mention the darkness of the night, of the sea, and of the whale's belly.

## Three parts of the duʿa
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *La ilaha illa anta* | Tawhid: no god but You |
| *Subhanaka* | Glorification: You are free of any injustice |
| *Inni kuntu minaz-zalimin* | Confession: the fault is mine |

## The rescue
﴿فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَمِّۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُـۨجِي ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾
- *And thus We save the believers*: the promise is not only for Yunus.

## The Prophet ﷺ on this duʿa
- He said that no Muslim calls on Allah with the duʿa of Dhun-Nun for anything except that Allah responds to him (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

## Lessons
- **In distress, admit your fault** and glorify Allah.
- **No darkness is too deep** for Allah's rescue.
