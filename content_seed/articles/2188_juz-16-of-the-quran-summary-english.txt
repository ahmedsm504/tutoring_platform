Juz 16 begins with ﴿۞قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكَ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرٗا﴾, "He said: did I not tell you that you would never be able to be patient with me?", so it is called **Juz Qal Alam**. It runs from **Al-Kahf 18:75 to the end of Surah Ta-Ha (20:135)**, with all of **Surah Maryam** in between. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 302 to 321**.

This is the juz of prophets par excellence. It completes the story of Musa and al-Khidr, then tells of Dhul-Qarnayn; Surah Maryam follows with a procession of prophets united by one description; then Surah Ta-Ha gives the fullest account of Musa from infancy to the golden calf, followed by the story of Adam.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **Allah's mercy to His prophets and servants, and His hidden wisdom in what He decrees**. Al-Khidr reveals that behind what looks like harm lies mercy; Dhul-Qarnayn says ﴿هَٰذَا رَحۡمَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّيۖ﴾, "This is a mercy from my Lord"; Surah Maryam opens with ﴿ذِكۡرُ رَحۡمَتِ رَبِّكَ عَبۡدَهُۥ زَكَرِيَّآ﴾, "A mention of the mercy of your Lord to His servant Zakariya", and uses the name **ar-Rahman** more than any other surah; and Ta-Ha opens with ﴿مَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِتَشۡقَىٰٓ﴾, "We did not send down the Quran to you to cause you distress", then shows Allah's care for Musa from the moment his mother placed him in the river.

The message: **Allah is more merciful to His servant than he imagines, even when the wisdom of His decree is not understood at the time.**

## Part one: the close of Surah Al-Kahf (75-110)

### 1. The wall and the explanation (75-82)
After the ship and the boy, they came to a town and asked its people for food, but they refused to host them. They found a wall about to collapse and al-Khidr set it straight; Musa said: had you wished, you could have taken payment for it. Al-Khidr said: ﴿هَٰذَا فِرَاقُ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنِكَۚ سَأُنَبِّئُكَ بِتَأۡوِيلِ مَا لَمۡ تَسۡتَطِع عَّلَيۡهِ صَبۡرًا﴾, "This is parting between me and you. I will tell you the interpretation of what you could not bear patiently." Then the explanation:
- **The ship** belonged to poor people, and beyond them was a king seizing every sound ship by force, so he damaged it to save it.
- **The boy**'s parents were believers, and he feared the boy would burden them with transgression and disbelief, so Allah willed to replace him with one better.
- **The wall** belonged to two orphan boys with a treasure beneath it, ﴿وَكَانَ أَبُوهُمَا صَٰلِحٗا﴾, "and their father had been righteous", so your Lord willed that they reach maturity and extract their treasure.

Then the decisive words: ﴿رَحۡمَةٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ وَمَا فَعَلۡتُهُۥ عَنۡ أَمۡرِيۚ﴾, "as a mercy from your Lord; I did not do it of my own accord."

**Lessons of the story**:
1. What looks like damage may be protection (the ship).
2. What looks like disaster may be mercy (the boy).
3. A parent's righteousness protects the children (the wall).
4. Human knowledge is limited; submitting to Allah's wisdom is a must.

Notice the courtesy in attributing actions: al-Khidr attributed the damage to himself, "so I intended to damage it", and the good to Allah, "so your Lord intended". See [Lessons of Surah Al-Kahf](/blog/surah-al-kahf-lessons-english/).

### 2. Dhul-Qarnayn: the trial of power (83-98)
﴿إِنَّا مَكَّنَّا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ سَبَبٗا﴾, "We established him on earth and gave him a means to everything." Three journeys:
- **To the west**: he found a people and was given a choice over them. He said: whoever wrongs, we will punish; whoever believes and does good will have the best reward. This is the balance of justice in rule.
- **To the east**: a people with no shelter from the sun.
- **Between the two barriers**: a people who could barely understand speech complained of Ya'juj and Ma'juj: ﴿إِنَّ يَأۡجُوجَ وَمَأۡجُوجَ مُفۡسِدُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَهَلۡ نَجۡعَلُ لَكَ خَرۡجًا﴾, "Ya'juj and Ma'juj are corrupting the land; may we pay you a tribute?" He refused payment, saying what his Lord gave him was better, and asked them for labour instead: help me with strength. He built the barrier from blocks of iron and molten copper. Then: ﴿قَالَ هَٰذَا رَحۡمَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّيۖ فَإِذَا جَآءَ وَعۡدُ رَبِّي جَعَلَهُۥ دَكَّآءَۖ﴾, "He said: this is a mercy from my Lord; when my Lord's promise comes, He will level it."

**Lesson**: a righteous leader is just, refuses people's money, involves them in the work, and credits the achievement to Allah. The Quran is silent on his name, era and location, so we do not busy ourselves with what is not established.

### 3. The greatest losers and the close (99-110)
﴿قُلۡ هَلۡ نُنَبِّئُكُم بِٱلۡأَخۡسَرِينَ أَعۡمَٰلًا﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ضَلَّ سَعۡيُهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَهُمۡ يَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّهُمۡ يُحۡسِنُونَ صُنۡعًا﴾, "Say: shall We inform you of the greatest losers in deeds? Those whose effort is lost in worldly life while they think they are doing well." The worst loss is working without guidance while thinking oneself a doer of good. Then: ﴿قُل لَّوۡ كَانَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ مِدَادٗا لِّكَلِمَٰتِ رَبِّي لَنَفِدَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ قَبۡلَ أَن تَنفَدَ كَلِمَٰتُ رَبِّي﴾, "Say: if the sea were ink for the words of my Lord, the sea would run out before the words of my Lord run out." Read ["If the Sea Were Ink": 18:109](/blog/if-the-sea-were-ink-18-109-meaning-english/). Then the comprehensive last verse: ﴿فَمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ لِقَآءَ رَبِّهِۦ فَلۡيَعۡمَلۡ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا وَلَا يُشۡرِكۡ بِعِبَادَةِ رَبِّهِۦٓ أَحَدَۢا﴾, "Whoever hopes to meet his Lord, let him do righteous deeds and associate no one in the worship of his Lord." Read [Quran 18:110 Explained](/blog/faman-kana-yarju-liqaa-rabbih-meaning-english/).

**Two conditions of acceptance**: a good deed in line with the Shari'ah, and sincerity free of shirk.

**The four trials concluded**: faith (the youths), cured by righteous company; wealth (the gardens), cured by attributing blessings to Allah; knowledge (al-Khidr), cured by humility; power (Dhul-Qarnayn), cured by justice and sincerity.

## Part two: Surah Maryam (1-98)

Maryam is Makki, with 98 verses, and opens with the letters ﴿كٓهيعٓصٓ﴾. It is the only surah named after a woman, and Maryam is the only woman named in the Quran. The name ar-Rahman appears in it sixteen times. Ja'far ibn Abi Talib recited its opening to the Negus, who wept. For the whole surah see [Surah Maryam Meaning in English](/blog/surah-maryam-meaning-english/).

### 1. Zakariya's hidden prayer (1-15)
﴿ذِكۡرُ رَحۡمَتِ رَبِّكَ عَبۡدَهُۥ زَكَرِيَّآ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥ نِدَآءً خَفِيّٗا﴾, "when he called on his Lord in a private call." Then his prayer in the tenderest words: ﴿قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي وَهَنَ ٱلۡعَظۡمُ مِنِّي وَٱشۡتَعَلَ ٱلرَّأۡسُ شَيۡبٗا وَلَمۡ أَكُنۢ بِدُعَآئِكَ رَبِّ شَقِيّٗا﴾, "My Lord, my bones have weakened and my head has filled with white, and I have never been disappointed in my prayer to You, my Lord." Allah gave him the good news of Yahya, and as a sign he would not speak to people for three nights. Then Yahya's qualities: given wisdom as a child, tenderness and purity from Us, God-conscious, dutiful to his parents, and ﴿وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡهِ يَوۡمَ وُلِدَ وَيَوۡمَ يَمُوتُ وَيَوۡمَ يُبۡعَثُ حَيّٗا﴾, "peace be upon him the day he was born, the day he dies, and the day he is raised alive."

**The etiquette of supplication**: privacy, humility in mentioning one's weakness, and remembering past answers.

### 2. Maryam and 'Isa (16-40)
- **Withdrawal**: she withdrew from her family to an eastern place; Allah sent Jibril to her in the form of a well-proportioned man, and she sought refuge in the Most Merciful from him.
- **The good news**: ﴿لِأَهَبَ لَكِ غُلَٰمٗا زَكِيّٗا﴾, "to give you a pure boy." She wondered: no man has touched me and I have not been unchaste. He said: it is easy for Me.
- **The labour pains**: ﴿فَأَجَآءَهَا ٱلۡمَخَاضُ إِلَىٰ جِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ قَالَتۡ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي مِتُّ قَبۡلَ هَٰذَا وَكُنتُ نَسۡيٗا مَّنسِيّٗا﴾, "The pains of childbirth drove her to the trunk of a palm tree. She said: I wish I had died before this and been forgotten." She was called: do not grieve, ﴿وَهُزِّيٓ إِلَيۡكِ بِجِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ تُسَٰقِطۡ عَلَيۡكِ رُطَبٗا جَنِيّٗا﴾, "and shake the trunk of the palm towards you; it will drop fresh ripe dates on you."
- **The fast of silence**: say, I have vowed a fast to the Most Merciful and will not speak to anyone today.
- **Speech in the cradle**: ﴿قَالَ إِنِّي عَبۡدُ ٱللَّهِ ءَاتَىٰنِيَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَجَعَلَنِي نَبِيّٗا﴾, "He said: I am the servant of Allah; He has given me the Book and made me a prophet." The first words 'Isa spoke: "I am the servant of Allah." Then: ﴿وَٱلسَّلَٰمُ عَلَيَّ يَوۡمَ وُلِدتُّ وَيَوۡمَ أَمُوتُ وَيَوۡمَ أُبۡعَثُ حَيّٗا﴾, "Peace is upon me the day I was born, the day I die, and the day I am raised alive."
- **The verdict**: that is 'Isa son of Maryam, the word of truth about which they dispute; it is not for Allah to take a son, glory be to Him.

**Reflect on taking the means**: Maryam, at her weakest, was told to shake the palm trunk, though a woman in labour cannot shake a tree. It is a lesson in taking the means, however weak, while Allah completes the rest.

### 3. Ibrahim and his father (41-50)
The most beautiful dialogue between a son and his father in the Quran, with Ibrahim opening each sentence with ﴿يَـٰٓأَبَتِ﴾, "O my dear father", out of gentleness:
- ﴿يَـٰٓأَبَتِ لِمَ تَعۡبُدُ مَا لَا يَسۡمَعُ وَلَا يُبۡصِرُ وَلَا يُغۡنِي عَنكَ شَيۡـٔٗا﴾, "O my father, why do you worship what neither hears nor sees nor benefits you at all?"
- O my father, knowledge has come to me that has not come to you, so follow me.
- O my father, do not worship Shaytan.
- O my father, I fear that a punishment from the Most Merciful will touch you.

The father replied with a threat: if you do not stop, I will stone you. Ibrahim answered: ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكَۖ سَأَسۡتَغۡفِرُ لَكَ رَبِّيٓۖ﴾, "Peace be upon you. I will ask my Lord to forgive you." Then he withdrew from them, and Allah gave him Ishaq and Ya'qub.

**Lesson**: calling one's parents to the truth is done with gentleness and respect, even amid disagreement in belief.

### 4. A procession of prophets (51-58)
- **Musa**: chosen and sincere, called from the side of the mount and brought near in private conversation.
- **Isma'il**: ﴿إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ صَادِقَ ٱلۡوَعۡدِ وَكَانَ رَسُولٗا نَّبِيّٗا﴾, "He was true to his promise, and he was a messenger and a prophet." He enjoined prayer and zakah on his family.
- **Idris**: a man of truth and a prophet, raised to a high station.

Then the comprehensive description: ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَ﴾, "Those are the prophets Allah blessed", ﴿إِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ خَرُّواْۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ وَبُكِيّٗا﴾, "when the verses of the Most Merciful were recited to them, they fell down in prostration, weeping": a place of prostration.

### 5. The generation that neglected prayer (59-65)
Hizb 32 begins with a contrast: ﴿۞فَخَلَفَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ خَلۡفٌ أَضَاعُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَٱتَّبَعُواْ ٱلشَّهَوَٰتِۖ فَسَوۡفَ يَلۡقَوۡنَ غَيًّا﴾, "Then there came after them a generation who neglected prayer and followed desires; they will meet ruin", ﴿إِلَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا﴾, "except those who repent, believe and do good." The prophets prostrated in tears; their successors neglected prayer.

Then: ﴿وَمَا نَتَنَزَّلُ إِلَّا بِأَمۡرِ رَبِّكَۖ﴾, "We do not descend except by the command of your Lord." In Sahih Al-Bukhari it was revealed when the Prophet ﷺ said to Jibril: "Why do you not visit us more often than you do?" Then: ﴿رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فَٱعۡبُدۡهُ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ لِعِبَٰدَتِهِۦۚ﴾, "Lord of the heavens and earth and all between them; so worship Him and be steadfast in His worship."

### 6. Resurrection and the passing over (66-82)
Man denies resurrection: when I die, will I be brought out alive? The answer: does man not remember that We created him before when he was nothing? Then: ﴿وَإِن مِّنكُمۡ إِلَّا وَارِدُهَاۚ كَانَ عَلَىٰ رَبِّكَ حَتۡمٗا مَّقۡضِيّٗا﴾, "There is none of you but will pass over it; this is a decree your Lord has made binding", then We will save those who were God-conscious. Many scholars explained this passing as crossing the Bridge over Hell.

### 7. The claim of a son, and love (83-98)
﴿وَقَالُواْ ٱتَّخَذَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَلَدٗا﴾ ﴿لَّقَدۡ جِئۡتُمۡ شَيۡـًٔا إِدّٗا﴾ ﴿تَكَادُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ يَتَفَطَّرۡنَ مِنۡهُ وَتَنشَقُّ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَتَخِرُّ ٱلۡجِبَالُ هَدًّا﴾, "They say the Most Merciful has taken a son. You have brought forth a monstrous thing. The heavens almost burst from it, the earth splits open and the mountains collapse in ruin." The whole universe is outraged by that claim. Everyone in the heavens and earth will come to the Most Merciful as a servant. Then the good news: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ سَيَجۡعَلُ لَهُمُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وُدّٗا﴾, "Those who believe and do good, the Most Merciful will grant them love." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim: "When Allah loves a servant, He calls Jibril: Allah loves so-and-so, so love him..." and acceptance is then placed for him on earth. The close: ﴿فَإِنَّمَا يَسَّرۡنَٰهُ بِلِسَانِكَ﴾, "We have made it easy in your tongue."

## Part three: Surah Ta-Ha (1-135)

Ta-Ha is Makki, with 135 verses, beginning with ﴿طه﴾ and then ﴿مَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِتَشۡقَىٰٓ﴾. Books of Sirah mention that reading its opening was a cause of 'Umar ibn al-Khattab's acceptance of Islam. Most of it is the story of Musa. For the whole surah see [Surah Ta-Ha Meaning in English](/blog/surah-taha-meaning-english/).

### 1. The Quran is not distress (1-8)
"We did not send down the Quran to you to cause you distress, but as a reminder for those who fear." The Quran is happiness, not hardship. Then: the Most Merciful rose over the Throne; His is what is in the heavens and earth; and if you speak aloud, He knows the secret and what is even more hidden.

### 2. The call in the sacred valley (9-24)
Musa saw a fire on his journey with his family, and when he came to it: ﴿إِنِّيٓ أَنَا۠ رَبُّكَ فَٱخۡلَعۡ نَعۡلَيۡكَ إِنَّكَ بِٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡمُقَدَّسِ طُوٗى﴾, "I am your Lord, so remove your sandals; you are in the sacred valley of Tuwa", then ﴿إِنَّنِيٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱعۡبُدۡنِي وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِذِكۡرِيٓ﴾, "I am Allah; there is no god but Me, so worship Me and establish prayer for My remembrance." The first things Musa received: monotheism, then prayer. Then the question: what is that in your right hand, O Musa? He answered at length, "It is my staff; I lean on it, beat leaves down for my sheep, and have other uses for it", savouring the conversation with his Lord. Then the miracles of the staff and the hand.

### 3. Musa's prayer (25-36)
﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشۡرَحۡ لِي صَدۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَيَسِّرۡ لِيٓ أَمۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَٱحۡلُلۡ عُقۡدَةٗ مِّن لِّسَانِي﴾ ﴿يَفۡقَهُواْ قَوۡلِي﴾, "My Lord, expand my chest for me, ease my task for me, and untie the knot from my tongue so they understand my speech." Then he asked for a helper from his family, Harun his brother, to strengthen him, "so that we may glorify You much and remember You much." Allah said: ﴿قَدۡ أُوتِيتَ سُؤۡلَكَ يَٰمُوسَىٰ﴾, "You have been granted your request, O Musa."

**Reflect**: Musa asked for an expanded chest before an easy task, because an open heart can bear any task, and he asked for his brother not for rest but to glorify and remember Allah more. Read [My Lord, Expand My Chest: 20:25](/blog/rabbi-ishrah-li-sadri-meaning-english/).

### 4. Allah's earlier favours (37-41)
Allah reminds him of the favours of his childhood: ﴿أَنِ ٱقۡذِفِيهِ فِي ٱلتَّابُوتِ فَٱقۡذِفِيهِ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ فَلۡيُلۡقِهِ ٱلۡيَمُّ بِٱلسَّاحِلِ يَأۡخُذۡهُ عَدُوࣱّ لِّي وَعَدُوࣱّ لَّهُۥ﴾, "Put him into the chest and cast it into the river; the river will throw it onto the bank, and an enemy of Mine and his will take him", and "I cast upon you love from Me, so you would be raised under My eye", then his return to his mother so she would be comforted, his killing a man and rescue, years in Madyan, and ﴿وَٱصۡطَنَعۡتُكَ لِنَفۡسِي﴾, "and I chose you for Myself."

**Lesson**: every stage of Musa's life was preparation, even the hard years in Madyan.

### 5. Going to Pharaoh (42-56)
﴿ٱذۡهَبَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَقُولَا لَهُۥ قَوۡلٗا لَّيِّنٗا لَّعَلَّهُۥ يَتَذَكَّرُ أَوۡ يَخۡشَىٰ﴾, "Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed. And speak to him gently, so perhaps he will remember or fear." If gentleness was commanded with the tyrant Pharaoh, how much more with others? When they feared, Allah said: ﴿لَا تَخَافَآۖ إِنَّنِي مَعَكُمَآ أَسۡمَعُ وَأَرَىٰ﴾, "Do not fear; I am with you both, I hear and I see." Read [Speak to Him Gently: 20:44](/blog/speak-to-him-gently-20-44-meaning-english/). Then the dialogue: Pharaoh asked, who is your Lord, O Musa? He gave the shortest definition: ﴿رَبُّنَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَعۡطَىٰ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلۡقَهُۥ ثُمَّ هَدَىٰ﴾, "Our Lord is the One who gave everything its form and then guided it." Then a new quarter: ﴿۞مِنۡهَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ وَفِيهَا نُعِيدُكُمۡ وَمِنۡهَا نُخۡرِجُكُمۡ تَارَةً أُخۡرَىٰ﴾, "From it We created you, into it We will return you, and from it We will bring you out once again."

### 6. The day of the festival and the magicians (57-76)
Pharaoh chose the day of festival as the appointment and gathered his scheme. The magicians threw their ropes and staffs, which seemed to Musa through their magic to be moving, ﴿فَأَوۡجَسَ فِي نَفۡسِهِۦ خِيفَةٗ مُّوسَىٰ﴾, "and Musa sensed fear within himself." He was told: do not fear, you will be uppermost, ﴿وَأَلۡقِ مَا فِي يَمِينِكَ تَلۡقَفۡ مَا صَنَعُوٓاْۖ إِنَّمَا صَنَعُواْ كَيۡدُ سَٰحِرٖۖ وَلَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلسَّاحِرُ حَيۡثُ أَتَىٰ﴾, "Throw what is in your right hand; it will swallow what they made. What they made is a magician's trick, and the magician never succeeds wherever he goes." The magicians prostrated, Pharaoh threatened them, and they said: ﴿لَن نُّؤۡثِرَكَ عَلَىٰ مَا جَآءَنَا مِنَ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنَاۖ فَٱقۡضِ مَآ أَنتَ قَاضٍۖ إِنَّمَا تَقۡضِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ﴾, "We will never prefer you over the clear proofs that came to us and over the One who created us. So decree whatever you will; you can only decree for this worldly life."

**Reflect**: a few hours moved the magicians from being Pharaoh's servants to patient martyrs. When faith enters the heart, this world becomes small.

### 7. Rescue and vast forgiveness (77-82)
Allah inspired Musa to travel by night with His servants and strike a dry path through the sea for them. Pharaoh was drowned. Then Allah's favours to the Children of Israel, and: ﴿وَإِنِّي لَغَفَّارࣱ لِّمَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا ثُمَّ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ﴾, "And I am the Perpetual Forgiver of whoever repents, believes, does good and then continues in guidance."

### 8. The Samiri and the calf (83-98)
A new quarter: Musa hurried ahead to his Lord, and Allah told him his people had been tested after him and the Samiri had misled them. Musa returned angry and grieved and seized his brother's beard, and Harun said: ﴿يَبۡنَؤُمَّ لَا تَأۡخُذۡ بِلِحۡيَتِي وَلَا بِرَأۡسِيٓۖ إِنِّي خَشِيتُ أَن تَقُولَ فَرَّقۡتَ بَيۡنَ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ﴾, "O son of my mother, do not seize me by my beard or my head. I feared you would say: you caused division among the Children of Israel." Then the Samiri's judgement: go, and in this life you will say "no touching", and the calf was burned and scattered into the sea. Then: ﴿إِنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ﴾, "Your god is only Allah, besides whom there is no god."

**Lesson**: Harun gave priority, temporarily, to keeping the community together until Musa returned, an example of weighing one harm against another.

### 9. Scenes of the Day and the etiquette of receiving revelation (99-114)
The Day the Horn is blown; they ask you about the mountains, say my Lord will blast them into dust; voices are hushed before the Most Merciful so you hear only whispers; and faces are humbled before the Ever-Living, the Sustainer. Then: ﴿فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۗ وَلَا تَعۡجَلۡ بِٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يُقۡضَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَحۡيُهُۥۖ وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدۡنِي عِلۡمٗا﴾, "Exalted is Allah, the True King. Do not hasten with the Quran before its revelation to you is completed, and say: My Lord, increase me in knowledge." Allah never commanded His Prophet to seek more of anything except knowledge. Read [My Lord, Increase Me in Knowledge: 20:114](/blog/rabbi-zidni-ilma-meaning-english/).

### 10. Adam's story in Ta-Ha (115-123)
﴿وَلَقَدۡ عَهِدۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ ءَادَمَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَنَسِيَ وَلَمۡ نَجِدۡ لَهُۥ عَزۡمٗا﴾, "We had made a covenant with Adam before, but he forgot, and We found in him no firm resolve." This telling focuses on **forgetfulness and weak resolve**, then the promise of Paradise: you will not be hungry or unclothed, nor thirsty or exposed to the sun. Then Shaytan's whisper: shall I guide you to the tree of eternity and a kingdom that never decays? Then: ﴿وَعَصَىٰٓ ءَادَمُ رَبَّهُۥ فَغَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ ٱجۡتَبَٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَتَابَ عَلَيۡهِ وَهَدَىٰ﴾, "Adam disobeyed his Lord and erred. Then his Lord chose him, accepted his repentance and guided him."

**Comparison**: Al-Baqarah focuses on honour, Al-A'raf on enmity, Al-Hijr on the material of creation, and Ta-Ha on forgetting, repentance and being chosen.

### 11. The life of hardship and the final commands (124-135)
﴿وَمَنۡ أَعۡرَضَ عَن ذِكۡرِي فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ مَعِيشَةٗ ضَنكٗا وَنَحۡشُرُهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ أَعۡمَىٰ﴾, "Whoever turns away from My remembrance will have a constricted life, and We will raise him blind on the Day of Resurrection." Real constriction lies in turning away from the Quran, not in lack of money. Read ["Whoever Turns Away from My Remembrance Will Have a Hard Life": Quran 20:124 Explained](/blog/maisha-danka-meaning-english/).

Then the closing commands:
- Be patient with what they say and glorify your Lord before sunrise and before sunset, in the hours of the night and the ends of the day, ﴿لَعَلَّكَ تَرۡضَىٰ﴾, "so that you may be content."
- ﴿وَلَا تَمُدَّنَّ عَيۡنَيۡكَ إِلَىٰ مَا مَتَّعۡنَا بِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ زَهۡرَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لِنَفۡتِنَهُمۡ فِيهِۚ﴾, "Do not extend your eyes towards what We have given some of them to enjoy, the splendour of worldly life, by which We test them."
- ﴿وَأۡمُرۡ أَهۡلَكَ بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ عَلَيۡهَاۖ لَا نَسۡـَٔلُكَ رِزۡقٗاۖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُكَۗ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰ﴾, "Command your family to pray and be steadfast in it. We do not ask you for provision; We provide for you, and the best outcome is for taqwa."

**Reflect**: *istabir*, with the added ta, is stronger than *isbir*: commanding one's family to pray needs sustained patience.

## Tajweed points in Juz 16

### ﴿كٓهيعٓصٓ﴾ at the start of Maryam
Read *Kaf, Ha, Ya, 'Ayn, Sad*. Kaf and Sad take six counts; Ha and Ya two counts; 'Ayn may take four or six counts, six being preferred. The noon of "'Ayn" is hidden (ikhfa') before the sad.

### ﴿طه﴾ at the start of Ta-Ha
Read *Ta, Ha*, each with a natural two-count madd.

### ﴿يَبۡنَؤُمَّ﴾ in Ta-Ha 94
Written as one word and read *yabna'umma*, unlike Al-A'raf, where it is ﴿ٱبۡنَ أُمَّ﴾.

### ﴿تَسۡتَطِع عَّلَيۡهِ﴾ in Al-Kahf 78
The 'ayn merges into the following 'ayn.

### The sajdah of Maryam
At ﴿خَرُّواْۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ وَبُكِيّٗا﴾, verse 58. For the madd types in the opening letters see [Madd Rules in Tajweed](/blog/madd-rules-tajweed-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. *Tastati'* and *tasti'*
- Al-Kahf 78: ﴿سَأُنَبِّئُكَ بِتَأۡوِيلِ مَا لَمۡ تَسۡتَطِع عَّلَيۡهِ صَبۡرًا﴾, with the ta.
- Al-Kahf 82: ﴿ذَٰلِكَ تَأۡوِيلُ مَا لَمۡ تَسۡطِع عَّلَيۡهِ صَبۡرٗا﴾, without it.
**Key**: before the explanation the matter weighed heavily, so the full verb; after it the matter was lighter, so the lighter verb.

### 2. Peace upon Yahya and 'Isa
- Yahya (15): ﴿وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡهِ﴾, indefinite, from Allah.
- 'Isa (33): ﴿وَٱلسَّلَٰمُ عَلَيَّ﴾, definite, from 'Isa himself.

### 3. Harun in Al-A'raf and Ta-Ha
- Al-A'raf 150: ﴿ٱبۡنَ أُمَّ إِنَّ ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱسۡتَضۡعَفُونِي﴾.
- Ta-Ha 94: ﴿يَبۡنَؤُمَّ لَا تَأۡخُذۡ بِلِحۡيَتِي وَلَا بِرَأۡسِيٓۖ﴾.

### 4. "Do not extend your eyes"
- Al-Hijr 88: followed by ﴿وَلَا تَحۡزَنۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَٱخۡفِضۡ جَنَاحَكَ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾.
- Ta-Ha 131: followed by ﴿زَهۡرَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لِنَفۡتِنَهُمۡ فِيهِۚ﴾.

## How to memorize Juz 16 with understanding

1. **Finish Al-Kahf** with the explanation of the three acts in the same order: ship, boy, wall.
2. **Maryam has short verses** with rhyming endings (*-iyya*), so it is easy to memorize by rhythm.
3. **Learn Ibrahim's dialogue with his father** by counting the "O my father": four times.
4. **Ta-Ha is Musa's story in sequence**: the fire, the call, the prayer, the favours, Pharaoh, the magicians, the sea, the calf.
5. **Compare Musa's story in Al-A'raf and Ta-Ha** in a written table.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 16

- **Submit to Allah's decree** even when you do not yet understand its wisdom.
- **Your righteousness protects your children** after you.
- **If you have power, be just** and refuse people's wealth.
- **Do sincere good deeds** to meet your Lord.
- **Pray to your Lord in private** and admit your weakness.
- **Take the means**, however weak.
- **Be gentle with your parents**, even if they disagree with you.
- **Do not neglect prayer** or follow desires.
- **Ask Allah to expand your chest** before easing your task.
- **Speak gently**, even to opponents.
- **Ask for more knowledge.**
- **Beware turning from the remembrance**: that is the constricted life.
- **Command your family to pray, and be patient with it.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 16 called "Qal Alam"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكَ﴾.

### Who was Dhul-Qarnayn?
A righteous ruler whom Allah established on earth. The Quran is silent on his name and era, so we do not assert what is not established.

### Where does Juz 16 end?
At the end of Ta-Ha. Juz 17 begins with Surah Al-Anbiya': ﴿ٱقۡتَرَبَ لِلنَّاسِ حِسَابُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ فِي غَفۡلَةࣲ مُّعۡرِضُونَ﴾, "Mankind's reckoning has drawn near, while they turn away in heedlessness."

### What were 'Isa's first words in the cradle?
﴿إِنِّي عَبۡدُ ٱللَّهِ﴾, "I am the servant of Allah" (Maryam 30).

### How many prostrations are in this juz?
One, in Surah Maryam, verse 58.

## Conclusion

Juz 16 is the juz of mercy: hidden mercy in al-Khidr's actions, mercy acknowledged by Dhul-Qarnayn, mercy named at the start of Maryam and flowing through its prophets' stories, mercy guarding Musa from the chest to the sea, and mercy accepting Adam's repentance and choosing him. Whoever lives these meanings sees only good in Allah's decree.
