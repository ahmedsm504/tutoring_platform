Surah Al-Waqiah takes its name from one of the names of the Day of Judgement: *al-waqi'ah*, the Event that will certainly happen. It is a surah that sorts humanity. On that Day, it says, you will be three groups: the foremost who are brought near, the companions of the right, and the companions of the left. Then it asks the reader four questions about the most ordinary things in life, a drop of fluid, a seed, water and fire, before ending at the bedside of a dying person.

This guide explains the whole surah in English, section by section.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Waqiah
- **Number of verses**: 96.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 56, in the twenty-seventh juz.
- **Name**: *Al-Waqi'ah*, the Inevitable Event, one of the names of the Day of Judgement.
- **Structure**: the Event, the three groups, four arguments from creation, the Quran, and the moment of death.

## A Note on the Virtues of Surah Al-Waqiah
A popular narration says that whoever recites Al-Waqiah every night will never be afflicted by poverty. Hadith scholars have judged its chain to be weak, so it should not be presented as an established saying of the Prophet ﷺ. Reciting the surah is still a righteous act, and its message, that provision and life are in Allah's hands, does strengthen a believer's trust.

At-Tirmidhi recorded from Ibn 'Abbas that Abu Bakr said to the Prophet ﷺ: "O Messenger of Allah, you have become grey." He replied: "Hud, Al-Waqiah, Al-Mursalat, 'Amma yatasa'alun and Idha ash-shamsu kuwwirat have made me grey." At-Tirmidhi graded it *hasan gharib*. The weight of these surahs, with their scenes of the Last Day, affected him deeply.

## Verses 1–12: When the Event Occurs
﴿إِذَا وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ﴾ ﴿لَيۡسَ لِوَقۡعَتِهَا كَاذِبَةٌ﴾ ﴿خَافِضَةࣱ رَّافِعَةٌ﴾ (Quran 56:1-3)

**Meaning:** When the Event befalls, there will be no denying its occurrence. It will bring some down and raise others up.

The Event lowers and raises. People who were high in this world may be brought low, and people who were overlooked may be raised. The earth will be shaken violently, mountains crushed into scattered dust, and humanity sorted:

﴿وَكُنتُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا ثَلَٰثَةٗ﴾ ﴿فَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ﴾ ﴿وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ ٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 56:7-10)

**Meaning:** And you will be three kinds: the companions of the right, what of the companions of the right? The companions of the left, what of the companions of the left? And the foremost, the foremost.

## Verses 10–26: The Foremost
﴿وَٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ ٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ﴾ ﴿فِي جَنَّـٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾ ﴿ثُلَّةࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَقَلِيلࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 56:10-14)

**Meaning:** And the foremost, the foremost. They are the ones brought near, in gardens of bliss. A large group from the earlier generations, and a few from the later generations.

*As-sabiqun*, the foremost, are those who raced to good in this world, and so they are first in the next. The repetition "the foremost, the foremost" means: those who were foremost in faith and good deeds are the foremost in Paradise. They are *al-muqarrabun*, those brought nearest to Allah.

The surah describes their reward: couches woven with gold, facing one another, served by youths who never age, with cups of pure drink that cause no headache or intoxication, fruit of their choosing, the meat of birds they desire, and companions with beautiful eyes like hidden pearls, "as a reward for what they used to do". Then:

﴿لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغۡوٗا وَلَا تَأۡثِيمًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّا قِيلٗا سَلَٰمٗا سَلَٰمٗا﴾ (Quran 56:25-26)

**Meaning:** They will hear in it no idle talk and no sinful speech, only the saying: Peace, peace.

Paradise is free not only of pain but of hurtful words. The atmosphere is pure peace.

## Verses 27–40: The Companions of the Right
﴿وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡيَمِينِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡيَمِينِ﴾ ﴿فِي سِدۡرٖ مَّخۡضُودٖ﴾ ﴿وَطَلۡحٖ مَّنضُودٖ﴾ ﴿وَظِلّٖ مَّمۡدُودٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَآءٖ مَّسۡكُوبٖ﴾ (Quran 56:27-31)

**Meaning:** And the companions of the right, what of the companions of the right? Among thornless lote trees, and clustered banana trees, and extended shade, and flowing water.

These are the believers who receive their record in their right hand. Their gardens are described with images that would have meant everything to desert dwellers: shade that never ends, water that is always flowing, fruit that is neither out of season nor forbidden. Of them, it says: "A large group from the earlier generations and a large group from the later generations." That gives hope to this Ummah: among the companions of the right, the later generations are many.

## Verses 41–56: The Companions of the Left
﴿وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ﴾ ﴿فِي سَمُومٖ وَحَمِيمٖ﴾ ﴿وَظِلّٖ مِّن يَحۡمُومٖ﴾ ﴿لَّا بَارِدٖ وَلَا كَرِيمٍ﴾ (Quran 56:41-44)

**Meaning:** And the companions of the left, what of the companions of the left? In scorching wind and boiling water, and a shade of black smoke, neither cool nor refreshing.

Notice the precise contrast. The companions of the right have "extended shade"; the companions of the left have a "shade" of black smoke that is neither cool nor kind. Then the surah explains why:

﴿إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَبۡلَ ذَٰلِكَ مُتۡرَفِينَ﴾ ﴿وَكَانُواْ يُصِرُّونَ عَلَى ٱلۡحِنثِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَكَانُواْ يَقُولُونَ أَئِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابٗا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ﴾ (Quran 56:45-47)

**Meaning:** Before that they were living in luxury, and they persisted in the great sin, and they used to say: When we die and become dust and bones, will we really be raised again?

Three causes: luxury that made them heedless, persistence in the great sin of associating partners with Allah, and denial of the resurrection.

## Verses 57–74: Four Questions About Everyday Life
This section is a masterpiece of argument. Allah asks four questions, each about something we see every day.

### 1. The Drop of Fluid
﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تُمۡنُونَ﴾ ﴿ءَأَنتُمۡ تَخۡلُقُونَهُۥٓ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 56:58-59)

**Meaning:** Have you considered what you emit? Is it you who create it, or are We the Creator?

### 2. The Seed
﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَحۡرُثُونَ﴾ ﴿ءَأَنتُمۡ تَزۡرَعُونَهُۥٓ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلزَّـٰرِعُونَ﴾ (Quran 56:63-64)

**Meaning:** Have you considered what you sow? Is it you who make it grow, or are We the ones who make it grow?

The farmer plants, but he does not give the seed life. If Allah willed, He could turn the crop into debris.

### 3. The Water
﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱلَّذِي تَشۡرَبُونَ﴾ ﴿ءَأَنتُمۡ أَنزَلۡتُمُوهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُزۡنِ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُنزِلُونَ﴾ ﴿لَوۡ نَشَآءُ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ أُجَاجٗا فَلَوۡلَا تَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 56:68-70)

**Meaning:** Have you considered the water you drink? Is it you who brought it down from the clouds, or are We the ones who send it down? If We willed, We could make it salty; so why do you not give thanks?

Sweet rain from salty seas is a daily miracle. In Sahih Muslim, Anas reports that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah is pleased with a servant who eats a meal and praises Him for it, and drinks a drink and praises Him for it."

### 4. The Fire
﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِي تُورُونَ﴾ ﴿ءَأَنتُمۡ أَنشَأۡتُمۡ شَجَرَتَهَآ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُنشِـُٔونَ﴾ ﴿نَحۡنُ جَعَلۡنَٰهَا تَذۡكِرَةٗ وَمَتَٰعٗا لِّلۡمُقۡوِينَ﴾ (Quran 56:71-73)

**Meaning:** Have you considered the fire you kindle? Is it you who produced its tree, or are We the producer? We made it a reminder and a provision for travellers.

Fire is "a reminder" of the Fire of the Hereafter. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reports that the Prophet ﷺ said: "This fire of yours is one part of seventy parts of the fire of Hell."

Each argument ends with the same conclusion, stated in verse 74: so glorify the name of your Lord, the Most Great.

﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ (Quran 56:74)

**Meaning:** So glorify the name of your Lord, the Most Great.

In Sahih Muslim, Hudhayfah describes the night prayer of the Prophet ﷺ and says that in his bowing he said: *Subhana rabbiyal-'azim*, "Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great."

## Verses 75–82: The Oath by the Positions of the Stars
﴿۞فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِمَوَٰقِعِ ٱلنُّجُومِ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَقَسَمࣱ لَّوۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ عَظِيمٌ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَقُرۡءَانࣱ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فِي كِتَٰبٖ مَّكۡنُونٖ﴾ ﴿لَّا يَمَسُّهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُطَهَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلࣱ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 56:75-80)

**Meaning:** I swear by the positions of the stars, and it is a mighty oath if you only knew, that this is a noble Quran, in a protected Book, which none touch except the purified, a revelation from the Lord of the worlds.

The positions of the stars are vast and precise, and Allah calls the oath "mighty". What is being affirmed is the nobility of the Quran. "None touch it except the purified" refers, according to many commentators, to the angels who handle the Preserved Book in the heavens. Many scholars also derive from it, together with other evidence, that a Muslim should be in a state of purity when touching the mushaf.

The section ends with a rebuke: do you make your livelihood that you deny it? Some reports explain this as attributing rain to the stars rather than to Allah. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Zayd ibn Khalid reports the hadith qudsi in which Allah says that some of His servants wake up believers and others disbelievers: whoever says "we were given rain by the grace of Allah and His mercy" is a believer, and whoever says "we were given rain by such-and-such star" is a disbeliever in Him.

## Verses 83–96: The Moment of Death
﴿فَلَوۡلَآ إِذَا بَلَغَتِ ٱلۡحُلۡقُومَ﴾ ﴿وَأَنتُمۡ حِينَئِذٖ تَنظُرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَنَحۡنُ أَقۡرَبُ إِلَيۡهِ مِنكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 56:83-85)

**Meaning:** Why then, when the soul reaches the throat, while you are looking on, and We are nearer to him than you but you do not see?

This is the most intimate scene in the surah. A person is dying. Family and doctors surround him. Yet Allah, through His angels and His knowledge, is nearer to him than they are. The surah challenges the deniers: if you are truly not subject to judgement, bring the soul back.

Then the three groups appear one final time, at the moment of death:
- **If he is one of those brought near**: rest, fragrance and a garden of bliss.
- **If he is one of the companions of the right**: "Peace be upon you" from the companions of the right.
- **If he is one of the deniers who went astray**: hospitality of boiling water and burning in Hell.

﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ حَقُّ ٱلۡيَقِينِ﴾ ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ (Quran 56:95-96)

**Meaning:** This is indeed the certain truth. So glorify the name of your Lord, the Most Great.

## Key Vocabulary
- **Al-Waqi'ah**: the Inevitable Event.
- **Khafidah rafi'ah**: lowering and raising.
- **As-Sabiqun**: the foremost.
- **Al-Muqarrabun**: those brought near.
- **Thullah**: a large group.
- **Ashab al-yamin**: the companions of the right.
- **Ashab ash-shimal**: the companions of the left.
- **Samum**: scorching wind.
- **Yahmum**: black smoke.
- **Mawaqi' an-nujum**: the positions of the stars.
- **Al-Hulqum**: the throat.

## Tajweed Notes for Reciting Al-Waqiah
- **Madd muttasil** in *jaza'an* (verse 24) and *insha'an* (verse 35): the alif is followed by a hamza in the same word, so it is stretched four or five counts.
- **Qalqalah sughra** in *khalaqnakum* (verse 57): the *qaf* has a sukun in the middle of the word, so it is pronounced with a light echo.
- **Ghunnah** in *jannat* (verse 12) and *inna* (verse 35): a doubled *nun* carries a nasal sound of two counts.
- **Madd 'arid**: most verses end in *-un* or *-in*, producing the surah's steady rhythm when you pause.

## Practical Lessons from Surah Al-Waqiah
1. **Decide which group you are working towards.** The surah sorts people by deeds, not status.
2. **Race to good.** The foremost in Paradise were the foremost in this life.
3. **Beware of luxury that breeds heedlessness.** It was the first cause listed for the companions of the left.
4. **Look at your food, water and fire** and recognise the Giver.
5. **Attribute blessings to Allah.** Say "by the grace of Allah and His mercy".
6. **Honour the Quran.** It is noble and comes from the Lord of the worlds.
7. **Prepare for the moment the soul reaches the throat.** It will come to everyone.
8. **Glorify your Lord.** Say *Subhana rabbiyal-'azim* with presence in every bowing.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "Al-Waqi'ah" mean?
The Inevitable Event, a name for the Day of Judgement.

### Who are the three groups in Surah Al-Waqiah?
The foremost who are brought near, the companions of the right, and the companions of the left.

### Does reciting Al-Waqiah every night prevent poverty?
The narration that says so has a weak chain according to hadith scholars, so it should not be attributed to the Prophet ﷺ with certainty.

### What does "none touch it except the purified" mean?
Many commentators say it refers to the angels who touch the Preserved Book; scholars also discuss it in relation to touching the mushaf in a state of purity.

### What are the four arguments in verses 57–74?
The creation of the human from a drop of fluid, the growth of crops, rain water, and fire.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Waqiah?
96 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Waqiah reminds you that one Event is coming that no one will be able to deny, and that it will place you in one of three groups. It shows you the reward of each, then turns to your daily bread, your water and your fire to remind you who provides them, and finally sits with you at the moment of death. Its final command sums up the right response: glorify the name of your Lord, the Most Great.
