﴿وَمَا نَتَنَزَّلُ إِلَّا بِأَمۡرِ رَبِّكَۖ لَهُۥ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِينَا وَمَا خَلۡفَنَا وَمَا بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَۚ وَمَا كَانَ رَبُّكَ نَسِيّٗا﴾ [Maryam 19:64]

## The context
- The Prophet ﷺ asked Jibril why he did not visit more often, and this verse was revealed (narrated by al-Bukhari from Ibn ʿAbbas).

## Jibril's words
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *We descend only by your Lord's command* | Revelation follows Allah's timing |
| *What is before us, behind us, and between* | Future, past and present are His |
| *Your Lord is never forgetful* | Delay is never neglect |

## Comfort in waiting
- When answers or relief seem delayed, remember: Allah has not forgotten you. Timing belongs to Him.

## The next verse
- *Lord of the heavens and earth and what is between them, so worship Him and be patient in His worship* (19:65).

## Lessons
- **Be patient with Allah's timing.**
- **Keep worshipping** while you wait.
