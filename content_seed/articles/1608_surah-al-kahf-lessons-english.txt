## Surah Al-Kahf: Four Stories, Four Trials, and Lessons for Today

**Surah Al-Kahf** (110 ayat) is built around **four stories**, and each one is about a trial people face in every age.

### The four stories
| Story | Trial | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| The young men of the cave | Faith | Hold on to your faith and keep good company |
| The owner of two gardens | Wealth | Don't be arrogant with blessings; say *mā shāʾ Allāh* |
| Musa and al-Khidr | Knowledge | Be humble; Allah's wisdom is greater than what we see |
| Dhul-Qarnayn | Power | Use power for justice and credit Allah |

### The du'a of the young men
﴿رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةٗ وَهَيِّئۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَمۡرِنَا رَشَدٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf: 10]

### The first ten ayat
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Whoever memorizes ten ayat from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjal» [Muslim].

### Friday
- A hadith reported by Al-Hakim and others mentions a light for whoever reads Al-Kahf on Friday, and several scholars graded it authentic.

### Lessons for today
| Trial | Today |
|---|---|
| Faith | Peer pressure and doubts online |
| Wealth | Showing off and comparison |
| Knowledge | Arrogance and quick judgments |
| Power | Using authority fairly |

## "Never Say of Anything: I Will Do That Tomorrow, Except If Allah Wills": Quran 18:23-24 Explained

﴿وَلَا تَقُولَنَّ لِشَاْيۡءٍ إِنِّي فَاعِلࣱ ذَٰلِكَ غَدًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَٱذۡكُر رَّبَّكَ إِذَا نَسِيتَ وَقُلۡ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَهۡدِيَنِ رَبِّي لِأَقۡرَبَ مِنۡ هَٰذَا رَشَدٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:23-24]

### The occasion
- Scholars mention that Quraysh asked the Prophet ﷺ about the people of the cave, Dhul-Qarnayn and the soul. He said *I will tell you tomorrow* without saying *in sha Allah*, and revelation was delayed, then these verses came.

### Etiquette of Allah's will
- Every future plan is tied to Allah's will, because no one owns tomorrow.

### "Remember your Lord when you forget"
- If you forget to say it, say it when you remember. Some said: when you forget something, remember Allah so He reminds you.

### Tajweed note
- *Li-shayʾin* is written with an alif after the shin that is **not pronounced**; it is read *li-shayʾin*, and the Mushaf marks it as silent.

### Lessons
- **Say in sha Allah** sincerely, not as a habit.
- **Do not use it** as an excuse for not keeping promises.

## "Keep Yourself Patiently with Those Who Call on Their Lord": Quran 18:28 Explained

﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ نَفۡسَكَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَدَوٰةِ وَٱلۡعَشِيِّ يُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَهُۥۖ وَلَا تَعۡدُ عَيۡنَاكَ عَنۡهُمۡ تُرِيدُ زِينَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَا تُطِعۡ مَنۡ أَغۡفَلۡنَا قَلۡبَهُۥ عَن ذِكۡرِنَا وَٱتَّبَعَ هَوَىٰهُ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُهُۥ فُرُطٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:28]

### The context
- Some leaders of Quraysh wanted the Prophet ﷺ to send away the poor Companions before they would sit with him. The verse commands the opposite: **stay with them**.

### Patience with good company
- *Wasbir nafsaka* means "hold yourself back with them". Good company sometimes requires patience, because it may not offer status or wealth.

### Who to stay with
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| They call on their Lord | People of duʿa and worship |
| Morning and evening | Constant, not occasional |
| Seeking His Face | Sincere, not showing off |

### Who not to obey
- The one whose heart Allah made heedless of His remembrance, who **follows his desire**, and whose affair is in excess and loss.

### Lessons
- **Choose friends by their hearts**, not their wealth.
- **Do not let glitter pull your eyes** away from people of good.

## "Why Did You Not Say, When You Entered Your Garden: Ma Sha Allah": Quran 18:39 Explained

﴿وَلَوۡلَآ إِذۡ دَخَلۡتَ جَنَّتَكَ قُلۡتَ مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ إِن تَرَنِ أَنَا۠ أَقَلَّ مِنكَ مَالٗا وَوَلَدٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:39]

### The story
- A man was given two gardens and became proud, saying: *I do not think this will ever perish.* His believing companion advised him with these words.

### *Ma sha Allah*
- This is what Allah willed and gave, not by my strength or intelligence.

### *La quwwata illa billah*
- Admitting that the strength to keep and grow a blessing is from Allah.
- *La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah is a treasure among the treasures of Paradise* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### The end
- *His fruits were destroyed, and he began turning his hands over in regret* (18:42).

### Lessons
- **Say ma sha Allah** when you see a blessing, yours or others'.
- **Do not be deceived** by what you own.

## "Wealth and Children Are the Adornment of This Life": Quran 18:46 Explained

﴿ٱلۡمَالُ وَٱلۡبَنُونَ زِينَةُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَٱلۡبَٰقِيَٰتُ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتُ خَيۡرٌ عِندَ رَبِّكَ ثَوَابٗا وَخَيۡرٌ أَمَلٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:46]

### Adornment, not essence
- *Zinah* is decoration: beautiful, enjoyable, but on the surface and temporary.

### The context
- Just before it, the parable of this life as plants that flourish after rain, then become dry stalks scattered by the wind (18:45).

### The lasting good deeds
- Many Companions and scholars explained *al-baqiyat as-salihat* as: *SubhanAllah, alhamdulillah, la ilaha illa Allah, Allahu akbar*, and some added *la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah*. Others said it includes every good deed.

### Two comparisons
| | Adornment | Lasting good deeds |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | Temporary | Remains |
| Reward | In this life | With your Lord |
| Hope | May disappoint | Better hope |

### Children as lasting deeds
- A righteous child who prays for you is among the deeds that continue after death (narrated by Muslim). Raising them well turns adornment into lasting reward.

### Lessons
- **Enjoy wealth and family**, but invest in what lasts.
- **Fill free moments with dhikr.**

## How to Memorize Surah Al-Kahf in One Month: A Plan Built Around Its Four Stories

[Surah Al-Kahf](/quran/18/) is one of the most loved surahs, recited by many Muslims every Friday. It's a great memorization project once you've finished Juz Amma and Tabarak, and it's easier than it looks because it's **built around stories**, and stories stick.

### Quick facts
- **110 verses**, pages 293–304 in the Madinah Mushaf (about 12 pages).
- A Makkan surah, in juz 15 and 16.

### Its authentic virtue
The Prophet ﷺ said: **"Whoever memorizes ten verses from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjal."** (Muslim). So the first ten verses come first.

### The surah's map
| Section | Verses | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 1–8 | Praise of Allah, the Book, the warning |
| People of the Cave | 9–26 | The young men in the cave |
| Reflection | 27–31 | Staying with the righteous; two outcomes |
| Owner of the two gardens | 32–44 | The arrogant man and his gardens |
| Lessons | 45–59 | The parable of this life, the Day of Judgement, Iblis |
| Musa and Al-Khidr | 60–82 | Musa's journey with the righteous servant |
| Dhul-Qarnayn | 83–98 | His journeys and the barrier |
| Closing | 99–110 | The Hereafter and deeds |

### The 4-week plan (5 days a week)
- **Week 1:** verses 1–26 (opening + People of the Cave), about 5 verses a day. Master the first ten before moving on.
- **Week 2:** verses 27–49, about 4–5 a day.
- **Week 3:** verses 50–82 (Iblis, then Musa and Al-Khidr), 6–7 short verses a day.
- **Week 4:** verses 83–110 (Dhul-Qarnayn and the closing), 5–6 a day.

Use one day each week to revise the whole week, and one to rest or read the full surah from the Mushaf.

### Tips specific to Al-Kahf
1. **Know the story before memorizing it.** Read a short tafsir of the section first.
2. **Watch the similar phrases in the Musa–Khidr story.** Compare verse 72, ﴿قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُلۡ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرًا﴾, with verse 75, ﴿قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكَ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرًا﴾, which adds "*laka*". Write them side by side.
3. **The Sakt in verse 1:** in Hafs there's a brief pause without breath on ﴿عِوَجَاۜ﴾ when continuing. Learn it from day one.
4. **Recite your portion on Fridays** to combine revision with the Friday habit.

### After memorizing
- Recite it fully from memory every Friday.
- Pray with parts of it at night.
- Revise one story a day for two weeks after finishing.

## "If the Sea Were Ink for the Words of My Lord": Quran 18:109 Explained

﴿قُل لَّوۡ كَانَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ مِدَادٗا لِّكَلِمَٰتِ رَبِّي لَنَفِدَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ قَبۡلَ أَن تَنفَدَ كَلِمَٰتُ رَبِّي وَلَوۡ جِئۡنَا بِمِثۡلِهِۦ مَدَدٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:109]

### The words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Midad* | Ink used for writing |
| *Words of my Lord* | His speech, knowledge and wisdom, without end |

### The image
- The whole sea as ink, with another like it added, would run out before Allah's words do.

### Luqman 31:27
- *If all the trees on earth were pens and the sea, with seven more seas added, the words of Allah would not be exhausted.*

### Its place in Al-Kahf
- Just before the final verse of a surah that speaks of knowledge in the story of Musa and al-Khidr: human knowledge is a drop before Allah's.

### Lessons
- **Stay humble** however much you learn.
- **Glorify Allah**, whose words never end.

## "Whoever Hopes to Meet His Lord": Quran 18:110 Explained

﴿قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ أَنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱۖ فَمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ لِقَآءَ رَبِّهِۦ فَلۡيَعۡمَلۡ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا وَلَا يُشۡرِكۡ بِعِبَادَةِ رَبِّهِۦٓ أَحَدَۢا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:110]

### A human with revelation
- The Prophet ﷺ is commanded to say: *I am only a human like you*, so no one exaggerates his status, yet *revelation comes to me*, so no one belittles it.

### The core message
- *Your God is one God*: the summary of every prophet's call.

### Two conditions of accepted deeds
| Condition | From the verse | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Righteous deed | *Let him do righteous work* | In line with the Sunnah |
| Sincerity | *Not associate anyone in the worship of his Lord* | For Allah alone, not for show |

- Scholars say a deed without either condition is not accepted.

### Hidden shirk
- Showing off (*riyaʾ*) is a form of minor shirk. The Prophet ﷺ feared it for his nation and called it the hidden shirk (narrated by Ahmad and Ibn Majah).

### A perfect ending
- Al-Kahf discusses the trials of faith, wealth, knowledge and power. Its last verse gives the protection: **sincere, correct deeds**.

### Lessons
- **Check intention** before each act of worship.
- **Learn the Sunnah** to do deeds correctly.

## Surah Maryam Meaning: Mercy, Miraculous Births and a Love Placed by Allah

**Surah Maryam** (98 ayat) is the only surah named after a woman, and the word **mercy** (*rahmah*) and the name **ar-Rahman** recur throughout it.

### Zakariya's quiet prayer
- An old man with a barren wife called his Lord **in a hidden voice**, and Allah gave him Yahya, a name given to no one before.

### Maryam and the birth of ʿIsa
﴿وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَرۡيَمَ إِذِ ٱنتَبَذَتۡ مِنۡ أَهۡلِهَا مَكَانٗا شَرۡقِيّٗا﴾
- An angel came to her with the news of a pure son. She asked how, since no man had touched her, and was told it is easy for Allah.
- When she returned with the baby, ʿIsa spoke from the cradle: *I am the servant of Allah.*

### Ibrahim and his father
- Ibrahim called his father four times with "*Ya abati*" (O my dear father), with gentle reasoning against idols. Even when threatened, he replied: *Peace be upon you; I will ask forgiveness for you from my Lord.*

### The line of prophets
- Musa, Ismaʿil and Idris are mentioned with their qualities: sincerity, keeping promises, and truthfulness.

### Love placed by Allah
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ سَيَجۡعَلُ لَهُمُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وُدّٗا﴾

### Lessons
- **Pray privately**: Allah hears a hidden call.
- **Speak gently even when you disagree**, as Ibrahim did with his father.

## "And Your Lord Is Never Forgetful": Quran 19:64 Explained

﴿وَمَا نَتَنَزَّلُ إِلَّا بِأَمۡرِ رَبِّكَۖ لَهُۥ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِينَا وَمَا خَلۡفَنَا وَمَا بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَۚ وَمَا كَانَ رَبُّكَ نَسِيّٗا﴾ [Maryam 19:64]

### The context
- The Prophet ﷺ asked Jibril why he did not visit more often, and this verse was revealed (narrated by al-Bukhari from Ibn ʿAbbas).

### Jibril's words
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *We descend only by your Lord's command* | Revelation follows Allah's timing |
| *What is before us, behind us, and between* | Future, past and present are His |
| *Your Lord is never forgetful* | Delay is never neglect |

### Comfort in waiting
- When answers or relief seem delayed, remember: Allah has not forgotten you. Timing belongs to Him.

### The next verse
- *Lord of the heavens and earth and what is between them, so worship Him and be patient in His worship* (19:65).

### Lessons
- **Be patient with Allah's timing.**
- **Keep worshipping** while you wait.
