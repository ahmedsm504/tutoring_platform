## Meaning
*Say: O Allah, Owner of sovereignty, You give sovereignty to whom You will and take it from whom You will. You honour whom You will and humble whom You will. In Your hand is all good. Indeed You are over all things capable.*

## Four actions, one Owner
| Action | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Giving power | No ruler holds power by his own strength |
| Removing power | No kingdom lasts except by His will |
| Honouring | True honour comes from obedience to Him |
| Humbling | Arrogance ends in humiliation |

## "In Your hand is all good"
- The verse mentions only **good** being in His hand, though He is in control of everything. Scholars explain this as a lesson in etiquette: evil is not attributed to Allah in speech, and His decrees always carry wisdom.

## The next verse
- Night enters into day and day into night, the living from the dead and the dead from the living: the same Power that rotates kingdoms rotates the hours.

## Lessons
- **Do not envy the powerful**; their power is a loan.
- **Seek honour from Allah**, not from people.
