## "Allah Does Not Forbid You from Those Who Do Not Fight You": Quran 60:8 Explained

﴿لَّا يَنۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يُقَٰتِلُوكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَلَمۡ يُخۡرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمۡ أَن تَبَرُّوهُمۡ وَتُقۡسِطُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُقۡسِطِينَ﴾ [Al-Mumtahanah 60:8]

### Who is meant?
- Non-Muslims who **did not fight** Muslims because of religion and **did not expel** them from their homes.

### Two permitted acts
| Act | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Birr* | Kindness, good relations, good treatment |
| *Qist* | Justice and giving rights |

### Asma and her mother
- Asma bint Abi Bakr's mother, who was not Muslim, came to visit her. Asma asked the Prophet ﷺ whether she should maintain ties, and he said: *Yes, keep ties with your mother* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim). Some scholars mention the verse was revealed about this.

### Allah loves the just
- Justice with everyone is a character Allah loves.

### Lessons
- **Be kind** to peaceful neighbours and colleagues of other faiths.
- **Be fair** in all dealings.

## Surah Al-Mumtahanah Meaning: Loyalty, Justice and the Example of Ibrahim

**Surah Al-Mumtahanah** (13 ayat) balances two principles: **loyalty to faith** and **justice to all people**.

### The story of Hatib
- Before the conquest of Makkah, the companion Hatib ibn Abi Baltaʿah sent a secret letter to Quraysh to protect his family there.
- The Prophet ﷺ forgave him because he had attended Badr, and the opening ayat were revealed [Al-Bukhari].

### The example of Ibrahim
- *There has been an excellent example for you in Ibrahim and those with him*: they separated clearly from what their people worshipped.

### Justice to peaceful non-Muslims
﴿لَّا يَنۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يُقَٰتِلُوكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَلَمۡ يُخۡرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمۡ أَن تَبَرُّوهُمۡ وَتُقۡسِطُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُقۡسِطِينَ﴾
- Allah does not forbid **kindness and justice** to those who do not fight you or drive you from your homes.
| Toward | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Those at war with Muslims | Not taken as allies |
| Peaceful non-Muslims | Kindness and justice are encouraged |

### The tested women
- The surah is named after the women who migrated, who were **tested** about their faith before being accepted.

### Lessons
- **Clarity in belief, fairness in dealings.**

## Surah As-Saff Meaning: Why Do You Say What You Do Not Do?

**Surah As-Saff** (14 ayat) begins with a question every believer must ask himself.

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لِمَ تَقُولُونَ مَا لَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ ﴿كَبُرَ مَقۡتًا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَن تَقُولُواْ مَا لَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾
- *O you who believe, why do you say what you do not do? It is greatly hateful to Allah that you say what you do not do.*

### The background
- Some companions said they wished to know the deed Allah loves most so they could do it; this surah was revealed, as reported in At-Tirmidhi.

### A solid structure
﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِهِۦ صَفّٗا كَأَنَّهُم بُنۡيَٰنࣱ مَّرۡصُوصࣱ﴾

### Glad tidings of Ahmad
- ʿIsa said he came confirming the Torah and *bringing good tidings of a messenger after me whose name is Ahmad*.

### The trade that saves
| Trade | Profit |
|---|---|
| Believing in Allah and His Messenger | Forgiveness |
| Striving with wealth and self | Gardens and pleasant homes |

### Helpers of Allah
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُوٓاْ أَنصَارَ ٱللَّهِ كَمَا قَالَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ لِلۡحَوَارِيِّـۧنَ مَنۡ أَنصَارِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ﴾

### Lessons
- **Match your words with actions.**
- **Unity** is beloved to Allah.

## Surah Al-Jumu'ah Meaning: Hasten to the Remembrance of Allah

**Surah Al-Jumu'ah** (11 ayat) was recited by the Prophet ﷺ in the Friday prayer, together with Al-Munafiqun [Muslim].

### The mission
- Allah sent among the unlettered a messenger **reciting** His ayat, **purifying** them and **teaching** them the Book and wisdom.

### Knowledge without action
- Those who were given the Torah and did not act on it are compared to a donkey carrying books.
- **For every student of the Quran**: memorizing is the beginning; acting on it is the goal.

### The Friday command
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَوٰةِ مِن يَوۡمِ ٱلۡجُمُعَةِ فَٱسۡعَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَذَرُواْ ٱلۡبَيۡعَۚ﴾
﴿فَإِذَا قُضِيَتِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةُ فَٱنتَشِرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾
| Before the prayer | After the prayer |
|---|---|
| Hurry to the remembrance of Allah | Spread out in the land |
| Leave trade | Seek Allah's bounty |
| | Remember Allah much |

### The caravan
- The last ayah mentions people who left the Prophet ﷺ standing in the sermon to go to a trade caravan: *what is with Allah is better than amusement and trade*.

### Lessons
- **Islam balances** worship and work.

## Surah Al-Munafiqun Meaning: The Signs of Hypocrisy

**Surah Al-Munafiqun** (11 ayat) exposes hypocrisy, then turns to the believers with a gentle warning.

﴿إِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ قَالُواْ نَشۡهَدُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱللَّهُ يَشۡهَدُ إِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ لَكَٰذِبُونَ﴾
- **Notice**: their words were true, but they were liars because their hearts did not believe them.

### Traits of the hypocrites
| Trait | From the surah |
|---|---|
| Oaths as a shield | They use their oaths to block people from Allah's way |
| Impressive appearance | Their bodies please you, their speech attracts you |
| Fear | They think every shout is against them |
| Arrogance | They turn away when invited to seek forgiveness |
| Plotting | *The more honoured will expel the humbler* |
- The surah answers: *Honour belongs to Allah, His Messenger and the believers*.

### A message to believers
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُلۡهِكُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾
﴿وَأَنفِقُواْ مِن مَّا رَزَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ أَحَدَكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ فَيَقُولَ رَبِّ لَوۡلَآ أَخَّرۡتَنِيٓ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ قَرِيبٖ فَأَصَّدَّقَ وَأَكُن مِّنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾
- **The dying wish**: more time to give charity.

### Lessons
- **Check your heart**, not only your words.
- **Give now**, before you wish for more time.

## Surah At-Taghabun Meaning: The Day of Mutual Loss and Gain

**Surah At-Taghabun** (18 ayat) takes its name from one of the names of the Day of Judgement.

### The Day of mutual loss
﴿يَوۡمَ يَجۡمَعُكُمۡ لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡجَمۡعِۖ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلتَّغَابُنِۗ﴾
- **At-Taghābun**: when people discover who truly gained and who truly lost, as commentators explained.

### Calamities and the heart
﴿مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِ قَلۡبَهُۥۚ﴾
- ʿAlqamah, a student of Ibn Masʿud, explained it as the man struck by a calamity who knows it is from Allah, so he accepts it and submits.

### Family and wealth
﴿إِنَّمَآ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ فِتۡنَةࣱۚ وَٱللَّهُ عِندَهُۥٓ أَجۡرٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾
- Some spouses and children may pull a person away from obedience; yet the surah adds: *if you pardon, overlook and forgive, Allah is Forgiving, Merciful*.

### As much as you can
﴿فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُمۡ وَٱسۡمَعُواْ وَأَطِيعُواْ وَأَنفِقُواْ خَيۡرٗا لِّأَنفُسِكُمۡۗ﴾

### Lessons
- **Real loss** is losing the Hereafter.
- **Do your best**; Allah asks no more.

## "Fear Allah as Much as You Are Able": Quran 64:16 Explained

﴿فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُمۡ وَٱسۡمَعُواْ وَأَطِيعُواْ وَأَنفِقُواْ خَيۡرٗا لِّأَنفُسِكُمۡۗ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفۡسِهِۦ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ [At-Taghabun 64:16]

### Within capacity
- Allah asks for taqwa **as much as you are able**. This matches the hadith: *What I commanded you, do of it what you can* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Four commands
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Fear Allah as much as you can | Do your best, without despair |
| Listen | Pay attention to guidance |
| Obey | Act on it |
| Spend | Charity benefits **you** |

### "Good for yourselves"
- Spending is described as good **for yourselves**: its reward returns to you.

### Stinginess of the soul
- *Shuhh* is greed combined with holding back. Being protected from it is called **success**.

### Balance
- "As much as you can" is not an excuse for laziness; it means using your full capacity.

### Lessons
- **Do what you can today**, and grow tomorrow.
- **Ask Allah to protect you** from greed.

## Surah At-Talaq Meaning: Whoever Fears Allah, He Makes a Way Out

**Surah At-Talaq** (12 ayat) deals with one of life's hardest moments, divorce, and fills it with **taqwa and hope**.

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِذَا طَلَّقۡتُمُ ٱلنِّسَآءَ فَطَلِّقُوهُنَّ لِعِدَّتِهِنَّ وَأَحۡصُواْ ٱلۡعِدَّةَۖ﴾

### Key principles
| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Divorce at the right time | With attention to the waiting period (ʿiddah) |
| Count the ʿiddah | Precision and fairness |
| Don't expel her from the home | During the ʿiddah, unless clear immorality |
| Keep or part with kindness | No harm in either case |
| Witnesses | Two just witnesses |
- *You do not know; perhaps Allah will bring about after that a new situation.*

### The promise
﴿وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مَخۡرَجٗا﴾ ﴿وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسۡبُهُۥٓۚ﴾
- **Placed in the middle of divorce rulings**: in the hardest situations, taqwa opens doors.

### Ease after hardship
- *Allah will bring about, after hardship, ease.*

### Lessons
- **Fairness** even in separation.
- **Taqwa** is the key to every way out.

## Quran 65:2-3 Explained: Taqwa, Unexpected Provision and Reliance on Allah

﴿وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مَخۡرَجٗا﴾ [At-Talaq 65:2]
﴿وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسۡبُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمۡرِهِۦۚ قَدۡ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدۡرٗا﴾ [At-Talaq 65:3]

### A surprising context
- These verses come in **Surah At-Talaq**, among the rulings on divorce. At the moment of family crisis, Allah promises a way out to those who stay just and mindful.

### Four promises
| Condition | Promise |
|---|---|
| Taqwa | A way out (*makhraj*) |
| Taqwa | Provision from unexpected places |
| Reliance (*tawakkul*) | Allah is sufficient for him |
| Certainty | Allah accomplishes His purpose |

### Tawakkul is not laziness
- The Prophet ﷺ said that if you relied on Allah as you should, He would provide for you as He provides for birds: they go out hungry in the morning and return full (narrated by al-Tirmidhi). The birds **go out**.

### "A measure for everything"
- The timing of relief is set by Allah. Patience is part of trust.

### Lessons
- **Fear Allah in hard situations**, especially in family conflicts.
- **Rely on Allah after taking the means.**

## Surah At-Tahrim Meaning: Protect Yourselves and Your Families from the Fire

**Surah At-Tahrim** (12 ayat) moves from a family matter in the Prophet's home ﷺ to a lesson for every family.

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ لِمَ تُحَرِّمُ مَآ أَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ لَكَۖ تَبۡتَغِي مَرۡضَاتَ أَزۡوَٰجِكَۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾
- In Sahih al-Bukhari, the background is that he ﷺ vowed not to drink honey to please some of his wives.

### Protect your families
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ قُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَأَهۡلِيكُمۡ نَارٗا وَقُودُهَا ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلۡحِجَارَةُ﴾
- **Your first circle of da'wah** is your home: teach, guide and set an example.

### Sincere repentance
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوۡبَةٗ نَّصُوحًا﴾
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stop the sin | Immediately |
| Regret | Sincerely |
| Resolve | Not to return |
| Restore rights | If others were wronged |

### Four women
| Example | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Wife of Nuh, wife of Lut | Closeness to a prophet doesn't save without faith |
| Asiyah, wife of Pharaoh | A tyrant's house can't destroy true faith |
| Maryam | Purity and devotion |

### Lessons
- **Faith is personal**: no one can believe for you.

## Quran 66:6 Explained: A Parent's Duty to Guide the Family

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ قُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَأَهۡلِيكُمۡ نَارٗا وَقُودُهَا ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلۡحِجَارَةُ عَلَيۡهَا مَلَـٰٓئِكَةٌ غِلَاظࣱ شِدَادࣱ لَّا يَعۡصُونَ ٱللَّهَ مَآ أَمَرَهُمۡ وَيَفۡعَلُونَ مَا يُؤۡمَرُونَ﴾ [At-Tahrim 66:6]

### Yourself first
- The verse starts with *yourselves*: you cannot guide your family while neglecting yourself.

### How to protect them
- ʿAli ibn Abi Talib is reported to have explained it: **teach them and discipline them** (reported by al-Tabari and others).
| Area | Practice |
|---|---|
| Belief | Teach them about Allah with love |
| Prayer | Teach it at seven, as in the hadith narrated by Abu Dawud |
| Quran | A daily portion, even small |
| Character | Be the example at home |

### A shepherd and his flock
- *Each of you is a shepherd and responsible for his flock; a man is a shepherd of his family* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### The description of the Fire
- Its fuel is people and stones, guarded by stern angels who never disobey. The image is meant to awaken responsibility.

### Lessons
- **Start with your own prayer.**
- **Invest time in your children's faith.**
