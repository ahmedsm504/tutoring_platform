Surah Ar-Ra'd (The Thunder) contains two verses that have shaped how Muslims think about personal and social change. The first: "Indeed, Allah will not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves." The second: "Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest." The surah fills the reader's eyes with signs: heavens raised without visible pillars, plots of land side by side watered by the same water yet producing fruits of different taste, lightning bringing fear and hope, and thunder glorifying its Lord. It offers a parable of foam on a flood: the foam disappears, and what benefits people remains. And it describes the people of understanding, who keep their promises, keep the ties Allah commanded, and repel evil with good.

This guide explains Surah Ar-Ra'd section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ar-Ra'd
- **Number of verses**: 43.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 13, in the thirteenth juz.
- **Name**: Ar-Ra'd means "the thunder", from verse 13.
- **Prostration of recitation**: at verse 15.
- **Main themes**: Allah's signs in creation, His knowledge and guardianship, change from within, the reality of truth and falsehood, and peace through remembrance.

## Verses 1–4: Signs in the Heavens and the Earth
﴿الٓمٓرۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِۗ وَٱلَّذِيٓ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي رَفَعَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ بِغَيۡرِ عَمَدٖ تَرَوۡنَهَاۖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلࣱّ يَجۡرِي لِأَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۚ يُدَبِّرُ ٱلۡأَمۡرَ يُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَعَلَّكُم بِلِقَآءِ رَبِّكُمۡ تُوقِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 13:1-2)

**"It is Allah who erected the heavens without pillars that you see."** He subjected the sun and the moon, each running for a specified term. **"He arranges each matter; He details the signs that you may, of the meeting with your Lord, be certain."**

﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي مَدَّ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَجَعَلَ فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَأَنۡهَٰرٗاۖ وَمِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ جَعَلَ فِيهَا زَوۡجَيۡنِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِۖ يُغۡشِي ٱلَّيۡلَ ٱلنَّهَارَۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ قِطَعࣱ مُّتَجَٰوِرَٰتࣱ وَجَنَّـٰتࣱ مِّنۡ أَعۡنَٰبٖ وَزَرۡعࣱ وَنَخِيلࣱ صِنۡوَانࣱ وَغَيۡرُ صِنۡوَانٖ يُسۡقَىٰ بِمَآءٖ وَٰحِدٖ وَنُفَضِّلُ بَعۡضَهَا عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ فِي ٱلۡأُكُلِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 13:3-4)

**"And within the land are neighbouring plots and gardens of grapevines and crops and palm trees, from the same root or otherwise, watered with one water; but We make some of them exceed others in quality of fruit."** Same soil, same water, different results: a sign of the Creator's will. Commentators also saw in it an image of people: hearts hear the same Quran, yet bear different fruits.

## Verses 5–11: Knowledge, Guardians and Change
﴿ٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تَحۡمِلُ كُلُّ أُنثَىٰ وَمَا تَغِيضُ ٱلۡأَرۡحَامُ وَمَا تَزۡدَادُۚ وَكُلُّ شَيۡءٍ عِندَهُۥ بِمِقۡدَارٍ﴾ ﴿عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ ٱلۡمُتَعَالِ﴾ ﴿سَوَآءࣱ مِّنكُم مَّنۡ أَسَرَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ وَمَن جَهَرَ بِهِۦ وَمَنۡ هُوَ مُسۡتَخۡفِۭ بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَسَارِبُۢ بِٱلنَّهَارِ﴾ (Quran 13:8-10)

**"Allah knows what every female carries and what the wombs lose or exceed."** **"Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, the Grand, the Exalted."** **"It is the same to Him whether one of you conceals his speech or publicises it."**

﴿لَهُۥ مُعَقِّبَٰتࣱ مِّنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ يَحۡفَظُونَهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡۗ وَإِذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ سُوٓءٗا فَلَا مَرَدَّ لَهُۥۚ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَالٍ﴾ (Quran 13:11)

**"For each one are successive angels before and behind him who protect him by the decree of Allah."** Guardian angels accompany every person.

**"Indeed, Allah will not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves."** A principle of individual and social change: Allah's change of a people's state follows their change from within. Blessings may be removed when people change for the worse, and hardship lifted when they change for the better.

## Verses 12–18: Lightning, Thunder and the Parable of Foam
﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُرِيكُمُ ٱلۡبَرۡقَ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَيُنشِئُ ٱلسَّحَابَ ٱلثِّقَالَ﴾ ﴿وَيُسَبِّحُ ٱلرَّعۡدُ بِحَمۡدِهِۦ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ مِنۡ خِيفَتِهِۦ وَيُرۡسِلُ ٱلصَّوَٰعِقَ فَيُصِيبُ بِهَا مَن يَشَآءُ وَهُمۡ يُجَٰدِلُونَ فِي ٱللَّهِ وَهُوَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡمِحَالِ﴾ (Quran 13:12-13)

**"It is He who shows you lightning, causing fear and aspiration, and generates the heavy clouds."** Fear of its strike, hope for rain. **"And the thunder exalts Allah with praise of Him, and the angels as well from fear of Him."**

﴿لَهُۥ دَعۡوَةُ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ لَا يَسۡتَجِيبُونَ لَهُم بِشَيۡءٍ إِلَّا كَبَٰسِطِ كَفَّيۡهِ إِلَى ٱلۡمَآءِ لِيَبۡلُغَ فَاهُ وَمَا هُوَ بِبَٰلِغِهِۦۚ وَمَا دُعَآءُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلٖ﴾ (Quran 13:14)

**"To Him alone is the supplication of truth."** Those invoked besides Him respond to nothing, like one who stretches his hands towards water for it to reach his mouth, and it will not reach it.

﴿وَلِلَّهِۤ يَسۡجُدُۤ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ طَوۡعٗا وَكَرۡهٗا وَظِلَٰلُهُم بِٱلۡغُدُوِّ وَٱلۡأٓصَالِ۩﴾ (Quran 13:15)

**"And to Allah prostrates whoever is within the heavens and the earth, willingly or by compulsion, and their shadows as well, in the mornings and the afternoons."** Here is the prostration of recitation.

﴿أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَسَالَتۡ أَوۡدِيَةُۢ بِقَدَرِهَا فَٱحۡتَمَلَ ٱلسَّيۡلُ زَبَدٗا رَّابِيٗاۖ وَمِمَّا يُوقِدُونَ عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلنَّارِ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ حِلۡيَةٍ أَوۡ مَتَٰعٖ زَبَدࣱ مِّثۡلُهُۥۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَٱلۡبَٰطِلَۚ فَأَمَّا ٱلزَّبَدُ فَيَذۡهَبُ جُفَآءٗۖ وَأَمَّا مَا يَنفَعُ ٱلنَّاسَ فَيَمۡكُثُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ﴾ (Quran 13:17)

**The parable of foam**: **"He sends down from the sky rain, and valleys flow according to their capacity, and the torrent carries a rising foam. And from that ore which they heat in the fire, desiring adornments and utensils, is a foam like it. Thus Allah presents the example of truth and falsehood. As for the foam, it vanishes, being cast off; but as for that which benefits the people, it remains on the earth."**

**The meaning**: revelation is like rain, and hearts are like valleys, each holding according to its capacity. Falsehood may rise to the surface and look impressive, like foam, but it disappears. Truth, like water and pure metal, stays and benefits. Do not be deceived by the noise of falsehood.

## Verses 19–24: People of Understanding
﴿۞أَفَمَن يَعۡلَمُ أَنَّمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ ٱلۡحَقُّ كَمَنۡ هُوَ أَعۡمَىٰٓۚ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُوفُونَ بِعَهۡدِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا يَنقُضُونَ ٱلۡمِيثَٰقَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَصِلُونَ مَآ أَمَرَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦٓ أَن يُوصَلَ وَيَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُمۡ وَيَخَافُونَ سُوٓءَ ٱلۡحِسَابِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُواْ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ وَجۡهِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنفَقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ وَيَدۡرَءُونَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ ٱلسَّيِّئَةَ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عُقۡبَى ٱلدَّارِ﴾ (Quran 13:19-22)

**"Then is he who knows that what has been revealed to you is the truth like one who is blind? Only those of understanding will be reminded."** Their qualities:
1. **They fulfil the covenant of Allah** and do not break the contract.
2. **They join what Allah has ordered to be joined**: family ties and every bond Allah commanded.
3. **They fear their Lord** and the evil of the account.
4. **They are patient, seeking the face of their Lord.**
5. **They establish prayer.**
6. **They spend from what We have provided them, secretly and publicly.**
7. **They repel evil with good.**

﴿جَنَّـٰتُ عَدۡنٖ يَدۡخُلُونَهَا وَمَن صَلَحَ مِنۡ ءَابَآئِهِمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِهِمۡۖ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ يَدۡخُلُونَ عَلَيۡهِم مِّن كُلِّ بَابٖ﴾ ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُم بِمَا صَبَرۡتُمۡۚ فَنِعۡمَ عُقۡبَى ٱلدَّارِ﴾ (Quran 13:23-24)

**Their reward**: gardens of perpetual residence, which they enter **"with whoever were righteous among their fathers, their spouses and their descendants"**. The angels enter upon them from every gate: **"Peace be upon you for what you patiently endured. And excellent is the final home."**

## Verses 25–29: Hearts Find Rest
﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَنقُضُونَ عَهۡدَ ٱللَّهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مِيثَٰقِهِۦ وَيَقۡطَعُونَ مَآ أَمَرَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦٓ أَن يُوصَلَ وَيُفۡسِدُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمُ ٱللَّعۡنَةُ وَلَهُمۡ سُوٓءُ ٱلدَّارِ﴾ (Quran 13:25)

Those who break the covenant, sever ties and spread corruption: **"for them is the curse, and they will have the worst home"**.

﴿ٱللَّهُ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقۡدِرُۚ وَفَرِحُواْ بِٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ إِلَّا مَتَٰعࣱ﴾ (Quran 13:26)

**"Allah extends provision for whom He wills and restricts it."** And they rejoice in worldly life, **"but the worldly life compared to the hereafter is not but brief enjoyment"**.

﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ طُوبَىٰ لَهُمۡ وَحُسۡنُ مَـَٔابٖ﴾ (Quran 13:28-29)

**"Those who have believed and whose hearts are assured by the remembrance of Allah. Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest."** Not in wealth, fame or entertainment, but in remembering Allah: through the Quran, prayer, *dhikr* and du'a. **"Those who have believed and done righteous deeds, a good state is theirs and a good return."** *Tuba*: a good life, and many commentators said it is also a tree in Paradise.

## Verses 30–43: Messengers, Decree and the Mother of the Book
﴿وَلَوۡ أَنَّ قُرۡءَانٗا سُيِّرَتۡ بِهِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ أَوۡ قُطِّعَتۡ بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَوۡ كُلِّمَ بِهِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰۗ بَل لِّلَّهِ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ جَمِيعًاۗ أَفَلَمۡ يَاْيۡـَٔسِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَن لَّوۡ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ لَهَدَى ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗاۗ وَلَا يَزَالُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ تُصِيبُهُم بِمَا صَنَعُواْ قَارِعَةٌ أَوۡ تَحُلُّ قَرِيبٗا مِّن دَارِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَ وَعۡدُ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُخۡلِفُ ٱلۡمِيعَادَ﴾ (Quran 13:31)

**"If there were any Quran by which the mountains would be removed or the earth would be broken apart or the dead would be made to speak, it would be this Quran. But to Allah belongs the affair entirely."**

﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلٗا مِّن قَبۡلِكَ وَجَعَلۡنَا لَهُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا وَذُرِّيَّةٗۚ وَمَا كَانَ لِرَسُولٍ أَن يَأۡتِيَ بِـَٔايَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۗ لِكُلِّ أَجَلٖ كِتَابࣱ﴾ ﴿يَمۡحُواْ ٱللَّهُ مَا يَشَآءُ وَيُثۡبِتُۖ وَعِندَهُۥٓ أُمُّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ﴾ (Quran 13:38-39)

**"For every term is a decree. Allah eliminates what He wills or confirms, and with Him is the Mother of the Book."**

﴿وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَسۡتَ مُرۡسَلٗاۚ قُلۡ كَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ شَهِيدَۢا بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡ وَمَنۡ عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ﴾ (Quran 13:43)

**"Sufficient is Allah as Witness between me and you, and whoever has knowledge of the Scripture."**

## Word Meanings
- **'Amad**: pillars.
- **Qita' mutajawirat**: neighbouring plots.
- **Sinwan**: from the same root.
- **Al-Ukul**: fruit, taste.
- **Ghayd**: losing, shrinking.
- **Mu'aqqibat**: successive angels.
- **Al-Barq**: lightning.
- **Ar-Ra'd**: thunder.
- **Zabad**: foam.
- **Jufa'**: cast off, worthless.
- **Ulul-albab**: people of understanding.
- **Yadra'una**: repel.
- **Tatma'innu**: find rest, are assured.
- **Tuba**: a good state.
- **Umm al-Kitab**: the Mother of the Book.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Without pillars that you see"**: wonder in a few words.
2. **"Watered with one water"**: different results from the same cause.
3. **"Allah will not change... until they change"**: a law of life.
4. **"The thunder exalts Allah"**: nature's worship.
5. **The foam and what remains**: a parable for every age.
6. **"Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest"**: emphatic and universal.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Alif Lam Mim Ra*, the lam and meem stretched six counts and the ra' two.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *bi-ma'in wahid* (verse 4).
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *khawfan wa tama'an* (verse 12), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Idgham without ghunnah**: in *zabadan rabiya* (verse 17), the tanween merges into the ra'.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yadra'una* (verse 22), the dal sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Prostration**: at verse 15.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Yusuf before it ended with "In their stories there is certainly a lesson for those of understanding", and Ar-Ra'd describes those of understanding. Surah Ibrahim after it continues with the Book that brings people from darkness into light.

## Changing From Within
1. **Begin with your heart**: intentions and beliefs.
2. **Change one habit at a time.**
3. **Change your company.**
4. **Change your daily routine** around prayer.
5. **Change your speech.**
6. **Ask Allah** to help you change.

## Finding Rest in Remembrance

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is it called Surah Ar-Ra'd?
Because verse 13 says "the thunder exalts Allah with praise of Him".

### What does "Allah will not change a people's condition until they change themselves" mean?
Allah changes the state of a people when they first change what is within them, for better or worse.

### What is the parable of foam?
Truth is like water and pure metal that remain and benefit; falsehood is like foam that vanishes.

### What does "in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest" mean?
True peace of heart comes from remembering Allah, not from worldly things.

### What are the "successive angels"?
Angels who accompany each person in front and behind, guarding him by Allah's command.

### Where is the prostration in Surah Ar-Ra'd?
At verse 15.

### How many verses are in Surah Ar-Ra'd?
43 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Ar-Ra'd shows you a universe full of signs, from pillarless heavens to thunder that praises its Lord. It teaches that falsehood is foam that vanishes, that change begins within you, and that the peace you are looking for is not in what you collect but in remembering Allah. Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest.
