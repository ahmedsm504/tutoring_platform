**Surah Hud** (123 ayat) is full of the stories of earlier prophets and their peoples.

## A perfected book
- It opens with a book whose verses are perfected, then explained in detail, from One Wise and Aware.

## Nuh and his son
- Nuh built the ark under Allah's eyes while his people mocked him.
- When the flood came, he called his son to board. The son refused, saying he would take refuge on a mountain, and was drowned. Nuh was taught that his son was **not of his family** in faith.

## The prophets and their peoples
| Prophet | People | Key message |
|---|---|---|
| Hud | ʿAd | Seek forgiveness and Allah will increase your strength |
| Salih | Thamud | The she-camel as a sign |
| Ibrahim | Angels as guests | Good news of Ishaq and Yaʿqub |
| Lut | His people | Rescue before the punishment |
| Shuʿayb | Madyan | Full measure and honest weights |

## Stand firm
﴿فَٱسۡتَقِمۡ كَمَآ أُمِرۡتَ وَمَن تَابَ مَعَكَ وَلَا تَطۡغَوۡاْۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرࣱ﴾

## Good deeds erase bad ones
- *Establish prayer at the two ends of the day and in some hours of the night. Indeed good deeds remove bad deeds.*

## Lessons
- **Faith is not inherited**, even by a prophet's son.
- **Steadfastness** means staying on the path without excess.
