Surah Adh-Dhariyat contains the verse that answers the biggest question a human being can ask: why am I here? "And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me." Around that verse the surah builds a powerful case. It swears by the winds that scatter dust and carry clouds that what you are promised is true. It describes the righteous who slept only a little at night and sought forgiveness before dawn. It points to signs in the earth and in yourselves, and tells you your provision is decreed in the heaven. It recounts the honoured guests of Ibrahim and the destruction of nations who denied. And it gives the clearest command of all: "So flee to Allah."

This guide explains Surah Adh-Dhariyat section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Adh-Dhariyat
- **Number of verses**: 60.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 51, beginning in the twenty-sixth juz and ending in the twenty-seventh.
- **Name**: Adh-Dhariyat means "the scattering winds", from the first verse.
- **Main themes**: the truth of the promise, the qualities of the righteous, provision, the stories of earlier nations and the purpose of creation.

## Verses 1–6: The Oaths
﴿وَٱلذَّـٰرِيَٰتِ ذَرۡوٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡحَٰمِلَٰتِ وِقۡرٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡجَٰرِيَٰتِ يُسۡرٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡمُقَسِّمَٰتِ أَمۡرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَصَادِقࣱ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ لَوَٰقِعࣱ﴾ (Quran 51:1-6)

Allah swears by:
1. **The winds that scatter dust.**
2. **The clouds carrying a load** of water.
3. **The ships sailing with ease.**
4. **The angels who distribute matters** by Allah's command.

**The answer**: **"Indeed, what you are promised is true. And indeed, the recompense is to occur."**

## Verses 7–14: The Sky With Pathways
﴿وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡحُبُكِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّكُمۡ لَفِي قَوۡلٖ مُّخۡتَلِفٖ﴾ ﴿يُؤۡفَكُ عَنۡهُ مَنۡ أُفِكَ﴾ ﴿قُتِلَ ٱلۡخَرَّـٰصُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي غَمۡرَةٖ سَاهُونَ﴾ ﴿يَسۡـَٔلُونَ أَيَّانَ يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ هُمۡ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ يُفۡتَنُونَ﴾ ﴿ذُوقُواْ فِتۡنَتَكُمۡ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 51:7-14)

**"By the heaven containing pathways"** (*al-hubuk*): its well-made structure. **"Indeed, you are in differing speech"**: confused about the Prophet ﷺ and the Quran. **"Destroyed are the falsifiers"** (*al-kharrasun*): those who guess and speak without knowledge. **"They ask: When is the Day of Recompense?"** It is the Day they will be tormented in the Fire.

## Verses 15–19: The Righteous
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ﴾ ﴿ءَاخِذِينَ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَبۡلَ ذَٰلِكَ مُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿كَانُواْ قَلِيلٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ مَا يَهۡجَعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَبِٱلۡأَسۡحَارِ هُمۡ يَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَفِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ حَقࣱّ لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَٱلۡمَحۡرُومِ﴾ (Quran 51:15-19)

**"Indeed, the righteous will be among gardens and springs."** Why? **"They were before that doers of good."** Their qualities:
1. **"They used to sleep but little of the night."**
2. **"And in the hours before dawn they would ask forgiveness."** After a night of prayer, they still sought forgiveness, seeing their worship as falling short. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Our Lord descends every night to the lowest heaven when the last third of the night remains, and says: Who is calling upon Me, that I may answer him? Who is asking of Me, that I may give him? Who is seeking My forgiveness, that I may forgive him?"
3. **"And from their properties was given the right of the one who asked and the deprived."**

## Verses 20–23: Signs Around You and Within You
﴿وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ءَايَٰتࣱ لِّلۡمُوقِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَفِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ رِزۡقُكُمۡ وَمَا تُوعَدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَوَرَبِّ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِنَّهُۥ لَحَقࣱّ مِّثۡلَ مَآ أَنَّكُمۡ تَنطِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 51:20-23)

**"And on the earth are signs for the certain in faith, and in yourselves. Then will you not see?"** Your own body: your eyes, heart, mind, the way you were formed, is a sign.

**"And in the heaven is your provision and whatever you are promised."** Rain comes from the sky, and your provision is decreed there. **"Then by the Lord of the heaven and earth, indeed, it is truth, just as it is that you are speaking."** As certain as the fact that you are talking now. This verse brings deep peace about provision: it is written, so work for it lawfully and do not sell your religion for it.

## Verses 24–37: The Honoured Guests of Ibrahim
﴿هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ضَيۡفِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ ٱلۡمُكۡرَمِينَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ دَخَلُواْ عَلَيۡهِ فَقَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗاۖ قَالَ سَلَٰمࣱ قَوۡمࣱ مُّنكَرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَرَاغَ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ فَجَآءَ بِعِجۡلٖ سَمِينٖ﴾ ﴿فَقَرَّبَهُۥٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡ قَالَ أَلَا تَأۡكُلُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَوۡجَسَ مِنۡهُمۡ خِيفَةٗۖ قَالُواْ لَا تَخَفۡۖ وَبَشَّرُوهُ بِغُلَٰمٍ عَلِيمٖ﴾ ﴿فَأَقۡبَلَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتُهُۥ فِي صَرَّةٖ فَصَكَّتۡ وَجۡهَهَا وَقَالَتۡ عَجُوزٌ عَقِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ كَذَٰلِكِ قَالَ رَبُّكِۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ (Quran 51:24-30)

**"Has the story of the honoured guests of Ibrahim reached you?"** They came and said "Peace", he replied "Peace", and recognised them as strangers. **He went quietly to his family and brought a fat calf**, placed it near them and said: **"Will you not eat?"** When they did not eat, he felt fear. They said: **"Fear not"**, and gave him good tidings of a knowledgeable son.

**Lessons in hospitality from Ibrahim**:
1. **He greeted them warmly.**
2. **He went quietly**, without making a show.
3. **He brought the best**: a fat calf.
4. **He brought it near to them.**
5. **He invited them gently**: "Will you not eat?"

**His wife came forward** in amazement, struck her face and said: **"A barren old woman!"** They said: **"Thus has your Lord said; indeed, He is the Wise, the Knowing."**

﴿۞قَالَ فَمَا خَطۡبُكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّآ أُرۡسِلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمٖ مُّجۡرِمِينَ﴾ ﴿لِنُرۡسِلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حِجَارَةٗ مِّن طِينٖ﴾ ﴿مُّسَوَّمَةً عِندَ رَبِّكَ لِلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ﴾ ﴿فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مَن كَانَ فِيهَا مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿فَمَا وَجَدۡنَا فِيهَا غَيۡرَ بَيۡتٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَتَرَكۡنَا فِيهَآ ءَايَةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ يَخَافُونَ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلۡأَلِيمَ﴾ (Quran 51:31-37)

**Their mission**: to the people of Lut, **to send down upon them stones of clay, marked by your Lord for the transgressors**. **"And We found not within them other than a house of Muslims"**, the house of Lut. **"And We left therein a sign for those who fear the painful punishment."**

## Verses 38–46: Nations Destroyed
﴿وَفِي مُوسَىٰٓ إِذۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ ﴿فَتَوَلَّىٰ بِرُكۡنِهِۦ وَقَالَ سَٰحِرٌ أَوۡ مَجۡنُونࣱ﴾ ﴿فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُ وَجُنُودَهُۥ فَنَبَذۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ وَهُوَ مُلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَفِي عَادٍ إِذۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلرِّيحَ ٱلۡعَقِيمَ﴾ ﴿مَا تَذَرُ مِن شَيۡءٍ أَتَتۡ عَلَيۡهِ إِلَّا جَعَلَتۡهُ كَٱلرَّمِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَفِي ثَمُودَ إِذۡ قِيلَ لَهُمۡ تَمَتَّعُواْ حَتَّىٰ حِينٖ﴾ ﴿فَعَتَوۡاْ عَنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّهِمۡ فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلصَّـٰعِقَةُ وَهُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَمَا ٱسۡتَطَٰعُواْ مِن قِيَامٖ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُنتَصِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَقَوۡمَ نُوحٖ مِّن قَبۡلُۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 51:38-46)

- **Musa and Pharaoh**: Pharaoh turned away and was cast into the sea, **"while he was blameworthy"**.
- **'Ad**: **"We sent against them the barren wind"**, which left nothing it came upon except that it made it like disintegrated ruins.
- **Thamud**: told "Enjoy yourselves for a time", but they were insolent, so the thunderbolt seized them while they were looking on.
- **The people of Nuh** before them: they were a defiantly disobedient people.

## Verses 47–49: The Expanding Heaven and the Pairs
﴿وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بَنَيۡنَٰهَا بِأَيۡيْدٖ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ فَرَشۡنَٰهَا فَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡمَٰهِدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَمِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَا زَوۡجَيۡنِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 51:47-49)

**"And the heaven We constructed with strength, and indeed, We are its expander."** Allah built the heaven with power, and continues to expand it, or provides it with vast extent; many contemporary writers have linked this to the expansion of the universe, while the verse's core message is Allah's power. **"And the earth We have spread out, and excellent is the preparer."** **"And of all things We created two mates; perhaps you will remember."**

## Verses 50–51: So Flee to Allah
﴿فَفِرُّوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّي لَكُم مِّنۡهُ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَجۡعَلُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَۖ إِنِّي لَكُم مِّنۡهُ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ (Quran 51:50-51)

**"So flee to Allah."** Normally a person flees *from* what he fears. With Allah, you flee from Him *to* Him: from His punishment to His mercy, from disobedience to obedience, from heedlessness to remembrance. **"Indeed, I am to you from Him a clear warner. And do not make with Allah another deity."**

## Verses 52–55: Remind
﴿كَذَٰلِكَ مَآ أَتَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا قَالُواْ سَاحِرٌ أَوۡ مَجۡنُونٌ﴾ ﴿أَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِهِۦۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ طَاغُونَ﴾ ﴿فَتَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡ فَمَآ أَنتَ بِمَلُومٖ﴾ ﴿وَذَكِّرۡ فَإِنَّ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ تَنفَعُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 51:52-55)

Every messenger was called a magician or madman. **"Did they suggest it to one another? Rather, they are a transgressing people."** **"So leave them, for you are not to be blamed. And remind, for indeed, the reminder benefits the believers."**

## Verses 56–58: The Purpose of Creation
﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡتُ ٱلۡجِنَّ وَٱلۡإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُونِ﴾ ﴿مَآ أُرِيدُ مِنۡهُم مِّن رِّزۡقٖ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَن يُطۡعِمُونِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلۡقُوَّةِ ٱلۡمَتِينُ﴾ (Quran 51:56-58)

**"And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me."** This is the purpose of your existence. Worship (*'ibadah*) is not only prayer and fasting; scholars defined it as everything Allah loves and is pleased with, of words and deeds, outward and inward. Work, family, study and kindness can all become worship with the right intention.

**"I do not want from them any provision, nor do I want them to feed Me."** Allah is in no need of our worship; we are in need of it. **"Indeed, it is Allah who is the Continual Provider, the Firm Possessor of strength."**

## Verses 59–60: The Closing Warning
﴿فَإِنَّ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ ذَنُوبٗا مِّثۡلَ ذَنُوبِ أَصۡحَٰبِهِمۡ فَلَا يَسۡتَعۡجِلُونِ﴾ ﴿فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن يَوۡمِهِمُ ٱلَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 51:59-60)

The wrongdoers have a share of punishment like the share of their predecessors, **so let them not impatiently urge Me. Woe to those who have disbelieved from their Day which they are promised.**

## Word Meanings
- **Adh-Dhariyat**: the scattering winds.
- **Hamilat wiqra**: carrying a load.
- **Al-Hubuk**: pathways, well-made structure.
- **Al-Kharrasun**: falsifiers, those who guess.
- **Yahja'un**: they sleep.
- **Al-Ashar**: the hours before dawn.
- **Al-Mahrum**: the deprived.
- **Hanidh / samin**: roasted / fat.
- **Sarrah**: a cry.
- **Musawwamah**: marked.
- **Ar-Rih al-'aqim**: the barren wind.
- **Musi'un**: expanders.
- **Fafirru**: so flee.
- **Ar-Razzaq**: the Continual Provider.
- **Al-Matin**: the Firm.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Oaths by moving things**: winds, clouds, ships, angels: the universe in motion.
2. **"Just as you are speaking"**: certainty compared to the most obvious fact.
3. **Ibrahim's hospitality in a few strokes.**
4. **"The barren wind"**: a wind that brings no rain or life, only destruction.
5. **"So flee to Allah"**: three words that redirect a life.
6. **The purpose of creation in one verse.**

## Tajweed Notes
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *qalilan mina al-layl* (verse 17), the tanween merges into the meem.
- **Ghunnah**: in *innahu la-haqq* (verse 23), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *bi-'ijlin samin* (verse 26), the tanween is hidden before the sin.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verses 7 and 22).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *khalaqna* (verse 49), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Qaf before it ended with "remind with the Quran whoever fears My threat", and Adh-Dhariyat says "remind, for the reminder benefits the believers". Surah At-Tur after it opens with oaths and "Indeed, the punishment of your Lord will occur", continuing the theme.

## Living the Purpose of Creation
1. **Renew your intention** each morning: everything for Allah.
2. **Guard the obligatory acts** first.
3. **Add some night prayer**, even a little.
4. **Seek forgiveness before dawn.**
5. **Set aside a known right for the needy.**
6. **Turn work and family into worship** with intention.
7. **Flee to Allah** whenever you feel lost.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Be certain about the hereafter.**
2. **Imitate the righteous**: little sleep, much forgiveness, generous giving.
3. **Reflect on yourself**: you are a sign.
4. **Trust Allah with your provision.**
5. **Be hospitable like Ibrahim.**
6. **Learn from destroyed nations.**
7. **Remember your purpose**: worship.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them the story of Ibrahim's guests** and practise hospitality together.
- **Teach them "I did not create jinn and mankind except to worship Me"** and what worship includes.
- **Explain that provision is from Allah** with examples from daily life.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does adh-Dhariyat mean?
The scattering winds that blow dust and carry clouds.

### What were the qualities of the righteous in this surah?
They slept little at night, sought forgiveness before dawn, and gave a right of their wealth to the asker and the deprived.

### What does "in the heaven is your provision" mean?
Rain comes from the sky, and everyone's provision is decreed by Allah.

### What is the story of Ibrahim's honoured guests?
Angels came to Ibrahim; he served them a fat calf, they gave him good news of a son, and told him they were sent to destroy the people of Lut.

### What does "So flee to Allah" mean?
Flee from His punishment to His mercy, from sin to obedience and from heedlessness to remembrance.

### Why did Allah create jinn and mankind?
To worship Him, as verse 56 says.

### How many verses are in Surah Adh-Dhariyat?
60 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Adh-Dhariyat answers the question of purpose: you were created to worship Allah. It shows you the righteous who lived that purpose in the quiet hours before dawn, assures you that your provision is written in the heaven, and tells you what to do whenever you feel lost or afraid: flee to Allah.
