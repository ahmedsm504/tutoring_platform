**Surah Al-Kafirun** (6 ayat) is a declaration of **pure worship**. The Prophet ﷺ recited it with Al-Ikhlas in the two units before Fajr [Muslim].

﴿قُلۡ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿لَآ أَعۡبُدُ مَا تَعۡبُدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَآ أَنتُمۡ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعۡبُدُ﴾ ﴿وَلَآ أَنَا۠ عَابِدࣱ مَّا عَبَدتُّمۡ﴾ ﴿وَلَآ أَنتُمۡ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعۡبُدُ﴾ ﴿لَكُمۡ دِينُكُمۡ وَلِيَ دِينِ﴾

## The context
- Commentators reported that some leaders of Quraysh proposed a compromise: worship their gods for a year and they would worship Allah for a year. This surah was the answer.

## Why the repetition?
| Ayat | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| 2–3 | Now: I do not worship what you worship |
| 4–5 | Past and future: I never have and never will |
- **Repetition closes every door** to compromise in worship.

## "To you your religion"
- This is **not approval** of their religion, but a **clear separation** in worship.
- At the same time, Islam forbids compulsion and calls for justice with all people.

## Lessons
- **Tolerance in dealings, clarity in belief.**
