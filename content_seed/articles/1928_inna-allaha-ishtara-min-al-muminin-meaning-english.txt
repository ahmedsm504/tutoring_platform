﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱشۡتَرَىٰ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلَهُم بِأَنَّ لَهُمُ ٱلۡجَنَّةَۚ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَقۡتُلُونَ وَيُقۡتَلُونَۖ وَعۡدًا عَلَيۡهِ حَقّٗا فِي ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلِ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ وَمَنۡ أَوۡفَىٰ بِعَهۡدِهِۦ مِنَ ٱللَّهِۚ فَٱسۡتَبۡشِرُواْ بِبَيۡعِكُمُ ٱلَّذِي بَايَعۡتُم بِهِۦۚ وَذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:111]

## The transaction
| Element | In the verse |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Allah |
| Seller | The believer |
| Goods | Life and wealth |
| Price | Paradise |

## A gift disguised as a trade
- Our lives and wealth already belong to Allah, yet He calls it a **purchase** and pays Paradise. Pure generosity.

## A promise in three scriptures
- *A true promise in the Torah, the Gospel and the Quran*, with the question: *Who is more faithful to his covenant than Allah?*

## Rejoice
- *Rejoice in the deal you made*: the believer should feel happy, not burdened.

## The next verse
- It describes the sellers: those who repent, worship, praise, fast, bow, prostrate, enjoin good, forbid evil and keep Allah's limits.

## Lessons
- **Spend your life** in what pleases Allah.
- **No deal is better.**
