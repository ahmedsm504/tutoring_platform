**Surah Al-Anam** (Cattle, 165 ayat) is a Makkan surah devoted to tawhid.

## Praise opens it
- *All praise is for Allah, who created the heavens and the earth and made darkness and light.*

## The keys of the unseen
﴿۞وَعِندَهُۥ مَفَاتِحُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ لَا يَعۡلَمُهَآ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۚ وَمَا تَسۡقُطُ مِن وَرَقَةٍ إِلَّا يَعۡلَمُهَا وَلَا حَبَّةٖ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا رَطۡبٖ وَلَا يَابِسٍ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾

## Ibrahim's argument
- Ibrahim pointed to a star, then the moon, then the sun, showing his people that whatever **sets** cannot be a god.
- He concluded: *I have turned my face toward He who created the heavens and the earth, inclining to truth.*

## The cattle and false rules
- The surah refutes rules the polytheists invented about crops and cattle, assigning parts to idols.

## The commandments of the straight path
| Command |
|---|
| Do not associate anything with Allah |
| Be good to parents |
| Do not kill your children out of poverty |
| Do not approach immoralities, open or hidden |
| Do not take a life Allah made sacred |
| Do not approach the orphan's wealth except to improve it |
| Give full measure and weight |
| Be just in speech, even with relatives |
| Fulfil the covenant of Allah |
| Follow this straight path and not other paths |

## A whole life for Allah
﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحۡيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾

## Lessons
- **Allah alone knows the unseen**: fortune-telling has no place.
- **Dedicate all of life**, not just prayer, to Allah.
