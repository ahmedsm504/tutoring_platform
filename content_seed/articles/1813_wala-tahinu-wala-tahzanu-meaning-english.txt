﴿وَلَا تَهِنُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَوۡنَ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:139]

## The context
- After Uhud, the Muslims were wounded and grieving the loss of many Companions. This verse came to **lift them up**.

## Two prohibitions
| Prohibition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Do not weaken* | Do not lose resolve or give up |
| *Do not grieve* | Do not let sorrow over the past paralyse you |

## The source of superiority
- *You are the superior*, with a condition: *if you are believers*. Elevation is tied to faith, not numbers or wealth.

## The following verse
- *If a wound has touched you, a similar wound has touched the other side. And these days We alternate among people.* Victory and loss rotate; faith is what lasts.

## For personal setbacks
- A failed exam, a lost opportunity, or criticism: the verse speaks to every believer who falls and must rise.

## Lessons
- **Get up after falling.**
- **Measure yourself by faith**, not by others' opinions.
