﴿ذَٰلِكَۖ وَمَن يُعَظِّمۡ شَعَـٰٓئِرَ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّهَا مِن تَقۡوَى ٱلۡقُلُوبِ﴾ [Al-Hajj 22:32]

## What are *shaʿaʾir*?
- Signs and rituals that Allah made marks of His religion.
| Symbol | Example |
|---|---|
| Hajj rites | Safa and Marwah, the sacrificial animals |
| Places | The Kaʿbah, the mosques |
| Acts | Prayer, the adhan, fasting |
| The Quran | Its recitation and the Mushaf |

## Honouring them
- In the context, scholars mention choosing good, healthy sacrificial animals. More broadly: respect, care and doing them well.

## "From the piety of hearts"
- Honour for the symbols **comes from** the heart. Outward carelessness often shows inner weakness.

## Taqwa is in the heart
- The Prophet ﷺ pointed to his chest and said: *Taqwa is here* (narrated by Muslim).

## Lessons
- **Treat the Mushaf and the mosque with respect.**
- **Do acts of worship beautifully.**
