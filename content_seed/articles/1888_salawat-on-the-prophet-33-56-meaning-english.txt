﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّۚ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ صَلُّواْ عَلَيۡهِ وَسَلِّمُواْ تَسۡلِيمًا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:56]

## Meaning of *salah* here
| From | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Allah | His praise of the Prophet ﷺ among the highest angels, as Abu al-ʿAliyah explained (reported by al-Bukhari) |
| The angels | Their duʿa for him |
| The believers | Asking Allah to bless him |

## The command
- After telling us what Allah and the angels do, the verse commands believers: **send blessings and greet with peace**.

## How to send blessings
- The Companions asked how, and the Prophet ﷺ taught them the Ibrahimi salawat recited in prayer: *Allahumma salli ʿala Muhammad wa ʿala ali Muhammad...* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## The reward
- *Whoever sends one blessing upon me, Allah sends ten blessings upon him* (narrated by Muslim).

## Best times
- On Friday, after the adhan, when his name is mentioned, and in duʿa.

## Lessons
- **Make salawat a daily habit.**
- **Never hear his name** without sending blessings.
