Surah Al-Baqarah is the longest surah in the Quran, and for many people it is the surah they dream of memorizing and the one that intimidates them most. It is two and a half juz' long, full of stories, laws and long verses, and it contains some of the most beloved passages in the Quran: Ayat al-Kursi and the closing verses of the surah.

This guide gives you a complete, realistic plan to memorize it in around **100 days** at half a page a day, with a revision system designed so that by the time you reach the end, you haven't lost the beginning.

## The Virtue of Surah Al-Baqarah

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Recite Surah Al-Baqarah, for taking it is a blessing, leaving it is a cause of regret, and the sorcerers cannot withstand it" (Muslim). He also said: "Do not make your houses into graves. Indeed, Shaytan flees from the house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited" (Muslim).

He ﷺ called Al-Baqarah and Al 'Imran "the two bright ones" (az-zahrawayn), and told us they will come on the Day of Resurrection like two clouds or shades, pleading for their companions (Muslim).

And about its final verses he ﷺ said: "Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah at night, they will suffice him" (al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## The Surah in Numbers

Before planning, know what you are planning for:
- **Verses**: 286.
- **Pages** in the standard 604-page Madinah Mushaf: from page 2 to page 49, which is **48 pages**.
- **Juz'**: all of Juz' 1 and 2, and the beginning of Juz' 3 (up to verse 252).
- **Longest verse in the Quran**: verse 282 (the verse of debt), which fills a whole page.

At half a page a day: 48 pages × 2 = **96 days of new memorization**, which is where the "100 days" comes from. Because the plan also has one consolidation day each week, the calendar time is about 16 weeks. If you use a different Mushaf (for example a 13-line or 16-line Indo-Pak Mushaf), the page count changes, so simply divide the surah into equal daily portions in your own Mushaf.

## Understand Before You Memorize: The Theme Map

Adults memorize much better when they know where they are in the story of the surah. Learn this map before you start:

| Verses | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–20 | Three types of people: believers, disbelievers, and hypocrites and their parables |
| 21–29 | A call to all people to worship Allah, and the challenge of the Quran |
| 30–39 | The creation of Adam and the prostration of the angels |
| 40–123 | Addressing the Children of Israel, including the story of the cow (67–73) |
| 124–141 | Ibrahim, building the Ka'bah, and his advice to his sons |
| 142–152 | The change of the qiblah |
| 153–157 | Patience and trials |
| 158–176 | Safa and Marwah, tawhid, and lawful food |
| 177 | The verse of righteousness (al-birr) |
| 178–182 | Retribution (qisas) and bequests |
| 183–187 | Rulings of fasting |
| 188–203 | Wealth, the new moons, fighting, and Hajj |
| 204–220 | Types of people, entering Islam completely, and the Companions' questions |
| 221–242 | Family law: marriage, divorce, waiting periods, nursing |
| 243–252 | The story of Talut and Jalut |
| 253–260 | The messengers, Ayat al-Kursi, no compulsion in religion, Ibrahim's debate |
| 261–274 | Spending in Allah's cause and its parables |
| 275–281 | The prohibition of riba |
| 282–283 | The verse of debt and collateral |
| 284–286 | The closing verses |

Read a short tafsir of each section before you reach it. Knowing that verses 142–152 are about the change of qiblah, for example, turns them from a block of text into a scene you can follow.

## The Daily Method: Half a Page, the Same Way Every Day

### Before memorizing (5 minutes)
1. Read the meaning of today's half page in a reliable translation or short tafsir.
2. Listen to it two or three times from one consistent, measured reciter.
3. Read it once from your Mushaf carefully, paying attention to Tajweed.

### Memorizing (15–25 minutes)
1. Take the first verse (or the first sentence of a long verse). Read it 5–10 times looking at the page.
2. Recite it 5 times without looking.
3. Move to the next verse and repeat.
4. Link: recite the first and second verses together, then add the third, and so on.
5. Finish by reciting the whole half page from memory 3–5 times.

### After memorizing
- Recite today's portion in one of your prayers.
- Before sleep, recite it once more and read tomorrow's portion once.

Use **one physical Mushaf** throughout. Your visual memory of where each verse sits on the page is a powerful aid, and changing Mushafs scrambles it.

## The Revision System: How Not to Forget the Beginning

The biggest danger in memorizing Al-Baqarah is reaching page 40 and discovering that pages 2–10 have faded. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Keep refreshing your knowledge of the Quran, for by the One in whose hand is my soul, it escapes more readily than camels from their tethers" (al-Bukhari and Muslim). So revision is built in from day one, in three layers.

### Layer 1: Recent (daily)
Every day, recite the **last 5 pages** you memorized. These are still fragile and need daily attention.

### Layer 2: Older (rotating daily)
Everything before those 5 pages is divided into groups (A, B, C…), and you revise one group each day so you pass over the whole memorized portion every week or so. As your memorization grows, the groups get bigger.

### Layer 3: Weekly consolidation
One day a week, no new memorization. Recite the whole week's new portion (three pages) in one go, ideally to a teacher or a friend.

## The Weekly Schedule

| Day | New memorization | Recent revision | Older revision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saturday | Half a page | Last 5 pages | Group A |
| Sunday | Half a page | Last 5 pages | Group B |
| Monday | Half a page | Last 5 pages | Group C |
| Tuesday | Half a page | Last 5 pages | Group D |
| Wednesday | Half a page | Last 5 pages | Group E |
| Thursday | Half a page | Last 5 pages | Group F |
| Friday | **Consolidation**: the whole week's portion | — | Surah Al-Kahf |

At six half-pages a week, that is three pages a week, so 48 pages take about 16 weeks. With careful consistency some people finish faster; others prefer to go more slowly. Either way, protect the revision.

## Milestones: Where You Will Be Each Month

| End of | Approximate position (at 3 pages a week) |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | Around verse 93 (pages 2–14): the hypocrites, Adam, and much of the address to the Children of Israel |
| Month 2 | Around verse 181 (to page 27): Ibrahim and the Ka'bah, the change of qiblah, and the verse of righteousness |
| Month 3 | Around verse 248 (to page 40): fasting, Hajj, family law, and the beginning of the story of Talut |
| Final 3–4 weeks | Talut and Jalut, Ayat al-Kursi, spending, riba, the verse of debt and the closing verses |

These are approximate. If you pause for a busy week, simply shift the table. The plan serves you, not the other way round.

## The Hardest Passages and How to Secure Them

### 1. The address to the Children of Israel (40–123)
Many verses begin "And [remember] when…" (wa idh). Make a list of each "wa idh" verse with a one-word summary: the sea, the calf, the cloud, the cow, the rock. Recite the list before revising the section.

### 2. The story of the cow (67–73)
Follow the dialogue: Musa's command, their questions about her age, her colour, and her description, then the slaughter. Memorizing it as a conversation keeps the order clear.

### 3. The law verses (178–242)
These are dense with rulings. Write the topic of each verse on a small card (qisas, bequest, fasting, i'tikaf, Hajj, marriage, divorce, nursing…) and revise the order of topics separately.

### 4. The verse of debt (282)
The longest verse in the Quran. Divide it into 5 or 6 sentences, memorize each sentence on a separate day, and only then join them.

### 5. Similar phrasing (mutashabihat)
Al-Baqarah has many phrases that resemble other surahs, for example the story of entering the town, which also appears in Surah Al-A'raf with different wording:

﴿وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا ٱدۡخُلُواْ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةَ فَكُلُواْ مِنۡهَا حَيۡثُ شِئۡتُمۡ رَغَدٗا وَٱدۡخُلُواْ ٱلۡبَابَ سُجَّدٗا وَقُولُواْ حِطَّةࣱ نَّغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ خَطَٰيَٰكُمۡۚ وَسَنَزِيدُ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:58)

Keep a small notebook of similar passages and note the difference in each place.

## Memorize Ayat al-Kursi and the Closing Verses Early

You don't need to wait 90 days to benefit from the surah's greatest passages. Many people memorize Ayat al-Kursi and the last two verses **in the first week**, alongside their daily portion, because they use them every day in their adhkar:

﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُۚ لَا تَأۡخُذُهُۥ سِنَةࣱ وَلَا نَوۡمࣱۚ لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يَشۡفَعُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِهِۦۚ يَعۡلَمُ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيۡءٖ مِّنۡ عِلۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَۚ وَسِعَ كُرۡسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۖ وَلَا يَـُٔودُهُۥ حِفۡظُهُمَاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ (Quran 2:255)

﴿ءَامَنَ ٱلرَّسُولُ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِ مِن رَّبِّهِۦ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَۚ كُلٌّ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتِهِۦ وَكُتُبِهِۦ وَرُسُلِهِۦ لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيۡنَ أَحَدٖ مِّن رُّسُلِهِۦۚ وَقَالُواْ سَمِعۡنَا وَأَطَعۡنَاۖ غُفۡرَانَكَ رَبَّنَا وَإِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ ﴿لَا يُكَلِّفُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتۡ وَعَلَيۡهَا مَا ٱكۡتَسَبَتۡۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذۡنَآ إِن نَّسِينَآ أَوۡ أَخۡطَأۡنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحۡمِلۡ عَلَيۡنَآ إِصۡرٗا كَمَا حَمَلۡتَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلۡنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِۦۖ وَٱعۡفُ عَنَّا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَآۚ أَنتَ مَوۡلَىٰنَا فَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:285-286)

When you reach these verses in the plan, they will already be secure, which gives you a boost at the end.

## Tips for Reaching the End

- **Never skip revision to catch up on new memorization.** If you fall behind, slow down the new, not the old.
- **Recite in prayer.** Every rak'ah of sunnah prayer is a revision opportunity.
- **Recite to someone weekly.** A teacher, a spouse, a friend. Knowing you will be heard keeps you honest.
- **Keep a log.** Date, page memorized, revision done. A chain of completed days is motivating.
- **Make du'a.** Ask Allah to make it easy and to make the surah a proof for you, not against you.

## After Completing Al-Baqarah

- Spend one to two weeks on revision only, reciting the surah in 5 or 6 sittings.
- Aim to recite the whole surah from memory once a week for the first two months, then fold it into your regular revision cycle.
- Consider continuing to Al 'Imran, its companion surah.

## Using a Teacher Weekly

A teacher makes the biggest difference in three areas: correcting recitation before mistakes become fixed, testing you from random points to reveal weak spots, and adjusting your pace. One or two 30-minute online lessons a week are enough for most adults following this plan.

## Adapting the Plan

- **For working professionals**: memorize after Fajr; revise on the commute and in prayers.
- **For students**: pause new memorization in exam weeks, keep revision.
- **For mothers of young children**: a quarter page a day is still excellent; the plan becomes 200 days, and that is fine.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is half a page a day too much for a beginner?
If you are new to memorization, start with a quarter page a day for the first two weeks, then increase. The plan works at any pace as long as revision is protected.

### Should I memorize Juz' Amma before Al-Baqarah?
Many people find it easier to start with Juz' Amma because the surahs are short and give quick wins, but there is no requirement. If your heart is set on Al-Baqarah, start with it, and memorize Ayat al-Kursi and the last two verses first.

### What if I miss days?
Return to the plan without trying to make up the missed portions in one day. Shift the schedule forward. Consistency beats intensity.

### How long does it take to revise the whole surah once it's memorized?
A confident memorizer can usually recite it in roughly one to two hours, depending on pace. Spreading it over several sittings in the week is easier.

### Can I memorize from an app instead of a printed Mushaf?
You can, but choose one layout and never change it, so your visual memory stays consistent. Many people find a printed Mushaf easier to anchor.

### Is it better to memorize in the morning or evening?
For most people, new memorization is best in the early morning, and revision fits well in the evening and in prayers.
