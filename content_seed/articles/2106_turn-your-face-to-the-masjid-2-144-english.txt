﴿قَدۡ نَرَىٰ تَقَلُّبَ وَجۡهِكَ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِۖ فَلَنُوَلِّيَنَّكَ قِبۡلَةٗ تَرۡضَىٰهَاۚ فَوَلِّ وَجۡهَكَ شَطۡرَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۚ وَحَيۡثُ مَا كُنتُمۡ فَوَلُّواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ شَطۡرَهُۥۗ وَإِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ لَيَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:144]

## The story
- After the Hijrah, the Prophet ﷺ prayed toward Jerusalem for sixteen or seventeen months and wished to face the Kaʿbah (narrated by al-Bukhari from al-Baraʾ).

## Waiting with manners
- He looked up toward the sky awaiting revelation, without asking aloud.

## "A qiblah that will please you"
- An honour: Allah gave him what he loved.

## *Shatr*
- Direction. Whoever is far faces the direction of the Kaʿbah; whoever sees it faces it directly.

## A test of obedience
- The change revealed who follows the Messenger and who turns back (2:143).

## Lessons
- **Allah sees your need** before you speak.
- **Obeying when commands change** is a sign of sincerity.
