﴿وَءَاتَىٰكُم مِّن كُلِّ مَا سَأَلۡتُمُوهُۚ وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَظَلُومࣱ كَفَّارࣱ﴾ [Ibrahim 14:34]
﴿وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:18]

## One sentence, two endings
| Surah | Ending | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Ibrahim | *Man is unjust and ungrateful* | Human failure to thank |
| An-Nahl | *Allah is Forgiving, Merciful* | Allah's response to that failure |

- Scholars note the beauty: the first ending describes **the receiver**, the second describes **the Giver**.

## "Blessing" in the singular
- In Surah Ibrahim the word is written *niʿmat* with an open taʾ, and in An-Nahl *niʿmah*; in both it is singular, yet you cannot count it: even **one** blessing, like sight, contains countless blessings within it.

## Practising gratitude
- Name three blessings daily, use them in obedience, and say *alhamdulillah* often.

## Lessons
- **Admit you can never thank enough.**
- **Rely on His forgiveness** for your shortfall.
