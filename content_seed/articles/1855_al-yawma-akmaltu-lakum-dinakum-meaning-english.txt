﴿ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أَكۡمَلۡتُ لَكُمۡ دِينَكُمۡ وَأَتۡمَمۡتُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ نِعۡمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمَ دِينٗاۚ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:3]

## When was it revealed?
- ʿUmar ibn al-Khattab said it was revealed on **Friday, at ʿArafah**, during the Farewell Hajj (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## A Jewish man's remark
- A man of the Jews told ʿUmar that if this verse had been revealed to them, they would have taken that day as a festival. ʿUmar replied that he knew the day and place of its revelation (same narration).

## Three statements
| Statement | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *I have perfected your religion* | Its beliefs and laws are complete |
| *I have completed My favour* | Guidance, victory and the Haram secured |
| *I have approved Islam as your religion* | Allah is pleased with it as the way of life |

## No additions needed
- Since the religion is complete, **inventing new acts of worship** is not needed. The Prophet ﷺ warned against innovation in religion.

## Within a verse on food
- It appears in the middle of rulings on forbidden foods, then the verse continues with the permission for one forced by hunger. Even the completion of the religion comes with mercy.

## Lessons
- **Be grateful** for a complete guidance.
- **Follow, do not invent.**
