The Prophet ﷺ said the Quran escapes the memory faster than camels escape their ropes [Al-Bukhari and Muslim]. **Spaced repetition** is a simple way to fight that.

## The idea
- After you memorize something, you start to forget it.
- If you **review it just before you forget**, it lasts longer each time.
- So the gaps between reviews **grow**: one day, three days, a week, a month.

## A simple schedule
| Review | When |
|---|---|
| 1 | Same day, a few hours later |
| 2 | Next day |
| 3 | After 3 days |
| 4 | After 7 days |
| 5 | After 30 days |
| Then | Monthly in your regular revision |

## Tracking it
- **A notebook**: write the page number and the next review date.
- **A spreadsheet or flashcard app**: let it remind you.

## Combining with the classic method
| Classic part | Spaced repetition role |
|---|---|
| New lesson | Day 0 |
| Recent revision (last week) | Reviews 2–4 |
| Old revision (the rest) | Monthly cycle |

## Tips
- **Weak pages** get shorter gaps.
- **Strong pages** can have longer gaps, but never stop revising them.
