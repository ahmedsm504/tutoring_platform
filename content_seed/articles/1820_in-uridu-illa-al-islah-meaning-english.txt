﴿قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن كُنتُ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّي وَرَزَقَنِي مِنۡهُ رِزۡقًا حَسَنٗاۚ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَنۡ أُخَالِفَكُمۡ إِلَىٰ مَآ أَنۡهَىٰكُمۡ عَنۡهُۚ إِنۡ أُرِيدُ إِلَّا ٱلۡإِصۡلَٰحَ مَا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُۚ وَمَا تَوۡفِيقِيٓ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُ وَإِلَيۡهِ أُنِيبُ﴾ [Hud 11:88]

## The context
- Shuʿayb told his people to give full measure and stop cheating. They mocked him: *Does your prayer command you to leave what our fathers worshipped?*

## Shuʿayb's principles
| Principle | Words in the verse |
|---|---|
| Clear evidence | *I am upon clear proof from my Lord* |
| Lawful income | *He provided me good provision* |
| Consistency | *I do not intend to do what I forbid you* |
| Sincere aim | *I only want reform* |
| Realism | *As much as I am able* |
| Humility | *My success is only through Allah* |
| Reliance | *Upon Him I rely, and to Him I return* |

## Practising what you preach
- A caller is most convincing when his actions match his words.

## "My success is only through Allah"
- Even with effort and sincerity, *tawfiq* is Allah's gift. Many speakers and writers open their work with this sentence.

## Lessons
- **Reform starts with you.**
- **Do your best and leave the result to Allah.**
