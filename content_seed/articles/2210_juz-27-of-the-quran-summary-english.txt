Juz 27 begins with ﴿۞قَالَ فَمَا خَطۡبُكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ﴾, "He said: then what is your business, O messengers?", so it is called **Juz Qala Fama Khatbukum**, and also known as **Juz Adh-Dhariyat**. It runs from **Adh-Dhariyat 51:31 to the end of Al-Hadid (57:29)**, with five complete surahs in between: **At-Tur**, **An-Najm**, **Al-Qamar**, **Ar-Rahman** and **Al-Waqi'ah**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 522 to 541**.

This is a juz from the *Mufassal*, often recited by people: it contains Ar-Rahman with its timeless refrain, Al-Waqi'ah which many memorize, and Al-Hadid with its calls to the believers. Its surahs mostly have short verses, a strong rhythm and a focus on the Hereafter.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **the purpose of creation, Allah's favours, and preparing for the Event**. Adh-Dhariyat declares the purpose: ﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡتُ ٱلۡجِنَّ وَٱلۡإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُونِ﴾, "I did not create jinn and mankind except to worship Me." Ar-Rahman lists favours and asks: ﴿فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ﴾, "So which of your Lord's favours will you both deny?" Al-Waqi'ah divides people into three groups, and Al-Hadid calls: ﴿أَلَمۡ يَأۡنِ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَن تَخۡشَعَ قُلُوبُهُمۡ لِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ﴾, "Has the time not come for the believers that their hearts be humbled at the remembrance of Allah?"

The message: **you were created to worship, Allah's favours surround you, the Event is coming, and the time has come for your heart to be humbled.**

## Part one: the rest of Surah Adh-Dhariyat (31-60)

### 1. Lut's people and the fates of nations (31-46)
"He said: then what is your business, O messengers?" They said: we have been sent to a criminal people, to send upon them stones of clay. We found there only one household of Muslims. Then signs in Musa and Pharaoh, 'Ad and the barren wind, Thamud, and Nuh's people.

### 2. Fleeing to Allah and the purpose of creation (47-60)
﴿وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بَنَيۡنَٰهَا بِأَيۡيْدٖ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ﴾, "We built the heaven with strength, and We are expanding it", ﴿وَمِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَا زَوۡجَيۡنِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾, "and of everything We created pairs, so you may remember." Then the command: ﴿فَفِرُّوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّي لَكُم مِّنۡهُ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾, "So flee to Allah; I am a clear warner to you from Him." Then: ﴿وَذَكِّرۡ فَإِنَّ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ تَنفَعُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "And remind, for the reminder benefits the believers."

Then the comprehensive verse: ﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡتُ ٱلۡجِنَّ وَٱلۡإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُونِ﴾ ﴿مَآ أُرِيدُ مِنۡهُم مِّن رِّزۡقٖ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَن يُطۡعِمُونِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلۡقُوَّةِ ٱلۡمَتِينُ﴾, "I did not create jinn and mankind except to worship Me. I want no provision from them, nor do I want them to feed Me. Allah is the Provider, the Possessor of strength, the Firm."

**Reflect on "flee to Allah"**: every fear drives you away from what you fear, except fear of Allah, which drives you towards Him. Read [Quran 51:56 Explained: The Purpose of Human Life](/blog/wama-khalaqtu-al-jinna-wal-ins-meaning-english/).

## Part two: Surah At-Tur (1-49)

At-Tur is Makki, with 49 verses. In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Jubayr ibn Mut'im said: I heard the Prophet ﷺ recite At-Tur in Maghrib, and when he reached ﴿أَمۡ خُلِقُواْ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ شَيۡءٍ أَمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ خَلَقُواْ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۚ بَل لَّا يُوقِنُونَ﴾, "Or were they created from nothing, or are they the creators? Or did they create the heavens and earth? Rather, they are not certain", my heart nearly flew. For the whole surah see [Surah At-Tur Meaning in English](/blog/surah-at-tur-meaning-english/).

### 1. The oath and the punishment that will come (1-16)
﴿وَٱلطُّورِ﴾, "By the Mount", by an inscribed Book, the much-visited House, the raised canopy and the kindled sea: your Lord's punishment will occur.

### 2. The bliss of the God-conscious and joining offspring (17-28)
The God-conscious will be in gardens and bliss. Then a merciful promise: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱتَّبَعَتۡهُمۡ ذُرِّيَّتُهُم بِإِيمَٰنٍ أَلۡحَقۡنَا بِهِمۡ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ وَمَآ أَلَتۡنَٰهُم مِّنۡ عَمَلِهِم مِّن شَيۡءٖۚ﴾, "Those who believed and whose offspring followed them in faith, We will join their offspring with them, and We will not deprive them of anything of their deeds." Then a new quarter: ﴿۞وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ غِلۡمَانࣱ لَّهُمۡ كَأَنَّهُمۡ لُؤۡلُؤࣱ مَّكۡنُونࣱ﴾, "Young servants will circulate among them, as if they were hidden pearls." Then the conversation of the people of Paradise: ﴿قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا كُنَّا قَبۡلُ فِيٓ أَهۡلِنَا مُشۡفِقِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كُنَّا مِن قَبۡلُ نَدۡعُوهُۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡبَرُّ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾, "They will say: we used to be fearful among our families before. We used to call on Him before; He is the Most Kind, the Merciful."

**Lesson**: fearing Allah in this world among one's family brings safety in the next. And from Allah's mercy, believing offspring are joined with their parents even if their deeds fall short.

### 3. Compelling questions (29-49)
A series of "or" questions corners the denier: or do they say he is a poet? Or that he made it up? Then the two rational questions: were they created from nothing, or are they the creators? Did they create the heavens and earth? The close: ﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ فَإِنَّكَ بِأَعۡيُنِنَاۖ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ﴾ ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡهُ وَإِدۡبَٰرَ ٱلنُّجُومِ﴾, "Be patient for your Lord's decision, for you are in Our eyes, and glorify your Lord with praise when you rise, and glorify Him in part of the night and at the setting of the stars."

**Reflect on "you are in Our eyes"**: the greatest comfort for the afflicted is knowing he is under Allah's care.

## Part three: Surah An-Najm (1-62)

An-Najm is Makki, with 62 verses. In Sahih Al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ recited An-Najm in Makkah and prostrated, and the Muslims and idolaters with him prostrated. For the whole surah see [Surah An-Najm Meaning in English](/blog/surah-an-najm-meaning-english/).

### 1. The truth of revelation (1-18)
﴿وَٱلنَّجۡمِ إِذَا هَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿مَا ضَلَّ صَاحِبُكُمۡ وَمَا غَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحۡيࣱ يُوحَىٰ﴾, "By the star when it descends, your companion has not strayed, nor has he erred, nor does he speak from desire. It is only revelation revealed." Then the sighting of Jibril: one mighty in power taught him, ﴿فَكَانَ قَابَ قَوۡسَيۡنِ أَوۡ أَدۡنَىٰ﴾, "and he was two bow-lengths away or nearer." Then the Ascension: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ رَءَاهُ نَزۡلَةً أُخۡرَىٰ﴾ ﴿عِندَ سِدۡرَةِ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ﴾, "He saw him in another descent, at the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary", near which is the Garden of Refuge. The sight did not swerve nor transgress, ﴿لَقَدۡ رَأَىٰ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّهِ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰٓ﴾, "he certainly saw some of the greatest signs of his Lord."

**Lesson**: "nor does he speak from desire": the Sunnah is revelation to be followed as the Quran is followed.

### 2. The falsehood of idols and intercession (19-32)
Have you considered Al-Lat, Al-'Uzza and Manat, the third? Is the male for you and the female for Him? ﴿تِلۡكَ إِذٗا قِسۡمَةࣱ ضِيزَىٰٓ﴾, "That then is an unjust division." Then a new quarter: ﴿۞وَكَم مِّن مَّلَكٖ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ لَا تُغۡنِي شَفَٰعَتُهُمۡ شَيۡـًٔا إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ أَن يَأۡذَنَ ٱللَّهُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَرۡضَىٰٓ﴾, "How many angels are in the heavens whose intercession avails nothing, except after Allah permits for whom He wills and approves." Then the good-doers: ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَجۡتَنِبُونَ كَبَـٰٓئِرَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ إِلَّا ٱللَّمَمَۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ وَٰسِعُ ٱلۡمَغۡفِرَةِۚ﴾, "who avoid major sins and indecencies, except minor slips; your Lord is vast in forgiveness", then: do not claim purity for yourselves; He knows best who is God-conscious.

### 3. The laws of recompense (33-62)
What is in the scriptures of Musa and of Ibrahim, who fulfilled:
- ﴿أَلَّا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةࣱ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰ﴾, "that no bearer of burdens bears another's burden",
- ﴿وَأَن لَّيۡسَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ﴾, "that man has only what he strives for",
- ﴿وَأَنَّ سَعۡيَهُۥ سَوۡفَ يُرَىٰ﴾, "that his effort will be seen", then he will be repaid in full,
- ﴿وَأَنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ﴾, "and that to your Lord is the final goal."

Then: He makes laugh and weep, causes death and gives life, and created the pairs. The close: the approaching Event has drawn near; do you marvel at this discourse, laugh and not weep? ﴿فَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ﴾, "So prostrate to Allah and worship": the place of prostration.

**Lesson**: your effort is your balance; it will be shown to you and repaid in full.

## Part four: Surah Al-Qamar (1-55)

Al-Qamar is Makki, with 55 verses. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ used to recite in the two 'Eid prayers ﴿قٓۚ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡمَجِيدِ﴾ and ﴿ٱقۡتَرَبَتِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ وَٱنشَقَّ ٱلۡقَمَرُ﴾. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Qamar Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-qamar-meaning-english/).

### 1. The splitting of the moon (1-8)
﴿ٱقۡتَرَبَتِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ وَٱنشَقَّ ٱلۡقَمَرُ﴾, "The Hour has drawn near and the moon has split." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud reported that the moon split into two parts in the Prophet's time ﷺ, and he said: "Bear witness." Yet if they see a sign, they turn away and say: continuous magic.

### 2. Five stories with one refrain (9-42)
A new quarter: ﴿۞كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ فَكَذَّبُواْ عَبۡدَنَا وَقَالُواْ مَجۡنُونࣱ وَٱزۡدُجِرَ﴾, "The people of Nuh denied before them; they denied Our servant, called him mad, and he was repelled." Then 'Ad, Thamud, Lut's people and Pharaoh's people. After each story come two refrains:
- "How then were My punishment and warnings?"
- ﴿وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ﴾, "We have made the Quran easy to remember; is there anyone who will remember?", repeated four times (17, 22, 32, 40).

**Reflect**: between stories of punishment comes the reminder that the Quran has been made easy. Salvation from their fate lies in taking heed through the Quran, which Allah has made easy for anyone who wants it.

### 3. Decree and the close (43-55)
Are your disbelievers better than those? Then: ﴿إِنَّا كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَٰهُ بِقَدَرٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَمۡرُنَآ إِلَّا وَٰحِدَةࣱ كَلَمۡحِۭ بِٱلۡبَصَرِ﴾, "We have created everything by measure, and Our command is but one, like a blink of the eye." The close: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ وَنَهَرٖ﴾ ﴿فِي مَقۡعَدِ صِدۡقٍ عِندَ مَلِيكٖ مُّقۡتَدِرِۭ﴾, "The God-conscious will be among gardens and a river, in a seat of truth near a Sovereign, Perfect in power."

## Part five: Surah Ar-Rahman (1-78)

Ar-Rahman has 78 verses and is Makki according to the majority; Hizb 54 begins with it. For the whole surah see [Surah Ar-Rahman Meaning in English](/blog/surah-ar-rahman-meaning-english/).

### 1. The favour of teaching and the universe (1-25)
﴿ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ﴾ ﴿عَلَّمَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ﴾ ﴿خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ﴾ ﴿عَلَّمَهُ ٱلۡبَيَانَ﴾, "The Most Merciful taught the Quran, created man and taught him eloquent speech." Then the sun and moon by precise reckoning, the stars and trees prostrating, the sky raised and the balance set, and the earth laid out for creatures. Then: ﴿مَرَجَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ يَلۡتَقِيَانِ﴾ ﴿بَيۡنَهُمَا بَرۡزَخࣱ لَّا يَبۡغِيَانِ﴾, "He released the two seas, meeting; between them is a barrier they do not transgress."

**Reflect on the order**: teaching the Quran comes before creating man, because a person's worth lies in the revelation he carries. This honours the people of the Quran.

### 2. Perishing and remaining (26-30)
﴿كُلُّ مَنۡ عَلَيۡهَا فَانٖ﴾ ﴿وَيَبۡقَىٰ وَجۡهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ﴾, "Everyone on it will perish, and the Face of your Lord will remain, Owner of majesty and honour." Then: ﴿يَسۡـَٔلُهُۥ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ كُلَّ يَوۡمٍ هُوَ فِي شَأۡنٖ﴾, "Whoever is in the heavens and earth asks of Him; every day He is about some matter."

### 3. Reckoning and Hell (31-45)
We will attend to you, O two burdened ones; flames of fire and smoke will be sent upon you; the criminals will be known by their marks.

### 4. The four gardens (46-78)
﴿وَلِمَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ جَنَّتَانِ﴾, "For whoever fears standing before his Lord are two gardens", with spreading branches, two flowing springs, and of every fruit two kinds. Then the rule: ﴿هَلۡ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ إِلَّا ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنُ﴾, "Is the reward for good anything but good?" Then, besides these, two other gardens, deep green. The close: ﴿تَبَٰرَكَ ٱسۡمُ رَبِّكَ ذِي ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ﴾, "Blessed is the name of your Lord, Owner of majesty and honour."

### The surah's refrain
﴿فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ﴾, "So which of your Lord's favours will you both deny?", recurs **31 times**, addressed to mankind and jinn. *Ala'* are favours. Each time a favour or warning is mentioned, the question awakens gratitude.

**Lesson**: pause at this refrain and recall one favour each time, saying: none of Your favours, our Lord, do we deny, so all praise is Yours.

## Part six: Surah Al-Waqi'ah (1-96)

Al-Waqi'ah is Makki, with 96 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Waqia Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-waqia-meaning-english/).

### 1. The three groups (1-56)
﴿إِذَا وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ﴾, "When the Event occurs", none will deny its occurrence; it will bring down and raise up. Then: ﴿وَكُنتُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا ثَلَٰثَةٗ﴾, "and you will be three groups":
1. **The foremost**: ﴿وَٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ ٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ﴾, "And the foremost, the foremost: they are the ones brought near", many from the earlier peoples and a few from the later.
2. **The companions of the right**: ﴿وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡيَمِينِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡيَمِينِ﴾, "And the companions of the right: what are the companions of the right?", among thornless lote trees, layered acacia and extended shade.
3. **The companions of the left**: ﴿وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ﴾, "And the companions of the left: what are the companions of the left?", in scorching fire, boiling water and the shade of black smoke.

**Lesson**: ask yourself daily, which group am I in? The foremost in the Hereafter are those who are foremost in good deeds in this world.

### 2. "Have you considered": four proofs (57-74)
- ﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تُمۡنُونَ﴾, "Have you considered what you emit?" Do you create it, or are We the Creator?
- ﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَحۡرُثُونَ﴾, "Have you considered what you sow?" Do you make it grow, or are We the grower?
- ﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱلَّذِي تَشۡرَبُونَ﴾, "Have you considered the water you drink?" Did you bring it down from the clouds?
- ﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِي تُورُونَ﴾, "Have you considered the fire you kindle?" Did you produce its tree?

**Reflect**: four necessities of life, offspring, food, drink and fire, all from Allah.

### 3. The noble Quran and the moment of death (75-96)
A new quarter: ﴿۞فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِمَوَٰقِعِ ٱلنُّجُومِ﴾, "I swear by the positions of the stars", and that is a great oath, if only you knew, ﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَقُرۡءَانࣱ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فِي كِتَٰبٖ مَّكۡنُونٖ﴾ ﴿لَّا يَمَسُّهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُطَهَّرُونَ﴾, "it is a noble Quran, in a protected Book, which none touch except the purified." Then the deathbed scene: ﴿فَلَوۡلَآ إِذَا بَلَغَتِ ٱلۡحُلۡقُومَ﴾, "Why then, when the soul reaches the throat", while you are looking on. Then the fate of the three groups again: ﴿فَأَمَّآ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبِينَ﴾, "if he was among those brought near", then rest, fragrance and a garden of bliss. The close: ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾, "So glorify the name of your Lord, the Most Great."

**The link**: the surah opens with the three groups on the Day of Resurrection and closes with them at the moment of death; death is a lesser resurrection.

## Part seven: Surah Al-Hadid (1-29)

Al-Hadid is Madani, with 29 verses, the first of the *Musabbihat* (surahs opening with Allah's glorification). For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Hadid Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-hadid-meaning-english/).

### 1. Allah's names and spending (1-15)
﴿سَبَّحَ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾, "Whatever is in the heavens and earth glorifies Allah, and He is the Almighty, the Wise." Then: ﴿هُوَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُ وَٱلۡأٓخِرُ وَٱلظَّـٰهِرُ وَٱلۡبَاطِنُۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٌ﴾, "He is the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden, and He knows all things." In Sahih Muslim the Prophet ﷺ explained it: "O Allah, You are the First, there is nothing before You; the Last, there is nothing after You; the Manifest, there is nothing above You; the Hidden, there is nothing beneath You."

Then the command to spend: ﴿ءَامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَأَنفِقُواْ مِمَّا جَعَلَكُم مُّسۡتَخۡلَفِينَ فِيهِۖ﴾, "Believe in Allah and His Messenger, and spend from what He has made you trustees of." Wealth belongs to Allah, and you are a trustee. Then: who will lend Allah a goodly loan? Then the scene of light on the Day: you will see the believing men and women with their light running before them and on their right, while the hypocrites say: wait for us so we may take some of your light.

### 2. The call to humility (16-19)
A new quarter: ﴿۞أَلَمۡ يَأۡنِ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَن تَخۡشَعَ قُلُوبُهُمۡ لِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا نَزَلَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا يَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَطَالَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡأَمَدُ فَقَسَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡۖ﴾, "Has the time not come for the believers that their hearts be humbled at the remembrance of Allah and the truth revealed, and that they not be like those given the Book before, whose period became long and whose hearts hardened?" In Sahih Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud said: "There were only four years between our accepting Islam and Allah reproaching us with this verse." Read [Has the Time Not Come? 57:16](/blog/alam-yani-lilladhina-amanu-meaning-english/).

**Lesson**: a long time without renewal hardens the heart, so renew your bond with the Quran.

### 3. The reality of this world and decree (20-24)
﴿ٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لَعِبࣱ وَلَهۡوࣱ وَزِينَةࣱ وَتَفَاخُرُۢ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَتَكَاثُرࣱ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَوۡلَٰدِۖ﴾, "Know that worldly life is only play, amusement, adornment, boasting among you and competition in wealth and children", like rain whose growth pleases the farmers, then it dries, you see it yellow, then it becomes debris. Then: ﴿سَابِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا كَعَرۡضِ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ﴾, "Race towards forgiveness from your Lord and a Garden as wide as the heaven and earth."

Then decree: ﴿مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن نَّبۡرَأَهَآۚ﴾ ﴿لِّكَيۡلَا تَأۡسَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَفۡرَحُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۗ﴾, "No calamity strikes on earth or within yourselves but it is in a record before We bring it into being, so you will not despair over what you missed, nor exult over what He gave you."

**Lesson**: belief in decree brings balance: no excessive grief over what is lost and no arrogant joy over what comes.

### 4. The Book, the balance and iron (25-29)
﴿لَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلَنَا بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَأَنزَلۡنَا مَعَهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَ لِيَقُومَ ٱلنَّاسُ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَأَنزَلۡنَا ٱلۡحَدِيدَ فِيهِ بَأۡسࣱ شَدِيدࣱ وَمَنَٰفِعُ لِلنَّاسِ﴾, "We sent Our messengers with clear proofs and sent down with them the Book and the balance so people would uphold justice, and We sent down iron, with great might and benefits for people." Then Nuh, Ibrahim and 'Isa, and the monasticism they invented. The close: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَءَامِنُواْ بِرَسُولِهِۦ يُؤۡتِكُمۡ كِفۡلَيۡنِ مِن رَّحۡمَتِهِۦ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ نُورٗا تَمۡشُونَ بِهِۦ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡۚ﴾, "O you who believe, fear Allah and believe in His Messenger; He will give you a double portion of His mercy, make for you a light by which to walk, and forgive you."

## Tajweed points in Juz 27

### ﴿بِأَيۡيْدٖ﴾ in Adh-Dhariyat 47
Written with two yas, the second not pronounced: *bi-aydin*.

### ﴿أَيُّهَ ٱلثَّقَلَانِ﴾ in Ar-Rahman 31
Written without an alif after the ha; read *ayyuha-th-thaqalan* when continuing. Hafs stops on it with a sakin ha.

### ﴿فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ﴾
In *ala'* the first madd, right after the opening hamzah, is a two-count *badal* madd; the second, before the final hamzah, is a connected madd of four or five counts. Keep the same length in all 31 occurrences.

### The prostration of the juz
At the end of An-Najm, ﴿فَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ﴾ (verse 62). See [Sujud al-Tilawah in English](/blog/sujud-al-tilawa-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. *Dhu* and *dhi* in Ar-Rahman
- 27: ﴿وَيَبۡقَىٰ وَجۡهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ﴾, nominative, describing "the Face".
- 78: ﴿تَبَٰرَكَ ٱسۡمُ رَبِّكَ ذِي ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ﴾, genitive, describing "your Lord".

### 2. "Race" and "hasten"
- Al 'Imran 133: ﴿وَسَارِعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾.
- Al-Hadid 21: ﴿سَابِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا كَعَرۡضِ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ﴾, adding "as wide as" and using the singular "heaven".

### 3. "Have you considered" in Al-Waqi'ah
Four in a row: ﴿مَّا تُمۡنُونَ﴾, ﴿مَّا تَحۡرُثُونَ﴾, ﴿ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱلَّذِي تَشۡرَبُونَ﴾, ﴿ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِي تُورُونَ﴾. Order: offspring, crops, water, fire.

### 4. "No calamity strikes"
- Al-Hadid 22: ﴿مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ﴾.
- At-Taghabun 11: "No calamity strikes except by Allah's permission."

## How to memorize Juz 27 with understanding

1. **Short verses, strong rhythm**: listen to a skilled reciter before memorizing.
2. **Al-Qamar**: learn the order of the five stories: Nuh, 'Ad, Thamud, Lut, Pharaoh's people.
3. **Ar-Rahman**: divide it by theme (the universe, perishing, reckoning, the first two gardens, the other two), with the refrain between.
4. **Al-Waqi'ah**: the three groups twice, with the four "have you considered" between.
5. **Al-Hadid**: its calls: believe and spend; has the time not come; know that worldly life; race; O believers, fear Allah.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 27

- **Flee to Allah** in every fear.
- **Remember the purpose of your creation**: worship.
- **Fear Allah among your family** in this world, and be safe in the next.
- **Follow the Sunnah**: it is revelation.
- **Do not claim purity for yourself.**
- **Know you have only what you strive for.**
- **Take heed through the Quran**: Allah made it easy.
- **Give thanks** at every "which of your Lord's favours".
- **Do good**: the reward of good is good.
- **Be among the foremost.**
- **Know that wealth is Allah's** and you are a trustee.
- **Renew your heart** before it hardens.
- **Find balance through decree**: no despair, no arrogance.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 27 called "Qala Fama Khatbukum"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞قَالَ فَمَا خَطۡبُكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ﴾.

### How many times does "Which of your Lord's favours will you deny?" occur?
Thirty-one times in Surah Ar-Rahman.

### How many prostrations are in Juz 27?
One, at the end of Surah An-Najm, verse 62.

### What are the three groups in Al-Waqi'ah?
The foremost who are brought near, the companions of the right, and the companions of the left.

### Where does Juz 27 end?
At the end of Al-Hadid. Juz 28 begins with Surah Al-Mujadilah: ﴿قَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّتِي تُجَٰدِلُكَ فِي زَوۡجِهَا﴾, "Allah has heard the words of the woman who pleads with you about her husband."

## Conclusion

Juz 27 reminds you of the purpose of your existence, stands you at the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary, shows you the moon split, repeats to you thirty-one times the question of gratitude, divides people into three groups before you so you can choose, and ends with a gentle call: has the time not come for your heart to be humbled?
