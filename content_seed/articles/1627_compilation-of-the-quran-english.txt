Allah promised: ﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ﴾ [Al-Hijr: 9]. History shows how that preservation happened.

## Stage 1: In the Prophet's lifetime ﷺ
- The Quran was revealed **over about 23 years**.
- **Memorized** by many companions.
- **Written** by scribes such as Zayd ibn Thabit, on materials like parchment, bones and palm stalks, but not yet gathered in one volume.

## Stage 2: Abu Bakr's collection
- After the battle of **Yamamah**, many reciters were killed.
- ʿUmar urged Abu Bakr to collect the Quran, and Abu Bakr assigned **Zayd ibn Thabit**.
- Zayd gathered it carefully from what was written and memorized, into **one manuscript (ṣuḥuf)** [Al-Bukhari].
- These ṣuḥuf stayed with Abu Bakr, then ʿUmar, then **Hafsah** bint ʿUmar.

## Stage 3: ʿUthman's copies
- Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman saw people differing in recitation and urged ʿUthman to act.
- ʿUthman borrowed the ṣuḥuf from Hafsah, and a committee led by Zayd made **copies**, sent to major cities [Al-Bukhari].
- These copies are the basis of the **ʿUthmani script** in every Mushaf today.

## Dots and vowels later
- Dots and vowel marks were added later to help non-Arabs read correctly, without changing the text.

## Summary
| Stage | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prophet's lifetime ﷺ | Memorized and written |
| 2 | Abu Bakr | Gathered in one manuscript |
| 3 | ʿUthman | Standard copies distributed |
