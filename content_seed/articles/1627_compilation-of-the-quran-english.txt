## How the Quran Was Compiled: From the Prophet ﷺ to ʿUthman

Allah promised: ﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ﴾ [Al-Hijr: 9]. History shows how that preservation happened.

### Stage 1: In the Prophet's lifetime ﷺ
- The Quran was revealed **over about 23 years**.
- **Memorized** by many companions.
- **Written** by scribes such as Zayd ibn Thabit, on materials like parchment, bones and palm stalks, but not yet gathered in one volume.

### Stage 2: Abu Bakr's collection
- After the battle of **Yamamah**, many reciters were killed.
- ʿUmar urged Abu Bakr to collect the Quran, and Abu Bakr assigned **Zayd ibn Thabit**.
- Zayd gathered it carefully from what was written and memorized, into **one manuscript (ṣuḥuf)** [Al-Bukhari].
- These ṣuḥuf stayed with Abu Bakr, then ʿUmar, then **Hafsah** bint ʿUmar.

### Stage 3: ʿUthman's copies
- Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman saw people differing in recitation and urged ʿUthman to act.
- ʿUthman borrowed the ṣuḥuf from Hafsah, and a committee led by Zayd made **copies**, sent to major cities [Al-Bukhari].
- These copies are the basis of the **ʿUthmani script** in every Mushaf today.

### Dots and vowels later
- Dots and vowel marks were added later to help non-Arabs read correctly, without changing the text.

### Summary
| Stage | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prophet's lifetime ﷺ | Memorized and written |
| 2 | Abu Bakr | Gathered in one manuscript |
| 3 | ʿUthman | Standard copies distributed |

## Etiquette of Reciting the Quran: 10 Adab Every Reader Should Know

The Quran is the speech of Allah, and reciting it has **etiquettes (ādāb)** that bring more reward and more benefit to the heart.

### 1. Sincere intention
- Recite to seek Allah's pleasure, not to be praised.

### 2. Purity
- Being in a state of wuḍūʾ is best, especially when touching the Mushaf.

### 3. Seeking refuge
﴿فَإِذَا قَرَأۡتَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ فَٱسۡتَعِذۡ بِٱللَّهِ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ ٱلرَّجِيمِ﴾ [An-Nahl: 98].

### 4. The basmalah
- Say *Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm* at the start of every surah except At-Tawbah.

### 5. Tarteel: recite slowly
﴿وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا﴾ [Al-Muzzammil: 4].
- Umm Salamah described the Prophet's recitation ﷺ as clear, letter by letter [Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi].

### 6. Beautify your voice
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Beautify the Quran with your voices» [Abu Dawud].

### 7. Reflect
﴿أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَۚ﴾ [An-Nisa: 82].

### 8. Respond to the ayat
- The Prophet ﷺ, when he passed an ayah of mercy, would ask Allah for it, and at an ayah of punishment would seek refuge [Muslim].

### 9. Don't interrupt with chatter
- Pause if you need to speak, then resume with seeking refuge again.

### 10. Respect the Mushaf
- Keep it in a clean, raised place, and don't place things on top of it.

## The Four Places of Sakt (Silent Pause) in Hafs Recitation

**Sakt** is a very short pause of about two counts, **without taking a breath**, then continuing. In the Madinah Mushaf it is marked with a small **سـ (sīn)**.

### The four places
| # | Surah | Words | Read as |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Al-Kahf 1–2 | ﴿عِوَجَاۜ﴾ then *qayyiman* | *ʿiwajā* (pause) *qayyiman* |
| 2 | Ya-Sin 52 | ﴿مِن مَّرۡقَدِنَاۜۗ هَٰذَا﴾ | *marqadinā* (pause) *hādhā* |
| 3 | Al-Qiyamah 27 | ﴿مَنۡۜ رَاقٖ﴾ | *man* (pause) *rāq* |
| 4 | Al-Mutaffifin 14 | ﴿بَلۡۜ رَانَ﴾ | *bal* (pause) *rāna* |

### Why sakt?
| Place | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Al-Kahf | So "qayyiman" (straight) is not heard as a description of "ʿiwajā" (crookedness) |
| Ya-Sin | Separates the words of the disbelievers from the reply that follows |
| Al-Qiyamah and Al-Mutaffifin | Keeps the noon and laam clear instead of merging them into the raa |

### How to do it
1. Stop the **sound** for about two counts.
2. **Don't breathe.**
3. Continue with the next word.

### Without sakt?
- In ﴿مَنۡۜ رَاقٖ﴾, without sakt the noon would merge into the raa (*marrāq*). Sakt keeps the noon heard.

### Optional sakt places
- Between Al-Anfal and At-Tawbah, and in ﴿مَالِيَهۡۜ﴾ in Al-Haqqah, Hafs has more than one option; learn them with a teacher.

## Heavy and Light Letters in Tajweed (Tafkhim and Tarqiq) for English Speakers

Arabic has letters pronounced with a **full, heavy** sound (tafkhīm) and others with a **thin, light** sound (tarqīq). English has nothing exactly like this, so it takes practice.

### The seven always-heavy letters
Gathered in: **خُصَّ ضَغۡطٍ قِظۡ** (*khuṣṣa ḍaghṭin qiẓ*).
| Letter | Name |
|---|---|
| خ | Khaa |
| ص | Ṣaad |
| ض | Ḍaad |
| غ | Ghayn |
| ط | Ṭaa |
| ق | Qaf |
| ظ | Ẓaa |
- **How:** raise the back of the tongue toward the roof of the mouth.
- **Strongest:** ṭaa, ḍaad, ṣaad and ẓaa, because the tongue also covers the roof of the mouth.

### Letters that change
| Letter | Heavy when | Light when |
|---|---|---|
| ر Raa | With fatḥah or ḍammah, as in ﴿رَبِّ﴾ | With kasrah, as in ﴿رِجَالࣱ﴾ |
| ل in "Allah" | After fatḥah or ḍammah: ﴿قَالَ ٱللَّهُ﴾ | After kasrah: ﴿بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ﴾ |
| ا Alif | After a heavy letter: ﴿قَالَ﴾ | After a light letter: ﴿مَٰلِكِ﴾ |

### All other letters are light
- Including ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ﴾: the ḥaa is light even though it comes from the throat.

### Tips for English speakers
1. **Don't make light letters heavy** before alif: say *kitāb* lightly, not *kotob*.
2. **Keep heavy letters heavy** even with kasrah: ﴿قِيلَ﴾ stays heavy, though a little less.
3. **Record yourself** and compare with a teacher.

## Memorizing the Quran as an Adult with ADHD: Methods That Work

> **Note:** These are study tips, not medical advice. If you have ADHD, your treatment plan with a specialist comes first.

### 1. Short, timed sessions
- Use a timer: **15 minutes on, 5 minutes off**.
- Stop while it's still going well, so you want to come back.

### 2. A fixed anchor
- Tie hifz to something you never skip: **after Fajr** or after your morning coffee.

### 3. Make it active
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Writing | Write the ayah from memory |
| Walking | Recite while walking around the room |
| Recording | Record yourself and listen back |
| Teaching | Explain the meaning to someone |

### 4. Tame the phone
- Use a **printed Mushaf** for memorizing.
- Put the phone in another room or on airplane mode.

### 5. Accountability
- A **teacher** or a **hifz partner** you recite to on fixed days.

### 6. Flexible revision
- On low-focus days, **revise instead of memorizing** new pages.
- Use **audio revision** while doing simple tasks.

### 7. Be kind to yourself
- The Prophet ﷺ said the one who recites with difficulty has **two rewards** [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

## Juz Amma Memorization Plan for Kids: Memorize the 30th Juz in 6 Months

**Juz Amma** is the 30th and final part of the Quran. It contains **37 surahs**, from [An-Naba (78)](/quran/78/) to [An-Nas (114)](/quran/114/), with a total of **564 verses**. Most children start their memorization journey here, because the surahs are short and every finished surah feels like a victory.

This plan is designed for a child aged **7 to 10**, memorizing **five days a week**. For younger children (4–6), simply double the time to a full year.

### Why start from An-Nas and go backwards?
- The last surahs are the shortest, so the child finishes a whole surah in just a few days.
- They are also the surahs children hear most in prayer and daily remembrance.
- As the surahs get longer, the child's memorization skill grows with them.

**Before starting:** if your child doesn't know [Surah Al-Fatiha](/quran/1/) yet, start there.

### The month-by-month plan
| Month | Surahs | Verses | Daily target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An-Nas → Al-Kawthar | 32 | 1–2 verses |
| 2 | Al-Maun → Al-Qariah | 47 | 2–3 verses |
| 3 | Al-Adiyat → Ash-Sharh | 67 | 3–4 verses |
| 4 | Ad-Duha → Al-Ghashiyah | 123 | 5–6 short verses |
| 5 | Al-Ala → Al-Infitar | 138 | 6–7 short verses |
| 6 | At-Takwir → An-Naba | 157 | 7–8 short verses |
| **Total** | **37 surahs** | **564** | |

#### Month 1 in detail
An-Nas, Al-Falaq, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Masad, An-Nasr, Al-Kafirun, Al-Kawthar. The goal this month is to **build the habit**, not to rush.

#### Month 3 note
Surah Al-Bayyinah (98) has longer verses than the surahs around it. Give it extra days if needed.

### A model week
| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Days 1–5 | New verses + yesterday's verses + one older surah |
| Day 6 | Review everything memorized this week |
| Day 7 | Rest, or a fun family recitation |

### The revision system
- **Every day:** recite yesterday's verses before starting new ones.
- **Every week:** review the whole week's work.
- **Every month:** in the last week, cut new memorization in half and review everything from the start.

### If your child falls behind
That's completely fine. The plan is a guide, not a rule. If a longer surah needs an extra week, give it. What matters is that your child finishes Juz Amma **loving it and knowing it well**.

### Tools that help
- **Daily listening:** play the surah recited by Sheikh Al-Husary (the *Mu'allim* teaching recitation is slow and clear, ideal for kids) on our [Quran pages](/quran/), with verse repetition.
- **Understanding:** tell your child the story behind surahs like Al-Fil (the army of elephants) before memorizing it.
- **Motivation:** a star chart, and a small celebration after every surah.
