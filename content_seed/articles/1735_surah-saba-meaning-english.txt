**Surah Saba** (54 ayat) is a story of **two attitudes to blessings**: gratitude and ingratitude.

## Dawud and Sulayman
﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ مِنَّا فَضۡلٗاۖ يَٰجِبَالُ أَوِّبِي مَعَهُۥ وَٱلطَّيۡرَۖ وَأَلَنَّا لَهُ ٱلۡحَدِيدَ﴾
- Mountains and birds glorified with Dawud; iron was made soft for him.
- Sulayman was given the wind and the jinn who worked for him.
- *Work, O family of Dawud, in gratitude. And few of My servants are grateful.*

## Sulayman's death
- He died leaning on his staff, and the jinn did not realize until a creature of the earth ate through it.
- **Lesson**: the jinn do not know the unseen.

## The people of Saba
| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Two gardens, right and left | Gardens of bitter fruit |
| *A good land and a forgiving Lord* | The flood of the dam |
| Safe routes between towns | Scattered into stories |
- **The cause**: they turned away from gratitude.

## Lessons
- **Gratitude is action**, not only words.
- **Blessings can be lost** through ingratitude.
