## Surah Saba Meaning: Gratitude, Dawud's Family and the Broken Dam

**Surah Saba** (54 ayat) is a story of **two attitudes to blessings**: gratitude and ingratitude.

### Dawud and Sulayman
﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ مِنَّا فَضۡلٗاۖ يَٰجِبَالُ أَوِّبِي مَعَهُۥ وَٱلطَّيۡرَۖ وَأَلَنَّا لَهُ ٱلۡحَدِيدَ﴾
- Mountains and birds glorified with Dawud; iron was made soft for him.
- Sulayman was given the wind and the jinn who worked for him.
- *Work, O family of Dawud, in gratitude. And few of My servants are grateful.*

### Sulayman's death
- He died leaning on his staff, and the jinn did not realize until a creature of the earth ate through it.
- **Lesson**: the jinn do not know the unseen.

### The people of Saba
| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Two gardens, right and left | Gardens of bitter fruit |
| *A good land and a forgiving Lord* | The flood of the dam |
| Safe routes between towns | Scattered into stories |
- **The cause**: they turned away from gratitude.

### Lessons
- **Gratitude is action**, not only words.
- **Blessings can be lost** through ingratitude.

## "To Him Ascends the Good Word, and Righteous Deeds Raise It": Quran 35:10 Explained

﴿مَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡعِزَّةَ فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ جَمِيعًاۚ إِلَيۡهِ يَصۡعَدُ ٱلۡكَلِمُ ٱلطَّيِّبُ وَٱلۡعَمَلُ ٱلصَّـٰلِحُ يَرۡفَعُهُۥۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡكُرُونَ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ شَدِيدࣱۖ وَمَكۡرُ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُوَ يَبُورُ﴾ [Fatir 35:10]

### Seeking honour
- *Whoever desires honour, all honour belongs to Allah*: seek it through obedience to Him.

### Words that ascend
- Scholars explain the good word as dhikr, Quran, truthful speech and good advice.

### Deeds that raise
| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| Good words | Ascend to Allah |
| Righteous deeds | Raise and support them |

- Words without deeds are weak; deeds give them strength.

### Evil plots
- *The plotting of those, it will perish*: schemes against truth end in failure.

### Lessons
- **Seek honour** in obedience, not status.
- **Back your words** with action.

## "O Mankind, You Are the Ones in Need of Allah": Quran 35:15-17 Explained

﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ أَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ﴾ ﴿إِن يَشَأۡ يُذۡهِبۡكُمۡ وَيَأۡتِ بِخَلۡقٖ جَدِيدٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَا ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ بِعَزِيزٖ﴾ [Fatir 35:15-17]

### Total need
- *You are the needy*: every breath, heartbeat, meal and moment of guidance depends on Allah.

### Two names
| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Al-Ghani* | Free of all need |
| *Al-Hamid* | Praiseworthy in all He does |

- Richness alone could be harsh; joined with *al-Hamid*, His richness is generous and praiseworthy.

### Replaceable
- *If He wills, He can remove you and bring a new creation.* Allah does not need us; we need Him.

### Why this frees the heart
- Recognising need for Allah frees you from depending on people. You ask Him first.

### Lessons
- **Start every task** by asking Allah's help.
- **Humility** is the natural result of knowing your need.

## Surah Fatir Meaning: Only Those with Knowledge Truly Fear Allah

**Surah Fatir** (45 ayat) means *The Originator*: the One who brought the heavens and earth into being from nothing.

### Mercy no one can block
- *Whatever mercy Allah opens to people, none can withhold it; and whatever He withholds, none can release it after Him.*

### You need Allah
﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ أَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ﴾

### Colours of creation
| Creation | Colours |
|---|---|
| Fruits | Of varied colours |
| Mountains | White and red streaks, and intensely black |
| People, animals, livestock | Different colours |

### Knowledge and fear
﴿إِنَّمَا يَخۡشَى ٱللَّهَ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡعُلَمَـٰٓؤُاْۗ﴾
- Placed **right after** the colours of creation: knowledge of Allah's signs leads to awe of Him.

### Three kinds of inheritors
- Those given the Book: **some wrong themselves, some are moderate, some race ahead in good deeds**.

### Lessons
- **Study creation** to know the Creator.
- **Race ahead**; don't settle for the minimum.

## Quran 35:28 Explained: Why Knowledge Leads to Awe of Allah

﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَٱلدَّوَآبِّ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ مُخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥ كَذَٰلِكَۗ إِنَّمَا يَخۡشَى ٱللَّهَ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡعُلَمَـٰٓؤُاْۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ غَفُورٌ﴾ [Fatir 35:28]

### The context: colours
- The previous verse mentions rain producing fruits of different colours, and mountains with white, red and black streaks. This verse adds people, animals and livestock.

### Knowledge leads to awe
- After describing this diversity, Allah says: **only those with knowledge truly fear Him**. The more one understands creation and the Creator, the deeper the awe.

### Which knowledge?
| Knowledge | Effect |
|---|---|
| Knowing Allah's names and attributes | Love, fear and hope |
| Knowing His religion | Obedience |
| Knowing His creation | Wonder and humility |

- Ibn Masʿud is reported to have said: knowledge is not abundance of narration, but knowledge is **khashyah** (awe).

### *Khashyah* versus fear
- *Khashyah* is fear based on knowledge and reverence, not panic.

### The ending
- *Indeed Allah is Mighty, Forgiving*: might that inspires awe, forgiveness that inspires hope.

### Lessons
- **Learn to grow closer**, not to boast.
- **Let science increase faith.**

## "Those Who Recite the Book of Allah Hope for a Trade That Never Fails": Quran 35:29-30 Explained

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَتۡلُونَ كِتَٰبَ ٱللَّهِ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنفَقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ يَرۡجُونَ تِجَٰرَةٗ لَّن تَبُورَ﴾ ﴿لِيُوَفِّيَهُمۡ أُجُورَهُمۡ وَيَزِيدَهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ غَفُورࣱ شَكُورࣱ﴾ [Fatir 35:29-30]

### Three qualities
| Quality | Form |
|---|---|
| Reciting the Book | Present tense: ongoing |
| Establishing prayer | Past tense: a settled habit |
| Spending secretly and openly | Giving in every state |

### A trade that never fails
- *Tabur* means to go unsold or perish. Trading with Allah never loses.

### Full reward and more
- *To pay them their rewards in full*, and *increase them from His bounty*: beyond what they earned.

### *Ghafur, Shakur*
- Forgiving of shortcomings; Appreciative, rewarding a little with much.

### Lessons
- **Make recitation daily**, not seasonal.
- **People of the Quran** combine recitation with prayer and charity.

## Surah Ya-Sin Meaning: Themes, Stories and Authentic Virtues

**Surah Ya-Sin** (83 ayat) is one of the most loved surahs. It focuses on **messengership** and **resurrection**.

﴿يسٓ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾

### Themes
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–12 | The Quran and the Prophet's message |
| 13–32 | The town, the messengers, and the man who came running |
| 33–44 | Signs: the dead earth revived, night and day, sun and moon, ships |
| 45–54 | The Hour and the blowing of the Trumpet |
| 55–68 | The people of Paradise and the people of the Fire |
| 69–83 | The Prophet is not a poet; the proof of resurrection |

### The man who came running
﴿وَجَآءَ مِنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ رَجُلࣱ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ [Ya-Sin: 20]
- He supported the messengers and was killed, yet wished his people knew how Allah honoured him.

### The closing proof
﴿إِنَّمَآ أَمۡرُهُۥٓ إِذَآ أَرَادَ شَيۡـًٔا أَن يَقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾ [Ya-Sin: 82]

### An honest note on virtues
- The hadith **"Everything has a heart, and the heart of the Quran is Ya-Sin"** is graded **weak** by many hadith scholars.
- The hadith **"Recite Ya-Sin over your dying"** is also debated, and many scholars graded it weak.
- Ya-Sin is still a great surah, part of the Quran with all its virtues.

## How to Memorize Surah Yasin in 6 Weeks (and a Note on Weak Hadiths About It)

[Surah Yasin](/quran/36/) is a surah many Muslims love to memorize and recite. It's a Makkan surah of medium length with mostly short verses, a moving story, and vivid scenes, all of which make it very memorable.

### Quick facts
- **83 verses**, pages 440–445 in the Madinah Mushaf (about six pages), in juz 22 and 23.

### An honest note on hadiths about Yasin
Many hadiths about Yasin circulate widely, such as "Everything has a heart, and the heart of the Quran is Yasin", and narrations about reciting it over the dying. Many hadith scholars have graded most of these narrations as **weak**.

Reciting and memorizing Yasin is still a great good deed, included in the general, authentic virtues of the Quran, such as **"Whoever recites a letter from the Book of Allah will have a good deed, multiplied tenfold"** (Tirmidhi). You don't need a weak hadith to love this surah. See [authentic hadiths on the virtues of the Quran](/blog/sun-and-moon-letters-english/#The-Virtues-of-Learning-and-Memorizing-the-Quran-in-Authenti).

### The surah's map
| Section | Verses | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 1–12 | Oath by the Quran; the Prophet's mission |
| The people of the town | 13–32 | The messengers and the man who came running |
| Signs in creation | 33–50 | Dead land revived, night and day, sun and moon, ships |
| Scenes of the Hereafter | 51–68 | The trumpet, the people of Paradise, the guilty |
| Closing | 69–83 | Answering the deniers; Allah's power to resurrect |

### The 6-week plan (5 days a week)
| Week | Verses | Approx. daily target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–12 | 2–3 verses |
| 2 | 13–27 | 3 verses |
| 3 | 28–40 | 2–3 verses |
| 4 | 41–54 | 3 verses |
| 5 | 55–68 | 3 verses |
| 6 | 69–83 | 3 verses |

### Spots that need attention
1. **The Sakt on ﴿مَّرۡقَدِنَاۜ﴾ (verse 52):** in Hafs, a brief pause without breath, then continue ﴿هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ﴾.
2. **The opening letters ﴿يسٓ﴾:** read *Yā Sīīīn*: Yaa for 2 counts, Seen for 6. In Hafs the Noon is pronounced clearly when continuing to ﴿وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ﴾.
3. **Sun and moon verses (38–40):** similar rhythm; memorize them together and watch the order.
4. **Very short consecutive verses** in the Hereafter scenes: link each verse to the next.

### Locking it in
- Recite what you memorize in night prayer.
- Start each day by reciting yesterday's portion.
- After finishing, recite it daily from memory for two weeks, then weekly.

## Surah As-Saffat Meaning: Ibrahim's Sacrifice and Yunus in the Whale

**Surah As-Saffat** (182 ayat) begins with angels standing in rows and ends with peace upon all messengers.

### A conversation in Paradise
- A believer in Paradise remembers a friend who denied resurrection, looks down and sees him in Hell: *By Allah, you almost ruined me.*
- **Lesson**: choose your companions carefully.

### Prophets with a refrain
| Prophet | Greeting |
|---|---|
| Nuh | *Peace upon Nuh among the worlds* |
| Ibrahim | *Peace upon Ibrahim* |
| Musa and Harun | *Peace upon Musa and Harun* |
| Ilyas | *Peace upon Ilyasin* |

### The great sacrifice
﴿فَلَمَّا بَلَغَ مَعَهُ ٱلسَّعۡيَ قَالَ يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰ فِي ٱلۡمَنَامِ أَنِّيٓ أَذۡبَحُكَ فَٱنظُرۡ مَاذَا تَرَىٰۚ﴾
- The son answered: *O my father, do as you are commanded; you will find me, if Allah wills, among the patient.*
- Allah ransomed him with a great sacrifice.

### Yunus
- He was swallowed by the whale, and *had he not been of those who glorify Allah, he would have remained inside it until the Day they are resurrected*.

### The ending
﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ ٱلۡعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَى ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾

### Lessons
- **Glorification** saves in hardship.
- **Obedience** with trust.
