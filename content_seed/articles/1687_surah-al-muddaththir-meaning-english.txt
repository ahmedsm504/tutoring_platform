**Surah Al-Muddaththir** (56 ayat) contains some of the first commands given to the Prophet ﷺ when revelation resumed after a pause [Al-Bukhari].

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُدَّثِّرُ﴾ ﴿قُمۡ فَأَنذِرۡ﴾ ﴿وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرۡ﴾ ﴿وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرۡ﴾ ﴿وَٱلرُّجۡزَ فَٱهۡجُرۡ﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَمۡنُن تَسۡتَكۡثِرُ﴾ ﴿وَلِرَبِّكَ فَٱصۡبِرۡ﴾

## Six commands
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Arise and warn | Begin the mission |
| Magnify your Lord | Allāhu akbar in heart and action |
| Purify your garments | Physical and moral purity |
| Avoid idols | Shun all impurity |
| Don't give to gain more | Give without expecting return |
| Be patient for your Lord | The road will be hard |

## The man who called it magic
- The surah describes a man who thought, measured, frowned, then said: *This is nothing but magic imitated*. Many commentators named him al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah.

## Saqar and the nineteen
- *Over it are nineteen*: the keepers of Hell, a test for those who doubt.

## Every soul is held
﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسِۭ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ رَهِينَةٌ﴾ — *Every soul, for what it has earned, is held in pledge.*

## Lessons
- **Start with yourself**: purify, magnify Allah, then call others.
