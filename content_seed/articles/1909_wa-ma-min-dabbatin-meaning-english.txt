﴿۞وَمَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ رِزۡقُهَا وَيَعۡلَمُ مُسۡتَقَرَّهَا وَمُسۡتَوۡدَعَهَاۚ كُلࣱّ فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ [Hud 11:6]

## Every creature
- *Dabbah* includes everything that moves on earth: humans, animals, insects. **None** is left without provision.

## "Upon Allah"
- Allah has made provision a commitment upon Himself, out of His generosity.

## Two kinds of knowledge
| Word | Meaning given by scholars |
|---|---|
| *Mustaqarr* | Where it lives and settles |
| *Mustawdaʿ* | Where it is kept temporarily, or where it dies |

## All recorded
- *All is in a clear record*: nothing happens by chance.

## Trust with effort
- Birds leave their nests to find food. Provision is guaranteed, but **seeking it** is a command. Worry should not push anyone to unlawful means.

## Lessons
- **Do not fear poverty** to the point of sin.
- **Work, then trust.**
