## "There Is No Creature on Earth But Its Provision Is upon Allah": Quran 11:6 Explained

﴿۞وَمَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ رِزۡقُهَا وَيَعۡلَمُ مُسۡتَقَرَّهَا وَمُسۡتَوۡدَعَهَاۚ كُلࣱّ فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ [Hud 11:6]

### Every creature
- *Dabbah* includes everything that moves on earth: humans, animals, insects. **None** is left without provision.

### "Upon Allah"
- Allah has made provision a commitment upon Himself, out of His generosity.

### Two kinds of knowledge
| Word | Meaning given by scholars |
|---|---|
| *Mustaqarr* | Where it lives and settles |
| *Mustawdaʿ* | Where it is kept temporarily, or where it dies |

### All recorded
- *All is in a clear record*: nothing happens by chance.

### Trust with effort
- Birds leave their nests to find food. Provision is guaranteed, but **seeking it** is a command. Worry should not push anyone to unlawful means.

### Lessons
- **Do not fear poverty** to the point of sin.
- **Work, then trust.**

## "He Produced You from the Earth and Settled You in It": Quran 11:61 Explained

﴿۞وَإِلَىٰ ثَمُودَ أَخَاهُمۡ صَٰلِحٗاۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥۖ هُوَ أَنشَأَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱسۡتَعۡمَرَكُمۡ فِيهَا فَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُوهُ ثُمَّ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي قَرِيبࣱ مُّجِيبࣱ﴾ [Hud 11:61]

### From the earth
- Humanity's origin is from the earth: Adam from dust, and food from its plants.

### *Istaʿmarakum*
- He made you its builders: you build, farm and live in it. Building the earth is a human mission, within obedience to Allah.

### Forgiveness then repentance
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Istighfar* | Asking that sins be erased |
| *Tawbah* | Turning back and leaving sin |

### "Near and Responsive"
- Near by His knowledge and mercy, responsive to those who call.

### Lessons
- **Build your world** with useful work and taqwa.
- **Allah is near**: do not hesitate to repent.

## "I Only Want Reform as Much as I Am Able": Quran 11:88 Explained

﴿قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن كُنتُ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّي وَرَزَقَنِي مِنۡهُ رِزۡقًا حَسَنٗاۚ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَنۡ أُخَالِفَكُمۡ إِلَىٰ مَآ أَنۡهَىٰكُمۡ عَنۡهُۚ إِنۡ أُرِيدُ إِلَّا ٱلۡإِصۡلَٰحَ مَا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُۚ وَمَا تَوۡفِيقِيٓ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُ وَإِلَيۡهِ أُنِيبُ﴾ [Hud 11:88]

### The context
- Shuʿayb told his people to give full measure and stop cheating. They mocked him: *Does your prayer command you to leave what our fathers worshipped?*

### Shuʿayb's principles
| Principle | Words in the verse |
|---|---|
| Clear evidence | *I am upon clear proof from my Lord* |
| Lawful income | *He provided me good provision* |
| Consistency | *I do not intend to do what I forbid you* |
| Sincere aim | *I only want reform* |
| Realism | *As much as I am able* |
| Humility | *My success is only through Allah* |
| Reliance | *Upon Him I rely, and to Him I return* |

### Practising what you preach
- A caller is most convincing when his actions match his words.

### "My success is only through Allah"
- Even with effort and sincerity, *tawfiq* is Allah's gift. Many speakers and writers open their work with this sentence.

### Lessons
- **Reform starts with you.**
- **Do your best and leave the result to Allah.**

## Surah Hud Meaning: Stand Firm as You Were Commanded

**Surah Hud** (123 ayat) is full of the stories of earlier prophets and their peoples.

### A perfected book
- It opens with a book whose verses are perfected, then explained in detail, from One Wise and Aware.

### Nuh and his son
- Nuh built the ark under Allah's eyes while his people mocked him.
- When the flood came, he called his son to board. The son refused, saying he would take refuge on a mountain, and was drowned. Nuh was taught that his son was **not of his family** in faith.

### The prophets and their peoples
| Prophet | People | Key message |
|---|---|---|
| Hud | ʿAd | Seek forgiveness and Allah will increase your strength |
| Salih | Thamud | The she-camel as a sign |
| Ibrahim | Angels as guests | Good news of Ishaq and Yaʿqub |
| Lut | His people | Rescue before the punishment |
| Shuʿayb | Madyan | Full measure and honest weights |

### Stand firm
﴿فَٱسۡتَقِمۡ كَمَآ أُمِرۡتَ وَمَن تَابَ مَعَكَ وَلَا تَطۡغَوۡاْۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرࣱ﴾

### Good deeds erase bad ones
- *Establish prayer at the two ends of the day and in some hours of the night. Indeed good deeds remove bad deeds.*

### Lessons
- **Faith is not inherited**, even by a prophet's son.
- **Steadfastness** means staying on the path without excess.

## "Good Deeds Remove Bad Deeds": Quran 11:114 Explained

﴿وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ طَرَفَيِ ٱلنَّهَارِ وَزُلَفٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّيۡلِۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡحَسَنَٰتِ يُذۡهِبۡنَ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِۚ ذَٰلِكَ ذِكۡرَىٰ لِلذَّـٰكِرِينَ﴾ [Hud 11:114]

### Prayer times in the verse
| Phrase | Explained by scholars as |
|---|---|
| The two ends of the day | Fajr, and Dhuhr and ʿAsr (or Maghrib) |
| Parts of the night | Maghrib and ʿIshaʾ |

### The occasion
- A man kissed a woman who was not permitted to him, then came to the Prophet ﷺ regretful. This verse was revealed. He asked: *Is this for me alone?* The Prophet ﷺ said: **"For all of my nation"** (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Good erases bad
- The five prayers, wudu, charity and dhikr wipe away minor sins. Major sins require sincere repentance.

### A Prophetic instruction
- *Fear Allah wherever you are, follow a bad deed with a good one and it will erase it, and treat people with good character* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### A reminder
- *That is a reminder for those who remember*: those who remember Allah benefit most.

### Lessons
- **Do not despair after a slip**: pray and do good.
- **Keep the five prayers**, they are the daily cleansing.

## Surah Yusuf Meaning: The Best of Stories and Never Losing Hope

**Surah Yusuf** (111 ayat) tells one complete story from beginning to end.

### The best of stories
﴿نَحۡنُ نَقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡقَصَصِ بِمَآ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ وَإِن كُنتَ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ لَمِنَ ٱلۡغَٰفِلِينَ﴾

### The story in scenes
| Scene | Event |
|---|---|
| The dream | Eleven stars, the sun and the moon prostrating |
| The well | The brothers' envy and their lie to their father |
| The house of al-ʿAziz | Temptation refused: *I seek refuge in Allah* |
| Prison | Inviting fellow prisoners to tawhid and interpreting dreams |
| The king's dream | Seven fat cows and seven lean ones, and a plan for the famine |
| The brothers' return | Recognising them, keeping his brother, and the shirt |
| Reunion | The family prostrates, and the first dream comes true |

### Yaqub's hope
﴿يَٰبَنِيَّ ٱذۡهَبُواْ فَتَحَسَّسُواْ مِن يُوسُفَ وَأَخِيهِ وَلَا تَاْيۡـَٔسُواْ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يَاْيۡـَٔسُ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾
- He described his response to grief as *beautiful patience*, and said: *I only complain of my grief and sorrow to Allah.*

### Forgiveness
- Yusuf told his brothers: *No blame upon you today. Allah will forgive you.*

### Lessons
- **Hard chapters are part of a bigger plan.**
- **Chastity and integrity** are worth prison.
- **Forgive when you have power.**

## "Indeed the Soul Constantly Commands Evil": Quran 12:53 Explained

﴿۞وَمَآ أُبَرِّئُ نَفۡسِيٓۚ إِنَّ ٱلنَّفۡسَ لَأَمَّارَةُۢ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ إِلَّا مَا رَحِمَ رَبِّيٓۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ [Yusuf 12:53]

### Who said it?
| View | Basis |
|---|---|
| The wife of al-ʿAziz | It continues her words *Now the truth has become clear*, a view favoured by a number of researchers |
| Yusuf | Said out of humility, a view of a group of commentators |

### *Ammarah*
- An intensive form: constantly urging evil.

### Three souls in the Quran
| Soul | Verse |
|---|---|
| Commanding evil | This verse |
| Self-reproaching | *I swear by the self-reproaching soul* (Al-Qiyamah 75:2) |
| Tranquil | *O tranquil soul* (Al-Fajr 89:27) |

### "Except whom my Lord has mercy on"
- Protection from the soul's evil is by **Allah's mercy**, not human strength alone.

### Lessons
- **Do not praise yourself** or feel safe from your soul.
- **Seek Allah's mercy** to be saved from it.

## "I Only Complain of My Grief and Sorrow to Allah": Quran 12:86 Explained

﴿قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَشۡكُواْ بَثِّي وَحُزۡنِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَأَعۡلَمُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ [Yusuf 12:86]

### The context
- His sons blamed Yaʿqub for constantly remembering Yusuf until his eyes turned white from grief.

### Two words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Bath* | Intense worry too heavy to hide |
| *Huzn* | Sorrow over what was lost |

### Complaining to Allah alone
- *Innama* restricts: I complain to Allah, not to you.
- This does not contradict his *beautiful patience*: that patience means not complaining to people.

### "I know from Allah what you do not know"
- He knew of Allah's mercy and the truth of Yusuf's dream, so he kept hope.

### Lessons
- **Grief is not weak faith**; what matters is where you take it.
- **Cry before Allah** in prostration.

## Quran 12:87 Explained: Yaqub's Hope After Years of Loss

﴿يَٰبَنِيَّ ٱذۡهَبُواْ فَتَحَسَّسُواْ مِن يُوسُفَ وَأَخِيهِ وَلَا تَاْيۡـَٔسُواْ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يَاْيۡـَٔسُ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ [Yusuf 12:87]

### The context
- Yaʿqub had lost Yusuf many years earlier, then his son Binyamin was held in Egypt. His eyes had turned white from grief. Yet he told his sons: **go and search**.

### Hope with action
- *Go and search*: hope is not sitting and waiting. It is **effort with trust**.

### What is *rawh*?
- *Rawh* means relief, ease and the breath of mercy that comes after distress.

### Why is despair so serious?
- Despair means thinking Allah cannot or will not help, which is a wrong belief about His power and mercy. That is why the verse links it with disbelief.

### The result
- Shortly after, the brothers returned, Yusuf revealed himself, the shirt restored Yaʿqub's sight, and the family was reunited.

### Lessons
- **Keep hope alive** after years of waiting.
- **Combine duʿa with effort.**

## "Whoever Fears Allah and Is Patient, Allah Does Not Waste the Reward": Quran 12:90 Explained

﴿قَالُوٓاْ أَءِنَّكَ لَأَنتَ يُوسُفُۖ قَالَ أَنَا۠ يُوسُفُ وَهَٰذَآ أَخِيۖ قَدۡ مَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَآۖ إِنَّهُۥ مَن يَتَّقِ وَيَصۡبِرۡ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ [Yusuf 12:90]

### The moment of recognition
- After years, the brothers stood before the minister of Egypt and suddenly realised: *Are you indeed Yusuf?*

### His answer
| Phrase | Lesson |
|---|---|
| *I am Yusuf, and this is my brother* | Simple, without blame |
| *Allah has been gracious to us* | He credits Allah, not himself |
| *Whoever fears Allah and is patient* | The summary of his journey |
| *Allah does not waste the reward of the good* | The result |

### Two words that explain a life
- **Taqwa** protected him in the house of al-ʿAziz.
- **Patience** carried him through the well and prison.

### No reproach
- In the next verses he says: *No blame upon you today.* Success did not make him arrogant or vengeful.

### Lessons
- **Combine taqwa and patience** in your trials.
- **Credit Allah** when you succeed.

## "No Blame upon You Today": Quran 12:92 Explained

﴿قَالَ لَا تَثۡرِيبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ يَغۡفِرُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾ [Yusuf 12:92]

### What is *tathrib*?
- Reproach and blame, repeating someone's mistake to them.

### Complete forgiveness
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *No blame upon you* | I will not remind you |
| *Today* | At the moment of my power |
| *May Allah forgive you* | A duʿa for them |
| *He is the Most Merciful* | Hope in Allah's mercy |

### Forgiveness with power
- The brothers stood guilty before the most powerful man in Egypt. He could have punished; he forgave.

### At the conquest of Makkah
- It is reported in the Seerah that when the Prophet ﷺ entered Makkah victorious, he told Quraysh he would say to them what Yusuf said to his brothers: *No blame upon you today.*

### Lessons
- **Forgive without reminding.**
- **Use power to forgive**, not to avenge.

## "This Is My Way: I Invite to Allah with Insight": Quran 12:108 Explained

﴿قُلۡ هَٰذِهِۦ سَبِيلِيٓ أَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ عَلَىٰ بَصِيرَةٍ أَنَا۠ وَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَنِيۖ وَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾ [Yusuf 12:108]

### "This is my way"
- My path and method: tawhid and calling to it.

### "To Allah"
- Not to oneself, a group or a party.

### *Basirah*
| Aspect | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Knowledge | Of what you call to |
| Certainty | In its truth |
| Wisdom | In method and timing |

### "And whoever follows me"
- Every follower of the Prophet ﷺ is a caller according to knowledge and ability.

### End of Surah Yusuf
- It closes a surah full of daʿwah: Yusuf called in prison and as a minister.

### Lessons
- **Learn before you call.**
- **Invite** at home and work by example and word.
