A young girl lives in the place of worship in Jerusalem, cared for by Prophet Zakariya. Every time Zakariya visits her, he finds food and fruit with her! Where did it come from? Nobody brought it! Amazed, he asks: "O Maryam, where did you get this from?" And the little girl answers confidently: "It is from Allah. Allah provides for whom He wills without measure." This is Maryam, the mother of Prophet Isa, and the story of her childhood is in Surah Al-Imran.

Surah Al-Imran is the third surah of the Quran, and together with Al-Baqarah they are called "az-Zahrawan", the two shining ones. It contains many lessons children need: working together instead of splitting apart, controlling anger and forgiving people, speaking gently, and reflecting on the sky and stars. In this guide, we explain Surah Al-Imran to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Imran

- **Position**: surah 3, in juz 3 and 4.
- **Number of verses**: 200.
- **Type**: Madinan.
- **Who are the family of Imran?** Imran was a righteous man from the Children of Israel, the father of Maryam. The "family of Imran" means his wife, his daughter Maryam and his grandson Isa, peace be upon them.

### Its virtue
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Recite the two shining ones, Al-Baqarah and Al-Imran, for they will come on the Day of Resurrection like two clouds... pleading for their companions" (Muslim).

## Lesson 1: The Du'a for a Steady Heart

﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾ (Quran 3:8)

### Explaining it to your child
"The believers pray: Our Lord, after You have guided us to Islam, don't let our hearts turn away from the truth, and give us mercy from You. You are Al-Wahhab, the One who gives generously."

### Activity: the morning du'a
Make this du'a part of your child's morning routine, after waking up or on the way to school, asking Allah to keep them firm on good all day.

## Lesson 2: Allah, Owner of All Sovereignty

﴿قُلِ ٱللَّهُمَّ مَٰلِكَ ٱلۡمُلۡكِ تُؤۡتِي ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتَنزِعُ ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مِمَّن تَشَآءُ وَتُعِزُّ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتُذِلُّ مَن تَشَآءُۖ بِيَدِكَ ٱلۡخَيۡرُۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 3:26)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah owns everything. He gives power to whom He wills and takes it from whom He wills. He honours whom He wills and humbles whom He wills. All good is in His hand, and He has power over everything. Kings and rich people have what they have from Allah, and He can take it away in a moment."

### Lesson for kids
"If someone has more toys or a bigger house than you, don't be jealous. Allah gives with wisdom, and all good is in His hand, so ask Him for what you want."

## Lesson 3: Maryam's Mother and Her Promise

﴿إِذۡ قَالَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتُ عِمۡرَٰنَ رَبِّ إِنِّي نَذَرۡتُ لَكَ مَا فِي بَطۡنِي مُحَرَّرٗا فَتَقَبَّلۡ مِنِّيٓۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا وَضَعَتۡهَا قَالَتۡ رَبِّ إِنِّي وَضَعۡتُهَآ أُنثَىٰ وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا وَضَعَتۡ وَلَيۡسَ ٱلذَّكَرُ كَٱلۡأُنثَىٰۖ وَإِنِّي سَمَّيۡتُهَا مَرۡيَمَ وَإِنِّيٓ أُعِيذُهَا بِكَ وَذُرِّيَّتَهَا مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ ٱلرَّجِيمِ﴾ (Quran 3:35-36)

### Explaining it to your child
"Imran's wife was pregnant, and she prayed: My Lord, I dedicate what is in my womb to You, to serve Your house and worship You. Accept it from me. When the baby was born, she found it was a girl! She said: My Lord, I have given birth to a girl. She named her Maryam and asked Allah to protect her and her children from Shaytan."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Every child that is born is touched by Shaytan at birth, so it cries out, except Maryam and her son." Then Abu Hurayrah recited: "And I seek refuge for her and her offspring with You from the accursed Shaytan" (al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lesson for kids: mothers' du'as
"Maryam's mother prayed for her daughter before and after she was born, and Allah answered. Your mother prays for you every day, so pray for her too: Rabbirhamhuma kama rabbayani saghira."

## Lesson 4: Maryam and the Provision in the Mihrab

﴿فَتَقَبَّلَهَا رَبُّهَا بِقَبُولٍ حَسَنٖ وَأَنۢبَتَهَا نَبَاتًا حَسَنٗا وَكَفَّلَهَا زَكَرِيَّاۖ كُلَّمَا دَخَلَ عَلَيۡهَا زَكَرِيَّا ٱلۡمِحۡرَابَ وَجَدَ عِندَهَا رِزۡقٗاۖ قَالَ يَٰمَرۡيَمُ أَنَّىٰ لَكِ هَٰذَاۖ قَالَتۡ هُوَ مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرۡزُقُ مَن يَشَآءُ بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٍ﴾ (Quran 3:37)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah accepted Maryam beautifully and helped her grow up beautifully in character and worship. Zakariya became her guardian. Every time he entered the mihrab, the place of worship, he found food with her. He asked: Where did you get this? She said: It is from Allah. Allah provides for whom He wills without measure."

### Lesson for kids: provision comes from Allah
"Maryam was a little girl, yet she knew that all provision comes from Allah. When you eat, remember that your dad works and your mum cooks, but the One who provides for all of you is Allah."

### Activity: "Where did this come from?"
At the dinner table, play "Where did this come from?" Ask about each food: "Where did the bread come from?" Your child traces it back: from the bakery, from flour, from wheat, from the earth, from rain Allah sent. Finally they say: "It is from Allah."

## Lesson 5: Zakariya's Du'a

﴿هُنَالِكَ دَعَا زَكَرِيَّا رَبَّهُۥۖ قَالَ رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي مِن لَّدُنكَ ذُرِّيَّةٗ طَيِّبَةًۖ إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ ٱلدُّعَآءِ﴾ (Quran 3:38)

### Explaining it to your child
"When Zakariya saw Allah's provision for Maryam, he remembered that Allah gives without limits. He was old and had no children. So right there, he prayed: My Lord, give me good children from You. You hear every du'a. Allah answered and gave him the good news of Yahya."

### Lesson for kids: learning from others
"Zakariya saw Allah's blessing on Maryam and wasn't jealous; he prayed for himself. When you see a blessing with a friend, say Masha'Allah, and ask Allah to give you from His bounty."

## Lesson 6: Hold Fast to the Rope of Allah

﴿وَٱعۡتَصِمُواْ بِحَبۡلِ ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعٗا وَلَا تَفَرَّقُواْۚ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ كُنتُمۡ أَعۡدَآءٗ فَأَلَّفَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِكُمۡ فَأَصۡبَحۡتُم بِنِعۡمَتِهِۦٓ إِخۡوَٰنٗا وَكُنتُمۡ عَلَىٰ شَفَا حُفۡرَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ فَأَنقَذَكُم مِّنۡهَاۗ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 3:103)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah tells us all to hold tight to the rope of Allah, the Quran and Islam, and not to split apart. Remember how you were enemies, with the tribes of Aws and Khazraj in Madinah fighting for years, and then Allah joined your hearts with Islam and you became brothers."

### Activity: the strong rope
Give your child a single thin thread and ask them to break it. Easy! Then twist ten threads together and ask them to break it. They can't! Say: "Muslims are like this: when they split apart they are weak, and when they stick together they are strong."

## Lesson 7: The Best Nation

﴿كُنتُمۡ خَيۡرَ أُمَّةٍ أُخۡرِجَتۡ لِلنَّاسِ تَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَتَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَتُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِۗ وَلَوۡ ءَامَنَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَكَانَ خَيۡرٗا لَّهُمۚ مِّنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 3:110)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah described the nation of Muhammad ﷺ as the best nation, for three reasons: they encourage what is good, they forbid what is wrong in a kind way, and they believe in Allah. If we do these things, we are part of the best nation."

## Lesson 8: Racing to Paradise and Controlling Anger

﴿۞وَسَارِعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ فِي ٱلسَّرَّآءِ وَٱلضَّرَّآءِ وَٱلۡكَٰظِمِينَ ٱلۡغَيۡظَ وَٱلۡعَافِينَ عَنِ ٱلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 3:133-134)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah says: Hurry towards forgiveness from your Lord and a Paradise as wide as the heavens and the earth! Who is it for? Those who give in good times and hard times, those who swallow their anger, holding it in when they're upset, and those who forgive people. Allah loves those who do good."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The strong person is not the one who wins in wrestling, but the one who controls himself when angry" (al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Activity: the three steps of anger
Teach your child three steps when they get angry:
1. **Hold it in**: stay quiet and say: A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim.
2. **Forgive**: let go of what the other person did.
3. **Do good**: be kind to them, like smiling or sharing something.
Each step is higher than the one before, and Allah loves those who do good.

## Lesson 9: The Prophet's Gentleness

﴿فَبِمَا رَحۡمَةٖ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ لِنتَ لَهُمۡۖ وَلَوۡ كُنتَ فَظًّا غَلِيظَ ٱلۡقَلۡبِ لَٱنفَضُّواْ مِنۡ حَوۡلِكَۖ فَٱعۡفُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ وَشَاوِرۡهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِۖ فَإِذَا عَزَمۡتَ فَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَوَكِّلِينَ﴾ (Quran 3:159)

### Explaining it to your child
"By Allah's mercy, the Prophet ﷺ was gentle with his companions and spoke to them kindly. If he had been harsh and hard-hearted, they would have left him. So Allah told him: Pardon them, ask forgiveness for them, consult them in decisions, and when you decide, rely on Allah."

### Activity: family shura
For a simple family decision, like where to go on an outing or what to eat on Friday, gather the family and ask everyone's opinion, even the youngest. Then say: "This is what Allah taught the Prophet ﷺ: consult them."

## Lesson 10: Reflecting on Creation

﴿إِنَّ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَذۡكُرُونَ ٱللَّهَ قِيَٰمٗا وَقُعُودٗا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِهِمۡ وَيَتَفَكَّرُونَ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقۡتَ هَٰذَا بَٰطِلٗا سُبۡحَٰنَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ (Quran 3:190-191)

### Explaining it to your child
"In the creation of the heavens and earth, and in night following day, there are signs for people of understanding. They remember Allah standing, sitting and lying on their sides, they think about the creation of the heavens and earth, and they say: Our Lord, You didn't create this without purpose."

### From the Sunnah
Al-Bukhari and Muslim reported that the Prophet ﷺ got up at night, looked at the sky, and recited these verses from the end of Surah Al-Imran.

### Activity: a night of reflection
On a clear night, go outside or onto the roof with your child and look at the sky, the moon and the stars. Ask: "Who raised this sky? Who lit these stars?" Then read the two verses together and say: "Rabbana ma khalaqta hadha batilan, subhanak."

## Let's Read Together: The Last Verse

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱصۡبِرُواْ وَصَابِرُواْ وَرَابِطُواْ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 3:200)

### Explaining it to your child
"The final advice of the surah: be patient in obeying Allah, be even more patient than others, stay firm, and be mindful of Allah so that you succeed. Success needs patience and steadiness."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Tug-of-War Team"

On sports day, there was a tug-of-war contest between two classes. Salman's team had strong children, but before the match they were arguing: "I'm going at the front!" "No, I'm the strongest!" And everyone pulled at different times.

The match started, and they lost the first round quickly, even though the other team was smaller. Salman sat down, upset. Their teacher came over and said: "Do you know why you lost? Everyone was pulling alone. Allah says: Hold fast to the rope of Allah together and do not split apart. Strength is in being united."

The teacher taught them: "Choose a leader who counts: one, two, three, and then you all pull at the same moment." Salman suggested his calm friend Umar as leader, because he never got angry, and everyone agreed.

In the second round, Umar counted clearly, the whole team pulled together, and they won! They won the third round too. They cheered and hugged each other.

At home, Salman told his father, who brought out the single thread and the ten twisted threads and said: "That's what happened to you: you were separate threads, then you became one rope."

Ask your child: "Why did Salman's team lose at first? What did they do to win?"

## What We Learn From Surah Al-Imran

- **Ask for a steady heart** on guidance.
- **Power and good** are in Allah's hand.
- **Mothers' du'as** for their children are answered.
- **Provision is from Allah** alone.
- **See others' blessings** and make du'a, without jealousy.
- **Unity is strength**, division is weakness.
- **Encourage good** kindly.
- **Control anger, forgive** and do good.
- **Be gentle** and consult others.
- **Reflect** on Allah's creation.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Should I tell my child about the Battle of Uhud mentioned in this surah?
Older children can learn it with a focus on obeying the leader. For younger ones, the story of Maryam and Zakariya and the lessons of character are enough.

### What are "the two shining ones"?
Surahs Al-Baqarah and Al-Imran, named for their light and guidance, as in the authentic hadith in Muslim.

### How can I teach my child to control their anger in practice?
Model it first: let them see us calm down when angry. Then teach them to seek refuge in Allah, change position from standing to sitting, and make wudu, all of which come in the Sunnah.
