﴿قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡۚ لِلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةࣱۗ وَأَرۡضُ ٱللَّهِ وَٰسِعَةٌۗ إِنَّمَا يُوَفَّى ٱلصَّـٰبِرُونَ أَجۡرَهُم بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ﴾ [Az-Zumar 39:10]

## Four parts
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Fear your Lord* | The foundation |
| *Good in this world for those who do good* | Excellence brings good even now |
| *Allah's earth is spacious* | If you cannot practise your faith in one place, move |
| *The patient receive their reward without measure* | A reward beyond counting |

## Without measure
- Most deeds are rewarded from ten to seven hundred times. Patience is singled out: its reward is **poured out** without calculation.

## Why patience?
- It includes all of religion: patience in obedience, patience away from sin, and patience in trials.

## The spacious earth
- Revealed in Makkah when believers were persecuted, it hinted at migration. For us it means: do not stay where your faith is lost if you can move.

## Lessons
- **Every moment of patience counts.**
- **Do not trap yourself** where you cannot obey Allah.
