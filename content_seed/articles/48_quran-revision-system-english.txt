Many students memorize several juz, then discover months later that most of it has slipped away. The reason is almost always the same: **focusing on new memorization and neglecting revision**. Every Quran teacher knows the rule: revision matters more than new memorization. If time is short, revise first.

## Why does memorization fade?
Memory weakens when it's not used. The Prophet ﷺ compared the Quran to a hobbled camel: "The example of the companion of the Quran is like the owner of hobbled camels: if he keeps watch over them he keeps them, and if he lets them loose they wander off" (Bukhari and Muslim).

## The three-track revision system
### Track 1: Recent memorization (daily)
What you memorized in roughly the **last two weeks**, about the last 5–10 pages. It's still fresh and needs daily repetition to settle into long-term memory.

**How:** before starting new memorization, recite yesterday's portion, then the last five pages, from memory.

### Track 2: Old memorization (rotating)
Everything else you've memorized, divided across the week so you go through all of it **every week or two**.

| Amount memorized | Suggested daily revision |
|---|---|
| 1–3 juz | a quarter of a juz |
| 5 juz | half a juz |
| 10 juz | one juz |
| 15+ juz | 1.5 to 2 juz |

### Track 3: Revision in prayer
Recite from your memorization in your Sunnah prayers and night prayer. Prayer tests your memory without a Mushaf, and you gain reward at the same time.

## The mistakes notebook
Keep a small notebook. Every time you stumble during revision, write the page number, the word, and the type of mistake. Before revising any juz, read your notes about it. After a few weeks you'll notice that your mistakes cluster in specific places, and those are the places that need focus.

## Handling similar verses (mutashābihāt)
Some verses repeat with tiny differences, and they're a classic cause of mix-ups. A famous example in Surah Al-Baqarah:
- Verse 48: ﴿وَلَا يُقۡبَلُ مِنۡهَا شَفَٰعَةٌ وَلَا يُؤۡخَذُ مِنۡهَا عَدۡلٌ﴾ (intercession first)
- Verse 123: ﴿وَلَا يُقۡبَلُ مِنۡهَا عَدۡلٌ وَلَا تَنفَعُهَا شَفَٰعَةٌ﴾ (compensation first)

**Tips:** write both verses side by side, highlight the difference, find a memory hook, and always revise them with the Mushaf open.

## Signs your system needs adjusting
- You stumble often in passages memorized less than a month ago.
- You can't connect the end of one surah to the next.
- Weeks pass without you revising some of your juz.

If you see these signs, **pause new memorization for a week or two** and focus only on revision. It's not lost time; it protects everything you've worked for.
