Welcome. If you have recently embraced Islam, the Quran may feel like a huge book in an unfamiliar language. You don't have to learn it all at once. The Prophet ﷺ said: «The best of you are those who learn the Quran and teach it» [Al-Bukhari].

## Step 1: Al-Fatihah for your prayer
- **Al-Fatihah is recited in every unit of prayer**, so learn it first.
- Learn it **ayah by ayah**, with its meaning.
- Read [Surah Al-Fatiha Common Mistakes](/blog/surah-al-fatiha-common-mistakes/).

## Step 2: A few short surahs
| Surah | Ayat |
|---|---|
| Al-Ikhlas | 4 |
| Al-Falaq | 5 |
| An-Nas | 6 |
| Al-Kawthar | 3 |
- These are enough to complete your prayers.

## Step 3: Read the meaning
- Choose a **trusted translation** and read a little every day.
- Remember: a translation is the meaning in another language, not the Quran itself.

## Step 4: Transliteration is a bridge, not a home
- Writing Arabic in English letters helps at the very start.
- But it **cannot show** letters like ع ح ق ض, so move to the Arabic script as soon as you can.

## Step 5: The Arabic letters
- Read [Arabic Letters Pronunciation for Non-Arabs](/blog/arabic-letters-pronunciation-for-non-arabs/).

## A 3-month starter plan
| Month | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1 | Al-Fatihah and Al-Ikhlas, with meaning |
| 2 | Al-Falaq, An-Nas, Al-Kawthar + Arabic letters |
| 3 | Reading short words in Arabic with a teacher |

## Be kind to yourself
- The Prophet ﷺ said the one who recites with difficulty gets **two rewards** [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].
