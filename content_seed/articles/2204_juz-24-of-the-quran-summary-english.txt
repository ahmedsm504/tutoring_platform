Juz 24 begins with ﴿۞فَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّن كَذَبَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ وَكَذَّبَ بِٱلصِّدۡقِ إِذۡ جَآءَهُۥٓۚ﴾, "Who does greater wrong than one who lies about Allah and denies the truth when it comes to him?", so it is called **Juz Fa Man Azlam**. It runs from **Az-Zumar 39:32 to Fussilat 41:46**, with all of **Surah Ghafir** (85 verses) in between. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 462 to 481**.

This is the juz of **hope, supplication and steadfastness**. It contains what many scholars consider the most hopeful verse in the Quran: ﴿يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of Allah's mercy." It begins the **Hawamim** with Surah Ghafir, contains the command to supplicate, ﴿ٱدۡعُونِيٓ أَسۡتَجِبۡ لَكُمۡۚ﴾, "Call on Me, I will answer you", and gives good news to the steadfast in Fussilat.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **the vastness of Allah's mercy and His acceptance of repentance, alongside His severe punishment of the obstinate**. The opening of Ghafir sums it up: ﴿غَافِرِ ٱلذَّنۢبِ وَقَابِلِ ٱلتَّوۡبِ شَدِيدِ ٱلۡعِقَابِ ذِي ٱلطَّوۡلِۖ﴾, "Forgiver of sin, Acceptor of repentance, severe in punishment, owner of abundance." The door is open to every transgressor in Az-Zumar, the angels seek forgiveness for those who repent in Ghafir, and the angels descend with good news upon the steadfast in Fussilat.

The message: **do not despair of Allah's mercy however much you have transgressed; call on Him, for He answers; then stand firm on the path.**

## Part one: the rest of Surah Az-Zumar (32-75)

### 1. The liar, the truthful and sufficiency (32-41)
"Who does greater wrong than one who lies about Allah and denies the truth when it comes to him? Is there not in Hell a residence for the disbelievers?" Then the one who brought the truth and those who affirmed it: they are the God-conscious. Then reassurance: ﴿أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِكَافٍ عَبۡدَهُۥۖ وَيُخَوِّفُونَكَ بِٱلَّذِينَ مِن دُونِهِۦۚ﴾, "Is not Allah sufficient for His servant? Yet they frighten you with those besides Him." Then: ﴿وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ لَيَقُولُنَّ ٱللَّهُۚ قُلۡ أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ إِنۡ أَرَادَنِيَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرٍّ هَلۡ هُنَّ كَٰشِفَٰتُ ضُرِّهِۦٓ﴾, "If you ask them who created the heavens and earth, they will say: Allah. Say: have you considered what you call on besides Allah? If Allah intended harm for me, could they remove His harm?"

**Lesson**: whoever has Allah as his sufficiency fears no one.

### 2. The lesser and greater death (42-52)
﴿ٱللَّهُ يَتَوَفَّى ٱلۡأَنفُسَ حِينَ مَوۡتِهَا وَٱلَّتِي لَمۡ تَمُتۡ فِي مَنَامِهَاۖ فَيُمۡسِكُ ٱلَّتِي قَضَىٰ عَلَيۡهَا ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَيُرۡسِلُ ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰٓ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمًّىۚ﴾, "Allah takes the souls at the time of their death, and those that have not died during their sleep. He keeps those for which He has decreed death and releases the others until an appointed term." Sleep is a lesser death, hence the bedtime supplication in Al-Bukhari and Muslim: "In Your name, my Lord, I lay down my side and by You I raise it. If You keep my soul, have mercy on it; and if You release it, protect it."

Then: when Allah alone is mentioned, the hearts of those who do not believe in the Hereafter shrink in aversion. Then man's fickleness: when harm touches him he calls on Us; then when We grant him a favour he says: I was only given it because of knowledge.

### 3. The most hopeful verse in the Quran (53-61)
A new quarter opens with the call of mercy: ﴿۞قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾, "Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of Allah's mercy. Allah forgives all sins. He is the Forgiving, the Merciful." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas reported that some idolaters who had killed much and committed zina much came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: what you call to is good; if only you would tell us there is an expiation for what we have done. Then this was revealed. Read [Quran 39:53 Explained: Allah Forgives All Sins for the Repentant](/blog/ya-ibadiya-alladhina-asrafu-meaning-english/).

**Reflect on the call**:
- "O My servants": He attributes them to Himself despite their excess.
- "transgressed against themselves": the excess harmed them, not Allah.
- "all sins": no sin is excluded for one who repents.

Then the practical condition: ﴿وَأَنِيبُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمۡ وَأَسۡلِمُواْ لَهُۥ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَكُمُ ٱلۡعَذَابُ ثُمَّ لَا تُنصَرُونَ﴾, "Turn back to your Lord and submit to Him before the punishment comes, when you will not be helped." Then a warning against regret: ﴿أَن تَقُولَ نَفۡسࣱ يَٰحَسۡرَتَىٰ عَلَىٰ مَا فَرَّطتُ فِي جَنۢبِ ٱللَّهِ﴾, "lest a soul should say: alas for my neglect of my duty to Allah."

### 4. Deeds nullified and Allah's greatness (62-67)
﴿وَلَقَدۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡكَ وَإِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكَ لَئِنۡ أَشۡرَكۡتَ لَيَحۡبَطَنَّ عَمَلُكَ وَلَتَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ﴾, "It has been revealed to you and to those before you: if you associate partners with Allah, your deeds will be nullified and you will be among the losers." Then: ﴿وَمَا قَدَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ حَقَّ قَدۡرِهِۦ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ جَمِيعٗا قَبۡضَتُهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ مَطۡوِيَّـٰتُۢ بِيَمِينِهِۦۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾, "They have not appraised Allah as He deserves, while the whole earth will be in His grip on the Day of Resurrection and the heavens folded in His right hand. Glory be to Him, exalted above what they associate."

### 5. The groups led to Hell and Paradise (68-75)
﴿وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَصَعِقَ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا مَن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۖ ثُمَّ نُفِخَ فِيهِ أُخۡرَىٰ فَإِذَا هُمۡ قِيَامࣱ يَنظُرُونَ﴾, "The Horn will be blown and whoever is in the heavens and earth will fall dead, except whom Allah wills; then it will be blown again and at once they will be standing, looking on." Then the Record is placed and the prophets and witnesses are brought. Then two contrasting scenes:
- ﴿وَسِيقَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ جَهَنَّمَ زُمَرًاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءُوهَا فُتِحَتۡ أَبۡوَٰبُهَا﴾, "Those who disbelieved will be driven to Hell in groups, until when they reach it, its gates are opened."
- ﴿وَسِيقَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ رَبَّهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡجَنَّةِ زُمَرًاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءُوهَا وَفُتِحَتۡ أَبۡوَٰبُهَا وَقَالَ لَهُمۡ خَزَنَتُهَا سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡ طِبۡتُمۡ فَٱدۡخُلُوهَا خَٰلِدِينَ﴾, "Those who feared their Lord will be driven to Paradise in groups, until when they reach it, its gates having been opened, its keepers will say: peace be upon you, you have done well, so enter it to abide forever."

**Reflect on the "and"**: for Hell, "its gates are opened" without "and": they open suddenly on arrival. For Paradise, "its gates having been opened" with "and": opened before they arrive, in honour of them.

The close: ﴿وَتَرَى ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ حَآفِّينَ مِنۡ حَوۡلِ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡۚ وَقُضِيَ بَيۡنَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ وَقِيلَ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "You will see the angels surrounding the Throne, glorifying their Lord with praise. It will be judged between them in truth, and it will be said: praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds."

## Part two: Surah Ghafir (1-85)

Ghafir is Makki, with 85 verses, also called Surah Al-Mu'min (The Believer) after the believer from Pharaoh's family. It is the first of the seven **Hawamim** (Ghafir to Al-Ahqaf). For the whole surah see [Surah Ghafir Meaning in English](/blog/surah-ghafir-meaning-english/).

### 1. Allah's comprehensive attributes (1-6)
﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ﴾ ﴿غَافِرِ ٱلذَّنۢبِ وَقَابِلِ ٱلتَّوۡبِ شَدِيدِ ٱلۡعِقَابِ ذِي ٱلطَّوۡلِۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ إِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾, "Ha Mim. The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Almighty, the All-Knowing, Forgiver of sin, Acceptor of repentance, severe in punishment, owner of abundance. There is no god but Him; to Him is the destination."

**Reflect on the order**: two attributes of mercy (forgiving sin, accepting repentance), then one of punishment, then one of bounty. Mercy comes first.

### 2. The prayer of the Throne-bearers (7-9)
﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَحۡمِلُونَ ٱلۡعَرۡشَ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهُۥ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَيُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦ وَيَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْۖ رَبَّنَا وَسِعۡتَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖ رَّحۡمَةٗ وَعِلۡمٗا﴾, "Those who carry the Throne and those around it glorify their Lord with praise, believe in Him and ask forgiveness for those who believe: our Lord, You encompass all things in mercy and knowledge", then ﴿رَبَّنَا وَأَدۡخِلۡهُمۡ جَنَّـٰتِ عَدۡنٍ ٱلَّتِي وَعَدتَّهُمۡ وَمَن صَلَحَ مِنۡ ءَابَآئِهِمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِهِمۡۚ﴾, "our Lord, admit them to the Gardens of 'Adn You promised them, and whoever was righteous among their fathers, spouses and offspring", and protect them from evils.

**Lesson**: the greatest of angels pray for you without your knowing, simply because you are a believer who repents. Notice how their prayer includes fathers, spouses and children: your righteousness lifts your family.

### 3. The Day of Meeting (10-20)
The disbelievers' confession: our Lord, You caused us to die twice and gave us life twice, so we admit our sins. Then the scene of absolute sovereignty: ﴿يَوۡمَ هُم بَٰرِزُونَۖ لَا يَخۡفَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مِنۡهُمۡ شَيۡءࣱۚ لِّمَنِ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡوَٰحِدِ ٱلۡقَهَّارِ﴾, "The Day they come forth, nothing of them hidden from Allah. Whose is the dominion today? It is Allah's, the One, the Subduer." Then Allah's precise knowledge: ﴿يَعۡلَمُ خَآئِنَةَ ٱلۡأَعۡيُنِ وَمَا تُخۡفِي ٱلصُّدُورُ﴾, "He knows the treachery of the eyes and what the chests conceal."

**Reflect**: even a treacherous glance no one else sees is known to Allah.

### 4. The believer of Pharaoh's family (21-55)
A new quarter: ﴿۞أَوَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَانُواْ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ﴾, "Have they not travelled through the land and seen the end of those before them?" Then the story of Musa and Pharaoh, when Pharaoh said: let me kill Musa. A man rose:

﴿وَقَالَ رَجُلࣱ مُّؤۡمِنࣱ مِّنۡ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ يَكۡتُمُ إِيمَٰنَهُۥٓ أَتَقۡتُلُونَ رَجُلًا أَن يَقُولَ رَبِّيَ ٱللَّهُ وَقَدۡ جَآءَكُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ وَإِن يَكُ كَٰذِبٗا فَعَلَيۡهِ كَذِبُهُۥۖ﴾, "A believing man from Pharaoh's family, who concealed his faith, said: will you kill a man for saying 'My Lord is Allah', when he has brought you clear proofs from your Lord? If he is lying, his lie is upon him."

**His method of calling**:
1. **Calm logic**: if he lies, it is on him; if he is truthful, some of what he warns of will befall you.
2. **Reminding of history**: my people, I fear for you a day like that of the confederates, like the fate of Nuh's people, 'Ad and Thamud.
3. **Reminding of Yusuf**: Yusuf came to you before with clear proofs.
4. **Open invitation**: ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَامَنَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُونِ أَهۡدِكُمۡ سَبِيلَ ٱلرَّشَادِ﴾ ﴿يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنَّمَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا مَتَٰعࣱ وَإِنَّ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ هِيَ دَارُ ٱلۡقَرَارِ﴾, "Follow me, my people, I will guide you to the right way. My people, this worldly life is only enjoyment, and the Hereafter is the home of permanence."
5. **The contrast**: Hizb 48 begins: ﴿۞وَيَٰقَوۡمِ مَا لِيٓ أَدۡعُوكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلنَّجَوٰةِ وَتَدۡعُونَنِيٓ إِلَى ٱلنَّارِ﴾, "My people, how is it that I invite you to salvation while you invite me to the Fire?"
6. **Entrusting**: ﴿فَسَتَذۡكُرُونَ مَآ أَقُولُ لَكُمۡۚ وَأُفَوِّضُ أَمۡرِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَصِيرُۢ بِٱلۡعِبَادِ﴾, "You will remember what I tell you, and I entrust my affair to Allah; Allah sees His servants."

The result: ﴿فَوَقَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ سَيِّـَٔاتِ مَا مَكَرُواْۖ وَحَاقَ بِـَٔالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ سُوٓءُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ﴾ ﴿ٱلنَّارُ يُعۡرَضُونَ عَلَيۡهَا غُدُوّٗا وَعَشِيّٗاۚ﴾, "So Allah protected him from the evils they plotted, and the worst punishment enveloped Pharaoh's people: the Fire, to which they are exposed morning and evening", among the evidences for punishment in the grave.

In between, Pharaoh's arrogance: ﴿وَقَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ يَٰهَٰمَٰنُ ٱبۡنِ لِي صَرۡحٗا لَّعَلِّيٓ أَبۡلُغُ ٱلۡأَسۡبَٰبَ﴾, "Pharaoh said: Haman, build me a tower, that I may reach the pathways."

Then the promise: ﴿إِنَّا لَنَنصُرُ رُسُلَنَا وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَيَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلۡأَشۡهَٰدُ﴾, "We will surely help Our messengers and those who believe in this worldly life and on the Day the witnesses stand", then ﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ وَٱلۡإِبۡكَٰرِ﴾, "So be patient; Allah's promise is true. Ask forgiveness for your sin and glorify your Lord with praise evening and morning."

### 5. "Call on Me, I will answer you" (56-65)
The creation of the heavens and earth is greater than the creation of people, and the blind and the seeing are not equal. Then the great command: ﴿وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدۡعُونِيٓ أَسۡتَجِبۡ لَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ عَنۡ عِبَادَتِي سَيَدۡخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ﴾, "Your Lord has said: call on Me, I will answer you. Those who are too proud to worship Me will enter Hell humiliated." In Sunan At-Tirmidhi the Prophet ﷺ said: "Supplication is worship", then recited this verse.

**Reflect**: Allah called abandoning supplication "being too proud to worship Me". Supplication is not merely asking; it is worship. Then: ﴿هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ فَٱدۡعُوهُ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَۗ﴾, "He is the Ever-Living; there is no god but Him, so call on Him, sincere to Him in religion."

### 6. The stages of creation and the close (66-85)
A new quarter: ﴿۞قُلۡ إِنِّي نُهِيتُ أَنۡ أَعۡبُدَ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ﴾, "Say: I have been forbidden to worship those you call on besides Allah." Then the stages of creation: ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ مِنۡ عَلَقَةٖ ثُمَّ يُخۡرِجُكُمۡ طِفۡلٗا ثُمَّ لِتَبۡلُغُوٓاْ أَشُدَّكُمۡ ثُمَّ لِتَكُونُواْ شُيُوخٗاۚ﴾, "He created you from dust, then a drop, then a clinging clot, then brings you out as a child, then lets you reach maturity, then become old." The close: the faith of despair that does not help: ﴿فَلَمۡ يَكُ يَنفَعُهُمۡ إِيمَٰنُهُمۡ لَمَّا رَأَوۡاْ بَأۡسَنَاۖ سُنَّتَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِي قَدۡ خَلَتۡ فِي عِبَادِهِۦۖ﴾, "Their faith did not benefit them once they saw Our punishment: the way of Allah that has been with His servants."

## Part three: the start of Surah Fussilat (1-46)

Fussilat is Makki, with 54 verses, also called "Ha Mim As-Sajdah"; 46 of its verses fall in this juz. For the whole surah see [Surah Fussilat Meaning in English](/blog/surah-fussilat-meaning-english/).

### 1. A detailed Quran and turning away (1-8)
﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلࣱ مِّنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾ ﴿كِتَٰبࣱ فُصِّلَتۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ قُرۡءَانًا عَرَبِيّٗا لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿بَشِيرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا فَأَعۡرَضَ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ﴾, "Ha Mim. A revelation from the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate: a Book whose verses are detailed, an Arabic Quran for people who know, giving good news and warning; but most of them turn away, so they do not hear." Then their declaration of separation: ﴿وَقَالُواْ قُلُوبُنَا فِيٓ أَكِنَّةٖ مِّمَّا تَدۡعُونَآ إِلَيۡهِ وَفِيٓ ءَاذَانِنَا وَقۡرࣱ وَمِنۢ بَيۡنِنَا وَبَيۡنِكَ حِجَابࣱ﴾, "They said: our hearts are covered from what you call us to, in our ears is deafness, and between us and you is a screen." The reply: ﴿قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ أَنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱ فَٱسۡتَقِيمُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُوهُۗ﴾, "Say: I am only a man like you; it is revealed to me that your god is one God, so be upright towards Him and seek His forgiveness."

### 2. Creation in six days (9-18)
A new quarter: ﴿۞قُلۡ أَئِنَّكُمۡ لَتَكۡفُرُونَ بِٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِي يَوۡمَيۡنِ وَتَجۡعَلُونَ لَهُۥٓ أَندَادٗاۚ﴾, "Say: do you disbelieve in the One who created the earth in two days, and attribute equals to Him?", then the mountains and provisions completing four days, then ﴿ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَهِيَ دُخَانࣱ فَقَالَ لَهَا وَلِلۡأَرۡضِ ٱئۡتِيَا طَوۡعًا أَوۡ كَرۡهٗا قَالَتَآ أَتَيۡنَا طَآئِعِينَ﴾, "Then He turned to the heaven while it was smoke, and said to it and to the earth: come, willingly or unwillingly. They said: we come willingly." He made them seven heavens in two days. Then a warning of a thunderbolt like that of 'Ad and Thamud.

**Reflect**: the heavens and earth answered "willingly"; why then does man disobey?

### 3. The testimony of the limbs (19-24)
﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا مَا جَآءُوهَا شَهِدَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَمۡعُهُمۡ وَأَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ وَجُلُودُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ لِجُلُودِهِمۡ لِمَ شَهِدتُّمۡ عَلَيۡنَاۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَنطَقَنَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنطَقَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖۚ﴾, "When they reach it, their hearing, sight and skins will testify against them about what they did. They will say to their skins: why did you testify against us? They will say: Allah made us speak, who made everything speak." Then: you were not hiding from your hearing, sight and skins testifying against you; rather you assumed that Allah did not know much of what you did.

**Lesson**: thinking poorly of Allah's knowledge is the root of boldness in sin.

### 4. Companions and noise against the Quran (25-29)
A new quarter: ﴿۞وَقَيَّضۡنَا لَهُمۡ قُرَنَآءَ فَزَيَّنُواْ لَهُم مَّا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡ﴾, "We appointed for them companions who made attractive to them what was before them and behind them." Then: the disbelievers said, do not listen to this Quran and make noise over it, that perhaps you may overcome. In the Fire they will say: our Lord, show us those of jinn and men who misled us, that we may put them beneath our feet.

### 5. Good news for the steadfast (30-36)
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ تَتَنَزَّلُ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ أَلَّا تَخَافُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَبۡشِرُواْ بِٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ﴾ ﴿نَحۡنُ أَوۡلِيَآؤُكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ﴾, "Those who said 'Our Lord is Allah' and then stood firm, the angels descend upon them: do not fear and do not grieve, and receive good news of Paradise, which you were promised. We are your allies in this world and the Hereafter", ﴿نُزُلٗا مِّنۡ غَفُورٖ رَّحِيمٖ﴾, "as hospitality from One Forgiving and Merciful." In Sahih Muslim, Sufyan ibn 'Abdullah said: Messenger of Allah, tell me something about Islam that I will not need to ask anyone after you. He said: "Say: I believe in Allah, then stand firm."

Then the best of speech: ﴿وَمَنۡ أَحۡسَنُ قَوۡلٗا مِّمَّن دَعَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا وَقَالَ إِنَّنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾, "Who is better in speech than one who calls to Allah, does good and says: I am of the Muslims?" Then the method for dealing with one who wrongs you: ﴿وَلَا تَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡحَسَنَةُ وَلَا ٱلسَّيِّئَةُۚ ٱدۡفَعۡ بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ فَإِذَا ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَكَ وَبَيۡنَهُۥ عَدَٰوَةࣱ كَأَنَّهُۥ وَلِيٌّ حَمِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُواْ وَمَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ذُو حَظٍّ عَظِيمٖ﴾, "Good and evil are not equal. Repel evil with what is better, and the one with whom there was enmity will become as a devoted friend. None is granted it except the patient, and none is granted it except one with a great share of good."

**Lesson**: steadfastness after faith is the secret of the good news, and repelling evil with good turns an enemy into a friend, though it needs patience and a great share of good. Read [Quran 41:34-35 Explained: Turning an Enemy into a Close Friend](/blog/idfa-billati-hiya-ahsan-meaning-english/).

### 6. Signs of night and day, and the prostration (37-39)
﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِ ٱلَّيۡلُ وَٱلنَّهَارُ وَٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُۚ لَا تَسۡجُدُواْ لِلشَّمۡسِ وَلَا لِلۡقَمَرِ وَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَهُنَّ إِن كُنتُمۡ إِيَّاهُ تَعۡبُدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَإِنِ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ فَٱلَّذِينَ عِندَ رَبِّكَ يُسَبِّحُونَ لَهُۥ بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡـَٔمُونَ﴾, "Among His signs are the night and day, the sun and moon. Do not prostrate to the sun or the moon, but prostrate to Allah who created them, if it is Him you worship. If they are arrogant, those with your Lord glorify Him night and day and never tire": the place of prostration. Then the reviving of the still earth as evidence of resurrection.

### 7. The preservation of the Quran and the close (40-46)
﴿لَّا يَأۡتِيهِ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَلَا مِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦۖ تَنزِيلࣱ مِّنۡ حَكِيمٍ حَمِيدٖ﴾, "Falsehood cannot approach it from before it or from behind it: a revelation from One Wise and Praiseworthy." Then: ﴿قُلۡ هُوَ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ هُدٗى وَشِفَآءࣱۚ﴾, "Say: for those who believe, it is guidance and healing." The last verse of the juz: ﴿مَّنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَنۡ أَسَآءَ فَعَلَيۡهَاۗ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِظَلَّـٰمٖ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ﴾, "Whoever does good, it is for himself; whoever does evil, it is against himself; and your Lord is never unjust to His servants."

## Tajweed points in Juz 24

### ﴿ءَا۬عۡجَمِيࣱّ﴾ in Fussilat 44
The only place in Hafs where the second hamzah must be eased (*tas-hil*, between hamzah and alif), marked in the Mushaf by a special dot.

### ﴿حمٓ﴾ at the start of Ghafir and Fussilat
Read *Ha, Mim*: Ha two counts, Mim a necessary six-count madd.

### ﴿ٱئۡتِيَا﴾ in Fussilat 11
If you begin a recitation with it, start with a kasrah connecting hamzah followed by a madd ya: *itiya*.

### ﴿يَسۡـَٔمُونَ﴾ in Fussilat 38
A fathah hamzah after a sakin sin: pronounce it clearly.

### The prostration of the juz
In Fussilat at ﴿وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡـَٔمُونَ﴾ (verse 38), as marked in the Madinah Mushaf. See [Sujud al-Tilawah in English](/blog/sujud-al-tilawa-english/).

## How to memorize Juz 24 with understanding

1. **Az-Zumar** ends with two contrasting scenes: memorize them together, noting the *wa*.
2. **Divide Ghafir into three**: Allah's attributes and the Throne-bearers (1-20), the believer of Pharaoh's family (21-55), supplication and the close (56-85).
3. **Learn the believer's speech** by the recurring "my people".
4. **Fussilat**: creation, testimony, companions, the steadfast, the prostration, the preservation of the Quran.
5. **Start a notebook for the Hawamim** recording their openings and endings.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 24

- **Let Allah suffice you**: He is enough for His servant.
- **Remember the lesser death** each night and say the bedtime supplication.
- **Do not despair of Allah's mercy**, however much you have transgressed.
- **Repent before punishment comes** and before regret.
- **Beware shirk**: it nullifies deeds.
- **Know that angels seek forgiveness for you** if you repent.
- **Guard your glances**: Allah knows the treachery of the eyes.
- **Call to Allah with wisdom**, like the believer of Pharaoh's family.
- **Entrust your affair to Allah** after doing your part.
- **Call on Allah**: supplication is worship.
- **Think rightly of Allah's knowledge**, so you are not bold in sin.
- **Stand firm** after saying "my Lord is Allah".
- **Repel evil with what is better** and win over your enemy.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 24 called "Fa Man Azlam"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞فَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّن كَذَبَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ﴾.

### What is the most hopeful verse in the Quran?
Many scholars count Az-Zumar 53 as the most hopeful verse: ﴿قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾.

### What are the Hawamim?
The seven surahs beginning with Ha Mim: Ghafir, Fussilat, Ash-Shura, Az-Zukhruf, Ad-Dukhan, Al-Jathiyah and Al-Ahqaf.

### How many prostrations are in Juz 24?
One, in Fussilat, verse 38.

### Where does Juz 24 end?
At Fussilat 46. Juz 25 begins with ﴿۞إِلَيۡهِ يُرَدُّ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِۚ﴾, "To Him is referred knowledge of the Hour."

## Conclusion

Juz 24 opens the widest doors of hope: a call to transgressors not to despair, Throne-bearing angels seeking forgiveness for those who repent, and a Lord who says "Call on Me, I will answer you." It shows you a believer who stood in Pharaoh's palace, called with wisdom, then entrusted his affair to Allah and was protected; and it gives the steadfast good news of angels descending upon them: do not fear and do not grieve.
