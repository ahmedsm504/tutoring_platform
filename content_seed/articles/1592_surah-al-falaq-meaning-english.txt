**Surah Al-Falaq** and **Surah An-Nas** are called *al-muʿawwidhatān*: the two surahs of seeking refuge.

## Ayah 1
﴿قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلۡفَلَقِ﴾ — *Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak.*
- **Al-Falaq**: the breaking of dawn. The One who brings light out of darkness can protect you from every darkness.

## The four evils
| Ayah | Evil |
|---|---|
| ﴿مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ﴾ | The evil of everything He created that has harm in it |
| ﴿وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ﴾ | The night when its darkness settles |
| ﴿وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّـٰثَٰتِ فِي ٱلۡعُقَدِ﴾ | Those who blow on knots: magic |
| ﴿وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ﴾ | The envier when he acts on his envy |

## When to recite it
- **After every prayer**: the Prophet ﷺ told ʿUqbah ibn ʿĀmir to recite the muʿawwidhāt after each prayer [Abu Dawud].
- **Before sleep**: the Prophet ﷺ would blow into his hands, recite Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas, and wipe over his body [Al-Bukhari].
- **Morning and evening**: three times each [Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi].

## Recitation tips
- ﴿ٱلۡفَلَقِ﴾: major qalqalah on the qaf when stopping.
- ﴿شَرِّ﴾: don't over-roll the raa.
- ﴿حَاسِدٍ إِذَا﴾: clear tanween before the hamza.
