## "Cooperate in Righteousness and Piety": Quran 5:2 Explained

﴿وَتَعَاوَنُواْ عَلَى ٱلۡبِرِّ وَٱلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُواْ عَلَى ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِۚ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:2]

### The context
- The verse speaks about the sanctity of Allah's symbols and the sacred months. It mentions people who once blocked Muslims from al-Masjid al-Haram, and says: **do not let hatred of them push you to aggression**. Then comes the general rule of cooperation.

### Two kinds of cooperation
| Command | Includes |
|---|---|
| Cooperate in *birr* | All good deeds: charity, teaching, helping, kindness |
| Cooperate in *taqwa* | Avoiding what Allah forbade, and helping others avoid it |
| Do not cooperate in sin | Not helping anyone do wrong, even a friend |
| Do not cooperate in aggression | Not joining in harming others' rights |

### Birr and taqwa together
- Scholars explain that *birr* is doing good and *taqwa* is guarding from evil. Together they cover the whole religion.

### Everyday applications
- Helping a neighbour, teaching a child the Quran, volunteering, supporting a family member to quit a bad habit.
- Refusing to share a harmful rumour or to sign a false document.

### Lessons
- **You are responsible for what you help with**, not only what you do.
- **Past wrongs do not justify injustice.**

## "Today I Have Perfected Your Religion for You": Quran 5:3 Explained

﴿ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أَكۡمَلۡتُ لَكُمۡ دِينَكُمۡ وَأَتۡمَمۡتُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ نِعۡمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمَ دِينٗاۚ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:3]

### When was it revealed?
- ʿUmar ibn al-Khattab said it was revealed on **Friday, at ʿArafah**, during the Farewell Hajj (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### A Jewish man's remark
- A man of the Jews told ʿUmar that if this verse had been revealed to them, they would have taken that day as a festival. ʿUmar replied that he knew the day and place of its revelation (same narration).

### Three statements
| Statement | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *I have perfected your religion* | Its beliefs and laws are complete |
| *I have completed My favour* | Guidance, victory and the Haram secured |
| *I have approved Islam as your religion* | Allah is pleased with it as the way of life |

### No additions needed
- Since the religion is complete, **inventing new acts of worship** is not needed. The Prophet ﷺ warned against innovation in religion.

### Within a verse on food
- It appears in the middle of rulings on forbidden foods, then the verse continues with the permission for one forced by hunger. Even the completion of the religion comes with mercy.

### Lessons
- **Be grateful** for a complete guidance.
- **Follow, do not invent.**

## "Let Not Hatred of a People Lead You to Injustice": Quran 5:8 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُواْ قَوَّـٰمِينَ لِلَّهِ شُهَدَآءَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَلَا يَجۡرِمَنَّكُمۡ شَنَـَٔانُ قَوۡمٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَعۡدِلُواْۚ ٱعۡدِلُواْ هُوَ أَقۡرَبُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:8]

### The words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *La yajrimannakum* | Let it not drive you |
| *Shanaʾan* | Intense hatred |

### Justice with those you dislike
- Hatred never justifies injustice. Justice is required even with opponents and enemies.

### Two complementary verses
| An-Nisa 4:135 | Al-Maidah 5:8 |
|---|---|
| *Standing firm for justice, witnesses for Allah* | *Standing firm for Allah, witnesses in justice* |
| Even against yourselves and relatives | Even with those you hate |

- Some scholars note: one prevents bias from love, the other prevents bias from hatred.

### Nearest to taqwa
- *Be just; that is nearer to taqwa.*

### Lessons
- **Do not wrong** someone who wronged you.
- **Speak the truth** about opponents as about friends.

## "Allah Only Accepts from the Pious": Quran 5:27 Explained

﴿۞وَٱتۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ نَبَأَ ٱبۡنَيۡ ءَادَمَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ إِذۡ قَرَّبَا قُرۡبَانٗا فَتُقُبِّلَ مِنۡ أَحَدِهِمَا وَلَمۡ يُتَقَبَّلۡ مِنَ ٱلۡأٓخَرِ قَالَ لَأَقۡتُلَنَّكَۖ قَالَ إِنَّمَا يَتَقَبَّلُ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:27]

### "In truth"
- The story is told as it truly happened, without additions.

### Two responses
| Brother | Response |
|---|---|
| The one rejected | Envy and a threat to kill |
| The one accepted | Advice pointing to taqwa |

### The condition of acceptance
- He did not say *I am better than you*. He pointed to the **reason**: taqwa. A deed is accepted by sincerity and correctness, not appearance.

### Fear after worship
- ʿAishah asked the Prophet ﷺ about *those who give what they give while their hearts are fearful* (Al-Muminun 23:60). He explained they are those who pray, fast and give charity, fearing it may not be accepted (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### Lessons
- **Do not envy**; improve your own deeds.
- **Ask Allah to accept** after every act of worship.

## "Whoever Saves One Life, It Is as If He Saved All Mankind": Quran 5:32 Explained

﴿مِنۡ أَجۡلِ ذَٰلِكَ كَتَبۡنَا عَلَىٰ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ أَنَّهُۥ مَن قَتَلَ نَفۡسَۢا بِغَيۡرِ نَفۡسٍ أَوۡ فَسَادٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَكَأَنَّمَا قَتَلَ ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗا وَمَنۡ أَحۡيَاهَا فَكَأَنَّمَآ أَحۡيَا ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗاۚ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُنَا بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ ثُمَّ إِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنۡهُم بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَمُسۡرِفُونَ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:32]

### "Because of that"
- The verse follows the story of the first murder: one son of Adam killed his brother out of envy. **Because of that**, the law was made clear.

### One life equals all
| Act | Weight |
|---|---|
| Killing one innocent | As if killing all humanity |
| Saving one life | As if saving all humanity |

- Scholars explain: whoever dares to kill one innocent has broken the sanctity of all lives; whoever protects one has honoured the sanctity of all.

### Saving a life today
- Medical care, rescue, donating blood, preventing violence, and feeding the hungry are all forms of "giving life".

### The first murderer's share
- The Prophet ﷺ said no soul is killed unjustly except that the first son of Adam has a share of its sin, because he was the first to establish killing (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Every life is sacred.**
- **Saving one person is a huge deed.**

## Surah Al-Maidah Meaning: Fulfil Your Contracts and the Completion of the Religion

**Surah Al-Maidah** (The Table Spread, 120 ayat) is among the last surahs revealed.

### Fulfil your contracts
- It opens: *O you who believe, fulfil your contracts*, covering promises with Allah and with people.

### The completion of the religion
- Within the verse on forbidden foods comes a historic statement:
*Today I have perfected for you your religion, completed My favour upon you, and approved Islam as your religion.*
- Revealed at ʿArafah during the Farewell Hajj, as reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Justice even with those you dislike
- *Do not let hatred of a people prevent you from being just. Be just; that is nearer to piety.*

### The two sons of Adam
- One son's offering was accepted and the other's was not; the second killed his brother out of envy, then learned from a raven how to bury him.
﴿مِنۡ أَجۡلِ ذَٰلِكَ كَتَبۡنَا عَلَىٰ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ أَنَّهُۥ مَن قَتَلَ نَفۡسَۢا بِغَيۡرِ نَفۡسٍ أَوۡ فَسَادٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَكَأَنَّمَا قَتَلَ ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗا وَمَنۡ أَحۡيَاهَا فَكَأَنَّمَآ أَحۡيَا ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗاۚ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُنَا بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ ثُمَّ إِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنۡهُم بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَمُسۡرِفُونَ﴾

### The table from heaven
- The disciples asked ʿIsa for a table of food from the sky. He prayed, and Allah answered with a warning to those who would disbelieve afterwards.

### ʿIsa on the Day of Judgment
- Allah will ask ʿIsa whether he told people to worship him and his mother; he will deny it and say: *You know what is within me.*

### Lessons
- **Keep promises**: they are part of faith.
- **Every life is sacred.**

## "He Will Bring a People He Loves and Who Love Him": Quran 5:54 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَن يَرۡتَدَّ مِنكُمۡ عَن دِينِهِۦ فَسَوۡفَ يَأۡتِي ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ يُحِبُّهُمۡ وَيُحِبُّونَهُۥٓ أَذِلَّةٍ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَعِزَّةٍ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ يُجَٰهِدُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا يَخَافُونَ لَوۡمَةَ لَآئِمٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:54]

### The religion needs no one
- Whoever turns away does not harm Allah; He brings a better people.

### Love first from Allah
- *He loves them* comes **before** *they love Him*: His love for them is what enabled their love for Him.

### Four qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Humble toward believers | Gentle, merciful, modest |
| Firm against disbelievers | Dignified, not yielding to falsehood |
| Striving in Allah's way | Effort to support truth |
| Not fearing blame | Saying the truth regardless of criticism |

### Allah's favour
- *That is the favour of Allah, He gives it to whom He wills*: ask Him for it.

### Lessons
- **Be humble with your brothers.**
- **Hold to truth** even if people blame you.

## "Take Care of Yourselves; Those Astray Cannot Harm You If You Are Guided": Quran 5:105 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَنفُسَكُمۡۖ لَا يَضُرُّكُم مَّن ضَلَّ إِذَا ٱهۡتَدَيۡتُمۡۚ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ جَمِيعٗا فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:105]

### The meaning
- Hold to reforming yourselves; if you are guided, the misguidance of others will not harm you.

### Abu Bakr's correction
- Abu Bakr said: *O people, you recite this verse and place it in the wrong place. I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say: When people see a wrongdoer and do not stop him, Allah may soon send a punishment upon them all* (narrated by Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi).

### "If you are guided"
- Part of being guided is enjoining good and forbidding evil as you are able. If you do your part and people refuse, their misguidance does not harm you.

### Return to Allah
- Everyone will be told what they did.

### Lessons
- **Reform yourself first.**
- **Advise, then do not carry** the burden of those who refuse.

## Surah Al-Anam Meaning: Tawhid, the Keys of the Unseen and a Life for Allah

**Surah Al-Anam** (Cattle, 165 ayat) is a Makkan surah devoted to tawhid.

### Praise opens it
- *All praise is for Allah, who created the heavens and the earth and made darkness and light.*

### The keys of the unseen
﴿۞وَعِندَهُۥ مَفَاتِحُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ لَا يَعۡلَمُهَآ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۚ وَمَا تَسۡقُطُ مِن وَرَقَةٍ إِلَّا يَعۡلَمُهَا وَلَا حَبَّةٖ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا رَطۡبٖ وَلَا يَابِسٍ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾

### Ibrahim's argument
- Ibrahim pointed to a star, then the moon, then the sun, showing his people that whatever **sets** cannot be a god.
- He concluded: *I have turned my face toward He who created the heavens and the earth, inclining to truth.*

### The cattle and false rules
- The surah refutes rules the polytheists invented about crops and cattle, assigning parts to idols.

### The commandments of the straight path
| Command |
|---|
| Do not associate anything with Allah |
| Be good to parents |
| Do not kill your children out of poverty |
| Do not approach immoralities, open or hidden |
| Do not take a life Allah made sacred |
| Do not approach the orphan's wealth except to improve it |
| Give full measure and weight |
| Be just in speech, even with relatives |
| Fulfil the covenant of Allah |
| Follow this straight path and not other paths |

### A whole life for Allah
﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحۡيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾

### Lessons
- **Allah alone knows the unseen**: fortune-telling has no place.
- **Dedicate all of life**, not just prayer, to Allah.

## "With Him Are the Keys of the Unseen": Quran 6:59 Explained

﴿۞وَعِندَهُۥ مَفَاتِحُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ لَا يَعۡلَمُهَآ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۚ وَمَا تَسۡقُطُ مِن وَرَقَةٍ إِلَّا يَعۡلَمُهَا وَلَا حَبَّةٖ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا رَطۡبٖ وَلَا يَابِسٍ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ [Al-Anam 6:59]

### The keys of the unseen
- The Prophet ﷺ explained the keys of the unseen as five, citing the last verse of Surah Luqman: knowledge of the Hour, the coming of rain, what is in the wombs, what a soul will earn tomorrow, and in which land it will die (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### Knowledge in detail
| Scene | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Land and sea | The visible and the hidden worlds |
| A falling leaf | Even the smallest movement |
| A seed in the darkness of the earth | What no eye can see |
| Wet and dry | Everything, without exception |

### A clear book
- All of this is written in *a clear record*, showing that Allah's knowledge precedes and records events.

### Tawhid in practice
- Since the unseen belongs only to Allah, **visiting fortune-tellers, horoscopes and claims of knowing the future** contradict this verse.

### Comfort for the believer
- If He knows every leaf that falls, He knows your worry, your effort and your duʿa.

### Lessons
- **Trust the One who knows** what you cannot see.
- **Reject superstition.**

## "Those Who Believe and Do Not Mix Their Faith with Injustice": Quran 6:82 Explained

﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَمۡ يَلۡبِسُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنَهُم بِظُلۡمٍ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡأَمۡنُ وَهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ﴾ [Al-Anam 6:82]

### The Companions' concern
- When this verse was revealed, the Companions were worried: *Which of us has not wronged himself?* The Prophet ﷺ explained that the injustice meant is **shirk**, citing Luqman: *Indeed shirk is a great injustice* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Two rewards
| Reward | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Security | In this world and the next |
| Guidance | Continuous direction from Allah |

### The context
- The verse concludes Ibrahim's argument with his people: who deserves security, the one who worships Allah alone or the one who associates partners?

### Degrees
- Scholars add: the purer your tawhid and the fewer your sins, the more complete your security and guidance.

### Lessons
- **Guard tawhid** above all.
- **Security begins in the heart.**

## "Come, I Will Recite What Your Lord Has Forbidden": The Ten Commandments of Quran 6:151-153

﴿۞قُلۡ تَعَالَوۡاْ أَتۡلُ مَا حَرَّمَ رَبُّكُمۡ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ أَلَّا تُشۡرِكُواْ بِهِۦ شَيۡـٔٗاۖ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنٗاۖ وَلَا تَقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَوۡلَٰدَكُم مِّنۡ إِمۡلَٰقٖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُكُمۡ وَإِيَّاهُمۡۖ وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ ٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ مَا ظَهَرَ مِنۡهَا وَمَا بَطَنَۖ وَلَا تَقۡتُلُواْ ٱلنَّفۡسَ ٱلَّتِي حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ وَصَّىٰكُم بِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ [Al-Anam 6:151]

### Ten commandments in three verses
| Verse | Commandments | Ending |
|---|---|---|
| 151 | Tawhid, parents, children, immoralities, life | *that you may reason* |
| 152 | Orphan's wealth, measure and weight, just speech, covenants | *that you may remember* |
| 153 | Follow the straight path | *that you may be mindful* |

### Ibn Masʿud
- He is reported to have said: *Whoever wishes to see the will of Muhammad ﷺ upon which is his seal, let him read these verses* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### A progression
- **Reason** understands, **remembrance** preserves, **taqwa** protects.

### "We provide for you and them"
- Here the parents' provision is mentioned first, addressing those already in poverty; in Al-Isra the children's is first, addressing those fearing future poverty.

### Lessons
- **Memorise these three verses** and measure your life by them.
- **Begin with tawhid**, the foundation of all.

## "This Is My Straight Path, So Follow It": Quran 6:153 Explained

﴿وَأَنَّ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطِي مُسۡتَقِيمٗا فَٱتَّبِعُوهُۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ ٱلسُّبُلَ فَتَفَرَّقَ بِكُمۡ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ وَصَّىٰكُم بِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ﴾ [Al-Anam 6:153]

### The hadith of the lines
- The Prophet ﷺ drew a line and said: *This is the path of Allah.* Then he drew lines to its right and left and said: *These are paths; on each is a devil calling to it*, then recited this verse (narrated by Ahmad and al-Nasaʾi in al-Kubra).

### Singular and plural
| The path | The ways |
|---|---|
| Singular: truth is one | Plural: falsehood branches |

### "They will divide you"
- Other ways separate you from Allah's path and from each other.

### The last commandment
- This closes the ten commandments, ending with *that you may be mindful*.

### Lessons
- **Hold to the Quran and Sunnah** as one path.
- **Beware** every call that leads away from them.

## "My Prayer, My Sacrifice, My Life and My Death Are for Allah": Quran 6:162-163 Explained

﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحۡيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُۥۖ وَبِذَٰلِكَ أُمِرۡتُ وَأَنَا۠ أَوَّلُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ [Al-Anam 6:162-163]

### Four things dedicated
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Salati* | My prayer |
| *Nusuki* | My sacrifice and acts of devotion |
| *Mahyaya* | Everything I do while alive |
| *Mamati* | The state I die upon |

### Life as worship
- The verse moves from rituals to **all of life**. Work, family and rest become worship when done for Allah and within His limits.

### No partner
- *He has no partner*: not in worship, not in intention. This is sincerity (*ikhlas*).

### In the prayer
- The Prophet ﷺ used these words in one of his opening supplications in prayer (narrated by Muslim from ʿAli).

### "The first of the Muslims"
- The Prophet ﷺ is the first of his nation to submit, the leader in practising what he preached.

### Lessons
- **Renew your intention** in daily actions.
- **Ask for a good ending**: death too is for Allah.
