﴿وَتَعَاوَنُواْ عَلَى ٱلۡبِرِّ وَٱلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُواْ عَلَى ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِۚ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:2]

## The context
- The verse speaks about the sanctity of Allah's symbols and the sacred months. It mentions people who once blocked Muslims from al-Masjid al-Haram, and says: **do not let hatred of them push you to aggression**. Then comes the general rule of cooperation.

## Two kinds of cooperation
| Command | Includes |
|---|---|
| Cooperate in *birr* | All good deeds: charity, teaching, helping, kindness |
| Cooperate in *taqwa* | Avoiding what Allah forbade, and helping others avoid it |
| Do not cooperate in sin | Not helping anyone do wrong, even a friend |
| Do not cooperate in aggression | Not joining in harming others' rights |

## Birr and taqwa together
- Scholars explain that *birr* is doing good and *taqwa* is guarding from evil. Together they cover the whole religion.

## Everyday applications
- Helping a neighbour, teaching a child the Quran, volunteering, supporting a family member to quit a bad habit.
- Refusing to share a harmful rumour or to sign a false document.

## Lessons
- **You are responsible for what you help with**, not only what you do.
- **Past wrongs do not justify injustice.**
