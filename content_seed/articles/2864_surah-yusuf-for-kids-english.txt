A young boy wakes up and runs to his father: "Father, I saw eleven stars, and the sun and the moon, all bowing down to me!" His father, Prophet Ya'qub, understood that this was a special dream, and he told his son lovingly: "Don't tell your brothers about this dream." This is how the story of Prophet Yusuf begins, a story Allah Himself called "ahsan al-qasas", the best of stories.

Surah Yusuf is the only surah that tells one prophet's story from beginning to end in a single place. It has everything children love in a story: a dream, jealous brothers, a deep well, a caravan, a palace, a prison, a king's dream, and a happy ending where the family is reunited. It also teaches big lessons: patience, honesty, staying away from wrong, never giving up hope, and forgiving people who hurt us. In this guide, we explain Surah Yusuf to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Yusuf

- **Position**: surah 12, in juz 12 and 13.
- **Number of verses**: 111.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: after Prophet Yusuf (Joseph), son of Ya'qub (Jacob), grandson of Ishaq, great-grandson of Ibrahim, peace be upon them all.

### When was it revealed?
Scholars mention that it was revealed in Makkah during a very hard time for the Prophet ﷺ, after the death of his wife Khadijah and his uncle Abu Talib. Its message was comforting: after hardship comes relief, just as it came for Yusuf.

## Lesson 1: The Best of Stories

﴿نَحۡنُ نَقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡقَصَصِ بِمَآ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ وَإِن كُنتَ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ لَمِنَ ٱلۡغَٰفِلِينَ﴾ (Quran 12:3)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah tells the Prophet ﷺ: We are telling you the best of stories. Why is it the best? Because it is true, it is complete, and it has a lesson in every scene."

### Activity: storyboard
As you go through the surah, draw a six-box storyboard with your child: the dream, the well, the palace, the prison, the king's dream, and the family reunited.

## Lesson 2: The Dream

﴿إِذۡ قَالَ يُوسُفُ لِأَبِيهِ يَـٰٓأَبَتِ إِنِّي رَأَيۡتُ أَحَدَ عَشَرَ كَوۡكَبٗا وَٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ رَأَيۡتُهُمۡ لِي سَٰجِدِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ يَٰبُنَيَّ لَا تَقۡصُصۡ رُءۡيَاكَ عَلَىٰٓ إِخۡوَتِكَ فَيَكِيدُواْ لَكَ كَيۡدًاۖ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ عَدُوࣱّ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ (Quran 12:4-5)

### Explaining it to your child
"Yusuf saw eleven stars, the sun and the moon bowing to him. His father knew this meant Allah would honour him greatly one day. He told him: Don't tell your brothers, they might plot against you. Shaytan is a clear enemy to people."

### Lesson for kids: a wise father
"Ya'qub knew his sons well. He knew that jealousy might grow in their hearts, so he protected Yusuf. Parents give advice because they love us, even when we don't understand the reason yet."

### Lesson for kids: be careful with jealousy
"Jealousy starts small: why does Dad love him more? Then it grows if we feed it. When you feel jealous, say: Masha'Allah, may Allah bless him, and ask Allah to give you from His bounty."

## Lesson 3: The Well and the False Blood

﴿وَجَآءُو عَلَىٰ قَمِيصِهِۦ بِدَمٖ كَذِبٖۚ قَالَ بَلۡ سَوَّلَتۡ لَكُمۡ أَنفُسُكُمۡ أَمۡرٗاۖ فَصَبۡرࣱ جَمِيلࣱۖ وَٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُسۡتَعَانُ عَلَىٰ مَا تَصِفُونَ﴾ (Quran 12:18)

### Explaining it to your child
"The brothers' jealousy grew. They took Yusuf out, threw him into a deep well, and came back to their father in the evening, crying fake tears. They brought Yusuf's shirt with fake blood on it and said a wolf had eaten him. But Ya'qub knew it was a lie, because the shirt was not torn! He said: Fa-sabrun jamil, so beautiful patience, and Allah is the One whose help is sought."

### What is "beautiful patience"?
"Beautiful patience is patience without complaining to people. Ya'qub was very sad, but he did not shout, insult or give up. He turned to Allah."

### Lesson for kids: lies get found out
"The brothers thought they had planned everything, but they forgot to tear the shirt! A lie always has a hole in it. Telling the truth is always safer."

## Lesson 4: From the Well to the Palace

"A caravan of travellers passed by, lowered a bucket into the well, and found a boy! They sold him in Egypt, and he was bought by a minister called Al-Aziz, who told his wife: Treat him kindly, he may benefit us. So Yusuf, who had been thrown into a well, grew up in a palace!"

### Lesson for kids: Allah plans for us
"The brothers wanted to get rid of Yusuf, but every step was bringing him closer to a high position. Allah's plan is always better than people's plans."

## Lesson 5: Prison Rather Than Wrongdoing

﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱلسِّجۡنُ أَحَبُّ إِلَيَّ مِمَّا يَدۡعُونَنِيٓ إِلَيۡهِۖ وَإِلَّا تَصۡرِفۡ عَنِّي كَيۡدَهُنَّ أَصۡبُ إِلَيۡهِنَّ وَأَكُن مِّنَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ﴾ (Quran 12:33)

### Explaining it to your child
"When Yusuf grew up, people in the palace wanted him to do something wrong. Yusuf refused, and said: My Lord, prison is dearer to me than what they are calling me to do. He chose to go to prison rather than disobey Allah!"

### Lesson for kids: saying no to wrong
"If your friends ask you to do something wrong, like cheating or taking something that isn't yours, be like Yusuf: say no, even if it costs you something. Allah will reward you."

### Activity: the brave no
Practise with your child how to say no politely and firmly: "No thanks, that's not right." "I'd rather not, my parents wouldn't like it." Role-play a few situations.

## Lesson 6: Yusuf in Prison

"In prison, Yusuf did not waste his time feeling sorry for himself. He invited his fellow prisoners to worship Allah, and he explained their dreams. One of them was later released and went to serve the king."

### Lesson for kids: be useful everywhere
"Even in the hardest place, Yusuf helped people and invited them to good. Wherever you are, at school, at the park, in the hospital, you can be useful."

## Lesson 7: The King's Dream

"The king saw a strange dream: seven fat cows eaten by seven thin cows, and seven green ears of grain and seven dry ones. Nobody could explain it, until the released prisoner remembered Yusuf. Yusuf explained: seven years of good harvests, then seven years of drought. Store the grain in its ears during the good years! So the king freed him and put him in charge of Egypt's storehouses."

### Activity: planning for later
Ask your child: "If you get pocket money this week, what could you save for later?" Yusuf taught Egypt to save in good times for hard times. Make a small savings jar together.

## Lesson 8: Never Lose Hope

﴿قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَشۡكُواْ بَثِّي وَحُزۡنِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَأَعۡلَمُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿يَٰبَنِيَّ ٱذۡهَبُواْ فَتَحَسَّسُواْ مِن يُوسُفَ وَأَخِيهِ وَلَا تَاْيۡـَٔسُواْ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يَاْيۡـَٔسُ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 12:86-87)

### Explaining it to your child
"Years passed, and Ya'qub was still sad about Yusuf. He said: I complain of my grief only to Allah. Then he told his sons: Go and search for Yusuf and his brother, and never despair of Allah's mercy. Only people who don't believe lose hope in Allah's mercy."

### Lesson for kids
"Ya'qub waited many years, but he never stopped hoping. When something is hard for you, like a difficult surah or a hard maths lesson, don't give up. Keep trying and keep making du'a."

## Lesson 9: "I Am Yusuf"

﴿قَالُوٓاْ أَءِنَّكَ لَأَنتَ يُوسُفُۖ قَالَ أَنَا۠ يُوسُفُ وَهَٰذَآ أَخِيۖ قَدۡ مَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَآۖ إِنَّهُۥ مَن يَتَّقِ وَيَصۡبِرۡ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 12:90)

### Explaining it to your child
"In the years of drought, the brothers came to Egypt to buy food from the minister in charge, not knowing he was Yusuf! After a few visits, Yusuf revealed himself. They were shocked: Are you really Yusuf? He said: I am Yusuf, and this is my brother. Allah has been gracious to us. Whoever is mindful of Allah and patient, Allah never lets the reward of good people go to waste."

## Lesson 10: Amazing Forgiveness

﴿قَالَ لَا تَثۡرِيبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ يَغۡفِرُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 12:92)

### Explaining it to your child
"Now Yusuf was powerful, and his brothers were in his hands. He could have punished them for throwing him in the well. But he said: No blame on you today. May Allah forgive you, He is the Most Merciful of the merciful. What a big heart!"

### From the Sunnah
On the day the Prophet ﷺ entered Makkah victorious, he forgave the people who had hurt him for years, and the scholars of seerah mention that he quoted these very words of Yusuf.

### Activity: the forgiveness note
Ask your child to think of someone who upset them recently. Together, write a small note: "I forgive you." They can give it, or simply forgive in their heart and make du'a for that person.

## Let's Read Together: The Family Reunited

﴿وَرَفَعَ أَبَوَيۡهِ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِ وَخَرُّواْ لَهُۥ سُجَّدٗاۖ وَقَالَ يَـٰٓأَبَتِ هَٰذَا تَأۡوِيلُ رُءۡيَٰيَ مِن قَبۡلُ قَدۡ جَعَلَهَا رَبِّي حَقّٗاۖ وَقَدۡ أَحۡسَنَ بِيٓ إِذۡ أَخۡرَجَنِي مِنَ ٱلسِّجۡنِ وَجَآءَ بِكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡبَدۡوِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ أَن نَّزَغَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَ إِخۡوَتِيٓۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي لَطِيفࣱ لِّمَا يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿۞رَبِّ قَدۡ ءَاتَيۡتَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُلۡكِ وَعَلَّمۡتَنِي مِن تَأۡوِيلِ ٱلۡأَحَادِيثِۚ فَاطِرَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَنتَ وَلِيِّۦ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ تَوَفَّنِي مُسۡلِمٗا وَأَلۡحِقۡنِي بِٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ (Quran 12:100-101)

### Explaining it to your child
"Yusuf raised his parents onto the throne, and the whole family bowed in respect, as was allowed in their law, and the dream from his childhood came true! Notice Yusuf's manners: he thanked Allah for taking him out of prison, but did not mention the well, so as not to embarrass his brothers. Then he made a beautiful du'a: my Lord, let me die as a Muslim and join me with the righteous."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Broken Model"

Hamza spent a whole week building a model plane for the school science fair. His younger brother Ali was jealous because everyone kept praising Hamza. One afternoon, Ali pushed the model off the table, and it broke into pieces.

Hamza was furious. He wanted to shout at Ali and tell their parents to punish him. Ali was crying in the corner, scared.

Their mother sat with Hamza and told him the ending of Yusuf's story: the brothers who threw him into the well, and how Yusuf, when he was powerful, said: "No blame on you today." Hamza listened quietly.

Then he walked to Ali and said: "I'm upset, but I forgive you. Will you help me fix it?" Ali stopped crying and nodded. They worked together for two evenings, and the new model was even better than the first. At the science fair, Hamza told his teacher: "My brother helped me build it."

That night, Ali whispered to Hamza: "You're like Prophet Yusuf." Hamza smiled: "And you're my brother."

Ask your child: "Why did Ali break the model? What did Hamza do instead of getting revenge?"

## What We Learn From Surah Yusuf

- **Listen to wise parents**, even when we don't understand yet.
- **Watch out for jealousy** before it grows.
- **Lies are always found out**.
- **Beautiful patience** without complaining to people.
- **Allah's plan** is better than people's plans.
- **Say no to wrong**, whatever it costs.
- **Be useful** everywhere.
- **Plan and save** for hard times.
- **Never lose hope** in Allah's mercy.
- **Forgive** those who hurt you.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Are some parts of the story unsuitable for young children?
The episode in the palace can be summarized simply: "People wanted Yusuf to do something wrong and he refused." The rest of the story is very suitable for children.

### Why did the family bow to Yusuf?
Scholars explain that it was a bow of respect and greeting allowed in their law, not worship. In our law, prostration is only for Allah.

### How long is the story to tell?
It works best in instalments: one scene each night for a week, so children look forward to the next part.
