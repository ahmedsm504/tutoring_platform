[Surah Yasin](/quran/36/) is a surah many Muslims love to memorize and recite. It's a Makkan surah of medium length with mostly short verses, a moving story, and vivid scenes, all of which make it very memorable.

## Quick facts
- **83 verses**, pages 440–445 in the Madinah Mushaf (about six pages), in juz 22 and 23.

## An honest note on hadiths about Yasin
Many hadiths about Yasin circulate widely, such as "Everything has a heart, and the heart of the Quran is Yasin", and narrations about reciting it over the dying. Many hadith scholars have graded most of these narrations as **weak**.

Reciting and memorizing Yasin is still a great good deed, included in the general, authentic virtues of the Quran, such as **"Whoever recites a letter from the Book of Allah will have a good deed, multiplied tenfold"** (Tirmidhi). You don't need a weak hadith to love this surah. See [authentic hadiths on the virtues of the Quran](/blog/virtues-of-learning-quran-hadith/).

## The surah's map
| Section | Verses | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 1–12 | Oath by the Quran; the Prophet's mission |
| The people of the town | 13–32 | The messengers and the man who came running |
| Signs in creation | 33–50 | Dead land revived, night and day, sun and moon, ships |
| Scenes of the Hereafter | 51–68 | The trumpet, the people of Paradise, the guilty |
| Closing | 69–83 | Answering the deniers; Allah's power to resurrect |

## The 6-week plan (5 days a week)
| Week | Verses | Approx. daily target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–12 | 2–3 verses |
| 2 | 13–27 | 3 verses |
| 3 | 28–40 | 2–3 verses |
| 4 | 41–54 | 3 verses |
| 5 | 55–68 | 3 verses |
| 6 | 69–83 | 3 verses |

## Spots that need attention
1. **The Sakt on ﴿مَّرۡقَدِنَاۜ﴾ (verse 52):** in Hafs, a brief pause without breath, then continue ﴿هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ﴾.
2. **The opening letters ﴿يسٓ﴾:** read *Yā Sīīīn*: Yaa for 2 counts, Seen for 6. In Hafs the Noon is pronounced clearly when continuing to ﴿وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ﴾.
3. **Sun and moon verses (38–40):** similar rhythm; memorize them together and watch the order.
4. **Very short consecutive verses** in the Hereafter scenes: link each verse to the next.

## Locking it in
- Recite what you memorize in night prayer.
- Start each day by reciting yesterday's portion.
- After finishing, recite it daily from memory for two weeks, then weekly.
