Surah Ar-Rum opens with a prophecy that seemed impossible when it was revealed: the Romans, freshly crushed by the Persians, would be victorious within a few years. It came true exactly as foretold. The surah then turns from the history of empires to the signs of Allah in daily life: your spouse and the love between you, the variety of your languages and colours, your sleep at night, the lightning and the rain. A series of verses begins "And of His signs", making Ar-Rum one of the great surahs of reflection. It describes the natural disposition (fitrah) every human being is born with, and warns that corruption appears on land and sea because of what people's hands have earned.

This guide explains Surah Ar-Rum section by section in English, with practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ar-Rum
- **Number of verses**: 60.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 30, in the twenty-first juz.
- **Name**: Ar-Rum means "the Romans", the Byzantine Empire.
- **Main themes**: Allah's control of history, the signs of Allah, the fitrah, gratitude, and the resurrection.

## Verses 1–7: The Prophecy of the Romans
﴿الٓمٓ﴾ ﴿غُلِبَتِ ٱلرُّومُ﴾ ﴿فِيٓ أَدۡنَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ غَلَبِهِمۡ سَيَغۡلِبُونَ﴾ ﴿فِي بِضۡعِ سِنِينَۗ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ مِن قَبۡلُ وَمِنۢ بَعۡدُۚ وَيَوۡمَئِذٖ يَفۡرَحُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿بِنَصۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ يَنصُرُ مَن يَشَآءُۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 30:1-5)

**"The Romans have been defeated in the nearest land. But they, after their defeat, will overcome within a few years."** The Persians had defeated the Byzantines. The idolaters of Makkah rejoiced, seeing fellow idol-worshippers beat the People of the Book. The Quran announced that within "a few years" (*bid'i sinin*, between three and nine years) the Romans would win, **"and that day the believers will rejoice in the victory of Allah"**. At-Tirmidhi reported that Abu Bakr made a wager with the idolaters about this before such wagers were forbidden, and he graded the report *hasan sahih*. The Romans won within the stated period.

﴿وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِۖ لَا يُخۡلِفُ ٱللَّهُ وَعۡدَهُۥ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿يَعۡلَمُونَ ظَٰهِرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ غَٰفِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:6-7)

**"It is the promise of Allah. Allah does not fail in His promise, but most of the people do not know. They know what is apparent of the worldly life, but they, of the hereafter, are unaware."** A sharp description: people expert in the surface of this world and ignorant of what matters most.

## Verses 8–16: Reflect and Travel
﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَتَفَكَّرُواْ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۗ مَّا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۗ وَإِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِلِقَآيِٕ رَبِّهِمۡ لَكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَيَنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ كَانُوٓاْ أَشَدَّ مِنۡهُمۡ قُوَّةٗ وَأَثَارُواْ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَعَمَرُوهَآ أَكۡثَرَ مِمَّا عَمَرُوهَا وَجَآءَتۡهُمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِۖ فَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيَظۡلِمَهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:8-9)

**"Do they not contemplate within themselves? Allah has not created the heavens and the earth and what is between them except in truth."** **"Have they not travelled through the land and observed how was the end of those before them?"** They were stronger and built more, and they were destroyed.

## Verses 17–19: Glorify Allah Morning and Evening
﴿فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ حِينَ تُمۡسُونَ وَحِينَ تُصۡبِحُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَهُ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَعَشِيّٗا وَحِينَ تُظۡهِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:17-18)

**"So exalted is Allah when you reach the evening and when you reach the morning. And to Him is all praise in the heavens and the earth. And exalted is He at night and when you are at noon."** Many commentators linked these times with the five daily prayers.

﴿يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ وَيُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَيِّتَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَيِّ وَيُحۡيِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَاۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ تُخۡرَجُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:19)

**"He brings the living out of the dead and brings the dead out of the living."**

## Verses 20–27: "And of His Signs"
﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنۡ خَلَقَكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ إِذَآ أَنتُم بَشَرࣱ تَنتَشِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:20)

**Your creation**: **"And of His signs is that He created you from dust; then, suddenly you are human beings dispersing."**

﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنۡ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا لِّتَسۡكُنُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَكُم مَّوَدَّةٗ وَرَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:21)

**Marriage**: **"And of His signs is that He created for you from yourselves mates that you may find tranquillity in them; and He placed between you affection and mercy. Indeed in that are signs for a people who give thought."** Tranquillity (*sakan*), affection (*mawaddah*) and mercy (*rahmah*): the three pillars of a marriage. Affection is strong in youth; mercy carries the marriage through age, illness and hardship.

﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ خَلۡقُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفُ أَلۡسِنَتِكُمۡ وَأَلۡوَٰنِكُمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّلۡعَٰلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 30:22)

**Languages and colours**: **"And of His signs is the creation of the heavens and the earth and the diversity of your languages and your colours. Indeed in that are signs for those of knowledge."** Human diversity is a sign of Allah, not a reason for pride or contempt.

﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ مَنَامُكُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَٱبۡتِغَآؤُكُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَسۡمَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:23)

**Sleep**: **"And of His signs is your sleep by night and day and your seeking of His bounty."**

﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ يُرِيكُمُ ٱلۡبَرۡقَ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَيُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَيُحۡيِۦ بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَآۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:24)

**Lightning and rain**: **"He shows you the lightning, causing fear and aspiration, and He sends down rain from the sky by which He brings to life the earth after its lifelessness."**

﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَن تَقُومَ ٱلسَّمَآءُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ بِأَمۡرِهِۦۚ ثُمَّ إِذَا دَعَاكُمۡ دَعۡوَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِذَآ أَنتُمۡ تَخۡرُجُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:25)

**The heavens and earth standing**: **"And of His signs is that the heaven and the earth remain by His command."** Then one call, and you will come out of the earth.

﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يَبۡدَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥ وَهُوَ أَهۡوَنُ عَلَيۡهِۚ وَلَهُ ٱلۡمَثَلُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ (Quran 30:27)

**"And it is He who begins creation; then He repeats it, and that is easier for Him."**

## Verses 28–32: The Fitrah
﴿فَأَقِمۡ وَجۡهَكَ لِلدِّينِ حَنِيفٗاۚ فِطۡرَتَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِي فَطَرَ ٱلنَّاسَ عَلَيۡهَاۚ لَا تَبۡدِيلَ لِخَلۡقِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡقَيِّمُ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:30)

**"So direct your face toward the religion, inclining to truth. Adhere to the fitrah of Allah upon which He has created people. No change should there be in the creation of Allah. That is the correct religion, but most of the people do not know."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Every child is born upon the fitrah; then his parents make him a Jew, a Christian or a Magian." Every human heart is created knowing its Lord.

﴿۞مُنِيبِينَ إِلَيۡهِ وَٱتَّقُوهُ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَلَا تَكُونُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾ ﴿مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ فَرَّقُواْ دِينَهُمۡ وَكَانُواْ شِيَعٗاۖ كُلُّ حِزۡبِۭ بِمَا لَدَيۡهِمۡ فَرِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:31-32)

**"Turning in repentance to Him, and fear Him and establish prayer and do not be of those who associate others with Allah, of those who have divided their religion and become sects, every faction rejoicing in what it has."**

## Verses 33–40: Ingratitude and Giving
﴿وَإِذَآ أَذَقۡنَا ٱلنَّاسَ رَحۡمَةٗ فَرِحُواْ بِهَاۖ وَإِن تُصِبۡهُمۡ سَيِّئَةُۢ بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ إِذَا هُمۡ يَقۡنَطُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:36)

**"And when We let the people taste mercy, they rejoice therein. But if evil afflicts them for what their hands have put forth, immediately they despair."**

﴿فَـَٔاتِ ذَا ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ حَقَّهُۥ وَٱلۡمِسۡكِينَ وَٱبۡنَ ٱلسَّبِيلِۚ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرࣱ لِّلَّذِينَ يُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَ ٱللَّهِۖ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ ءَاتَيۡتُم مِّن رِّبٗا لِّيَرۡبُوَاْ فِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِ ٱلنَّاسِ فَلَا يَرۡبُواْ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۖ وَمَآ ءَاتَيۡتُم مِّن زَكَوٰةٖ تُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَ ٱللَّهِ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُضۡعِفُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:38-39)

**"So give the relative his right, as well as the needy and the traveller."** **"And whatever you give for interest to increase within the wealth of people will not increase with Allah. But what you give in zakah, desiring the face of Allah, those are the multipliers."** An early step in the gradual prohibition of riba.

## Verses 41–45: Corruption on Land and Sea
﴿ظَهَرَ ٱلۡفَسَادُ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِي ٱلنَّاسِ لِيُذِيقَهُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي عَمِلُواْ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:41)

**"Corruption has appeared throughout the land and sea by what the hands of people have earned, so He may let them taste part of what they have done that perhaps they will return."** Sin has consequences in the world itself; the purpose of the taste is return to Allah.

## Verses 46–53: Winds and the Dead Earth
﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي يُرۡسِلُ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ فَتُثِيرُ سَحَابٗا فَيَبۡسُطُهُۥ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ كَيۡفَ يَشَآءُ وَيَجۡعَلُهُۥ كِسَفٗا فَتَرَى ٱلۡوَدۡقَ يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡ خِلَٰلِهِۦۖ فَإِذَآ أَصَابَ بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦٓ إِذَا هُمۡ يَسۡتَبۡشِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:48)

**"It is Allah who sends the winds, and they stir the clouds and spread them in the sky however He wills."**

﴿فَٱنظُرۡ إِلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِ رَحۡمَتِ ٱللَّهِ كَيۡفَ يُحۡيِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَآۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ لَمُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 30:50)

**"So observe the effects of the mercy of Allah, how He gives life to the earth after its lifelessness. Indeed, that is the Giver of life to the dead."**

## Verses 54–60: Weakness, Strength, Weakness
﴿۞ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن ضَعۡفٖ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ضَعۡفٖ قُوَّةٗ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ قُوَّةٖ ضَعۡفٗا وَشَيۡبَةٗۚ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡقَدِيرُ﴾ (Quran 30:54)

**"Allah is the one who created you from weakness, then made after weakness strength, then made after strength weakness and white hair. He creates what He wills, and He is the Knowing, the Competent."** The whole human life in one verse.

﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّۖ وَلَا يَسۡتَخِفَّنَّكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُوقِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 30:60)

**"So be patient. Indeed, the promise of Allah is truth. And let them not disquiet you who are not certain in faith."** The surah begins and ends with the promise of Allah.

## Word Meanings
- **Ar-Rum**: the Romans.
- **Adna al-ard**: the nearest land.
- **Bid'**: a few, between three and nine.
- **Zahir**: the apparent, outward.
- **Tumsun / tusbihun**: you reach evening / morning.
- **Sakan**: tranquillity.
- **Mawaddah**: affection.
- **Fitrah**: natural disposition.
- **Hanif**: inclining to truth.
- **Al-Fasad**: corruption.
- **Shaybah**: white hair.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **A prophecy with a deadline**: fulfilled on time.
2. **"The apparent of worldly life"**: a precise diagnosis of materialism.
3. **The "And of His signs" series**: each ending with a different human faculty: thought, knowledge, hearing, reason.
4. **Weakness, strength, weakness**: a life in eleven words.
5. **The promise of Allah** at both the beginning and the end.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Ikhfa'**: in *anfusikum* (verse 21), the noon sakinah is hidden before the fa'.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *mawaddatan wa rahmah* (verse 21), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'u* (verse 25).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *fitrata* (verse 30), the ta' (ط) sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Lam shamsiyyah**: in *ar-Rum* (verse 2).

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-'Ankabut before it ends with striving for Allah, and Ar-Rum opens with the promise of victory. Ar-Rum ends with patience and certainty, and Surah Luqman after it opens with the Book of wisdom and the doers of good.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Trust Allah's promises** even when events seem to say otherwise.
2. **Do not be expert in this world only.**
3. **Glorify Allah morning and evening.**
4. **Build your marriage on tranquillity, affection and mercy.**
5. **Respect human diversity** as a sign of Allah.
6. **Protect the fitrah** of your children.
7. **Avoid riba and give zakah.**
8. **Recognise corruption** as a call to return to Allah.
9. **Use your years of strength** before weakness returns.

## How to Memorise Surah Ar-Rum
- **Week 1**: verses 1–27.
- **Week 2**: verses 28–60, with full review.

## Teaching Children
- **Make a "signs" journal**: each day, write one of Allah's signs they noticed.
- **Read verse 22** and talk about the languages and colours of their friends.
- **Tell the story of the Roman prophecy** as a true promise fulfilled.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the prophecy of Surah Ar-Rum?
That the defeated Romans would be victorious within a few years, which happened as foretold.

### What does "a few years" mean?
The Arabic *bid'* refers to a number between three and nine.

### What are the three pillars of marriage in Ar-Rum?
Tranquillity, affection and mercy, from verse 21.

### What is the fitrah?
The natural disposition to know and worship Allah that every human is born with.

### Why has corruption appeared on land and sea?
Because of what people's hands have earned, so that they may return to Allah.

### What does verse 54 describe?
The stages of human life: weakness, strength, then weakness and white hair.

### How many verses are in Surah Ar-Rum?
60 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Ar-Rum teaches you to read the world with two eyes: one on history, where Allah's promises come true on time, and one on daily life, where His signs fill every moment: your spouse's mercy, your friend's language, your night's sleep, the rain after drought. Return to your fitrah, glorify Him morning and evening, and be patient: the promise of Allah is truth.
