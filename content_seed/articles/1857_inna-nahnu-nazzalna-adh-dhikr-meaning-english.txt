﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ﴾ [Al-Hijr 15:9]

## Strong emphasis
| Feature | Effect |
|---|---|
| *Inna* (twice) | Certainty |
| *Nahnu* | "We Ourselves" |
| *Lahu* placed before | Preservation **specifically** for it |
| *La-hafizun* | The added "la" for extra emphasis |

## Who preserves?
- Earlier scriptures were entrusted to their people: *of what they were entrusted to preserve of the Book of Allah* (Al-Maidah 5:44). The Quran is preserved **by Allah Himself**.

## How it was preserved
- **In hearts**: millions of memorisers across generations.
- **In writing**: recorded during the Prophet's life, compiled under Abu Bakr, and copies sent out under ʿUthman.
- **In recitation**: chains of transmission (*isnad*) back to the Prophet ﷺ.

## The response of a disbeliever
- The verse answers those who mocked the Prophet ﷺ and called the Quran madness. Their words faded; the Quran remains.

## Lessons
- **Trust the Mushaf you hold.**
- **Be among the preservers**: memorise even a little.
