**Surah Ghafir** (85 ayat) opens with Allah's names of mercy and power, and tells the story of a hidden believer who spoke up.

## Who is Allah?
- *The Forgiver of sin, the Accepter of repentance, severe in punishment, owner of abundance.*
- **Mercy first**, then a warning, then generosity.

## Angels praying for you
- Those who carry the Throne and those around it glorify their Lord and **ask forgiveness for the believers**.
- Read [Rabbana Du'as in the Quran](/blog/rabbana-duas-english/).

## The believer from Pharaoh's family
﴿وَقَالَ رَجُلࣱ مُّؤۡمِنࣱ مِّنۡ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ يَكۡتُمُ إِيمَٰنَهُۥٓ أَتَقۡتُلُونَ رَجُلًا أَن يَقُولَ رَبِّيَ ٱللَّهُ﴾
| His argument | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Will you kill a man for saying "my Lord is Allah"? | Courage in truth |
| If he lies, it's on him; if truthful, you'll be struck | Wisdom in persuasion |
| Remember the nations before you | History as warning |
| *I entrust my affair to Allah* | Tawakkul |

## Call upon Me
﴿وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدۡعُونِيٓ أَسۡتَجِبۡ لَكُمۡۚ﴾
- **Du'a is worship**: the surah calls refusing it arrogance.

## Lessons
- **Speak the truth wisely**, even when alone.
