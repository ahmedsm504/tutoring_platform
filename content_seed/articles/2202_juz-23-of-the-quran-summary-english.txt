Juz 23 begins with ﴿۞وَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ مِن جُندٖ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا كُنَّا مُنزِلِينَ﴾, "And We did not send down upon his people after him any army from the sky, nor would We have done so", and is popularly known as **Juz Wa Mali** after the verse just before it: ﴿وَمَالِيَ لَآ أَعۡبُدُ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنِي﴾, "Why should I not worship the One who created me?", on the last page of the previous juz. It runs from **Ya-Sin 36:28 to Az-Zumar 39:31**, with **As-Saffat** and **Sad** complete in between. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 442 to 461**.

This is the juz of **monotheism, resurrection and the stories of the oft-returning prophets**. It presents signs in creation in Ya-Sin, a procession of prophets in As-Saffat with the refrain "peace be upon...", the prophets who constantly returned to Allah in Sad (Dawud, Sulayman and Ayyub), then opens Az-Zumar with a call to sincere religion.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **devoting worship to Allah alone, and the certainty of resurrection**. Ya-Sin gives a decisive reply to the denier of resurrection: ﴿قُلۡ يُحۡيِيهَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَهَآ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖۖ﴾, "Say: He who produced them the first time will give them life." As-Saffat repeatedly calls the prophets ﴿مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "among Our believing servants", and Sad repeatedly calls them *awwab*, "constantly returning to Allah". Az-Zumar then opens: ﴿فَٱعۡبُدِ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصٗا لَّهُ ٱلدِّينَ﴾ ﴿أَلَا لِلَّهِ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡخَالِصُۚ﴾, "So worship Allah, sincere to Him in religion. Unquestionably, for Allah is the pure religion."

The message: **devote your worship to Allah, keep returning to Him like the prophets, and prepare for a meeting about which there is no doubt.**

## Part one: the rest of Surah Ya-Sin (28-83)

### 1. Alas for the servants (28-32)
After the man of Ya-Sin was killed: "We did not send down upon his people after him any army from the sky"; it was only a single blast, and they were extinguished. Then: ﴿يَٰحَسۡرَةً عَلَى ٱلۡعِبَادِۚ مَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ﴾, "Alas for the servants! No messenger comes to them but they mock him."

**Lesson**: their destruction needed no army; one blast sufficed. Do not be awed by the strength of the stubborn.

### 2. Three signs in creation (33-44)
1. **The dead earth**: ﴿وَءَايَةࣱ لَّهُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ ٱلۡمَيۡتَةُ أَحۡيَيۡنَٰهَا وَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مِنۡهَا حَبّٗا فَمِنۡهُ يَأۡكُلُونَ﴾, "A sign for them is the dead earth: We gave it life and brought out grain from it, of which they eat."
2. **Night, sun and moon**: ﴿وَٱلشَّمۡسُ تَجۡرِي لِمُسۡتَقَرّٖ لَّهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقۡدِيرُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡقَمَرَ قَدَّرۡنَٰهُ مَنَازِلَ حَتَّىٰ عَادَ كَٱلۡعُرۡجُونِ ٱلۡقَدِيمِ﴾ ﴿لَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ يَنۢبَغِي لَهَآ أَن تُدۡرِكَ ٱلۡقَمَرَ وَلَا ٱلَّيۡلُ سَابِقُ ٱلنَّهَارِۚ وَكُلࣱّ فِي فَلَكٖ يَسۡبَحُونَ﴾, "The sun runs to a resting place of its own; that is the decree of the Almighty, the All-Knowing. The moon We have measured in phases until it returns like an old date stalk. It is not for the sun to overtake the moon, nor does the night outpace the day; each swims in an orbit."
3. **The laden ship**: We carried their offspring in the laden ship, and created for them the like of it to ride.

**Reflect**: the "old date stalk" is the dried, curved stem of a palm cluster: a precise image of the moon at month's end. The whole universe swims in an order that never falters.

### 3. Scenes of resurrection (45-59)
﴿وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَإِذَا هُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَنسِلُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَا مَنۢ بَعَثَنَا مِن مَّرۡقَدِنَاۜۗ هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَصَدَقَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ﴾, "The Horn will be blown, and at once from their graves they will hasten to their Lord. They will say: woe to us, who raised us from our sleeping place? This is what the Most Merciful promised, and the messengers told the truth." Then the people of Paradise: ﴿إِنَّ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ فِي شُغُلٖ فَٰكِهُونَ﴾, "The companions of Paradise that Day will be joyfully occupied", they and their spouses in shade, reclining on couches, ﴿سَلَٰمࣱ قَوۡلٗا مِّن رَّبّٖ رَّحِيمٖ﴾, "Peace: a word from a Merciful Lord."

### 4. The covenant with the children of Adam and the testimony of limbs (60-70)
A new quarter: ﴿۞أَلَمۡ أَعۡهَدۡ إِلَيۡكُمۡ يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ أَن لَّا تَعۡبُدُواْ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوࣱّ مُّبِينࣱ﴾, "Did I not enjoin upon you, children of Adam, not to worship Shaytan? He is a clear enemy to you." Then: ﴿ٱلۡيَوۡمَ نَخۡتِمُ عَلَىٰٓ أَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَتُكَلِّمُنَآ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَتَشۡهَدُ أَرۡجُلُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾, "That Day We will seal their mouths; their hands will speak to Us, and their feet will testify about what they used to earn." Then: ﴿وَمَن نُّعَمِّرۡهُ نُنَكِّسۡهُ فِي ٱلۡخَلۡقِۚ أَفَلَا يَعۡقِلُونَ﴾, "Whoever We grant long life, We reverse in creation. Will they not then reason?"

**Lesson**: the limbs you sin with today will testify against you tomorrow, so use them in what pleases Allah.

### 5. The decisive proof of resurrection (71-83)
﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ فَإِذَا هُوَ خَصِيمࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ ﴿وَضَرَبَ لَنَا مَثَلٗا وَنَسِيَ خَلۡقَهُۥۖ قَالَ مَن يُحۡيِ ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ وَهِيَ رَمِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ يُحۡيِيهَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَهَآ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ خَلۡقٍ عَلِيمٌ﴾, "Does man not see that We created him from a drop, and then he is an open adversary? He presents an example for Us and forgets his own creation, saying: who will give life to bones when they have crumbled? Say: He who produced them the first time will give them life, and He knows every creation." Then fire from the green tree, then the rule: ﴿إِنَّمَآ أَمۡرُهُۥٓ إِذَآ أَرَادَ شَيۡـًٔا أَن يَقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾ ﴿فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾, "His command, when He wills a thing, is only to say to it: Be, and it is. So glory be to Him in whose hand is the dominion of all things, and to Him you will be returned."

**Reflect on the argument**: "and forgets his own creation". Had he remembered how he was made from nothing, he would not have thought re-creation far-fetched. For the whole surah see [Surah Yasin Meaning in English](/blog/surah-yasin-meaning-english/).

## Part two: Surah As-Saffat (1-182)

As-Saffat is Makki, with 182 verses, opening with an oath by the angels: ﴿وَٱلصَّـٰٓفَّـٰتِ صَفّٗا﴾, "By those lined up in rows." For the whole surah see [Surah As-Saffat Meaning in English](/blog/surah-as-saffat-meaning-english/).

### 1. Monotheism and the shooting stars (1-21)
An oath by the angels who line up, drive and recite, that your God is One. Then: ﴿إِنَّا زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِزِينَةٍ ٱلۡكَوَاكِبِ﴾, "We have adorned the nearest heaven with the stars", as protection from every rebellious devil. Then the idolaters deny resurrection, and the reply: yes, and you will be humbled.

### 2. Dialogue at the Gathering and in the Fire (22-74)
A new quarter: ﴿۞ٱحۡشُرُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ وَأَزۡوَٰجَهُمۡ وَمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡبُدُونَ﴾, "Gather those who did wrong, their kind, and what they used to worship", then ﴿وَقِفُوهُمۡۖ إِنَّهُم مَّسۡـُٔولُونَ﴾, "and stop them; they are to be questioned." Then followers and leaders blame one another. Then Allah's sincere servants in Paradise, and a believer who looks down to see his former companion in the midst of Hell: by Allah, you almost ruined me. Then the tree of Zaqqum, food of the sinner.

### 3. The procession of prophets with the refrain of peace (75-148)
A refrain recurs: "Peace be upon... Thus do We reward the doers of good... He was among Our believing servants":
- **Nuh**: ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ نُوحٖ فِي ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾, "Peace upon Nuh among the worlds. Thus do We reward the doers of good."
- **Ibrahim**: a new quarter: ﴿۞وَإِنَّ مِن شِيعَتِهِۦ لَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾, "And among his followers was Ibrahim", when he came to his Lord with a sound heart. Then: ﴿فَنَظَرَ نَظۡرَةٗ فِي ٱلنُّجُومِ﴾ ﴿فَقَالَ إِنِّي سَقِيمࣱ﴾, "He cast a glance at the stars and said: I am ill"; they turned away, and he struck their idols with his right hand. Then his emigration: ﴿وَقَالَ إِنِّي ذَاهِبٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّي سَيَهۡدِينِ﴾ ﴿رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿فَبَشَّرۡنَٰهُ بِغُلَٰمٍ حَلِيمٖ﴾, "He said: I am going to my Lord; He will guide me. My Lord, grant me a righteous child. So We gave him good news of a forbearing boy."

### 4. The story of the sacrifice (101-113)
﴿فَلَمَّا بَلَغَ مَعَهُ ٱلسَّعۡيَ قَالَ يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰ فِي ٱلۡمَنَامِ أَنِّيٓ أَذۡبَحُكَ فَٱنظُرۡ مَاذَا تَرَىٰۚ قَالَ يَـٰٓأَبَتِ ٱفۡعَلۡ مَا تُؤۡمَرُۖ سَتَجِدُنِيٓ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّآ أَسۡلَمَا وَتَلَّهُۥ لِلۡجَبِينِ﴾, "When he reached the age of working with him, he said: my dear son, I see in a dream that I am sacrificing you, so see what you think. He said: father, do as you are commanded; you will find me, if Allah wills, among the patient. When they had both submitted and he laid him on his forehead", We called to him: O Ibrahim, ﴿قَدۡ صَدَّقۡتَ ٱلرُّءۡيَآۚ إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾, "you have fulfilled the vision; thus do We reward the doers of good." This was the clear trial, ﴿وَفَدَيۡنَٰهُ بِذِبۡحٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾, "and We ransomed him with a great sacrifice", then ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾, "peace upon Ibrahim."

**Who was the son?** The stronger view of the majority of careful scholars is that it was **Isma'il**, because the good news of Ishaq comes after the story ends ("We gave him good news of Ishaq, a prophet among the righteous", verse 112), and because Isma'il was the firstborn who reached the age of working with his father.

**Lessons of the story**:
1. **Dialogue with the son**: "see what you think", though the command was from Allah, to share the reward with him.
2. **The son's courtesy**: "do as you are commanded", not "as you wish", with "if Allah wills".
3. **Complete submission**: "when they had both submitted", together.
4. **The reward**: the ransom, the greeting of peace, a lasting good name, and the Sunnah of sacrifice until the Day of Resurrection.

### 5. The rest of the procession (114-148)
- **Musa and Harun**: ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ﴾, "Peace upon Musa and Harun."
- **Ilyas**: he condemned his people's worship of Ba'l; ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰٓ إِلۡ يَاسِينَ﴾, "Peace upon Ilyas."
- **Lut**: We saved him and all his family except an old woman among those who remained behind.
- **Yunus**: ﴿وَإِنَّ يُونُسَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾, "And Yunus was among the messengers." He fled to the laden ship, drew lots and lost, ﴿فَٱلۡتَقَمَهُ ٱلۡحُوتُ وَهُوَ مُلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فَلَوۡلَآ أَنَّهُۥ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسَبِّحِينَ﴾ ﴿لَلَبِثَ فِي بَطۡنِهِۦٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾, "and the whale swallowed him while he was blameworthy. Had he not been among those who glorify Allah, he would have remained in its belly until the Day they are resurrected." Then Hizb 46 begins: ﴿۞فَنَبَذۡنَٰهُ بِٱلۡعَرَآءِ وَهُوَ سَقِيمࣱ﴾, "We threw him onto the open shore while he was ill", We made a gourd plant grow over him, and sent him to a hundred thousand or more, who believed.

**The lesson from Yunus**: his glorification in times of ease saved him in hardship. Remember Allah much while you are well, and it will protect you in trial.

### 6. Refuting "daughters of Allah", and the close (149-182)
Then the refutation of the claim that the angels are Allah's daughters, and the angels' words: each of us has a known station; we are those who line up in rows, and we glorify. Then the promise: Our word has gone forth to Our servants the messengers that they will be victorious, and that Our soldiers will prevail. Then the comprehensive close: ﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ ٱلۡعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَى ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "Glory be to your Lord, Lord of might, above what they describe. Peace upon the messengers. And praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds."

**Reflect on the close**: three verses gathering Allah's transcendence, peace upon His messengers and praise to Him: the summary of the surah.

## Part three: Surah Sad (1-88)

Sad is Makki, with 88 verses, opening with ﴿صٓۚ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ذِي ٱلذِّكۡرِ﴾, "Sad. By the Quran, full of remembrance." For the whole surah see [Surah Sad Meaning in English](/blog/surah-sad-meaning-english/).

### 1. The idolaters' astonishment (1-16)
They marvelled that a warner from among them came to them, and said: has he made the gods one God? That is truly astonishing. Their chiefs went off saying: carry on and stay patient with your gods.

### 2. Dawud, oft-returning, and the two litigants (17-26)
﴿ٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَٱذۡكُرۡ عَبۡدَنَا دَاوُۥدَ ذَا ٱلۡأَيۡدِۖ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ﴾, "Be patient with what they say, and remember Our servant Dawud, the man of strength; he constantly turned back to Allah." We subjected the mountains to glorify with him evening and morning, and the birds gathered. Then a new quarter: ﴿۞وَهَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ نَبَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَصۡمِ إِذۡ تَسَوَّرُواْ ٱلۡمِحۡرَابَ﴾, "Has the story of the litigants reached you, when they climbed the wall of the prayer chamber?" Two litigants, one with ninety-nine ewes and the other with one. Dawud judged: ﴿قَالَ لَقَدۡ ظَلَمَكَ بِسُؤَالِ نَعۡجَتِكَ إِلَىٰ نِعَاجِهِۦۖ﴾, "He has wronged you by demanding your ewe in addition to his ewes." Then Dawud realised he had been tested, sought his Lord's forgiveness, fell down bowing and turned back: the place of prostration.

Then the command that applies to every ruler: ﴿يَٰدَاوُۥدُ إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰكَ خَلِيفَةٗ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تَتَّبِعِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ فَيُضِلَّكَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "O Dawud, We have made you a successor on earth, so judge between people in truth and do not follow desire, lest it lead you from the way of Allah."

**Lesson**: a judge hears both sides before ruling, and desire is the greatest corrupter of justice.

### 3. The Quran is for reflection (27-29)
We did not create the heaven, earth and all between them in vain. Then the comprehensive verse: ﴿كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡكَ مُبَٰرَكࣱ لِّيَدَّبَّرُوٓاْ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَلِيَتَذَكَّرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾, "A blessed Book We have sent down to you, so they may reflect on its verses and those of understanding may take heed." The purpose of revelation is reflection and remembrance, not mere recitation. Read [A Method for Tadabbur](/blog/tadabbur-method-english/).

### 4. Sulayman, oft-returning (30-40)
﴿وَوَهَبۡنَا لِدَاوُۥدَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَۚ نِعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ﴾, "We gave Dawud Sulayman: an excellent servant; he constantly turned back." Then the parade of fine horses. Then: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ فَتَنَّا سُلَيۡمَٰنَ وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا عَلَىٰ كُرۡسِيِّهِۦ جَسَدٗا ثُمَّ أَنَابَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَهَبۡ لِي مُلۡكٗا لَّا يَنۢبَغِي لِأَحَدٖ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِيٓۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾, "We tried Sulayman and placed a body on his throne; then he turned back. He said: my Lord, forgive me and grant me a kingdom such as no one after me will have; You are the Bestower." He asked forgiveness before asking for a kingdom. In Sahih Al-Bukhari the Prophet ﷺ seized a jinn that attacked him in prayer, then remembered the prayer of his brother Sulayman and let it go.

### 5. Patient Ayyub (41-44)
﴿وَٱذۡكُرۡ عَبۡدَنَآ أَيُّوبَ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ بِنُصۡبٖ وَعَذَابٍ﴾, "Remember Our servant Ayyub, when he called to his Lord: Shaytan has touched me with hardship and pain." He was told: strike the ground with your foot; here is cool water to bathe in and drink. We gave him his family and as many again. Then: ﴿وَخُذۡ بِيَدِكَ ضِغۡثٗا فَٱضۡرِب بِّهِۦ وَلَا تَحۡنَثۡۗ إِنَّا وَجَدۡنَٰهُ صَابِرٗاۚ نِّعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابࣱ﴾, "Take in your hand a bunch of stalks, strike with it and do not break your oath. We found him patient: an excellent servant; he constantly turned back."

**The shared trait**: Dawud, Sulayman and Ayyub are all called *awwab*, "constantly returning to Allah". Dawud returned after his test, Sulayman after his, and Ayyub in his affliction.

### 6. The other prophets and scenes of the Hereafter (45-64)
Ibrahim, Ishaq and Ya'qub, men of strength and vision, whom We purified with the remembrance of the Home. Then Isma'il, Al-Yasa' and Dhul-Kifl. Then the Gardens of 'Adn, and a new quarter: ﴿۞وَعِندَهُمۡ قَٰصِرَٰتُ ٱلطَّرۡفِ أَتۡرَابٌ﴾, "With them will be companions of modest gaze, of equal age." Then the people of the Fire disputing with one another.

### 7. The story of Iblis and the close (65-88)
When your Lord said to the angels: I am creating a human from clay, ﴿فَإِذَا سَوَّيۡتُهُۥ وَنَفَخۡتُ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِي فَقَعُواْ لَهُۥ سَٰجِدِينَ﴾, "so when I have proportioned him and breathed into him of My spirit, fall down in prostration to him." They prostrated except Iblis: ﴿قَالَ يَـٰٓإِبۡلِيسُ مَا مَنَعَكَ أَن تَسۡجُدَ لِمَا خَلَقۡتُ بِيَدَيَّۖ أَسۡتَكۡبَرۡتَ أَمۡ كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡعَالِينَ﴾, "He said: O Iblis, what prevented you from prostrating to what I created with My hands? Were you arrogant, or among the exalted?" He said: I am better than him; You created me from fire and him from clay. Then he swore: ﴿قَالَ فَبِعِزَّتِكَ لَأُغۡوِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا عِبَادَكَ مِنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ﴾, "By Your might, I will mislead them all, except Your sincere servants among them."

The close: ﴿قُلۡ مَآ أَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٖ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُتَكَلِّفِينَ﴾, "Say: I ask no payment of you, nor am I one who pretends", then ﴿وَلَتَعۡلَمُنَّ نَبَأَهُۥ بَعۡدَ حِينِۭ﴾, "and you will surely know its news after a time."

**Reflect**: Iblis himself admits he has no power over the sincere servants. Sincerity is a fortress against Shaytan, and it is the theme of the next surah.

## Part four: the start of Surah Az-Zumar (1-31)

Az-Zumar is Makki, with 75 verses, 31 of which fall in this juz. For the whole surah see [Surah Az-Zumar Meaning in English](/blog/surah-az-zumar-meaning-english/).

### 1. Sincere religion (1-7)
﴿تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ فَٱعۡبُدِ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصٗا لَّهُ ٱلدِّينَ﴾ ﴿أَلَا لِلَّهِ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡخَالِصُۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مَا نَعۡبُدُهُمۡ إِلَّا لِيُقَرِّبُونَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ زُلۡفَىٰٓ﴾, "The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Almighty, the Wise. We have sent down the Book to you in truth, so worship Allah, sincere to Him in religion. Unquestionably, for Allah is the pure religion. Those who take protectors besides Him say: we only worship them so they may bring us nearer to Allah." This is the idolaters' argument in every age: intermediaries.

### 2. The devout and the knowing (8-10)
A new quarter: ﴿۞وَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ ضُرࣱّ دَعَا رَبَّهُۥ مُنِيبًا إِلَيۡهِ ثُمَّ إِذَا خَوَّلَهُۥ نِعۡمَةٗ مِّنۡهُ نَسِيَ مَا كَانَ يَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ مِن قَبۡلُ﴾, "When harm touches man, he calls on his Lord, turning to Him; then when He grants him a favour, he forgets what he used to call for before." Then the contrast: ﴿أَمَّنۡ هُوَ قَٰنِتٌ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيۡلِ سَاجِدٗا وَقَآئِمٗا يَحۡذَرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَيَرۡجُواْ رَحۡمَةَ رَبِّهِۦۗ قُلۡ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡلَمُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَۗ﴾, "Is one who is devout in the hours of the night, prostrating and standing, wary of the Hereafter and hoping for his Lord's mercy...? Say: are those who know equal to those who do not know?" Then: ﴿إِنَّمَا يُوَفَّى ٱلصَّـٰبِرُونَ أَجۡرَهُم بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ﴾, "The patient will be given their reward without measure."

**Reflect**: knowledge is mentioned right after the one who stands at night; the truly knowledgeable is the one whose knowledge leads him to worship and awe.

### 3. The best speech and the best discourse (11-23)
Say: I have been commanded to worship Allah, sincere to Him in religion. Then the description of people of understanding: ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ فَيَتَّبِعُونَ أَحۡسَنَهُۥٓۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَدَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمۡ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾, "who listen to speech and follow the best of it. Those are the ones Allah has guided, and they are the people of understanding." Then: ﴿أَفَمَن شَرَحَ ٱللَّهُ صَدۡرَهُۥ لِلۡإِسۡلَٰمِ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ نُورٖ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡقَٰسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مِّن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "Is one whose chest Allah has opened to Islam, so he is on a light from his Lord...? Woe to those whose hearts are hardened against the remembrance of Allah."

Then the description of the Quran and its effect: ﴿ٱللَّهُ نَزَّلَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡحَدِيثِ كِتَٰبٗا مُّتَشَٰبِهٗا مَّثَانِيَ تَقۡشَعِرُّ مِنۡهُ جُلُودُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُمۡ ثُمَّ تَلِينُ جُلُودُهُمۡ وَقُلُوبُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "Allah has sent down the best discourse: a Book consistent in itself, often repeated; the skins of those who fear their Lord shiver from it, then their skins and hearts soften to the remembrance of Allah."

**Lesson**: the sign of a living heart is that it shivers at verses of warning and softens at verses of mercy.

### 4. The parable of the two men, and death (24-31)
﴿ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا رَّجُلٗا فِيهِ شُرَكَآءُ مُتَشَٰكِسُونَ وَرَجُلٗا سَلَمٗا لِّرَجُلٍ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِيَانِ مَثَلًاۚ﴾, "Allah presents an example: a slave owned by quarrelling partners, and a slave belonging entirely to one man. Are they equal in comparison?" The idolater is a slave of quarrelling masters; the monotheist serves one Master. Then the truth: ﴿إِنَّكَ مَيِّتࣱ وَإِنَّهُم مَّيِّتُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ عِندَ رَبِّكُمۡ تَخۡتَصِمُونَ﴾, "You will die and they will die; then on the Day of Resurrection you will dispute before your Lord." Abu Bakr recited this verse of Az-Zumar, together with the verse of Al 'Imran, on the day the Prophet ﷺ died, as reported in Sahih Al-Bukhari.

## Tajweed points in Juz 23

### The pause in ﴿مِن مَّرۡقَدِنَاۜ﴾, Ya-Sin 52
One of the four *saktah* places in Hafs: a brief pause of two counts without taking breath, then continue with ﴿هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ﴾.

### ﴿صٓۚ﴾ at the start of Sad
Read *Sad* with a six-count necessary madd. The dal is sakin; when continuing to "wal-Qur'an" it is pronounced clearly.

### ﴿إِلۡ يَاسِينَ﴾ in As-Saffat 130
Read in Hafs as *il yasin*, with a kasrah on the hamzah and a sakin lam.

### ﴿مَسۡـُٔولُونَ﴾ in As-Saffat 24
A dammah hamzah on a seat followed by a madd waw: *mas'ulun*.

### The prostration of the juz
At ﴿وَخَرَّ رَاكِعٗا وَأَنَابَ﴾ in Sad 24. See [Sujud al-Tilawah in English](/blog/sujud-al-tilawa-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. The refrain of peace in As-Saffat
- Nuh (79): ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ نُوحٖ فِي ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, adding "among the worlds".
- Ibrahim (109): ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾.
- Musa and Harun (120): ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ﴾.
- Ilyas (130): ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰٓ إِلۡ يَاسِينَ﴾.
- No greeting of peace is given for Lut or Yunus in the surah.

### 2. "Thus do We reward the doers of good"
It follows the peace upon Nuh (80), Ibrahim (110), Musa and Harun (121) and Ilyas (131), and in the sacrifice story (105) it comes before the greeting.

### 3. "An excellent servant; he constantly turned back" in Sad
- Sulayman (30): ﴿نِعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ﴾.
- Ayyub (44): ﴿نِّعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابࣱ﴾.
- Dawud (17): ﴿إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ﴾ without "an excellent servant".

### 4. The story of Iblis in Sad and elsewhere
- Sad 75: ﴿لِمَا خَلَقۡتُ بِيَدَيَّۖ﴾, uniquely mentioning "with My hands".
- Sad 82: ﴿فَبِعِزَّتِكَ لَأُغۡوِيَنَّهُمۡ﴾, "by Your might"; in Al-Hijr 39 it is "because You have put me in error".

## How to memorize Juz 23 with understanding

1. **Ya-Sin**: order the three signs: the earth; night, sun and moon; the ship.
2. **As-Saffat has short verse endings**: learn the refrain once, then focus on what precedes it in each story.
3. **Order the prophets of As-Saffat**: Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa and Harun, Ilyas, Lut, Yunus.
4. **Sad**: Dawud, Sulayman, Ayyub, then the other prophets, then Iblis.
5. **Az-Zumar**: link its opening to the word "sincere", which recurs in it.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 23

- **Reflect on signs in creation**, from the earth to the moon.
- **Remember that your limbs will testify against you.**
- **Remember your own creation** when resurrection seems far-fetched.
- **Talk with your children**, even about the greatest commands.
- **Submit to Allah's command** as Ibrahim and his son did.
- **Glorify Allah in ease**, and He will save you in hardship.
- **Judge with truth** and do not follow desire.
- **Reflect on the Quran**: that is why it was sent down.
- **Be oft-returning** to Allah.
- **Seek forgiveness before you ask.**
- **Make your religion sincere for Allah**: sincerity is your fortress against Shaytan.
- **Follow the best of speech.**
- **Let your heart soften to the remembrance of Allah.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 23 called "Wa Mali"?
Because the verse just before it is ﴿وَمَالِيَ لَآ أَعۡبُدُ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنِي﴾; its first verse is ﴿۞وَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ﴾.

### Who was the son to be sacrificed in As-Saffat?
The stronger view of the majority of careful scholars is Isma'il, peace be upon him, since the good news of Ishaq comes after the story in verse 112.

### How many prostrations are in Juz 23?
One, in Surah Sad, verse 24.

### Which prophets are called *awwab* in Surah Sad?
Dawud, Sulayman and Ayyub, peace be upon them.

### Where does Juz 23 end?
At Az-Zumar 31. Juz 24 begins with ﴿۞فَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّن كَذَبَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ﴾, "Who does greater wrong than one who lies about Allah?"

## Conclusion

Juz 23 answers the denier of resurrection that the One who first produced the bones will give them life, shows you a procession of prophets whom Allah greets with peace one by one, stops with a father who lays his son down for sacrifice in obedience and is given a great ransom, then shows you the oft-returning prophets Dawud, Sulayman and Ayyub, and finally calls you in Az-Zumar to sincere religion.
