Surah An-Nur is the surah of light: the light of Allah in the heavens and earth, the light of guidance in the believer's heart, and the light of purity in society. It begins by declaring itself "a surah which We have sent down and made obligatory", then sets out laws that protect honour and chastity. At its heart is the story of the slander against 'A'ishah, the Mother of the Believers, which Allah Himself answered from above the seven heavens, teaching the Muslim community how to deal with rumours forever. It gives the etiquette of entering homes, commands men and women to lower their gaze, encourages marriage, and contains the magnificent Verse of Light: "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth... Light upon light."

This guide explains Surah An-Nur section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons for families and communities.

## Quick Facts About Surah An-Nur
- **Number of verses**: 64.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 24, in the eighteenth juz.
- **Name**: An-Nur means "the light", from verse 35.
- **Main themes**: chastity and family protection, dealing with slander, social etiquette, the light of guidance and obedience to the Messenger ﷺ.

## Verses 1–10: Laws Protecting Honour
﴿سُورَةٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهَا وَفَرَضۡنَٰهَا وَأَنزَلۡنَا فِيهَآ ءَايَٰتِۭ بَيِّنَٰتٖ لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلزَّانِيَةُ وَٱلزَّانِي فَٱجۡلِدُواْ كُلَّ وَٰحِدٖ مِّنۡهُمَا مِاْئَةَ جَلۡدَةٖۖ وَلَا تَأۡخُذۡكُم بِهِمَا رَأۡفَةࣱ فِي دِينِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۖ وَلۡيَشۡهَدۡ عَذَابَهُمَا طَآئِفَةࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلزَّانِي لَا يَنكِحُ إِلَّا زَانِيَةً أَوۡ مُشۡرِكَةٗ وَٱلزَّانِيَةُ لَا يَنكِحُهَآ إِلَّا زَانٍ أَوۡ مُشۡرِكࣱۚ وَحُرِّمَ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 24:1-3)

**"A surah which We have sent down and made obligatory."** The opening shows that its rulings are binding.

**The punishment for zina** (unlawful sexual relations) between unmarried people is a hundred lashes, carried out by the authorities after proof, with strict conditions. Islam makes proving it extremely difficult, aiming to protect society more than to punish.

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَرۡمُونَ ٱلۡمُحۡصَنَٰتِ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَأۡتُواْ بِأَرۡبَعَةِ شُهَدَآءَ فَٱجۡلِدُوهُمۡ ثَمَٰنِينَ جَلۡدَةٗ وَلَا تَقۡبَلُواْ لَهُمۡ شَهَٰدَةً أَبَدٗاۚ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ تَابُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ وَأَصۡلَحُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 24:4-5)

**False accusation (*qadhf*)**: whoever accuses chaste women without four witnesses receives eighty lashes, his testimony is never accepted, and he is called defiantly disobedient, **"except for those who repent thereafter and reform"**. Reputation is sacred.

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَرۡمُونَ أَزۡوَٰجَهُمۡ وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُمۡ شُهَدَآءُ إِلَّآ أَنفُسُهُمۡ فَشَهَٰدَةُ أَحَدِهِمۡ أَرۡبَعُ شَهَٰدَٰتِۭ بِٱللَّهِ إِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡخَٰمِسَةُ أَنَّ لَعۡنَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡهِ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ﴾ ﴿وَيَدۡرَؤُاْ عَنۡهَا ٱلۡعَذَابَ أَن تَشۡهَدَ أَرۡبَعَ شَهَٰدَٰتِۭ بِٱللَّهِ إِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡخَٰمِسَةَ أَنَّ غَضَبَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡهَآ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَوۡلَا فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَرَحۡمَتُهُۥ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ تَوَّابٌ حَكِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 24:6-10)

**Li'an**: if a husband accuses his wife without witnesses, each swears oaths before Allah, and the marriage is ended. Allah's mercy kept the family from greater harm.

## Verses 11–26: The Slander Against 'A'ishah
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو بِٱلۡإِفۡكِ عُصۡبَةࣱ مِّنكُمۡۚ لَا تَحۡسَبُوهُ شَرّٗا لَّكُمۖ بَلۡ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡۚ لِكُلِّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُم مَّا ٱكۡتَسَبَ مِنَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِۚ وَٱلَّذِي تَوَلَّىٰ كِبۡرَهُۥ مِنۡهُمۡ لَهُۥ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 24:11)

**"Indeed, those who came with falsehood are a group among you. Do not think it bad for you; rather, it is good for you."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'A'ishah narrated the full story: on the return from an expedition she went to look for a lost necklace, the army left without her, and Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal found her and brought her back. The hypocrites, led by 'Abdullah ibn Ubayy, spread a lie about her. For a month she suffered without revelation, until Allah revealed these verses declaring her innocence.

**How was it "good"?** It exposed the hypocrites, raised 'A'ishah's rank with a revelation recited until the Day of Judgement, and taught the community lasting rules about rumours.

﴿لَّوۡلَآ إِذۡ سَمِعۡتُمُوهُ ظَنَّ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ بِأَنفُسِهِمۡ خَيۡرٗا وَقَالُواْ هَٰذَآ إِفۡكࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّوۡلَا جَآءُو عَلَيۡهِ بِأَرۡبَعَةِ شُهَدَآءَۚ فَإِذۡ لَمۡ يَأۡتُواْ بِٱلشُّهَدَآءِ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ هُمُ ٱلۡكَٰذِبُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَوۡلَا فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَرَحۡمَتُهُۥ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لَمَسَّكُمۡ فِي مَآ أَفَضۡتُمۡ فِيهِ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ تَلَقَّوۡنَهُۥ بِأَلۡسِنَتِكُمۡ وَتَقُولُونَ بِأَفۡوَاهِكُم مَّا لَيۡسَ لَكُم بِهِۦ عِلۡمࣱ وَتَحۡسَبُونَهُۥ هَيِّنٗا وَهُوَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَلَوۡلَآ إِذۡ سَمِعۡتُمُوهُ قُلۡتُم مَّا يَكُونُ لَنَآ أَن نَّتَكَلَّمَ بِهَٰذَا سُبۡحَٰنَكَ هَٰذَا بُهۡتَٰنٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿يَعِظُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ أَن تَعُودُواْ لِمِثۡلِهِۦٓ أَبَدًا إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 24:12-17)

**Lessons on rumours**:
1. **"Why, when you heard it, did the believing men and women not think good of one another?"** Assume good about your brothers and sisters.
2. **"Why did they not produce four witnesses?"** Demand proof.
3. **"When you received it with your tongues and said with your mouths that of which you had no knowledge, and thought it was insignificant while it was, in the sight of Allah, tremendous."** Notice "received it with your tongues", as if the rumour skipped the mind entirely.
4. **"Why, when you heard it, did you not say: It is not for us to speak of this. Exalted are You; this is a great slander."** Refuse to repeat it.
5. **"Allah warns you against returning to the like of it, ever."**

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُحِبُّونَ أَن تَشِيعَ ٱلۡفَٰحِشَةُ فِي ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 24:19)

**"Indeed, those who like that immorality should be spread among those who have believed will have a painful punishment in this world and the hereafter."** Even *liking* that indecency spreads is condemned.

﴿وَلَا يَأۡتَلِ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡفَضۡلِ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلسَّعَةِ أَن يُؤۡتُوٓاْ أُوْلِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينَ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَلۡيَعۡفُواْ وَلۡيَصۡفَحُوٓاْۗ أَلَا تُحِبُّونَ أَن يَغۡفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 24:22)

**"Do you not love that Allah should forgive you?"** Abu Bakr used to support his relative Mistah, who had taken part in spreading the slander. After the revelation, Abu Bakr swore he would stop. Allah revealed: **"And let not those of virtue among you and wealth swear not to give to their relatives and the needy and the emigrants for the cause of Allah, and let them pardon and overlook. Would you not like that Allah should forgive you?"** Abu Bakr said: "Yes, by Allah, I love that Allah should forgive me," and he resumed his support, as reported in al-Bukhari.

## Verses 27–34: Etiquette of the Home and Modesty
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَدۡخُلُواْ بُيُوتًا غَيۡرَ بُيُوتِكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ تَسۡتَأۡنِسُواْ وَتُسَلِّمُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهَاۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَإِن لَّمۡ تَجِدُواْ فِيهَآ أَحَدٗا فَلَا تَدۡخُلُوهَا حَتَّىٰ يُؤۡذَنَ لَكُمۡۖ وَإِن قِيلَ لَكُمُ ٱرۡجِعُواْ فَٱرۡجِعُواْۖ هُوَ أَزۡكَىٰ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحٌ أَن تَدۡخُلُواْ بُيُوتًا غَيۡرَ مَسۡكُونَةٖ فِيهَا مَتَٰعࣱ لَّكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تُبۡدُونَ وَمَا تَكۡتُمُونَ﴾ (Quran 24:27-29)

**"Do not enter houses other than your own houses until you ascertain welcome and greet their inhabitants."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Seeking permission is three times; if you are given permission, enter, otherwise go back." And if told to go back, **"go back; it is purer for you"**.

﴿قُل لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يَغُضُّواْ مِنۡ أَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡ وَيَحۡفَظُواْ فُرُوجَهُمۡۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَزۡكَىٰ لَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا يَصۡنَعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقُل لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ يَغۡضُضۡنَ مِنۡ أَبۡصَٰرِهِنَّ وَيَحۡفَظۡنَ فُرُوجَهُنَّ وَلَا يُبۡدِينَ زِينَتَهُنَّ إِلَّا مَا ظَهَرَ مِنۡهَاۖ وَلۡيَضۡرِبۡنَ بِخُمُرِهِنَّ عَلَىٰ جُيُوبِهِنَّۖ وَلَا يُبۡدِينَ زِينَتَهُنَّ إِلَّا لِبُعُولَتِهِنَّ أَوۡ ءَابَآئِهِنَّ أَوۡ ءَابَآءِ بُعُولَتِهِنَّ أَوۡ أَبۡنَآئِهِنَّ أَوۡ أَبۡنَآءِ بُعُولَتِهِنَّ أَوۡ إِخۡوَٰنِهِنَّ أَوۡ بَنِيٓ إِخۡوَٰنِهِنَّ أَوۡ بَنِيٓ أَخَوَٰتِهِنَّ أَوۡ نِسَآئِهِنَّ أَوۡ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُنَّ أَوِ ٱلتَّـٰبِعِينَ غَيۡرِ أُوْلِي ٱلۡإِرۡبَةِ مِنَ ٱلرِّجَالِ أَوِ ٱلطِّفۡلِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يَظۡهَرُواْ عَلَىٰ عَوۡرَٰتِ ٱلنِّسَآءِۖ وَلَا يَضۡرِبۡنَ بِأَرۡجُلِهِنَّ لِيُعۡلَمَ مَا يُخۡفِينَ مِن زِينَتِهِنَّۚ وَتُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعًا أَيُّهَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 24:30-31)

**For men**: **"Tell the believing men to reduce some of their vision and guard their private parts. That is purer for them."**

**For women**: likewise to lower their gaze and guard their chastity, **"and not expose their adornment except that which appears thereof, and to wrap a portion of their head covers over their chests"**, and not to show their adornment except to their husbands and close relatives listed in the verse.

**"And turn to Allah in repentance, all of you, O believers, that you might succeed."** After all these commands, the door of repentance is opened for everyone.

﴿وَأَنكِحُواْ ٱلۡأَيَٰمَىٰ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ مِنۡ عِبَادِكُمۡ وَإِمَآئِكُمۡۚ إِن يَكُونُواْ فُقَرَآءَ يُغۡنِهِمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَلۡيَسۡتَعۡفِفِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَجِدُونَ نِكَاحًا حَتَّىٰ يُغۡنِيَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَبۡتَغُونَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِمَّا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡ فَكَاتِبُوهُمۡ إِنۡ عَلِمۡتُمۡ فِيهِمۡ خَيۡرٗاۖ وَءَاتُوهُم مِّن مَّالِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۚ وَلَا تُكۡرِهُواْ فَتَيَٰتِكُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡبِغَآءِ إِنۡ أَرَدۡنَ تَحَصُّنٗا لِّتَبۡتَغُواْ عَرَضَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَمَن يُكۡرِههُّنَّ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ إِكۡرَٰهِهِنَّ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 24:32-33)

**Encouraging marriage**: **"Marry the unmarried among you and the righteous among your male and female slaves. If they should be poor, Allah will enrich them from His bounty."** Poverty should not prevent marriage. And **"let those who find not the means to marry abstain until Allah enriches them"**.

## Verses 35–40: The Verse of Light
﴿۞ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِۦ كَمِشۡكَوٰةٖ فِيهَا مِصۡبَاحٌۖ ٱلۡمِصۡبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍۖ ٱلزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوۡكَبࣱ دُرِّيࣱّ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٖ زَيۡتُونَةٖ لَّا شَرۡقِيَّةٖ وَلَا غَرۡبِيَّةٖ يَكَادُ زَيۡتُهَا يُضِيٓءُ وَلَوۡ لَمۡ تَمۡسَسۡهُ نَارࣱۚ نُّورٌ عَلَىٰ نُورٖۚ يَهۡدِي ٱللَّهُ لِنُورِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلَ لِلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 24:35)

**"Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth. The example of His light is like a niche within which is a lamp; the lamp is within glass, the glass as if it were a pearly white star lit from the oil of a blessed olive tree, neither of the east nor of the west, whose oil would almost glow even if untouched by fire. Light upon light. Allah guides to His light whom He wills."**

**Commentators explained the parable** as the light of faith in the believer's heart: the chest is the niche, the heart is the glass, faith is the lamp, and the oil is the pure revelation and natural disposition (*fitrah*). When revelation meets a sound fitrah, it is "light upon light".

﴿فِي بُيُوتٍ أَذِنَ ٱللَّهُ أَن تُرۡفَعَ وَيُذۡكَرَ فِيهَا ٱسۡمُهُۥ يُسَبِّحُ لَهُۥ فِيهَا بِٱلۡغُدُوِّ وَٱلۡأٓصَالِ﴾ ﴿رِجَالࣱ لَّا تُلۡهِيهِمۡ تِجَٰرَةࣱ وَلَا بَيۡعٌ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَإِقَامِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَإِيتَآءِ ٱلزَّكَوٰةِ يَخَافُونَ يَوۡمٗا تَتَقَلَّبُ فِيهِ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ﴾ ﴿لِيَجۡزِيَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ أَحۡسَنَ مَا عَمِلُواْ وَيَزِيدَهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَرۡزُقُ مَن يَشَآءُ بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ﴾ (Quran 24:36-38)

**Where is this light found?** **"In houses which Allah has ordered to be raised and that His name be mentioned therein"**: the mosques. **"Men whom neither commerce nor sale distracts from the remembrance of Allah and performance of prayer and giving of zakah."**

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ كَسَرَابِۭ بِقِيعَةٖ يَحۡسَبُهُ ٱلظَّمۡـَٔانُ مَآءً حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَهُۥ لَمۡ يَجِدۡهُ شَيۡـٔٗا وَوَجَدَ ٱللَّهَ عِندَهُۥ فَوَفَّىٰهُ حِسَابَهُۥۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ﴾ ﴿أَوۡ كَظُلُمَٰتٖ فِي بَحۡرٖ لُّجِّيّٖ يَغۡشَىٰهُ مَوۡجࣱ مِّن فَوۡقِهِۦ مَوۡجࣱ مِّن فَوۡقِهِۦ سَحَابࣱۚ ظُلُمَٰتُۢ بَعۡضُهَا فَوۡقَ بَعۡضٍ إِذَآ أَخۡرَجَ يَدَهُۥ لَمۡ يَكَدۡ يَرَىٰهَاۗ وَمَن لَّمۡ يَجۡعَلِ ٱللَّهُ لَهُۥ نُورٗا فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن نُّورٍ﴾ (Quran 24:39-40)

**The disbelievers' deeds** are like a mirage in a desert, or like **"darknesses within an unfathomable sea, covered by waves, upon which are waves, over which are clouds: darknesses, some of them upon others"**. **"And he to whom Allah has not granted light, for him there is no light."**

## Verses 41–57: Creation and the Promise
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُسَبِّحُ لَهُۥ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلطَّيۡرُ صَـٰٓفَّـٰتٖۖ كُلࣱّ قَدۡ عَلِمَ صَلَاتَهُۥ وَتَسۡبِيحَهُۥۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِمَا يَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 24:41)

**"Do you not see that Allah is exalted by whomever is within the heavens and the earth and by the birds with wings spread? Each has known his prayer and exalting."**

﴿وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ لَيَسۡتَخۡلِفَنَّهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَمَا ٱسۡتَخۡلَفَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ وَلَيُمَكِّنَنَّ لَهُمۡ دِينَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي ٱرۡتَضَىٰ لَهُمۡ وَلَيُبَدِّلَنَّهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ خَوۡفِهِمۡ أَمۡنٗاۚ يَعۡبُدُونَنِي لَا يُشۡرِكُونَ بِي شَيۡـٔٗاۚ وَمَن كَفَرَ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 24:55)

**"Allah has promised those who have believed among you and done righteous deeds that He will surely grant them succession upon the earth... and that He will surely substitute for them, after their fear, security, for they worship Me, not associating anything with Me."** The condition: sincere worship.

## Verses 58–64: Privacy and Obedience
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لِيَسۡتَـٔۡذِنكُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يَبۡلُغُواْ ٱلۡحُلُمَ مِنكُمۡ ثَلَٰثَ مَرَّـٰتٖۚ مِّن قَبۡلِ صَلَوٰةِ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ وَحِينَ تَضَعُونَ ثِيَابَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظَّهِيرَةِ وَمِنۢ بَعۡدِ صَلَوٰةِ ٱلۡعِشَآءِۚ ثَلَٰثُ عَوۡرَٰتٖ لَّكُمۡۚ لَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَلَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ جُنَاحُۢ بَعۡدَهُنَّۚ طَوَّـٰفُونَ عَلَيۡكُم بَعۡضُكُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 24:58)

**Three times of privacy**: children and servants should seek permission before entering at three times: before Fajr, at midday when clothes are set aside, and after 'Isha.

﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَإِذَا كَانُواْ مَعَهُۥ عَلَىٰٓ أَمۡرٖ جَامِعٖ لَّمۡ يَذۡهَبُواْ حَتَّىٰ يَسۡتَـٔۡذِنُوهُۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَـٔۡذِنُونَكَ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۚ فَإِذَا ٱسۡتَـٔۡذَنُوكَ لِبَعۡضِ شَأۡنِهِمۡ فَأۡذَن لِّمَن شِئۡتَ مِنۡهُمۡ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمُ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّا تَجۡعَلُواْ دُعَآءَ ٱلرَّسُولِ بَيۡنَكُمۡ كَدُعَآءِ بَعۡضِكُم بَعۡضٗاۚ قَدۡ يَعۡلَمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَتَسَلَّلُونَ مِنكُمۡ لِوَاذٗاۚ فَلۡيَحۡذَرِ ٱلَّذِينَ يُخَالِفُونَ عَنۡ أَمۡرِهِۦٓ أَن تُصِيبَهُمۡ فِتۡنَةٌ أَوۡ يُصِيبَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 24:62-63)

**"Do not make the calling of the Messenger among yourselves as the call of one of you to another."** **"So let those beware who dissent from his order, lest fitnah strike them or a painful punishment."**

## Word Meanings

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Do not think it bad for you; rather, it is good"**: hidden good in trials.
2. **"Received it with your tongues"**: rumours that skip the mind.
3. **"Do you not love that Allah should forgive you?"**: a question that moves hearts to forgive.
4. **The Verse of Light**: a layered parable of niche, glass, lamp and oil.
5. **"Darknesses, some upon others"**: layer upon layer of darkness.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Izhar**: in *nurun 'ala nur* (verse 35), the tanween before the throat letter 'ayn is pronounced clearly.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *ka-mishkatin fiha* (verse 35), the tanween is hidden before the fa'.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna alladhina* (verse 11), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yubdina* (verse 31), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 43).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Mu'minun before it praised "those who guard their private parts", and An-Nur gives detailed laws protecting chastity. Surah Al-Furqan after it opens with the Criterion, continuing the theme of guidance as light.

## Dealing With Rumours: A Practical Guide
1. **Think good of people first.**
2. **Ask for proof.**
3. **Do not repeat it**, even to "check".
4. **Say: It is not for us to speak of this.**
5. **Defend the absent.**
6. **Do not enjoy indecency being spread.**
7. **Forgive those who wronged you**, hoping for Allah's forgiveness.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Guard people's honour.**
2. **Seek permission before entering.**
3. **Lower your gaze.**
4. **Encourage and facilitate marriage.**
5. **Fill your heart with the light of revelation.**
6. **Do not let trade distract you from prayer.**
7. **Obey the Messenger ﷺ.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the story of al-Ifk?
The slander spread by hypocrites against 'A'ishah; Allah revealed her innocence in verses 11–26, as she narrated in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does "Do you not love that Allah should forgive you?" refer to?
Abu Bakr's oath to stop supporting Mistah, who took part in the slander; he forgave and resumed his support.

### What does the surah command about the gaze?
Both believing men and women are commanded to lower their gaze and guard their chastity.

### What is the Verse of Light?
Verse 35, which describes Allah's light with the parable of a niche, a lamp, glass and blessed oil.

### How many times should one seek permission to enter?
Three times; if not permitted, one should leave, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the punishment for false accusation of chastity?
Eighty lashes and rejection of testimony, unless the person repents.

### How many verses are in Surah An-Nur?
64 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah An-Nur builds a society of light: honour protected, rumours stopped at the tongue, homes entered with permission, gazes lowered, marriage made easy, and hearts filled with the light of revelation. And in the middle of its laws it asks a question that softens every hard heart: do you not love that Allah should forgive you? Then forgive.
