﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ فَسۡـَٔلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلذِّكۡرِ إِن كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:43]

## The context
- The polytheists denied that a messenger could be human. Allah said all previous messengers were men who received revelation.

## Who are *ahl adh-dhikr*?
- In context: the People of the Book who knew earlier messengers were human.
- By the general wording: **people of knowledge** in any field.

## A general rule
| Field | Ask |
|---|---|
| Religion | Trustworthy scholars |
| Tajweed | A qualified, certified teacher |
| Medicine | A specialist doctor |

## Etiquette of asking
- Choose someone trustworthy, ask to learn rather than argue, and remember the Prophet's words: *The cure for ignorance is asking* (narrated by Abu Dawud).

## Lessons
- **Do not be shy** to ask.
- **Do not give religious rulings** without knowledge.
