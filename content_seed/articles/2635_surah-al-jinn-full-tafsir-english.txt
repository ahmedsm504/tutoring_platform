Surah Al-Jinn opens a window onto a world we cannot see. A group of jinn, passing by while the Prophet ﷺ led his Companions in the dawn prayer, heard the Quran and were so moved that they returned to their people saying: "Indeed, we have heard an amazing Quran. It guides to the right course, and we have believed in it." Most of the surah is their own testimony, in their own words: about Allah, about the foolish among them, about humans who sought refuge in them, about the sky they could no longer eavesdrop on, and about the believers and deviators among them. The surah then turns to the Prophet ﷺ: the mosques belong to Allah, the Messenger only conveys, and the unseen belongs to Allah alone.

This guide explains Surah Al-Jinn verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons, avoiding the many unfounded tales told about the jinn.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Jinn
- **Number of verses**: 28.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 72, in the twenty-ninth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Jinn, from the first verse.
- **Main themes**: the effect of the Quran, tawhid, the reality of the jinn, the guarded heavens, sincere worship and the unseen.

## The Occasion of Revelation
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas reported that the Prophet ﷺ set out with some of his Companions toward the market of 'Ukaz. The devils had been prevented from receiving news from the heavens and were struck with flames. They spread out east and west to find the cause, and some of them came upon the Prophet ﷺ at Nakhlah while he was leading his Companions in the Fajr prayer. When they heard the Quran, they listened and said: "This is what has come between us and the news of the heavens." They returned to their people and said: "Indeed, we have heard an amazing Quran." Allah revealed: "Say: It has been revealed to me that a group of the jinn listened." In the narration of Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas noted that the Prophet ﷺ did not see them on that occasion; he learned of it through revelation.

## Verses 1–2: An Amazing Quran
﴿قُلۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ أَنَّهُ ٱسۡتَمَعَ نَفَرࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا سَمِعۡنَا قُرۡءَانًا عَجَبٗا﴾ ﴿يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلرُّشۡدِ فَـَٔامَنَّا بِهِۦۖ وَلَن نُّشۡرِكَ بِرَبِّنَآ أَحَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:1-2)

**"Say: It has been revealed to me that a group of the jinn listened and said: Indeed, we have heard an amazing Quran. It guides to the right course, and we have believed in it. And we will never associate with our Lord anyone."** They heard once and believed at once; how many humans hear it a thousand times without being moved?

## Verses 3–5: Their Testimony About Allah
﴿وَأَنَّهُۥ تَعَٰلَىٰ جَدُّ رَبِّنَا مَا ٱتَّخَذَ صَٰحِبَةٗ وَلَا وَلَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:3)

**"And exalted is the nobleness of our Lord; He has not taken a wife or a son."**

﴿وَأَنَّهُۥ كَانَ يَقُولُ سَفِيهُنَا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ شَطَطٗا﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّآ أَن لَّن تَقُولَ ٱلۡإِنسُ وَٱلۡجِنُّ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبٗا﴾ (Quran 72:4-5)

**"And that our foolish one has been saying about Allah an excessive transgression. And we had thought that mankind and the jinn would never speak about Allah a lie."** They had trusted what others said, assuming no one would lie about Allah. When they heard the truth, they recognised the lie.

## Verse 6: Seeking Refuge in Jinn
﴿وَأَنَّهُۥ كَانَ رِجَالࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡإِنسِ يَعُوذُونَ بِرِجَالٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَزَادُوهُمۡ رَهَقٗا﴾ (Quran 72:6)

**"And there were men from mankind who sought refuge in men from the jinn, so they only increased them in burden."** In the time of ignorance, a traveller who stopped in a valley would say: "I seek refuge in the master of this valley from the foolish ones of his people." This only made the jinn more arrogant and the humans more afraid. Islam replaced it with seeking refuge in Allah: in Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever stops at a place and says: I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created, nothing will harm him until he leaves that place."

## Verse 7: Denial of Resurrection
﴿وَأَنَّهُمۡ ظَنُّواْ كَمَا ظَنَنتُمۡ أَن لَّن يَبۡعَثَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:7)

**"And they had thought, as you thought, that Allah would never send anyone."**

## Verses 8–10: The Guarded Sky
﴿وَأَنَّا لَمَسۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ فَوَجَدۡنَٰهَا مُلِئَتۡ حَرَسٗا شَدِيدٗا وَشُهُبٗا﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّا كُنَّا نَقۡعُدُ مِنۡهَا مَقَٰعِدَ لِلسَّمۡعِۖ فَمَن يَسۡتَمِعِ ٱلۡأٓنَ يَجِدۡ لَهُۥ شِهَابٗا رَّصَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:8-9)

**"And we have sought the heaven but found it filled with powerful guards and burning flames. And we used to sit therein in positions for hearing, but whoever listens now will find a burning flame lying in wait for him."** With the coming of the final revelation, the heavens were guarded.

﴿وَأَنَّا لَا نَدۡرِيٓ أَشَرٌّ أُرِيدَ بِمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمۡ أَرَادَ بِهِمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ رَشَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:10)

**"And we do not know whether evil is intended for those on earth or whether their Lord intends for them a right course."** Notice their courtesy: they attributed evil to no one ("is intended"), but attributed good to their Lord.

## Verses 11–15: Believers and Deviators Among the Jinn
﴿وَأَنَّا مِنَّا ٱلصَّـٰلِحُونَ وَمِنَّا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَۖ كُنَّا طَرَآئِقَ قِدَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:11)

**"And among us are the righteous, and among us are others not so; we were of divided ways."** The jinn, like humans, are accountable: some are Muslims, some disbelievers, and they differ in righteousness.

﴿وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّآ أَن لَّن نُّعۡجِزَ ٱللَّهَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَن نُّعۡجِزَهُۥ هَرَبٗا﴾ (Quran 72:12)

**"And we have become certain that we will never cause failure to Allah upon the earth, nor can we escape Him by flight."**

﴿وَأَنَّا لَمَّا سَمِعۡنَا ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦۖ فَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِرَبِّهِۦ فَلَا يَخَافُ بَخۡسٗا وَلَا رَهَقٗا﴾ (Quran 72:13)

**"And when we heard the guidance, we believed in it. And whoever believes in his Lord will not fear deprivation or burden."**

﴿وَأَنَّا مِنَّا ٱلۡمُسۡلِمُونَ وَمِنَّا ٱلۡقَٰسِطُونَۖ فَمَنۡ أَسۡلَمَ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ تَحَرَّوۡاْ رَشَدٗا﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّا ٱلۡقَٰسِطُونَ فَكَانُواْ لِجَهَنَّمَ حَطَبٗا﴾ (Quran 72:14-15)

**"And among us are Muslims, and among us are the unjust. And whoever has become Muslim, those have sought out the right course. But as for the unjust, they will be, for Hell, firewood."**

## Verses 16–17: Straightness and Abundance
﴿وَأَلَّوِ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ عَلَى ٱلطَّرِيقَةِ لَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰهُم مَّآءً غَدَقٗا﴾ ﴿لِّنَفۡتِنَهُمۡ فِيهِۚ وَمَن يُعۡرِضۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ رَبِّهِۦ يَسۡلُكۡهُ عَذَابٗا صَعَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:16-17)

**"And if they had gone straight on the way, We would have given them abundant water to drink, so We might test them therein."** Uprightness brings provision, and provision itself is a test.

## Verses 18–19: The Mosques Belong to Allah
﴿وَأَنَّ ٱلۡمَسَٰجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدۡعُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:18)

**"And the mosques are for Allah, so do not invoke with Allah anyone."** The places of prostration are for Allah alone; no one is called upon in them alongside Him.

﴿وَأَنَّهُۥ لَمَّا قَامَ عَبۡدُ ٱللَّهِ يَدۡعُوهُ كَادُواْ يَكُونُونَ عَلَيۡهِ لِبَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:19)

**"And when the servant of Allah stood up supplicating Him, they almost became about him a compacted mass."** The Prophet ﷺ is described with the noblest title: "the servant of Allah".

## Verses 20–25: The Messenger Only Conveys
﴿قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَدۡعُواْ رَبِّي وَلَآ أُشۡرِكُ بِهِۦٓ أَحَدٗا﴾ ﴿قُلۡ إِنِّي لَآ أَمۡلِكُ لَكُمۡ ضَرّٗا وَلَا رَشَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:20-21)

**"Say: I only invoke my Lord and do not associate with Him anyone. Say: Indeed, I do not possess for you the power of harm or right direction."**

﴿قُلۡ إِنِّي لَن يُجِيرَنِي مِنَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدࣱ وَلَنۡ أَجِدَ مِن دُونِهِۦ مُلۡتَحَدًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّا بَلَٰغٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِسَٰلَٰتِهِۦۚ وَمَن يَعۡصِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ نَارَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدًا﴾ (Quran 72:22-23)

**"Say: Indeed, there will never protect me from Allah anyone, nor will I find in other than Him a refuge. But I only convey from Allah and His messages."** If even the Messenger ﷺ cannot protect himself except by Allah, how could anyone seek protection from jinn, fortune tellers or amulets?

﴿قُلۡ إِنۡ أَدۡرِيٓ أَقَرِيبࣱ مَّا تُوعَدُونَ أَمۡ يَجۡعَلُ لَهُۥ رَبِّيٓ أَمَدًا﴾ (Quran 72:25)

**"Say: I do not know if what you are promised is near or if my Lord will grant for it a long period."**

## Verses 26–28: The Knower of the Unseen
﴿عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ فَلَا يُظۡهِرُ عَلَىٰ غَيۡبِهِۦٓ أَحَدًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَنِ ٱرۡتَضَىٰ مِن رَّسُولٖ فَإِنَّهُۥ يَسۡلُكُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ رَصَدٗا﴾ (Quran 72:26-27)

**"He is the Knower of the unseen, and He does not disclose His unseen to anyone, except whom He has approved of messengers; and indeed, He sends before each messenger and behind him observers."** No jinn, fortune teller or astrologer knows the unseen. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever goes to a fortune teller and asks him about something, his prayer will not be accepted for forty nights."

﴿لِّيَعۡلَمَ أَن قَدۡ أَبۡلَغُواْ رِسَٰلَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَأَحَاطَ بِمَا لَدَيۡهِمۡ وَأَحۡصَىٰ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ عَدَدَۢا﴾ (Quran 72:28)

**"That he may know that they have conveyed the messages of their Lord; and He has encompassed whatever is with them and has enumerated all things in number."**

## What Muslims Believe About the Jinn
- **They are real**: created from smokeless fire, as Surah Ar-Rahman states.
- **They are accountable**: some believe and some disbelieve, as this surah shows.
- **They do not know the unseen**: as Surah Saba showed in the death of Sulayman.
- **Protection is from Allah**: through dhikr, Ayat al-Kursi, the last two surahs and the du'a of stopping at a place.
- **Avoid exaggeration**: most stories told about the jinn have no authentic source and only spread fear.

## Word Meanings
- **Nafar**: a group (three to ten).
- **'Ajab**: amazing.
- **Ar-Rushd**: the right course.
- **Jadd**: nobleness, majesty.
- **Shatat**: excessive transgression.
- **Rahaq**: burden, increased fear and folly.
- **Haras**: guards.
- **Shuhub**: burning flames.
- **Rasad**: lying in wait.
- **Tara'iq qidada**: divided ways.
- **Al-Qasitun**: the unjust.
- **Ghadaq**: abundant.
- **Libad**: a compacted mass.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The testimony of the jinn**: the Quran's power shown through outsiders.
2. **Evil unattributed, good attributed to the Lord**: verse 10.
3. **"The servant of Allah"**: the Prophet's ﷺ highest title.
4. **Short rhyming verses ending in *-ada***: a calm, steady rhythm.
5. **"Say" repeated**: the Messenger declaring his limits.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *nafarun min al-jinn* (verse 1), the tanween merges into the mim.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *rijalun min al-ins* (verse 6).
- **Idgham without ghunnah**: in *shihaban rasada* (verse 9), the tanween merges into the ra'.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yajid lahu* (verse 9), the dal sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'a* (verse 8) and *ma'an* (verse 16).

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Nuh before it ends with Nuh's prayer against the disbelievers who would not believe, and Al-Jinn opens with jinn who believed after one hearing. Al-Jinn ends with the messengers who convey, and Surah Al-Muzzammil after it prepares the Messenger ﷺ for the heavy word.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Let the Quran move you** as it moved the jinn.
2. **Seek refuge only in Allah**, never in jinn.
3. **Say the du'a of stopping at a place** when you travel.
4. **Never visit fortune tellers** or believe in horoscopes.
5. **Keep mosques for Allah alone.**
6. **Stay upright**, for uprightness brings blessings.
7. **Do not spread unfounded jinn stories.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Jinn
- **Day 1**: verses 1–7.
- **Day 2**: verses 8–15.
- **Day 3**: verses 16–22.
- **Day 4**: verses 23–28, then the whole surah.

## Teaching Children
- **Explain the jinn simply**: creatures made from fire, some good and some bad.
- **Teach them the du'a** for stopping at a place.
- **Reassure them**: Allah protects those who remember Him, so there is no need to fear.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Did the Prophet ﷺ see the jinn in this event?
Ibn 'Abbas said he did not see them on that occasion; he learned of it through revelation, as in Sahih Muslim.

### Are there Muslim jinn?
Yes; verse 14 says: "Among us are Muslims, and among us are the unjust."

### Can jinn know the unseen?
No; the unseen belongs to Allah, and He discloses what He wills only to messengers He approves.

### What does "the mosques are for Allah" mean?
Places of prostration are for worshipping Allah alone, with no one invoked alongside Him.

### How do I protect myself from jinn?
By dhikr, Ayat al-Kursi, the last three surahs and the du'a reported in Sahih Muslim when stopping at a place.

### Is visiting fortune tellers forbidden?
Yes; whoever asks a fortune teller has his prayer unaccepted for forty nights, as in Sahih Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Jinn?
28 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Jinn is a mirror: beings from another world heard the Quran once and said, "We have heard an amazing Quran... and we have believed." The surah frees you from fear of the unseen world by tying your heart to the One who knows the unseen. Seek refuge only in Him, keep His mosques for Him alone, and let the Quran move you as it moved them.
