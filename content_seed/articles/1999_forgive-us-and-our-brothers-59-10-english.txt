﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَلِإِخۡوَٰنِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ فِي قُلُوبِنَا غِلّٗا لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ رَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمٌ﴾ [Al-Hashr 59:10]

## Three groups
| Verse | Group |
|---|---|
| 59:8 | The Emigrants |
| 59:9 | The Helpers |
| 59:10 | Those who came after them |

- The third group includes every believer until the Day of Judgment who carries this quality.

## Their qualities
- They seek forgiveness for those who preceded them in faith, and ask Allah to clean their hearts of **ghill** (rancour).

## Love for the Companions
- Scholars note that the verse shows loving and praying for the Companions is part of complete faith.

## Lessons
- **Pray for those before you**: parents, teachers, the Companions.
- **Check your heart** for resentment toward any believer.
