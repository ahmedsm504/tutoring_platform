﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدۡحٗا فَمُلَٰقِيهِ﴾ [Al-Inshiqaq 84:6]

## *Kadh*
- Effort that wears a person out. Every human is working hard, whether for good or evil.

## Toward your Lord
- All paths end **with Allah**. The question is what you carry when you arrive.

## "You will meet Him"
- *Fa mulaqih*: the meeting is certain.

## Two outcomes
| Record received | Result |
|---|---|
| In the right hand | An easy reckoning and joyful return to family (84:7-9) |
| Behind the back | Calling for destruction (84:10-12) |

## Direct your effort
- Since you will toil anyway, make the toil **for Allah**.

## Lessons
- **Choose where your effort goes.**
- **Prepare for the meeting.**
