## "Do Not Grieve, Indeed Allah Is With Us": Quran 9:40 Explained

﴿إِلَّا تَنصُرُوهُ فَقَدۡ نَصَرَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِذۡ أَخۡرَجَهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ثَانِيَ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ إِذۡ هُمَا فِي ٱلۡغَارِ إِذۡ يَقُولُ لِصَٰحِبِهِۦ لَا تَحۡزَنۡ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَنَاۖ فَأَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ سَكِينَتَهُۥ عَلَيۡهِ وَأَيَّدَهُۥ بِجُنُودٖ لَّمۡ تَرَوۡهَا وَجَعَلَ كَلِمَةَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱلسُّفۡلَىٰۗ وَكَلِمَةُ ٱللَّهِ هِيَ ٱلۡعُلۡيَاۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:40]

### The scene
- The Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr hid in a cave during the Hijrah. The pursuers stood at its entrance. Abu Bakr said: *If one of them looked at his feet, he would see us.* The Prophet ﷺ replied: **"What do you think of two, when Allah is their third?"** (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Key phrases
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Second of two* | Only two people, against a whole city |
| *His companion* | Abu Bakr, honoured by being described in the Quran as the Prophet's companion |
| *Do not grieve* | Fear for the Prophet ﷺ, not for himself |
| *Allah is with us* | Allah's special support and protection |
| *Sakinah* | Tranquility sent into the heart |
| *Unseen soldiers* | Support you cannot see |

### The final word
- *The word of Allah is the highest*: not because of numbers, but because it is the truth.

### Lessons
- **When all doors close, Allah is with the sincere.**
- **Calm comes from Allah**: ask Him for sakinah.

## "Nothing Will Befall Us Except What Allah Has Decreed": Quran 9:51 Explained

﴿قُل لَّن يُصِيبَنَآ إِلَّا مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَنَا هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰنَاۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:51]

### The context
- The hypocrites rejoiced when believers were hurt. Allah taught the believers this reply.

### "For us", not "against us"
- *What Allah has written for us*: scholars note the word **lana** (for us). What Allah decrees for a believer carries good, even when it hurts.

### Three statements
| Statement | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Only what Allah wrote | Nothing happens by chance |
| He is our Protector | We are not alone |
| Upon Allah let believers rely | The right reaction |

### A Prophetic counterpart
- *Know that if the whole nation gathered to benefit you, they could not except with what Allah wrote for you* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### Not an excuse for laziness
- Belief in decree comes **with** taking means, as the Prophet ﷺ did in every battle.

### Lessons
- **Peace comes from accepting decree** after effort.
- **Trust that what is written for you is good.**

## "The Believing Men and Women Are Allies of One Another": Quran 9:71 Explained

﴿وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٖۚ يَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَيُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَيُطِيعُونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ سَيَرۡحَمُهُمُ ٱللَّهُۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:71]

### A contrast
| Hypocrites (9:67) | Believers (9:71) |
|---|---|
| *Some of them are of others* | *Some of them are allies of others* |
| Enjoin evil | Enjoin good |
| Close their hands | Give zakah |

### *Wilayah*
- Love, support and cooperation among believing men and women.

### Five qualities
- Enjoining good, forbidding evil, establishing prayer, giving zakah, obeying Allah and His Messenger.

### Women as partners
- Believing women are named alongside men in loyalty and in enjoining good: partners in reform.

### "Allah will have mercy on them"
- The *sa-* prefix confirms a promised future.

### Lessons
- **Support your brothers and sisters** in good.
- **Measure your community** by these qualities.

## "Say: Work, and Allah Will See Your Work": Quran 9:105 Explained

﴿وَقُلِ ٱعۡمَلُواْ فَسَيَرَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَلَكُمۡ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَۖ وَسَتُرَدُّونَ إِلَىٰ عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:105]

### The context
- It follows verses about those who stayed behind from Tabuk and then admitted their sins. The message: **prove repentance with deeds**.

### Who sees?
| Seer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Allah | Sees outer and inner |
| His Messenger | By what Allah informs him |
| The believers | See the effect in reality |

### The unseen and the seen
- Allah knows intentions (unseen) and actions (seen); both will be accounted.

### Excellence
- *Allah loves that when one of you does a job, he does it well* (narrated by Abu Yaʿla and al-Tabarani).

### Lessons
- **Work as if you are watched**, because you are.
- **Turn repentance into action.**

## "Allah Has Purchased from the Believers Their Lives and Wealth": Quran 9:111 Explained

﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱشۡتَرَىٰ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلَهُم بِأَنَّ لَهُمُ ٱلۡجَنَّةَۚ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَقۡتُلُونَ وَيُقۡتَلُونَۖ وَعۡدًا عَلَيۡهِ حَقّٗا فِي ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلِ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ وَمَنۡ أَوۡفَىٰ بِعَهۡدِهِۦ مِنَ ٱللَّهِۚ فَٱسۡتَبۡشِرُواْ بِبَيۡعِكُمُ ٱلَّذِي بَايَعۡتُم بِهِۦۚ وَذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:111]

### The transaction
| Element | In the verse |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Allah |
| Seller | The believer |
| Goods | Life and wealth |
| Price | Paradise |

### A gift disguised as a trade
- Our lives and wealth already belong to Allah, yet He calls it a **purchase** and pays Paradise. Pure generosity.

### A promise in three scriptures
- *A true promise in the Torah, the Gospel and the Quran*, with the question: *Who is more faithful to his covenant than Allah?*

### Rejoice
- *Rejoice in the deal you made*: the believer should feel happy, not burdened.

### The next verse
- It describes the sellers: those who repent, worship, praise, fast, bow, prostrate, enjoin good, forbid evil and keep Allah's limits.

### Lessons
- **Spend your life** in what pleases Allah.
- **No deal is better.**

## "Fear Allah and Be with the Truthful": Quran 9:119 and the Story of Kab ibn Malik

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَكُونُواْ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:119]

### Right after the three
- The verse comes immediately after the acceptance of repentance of the three who stayed behind from Tabuk (9:118). What saved them was **truthfulness**.

### Kaʿb ibn Malik
- The hypocrites gave false excuses. Kaʿb told the truth: he had no excuse. He was boycotted for fifty nights, then his repentance was revealed. He said: *O Messenger of Allah, Allah saved me only through truthfulness* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### "With the truthful"
| Meaning | Practice |
|---|---|
| Be among them | Truthful in word and deed |
| Accompany them | Choose truthful friends |

### Truth leads to Paradise
- *Truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Truth saves**, even when it hurts at first.
- **Choose friends** who are truthful.

## "There Has Come to You a Messenger from Among Yourselves": Quran 9:128 Explained

﴿لَقَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولࣱ مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ عَزِيزٌ عَلَيۡهِ مَا عَنِتُّمۡ حَرِيصٌ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:128]

### Five descriptions
| Description | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *From among yourselves* | A human you know, of your people and language |
| *Your hardship weighs on him* | Your suffering grieves him |
| *Eager for you* | Keen on your guidance and good |
| *Kind (raʾuf)* | Gentle care that removes harm |
| *Merciful (rahim)* | Mercy that brings good |

### Names shared with Allah
- *Ar-Raʾuf* and *ar-Rahim* are names of Allah. Describing the Prophet ﷺ with these qualities shows the honour of his character, while Allah's attributes remain perfect and incomparable.

### Seen in his life
- He shortened prayers when he heard a baby crying, to spare the mother.
- He saved his special duʿa for intercession for his nation on the Day of Judgment (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### The final verse
- The surah then ends: *If they turn away, say: Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but Him; upon Him I rely.*

### Lessons
- **Love the Prophet ﷺ** who loved you before you were born.
- **Follow his Sunnah**: that is the proof of love.

## Surah At-Tawbah Meaning: Sincerity Tested and the Door of Repentance

**Surah At-Tawbah** (Repentance, 129 ayat) is the only surah that does not begin with *Bismillah*.

### No Bismillah
- Scholars mention that it begins with a declaration of disassociation from the treaty-breakers, and the Companions wrote it without the Basmalah as it was transmitted.

### The companion in the cave
- The surah recalls the Hijrah: when the Prophet ﷺ said to his companion Abu Bakr, *Do not grieve; indeed Allah is with us.*

### Tabuk and the test of sincerity
- A long, hot journey in a time of hardship revealed who was sincere.
- The hypocrites made excuses, mocked believers and spread doubt; the surah exposes their methods in detail.

### The three who stayed behind
- Three sincere believers stayed behind without excuse and told the truth. They were boycotted for fifty nights until the earth felt narrow for them, then Allah accepted their repentance.
- *O you who believe, fear Allah and be with the truthful.*

### A caring Messenger
﴿لَقَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولࣱ مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ عَزِيزٌ عَلَيۡهِ مَا عَنِتُّمۡ حَرِيصٌ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾

### Lessons
- **Truthfulness saves**, even when it is hard.
- **The door of repentance is open** to those who return sincerely.

## "In the Bounty and Mercy of Allah, Let Them Rejoice": Quran 10:57-58 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءࣱ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ بِفَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَبِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَفۡرَحُواْ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ﴾ [Yunus 10:57-58]

### Four descriptions
| Description | For |
|---|---|
| Admonition | Hearts that need reminding |
| Healing for what is in the chests | Doubts, envy, sorrow |
| Guidance | The path |
| Mercy | Believers who act on it |

### Rejoice!
- Islam does not forbid joy; it **directs** it. The best joy is in Allah's bounty and mercy.
- Many scholars explained *bounty* as Islam and *mercy* as the Quran, or the reverse.

### Better than what they amass
- Money, property and status cannot compare with guidance.

### Lessons
- **Celebrate** finishing a surah or a khatmah.
- **Value guidance** above wealth.

## Surah Yunus Meaning: A Healing for Hearts and the Friends of Allah

**Surah Yunus** (109 ayat) is named after the prophet whose people believed before punishment struck.

### Signs of order
- The sun made a source of brightness and the moon a light, with phases to know the count of years and time.

### Praying only in hardship
- When harm touches a person, he calls on Allah lying down, sitting or standing. When the harm is removed, he goes on **as if he never called**.

### The Quran as healing
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءࣱ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾

### The friends of Allah
﴿أَلَآ إِنَّ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾
- The next verse defines them: *those who believed and were mindful of Allah*.

### Faith too late
- As he drowned, Pharaoh declared belief. He was told: *Now? When you disobeyed before?* His body was preserved as a sign.

### The people of Yunus
- They are the one people who believed when they saw the punishment approaching, and Allah lifted it from them.

### Lessons
- **Remember Allah in ease**, not only in hardship.
- **Repentance must come before the end.**

## "The Friends of Allah Have No Fear Nor Grief": Quran 10:62-64 Explained

﴿أَلَآ إِنَّ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَكَانُواْ يَتَّقُونَ﴾ ﴿لَهُمُ ٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ لَا تَبۡدِيلَ لِكَلِمَٰتِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ [Yunus 10:62-64]

### Who are the awliya?
- The Quran defines them itself: **those who believe and are mindful of Allah**. Not a special class with strange claims, but every sincere believer according to his level of taqwa.

### No fear, no grief
| | Meaning |
|---|---|
| No fear | For what lies ahead |
| No grief | For what they left behind |

### Good news in this life
- Scholars mention: a good dream, people's sincere praise, and the angels' good news at death.
- The Prophet ﷺ described a good dream that a believer sees or is shown as part of these glad tidings (narrated by al-Tirmidhi and Ahmad).

### A Hadith Qudsi
- Allah says: *Whoever shows enmity to a friend of Mine, I have declared war on him. My servant draws near to Me with nothing more beloved than the obligations, and continues to draw near with voluntary deeds until I love him* (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### Lessons
- **Wilayah is open to all**: faith and taqwa.
- **Start with obligations**, then add voluntary deeds.

## "If Allah Touches You with Harm, None Can Remove It But Him": Quran 10:107 Explained

﴿وَإِن يَمۡسَسۡكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرّٖ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَۖ وَإِن يُرِدۡكَ بِخَيۡرٖ فَلَا رَآدَّ لِفَضۡلِهِۦۚ يُصِيبُ بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ [Yunus 10:107]

### Two truths
| State | Truth |
|---|---|
| Harm | Only Allah removes it |
| Good | No one can repel it |

### Different verbs
- For harm: *touches you*. For good: *intends for you*. Some scholars note good is intended by Allah for itself, while harm occurs for a wisdom.

### "His favour"
- Good is called **favour**: a gift, not something owed.

### A matching hadith
- *If the whole nation gathered to benefit you, they could not except with what Allah wrote for you* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### Ending with mercy
- *He is the Forgiving, the Merciful*: even harm comes with mercy for the patient.

### Lessons
- **Attach your heart** to Allah, not to means.
- **Ask Allah alone** to remove harm.
