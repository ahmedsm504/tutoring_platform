﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَن يَرۡتَدَّ مِنكُمۡ عَن دِينِهِۦ فَسَوۡفَ يَأۡتِي ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ يُحِبُّهُمۡ وَيُحِبُّونَهُۥٓ أَذِلَّةٍ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَعِزَّةٍ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ يُجَٰهِدُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَا يَخَافُونَ لَوۡمَةَ لَآئِمٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:54]

## The religion needs no one
- Whoever turns away does not harm Allah; He brings a better people.

## Love first from Allah
- *He loves them* comes **before** *they love Him*: His love for them is what enabled their love for Him.

## Four qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Humble toward believers | Gentle, merciful, modest |
| Firm against disbelievers | Dignified, not yielding to falsehood |
| Striving in Allah's way | Effort to support truth |
| Not fearing blame | Saying the truth regardless of criticism |

## Allah's favour
- *That is the favour of Allah, He gives it to whom He wills*: ask Him for it.

## Lessons
- **Be humble with your brothers.**
- **Hold to truth** even if people blame you.
