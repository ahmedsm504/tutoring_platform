Online Quran classes are a blessing, especially for families living in non-Arabic-speaking countries. But with so many academies and freelance tutors, choosing can be hard. These questions will help you decide, whether you choose us or someone else.

## 1. Is the teacher certified or truly proficient?
An Ijazah (a certificate with a chain of transmission) isn't required to teach a beginner, but it's a strong sign of mastery. If there's no Ijazah, ask about the teacher's experience and who they studied with. Read [what an Ijazah is](/blog/what-is-ijazah-quran-english/).

## 2. Does the teacher have experience with children specifically?
Teaching a five-year-old is completely different from teaching an adult. Good children's teachers know how to switch activities when a child gets bored, use stories and games, and when to stop.

## 3. Is there a free trial class?
The trial is the most important step. Watch for:
- Did the teacher start by assessing your child's level?
- Were they patient and kind?
- Did your child enjoy it and ask for another class?

## 4. One-to-one or group?
One-to-one classes give your child the teacher's full attention. Group classes can suit very social children, but each child recites for much less time.

## 5. What happens between classes?
Memorization doesn't stick from the class alone. Is there homework? Can your child record their recitation and send it to the teacher? Does the teacher reply with corrections and encouragement?

## 6. Will you get regular progress reports?
You have the right to know where your child stands and what needs work. A written monthly report is a sign of a serious, organised academy.

## 7. How is your child protected?
This question is rarely asked, and it's one of the most important:
- Does all communication between teacher and child happen **inside the platform**, or through private phone numbers and social media?
- Does the administration supervise conversations?
- Is there a clear policy against private contact?

At Al-Agmi, all teacher–student communication happens inside our platform. Nobody's phone number is shown to the other party, messages containing numbers or social handles are blocked automatically, and the administration oversees everything.

## 8. How flexible is the schedule?
If you live in a different time zone, make sure class times suit you, and that rescheduling or making up a missed class is possible.

## 9. What's the curriculum?
Where does the child start? How much will they memorize weekly? Do they learn reading and Tajweed alongside memorization? Is there an educational side, such as simple stories of the Prophets?

## 10. What do other parents say?
Ask for real experiences, and talk to parents you know if possible. Real feedback beats any advert.

## Quick checklist
- Proficient teacher with experience teaching children
- Free trial class
- One-to-one sessions
- Homework and follow-up between classes
- Written monthly reports
- Safe, supervised communication
- Flexible times in your time zone
- A clear plan
