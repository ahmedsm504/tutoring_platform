﴿أَمَّنۡ هُوَ قَٰنِتٌ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيۡلِ سَاجِدٗا وَقَآئِمٗا يَحۡذَرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَيَرۡجُواْ رَحۡمَةَ رَبِّهِۦۗ قُلۡ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡلَمُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَۗ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ [Az-Zumar 39:9]

## The devout one
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Qanit* | Devoutly obedient |
| Hours of the night | In night prayer |
| Prostrating and standing | In prayer |
| Fears the Hereafter | Fear |
| Hopes for his Lord's mercy | Hope |

## True knowledge
- The question about knowledge comes **after** describing the worshipper: true knowledge leads to worship and awe.

## Fear and hope
- The two wings of the believer, joined in one sentence.

## People of understanding
- *Only people of understanding take heed.*

## Lessons
- **Let knowledge lead to worship.**
- **Balance fear and hope.**
