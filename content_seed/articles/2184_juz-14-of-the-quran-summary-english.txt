Juz 14 begins with **Surah Al-Hijr**: ﴿الٓرۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَقُرۡءَانٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾, "Alif Lam Ra. These are the verses of the Book and a clear Quran." Many memorizers call it **Juz Rubama**, after the second verse: ﴿رُّبَمَا يَوَدُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡ كَانُواْ مُسۡلِمِينَ﴾, "Perhaps those who disbelieve will wish they had been Muslims." It contains two complete surahs: **Al-Hijr** (99 verses) and **An-Nahl** (128 verses). In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 262 to 281**.

It is one of the few juz that begin with the start of a surah and end with the end of one, which makes it easy to memorize and understand as a complete unit. Al-Hijr strengthens the Prophet ﷺ against mockery, and An-Nahl lists so many of Allah's blessings that some scholars called it **the surah of blessings**.

## The big idea

Both surahs share one aim: **steadying the heart with truth and introducing it to the Giver of blessings.** Al-Hijr tells the Prophet ﷺ: do not grieve at their denial; the Reminder is preserved, the enemy is known, and the denying nations perished. An-Nahl tells all people: look at Allah's blessings around you, in cattle, rain, the sea, bees and milk, then be grateful and worship Him sincerely.

The message: **whoever knows Allah's blessings knows His right, and whoever knows His right stands firm on His path.**

## Part one: Surah Al-Hijr (1-99)

Al-Hijr is Makki, named after the people of al-Hijr, Thamud, the people of Salih, whose dwellings are at al-Hijr between Madinah and Syria. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Hijr Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-hijr-meaning-english/).

### 1. The disbelievers' wish and the preservation of the Reminder (1-15)
﴿رُّبَمَا يَوَدُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡ كَانُواْ مُسۡلِمِينَ﴾: a day will come when they wish they had been Muslims. Then their mockery: "O you to whom the Reminder has been sent down, you are insane; why do you not bring us the angels?" Then the eternal promise: ﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ﴾, "Indeed, We sent down the Reminder, and indeed We will be its guardian."

This verse is among the strongest proofs of the Quran's truth: fourteen centuries have passed and not a letter of it has changed, unlike earlier scriptures whose people were entrusted with them and altered them. Read [We Sent Down the Reminder: 15:9](/blog/inna-nahnu-nazzalna-adh-dhikr-meaning-english/).

Then: even if We opened a gate in the sky and they kept ascending through it, they would say "our eyes have been dazzled." Stubbornness is not convinced by proof.

### 2. Signs in creation (16-25)
﴿وَلَقَدۡ جَعَلۡنَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ بُرُوجٗا وَزَيَّنَّٰهَا لِلنَّـٰظِرِينَ﴾, "We placed constellations in the sky and beautified it for observers", and guarded it from every accursed devil. Then the spread earth, firm mountains and balanced plants. Then: ﴿وَإِن مِّن شَيۡءٍ إِلَّا عِندَنَا خَزَآئِنُهُۥ وَمَا نُنَزِّلُهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِقَدَرٖ مَّعۡلُومࣲ﴾, "There is nothing whose treasuries are not with Us, and We send it down only in a known measure", and ﴿وَأَرۡسَلۡنَا ٱلرِّيَٰحَ لَوَٰقِحَ فَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰكُمُوهُ وَمَآ أَنتُمۡ لَهُۥ بِخَٰزِنِينَ﴾, "We send the fertilising winds and send down water from the sky and give it to you to drink, and you are not its keepers."

### 3. Adam's creation and Iblis's enmity (26-44)
A third telling of Adam's story after Al-Baqarah and Al-A'raf, focusing here on **the material of creation** and **the limits of Shaytan's power**:
- ﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي خَٰلِقُۢ بَشَرٗا مِّن صَلۡصَٰلٖ مِّنۡ حَمَإٖ مَّسۡنُونٖ﴾, "When your Lord said to the angels: I will create a human being from dry clay of altered black mud", while the jinn were created before from scorching fire.
- Iblis's refusal, his request for respite and his vow to mislead.
- The decisive rule: ﴿إِنَّ عِبَادِي لَيۡسَ لَكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٌ إِلَّا مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَاوِينَ﴾, "Over My servants you have no authority, except those who follow you among the deviators."
- Hell has seven gates, each with its assigned portion.

**Lesson**: Shaytan has no authority over a sincere servant; he gains power only over those who follow him.

### 4. The God-conscious, and the balance of hope and fear (45-50)
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ﴾, "The God-conscious will be among gardens and springs", then ﴿وَنَزَعۡنَا مَا فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنۡ غِلٍّ إِخۡوَٰنًا عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ﴾, "We will remove any resentment from their hearts; they will be brothers on thrones facing each other." See [Rancour Removed in Paradise: 15:47](/blog/rancour-removed-in-paradise-15-47-english/). Then a new quarter opens with two balanced verses: ﴿۞نَبِّئۡ عِبَادِيٓ أَنِّيٓ أَنَا ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّ عَذَابِي هُوَ ٱلۡعَذَابُ ٱلۡأَلِيمُ﴾, "Inform My servants that I am the Forgiving, the Merciful, and that My punishment is the painful punishment."

**Reflect**: forgiveness and mercy come first, emphasised with "I", while the punishment is mentioned without Allah calling Himself "the Punisher." The believer walks between hope and fear, and hope is the wider of the two.

### 5. Ibrahim's guests and Lut (51-77)
The story comes in brief here, illustrating the two previous verses: good news for Ibrahim is mercy; punishment for the people of Lut is pain. Ibrahim said: ﴿وَمَن يَقۡنَطُ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ رَبِّهِۦٓ إِلَّا ٱلضَّآلُّونَ﴾, "Who despairs of his Lord's mercy except those astray?" Then the people of Lut rejoicing at the guests, the blast at sunrise, and the town turned upside down. Then: ﴿إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتࣲ لِّلۡمُتَوَسِّمِينَ﴾, "In that are signs for those who discern", those who read the signs.

### 6. The people of the Wood and the people of al-Hijr (78-86)
- **The people of the Wood (al-Aykah)**: according to many commentators the people of Shu'ayb; Allah took retribution from them.
- **The people of al-Hijr**: they denied the messengers, ﴿وَكَانُواْ يَنۡحِتُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِبَالِ بُيُوتًا ءَامِنِينَ﴾, "and they used to carve homes from the mountains, feeling secure", until the blast seized them at morning, ﴿فَمَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾, "and what they used to earn did not avail them."

In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, when the Prophet ﷺ passed al-Hijr on the way to Tabuk, he told his Companions not to enter upon those who were punished except weeping.

### 7. The Seven Oft-Repeated and commands for steadfastness (87-99)
﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَٰكَ سَبۡعٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمَثَانِي وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡعَظِيمَ﴾, "We have given you seven of the oft-repeated verses and the great Quran." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ said of Al-Fatiha: "It is the Seven Oft-Repeated and the Great Quran that I have been given."

Then the instructions:
- ﴿لَا تَمُدَّنَّ عَيۡنَيۡكَ إِلَىٰ مَا مَتَّعۡنَا بِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ﴾, "Do not extend your eyes towards what We have given some of them to enjoy": one who has been given the Quran does not gaze longingly at worldly goods.
- ﴿وَٱخۡفِضۡ جَنَاحَكَ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "and lower your wing to the believers."
- ﴿فَٱصۡدَعۡ بِمَا تُؤۡمَرُ وَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾, "So proclaim openly what you are commanded and turn away from the idolaters", with which open preaching began.
- The cure for a tight chest: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ نَعۡلَمُ أَنَّكَ يَضِيقُ صَدۡرُكَ بِمَا يَقُولُونَ﴾, "We know your chest is tightened by what they say", then the remedy: ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَكُن مِّنَ ٱلسَّـٰجِدِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱعۡبُدۡ رَبَّكَ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَكَ ٱلۡيَقِينُ﴾, "So glorify your Lord with praise and be among those who prostrate, and worship your Lord until the certainty comes to you", the certainty being death.

**Lesson**: when people's words weigh on your chest, the cure is glorification, prostration and steady worship until death.

## Part two: Surah An-Nahl (1-128)

An-Nahl is Makki, with 128 verses, named "The Bee" after ﴿وَأَوۡحَىٰ رَبُّكَ إِلَى ٱلنَّحۡلِ﴾, "And your Lord inspired the bee." It is also called **the surah of blessings** because of how many of Allah's favours it lists. The comment "In that is a sign" recurs, and so does the call to gratitude. For the whole surah see [Surah An-Nahl Meaning in English](/blog/surah-an-nahl-meaning-english/).

### 1. "The command of Allah has come", and the first blessings (1-9)
﴿أَتَىٰٓ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ فَلَا تَسۡتَعۡجِلُوهُۚ﴾, "The command of Allah has come, so do not seek to hasten it", in the past tense because it is certain. Then angels descend with revelation by His command. Then the human being created from a drop, and suddenly a clear opponent. Then:
- ﴿وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمَ خَلَقَهَاۖ لَكُمۡ فِيهَا دِفۡءࣱ وَمَنَٰفِعُ وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ﴾, "And the cattle He created; in them is warmth and benefits for you, and from them you eat", with beauty when you bring them home and when you send them out, and they carry your loads.
- ﴿وَٱلۡخَيۡلَ وَٱلۡبِغَالَ وَٱلۡحَمِيرَ لِتَرۡكَبُوهَا وَزِينَةٗۚ وَيَخۡلُقُ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾, "And horses, mules and donkeys for you to ride and as adornment; and He creates what you do not know." "He creates what you do not know" opens the door to every means of transport unknown at the time of revelation.

### 2. Rain, the universe and the sea (10-18)
- Rain gives drink and trees for grazing, and grows crops, olives, palms and grapes: ﴿إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾, "a sign for people who reflect."
- Night and day, sun, moon and stars: "signs for people who use reason."
- What He spread on earth in different colours: "a sign for people who remember."
- The sea: tender meat, ornaments you wear, and ships ploughing through it.
- Mountains, rivers, paths, landmarks and the stars.

Then the decisive question: ﴿أَفَمَن يَخۡلُقُ كَمَن لَّا يَخۡلُقُۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾, "Is He who creates like one who does not create? Will you not remember?", then ﴿وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾, "If you counted Allah's blessings, you could not number them; Allah is Forgiving, Merciful."

**Notice the progression of verse endings**: reflect, then reason, then remember. Reflection begins, reason understands, remembrance is the fruit.

### 3. The arrogant and the God-conscious (19-34)
The false gods create nothing and are themselves created, dead and not alive. Then the arrogant who, when asked "What has your Lord sent down?", say "legends of the ancients", and in contrast: ﴿وَقِيلَ لِلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ مَاذَآ أَنزَلَ رَبُّكُمۡۚ قَالُواْ خَيۡرࣰاۗ﴾, "And it was said to the God-conscious: what has your Lord sent down? They said: good." Then the angels take the souls of the wrongdoers, while to the good they say: peace be upon you, enter Paradise.

### 4. A messenger to every nation (35-50)
﴿وَلَقَدۡ بَعَثۡنَا فِي كُلِّ أُمَّةٖ رَّسُولًا أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱجۡتَنِبُواْ ٱلطَّـٰغُوتَۖ﴾, "We sent to every nation a messenger: worship Allah and avoid false gods." This is the summary of every prophet's message. Then: ﴿إِنَّمَا قَوۡلُنَا لِشَيۡءٍ إِذَآ أَرَدۡنَٰهُ أَن نَّقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾, "Our word to a thing when We intend it is only that We say to it: Be, and it is." Then a promise to those who migrated for Allah after being wronged. Then: ﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ فَسۡـَٔلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلذِّكۡرِ إِن كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾, "We sent before you only men to whom We revealed; so ask the people of knowledge if you do not know", a foundation for referring to scholars. Then the prostration of shadows, creatures and angels, a place of prostration: ﴿يَخَافُونَ رَبَّهُم مِّن فَوۡقِهِمۡ وَيَفۡعَلُونَ مَا يُؤۡمَرُونَ﴾, "They fear their Lord above them and do what they are commanded."

### 5. "Do not take two gods", and the blessing of daughters (51-65)
Hizb 28 begins: ﴿۞وَقَالَ ٱللَّهُ لَا تَتَّخِذُوٓاْ إِلَٰهَيۡنِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِۖ إِنَّمَا هُوَ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱ﴾, "Allah has said: do not take two gods; He is only one God." Then: ﴿وَمَا بِكُم مِّن نِّعۡمَةٖ فَمِنَ ٱللَّهِۖ ثُمَّ إِذَا مَسَّكُمُ ٱلضُّرُّ فَإِلَيۡهِ تَجۡـَٔرُونَ﴾, "Whatever blessing you have is from Allah, and when harm touches you, to Him you cry out."

The Quran then condemns a pre-Islamic custom: ﴿وَإِذَا بُشِّرَ أَحَدُهُم بِٱلۡأُنثَىٰ ظَلَّ وَجۡهُهُۥ مُسۡوَدّٗا وَهُوَ كَظِيمࣱ﴾, "When one of them is given news of a daughter, his face darkens and he suppresses grief", hiding from people, wondering whether to keep her in humiliation or bury her in the earth: "How evil is their judgement!" Islam raised the daughter from being buried alive to being good news.

Then: ﴿وَلَوۡ يُؤَاخِذُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلنَّاسَ بِظُلۡمِهِم مَّا تَرَكَ عَلَيۡهَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ﴾, "If Allah were to seize people for their wrongdoing, He would not leave on earth a single creature": His forbearance gives respite.

### 6. Milk, fruit and the bee (66-70)
Three miracles of daily life:
- **Milk**: ﴿نُّسۡقِيكُم مِّمَّا فِي بُطُونِهِۦ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ فَرۡثٖ وَدَمٖ لَّبَنًا خَالِصٗا سَآئِغٗا لِّلشَّـٰرِبِينَ﴾, "We give you to drink from what is in their bellies, between excretion and blood, pure milk, pleasant to drinkers." Purity comes out from between blood and waste.
- **The fruits of palms and vines**: from which you take intoxicant and good provision. This was before wine was forbidden, and the wording hints that "good provision" is something other than intoxicants.
- **The bee**: ﴿وَأَوۡحَىٰ رَبُّكَ إِلَى ٱلنَّحۡلِ أَنِ ٱتَّخِذِي مِنَ ٱلۡجِبَالِ بُيُوتٗا وَمِنَ ٱلشَّجَرِ وَمِمَّا يَعۡرِشُونَ﴾, "Your Lord inspired the bee: take homes in the mountains, the trees and what people build", then ﴿ثُمَّ كُلِي مِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ فَٱسۡلُكِي سُبُلَ رَبِّكِ ذُلُلٗاۚ يَخۡرُجُ مِنۢ بُطُونِهَا شَرَابࣱ مُّخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥ فِيهِ شِفَآءࣱ لِّلنَّاسِۚ﴾, "then eat from all the fruits and follow the ways of your Lord made easy. From their bellies comes a drink of varying colours, in which there is healing for people."

Notice that the verbs addressed to the bee are feminine (take, eat, follow), in keeping with what people later learned: the worker bees are female.

Then: Allah creates you and then takes your souls, and some of you are returned to the most decrepit age, so that after knowledge they know nothing.

### 7. Blessings of family, home and the senses (71-83)
- Some are favoured over others in provision.
- ﴿وَٱللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِكُم بَنِينَ وَحَفَدَةࣰ﴾, "Allah made for you spouses from yourselves and gave you from your spouses children and grandchildren."
- Two parables: an owned slave able to do nothing, and two men, one of them mute.
- ﴿وَٱللَّهُ أَخۡرَجَكُم مِّنۢ بُطُونِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ شَيۡـٔٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ﴾, "Allah brought you out of your mothers' wombs knowing nothing, and gave you hearing, sight and hearts so that you might be grateful."
- Birds held in the sky.
- Homes for rest, tents from animal hides, shade and shelters, garments that protect from heat and from harm in battle.
- Then the verdict: ﴿يَعۡرِفُونَ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ يُنكِرُونَهَا وَأَكۡثَرُهُمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾, "They recognise Allah's blessing and then deny it, and most of them are ungrateful."

### 8. The verse of justice and excellence (84-97)
﴿وَنَزَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ تِبۡيَٰنٗا لِّكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ﴾, "We sent down to you the Book as clarification of all things." Then the verse Ibn Mas'ud called the most comprehensive verse in the Quran for good and evil: ﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِ وَٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ وَإِيتَآيِٕ ذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَيَنۡهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱلۡبَغۡيِۚ يَعِظُكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾, "Allah commands justice, excellence and giving to relatives, and forbids indecency, wrongdoing and oppression. He admonishes you so you may take heed." Three commands and three prohibitions; many preachers end the Friday sermon with it. Read [Allah Commands Justice and Excellence: 16:90](/blog/inna-allaha-yamuru-bil-adl-meaning-english/).

Then keeping covenants: ﴿وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّتِي نَقَضَتۡ غَزۡلَهَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ قُوَّةٍ أَنكَٰثٗا﴾, "Do not be like the woman who unravelled her yarn after it was firmly spun": a picture of one who makes a pledge and then breaks it.

Then: ﴿مَا عِندَكُمۡ يَنفَدُ وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ بَاقٖۗ﴾, "What you have will run out, and what Allah has will remain", then the promise of a good life: ﴿مَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنࣱ فَلَنُحۡيِيَنَّهُۥ حَيَوٰةٗ طَيِّبَةٗۖ﴾, "Whoever does righteous deeds, male or female, while a believer, We will surely give them a good life." The good life begins in this world: contentment, tranquillity and satisfaction. Read [A Good Life: 16:97](/blog/a-good-life-16-97-meaning-english/).

### 9. Seeking refuge, compulsion and the secure town (98-119)
- ﴿فَإِذَا قَرَأۡتَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ فَٱسۡتَعِذۡ بِٱللَّهِ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ ٱلرَّجِيمِ﴾, "When you recite the Quran, seek refuge in Allah from Shaytan, the accursed": the basis of saying *a'udhu billah* before recitation.
- The reply to those who said "a human teaches him": ﴿لِّسَانُ ٱلَّذِي يُلۡحِدُونَ إِلَيۡهِ أَعۡجَمِيࣱّ وَهَٰذَا لِسَانٌ عَرَبِيࣱّ مُّبِينٌ﴾, "The tongue of the one they refer to is foreign, while this is a clear Arabic tongue."
- **The concession under compulsion**: ﴿إِلَّا مَنۡ أُكۡرِهَ وَقَلۡبُهُۥ مُطۡمَئِنُّۢ بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ﴾, "except one who is forced while his heart is at rest in faith"; commentators link it to 'Ammar ibn Yasir.
- **The parable of the secure town**: ﴿وَضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا قَرۡيَةٗ كَانَتۡ ءَامِنَةٗ مُّطۡمَئِنَّةٗ يَأۡتِيهَا رِزۡقُهَا رَغَدٗا مِّن كُلِّ مَكَانٖ فَكَفَرَتۡ بِأَنۡعُمِ ٱللَّهِ فَأَذَٰقَهَا ٱللَّهُ لِبَاسَ ٱلۡجُوعِ وَٱلۡخَوۡفِ﴾, "Allah presents the example of a town that was secure and at peace, its provision coming abundantly from everywhere, but it denied Allah's blessings, so Allah made it taste the garment of hunger and fear." Security and provision depart with ingratitude.
- Eat what is lawful and good, and do not declare things lawful and unlawful by lying.

### 10. Ibrahim, a nation in himself, and inviting with wisdom (120-128)
﴿إِنَّ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ كَانَ أُمَّةٗ قَانِتٗا لِّلَّهِ حَنِيفٗا وَلَمۡ يَكُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾, "Ibrahim was a nation in himself, devoutly obedient to Allah, upright, and not one of the polytheists", ﴿شَاكِرٗا لِّأَنۡعُمِهِۚ﴾, "grateful for His blessings." The surah that listed blessings ends with the model of a grateful man. Then the method of da'wah:

﴿ٱدۡعُ إِلَىٰ سَبِيلِ رَبِّكَ بِٱلۡحِكۡمَةِ وَٱلۡمَوۡعِظَةِ ٱلۡحَسَنَةِۖ وَجَٰدِلۡهُم بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ﴾, "Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction, and argue with them in the best way":
1. **Wisdom**: the right word for the right person at the right time.
2. **Good instruction**: encouragement and warning, given gently.
3. **Arguing in the best way**: with the stubborn, in the finest manner, not any manner.

Then how to respond to harm: ﴿وَإِنۡ عَاقَبۡتُمۡ فَعَاقِبُواْ بِمِثۡلِ مَا عُوقِبۡتُم بِهِۦۖ وَلَئِن صَبَرۡتُمۡ لَهُوَ خَيۡرࣱ لِّلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾, "If you retaliate, retaliate only to the extent you were harmed; but if you are patient, that is better for the patient." Then: "And be patient, and your patience is only through Allah", and the close of the juz: ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَواْ وَّٱلَّذِينَ هُم مُّحۡسِنُونَ﴾, "Allah is with those who are God-conscious and those who do good."

## Tajweed points in Juz 14

### ﴿رُّبَمَا﴾
The ra is doubled with a dammah and pronounced heavy; it opens the second verse.

### ﴿فَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰكُمُوهُ﴾
One of the longest words in the Quran; read it slowly, giving each letter its proper point of articulation.

### ﴿وَإِيتَآيِٕ ذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ﴾
Written with a ya after the alif, with a connected madd; pronounced *wa ita'i*.

### The sajdah of An-Nahl
At ﴿وَيَفۡعَلُونَ مَا يُؤۡمَرُونَ﴾ in verse 50 is the prostration sign ۩.

### Seeking refuge before recitation
Verse 98 commands seeking refuge when reciting; most scholars consider it recommended before recitation. For the full system see [A Roadmap for Learning Tajweed](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## How to memorize Juz 14 with understanding

1. **The juz is two complete surahs**: memorize each as its own unit.
2. **Al-Hijr has short verses**: it suits quick memorization in one or two weeks.
3. **For An-Nahl, write a list of its blessings** in order: cattle, horses, rain, night and day, the sea, mountains, milk, fruit, the bee, spouses, the senses, birds, homes.
4. **Learn the verse of justice and excellence** by counting: three commands and three prohibitions.
5. **The close of An-Nahl (125-128)** is a complete method of da'wah; memorize it and apply it.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 14

- **Trust Allah's preservation of His Book**, and ignore the doubters.
- **Be among Allah's sincere servants**, over whom Shaytan has no authority.
- **Hold hope and fear together**, with hope the wider.
- **Do not gaze at others' possessions** when you have been given the Quran.
- **When your chest feels tight, glorify Allah and prostrate.**
- **Count Allah's blessings** in your day and be grateful.
- **Ask people of knowledge** about what you do not know.
- **Honour daughters**; they are good news, not shame.
- **Be just, excel and maintain kinship.**
- **Never break a pledge** once you have confirmed it.
- **Do good to live a good life.**
- **Seek refuge in Allah before reciting.**
- **Invite with wisdom** and argue in the best way.
- **If you retaliate, do so only in equal measure**; patience is better.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 14 called "Rubama"?
Many memorizers name it after the second verse of Al-Hijr, ﴿رُّبَمَا يَوَدُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ﴾, since the first verse is the separate letters.

### What are the Seven Oft-Repeated?
They are Surah Al-Fatiha, as the Prophet ﷺ said in Sahih Al-Bukhari.

### Why is An-Nahl called the surah of blessings?
Because of how many of Allah's blessings it lists: cattle, rain, the sea, bees, milk, homes, the senses and more.

### Where does Juz 14 end?
At the end of Surah An-Nahl. Juz 15 begins with Surah Al-Isra': ﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَسۡرَىٰ بِعَبۡدِهِۦ لَيۡلٗا﴾, "Glory be to the One who took His servant by night."

### How many prostrations are in this juz?
One, in Surah An-Nahl, verse 50.

## Conclusion

Juz 14 steadies the heart and introduces it to the Giver of blessings. In Al-Hijr there is the promise to preserve the Reminder, a clear statement of the limits of Shaytan's power, and a command to proclaim the truth while glorifying Allah and prostrating. In An-Nahl there is a journey through Allah's blessings from cattle to honey, then the charter of justice and excellence, the promise of a good life, and the method of inviting with wisdom.
