## "He Created Death and Life to Test Who Is Best in Deeds": Quran 67:2 Explained

﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ أَيُّكُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلٗاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡغَفُورُ﴾ [Al-Mulk 67:2]

### Death first
- Death is mentioned **before** life. Scholars note that keeping death in mind improves how one lives.

### The purpose: a test
- Life is an exam; death ends the exam time.

### "Best", not "most"
- *Ahsanu ʿamala*: the **best** in deeds, not the most. Al-Fudayl ibn ʿIyad is reported to have explained: the most sincere and the most correct.
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sincere | For Allah alone |
| Correct | According to the Sunnah |

### Two names
| Name | Message |
|---|---|
| *Al-ʿAziz* | Mighty: a reason to fear |
| *Al-Ghafur* | Forgiving: a reason to hope |

### Lessons
- **Focus on quality** in worship.
- **Remember death** to live well.

## Surah Al-Mulk Meaning: The Surah That Intercedes

**Surah Al-Mulk** (The Dominion) has 30 ayat, and many Muslims recite it every night. The Prophet ﷺ said: «A surah of the Quran of thirty ayat interceded for a man until he was forgiven: *Tabārak alladhī biyadihil-mulk*» [Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi].

### Themes
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Allah's dominion; death and life as a test |
| 3–5 | Seven heavens with no flaw; the stars as lamps |
| 6–11 | The Fire and the regret of its people |
| 12–14 | Those who fear their Lord unseen; He knows all |
| 15 | The earth made easy for you |
| 16–22 | Warnings and the birds in the sky |
| 23–30 | Gifts of hearing, sight and hearts; the question of water |

### Key ayat
﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ أَيُّكُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلٗاۚ﴾ [Al-Mulk: 2]
- Not "most deeds" but **best deeds**: sincere and correct.

﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَى ٱلطَّيۡرِ فَوۡقَهُمۡ صَـٰٓفَّـٰتٖ وَيَقۡبِضۡنَۚ مَا يُمۡسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُۚ﴾ [Al-Mulk: 19]

﴿قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَصۡبَحَ مَآؤُكُمۡ غَوۡرٗا فَمَن يَأۡتِيكُم بِمَآءٖ مَّعِينِۭ﴾ [Al-Mulk: 30]

### The regret of the people of the Fire
- They will say: had we listened or reasoned, we would not be among the companions of the Blaze [Al-Mulk: 10].
- **Lesson:** use your hearing and reason now.

### Practical tips
- **Recite it before sleep** as many Muslims do.
- **Memorize** it in two to four weeks.

## How to Memorize Surah Al-Mulk: A 2-Week Plan for Adults and 4 Weeks for Kids

After Juz Amma, [Surah Al-Mulk](/quran/67/) is usually the first surah students memorize from Juz Tabarak. Its verses are similar in length and its themes are easy for children to picture.

### Quick facts
- **30 verses**, pages 562–564 in the Madinah Mushaf.
- Also called **Surah Tabarak**, after its first word.

### Its virtue
The Prophet ﷺ said: **"A surah of the Quran of thirty verses interceded for a man until he was forgiven: *Tabārak alladhī biyadihil-mulk*."** (Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi, who graded it hasan)

### Thematic breakdown
| Section | Verses | Simple meaning for kids |
|---|---|---|
| Allah's greatness and the sky | 1–5 | Allah made the heavens in layers and decorated them with stars |
| The regret of the disbelievers | 6–11 | The sorrow of those who didn't listen to the warning |
| Allah's knowledge and the earth | 12–15 | Allah knows secrets, and made the earth easy to walk on |
| Allah's power | 16–22 | Only Allah holds the birds up in the sky |
| Allah's blessings | 23–27 | He gave us hearing, sight and hearts |
| Closing | 28–30 | Who would bring us water if it disappeared? |

### Adults: 2 weeks
- 5 days a week, **3 verses a day**.
- Day 6: revise the week.
- After finishing, recite it from memory every night for two weeks.

### Kids: 4 weeks
| Week | Verses | Daily target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–8 | 1–2 verses |
| 2 | 9–15 | 1–2 verses |
| 3 | 16–23 | 2 verses |
| 4 | 24–30 | 2 verses |

### Ideas for children
- At ﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡا إِلَى ٱلطَّيۡرِ فَوۡقَهُمۡ صَٰٓفَّٰتٍ﴾, look at birds together from the window.
- The final verse, ﴿قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَصۡبَحَ مَآؤُكُمۡ غَوۡرًا فَمَن يَأۡتِيكُم بِمَآءٍ مَّعِينِۭ﴾: ask, "What if the water in our tap disappeared? Who would bring it back?"
- Play it daily in Sheikh Al-Husary's slow teaching recitation.

### Spots that need attention
- **Verse 3:** ﴿مِن تَفَٰوُتٍ﴾, Noon hidden before Taa (Ikhfa).
- **Verses 3–4:** ﴿ٱلۡبَصَرَ﴾ repeats: first ﴿فَٱرۡجِعِ ٱلۡبَصَرَ﴾, then ﴿ثُمَّ ٱرۡجِعِ ٱلۡبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيۡنِ﴾.
- **Verse 22:** ﴿عَلَىٰ وَجۡهِهِۦٓ أَهۡدَىٰٓ﴾ has a long *ṣilah* (4–5 counts) because a hamza follows.

## Surah Al-Qalam Meaning: By the Pen, and the Prophet's Great Character

**Surah Al-Qalam** (52 ayat) opens with an oath by the pen and a beautiful testimony about the Prophet ﷺ.

﴿نٓۚ وَٱلۡقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسۡطُرُونَ﴾ ﴿مَآ أَنتَ بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ بِمَجۡنُونٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ لَكَ لَأَجۡرًا غَيۡرَ مَمۡنُونٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾

### His character ﷺ
- When ʿAishah was asked about his character she said: «His character was the Quran» [Muslim].

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–7 | The pen and the Prophet's character |
| 8–16 | Do not obey the deniers and slanderers |
| 17–33 | The owners of the garden |
| 34–47 | The righteous and the deniers compared |
| 48–52 | Be patient, unlike the man of the whale |

### The owners of the garden
- They swore to harvest at dawn so **the poor would get nothing**.
- A calamity struck the garden at night; it became like a harvested field.
- They admitted: *we were wrongdoers*.

### The patience of Yunus
- *So be patient for the decision of your Lord, and do not be like the man of the whale.*

### Lessons
- **Character** is the heart of the message.
- **Stinginess** destroys blessings.

## Surah Al-Haqqah Meaning: The Inevitable Reality

**Surah Al-Haqqah** (52 ayat) is named after one of the names of the Day of Judgement: **the inevitable reality**.

﴿ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ﴾ ﴿مَا ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ﴾

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–12 | Thamud, ʿAd, Pharaoh and the overturned cities |
| 13–18 | The Trumpet and the Day of exposure |
| 19–24 | The one given his record in his right hand |
| 25–37 | The one given his record in his left hand |
| 38–52 | The Quran is the word of a noble messenger |

### Two records
| Right hand | Left hand |
|---|---|
| ﴿فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ هَآؤُمُ ٱقۡرَءُواْ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ﴾ | ﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِشِمَالِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أُوتَ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ﴾ |
| *Here, read my record!* | *I wish I had not been given my record* |
| ﴿إِنِّي ظَنَنتُ أَنِّي مُلَٰقٍ حِسَابِيَهۡ﴾ | ﴿وَلَمۡ أَدۡرِ مَا حِسَابِيَهۡ﴾ |
- **The secret of success**: *I was certain I would meet my account.*

### A sin to notice
- Among the crimes of the doomed: *he did not encourage feeding the poor*.

### Recitation tip
- The words ending in *-iyah* (﴿كِتَٰبِيَهۡ﴾, ﴿حِسَابِيَهۡ﴾) have a **haa of silence** pronounced in both joining and stopping.

## "So Be Patient with Beautiful Patience": Quran 70:5-7 Explained

﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ صَبۡرٗا جَمِيلًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُمۡ يَرَوۡنَهُۥ بَعِيدٗا﴾ ﴿وَنَرَىٰهُ قَرِيبٗا﴾ [Al-Maarij 70:5-7]

### Beautiful patience
- Scholars describe *sabr jamil* as patience **without complaining to people**. Complaining to Allah, as Yaʿqub did, does not break it.

### Far or near?
| They | Allah |
|---|---|
| See the Day as far | Sees it as near |

- Everything that is coming is near.

### Perspective
- The hardship that feels endless is short compared to eternity.

### Three "beautiful" acts in the Quran
- Beautiful patience, beautiful forgiveness (15:85), and beautiful distancing (73:10).

### Lessons
- **Complain to Allah**, not to everyone.
- **Remember the end** to endure the present.

## "Man Was Created Anxious, Except Those Who Pray": Quran 70:19-22 Explained

﴿۞إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ خُلِقَ هَلُوعًا﴾ ﴿إِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ جَزُوعٗا﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلۡخَيۡرُ مَنُوعًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱلۡمُصَلِّينَ﴾ [Al-Maarij 70:19-22]

### What is *haluʿ*?
- The Quran explains it immediately:
| State | Reaction |
|---|---|
| When harm touches him | *Jazuʿ*: panics and despairs |
| When good touches him | *Manuʿ*: withholds and refuses to share |

### The exception
- *Except those who pray.* Then the surah lists their qualities:
| Quality | Verse |
|---|---|
| Constant in prayer | 70:23 |
| A known right in wealth for the asker and deprived | 70:24-25 |
| Believe in the Day of Judgment | 70:26 |
| Fear their Lord's punishment | 70:27 |
| Guard chastity | 70:29 |
| Keep trusts and promises | 70:32 |
| Stand by their testimonies | 70:33 |
| Guard their prayers | 70:34 |

### Prayer brackets the list
- The qualities **start and end with prayer**: it is the frame that holds character together.

### Anxiety and faith
- The verse describes human nature honestly. Prayer does not remove feelings, but it gives the heart balance.

### Lessons
- **Make prayer constant**, not occasional.
- **Share in ease**, stay calm in hardship.

## "Seek Forgiveness of Your Lord, He Is Ever Forgiving": Quran 71:10-12 Explained

﴿فَقُلۡتُ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفَّارٗا﴾ ﴿يُرۡسِلِ ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّدۡرَارٗا﴾ ﴿وَيُمۡدِدۡكُم بِأَمۡوَٰلٖ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ أَنۡهَٰرٗا﴾ [Nuh 71:10-12]

### *Ghaffar*
- An intensive form: forgiving again and again.

### The fruits of istighfar
| Fruit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Abundant rain | Continuous, beneficial rain |
| Wealth | Wider provision |
| Children | Offspring |
| Gardens and rivers | Prosperity and greenery |

### Al-Hasan al-Basri
- Al-Qurtubi and others mention that people complained to al-Hasan al-Basri of drought, poverty, lack of children and dry gardens, and he told each of them to seek forgiveness, then recited these verses.

### Real istighfar
- It comes with leaving the sin and regret, not words alone.

### Lessons
- **Keep a daily istighfar routine.**
- **When provision is tight**, increase istighfar along with work.

## Surah Nuh Meaning: 950 Years of Patient Calling

**Surah Nuh** (28 ayat) is entirely about one prophet's long struggle: Nuh stayed among his people *a thousand years minus fifty* [Al-'Ankabut: 14].

### Every method of da'wah
| Method | Ayah meaning |
|---|---|
| Night and day | Constant effort |
| Openly | Public calling |
| Privately | Personal conversations |
- Yet they put their fingers in their ears and covered themselves with their garments.

### The fruits of seeking forgiveness
﴿فَقُلۡتُ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفَّارٗا﴾ ﴿يُرۡسِلِ ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّدۡرَارٗا﴾ ﴿وَيُمۡدِدۡكُم بِأَمۡوَٰلٖ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ أَنۡهَٰرٗا﴾
| Blessing | |
|---|---|
| Abundant rain | Wealth |
| Children | Gardens and rivers |

### The idols
- His people told each other not to abandon Wadd, Suwaʿ, Yaghuth, Yaʿuq and Nasr.
- Ibn ʿAbbas explained these were names of righteous men; later generations made statues of them, then worshipped them [Al-Bukhari].

### Nuh's du'a
﴿رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيۡتِيَ مُؤۡمِنٗا وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۖ﴾

### Lessons
- **Never give up** calling to good.
- **Istighfar** opens doors of provision.

## "My Lord, Forgive Me and My Parents": The Duʿa of Nuh in Quran 71:28

﴿رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيۡتِيَ مُؤۡمِنٗا وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۖ وَلَا تَزِدِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا تَبَارَۢا﴾ [Nuh 71:28]

### A beautiful order
| For | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Himself | Start with yourself |
| His parents | The closest right |
| Whoever enters his house a believer | Household and companions |
| All believing men and women | The whole Ummah |

### "My house"
- Explained as his home, his place of worship, or the ark. The condition *a believer* excludes those who entered without faith.

### After centuries
- He made this duʿa after *a thousand years less fifty* of calling his people (Al-Ankabut 29:14), yet he did not forget the believers.

### Lessons
- **Say this duʿa** after your prayers.
- **Widen your duʿa** to include all believers.
