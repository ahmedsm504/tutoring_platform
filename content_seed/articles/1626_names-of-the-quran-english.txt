Allah called His Book by several names. Each name shows a different side of it.

## The five best-known names
| Name | Meaning | Ayah |
|---|---|---|
| Al-Qurʾān | The recited | ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ يَهۡدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقۡوَمُ﴾ [Al-Isra: 9] |
| Al-Kitāb | The written Book | ﴿ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيۡبَۛ فِيهِۛ هُدٗى لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah: 2] |
| Al-Furqān | The criterion between truth and falsehood | ﴿تَبَارَكَ ٱلَّذِي نَزَّلَ ٱلۡفُرۡقَانَ عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِۦ﴾ [Al-Furqan: 1] |
| Adh-Dhikr | The reminder | ﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ﴾ [Al-Hijr: 9] |
| At-Tanzīl | The revelation sent down | ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَنزِيلُ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ [Ash-Shu'ara: 192] |

## Recited and written
- Some scholars noted that the names **Al-Qurʾān** (recited) and **Al-Kitāb** (written) point to how it is preserved: **in hearts** and **on pages**.

## Descriptions in the Quran
| Description | Ayah |
|---|---|
| Light | ﴿وَأَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ نُورٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾ [An-Nisa: 174] |
| Blessed | ﴿وَهَٰذَا ذِكۡرࣱ مُّبَارَكٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُۚ﴾ [Al-Anbiya: 50] |
| Healing and mercy | ﴿وَنُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَا هُوَ شِفَآءࣱ وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ [Al-Isra: 82] |

## Why it matters
- Each name is a **relationship**: recite it, write it, judge by it, be reminded by it.
