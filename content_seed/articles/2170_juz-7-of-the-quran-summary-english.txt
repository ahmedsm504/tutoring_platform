Juz 7 begins with ﴿۞لَتَجِدَنَّ أَشَدَّ ٱلنَّاسِ عَدَٰوَةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱلۡيَهُودَ وَٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُواْۖ﴾, "You will surely find the most intense of people in animosity towards the believers to be the Jews and the polytheists", so it is called **Juz Latajidanna**; some call it **Wa Idha Sami'u** after the next verse. It runs from **Al-Ma'idah 5:82 to Al-An'am 6:110**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 121 to 141**.

This juz is a bridge between two worlds. It closes the Madani **Surah Al-Ma'idah** with its final rulings and a powerful scene from the Day of Judgement, and then takes you into the Makki **Surah Al-An'am**, one of the greatest surahs on monotheism, which builds proofs, debates the idolaters and presents the argument of Ibrahim, peace be upon him.

## The big idea

The juz moves from **detailing the law** to **founding belief**. At the end of Al-Ma'idah the rulings on lawful and unlawful are completed, and then comes a scene in which the messengers are questioned about their nations and 'Isa disowns those who worshipped him. When you reach Al-An'am, almost the whole surah is a dialogue about oneness: Who created? Who controls benefit and harm? Who knows the unseen? What was the argument of the prophets?

The message: **rulings rest on belief, and belief rests on proof.**

## Part one: the close of Surah Al-Ma'idah (82-120)

### 1. Eyes overflowing with tears (82-86)
Allah states a reality about the hostility of the idolaters and many Jews towards the believers, then says: ﴿وَلَتَجِدَنَّ أَقۡرَبَهُم مَّوَدَّةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا نَصَٰرَىٰۚ﴾, "and you will find the nearest of them in affection to the believers those who say, 'We are Christians'", explaining that among them are priests and monks and that they are not arrogant. Then the moving scene: ﴿وَإِذَا سَمِعُواْ مَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَى ٱلرَّسُولِ تَرَىٰٓ أَعۡيُنَهُمۡ تَفِيضُ مِنَ ٱلدَّمۡعِ مِمَّا عَرَفُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّۖ﴾, "When they hear what has been revealed to the Messenger, you see their eyes overflowing with tears because of the truth they recognise." Many commentators say it refers to the Negus and those with him when they heard the Quran from Ja'far ibn Abi Talib.

**Lesson**: a humble heart recognises the truth when it hears it; arrogance is the barrier.

### 2. Do not forbid good things (87-89)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُحَرِّمُواْ طَيِّبَٰتِ مَآ أَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ وَلَا تَعۡتَدُوٓاْۚ﴾, "O you who believe, do not forbid the good things Allah has made lawful for you, and do not transgress." Extreme asceticism is forbidden, just as indulging in the unlawful is. In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, three men asked about the Prophet's worship and thought it little; one said he would pray all night forever, another that he would fast every day, and the third that he would never marry. The Prophet ﷺ said: "By Allah, I fear Allah most and am most conscious of Him among you, yet I fast and break my fast, I pray and I sleep, and I marry women. Whoever turns away from my Sunnah is not from me." Then the expiation for broken oaths: feeding or clothing ten poor people or freeing a slave, and if unable, fasting three days.

### 3. The final prohibition of wine (90-93)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡخَمۡرُ وَٱلۡمَيۡسِرُ وَٱلۡأَنصَابُ وَٱلۡأَزۡلَٰمُ رِجۡسࣱ مِّنۡ عَمَلِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ فَٱجۡتَنِبُوهُ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾, "O you who believe, intoxicants, gambling, stone altars and divining arrows are filth from the work of Shaytan, so avoid them so that you may succeed." The wisdom follows: they cause enmity and hatred and block remembrance of Allah and prayer. Then the decisive question: ﴿فَهَلۡ أَنتُم مُّنتَهُونَ﴾, "So will you not desist?" And the Companions said: we have desisted.

**The stages of prohibition**: a question and answer in Al-Baqarah 219 (their sin is greater than their benefit), then a ban on praying while intoxicated in An-Nisa 43, then the final ban here, an example of the wisdom of gradual legislation.

### 4. Hunting in ihram and the sanctity of the House (94-100)
Rulings on hunting during ihram and its compensation, and the permission to catch sea game. Then: ﴿۞جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡكَعۡبَةَ ٱلۡبَيۡتَ ٱلۡحَرَامَ قِيَٰمٗا لِّلنَّاسِ﴾, "Allah made the Kaaba, the Sacred House, a means of support for people", for their religion and their worldly life. Then a principle: ﴿قُل لَّا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡخَبِيثُ وَٱلطَّيِّبُ وَلَوۡ أَعۡجَبَكَ كَثۡرَةُ ٱلۡخَبِيثِۚ﴾, "Say: the bad and the good are not equal, even if the abundance of the bad impresses you." Numbers are not the measure of truth.

### 5. The etiquette of asking (101-108)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَسۡـَٔلُواْ عَنۡ أَشۡيَآءَ إِن تُبۡدَ لَكُمۡ تَسُؤۡكُمۡ﴾, "O you who believe, do not ask about things which, if made plain to you, would trouble you." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, a man asked the Prophet ﷺ "Who is my father?" and others pressed him with questions, and this was revealed. Then pre-Islamic customs about livestock (*bahirah*, *sa'ibah*, *wasilah*, *ham*) are cancelled, and then: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَنفُسَكُمۡۖ لَا يَضُرُّكُم مَّن ضَلَّ إِذَا ٱهۡتَدَيۡتُمۡۚ﴾, "O you who believe, upon you is responsibility for yourselves; those who stray cannot harm you if you are guided."

Abu Bakr warned in a sermon that people put this verse in the wrong place: abandoning enjoining good is not part of its meaning, because full guidance includes enjoining good. Then rulings on witnesses to a will made while travelling.

### 6. The Day Allah gathers the messengers (109-111)
﴿۞يَوۡمَ يَجۡمَعُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَيَقُولُ مَاذَآ أُجِبۡتُمۡۖ قَالُواْ لَا عِلۡمَ لَنَآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ عَلَّـٰمُ ٱلۡغُيُوبِ﴾, "The Day Allah gathers the messengers and says: what response did you receive? They will say: we have no knowledge; You are the Knower of the unseen." Allah reminds 'Isa of His favours: ﴿إِذۡ أَيَّدتُّكَ بِرُوحِ ٱلۡقُدُسِ تُكَلِّمُ ٱلنَّاسَ فِي ٱلۡمَهۡدِ وَكَهۡلٗاۖ﴾, "when I supported you with the Holy Spirit, and you spoke to people in the cradle and in maturity", teaching him scripture and wisdom, the bird from clay, healing the blind and the leper, bringing out the dead, all ﴿بِإِذۡنِيۖ﴾, "by My permission", a phrase repeated four times in the verse to stress that the miracles were from Allah, not from 'Isa. Then Allah's inspiration to the disciples.

### 7. The Table Spread (112-115)
﴿إِذۡ قَالَ ٱلۡحَوَارِيُّونَ يَٰعِيسَى ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ هَلۡ يَسۡتَطِيعُ رَبُّكَ أَن يُنَزِّلَ عَلَيۡنَا مَآئِدَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِۖ﴾, "When the disciples said: O 'Isa son of Maryam, can your Lord send down to us a table spread from heaven?" He told them to fear Allah. They explained: we want to eat from it and have our hearts reassured. So 'Isa prayed: ﴿ٱللَّهُمَّ رَبَّنَآ أَنزِلۡ عَلَيۡنَا مَآئِدَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ تَكُونُ لَنَا عِيدٗا لِّأَوَّلِنَا وَءَاخِرِنَا وَءَايَةٗ مِّنكَۖ وَٱرۡزُقۡنَا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰزِقِينَ﴾, "O Allah, our Lord, send down to us a table from heaven, to be a festival for the first and last of us and a sign from You; and provide for us, for You are the best of providers." The answer: I will send it down, with a severe warning for anyone who disbelieves afterwards.

**Lesson**: signs that are demanded increase responsibility. Whoever asks for proof and receives it stands before an even greater argument.

### 8. The final dialogue (116-120)
﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ ٱللَّهُ يَٰعِيسَى ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ ءَأَنتَ قُلۡتَ لِلنَّاسِ ٱتَّخِذُونِي وَأُمِّيَ إِلَٰهَيۡنِ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۖ قَالَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ مَا يَكُونُ لِيٓ أَنۡ أَقُولَ مَا لَيۡسَ لِي بِحَقٍّۚ﴾, "And when Allah says: O 'Isa son of Maryam, did you say to people, take me and my mother as gods besides Allah? He will say: Glory be to You! It was not for me to say what I had no right to." 'Isa says he told them only what Allah commanded: worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord. Then his timeless words: ﴿إِن تُعَذِّبۡهُمۡ فَإِنَّهُمۡ عِبَادُكَۖ وَإِن تَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ فَإِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾, "If You punish them, they are Your servants; and if You forgive them, You are the Mighty, the Wise."

In Sahih Muslim, 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr reported that the Prophet ﷺ recited Ibrahim's words from Surah Ibrahim and these words of 'Isa, then raised his hands and said, "O Allah, my nation, my nation", and wept. Allah sent Jibril to say: "We will please you concerning your nation and will not grieve you."

Then the close: ﴿هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ يَنفَعُ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ صِدۡقُهُمۡۚ﴾, "This is the Day when the truthful will benefit from their truthfulness", and ﴿رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾, "Allah is pleased with them and they with Him; that is the great success." The surah that began with fulfilling contracts ends with truthfulness, the spirit of fulfilment.

## Part two: the opening of Surah Al-An'am (1-110)

Al-An'am is a Makki surah of 165 verses, named "The Cattle" after the livestock and the practices the idolaters invented concerning them. It is one of the greatest surahs on monotheism, and the word **"Say" (*qul*)** recurs throughout its debates with the idolaters. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-An'am Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-anam-meaning-english/).

### 1. Praise to the Creator (1-11)
﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَجَعَلَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ وَٱلنُّورَۖ ثُمَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡ يَعۡدِلُونَ﴾, "All praise is for Allah who created the heavens and the earth and made darkness and light; yet those who disbelieve set up equals to their Lord." Notice "darkness" is plural and "light" singular: the truth is one, while falsehood has many paths. Then the creation of the human being from clay and the fixing of his term, the turning away of deniers, and: ﴿قُلۡ سِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ثُمَّ ٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾, "Say: travel through the land and see what was the end of the deniers."

### 2. The chain of "Say" (12-24)
- ﴿قُل لِّمَن مَّا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ قُل لِّلَّهِۚ كَتَبَ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَةَۚ﴾, "Say: to whom belongs what is in the heavens and earth? Say: to Allah. He has decreed mercy upon Himself."
- ﴿قُلۡ أَغَيۡرَ ٱللَّهِ أَتَّخِذُ وَلِيّٗا فَاطِرِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَهُوَ يُطۡعِمُ وَلَا يُطۡعَمُۗ﴾, "Say: shall I take as a protector other than Allah, Originator of the heavens and earth, who feeds and is not fed?"
- ﴿قُلۡ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ إِنۡ عَصَيۡتُ رَبِّي عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾, "Say: I fear, if I disobey my Lord, the punishment of a tremendous Day."
- ﴿وَإِن يَمۡسَسۡكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرࣲّ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَۖ﴾, "If Allah touches you with harm, none can remove it but Him."
- ﴿قُلۡ أَيُّ شَيۡءٍ أَكۡبَرُ شَهَٰدَةٗۖ قُلِ ٱللَّهُۖ شَهِيدُۢ بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡۚ وَأُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ لِأُنذِرَكُم بِهِۦ وَمَنۢ بَلَغَۚ﴾, "Say: what is the greatest testimony? Say: Allah is witness between me and you, and this Quran was revealed to me so that I may warn you by it and whomever it reaches." Everyone the Quran reaches has received the warning.

### 3. Scenes of denial and regret (25-35)
They listen without understanding and call it "legends of the ancients". Then the scene of them standing before the Fire: if only we could be sent back and not deny. Then: ﴿وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا لَعِبࣱ وَلَهۡوࣱۖ وَلَلدَّارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ لِّلَّذِينَ يَتَّقُونَۚ﴾, "The life of this world is nothing but play and amusement, and the home of the Hereafter is better for those who are God-conscious." Then comfort for the Prophet ﷺ: ﴿قَدۡ نَعۡلَمُ إِنَّهُۥ لَيَحۡزُنُكَ ٱلَّذِي يَقُولُونَۖ فَإِنَّهُمۡ لَا يُكَذِّبُونَكَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ يَجۡحَدُونَ﴾, "We know that what they say grieves you; it is not you they deny, but the wrongdoers reject the signs of Allah."

### 4. Communities like you, and the law of gradual ruin (36-49)
Hizb 14 begins: ﴿۞إِنَّمَا يَسۡتَجِيبُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡمَعُونَۘ﴾, "Only those who listen will respond." Then: ﴿وَمَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا طَـٰٓئِرٖ يَطِيرُ بِجَنَاحَيۡهِ إِلَّآ أُمَمٌ أَمۡثَالُكُمۚ مَّا فَرَّطۡنَا فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِن شَيۡءٖۚ﴾, "There is no creature on earth or bird flying with its wings except that they are communities like you. We have neglected nothing in the Record."

Then one of Allah's most serious laws, **gradual ruin** (*istidraj*): nations are struck with hardship so they might humble themselves; if their hearts harden, gradual ruin follows: ﴿فَلَمَّا نَسُواْ مَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهِۦ فَتَحۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَبۡوَٰبَ كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فَرِحُواْ بِمَآ أُوتُوٓاْ أَخَذۡنَٰهُم بَغۡتَةٗ فَإِذَا هُم مُّبۡلِسُونَ﴾, "When they forgot what they had been reminded of, We opened to them the doors of everything, until, when they rejoiced in what they were given, We seized them suddenly, and they were in despair." Not every blessing is a sign of Allah's pleasure.

### 5. Who the Messenger is, and the humble believers (50-58)
﴿قُل لَّآ أَقُولُ لَكُمۡ عِندِي خَزَآئِنُ ٱللَّهِ وَلَآ أَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ وَلَآ أَقُولُ لَكُمۡ إِنِّي مَلَكٌۖ﴾, "Say: I do not tell you that I have the treasures of Allah, or that I know the unseen, or that I am an angel." The Messenger is a human being who follows revelation. Then a great instruction: ﴿وَلَا تَطۡرُدِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَدَوٰةِ وَٱلۡعَشِيِّ يُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَهُۥۖ﴾, "Do not send away those who call upon their Lord morning and evening, seeking His face." In Sahih Muslim, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas said it was revealed when Quraysh leaders asked that poor Companions be kept away from them. Then: ﴿وَإِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا فَقُلۡ سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ كَتَبَ رَبُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَةَ﴾, "When those who believe in Our signs come to you, say: Peace be upon you. Your Lord has decreed mercy upon Himself."

**Lesson**: a person's worth with Allah is in turning to Him, not in wealth or status.

### 6. The keys of the unseen and Allah's power (59-73)
﴿۞وَعِندَهُۥ مَفَاتِحُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ لَا يَعۡلَمُهَآ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۚ وَمَا تَسۡقُطُ مِن وَرَقَةٍ إِلَّا يَعۡلَمُهَا﴾, "With Him are the keys of the unseen; none knows them but Him. He knows what is on land and sea, and not a leaf falls but that He knows it." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ explained the keys of the unseen as the five things mentioned at the end of Surah Luqman. Read [The Keys of the Unseen: 6:59](/blog/wa-indahu-mafatih-al-ghayb-meaning-english/). Then: ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يَتَوَفَّىٰكُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا جَرَحۡتُم بِٱلنَّهَارِ﴾, "It is He who takes your souls by night and knows what you did by day": sleep is a lesser death. Who rescues you from the darkness of land and sea? Then: ﴿قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلۡقَادِرُ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَبۡعَثَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ عَذَابٗا مِّن فَوۡقِكُمۡ أَوۡ مِن تَحۡتِ أَرۡجُلِكُمۡ أَوۡ يَلۡبِسَكُمۡ شِيَعٗا﴾, "Say: He is able to send punishment on you from above or from beneath your feet, or to confuse you into factions." Then the command to leave gatherings that mock Allah's verses.

### 7. Ibrahim's argument (74-83)
Ibrahim's dialogue with his father Azar: ﴿أَتَتَّخِذُ أَصۡنَامًا ءَالِهَةًۖ﴾, "Do you take idols as gods?" Then the kingdom of the heavens and earth, then the star, the moon and the sun: each time he said "This is my Lord", and when it set he said "I do not love things that set." Commentators explain this was a debate leading his people step by step to the truth, not doubt on his part. Then his declaration: ﴿إِنِّي وَجَّهۡتُ وَجۡهِيَ لِلَّذِي فَطَرَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ حَنِيفٗاۖ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾, "I have turned my face to the One who created the heavens and the earth, as an upright monotheist, and I am not of the polytheists."

Then the verdict: ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَمۡ يَلۡبِسُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنَهُم بِظُلۡمٍ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡأَمۡنُ وَهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ﴾, "Those who believe and do not mix their faith with wrongdoing: for them is security, and they are rightly guided." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud said the Companions found this hard and asked, "Which of us has not wronged himself?" The Prophet ﷺ said: it is not as you think; it is shirk. Have you not heard Luqman's words, "Indeed shirk is a great wrong"? Then: ﴿وَتِلۡكَ حُجَّتُنَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦۚ﴾, "That was Our argument which We gave Ibrahim against his people." Read [Not Mixing Faith with Wrongdoing: 6:82](/blog/wa-lam-yalbisu-imanahum-bi-zulm-meaning-english/).

### 8. Eighteen prophets (84-92)
In verses 83-86 Allah names eighteen prophets: Ibrahim, Ishaq, Ya'qub, Nuh, Dawud, Sulayman, Ayyub, Yusuf, Musa, Harun, Zakariya, Yahya, 'Isa, Ilyas, Isma'il, Al-Yasa', Yunus and Lut, the largest number of prophets named in one place in the Quran. Then the command: ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَدَى ٱللَّهُۖ فَبِهُدَىٰهُمُ ٱقۡتَدِهۡۗ﴾, "Those are the ones Allah guided, so follow their guidance." Then a reply to those who said Allah revealed nothing to any human, and a description of the Quran: ﴿وَهَٰذَا كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مُبَارَكࣱ مُّصَدِّقُ ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ﴾, "And this is a blessed Book We revealed, confirming what came before it."

### 9. Signs of creation (93-104)
After the scene of wrongdoers in the agonies of death comes a panorama of creation: ﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ فَالِقُ ٱلۡحَبِّ وَٱلنَّوَىٰۖ يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ وَمُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ مِنَ ٱلۡحَيِّۚ﴾, "Allah is the splitter of grain and date-stone; He brings the living out of the dead and the dead out of the living." Then the One who splits the dawn, makes night for rest, the sun and moon for reckoning, the stars for guidance on land and sea, then rain, plants, palms, grapes, olives and pomegranates: ﴿ٱنظُرُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ ثَمَرِهِۦٓ إِذَآ أَثۡمَرَ وَيَنۡعِهِۦٓۚ﴾, "Look at its fruit when it bears fruit and at its ripening." Then: ﴿لَّا تُدۡرِكُهُ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَهُوَ يُدۡرِكُ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَۖ وَهُوَ ٱللَّطِيفُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ﴾, "Vision does not encompass Him, but He encompasses all vision; He is the Subtle, the Aware." Sight cannot encompass Him, while other texts establish that the believers will see their Lord in the Hereafter.

### 10. The etiquette of calling to Allah (105-110)
﴿وَلَا تَسُبُّواْ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَسُبُّواْ ٱللَّهَ عَدۡوَۢا بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖۗ﴾, "Do not insult those they call upon besides Allah, lest they insult Allah in enmity without knowledge." Insulting what idolaters worship is forbidden when it leads them to insult Allah; this is a foundation of the principle of blocking the means to harm. The juz ends with the idolaters swearing that if a sign came they would believe, and the answer: ﴿وَنُقَلِّبُ أَفۡـِٔدَتَهُمۡ وَأَبۡصَٰرَهُمۡ كَمَا لَمۡ يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِهِۦٓ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةࣲ وَنَذَرُهُمۡ فِي طُغۡيَٰنِهِمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ﴾, "And We will turn away their hearts and their eyes just as they refused to believe the first time, and leave them wandering blindly in their transgression." The problem is not a shortage of signs; it is in the hearts.

## Tajweed points in Juz 7

### The forbidden-stop sign
There is no compulsory pause for Hafs in this juz, but there is the sign ۘ (do not stop here), as in ﴿إِنَّمَا يَسۡتَجِيبُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡمَعُونَۘ﴾. Stopping there would distort the meaning.

### Merging in ﴿أَيَّدتُّكَ﴾
The sakin dal merges fully into the ta, since both come from the same point of articulation.

### ﴿أَنَا۠﴾ in 6:79
The alif is pronounced when stopping and dropped when continuing.

### Clear noon in ﴿مِنۡ عِلۡمٍ﴾ and ﴿مِنۡ حِسَابِهِم﴾
Sakin noon before 'ayn and ha' is pronounced clearly.

### Compulsory madd in ﴿أَتُحَٰٓجُّوٓنِّي﴾
In 6:80 the jeem is doubled after an alif, so the madd is six counts. For all types see [Madd Rules in Tajweed](/blog/madd-rules-tajweed-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. "He has decreed mercy upon Himself"
- 6:12: ﴿كَتَبَ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَةَۚ﴾, without "your Lord".
- 6:54: ﴿كَتَبَ رَبُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَةَ﴾, with "your Lord".
**Key**: the second addresses believers who came to the Prophet ﷺ, so "your Lord" was added to put them at ease.

### 2. "Travel through the land"
- 6:11: ﴿ثُمَّ ٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾, with *thumma* ("then").
- An-Naml 69: ﴿فَٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ﴾, with *fa*.
**Key**: "thumma unzuru" occurs only in Al-An'am.

### 3. Play and amusement
- 6:32: ﴿لَعِبࣱ وَلَهۡوࣱۖ﴾, play then amusement.
- Al-'Ankabut 64: ﴿لَهۡوࣱ وَلَعِبࣱۚ﴾, amusement then play.

### 4. *Mushtabihan* and *mutashabihan*
- 6:99: ﴿مُشۡتَبِهٗا وَغَيۡرَ مُتَشَٰبِهٍۗ﴾.
- 6:141: ﴿مُتَشَٰبِهٗا وَغَيۡرَ مُتَشَٰبِهٖۚ﴾.
**Key**: the first (in Juz 7) is *mushtabihan*; the second (in Juz 8) is *mutashabihan*.

## How to memorize Juz 7 with understanding

1. **The end of Al-Ma'idah is rulings, then a scene**: memorize the rulings (wine, hunting, wills) and then the scene of 'Isa as one continuous story.
2. **Al-An'am is a surah of dialogue**: underline every "Say" in your memorization copy; they are signposts.
3. **Memorize the Ibrahim passage in sequence**: his father, the kingdom of heaven, the star, the moon, the sun, the declaration, the debate with his people.
4. **Learn the eighteen prophets** in the order the verses give them.
5. **Picture the creation verses (95-99)** as a sequence of images: seed, dawn, night, sun, stars, rain, plants.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 7

- **Keep your heart humble before the truth**, and your tears will come when you hear it.
- **Do not forbid yourself what Allah made lawful** in the name of asceticism.
- **Avoid intoxicants and gambling** and all that blocks remembrance of Allah.
- **Numbers do not prove truth.**
- **Do not ask about what will not benefit you.**
- **Be guided first**, then call others.
- **Do not be deceived by blessings alongside sin**; they may be gradual ruin.
- **Honour the poor who turn to Allah.**
- **Follow the guidance of the prophets.**
- **Do not insult what others hold sacred**, lest they insult Allah.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 7 called "Latajidanna"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞لَتَجِدَنَّ أَشَدَّ ٱلنَّاسِ عَدَٰوَةٗ﴾. Some call it "Wa Idha Sami'u" after the next verse.

### Where does Juz 7 end?
At Al-An'am 6:110. Juz 8 begins with ﴿۞وَلَوۡ أَنَّنَا نَزَّلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ﴾, "And even if We had sent down angels to them..."

### How many prophets are named together in Al-An'am?
Eighteen, in verses 83-86.

### What were the stages of prohibiting wine?
Stating that its sin is greater than its benefit (2:219), forbidding prayer while intoxicated (4:43), then the final prohibition (5:90-91).

### Did Ibrahim doubt his Lord when he said "This is my Lord"?
No. Commentators explain he was debating his people and leading them to see that heavenly bodies cannot be gods, which is why Allah closes the passage with "That was Our argument which We gave Ibrahim against his people."

## Conclusion

Juz 7 travels from the last of the law to the root of belief. It closes Al-Ma'idah with rulings that protect the mind, wealth and worship, then a scene in which 'Isa disowns the excess about him. It then opens Al-An'am with praise of the Creator, builds proofs, and presents Ibrahim's argument and the procession of prophets. Understanding it shows that rulings without belief are a body without a soul, and that sound belief is built on proof and reflection.
