﴿لَن تَنَالُواْ ٱلۡبِرَّ حَتَّىٰ تُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِن شَيۡءٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِهِۦ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ [Al Imran 3:92]

## Meaning
*You will never attain righteousness until you spend from what you love. And whatever you spend, Allah knows it.*

## Abu Talhah's response
- When this verse was revealed, Abu Talhah went to the Prophet ﷺ and gave away **Bayruha**, his most beloved garden near the mosque. The Prophet ﷺ praised it as profitable wealth and advised him to give it to his relatives (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## What counts as "what you love"?
| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| Wealth | Giving from the best, not the leftovers |
| Time | Visiting the sick when you want to rest |
| Effort | Teaching someone what you worked hard to learn |
| Comfort | Waking for Fajr or night prayer |

## "Allah knows it"
- No gift is too small to be seen. Even a small sincere amount is recorded.

## Connection with Al-Baqarah 2:177
- There, righteousness includes *giving wealth despite loving it*. Both verses agree: **love tests sincerity**.

## Lessons
- **Give your best** to Allah.
- **Start small but sincere.**
