Surah At-Tur had a remarkable effect on a nobleman of Quraysh. In Sahih al-Bukhari, Jubayr ibn Mut'im said: "I heard the Prophet ﷺ recite At-Tur in Maghrib. When he reached this verse: *Or were they created by nothing, or were they the creators? Or did they create the heavens and the earth? Rather, they are not certain. Or have they the depositories of your Lord, or are they the controllers?*, my heart almost flew." Jubayr was still an idolater then, having come to Madinah to negotiate for the prisoners of Badr, and that was the first time faith settled in his heart. The surah opens with majestic oaths, warns of a punishment that cannot be repelled, describes the bliss of the righteous, then surrounds the deniers with questions they cannot answer.

This guide explains Surah At-Tur section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah At-Tur
- **Number of verses**: 49.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 52, in the twenty-seventh juz.
- **Name**: At-Tur means "the Mount", the mountain where Allah spoke to Musa.
- **Recited in Maghrib**: the Prophet ﷺ recited it in the Maghrib prayer, as Jubayr reported.
- **Main themes**: the certainty of punishment, the reward of the righteous, rational arguments against the deniers, and comfort for the Prophet ﷺ.

## Verses 1–8: Five Oaths
﴿وَٱلطُّورِ﴾ ﴿وَكِتَٰبٖ مَّسۡطُورٖ﴾ ﴿فِي رَقّٖ مَّنشُورٖ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡبَيۡتِ ٱلۡمَعۡمُورِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلسَّقۡفِ ٱلۡمَرۡفُوعِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ ٱلۡمَسۡجُورِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ عَذَابَ رَبِّكَ لَوَٰقِعࣱ﴾ ﴿مَّا لَهُۥ مِن دَافِعٖ﴾ (Quran 52:1-8)

1. **The Mount**: the mountain where Allah spoke to Musa.
2. **"A Book inscribed in parchment spread open"**: the Quran, or the Preserved Tablet, or the records of deeds.
3. **"The Frequented House"** (*al-Bayt al-Ma'mur*): a house in the seventh heaven. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, in the hadith of the Night Journey, the Prophet ﷺ said: "The Frequented House was shown to me. I asked Jibril, and he said: This is the Frequented House; seventy thousand angels pray in it every day, and when they leave they never return to it."
4. **"The heaven raised high."**
5. **"The sea filled"**, or set on fire on the Day of Resurrection.

**The answer**: **"Indeed, the punishment of your Lord will occur. Of it there is no preventer."** All these great things testify that the promise is true.

## Verses 9–16: The Day the Heaven Sways
﴿يَوۡمَ تَمُورُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ مَوۡرٗا﴾ ﴿وَتَسِيرُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ سَيۡرٗا﴾ ﴿فَوَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي خَوۡضٖ يَلۡعَبُونَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يُدَعُّونَ إِلَىٰ نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ دَعًّا﴾ ﴿هَٰذِهِ ٱلنَّارُ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُم بِهَا تُكَذِّبُونَ﴾ ﴿أَفَسِحۡرٌ هَٰذَآ أَمۡ أَنتُمۡ لَا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱصۡلَوۡهَا فَٱصۡبِرُوٓاْ أَوۡ لَا تَصۡبِرُواْ سَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ إِنَّمَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 52:9-16)

On the Day the heaven sways and the mountains move, the deniers will be **pushed into the Fire with a violent push**, and told: **"This is the Fire which you used to deny. Is this magic, or do you not see?"** **"Be patient or impatient, it is all the same for you."** Neither patience nor panic will help them then.

## Verses 17–28: The Bliss of the Righteous
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ وَنَعِيمٖ﴾ ﴿فَٰكِهِينَ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ وَوَقَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓـَٔۢا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿مُتَّكِـِٔينَ عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مَّصۡفُوفَةٖۖ وَزَوَّجۡنَٰهُم بِحُورٍ عِينٖ﴾ (Quran 52:17-20)

The righteous are in gardens and bliss, enjoying what their Lord gave them, eating and drinking in satisfaction, reclining on couches lined up, married to fair women with large eyes. Then a great glad tiding for parents:

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱتَّبَعَتۡهُمۡ ذُرِّيَّتُهُم بِإِيمَٰنٍ أَلۡحَقۡنَا بِهِمۡ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ وَمَآ أَلَتۡنَٰهُم مِّنۡ عَمَلِهِم مِّن شَيۡءٖۚ كُلُّ ٱمۡرِيِٕۭ بِمَا كَسَبَ رَهِينࣱ﴾ (Quran 52:21)

**Children joined to their parents**: if parents are in a high level of Paradise and their believing children are in a lower one, Allah raises the children to the parents' level so their eyes are delighted, without reducing anything from the parents' reward. **"Every person, for what he earned, is retained"**: yet each person is still bound by his own deeds.

﴿وَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا كُنَّا قَبۡلُ فِيٓ أَهۡلِنَا مُشۡفِقِينَ﴾ ﴿فَمَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَا وَوَقَىٰنَا عَذَابَ ٱلسَّمُومِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كُنَّا مِن قَبۡلُ نَدۡعُوهُۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡبَرُّ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 52:25-28)

**The secret of their salvation**: they turn to each other and say: **"Indeed, we were previously among our people fearful."** In this world, even surrounded by their families, they feared Allah. **"Indeed, we used to supplicate Him before."** Fear and du'a in this world led to safety in the next. **"Indeed, it is He who is the Beneficent, the Merciful."**

## Verses 29–34: Not a Soothsayer, Not a Madman
﴿فَذَكِّرۡ فَمَآ أَنتَ بِنِعۡمَتِ رَبِّكَ بِكَاهِنٖ وَلَا مَجۡنُونٍ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ شَاعِرࣱ نَّتَرَبَّصُ بِهِۦ رَيۡبَ ٱلۡمَنُونِ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ تَرَبَّصُواْ فَإِنِّي مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُتَرَبِّصِينَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ تَأۡمُرُهُمۡ أَحۡلَٰمُهُم بِهَٰذَآۚ أَمۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ طَاغُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ تَقَوَّلَهُۥۚ بَل لَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿فَلۡيَأۡتُواْ بِحَدِيثٖ مِّثۡلِهِۦٓ إِن كَانُواْ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 52:29-34)

**"So remind, for you are not, by the favour of your Lord, a soothsayer or a madman."** They called him a poet and waited for him to die. **"Say: Wait, for indeed I am with you among the waiters."**

**"Or do their minds command them to this?"** They called him a soothsayer, a madman and a poet, contradictory claims. **"Or are they a transgressing people?"** The real reason was transgression, not reason.

**The challenge**: **"Then let them produce a statement like it, if they should be truthful."**

## Verses 35–43: The Questions That Shook Jubayr
﴿أَمۡ خُلِقُواْ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ شَيۡءٍ أَمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ خَلَقُواْ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۚ بَل لَّا يُوقِنُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ عِندَهُمۡ خَزَآئِنُ رَبِّكَ أَمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡمُصَۜيۡطِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 52:35-37)

**The argument from creation**: either they were created by nothing, which is impossible, or they created themselves, which is even more impossible, so they must have a Creator. Then the questions rise: did they create the heavens and the earth? Do they own the treasures of Allah? Are they in control?

**The series continues**: Do they have a ladder to listen to heaven? Does He have daughters while you have sons? Do you ask them for payment? Do they have the unseen? Do they intend a plot? Do they have a deity other than Allah? **"Exalted is Allah above whatever they associate with Him."**

**The power of this method**: the word "or" (*am*) is repeated about fifteen times. Each question closes a door, until the denier has no exit left. This is why these verses shook Jubayr's heart.

## Verses 44–47: Stubbornness and Respite
﴿وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ كِسۡفٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ سَاقِطٗا يَقُولُواْ سَحَابࣱ مَّرۡكُومࣱ﴾ ﴿فَذَرۡهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ يُلَٰقُواْ يَوۡمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي فِيهِ يُصۡعَقُونَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ لَا يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُمۡ كَيۡدُهُمۡ شَيۡـٔٗا وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ عَذَابٗا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 52:44-47)

Even if they saw a fragment of the sky falling as punishment, they would say: "Clouds piled up." So leave them until they meet their Day, when their plotting will not help them. **"And indeed, for those who have wronged is a punishment before that"**: in this world, or in the grave.

## Verses 48–49: You Are in Our Eyes
﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ فَإِنَّكَ بِأَعۡيُنِنَاۖ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ﴾ ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡهُ وَإِدۡبَٰرَ ٱلنُّجُومِ﴾ (Quran 52:48-49)

**"And be patient for the decision of your Lord, for indeed, you are in Our eyes."** Under Our watch and protection. What a comfort for every believer who is patient under harm. Then the command to glorify: **"when you arise"**, from sleep or from a gathering, **"and in a part of the night"**, **"and at the retreat of the stars"**, the time of Fajr.

## Word Meanings
- **At-Tur**: the Mount.
- **Mastur**: inscribed.
- **Raqq manshur**: parchment spread open.
- **Al-Bayt al-Ma'mur**: the Frequented House.
- **Al-Masjur**: filled, or set on fire.
- **Tamur**: sways.
- **Yuda''un**: pushed violently.
- **Alhaqna**: We joined.
- **Rahin**: retained, in pledge.
- **Mushfiqin**: fearful.
- **Kahin**: soothsayer.
- **Rayb al-manun**: misfortune of death.
- **Musaytirun**: controllers.
- **Bi-a'yunina**: in Our eyes, under Our watch.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Five oaths** joining the earth, the Book, the heaven and the sea.
2. **"Of it there is no preventer"**: a decisive denial.
3. **"Pushed with a violent push"**: a word that sounds like what it describes.
4. **The repeated "or"**: a method of argument that corners the denier.
5. **"You are in Our eyes"**: the plural of majesty conveys complete care.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Idgham without ghunnah**: in *yawma'idhin lil-mukadhdhibin* (verse 11), the tanween merges into the lam.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *min qablu* (verse 26), the noon sakinah is hidden before the qaf.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *wa idbara an-nujum* (verse 49), the dal sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'u* (verse 9).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Adh-Dhariyat before it ended with "woe to those who disbelieve from their Day", and At-Tur begins with "the punishment of your Lord will occur". Surah An-Najm after it begins with an oath by the star when it descends, and At-Tur ends with "the retreat of the stars".

## Children Joined to Their Parents
Verse 21 is one of the greatest glad tidings for parents. Practical lessons:
1. **Raise your children on faith**: the condition is "followed them in faith".
2. **Make du'a for them**: "Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and offspring comfort to our eyes."
3. **Do good deeds**: Allah may raise your children through them.
4. **Do not rely on your parents' deeds**: "every person, for what he earned, is retained."

## Practical Lessons
1. **Recite At-Tur in Maghrib sometimes**, following the Prophet ﷺ.
2. **Fear Allah even among your family.**
3. **Make du'a often**: "we used to supplicate Him before".
4. **Raise your children on faith.**
5. **Use questions in dialogue**, as the Quran does.
6. **Be patient under harm**: you are in Allah's eyes.
7. **Glorify Allah when you rise, at night and at dawn.**

## How to Memorise Surah At-Tur
- **Week 1**: verses 1–28.
- **Week 2**: verses 29–49.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them about the Frequented House** and the angels who pray in it.
- **Ask them the question of the verse**: who created us? Did we create ourselves?
- **Teach them that Allah watches over them**: "you are in Our eyes".

## Fear and Hope in Surah At-Tur
The surah balances fear and hope beautifully. It opens with a threat, "the punishment of your Lord will occur", describes the Fire and the deniers, then moves to Paradise and the joining of families, then reveals the secret of the people of Paradise: they were "fearful" in this world and they used to "supplicate". Fear alone leads to despair; hope alone leads to complacency; the believer combines them. That is why the people of Paradise end their conversation by saying: "Indeed, it is He who is the Beneficent, the Merciful." They admit they were saved by His mercy, not only by their deeds. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "No one's deeds will admit him to Paradise." They said: "Not even you, O Messenger of Allah?" He said: "Not even me, unless Allah covers me with His grace and mercy."

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is at-Tur?
The mountain on which Allah spoke to Musa ﷺ in Sinai.

### What is the Frequented House?
A house in the seventh heaven that seventy thousand angels enter every day, never to return, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does "We will join with them their descendants" mean?
Allah raises believing children to their parents' level in Paradise, even if their deeds were less, without reducing the parents' reward.

### What is the story of Jubayr ibn Mut'im and this surah?
He heard the Prophet ﷺ recite it in Maghrib while still an idolater, and when he reached verse 35 his heart "almost flew", as in Sahih al-Bukhari.

### What does "Or were they created by nothing?" mean?
A question that forces the denier to admit he has a Creator, since he was not created from nothing and did not create himself.

### What does "you are in Our eyes" mean?
You are under Our protection and care, so their plots cannot harm you.

### How many verses are in Surah At-Tur?
49 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

### What does "Be patient or impatient, it is all the same for you" mean?
On the Day of Judgement, neither patience nor panic will change anything for the deniers.

## Conclusion
Surah At-Tur surrounds the heart with questions until it admits: who created me? Who created the heavens and the earth? Who holds the treasures? When the answer is Allah, the heart should fear His punishment, call on Him as the people of Paradise did, and be patient under harm, knowing it is in Allah's eyes.
