## "Those Who Say Our Lord Is Allah, Then Stand Firm": Quran 41:30 Explained

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ تَتَنَزَّلُ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ أَلَّا تَخَافُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَبۡشِرُواْ بِٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ﴾ [Fussilat 41:30]

### Word and deed
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *They said: Our Lord is Allah* | Tawhid in word and belief |
| *Then stood firm* | Living it until death |

### *Istiqamah*
- Reported from Abu Bakr: *They did not associate anything with Allah.* From ʿUmar: *They stood firm on His commands and prohibitions and did not slip away like foxes.*

### When do angels descend?
- Scholars: at death, in the grave, and at resurrection.

### Three glad tidings
- Do not fear what is ahead, do not grieve over what is behind, and rejoice in Paradise.

### A comprehensive hadith
- A man asked for a word in Islam after which he would need no one else. The Prophet ﷺ said: *Say: I believe in Allah, then be steadfast* (narrated by Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Steadfastness** is the greatest honour.
- **Stay firm** even with small deeds.

## Surah Fussilat Meaning: Repel Evil with What Is Better

**Surah Fussilat** (54 ayat), also called **Ḥā Mīm As-Sajdah**, contains one of the most beautiful lessons in human relations.

### A detailed Book
- *A Book whose verses have been detailed, an Arabic Quran for people who know.*

### Testifying limbs
- On that Day, a person's **hearing, sight and skin** will testify against him.
- They will ask their skins: *Why did you testify against us?* They will say: *Allah made us speak.*

### The upright
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ تَتَنَزَّلُ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ أَلَّا تَخَافُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَبۡشِرُواْ بِٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ﴾
| Step | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Say "Our Lord is Allah" | Correct belief |
| Then stay upright | Consistent obedience |
| Reward | Angels bring reassurance and glad tidings |

### Repel with what is better
﴿وَلَا تَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡحَسَنَةُ وَلَا ٱلسَّيِّئَةُۚ ٱدۡفَعۡ بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ فَإِذَا ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَكَ وَبَيۡنَهُۥ عَدَٰوَةࣱ كَأَنَّهُۥ وَلِيٌّ حَمِيمࣱ﴾
- **Respond to harm with kindness**, and an enemy may become a close friend.
- *None is granted it except those who are patient.*

### Lessons
- **Your body is a witness**: use it for good.

## "Who Is Better in Speech Than One Who Calls to Allah?": Quran 41:33 Explained

﴿وَمَنۡ أَحۡسَنُ قَوۡلٗا مِّمَّن دَعَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا وَقَالَ إِنَّنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ [Fussilat 41:33]

### A rhetorical question
- *Who is better in speech?* The answer: **no one**.

### Three qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Calls to Allah | Guides people to Him, not to himself |
| Does righteous deeds | Practises what he calls to |
| Says "I am of the Muslims" | Proud and clear in his identity |

### Who is meant?
- The Prophet ﷺ first, then all who call to Allah. Some early scholars, including ʿAishah as reported, mentioned the **muezzin** who calls to prayer.

### The next verses
- They command repelling evil with what is better (41:34), a sign that the caller needs patience and good character.

### Lessons
- **Your daily words can be daʿwah**: kind advice, sharing knowledge.
- **Live what you say.**

## Quran 41:34-35 Explained: Turning an Enemy into a Close Friend

﴿وَلَا تَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡحَسَنَةُ وَلَا ٱلسَّيِّئَةُۚ ٱدۡفَعۡ بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ فَإِذَا ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَكَ وَبَيۡنَهُۥ عَدَٰوَةࣱ كَأَنَّهُۥ وَلِيٌّ حَمِيمࣱ﴾ [Fussilat 41:34]

### Good and evil are not equal
- The verse begins with a principle: a good deed and a bad deed never have the same effect or weight.

### The method
- *Repel with what is better*: not just ignoring harm, but responding with **something better**.
| They do | You respond |
|---|---|
| Harsh words | Gentle words |
| Ignoring you | Greeting them |
| Cutting ties | Keeping ties |
| Wronging you | Forgiving and praying for them |

### The result
- *The one with whom there was enmity becomes as though a devoted friend.* Hearts change through kindness.

### Not for everyone
﴿وَمَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُواْ وَمَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ذُو حَظٍّ عَظِيمٖ﴾
- This level needs **patience** and is a **great fortune**.

### Limits
- This applies to personal wrongs. It does not mean accepting injustice to others or abandoning one's rights in law.

### Lessons
- **Choose the better response**, especially with family and neighbours.
- **Patience turns enemies into friends.**

## "We Will Show Them Our Signs in the Horizons and in Themselves": Quran 41:53 Explained

﴿سَنُرِيهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا فِي ٱلۡأٓفَاقِ وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَتَبَيَّنَ لَهُمۡ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّۗ أَوَلَمۡ يَكۡفِ بِرَبِّكَ أَنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ﴾ [Fussilat 41:53]

### Horizons and selves
| Place | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The horizons | The regions of the world: sky, earth and what is in them |
| Themselves | The precise creation of the human being |

### Two views
- The victories granted to the Muslims and the defeat of those who denied, showing the truth of the message.
- The signs of creation in the universe and the self that point to the Creator. The verse can carry both.

### "Until it becomes clear"
- The truth of the Quran becomes evident through visible evidence.

### "Is it not enough?"
- Allah's witness over everything is sufficient, even without another sign.

### Lessons
- **Reflect on your own body**: heart, eyes, mind.
- **Let knowledge increase certainty.**

## "There Is Nothing Like Him": Quran 42:11 Explained

﴿فَاطِرُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا وَمِنَ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ أَزۡوَٰجٗا يَذۡرَؤُكُمۡ فِيهِۚ لَيۡسَ كَمِثۡلِهِۦ شَيۡءࣱۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ﴾ [Ash-Shura 42:11]

### The Originator
- *Fatir* means the One who brought the heavens and earth into existence without a previous model.

### Pairs everywhere
- Spouses for humans, pairs for cattle, multiplying generations. Everything created is in **pairs**; the Creator alone is **One**.

### A golden rule
| Part | Principle |
|---|---|
| *Nothing is like Him* | Negating any resemblance to creation |
| *He is All-Hearing, All-Seeing* | Affirming His attributes |

- Scholars of Ahl al-Sunnah take this verse as a principle: affirm what Allah affirmed for Himself, **without likening** Him to creation and **without denying** His attributes.

### Hearing and seeing
- Humans hear and see too, but His hearing and sight are perfect, unlimited and unlike ours.

### Comfort
- He hears your whispered duʿa and sees your hidden effort.

### Lessons
- **Do not imagine Allah** in any created form.
- **Speak to Him knowing He hears.**

## Surah Ash-Shura Meaning: Nothing Is Like Him, and Consultation

**Surah Ash-Shura** (53 ayat) is named after one of the qualities of believers: **consultation**.

### One religion
- Allah ordained the same religion for Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, ʿIsa and Muhammad ﷺ: *establish the religion and do not be divided in it*.

### Nothing is like Him
﴿لَيۡسَ كَمِثۡلِهِۦ شَيۡءࣱۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ﴾ [Ash-Shura: 11]
- **A foundation of belief**: Allah has attributes like hearing and sight, but **nothing resembles Him**.

### Qualities of believers
| Quality | Ayah meaning |
|---|---|
| Trust in their Lord | Rely on Him |
| Avoid major sins | And immoralities |
| Forgive when angry | Self-control |
| Respond to their Lord | And establish prayer |
| Consultation | ﴿وَأَمۡرُهُمۡ شُورَىٰ بَيۡنَهُمۡ﴾ |
| Spend | From what Allah provided |
| Patience and forgiveness | *Indeed, that is of the matters of determination* |

### How Allah speaks
- By **inspiration**, from **behind a veil**, or by sending a **messenger angel**.

### Lessons
- **Consult** your family and colleagues.
- **Forgive when angry**: true strength.

## "Whatever Misfortune Befalls You Is Because of What Your Hands Earned": Quran 42:30 Explained

﴿وَمَآ أَصَٰبَكُم مِّن مُّصِيبَةٖ فَبِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَن كَثِيرٖ﴾ [Ash-Shura 42:30]

### Two parts
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Because of what your hands earned | Many hardships result from our own sins |
| He pardons much | Most sins are forgiven without punishment |

### Self-review, not blame
- When hardship comes, the verse directs you to **look at yourself**: repent, correct, and seek forgiveness.

### What about the righteous?
- Prophets and righteous people are tested too, to raise their ranks, as other verses and hadiths state. So this verse is not a judgement on every suffering person.

### Expiation
- The Prophet ﷺ said no fatigue, illness, worry or even a thorn's prick afflicts a Muslim except that Allah expiates some of his sins by it (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Respond to hardship** with istighfar.
- **Thank Allah** for the much He pardons.

## "The Recompense of an Evil Is an Evil Like It, But Whoever Pardons": Quran 42:40 Explained

﴿وَجَزَـٰٓؤُاْ سَيِّئَةٖ سَيِّئَةࣱ مِّثۡلُهَاۖ فَمَنۡ عَفَا وَأَصۡلَحَ فَأَجۡرُهُۥ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ [Ash-Shura 42:40]

### Three levels
| Level | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Justice | Responding with the like, no more: permitted |
| Pardon with reform | Better, with reward from Allah |
| Injustice | Going beyond the like: forbidden |

### Why call it "evil"?
- Scholars explain it is a matching of words: it harms the one it falls on, though it is just and permitted.

### Pardon with reform
- Praiseworthy pardon brings **reform**. If pardon only increases the wrongdoer's injustice, it is not praiseworthy.

### "His reward is with Allah"
- The reward is left unspecified to show its greatness.

### Lessons
- **Pardon is strength**, not weakness.
- **Never exceed** in retaliation.

## Surah Az-Zukhruf Meaning: Gold Ornaments and True Friendship

**Surah Az-Zukhruf** (89 ayat) takes its name from **gold ornaments**, and shows how little they weigh before Allah.

### The travel du'a
- From this surah comes the du'a when mounting a vehicle: *Subḥānal-ladhī sakhkhara lanā hādhā wa mā kunnā lahū muqrinīn, wa innā ilā rabbinā la-munqalibūn* [Az-Zukhruf: 13–14].

### Worthless gold
- If not for people becoming one community of disbelief, Allah would have given disbelievers **roofs of silver**, stairs and doors, and gold ornaments.
- *All that is only the enjoyment of worldly life, and the Hereafter with your Lord is for the righteous.*

### Stories
| Prophet | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Ibrahim | Declared disavowal of what his people worshipped |
| Musa | Pharaoh boasted of his rivers and mocked Musa |
| ʿIsa | A servant whom Allah favoured and made an example |

### True friendship
﴿ٱلۡأَخِلَّآءُ يَوۡمَئِذِۭ بَعۡضُهُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ عَدُوٌّ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ — *Close friends that Day will be enemies to one another, except the righteous.*
- **Choose friends** whose friendship lasts into the Hereafter.

### Lessons
- **Don't be impressed** by wealth alone.

## Surah Ad-Dukhan Meaning: The Blessed Night and the Smoke

**Surah Ad-Dukhan** (59 ayat) begins with the night the Quran came down.

﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٍۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُنذِرِينَ﴾
- Most commentators said this **blessed night** is Laylat al-Qadr in Ramadan, *in which every wise matter is made distinct*.
- Read [Surah Al-Qadr Meaning](/blog/surah-ash-sharh-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Qadr-Meaning-The-Night-Better-than-a-Thousand-Month).

### The smoke
- *So wait for the Day when the sky will bring a visible smoke covering the people.*
- Ibn Masʿud explained it as the famine that struck Quraysh, when they saw something like smoke from hunger [Al-Bukhari]; other scholars said it is a sign before the Hour.

### Pharaoh's end
- Pharaoh and his people left gardens, springs, crops and noble places.
- *And the heaven and earth did not weep for them.*
- **The meaning**: no good deeds of theirs rose to the sky, and no place of prayer on earth missed them, as some commentators explained.

### Two fates
| The sinful | The righteous |
|---|---|
| The tree of Zaqqum as food | Gardens and springs |
| Boiling water | Silk garments, facing one another |
| | Secure from death after the first death |

### Lessons
- **Would anything weep for you?** Leave good deeds behind.
