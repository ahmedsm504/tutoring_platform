In one single night, the Prophet ﷺ travelled from Al-Masjid Al-Haram in Makkah to Al-Masjid Al-Aqsa in Jerusalem, a journey that took caravans more than a month back then! Then he ascended to the heavens and returned before morning. This miraculous journey is called Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj, and Surah Al-Isra begins by mentioning it: "Glory be to the One who took His servant by night."

Surah Al-Isra also contains some of the most beautiful verses about parents in the whole Quran: Allah commands us to be so kind to our parents that we don't even say "uff", a small sound of annoyance, to them. And it teaches the du'a every child should know: "My Lord, have mercy on them as they raised me when I was small." In this guide, we explain Surah Al-Isra to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Isra

- **Position**: surah 17, at the start of juz 15.
- **Number of verses**: 111.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Other name**: Surah Bani Isra'il, "The Children of Israel".
- **Name**: al-Isra', "The Night Journey".

## Lesson 1: The Night Journey

﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَسۡرَىٰ بِعَبۡدِهِۦ لَيۡلٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِ إِلَى ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡأَقۡصَا ٱلَّذِي بَٰرَكۡنَا حَوۡلَهُۥ لِنُرِيَهُۥ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَآۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 17:1)

### Explaining it to your child
"Glory be to Allah, who took His servant Muhammad ﷺ by night from Al-Masjid Al-Haram in Makkah to Al-Masjid Al-Aqsa in Jerusalem, whose surroundings Allah blessed, to show him some of His amazing signs. Allah is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing."

### What happened on this journey?
"According to authentic hadiths, the Prophet ﷺ rode the Buraq, a white animal whose every step reached as far as its eye could see. In Al-Aqsa, he led all the prophets in prayer. Then he ascended through the heavens, met prophets in each heaven, and Allah gave him the five daily prayers as a gift. That is why prayer is so special: it was given to us in the heavens."

### Activity: the journey map
Draw a simple map: Makkah and the Kaaba, an arrow to Jerusalem and the Dome, then an arrow up to the sky. Say: "All this happened in one night! It's a miracle from Allah."

### Lesson for kids: prayer is a gift
"Allah gave the prayers to the Prophet ﷺ in the heavens, not on earth. When you pray, you're receiving a gift from Allah."

## Lesson 2: Kindness to Parents

﴿۞وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنًاۚ إِمَّا يَبۡلُغَنَّ عِندَكَ ٱلۡكِبَرَ أَحَدُهُمَآ أَوۡ كِلَاهُمَا فَلَا تَقُل لَّهُمَآ أُفّٖ وَلَا تَنۡهَرۡهُمَا وَقُل لَّهُمَا قَوۡلٗا كَرِيمٗا﴾ (Quran 17:23)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah commanded that we worship only Him, and be excellent to our parents. When they grow old, don't even say 'uff' to them, which is just a small sound of annoyance. Don't shout at them, and speak to them with gentle, respectful words."

### Notice how gentle this is
"Allah forbade even 'uff', the tiniest sound of irritation! So what about shouting, slamming doors or rolling your eyes? Our parents deserve the gentlest words."

### Activity: the "uff jar"
Keep a jar at home. Every time your child says "uff" or sighs in annoyance when asked to do something, they put a small paper in the jar. And every time they reply happily, "Yes, Mum!", they take one out. The goal is an empty jar by the end of the week!

## Lesson 3: The Du'a for Parents

﴿وَٱخۡفِضۡ لَهُمَا جَنَاحَ ٱلذُّلِّ مِنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَةِ وَقُل رَّبِّ ٱرۡحَمۡهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 17:24)

### Explaining it to your child
"Lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy, like a bird spreading its wings over its chicks, and say: My Lord, have mercy on them as they raised me when I was small. Your parents looked after you when you were little and weak, and now you pray for them."

### Activity: the daily du'a
Make "Rabbirhamhuma kama rabbayani saghira" part of your child's du'a after every prayer. Tell your child: "This is the best gift you can give Mum and Dad."

## Lesson 4: Giving Without Wasting

﴿وَءَاتِ ذَا ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ حَقَّهُۥ وَٱلۡمِسۡكِينَ وَٱبۡنَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَلَا تُبَذِّرۡ تَبۡذِيرًا﴾ (Quran 17:26)

### Explaining it to your child
"Give your relatives their rights, and the poor, and the traveller in need, but don't waste money. Wasting means spending money on useless things or throwing away what is good."

### Activity: needs and wants
Make two lists with your child: "Things I need" (food, school supplies) and "Things I want" (a new toy). Talk about balance: we enjoy halal things, we give to others, and we don't waste.

## Lesson 5: Don't Follow What You Don't Know

﴿وَلَا تَقۡفُ مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمٌۚ إِنَّ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡبَصَرَ وَٱلۡفُؤَادَ كُلُّ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ كَانَ عَنۡهُ مَسۡـُٔولٗا﴾ (Quran 17:36)

### Explaining it to your child
"Don't follow or say something you don't know for sure. Your hearing, your sight and your heart will all be asked about what they did. So don't say 'my friend did this' unless you know it's true, and don't repeat rumours."

### Lesson for kids
"Before you say something about someone, ask yourself: Did I see it myself? Am I sure? If not, stay quiet."

## Lesson 6: Walk Without Arrogance

﴿وَلَا تَمۡشِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَحًاۖ إِنَّكَ لَن تَخۡرِقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَن تَبۡلُغَ ٱلۡجِبَالَ طُولٗا﴾ (Quran 17:37)

### Explaining it to your child
"Don't walk on the earth arrogantly, showing off. You can't tear the earth apart with your feet, and you'll never be as tall as the mountains! However big we think we are, we are small compared to Allah's creation."

### Activity: the mountain and me
Show your child a picture of a huge mountain and stand them next to a wall. Ask: "How tall are you? How tall is the mountain?" Then say: "That's why we never show off."

## Lesson 7: Say Only the Best Words

﴿وَقُل لِّعِبَادِي يَقُولُواْ ٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ يَنزَغُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ كَانَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ عَدُوّٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾ (Quran 17:53)

### Explaining it to your child
"Tell My servants to say only what is best. Shaytan tries to cause trouble between people through bad words. So when you speak, choose the best word: not just a good word, but the best one."

### Activity: the word upgrade
Practise "upgrading" sentences with your child:
- "Give me that!" → "Could you pass me that, please?"
- "You're annoying!" → "I need some quiet time, please."
- "That's ugly!" → "I like it in a different colour."

## Lesson 8: The Honour of the Children of Adam

﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ كَرَّمۡنَا بَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ وَحَمَلۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَرَزَقۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَفَضَّلۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٖ مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقۡنَا تَفۡضِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 17:70)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah has honoured the children of Adam, meaning all humans. He carries them on land and sea, gives them good food, and favours them over much of His creation. Every human being is honoured, so we respect everyone, whatever their colour or country."

## Lesson 9: The Prayer Times

﴿أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِدُلُوكِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ إِلَىٰ غَسَقِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَقُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِۖ إِنَّ قُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ كَانَ مَشۡهُودٗا﴾ (Quran 17:78)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah tells us to establish prayer from when the sun starts to decline at midday until the darkness of night, which includes Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, and the recitation at dawn, Fajr. The Fajr recitation is witnessed by the angels!"

### Activity: the prayer clock
Draw a big clock with your child and mark the five prayer times with pictures: the sunrise for Fajr, the high sun for Dhuhr, the afternoon sun for Asr, the sunset for Maghrib, and the moon for Isha.

## Let's Read Together: The Quran Is Healing

﴿وَنُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَا هُوَ شِفَآءࣱ وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَلَا يَزِيدُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا خَسَارٗا﴾ (Quran 17:82)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah sends down in the Quran what is healing and mercy for the believers. The Quran heals the heart from sadness, fear and worry. When you feel upset, read or listen to some Quran, and feel how it calms your heart."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Uff Jar"

Laila loved her mother very much, but she had a habit: whenever her mother asked her to do something, like tidy up or set the table, she would say "Uffff!" and roll her eyes. She didn't mean to be rude; it just slipped out.

One evening, her grandmother read Surah Al-Isra with her and stopped at the verse about parents. "Laila, do you know that Allah forbade even 'uff' to our parents?" Laila was shocked. "Even uff, Grandma? It's such a tiny word!" Her grandmother smiled: "If Allah forbade even the tiniest word of annoyance, how precious must our parents be?"

Laila felt embarrassed. She and her grandmother made an "uff jar" together. Every time she said uff, a paper went in. Every time she answered happily, one came out. On the first day, the jar had five papers! On the third day, only two. By the end of the week, it was empty.

On Friday, her mother asked her to help with the dishes. Laila jumped up and said: "Yes, Mum, with pleasure!" Her mother stopped, surprised, then hugged her tightly. That night, Laila added a new du'a after her prayer: "Rabbirhamhuma kama rabbayani saghira."

Ask your child: "What was Laila's habit? How did she change it?"

## What We Learn From Surah Al-Isra

- **The Night Journey** was a miracle from Allah.
- **Prayer** is a gift from the heavens.
- **Be excellent to parents**: not even "uff".
- **Pray for your parents** every day.
- **Give to others** without wasting.
- **Don't say** what you aren't sure about.
- **Walk humbly**, without showing off.
- **Choose the best words** always.
- **Every human being** is honoured.
- **The Quran heals** the heart.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Was the Night Journey with the body or just the soul?
The majority of scholars hold that it was with the body and soul while awake, which is why it was a great miracle and why the Quraysh found it so hard to believe.

### How do I teach kindness to parents without making my child feel guilty?
Focus on love rather than fear. Praise every kind word and action, and model kindness to your own parents in front of your children.

### Is "uff" really forbidden?
The verse uses "uff" to show that even the smallest expression of annoyance is not acceptable towards parents. Anything bigger, like shouting, is even more clearly wrong.
