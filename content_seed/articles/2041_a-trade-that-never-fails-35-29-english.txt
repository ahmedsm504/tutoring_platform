﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَتۡلُونَ كِتَٰبَ ٱللَّهِ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنفَقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ يَرۡجُونَ تِجَٰرَةٗ لَّن تَبُورَ﴾ ﴿لِيُوَفِّيَهُمۡ أُجُورَهُمۡ وَيَزِيدَهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ غَفُورࣱ شَكُورࣱ﴾ [Fatir 35:29-30]

## Three qualities
| Quality | Form |
|---|---|
| Reciting the Book | Present tense: ongoing |
| Establishing prayer | Past tense: a settled habit |
| Spending secretly and openly | Giving in every state |

## A trade that never fails
- *Tabur* means to go unsold or perish. Trading with Allah never loses.

## Full reward and more
- *To pay them their rewards in full*, and *increase them from His bounty*: beyond what they earned.

## *Ghafur, Shakur*
- Forgiving of shortcomings; Appreciative, rewarding a little with much.

## Lessons
- **Make recitation daily**, not seasonal.
- **People of the Quran** combine recitation with prayer and charity.
