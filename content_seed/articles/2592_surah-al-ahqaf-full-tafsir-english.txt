Surah Al-Ahqaf brings together two of the most moving scenes in the Quran. The first is a human life seen from the mother's side: "His mother carried him with hardship and gave birth to him with hardship, and his gestation and weaning are thirty months." When that child reaches forty, he prays a du'a that many Muslims memorise: "My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents." The second scene is a group of jinn who hear the Quran, tell each other "Listen quietly", and go back to their people as callers to Allah. Between them is the story of 'Ad, who saw a cloud and rejoiced, "This is a cloud bringing us rain", when it was the punishment they had demanded.

This guide explains Surah Al-Ahqaf section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Ahqaf
- **Number of verses**: 35.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 46, the first surah of the twenty-sixth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Ahqaf means "the curved sand dunes", the land of 'Ad, from verse 21.
- **The last of the *Hawamim***: the seven surahs beginning with *Ha Mim*.
- **Main themes**: tawhid, the truth of the Quran, kindness to parents, steadfastness, the fate of 'Ad and the faith of the jinn.

## Verses 1–6: The Helplessness of False Gods
﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ ﴿مَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ عَمَّآ أُنذِرُواْ مُعۡرِضُونَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَرُونِي مَاذَا خَلَقُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمۡ لَهُمۡ شِرۡكࣱ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِۖ ٱئۡتُونِي بِكِتَٰبٖ مِّن قَبۡلِ هَٰذَآ أَوۡ أَثَٰرَةٖ مِّنۡ عِلۡمٍ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿وَمَنۡ أَضَلُّ مِمَّن يَدۡعُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَن لَّا يَسۡتَجِيبُ لَهُۥٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وَهُمۡ عَن دُعَآئِهِمۡ غَٰفِلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا حُشِرَ ٱلنَّاسُ كَانُواْ لَهُمۡ أَعۡدَآءٗ وَكَانُواْ بِعِبَادَتِهِمۡ كَٰفِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 46:1-6)

**"Ha Mim. The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Wise."**

**A challenge to idol worship**: **"Show me what they have created of the earth, or have they a partnership in the heavens?"** Bring a scripture or a trace of knowledge to support your claim.

**"And who is more astray than he who invokes besides Allah those who will not respond to him until the Day of Resurrection, and they, of their invocation, are unaware."** And on the Day of Judgement those false gods will become enemies of their worshippers.

## Verses 7–14: Not Something New Among the Messengers
﴿وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِلۡحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ هَٰذَا سِحۡرࣱ مُّبِينٌ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُۖ قُلۡ إِنِ ٱفۡتَرَيۡتُهُۥ فَلَا تَمۡلِكُونَ لِي مِنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡـًٔاۖ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا تُفِيضُونَ فِيهِۚ كَفَىٰ بِهِۦ شَهِيدَۢا بَيۡنِي وَبَيۡنَكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ مَا كُنتُ بِدۡعٗا مِّنَ ٱلرُّسُلِ وَمَآ أَدۡرِي مَا يُفۡعَلُ بِي وَلَا بِكُمۡۖ إِنۡ أَتَّبِعُ إِلَّا مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ إِلَّا نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِن كَانَ مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ وَكَفَرۡتُم بِهِۦ وَشَهِدَ شَاهِدࣱ مِّنۢ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ عَلَىٰ مِثۡلِهِۦ فَـَٔامَنَ وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرۡتُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 46:7-10)

The disbelievers called the Quran obvious magic, then said he invented it. The Prophet ﷺ was told to say: **"I am not something original among the messengers, nor do I know what will be done with me or with you. I only follow what is revealed to me."** Humility and clarity: he claimed no knowledge of the unseen beyond revelation.

**"A witness from the Children of Israel"**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas said that this verse was revealed about 'Abdullah ibn Salam, the Jewish scholar who recognised the truth and accepted Islam.

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ فَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 46:13-14)

**"Indeed, those who have said, 'Our Lord is Allah,' and then remained on a right course, there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve."** Two words that summarise the religion: belief and steadfastness. In Sahih Muslim, Sufyan ibn 'Abdullah asked the Prophet ﷺ: "Tell me something about Islam that I will not need to ask anyone else about." He said: "Say: I believe in Allah, then be steadfast."

## Verses 15–16: Kindness to Parents
﴿وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ إِحۡسَٰنًاۖ حَمَلَتۡهُ أُمُّهُۥ كُرۡهٗا وَوَضَعَتۡهُ كُرۡهٗاۖ وَحَمۡلُهُۥ وَفِصَٰلُهُۥ ثَلَٰثُونَ شَهۡرًاۚ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا بَلَغَ أَشُدَّهُۥ وَبَلَغَ أَرۡبَعِينَ سَنَةٗ قَالَ رَبِّ أَوۡزِعۡنِيٓ أَنۡ أَشۡكُرَ نِعۡمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَيَّ وَأَنۡ أَعۡمَلَ صَٰلِحٗا تَرۡضَىٰهُ وَأَصۡلِحۡ لِي فِي ذُرِّيَّتِيٓۖ إِنِّي تُبۡتُ إِلَيۡكَ وَإِنِّي مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 46:15)

**"And We have enjoined upon man, to his parents, good treatment."** Then the mother's sacrifice is described:
- **"His mother carried him with hardship"**: months of difficulty.
- **"And gave birth to him with hardship"**: the pain of delivery.
- **"His gestation and weaning are thirty months."** Scholars noted that combining this with "weaning in two years" in Surah Luqman shows that the minimum pregnancy is six months.

**"Until, when he reaches maturity and reaches forty years, he says: My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and to work righteousness of which You will approve, and make righteous for me my offspring. Indeed, I have repented to You, and indeed, I am of the Muslims."**

**The du'a at forty**: forty is the age of full maturity. The du'a covers gratitude for oneself and one's parents, righteous deeds, righteous children, repentance and submission. It looks back to the parents and forward to the children.

﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ نَتَقَبَّلُ عَنۡهُمۡ أَحۡسَنَ مَا عَمِلُواْ وَنَتَجَاوَزُ عَن سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ فِيٓ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۖ وَعۡدَ ٱلصِّدۡقِ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يُوعَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 46:16)

**"Those are the ones from whom We will accept the best of what they did and overlook their misdeeds."**

## Verses 17–20: The Disobedient Son
﴿وَٱلَّذِي قَالَ لِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ أُفّٖ لَّكُمَآ أَتَعِدَانِنِيٓ أَنۡ أُخۡرَجَ وَقَدۡ خَلَتِ ٱلۡقُرُونُ مِن قَبۡلِي وَهُمَا يَسۡتَغِيثَانِ ٱللَّهَ وَيۡلَكَ ءَامِنۡ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّ فَيَقُولُ مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّآ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ حَقَّ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ فِيٓ أُمَمٖ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ خَٰسِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 46:17-18)

**The opposite picture**: a son who says to his parents: **"Uff to you! Do you promise me that I will be brought forth, when generations before me have already passed?"** His parents, seeking Allah's help, plead: **"Woe to you! Believe! Indeed, the promise of Allah is truth."** He says: **"This is nothing but legends of the former people."**

**A historical note**: in Sahih al-Bukhari, Marwan claimed that this verse was about 'Abdur-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr, and 'A'ishah denied it, saying Allah revealed nothing about them in the Quran except her innocence. The verse describes a type of person, not a specific Companion.

﴿وَلِكُلّٖ دَرَجَٰتࣱ مِّمَّا عَمِلُواْۖ وَلِيُوَفِّيَهُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَيَوۡمَ يُعۡرَضُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ أَذۡهَبۡتُمۡ طَيِّبَٰتِكُمۡ فِي حَيَاتِكُمُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱسۡتَمۡتَعۡتُم بِهَا فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُجۡزَوۡنَ عَذَابَ ٱلۡهُونِ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَبِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَفۡسُقُونَ﴾ (Quran 46:19-20)

**"You exhausted your pleasures during your worldly life and enjoyed them."** A warning against spending all one's good things in this world without gratitude.

## Verses 21–28: 'Ad at the Sand Dunes
﴿۞وَٱذۡكُرۡ أَخَا عَادٍ إِذۡ أَنذَرَ قَوۡمَهُۥ بِٱلۡأَحۡقَافِ وَقَدۡ خَلَتِ ٱلنُّذُرُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦٓ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾ ﴿قَالُوٓاْ أَجِئۡتَنَا لِتَأۡفِكَنَا عَنۡ ءَالِهَتِنَا فَأۡتِنَا بِمَا تَعِدُنَآ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡعِلۡمُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَأُبَلِّغُكُم مَّآ أُرۡسِلۡتُ بِهِۦ وَلَٰكِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰكُمۡ قَوۡمٗا تَجۡهَلُونَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡهُ عَارِضٗا مُّسۡتَقۡبِلَ أَوۡدِيَتِهِمۡ قَالُواْ هَٰذَا عَارِضࣱ مُّمۡطِرُنَاۚ بَلۡ هُوَ مَا ٱسۡتَعۡجَلۡتُم بِهِۦۖ رِيحࣱ فِيهَا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿تُدَمِّرُ كُلَّ شَيۡءِۭ بِأَمۡرِ رَبِّهَا فَأَصۡبَحُواْ لَا يُرَىٰٓ إِلَّا مَسَٰكِنُهُمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 46:21-25)

**"And mention the brother of 'Ad"**, the Prophet Hud, **"when he warned his people in the curved sand dunes"**. He told them: worship none but Allah. They demanded the punishment.

**The cloud**: **"When they saw it as a cloud approaching their valleys, they said: This is a cloud bringing us rain. Rather, it is that for which you were impatient: a wind, within it a painful punishment, destroying everything by command of its Lord."** By morning, nothing could be seen except their dwellings.

**The Prophet's ﷺ fear**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'A'ishah said that when the Prophet ﷺ saw clouds or wind, his face showed concern. She asked him why, when people rejoiced at clouds hoping for rain. He said: "O 'A'ishah, what assures me that there is no punishment in it? A people were punished with wind, and a people saw the punishment and said: *This is a cloud bringing us rain*."

﴿وَلَقَدۡ مَكَّنَّـٰهُمۡ فِيمَآ إِن مَّكَّنَّـٰكُمۡ فِيهِ وَجَعَلۡنَا لَهُمۡ سَمۡعٗا وَأَبۡصَٰرٗا وَأَفۡـِٔدَةٗ فَمَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُمۡ سَمۡعُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَفۡـِٔدَتُهُم مِّن شَيۡءٍ إِذۡ كَانُواْ يَجۡحَدُونَ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مَا حَوۡلَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ وَصَرَّفۡنَا ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾ ﴿فَلَوۡلَا نَصَرَهُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ قُرۡبَانًا ءَالِهَةَۢۖ بَلۡ ضَلُّواْ عَنۡهُمۡۚ وَذَٰلِكَ إِفۡكُهُمۡ وَمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡتَرُونَ﴾ (Quran 46:26-28)

**'Ad were given hearing, sight and hearts**, more than Quraysh, but these did not help them because they rejected Allah's signs.

## Verses 29–32: The Jinn Who Listened
﴿وَإِذۡ صَرَفۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ نَفَرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ فَلَمَّا حَضَرُوهُ قَالُوٓاْ أَنصِتُواْۖ فَلَمَّا قُضِيَ وَلَّوۡاْ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِم مُّنذِرِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ يَٰقَوۡمَنَآ إِنَّا سَمِعۡنَا كِتَٰبًا أُنزِلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مُوسَىٰ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِلَىٰ طَرِيقٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ﴾ ﴿يَٰقَوۡمَنَآ أَجِيبُواْ دَاعِيَ ٱللَّهِ وَءَامِنُواْ بِهِۦ يَغۡفِرۡ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمۡ وَيُجِرۡكُم مِّنۡ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَن لَّا يُجِبۡ دَاعِيَ ٱللَّهِ فَلَيۡسَ بِمُعۡجِزٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَيۡسَ لَهُۥ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءُۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ﴾ (Quran 46:29-32)

**"And when We directed to you a few of the jinn, listening to the Quran. When they attended it, they said: Listen quietly. And when it was concluded, they went back to their people as warners."** They told their people: **"We have heard a Book revealed after Musa, confirming what was before it, which guides to the truth and to a straight path. O our people, respond to the caller of Allah and believe in him."**

**Lessons from the jinn**: they listened in silence, believed immediately, and became callers. Silence when the Quran is recited is the first step towards being moved by it.

## Verses 33–35: Be Patient Like the Resolute Messengers
﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَمۡ يَعۡيَ بِخَلۡقِهِنَّ بِقَٰدِرٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يُحۡـِۧيَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰۚ بَلَىٰٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿وَيَوۡمَ يُعۡرَضُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ أَلَيۡسَ هَٰذَا بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّنَاۚ قَالَ فَذُوقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ كَمَا صَبَرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡعَزۡمِ مِنَ ٱلرُّسُلِ وَلَا تَسۡتَعۡجِل لَّهُمۡۚ كَأَنَّهُمۡ يَوۡمَ يَرَوۡنَ مَا يُوعَدُونَ لَمۡ يَلۡبَثُوٓاْ إِلَّا سَاعَةٗ مِّن نَّهَارِۭۚ بَلَٰغࣱۚ فَهَلۡ يُهۡلَكُ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 46:33-35)

**"Do they not see that Allah, who created the heavens and earth and did not fail in their creation, is able to give life to the dead?"**

**"So be patient, as were those of determination among the messengers."** The *ulu al-'azm* are commonly listed as Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, 'Isa and Muhammad ﷺ. **"On the Day they see that which they are promised, it will be as if they had not remained except an hour of a day."**

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Ahqaf**: curved sand dunes.
- **Atharah**: a trace, remnant.
- **Bid'**: something new, unprecedented.
- **Istaqamu**: remained steadfast.
- **Kurh**: hardship.
- **Fisal**: weaning.
- **Ashudd**: full maturity.
- **Awzi'ni**: enable me, inspire me.
- **Uff**: an expression of annoyance.
- **'Arid**: a cloud spreading across the sky.
- **Ansitu**: listen quietly.
- **Ulu al-'azm**: those of determination.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Carried him with hardship and gave birth with hardship"**: the repetition makes the mother's suffering felt.
2. **The du'a at forty**: looking back to parents and forward to children.
3. **The good son and the disobedient son**: two contrasting portraits.
4. **"A cloud bringing us rain"**: joy turned to destruction.
5. **"Listen quietly"**: the jinn's manners with the Quran.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Ha Mim*, the meem is stretched six counts.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *kurhan wa wada'athu* (verse 15), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna alladhina qalu* (verse 13), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *awliya'u* (verse 32).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Jathiyah before it ended with praise of the Lord of the heavens and the earth, and Al-Ahqaf begins by challenging false gods to show what they created. Surah Muhammad after it begins with deeds wasted, connecting to the 'Ad whose hearing, sight and hearts did not help them.

## Living the Verse of Parents
1. **Remember your mother's hardship** in pregnancy, birth and nursing.
2. **Speak gently**: never say "uff".
3. **Serve them practically**: time, money and care.
4. **Pray the du'a of forty**, at any age.
5. **Pray for your children** in the same du'a.
6. **If your parents have died**, pray for them, give charity for them and keep their friendships.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Say "Our Lord is Allah" and be steadfast.**
2. **Honour your parents.**
3. **Memorise the du'a of verse 15.**
4. **Do not exhaust your good things in this world without gratitude.**
5. **Fear Allah's punishment even in what looks like blessing.**
6. **Listen quietly to the Quran.**
7. **Be patient like the resolute messengers.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Ahqaf
- **Week 1**: verses 1–14.
- **Week 2**: verses 15–20.
- **Week 3**: verses 21–28.
- **Week 4**: verses 29–35.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them about the months their mother carried them**, and teach them gratitude.
- **Teach them to listen quietly** when the Quran is recited, like the jinn.
- **Tell them about the people of 'Ad** and the cloud.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does al-Ahqaf mean?
Curved sand dunes, the land where the people of 'Ad lived.

### What is the du'a in verse 15?
"My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and to work righteousness of which You will approve, and make righteous for me my offspring. Indeed, I have repented to You, and indeed, I am of the Muslims."

### What does "thirty months" in verse 15 indicate?
The combined period of pregnancy and weaning; scholars deduced from it that the minimum pregnancy is six months.

### Who is the witness from the Children of Israel?
'Abdullah ibn Salam, as Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What happened to 'Ad?
They saw a cloud and thought it brought rain, but it was a wind of punishment that destroyed them.

### What did the jinn do when they heard the Quran?
They said "Listen quietly", believed, and returned to their people as warners.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Ahqaf?
35 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Ahqaf calls you to say "Our Lord is Allah" and stand firm, to honour the mother who carried you with hardship, and to pray at every stage of life for gratitude, righteous deeds and righteous children. It warns through 'Ad that a blessing can carry punishment for the ungrateful, and it shows through the jinn how a heart should meet the Quran: listening quietly, believing and calling others.
