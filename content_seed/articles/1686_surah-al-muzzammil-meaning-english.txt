**Surah Al-Muzzammil** (20 ayat) is the surah of **night prayer and tarteel**.

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُزَّمِّلُ﴾ ﴿قُمِ ٱلَّيۡلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا﴾ ﴿نِّصۡفَهُۥٓ أَوِ ٱنقُصۡ مِنۡهُ قَلِيلًا﴾ ﴿أَوۡ زِدۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا﴾
- *O you wrapped in garments, stand at night except a little, half of it or a little less, or a little more, and recite the Quran with tarteel.*

## Why the night?
| Reason | From the surah |
|---|---|
| Preparation | *We will cast upon you a heavy word* |
| Focus | The night is stronger in impact and clearer in speech |
| Balance | In the day you have long work |

## Patience
- *Be patient over what they say, and leave them gracefully.*

## The ayah of ease
﴿فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ﴾ [Al-Muzzammil: 20]
- Revealed later, it recognised that people would be **ill, travelling for work, or striving in Allah's cause**, and made night prayer easier.

## Lessons
- **A little night prayer** is better than none.
- **Tarteel**: recite slowly, clearly, with reflection.
