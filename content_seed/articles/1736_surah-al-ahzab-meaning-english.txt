**Surah Al-Ahzab** (73 ayat) was revealed around the **Battle of the Confederates** (the Trench), when armies surrounded Madinah.

## The best example
﴿لَّقَدۡ كَانَ لَكُمۡ فِي رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ أُسۡوَةٌ حَسَنَةࣱ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَ وَذَكَرَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا﴾
- Revealed in a time of fear: follow him in patience, courage and trust.

## Believers in crisis
| Group | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Believers | *This is what Allah and His Messenger promised us*; it increased them in faith |
| Hypocrites | *Allah and His Messenger promised us only delusion* |

## Men and women equally
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ وَٱلۡمُسۡلِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ﴾
- Ten qualities, each mentioned for **men and women**, with the same reward.

## Salawat
﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّۚ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ صَلُّواْ عَلَيۡهِ وَسَلِّمُواْ تَسۡلِيمًا﴾
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Whoever sends one blessing upon me, Allah sends ten upon him» [Muslim].

## The trust
- Allah offered the trust to the heavens, earth and mountains; they declined, and **man carried it**.

## Lessons
- **Follow the Prophet ﷺ**, especially in hard times.
