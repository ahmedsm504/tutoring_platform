If **makharij** tell you *where* a letter comes from, **ṣifāt** tell you *how* it sounds. Two letters can share a makhraj but differ in their qualities, like س and ص.

## Qualities with opposites
| Quality | Opposite | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Hams (whisper) | Jahr (voiced) | Breath flows with the letter, or is held |
| Shiddah (strong) | Rakhāwah (soft), with Tawassuṭ (middle) between them | Sound is blocked, flows, or is in between |
| Istiʿlāʾ (raising) | Istifāl (lowering) | Back of the tongue rises, or stays low |
| Iṭbāq (covering) | Infitāḥ (opening) | Tongue covers the roof of the mouth, or not |
| Idhlāq (fluency) | Iṣmāt (restraint) | Ease of pronunciation |

## The whispered letters
- Gathered in **فَحَثَّهُ شَخۡصٌ سَكَتَ**.
- **Tip:** let a little breath out with ت and ك when they have a sukūn, as in ﴿أَنۡعَمۡتَ﴾.

## The strong letters
- Gathered in **أَجِدُ قَطٍ بَكَتۡ**.

## Qualities without opposites
| Quality | Letters | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ṣafīr (whistling) | ص ز س | ﴿ٱلصِّرَٰطَ﴾ |
| Qalqalah (echo) | ق ط ب ج د | ﴿ٱلۡفَلَقِ﴾ |
| Līn (softness) | و ي with sukūn after fatḥah | ﴿قُرَيۡشٍ﴾ |
| Inḥirāf (deviation) | ل ر | |
| Takrīr (repetition) | ر | Avoid rolling it too much |
| Tafashshī (spreading) | ش | ﴿ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ﴾ |
| Istiṭālah (extension) | ض | ﴿وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ﴾ |

## Why it matters
- Without ṣifāt, س and ص sound the same, and so do ذ and ظ.
- A wrong letter can change the meaning of a word.
