Surah Al-Mu'minun opens with a declaration of success: "Certainly will the believers have succeeded." Then it immediately tells you who they are, in seven qualities: humble in their prayer, turning away from vain talk, active in zakah, guarding their chastity, keeping their trusts and promises, and carefully maintaining their prayers. "Those are the inheritors who will inherit al-Firdaws." The surah then traces man's creation in stages, tells the stories of messengers rejected by their peoples, describes believers who give while their hearts tremble that it might not be accepted, shows the dying man begging to go back, and ends with a question every person must answer: "Then did you think that We created you uselessly and that to Us you would not be returned?"

This guide explains Surah Al-Mu'minun section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Mu'minun
- **Number of verses**: 118.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 23, in the eighteenth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Mu'minun means "the believers", from the first verse.
- **Main themes**: the qualities of successful believers, creation and resurrection, the messengers, and the purpose of life.

## Verses 1–11: The Successful Believers
﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي صَلَاتِهِمۡ خَٰشِعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَنِ ٱللَّغۡوِ مُعۡرِضُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِلزَّكَوٰةِ فَٰعِلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِفُرُوجِهِمۡ حَٰفِظُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا عَلَىٰٓ أَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ أَوۡ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُمۡ فَإِنَّهُمۡ غَيۡرُ مَلُومِينَ﴾ ﴿فَمَنِ ٱبۡتَغَىٰ وَرَآءَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡعَادُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِأَمَٰنَٰتِهِمۡ وَعَهۡدِهِمۡ رَٰعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَوَٰتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَرِثُونَ ٱلۡفِرۡدَوۡسَ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:1-11)

**"Certainly will the believers have succeeded"**: *qad aflaha*, success that is certain. Their qualities:
1. **"Those who are during their prayer humbly submissive"** (*khashi'un*): heart present, limbs still, eyes lowered. Khushu' is the soul of prayer.
2. **"Those who turn away from ill speech"** (*laghw*): every word and action without benefit.
3. **"Those who are observant of zakah"**: purifying their wealth and themselves.
4. **"Those who guard their private parts"**, except with their spouses.
5. **"Those who are to their trusts and their promises attentive."**
6. **"Those who carefully maintain their prayers"**: their times, conditions and pillars.

**Notice**: the list begins with prayer (khushu') and ends with prayer (maintenance). Prayer frames the believer's whole life.

**"Those are the inheritors who will inherit al-Firdaws; they will abide therein eternally."** In Sahih al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ said: "When you ask Allah, ask Him for al-Firdaws, for it is the middle of Paradise and the highest part of Paradise, and above it is the Throne of the Most Merciful."

## Verses 12–22: The Stages of Creation
﴿وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن سُلَٰلَةٖ مِّن طِينٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ نُطۡفَةٗ فِي قَرَارٖ مَّكِينٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلنُّطۡفَةَ عَلَقَةٗ فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡعَلَقَةَ مُضۡغَةٗ فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡمُضۡغَةَ عِظَٰمٗا فَكَسَوۡنَا ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ لَحۡمٗا ثُمَّ أَنشَأۡنَٰهُ خَلۡقًا ءَاخَرَۚ فَتَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ أَحۡسَنُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 23:12-14)

**The stages**: an extract of clay, then a drop in a firm lodging, then a clinging clot ('*alaqah*), then a lump (*mudghah*), then bones, then the bones clothed with flesh, **"then We developed him into another creation. So blessed is Allah, the best of creators."** The final stage, "another creation", is when the soul is breathed in and the human being becomes a person.

﴿ثُمَّ إِنَّكُم بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ لَمَيِّتُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ تُبۡعَثُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:15-16)

**"Then indeed, after that you are to die. Then indeed you, on the Day of Resurrection, will be resurrected."**

﴿وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمۡ سَبۡعَ طَرَآئِقَ وَمَا كُنَّا عَنِ ٱلۡخَلۡقِ غَٰفِلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءَۢ بِقَدَرٖ فَأَسۡكَنَّـٰهُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰ ذَهَابِۭ بِهِۦ لَقَٰدِرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَنشَأۡنَا لَكُم بِهِۦ جَنَّـٰتٖ مِّن نَّخِيلٖ وَأَعۡنَٰبٖ لَّكُمۡ فِيهَا فَوَٰكِهُ كَثِيرَةࣱ وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَشَجَرَةٗ تَخۡرُجُ مِن طُورِ سَيۡنَآءَ تَنۢبُتُ بِٱلدُّهۡنِ وَصِبۡغٖ لِّلۡأٓكِلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ لَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ لَعِبۡرَةٗۖ نُّسۡقِيكُم مِّمَّا فِي بُطُونِهَا وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَنَٰفِعُ كَثِيرَةࣱ وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَعَلَيۡهَا وَعَلَى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ تُحۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:17-22)

Seven heavens above, rain in measure, gardens of palms and grapes, the olive tree of Sinai, and livestock that provide milk and transport.

## Verses 23–50: The Messengers
**Nuh** called his people to worship Allah; they said he was only a man seeking superiority. He built the ark under Allah's eyes and was saved. Another messenger was sent to a later people, who said: **"This is not but a man like yourselves; he eats of that from which you eat."** They were seized by the blast. Then messengers in succession, **"Every time there came to a nation its messenger, they denied him"**. Then Musa and Harun to Pharaoh, and **'Isa and his mother as a sign**, sheltered on high ground with flowing water.

## Verses 51–56: Eat From the Good Things
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلرُّسُلُ كُلُواْ مِنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَٱعۡمَلُواْ صَٰلِحًاۖ إِنِّي بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 23:51)

**"O messengers, eat from the good things and work righteousness."** In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah is good and accepts only what is good. Allah has commanded the believers with what He commanded the messengers," and recited this verse. Then he mentioned a man who travels far, dishevelled and dusty, raising his hands to the sky, "O Lord, O Lord", while his food, drink and clothing are unlawful: "How can he be answered?" Halal provision is a condition for accepted du'a.

﴿وَإِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أُمَّتُكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱتَّقُونِ﴾ ﴿فَتَقَطَّعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَهُم بَيۡنَهُمۡ زُبُرٗاۖ كُلُّ حِزۡبِۭ بِمَا لَدَيۡهِمۡ فَرِحُونَ﴾ ﴿فَذَرۡهُمۡ فِي غَمۡرَتِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ حِينٍ﴾ ﴿أَيَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّمَا نُمِدُّهُم بِهِۦ مِن مَّالٖ وَبَنِينَ﴾ ﴿نُسَارِعُ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِۚ بَل لَّا يَشۡعُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:52-56)

**"This religion of yours is one religion."** But people divided into sects, **each faction rejoicing in what it has**. **"Do they think that what We extend to them of wealth and children is that We hasten for them good things? Rather, they do not perceive."** Wealth is not proof of Allah's pleasure.

## Verses 57–62: Hearts That Fear While Giving
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ هُم مِّنۡ خَشۡيَةِ رَبِّهِم مُّشۡفِقُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِـَٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِرَبِّهِمۡ لَا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡتُونَ مَآ ءَاتَواْ وَّقُلُوبُهُمۡ وَجِلَةٌ أَنَّهُمۡ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ رَٰجِعُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَهُمۡ لَهَا سَٰبِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:57-61)

**"Indeed, they who are apprehensive from fear of their Lord, and who believe in the signs of their Lord, and who do not associate anything with their Lord, and who give what they give while their hearts are fearful because they will be returning to their Lord: it is those who hasten to good deeds."**

At-Tirmidhi reported that 'A'ishah asked the Prophet ﷺ about this verse: "Are they those who drink wine and steal?" He said: "No, daughter of as-Siddiq. They are those who fast, pray and give charity, and they fear that it will not be accepted from them. Those are the ones who hasten to good deeds." Al-Albani graded it authentic.

﴿وَلَا نُكَلِّفُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ وَلَدَيۡنَا كِتَٰبࣱ يَنطِقُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:62)

**"And We charge no soul except according to its capacity."**

## Verses 63–98: Heedlessness and Arguments
The deniers are in heedlessness. When the luxurious among them are seized by punishment, they cry out. They are asked: Who owns the earth? Who is the Lord of the seven heavens? In whose hand is the dominion of all things? **They will say: Allah.** **"Say: Then how are you deluded?"**

﴿وَقُل رَّبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنۡ هَمَزَٰتِ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ﴾ ﴿وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ رَبِّ أَن يَحۡضُرُونِ﴾ (Quran 23:97-98)

**"And say: My Lord, I seek refuge in You from the incitements of the devils, and I seek refuge in You, my Lord, lest they be present with me."**

## Verses 99–111: The Barrier and the Scales
﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَ أَحَدَهُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ قَالَ رَبِّ ٱرۡجِعُونِ﴾ ﴿لَعَلِّيٓ أَعۡمَلُ صَٰلِحٗا فِيمَا تَرَكۡتُۚ كَلَّآۚ إِنَّهَا كَلِمَةٌ هُوَ قَآئِلُهَاۖ وَمِن وَرَآئِهِم بَرۡزَخٌ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:99-100)

**"Until, when death comes to one of them, he says: My Lord, send me back, that I might do righteousness in that which I left behind. No! It is only a word he is saying. And behind them is a barrier (*barzakh*) until the Day they are resurrected."** The *barzakh* is the life of the grave between death and resurrection.

﴿فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَلَآ أَنسَابَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ وَلَا يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾ ﴿فَمَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ ﴿وَمَنۡ خَفَّتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ فِي جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدُونَ﴾ ﴿تَلۡفَحُ وُجُوهَهُمُ ٱلنَّارُ وَهُمۡ فِيهَا كَٰلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:101-104)

**"So when the Horn is blown, no relationship will there be among them that Day, nor will they ask about one another."** **"Those whose scales are heavy, it is they who are the successful. But those whose scales are light, those are the ones who have lost their souls."**

﴿قَالَ ٱخۡسَـُٔواْ فِيهَا وَلَا تُكَلِّمُونِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فَرِيقࣱ مِّنۡ عِبَادِي يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَآ ءَامَنَّا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱتَّخَذۡتُمُوهُمۡ سِخۡرِيًّا حَتَّىٰٓ أَنسَوۡكُمۡ ذِكۡرِي وَكُنتُم مِّنۡهُمۡ تَضۡحَكُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنِّي جَزَيۡتُهُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ بِمَا صَبَرُوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡفَآئِزُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:108-111)

**"Remain despised therein and do not speak to Me."** They used to mock the believers who said "Our Lord, we have believed, so forgive us and have mercy upon us". **"Indeed, I have rewarded them this Day for their patience; they are the attainers of success."**

## Verses 112–118: Not Created in Vain
﴿قَٰلَ كَمۡ لَبِثۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ عَدَدَ سِنِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ لَبِثۡنَا يَوۡمًا أَوۡ بَعۡضَ يَوۡمٖ فَسۡـَٔلِ ٱلۡعَآدِّينَ﴾ ﴿قَٰلَ إِن لَّبِثۡتُمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗاۖ لَّوۡ أَنَّكُمۡ كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 23:112-114)

**"How long did you remain on earth in number of years?"** **"A day or part of a day."** The whole of life will seem that short.

﴿أَفَحَسِبۡتُمۡ أَنَّمَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ عَبَثٗا وَأَنَّكُمۡ إِلَيۡنَا لَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ ﴿فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ﴾ (Quran 23:115-116)

**"Then did you think that We created you uselessly and that to Us you would not be returned?"** Life has a purpose: worship and accountability. **"So exalted is Allah, the Sovereign, the Truth."**

﴿وَمَن يَدۡعُ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ لَا بُرۡهَٰنَ لَهُۥ بِهِۦ فَإِنَّمَا حِسَابُهُۥ عِندَ رَبِّهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقُل رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ وَٱرۡحَمۡ وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 23:117-118)

The surah that began with **"the believers have succeeded"** ends with **"the disbelievers will not succeed"**, and with a du'a: **"My Lord, forgive and have mercy, and You are the best of the merciful."**

## Word Meanings
- **Aflaha**: succeeded.
- **Khashi'un**: humbly submissive.
- **Al-Laghw**: vain speech and deeds.
- **Ra'un**: attentive, keeping.
- **Al-Firdaws**: the highest garden.
- **Sulalah**: extract.
- **'Alaqah**: clinging clot.
- **Mudghah**: lump of flesh.
- **At-Tayyibat**: the good, lawful things.
- **Wajilah**: fearful.
- **Hamazat**: incitements.
- **Barzakh**: barrier.
- **Al-Mawazin**: the scales.
- **'Abathan**: uselessly, without purpose.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Certainly will the believers have succeeded"** at the start, **"the disbelievers will not succeed"** at the end.
2. **Prayer framing the qualities**: khushu' first, maintenance last.
3. **"Another creation"**: the mystery of the soul in two words.
4. **"Hearts fearful while giving"**: humility after good deeds.
5. **"A day or part of a day"**: a lifetime compressed.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *qad aflaha* (verse 1), the dal sakinah bounces lightly; and in *nutfah* (verse 13), the ta' sakinah.
- **Izhar**: in *khalqan akhar* (verse 14), the tanween before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ja'a ahadahum* (verse 99).
- **Ghunnah**: in *innahu* (verse 117), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Hajj before it ended with "bow and prostrate and worship your Lord and do good, that you may succeed", and Al-Mu'minun begins with "Certainly will the believers have succeeded". Surah An-Nur after it builds on "those who guard their private parts" with detailed laws of chastity.

## Building Khushu' in Prayer
1. **Prepare**: wudu' with presence, arriving early.
2. **Understand what you recite.**
3. **Look at the place of prostration.**
4. **Slow down** in every position.
5. **Remember that this may be your last prayer.**
6. **Make du'a in prostration.**
7. **Remove distractions**: phone, noise, worries.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Aim for the seven qualities.**
2. **Ask Allah for al-Firdaws.**
3. **Reflect on your creation in stages.**
4. **Eat halal** so your du'a is answered.
5. **Do good and fear it may not be accepted.**
6. **Seek refuge from the devils.**
7. **Remember the barzakh and the scales.**
8. **Live with purpose.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Mu'minun
- **Week 1**: verses 1–22.
- **Week 2**: verses 23–56.
- **Week 3**: verses 57–92.
- **Week 4**: verses 93–118.

## Teaching Children
- **Memorise verses 1–11 together** as a checklist.
- **Explain the stages of creation** in simple words.
- **Teach them the closing du'a**: *Rabbighfir warham wa anta khayr ar-rahimin*.

Humility in prayer, avoiding vain talk, giving zakah, guarding chastity, keeping trusts and promises, and maintaining their prayers.

Humility and presence of heart in prayer, with calm limbs.

### What are the stages of creation in verses 12–14?
Clay, a drop, a clinging clot, a lump, bones, flesh, and then "another creation" when the soul is breathed in.

### What is the barzakh?
The barrier between death and resurrection, the life of the grave.

### Who are "those who give while their hearts are fearful"?
Those who pray, fast and give charity yet fear it may not be accepted, as the Prophet ﷺ explained to 'A'ishah.

### What does "Did you think We created you uselessly?" mean?
Life has a purpose, and everyone will return to Allah to be held to account.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Mu'minun?
118 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Mu'minun begins and ends with success: who has it and who does not. It shows you the believers' qualities, framed by prayer, and reminds you that you came from clay and will stand before the scales. You were not created in vain. Pray with humility, give with a trembling heart, eat what is good, and say: My Lord, forgive and have mercy, and You are the best of the merciful.
