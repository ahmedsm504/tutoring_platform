﴿مَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡعِزَّةَ فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ جَمِيعًاۚ إِلَيۡهِ يَصۡعَدُ ٱلۡكَلِمُ ٱلطَّيِّبُ وَٱلۡعَمَلُ ٱلصَّـٰلِحُ يَرۡفَعُهُۥۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡكُرُونَ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ شَدِيدࣱۖ وَمَكۡرُ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُوَ يَبُورُ﴾ [Fatir 35:10]

## Seeking honour
- *Whoever desires honour, all honour belongs to Allah*: seek it through obedience to Him.

## Words that ascend
- Scholars explain the good word as dhikr, Quran, truthful speech and good advice.

## Deeds that raise
| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| Good words | Ascend to Allah |
| Righteous deeds | Raise and support them |

- Words without deeds are weak; deeds give them strength.

## Evil plots
- *The plotting of those, it will perish*: schemes against truth end in failure.

## Lessons
- **Seek honour** in obedience, not status.
- **Back your words** with action.
