﴿أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَتَكُونَ لَهُمۡ قُلُوبࣱ يَعۡقِلُونَ بِهَآ أَوۡ ءَاذَانࣱ يَسۡمَعُونَ بِهَاۖ فَإِنَّهَا لَا تَعۡمَى ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَلَٰكِن تَعۡمَى ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلَّتِي فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾ [Al-Hajj 22:46]

## Travel with purpose
- Seeing ruins of past nations should lead to **reflection**, not just sightseeing.

## Hearts that reason
- The verse links reasoning to the **heart**: true understanding includes conscience and faith.

## Real blindness
| Blind eyes | Blind heart |
|---|---|
| Cannot see colours | Cannot see truth |
| Not blameworthy | Dangerous |

## Signs of a blind heart
- Feeling nothing at the Quran, repeating sins without regret, and seeing reminders without learning.

## Cure
- Repentance, remembrance, the Quran, and good company.

## Lessons
- **Ask for a seeing heart.**
- **Reflect** on what you see and hear.
