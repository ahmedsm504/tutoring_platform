Have you ever wondered why the sun feels hot and bright while the moon only glows softly? In Surah Yunus, Allah tells us that He made the sun a diya', a bright light that shines by itself with heat, and the moon a nur, a gentle light. And He gave the moon phases so that we can count the years and keep track of time. Thousands of years before telescopes, the Quran described the difference between the sun's light and the moon's light!

Surah Yunus is named after Prophet Yunus, peace be upon him, and it tells us something amazing about his people: they are the only town in the Quran whose belief benefited them when they saw punishment approaching, because they all turned back to Allah together. The surah also teaches children that the Quran is healing for the heart, and that the friends of Allah have no fear and no sadness. In this guide, we explain Surah Yunus to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Yunus

- **Position**: surah 10, in juz 11.
- **Number of verses**: 109.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: after Prophet Yunus, also called Dhun-Nun, "the man of the whale".

## Lesson 1: The Sun's Light and the Moon's Glow

﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ ضِيَآءٗ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ نُورٗا وَقَدَّرَهُۥ مَنَازِلَ لِتَعۡلَمُواْ عَدَدَ ٱلسِّنِينَ وَٱلۡحِسَابَۚ مَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ ذَٰلِكَ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ يُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:5)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah made the sun a bright, shining light, and the moon a soft glow. He set phases for the moon so that we can know how to count the years and do our calculations. Allah didn't create any of this without purpose. He explains His signs for people who know."

### A fun science fact
"Scientists discovered that the sun makes its own light and heat, like a huge fire, while the moon has no light of its own: it reflects the sun's light, like a mirror. The Quran used two different words for them: diya' for the sun and nur for the moon."

### Activity: torch and mirror
In a dark room, shine a torch onto a small mirror and point the reflection at the wall. Say: "The torch is like the sun: it makes light. The mirror is like the moon: it reflects the light. Allah described this in the Quran!"

### Activity: the Islamic calendar
Show your child the Islamic months: Muharram, Safar, Rabi' al-Awwal... Ramadan, Shawwal, Dhul-Hijjah. Explain that each month starts with the new crescent moon. That's how we know when Ramadan starts, just as the verse says: to know the number of years.

## Lesson 2: The Quran Is Healing

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءࣱ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 10:57)

### Explaining it to your child
"O people, there has come to you advice from your Lord, healing for what is in the hearts, and guidance and mercy for the believers. The Quran is like medicine for the heart: when you feel sad, scared or worried, reading the Quran makes your heart feel better."

### Activity: the calm corner
Set up a "calm corner" at home with a cushion, a mushaf or a device with Quran recitation. When your child feels upset, they can sit there for a few minutes and listen or read. Afterwards, ask them how they feel.

## Lesson 3: Be Happy With Allah's Grace

﴿قُلۡ بِفَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَبِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَفۡرَحُواْ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:58)

### Explaining it to your child
"Say: In Allah's grace and mercy, let them rejoice. That is better than all the money and things people collect. The best thing to be happy about is that Allah guided us to Islam and gave us the Quran. That's worth more than any toy or game."

### Lesson for kids
"When you memorize a new surah, celebrate! Allah tells us to be happy with His grace. Memorizing the Quran is a real reason to be happy."

## Lesson 4: The Friends of Allah

﴿أَلَآ إِنَّ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:62)

### Explaining it to your child
"The awliya', the friends of Allah, will have no fear and no sadness. Who are they? The next verse tells us: those who believe and are mindful of Allah. Anyone can be a friend of Allah by believing and obeying Him, even a child!"

### Activity: friend of Allah checklist
Make a simple daily checklist with your child: "I prayed", "I told the truth", "I helped someone", "I read some Quran". Tell them: "These are the things that make you a friend of Allah."

## Lesson 5: Musa and Pharaoh

"The surah also tells part of the story of Musa and Pharaoh. Pharaoh's magicians tried to defeat Musa, but Allah made the truth win. In the end, Pharaoh chased Musa and the Children of Israel into the sea, and when he was drowning, he said: I believe! But it was too late. His time had run out."

### Lesson for kids: don't leave it too late
"Pharaoh waited until the very last moment to believe, and it was too late. We don't wait to do good. We pray now, say sorry now, and fix our mistakes now."

## Lesson 6: The People of Yunus

﴿فَلَوۡلَا كَانَتۡ قَرۡيَةٌ ءَامَنَتۡ فَنَفَعَهَآ إِيمَٰنُهَآ إِلَّا قَوۡمَ يُونُسَ لَمَّآ ءَامَنُواْ كَشَفۡنَا عَنۡهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡخِزۡيِ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمَتَّعۡنَٰهُمۡ إِلَىٰ حِينٖ﴾ (Quran 10:98)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah sent Yunus to his people, but they refused to believe. Yunus left them angry. Then his people saw signs that punishment was coming. Instead of being stubborn, they all turned back to Allah together: men, women and children, crying and asking for forgiveness. So Allah removed the punishment from them and let them enjoy life for a while. They are the only town in the Quran whose belief helped them at that moment, because they believed sincerely before it was too late."

### Lesson for kids: Allah accepts sincere repentance
"Yunus's people turned back to Allah together, and Allah forgave them. It's never too late to say sorry to Allah, as long as we're still alive and sincere. Allah loves those who turn back to Him."

### Activity: the family istighfar
Choose a moment with the whole family to say "Astaghfirullah wa atubu ilayh" together a few times, and tell your child the story of Yunus's people who turned back to Allah together.

## Lesson 7: Yunus in the Whale

"In other surahs, Allah tells us how Yunus boarded a ship, was thrown into the sea and swallowed by a whale. In the darkness, he called: La ilaha illa anta, subhanaka, inni kuntu minaz-zalimin. Allah saved him, and later sent him back to his people, who had believed."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said that no Muslim calls with the du'a of Yunus for anything except that Allah answers him (at-Tirmidhi; graded authentic by al-Albani).

### Activity: the whale du'a
Teach your child the du'a of Yunus and draw a whale with them, writing the du'a inside it. They can say it whenever they feel worried.

## Lesson 8: Allah Knows Everything

"The surah reminds us that nothing in the heavens or the earth, not even the weight of a tiny speck, is hidden from Allah. Whatever we do, Allah is a witness."

### Lesson for kids
"When nobody is watching, Allah is. That's why we do the right thing even when we're alone."

## Lesson 9: No One Can Bring Harm or Good Except Allah

"The surah teaches that if Allah touches you with harm, no one can remove it except Him, and if He wants good for you, no one can stop His grace. So we ask only Allah for help."

### Activity: who can help?
Ask your child: "If you lose something precious, who can help you find it?" They might say Mum or Dad. Then ask: "And who makes Mum and Dad able to help?" Allah. So we ask Allah first.

## Lesson 10: Patience Until Allah Judges

"The surah ends with Allah telling the Prophet ﷺ: follow what is revealed to you and be patient until Allah judges, and He is the best of judges."

### Lesson for kids
"Patience is the key: patience in memorizing, patience with a hard lesson, patience with a sibling who annoys you. Allah is the best judge, and He rewards the patient."

## Let's Read Together: Happiness With Allah's Grace

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءࣱ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ بِفَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَبِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَفۡرَحُواْ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:57-58)

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Last-Minute Apology"

Bilal had a fight with his best friend Sami at school. Sami had accidentally knocked over Bilal's juice, and Bilal shouted at him and said hurtful things. For three days they didn't speak. Bilal kept thinking: "I'll say sorry later."

On the fourth day, the teacher announced that Sami's family was moving to another city, and that Friday would be his last day at school. Bilal's heart sank. Friday was tomorrow!

That evening, his mother read Surah Yunus with him and told him the story of the people of Yunus: how they turned back to Allah before it was too late, and how Pharaoh waited until the very last moment, when it was too late. "Some chances don't wait for us," she said.

The next morning, Bilal went straight to Sami before class. "Sami, I'm really sorry for what I said. You didn't mean to spill the juice. I was wrong." Sami smiled with relief. "I'm sorry too. I missed you." They spent the whole day together, swapped phone numbers, and promised to keep in touch.

That night, Bilal told his mother: "I'm so glad I didn't leave it too late." His mother hugged him. "Like the people of Yunus, you turned back in time."

Ask your child: "Why did Bilal almost miss his chance? What did he learn from the people of Yunus?"

## What We Learn From Surah Yunus

- **The sun shines** and the moon reflects, by Allah's design.
- **The moon's phases** help us count time.
- **The Quran heals** the heart.
- **Be happy** with Allah's grace and guidance.
- **Anyone can be a friend of Allah** through faith and obedience.
- **Don't leave good** until it's too late.
- **Sincere repentance** is accepted.
- **Allah sees everything**, even when we're alone.
- **Be patient**: Allah is the best judge.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Is the story of Yunus and the whale in this surah?
Surah Yunus mentions his people and how they believed; the whale story itself is told in Surahs Al-Anbiya, As-Saffat and Al-Qalam. Together they form the full picture.

### How do I explain Pharaoh's late belief to a child?
"Pharaoh had many chances to believe, but he waited until he was drowning. Allah accepts repentance while we still have time, so we don't wait."

### Can children understand the sun and moon science?
Yes, with simple experiments like the torch and mirror. It also helps them see the Quran and science as friends.
