## "Our Lord, We Have Wronged Ourselves": The Duʿa of Adam in Quran 7:23 Explained

﴿قَالَا رَبَّنَا ظَلَمۡنَآ أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمۡ تَغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَتَرۡحَمۡنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ﴾ [Al-Araf 7:23]

### The first repentance
- Adam and his wife ate from the forbidden tree after Satan deceived them. They did not blame Satan or each other; they said **we have wronged ourselves**.

### The parts of the duʿa
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Our Lord* | Calling on Him with lordship and care |
| *We have wronged ourselves* | Admitting the fault |
| *If You do not forgive us* | Need for forgiveness |
| *And have mercy on us* | Need for mercy |
| *We will be among the losers* | Awareness of the danger |

### "Words from his Lord"
- In Al-Baqarah: *Adam received words from his Lord, and He accepted his repentance.* Many scholars explain these words as this duʿa.

### Adam versus Iblis
| Adam | Iblis |
|---|---|
| Admitted his mistake | Justified his refusal |
| Asked forgiveness | Asked for respite to mislead |
| Was chosen and guided | Was expelled |

### Lessons
- **Admit without excuses.**
- **Use this duʿa after every mistake.**

## Surah Al-Araf Meaning: Adam's Repentance and the People of the Heights

**Surah Al-Araf** (The Heights, 206 ayat) is among the longest Makkan surahs.

### Adam and Iblis
- Iblis refused to prostrate out of arrogance, then promised to come at people from every direction.
- Adam and his wife ate from the tree, then said:
﴿قَالَا رَبَّنَا ظَلَمۡنَآ أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمۡ تَغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَتَرۡحَمۡنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ﴾
- The difference between Adam and Iblis: **one repented, the other justified himself**.

### The dress of piety
- Allah gave clothing to cover and adorn, *but the garment of piety, that is best*.

### The people of the Heights
- Between Paradise and Hell is a barrier, and on its heights are people who know everyone by their marks. Many scholars explain them as those whose good and bad deeds were equal, hoping to enter Paradise.

### Stories of the prophets
| Prophet | People |
|---|---|
| Nuh, Hud, Salih, Lut, Shuʿayb | Each called: *O my people, worship Allah* |
| Musa | The longest account: Pharaoh, the plagues, the calf, and the seventy chosen men |

### The Sabbath-breakers
- A town by the sea tested with fish appearing on their Sabbath. Some broke the rule, some warned them, and some stayed silent.

### The covenant
- Allah took from the children of Adam a testimony: *Am I not your Lord?* They said: *Yes.*

### Good character
﴿خُذِ ٱلۡعَفۡوَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡعُرۡفِ وَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنِ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ﴾

### Lessons
- **Admit mistakes** like Adam, never justify them like Iblis.
- **Forgive, enjoin good, and ignore ignorance.**

## "Take Your Adornment at Every Place of Prayer, and Eat and Drink Without Excess": Quran 7:31 Explained

﴿۞يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ خُذُواْ زِينَتَكُمۡ عِندَ كُلِّ مَسۡجِدٖ وَكُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ وَلَا تُسۡرِفُوٓاْۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ﴾ [Al-Araf 7:31]

### The occasion
- Some people in pre-Islamic times performed tawaf without clothes, and this verse was revealed (narrated by Muslim from Ibn ʿAbbas).

### Adornment for prayer
- The minimum is covering what must be covered; the best is clean, good clothing: etiquette when standing before Allah.

### Eat, drink, do not waste
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Eat and drink | Good things are permitted |
| Do not waste | No excess in amount or in the unlawful |

### A Prophetic measure
- *No human fills a vessel worse than his stomach; a few bites to keep his back straight are enough* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### The next verse
- *Say: Who has forbidden the adornment of Allah which He produced for His servants, and the good things of provision?* (7:32).

### Lessons
- **Wear your best** for prayer and Jumuʿah.
- **Eat moderately.**

## "Call Upon Your Lord Humbly and Privately": Quran 7:55 Explained

﴿ٱدۡعُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ تَضَرُّعٗا وَخُفۡيَةًۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُعۡتَدِينَ﴾ [Al-Araf 7:55]

### Two manners of duʿa
| Manner | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Tadarruʿ* | Humility, neediness and earnestness |
| *Khufyah* | Privately, in a low voice |

### Why privately?
- It is closer to sincerity and farther from showing off. Zakariya *called his Lord with a hidden call* (Maryam 19:3).
- The Prophet ﷺ told people raising their voices in dhikr: *You are not calling one who is deaf or absent* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Transgression in duʿa
- Scholars give examples: asking for sin, cutting ties, asking for what is impossible by Allah's law, or excessive detail and shouting.

### The next verse
- *Do not cause corruption on earth after its reformation, and call on Him in fear and hope* (7:56): duʿa is joined with right conduct.

### Lessons
- **Lower your voice** in duʿa.
- **Ask with humility**, not demands.

## "Do Not Cause Corruption on Earth After Its Reformation": Quran 7:56 Explained

﴿وَلَا تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بَعۡدَ إِصۡلَٰحِهَا وَٱدۡعُوهُ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعًاۚ إِنَّ رَحۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ قَرِيبࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ [Al-Araf 7:56]

### After its reformation
- Allah set the earth in order with messengers, laws and a balanced creation. **Corruption** is any act that breaks this order.

### Forms of corruption
| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| Belief | Shirk and spreading falsehood |
| Society | Injustice, bribery, cutting family ties |
| Morals | Spreading immorality |
| Environment | Destroying crops, polluting water, needless waste |

### Fear and hope
- *Call on Him in fear and hope*: fear that stops you from sin, and hope that stops you from despair. Scholars describe them as **the two wings** of the believer.

### Mercy is near
- *Allah's mercy is near to the doers of good.* The closer you come to excellence (*ihsan*) in worship and in dealing with people, the closer His mercy is to you.

### Lessons
- **Protect what Allah set right**: in your home and your world.
- **Balance fear and hope in duʿa.**

## "Had the People of the Towns Believed, We Would Have Opened Blessings": Quran 7:96 Explained

﴿وَلَوۡ أَنَّ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡقُرَىٰٓ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱتَّقَوۡاْ لَفَتَحۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم بَرَكَٰتٖ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَٰكِن كَذَّبُواْ فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾ [Al-Araf 7:96]

### The context
- It follows the stories of Nuh, Hud, Salih, Lut and Shuʿayb and what befell their peoples.

### Two keys
| Key | Result |
|---|---|
| Faith | Blessings opened |
| Taqwa | From the sky and the earth |

### What is barakah?
- From the sky: rain and good sent down. From the earth: plants, fruits and resources.
- Barakah means abundant, lasting, beneficial good, even if the amount is small.

### "We would have opened"
- *Opening* suggests closed doors swung wide with good.

### Lessons
- **Seek barakah** in your home through faith and taqwa.
- **Sins remove blessings**, as they did from earlier nations.

## "To Allah Belong the Most Beautiful Names, So Call on Him by Them": Quran 7:180 Explained

﴿وَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ فَٱدۡعُوهُ بِهَاۖ وَذَرُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ يُلۡحِدُونَ فِيٓ أَسۡمَـٰٓئِهِۦۚ سَيُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ [Al-Araf 7:180]

### *Al-Husna*
- The feminine of *ahsan*: names that reach the height of beauty and perfection. *To Allah* placed first shows they are His alone in perfection.

### Call on Him by them
| Need | Name |
|---|---|
| Forgiveness | Ya Ghafur |
| Provision | Ya Razzaq |
| Healing | Ya Shafi |
| Mercy | Ya Rahman, Ya Rahim |

### Distortion (*ilhad*)
- Deviating from the truth in His names: naming idols with derivatives of His names, denying their meanings, or likening them to creation.

### The ninety-nine names
- *Allah has ninety-nine names, one hundred less one; whoever enumerates them will enter Paradise* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).
- Enumerating includes memorising, understanding and living by them.

### Lessons
- **Call on Allah** with the name that fits your need.
- **Learn one name** each week with its meaning.

## "Hold to Forgiveness, Enjoin Good, Turn from the Ignorant": Quran 7:199 Explained

﴿خُذِ ٱلۡعَفۡوَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡعُرۡفِ وَأَعۡرِضۡ عَنِ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ﴾ [Al-Araf 7:199]

### Three commands
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Take al-ʿafw* | Accept what comes easily from people's character and do not demand perfection; also, forgive |
| *Command al-ʿurf* | Call to what is known to be good |
| *Turn from the ignorant* | Do not answer foolishness with foolishness |

### A complete code of character
- Scholars said this short verse gathers good manners: easy dealing, guiding others, and self-control.

### ʿUmar and the verse
- Al-Bukhari narrates that a man spoke harshly to ʿUmar ibn al-Khattab, who became angry. His companion al-Hurr ibn Qays recited this verse, and ʿUmar **stopped immediately**. He was known to halt at the Book of Allah.

### The next verse
- *And if an evil suggestion comes to you from Satan, seek refuge in Allah.* Anger is often where Satan enters.

### Applying it
- With family: overlook small faults.
- At work: give advice kindly and clearly.
- Online: do not reply to every provocation.

### Lessons
- **Expect less, forgive more.**
- **Silence is sometimes the strongest answer.**

## "When a Suggestion from Satan Touches the Pious, They Remember": Quran 7:201 Explained

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ إِذَا مَسَّهُمۡ طَـٰٓئِفࣱ مِّنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ تَذَكَّرُواْ فَإِذَا هُم مُّبۡصِرُونَ﴾ [Al-Araf 7:201]

### *Taʾif*
- A whisper that circles the heart: anger, desire, or an evil thought. *Touches* suggests a light touch that does not take hold.

### "They remember"
- They remember Allah, His punishment and reward, so they seek refuge and return.

### "Then they see"
- *Fa idha* conveys suddenness: insight returns at once, as if a veil were lifted.

### The context
- Before: *If a whisper from Satan provokes you, seek refuge in Allah* (7:200).
- After: *Their brothers draw them deeper into error and do not stop* (7:202): for those without taqwa, devils increase their error.

### Lessons
- **Seek refuge** at the first bad thought.
- **Dhikr restores clarity.**

## "When the Quran Is Recited, Listen to It and Pay Attention": Quran 7:204 Explained

﴿وَإِذَا قُرِئَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ فَٱسۡتَمِعُواْ لَهُۥ وَأَنصِتُواْ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ﴾ [Al-Araf 7:204]

### Two commands
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Istamiʿu* | Direct your hearing and heart to the recitation |
| *Ansitu* | Be silent |

### The scholars' views
- Many said it was revealed about prayer when the imam recites aloud, and includes the Friday sermon because of its Quran. The general wording teaches etiquette everywhere.

### Mercy
- *That you may receive mercy*: attentive listening brings mercy.

### Practical etiquette
- At home: do not make the Quran background noise for chatter.
- In the car: if you cannot attend, lower it or pause until you can.

### Lessons
- **Listen** as you would to a great speech.
- **Teach children** calm when the Quran is played.

## "When Allah Is Mentioned, Their Hearts Tremble": Quran 8:2 Explained

﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَجِلَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَإِذَا تُلِيَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ زَادَتۡهُمۡ إِيمَٰنٗا وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ﴾ [Al-Anfal 8:2]

### "Only" the believers
- *Innama* restricts: the **true** believers are those described here.

### Five qualities
| Quality | Where |
|---|---|
| Hearts tremble at His mention | 8:2 |
| Faith increases with the verses | 8:2 |
| Rely on their Lord | 8:2 |
| Establish prayer | 8:3 |
| Spend from what We provided | 8:3 |

- Their reward (8:4): *They are the true believers; for them are ranks with their Lord, forgiveness and noble provision.*

### Faith increases
- The verse shows that faith **grows**. Scholars of Ahl al-Sunnah use it as evidence that faith increases with obedience and decreases with sin.

### Trembling and peace
- In Ar-Rad 13:28 hearts find **rest** in remembrance; here they **tremble**. Both are true: awe at His greatness, and peace in His closeness.

### Lessons
- **Test your heart** when you hear the Quran.
- **Feed faith** with recitation and reflection.

## Surah Al-Anfal Meaning: Badr and the Qualities of True Believers

**Surah Al-Anfal** (The Spoils, 75 ayat) was revealed about the Battle of Badr.

### Who are the true believers?
﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَجِلَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَإِذَا تُلِيَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ زَادَتۡهُمۡ إِيمَٰنٗا وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ﴾
- Then: they establish prayer and spend from what Allah has given them.

### The Battle of Badr
| Moment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The believers wanted the caravan | Allah wanted the truth established |
| The Prophet ﷺ prayed for help | Allah sent angels in succession |
| Drowsiness came over the believers | A sense of security before battle |
| Rain fell | Purification and firm footing |
| A handful of dust thrown | *You did not throw when you threw, but Allah threw* |

### Unity and steadfastness
- *Obey Allah and His Messenger and do not dispute, lest you lose courage and your strength departs, and be patient.*

### Allah's protection of His Prophet
- The surah recalls the plot in Makkah to imprison, kill or expel the Prophet ﷺ, and Allah's better planning.

### Lessons
- **Victory is from Allah**, not numbers.
- **Disputes destroy strength**; unity preserves it.

## "Respond to Allah and the Messenger When He Calls You to What Gives You Life": Quran 8:24 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱسۡتَجِيبُواْ لِلَّهِ وَلِلرَّسُولِ إِذَا دَعَاكُمۡ لِمَا يُحۡيِيكُمۡۖ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَحُولُ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡمَرۡءِ وَقَلۡبِهِۦ وَأَنَّهُۥٓ إِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ﴾ [Al-Anfal 8:24]

### "What gives you life"
- True life is the life of the heart through faith, the Quran and good deeds. Scholars mentioned the Quran, Islam, or striving that brings honour.

### Abu Saʿid ibn al-Muʿalla
- The Prophet ﷺ called him while he was praying, and he did not answer until he finished. The Prophet ﷺ said: *Did Allah not say: Respond to Allah and the Messenger when he calls you?* Then he taught him the greatest surah in the Quran, al-Fatihah (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### "Allah comes between a man and his heart"
- Allah can turn hearts, so respond **now** before the chance passes.
- The Prophet ﷺ often prayed: *O Turner of hearts, keep my heart firm on Your religion* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### Lessons
- **Do not delay** obedience.
- **Revive your heart** with daily Quran.

## "Allah Would Not Punish Them While You Are Among Them": Quran 8:33 Explained

﴿وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيُعَذِّبَهُمۡ وَأَنتَ فِيهِمۡۚ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ مُعَذِّبَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ يَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ﴾ [Al-Anfal 8:33]

### The context
- The polytheists had challenged: *If this is the truth, rain stones on us.* This verse explains why the punishment did not come.

### Two safeguards
| Safeguard | Status |
|---|---|
| The Prophet ﷺ among them | Ended with his death |
| Seeking forgiveness | Remains until the Day of Judgment |

### Ibn ʿAbbas's words
- It is reported from Ibn ʿAbbas that there were two safeguards: the Prophet ﷺ and istighfar; the Prophet ﷺ departed and istighfar remains (reported by al-Tabari).

### Continuous istighfar
- *Yastaghfirun* is a present-tense verb: **ongoing** seeking of forgiveness.

### Lessons
- **Make istighfar a daily habit** for yourself and your family.
- **In times of trial**, increase it.

## "Do Not Dispute, or You Will Lose Courage and Your Strength Will Depart": Quran 8:46 Explained

﴿وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَا تَنَٰزَعُواْ فَتَفۡشَلُواْ وَتَذۡهَبَ رِيحُكُمۡۖ وَٱصۡبِرُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ [Al-Anfal 8:46]

### The context
- Among the etiquette of battle, after commands to stand firm and remember Allah much.

### A chain of causes
| Cause | Result |
|---|---|
| Disputes | Failure and cowardice |
| Failure | Loss of strength |

### "Your wind"
- Scholars explained it as strength, victory and dignity, as Arabs say someone's wind blew when his power rose.

### The cure
- **Obedience** unites the reference point; **patience** prevents the anger that feeds disputes.

### In our lives
- At home: quarrels break families. At work: disagreement without manners wastes effort.

### Lessons
- **Resolve differences** by returning to truth, not desire.
- **Be patient** with your brothers.

## "If They Incline to Peace, Then Incline to It": Quran 8:61 Explained

﴿۞وَإِن جَنَحُواْ لِلسَّلۡمِ فَٱجۡنَحۡ لَهَا وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ [Al-Anfal 8:61]

### *Janaha*
- To incline toward something. If the enemy inclines to peace and stopping war, **incline to it** as well.

### Peace has value
- Accepting genuine peace preserves lives and is the default when sincerely offered.

### Fear of deception
- The next verse: *If they intend to deceive you, then Allah is sufficient for you* (8:62). Fear of betrayal should not prevent peace; Allah suffices.

### Reliance with caution
- Tawakkul combines accepting peace with alertness.

### Lessons
- **Accept reconciliation** in disputes when sincerely offered.
- **Trust Allah** rather than letting fear block good.
