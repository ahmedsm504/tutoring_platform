Surah Al-Mursalat has a powerful, driving rhythm, and a single verse repeated ten times: "Woe that Day to the deniers." It sounds like an alarm bell after every scene. It begins with oaths that what you are promised will certainly happen, shows the stars extinguished and the mountains blown away, brings proofs from your own creation and the earth beneath you, describes the Fire throwing sparks as big as palaces, contrasts it with the shade and springs of the righteous, and ends with a question: "Then in what statement after this will they believe?" It was revealed to the Prophet ﷺ in a cave at Mina, and it was the last surah Umm al-Fadl heard him recite.

This guide explains Surah Al-Mursalat section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Mursalat
- **Number of verses**: 50.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 77, the last surah of the twenty-ninth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Mursalat means "those sent forth", from the first verse.
- **Main themes**: the certainty of the Day of Judgement, proofs from creation, and the repeated warning to the deniers.

## The Story of Its Revelation
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud said: "While we were with the Prophet ﷺ in a cave at Mina, *Wal-mursalat* was revealed to him. He was reciting it, and I was receiving it fresh from his mouth, when a snake leapt out at us. The Prophet ﷺ said: Kill it. We hurried towards it, but it escaped. The Prophet ﷺ said: It was protected from your harm as you were protected from its harm."

**The last recitation Umm al-Fadl heard**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas reported that his mother, Umm al-Fadl, heard him recite *Wal-mursalati 'urfa* and said: "My son, by reciting this surah you have reminded me that it was the last thing I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ recite, in the Maghrib prayer."

## Verses 1–7: The Oaths
﴿وَٱلۡمُرۡسَلَٰتِ عُرۡفٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡعَٰصِفَٰتِ عَصۡفٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱلنَّـٰشِرَٰتِ نَشۡرٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡفَٰرِقَٰتِ فَرۡقٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡمُلۡقِيَٰتِ ذِكۡرًا﴾ ﴿عُذۡرًا أَوۡ نُذۡرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَوَٰقِعࣱ﴾ (Quran 77:1-7)

The commentators differed over who is meant:
1. **"Those sent forth in succession"**: the winds following one another like a horse's mane, or angels sent with what is good.
2. **"Those blowing violently"**: strong winds.
3. **"Those spreading"**: winds spreading the clouds, or angels spreading the scrolls.
4. **"Those separating"**: angels who bring what separates truth from falsehood.
5. **"Those delivering a reminder"**: angels who bring revelation to the messengers.

**"As justification or warning"**: revelation comes to remove people's excuses and to warn them.

**The answer to the oaths**: **"Indeed, what you are promised is to occur."**

## Verses 8–15: The Day of Judgement
﴿فَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ طُمِسَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ فُرِجَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡجِبَالُ نُسِفَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلرُّسُلُ أُقِّتَتۡ﴾ ﴿لِأَيِّ يَوۡمٍ أُجِّلَتۡ﴾ ﴿لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ﴾ ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ (Quran 77:8-15)

**The events**: the stars are extinguished, the heaven is opened, the mountains are blown away, and **the messengers' time is set**, gathered to testify about their nations. **"For what Day was it postponed? For the Day of Judgement"** (*Yawm al-Fasl*), the Day of separation and decision. Then the first **"Woe that Day to the deniers."**

## Verses 16–19: Nations Destroyed Before
﴿أَلَمۡ نُهۡلِكِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ نُتۡبِعُهُمُ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿كَذَٰلِكَ نَفۡعَلُ بِٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ (Quran 77:16-19)

**"Did We not destroy the former peoples? Then We will follow them with the later ones."** This is Allah's way with criminals: the people of Nuh, 'Ad and Thamud were destroyed, and whoever denies today will follow them.

## Verses 20–24: Your Own Creation
﴿أَلَمۡ نَخۡلُقكُّم مِّن مَّآءٖ مَّهِينٖ﴾ ﴿فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ فِي قَرَارٖ مَّكِينٍ﴾ ﴿إِلَىٰ قَدَرٖ مَّعۡلُومٖ﴾ ﴿فَقَدَرۡنَا فَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡقَٰدِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ (Quran 77:20-24)

**"Did We not create you from a despised fluid, and placed it in a firm lodging, for a known extent? And We determined, and excellent are We to determine."** A weak drop, protected in the womb for a fixed term, shaped with precision. The One who did this can bring you back.

## Verses 25–28: The Earth as a Container
﴿أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَلِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ كِفَاتًا﴾ ﴿أَحۡيَآءٗ وَأَمۡوَٰتٗا﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ شَٰمِخَٰتٖ وَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰكُم مَّآءٗ فُرَاتٗا﴾ ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ (Quran 77:25-28)

**"Have We not made the earth a container, for the living and the dead?"** *Kifat* is a container that gathers and holds. The earth carries the living on its back and holds the dead in its belly. **"And placed therein lofty, firmly set mountains, and given you to drink sweet water?"**

## Verses 29–40: The Fire
﴿ٱنطَلِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَا كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱنطَلِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ ظِلّٖ ذِي ثَلَٰثِ شُعَبٖ﴾ ﴿لَّا ظَلِيلٖ وَلَا يُغۡنِي مِنَ ٱللَّهَبِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهَا تَرۡمِي بِشَرَرٖ كَٱلۡقَصۡرِ﴾ ﴿كَأَنَّهُۥ جِمَٰلَتࣱ صُفۡرࣱ﴾ ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ (Quran 77:29-34)

**"Proceed to that which you used to deny."** **"Proceed to a shadow having three columns"**: a shadow of the smoke of Hell, split into three branches, **"no cool shade and of no use against the flame"**.

**"Indeed, it throws sparks as huge as palaces, as if they were yellowish camels."** A single spark the size of a palace; the sparks like a herd of yellowish camels in colour, number and succession. If the sparks are like this, what is the Fire itself?

﴿هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ لَا يَنطِقُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَا يُؤۡذَنُ لَهُمۡ فَيَعۡتَذِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ ﴿هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِۖ جَمَعۡنَٰكُمۡ وَٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ ﴿فَإِن كَانَ لَكُمۡ كَيۡدࣱ فَكِيدُونِ﴾ ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ (Quran 77:35-40)

**"This is a Day they will not speak, nor will it be permitted for them to make excuses."** **"This is the Day of Judgement; We will have assembled you and the former peoples. So if you have a plan, then plan against Me"**: a mocking challenge, for they have no plan.

## Verses 41–45: The Righteous
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي ظِلَٰلٖ وَعُيُونٖ﴾ ﴿وَفَوَٰكِهَ مِمَّا يَشۡتَهُونَ﴾ ﴿كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓـَٔۢا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 77:41-44)

**The contrast**: instead of the smoky shadow, the righteous are **among shades and springs and fruits from whatever they desire**. They are told: **"Eat and drink in satisfaction for what you used to do."** **"Indeed, We thus reward the doers of good"**: a general rule: excellence is rewarded with excellence.

## Verses 46–50: The Closing
﴿كُلُواْ وَتَمَتَّعُواْ قَلِيلًا إِنَّكُم مُّجۡرِمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱرۡكَعُواْ لَا يَرۡكَعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ ﴿فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثِۭ بَعۡدَهُۥ يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 77:46-50)

**"Eat and enjoy yourselves a little; indeed, you are criminals."** **"And when it is said to them: Bow, they do not bow."** Refusing to bow in prayer is named as one of the causes of their ruin.

**"Then in what statement after this will they believe?"** If they do not believe in the Quran, there is no speech more eloquent or more convincing.

## Why "Woe That Day to the Deniers" Is Repeated Ten Times
The verse comes after:
1. The scenes of the Day of Judgement.
2. The destruction of former nations.
3. Man's creation.
4. The earth, mountains and water.
5. The command to proceed to the Fire.
6. The sparks like palaces.
7. "They will not speak."
8. "Plan against Me."
9. The reward of the righteous.
10. "When told to bow, they do not bow."

**The wisdom**: each section brings a new proof, and the woe is renewed for whoever denies each one. It resembles the repetition of "Which of the favours of your Lord will you deny?" in Surah Ar-Rahman, but here in the language of warning.

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Mursalat**: those sent forth.
- **'Urfan**: in succession.
- **Al-'Asifat**: those blowing violently.
- **Al-Fariqat**: those separating.
- **'Udhran aw nudhra**: as justification or warning.
- **Tumisat**: extinguished.
- **Nusifat**: blown away.
- **Uqqitat**: their time was set.
- **Mahin**: despised, weak.
- **Kifat**: container.
- **Furat**: sweet water.
- **Shu'ab**: branches, columns.
- **Sharar**: sparks.
- **Jimalat sufr**: yellowish camels.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Rapid oaths**: a fast rhythm that fits a warning.
2. **"For what Day was it postponed? For the Day of Judgement"**: question and answer to build suspense.
3. **"Kifat"**: one word holding the living and the dead.
4. **"Sparks like palaces... like yellowish camels"**: two images of size and number.
5. **"A shadow of three columns"** against "shades and springs".
6. **"If you have a plan, then plan against Me"**: a mocking challenge.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'u* (verse 9) and *ma'in* (verse 20).
- **Ikhfa'**: in *ma'an furata* (verse 27), the tanween is hidden before the fa'; and in *intaliqu* (verse 29), the noon sakinah before the ta'.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *naj'al* (verse 25), the jim sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna* (verses 7 and 44), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Insan before it ended with "for the wrongdoers He has prepared a painful punishment", and Al-Mursalat details that warning. Surah An-Naba' after it speaks of the same Day: "Indeed, the Day of Judgement is an appointed time."

## Living With Surah Al-Mursalat
1. **Be certain of the hereafter**: "What you are promised is to occur."
2. **Remember your origin**: a despised fluid, so do not be arrogant.
3. **Remember the grave**: the earth is a container for the dead.
4. **Learn from history**: Allah's way with criminals continues.
5. **Be among the doers of good**: "We thus reward the doers of good."
6. **Guard your prayer**: refusing to bow is a mark of the deniers.
7. **Make the Quran the greatest speech in your life.**

## Practical Lessons
1. **Pause at every "Woe that Day to the deniers"** and ask whether anything in your life resembles denial.
2. **Thank Allah for sweet water** each time you drink.
3. **Visit graves** and remember that the earth will hold you.
4. **Excel in your worship and dealings**: excellence is rewarded.
5. **Pray on time**: bowing to Allah is the opposite of the deniers' arrogance.

## Teaching Children
- **Teach them the repeated verse** and ask them to count how many times it appears.
- **Look at the earth together**: how it carries people and plants.
- **Tell them the story of the snake in the cave** and how Allah protected the Prophet ﷺ and his companions.

## The Day of Judgement: Why "Separation"?
The surah repeatedly calls the Last Day *Yawm al-Fasl*, the Day of Separation or Decision. In this world truth and falsehood are mixed: the oppressor often escapes, the oppressed is often ignored, and the honest and the dishonest look alike from the outside. On that Day everything is separated and decided. In Sahih Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Rights will certainly be returned to their owners on the Day of Resurrection, even the hornless sheep will be given retribution from the horned sheep." If justice reaches animals, how much more will it reach accountable human beings? This is a comfort to everyone who has been wronged and a warning to everyone who has wronged others.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Where was Surah Al-Mursalat revealed?
In a cave at Mina, as Ibn Mas'ud reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does "those sent forth in succession" mean?
Either winds following one another, or angels sent with what is good.

### How many times is "Woe that Day to the deniers" repeated?
Ten times.

### What does "Have We not made the earth a container" mean?
The earth holds the living on its surface and the dead within it.

### What does "sparks as huge as palaces" mean?
The sparks of Hell are each as large as a palace, and as numerous and coloured as yellowish camels.

### What was the last surah Umm al-Fadl heard the Prophet ﷺ recite?
Surah Al-Mursalat, in Maghrib prayer, as reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Mursalat?
50 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

### What does "as justification or warning" mean?
Revelation comes to remove people's excuses so that no one can claim he was not told, and to warn them of Allah's punishment.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Mursalat strikes the heart ten times: "Woe that Day to the deniers." Between each strike comes a new proof: your creation, your earth, your water, the fate of those before you. If a person will not believe after all of this, then in what statement after this will he believe?
