﴿فَٱذۡكُرُونِيٓ أَذۡكُرۡكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لِي وَلَا تَكۡفُرُونِ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:152]

## An incredible exchange
- You remember Allah, and **Allah remembers you**. The servant's small act is answered by the Lord of the worlds.

## The Hadith Qudsi
- Allah says: *If he remembers Me within himself, I remember him within Myself. If he remembers Me in a gathering, I remember him in a better gathering* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Gratitude and its opposite
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Be grateful to Me | Recognise and use blessings in obedience |
| Do not be ungrateful | Do not deny or misuse them |

## The context
- The verse comes after the change of the qiblah and the mention of sending a Messenger who recites, purifies and teaches. Remembrance and gratitude are the response to such blessings.

## Ways to remember Allah
- Reciting the Quran, morning and evening adhkar, duʿa, and remembering Him at the moment of temptation.

## Lessons
- **Remember Allah in private**: He remembers you in private.
- **Gratitude completes remembrance.**
