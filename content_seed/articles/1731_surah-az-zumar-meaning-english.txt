**Surah Az-Zumar** (75 ayat) is named after the **groups** (zumar) driven on the Day of Judgement, and holds one of the greatest ayat of hope.

## Sincerity
- *So worship Allah, being sincere to Him in religion. Unquestionably, for Allah is the pure religion.*

## The night worshipper
﴿أَمَّنۡ هُوَ قَٰنِتٌ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيۡلِ سَاجِدٗا وَقَآئِمٗا يَحۡذَرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَيَرۡجُواْ رَحۡمَةَ رَبِّهِۦۗ﴾
- *Say: are those who know equal to those who do not know?*
- **Balance**: fear of the Hereafter, hope for mercy.

## Never despair
﴿۞قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًاۚ﴾
- Allah calls even sinners **"My servants"**.
- Followed immediately by: *and turn back to your Lord and submit to Him*.

## The groups
| Group | Their arrival |
|---|---|
| The disbelievers | Driven to Hell in groups; its gates opened |
| The righteous | Driven to Paradise in groups; its keepers greet them with peace |

## Lessons
- **No sin is too big** for repentance.
- **Knowledge with worship** raises a person.
