Surah Ibrahim is named after the father of the prophets, and its most beautiful verses are his du'as: for Makkah to be secure, for his children to be kept away from idols, for hearts to be drawn to the barren valley where he left his family, and for himself, his parents and all believers on the Day of Account. Around these du'as, the surah teaches a law of life: "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you; but if you deny, indeed, My punishment is severe." It compares a good word to a good tree, firm in its root with its branches in the sky, and promises that Allah keeps the believers firm with the firm word in this life and in the grave. It also records a speech few people think about: Satan disowning his followers on the Day of Judgement.

This guide explains Surah Ibrahim section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ibrahim
- **Number of verses**: 52.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 14, in the thirteenth juz.
- **Name**: named after the Prophet Ibrahim ﷺ, whose du'as appear in verses 35–41.
- **Main themes**: the Quran as light, gratitude, the messengers' trust, the good and evil word, firmness in the grave, and Ibrahim's du'as.

## Verses 1–4: From Darkness to Light
﴿الٓرۚ كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡكَ لِتُخۡرِجَ ٱلنَّاسَ مِنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ﴾ (Quran 14:1)

**"Alif Lam Ra. A Book which We have revealed to you that you might bring mankind out of darknesses into the light by permission of their Lord."** Darknesses (plural) and light (singular): many paths of misguidance, one path of truth.

﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا بِلِسَانِ قَوۡمِهِۦ لِيُبَيِّنَ لَهُمۡۖ فَيُضِلُّ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ (Quran 14:4)

**"And We did not send any messenger except in the language of his people to state clearly for them."**

## Verses 5–8: Gratitude and Increase
﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مُوسَىٰ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآ أَنۡ أَخۡرِجۡ قَوۡمَكَ مِنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِ وَذَكِّرۡهُم بِأَيَّىٰمِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّكُلِّ صَبَّارٖ شَكُورٖ﴾ (Quran 14:5)

Musa was sent with the same mission: **"Bring out your people from darknesses into the light and remind them of the days of Allah."** The days of Allah are His favours and His punishments in history.

﴿وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمۡ لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 14:7)

**"And when your Lord proclaimed: If you are grateful, I will surely increase you; but if you deny, indeed, My punishment is severe."** Gratitude with the heart, the tongue and the limbs brings increase. Ingratitude brings loss.

## Verses 9–17: The Messengers' Trust
﴿۞قَالَتۡ رُسُلُهُمۡ أَفِي ٱللَّهِ شَكࣱّ فَاطِرِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ يَدۡعُوكُمۡ لِيَغۡفِرَ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمۡ وَيُؤَخِّرَكُمۡ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۚ قَالُوٓاْ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُنَا تُرِيدُونَ أَن تَصُدُّونَا عَمَّا كَانَ يَعۡبُدُ ءَابَآؤُنَا فَأۡتُونَا بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 14:10)

**"Can there be doubt about Allah, Creator of the heavens and earth?"**

﴿قَالَتۡ لَهُمۡ رُسُلُهُمۡ إِن نَّحۡنُ إِلَّا بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَمُنُّ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦۖ وَمَا كَانَ لَنَآ أَن نَّأۡتِيَكُم بِسُلۡطَٰنٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَمَا لَنَآ أَلَّا نَتَوَكَّلَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ وَقَدۡ هَدَىٰنَا سُبُلَنَاۚ وَلَنَصۡبِرَنَّ عَلَىٰ مَآ ءَاذَيۡتُمُونَاۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُتَوَكِّلُونَ﴾ (Quran 14:11-12)

The messengers said: **"Upon Allah let the believers rely. And why should we not rely upon Allah while He has guided us to our ways? And we will surely be patient against whatever harm you should cause us."** Their reasoning: the One who guided us will not abandon us.

﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِرُسُلِهِمۡ لَنُخۡرِجَنَّكُم مِّنۡ أَرۡضِنَآ أَوۡ لَتَعُودُنَّ فِي مِلَّتِنَاۖ فَأَوۡحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ لَنُهۡلِكَنَّ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَنُسۡكِنَنَّكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامِي وَخَافَ وَعِيدِ﴾ ﴿وَٱسۡتَفۡتَحُواْ وَخَابَ كُلُّ جَبَّارٍ عَنِيدٖ﴾ ﴿مِّن وَرَآئِهِۦ جَهَنَّمُ وَيُسۡقَىٰ مِن مَّآءٖ صَدِيدٖ﴾ ﴿يَتَجَرَّعُهُۥ وَلَا يَكَادُ يُسِيغُهُۥ وَيَأۡتِيهِ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ مِن كُلِّ مَكَانٖ وَمَا هُوَ بِمَيِّتٖۖ وَمِن وَرَآئِهِۦ عَذَابٌ غَلِيظࣱ﴾ (Quran 14:13-17)

The disbelievers threatened to expel them. Allah promised: **"We will surely destroy the wrongdoers, and We will surely cause you to dwell in the land after them. That is for he who fears My position and fears My threat."**

## Verses 18–23: Ashes in the Wind and Satan's Speech
﴿مَّثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡۖ أَعۡمَٰلُهُمۡ كَرَمَادٍ ٱشۡتَدَّتۡ بِهِ ٱلرِّيحُ فِي يَوۡمٍ عَاصِفٖۖ لَّا يَقۡدِرُونَ مِمَّا كَسَبُواْ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلضَّلَٰلُ ٱلۡبَعِيدُ﴾ (Quran 14:18)

**"The example of those who disbelieve in their Lord: their deeds are like ashes which the wind blows forcefully on a stormy day."** Deeds without faith are scattered and lost.

﴿وَقَالَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ لَمَّا قُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَعَدَكُمۡ وَعۡدَ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَوَعَدتُّكُمۡ فَأَخۡلَفۡتُكُمۡۖ وَمَا كَانَ لِيَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّن سُلۡطَٰنٍ إِلَّآ أَن دَعَوۡتُكُمۡ فَٱسۡتَجَبۡتُمۡ لِيۖ فَلَا تَلُومُونِي وَلُومُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۖ مَّآ أَنَا۠ بِمُصۡرِخِكُمۡ وَمَآ أَنتُم بِمُصۡرِخِيَّ إِنِّي كَفَرۡتُ بِمَآ أَشۡرَكۡتُمُونِ مِن قَبۡلُۗ إِنَّ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 14:22)

**Satan's speech** after judgement: **"Indeed, Allah had promised you the promise of truth. And I promised you, but I betrayed you. But I had no authority over you except that I invited you, and you responded to me. So do not blame me; but blame yourselves. I cannot be called to your aid, nor can you be called to my aid. Indeed, I deny your association of me with Allah before."**

**The lesson**: Satan has no power to force anyone. Every sin is a response to an invitation. On that Day he will abandon those who followed him.

## Verses 24–27: The Good Word and the Firm Word
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا كَلِمَةٗ طَيِّبَةٗ كَشَجَرَةٖ طَيِّبَةٍ أَصۡلُهَا ثَابِتࣱ وَفَرۡعُهَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ﴾ ﴿تُؤۡتِيٓ أُكُلَهَا كُلَّ حِينِۭ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهَاۗ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ لِلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 14:24-25)

**"Have you not considered how Allah presents an example: a good word is like a good tree, whose root is firmly fixed and its branches high in the sky? It produces its fruit all the time, by permission of its Lord."**

The good word is *la ilaha illa Allah*, and everything that grows from it. Its root is faith in the heart; its branches are good deeds rising to Allah; its fruit is constant benefit. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Umar reported that the Prophet ﷺ asked his Companions about a tree whose leaves do not fall and which is like the Muslim, and then told them: "It is the palm tree."

﴿وَمَثَلُ كَلِمَةٍ خَبِيثَةٖ كَشَجَرَةٍ خَبِيثَةٍ ٱجۡتُثَّتۡ مِن فَوۡقِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَا لَهَا مِن قَرَارٖ﴾ (Quran 14:26)

**"And the example of a bad word is like a bad tree, uprooted from the surface of the earth, not having any stability."** Falsehood has no roots.

﴿يُثَبِّتُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ ٱلثَّابِتِ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَيُضِلُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَۚ وَيَفۡعَلُ ٱللَّهُ مَا يَشَآءُ﴾ (Quran 14:27)

**"Allah keeps firm those who believe, with the firm word, in worldly life and in the hereafter."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, al-Bara' ibn 'Azib reported that the Prophet ﷺ said this verse was revealed about the punishment of the grave: the believer is asked "Who is your Lord?" and he says "Allah is my Lord, and Muhammad is my Prophet."

## Verses 28–34: Exchanging Favours for Disbelief
﴿۞أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ بَدَّلُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ كُفۡرٗا وَأَحَلُّواْ قَوۡمَهُمۡ دَارَ ٱلۡبَوَارِ﴾ ﴿جَهَنَّمَ يَصۡلَوۡنَهَاۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡقَرَارُ﴾ (Quran 14:28-29)

**"Have you not considered those who exchanged the favour of Allah for disbelief and settled their people in the home of ruin?"**

﴿قُل لِّعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ يُقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ يَوۡمࣱ لَّا بَيۡعࣱ فِيهِ وَلَا خِلَٰلٌ﴾ (Quran 14:31)

**"Tell My servants who have believed to establish prayer and spend from what We have provided them, secretly and publicly, before a Day comes in which there will be no exchange and no friendships."**

﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَأَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَخۡرَجَ بِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ رِزۡقٗا لَّكُمۡۖ وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡفُلۡكَ لِتَجۡرِيَ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِأَمۡرِهِۦۖ وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرَ﴾ ﴿وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ دَآئِبَيۡنِۖ وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ﴾ ﴿وَءَاتَىٰكُم مِّن كُلِّ مَا سَأَلۡتُمُوهُۚ وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَظَلُومࣱ كَفَّارࣱ﴾ (Quran 14:32-34)

The heavens, the earth, the rain, the ships, the rivers, the sun, the moon, the night and the day, all subjected for you. **"And He gave you from all you asked of Him. And if you should count the favour of Allah, you could not enumerate them. Indeed, mankind is generally most unjust and ungrateful."**

## Verses 35–41: The Du'as of Ibrahim
﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ رَبِّ ٱجۡعَلۡ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدَ ءَامِنٗا وَٱجۡنُبۡنِي وَبَنِيَّ أَن نَّعۡبُدَ ٱلۡأَصۡنَامَ﴾ ﴿رَبِّ إِنَّهُنَّ أَضۡلَلۡنَ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِۖ فَمَن تَبِعَنِي فَإِنَّهُۥ مِنِّيۖ وَمَنۡ عَصَانِي فَإِنَّكَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 14:35-36)

**"My Lord, make this city secure and keep me and my sons away from worshipping idols."** The father of tawhid feared idolatry for himself and his children.

﴿رَّبَّنَآ إِنِّيٓ أَسۡكَنتُ مِن ذُرِّيَّتِي بِوَادٍ غَيۡرِ ذِي زَرۡعٍ عِندَ بَيۡتِكَ ٱلۡمُحَرَّمِ رَبَّنَا لِيُقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ فَٱجۡعَلۡ أَفۡـِٔدَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ تَهۡوِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَٱرۡزُقۡهُم مِّنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 14:37)

**"Our Lord, I have settled some of my descendants in an uncultivated valley near Your sacred House, our Lord, that they may establish prayer. So make the hearts of some people incline toward them and provide for them from the fruits, that they might be grateful."** He left Hajar and Isma'il in a barren valley for the sake of prayer. Allah answered: hearts from every land long for Makkah, and fruits of the world reach it.

﴿رَبِّ ٱجۡعَلۡنِي مُقِيمَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِيۚ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلۡ دُعَآءِ﴾ ﴿رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلۡحِسَابُ﴾ (Quran 14:40-41)

**"My Lord, make me an establisher of prayer, and from my descendants. Our Lord, and accept my supplication. Our Lord, forgive me and my parents and the believers the Day the account is established."** A du'a many Muslims recite at the end of their prayer.

## Verses 42–52: Allah Is Not Unaware
﴿وَلَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَٰفِلًا عَمَّا يَعۡمَلُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَۚ إِنَّمَا يُؤَخِّرُهُمۡ لِيَوۡمٖ تَشۡخَصُ فِيهِ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ﴾ ﴿مُهۡطِعِينَ مُقۡنِعِي رُءُوسِهِمۡ لَا يَرۡتَدُّ إِلَيۡهِمۡ طَرۡفُهُمۡۖ وَأَفۡـِٔدَتُهُمۡ هَوَآءࣱ﴾ (Quran 14:42-43)

**"And never think that Allah is unaware of what the wrongdoers do. He only delays them for a Day when eyes will stare in horror."** A comfort to every oppressed person and a warning to every oppressor.

﴿يَوۡمَ تُبَدَّلُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُۖ وَبَرَزُواْ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡوَٰحِدِ ٱلۡقَهَّارِ﴾ (Quran 14:48)

**"On the Day the earth will be replaced by another earth, and the heavens as well, and all creatures will come out before Allah, the One, the Prevailing."**

﴿هَٰذَا بَلَٰغࣱ لِّلنَّاسِ وَلِيُنذَرُواْ بِهِۦ وَلِيَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا هُوَ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱ وَلِيَذَّكَّرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ (Quran 14:52)

**"This is notification for the people that they may be warned thereby and that they may know that He is but one God and that those of understanding will be reminded."**

## Word Meanings
- **Az-Zulumat**: darknesses.
- **An-Nur**: the light.
- **Ayyam Allah**: the days of Allah.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Darknesses" and "the light"**: plural and singular.
2. **"If you are grateful, I will increase you"**: a divine law in a single sentence.
3. **Ashes in a storm**: deeds without faith.
4. **Satan's honest betrayal**: a speech that should wake every follower.
5. **The good tree and the bad tree**: rooted truth and rootless falsehood.
6. **Ibrahim's du'as**: a father's love, fear and hope in a few lines.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Alif Lam Ra*.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *kalimatan tayyibatan ka-shajaratin* (verse 24), the tanween is hidden before the kaf.
- **Izhar**: in *bi-wadin ghayri* (verse 37), the tanween before the throat letter ghayn is pronounced clearly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 24) and *du'a'* (verse 40).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *ij'alni* (verse 40), the jim sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Ar-Ra'd before it ended with "and whoever has knowledge of the Scripture", and Ibrahim opens with "A Book which We have revealed to you". Surah Al-Hijr after it continues with the preservation of that Book.

## Practising Gratitude
1. **Count blessings daily.**
2. **Thank Allah with your tongue**: *al-hamdu lillah*.
3. **Use blessings in obedience.**
4. **Thank people**: in a hadith graded authentic, the Prophet ﷺ said whoever does not thank people has not thanked Allah.
5. **Prostrate in gratitude** when good news comes.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Follow the light of the Quran out of every darkness.**
2. **Be grateful, and expect increase.**
3. **Rely on Allah as the messengers did.**
4. **Blame yourself, not Satan.**
5. **Strengthen your good word** with good deeds.
6. **Ask Allah for firmness in the grave.**
7. **Pray Ibrahim's du'as** for your children and parents.
8. **Trust that Allah sees every wrongdoer.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you" mean?
Gratitude to Allah brings more of His blessings, while ingratitude brings His punishment.

### What is the "good word" in verse 24?
Commentators explained it as *la ilaha illa Allah*, compared to a good tree with firm roots and high branches.

### What does verse 27 say about the grave?
Allah keeps the believers firm with the firm word when questioned in the grave, as in the hadith of al-Bara' in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What will Satan say on the Day of Judgement?
That he had no power over people except to invite them, and they should blame themselves, not him.

### What did Ibrahim pray for Makkah?
That it be secure, that his children avoid idols, that hearts incline towards them, and that they be provided with fruits.

### What is Ibrahim's du'a for his parents?
"Our Lord, forgive me and my parents and the believers the Day the account is established."

### How many verses are in Surah Ibrahim?
52 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Ibrahim brings you out of darkness into light, teaches you the law of gratitude, and shows you how a good word grows into a tree whose roots are firm and whose branches reach the sky. It warns that Satan will abandon his followers, promises firmness in the grave, and leaves you with the du'as of a father who wanted his children to pray and his parents to be forgiven. Make his du'as yours.
