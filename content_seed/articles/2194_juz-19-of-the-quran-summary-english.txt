Juz 19 begins with ﴿۞وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا﴾, "And those who do not expect to meet Us said", so it is called **Juz Wa Qala Alladhina**. It runs from **Al-Furqan 25:21 to An-Naml 27:55**, with all of **Surah Ash-Shu'ara'** (227 verses) in between. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 362 to 381**.

This is the juz of **the message and the messengers**. It opens with the Prophet's complaint ﷺ that the Quran was abandoned, paints the portrait of the servants of the Most Merciful, presents seven prophets in Ash-Shu'ara' in a repeating pattern that fixes the meaning, then opens An-Naml with Sulayman, the grateful prophet-king.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **the truth of the message and the unity of the messengers' call**. The idolaters object to the Messenger and the Quran, and the Quran replies that all messengers brought one word: ﴿فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ﴾, "So fear Allah and obey me", and that none asked people for payment. Ash-Shu'ara' repeats eight times: ﴿وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾, "And your Lord is the Almighty, the Most Merciful": almighty in punishing the deniers, merciful in saving the believers. Then Sulayman shows us gratitude for blessings at the height of kingship.

The message: **the messengers' call is one, Allah is Almighty and Merciful, and gratitude is how blessings are preserved.**

## Part one: the rest of Surah Al-Furqan (21-77)

### 1. Regret and abandoning the Quran (21-34)
The idolaters objected: why are angels not sent down to us, or why do we not see our Lord? Then the scene of regret: ﴿وَيَوۡمَ يَعَضُّ ٱلظَّالِمُ عَلَىٰ يَدَيۡهِ يَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي ٱتَّخَذۡتُ مَعَ ٱلرَّسُولِ سَبِيلٗا﴾, "The Day the wrongdoer will bite his hands, saying: I wish I had taken a path with the Messenger", and: woe to me, I wish I had not taken so-and-so as a close friend. A bad companion leads one away from the reminder.

Then the Prophet's complaint ﷺ: ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلرَّسُولُ يَٰرَبِّ إِنَّ قَوۡمِي ٱتَّخَذُواْ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ مَهۡجُورٗا﴾, "And the Messenger said: My Lord, my people have taken this Quran as something abandoned." Scholars mention that abandoning the Quran takes several forms: not listening to it, not acting on it, not reflecting on it, not referring to it for judgement, and not seeking healing through it.

Then the wisdom of gradual revelation: ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡلَا نُزِّلَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ جُمۡلَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗۚ كَذَٰلِكَ لِنُثَبِّتَ بِهِۦ فُؤَادَكَۖ وَرَتَّلۡنَٰهُ تَرۡتِيلٗا﴾, "The disbelievers said: why was the Quran not sent down to him all at once? Thus it is, so We may strengthen your heart with it, and We have recited it in measured stages." Revelation in parts steadied the heart and eased memorization and practice.

**Lesson**: beware being among those the Prophet ﷺ complains about; keep a steady daily portion of recitation and reflection. Read [Recite the Quran with Tartil: 73:4](/blog/recite-the-quran-with-tartil-73-4-english/).

### 2. The ends of the deniers and following desire (35-44)
Musa and Harun, the people of Nuh, 'Ad, Thamud, the people of the Well and many generations in between. Then: ﴿أَرَءَيۡتَ مَنِ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَٰهَهُۥ هَوَىٰهُ أَفَأَنتَ تَكُونُ عَلَيۡهِ وَكِيلًا﴾, "Have you seen the one who takes his own desire as his god? Will you then be his guardian?" Whoever makes his desire the judge over his religion has taken it as a god.

### 3. Signs in creation (45-62)
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَيۡفَ مَدَّ ٱلظِّلَّ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ لَجَعَلَهُۥ سَاكِنٗا ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَا ٱلشَّمۡسَ عَلَيۡهِ دَلِيلٗا﴾, "Have you not seen how your Lord extends the shadow? Had He willed, He could have made it still. Then We made the sun its guide." Then night as a covering and sleep as rest, winds as good news, and pure water. Then a new quarter: ﴿۞وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي مَرَجَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ هَٰذَا عَذۡبࣱ فُرَاتࣱ وَهَٰذَا مِلۡحٌ أُجَاجࣱ وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَهُمَا بَرۡزَخٗا وَحِجۡرٗا مَّحۡجُورٗا﴾, "He is the One who released the two seas, one fresh and sweet, the other salty and bitter, and placed between them a barrier and a forbidding partition."

Then the place of prostration: ﴿وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ قَالُواْ وَمَا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ أَنَسۡجُدُ لِمَا تَأۡمُرُنَا وَزَادَهُمۡ نُفُورٗا﴾, "When it is said to them, prostrate to the Most Merciful, they say: what is the Most Merciful? Shall we prostrate to what you command us? And it increases them in aversion." Then: blessed is He who placed great stars in the sky and placed in it a lamp and a shining moon.

### 4. The servants of the Most Merciful (63-77)
After the idolaters rejected "the Most Merciful", the description of His servants followed:
1. **Humility and forbearance**: ﴿وَعِبَادُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡشُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ هَوۡنٗا وَإِذَا خَاطَبَهُمُ ٱلۡجَٰهِلُونَ قَالُواْ سَلَٰمٗا﴾, "The servants of the Most Merciful are those who walk on the earth humbly, and when the ignorant address them, they say: peace."
2. **Night prayer**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَبِيتُونَ لِرَبِّهِمۡ سُجَّدٗا وَقِيَٰمٗا﴾, "who spend the night prostrating and standing for their Lord."
3. **Fear of the Fire**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا ٱصۡرِفۡ عَنَّا عَذَابَ جَهَنَّمَۖ إِنَّ عَذَابَهَا كَانَ غَرَامًا﴾, "who say: our Lord, avert from us the punishment of Hell; its punishment is unrelenting."
4. **Moderation in spending**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَنفَقُواْ لَمۡ يُسۡرِفُواْ وَلَمۡ يَقۡتُرُواْ وَكَانَ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ قَوَامٗا﴾, "who, when they spend, are neither wasteful nor stingy, but between the two, moderate."
5. **Avoiding the major sins**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَدۡعُونَ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ وَلَا يَقۡتُلُونَ ٱلنَّفۡسَ ٱلَّتِي حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا يَزۡنُونَۚ﴾, "who do not call on another god with Allah, do not kill the soul Allah has forbidden except by right, and do not commit zina", then the door of repentance: ﴿إِلَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يُبَدِّلُ ٱللَّهُ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ حَسَنَٰتٖۗ﴾, "except whoever repents, believes and does righteous deeds: for them Allah will replace their evil deeds with good ones."
6. **Shunning falsehood and idle talk**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَشۡهَدُونَ ٱلزُّورَ وَإِذَا مَرُّواْ بِٱللَّغۡوِ مَرُّواْ كِرَامٗا﴾, "who do not witness falsehood, and when they pass by idle talk, pass by with dignity."
7. **Responding to the verses**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ لَمۡ يَخِرُّواْ عَلَيۡهَا صُمّٗا وَعُمۡيَانٗا﴾, "who, when reminded of their Lord's verses, do not fall upon them deaf and blind."
8. **Praying for family and leadership in good**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعۡيُنٖ وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا﴾, "who say: our Lord, grant us from our spouses and children the comfort of our eyes, and make us leaders of the God-conscious."

The reward: they will be given the high chambers for their patience. The close: ﴿قُلۡ مَا يَعۡبَؤُاْ بِكُمۡ رَبِّي لَوۡلَا دُعَآؤُكُمۡۖ﴾, "Say: my Lord would not care for you were it not for your supplication."

**Reflect**: they are attributed not to "the Subduer" or "the Compeller" but to "the Most Merciful", and all their traits are mercy, gentleness and humility. The promise of repentance here is the greatest of promises: evil deeds replaced with good ones.

## Part two: Surah Ash-Shu'ara' (1-227)

Ash-Shu'ara' is Makki, with 227 verses, and opens with ﴿طسٓمٓ﴾. It is named after the poets mentioned at its end. For the whole surah see [Surah Ash-Shu'ara Meaning in English](/blog/surah-ash-shuara-meaning-english/).

### The surah's structure
Two refrains recur:
- After each story: ﴿إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "In that is a sign, yet most of them were not believers", then ﴿وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾, the second repeated eight times.
- In the stories of Nuh, Hud, Salih, Lut and Shu'ayb: ﴿وَمَآ أَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٍۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "I ask you no payment for it; my reward is only from the Lord of the worlds", five times.

### 1. Consoling the Prophet ﷺ (1-9)
﴿لَعَلَّكَ بَٰخِعࣱ نَّفۡسَكَ أَلَّا يَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "Perhaps you would destroy yourself with grief that they do not believe." The whole surah consoles him ﷺ: the prophets before him faced the same.

### 2. Musa and Pharaoh (10-68)
﴿وَإِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبُّكَ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنِ ٱئۡتِ ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾, "When your Lord called Musa: go to the wrongdoing people." The longest story in the surah: the debate with Pharaoh about the Lord of the worlds, the magicians and their faith, the escape by night, and the decisive scene: ﴿فَلَمَّا تَرَـٰٓءَا ٱلۡجَمۡعَانِ قَالَ أَصۡحَٰبُ مُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّا لَمُدۡرَكُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ كَلَّآۖ إِنَّ مَعِيَ رَبِّي سَيَهۡدِينِ﴾, "When the two groups saw each other, Musa's companions said: we will surely be overtaken! He said: No! My Lord is with me; He will guide me." The sea parted, Musa and those with him were saved, and the others drowned.

**Lesson**: the sea ahead, the enemy behind; material calculation says "we are finished", but certainty says "No! My Lord is with me; He will guide me."

### 3. Ibrahim and the sound heart (69-104)
Ibrahim questioned his people: what do you worship? Do they hear you or benefit you? They said: we found our fathers doing so. Then he described his Lord in the most beautiful terms: ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَنِي فَهُوَ يَهۡدِينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي هُوَ يُطۡعِمُنِي وَيَسۡقِينِ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا مَرِضۡتُ فَهُوَ يَشۡفِينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي يُمِيتُنِي ثُمَّ يُحۡيِينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡمَعُ أَن يَغۡفِرَ لِي خَطِيٓـَٔتِي يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ﴾, "who created me and guides me, who feeds me and gives me drink, and when I am ill, He heals me; who will cause me to die and then give me life, and who I hope will forgive my faults on the Day of Judgement." Read [How Ibrahim Describes His Lord: 26:78](/blog/ibrahim-describes-his-lord-26-78-english/).

**Reflect on the courtesy**: "when I am ill" attributes illness to himself, and "He heals me" attributes healing to Allah.

Then his prayer: ﴿رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي حُكۡمٗا وَأَلۡحِقۡنِي بِٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱجۡعَل لِّي لِسَانَ صِدۡقٖ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱجۡعَلۡنِي مِن وَرَثَةِ جَنَّةِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾, "My Lord, grant me wisdom and join me with the righteous, grant me an honourable mention among later generations, and make me among the inheritors of the Garden of Bliss", then ﴿وَلَا تُخۡزِنِي يَوۡمَ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ لَا يَنفَعُ مَالࣱ وَلَا بَنُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَنۡ أَتَى ٱللَّهَ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٖ﴾, "and do not disgrace me on the Day they are raised, the Day when neither wealth nor children will benefit, except whoever comes to Allah with a sound heart."

The sound heart is one free of shirk, doubt and rancour. Read ["Except One Who Comes to Allah with a Sound Heart": Quran 26:87-89 Explained](/blog/sound-heart-26-89-meaning-english/).

### 4. Five prophets in one pattern (105-191)
- **Nuh**: ﴿كَذَّبَتۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٍ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾, "The people of Nuh denied the messengers." They objected: shall we believe you when the lowliest follow you? He said: I will not drive away the believers.
- **Hud**: ﴿كَذَّبَتۡ عَادٌ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾, with Hizb 38 starting at verse 111. He condemned their building for vanity and their tyranny: do you build a landmark on every height for amusement, and when you strike, strike as tyrants?
- **Salih**: ﴿كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾, condemning their luxury, carving houses in the mountains with pride, and obeying the extravagant.
- **Lut**: ﴿كَذَّبَتۡ قَوۡمُ لُوطٍ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾, condemning their indecency.
- **Shu'ayb**: ﴿كَذَّبَ أَصۡحَٰبُ لۡـَٔيۡكَةِ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾, commanding full measure and weighing with a straight balance; the punishment of the Day of the Shadow seized them.

**The repeating structure**: "denied the messengers", "when their brother said to them: will you not fear Allah?", "I am a trustworthy messenger to you", "so fear Allah and obey me", "I ask you no payment". Then the specific condemnation for each people.

**Reflect**: "the people of Nuh denied the messengers", though they denied only Nuh, because whoever denies one messenger denies them all; their call is one. And with Shu'ayb the word "their brother" is not used, because the people of the Thicket were not his kin by lineage.

### 5. The Quran from the Lord of the worlds (192-227)
﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَنزِيلُ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿نَزَلَ بِهِ ٱلرُّوحُ ٱلۡأَمِينُ﴾ ﴿عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِكَ لِتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنذِرِينَ﴾ ﴿بِلِسَانٍ عَرَبِيّٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾, "It is the revelation of the Lord of the worlds, brought down by the Trustworthy Spirit upon your heart, so you may be among the warners, in a clear Arabic tongue." Then: ﴿وَأَنذِرۡ عَشِيرَتَكَ ٱلۡأَقۡرَبِينَ﴾, "Warn your nearest kin." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, when it was revealed the Prophet ﷺ climbed As-Safa and called the clans of Quraysh, saying: "O Fatimah daughter of Muhammad, ask me for whatever you wish of my wealth; I cannot avail you anything against Allah." Then: lower your wing to the believers who follow you, and rely on the Almighty, the Most Merciful.

Then the difference between revelation and poetry: ﴿وَٱلشُّعَرَآءُ يَتَّبِعُهُمُ ٱلۡغَاوُۥنَ﴾, "As for the poets, the deviators follow them"; they wander in every valley and say what they do not do. Then the exception: ﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَذَكَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا وَٱنتَصَرُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا ظُلِمُواْۗ﴾, "except those who believe, do good, remember Allah often and defend themselves after being wronged." Truthful, committed words are praiseworthy.

## Part three: the start of Surah An-Naml (1-55)

An-Naml is Makki, with 93 verses, opening with ﴿طسٓۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ وَكِتَابٖ مُّبِينٍ﴾, "Ta Sin. These are the verses of the Quran and a clear Book." The basmalah appears in it twice: at the start, and within Sulayman's letter (verse 30). For the whole surah see [Surah An-Naml Meaning in English](/blog/surah-an-naml-meaning-english/).

### 1. Musa and the nine signs (1-14)
Guidance and good news for believers who establish prayer and give zakah. Then Musa and the fire, and the call: O Musa, it is I, Allah, the Almighty, the Wise; then ﴿وَأَدۡخِلۡ يَدَكَ فِي جَيۡبِكَ تَخۡرُجۡ بَيۡضَآءَ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ سُوٓءٖۖ فِي تِسۡعِ ءَايَٰتٍ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَقَوۡمِهِۦٓۚ﴾, "Put your hand into your garment; it will come out white without harm, among nine signs to Pharaoh and his people." They rejected them, though their souls were certain of them, out of injustice and arrogance.

### 2. Dawud, Sulayman and the ant (15-19)
﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ عِلۡمٗاۖ وَقَالَا ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي فَضَّلَنَا عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٖ مِّنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "We gave Dawud and Sulayman knowledge, and they said: praise be to Allah who has favoured us over many of His believing servants." Then: we have been taught the speech of birds. Then the ant: ﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَتَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ وَادِ ٱلنَّمۡلِ قَالَتۡ نَمۡلَةࣱ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّمۡلُ ٱدۡخُلُواْ مَسَٰكِنَكُمۡ لَا يَحۡطِمَنَّكُمۡ سُلَيۡمَٰنُ وَجُنُودُهُۥ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ﴾, "When they came to the valley of the ants, an ant said: O ants, enter your dwellings lest Sulayman and his soldiers crush you while they do not perceive."

**Reflect on the ant**: she called, alerted, named, commanded, explained and excused Sulayman ("while they do not perceive"). She was a leader responsible for her people.

Then Sulayman's response: ﴿فَتَبَسَّمَ ضَاحِكٗا مِّن قَوۡلِهَا وَقَالَ رَبِّ أَوۡزِعۡنِيٓ أَنۡ أَشۡكُرَ نِعۡمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَيَّ وَأَنۡ أَعۡمَلَ صَٰلِحٗا تَرۡضَىٰهُ وَأَدۡخِلۡنِي بِرَحۡمَتِكَ فِي عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾, "He smiled, amused at her words, and said: my Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour on me and my parents, to do righteous deeds that please You, and admit me by Your mercy among Your righteous servants." Great kingship did not make him arrogant; it made him ask for gratitude.

### 3. The hoopoe and the Queen of Sheba (20-44)
﴿وَتَفَقَّدَ ٱلطَّيۡرَ فَقَالَ مَالِيَ لَآ أَرَى ٱلۡهُدۡهُدَ أَمۡ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَآئِبِينَ﴾, "He inspected the birds and said: why do I not see the hoopoe, or is he absent?" A leader checks on his people, even the smallest soldier. Then the hoopoe came: ﴿فَمَكَثَ غَيۡرَ بَعِيدٖ فَقَالَ أَحَطتُ بِمَا لَمۡ تُحِطۡ بِهِۦ وَجِئۡتُكَ مِن سَبَإِۭ بِنَبَإٖ يَقِينٍ﴾, "He stayed not long and said: I have learned what you have not, and brought you from Sheba certain news": a woman rules them, and they prostrate to the sun instead of Allah. The hoopoe was zealous for monotheism: ﴿أَلَّاۤ يَسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡخَبۡءَ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ﴾ ﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾, "so they do not prostrate to Allah, who brings out what is hidden in the heavens and earth... Allah, there is no god but Him, Lord of the Great Throne", a place of prostration. A new quarter begins at verse 27.

Then the letter: ﴿إِنَّهُۥ مِن سُلَيۡمَٰنَ وَإِنَّهُۥ بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾, "It is from Sulayman, and it is: In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate": do not exalt yourselves against me, and come to me in submission. The queen consulted her people, then sent a gift, which Sulayman returned. Then the throne was brought: ﴿قَالَ ٱلَّذِي عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَنَا۠ ءَاتِيكَ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن يَرۡتَدَّ إِلَيۡكَ طَرۡفُكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهُ مُسۡتَقِرًّا عِندَهُۥ قَالَ هَٰذَا مِن فَضۡلِ رَبِّي لِيَبۡلُوَنِيٓ ءَأَشۡكُرُ أَمۡ أَكۡفُرُۖ﴾, "One who had knowledge of the Book said: I will bring it to you before your glance returns to you. When he saw it settled before him, he said: this is from the favour of my Lord, to test me whether I am grateful or ungrateful."

Then the polished palace: ﴿قِيلَ لَهَا ٱدۡخُلِي ٱلصَّرۡحَۖ فَلَمَّا رَأَتۡهُ حَسِبَتۡهُ لُجَّةٗ وَكَشَفَتۡ عَن سَاقَيۡهَاۚ قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ صَرۡحࣱ مُّمَرَّدࣱ مِّن قَوَارِيرَۗ قَالَتۡ رَبِّ إِنِّي ظَلَمۡتُ نَفۡسِي وَأَسۡلَمۡتُ مَعَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "She was told: enter the palace. When she saw it, she thought it a pool of water and uncovered her shins. He said: it is a palace smoothed with glass. She said: my Lord, I have wronged myself, and I submit with Sulayman to Allah, Lord of the worlds."

**Lessons**:
1. The hoopoe, a small soldier, carried the concern for monotheism.
2. Sulayman did not accept his report without verification: we will see whether you are truthful or a liar.
3. The queen consulted rather than ruling alone, showing wisdom.
4. Sulayman attributed every achievement to "the favour of my Lord".
5. She said "I submit with Sulayman", not "to Sulayman": submission is to Allah alone.

### 4. Salih and Lut (45-55)
Salih was sent to Thamud, who split into two disputing groups, and nine men who spread corruption conspired: swear by Allah that we will attack him and his family by night. They plotted, and Allah plotted while they did not perceive. Then Lut condemning indecency, and the juz ends with: rather, you are an ignorant people.

## Tajweed points in Juz 19

### ﴿طسٓمٓ﴾ at the start of Ash-Shu'ara'
Read *Ta, Sin, Mim*. Ta takes two counts; Sin and Mim take a necessary six-count madd. The noon of "Sin" merges with ghunnah into the meem of "Mim".

### ﴿طسٓۚ﴾ at the start of An-Naml
Read *Ta, Sin* with a six-count madd on Sin; the noon is then hidden (ikhfa') before the ta of "tilka".

### ﴿لۡـَٔيۡكَةِ﴾ in Ash-Shu'ara' 176
Written without an alif before the lam, and read in Hafs as *al-aykah*.

### ﴿ٱلۡغَاوُۥنَ﴾ in Ash-Shu'ara' 224
The small waw is pronounced and lengthened by two counts.

### The two prostrations
The first at Al-Furqan 60, at ﴿وَزَادَهُمۡ نُفُورٗا﴾; the second at An-Naml 26, at ﴿رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾. See [Sujud al-Tilawah in English](/blog/sujud-al-tilawa-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. The refrain of Ash-Shu'ara'
﴿إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ then ﴿وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾; the second recurs eight times: after the opening (9), Musa (68), Ibrahim (104), Nuh (122), Hud (140), Salih (159), Lut (175) and Shu'ayb (191).

### 2. "So fear Allah and obey me"
It occurs eight times in Ash-Shu'ara': twice each with Nuh, Hud and Salih, and once each with Lut and Shu'ayb.

### 3. The white hand
- An-Naml 12: ﴿وَأَدۡخِلۡ يَدَكَ فِي جَيۡبِكَ﴾.
- Al-Qasas 32: ﴿ٱسۡلُكۡ يَدَكَ فِي جَيۡبِكَ﴾.

### 4. "Their brother" in every story but Shu'ayb's
With Nuh, Hud, Salih and Lut: ﴿إِذۡ قَالَ لَهُمۡ أَخُوهُمۡ﴾; with Shu'ayb and the people of the Thicket: "when Shu'ayb said to them".

## How to memorize Juz 19 with understanding

1. **The servants of the Most Merciful have eight traits**: write them numbered and review them in order.
2. **Ash-Shu'ara' is a surah of templates**: learn the template once, then focus on what distinguishes each people (the lowly followers, landmarks on heights, palms and carved houses, indecency, measure).
3. **Order the prophets of Ash-Shu'ara'**: Musa, Ibrahim, Nuh, Hud, Salih, Lut, Shu'ayb.
4. **An-Naml is a continuous story**: the ant, the hoopoe, the letter, the gift, the throne, the palace.
5. **Use the verse endings** of Ash-Shu'ara' (*-in*, *-un*) and watch for the places that break the pattern.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 19

- **Choose your companion** before you bite your hands in regret.
- **Do not abandon the Quran** in listening, reciting, practice or reflection.
- **Do not make your desire your god.**
- **Walk humbly** and say "peace" to the ignorant.
- **Pray at night**, even a little.
- **Spend moderately**, neither wasteful nor stingy.
- **Repent**: Allah replaces evil deeds with good ones.
- **Pray for your spouse and children** to be the comfort of your eyes.
- **In hardship say**: No! My Lord is with me; He will guide me.
- **Prepare a sound heart** for the Day wealth and children do not help.
- **Begin your call with your family.**
- **Be grateful for blessings** as Sulayman was.
- **Verify news** before judging.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 19 called "Wa Qala Alladhina"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا﴾.

### How many times does "And your Lord is the Almighty, the Most Merciful" occur in Ash-Shu'ara'?
Eight times.

### How many prostrations are in Juz 19?
Two: Al-Furqan 60 and An-Naml 26.

### Where does Juz 19 end?
At An-Naml 55. Juz 20 begins with ﴿۞فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوٓاْ أَخۡرِجُوٓاْ ءَالَ لُوطٖ مِّن قَرۡيَتِكُمۡۖ﴾, "The only answer of his people was to say: expel the family of Lut from your town."

### What is a sound heart?
A heart free of shirk, doubt and spiritual disease, the one that benefits its owner on the Day of Resurrection.

## Conclusion

Juz 19 warns you against abandoning the Quran, paints the portrait of the servants of the Most Merciful, then presents seven prophets with one call, "So fear Allah and obey me", reminding you eight times that your Lord is Almighty and Merciful. It then shows you Sulayman, the grateful king, smiling at an ant's words and saying: "My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour."
