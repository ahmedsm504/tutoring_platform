﴿مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن نَّبۡرَأَهَآۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿لِّكَيۡلَا تَأۡسَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَفۡرَحُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالٖ فَخُورٍ﴾ [Al-Hadid 57:22-23]

## Written before
- Every calamity on earth or in ourselves is recorded **before** creation.

## Why tell us?
| Purpose | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Do not despair over loss | It was not meant for you |
| Do not exult over gain | It was given, not earned alone |

## What kind of joy is forbidden?
- Scholars clarify: the forbidden joy is **arrogant pride**, not gratitude. Natural sadness is fine; despair is not.

## The ending
- *Allah does not love the arrogant and boastful*: pride in gains is the danger.

## Lessons
- **Hold blessings lightly** and losses calmly.
- **Thank Allah** for gains without pride.
