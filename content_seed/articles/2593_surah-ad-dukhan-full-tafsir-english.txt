Surah Ad-Dukhan opens with the night on which the Quran came down: "Indeed, We sent it down during a blessed night... On that night is made distinct every wise matter." It then warns of a Day when the sky will bring a visible smoke covering the people, tells the story of Pharaoh's people who left behind gardens, springs and fine homes, so insignificant that "the heaven and earth did not weep for them", and closes with two opposite destinies: the tree of Zaqqum for the sinner, and a secure station among gardens and springs for the righteous.

This guide explains Surah Ad-Dukhan section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ad-Dukhan
- **Number of verses**: 59.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 44, in the twenty-fifth juz.
- **Name**: Ad-Dukhan means "the smoke", from verse 10.
- **One of the *Hawamim***: the seven surahs beginning with *Ha Mim*.
- **A note on narrations**: hadiths about the special virtue of reading Ad-Dukhan on Thursday night or Friday night are graded weak by hadith scholars, so they should not be relied on as authentic.

## Verses 1–8: The Blessed Night
﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٍۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُنذِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فِيهَا يُفۡرَقُ كُلُّ أَمۡرٍ حَكِيمٍ﴾ ﴿أَمۡرٗا مِّنۡ عِندِنَآۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُرۡسِلِينَ﴾ ﴿رَحۡمَةٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ ﴿رَبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآۖ إِن كُنتُم مُّوقِنِينَ﴾ ﴿لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۖ رَبُّكُمۡ وَرَبُّ ءَابَآئِكُمُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ (Quran 44:1-8)

**"By the clear Book, indeed We sent it down during a blessed night."** Most scholars say this is *Laylat al-Qadr*, the Night of Decree in Ramadan, as Surah Al-Qadr says: "Indeed, We sent it down during the Night of Decree." **"On that night is made distinct every wise matter"**: the decrees of the year are detailed and passed to the angels.

**"As mercy from your Lord"**: the Quran is a mercy, and so is the night it came down in.

## Verses 9–16: The Visible Smoke
﴿بَلۡ هُمۡ فِي شَكّٖ يَلۡعَبُونَ﴾ ﴿فَٱرۡتَقِبۡ يَوۡمَ تَأۡتِي ٱلسَّمَآءُ بِدُخَانٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ ﴿يَغۡشَى ٱلنَّاسَۖ هَٰذَا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿رَّبَّنَا ٱكۡشِفۡ عَنَّا ٱلۡعَذَابَ إِنَّا مُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿أَنَّىٰ لَهُمُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ وَقَدۡ جَآءَهُمۡ رَسُولࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ تَوَلَّوۡاْ عَنۡهُ وَقَالُواْ مُعَلَّمࣱ مَّجۡنُونٌ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كَاشِفُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابِ قَلِيلًاۚ إِنَّكُمۡ عَآئِدُونَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ نَبۡطِشُ ٱلۡبَطۡشَةَ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰٓ إِنَّا مُنتَقِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 44:9-16)

**"Then watch for the Day when the sky will bring a visible smoke, covering the people. This is a painful torment."**

**What is the smoke?** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud explained that when Quraysh persisted in rejecting the Prophet ﷺ, he prayed against them for years like the years of Yusuf. They were struck by famine until they ate bones, and a man would look at the sky and see something like smoke between himself and it from hunger and exhaustion. They asked the Prophet ﷺ to pray for rain, and it was lifted, but they returned to their ways. Ibn Mas'ud said: "*Indeed, We will remove the torment for a little; indeed, you will return*", and "*the Day We will strike with the greatest assault*" was the Day of Badr. Other scholars said the smoke is also one of the major signs before the Hour, as in the hadith of Hudhayfah in Sahih Muslim listing ten signs, including the smoke.

## Verses 17–33: Pharaoh's People
﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ فَتَنَّا قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَجَآءَهُمۡ رَسُولࣱ كَرِيمٌ﴾ ﴿أَنۡ أَدُّوٓاْ إِلَيَّ عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّي لَكُمۡ رَسُولٌ أَمِينࣱ﴾ ﴿وَأَن لَّا تَعۡلُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّيٓ ءَاتِيكُم بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِنِّي عُذۡتُ بِرَبِّي وَرَبِّكُمۡ أَن تَرۡجُمُونِ﴾ ﴿وَإِن لَّمۡ تُؤۡمِنُواْ لِي فَٱعۡتَزِلُونِ﴾ (Quran 44:17-21)

Musa came to Pharaoh: **"Hand over to me the servants of Allah"**, the Children of Israel. **"I am a trustworthy messenger."** He sought refuge in Allah from being stoned by them.

﴿فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنَّ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ قَوۡمࣱ مُّجۡرِمُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَسۡرِ بِعِبَادِي لَيۡلًا إِنَّكُم مُّتَّبَعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱتۡرُكِ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ رَهۡوًاۖ إِنَّهُمۡ جُندࣱ مُّغۡرَقُونَ﴾ ﴿كَمۡ تَرَكُواْ مِن جَنَّـٰتٖ وَعُيُونٖ﴾ ﴿وَزُرُوعٖ وَمَقَامٖ كَرِيمٖ﴾ ﴿وَنَعۡمَةٖ كَانُواْ فِيهَا فَٰكِهِينَ﴾ ﴿كَذَٰلِكَۖ وَأَوۡرَثۡنَٰهَا قَوۡمًا ءَاخَرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَمَا بَكَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُنظَرِينَ﴾ (Quran 44:22-29)

**The escape**: **"Set out with My servants by night"**, and **"leave the sea in stillness"**: the parted path, so Pharaoh's army would follow and drown.

**What they left behind**: **"How much they left behind of gardens and springs, and crops and noble sites, and comfort wherein they were amused."** All of it passed to others.

**"And the heaven and earth did not weep for them, nor were they reprieved."** A powerful image: their passing left no gap. Commentators mention that when a believer dies, the place where he prayed on earth and the gate through which his deeds ascended to heaven weep for him; but these people had no good deeds for anything to miss.

﴿وَلَقَدۡ نَجَّيۡنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ مِنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡمُهِينِ﴾ ﴿مِن فِرۡعَوۡنَۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ عَالِيٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدِ ٱخۡتَرۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٍ عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ مَا فِيهِ بَلَـٰٓؤࣱاْ مُّبِينٌ﴾ (Quran 44:30-33)

Allah saved the Children of Israel from the humiliating torment, **"and We certainly chose them by knowledge over the worlds"** of their time.

## Verses 34–42: The Day of Judgement
﴿إِنَّ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ لَيَقُولُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّا مَوۡتَتُنَا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُنشَرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَأۡتُواْ بِـَٔابَآئِنَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿أَهُمۡ خَيۡرٌ أَمۡ قَوۡمُ تُبَّعٖ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ مُجۡرِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 44:34-37)

The disbelievers said: **"There is only our first death, and we will not be resurrected."** **"Are they better or the people of Tubba' and those before them? We destroyed them."**

﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ﴾ ﴿مَا خَلَقۡنَٰهُمَآ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ مِيقَٰتُهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ لَا يُغۡنِي مَوۡلًى عَن مَّوۡلٗى شَيۡـٔٗا وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَن رَّحِمَ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 44:38-42)

**"We did not create the heavens and earth and what is between them in play. We created them only in truth."** Creation has a purpose. **"Indeed, the Day of Judgement is the appointed time for all of them."** **"The Day when no relation will avail a relation at all."**

## Verses 43–50: The Tree of Zaqqum
﴿إِنَّ شَجَرَتَ ٱلزَّقُّومِ﴾ ﴿طَعَامُ ٱلۡأَثِيمِ﴾ ﴿كَٱلۡمُهۡلِ يَغۡلِي فِي ٱلۡبُطُونِ﴾ ﴿كَغَلۡيِ ٱلۡحَمِيمِ﴾ ﴿خُذُوهُ فَٱعۡتِلُوهُ إِلَىٰ سَوَآءِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ صُبُّواْ فَوۡقَ رَأۡسِهِۦ مِنۡ عَذَابِ ٱلۡحَمِيمِ﴾ ﴿ذُقۡ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡكَرِيمُ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا مَا كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَمۡتَرُونَ﴾ (Quran 44:43-50)

**"Indeed, the tree of Zaqqum is food for the sinful. Like murky oil, it boils within bellies like the boiling of scalding water."** The sinner is told to be dragged into the middle of Hellfire, with scalding water poured over his head. **"Taste! Indeed, you are the honoured, the noble!"** Biting irony for one who thought himself mighty and noble in this world.

## Verses 51–57: The Secure Station
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي مَقَامٍ أَمِينٖ﴾ ﴿فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ وَعُيُونٖ﴾ ﴿يَلۡبَسُونَ مِن سُندُسٖ وَإِسۡتَبۡرَقٖ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ﴾ ﴿كَذَٰلِكَ وَزَوَّجۡنَٰهُم بِحُورٍ عِينٖ﴾ ﴿يَدۡعُونَ فِيهَا بِكُلِّ فَٰكِهَةٍ ءَامِنِينَ﴾ ﴿لَا يَذُوقُونَ فِيهَا ٱلۡمَوۡتَ إِلَّا ٱلۡمَوۡتَةَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰۖ وَوَقَىٰهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿فَضۡلٗا مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ (Quran 44:51-57)

**"Indeed, the righteous will be in a secure place, within gardens and springs, wearing fine silk and brocade, facing each other."** Contrast "a secure place" with the gardens and springs Pharaoh's people left behind: theirs were lost, these are permanent.

**"They will not taste death therein except the first death."** No more death. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said that death will be brought in the form of a ram and slaughtered, and it will be said: "O people of Paradise, eternity, no death; O people of the Fire, eternity, no death."

**"As bounty from your Lord. That is what is the great attainment."**

## Verses 58–59: Made Easy in Your Tongue
﴿فَإِنَّمَا يَسَّرۡنَٰهُ بِلِسَانِكَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَٱرۡتَقِبۡ إِنَّهُم مُّرۡتَقِبُونَ﴾ (Quran 44:58-59)

**"And We have only made it easy in your tongue that they might be reminded. So watch; indeed, they are watching."**

## Word Meanings
- **Laylah mubarakah**: a blessed night.
- **Yufraqu**: made distinct, detailed.
- **Dukhan**: smoke.
- **Yaghsha**: covers.
- **Al-Batshah al-kubra**: the greatest assault.
- **Rahwan**: still, parted.
- **Maqam karim**: noble sites.
- **Fakihin**: amused, enjoying.
- **Munzarin**: reprieved.
- **Tubba'**: a king of Yemen and his people.
- **Az-Zaqqum**: a tree in Hell.
- **Al-Muhl**: murky oil, molten metal.
- **Maqam amin**: a secure station.
- **Irtaqib**: watch, wait.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"A blessed night"**: the Quran's descent honours a single night.
2. **"Leave the sea in stillness"**: calm before destruction.
3. **"The heaven and earth did not weep for them"**: their absence went unnoticed by creation.
4. **"Taste! You are the honoured, the noble!"**: sharp irony.
5. **Gardens and springs lost vs. gardens and springs in security.**

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Ha Mim*, the meem stretched six counts.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *laylatin mubarakah* (verse 3) and *bi-dukhanin mubin* (verse 10), the tanween merges into the meem.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'u* (verse 10).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *nabtishu* (verse 16), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna*, on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Az-Zukhruf before it ended with "So turn aside from them and say: Peace. But they are going to know", and Ad-Dukhan describes what they will come to know. Surah Al-Jathiyah after it begins with the same letters *Ha Mim* and continues the signs of Allah.

## Lessons From the People Who Left Gardens Behind
1. **Possessions pass to others**: plan for what remains.
2. **Leave a gap when you go**: be someone whose absence is felt by mosques and good causes.
3. **Arrogance ends in humiliation**: "You are the honoured, the noble!"
4. **Allah saves the oppressed**, even after long suffering.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Honour Laylat al-Qadr** and seek it in the last ten nights of Ramadan.
2. **Return to Allah after relief**: do not be like those who returned to their ways.
3. **Remember that creation has purpose.**
4. **Do good deeds the earth and heaven will miss.**
5. **Ask Allah for the secure station.**
6. **Use the ease of the Quran**: read and reflect.

## How to Memorise Surah Ad-Dukhan

- **Ask them: what good deed will the earth remember you for?**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the blessed night in Surah Ad-Dukhan?
Most scholars say it is Laylat al-Qadr in Ramadan, when the Quran was sent down.

### What is the smoke in verse 10?
Ibn Mas'ud explained it as what Quraysh saw during their famine, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim; other scholars said it is also one of the signs before the Hour.

### What does "the heaven and earth did not weep for them" mean?
Their passing left nothing to be missed, because they had no good deeds.

### What is the tree of Zaqqum?
A tree in Hell whose fruit is the food of the sinners, boiling in their bellies.

### Is there an authentic hadith about reading Ad-Dukhan on Friday night?
The narrations about its special virtue on Thursday or Friday night are graded weak by hadith scholars.

### What does "They will not taste death therein except the first death" mean?
In Paradise there is no more death; the only death was the one in this world.

### How many verses are in Surah Ad-Dukhan?
59 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

### What does "leave the sea in stillness" mean?
Musa was told to leave the parted sea as it was, calm and open, so that Pharaoh's army would follow and be drowned.

### What does "Taste! Indeed, you are the honoured, the noble" mean?
It is irony addressed to the sinner who thought himself mighty and noble in this world.

## Conclusion
Surah Ad-Dukhan begins with the blessed night of the Quran and ends with a secure station in Paradise. In between, it warns of smoke and the greatest assault, and shows a people who left behind gardens and springs without the heaven or earth shedding a tear. Live so that your absence will be felt, and work for the station where there is no more death.
