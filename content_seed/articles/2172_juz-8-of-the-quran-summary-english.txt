Juz 8 begins with ﴿۞وَلَوۡ أَنَّنَا نَزَّلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ وَكَلَّمَهُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَحَشَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖ قُبُلٗا مَّا كَانُواْ لِيُؤۡمِنُوٓاْ﴾, "Even if We had sent down angels to them, the dead had spoken to them, and We had gathered everything before them, they would not have believed", so it is called **Juz Wa Law Annana**. It runs from **Al-An'am 6:111 to Al-A'raf 7:87**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 142 to 161**.

This juz gathers the close of **Surah Al-An'am**, with its ten commandments and pure declaration of monotheism, and the opening of **Surah Al-A'raf**, the longest Makki surah, with the fullest account of Adam and Iblis and then the procession of prophets with their nations.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **the struggle between guidance and misguidance since the beginning of creation**. It starts by stating that every prophet has enemies among the devils of mankind and jinn, exposes the idolaters' invented laws, and sets against them the ten divine commandments and Allah's straight path. Al-A'raf then begins with the root of the struggle: Iblis refuses to bow to Adam and vows to mislead his descendants, so the calls "O children of Adam" follow, warning against his temptation. The surah then shows the struggle's outcome in the Hereafter between the people of Paradise and the people of the Fire, and its examples in history with Nuh, Hud, Salih, Lut and Shu'ayb.

The message: **the path is one, the enemy is known, and the ending is written.**

## Part one: the close of Surah Al-An'am (111-165)

### 1. Even if every sign came (111-117)
Allah states that the stubborn will not believe even if angels descended and the dead spoke to them. Then: ﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ جَعَلۡنَا لِكُلِّ نَبِيٍّ عَدُوّٗا شَيَٰطِينَ ٱلۡإِنسِ وَٱلۡجِنِّ يُوحِي بَعۡضُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ زُخۡرُفَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ غُرُورٗاۚ﴾, "And thus We have made for every prophet an enemy: devils from mankind and jinn, inspiring one another with decorated speech to deceive." Then a warning against following the majority: ﴿وَإِن تُطِعۡ أَكۡثَرَ مَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يُضِلُّوكَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّ﴾, "If you obey most of those on earth, they will lead you astray from Allah's way; they follow nothing but assumption."

**Lesson**: falsehood comes with decoration and fine words, and truth is not measured by the number of its followers.

### 2. Slaughtered food, life and light (118-127)
The command to eat what Allah's name has been mentioned over, and not what it has not. Then a beautiful image of the difference between believer and disbeliever: ﴿أَوَمَن كَانَ مَيۡتٗا فَأَحۡيَيۡنَٰهُ وَجَعَلۡنَا لَهُۥ نُورٗا يَمۡشِي بِهِۦ فِي ٱلنَّاسِ كَمَن مَّثَلُهُۥ فِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ لَيۡسَ بِخَارِجٖ مِّنۡهَاۚ﴾, "Is one who was dead and We gave him life and made for him a light by which to walk among people like one who is in darkness, never emerging from it?" Faith is life and light; disbelief is death and darkness.

Then: ﴿فَمَن يُرِدِ ٱللَّهُ أَن يَهۡدِيَهُۥ يَشۡرَحۡ صَدۡرَهُۥ لِلۡإِسۡلَٰمِۖ وَمَن يُرِدۡ أَن يُضِلَّهُۥ يَجۡعَلۡ صَدۡرَهُۥ ضَيِّقًا حَرَجٗا كَأَنَّمَا يَصَّعَّدُ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِۚ﴾, "Whoever Allah wants to guide, He opens his chest to Islam; and whoever He wants to leave astray, He makes his chest tight and constricted as if he were climbing into the sky." The image of tightness while rising into the sky is one people know well today.

### 3. Jinn and humans questioned on the Day (128-135)
Allah gathers all and asks: ﴿يَٰمَعۡشَرَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِ أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِكُمۡ رُسُلࣱ مِّنكُمۡ يَقُصُّونَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِي﴾, "O company of jinn and mankind, did there not come to you messengers from among you, relating to you My verses?" They testify against themselves. Then the rule of Allah's justice: your Lord would not destroy towns unjustly while their people were unaware, and each has ranks according to what they did.

### 4. The idolaters' invented laws (136-140)
The Quran exposes pre-Islamic superstitions: they assigned Allah a share of crops and livestock and a share to their idols, forbade some animals and permitted others without knowledge, and worse, killed their children: ﴿قَدۡ خَسِرَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَتَلُوٓاْ أَوۡلَٰدَهُمۡ سَفَهَۢا بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖ﴾, "Those who killed their children foolishly, without knowledge, have lost." This is where the surah's name, The Cattle, comes from.

**Lesson**: making things lawful or unlawful is Allah's right alone; whoever legislates without knowledge has invented a lie about Allah.

### 5. Real blessings and the right of the harvest (141-147)
﴿۞وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَ جَنَّـٰتٖ مَّعۡرُوشَٰتٖ وَغَيۡرَ مَعۡرُوشَٰتٖ وَٱلنَّخۡلَ وَٱلزَّرۡعَ مُخۡتَلِفًا أُكُلُهُۥ﴾, "It is He who produces gardens trellised and untrellised, palm trees and crops of different taste", then ﴿وَءَاتُواْ حَقَّهُۥ يَوۡمَ حَصَادِهِۦۖ وَلَا تُسۡرِفُوٓاْۚ﴾, "and give its due on the day of its harvest, and do not be wasteful." Then the eight pairs of livestock, and a challenge to the idolaters to bring knowledge for what they forbade. Then the true prohibitions: ﴿قُل لَّآ أَجِدُ فِي مَآ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ مُحَرَّمًا عَلَىٰ طَاعِمٖ يَطۡعَمُهُۥٓ إِلَّآ أَن يَكُونَ مَيۡتَةً أَوۡ دَمٗا مَّسۡفُوحًا أَوۡ لَحۡمَ خِنزِيرٖ﴾, "Say: I do not find in what has been revealed to me anything forbidden to one who eats, except carrion, spilled blood or pork..."

### 6. The false argument from fate (148-150)
﴿سَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ أَشۡرَكُواْ لَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مَآ أَشۡرَكۡنَا وَلَآ ءَابَآؤُنَا وَلَا حَرَّمۡنَا مِن شَيۡءٖۚ﴾, "Those who associate partners will say: if Allah had willed, we would not have associated partners, nor our fathers, nor forbidden anything." Allah answers that people before them denied in the same way, and challenges them: do you have any knowledge to show us? ﴿قُلۡ فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡحُجَّةُ ٱلۡبَٰلِغَةُۖ﴾, "Say: to Allah belongs the conclusive argument."

**Lesson**: using fate to excuse sin is a false argument. Allah gave people choice, sent messengers and established proof.

### 7. The ten commandments (151-153)
One of the greatest passages of the Quran, answering invented prohibitions with the true ones: ﴿۞قُلۡ تَعَالَوۡاْ أَتۡلُ مَا حَرَّمَ رَبُّكُمۡ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ﴾, "Say: come, I will recite what your Lord has truly forbidden you":
1. Associate nothing with Him.
2. Be good to parents.
3. Do not kill your children out of poverty.
4. Do not approach indecencies, open or hidden.
5. Do not kill the soul Allah has made sacred, except by right.
6. Do not approach the orphan's wealth except in the best way.
7. Give full measure and weight in justice.
8. When you speak, be just, even towards a relative.
9. Fulfil Allah's covenant.
10. ﴿وَأَنَّ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطِي مُسۡتَقِيمٗا فَٱتَّبِعُوهُۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ ٱلسُّبُلَ فَتَفَرَّقَ بِكُمۡ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۚ﴾, "And this is My path, straight, so follow it, and do not follow other ways, for they will separate you from His way."

The three verses end with three rising endings: ﴿لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ "so that you may understand", then ﴿لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ "so that you may remember", then ﴿لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ﴾ "so that you may be God-conscious." The mind grasps, remembrance makes it firm, and taqwa protects. Imam Ahmad reports from Ibn Mas'ud that the Prophet ﷺ drew a line and said "This is the path of Allah", then drew lines to its right and left and said "These are paths, on each of which is a devil calling to it", and recited this verse. Read [The Ten Commandments of Al-An'am](/blog/the-ten-commandments-of-al-anam-english/).

### 8. Musa, the Quran and the awaited signs (154-160)
The Book given to Musa is mentioned, then: ﴿وَهَٰذَا كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مُبَارَكࣱ فَٱتَّبِعُوهُ وَٱتَّقُواْ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ﴾, "And this is a blessed Book We revealed, so follow it and be God-conscious, that you may receive mercy." Then a warning about the Day when some of your Lord's signs come, and faith will not benefit a soul that did not believe before; the Prophet ﷺ explained it in Al-Bukhari and Muslim as the rising of the sun from the west. Then: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ فَرَّقُواْ دِينَهُمۡ وَكَانُواْ شِيَعٗا لَّسۡتَ مِنۡهُمۡ فِي شَيۡءٍۚ﴾, "Those who divided their religion and became sects, you have nothing to do with them." Then Allah's generosity: ﴿مَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُۥ عَشۡرُ أَمۡثَالِهَاۖ وَمَن جَآءَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ فَلَا يُجۡزَىٰٓ إِلَّا مِثۡلَهَا﴾, "Whoever brings a good deed will have ten like it, and whoever brings an evil deed will be recompensed only with its like."

### 9. The declaration of pure monotheism (161-165)
The surah closes with a complete declaration: ﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحۡيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "Say: my prayer, my sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds", ﴿لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُۥۖ وَبِذَٰلِكَ أُمِرۡتُ وَأَنَا۠ أَوَّلُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾, "He has no partner; this I have been commanded, and I am the first of the Muslims." Worship, life and death all belong to Allah. Then ﴿وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةࣱ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۚ﴾, "no bearer of burdens bears another's burden", and the last verse: Allah made you successors on earth and raised some above others in rank to test you. Read [My Prayer and My Sacrifice: 6:162](/blog/inna-salati-wa-nusuki-meaning-english/).

**Notice the beginning and the end**: Al-An'am opened with praise of the Creator and closes by dedicating all of life to Him. The surah is a journey from knowing the Creator to devoting one's life to Him.

## Part two: the opening of Surah Al-A'raf (1-87)

Al-A'raf is a Makki surah of 206 verses, the longest Makki surah. It is named "The Heights" after the barrier between Paradise and the Fire on which there are men who recognise everyone by their marks. It opens with the letters ﴿الٓمٓصٓ﴾. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-A'raf Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-araf-meaning-english/).

### 1. The Book and the Scale (1-9)
﴿كِتَٰبٌ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ فَلَا يَكُن فِي صَدۡرِكَ حَرَجࣱ مِّنۡهُ لِتُنذِرَ بِهِۦ وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "A Book revealed to you, so let there be no discomfort in your chest from it, that you may warn by it and as a reminder to the believers." Then the command to follow what Allah revealed, the ruins of destroyed towns, the questioning of messengers and those sent to, and ﴿وَٱلۡوَزۡنُ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ٱلۡحَقُّۚ﴾, "and the weighing that Day will be the truth."

### 2. Adam and Iblis (10-25)
This is the fullest telling of Adam's story in the Quran:
- Creation, shaping and the command to prostrate.
- Iblis's refusal and his argument: ﴿قَالَ مَا مَنَعَكَ أَلَّا تَسۡجُدَ إِذۡ أَمَرۡتُكَۖ قَالَ أَنَا۠ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡهُ خَلَقۡتَنِي مِن نَّارٖ وَخَلَقۡتَهُۥ مِن طِينٖ﴾, "He said: what prevented you from prostrating when I commanded you? He said: I am better than him; You created me from fire and him from clay." He was the first to reason falsely and the first to be arrogant because of origin.
- His request for respite and his vow: ﴿لَأَقۡعُدَنَّ لَهُمۡ صِرَٰطَكَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ﴾, "I will surely sit in wait for them on Your straight path", then "I will come at them from before them, from behind them, from their right and from their left." Notice he did not say "from above them": the door of heaven and of supplication stays open.
- The whispering, eating from the tree, and the exposure of their nakedness.
- Repentance: ﴿قَالَا رَبَّنَا ظَلَمۡنَآ أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمۡ تَغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَتَرۡحَمۡنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ﴾, "They said: our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we will surely be among the losers."
- The descent to earth: in it you will live, in it you will die, and from it you will be brought out.

**Compared with Al-Baqarah**: Al-Baqarah focuses on honouring Adam with knowledge and stewardship; Al-A'raf focuses on Iblis's enmity and methods of temptation, because the whole surah is about the struggle with Shaytan and his followers.

### 3. The calls "O children of Adam" (26-36)
After the story come four addresses to Adam's descendants:
1. ﴿يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ قَدۡ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكُمۡ لِبَاسٗا يُوَٰرِي سَوۡءَٰتِكُمۡ وَرِيشٗاۖ وَلِبَاسُ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرࣱۚ﴾, "O children of Adam, We have sent down clothing to cover your nakedness and as adornment, but the clothing of God-consciousness is best."
2. ﴿يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ لَا يَفۡتِنَنَّكُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ كَمَآ أَخۡرَجَ أَبَوَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ﴾, "O children of Adam, do not let Shaytan tempt you as he removed your parents from Paradise."
3. ﴿۞يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ خُذُواْ زِينَتَكُمۡ عِندَ كُلِّ مَسۡجِدٖ وَكُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ وَلَا تُسۡرِفُوٓاْۚ﴾, "O children of Adam, take your adornment at every place of prayer, and eat and drink, but do not be excessive." Some Arabs used to circle the Kaaba naked; in Sahih Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas said a woman would circle the House naked, and this was revealed.
4. ﴿يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ إِمَّا يَأۡتِيَنَّكُمۡ رُسُلࣱ مِّنكُمۡ يَقُصُّونَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِي﴾, "O children of Adam, if messengers come to you from among you relating My verses..."

**The link**: Iblis began his temptation by stripping the clothing, so the calls speak of sending down clothing and taking adornment, with the clothing of taqwa best of all. Then: ﴿قُلۡ مَنۡ حَرَّمَ زِينَةَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَخۡرَجَ لِعِبَادِهِۦ وَٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ مِنَ ٱلرِّزۡقِۚ﴾, "Say: who has forbidden the adornment of Allah which He produced for His servants, and the good things of provision?", followed by what Allah truly forbade: indecencies, sin, oppression, shirk, and speaking about Allah without knowledge.

### 4. Scenes of the Hereafter and the people of the heights (37-53)
- The people of the Fire curse one another; every nation that enters curses its sister.
- ﴿لَا تُفَتَّحُ لَهُمۡ أَبۡوَٰبُ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَلَا يَدۡخُلُونَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ حَتَّىٰ يَلِجَ ٱلۡجَمَلُ فِي سَمِّ ٱلۡخِيَاطِۚ﴾, "The gates of heaven will not be opened for them, nor will they enter Paradise until a camel passes through the eye of a needle."
- The purity of the people of Paradise: ﴿وَنَزَعۡنَا مَا فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنۡ غِلّٖ﴾, "We will remove whatever resentment is in their hearts", and their words: ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي هَدَىٰنَا لِهَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لِنَهۡتَدِيَ لَوۡلَآ أَنۡ هَدَىٰنَا ٱللَّهُۖ﴾, "Praise be to Allah who guided us to this; we would never have been guided had Allah not guided us."
- The call between the two groups: have you found what your Lord promised to be true? They will say: yes.
- **The people of the heights**: ﴿وَبَيۡنَهُمَا حِجَابࣱۚ وَعَلَى ٱلۡأَعۡرَافِ رِجَالࣱ يَعۡرِفُونَ كُلَّۢا بِسِيمَىٰهُمۡۚ﴾, "Between them is a barrier, and on the heights are men who recognise all by their marks." The best-known view among commentators is that they are people whose good and bad deeds are equal, who stand there and then enter Paradise by Allah's mercy.
- The people of the Fire ask the people of Paradise for water.

### 5. Signs of creation and the etiquette of supplication (54-58)
﴿إِنَّ رَبَّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ﴾, "Your Lord is Allah who created the heavens and the earth in six days, then rose over the Throne", then ﴿أَلَا لَهُ ٱلۡخَلۡقُ وَٱلۡأَمۡرُۗ﴾, "His is the creation and the command." Then the etiquette of supplication: ﴿ٱدۡعُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ تَضَرُّعٗا وَخُفۡيَةًۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُعۡتَدِينَ﴾, "Call upon your Lord humbly and privately; He does not like transgressors", ﴿وَلَا تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بَعۡدَ إِصۡلَٰحِهَا وَٱدۡعُوهُ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعًاۚ إِنَّ رَحۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ قَرِيبࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾, "Do not cause corruption on earth after its reform, and call upon Him in fear and hope; Allah's mercy is near to those who do good." Then the winds as good news, the rain, and the good land whose plants come out by its Lord's permission. Read [Call Upon Your Lord Humbly: 7:55](/blog/udu-rabbakum-tadarruan-meaning-english/).

### 6. The procession of prophets (59-87)
The surah presents five stories in a similar pattern, and each prophet begins with the same words: ﴿يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥٓ﴾, "O my people, worship Allah; you have no god other than Him."

| Prophet | People | Verses | Main problem | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nuh | His people | 59-64 | Shirk and arrogance | The flood |
| Hud | 'Ad | 65-72 | Pride in their strength | The wind |
| Salih | Thamud | 73-79 | Killing the she-camel | The earthquake |
| Lut | His people | 80-84 | Indecency | A rain of stones |
| Shu'ayb | Madyan | 85-93 | Cheating in measure | The earthquake |

- **Nuh**: the chiefs said "we see you in clear error", and he replied: "There is no error in me; I am a messenger from the Lord of the worlds." See [Surah Nuh Meaning in English](/blog/surah-nuh-meaning-english/).
- **Hud**: reminded them that they were successors after the people of Nuh and that Allah had increased them in stature.
- **Salih**: ﴿هَٰذِهِۦ نَاقَةُ ٱللَّهِ لَكُمۡ ءَايَةࣰۖ﴾, "This is the she-camel of Allah, a sign for you." They hamstrung her and defied their Lord's command: ﴿فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلرَّجۡفَةُ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ فِي دَارِهِمۡ جَٰثِمِينَ﴾, "So the earthquake seized them, and they lay lifeless in their home."
- **Lut**: ﴿أَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلۡفَٰحِشَةَ مَا سَبَقَكُم بِهَا مِنۡ أَحَدٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "Do you commit an indecency no one in the worlds committed before you?" Their answer: expel them from your town; they are people who keep themselves pure.
- **Shu'ayb**: ﴿فَأَوۡفُواْ ٱلۡكَيۡلَ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَ وَلَا تَبۡخَسُواْ ٱلنَّاسَ أَشۡيَآءَهُمۡ وَلَا تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بَعۡدَ إِصۡلَٰحِهَاۚ﴾, "Give full measure and weight, do not cheat people of their things, and do not cause corruption on earth after its reform." The juz ends with his words to his people: ﴿فَٱصۡبِرُواْ حَتَّىٰ يَحۡكُمَ ٱللَّهُ بَيۡنَنَاۚ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ﴾, "So be patient until Allah judges between us, and He is the best of judges." See [Surah Hud Meaning in English](/blog/surah-hud-meaning-english/) for the same prophets told in another surah.

**Why repeat the pattern?** Because the message is the same in every age while the diseases vary: shirk everywhere, plus each people's particular illness: arrogance, pride in power, indecency, or economic corruption. Each prophet brought monotheism and then treated his people's specific disease.

## Tajweed points in Juz 8

### Compulsory letter madd in ﴿الٓمٓصٓ﴾
Read *Alif, Laam, Meem, Saad*. Laam, Meem and Saad are each stretched six counts, and the meem of "Laam" merges into "Meem".

### ﴿يَصَّعَّدُ﴾
Two doubled letters in a row, saad and 'ayn: give each its full weight.

### Iqlab in ﴿مِّنۢ بَيۡنِ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ﴾
Sakin noon before ba becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah.

### ﴿أَنَا۠﴾ in 6:163 and 7:12
The alif is pronounced when stopping and dropped when continuing.

### The repeated ﴿يَٰقَوۡمِ﴾
Read with a kasrah on the meem and no ya; do not add a ya sound. For the madd rules in this juz see [Madd Rules in Tajweed](/blog/madd-rules-tajweed-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. "What prevented you from prostrating?"
- Al-A'raf 12: ﴿مَا مَنَعَكَ أَلَّا تَسۡجُدَ إِذۡ أَمَرۡتُكَۖ﴾, with an extra *la*.
- Sad 75: ﴿يَـٰٓإِبۡلِيسُ مَا مَنَعَكَ أَن تَسۡجُدَ لِمَا خَلَقۡتُ بِيَدَيَّۖ﴾, without *la*.

### 2. How the prophets' stories open
- Nuh: ﴿لَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ فَقَالَ﴾, with *fa*.
- Hud, Salih and Shu'ayb: ﴿وَإِلَىٰ عَادٍ أَخَاهُمۡ هُودٗاۚ قَالَ﴾, without *fa*.

### 3. The chiefs in the stories of Nuh and Hud
- Nuh, 60: ﴿قَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦٓ﴾, "the chiefs of his people said."
- Hud, 66: ﴿قَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦٓ﴾, "the chiefs **who disbelieved** among his people said."
**Key**: commentators note that some of Hud's chiefs believed, so the disbelievers among them are specified.

### 4. The punishment of Thamud in Al-A'raf and Hud
- Al-A'raf 78: ﴿فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلرَّجۡفَةُ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ فِي دَارِهِمۡ جَٰثِمِينَ﴾, "the earthquake" with "home" (singular).
- Hud 67: ﴿وَأَخَذَ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ فِي دِيَٰرِهِمۡ جَٰثِمِينَ﴾, "the blast" with "homes" (plural).

### 5. Endings of the commandments
*Ta'qilun*, then *tadhakkarun*, then *tattaqun*, in that order.

## How to memorize Juz 8 with understanding

1. **The end of Al-An'am is a series of arguments**: link each section to its question: who misleads? who legislates? what is truly forbidden?
2. **Learn the ten commandments by counting** them on your fingers in order.
3. **Compare Adam's story in Al-A'raf with Al-Baqarah** in a written table to prevent mixing them.
4. **Number the four "O children of Adam" calls** in your Mushaf.
5. **Memorize the five prophets' stories as a fixed template** with different details, and write a table of the differences.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 8

- **Do not be deceived by decorated falsehood** or by its many followers.
- **Faith is life and light**; keep seeking it.
- **Do not forbid or permit without knowledge.**
- **Pay what is due on your wealth at harvest time.**
- **Do not use fate as an excuse for falling short.**
- **Check yourself against the ten commandments.**
- **Make your prayer, sacrifice, life and death for Allah.**
- **Beware arrogance**, the first sin.
- **When you slip, say: our Lord, we have wronged ourselves.**
- **Dress well for prayer, and do not be wasteful.**
- **Call upon your Lord humbly and privately.**
- **Give full measure and weight** in all your dealings.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 8 called "Wa Law Annana"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞وَلَوۡ أَنَّنَا نَزَّلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ﴾.

### What are the ten commandments in the Quran?
Those in Al-An'am 6:151-153: monotheism, goodness to parents, not killing children, avoiding indecencies, not killing unlawfully, protecting orphans' wealth, full measure and weight, justice in speech, fulfilling Allah's covenant, and following the straight path.

### Where does Juz 8 end?
At Al-A'raf 7:87. Juz 9 begins with ﴿۞قَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦ لَنُخۡرِجَنَّكَ يَٰشُعَيۡبُ﴾, "The arrogant chiefs of his people said: we will surely drive you out, O Shu'ayb."

### Who are the people of the heights?
The best-known view is that they are people whose good and bad deeds are equal; they stand on a barrier between Paradise and the Fire and then Allah admits them to Paradise by His mercy.

### How does Adam's story differ between Al-Baqarah and Al-A'raf?
Al-Baqarah highlights Adam's honour through knowledge and stewardship; Al-A'raf highlights Iblis's enmity and his methods, then warns the children of Adam against him.

## Conclusion

Juz 8 draws the map of the great struggle: an ancient enemy who vowed to lie in wait on the straight path, a clear path drawn by the ten commandments, a human being choosing between the clothing of taqwa and Shaytan's temptation, clear destinies in the Hereafter, and examples in history of peoples who chose and then met what they chose.
