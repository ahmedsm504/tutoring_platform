**Surah An-Nasr** (Victory) is short, but it carries a deep message: success should lead to **humility**, not pride.

## Ayah by ayah
| Ayah | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿إِذَا جَآءَ نَصۡرُ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡفَتۡحُ﴾ | When the help of Allah and the victory come |
| ﴿وَرَأَيۡتَ ٱلنَّاسَ يَدۡخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ ٱللَّهِ أَفۡوَاجٗا﴾ | And you see people entering Allah's religion in crowds |
| ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ تَوَّابَۢا﴾ | Glorify with praise of your Lord and seek His forgiveness; He is ever accepting of repentance |

## Ibn ʿAbbas's understanding
- ʿUmar asked the senior companions about this surah, and Ibn ʿAbbas said it was a sign that **the Prophet's life ﷺ was nearing its end**. ʿUmar said: I know of it only what you know [Al-Bukhari].

## The Prophet's practice
- ʿAishah said that after it was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ often said in his bowing and prostration: «Subḥānakallāhumma Rabbanā wa biḥamdik, Allāhummaghfir lī» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

## Lessons
- **After every success**: praise Allah and seek forgiveness.
- **Finishing a task** is a time for istighfār, as after prayer and Hajj.
- **Victory belongs to Allah**: *naṣrullāh*.
