﴿وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌۖ أُجِيبُ دَعۡوَةَ ٱلدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِۖ فَلۡيَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لِي وَلۡيُؤۡمِنُواْ بِي لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡشُدُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:186]

## Meaning
*And when My servants ask you about Me, indeed I am near. I respond to the call of the caller when he calls upon Me. So let them respond to Me and believe in Me, that they may be rightly guided.*

## No intermediary
- Elsewhere when people ask a question, Allah says *Say* (to the Prophet ﷺ). Here the answer comes **directly**: *I am near.* Scholars note this shows there is no barrier between the servant and his Lord in duʿa.

## Why among the fasting verses?
- The verse sits between the rulings of fasting, a hint that the fasting person's duʿa is especially hoped to be answered.

## Two conditions in the verse
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Let them respond to Me* | Obey Allah's commands |
| *Let them believe in Me* | Trust that He hears and answers |

## How Allah answers
- The Prophet ﷺ taught that every duʿa is answered in one of three ways: granting it, averting an equal harm, or saving its reward for the Hereafter (narrated by Ahmad).

## Lessons
- **Call on Allah yourself**, in any language and at any time.
- **Do not lose patience**: a delayed answer is not a rejected one.
