﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلتَّوَّـٰبِينَ وَيُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَطَهِّرِينَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:222]

## The context
- The verse discusses rulings about menstruation and marital relations, then closes with this general statement of Allah's love.

## Two loved groups
| Group | Purity |
|---|---|
| *At-tawwabin* | Inner purity from sins through repentance |
| *Al-mutatahhirin* | Outer purity through wudu, ghusl and cleanliness |

## "Tawwab": again and again
- *Tawwab* is an intensive form: one who repents **repeatedly**. Allah loves the one who returns every time he falls.

## Cleanliness is worship
- The Prophet ﷺ said: *Purity is half of faith* (narrated by Muslim).

## Love, not just forgiveness
- The verse does not only say Allah forgives the repentant, but that He **loves** them. Repentance raises you.

## Lessons
- **Do not tire of repenting.**
- **Keep body and heart clean.**
