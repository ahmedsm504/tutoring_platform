﴿فَإِذَا قَضَيۡتُم مَّنَٰسِكَكُمۡ فَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَذِكۡرِكُمۡ ءَابَآءَكُمۡ أَوۡ أَشَدَّ ذِكۡرٗاۗ فَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَقُولُ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمَا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مِنۡ خَلَٰقٖ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:200]

## The custom
- Scholars mention that after Hajj, people in pre-Islamic times would stand and boast of their fathers' glories. Allah told them to replace that with **His remembrance**, even more.

## "Or more"
- Allah is more deserving of remembrance than ancestors; He is the true Giver.

## Two kinds of people
| Group | Their duʿa |
|---|---|
| Seekers of this world only | *Our Lord, give us in this world*, with no share in the Hereafter |
| Seekers of both | *Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter* (2:201) |

## Ending worship with dhikr
- After prayer: *remember Allah* (An-Nisa 4:103). After Hajj: this verse.

## Lessons
- **Take pride in obedience**, not lineage.
- **End every act of worship** with dhikr and istighfar.
