## "Remember Allah as You Remember Your Forefathers or More": Quran 2:200 Explained

﴿فَإِذَا قَضَيۡتُم مَّنَٰسِكَكُمۡ فَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَذِكۡرِكُمۡ ءَابَآءَكُمۡ أَوۡ أَشَدَّ ذِكۡرٗاۗ فَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَقُولُ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمَا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مِنۡ خَلَٰقٖ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:200]

### The custom
- Scholars mention that after Hajj, people in pre-Islamic times would stand and boast of their fathers' glories. Allah told them to replace that with **His remembrance**, even more.

### "Or more"
- Allah is more deserving of remembrance than ancestors; He is the true Giver.

### Two kinds of people
| Group | Their duʿa |
|---|---|
| Seekers of this world only | *Our Lord, give us in this world*, with no share in the Hereafter |
| Seekers of both | *Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter* (2:201) |

### Ending worship with dhikr
- After prayer: *remember Allah* (An-Nisa 4:103). After Hajj: this verse.

### Lessons
- **Take pride in obedience**, not lineage.
- **End every act of worship** with dhikr and istighfar.

## "Our Lord, Give Us Good in This World and the Next": Quran 2:201 Explained

﴿وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَقُولُ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٗ وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ حَسَنَةٗ وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:201]

### Meaning
*And among them are those who say: Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.*

### The context
- The verse comes in the passage on Hajj. Allah describes two types of people: those who ask only for this world, and those who ask for **both**.

### The Prophet's most frequent duʿa
- Anas ibn Malik reported that the most frequent duʿa of the Prophet ﷺ was this one (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### What is "good"?
| In this world | In the Hereafter |
|---|---|
| Health and safety | Forgiveness |
| Lawful provision | Easy reckoning |
| A righteous spouse and family | Paradise |
| Beneficial knowledge | Salvation from the Fire |

- Scholars say the word *hasanah* is left open so it includes **every good**.

### Balance
- Islam does not ask you to abandon this world, nor to make it your only goal. This duʿa holds both together.

### Lessons
- **Make it a daily duʿa**, especially between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone in tawaf, where it is commonly recited.
- **Ask for the world without forgetting the Hereafter.**

## "Enter into Islam Completely": Quran 2:208 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱدۡخُلُواْ فِي ٱلسِّلۡمِ كَآفَّةٗ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوࣱّ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:208]

### What is *silm*?
- Ibn ʿAbbas and others explained it here as **Islam**.

### *Kaffah*
| Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Describing the religion | Enter all the laws of Islam |
| Describing the believers | Enter all together |

- Both meanings go together: everyone takes the whole religion.

### Satan's footsteps
- Satan rarely invites to a major sin at once; he leads **step by step**. Beware the small beginnings.

### No picking and choosing
- A Muslim does not take what suits his desires and leave what does not.

### Lessons
- **Review your life**: is any area outside Islam's guidance?
- **Close the door** at the first step.

## "Perhaps You Dislike Something and It Is Good for You": Quran 2:216 Explained

﴿كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡقِتَالُ وَهُوَ كُرۡهࣱ لَّكُمۡۖ وَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تَكۡرَهُواْ شَيۡـٔٗا وَهُوَ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡۖ وَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تُحِبُّواْ شَيۡـٔٗا وَهُوَ شَرࣱّ لَّكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:216]

### The context
- The verse was revealed about fighting in defence, which people naturally dislike. But its principle applies to **all of life**.

### Two sides of the same truth
| We dislike | But it may be |
|---|---|
| A closed door | Protection from harm |
| Losing a job | The start of something better |
| Hard effort | The path to reward |

| We love | But it may be |
|---|---|
| Easy pleasure | Harmful in the end |
| Something we insisted on | A trial we were not ready for |

### The key sentence
- *Allah knows and you do not know.* Our view is limited to the present; Allah sees beginning and end.

### A Quranic example
- Musa's mother threw her baby into the river, the hardest thing for a mother. It became his rescue and his return to her.

### Peace with decree
- This verse does not stop you from working and planning. It teaches you to **accept the result** after doing your best.

### Lessons
- **Do not judge too quickly** what happens to you.
- **Pray istikharah** and trust Allah's choice.

## "Allah Loves Those Who Repent and Purify Themselves": Quran 2:222 Explained

﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلتَّوَّـٰبِينَ وَيُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَطَهِّرِينَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:222]

### The context
- The verse discusses rulings about menstruation and marital relations, then closes with this general statement of Allah's love.

### Two loved groups
| Group | Purity |
|---|---|
| *At-tawwabin* | Inner purity from sins through repentance |
| *Al-mutatahhirin* | Outer purity through wudu, ghusl and cleanliness |

### "Tawwab": again and again
- *Tawwab* is an intensive form: one who repents **repeatedly**. Allah loves the one who returns every time he falls.

### Cleanliness is worship
- The Prophet ﷺ said: *Purity is half of faith* (narrated by Muslim).

### Love, not just forgiveness
- The verse does not only say Allah forgives the repentant, but that He **loves** them. Repentance raises you.

### Lessons
- **Do not tire of repenting.**
- **Keep body and heart clean.**

## "Do Not Forget Graciousness Between You": Quran 2:237 Explained

﴿وَأَن تَعۡفُوٓاْ أَقۡرَبُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۚ وَلَا تَنسَوُاْ ٱلۡفَضۡلَ بَيۡنَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:237]

### The context
- The verse discusses divorce **before consummation** after a dowry was set: half of the dowry is due, unless one side pardons the rest.

### Pardon is nearer to taqwa
- Giving up part of your right voluntarily is closer to piety than insisting on every detail.

### "Do not forget graciousness"
- *Fadl* is kindness beyond what is owed. Even when a relationship ends, do not forget the good that was between you.

### A model for all separations
| Situation | Application |
|---|---|
| Divorce | No humiliation, no exposing secrets |
| Ending a partnership | Fair settlement and kind words |
| Leaving a job | Gratitude and honesty |

### Allah sees
- *Allah sees what you do*: how you treat people at the end shows your character.

### Lessons
- **End things gracefully.**
- **Be generous** with your rights when you can.

## "Guard the Prayers, and the Middle Prayer": Quran 2:238 Explained

﴿حَٰفِظُواْ عَلَى ٱلصَّلَوَٰتِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِ ٱلۡوُسۡطَىٰ وَقُومُواْ لِلَّهِ قَٰنِتِينَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:238]

### Guarding prayer
- Praying on time, with its conditions and pillars, and with humility.

### The middle prayer
- Mentioned separately for its virtue. It is **ʿAsr** according to the sound view: on the day of the Trench the Prophet ﷺ said, *They kept us from the middle prayer, the ʿAsr prayer* (narrated by Muslim).

### *Qanitin*
- Here it means silence and humility. Zayd ibn Arqam said people used to talk in prayer until this verse came and they were commanded to be silent (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Among the divorce verses
- The verse sits in the middle of rulings on divorce and waiting periods: prayer protects the heart from injustice during family conflict.

### Lessons
- **Guard ʿAsr** especially at work.
- **Turn to prayer** in family crises.

## "Who Will Lend Allah a Goodly Loan?": Quran 2:245 Explained

﴿مَّن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يُقۡرِضُ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا فَيُضَٰعِفَهُۥ لَهُۥٓ أَضۡعَافٗا كَثِيرَةٗۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَقۡبِضُ وَيَبۡصُۜطُ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:245]

### Why a "loan"?
- Allah needs nothing, yet He calls charity a **loan** so the giver knows it is safe, returned and multiplied.

### Abu al-Dahdah
- Scholars mention that when it was revealed, Abu al-Dahdah asked: *Does Allah want a loan from us?* The Prophet ﷺ said yes, and he gave his garden of six hundred palms (mentioned by Ibn Kathir and others).

### Conditions
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sincerity | For Allah |
| Lawful | From good earnings |
| Willingly | Without reminders or harm |

### "Allah withholds and extends"
- Provision is in His hand: giving does not impoverish, holding back does not enrich.
- Tajweed note: in Hafs, *yabsut* here is read with a *sin*, indicated by the small sin over the sad.

### Lessons
- **Give**, confident it is a loan with Allah.
- **Do not fear poverty** because of charity.

## Ayat al-Kursi Meaning: The Greatest Ayah in the Quran Explained

The Prophet ﷺ asked Ubayy ibn Kaʿb: «Which ayah in the Book of Allah is the greatest?» He answered: Ayat al-Kursi. The Prophet ﷺ said: «May knowledge be pleasant for you, O Abu al-Mundhir» [Muslim].

﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُۚ﴾ [Al-Baqarah: 255]

### Phrase by phrase
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ﴾ | None deserves worship but Him |
| ﴿ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُۚ﴾ | The Ever-Living, who sustains everything |
| ﴿لَا تَأۡخُذُهُۥ سِنَةࣱ وَلَا نَوۡمࣱۚ﴾ | Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him |
| ﴿لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ﴾ | Everything belongs to Him |
| ﴿مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يَشۡفَعُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِهِۦۚ﴾ | No one intercedes without His permission |
| ﴿يَعۡلَمُ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡۖ﴾ | He knows their past and future |
| ﴿وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيۡءٖ مِّنۡ عِلۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَۚ﴾ | They know only what He allows |
| ﴿وَسِعَ كُرۡسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۖ﴾ | His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth |
| ﴿وَلَا يَـُٔودُهُۥ حِفۡظُهُمَاۚ﴾ | Preserving them does not tire Him |
| ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ | The Most High, the Most Great |

### Why is it the greatest ayah?
- It is entirely about **Allah**: His names, His life, His knowledge, His power and His dominion.
- It contains **two of His greatest names**: Al-Ḥayy and Al-Qayyūm.

### When to recite it
- **Before sleep**: whoever recites it will have a guardian from Allah until morning, and no devil will come near him [Al-Bukhari].
- **After each obligatory prayer**: as reported in a hadith by An-Nasa'i.

## Ayat al-Kursi with Tajweed: How to Memorize and Recite It Correctly, Phrase by Phrase

Ayat al-Kursi is verse 255 of [Surah Al-Baqarah](/quran/2/) and **the greatest verse in the Book of Allah**, as the Prophet ﷺ confirmed to Ubayy ibn Kaʿb (Muslim). Since we recite it so often in our daily remembrance, reciting it correctly matters a lot.

### The verse in nine phrases
1. ﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُ﴾
2. ﴿لَا تَأۡخُذُهُۥ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوۡمٌ﴾
3. ﴿لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ﴾
4. ﴿مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يَشۡفَعُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِهِۦ﴾
5. ﴿يَعۡلَمُ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡ﴾
6. ﴿وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيۡءٍ مِّنۡ عِلۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَ﴾
7. ﴿وَسِعَ كُرۡسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ﴾
8. ﴿وَلَا يَـُٔودُهُۥ حِفۡظُهُمَا﴾
9. ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾

**Memorization:** one phrase a day for children; two or three a day for adults. On the last day, recite the whole verse.

### Tajweed, phrase by phrase
1. **﴿ٱللَّهُ﴾:** heavy Laam at the start. **﴿لَآ إِلَٰهَ﴾:** separated Madd, 4–5 counts. **﴿ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُ﴾:** doubled Yaa in both, and a real Ḥaa (not "h").
2. **﴿تَأۡخُذُهُۥ﴾:** short *ṣilah* (2 counts). **﴿سِنَةٌ وَلَا﴾:** Tanween merges into Waw with nasal sound (*sinatuw-walā*).
3. **﴿لَّهُۥ مَا﴾:** short *ṣilah*. **﴿ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ﴾:** natural 2-count Madd on the small Alifs.
4. **﴿مَن ذَا﴾:** Noon hidden before Dhaal (Ikhfa). **﴿يَشۡفَعُ﴾:** a real ʿAyn. **﴿عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا﴾:** Ikhfa of the Noon, and a long *ṣilah* (4–5 counts) because a hamza follows.
5. **﴿أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا﴾:** Meem before Waw must be **clear** (Izhar Shafawi); don't hide it.
6. **﴿بِشَيۡءٍ﴾:** no elongation of the soft Yaa when continuing. **﴿شَيۡءٍ مِّنۡ﴾:** Tanween merges into Meem with nasal sound. **﴿مِّنۡ عِلۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّا﴾:** clear Noon before ʿAyn, and a long *ṣilah*. **﴿شَآءَ﴾:** connected Madd, 4–5 counts.
7. **﴿كُرۡسِيُّهُ﴾:** no *ṣilah*, because a silent letter follows. **﴿وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ﴾:** a real Ḍaad.
8. **﴿حِفۡظُهُمَا﴾:** Ẓaa is pronounced with the tongue tip touching the upper front teeth, never as "z".
9. **﴿ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾:** when stopping, the final Madd can be 2, 4 or 6 counts.

### Most common mistakes
1. "h" instead of Ḥaa in ﴿ٱلۡحَيُّ﴾.
2. Dropping the doubled Yaa in ﴿ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُ﴾.
3. "z" instead of Ẓaa in ﴿حِفۡظُهُمَا﴾.
4. Hiding the Meem in ﴿أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا﴾.
5. Shortening the Madd in ﴿لَآ إِلَٰهَ﴾.
