Surah At-Taghabun takes its name from a striking description of the Day of Resurrection: *Yawm at-Taghabun*, the Day of mutual loss and gain. On that Day it will become clear who truly profited and who truly lost: the people of Paradise gain the places the people of the Fire would have had, and the people of the Fire lose everything. The surah moves from Allah's creation and knowledge to the denial of resurrection, then gives one of the most comforting verses for anyone facing hardship: "No calamity strikes except by permission of Allah. And whoever believes in Allah, He will guide his heart." It ends with practical guidance about family, wealth and charity, including the verse that sets the limit of every obligation: "So fear Allah as much as you are able."

This guide explains Surah At-Taghabun verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah At-Taghabun
- **Number of verses**: 18.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 64, in the twenty-eighth juz.
- **Name**: At-Taghabun means "mutual loss and gain", from verse 9.
- **One of the *musabbihat***: it begins with glorification.
- **Main themes**: Allah's creation and knowledge, the resurrection, accepting calamities, and the trials of family and wealth.

## Verses 1–4: Allah's Creation and Knowledge
﴿يُسَبِّحُ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ لَهُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ وَلَهُ ٱلۡحَمۡدُۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُمۡ فَمِنكُمۡ كَافِرࣱ وَمِنكُم مُّؤۡمِنࣱۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ﴾ ﴿خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَصَوَّرَكُمۡ فَأَحۡسَنَ صُوَرَكُمۡۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ ﴿يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا تُسِرُّونَ وَمَا تُعۡلِنُونَۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾ (Quran 64:1-4)

**"Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth is exalting Allah. To Him belongs dominion, and to Him belongs praise, and He is over all things competent."**

**"It is He who created you, and among you is the disbeliever, and among you is the believer."** Allah created all people with the ability to choose, and people divided into two groups by their choice.

**"He created the heavens and earth in truth and formed you and perfected your forms."** Look at the human form: upright, balanced, beautiful. **"He knows what is within the heavens and earth, and knows what you conceal and what you declare. And Allah is Knowing of that within the breasts."** Three levels of knowledge: the universe, people's open and hidden deeds, and their hearts.

## Verses 5–7: Denial of the Resurrection
﴿أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِكُمۡ نَبَؤُاْ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن قَبۡلُ فَذَاقُواْ وَبَالَ أَمۡرِهِمۡ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُۥ كَانَت تَّأۡتِيهِمۡ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَقَالُوٓاْ أَبَشَرࣱ يَهۡدُونَنَا فَكَفَرُواْ وَتَوَلَّواْۖ وَّٱسۡتَغۡنَى ٱللَّهُۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَنِيٌّ حَمِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 64:5-6)

The surah reminds of former nations who disbelieved. Their excuse: **"Should human beings guide us?"** They refused messengers because they were human. So they disbelieved and turned away, **"and Allah dispensed with them"**.

﴿زَعَمَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَن لَّن يُبۡعَثُواْۚ قُلۡ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّي لَتُبۡعَثُنَّ ثُمَّ لَتُنَبَّؤُنَّ بِمَا عَمِلۡتُمۡۚ وَذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 64:7)

**"Those who disbelieve claim that they will never be resurrected. Say: Yes, by my Lord, you will surely be resurrected; then you will surely be informed of what you did. And that, for Allah, is easy."** The Prophet ﷺ is commanded to swear by his Lord: one of three places in the Quran where he is told to swear about the resurrection.

## Verses 8–10: The Day of Mutual Loss and Gain
﴿فَـَٔامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَٱلنُّورِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلۡنَاۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يَجۡمَعُكُمۡ لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡجَمۡعِۖ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلتَّغَابُنِۗ وَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ وَيَعۡمَلۡ صَٰلِحٗا يُكَفِّرۡ عَنۡهُ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِۦ وَيُدۡخِلۡهُ جَنَّـٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 64:8-10)

**"So believe in Allah and His Messenger and the light which We have sent down"**: the Quran.

**"The Day He will assemble you for the Day of Assembly; that is the Day of mutual loss and gain."** Commentators explained *taghabun* in this way: in trade, one party wins and the other loses. On that Day the true winners and losers will appear. The people of Paradise take the places in Paradise that would have belonged to the people of the Fire had they believed, and the people of the Fire inherit the places in the Fire that would have belonged to the believers had they disbelieved. The real loss is not the loss of money; it is the loss of oneself and one's family on the Day of Resurrection.

## Verse 11: No Calamity Except by Allah's Permission
﴿مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِ قَلۡبَهُۥۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 64:11)

**"No calamity strikes except by permission of Allah. And whoever believes in Allah, He will guide his heart."** One of the most healing verses in the Quran.

**How does Allah guide the heart?** 'Alqamah, one of the students of Ibn Mas'ud, explained: "It is the man who is struck by a calamity and knows that it is from Allah, so he is content and submits." Notice that the reward for faith in a calamity is not necessarily the removal of the calamity, but the guidance of the heart: calm, acceptance and wisdom.

In Sahih Muslim, Suhayb reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "How wonderful is the affair of the believer, for all his affairs are good, and that is for no one except the believer. If something good happens to him, he is grateful, and that is good for him. If something harmful happens to him, he is patient, and that is good for him."

## Verses 12–13: Obey and Rely
﴿وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَۚ فَإِن تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ فَإِنَّمَا عَلَىٰ رَسُولِنَا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾ ﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 64:12-13)

**"Obey Allah and obey the Messenger; but if you turn away, then upon Our Messenger is only clear notification."** **"Allah, there is no deity except Him. And upon Allah let the believers rely."**

## Verses 14–15: Family and Wealth as Trials
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِكُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدِكُمۡ عَدُوّٗا لَّكُمۡ فَٱحۡذَرُوهُمۡۚ وَإِن تَعۡفُواْ وَتَصۡفَحُواْ وَتَغۡفِرُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 64:14)

**"O you who believe, indeed, among your wives and your children are enemies to you, so beware of them."** Not every spouse or child, but some, when they pull a person away from obedience to Allah, or into sin, or away from good deeds. Commentators mention that some men in Makkah wanted to emigrate and their families stopped them. When they later came and saw that others had learned the religion, they wanted to punish their families. So Allah added:

**"But if you pardon and overlook and forgive, then indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."** Even when family members hold you back, the answer is forgiveness and gentleness, not harshness.

﴿إِنَّمَآ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ فِتۡنَةࣱۚ وَٱللَّهُ عِندَهُۥٓ أَجۡرٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 64:15)

**"Your wealth and your children are but a trial, and Allah has with Him a great reward."** At-Tirmidhi reported from Buraydah that the Prophet ﷺ was giving a sermon when al-Hasan and al-Husayn came walking and stumbling in red shirts. He came down from the pulpit, picked them up and placed them in front of him, then said: "Allah spoke the truth: *Your wealth and your children are but a trial*. I looked at these two boys walking and stumbling, and I could not be patient until I interrupted my speech and picked them up." Al-Albani graded it authentic.

## Verse 16: As Much as You Are Able
﴿فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُمۡ وَٱسۡمَعُواْ وَأَطِيعُواْ وَأَنفِقُواْ خَيۡرٗا لِّأَنفُسِكُمۡۗ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفۡسِهِۦ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 64:16)

**"So fear Allah as much as you are able and listen and obey and spend; it is better for yourselves."** This is the limit of every obligation: your ability. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "When I command you to do something, do of it as much as you are able."

**"And whoever is protected from the stinginess of his soul, it is those who will be the successful."**

## Verses 17–18: A Goodly Loan
﴿إِن تُقۡرِضُواْ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا يُضَٰعِفۡهُ لَكُمۡ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ شَكُورٌ حَلِيمٌ﴾ ﴿عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ (Quran 64:17-18)

**"If you loan Allah a goodly loan, He will multiply it for you and forgive you."** Allah calls charity a "loan" to Him, although everything belongs to Him, to show how certain its return is. **"And Allah is Most Appreciative and Forbearing."** **"Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, the Almighty, the Wise."**

## Word Meanings
- **Yusabbihu**: exalts, glorifies.
- **Sawwarakum**: formed you.
- **Wabal**: the bad consequence.
- **Istaghna**: dispensed with them, was free of need.
- **At-Taghabun**: mutual loss and gain.
- **Musibah**: calamity.
- **Yahdi qalbahu**: He guides his heart.
- **Tasfahu**: overlook.
- **Fitnah**: trial.
- **Mastata'tum**: as much as you are able.
- **Shuhh**: stinginess of the soul.
- **Qard hasan**: a goodly loan.
- **Shakur**: Most Appreciative.
- **Halim**: Forbearing.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Yawm at-Taghabun"**: a trade term applied to the hereafter.
2. **"He will guide his heart"**: a reward of inner peace, not only outward relief.
3. **"Among your wives and children are enemies"**: honesty about family life.
4. **"Pardon, overlook and forgive"**: three levels of mercy.
5. **"Lend Allah a goodly loan"**: generosity described as a loan to the Generous.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Ikhfa'**: in *fa-minkum kafir* (verse 2), the noon sakinah is hidden before the kaf.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yajma'ukum* (verse 9), the jim sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Izhar**: in *qardan hasanan* (verse 17), the tanween before the throat letter ha' is pronounced clearly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna min azwajikum* (verse 14), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Munafiqun before it warned "let not your wealth and children divert you", and At-Taghabun says "your wealth and your children are but a trial". Surah At-Talaq after it deals with family law and promises: "Whoever fears Allah, He will make for him a way out", building on "fear Allah as much as you are able".

## Facing Calamities With Verse 11
1. **Remember it is by Allah's permission**: nothing happens outside His decree.
2. **Say *inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un***.
3. **Ask Allah to guide your heart**, not only to remove the pain.
4. **Look for the good hidden in the trial.**
5. **Be patient, and it becomes good for you.**

## Practical Lessons
1. **Believe in the resurrection with certainty.**
2. **Think of the Day of mutual loss and gain** when you make decisions.
3. **Accept calamities with a guided heart.**
4. **Love your family without letting them pull you away from Allah.**
5. **Pardon, overlook and forgive family members.**
6. **Do what you can**; Allah does not ask for more.
7. **Lend Allah a goodly loan** through charity.

## How to Memorise Surah At-Taghabun
- **Week 1**: verses 1–8.

- **Week 3**: verses 14–18.

## Teaching Children
- **Teach them to say *inna lillah*** when something is lost or broken.
- **Tell them about the Prophet ﷺ coming down from the pulpit** to carry his grandsons.
- **Explain charity as a loan to Allah** that He repays many times over.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does at-Taghabun mean?
Mutual loss and gain: on the Day of Resurrection the true winners and losers will be revealed.

### What does "whoever believes in Allah, He will guide his heart" mean?
Allah gives the believer calm and acceptance in calamity; 'Alqamah explained it as the person who knows his calamity is from Allah and accepts it.

### Are spouses and children enemies?
Some may act as enemies when they pull a person away from Allah's obedience, but the verse also commands pardon and forgiveness towards them.

### What does "Your wealth and children are a trial" mean?
They test whether a person will remain obedient to Allah or be distracted by them.

### What does "fear Allah as much as you are able" mean?
Obligations are according to ability; a person is not burdened beyond what he can do.

### Why is charity called a loan to Allah?
To show that its return is guaranteed and multiplied.

### How many verses are in Surah At-Taghabun?
18 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah At-Taghabun shows you the real balance sheet: on the Day of mutual loss and gain, profit and loss will be measured in faith and deeds, not money. Until then, accept what Allah decrees and He will guide your heart, love your family without letting them pull you from Him, do what you are able, and lend Allah a goodly loan that He will multiply.
