﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ وَٱلۡمُسۡلِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰدِقَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرَٰتِ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعِينَ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُتَصَدِّقِينَ وَٱلۡمُتَصَدِّقَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰٓئِمِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰٓئِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظِينَ فُرُوجَهُمۡ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظَٰتِ وَٱلذَّـٰكِرِينَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا وَٱلذَّـٰكِرَٰتِ أَعَدَّ ٱللَّهُ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٗ وَأَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:35]

## The occasion
- Scholars mention that Umm Salamah asked the Prophet ﷺ why women were not mentioned in the Quran as men were, and this verse was revealed (reported by Ahmad and al-Nasaʾi in al-Kubra).

## Ten qualities
| # | Quality |
|---|---|
| 1 | Submission (islam) |
| 2 | Faith |
| 3 | Devout obedience |
| 4 | Truthfulness |
| 5 | Patience |
| 6 | Humility |
| 7 | Charity |
| 8 | Fasting |
| 9 | Guarding chastity |
| 10 | Remembering Allah much |

## Equal reward
- Every quality is mentioned for **both** men and women.

## Ending with dhikr
- The list ends with abundant remembrance, which protects all the others.

## Lessons
- **Measure yourself** against these ten.
- **Increase dhikr**, the crown of the qualities.
