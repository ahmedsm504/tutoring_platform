Surah Al-Muzzammil was revealed in the earliest days of prophethood, when the Prophet ﷺ was still adjusting to the weight of revelation. Allah called him gently, "O you who wraps himself", and gave him a programme to carry the mission ahead: stand in prayer at night, recite the Quran slowly and beautifully, devote yourself to your Lord, rely on Him, and be patient with what people say. The final verse, revealed later, lightened the night prayer and told the believers: "So recite what is easy of the Quran." Together, the beginning and end of the surah teach a balance between devotion and ease.

This guide explains Surah Al-Muzzammil verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and a practical plan for night prayer.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Muzzammil
- **Number of verses**: 20.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 73, in the twenty-ninth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Muzzammil means "the one wrapped in garments", a gentle address to the Prophet ﷺ.
- **Main themes**: night prayer, tartil, the weight of revelation, patience and reliance on Allah.

## How the Night Prayer Was First Obligatory, Then Lightened
In Sahih Muslim, Sa'd ibn Hisham asked 'A'ishah about the night prayer of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. She said: "Do you not read *O you who wraps himself*?" He said yes. She said: "Allah made the night prayer obligatory at the beginning of this surah, so the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions stood in prayer for a year, until their feet swelled. Allah held back the end of the surah in the heaven for twelve months, then Allah revealed the lightening at the end of the surah, and the night prayer became voluntary after having been obligatory."

## Verses 1–4: Arise in the Night
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُزَّمِّلُ﴾ ﴿قُمِ ٱلَّيۡلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا﴾ ﴿نِّصۡفَهُۥٓ أَوِ ٱنقُصۡ مِنۡهُ قَلِيلًا﴾ ﴿أَوۡ زِدۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا﴾ (Quran 73:1-4)

**"O you who wraps himself"**: a gentle, affectionate call. Commentators mention that the Prophet ﷺ had wrapped himself in his garment, and Allah addressed him by his state, as a mark of tenderness.

**"Arise in the night, except for a little"**: stand most of the night in prayer. Then the amount is specified: **"half of it, or subtract from it a little, or add to it"**: around half the night, a little more or less.

**"And recite the Quran with measured recitation"** (*tartil*): slowly, clearly, giving each letter its right, pausing to reflect. 'Ali ibn Abi Talib explained *tartil* as "proper pronunciation of the letters and knowing the places to stop." In Sahih al-Bukhari, Anas was asked about the Prophet's ﷺ recitation and said: "It was with elongation." Then he recited *Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim*, stretching *Bismillah*, stretching *ar-Rahman* and stretching *ar-Rahim*.

## Verse 5: A Heavy Word
﴿إِنَّا سَنُلۡقِي عَلَيۡكَ قَوۡلٗا ثَقِيلًا﴾ (Quran 73:5)

**"Indeed, We will cast upon you a heavy word"**: the Quran is heavy in its responsibilities, its rulings and the effort of conveying it, and revelation was physically heavy too. In Sahih al-Bukhari, 'A'ishah said: "I saw him when revelation came down to him on an intensely cold day, and when it left him his forehead was dripping with sweat."

**The connection**: night prayer is the preparation for carrying this heavy word. Whoever wants to carry a great responsibility in the day needs strength built in the night.

## Verses 6–7: Why the Night?
﴿إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ ٱلَّيۡلِ هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطۡـٔٗا وَأَقۡوَمُ قِيلًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ لَكَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ سَبۡحٗا طَوِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 73:6-7)

**"Indeed, the hours of the night are more effective for concurrence and more suitable for words."** *Nashi'at al-layl* means the hours that arise in the night, after sleep. At that time:
- **"More effective for concurrence"** (*ashaddu wat'an*): the heart and tongue agree more, and the effort weighs more on the self, so the reward is greater.
- **"More suitable for words"** (*aqwamu qila*): recitation is clearer and reflection deeper, because the world is quiet.

**"Indeed, for you by day is prolonged occupation"**: the day is full of work, people and movement. The night is for your Lord.

## Verses 8–9: Devotion and Reliance
﴿وَٱذۡكُرِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ وَتَبَتَّلۡ إِلَيۡهِ تَبۡتِيلٗا﴾ ﴿رَّبُّ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبِ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ فَٱتَّخِذۡهُ وَكِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 73:8-9)

**"And remember the name of your Lord and devote yourself to Him with complete devotion"** (*tabattul*): cut yourself off from everything else when you turn to Him in worship. **"Lord of the east and the west; there is no deity except Him, so take Him as Disposer of affairs"** (*wakil*): rely on Him in everything.

## Verses 10–14: Patience and Beautiful Avoidance
﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَٱهۡجُرۡهُمۡ هَجۡرٗا جَمِيلٗا﴾ ﴿وَذَرۡنِي وَٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ أُوْلِي ٱلنَّعۡمَةِ وَمَهِّلۡهُمۡ قَلِيلًا﴾ (Quran 73:10-11)

**"And be patient over what they say and avoid them with gracious avoidance"** (*hajran jamila*): leave them without insult, revenge or harshness. **"And leave Me with the deniers, those of ease and luxury, and allow them respite a little."**

﴿إِنَّ لَدَيۡنَآ أَنكَالٗا وَجَحِيمٗا﴾ ﴿وَطَعَامٗا ذَا غُصَّةٖ وَعَذَابًا أَلِيمٗا﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ تَرۡجُفُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَٱلۡجِبَالُ وَكَانَتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَثِيبٗا مَّهِيلًا﴾ (Quran 73:12-14)

**What awaits the deniers**: shackles, a burning Fire, food that chokes and a painful punishment, **"on the Day the earth and the mountains will convulse and the mountains will become a heap of sand pouring down"**.

## Verses 15–19: A Day That Turns Children Grey
﴿إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ رَسُولٗا شَٰهِدًا عَلَيۡكُمۡ كَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ رَسُولٗا﴾ ﴿فَعَصَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنُ ٱلرَّسُولَ فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُ أَخۡذٗا وَبِيلٗا﴾ ﴿فَكَيۡفَ تَتَّقُونَ إِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ يَوۡمٗا يَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡوِلۡدَٰنَ شِيبًا﴾ ﴿ٱلسَّمَآءُ مُنفَطِرُۢ بِهِۦۚ كَانَ وَعۡدُهُۥ مَفۡعُولًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦ تَذۡكِرَةࣱۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلًا﴾ (Quran 73:15-19)

**"Indeed, We have sent to you a Messenger as a witness upon you, just as We sent to Pharaoh a messenger. But Pharaoh disobeyed the messenger, so We seized him with a ruinous seizure."** The warning: do not repeat Pharaoh's mistake.

**"Then how can you fear, if you disbelieve, a Day that will make the children white-haired?"** A Day so terrible that it would turn a child's hair grey. **"The heaven will break apart therefrom."**

**"Indeed, this is a reminder, so whoever wills may take to his Lord a way."**

## Verse 20: The Lightening
﴿۞إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَعۡلَمُ أَنَّكَ تَقُومُ أَدۡنَىٰ مِن ثُلُثَيِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَنِصۡفَهُۥ وَثُلُثَهُۥ وَطَآئِفَةࣱ مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ مَعَكَۚ وَٱللَّهُ يُقَدِّرُ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَۚ عَلِمَ أَن لَّن تُحۡصُوهُ فَتَابَ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ عَلِمَ أَن سَيَكُونُ مِنكُم مَّرۡضَىٰ وَءَاخَرُونَ يَضۡرِبُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَبۡتَغُونَ مِن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَءَاخَرُونَ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنۡهُۚ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَقۡرِضُواْ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗاۚ وَمَا تُقَدِّمُواْ لِأَنفُسِكُم مِّنۡ خَيۡرٖ تَجِدُوهُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ هُوَ خَيۡرٗا وَأَعۡظَمَ أَجۡرٗاۚ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ ٱللَّهَۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمُۢ﴾ (Quran 73:20)

This long verse, revealed a year later, brings mercy:
1. **Allah knows that you stand** nearly two thirds of the night, or half, or a third, along with a group of those with you.
2. **"He has known that you will not be able to do it"** precisely every night, **"so He has turned to you in forgiveness"**.
3. **"So recite what is easy of the Quran."**
4. **The reasons**: some of you will be ill, others travelling in the land seeking Allah's bounty, and others fighting in the cause of Allah.
5. **"So recite what is easy from it, and establish prayer and give zakah and loan Allah a goodly loan."**
6. **"And whatever good you put forward for yourselves, you will find it with Allah. It is better and greater in reward."**
7. **"And seek forgiveness of Allah. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."**

**Notice**: the verse mentions travellers "seeking the bounty of Allah", meaning traders and workers, next to those fighting in His cause. Earning a halal living is honoured in the Quran.

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Muzzammil**: the one wrapped in garments.
- **Rattil**: recite slowly and clearly.
- **Thaqil**: heavy.
- **Nashi'at al-layl**: the hours that arise in the night.
- **Wat'**: concurrence, or effort.
- **Qila**: speech, words.
- **Sabh**: occupation, movement.
- **Tabattul**: complete devotion.
- **Wakil**: Disposer of affairs, the One relied upon.
- **Hajran jamila**: gracious avoidance.
- **Ankal**: shackles.
- **Kathiban mahila**: a heap of sand pouring down.
- **Wabil**: ruinous.
- **Shiba**: white-haired.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"O you who wraps himself"**: addressing the Prophet ﷺ by his state, with tenderness.
2. **"Half of it, or subtract a little, or add"**: flexibility built in from the start.
3. **"A heavy word"**: the weight of the Quran captured in two words.
4. **"Gracious avoidance"**: even the way of leaving people must be beautiful.
5. **"A Day that will make the children white-haired"**: an image of overwhelming terror.
6. **The final long verse**: its length and gentle tone match its message of ease.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *qalilan nisfahu* (verses 2–3, when joined), the tanween merges into the noon.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *qawlan thaqila* (verse 5), the tanween is hidden before the tha'.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *tabtila* (verse 8), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'u* (verse 18).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Jinn before it said "when the servant of Allah stood up supplicating Him", and Al-Muzzammil gives the programme of that standing in prayer. Surah Al-Muddaththir after it begins with a similar call, "O you who covers himself", and commands him to arise and warn: the night prepares, and the day proclaims.

## A Practical Plan for Night Prayer
1. **Start small**: two rak'ahs before Witr, even if only ten minutes.
2. **Sleep early**: night prayer begins with a good evening routine.
3. **Use the last third of the night**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah descends to the lowest heaven in the last third of every night and says: "Who is calling upon Me, that I may answer him?"
4. **Recite what you have memorised** with tartil, slowly.
5. **Be consistent**: a little every night is better than a lot once.
6. **Make du'a in prostration.**
7. **If you miss it**, pray during the day: in Sahih Muslim, when the Prophet ﷺ missed his night prayer due to illness or sleep, he prayed twelve rak'ahs in the day.

## Tartil in Practice
1. **Learn the basic rules of tajweed** with a qualified teacher.
2. **Slow down**: quality over quantity.
3. **Stop at the right places**: the end of a meaning.
4. **Repeat a verse that moves you**: the Prophet ﷺ once spent a night repeating one verse, according to a report in an-Nasa'i graded hasan.
5. **Listen to a skilled reciter** and imitate.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Build your strength at night** for the responsibilities of the day.
2. **Recite with tartil**, not in a rush.
3. **Devote time purely to Allah**, cut off from everything else.
4. **Rely on Allah**, the Lord of the east and the west.
5. **Leave hurtful people with grace**, not with insults.
6. **Recite what is easy**: do not let perfectionism stop you.
7. **Give a goodly loan to Allah** through charity.
8. **Seek forgiveness constantly.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Muzzammil
- **Week 1**: verses 1–10.
- **Week 2**: verses 11–19.
- **Week 3**: verse 20, the long final verse.

## Teaching Children
- **Wake them once in a while for a short night prayer** before Fajr and make it special.
- **Teach them tartil** by reciting slowly together.
- **Explain that Allah is the best One to rely on.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does al-Muzzammil mean?
The one wrapped in garments, a gentle address to the Prophet ﷺ.

### Is night prayer obligatory?
It was obligatory at first and then made voluntary by the last verse of this surah, as 'A'ishah explained in Sahih Muslim. It remains a highly emphasised Sunnah.

### What is tartil?
Reciting the Quran slowly and clearly, giving each letter its right and stopping in the right places.

### What is the "heavy word"?
The Quran, heavy in its responsibilities and in the experience of receiving revelation.

### Why is night worship stronger?
Because the heart and tongue agree more in the quiet of the night, and the effort is greater, as verse 6 says.

### What does "recite what is easy of the Quran" mean?
Recite as much as you are able in night prayer without hardship, taking into account illness, travel and other duties.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Muzzammil?
20 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Muzzammil gives the formula for carrying a heavy message: stand at night, recite slowly, devote yourself to your Lord, rely on Him, and be patient with people. Then its final verse reminds you that Allah knows your limits: recite what is easy, pray, give and seek forgiveness. Start tonight with what is easy for you.
