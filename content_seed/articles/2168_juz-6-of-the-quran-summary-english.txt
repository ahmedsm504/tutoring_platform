Juz 6 begins with ﴿۞لَّا يُحِبُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ إِلَّا مَن ظُلِمَۚ﴾, "Allah does not like the public mention of evil, except by one who has been wronged", so it is called **Juz La Yuhibbullah**. It runs from **An-Nisa 4:148 to Al-Ma'idah 5:81**; Juz 7 begins with ﴿۞لَتَجِدَنَّ أَشَدَّ ٱلنَّاسِ عَدَٰوَةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ﴾. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 102 to 120**.

In this juz Allah closes Surah An-Nisa with discussion of the People of the Book and the reality of 'Isa, peace be upon him, and then opens **Surah Al-Ma'idah**, one of the last revealed parts of the Quran, containing the verse that announced **the perfection of the religion**. The dominant theme is **keeping covenants and pledges**: with Allah, with people, and in judgement.

## The big idea: the covenant

The key word of this juz is **covenant** (*mithaq*). The Children of Israel broke their covenant at the end of An-Nisa, and the Christians forgot part of what they were reminded of. Then Al-Ma'idah opens with: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَوۡفُواْ بِٱلۡعُقُودِۚ﴾, "O you who believe, fulfil your contracts." The juz shows covenants broken by those before us, commands us to keep ours, details what Allah made lawful and unlawful, and stresses that judging by what Allah revealed is the heart of keeping faith with Him.

## Part one: the close of Surah An-Nisa (148-176)

### 1. The etiquette of speech, and pardon (148-149)
﴿۞لَّا يُحِبُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ إِلَّا مَن ظُلِمَۚ﴾. The default is not to broadcast evil, except for the wronged person who speaks of the wrong to obtain justice. Then a higher horizon: ﴿إِن تُبۡدُواْ خَيۡرًا أَوۡ تُخۡفُوهُ أَوۡ تَعۡفُواْ عَن سُوٓءٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَفُوّٗا قَدِيرًا﴾, "Whether you show good or hide it, or pardon an offence, Allah is ever Pardoning, All-Powerful." You may speak if wronged, but pardon is higher, and Allah pardons while having full power.

### 2. Those who separate Allah from His messengers (150-152)
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَكۡفُرُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦ وَيُرِيدُونَ أَن يُفَرِّقُواْ بَيۡنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦ وَيَقُولُونَ نُؤۡمِنُ بِبَعۡضٖ وَنَكۡفُرُ بِبَعۡضٖ﴾, "Those who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers, wish to separate between Allah and His messengers, and say: we believe in some and disbelieve in others": these are the true disbelievers. Believers make no distinction between any of the messengers.

### 3. The Children of Israel breaking the covenant (153-162)
The Quran lists their positions: demanding a book from the sky, demanding to see Allah openly, worshipping the calf, the mountain raised over them, transgressing the Sabbath, then ﴿فَبِمَا نَقۡضِهِم مِّيثَٰقَهُمۡ﴾, "for their breaking of the covenant", their rejection of Allah's signs, killing prophets, saying "our hearts are wrapped", and their slander against Maryam.

Then the decisive verse in Muslim belief about 'Isa: ﴿وَقَوۡلِهِمۡ إِنَّا قَتَلۡنَا ٱلۡمَسِيحَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ رَسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا قَتَلُوهُ وَمَا صَلَبُوهُ وَلَٰكِن شُبِّهَ لَهُمۡۚ﴾, "And for their saying, 'We killed the Messiah, 'Isa son of Maryam, the messenger of Allah.' They did not kill him, nor did they crucify him, but it was made to appear so to them." Then: ﴿بَل رَّفَعَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِلَيۡهِۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَزِيزًا حَكِيمٗا﴾, "Rather, Allah raised him to Himself; Allah is Mighty, Wise." 'Isa was neither killed nor crucified; Allah raised him. Riba and consuming people's wealth unjustly are named among the reasons some good things were forbidden to them. Then fairness: ﴿لَّـٰكِنِ ٱلرَّٰسِخُونَ فِي ٱلۡعِلۡمِ مِنۡهُمۡ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ﴾, "But those firm in knowledge among them, and the believers, believe in what was revealed to you."

### 4. The chain of revelation (163-170)
﴿۞إِنَّآ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ كَمَآ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ نُوحٖ وَٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦۚ﴾, "We have revealed to you as We revealed to Nuh and the prophets after him", followed by many prophets' names, then: ﴿وَرُسُلٗا قَدۡ قَصَصۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَيۡكَ مِن قَبۡلُ وَرُسُلٗا لَّمۡ نَقۡصُصۡهُمۡ عَلَيۡكَۚ وَكَلَّمَ ٱللَّهُ مُوسَىٰ تَكۡلِيمٗا﴾, "and messengers We have told you about before, and messengers We have not told you about; and Allah spoke to Musa directly."

Lessons from these verses:
- Revelation to Muhammad ﷺ is nothing new; it is a link in a long chain.
- Some messengers are not named in the Quran, so we do not fix their number.
- The purpose of messengers: ﴿لِئَلَّا يَكُونَ لِلنَّاسِ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ حُجَّةُۢ بَعۡدَ ٱلرُّسُلِۚ﴾, "so that people would have no argument against Allah after the messengers."

### 5. Do not go to extremes in your religion (171-175)
﴿يَـٰٓأَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا تَغۡلُواْ فِي دِينِكُمۡ وَلَا تَقُولُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡحَقَّۚ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمَسِيحُ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ وَكَلِمَتُهُۥٓ أَلۡقَىٰهَآ إِلَىٰ مَرۡيَمَ وَرُوحࣱ مِّنۡهُۖ﴾, "O People of the Book, do not go to extremes in your religion and do not say about Allah except the truth. The Messiah, 'Isa son of Maryam, was only a messenger of Allah and His word which He directed to Maryam, and a soul from Him." "His word" means he was created by the word "Be", and "a soul from Him" means one of the souls Allah created, the attribution being one of honour. This applies the method from the start of Al Imran: read the ambiguous in light of the clear. Then: ﴿لَّن يَسۡتَنكِفَ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ أَن يَكُونَ عَبۡدٗا لِّلَّهِ وَلَا ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَۚ﴾, "Never would the Messiah disdain to be a servant of Allah, nor would the angels near to Him."

**A general lesson**: excessive veneration of people has led nations astray. That is why the Prophet ﷺ said, as reported in Sahih Al-Bukhari: "Do not exaggerate in praising me as the Christians exaggerated about the son of Maryam. I am only His servant, so say: the servant of Allah and His Messenger."

### 6. The kalalah verse (176)
﴿يَسۡتَفۡتُونَكَ قُلِ ٱللَّهُ يُفۡتِيكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡكَلَٰلَةِۚ﴾, "They ask you for a ruling. Say: Allah gives you a ruling concerning kalalah." Kalalah is someone who dies leaving neither child nor parent, whose siblings inherit. With it the inheritance verses of An-Nisa are complete. In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, al-Bara' said the last verse revealed was this one; other Companions named other verses, each reporting what he knew.

**The close of An-Nisa**: the surah began with Allah creating people from one soul and ends with inheritance between siblings. The whole surah is a journey through the rights of the human family.

## Part two: the opening of Surah Al-Ma'idah (1-81)

Al-Ma'idah is a Madani surah of 120 verses and one of the last surahs revealed, which is why it contains many rulings that complete the law. It is named "The Table Spread" after the table requested by the disciples of 'Isa, which comes in Juz 7. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Ma'idah Meaning in English](/blog/wa-taawanu-ala-al-birr-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Maidah-Meaning-Fulfil-Your-Contracts-and-the-Comple).

### 1. Fulfil your contracts (1-2)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَوۡفُواْ بِٱلۡعُقُودِۚ﴾. Contracts include Allah's covenants of obligation and the contracts between people in trade, marriage and more. Then rulings on ihram, hunting and Allah's symbols. Then the great social rule: ﴿وَتَعَاوَنُواْ عَلَى ٱلۡبِرِّ وَٱلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُواْ عَلَى ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِۚ﴾, "Cooperate in righteousness and piety, and do not cooperate in sin and aggression." It comes right after forbidding aggression against those who had blocked them from the Sacred Mosque: justice even towards those who wronged you. Read [Cooperate in Righteousness: 5:2](/blog/wa-taawanu-ala-al-birr-meaning-english/#Cooperate-in-Righteousness-and-Piety-Quran-5-2-Explained).

### 2. Forbidden foods and the perfection of the religion (3-5)
Verse 3 details the forbidden: carrion, blood, pork, what is dedicated to other than Allah, animals strangled, beaten, fallen, gored or partly eaten by a predator unless properly slaughtered, what is sacrificed on stone altars, and seeking decisions by divining arrows.

In the middle of this verse comes the great announcement: ﴿ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أَكۡمَلۡتُ لَكُمۡ دِينَكُمۡ وَأَتۡمَمۡتُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ نِعۡمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمَ دِينٗاۚ﴾, "This day I have perfected for you your religion, completed My favour upon you, and approved for you Islam as religion." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, a Jewish man told 'Umar: there is a verse in your Book which, had it been revealed to us, we would have taken that day as a festival. 'Umar replied: I know the day and the place it was revealed; it came while the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was standing at 'Arafah on a Friday. Read [This Day I Have Perfected Your Religion](/blog/wa-taawanu-ala-al-birr-meaning-english/#Today-I-Have-Perfected-Your-Religion-for-You-Quran-5-3-Expla).

Then good things are made lawful: game caught by trained hunting animals, the food of the People of the Book, and marriage to chaste women from among them.

### 3. Purification and justice (6-11)
The full **verse of wudu'**: ﴿إِذَا قُمۡتُمۡ إِلَى ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ فَٱغۡسِلُواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ وَأَيۡدِيَكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡمَرَافِقِ﴾, "When you rise for prayer, wash your faces and your arms to the elbows", then ghusl and tayammum, ending with ﴿مَا يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ لِيَجۡعَلَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنۡ حَرَجٖ﴾, "Allah does not intend to place hardship upon you."

Then **justice towards enemies**: ﴿كُونُواْ قَوَّـٰمِينَ لِلَّهِ شُهَدَآءَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَلَا يَجۡرِمَنَّكُمۡ شَنَـَٔانُ قَوۡمٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَعۡدِلُواْۚ ٱعۡدِلُواْ هُوَ أَقۡرَبُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ﴾, "Stand firm for Allah as witnesses in justice, and do not let hatred of a people lead you to be unjust. Be just; that is nearer to piety." Hatred never permits injustice. Read ["Let Not Hatred of a People Lead You to Injustice": Quran 5:8 Explained](/blog/wa-taawanu-ala-al-birr-meaning-english/#Let-Not-Hatred-of-a-People-Lead-You-to-Injustice-Quran-5-8-E).

### 4. The covenant of the Children of Israel and the Christians (12-19)
﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ أَخَذَ ٱللَّهُ مِيثَٰقَ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ وَبَعَثۡنَا مِنۡهُمُ ٱثۡنَيۡ عَشَرَ نَقِيبٗاۖ﴾, "Allah took a covenant from the Children of Israel and We appointed from among them twelve leaders", and they broke it: ﴿فَبِمَا نَقۡضِهِم مِّيثَٰقَهُمۡ لَعَنَّـٰهُمۡ وَجَعَلۡنَا قُلُوبَهُمۡ قَٰسِيَةٗۖ يُحَرِّفُونَ ٱلۡكَلِمَ عَن مَّوَاضِعِهِۦ وَنَسُواْ حَظّٗا مِّمَّا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهِۦۚ﴾, "For breaking their covenant We cursed them and made their hearts hard. They distort words from their places and have forgotten a portion of what they were reminded of." Then the Christians: Allah took their covenant, they forgot a portion, and He stirred enmity and hatred among them.

Then the universal message: ﴿قَدۡ جَآءَكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ نُورࣱ وَكِتَٰبࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾, "There has come to you from Allah a light and a clear Book", and replies to those who said Allah is the Messiah son of Maryam and those who said "we are the children of Allah and His beloved".

### 5. The wandering in the desert (20-26)
Musa reminds his people of Allah's favours and commands them to enter the Holy Land, but they fear its mighty inhabitants. In the middle of the fear, the voice of faith: ﴿قَالَ رَجُلَانِ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخَافُونَ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِمَا ٱدۡخُلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡبَابَ فَإِذَا دَخَلۡتُمُوهُ فَإِنَّكُمۡ غَٰلِبُونَۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَتَوَكَّلُوٓاْ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "Two men among those who feared Allah, whom He had blessed, said: Enter upon them through the gate; once you enter it, you will prevail. And rely upon Allah, if you are believers." But they said: ﴿فَٱذۡهَبۡ أَنتَ وَرَبُّكَ فَقَٰتِلَآ إِنَّا هَٰهُنَا قَٰعِدُونَ﴾, "So go, you and your Lord, and fight; we will sit right here." The result: forty years wandering in the land.

In Sahih Al-Bukhari, al-Miqdad ibn al-Aswad said to the Prophet ﷺ at Badr: we will not say as the people of Musa said, "Go, you and your Lord, and fight"; rather we will fight on your right, your left, in front of you and behind you. And the Prophet's face lit up.

**Lesson**: cowardice before obedience is punished by losing the way; trust in Allah together with taking the means is the road to victory.

### 6. The two sons of Adam (27-32)
﴿۞وَٱتۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ نَبَأَ ٱبۡنَيۡ ءَادَمَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ إِذۡ قَرَّبَا قُرۡبَانٗا فَتُقُبِّلَ مِنۡ أَحَدِهِمَا وَلَمۡ يُتَقَبَّلۡ مِنَ ٱلۡأٓخَرِ﴾, "Recite to them the true story of the two sons of Adam, when they both offered a sacrifice and it was accepted from one and not the other." The envious one said, "I will kill you", and the other replied: ﴿إِنَّمَا يَتَقَبَّلُ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾, "Allah only accepts from the God-conscious", and then: ﴿لَئِنۢ بَسَطتَ إِلَيَّ يَدَكَ لِتَقۡتُلَنِي مَآ أَنَا۠ بِبَاسِطࣲ يَدِيَ إِلَيۡكَ لِأَقۡتُلَكَۖ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "If you stretch out your hand to kill me, I will not stretch out mine to kill you; I fear Allah, Lord of the worlds." He killed him, and then: ﴿فَبَعَثَ ٱللَّهُ غُرَابٗا يَبۡحَثُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لِيُرِيَهُۥ كَيۡفَ يُوَٰرِي سَوۡءَةَ أَخِيهِۚ﴾, "Allah sent a crow scratching the ground to show him how to hide his brother's body", and he became one of the regretful.

The Quran does not name the two sons; the names in circulation come from reports that are not established, so we stop where the Quran stops. Then the principle drawn from the story: ﴿مَن قَتَلَ نَفۡسَۢا بِغَيۡرِ نَفۡسٍ أَوۡ فَسَادٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَكَأَنَّمَا قَتَلَ ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗا وَمَنۡ أَحۡيَاهَا فَكَأَنَّمَآ أَحۡيَا ٱلنَّاسَ جَمِيعٗاۚ﴾, "Whoever kills a soul, unless for a soul or for corruption in the land, it is as if he killed all mankind; and whoever saves one, it is as if he saved all mankind." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim: "No soul is killed unjustly except that the first son of Adam bears a share of its blood, because he was the first to establish killing." Read ["Whoever Saves One Life, It Is as If He Saved All Mankind": Quran 5:32 Explained](/blog/wa-taawanu-ala-al-birr-meaning-english/#Whoever-Saves-One-Life-It-Is-as-If-He-Saved-All-Mankind-Qura).

### 7. Penalties and drawing near to Allah (33-40)
- The penalty of *hirabah* for those who wage war against Allah and His Messenger and spread corruption, with an exception for those who repent before being captured.
- ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱبۡتَغُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡوَسِيلَةَ وَجَٰهِدُواْ فِي سَبِيلِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾, "O you who believe, fear Allah, seek the means of nearness to Him, and strive in His cause, so that you may succeed." The "means" here is drawing near to Allah through acts of obedience.
- The penalty for theft, with the door of repentance: ﴿فَمَن تَابَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ظُلۡمِهِۦ وَأَصۡلَحَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَتُوبُ عَلَيۡهِۚ﴾, "Whoever repents after his wrongdoing and reforms, Allah will accept his repentance."

**Notice**: each penalty here is followed by mention of repentance. Punishment protects society; repentance rescues the individual.

### 8. Judging by what Allah revealed (41-50)
﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلرَّسُولُ لَا يَحۡزُنكَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡكُفۡرِ﴾, "O Messenger, do not be grieved by those who hasten into disbelief." Then the Torah: ﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَا ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ فِيهَا هُدٗى وَنُورࣱۚ﴾, "We revealed the Torah, in which was guidance and light", then the Gospel, then the Quran: ﴿وَأَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَمُهَيۡمِنًا عَلَيۡهِۖ﴾, "And We revealed to you the Book in truth, confirming the scripture before it and as a guardian over it." The Quran confirms what came before and stands as its trustee and criterion.

This section contains the three famous endings:
- 44: ﴿فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾, "those are the disbelievers."
- 45: ﴿فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَ﴾, "those are the wrongdoers."
- 47: ﴿فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ﴾, "those are the defiantly disobedient."

Then: ﴿لِكُلࣲّ جَعَلۡنَا مِنكُمۡ شِرۡعَةࣰ وَمِنۡهَاجࣰاۚ﴾, "To each of you We prescribed a law and a method": revealed religions share the foundation of monotheism and differ in their laws. Then: ﴿فَٱسۡتَبِقُواْ ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِۚ﴾, "so race to all that is good." The section ends: ﴿أَفَحُكۡمَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِ يَبۡغُونَۚ وَمَنۡ أَحۡسَنُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ حُكۡمࣰا لِّقَوۡمࣲ يُوقِنُونَ﴾, "Is it the judgement of ignorance they seek? And who is better than Allah in judgement for a people who are certain?"

### 9. Allegiance, and those Allah loves (51-66)
- A prohibition on taking Jews and Christians as allies in the sense of alliance and support against the believers.
- ﴿مَن يَرۡتَدَّ مِنكُمۡ عَن دِينِهِۦ فَسَوۡفَ يَأۡتِي ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ يُحِبُّهُمۡ وَيُحِبُّونَهُۥٓ أَذِلَّةٍ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَعِزَّةٍ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾, "Whoever of you turns from his religion, Allah will bring a people He loves and who love Him, humble towards the believers, firm against the disbelievers." Four qualities: mutual love with Allah, humility with believers, dignity before enemies, and striving without fear of blame.
- ﴿إِنَّمَا وَلِيُّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ﴾, "Your ally is only Allah, His Messenger and the believers."
- Then those who mocked the prayer, and those who said ﴿يَدُ ٱللَّهِ مَغۡلُولَةٌۚ﴾, "Allah's hand is chained", answered by ﴿بَلۡ يَدَاهُ مَبۡسُوطَتَانِ يُنفِقُ كَيۡفَ يَشَآءُۚ﴾, "Rather, both His hands are extended; He spends however He wills."

### 10. "Convey", and the reality of the Messiah (67-81)
﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلرَّسُولُ بَلِّغۡ مَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَۖ وَإِن لَّمۡ تَفۡعَلۡ فَمَا بَلَّغۡتَ رِسَالَتَهُۥۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡصِمُكَ مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِۗ﴾, "O Messenger, convey what has been revealed to you from your Lord; if you do not, you have not conveyed His message. And Allah will protect you from people." 'A'ishah said, as in Al-Bukhari and Muslim: whoever tells you that Muhammad ﷺ concealed anything revealed to him has lied, and she recited this verse.

Then the verse about followers of different religions (69), the Children of Israel breaking the covenant and killing prophets, and the decisive word on the Messiah:
- ﴿لَقَدۡ كَفَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَۖ وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ رَبِّي وَرَبَّكُمۡۖ﴾, "They have disbelieved who say Allah is the Messiah son of Maryam, while the Messiah said: O Children of Israel, worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord." 'Isa himself called to worship Allah.
- A reply to the trinity, and a call to repentance.
- A tangible argument: ﴿مَّا ٱلۡمَسِيحُ ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ إِلَّا رَسُولࣱ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهِ ٱلرُّسُلُ وَأُمُّهُۥ صِدِّيقَةࣱۖ كَانَا يَأۡكُلَانِ ٱلطَّعَامَۗ﴾, "The Messiah son of Maryam was only a messenger; messengers passed before him, and his mother was a woman of truth. They both used to eat food." One who needs food cannot be God.

The juz closes with why those who disbelieved among the Children of Israel were cursed: ﴿كَانُواْ لَا يَتَنَاهَوۡنَ عَن مُّنكَرٖ فَعَلُوهُۚ لَبِئۡسَ مَا كَانُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ﴾, "They used not to stop one another from the wrong they did. How evil was what they used to do." A warning for this nation: abandoning the forbidding of wrong brings general punishment.

## Tajweed points in Juz 6

### The paired stop in Al-Ma'idah 41
In ﴿قُلُوبُهُمۡۛ وَمِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ هَادُواْۛ﴾ the three-dot sign ۛ appears twice; if you stop at one, do not stop at the other.

### ﴿أَنَا۠﴾ in Al-Ma'idah 28
The alif after the noon in ﴿مَآ أَنَا۠ بِبَاسِطࣲ﴾ is dropped when continuing and pronounced when stopping, as the small oval above it shows.

### Partial merging in ﴿بَسَطتَ﴾
The sakin ta' (heavy t) before ta merges incompletely, keeping its heaviness, so it is not pronounced as a plain t.

### Iqlab in ﴿لَئِنۢ بَسَطتَ﴾ and ﴿مِنۢ بَعۡدِ﴾
Sakin noon before ba becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah.

### Compulsory madd in ﴿ءَآمِّينَ﴾
In 5:2 the meem is doubled after a madd letter, so the madd is six counts. For all madd types see [Madd Rules in Tajweed](/blog/madd-rules-tajweed-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. Distorting words
- An-Nisa 46 and Al-Ma'idah 13: ﴿يُحَرِّفُونَ ٱلۡكَلِمَ عَن مَّوَاضِعِهِۦ﴾, "from their places."
- Al-Ma'idah 41: ﴿يُحَرِّفُونَ ٱلۡكَلِمَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَوَاضِعِهِۦۖ﴾, "after their places."
**Key**: "after their places" appears only in the second Ma'idah occurrence.

### 2. "They have disbelieved who say Allah is the Messiah" twice
- 5:17: followed by ﴿قُلۡ فَمَن يَمۡلِكُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡـًٔا﴾, "Say: who could prevent Allah at all..."
- 5:72: followed by ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَسِيحُ يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ﴾, "while the Messiah said..."

### 3. The three endings on judgement
The order is: disbelievers (44), wrongdoers (45), defiantly disobedient (47). Remember it as **K-Z-F**: *kafirun*, *zalimun*, *fasiqun*.

### 4. Forbidden foods
Al-Ma'idah 3 has ﴿وَمَآ أُهِلَّ لِغَيۡرِ ٱللَّهِ بِهِۦ﴾ with *bihi* at the end, like Al-An'am and An-Nahl, unlike Al-Baqarah, which puts *bihi* first.

## How to memorize Juz 6 with understanding

1. **Memorize the end of An-Nisa as an accusation and an acquittal**: the charges of the People of the Book, then the innocence and raising of 'Isa, then the reply to extremism.
2. **Al-Ma'idah opens with rulings**; write out the list of forbidden things in verse 3 in order.
3. **Treat the wandering and the two sons of Adam as scenes**, and repeat the dialogue aloud.
4. **In the section on judging by revelation**, focus on the three endings.
5. **Link the verses on the Messiah at the end of the juz** with those at the end of An-Nisa; they complete each other.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 6

- **Do not broadcast evil** except to lift a wrong; pardon is higher.
- **Believe in all the messengers** without distinction.
- **Do not overpraise any human being**; the prophets are servants of Allah.
- **Fulfil your contracts** at work, in trade and in marriage.
- **Cooperate in righteousness**, even with those who wronged you.
- **Be just with those you dislike.**
- **Do not be among those who sit back** when action is required.
- **If wronged, do not reach out your hand in wrong**; fear Allah.
- **Judge by what Allah revealed** in yourself and your home.
- **Do not stay silent about wrong**; it leads to ruin.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 6 called "La Yuhibbullah"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞لَّا يُحِبُّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ﴾.

### Where does Juz 6 end?
At Al-Ma'idah 5:81. Juz 7 begins with ﴿۞لَتَجِدَنَّ أَشَدَّ ٱلنَّاسِ عَدَٰوَةٗ لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ﴾.

### When was "This day I have perfected your religion" revealed?
On the day of 'Arafah during the Farewell Pilgrimage, which was a Friday, as reported in Al-Bukhari and Muslim from 'Umar.

### What does the Quran say about the crucifixion of 'Isa?
That he was neither killed nor crucified, but it was made to appear so to them, and Allah raised him to Himself (4:157-158).

### Does the Quran name the two sons of Adam?
No. It tells their story and its lessons without naming them.

## Conclusion

Juz 6 is the juz of covenants. It shows how earlier nations broke their pledges, commands us to fulfil our contracts, announces the perfection of the religion, and tells stories that reveal the root of crime in envy and the root of defeat in cowardice. It then establishes that true faithfulness to Allah is judging by what He revealed and cooperating in righteousness and piety.
