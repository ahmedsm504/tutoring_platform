﴿لَقَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولࣱ مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ عَزِيزٌ عَلَيۡهِ مَا عَنِتُّمۡ حَرِيصٌ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:128]

## Five descriptions
| Description | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *From among yourselves* | A human you know, of your people and language |
| *Your hardship weighs on him* | Your suffering grieves him |
| *Eager for you* | Keen on your guidance and good |
| *Kind (raʾuf)* | Gentle care that removes harm |
| *Merciful (rahim)* | Mercy that brings good |

## Names shared with Allah
- *Ar-Raʾuf* and *ar-Rahim* are names of Allah. Describing the Prophet ﷺ with these qualities shows the honour of his character, while Allah's attributes remain perfect and incomparable.

## Seen in his life
- He shortened prayers when he heard a baby crying, to spare the mother.
- He saved his special duʿa for intercession for his nation on the Day of Judgment (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## The final verse
- The surah then ends: *If they turn away, say: Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but Him; upon Him I rely.*

## Lessons
- **Love the Prophet ﷺ** who loved you before you were born.
- **Follow his Sunnah**: that is the proof of love.
