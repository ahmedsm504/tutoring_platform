Surah As-Saff is short in verses but great in its message. It opens with a stern reproach to anyone who says what he does not do, then declares what Allah loves: those who fight in His cause in a row, as though they were a solid structure. It gives two examples: the people of Musa who hurt their prophet, and the disciples of 'Isa who supported theirs. In between, it records 'Isa's glad tidings of a messenger coming after him named Ahmad, exposes those who try to blow out Allah's light with their mouths, and offers the most profitable trade there is. It closes with a call: "Be helpers of Allah."

This guide explains Surah As-Saff verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah As-Saff
- **Number of verses**: 14.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 61, in the twenty-eighth juz.
- **Name**: As-Saff means "the row" or "the ranks", from verse 4.
- **One of the *musabbihat***: the surahs that begin with glorification of Allah: Al-Hadid, Al-Hashr, As-Saff, Al-Jumu'ah and At-Taghabun.

## The Reason for Its Revelation
At-Tirmidhi reported that 'Abdullah ibn Salam said: "A group of us Companions sat and discussed among ourselves: If we knew which deed is most beloved to Allah, we would do it. Then Allah revealed: *Whatever is in the heavens and earth glorifies Allah, and He is the Almighty, the Wise. O you who believe, why do you say what you do not do?*" 'Abdullah ibn Salam said: "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ recited it to us." Al-Albani graded it authentic. The surah came as an answer to the question: what does Allah love most?

## Verses 1–4: Why Do You Say What You Do Not Do?
﴿سَبَّحَ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لِمَ تَقُولُونَ مَا لَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ ﴿كَبُرَ مَقۡتًا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَن تَقُولُواْ مَا لَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِهِۦ صَفّٗا كَأَنَّهُم بُنۡيَٰنࣱ مَّرۡصُوصࣱ﴾ (Quran 61:1-4)

**Glorification first**: the whole universe glorifies Allah; how can the human being lag behind?

**"Why do you say what you do not do?"** A reproach for those who promise and do not deliver, or claim and do not act. Some Companions had said they wished to know the most beloved deed so they could do it, and when fighting was prescribed, some disliked it, so the reproach came.

**"Great is hatred in the sight of Allah that you say what you do not do."** *Maqt* is the most intense hatred. A gap between words and deeds is among the things Allah hates most. It includes:
- **Breaking promises**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "The signs of a hypocrite are three: when he speaks he lies, when he promises he breaks it, and when he is entrusted he betrays."
- **Commanding good without doing it**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Usamah ibn Zayd reported that the Prophet ﷺ described a man thrown into the Fire, going round like a donkey around a millstone. The people of the Fire gather and say: "Did you not used to command us to do good and forbid evil?" He says: "I used to command you to do good but did not do it, and forbid you from evil but did it."
- **Claiming qualities one does not have.**

**"Indeed, Allah loves those who fight in His cause in a row, as though they are a single structure joined firmly."** This is the answer to the Companions' question. *Bunyan marsus* is a building whose bricks are pressed together so tightly there is no gap. The meaning extends to every collective effort: Allah loves unity and cohesion in prayer, in da'wah and in work.

**Rows in prayer**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Anas reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Straighten your rows, for straightening the rows is part of establishing the prayer." A solid row in prayer trains a solid row in life.

## Verse 5: Musa's People Hurt Him
﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِۦ يَٰقَوۡمِ لِمَ تُؤۡذُونَنِي وَقَد تَّعۡلَمُونَ أَنِّي رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ إِلَيۡكُمۡۖ فَلَمَّا زَاغُوٓاْ أَزَاغَ ٱللَّهُ قُلُوبَهُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 61:5)

**The first example**: the people of Musa hurt him although they knew he was Allah's messenger to them. **"And when they deviated, Allah caused their hearts to deviate."** When they chose to turn away from the truth, Allah turned their hearts away as a just consequence. A divine rule: whoever leaves the truth after knowing it is punished with deviation.

In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud reported that when the Prophet ﷺ was hurt by someone's words, he said: "May Allah have mercy on Musa; he was hurt with more than this and was patient."

## Verse 6: 'Isa's Glad Tidings of Ahmad
﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ إِنِّي رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ إِلَيۡكُم مُّصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيَّ مِنَ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَمُبَشِّرَۢا بِرَسُولٖ يَأۡتِي مِنۢ بَعۡدِي ٱسۡمُهُۥٓ أَحۡمَدُۖ فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ قَالُواْ هَٰذَا سِحۡرࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ (Quran 61:6)

**The second example**: 'Isa ﷺ came confirming the Torah before him and **"bringing good tidings of a messenger to come after me, whose name is Ahmad"**. *Ahmad* is one of the names of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Jubayr ibn Mut'im reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "I have five names: I am Muhammad, and Ahmad, and I am al-Mahi through whom Allah erases disbelief, and I am al-Hashir at whose feet people will be gathered, and I am al-'Aqib."

**The meaning of Ahmad**: a superlative form: the one who praises Allah most. **"But when he came to them with clear evidences, they said: This is obvious magic."**

## Verses 7–9: Extinguishing Allah's Light
﴿وَمَنۡ أَظۡلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡكَذِبَ وَهُوَ يُدۡعَىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلۡإِسۡلَٰمِۚ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿يُرِيدُونَ لِيُطۡفِـُٔواْ نُورَ ٱللَّهِ بِأَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَٱللَّهُ مُتِمُّ نُورِهِۦ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَرۡسَلَ رَسُولَهُۥ بِٱلۡهُدَىٰ وَدِينِ ٱلۡحَقِّ لِيُظۡهِرَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلدِّينِ كُلِّهِۦ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ (Quran 61:7-9)

**"They want to extinguish the light of Allah with their mouths"**: like someone blowing at the sun to put it out. A picture of how futile their efforts are. **"But Allah will perfect His light, although the disbelievers dislike it."**

**"It is He who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth to manifest it over all religion."** A promise that Islam will prevail by proof and argument, and in its spread. In Sahih Muslim, Thawban reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah gathered the earth for me so that I saw its east and west, and the dominion of my nation will reach what was gathered for me of it."

## Verses 10–13: The Trade That Saves
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ تِجَٰرَةٖ تُنجِيكُم مِّنۡ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ﴾ ﴿تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَتُجَٰهِدُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِأَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنفُسِكُمۡۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿يَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ ذُنُوبَكُمۡ وَيُدۡخِلۡكُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ وَمَسَٰكِنَ طَيِّبَةٗ فِي جَنَّـٰتِ عَدۡنٖۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ ﴿وَأُخۡرَىٰ تُحِبُّونَهَاۖ نَصۡرࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَفَتۡحࣱ قَرِيبࣱۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 61:10-13)

**"Shall I guide you to a transaction that will save you from a painful punishment?"** People love trade and profit, so Allah offers the most profitable deal of all.

**The capital**:
1. **Believing in Allah and His Messenger.**
2. **Striving in His cause with your wealth and your lives.** Wealth comes first because it is usually given first and is easier for most people.

**The profits**:
1. **Forgiveness of sins.**
2. **Gardens beneath which rivers flow.**
3. **Pleasant dwellings in gardens of perpetual residence.**
4. **"That is the great attainment."**
5. **"And another that you love: victory from Allah and an imminent conquest."** A profit in this world too.

**"And give good tidings to the believers."**

## Verse 14: Be Helpers of Allah
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُوٓاْ أَنصَارَ ٱللَّهِ كَمَا قَالَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ لِلۡحَوَارِيِّـۧنَ مَنۡ أَنصَارِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ قَالَ ٱلۡحَوَارِيُّونَ نَحۡنُ أَنصَارُ ٱللَّهِۖ فَـَٔامَنَت طَّآئِفَةࣱ مِّنۢ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ وَكَفَرَت طَّآئِفَةࣱۖ فَأَيَّدۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ عَلَىٰ عَدُوِّهِمۡ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ ظَٰهِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 61:14)

**"O you who believe, be helpers of Allah, as 'Isa son of Maryam said to the disciples: Who are my helpers for Allah? The disciples said: We are helpers of Allah."** The *hawariyyun* were the chosen, sincere companions of 'Isa. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Jabir reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Every prophet had a disciple, and my disciple is az-Zubayr."

**The outcome**: a faction of the Children of Israel believed and a faction disbelieved, and Allah supported those who believed until they prevailed. **The lesson**: whoever supports Allah's cause, Allah supports him. "If you support Allah, He will support you" (Surah Muhammad).

## Word Meanings
- **Maqt**: intense hatred.
- **Saff**: row, rank.
- **Bunyan marsus**: a solid structure with no gaps.
- **Zaghu**: deviated.
- **Mubashshir**: bringer of good tidings.
- **Ahmad**: one who praises most, a name of the Prophet ﷺ.
- **Yutfi'u**: extinguish.
- **Mutimm**: perfecting.
- **Tijarah**: trade, transaction.
- **Tujahidun**: strive.
- **Masakin tayyibah**: pleasant dwellings.
- **Ansar**: helpers.
- **Al-Hawariyyun**: the disciples.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Why do you say what you do not do?"**: a question that rebukes.
2. **"Great is hatred"**: a phrase that conveys extreme disapproval.
3. **"A solid structure"**: an image of unity.
4. **"Extinguish Allah's light with their mouths"**: mocking the futility of the enemies.
5. **"Shall I guide you to a transaction?"**: an inviting metaphor of trade.
6. **Two contrasting examples**: a people who hurt Musa, and disciples who helped 'Isa.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ja'ahum* (verse 6) and *isra'il* (verses 6 and 14).
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna Allaha* (verse 4), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *maqtan* (verse 3), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Iqlab**: in *min ba'di* (verse 6), the noon sakinah before the ba' becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Mumtahanah before it forbade alliance with those angered upon, and As-Saff calls to support Allah's cause in united ranks. Surah Al-Jumu'ah after it also begins with glorification and speaks of the sending of the Prophet ﷺ to the unlettered: after the glad tidings of Ahmad in As-Saff comes their fulfilment.

## Matching Words and Deeds Today
1. **Keep promises** to family, friends and clients.
2. **Act on what you know**: knowledge without action is evidence against you.
3. **Be a role model at home**: do not command your children to do what you do not do.
4. **Honour commitments** at work and in study.
5. **Do not present yourself online** as something you are not.

## Unity in the Ranks
Al-Bukhari and Muslim reported from Abu Musa that the Prophet ﷺ said: "The believer to the believer is like a building, each part strengthening the other," and he interlaced his fingers. Causes of cracks in the ranks: disputes, arrogance, words without deeds, and backbiting. Causes of strength: sincerity, humility, good opinion of others, cooperation and quiet, excellent work.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Make your deeds match your words**, or stay quiet.
2. **Keep your promises.**
3. **Be a solid brick in the structure**, not a gap.
4. **Straighten your row in prayer.**
5. **Beware of deviation**: whoever deviates, Allah turns his heart.
6. **Trust Allah's promise**: His light will be perfected.
7. **Enter the profitable trade** with your wealth and effort.
8. **Be among the helpers of Allah.**

## How to Memorise Surah As-Saff

- **Week 2**: verses 8–14.

## Teaching Children
- **Teach them honesty and keeping promises** from "why do you say what you do not do".
- **Build a wall with blocks** to explain "a solid structure".
- **Teach them the names of the Prophet ﷺ**: Muhammad and Ahmad.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the reason for the revelation of Surah As-Saff?
Some Companions wished to know the most beloved deed to Allah, and the surah was revealed, as at-Tirmidhi reported from 'Abdullah ibn Salam and al-Albani graded authentic.

### What does "Great is hatred in the sight of Allah that you say what you do not do" mean?
Allah intensely dislikes a person whose words do not match his actions.

### What is a "solid structure"?
A building whose bricks are pressed tightly together with no gaps, a picture of unity.

### Who is Ahmad whom 'Isa foretold?
The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ; Ahmad is one of his names, as in the hadith of Jubayr in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the trade that saves from a painful punishment?
Believing in Allah and His Messenger and striving in His cause with wealth and life.

### Who were the disciples (hawariyyun)?
The chosen, sincere companions and helpers of 'Isa ﷺ.

### How many verses are in Surah As-Saff?
14 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah As-Saff asks every believer a direct question: do your deeds match your words? Then it invites you to be a brick in a solid structure, to enter the most profitable trade, and to be among the helpers of Allah. Allah will perfect His light even if the disbelievers dislike it; the fortunate are those who have a share in carrying it.
