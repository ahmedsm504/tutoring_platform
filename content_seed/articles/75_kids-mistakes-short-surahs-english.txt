From years in the classroom, we see the same mistakes in children's recitation of the short surahs, whatever country they live in. Knowing them in advance helps you catch them early, before they become habits.

## Surah An-Nas
- ﴿ٱلۡوَسۡوَاسِ﴾: many kids make the Seen heavy, like a Ṣaad. It should be **light**.
- ﴿ٱلۡخَنَّاسِ﴾: Khaa from the top of the throat (like "ch" in *loch*), plus the nasal sound of the doubled Noon.
- ﴿ٱلنَّاسِ﴾: repeated often; keep the **2-count nasal sound** on the doubled Noon.

## Surah Al-Falaq
- ﴿ٱلۡفَلَقِ﴾ and ﴿خَلَقَ﴾: a light **bounce (qalqalah)** on the Qaaf when stopping, without adding a full vowel.
- ﴿مِن شَرِّ﴾: Noon before Sheen is **hidden (Ikhfa)**; kids often pronounce a clear "n".
- ﴿ٱلنَّفَّٰثَٰتِ﴾: the Thaa is "th" as in *think*, not "s".

## Surah Al-Ikhlas
- ﴿ٱلصَّمَدُ﴾: the Ṣaad must be **heavy**; many kids say *as-samad* with a light "s".
- ﴿كُفُوًا﴾: in Hafs it's read *kufuwan* **with a Waw**. Some children say *kufu'an* with a hamza because they've heard it that way; that's a valid reading for other reciters, but in Hafs it's with Waw.
- ﴿لَمۡ يَلِدۡ وَلَمۡ يُولَدۡ﴾: a light bounce on the silent Daal.

## Surah Al-Masad
- ﴿تَبَّتۡ يَدَآ أَبِي لَهَبٍ﴾: a **4–5 count Madd** in *yadā abī*; kids often shorten it.
- ﴿حَمَّالَةَ ٱلۡحَطَبِ﴾: Ḥaa in both words, and a heavy Ṭaa.

## Surah Al-Kawthar
- ﴿وَٱنۡحَرۡ﴾: the Noon before Ḥaa is **clear** (Izhar), and the Ḥaa must be a real Ḥaa.
- ﴿إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ﴾: pronounce the hamza in *shāniʾaka* clearly.

## Surah Al-Fil
- ﴿تَرۡمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ﴾: Meem before Baa is **hidden** with a nasal sound (Ikhfa Shafawi).
- ﴿سِجِّيلٍ﴾: doubled Jeem.
- ﴿كَعَصۡفٍ مَّأۡكُولِۭ﴾: the Tanween **merges** into the Meem with nasal sound.

## Surah Al-Asr
- ﴿وَٱلۡعَصۡرِ﴾: clear ʿAyn, heavy Ṣaad, and a heavy Raa when stopping.
- ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ﴾: the Noon in *al-insān* is **hidden** before the Seen.

## Surah Ad-Duha
- ﴿وَٱلضُّحَىٰ﴾: a real Ḍaad, not a "d".
- ﴿مَا وَدَّعَكَ﴾: doubled Daal.

## Gentle correction tips
1. **Don't interrupt every mistake.** Let them finish the verse, then fix just one thing.
2. **Model it:** say the word clearly and ask them to copy you.
3. **Make it a game:** "Who can say the Ṣaad the heaviest?"
4. **Praise progress:** "Your Ṣaad is so much better than yesterday!"
5. **Listen together** to a clear reciter like Sheikh Al-Husary's teaching recitation.
