**Surah At-Takwir** (29 ayat) paints the end of the world in short, powerful scenes. The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever wishes to look at the Day of Resurrection as if seeing it with his eyes should read At-Takwir, Al-Infitar and Al-Inshiqaq [At-Tirmidhi].

## The scenes
﴿إِذَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ كُوِّرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ ٱنكَدَرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتۡ﴾
| Scene | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The sun wrapped up | Its light folded away |
| The stars fall | They lose their light |
| Mountains moved | Uprooted from their places |
| Seas set on fire | Boiling and overflowing |
| Souls paired | Each joined with its like |

## The buried girl
﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡمَوۡءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتۡ﴾ ﴿بِأَيِّ ذَنۢبٖ قُتِلَتۡ﴾ — *And when the girl buried alive is asked, for what sin was she killed?*
- Some Arabs before Islam buried infant daughters. The Quran declares that **every life will have justice**.

## The Quran is truth
- Allah swears by the stars and the night and the dawn that the Quran is the word of a **noble messenger**: Jibril.
﴿فَأَيۡنَ تَذۡهَبُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرࣱ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمۡ أَن يَسۡتَقِيمَ﴾ ﴿وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾

## Lessons
- **Justice is certain**, even for the weakest.
- **Choose uprightness**, and ask Allah to will it for you.
