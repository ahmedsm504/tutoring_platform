## Surah At-Takwir Meaning: When the Sun Is Wrapped Up

**Surah At-Takwir** (29 ayat) paints the end of the world in short, powerful scenes. The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever wishes to look at the Day of Resurrection as if seeing it with his eyes should read At-Takwir, Al-Infitar and Al-Inshiqaq [At-Tirmidhi].

### The scenes
﴿إِذَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ كُوِّرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ ٱنكَدَرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتۡ﴾
| Scene | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The sun wrapped up | Its light folded away |
| The stars fall | They lose their light |
| Mountains moved | Uprooted from their places |
| Seas set on fire | Boiling and overflowing |
| Souls paired | Each joined with its like |

### The buried girl
﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡمَوۡءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتۡ﴾ ﴿بِأَيِّ ذَنۢبٖ قُتِلَتۡ﴾ — *And when the girl buried alive is asked, for what sin was she killed?*
- Some Arabs before Islam buried infant daughters. The Quran declares that **every life will have justice**.

### The Quran is truth
- Allah swears by the stars and the night and the dawn that the Quran is the word of a **noble messenger**: Jibril.
﴿فَأَيۡنَ تَذۡهَبُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرࣱ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمۡ أَن يَسۡتَقِيمَ﴾ ﴿وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾

### Lessons
- **Justice is certain**, even for the weakest.
- **Choose uprightness**, and ask Allah to will it for you.

## Surah Al-Infitar Meaning: What Has Deceived You About Your Generous Lord?

**Surah Al-Infitar** (19 ayat) is short, but its central question touches every heart.

### The scenes
﴿إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنفَطَرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡكَوَاكِبُ ٱنتَثَرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡقُبُورُ بُعۡثِرَتۡ﴾ ﴿عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسࣱ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ وَأَخَّرَتۡ﴾
- Then **every soul will know** what it sent ahead and what it left behind.

### The question
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّىٰكَ فَعَدَلَكَ﴾ ﴿فِيٓ أَيِّ صُورَةٖ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ﴾
- *O man, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Generous, who created you, proportioned you and balanced you?*
- **The name "Al-Karim"** here is both comfort and reproach: how can you disobey One so generous?

### The recording angels
﴿وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَحَٰفِظِينَ﴾ ﴿كِرَامٗا كَٰتِبِينَ﴾ ﴿يَعۡلَمُونَ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾
| Description | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Guardians | Watching over you |
| Noble | Honoured by Allah |
| Recording | Writing down deeds |

### The two groups
- **The righteous** in bliss, **the wicked** in fire, and on that Day *the command belongs to Allah alone*.

### Lessons
- **Don't let His generosity make you careless**; let it make you grateful.

## Surah Al-Mutaffifin Meaning: Woe to Those Who Give Less in Measure

**Surah Al-Mutaffifin** (36 ayat) begins with business ethics and ends with Paradise.

﴿وَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُطَفِّفِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ٱكۡتَالُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ يَسۡتَوۡفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا كَالُوهُمۡ أَو وَّزَنُوهُمۡ يُخۡسِرُونَ﴾
- *Woe to those who give less: who take full measure from people, but give less when they measure or weigh for them.*

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–6 | Cheating in measure, and the Day people stand before the Lord |
| 7–17 | The record of the wicked in Sijjin |
| 18–28 | The record of the righteous in ʿIlliyyin and their bliss |
| 29–36 | The disbelievers who mocked the believers |

### Rust on the heart
﴿كَلَّاۖ بَلۡۜ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾ — *No! Rather, the stain has covered their hearts from what they used to earn.*
- The Prophet ﷺ explained that each sin leaves a black spot on the heart; repentance polishes it, and repeated sins spread it [At-Tirmidhi].

### Compete in this
﴿خِتَٰمُهُۥ مِسۡكࣱۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَتَنَافَسِ ٱلۡمُتَنَٰفِسُونَ﴾ — *Its seal is musk; so for this let the competitors compete.*

### Lessons
- **Honesty in business** is part of faith.
- **Repent quickly** before sins harden the heart.
- **Compete for the Hereafter**, not for small worldly gains.

## Surah Al-Inshiqaq Meaning: You Are Toiling Toward Your Lord

**Surah Al-Inshiqaq** (25 ayat) contains one of the most personal addresses in the Quran: *O man, you are toiling toward your Lord.*

### The sky obeys
﴿إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنشَقَّتۡ﴾ ﴿وَأَذِنَتۡ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتۡ﴾ — *When the sky splits open and listens to its Lord, as it must.*
- **Even the sky** obeys its Lord. What about you?

### Toiling toward your Lord
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدۡحٗا فَمُلَٰقِيهِ﴾
- **Every effort** you make, good or bad, is a step toward meeting Allah.

### The two records
﴿فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ﴾ ﴿فَسَوۡفَ يُحَاسَبُ حِسَابٗا يَسِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ مَسۡرُورٗا﴾
| Record | Result |
|---|---|
| In the right hand | An easy account and returning to family in joy |
| Behind the back | Calling for destruction |
- The Prophet ﷺ explained to ʿAishah that the "easy account" is only the presentation of deeds [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

### The prostration
- At ayah 21 there is a **prostration of recitation**. Abu Hurayrah prostrated there and said he had seen the Prophet ﷺ do so [Muslim].

### Lessons
- **Make your toil worthwhile**: it will be shown to you.

## Quran 84:6 Explained: Every Effort Ends in Meeting Allah

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدۡحٗا فَمُلَٰقِيهِ﴾ [Al-Inshiqaq 84:6]

### *Kadh*
- Effort that wears a person out. Every human is working hard, whether for good or evil.

### Toward your Lord
- All paths end **with Allah**. The question is what you carry when you arrive.

### "You will meet Him"
- *Fa mulaqih*: the meeting is certain.

### Two outcomes
| Record received | Result |
|---|---|
| In the right hand | An easy reckoning and joyful return to family (84:7-9) |
| Behind the back | Calling for destruction (84:10-12) |

### Direct your effort
- Since you will toil anyway, make the toil **for Allah**.

### Lessons
- **Choose where your effort goes.**
- **Prepare for the meeting.**

## Surah Al-Buruj Meaning: The People of the Ditch

**Surah Al-Buruj** (22 ayat) tells of believers who stayed firm even in the face of fire. It was revealed in Makkah when Muslims were being persecuted.

### The people of the ditch
﴿قُتِلَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأُخۡدُودِ﴾ ﴿ٱلنَّارِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡوَقُودِ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ هُمۡ عَلَيۡهَا قُعُودࣱ﴾ ﴿وَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفۡعَلُونَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ شُهُودࣱ﴾
- Tyrants dug a ditch, lit a fire, and threw believers into it while watching.
﴿وَمَا نَقَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ﴾ — *Their only fault was believing in Allah.*
- Sahih Muslim has the story of the boy, the king and the monk, which many scholars linked to this surah.

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Oaths: the sky with great stars, the promised Day, the witness |
| 4–9 | The people of the ditch |
| 10–11 | Punishment for persecutors who don't repent; gardens for believers |
| 12–16 | Allah's power and names |
| 17–22 | Pharaoh and Thamud; the Quran preserved |

### Allah's names
﴿وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلۡوَدُودُ﴾ ﴿ذُو ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡمَجِيدُ﴾ ﴿فَعَّالࣱ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ﴾
- **Even for persecutors** the door of repentance was mentioned: *those who then do not repent*.

### Lessons
- **Faith is worth any sacrifice.**
- **Persecutors never win** in the end.

## Surah At-Tariq Meaning: Every Soul Has a Guardian

**Surah At-Tariq** (17 ayat) begins with a star that pierces the darkness and ends with a word that pierces doubt.

### The night-comer
﴿وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ﴾ ﴿ٱلنَّجۡمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ﴾
- **At-Ṭāriq**: what comes at night, explained here as **the piercing star**.

### Every soul has a guardian
﴿إِن كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ لَّمَّا عَلَيۡهَا حَافِظࣱ﴾ — *There is no soul but that it has a guardian over it.*
- Angels who protect and record, by Allah's command.

### How you were created
- *Let man look at what he was created from*: a fluid that gushes forth.
- **The argument**: the One who created you the first time can bring you back.

### The Day secrets are tested
- On that Day, **hidden secrets** will be examined, and man will have no power and no helper.

### A decisive word
﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلࣱ فَصۡلࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمَا هُوَ بِٱلۡهَزۡلِ﴾ — *Indeed, it is a decisive word, and it is not amusement.*

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Oath and the guardian over every soul |
| 5–10 | Creation and resurrection |
| 11–17 | The Quran is decisive; the plotters are given respite |

### Lessons
- **Someone is always watching**: live accordingly.
- **Take the Quran seriously.**

## Surah Al-A'la Meaning: Glorify the Name of Your Lord, the Most High

**Surah Al-A'la** (19 ayat) was loved by the Prophet ﷺ, who recited it with Al-Ghashiyah in the Friday and Eid prayers [Muslim].

﴿سَبِّحِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡأَعۡلَى﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ﴾
- *Glorify the name of your Lord, the Most High, who created and proportioned, who measured and guided.*
- When this was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ instructed that it be said in prostration: *Subḥāna Rabbiyal-Aʿlā*, as reported by Abu Dawud.

### Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Glorifying Allah, the Creator and Guide |
| 6–8 | The promise: you will not forget, and ease |
| 9–13 | Remind; the fearful will take heed, the wretched will avoid it |
| 14–19 | Success through purification and prayer; the Hereafter is better |

### The promise to the Prophet ﷺ
- *We will make you recite, and you will not forget, except what Allah wills.*

### The path to success
﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَذَكَرَ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّهِۦ فَصَلَّىٰ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ تُؤۡثِرُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓ﴾
| Step | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Purify yourself | From sins and bad character |
| Remember Allah | With your tongue and heart |
| Pray | The fruit of remembrance |

### Lessons
- **Better and more lasting**: weigh every choice by this.

## Surah Al-Ghashiyah Meaning: The Overwhelming Event and Four Signs to Look At

**Surah Al-Ghashiyah** (26 ayat) was recited by the Prophet ﷺ with Al-A'la in the Friday and Eid prayers [Muslim].

﴿هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلۡغَٰشِيَةِ﴾ — *Has there reached you the report of the Overwhelming Event?*

### Two kinds of faces
| Faces | Ayat | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Humbled | ﴿وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٍ خَٰشِعَةٌ﴾ ﴿عَامِلَةࣱ نَّاصِبَةࣱ﴾ | Labouring and exhausted, for nothing |
| Joyful | ﴿وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ نَّاعِمَةࣱ﴾ ﴿لِّسَعۡيِهَا رَاضِيَةࣱ﴾ ﴿فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٖ﴾ | Pleased with their efforts, in a lofty garden |

### Four signs to look at
﴿أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡإِبِلِ كَيۡفَ خُلِقَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ كَيۡفَ رُفِعَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِلَى ٱلۡجِبَالِ كَيۡفَ نُصِبَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَيۡفَ سُطِحَتۡ﴾
- Four things the Arabs saw every day in the desert: **look closer**.

### Your task is to remind
- *So remind; you are only a reminder. You are not a controller over them.*
- **Guidance belongs to Allah**; our role is to convey with wisdom.

### Lessons
- **Effort counts** only when it is for Allah.
- **Everyday sights** are signs.
