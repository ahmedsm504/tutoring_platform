## Surah Ash-Sharh Meaning: With Hardship Comes Ease

**Surah Ash-Sharh** (also called Al-Inshirāḥ) is a natural companion to Surah Ad-Duha, continuing its comfort.

### Three gifts
| Ayah | Gift |
|---|---|
| ﴿أَلَمۡ نَشۡرَحۡ لَكَ صَدۡرَكَ﴾ | An expanded, peaceful chest |
| ﴿وَوَضَعۡنَا عَنكَ وِزۡرَكَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنقَضَ ظَهۡرَكَ﴾ | A heavy burden removed |
| ﴿وَرَفَعۡنَا لَكَ ذِكۡرَكَ﴾ | His mention raised: in the adhan, the shahadah and the prayer |

### The double promise
﴿فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرٗا﴾
- **"With"**, not "after": ease comes **alongside** hardship.
- Scholars noted a linguistic point: *al-ʿusr* (hardship) is definite and repeated, so it is the **same** hardship, while *yusr* (ease) is indefinite and repeated, so it is **two** eases. One hardship cannot overcome two eases.

### After your work
﴿فَإِذَا فَرَغۡتَ فَٱنصَبۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرۡغَب﴾
- When you finish one task, **strive** in worship, and **turn your hopes** to your Lord.

### Lessons for stress
- **Hardship is not permanent.**
- **Turn to Allah** between tasks, not only at the end of the day.

## Quran 94:5-6 Explained: One Hardship Cannot Overcome Two Eases

﴿فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرٗا﴾ [Ash-Sharh 94:5-6]

### "With", not "after"
- The verse says ease is **with** hardship: even inside the difficulty, there is ease, and relief is close.

### Repeated twice
- The promise is repeated for emphasis and certainty.

### One hardship, two eases
- In Arabic, *al-ʿusr* (with "the") repeated refers to **the same** hardship, while *yusran* (without "the") repeated refers to **different** eases. Scholars derived from this: one hardship cannot overcome two eases. This meaning is reported from Ibn ʿAbbas and others.

### The context
- Surah Ash-Sharh reminds the Prophet ﷺ of blessings: an expanded chest, a removed burden, an elevated mention. Then the promise of ease, then: *When you are free, work hard, and to your Lord direct your longing.*

### Living by it
| In hardship | Do |
|---|---|
| Illness | Seek treatment and expect reward |
| Debt | Work and make duʿa |
| Grief | Pray and reach out to people |

### Lessons
- **Look for the ease** already present.
- **Turn to Allah** after every effort.

## Surah At-Tin Meaning: Created in the Best Form

**Surah At-Tin** (8 ayat) was recited by the Prophet ﷺ in the ʿIsha prayer during a journey, and al-Baraʾ said he never heard anyone with a more beautiful voice [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

### The oaths
﴿وَٱلتِّينِ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونِ﴾ ﴿وَطُورِ سِينِينَ﴾ ﴿وَهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ ٱلۡأَمِينِ﴾
| Oath | Commonly linked with |
|---|---|
| The fig and the olive | The land of Shaam, where many prophets lived, as some commentators said |
| Mount Sinai | Where Allah spoke to Musa |
| The secure city | Makkah |

### The best form
﴿لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِيٓ أَحۡسَنِ تَقۡوِيمٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ رَدَدۡنَٰهُ أَسۡفَلَ سَٰفِلِينَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونٖ﴾
- **The best stature**: in body, mind and nature.
- **The lowest of the low**: explained as weak old age, or the Fire for those who reject faith.
- **The exception**: faith and good deeds bring an **uninterrupted reward**.

### The closing question
- *Is Allah not the most just of judges?*

### Lessons
- **You were created with honour**: don't lower yourself with sin.

## Surah Al-Alaq Meaning: Read in the Name of Your Lord

**Surah Al-'Alaq** (19 ayat) begins with the first words of the Quran ever revealed, in the cave of Hira [Al-Bukhari].

### The first revelation
﴿ٱقۡرَأۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ﴾ ﴿خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِنۡ عَلَقٍ﴾ ﴿ٱقۡرَأۡ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلۡأَكۡرَمُ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِٱلۡقَلَمِ﴾ ﴿عَلَّمَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمۡ يَعۡلَمۡ﴾
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Iqraʾ | Read, recite |
| ʿAlaq | A clinging substance |
| The pen | The tool of knowledge |
- **Islam began with "Read"**: knowledge connected to the name of Allah.
- Read [How the Quran Was Compiled](/blog/compilation-of-the-quran-english/#How-the-Quran-Was-Compiled-From-the-Prophet-ﷺ-to-ʿUthman).

### The transgression
﴿كَلَّآ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطۡغَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجۡعَىٰٓ﴾
- Man transgresses when he **thinks** he needs no one.

### The man who forbade prayer
- The later ayat describe one who tried to stop the Prophet ﷺ from praying at the Kaʿbah; Sahih Muslim reports this was Abu Jahl.

### The final command
﴿كَلَّا لَا تُطِعۡهُ وَٱسۡجُدۡۤ وَٱقۡتَرِب۩﴾ — *No! Do not obey him. Prostrate and draw near.*
- There is a **prostration of recitation** here.

### Lessons
- **Seek knowledge** in the name of Allah.
- **Prostration** is the closest you get to Allah.

## "Man Transgresses When He Sees Himself Self-Sufficient": Quran 96:6-8 Explained

﴿كَلَّآ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطۡغَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجۡعَىٰٓ﴾ [Al-Alaq 96:6-8]

### After the first revelation
- The first five verses of Al-ʿAlaq speak of reading, creation and knowledge. Then comes *Kalla*: a sharp warning.

### "When he sees himself self-sufficient"
- The problem is the **feeling** of not needing Allah. Wealth, power or success can create this illusion.

### The context
- Many scholars mention that the following verses refer to Abu Jahl, who tried to stop the Prophet ﷺ from praying at the Kaʿbah.

### The cure
- *Indeed to your Lord is the return*: every power ends, and everyone returns to Allah.

### Signs of this illness
| Sign | Remedy |
|---|---|
| Neglecting prayer when busy and successful | Remember who gave success |
| Looking down on people | Remember your origin |
| Forgetting duʿa | Ask Allah in everything |

### Lessons
- **Success should increase humility.**
- **Never feel independent of Allah.**

## Surah Al-Qadr Meaning: The Night Better than a Thousand Months

Five ayat about one night that changed history: the night the Quran began to be revealed.

### Ayah by ayah
| Ayah | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةِ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ﴾ | We sent it down on the Night of Decree |
| ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا لَيۡلَةُ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ﴾ | And what will make you know what it is? |
| ﴿لَيۡلَةُ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡ أَلۡفِ شَهۡرٖ﴾ | Better than a thousand months |
| ﴿تَنَزَّلُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمۡرٖ﴾ | The angels and the Spirit (Jibril) descend |
| ﴿سَلَٰمٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطۡلَعِ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ﴾ | Peace until the break of dawn |

### A thousand months
- A thousand months is more than **83 years**: a lifetime of worship in one night.

### When is it?
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Seek it in the odd nights of the last ten of Ramadan» [Al-Bukhari].

### What to do
- **Pray**: «Whoever stands in prayer on Laylat al-Qadr with faith and hoping for reward, his past sins will be forgiven» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].
- **The du'a**: ʿAishah asked what to say, and he taught her: «Allāhumma innaka ʿafuwwun tuḥibbul-ʿafwa faʿfu ʿannī» [At-Tirmidhi].
- **Recite and reflect** on the Quran that came down that night.

## Surah Al-Bayyinah Meaning: The Clear Proof and the Best of Creation

**Surah Al-Bayyinah** (8 ayat) has a special story: the Prophet ﷺ told Ubayy ibn Kaʿb, «Allah has commanded me to recite to you: *Lam yakunil-ladhīna kafarū*». Ubayy asked: "Did He name me?" He said: «Yes», and Ubayy wept [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

### The clear proof
- People of the Book and the polytheists would not leave their ways until **the clear proof** came: a messenger from Allah reciting **purified pages**.

### The core of religion
﴿وَمَآ أُمِرُوٓاْ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ حُنَفَآءَ وَيُقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَۚ وَذَٰلِكَ دِينُ ٱلۡقَيِّمَةِ﴾
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Worship Allah sincerely | Pure intention |
| Ḥunafāʾ | Inclining to truth, away from shirk |
| Prayer | Connection with Allah |
| Zakah | Care for others |

### The best of creation
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمۡ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡبَرِيَّةِ﴾
﴿رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنۡ خَشِيَ رَبَّهُۥ﴾

### Lessons
- **Religion is simple at its core**: sincerity, prayer and zakah.
- **Mutual pleasure** between Allah and His servants is the highest reward.

## Surah Az-Zalzalah Meaning: An Atom's Weight of Good

**Surah Az-Zalzalah** (8 ayat) ends with two ayat that many scholars called the most comprehensive in the Quran.

﴿إِذَا زُلۡزِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زِلۡزَالَهَا﴾ ﴿وَأَخۡرَجَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَثۡقَالَهَا﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا لَهَا﴾

### The earth speaks
﴿يَوۡمَئِذٖ تُحَدِّثُ أَخۡبَارَهَا﴾ ﴿بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوۡحَىٰ لَهَا﴾
- **The earth will testify** about what was done on it, by Allah's command, as reported in a hadith in At-Tirmidhi.
- **Think**: what will the ground you walk on say about you?

### Seeing your deeds
﴿يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَصۡدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشۡتَاتٗا لِّيُرَوۡاْ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾

### An atom's weight
﴿فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيۡرٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾ ﴿وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖ شَرّٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾
| Deed | Example |
|---|---|
| Small good | A smile, removing harm from the road, half a date in charity |
| Small evil | A hurtful word, a small lie |
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Protect yourselves from the Fire, even with half a date» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

### Lessons
- **Never belittle a good deed.**
- **Never dismiss a small sin.**

## Surah Al-Adiyat Meaning: Man Is Ungrateful to His Lord

**Surah Al-'Adiyat** (11 ayat) begins with a dramatic scene of charging horses, then turns to the human heart.

### The oaths
﴿وَٱلۡعَٰدِيَٰتِ ضَبۡحٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡمُورِيَٰتِ قَدۡحٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡمُغِيرَٰتِ صُبۡحٗا﴾ ﴿فَأَثَرۡنَ بِهِۦ نَقۡعٗا﴾ ﴿فَوَسَطۡنَ بِهِۦ جَمۡعًا﴾
| Ayah | Scene |
|---|---|
| 1 | Charging, panting |
| 2 | Striking sparks with their hooves |
| 3 | Raiding at dawn |
| 4 | Raising clouds of dust |
| 5 | Plunging into the midst of the enemy |
- Most commentators said these are **horses**; it was reported from ʿAli that they are the camels of pilgrims.

### The truth about man
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودࣱ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدࣱ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لِحُبِّ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ لَشَدِيدٌ﴾
- **Kanūd**: one who counts the hardships and forgets the blessings.
- **Al-khayr** here means **wealth**.
- **The contrast**: a horse gives everything for its owner, while man is ungrateful to his Lord.

### The reminder
- *Does he not know that when what is in the graves is scattered, and what is in the hearts is brought out, their Lord will be fully aware of them that Day?*

### Lessons
- **Count your blessings** before your problems.

## Surah Al-Qari'ah Meaning: The Striking Calamity and the Scales

**Surah Al-Qari'ah** (11 ayat) is a short warning about the Day the scales decide everything.

﴿ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ﴾ ﴿مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ﴾
- **Al-Qāriʿah**: the one that **strikes** hearts with terror, one of the names of the Day of Resurrection.

### Two images
﴿يَوۡمَ يَكُونُ ٱلنَّاسُ كَٱلۡفَرَاشِ ٱلۡمَبۡثُوثِ﴾ ﴿وَتَكُونُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَٱلۡعِهۡنِ ٱلۡمَنفُوشِ﴾
| Image | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Scattered moths | Confused, rushing in every direction |
| Fluffed wool | Mountains losing their solidity |

### The scales
﴿فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ﴾ ﴿فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةٖ رَّاضِيَةٖ﴾
﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَفَّتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ﴾ ﴿فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا هِيَهۡ﴾ ﴿نَارٌ حَامِيَةُۢ﴾
- **"His mother is Hawiyah"**: the abyss will embrace him as a mother holds her child, a powerful image of no escape.

### What makes the scale heavy?
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Nothing is heavier in the scale than good character» [Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi].
- And: «Two words light on the tongue, heavy in the scale: *Subḥānallāhi wa biḥamdih, Subḥānallāhil-ʿAẓīm*» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].
