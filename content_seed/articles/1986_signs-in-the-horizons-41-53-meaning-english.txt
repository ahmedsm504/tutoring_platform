﴿سَنُرِيهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا فِي ٱلۡأٓفَاقِ وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَتَبَيَّنَ لَهُمۡ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّۗ أَوَلَمۡ يَكۡفِ بِرَبِّكَ أَنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ﴾ [Fussilat 41:53]

## Horizons and selves
| Place | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The horizons | The regions of the world: sky, earth and what is in them |
| Themselves | The precise creation of the human being |

## Two views
- The victories granted to the Muslims and the defeat of those who denied, showing the truth of the message.
- The signs of creation in the universe and the self that point to the Creator. The verse can carry both.

## "Until it becomes clear"
- The truth of the Quran becomes evident through visible evidence.

## "Is it not enough?"
- Allah's witness over everything is sufficient, even without another sign.

## Lessons
- **Reflect on your own body**: heart, eyes, mind.
- **Let knowledge increase certainty.**
