Surah Az-Zalzalah is eight verses that shake the heart as they describe the earth shaking. It presents a final scene of the planet we have lived on: trembling with its greatest quake, throwing out what is inside it, and then speaking, testifying to what was done on its surface. People emerge in scattered groups to be shown their deeds, and the surah closes with a scale that misses nothing: "Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it." The Prophet ﷺ called these last two verses "the unique, comprehensive verse".

This guide explains the surah verse by verse in English and turns it into a practical approach to small deeds.

## Quick Facts About Surah Az-Zalzalah
- **Number of verses**: 8.
- **Type**: Medinan in the standard mushaf; the commentators differ on whether it is Meccan or Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 99, in the thirtieth juz.
- **Names**: Az-Zalzalah, Az-Zilzal, and *Idha zulzilat*.

## The Full Text of Surah Az-Zalzalah
﴿إِذَا زُلۡزِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زِلۡزَالَهَا﴾ ﴿وَأَخۡرَجَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَثۡقَالَهَا﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا لَهَا﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَئِذٖ تُحَدِّثُ أَخۡبَارَهَا﴾ ﴿بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوۡحَىٰ لَهَا﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَصۡدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشۡتَاتٗا لِّيُرَوۡاْ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيۡرٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾ ﴿وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖ شَرّٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾ (Quran 99:1-8)

**Meaning:** When the earth is shaken with its final quake, and the earth brings out its burdens, and man says: What is wrong with it? That Day it will report its news, because your Lord has inspired it. That Day people will emerge in scattered groups to be shown their deeds. So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it.

## Verse 1: Its Final Quake
﴿إِذَا زُلۡزِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زِلۡزَالَهَا﴾ (Quran 99:1)

**"When the earth is shaken"**: violently and completely. **"Its quake"** (*zilzalaha*): the quake that belongs to it, the one decreed for it, after which there is no other. Every earthquake humanity has known is small beside it, as Surah Al-Hajj begins: "O people, fear your Lord; the quake of the Hour is a tremendous thing."

**Reflect**: we are terrified by an earthquake lasting a few seconds that destroys a few buildings. What of a quake that covers the whole earth and ends this world?

## Verse 2: Its Burdens
﴿وَأَخۡرَجَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَثۡقَالَهَا﴾ (Quran 99:2)

**"Its burdens"** (*athqalaha*): the dead inside it, cast out alive for the Reckoning; some also explain it as its buried treasures. The first view is better known and matches Surah Al-Inshiqaq: "and throws out what is in it and becomes empty." The earth in which generation after generation was buried will release them all in a single moment.

## Verse 3: What Is Wrong With It?
﴿وَقَالَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا لَهَا﴾ (Quran 99:3)

Man asks in shock and terror: what has happened to the earth? Why is it moving like this? The ground that was stable throughout his life suddenly changes. The verse pictures human confusion when the unexpected arrives.

## Verses 4–5: The Earth Reports Its News
﴿يَوۡمَئِذٖ تُحَدِّثُ أَخۡبَارَهَا﴾ ﴿بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوۡحَىٰ لَهَا﴾ (Quran 99:4-5)

**"That Day it will report its news"**: the earth will tell what was done on it, good and evil. Every place where you prayed or sinned will testify. **"Because your Lord has inspired it"**: it speaks because Allah has permitted and commanded it; the One who gives speech to everything can give speech to the earth.

**Its effect on the heart**: when you know the ground you walk on will testify for or against you, you choose where you place your feet and what you do in private. Some of the early Muslims used to say: pray in different places so that they may testify for you.

## Verse 6: Scattered Groups
﴿يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَصۡدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشۡتَاتٗا لِّيُرَوۡاْ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ (Quran 99:6)

**"People will emerge"**: from their graves, or return from the place of judgement. **"In scattered groups"** (*ashtatan*): separated into kinds, the happy and the wretched, those given their record in the right hand and those in the left. **"To be shown their deeds"**: to see the reward of their deeds, or to have their records displayed.

## Verses 7–8: An Atom's Weight
﴿فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيۡرٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾ ﴿وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖ شَرّٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾ (Quran 99:7-8)

**"Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it"**: even if it weighs as little as the smallest thing imaginable. **"And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it"**: unless Allah pardons it or the person repents.

### The unique, comprehensive verse
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ was asked about donkeys, whether there was reward in them as in horses, and he said: "Nothing has been revealed to me about them except this unique, comprehensive verse: *So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it.*" *Comprehensive* (*jami'ah*) because it covers every deed of good and evil; *unique* (*fadhdhah*) because no other verse matches it in this breadth.

## Word Meanings
- **Zulzilat**: was shaken violently.
- **Zilzalaha**: its own, final quake.
- **Athqalaha**: its burdens, the dead or its treasures.
- **Ma laha**: what is wrong with it?
- **Tuhaddithu akhbaraha**: reports its news.
- **Awha laha**: inspired it, commanded it.
- **Yasduru**: emerge, return.
- **Ashtatan**: in scattered groups.
- **Mithqal**: weight.
- **Dharrah**: an atom, the smallest thing; explained as a tiny ant or a speck of dust in a sunbeam.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Zulzilat... zilzalaha"**: the verb with its own noun stresses the force of the quake.
2. **"The earth" repeated** in the first two verses to magnify the scene.
3. **"That Day" repeated** to fix it in the mind.
4. **A perfect balance at the end**: "good... see it" and "evil... see it".
5. **"Man" with the definite article**: every human will be amazed.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Lam qamariyyah** in *al-ard* and *al-insan*; **lam shamsiyyah** in *an-nas*.
- **Ikhfa** in *yawma'idhin tuhaddithu*: tanween hidden before *ta*.
- **Idgham without ghunnah** in *ashtatan li-yuraw*: tanween merges into lam.
- **Idgham with ghunnah** in *khayran yarah* and *sharran yarah*: tanween merges into ya with ghunnah.
- **Ghunnah** on the doubled nun in *bi-anna*.
- **Natural madd** in *zilzalaha*, *athqalaha* and *akhbaraha*.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Before Az-Zalzalah comes Al-Bayyinah, which ends with the recompense of those who disbelieve, "the worst of creatures", and those who believe, "the best of creatures". Az-Zalzalah then shows when and how that recompense comes: when the earth quakes and every atom is seen. After it comes Al-'Adiyat, ending with "when what is in the graves is scattered", continuing the scene of the earth casting out its burdens.

## The "Atom's Weight" Approach to Daily Life
This verse changes how a believer sees every detail of the day. No good deed is too small to matter, and no sin is too small to ignore.

**On the side of good:**
- **Do not belittle any kindness.** In Sahih Muslim, Abu Dharr reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not belittle any good deed, even meeting your brother with a cheerful face."
- **Even half a date.** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'Adi ibn Hatim reported: "Protect yourselves from the Fire, even with half a date."
- **Water for a dog.** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ told of a man who found a dog panting with thirst, went down a well, filled his shoe with water and gave it to drink, "so Allah thanked him and forgave him".
- **Removing harm from the road.** In Sahih Muslim: "I saw a man moving about in Paradise because of a tree he cut from the road that was harming people."

**On the side of evil:**
- **A cat.** In al-Bukhari and Muslim: "A woman was punished because of a cat she imprisoned until it died, and she entered the Fire because of it."
- **Sins we consider small.** In Sahih al-Bukhari, Anas said: "You do deeds that are finer than a hair in your eyes, but we used to count them in the time of the Prophet ﷺ among the destroying sins."
- **One word.** In Sahih al-Bukhari: "A servant may speak a word that angers Allah without giving it a thought, and because of it he falls into Hell."

A believer lives between hope in small good and fear of small evil.

## The Earth as Witness: Preparing for Its Testimony
1. **Pray in many places**: at home, at work, while travelling.
2. **Remember Allah on the road**.
3. **Remove harm from the road**.
4. **Avoid sins in private**: the earth sees even when no one else does.
5. **Do good to those around you**: its traces remain.

## Is There an Authentic Hadith on the Virtue of Az-Zalzalah?
Narrations say it equals half or a quarter of the Quran, but their chains are weak according to many specialists, so they should not be relied on. It is enough that the Prophet ﷺ called its final verses "the unique, comprehensive verse" in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

## Worldly Earthquakes as Reminders
When a Muslim hears of an earthquake somewhere in the world, it is a small picture of what will happen on the Day of Resurrection. Natural signs are among the ways Allah reminds His servants: "We send signs only as a warning" (17:59). Appropriate responses: turn to remembrance and prayer, as the Prophet ﷺ taught for eclipses in al-Bukhari and Muslim ("supplicate to Allah, say takbir, pray and give charity"); repent; help those affected; and never gloat, for a calamity may be mercy and expiation for its people.

## Practical Lessons
1. Prepare for the great quake through repentance and good deeds.
2. Do not belittle any good deed.
3. Do not underestimate any sin.
4. Watch your private conduct; the earth testifies.
5. Let worldly earthquakes remind you of the final one.
6. Train your tongue in good words.
7. Do many small, constant good deeds: smiling, greeting, removing harm.

## Teaching Children
- Explain "an atom" as something smaller than a grain of sand that Allah still sees.
- Keep an "atoms notebook" where they record small good deeds each day.
- Teach them that the earth testifies, so they behave well everywhere.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "when the earth is shaken with its quake" mean?
When the earth undergoes its greatest, decreed quake at the start of the Hour.

### What are "its burdens"?
The dead inside it, cast out for the Reckoning; some say its treasures.

### How will the earth report its news?
It will tell what people did on it, good and evil, by Allah's permission.

### What is "the unique, comprehensive verse"?
"Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it," as the Prophet ﷺ named it in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Does Az-Zalzalah equal half the Quran?
The narrations saying so have weak chains according to many specialists.

### How many verses are in Surah Az-Zalzalah?
8 verses.

### Will a believer see his small sins on the Day of Judgement?
Allah may show them to him and then forgive him by His grace, or they may already have been expiated in this life by repentance, good deeds and hardships.

### Will a disbeliever see his good deeds?
He is rewarded for them in this world. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said that a disbeliever is given food in this world for the good he did for Allah's sake, until when he reaches the Hereafter he has no good deed left to be rewarded for.

## Conclusion
Surah Az-Zalzalah tells you that the ground you walk on will one day speak, and every atom of your deeds is recorded. So do not belittle any kindness and do not take any sin lightly. Fill your day with good atoms: a smile, a kind word, a small charity, a humble prostration. You will see all of it on the Day when people emerge in scattered groups to be shown their deeds.
