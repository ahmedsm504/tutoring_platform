Juz 21 begins with ﴿۞وَلَا تُجَٰدِلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِلَّا بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ﴾, "Do not argue with the People of the Book except in the best manner", so it is known as **Juz Wa La Tujadilu**. It runs from **Al-'Ankabut 29:46 to Al-Ahzab 33:30**, with three complete surahs in between: **Ar-Rum**, **Luqman** and **As-Sajdah**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 402 to 421**.

This is the juz of **Allah's signs in the universe, the self and history**: a prophecy fulfilled in Ar-Rum, signs in creation, marriage and the diversity of languages, a wise father advising his son in Luqman, the scene of those who prostrate in As-Sajdah, then the test of the Confederates when hearts reached the throats.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **reflecting on Allah's signs, both seen and revealed, and holding to the fitrah and His promise**. Ar-Rum opens with an unseen promise that came true, then repeats "and among His signs" to awaken the mind, and declares: ﴿فِطۡرَتَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِي فَطَرَ ٱلنَّاسَ عَلَيۡهَاۚ﴾, "the natural disposition Allah instilled in people." Luqman brings the upbringing that preserves this fitrah; As-Sajdah shows believers whose ﴿تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمَضَاجِعِ﴾, "sides forsake their beds"; and Al-Ahzab shows the believers whom the siege ﴿وَمَا زَادَهُمۡ إِلَّآ إِيمَٰنٗا وَتَسۡلِيمٗا﴾, "only increased in faith and submission."

The message: **Allah's promise is true, His signs surround you, your fitrah points to Him, so stand firm as the people of the Trench did.**

## Part one: the close of Surah Al-'Ankabut (46-69)

### 1. The etiquette of dialogue and the Quran in hearts (46-55)
"Do not argue with the People of the Book except in the best manner, except those of them who do wrong, and say: we believe in what was revealed to us and to you; our God and your God is one, and to Him we submit." Then the proof from the Prophet's unlettered state: you did not recite any scripture before it, nor write one with your right hand. Then where the Quran lives: ﴿بَلۡ هُوَ ءَايَٰتُۢ بَيِّنَٰتࣱ فِي صُدُورِ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَۚ﴾, "Rather, it is clear verses in the hearts of those given knowledge."

**Reflect**: among this ummah's greatest distinctions is that its Book is preserved in hearts before pages. A hafiz carries clear verses in his chest. See [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

### 2. Emigration, provision and the Hereafter (56-69)
﴿يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ أَرۡضِي وَٰسِعَةࣱ فَإِيَّـٰيَ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ﴾ ﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۖ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡنَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾, "O My servants who believe, My earth is spacious, so worship Me alone. Every soul will taste death, then to Us you will be returned." Then reassurance about provision: ﴿وَكَأَيِّن مِّن دَآبَّةٖ لَّا تَحۡمِلُ رِزۡقَهَا ٱللَّهُ يَرۡزُقُهَا وَإِيَّاكُمۡۚ﴾, "How many creatures do not carry their provision! Allah provides for them and for you."

Then the truth of the two lives: ﴿وَمَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا لَهۡوࣱ وَلَعِبࣱۚ وَإِنَّ ٱلدَّارَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ لَهِيَ ٱلۡحَيَوَانُۚ﴾, "This worldly life is only amusement and play, and the home of the Hereafter is the true life." The close is a great promise: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ جَٰهَدُواْ فِينَا لَنَهۡدِيَنَّهُمۡ سُبُلَنَاۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَمَعَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾, "Those who strive for Us, We will surely guide them to Our ways; and Allah is with those who do good."

**Lesson**: striving comes before guidance. Whoever strives with himself to memorize the Quran or leave a sin, Allah opens ways for him he never knew. Read [Those Who Strive for Us: 29:69](/blog/walladhina-jahadu-fina-meaning-english/).

## Part two: Surah Ar-Rum (1-60)

Ar-Rum is Makki, with 60 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah Ar-Rum Meaning in English](/blog/surah-ar-rum-meaning-english/).

### 1. A prophecy of victory (1-10)
﴿الٓمٓ﴾ ﴿غُلِبَتِ ٱلرُّومُ﴾ ﴿فِيٓ أَدۡنَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ غَلَبِهِمۡ سَيَغۡلِبُونَ﴾ ﴿فِي بِضۡعِ سِنِينَۗ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ مِن قَبۡلُ وَمِنۢ بَعۡدُۚ وَيَوۡمَئِذٖ يَفۡرَحُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾, "Alif Lam Mim. The Romans have been defeated in the nearest land, but after their defeat they will overcome, within a few years. To Allah belongs the command before and after, and on that day the believers will rejoice." The Persians had defeated the Romans; the idolaters rejoiced because the Persians were idol-worshippers like them, and the Muslims grieved because the Romans were People of the Book. The promise was revealed and fulfilled within a few years, as the Quran foretold.

Then a precise description of the heedless: ﴿يَعۡلَمُونَ ظَٰهِرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ غَٰفِلُونَ﴾, "They know the outward aspect of worldly life, but of the Hereafter they are heedless."

**Lesson**: a person may master worldly sciences while neglecting the most important truths. Real knowledge links this world to the next.

### 2. Glorification and "among His signs" (11-27)
﴿فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ حِينَ تُمۡسُونَ وَحِينَ تُصۡبِحُونَ﴾, "So glorify Allah when you reach the evening and when you reach the morning." Then the chain of signs:
1. **Man from dust**: ﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنۡ خَلَقَكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ إِذَآ أَنتُم بَشَرࣱ تَنتَشِرُونَ﴾, "Among His signs is that He created you from dust, and then you are humans spreading out."
2. **Marriage**: ﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنۡ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا لِّتَسۡكُنُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَكُم مَّوَدَّةٗ وَرَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾, "Among His signs is that He created for you spouses from yourselves to find tranquillity in them, and placed between you affection and mercy. In that are signs for people who reflect."
3. **Languages and colours**: ﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ خَلۡقُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفُ أَلۡسِنَتِكُمۡ وَأَلۡوَٰنِكُمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّلۡعَٰلِمِينَ﴾, "Among His signs are the creation of the heavens and earth and the diversity of your languages and colours. In that are signs for those of knowledge."
4. **Sleep and work**: ﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ مَنَامُكُم بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَٱبۡتِغَآؤُكُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَسۡمَعُونَ﴾, "Among His signs is your sleep by night and day and your seeking His bounty. In that are signs for people who listen."
5. **Lightning and rain**: ﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ يُرِيكُمُ ٱلۡبَرۡقَ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَيُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَيُحۡيِۦ بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَآۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ﴾, "Among His signs is that He shows you lightning in fear and hope, and sends down water from the sky, reviving the earth after its death. In that are signs for people who reason."
6. **The heavens and earth standing by His command**, then when He calls you once from the earth, you will come out.

**Reflect on the endings**: marriage needs "reflection" to grasp its secrets; the diversity of languages is grasped by "those of knowledge"; the effect of sleep by those who "listen"; and rain needs "reason" to connect the revival of the earth with resurrection. Read [Affection and Mercy: 30:21](/blog/mawadda-wa-rahma-meaning-english/).

### 3. The fitrah (28-32)
﴿فَأَقِمۡ وَجۡهَكَ لِلدِّينِ حَنِيفٗاۚ فِطۡرَتَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِي فَطَرَ ٱلنَّاسَ عَلَيۡهَاۚ لَا تَبۡدِيلَ لِخَلۡقِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡقَيِّمُ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾, "So set your face to the religion, inclining to truth: the fitrah of Allah on which He created people. There is no altering Allah's creation. That is the upright religion, but most people do not know." In Sahih Al-Bukhari the Prophet ﷺ said: "Every child is born upon the fitrah; then his parents make him a Jew, a Christian or a Magian", and Abu Hurayrah recited this verse.

Then a new quarter: ﴿۞مُنِيبِينَ إِلَيۡهِ وَٱتَّقُوهُ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَلَا تَكُونُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾, "turning to Him in repentance; fear Him, establish prayer and do not be among the idolaters", those who divided their religion into sects, each party rejoicing in what it has.

**Lesson**: faith in Allah is rooted in the fitrah; upbringing preserves it rather than creating it.

### 4. Corruption and returning (33-53)
Man swings between mercy and hardship; then usury and zakah: what you give in usury to grow in people's wealth does not grow with Allah, and what you give in zakah seeking Allah's face, those are the ones multiplied. Then the rule: ﴿ظَهَرَ ٱلۡفَسَادُ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِي ٱلنَّاسِ لِيُذِيقَهُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي عَمِلُواْ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾, "Corruption has appeared on land and sea because of what people's hands have earned, so He may let them taste part of what they did, that perhaps they will return." Then the winds bearing good news, and the traces of Allah's mercy: how He revives the earth after its death.

**Reflect**: the purpose of calamities is ﴿لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾, "that perhaps they will return": a merciful warning before the greater punishment.

### 5. The stages of life and the close (54-60)
A new quarter: ﴿۞ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن ضَعۡفٖ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ضَعۡفٖ قُوَّةٗ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ قُوَّةٖ ضَعۡفٗا وَشَيۡبَةٗۚ﴾, "Allah is the One who created you from weakness, then gave strength after weakness, then after strength weakness and grey hair": the weakness of childhood, the strength of youth, the weakness of old age. Then a scene of the Day: the criminals swear they remained only an hour. The close: ﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّۖ وَلَا يَسۡتَخِفَّنَّكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُوقِنُونَ﴾, "So be patient; Allah's promise is true. And do not let those without certainty unsettle you."

**The link**: the surah began with a promise fulfilled (the Roman victory) and ends with "Allah's promise is true"; whoever saw the first promise come true can be certain of the greater one.

## Part three: Surah Luqman (1-34)

Luqman is Makki, with 34 verses, named after the sage Luqman whom Allah gave wisdom. For the whole surah see [Surah Luqman Meaning in English](/blog/surah-luqman-meaning-english/).

### 1. The doers of good and idle talk (1-11)
Guidance and mercy for the doers of good, who establish prayer, give zakah and are certain of the Hereafter. In contrast: ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَشۡتَرِي لَهۡوَ ٱلۡحَدِيثِ لِيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖ وَيَتَّخِذَهَا هُزُوًاۚ﴾, "Among people is one who buys idle talk to lead others from Allah's way without knowledge, and takes it in mockery." Then: this is Allah's creation; show me what those besides Him have created.

### 2. Luqman's advice to his son (12-19)
﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا لُقۡمَٰنَ ٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ أَنِ ٱشۡكُرۡ لِلَّهِۚ﴾, "We gave Luqman wisdom: be grateful to Allah." Then the advice:
1. **Monotheism first**: ﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ لُقۡمَٰنُ لِٱبۡنِهِۦ وَهُوَ يَعِظُهُۥ يَٰبُنَيَّ لَا تُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّ ٱلشِّرۡكَ لَظُلۡمٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾, "Luqman said to his son, advising him: O my dear son, do not associate anything with Allah; shirk is a tremendous wrong." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, when ﴿وَلَمۡ يَلۡبِسُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنَهُم بِظُلۡمٍ﴾, "and did not mix their faith with wrong", was revealed, it weighed on the Companions, and the Prophet ﷺ said: "It is not as you think; it is as Luqman said to his son", citing this verse.
2. **Kindness to parents** (a divine interjection within the advice): ﴿وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ حَمَلَتۡهُ أُمُّهُۥ وَهۡنًا عَلَىٰ وَهۡنٖ وَفِصَٰلُهُۥ فِي عَامَيۡنِ أَنِ ٱشۡكُرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيۡكَ إِلَيَّ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾, "We enjoined on man his parents: his mother carried him in weakness upon weakness, and his weaning is in two years. Be grateful to Me and to your parents; to Me is the return", then ﴿وَإِن جَٰهَدَاكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن تُشۡرِكَ بِي مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمࣱ فَلَا تُطِعۡهُمَاۖ وَصَاحِبۡهُمَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا مَعۡرُوفٗاۖ﴾, "But if they strive to make you associate with Me what you have no knowledge of, do not obey them, yet keep them company in this world kindly."
3. **Awareness of Allah**: ﴿يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنَّهَآ إِن تَكُ مِثۡقَالَ حَبَّةٖ مِّنۡ خَرۡدَلٖ فَتَكُن فِي صَخۡرَةٍ أَوۡ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ أَوۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَأۡتِ بِهَا ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَطِيفٌ خَبِيرࣱ﴾, "O my son, even if it is the weight of a mustard seed, in a rock, in the heavens or on earth, Allah will bring it forth. Allah is Subtle, Aware."
4. **Worship, calling to good and patience**: ﴿يَٰبُنَيَّ أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَٱنۡهَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَصَابَكَۖ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾, "O my son, establish prayer, enjoin good, forbid evil and be patient with what befalls you; that is of the matters requiring resolve."
5. **Humility**: ﴿وَلَا تُصَعِّرۡ خَدَّكَ لِلنَّاسِ وَلَا تَمۡشِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَحًاۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالٖ فَخُورٖ﴾, "Do not turn your cheek in contempt from people, nor walk on earth arrogantly; Allah does not love the self-deluded and boastful."
6. **Moderation**: ﴿وَٱقۡصِدۡ فِي مَشۡيِكَ وَٱغۡضُضۡ مِن صَوۡتِكَۚ إِنَّ أَنكَرَ ٱلۡأَصۡوَٰتِ لَصَوۡتُ ٱلۡحَمِيرِ﴾, "Be moderate in your pace and lower your voice; the harshest of sounds is the braying of donkeys."

**The method of upbringing in the advice**:
- Calling "O my dear son" with affection, three times.
- Giving the reason with the command: "shirk is a tremendous wrong", "that is of the matters requiring resolve".
- The order: belief, then awareness of Allah, then worship, then manners.

### 3. The firmest handhold and Allah's words (20-30)
All in the heavens and earth subjected to you. Then Hizb 42 begins: ﴿۞وَمَن يُسۡلِمۡ وَجۡهَهُۥٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَهُوَ مُحۡسِنࣱ فَقَدِ ٱسۡتَمۡسَكَ بِٱلۡعُرۡوَةِ ٱلۡوُثۡقَىٰۗ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾, "Whoever submits his face to Allah while doing good has grasped the firmest handhold, and to Allah is the outcome of all matters." Then: ﴿وَلَوۡ أَنَّمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِن شَجَرَةٍ أَقۡلَٰمࣱ وَٱلۡبَحۡرُ يَمُدُّهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ سَبۡعَةُ أَبۡحُرٖ مَّا نَفِدَتۡ كَلِمَٰتُ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "If all the trees on earth were pens and the sea, replenished by seven more seas, were ink, the words of Allah would not be exhausted."

### 4. The keys of the unseen (31-34)
Ships sail by Allah's favour, and when waves like canopies cover them, they call on Allah sincerely. Then: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ وَٱخۡشَوۡاْ يَوۡمٗا لَّا يَجۡزِي وَالِدٌ عَن وَلَدِهِۦ وَلَا مَوۡلُودٌ هُوَ جَازٍ عَن وَالِدِهِۦ شَيۡـًٔاۚ﴾, "O mankind, fear your Lord and fear a Day when no father will avail his child, nor any child avail his father at all." The close: ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِ وَيُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡغَيۡثَ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِۖ وَمَا تَدۡرِي نَفۡسࣱ مَّاذَا تَكۡسِبُ غَدٗاۖ وَمَا تَدۡرِي نَفۡسُۢ بِأَيِّ أَرۡضٖ تَمُوتُۚ﴾, "With Allah is knowledge of the Hour; He sends down the rain and knows what is in the wombs. No soul knows what it will earn tomorrow, and no soul knows in what land it will die." In Sahih Al-Bukhari the Prophet ﷺ said: "The keys of the unseen are five; none knows them but Allah", then recited this verse.

## Part four: Surah As-Sajdah (1-30)

As-Sajdah is Makki, with 30 verses, also called "Alif Lam Mim Tanzil". In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ used to recite in the Fajr prayer on Friday Alif Lam Mim Tanzil (As-Sajdah) and ﴿هَلۡ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ﴾ (Al-Insan). For the whole surah see [Surah As-Sajda Meaning in English](/blog/surah-as-sajda-meaning-english/).

### 1. Creation and governance (1-11)
﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ﴾, "Allah is the One who created the heavens, the earth and all between them in six days, then rose over the Throne." Then: ﴿ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحۡسَنَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقَهُۥۖ وَبَدَأَ خَلۡقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ مِن طِينٖ﴾, "who perfected everything He created, and began the creation of man from clay", then made his offspring from a humble fluid, then proportioned him and breathed into him of His spirit. Then a new quarter: ﴿۞قُلۡ يَتَوَفَّىٰكُم مَّلَكُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ ٱلَّذِي وُكِّلَ بِكُمۡ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمۡ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾, "Say: the angel of death put in charge of you will take you, then to your Lord you will be returned."

### 2. Those who prostrate and night prayer (12-22)
A scene of the criminals hanging their heads: our Lord, we have seen and heard, so send us back. Then the contrast: ﴿إِنَّمَا يُؤۡمِنُ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهَا خَرُّواْۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ وَسَبَّحُواْ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ﴾, "Only those believe in Our verses who, when reminded of them, fall in prostration and glorify their Lord with praise, and are not arrogant": the place of prostration. Then: ﴿تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمَضَاجِعِ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُمۡ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾ ﴿فَلَا تَعۡلَمُ نَفۡسࣱ مَّآ أُخۡفِيَ لَهُم مِّن قُرَّةِ أَعۡيُنٖ جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾, "Their sides forsake their beds as they call their Lord in fear and hope, and spend from what We provided them. No soul knows what joy has been hidden for them as a reward for what they used to do."

In Sahih Al-Bukhari the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah said: I have prepared for My righteous servants what no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and has never occurred to a human heart", then said: recite if you wish, "No soul knows what joy has been hidden for them."

**Reflect**: they hid their deeds in the darkness of night, so Allah hid their reward for them; the reward matches the deed.

Then: ﴿وَلَنُذِيقَنَّهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَدۡنَىٰ دُونَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾, "We will let them taste the nearer punishment before the greater punishment, that perhaps they will return."

### 3. Musa and leadership through patience (23-30)
We gave Musa the Book, and made among them leaders guiding by Our command when they were patient and certain of Our verses. Leadership in religion is earned through patience and certainty. Then: turn away from them and wait; they too are waiting.

## Part five: the start of Surah Al-Ahzab (1-30)

Al-Ahzab is Madani, with 73 verses, 30 of which fall in this juz. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Ahzab Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-ahzab-meaning-english/).

### 1. Correcting social concepts (1-8)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ ٱتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ وَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَۚ﴾, "O Prophet, fear Allah and do not obey the disbelievers and hypocrites." Then: ﴿مَّا جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِرَجُلٖ مِّن قَلۡبَيۡنِ فِي جَوۡفِهِۦۚ﴾, "Allah has not placed two hearts in any man's chest": a wife does not become a mother through zihar, nor an adopted son a real son; call them by their fathers' names, that is more just with Allah. Then: ﴿ٱلنَّبِيُّ أَوۡلَىٰ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ مِنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡۖ وَأَزۡوَٰجُهُۥٓ أُمَّهَٰتُهُمۡۗ﴾, "The Prophet is closer to the believers than their own selves, and his wives are their mothers."

### 2. The Battle of the Confederates (9-27)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ جَآءَتۡكُمۡ جُنُودࣱ فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ رِيحٗا وَجُنُودٗا لَّمۡ تَرَوۡهَاۚ﴾, "O you who believe, remember Allah's favour to you when armies came at you and We sent against them a wind and forces you did not see." Then the severity: ﴿إِذۡ جَآءُوكُم مِّن فَوۡقِكُمۡ وَمِنۡ أَسۡفَلَ مِنكُمۡ وَإِذۡ زَاغَتِ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَبَلَغَتِ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلۡحَنَاجِرَ وَتَظُنُّونَ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلظُّنُونَا۠﴾, "when they came at you from above and below you, when eyes shifted and hearts reached the throats, and you assumed various assumptions about Allah."

Then people divided:
- **The hypocrites**: Allah and His Messenger promised us only delusion; and: our homes are exposed, though they were not. Then a new quarter: ﴿۞قَدۡ يَعۡلَمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُعَوِّقِينَ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلۡقَآئِلِينَ لِإِخۡوَٰنِهِمۡ هَلُمَّ إِلَيۡنَاۖ﴾, "Allah knows those among you who hold others back, and those who say to their brothers: come to us."
- **The example**: ﴿لَّقَدۡ كَانَ لَكُمۡ فِي رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ أُسۡوَةٌ حَسَنَةࣱ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَ وَذَكَرَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا﴾, "There has certainly been for you in the Messenger of Allah an excellent example, for whoever hopes in Allah and the Last Day and remembers Allah often." Read [An Excellent Example: 33:21](/blog/uswa-hasana-meaning-english/).
- **The believers**: ﴿وَلَمَّا رَءَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابَ قَالُواْ هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَنَا ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَصَدَقَ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥۚ وَمَا زَادَهُمۡ إِلَّآ إِيمَٰنٗا وَتَسۡلِيمٗا﴾, "When the believers saw the Confederates, they said: this is what Allah and His Messenger promised us, and Allah and His Messenger spoke the truth. It only increased them in faith and submission."
- **The truthful**: ﴿مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رِجَالࣱ صَدَقُواْ مَا عَٰهَدُواْ ٱللَّهَ عَلَيۡهِۖ فَمِنۡهُم مَّن قَضَىٰ نَحۡبَهُۥ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَنتَظِرُۖ وَمَا بَدَّلُواْ تَبۡدِيلٗا﴾, "Among the believers are men who were true to their covenant with Allah: some have fulfilled their vow, some are waiting, and they have not altered in the least." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, the Companions held that it was revealed about Anas ibn an-Nadr and those like him.

Then the end: Allah turned back the disbelievers in their rage, having gained no good, and spared the believers from fighting. Then Banu Qurayzah, who had backed the Confederates.

**The great lesson**: the same hardship looked like "delusion" to the hypocrite and like "a true promise" to the believer. The heart makes the difference.

### 3. The choice given to the Mothers of the Believers (28-30)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ قُل لِّأَزۡوَٰجِكَ إِن كُنتُنَّ تُرِدۡنَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَزِينَتَهَا فَتَعَالَيۡنَ أُمَتِّعۡكُنَّ وَأُسَرِّحۡكُنَّ سَرَاحٗا جَمِيلٗا﴾, "O Prophet, say to your wives: if you desire worldly life and its adornment, come, I will provide for you and release you gracefully", but if you desire Allah, His Messenger and the Hereafter, Allah has prepared a great reward for the doers of good among you. In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'A'ishah chose Allah, His Messenger and the Hereafter, and his other wives did the same. The juz ends noting that whoever of them commits a clear indecency will have the punishment doubled.

## Tajweed points in Juz 21

### ﴿ٱلظُّنُونَا۠﴾ in Al-Ahzab 10
The final alif is written but not pronounced when continuing; Hafs pronounces it when stopping.

### ﴿ٱلَّـٰٓـِٔي﴾ in Al-Ahzab 4
Read in Hafs as *al-la'i*, with a kasrah hamzah after an alif lengthened as a connected madd.

### ﴿مِن ضَعۡفٖ﴾ in Ar-Rum 54
In its three occurrences here, Hafs may read *da'f* with fathah or *du'f* with dammah; both are authentic readings.

### ﴿الٓمٓ﴾ at the start of Ar-Rum, Luqman and As-Sajdah
Alif without madd; Lam and Mim with a necessary six-count madd, with the meem of "Lam" merged into "Mim".

### The prostration of the juz
At ﴿وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ﴾ in As-Sajdah 15. See [Sujud al-Tilawah in English](/blog/sujud-al-tilawa-english/).

## How to memorize Juz 21 with understanding

1. **Learn the "among His signs" verses with their endings** in a table: topic and ending.
2. **Luqman's advice has six points**: monotheism, parents, awareness of Allah, prayer with calling to good and patience, humility, moderation.
3. **As-Sajdah is short and cohesive**: creation, death, criminals, those who prostrate, Musa. Recite it at Fajr on Friday to fix it.
4. **Al-Ahzab is a battle narrative**: the armies, the severity, the hypocrites, the example, the believers, the truthful, victory, Qurayzah.
5. **Watch the surah openings**: three consecutive surahs begin with Alif Lam Mim.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 21

- **Argue in the best manner.**
- **Carry the Quran in your heart**: it is clear verses.
- **Strive with yourself**, and Allah will guide you to His ways.
- **Trust Allah's promise**: you have seen promises fulfilled.
- **Reflect on Allah's signs** in yourself, your spouse, your language and your sleep.
- **Protect your children's fitrah**; do not corrupt it.
- **Know that calamities are warnings**, so that we may return.
- **Raise your children on Luqman's method**: belief, then awareness, then worship, then manners.
- **Lower your voice** and walk humbly.
- **Pray at night**: a hidden reward awaits.
- **Follow the Prophet ﷺ** in hardship.
- **Be among the truthful** who did not alter.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 21 called "Wa La Tujadilu"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞وَلَا تُجَٰدِلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ﴾.

### What was Luqman's advice to his son?
Avoid shirk, honour parents, be aware of Allah, establish prayer, enjoin good, forbid evil and be patient, avoid arrogance, and be moderate in walking and voice.

### How many prostrations are in Juz 21?
One, in Surah As-Sajdah, verse 15.

### Which two surahs did the Prophet ﷺ recite at Fajr on Friday?
As-Sajdah and Al-Insan, as in Al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Where does Juz 21 end?
At Al-Ahzab 30. Juz 22 begins with ﴿۞وَمَن يَقۡنُتۡ مِنكُنَّ لِلَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ﴾, "And whoever of you is devoutly obedient to Allah and His Messenger."

## Conclusion

Juz 21 opens your eyes to Allah's signs: a promise of victory fulfilled, marriage full of affection and mercy, languages and colours that differ, and a fitrah that points to the Creator. With Luqman it teaches you how to preserve that fitrah in your children; in As-Sajdah it shows you hearts that forsake their beds; and then it places you in the Trench to choose: will you see hardship as delusion, or as a true promise?
