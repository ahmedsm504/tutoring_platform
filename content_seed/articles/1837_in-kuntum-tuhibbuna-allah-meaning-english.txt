## The verse of testing
- Some scholars of the early generations called it *ayat al-mihnah*, the verse of testing: anyone can claim to love Allah; the proof is **following the Messenger**.

## A chain of results
| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| You love Allah | A claim |
| You follow the Prophet ﷺ | The proof |
| Allah loves you | The greater reward |
| He forgives your sins | An added gift |

## Being loved is greater
- Scholars note: the important thing is not that you love, but that **you are loved** by Allah.

## Following in practice
- Learning the Sunnah, praying as he prayed, his character with people, and his duʿas.

## Love without innovation
- Following means doing what he did in the way he did it, not inventing new acts of worship.

## Lessons
- **Show love through action.**
- **Learn one Sunnah each week** and practise it.
