**Surah Yunus** (109 ayat) is named after the prophet whose people believed before punishment struck.

## Signs of order
- The sun made a source of brightness and the moon a light, with phases to know the count of years and time.

## Praying only in hardship
- When harm touches a person, he calls on Allah lying down, sitting or standing. When the harm is removed, he goes on **as if he never called**.

## The Quran as healing
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءࣱ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾

## The friends of Allah
﴿أَلَآ إِنَّ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾
- The next verse defines them: *those who believed and were mindful of Allah*.

## Faith too late
- As he drowned, Pharaoh declared belief. He was told: *Now? When you disobeyed before?* His body was preserved as a sign.

## The people of Yunus
- They are the one people who believed when they saw the punishment approaching, and Allah lifted it from them.

## Lessons
- **Remember Allah in ease**, not only in hardship.
- **Repentance must come before the end.**
