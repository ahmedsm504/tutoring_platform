Children memorize faster, and love the Quran more, when they understand what they're saying. Here are 30 words from the short surahs, explained in simple language based on concise, reliable tafsir. Use one or two words after your child memorizes each surah.

## An-Nas to Al-Ikhlas
| Word | Surah | Simple meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ﴿ٱلۡوَسۡوَاسِ﴾ *al-waswās* | An-Nas | Shaytan, who whispers bad thoughts |
| ﴿ٱلۡخَنَّاسِ﴾ *al-khannās* | An-Nas | The one who hides and retreats when Allah is remembered |
| ﴿ٱلۡفَلَقِ﴾ *al-falaq* | Al-Falaq | Daybreak, when morning splits the darkness |
| ﴿غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ﴾ | Al-Falaq | The night when it falls and grows dark |
| ﴿ٱلنَّفَّٰثَٰتِ فِي ٱلۡعُقَدِ﴾ | Al-Falaq | Those who practise magic by blowing on knots |
| ﴿ٱلصَّمَدُ﴾ *aṣ-ṣamad* | Al-Ikhlas | The Master whom all creation needs and turns to |
| ﴿كُفُوًا﴾ *kufuwan* | Al-Ikhlas | Equal or comparable |

## How to use this glossary
1. **One or two words per surah**, not all at once.
2. **Make it visual:** draw the birds of Al-Fil, or show your child some fluffy wool for ﴿كَٱلۡعِهۡنِ ٱلۡمَنفُوشِ﴾.
3. **Ask again two days later:** "Do you remember what *aṣ-ṣamad* means?"
4. **Check a reliable tafsir** for deeper questions rather than guessing.
