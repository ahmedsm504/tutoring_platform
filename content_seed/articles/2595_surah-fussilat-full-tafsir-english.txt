Surah Fussilat is the surah that made a leader of Quraysh fall silent. The biographers of the Prophet ﷺ relate that 'Utbah ibn Rabi'ah came to offer him wealth and power in exchange for abandoning his message, and the Prophet ﷺ recited the opening of this surah until he reached the warning of a thunderbolt like that of 'Ad and Thamud, at which 'Utbah put his hand on the Prophet's ﷺ mouth and begged him to stop. The surah contains some of the most moving verses for believers: angels descending on those who say "Our Lord is Allah" and stand firm, telling them "Do not fear and do not grieve"; and some of the most practical: "Repel evil with that which is better, and the one between you and him is enmity will become as though he were a devoted friend."

This guide explains Surah Fussilat section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Fussilat
- **Number of verses**: 54.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 41, in the twenty-fourth and twenty-fifth juz.
- **Names**: Fussilat ("detailed"), from verse 3, and Ha Mim As-Sajdah.
- **Prostration of recitation**: at verse 37 or 38.
- **One of the *Hawamim*.**
- **Main themes**: the Quran, creation, the fate of deniers, the testimony of the body, steadfastness and good character.

## Verses 1–8: A Detailed Book
﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلࣱ مِّنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾ ﴿كِتَٰبࣱ فُصِّلَتۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ قُرۡءَانًا عَرَبِيّٗا لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿بَشِيرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا فَأَعۡرَضَ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ قُلُوبُنَا فِيٓ أَكِنَّةٖ مِّمَّا تَدۡعُونَآ إِلَيۡهِ وَفِيٓ ءَاذَانِنَا وَقۡرࣱ وَمِنۢ بَيۡنِنَا وَبَيۡنِكَ حِجَابࣱ فَٱعۡمَلۡ إِنَّنَا عَٰمِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 41:1-5)

**"A Book whose verses have been detailed, an Arabic Quran for a people who know, as a giver of good tidings and a warner."** **"But most of them turn away, so they do not hear."** They said: **"Our hearts are within coverings from that to which you invite us, and in our ears is deafness, and between us and you is a partition."**

﴿قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ أَنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱ فَٱسۡتَقِيمُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُوهُۗ وَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ كَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونٖ﴾ (Quran 41:6-8)

**"Say: I am only a man like you, to whom it has been revealed that your god is one God, so take a straight course to Him and seek His forgiveness."** Notice **"woe to those who associate others with Allah, those who do not give zakah"**: neglecting zakah is mentioned beside shirk.

## Verses 9–12: Creation in Days
﴿۞قُلۡ أَئِنَّكُمۡ لَتَكۡفُرُونَ بِٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِي يَوۡمَيۡنِ وَتَجۡعَلُونَ لَهُۥٓ أَندَادٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلَ فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ مِن فَوۡقِهَا وَبَٰرَكَ فِيهَا وَقَدَّرَ فِيهَآ أَقۡوَٰتَهَا فِيٓ أَرۡبَعَةِ أَيَّامٖ سَوَآءٗ لِّلسَّآئِلِينَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَهِيَ دُخَانࣱ فَقَالَ لَهَا وَلِلۡأَرۡضِ ٱئۡتِيَا طَوۡعًا أَوۡ كَرۡهٗا قَالَتَآ أَتَيۡنَا طَآئِعِينَ﴾ ﴿فَقَضَىٰهُنَّ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَاتٖ فِي يَوۡمَيۡنِ وَأَوۡحَىٰ فِي كُلِّ سَمَآءٍ أَمۡرَهَاۚ وَزَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِمَصَٰبِيحَ وَحِفۡظٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقۡدِيرُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ﴾ (Quran 41:9-12)

**The earth** was created in two days, its mountains, blessings and provisions measured in four days in total. **Then He directed Himself to the heaven while it was smoke**, and said to it and to the earth: **"Come, willingly or by compulsion." They said: "We have come willingly."** The whole universe submits obediently. Then He made them seven heavens in two days, and adorned the nearest heaven with lamps and protection.

## Verses 13–18: 'Ad and Thamud
﴿فَإِنۡ أَعۡرَضُواْ فَقُلۡ أَنذَرۡتُكُمۡ صَٰعِقَةٗ مِّثۡلَ صَٰعِقَةِ عَادٖ وَثَمُودَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ جَآءَتۡهُمُ ٱلرُّسُلُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۖ قَالُواْ لَوۡ شَآءَ رَبُّنَا لَأَنزَلَ مَلَـٰٓئِكَةٗ فَإِنَّا بِمَآ أُرۡسِلۡتُم بِهِۦ كَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَمَّا عَادࣱ فَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَقَالُواْ مَنۡ أَشَدُّ مِنَّا قُوَّةًۖ أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَهُمۡ هُوَ أَشَدُّ مِنۡهُمۡ قُوَّةٗۖ وَكَانُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا يَجۡحَدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ رِيحٗا صَرۡصَرٗا فِيٓ أَيَّامٖ نَّحِسَاتٖ لِّنُذِيقَهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡخِزۡيِ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَخۡزَىٰۖ وَهُمۡ لَا يُنصَرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَهَدَيۡنَٰهُمۡ فَٱسۡتَحَبُّواْ ٱلۡعَمَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰ فَأَخَذَتۡهُمۡ صَٰعِقَةُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡهُونِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾ ﴿وَنَجَّيۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَكَانُواْ يَتَّقُونَ﴾ (Quran 41:13-18)

**"If they turn away, then say: I have warned you of a thunderbolt like the thunderbolt that struck 'Ad and Thamud."**

**'Ad** were arrogant and said: **"Who is greater than us in strength?"** **"Did they not consider that Allah who created them was greater than them in strength?"** So a screaming wind was sent on them in days of misfortune.

**Thamud** were guided, **"but they preferred blindness over guidance"**, so the thunderbolt of humiliating punishment seized them. **And We saved those who believed and feared Allah.**

## Verses 19–24: When the Skin Testifies
﴿وَيَوۡمَ يُحۡشَرُ أَعۡدَآءُ ٱللَّهِ إِلَى ٱلنَّارِ فَهُمۡ يُوزَعُونَ﴾ ﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا مَا جَآءُوهَا شَهِدَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَمۡعُهُمۡ وَأَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ وَجُلُودُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ لِجُلُودِهِمۡ لِمَ شَهِدتُّمۡ عَلَيۡنَاۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَنطَقَنَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنطَقَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖۚ وَهُوَ خَلَقَكُمۡ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 41:19-21)

When the enemies of Allah reach the Fire, **their hearing, their eyes and their skins will testify against them** about what they used to do. They will say to their skins: **"Why have you testified against us?"** They will answer: **"We were made to speak by Allah, who has made everything speak."**

In Sahih Muslim, Anas said: "We were with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and he laughed and said: Do you know why I am laughing? ... At a servant's argument with his Lord. He will say: O Lord, did You not protect me from injustice? He will say: Yes. He will say: I will not allow any witness against me except from myself. Then a seal will be placed on his mouth, and his limbs will be told: Speak. And they will speak of his deeds."

﴿وَمَا كُنتُمۡ تَسۡتَتِرُونَ أَن يَشۡهَدَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ سَمۡعُكُمۡ وَلَآ أَبۡصَٰرُكُمۡ وَلَا جُلُودُكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن ظَنَنتُمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَعۡلَمُ كَثِيرٗا مِّمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَذَٰلِكُمۡ ظَنُّكُمُ ٱلَّذِي ظَنَنتُم بِرَبِّكُمۡ أَرۡدَىٰكُمۡ فَأَصۡبَحۡتُم مِّنَ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَإِن يَصۡبِرُواْ فَٱلنَّارُ مَثۡوٗى لَّهُمۡۖ وَإِن يَسۡتَعۡتِبُواْ فَمَا هُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُعۡتَبِينَ﴾ (Quran 41:22-24)

**"You were not concealing yourselves, lest your hearing testify against you or your sight or your skins, but you assumed that Allah does not know much of what you do. And that was your assumption which you assumed about your Lord. It has brought you to ruin."** A bad opinion of Allah destroyed them. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Let none of you die except while thinking well of Allah."

## Verses 25–29: Companions and Noise Against the Quran
﴿۞وَقَيَّضۡنَا لَهُمۡ قُرَنَآءَ فَزَيَّنُواْ لَهُم مَّا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡ وَحَقَّ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ فِيٓ أُمَمٖ قَدۡ خَلَتۡ مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ خَٰسِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَا تَسۡمَعُواْ لِهَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ وَٱلۡغَوۡاْ فِيهِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَغۡلِبُونَ﴾ ﴿فَلَنُذِيقَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ عَذَابٗا شَدِيدٗا وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّهُمۡ أَسۡوَأَ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ أَعۡدَآءِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلنَّارُۖ لَهُمۡ فِيهَا دَارُ ٱلۡخُلۡدِ جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا يَجۡحَدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ رَبَّنَآ أَرِنَا ٱلَّذَيۡنِ أَضَلَّانَا مِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِ نَجۡعَلۡهُمَا تَحۡتَ أَقۡدَامِنَا لِيَكُونَا مِنَ ٱلۡأَسۡفَلِينَ﴾ (Quran 41:25-29)

**"We appointed for them companions who made attractive to them what was before them and what was behind them."** The disbelievers said: **"Do not listen to this Quran and speak noisily during its recitation, that perhaps you will overcome."** They knew its power and tried to drown it out.

## Verses 30–32: Angels Descend on the Steadfast
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ تَتَنَزَّلُ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ أَلَّا تَخَافُواْ وَلَا تَحۡزَنُواْ وَأَبۡشِرُواْ بِٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ﴾ ﴿نَحۡنُ أَوۡلِيَآؤُكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَا تَشۡتَهِيٓ أَنفُسُكُمۡ وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَا تَدَّعُونَ﴾ ﴿نُزُلٗا مِّنۡ غَفُورٖ رَّحِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 41:30-32)

**"Indeed, those who have said, 'Our Lord is Allah,' and then remained steadfast, the angels will descend upon them, saying: Do not fear and do not grieve, but receive good tidings of Paradise, which you were promised."** Many commentators said this descent happens at death, in the grave and at the resurrection.

**"We were your allies in worldly life and in the hereafter."** **"A hospitality from One who is Forgiving and Merciful."**

**Steadfastness (*istiqamah*)**: in Sahih Muslim, Sufyan ibn 'Abdullah asked the Prophet ﷺ for one sentence about Islam, and he said: "Say: I believe in Allah, then be steadfast."

## Verse 33: The Best Speech
﴿وَمَنۡ أَحۡسَنُ قَوۡلٗا مِّمَّن دَعَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا وَقَالَ إِنَّنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 41:33)

**"And who is better in speech than one who invites to Allah and does righteousness and says: Indeed, I am of the Muslims."** Three qualities: calling to Allah, doing good, and openly identifying as a Muslim. Many scholars said this applies first to the muezzins, who call people to prayer, and then to everyone who calls to Allah.

## Verses 34–36: Repel Evil With What Is Better
﴿وَلَا تَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡحَسَنَةُ وَلَا ٱلسَّيِّئَةُۚ ٱدۡفَعۡ بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ فَإِذَا ٱلَّذِي بَيۡنَكَ وَبَيۡنَهُۥ عَدَٰوَةࣱ كَأَنَّهُۥ وَلِيٌّ حَمِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُواْ وَمَا يُلَقَّىٰهَآ إِلَّا ذُو حَظٍّ عَظِيمٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِمَّا يَنزَغَنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ نَزۡغࣱ فَٱسۡتَعِذۡ بِٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ (Quran 41:34-36)

**"Good and evil are not equal. Repel evil by that which is better, and thereupon the one whom between you and him is enmity will become as though he was a devoted friend."** Answer an insult with patience, rudeness with kindness, cutting off with keeping ties. Hearts change.

**"But none is granted it except those who are patient, and none is granted it except one having a great portion."** It is difficult, and only the patient and the fortunate achieve it.

**"And if there comes to you from Satan an evil suggestion, then seek refuge in Allah."** When anger rises, seek refuge in Allah. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ saw a man angry and said that if he said "I seek refuge in Allah from Satan the accursed", what he felt would leave him.

## Verses 37–39: Signs and the Prostration
﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِ ٱلَّيۡلُ وَٱلنَّهَارُ وَٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُۚ لَا تَسۡجُدُواْ لِلشَّمۡسِ وَلَا لِلۡقَمَرِ وَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَهُنَّ إِن كُنتُمۡ إِيَّاهُ تَعۡبُدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَإِنِ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ فَٱلَّذِينَ عِندَ رَبِّكَ يُسَبِّحُونَ لَهُۥ بِٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡـَٔمُونَ۩﴾ ﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنَّكَ تَرَى ٱلۡأَرۡضَ خَٰشِعَةٗ فَإِذَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهَا ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱهۡتَزَّتۡ وَرَبَتۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحۡيَاهَا لَمُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ﴾ (Quran 41:37-39)

**"Do not prostrate to the sun or to the moon, but prostrate to Allah, who created them."** Here is the prostration of recitation. **"And of His signs is that you see the earth stilled, but when We send down upon it rain, it quivers and grows."** **"Indeed, He who has given it life is the Giver of life to the dead."**

## Verses 40–46: Falsehood Cannot Approach It
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِٱلذِّكۡرِ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡۖ وَإِنَّهُۥ لَكِتَٰبٌ عَزِيزࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّا يَأۡتِيهِ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَلَا مِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦۖ تَنزِيلࣱ مِّنۡ حَكِيمٍ حَمِيدٖ﴾ (Quran 41:41-42)

**"Indeed, it is a mighty Book. Falsehood cannot approach it from before it or from behind it; it is a revelation from a Wise and Praiseworthy."** The Quran is protected from every kind of falsehood.

﴿وَلَوۡ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ قُرۡءَانًا أَعۡجَمِيّٗا لَّقَالُواْ لَوۡلَا فُصِّلَتۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥٓۖ ءَا۬عۡجَمِيࣱّ وَعَرَبِيࣱّۗ قُلۡ هُوَ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ هُدٗى وَشِفَآءࣱۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ فِيٓ ءَاذَانِهِمۡ وَقۡرࣱ وَهُوَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ عَمًىۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يُنَادَوۡنَ مِن مَّكَانِۭ بَعِيدٖ﴾ (Quran 41:44)

**"Say: It is, for those who believe, a guidance and cure."** For the believer, the Quran heals doubts, diseases of the heart and many burdens. For others, it is deafness and blindness.

﴿مَّنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَنۡ أَسَآءَ فَعَلَيۡهَاۗ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِظَلَّـٰمٖ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ﴾ (Quran 41:46)

**"Whoever does righteousness, it is for his soul; and whoever does evil, it is against it. And your Lord is not ever unjust to His servants."**

## Verses 47–54: Man's Nature and the Signs
﴿لَّا يَسۡـَٔمُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مِن دُعَآءِ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ وَإِن مَّسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ فَيَـُٔوسࣱ قَنُوطࣱ﴾ ﴿وَلَئِنۡ أَذَقۡنَٰهُ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ضَرَّآءَ مَسَّتۡهُ لَيَقُولَنَّ هَٰذَا لِي وَمَآ أَظُنُّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ قَآئِمَةٗ وَلَئِن رُّجِعۡتُ إِلَىٰ رَبِّيٓ إِنَّ لِي عِندَهُۥ لَلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ فَلَنُنَبِّئَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِمَا عَمِلُواْ وَلَنُذِيقَنَّهُم مِّنۡ عَذَابٍ غَلِيظٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَآ أَنۡعَمۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ أَعۡرَضَ وَنَـَٔا بِجَانِبِهِۦ وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ فَذُو دُعَآءٍ عَرِيضٖ﴾ (Quran 41:49-51)

**"Man is not weary of supplication for good, but if evil touches him, he is hopeless and despairing."** And when Allah gives him mercy after hardship, he says "This is due to me." When blessed, he turns away; when afflicted, he makes long supplication.

﴿سَنُرِيهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا فِي ٱلۡأٓفَاقِ وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَتَبَيَّنَ لَهُمۡ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّۗ أَوَلَمۡ يَكۡفِ بِرَبِّكَ أَنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ﴾ ﴿أَلَآ إِنَّهُمۡ فِي مِرۡيَةٖ مِّن لِّقَآءِ رَبِّهِمۡۗ أَلَآ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مُّحِيطُۢ﴾ (Quran 41:53-54)

**"We will show them Our signs in the horizons and within themselves until it becomes clear to them that it is the truth."** In the universe and in the human body, discovery keeps revealing signs. **"Is it not sufficient concerning your Lord that He is, over all things, a Witness?"**

## Word Meanings
- **Fussilat**: detailed, explained.
- **Akinnah**: coverings.
- **Waqr**: deafness.
- **Aqwat**: provisions.
- **Taw'an aw karhan**: willingly or by compulsion.
- **Sa'iqah**: thunderbolt.
- **Ardakum**: brought you to ruin.
- **Qurana'**: companions.
- **Ilghaw**: speak noisily.
- **Istaqamu**: remained steadfast.
- **Wali hamim**: devoted friend.
- **Nazgh**: evil suggestion.
- **Ihtazzat**: quivered.
- **Shifa'**: cure.
- **Al-Afaq**: the horizons.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"We have come willingly"**: the obedient universe speaks.
2. **"Why have you testified against us?"**: a dialogue between a man and his own skin.
3. **"Do not fear and do not grieve"**: the angels' words to the steadfast.
4. **"As though he were a devoted friend"**: enmity transformed.
5. **"In the horizons and within themselves"**: the outer and inner universe.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Izhar**: in *qur'anan 'arabiyyan* (verse 3), and in *taw'an aw karhan* (verse 11), the tanween before a throat letter is pronounced clearly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 11).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *aqwataha* (verse 10), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna alladhina* (verse 30), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Ghafir before it ended with nations who saw the punishment and believed too late, and Fussilat opens by warning of the thunderbolt of 'Ad and Thamud. Surah Ash-Shura after it continues the *Hawamim* and the theme of revelation.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "Fussilat" mean?
"Detailed": the verses of the Quran are clearly explained.

### What happens to those who say "Our Lord is Allah" and stand firm?
The angels descend upon them saying "Do not fear and do not grieve", with good tidings of Paradise.

### Will the body really testify on the Day of Judgement?
Yes. Hearing, sight and skin will testify, as the surah says and as in the hadith of Anas in Sahih Muslim.

### What does "repel evil with what is better" mean?
Answer harm with kindness, which can turn an enemy into a close friend.

### Where is the prostration in Surah Fussilat?
At verse 37 or 38, according to different scholarly opinions.

### What does "a guidance and cure" mean?
The Quran guides believers and heals their hearts from doubt and spiritual disease.

### How many verses are in Surah Fussilat?
54 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Fussilat asks you to say "Our Lord is Allah" and stand firm, promising angels who will tell you "Do not fear and do not grieve". It warns that your own skin will one day speak, so think well of Allah and act accordingly. And it gives one of the most transformative pieces of advice ever given: repel evil with what is better, and watch enemies become friends.
