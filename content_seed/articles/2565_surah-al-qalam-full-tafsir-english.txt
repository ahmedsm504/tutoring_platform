Surah Al-Qalam is among the earliest surahs revealed. It came when the Prophet ﷺ had just begun his mission and Quraysh were calling him mad. Allah answered by swearing by the pen and what people write that he is not mad, and then gave him a testimony no one else has received: "And indeed, you are of a great moral character." The surah then forbids him from yielding to the deniers, describes their ugly character in nine strokes, tells the story of brothers who tried to keep their harvest from the poor and woke up to a burnt garden, debates the deniers, and closes with the story of Yunus and the command to be patient.

This guide explains Surah Al-Qalam section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Qalam
- **Number of verses**: 52.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 68, in the twenty-ninth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Qalam means "the pen", from the oath in the first verse. It is also called *Nun*.
- **Main themes**: defending the Prophet ﷺ and his character, the ugly traits of the deniers, the punishment of stinginess and arrogance, and patience.

## Verses 1–7: You Are Not Mad
﴿نٓۚ وَٱلۡقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسۡطُرُونَ﴾ ﴿مَآ أَنتَ بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ بِمَجۡنُونٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ لَكَ لَأَجۡرًا غَيۡرَ مَمۡنُونٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾ ﴿فَسَتُبۡصِرُ وَيُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ ﴿بِأَييِّكُمُ ٱلۡمَفۡتُونُ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ﴾ (Quran 68:1-7)

**"Nun"**: one of the separated letters that open some surahs. Allah knows best what is intended, and they point to the miracle of a Quran composed of the same letters the Arabs used.

**"By the pen and what they inscribe"**: Allah swears by the pen, the tool of knowledge and writing, and by what writers write. In a nation that was largely unlettered, this oath raised the status of the pen and of learning. The very first revelation, in Surah Al-'Alaq, also mentioned "who taught by the pen".

**The answer to the oath**: **"You are not, by the favour of your Lord, a madman."** **"And indeed, for you is a reward never cut off"** for your patience.

**"And indeed, you are of a great moral character."** In Sahih Muslim, Sa'd ibn Hisham asked 'A'ishah about the character of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. She said: "Do you not read the Quran?" He said: "Yes." She said: "The character of the Prophet of Allah ﷺ was the Quran." And in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Anas said: "I served the Prophet ﷺ for ten years, and he never once said to me 'Uff', nor 'Why did you do that?', nor 'Why did you not do that?'"

**"Soon you will see and they will see which of you is the afflicted"**: time would show who was truly mad.

## Verses 8–16: The Character of the Denier
﴿فَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ ﴿وَدُّواْ لَوۡ تُدۡهِنُ فَيُدۡهِنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَا تُطِعۡ كُلَّ حَلَّافٖ مَّهِينٍ﴾ ﴿هَمَّازٖ مَّشَّآءِۭ بِنَمِيمٖ﴾ ﴿مَّنَّاعٖ لِّلۡخَيۡرِ مُعۡتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ﴾ ﴿عُتُلِّۭ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ زَنِيمٍ﴾ (Quran 68:8-13)

**"They wish that you would compromise, so they would compromise"**: they wanted the Prophet ﷺ to soften on his religion so they could soften in return, a trade of concessions in belief. Compromise in matters of faith is rejected.

**Nine ugly qualities**:
1. **Hallaf**: one who swears oaths constantly; excessive swearing is a sign of lying.
2. **Mahin**: contemptible in opinion and mind.
3. **Hammaz**: a fault-finder who attacks people.
4. **Mashsha' bi-namim**: one who walks around spreading gossip to cause division. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Hudhayfah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "The talebearer will not enter Paradise."
5. **Manna' lil-khayr**: one who prevents good, for himself and others.
6. **Mu'tad**: a transgressor who goes beyond limits.
7. **Athim**: sinful.
8. **'Utull**: harsh and coarse.
9. **Zanim**: known for his evil, as a sheep is known by a mark on its neck.

﴿أَن كَانَ ذَا مَالٖ وَبَنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَٰتُنَا قَالَ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ ﴿سَنَسِمُهُۥ عَلَى ٱلۡخُرۡطُومِ﴾ (Quran 68:14-16)

**The cause of his arrogance**: **"Because he had wealth and children"**, his money and sons made him reject the verses as "legends of the former peoples". **"We will brand him upon the snout"**: a mark of humiliation on the nose, the symbol of pride among the Arabs.

## Verses 17–33: The Owners of the Garden
﴿إِنَّا بَلَوۡنَٰهُمۡ كَمَا بَلَوۡنَآ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ إِذۡ أَقۡسَمُواْ لَيَصۡرِمُنَّهَا مُصۡبِحِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَا يَسۡتَثۡنُونَ﴾ ﴿فَطَافَ عَلَيۡهَا طَآئِفࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكَ وَهُمۡ نَآئِمُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَصۡبَحَتۡ كَٱلصَّرِيمِ﴾ (Quran 68:17-20)

**The story**: commentators relate that a righteous man owned a garden and used to give the poor their share on harvest day. When he died, his sons said: "We are more entitled to its fruit." They swore to harvest it early in the morning before the poor arrived, and they made no exception, not even saying "if Allah wills". During the night **"there came upon it an affliction from your Lord while they were asleep"**, and by morning it was **"like the dark night"**, burnt black, or like a harvested field.

﴿فَتَنَادَوۡاْ مُصۡبِحِينَ﴾ ﴿أَنِ ٱغۡدُواْ عَلَىٰ حَرۡثِكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰرِمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱنطَلَقُواْ وَهُمۡ يَتَخَٰفَتُونَ﴾ ﴿أَن لَّا يَدۡخُلَنَّهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّسۡكِينࣱ﴾ ﴿وَغَدَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ حَرۡدٖ قَٰدِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡهَا قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا لَضَآلُّونَ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ نَحۡنُ مَحۡرُومُونَ﴾ (Quran 68:21-27)

**The scene**: they called to each other at dawn, set off whispering: "No poor person will enter it upon you today." They went out confident they could keep the poor away. When they saw it, they said: **"Indeed, we are lost"**, thinking they had taken the wrong road. Then the truth sank in: **"Rather, we have been deprived."**

﴿قَالَ أَوۡسَطُهُمۡ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكُمۡ لَوۡلَا تُسَبِّحُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ يَتَلَٰوَمُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا طَٰغِينَ﴾ ﴿عَسَىٰ رَبُّنَآ أَن يُبۡدِلَنَا خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهَآ إِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا رَٰغِبُونَ﴾ ﴿كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡعَذَابُۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَكۡبَرُۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 68:28-33)

**The repentance**: **"The most moderate of them said: Did I not say to you, why do you not exalt Allah?"** that is, why did you not say "if Allah wills". They said: **"Exalted is our Lord! Indeed, we were wrongdoers."** They blamed each other, admitted **"we were transgressors"**, and hoped: **"Perhaps our Lord will give us something better than it."**

**"Such is the punishment, and the punishment of the hereafter is greater, if only they knew."**

**Lessons from the story**:
1. **Stinginess destroys blessing**: they withheld the poor's share and lost everything.
2. **Say "in sha' Allah"**: the one who makes no exception has trusted his own power.
3. **Listen to the moderate voice**: every group has someone who advises well.
4. **Repent and admit fault**: "Exalted is our Lord! We were wrongdoers."
5. **Hope after failure**: "Perhaps our Lord will give us something better."
6. **A parable for Quraysh**: they were given the blessing of the message and rejected it.

## Verses 34–47: Debating the Deniers
﴿إِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ جَنَّـٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾ ﴿أَفَنَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ كَٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ﴾ ﴿مَا لَكُمۡ كَيۡفَ تَحۡكُمُونَ﴾ (Quran 68:34-36)

**"Should We treat the Muslims like the criminals? What is wrong with you? How do you judge?"** Then question after question: Do you have a scripture saying you will get whatever you choose? Do you have binding oaths from Us until the Day of Resurrection? Ask them which of them guarantees that. Or do they have partners? Let them bring them.

﴿يَوۡمَ يُكۡشَفُ عَن سَاقٖ وَيُدۡعَوۡنَ إِلَى ٱلسُّجُودِ فَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ﴾ ﴿خَٰشِعَةً أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ تَرۡهَقُهُمۡ ذِلَّةࣱۖ وَقَدۡ كَانُواْ يُدۡعَوۡنَ إِلَى ٱلسُّجُودِ وَهُمۡ سَٰلِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 68:42-43)

**"The Day the Shin will be uncovered"**: in Sahih al-Bukhari, Abu Sa'id al-Khudri reported that he heard the Prophet ﷺ say: "Our Lord will uncover His Shin, and every believing man and woman will prostrate to Him. There will remain those who used to prostrate in the world to show off and for reputation; they will try to prostrate, but their backs will become a single rigid plate." **"And they used to be invited to prostration while they were sound"**: in this world they were healthy and able, and refused.

**Gradual seizing**: **"We will lead them on gradually from where they do not know. And I give them time. Indeed, My plan is firm."** Allah gives them blessings while they persist in sin, and they think it is approval, until they are seized.

## Verses 48–50: The Companion of the Fish
﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تَكُن كَصَاحِبِ ٱلۡحُوتِ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ وَهُوَ مَكۡظُومࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّوۡلَآ أَن تَدَٰرَكَهُۥ نِعۡمَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ لَنُبِذَ بِٱلۡعَرَآءِ وَهُوَ مَذۡمُومࣱ﴾ ﴿فَٱجۡتَبَٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَجَعَلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ (Quran 68:48-50)

**"So be patient for the decision of your Lord, and do not be like the companion of the fish"**: Yunus ﷺ, who left his people in anger before Allah permitted him, and was swallowed by the whale. **"When he called out while he was distressed"**, crying in the darkness: "There is no god except You; exalted are You; indeed I have been of the wrongdoers." **"If not that a favour from his Lord overtook him, he would have been thrown onto the naked shore while he was blamed."** **"And his Lord chose him and made him of the righteous."**

In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "It is not fitting for any servant to say that I am better than Yunus ibn Matta."

## Verses 51–52: The Evil Eye
﴿وَإِن يَكَادُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَيُزۡلِقُونَكَ بِأَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡ لَمَّا سَمِعُواْ ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَيَقُولُونَ إِنَّهُۥ لَمَجۡنُونࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمَا هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرࣱ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 68:51-52)

**"And indeed, those who disbelieve would almost make you slip with their eyes"**: their glares were so full of hatred and envy that they almost knocked him down. Many commentators explained this verse in terms of the evil eye. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "The evil eye is real." **"But it is not except a reminder to the worlds"**: the Quran is a reminder for all, not the words of a madman.

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Qalam**: the pen.
- **Yasturun**: they write, inscribe.
- **Mamnun**: cut off.
- **Khuluq**: character.
- **Tudhin**: you compromise.
- **Hallaf**: one who swears often.
- **Namim**: talebearing, gossip.
- **Zanim**: one known for his evil.
- **Al-Khurtum**: the snout, the nose.
- **Yasrimunnaha**: they will harvest it.
- **As-Sarim**: dark like night, or a harvested field.
- **Saq**: shin.
- **Makzum**: distressed, full of grief.
- **Yuzliqunaka**: make you slip.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The oath by the pen**: in an unlettered nation, honouring knowledge.
2. **"Upon (*'ala*) a great character"**: the preposition shows mastery, as if he stands above great character.
3. **Nine traits in a row**: each worse than the last.
4. **"We will brand him upon the snout"**: a term used for animals, adding contempt.
5. **"We are lost... rather, we are deprived"**: a psychological journey from denial to admission.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Izhar in "Nun wal-qalam"**: in the narration of Hafs, the noon of *Nun* is pronounced clearly before the waw.
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in the letter *Nun*, stretched six counts.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *anta* (verse 2), the noon sakinah is hidden before the ta'.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yubdilana* (verse 32), the ba' sakinah, and in *yud'awna* (verse 42), the dal sakinah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Mulk before it ended with "If your water were to sink away, who could bring you flowing water?", and Al-Qalam tells of a garden that vanished overnight; both remind us how quickly blessings can disappear. Surah Al-Haqqah after it describes the Day of Judgement and confirms that the Quran is "the word of a noble messenger", just as Al-Qalam ends by calling it "a reminder to the worlds".

## Following the Great Character
1. **Live the Quran**: his character was the Quran.
2. **Forbearance**: he never took revenge for himself.
3. **Gentleness**: "Allah is gentle and loves gentleness in all matters," as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.
4. **Humility**: he helped with the work of his household.
5. **Kindness to those under you**: he never said "uff" to Anas.
6. **Mercy to opponents**: at Ta'if, when the angel of the mountains offered to crush the people who had hurt him, he said: "Rather, I hope that Allah will bring from their descendants people who worship Allah alone," as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Honour the pen and knowledge.**
2. **Make the Prophet's ﷺ character your model.**
3. **Do not compromise your religion.**
4. **Avoid excessive oaths, gossip and fault-finding.**
5. **Give the poor their right**: do not be like the owners of the garden.
6. **Say "in sha' Allah"** when you plan.
7. **Prostrate while you are able**, before the Day you are unable.
8. **Be patient**, and do not be hasty like the companion of the fish.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "Nun, by the pen and what they inscribe" mean?
Nun is one of the separated letters, and Allah swears by the pen and what writers write, honouring knowledge.

### What does "you are of a great moral character" mean?
It is Allah's testimony that the Prophet ﷺ had the highest character; 'A'ishah said his character was the Quran, as in Sahih Muslim.

### What is the story of the owners of the garden?
Brothers who inherited a garden swore to harvest it before the poor came, without saying "if Allah wills"; Allah burnt it while they slept, and they repented.

### What does "the Day the Shin will be uncovered" mean?
On the Day of Resurrection Allah will uncover His Shin and the believers will prostrate, as in the hadith of Abu Sa'id in Sahih al-Bukhari.

### Who is the companion of the fish?
The Prophet Yunus ﷺ, who was swallowed by the whale.

### Does Surah Al-Qalam mention the evil eye?
Many commentators explained verse 51 as referring to the evil eye, and the Prophet ﷺ said "The evil eye is real" in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Qalam?
52 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Qalam paints two portraits: the Prophet ﷺ with his great character, and the denier with his nine ugly traits. It warns through the burnt garden that stinginess wipes out blessings, and it closes with Yunus to teach patience. Follow the one of great character, give the poor their right, and be patient for the decision of your Lord.
