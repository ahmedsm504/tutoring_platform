**Surah Al-Mutaffifin** (36 ayat) begins with business ethics and ends with Paradise.

﴿وَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُطَفِّفِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ٱكۡتَالُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ يَسۡتَوۡفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا كَالُوهُمۡ أَو وَّزَنُوهُمۡ يُخۡسِرُونَ﴾
- *Woe to those who give less: who take full measure from people, but give less when they measure or weigh for them.*

## Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–6 | Cheating in measure, and the Day people stand before the Lord |
| 7–17 | The record of the wicked in Sijjin |
| 18–28 | The record of the righteous in ʿIlliyyin and their bliss |
| 29–36 | The disbelievers who mocked the believers |

## Rust on the heart
﴿كَلَّاۖ بَلۡۜ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾ — *No! Rather, the stain has covered their hearts from what they used to earn.*
- The Prophet ﷺ explained that each sin leaves a black spot on the heart; repentance polishes it, and repeated sins spread it [At-Tirmidhi].

## Compete in this
﴿خِتَٰمُهُۥ مِسۡكࣱۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَتَنَافَسِ ٱلۡمُتَنَٰفِسُونَ﴾ — *Its seal is musk; so for this let the competitors compete.*

## Lessons
- **Honesty in business** is part of faith.
- **Repent quickly** before sins harden the heart.
- **Compete for the Hereafter**, not for small worldly gains.
