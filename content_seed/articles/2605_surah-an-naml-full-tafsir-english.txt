Surah An-Naml is named after a tiny creature that taught a great lesson. When the army of Sulayman ﷺ marched through the valley of the ants, one ant called out: "O ants, enter your dwellings, that you not be crushed by Sulayman and his soldiers while they perceive not." Sulayman heard her, smiled, and turned to Allah in gratitude. The surah is full of such moments: a hoopoe that brings news of a kingdom worshipping the sun, a letter that begins "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful", a throne transported before an eye can blink, and a queen who says at the end: "I submit with Sulayman to Allah, Lord of the worlds." It also contains a verse every person in distress should know: "Is He not best who responds to the desperate one when he calls upon Him and removes the evil?"

This guide explains Surah An-Naml section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah An-Naml
- **Number of verses**: 93.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 27, in the nineteenth and twentieth juz.
- **Name**: An-Naml means "the ants", from verse 18.
- **Prostration of recitation**: at verse 26.
- **Main themes**: the Quran as guidance, the stories of Musa, Sulayman, Salih and Lut, gratitude for blessings, and the signs of Allah's oneness.

## Verses 1–6: Guidance for the Believers
﴿طسٓۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ وَكِتَابٖ مُّبِينٍ﴾ ﴿هُدٗى وَبُشۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ يُوقِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:1-3)

**"Ta Sin. These are the verses of the Quran and a clear Book, as guidance and good tidings for the believers who establish prayer and give zakah, and they, of the hereafter, are certain."**

## Verses 7–14: Musa at the Fire
﴿إِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِأَهۡلِهِۦٓ إِنِّيٓ ءَانَسۡتُ نَارٗا سَـَٔاتِيكُم مِّنۡهَا بِخَبَرٍ أَوۡ ءَاتِيكُم بِشِهَابٖ قَبَسٖ لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَصۡطَلُونَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا جَآءَهَا نُودِيَ أَنۢ بُورِكَ مَن فِي ٱلنَّارِ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهَا وَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿وَأَلۡقِ عَصَاكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهَا تَهۡتَزُّ كَأَنَّهَا جَآنࣱّ وَلَّىٰ مُدۡبِرٗا وَلَمۡ يُعَقِّبۡۚ يَٰمُوسَىٰ لَا تَخَفۡ إِنِّي لَا يَخَافُ لَدَيَّ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَن ظَلَمَ ثُمَّ بَدَّلَ حُسۡنَۢا بَعۡدَ سُوٓءٖ فَإِنِّي غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَأَدۡخِلۡ يَدَكَ فِي جَيۡبِكَ تَخۡرُجۡ بَيۡضَآءَ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ سُوٓءٖۖ فِي تِسۡعِ ءَايَٰتٍ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَقَوۡمِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:7-12)

Musa saw a fire on his journey and said to his family: **"I will bring you from there information or bring you a burning torch."** When he came to it, he was called: **"Blessed is whoever is at the fire and whoever is around it."** Allah gave him the signs of the staff and the shining hand, and sent him to Pharaoh.

﴿وَجَحَدُواْ بِهَا وَٱسۡتَيۡقَنَتۡهَآ أَنفُسُهُمۡ ظُلۡمٗا وَعُلُوّٗاۚ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:14)

**"And they rejected them, while their souls were convinced thereof, out of injustice and haughtiness."** They knew the truth and rejected it anyway.

## Verses 15–19: Sulayman and the Ant
﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ عِلۡمٗاۖ وَقَالَا ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي فَضَّلَنَا عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٖ مِّنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَوَرِثَ سُلَيۡمَٰنُ دَاوُۥدَۖ وَقَالَ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ عُلِّمۡنَا مَنطِقَ ٱلطَّيۡرِ وَأُوتِينَا مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٍۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡفَضۡلُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾ (Quran 27:15-16)

**"And We had certainly given to Dawud and Sulayman knowledge, and they said: Praise to Allah, who has favoured us over many of His believing servants."** Their first reaction to knowledge was gratitude. **"We have been taught the language of birds."**

﴿وَحُشِرَ لِسُلَيۡمَٰنَ جُنُودُهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ وَٱلۡإِنسِ وَٱلطَّيۡرِ فَهُمۡ يُوزَعُونَ﴾ ﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَتَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ وَادِ ٱلنَّمۡلِ قَالَتۡ نَمۡلَةࣱ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّمۡلُ ٱدۡخُلُواْ مَسَٰكِنَكُمۡ لَا يَحۡطِمَنَّكُمۡ سُلَيۡمَٰنُ وَجُنُودُهُۥ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَتَبَسَّمَ ضَاحِكٗا مِّن قَوۡلِهَا وَقَالَ رَبِّ أَوۡزِعۡنِيٓ أَنۡ أَشۡكُرَ نِعۡمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَيَّ وَأَنۡ أَعۡمَلَ صَٰلِحٗا تَرۡضَىٰهُ وَأَدۡخِلۡنِي بِرَحۡمَتِكَ فِي عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:17-19)

**The ant**: **"O ants, enter your dwellings, that you not be crushed by Sulayman and his soldiers while they perceive not."** Notice her qualities: she cared for her community, gave clear instructions, and even excused Sulayman: "while they perceive not". She assumed good intentions.

**Sulayman's response**: **"He smiled, amused at her speech, and said: My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents and to do righteousness of which You approve. And admit me by Your mercy into the ranks of Your righteous servants."** A king with power over jinn, birds and armies stops to listen to an ant and responds with du'a.

**Respect for small creatures**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said that one of the prophets was bitten by an ant and ordered the colony to be burnt, and Allah revealed to him: "Because one ant bit you, you burnt a nation among the nations that glorify Allah?" Abu Dawud reported from Ibn 'Abbas that the Prophet ﷺ forbade killing four creatures: the ant, the bee, the hoopoe and the shrike; al-Albani graded it authentic.

## Verses 20–28: The Hoopoe's Report
﴿وَتَفَقَّدَ ٱلطَّيۡرَ فَقَالَ مَالِيَ لَآ أَرَى ٱلۡهُدۡهُدَ أَمۡ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَآئِبِينَ﴾ ﴿لَأُعَذِّبَنَّهُۥ عَذَابٗا شَدِيدًا أَوۡ لَأَاْذۡبَحَنَّهُۥٓ أَوۡ لَيَأۡتِيَنِّي بِسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 27:20-21)

Sulayman inspected the birds and did not see the hoopoe. **"I will surely punish it... unless it brings me a clear authorisation."** A leader who notices every absence and also listens to excuses.

﴿فَمَكَثَ غَيۡرَ بَعِيدٖ فَقَالَ أَحَطتُ بِمَا لَمۡ تُحِطۡ بِهِۦ وَجِئۡتُكَ مِن سَبَإِۭ بِنَبَإٖ يَقِينٍ﴾ ﴿إِنِّي وَجَدتُّ ٱمۡرَأَةٗ تَمۡلِكُهُمۡ وَأُوتِيَتۡ مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَلَهَا عَرۡشٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَجَدتُّهَا وَقَوۡمَهَا يَسۡجُدُونَ لِلشَّمۡسِ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ فَصَدَّهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ فَهُمۡ لَا يَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿أَلَّاۤ يَسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡخَبۡءَ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا تُخۡفُونَ وَمَا تُعۡلِنُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ۩﴾ (Quran 27:22-26)

**The hoopoe**: **"I have encompassed that which you have not encompassed, and I have come to you from Saba' with certain news."** A small bird speaks confidently to a king, because it carries the truth. It found **a woman ruling them**, given of all things, with a great throne, and **"I found her and her people prostrating to the sun instead of Allah"**. The hoopoe's concern was tawhid: **"so they do not prostrate to Allah, who brings forth what is hidden within the heavens and the earth"**. **"Allah, there is no deity except Him, Lord of the Great Throne."** Here is the prostration of recitation.

﴿۞قَالَ سَنَنظُرُ أَصَدَقۡتَ أَمۡ كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱذۡهَب بِّكِتَٰبِي هَٰذَا فَأَلۡقِهۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ ثُمَّ تَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡ فَٱنظُرۡ مَاذَا يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:27-28)

Sulayman verified: **"We will see whether you were truthful or were of the liars."** He sent a letter with the hoopoe.

## Verses 29–44: The Queen of Saba'
﴿قَالَتۡ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَؤُاْ إِنِّيٓ أُلۡقِيَ إِلَيَّ كِتَٰبࣱ كَرِيمٌ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ مِن سُلَيۡمَٰنَ وَإِنَّهُۥ بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾ ﴿أَلَّا تَعۡلُواْ عَلَيَّ وَأۡتُونِي مُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:29-31)

**The letter**: **"Indeed, it is from Sulayman, and indeed, it reads: In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Be not haughty with me but come to me in submission."** Short, clear and beginning with Allah's name.

﴿قَالَتۡ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَؤُاْ أَفۡتُونِي فِيٓ أَمۡرِي مَا كُنتُ قَاطِعَةً أَمۡرًا حَتَّىٰ تَشۡهَدُونِ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ نَحۡنُ أُوْلُواْ قُوَّةٖ وَأُوْلُواْ بَأۡسٖ شَدِيدٖ وَٱلۡأَمۡرُ إِلَيۡكِ فَٱنظُرِي مَاذَا تَأۡمُرِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَتۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡمُلُوكَ إِذَا دَخَلُواْ قَرۡيَةً أَفۡسَدُوهَا وَجَعَلُوٓاْ أَعِزَّةَ أَهۡلِهَآ أَذِلَّةٗۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ يَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنِّي مُرۡسِلَةٌ إِلَيۡهِم بِهَدِيَّةٖ فَنَاظِرَةُۢ بِمَ يَرۡجِعُ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:32-35)

**The queen consulted her advisers**: **"I would not decide a matter until you witness for me."** They boasted of their strength but left the decision to her. Wisely, she said: **"Indeed, kings, when they enter a city, ruin it and render the honoured of its people humbled."** She decided to send a gift and see the response.

﴿فَلَمَّا جَآءَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَ قَالَ أَتُمِدُّونَنِ بِمَالٖ فَمَآ ءَاتَىٰنِۦَ ٱللَّهُ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۚ بَلۡ أَنتُم بِهَدِيَّتِكُمۡ تَفۡرَحُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱرۡجِعۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ فَلَنَأۡتِيَنَّهُم بِجُنُودٖ لَّا قِبَلَ لَهُم بِهَا وَلَنُخۡرِجَنَّهُم مِّنۡهَآ أَذِلَّةٗ وَهُمۡ صَٰغِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:36-37)

Sulayman rejected the gift: **"Do you provide me with wealth? But what Allah has given me is better than what He has given you."** His mission was not wealth but tawhid.

﴿قَالَ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَؤُاْ أَيُّكُمۡ يَأۡتِينِي بِعَرۡشِهَا قَبۡلَ أَن يَأۡتُونِي مُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ عِفۡرِيتࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ أَنَا۠ ءَاتِيكَ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن تَقُومَ مِن مَّقَامِكَۖ وَإِنِّي عَلَيۡهِ لَقَوِيٌّ أَمِينࣱ﴾ ﴿قَالَ ٱلَّذِي عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَنَا۠ ءَاتِيكَ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن يَرۡتَدَّ إِلَيۡكَ طَرۡفُكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهُ مُسۡتَقِرًّا عِندَهُۥ قَالَ هَٰذَا مِن فَضۡلِ رَبِّي لِيَبۡلُوَنِيٓ ءَأَشۡكُرُ أَمۡ أَكۡفُرُۖ وَمَن شَكَرَ فَإِنَّمَا يَشۡكُرُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ رَبِّي غَنِيࣱّ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 27:38-40)

**The throne**: Sulayman asked who would bring her throne. A powerful jinn offered to bring it before Sulayman rose from his place. **"Said one who had knowledge from the Scripture: I will bring it to you before your glance returns to you."** And it was there. Sulayman's response: **"This is from the favour of my Lord to test me whether I will be grateful or ungrateful."** Blessings are tests.

﴿قَالَ نَكِّرُواْ لَهَا عَرۡشَهَا نَنظُرۡ أَتَهۡتَدِيٓ أَمۡ تَكُونُ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا جَآءَتۡ قِيلَ أَهَٰكَذَا عَرۡشُكِۖ قَالَتۡ كَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَۚ وَأُوتِينَا ٱلۡعِلۡمَ مِن قَبۡلِهَا وَكُنَّا مُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَصَدَّهَا مَا كَانَت تَّعۡبُدُ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهَا كَانَتۡ مِن قَوۡمٖ كَٰفِرِينَ﴾ ﴿قِيلَ لَهَا ٱدۡخُلِي ٱلصَّرۡحَۖ فَلَمَّا رَأَتۡهُ حَسِبَتۡهُ لُجَّةٗ وَكَشَفَتۡ عَن سَاقَيۡهَاۚ قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ صَرۡحࣱ مُّمَرَّدࣱ مِّن قَوَارِيرَۗ قَالَتۡ رَبِّ إِنِّي ظَلَمۡتُ نَفۡسِي وَأَسۡلَمۡتُ مَعَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:41-44)

Her throne was disguised to test her recognition; she said: **"It is as though it was it."** Then she was shown a palace with a floor of glass over water. Realising her error, she said: **"My Lord, indeed I have wronged myself, and I submit with Sulayman to Allah, Lord of the worlds."** Notice "with Sulayman": not to Sulayman, but alongside him, to Allah.

## Verses 45–58: Salih and Lut
﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ ثَمُودَ أَخَاهُمۡ صَٰلِحًا أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَإِذَا هُمۡ فَرِيقَانِ يَخۡتَصِمُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ لِمَ تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ قَبۡلَ ٱلۡحَسَنَةِۖ لَوۡلَا تَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ ٱطَّيَّرۡنَا بِكَ وَبِمَن مَّعَكَۚ قَالَ طَـٰٓئِرُكُمۡ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۖ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ تُفۡتَنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَكَانَ فِي ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ تِسۡعَةُ رَهۡطٖ يُفۡسِدُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا يُصۡلِحُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ تَقَاسَمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ لَنُبَيِّتَنَّهُۥ وَأَهۡلَهُۥ ثُمَّ لَنَقُولَنَّ لِوَلِيِّهِۦ مَا شَهِدۡنَا مَهۡلِكَ أَهۡلِهِۦ وَإِنَّا لَصَٰدِقُونَ﴾ ﴿وَمَكَرُواْ مَكۡرٗا وَمَكَرۡنَا مَكۡرٗا وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ مَكۡرِهِمۡ أَنَّا دَمَّرۡنَٰهُمۡ وَقَوۡمَهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ﴾ ﴿فَتِلۡكَ بُيُوتُهُمۡ خَاوِيَةَۢ بِمَا ظَلَمُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَأَنجَيۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَكَانُواْ يَتَّقُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:45-53)

**Thamud** split into two groups. Nine men in the city spread corruption and plotted to kill Salih and his family at night. **"They planned a plan, and We planned a plan, while they perceived not."** They were destroyed, and their houses stand empty.

﴿وَلُوطًا إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ أَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلۡفَٰحِشَةَ وَأَنتُمۡ تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ ﴿أَئِنَّكُمۡ لَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلرِّجَالَ شَهۡوَةٗ مِّن دُونِ ٱلنِّسَآءِۚ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ تَجۡهَلُونَ﴾ ﴿۞فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوٓاْ أَخۡرِجُوٓاْ ءَالَ لُوطٖ مِّن قَرۡيَتِكُمۡۖ إِنَّهُمۡ أُنَاسࣱ يَتَطَهَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱمۡرَأَتَهُۥ قَدَّرۡنَٰهَا مِنَ ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَأَمۡطَرۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم مَّطَرٗاۖ فَسَآءَ مَطَرُ ٱلۡمُنذَرِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:54-58)

**Lut's people** answered his warning by saying: **"Expel the family of Lut from your city. Indeed, they are people who keep themselves pure."** Purity had become a crime in their eyes.

## Verses 59–66: Who Is Better?
﴿قُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ عِبَادِهِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱصۡطَفَىٰٓۗ ءَآللَّهُ خَيۡرٌ أَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَأَنزَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا بِهِۦ حَدَآئِقَ ذَاتَ بَهۡجَةٖ مَّا كَانَ لَكُمۡ أَن تُنۢبِتُواْ شَجَرَهَآۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ يَعۡدِلُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمَّن جَعَلَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ قَرَارٗا وَجَعَلَ خِلَٰلَهَآ أَنۡهَٰرٗا وَجَعَلَ لَهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ حَاجِزًاۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمَّن يُجِيبُ ٱلۡمُضۡطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكۡشِفُ ٱلسُّوٓءَ وَيَجۡعَلُكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمَّن يَهۡدِيكُمۡ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَمَن يُرۡسِلُ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ بُشۡرَۢا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ رَحۡمَتِهِۦٓۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ تَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمَّن يَبۡدَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥ وَمَن يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ قُلۡ هَاتُواْ بُرۡهَٰنَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:59-64)

A series of questions, each ending with **"Is there a deity with Allah?"**:
- **Who created the heavens and earth and sent down rain**, causing beautiful gardens to grow?
- **Who made the earth a stable ground**, placed rivers in it, firm mountains, and a barrier between the two seas?
- **"Who responds to the desperate one when he calls upon Him and removes the evil and makes you successors upon the earth?"**
- **Who guides you in the darknesses of land and sea**, and sends the winds as good tidings?
- **Who begins creation and then repeats it**, and provides for you from the heaven and earth?

**Verse 62 for every distress**: when you are desperate, with every door closed, Allah alone answers. Even the idolaters turned to Him sincerely at such moments.

﴿قُل لَّا يَعۡلَمُ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُۚ وَمَا يَشۡعُرُونَ أَيَّانَ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:65)

**"No one in the heavens and earth knows the unseen except Allah."**

## Verses 67–93: Signs of the Hour and the Final Command
﴿۞وَإِذَا وَقَعَ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَخۡرَجۡنَا لَهُمۡ دَآبَّةٗ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تُكَلِّمُهُمۡ أَنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ كَانُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا لَا يُوقِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:82)

**"And when the word befalls them, We will bring forth for them a creature from the earth speaking to them."** The emergence of the beast is one of the major signs of the Hour, mentioned in Sahih Muslim among the ten signs.

﴿وَتَرَى ٱلۡجِبَالَ تَحۡسَبُهَا جَامِدَةٗ وَهِيَ تَمُرُّ مَرَّ ٱلسَّحَابِۚ صُنۡعَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَتۡقَنَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍۚ إِنَّهُۥ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:88)

**"And you see the mountains, thinking them rigid, while they will pass as the passing of clouds. It is the work of Allah, who perfected all things."**

﴿مَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُۥ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡهَا وَهُم مِّن فَزَعٖ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ءَامِنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَمَن جَآءَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ فَكُبَّتۡ وُجُوهُهُمۡ فِي ٱلنَّارِ هَلۡ تُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَآ أُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَعۡبُدَ رَبَّ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡبَلۡدَةِ ٱلَّذِي حَرَّمَهَا وَلَهُۥ كُلُّ شَيۡءٖۖ وَأُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَأَنۡ أَتۡلُوَاْ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَۖ فَمَنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَهۡتَدِي لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن ضَلَّ فَقُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنذِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَقُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ سَيُرِيكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ فَتَعۡرِفُونَهَاۚ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:89-93)

**"Whoever comes with a good deed will have better than it."** The Prophet ﷺ was commanded: **"I have only been commanded to worship the Lord of this city... and to recite the Quran."** **"Praise to Allah; He will show you His signs, and you will recognise them."**

## Word Meanings
- **An-Naml**: the ants.
- **Shihab qabas**: a burning torch.
- **Mantiq at-tayr**: the language of birds.
- **Yuza'un**: kept in ranks.
- **La yahtimannakum**: not be crushed.
- **Awzi'ni**: enable me.
- **Al-Hudhud**: the hoopoe.
- **Saba'**: the kingdom of Saba' (Sheba) in Yemen.
- **Al-Khab'**: what is hidden.
- **Yartadda ilayka tarfuk**: your glance returns to you.
- **As-Sarh**: the palace.
- **Mumarrad min qawarir**: paved with glass.
- **Al-Mudtarr**: the desperate one.
- **Dabbah**: a creature.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The ant's speech**: a model of leadership and good assumptions.
2. **Sulayman's smile followed by du'a**: power joined to gratitude.
3. **The letter**: brevity and clarity beginning with Bismillah.
4. **"Before your glance returns to you"**: speed beyond imagination.
5. **"Is there a deity with Allah?"** repeated five times.
6. **Mountains passing like clouds**: the stable becoming fluid.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Ta Sin*, the sin stretched six counts.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *min Sulayman* (verse 30), the noon sakinah is hidden before the sin.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *bi-naba'in yaqin* (verse 22), the tanween merges into the ya'.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ja'at* (verse 42) and *as-sama'i* (verse 60).
- **Prostration**: at verse 26.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Ash-Shu'ara' before it ended with "those who have wronged are going to know", and An-Naml begins with the Quran as guidance for believers. Surah Al-Qasas after it tells the full story of Musa, expanding the brief mention at the start of An-Naml.

## Lessons in Leadership From Sulayman and the Queen
1. **Listen to the smallest voices**: Sulayman listened to an ant and a hoopoe.
2. **Notice absences** and ask before judging.
3. **Verify news.**
4. **Write clearly** and begin with Allah's name.
5. **Consult** like the queen, and think about consequences.
6. **See power as a test of gratitude.**
7. **Admit error** and submit to the truth.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Make Sulayman's du'a for gratitude** your own.
2. **Respect animals** and do not harm them without need.
3. **Speak the truth with confidence**, like the hoopoe.
4. **Begin your important writing with Bismillah.**
5. **Turn to Allah in desperation**: He responds.
6. **Prostrate at verse 26.**

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them the story of the ant**, and teach them not to step on ants.
- **Tell them about the hoopoe** and how it cared that people worship Allah.
- **Teach them Sulayman's du'a** in verse 19.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is the surah called An-Naml?
Because of the ant who warned her colony as Sulayman's army approached.

### What did Sulayman do when he heard the ant?
He smiled and made a du'a of gratitude to Allah, asking to be among His righteous servants.

### Who was the Queen of Saba'?
A queen of Saba' in Yemen whose people worshipped the sun; she later submitted with Sulayman to Allah. The Quran does not mention her name.

### Who brought the throne?
"One who had knowledge from the Scripture", who brought it before the blink of an eye.

### What does "Who responds to the desperate one when he calls?" teach?
That in desperate need, Allah alone answers and removes harm.

### Where is the prostration in Surah An-Naml?
At verse 26.

### How many verses are in Surah An-Naml?
93 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah An-Naml teaches through an ant, a hoopoe and a queen. It shows a king who smiled at an ant and turned to gratitude, a bird that cared about tawhid, and a ruler who admitted her error and submitted with Sulayman to Allah. Every blessing is a test of gratitude, and in every distress, the One who responds is Allah.
