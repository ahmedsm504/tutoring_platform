**Surah An-Nur** (The Light, 64 ayat) is a Madinan surah that builds a society protected by modesty and trust.

## A surah of obligations
- It opens: *A surah which We have sent down and made obligatory*, signalling that its rulings are binding.

## Protecting honour
- Severe penalties for those who accuse chaste women without four witnesses.
- **The incident of the lie (al-Ifk):** a false rumour against Aisha, the Mother of the Believers. Allah revealed her innocence and taught believers to think well of each other and not to spread what they cannot prove.

## Etiquette of the home
| Ruling | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Asking permission | Do not enter others' homes until you seek permission and greet |
| Lowering the gaze | For believing men and women |
| Covering | Guidance on the dress of believing women |
| Children's permission | At three times of privacy during the day |

## The Verse of Light
﴿۞ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِۦ كَمِشۡكَوٰةٖ فِيهَا مِصۡبَاحٌۖ ٱلۡمِصۡبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍۖ ٱلزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوۡكَبࣱ دُرِّيࣱّ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٖ زَيۡتُونَةٖ لَّا شَرۡقِيَّةٖ وَلَا غَرۡبِيَّةٖ يَكَادُ زَيۡتُهَا يُضِيٓءُ وَلَوۡ لَمۡ تَمۡسَسۡهُ نَارࣱۚ نُّورٌ عَلَىٰ نُورٖۚ يَهۡدِي ٱللَّهُ لِنُورِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلَ لِلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾
- Scholars explain it as the light of guidance in the believer's heart: pure, steady, and lit from revelation.

## The promise
- Allah promises those who believe and do good that He will establish them and replace their fear with security, as long as they worship Him alone.

## Lessons
- **Guard tongues**: rumours destroy families and communities.
- **Modesty protects light** in the heart.
