**Surah Ar-Rum** (60 ayat) begins with a prophecy that came true within a few years.

## The prophecy
﴿غُلِبَتِ ٱلرُّومُ﴾ ﴿فِيٓ أَدۡنَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ غَلَبِهِمۡ سَيَغۡلِبُونَ﴾ ﴿فِي بِضۡعِ سِنِينَۗ﴾
- The Romans had been defeated by the Persians. The Quran declared they would win **within a few years** (bidʿ: three to nine), and they did.

## The signs of Allah
| Sign | Ayah meaning |
|---|---|
| Creation from dust | Then you are humans spreading |
| Spouses | *Affection and mercy between you* |
| Languages and colours | A sign for those with knowledge |
| Sleep by night and day | A sign for those who listen |
| Lightning and rain | Fear and hope |

## Fitrah
- *So direct your face toward the religion, inclining to truth, the fitrah of Allah upon which He created people.*

## Corruption and return
﴿ظَهَرَ ٱلۡفَسَادُ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِي ٱلنَّاسِ لِيُذِيقَهُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي عَمِلُواْ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾

## Lessons
- **The Quran's promises come true.**
- **Marriage is a sign** of Allah: built on affection and mercy.
