## Surah Ar-Rum Meaning: A Prophecy Fulfilled and Signs of Love

**Surah Ar-Rum** (60 ayat) begins with a prophecy that came true within a few years.

### The prophecy
﴿غُلِبَتِ ٱلرُّومُ﴾ ﴿فِيٓ أَدۡنَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ غَلَبِهِمۡ سَيَغۡلِبُونَ﴾ ﴿فِي بِضۡعِ سِنِينَۗ﴾
- The Romans had been defeated by the Persians. The Quran declared they would win **within a few years** (bidʿ: three to nine), and they did.

### The signs of Allah
| Sign | Ayah meaning |
|---|---|
| Creation from dust | Then you are humans spreading |
| Spouses | *Affection and mercy between you* |
| Languages and colours | A sign for those with knowledge |
| Sleep by night and day | A sign for those who listen |
| Lightning and rain | Fear and hope |

### Fitrah
- *So direct your face toward the religion, inclining to truth, the fitrah of Allah upon which He created people.*

### Corruption and return
﴿ظَهَرَ ٱلۡفَسَادُ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِي ٱلنَّاسِ لِيُذِيقَهُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي عَمِلُواْ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾

### Lessons
- **The Quran's promises come true.**
- **Marriage is a sign** of Allah: built on affection and mercy.

## "He Placed Between You Affection and Mercy": Quran 30:21 Explained

﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنۡ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا لِّتَسۡكُنُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَكُم مَّوَدَّةٗ وَرَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ [Ar-Rum 30:21]

### Marriage is a sign
- The verse lists marriage among Allah's signs, alongside the creation of the heavens and the earth.

### Three gifts
| Gift | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *From yourselves* | The same human nature, so they understand each other |
| *Sakan* (tranquility) | The home is a place of rest |
| *Mawaddah and rahmah* | Affection and mercy |

### Affection and mercy
- Some scholars explain: **mawaddah** is love that shows in actions, strongest in youth; **rahmah** is compassion that keeps a marriage going in illness, age and hardship.
- A marriage needs both: love to enjoy, mercy to endure.

### "He placed"
- Love between spouses is **from Allah**, so ask Him for it and protect it from what destroys it.

### Reflect
- *Signs for people who reflect*: two strangers become the closest people to each other.

### Lessons
- **Make your home a place of peace.**
- **Show mercy** when love feels tired.

## "Corruption Has Appeared on Land and Sea": Quran 30:41 Explained

﴿ظَهَرَ ٱلۡفَسَادُ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِي ٱلنَّاسِ لِيُذِيقَهُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي عَمِلُواْ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾ [Ar-Rum 30:41]

### What corruption?
- Scholars mention drought, scarcity, disease and loss of blessings, as well as wars, injustice and moral decay.

### "Because of what people's hands have earned"
- Human actions have consequences in the world.

### "Some of what they did"
- Only **part** of the consequence is tasted; Allah forgives much.

### The purpose
- *So that they may return*: hardship is a wake-up call, not only punishment.

### Today's application
| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| Environment | Pollution, waste, destroying resources |
| Society | Injustice, corruption, cutting family ties |
| Personal | Sins that remove blessing |

### Lessons
- **Reform starts with your own actions.**
- **Read hardships** as calls to return.

## Surah Luqman Meaning: A Father's Advice to His Son

**Surah Luqman** (34 ayat) contains a short parenting curriculum: the advice of a wise father to his son.

### The first advice
﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ لُقۡمَٰنُ لِٱبۡنِهِۦ وَهُوَ يَعِظُهُۥ يَٰبُنَيَّ لَا تُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّ ٱلشِّرۡكَ لَظُلۡمٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾
- **"Yā bunayya"**: *my dear son*, a tender way to begin.

### Luqman's advice
| Advice | Area |
|---|---|
| Don't associate partners with Allah | Belief |
| Allah sees even a mustard seed in a rock | Allah's knowledge |
| Establish prayer | Worship |
| Enjoin good, forbid evil | Society |
| Be patient over what befalls you | Character |
| Don't turn your cheek in arrogance | Humility |
| Be moderate in walking, lower your voice | Manners |
﴿يَٰبُنَيَّ أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَٱنۡهَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَصَابَكَۖ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾

### Parents
- In the middle of Luqman's advice, Allah commands **kindness to parents**, mentioning the mother's hardship, while making clear that parents are not obeyed in shirk.

### The five keys of the unseen
- The knowledge of the Hour, the rain, what is in the wombs, what a soul will earn tomorrow, and where it will die.

### Lessons
- **Parenting with love**: start with "my dear son".

## Surah As-Sajdah Meaning: Their Sides Leave Their Beds

**Surah As-Sajdah** (30 ayat) was recited by the Prophet ﷺ in Fajr on Fridays with Al-Insan [Al-Bukhari and Muslim], and he would not sleep until he had recited it and Al-Mulk [At-Tirmidhi].

### Creation of man
- *He began the creation of man from clay, then made his offspring from a humble fluid, then proportioned him and breathed into him from His spirit.*

### The believers
- When reminded of the ayat, they **fall in prostration** and glorify their Lord, and are not arrogant. Here is a **prostration of recitation**.
﴿تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمَضَاجِعِ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُمۡ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sides leave their beds | Night prayer |
| Call upon their Lord in fear and hope | Balanced du'a |
| Spend from what We provided | Generosity |

### Hidden joy
- *No soul knows what has been hidden for them of comfort for the eyes, as reward for what they used to do.*

### The criminals
- They will hang their heads: *Our Lord, we have seen and heard, so return us and we will do good.*

### Lessons
- **A little night prayer** earns a hidden reward.

## Surah Al-Ahzab Meaning: The Prophet as the Best Example

**Surah Al-Ahzab** (73 ayat) was revealed around the **Battle of the Confederates** (the Trench), when armies surrounded Madinah.

### The best example
﴿لَّقَدۡ كَانَ لَكُمۡ فِي رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ أُسۡوَةٌ حَسَنَةࣱ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَ وَذَكَرَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا﴾
- Revealed in a time of fear: follow him in patience, courage and trust.

### Believers in crisis
| Group | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Believers | *This is what Allah and His Messenger promised us*; it increased them in faith |
| Hypocrites | *Allah and His Messenger promised us only delusion* |

### Men and women equally
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ وَٱلۡمُسۡلِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ﴾
- Ten qualities, each mentioned for **men and women**, with the same reward.

### Salawat
﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّۚ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ صَلُّواْ عَلَيۡهِ وَسَلِّمُواْ تَسۡلِيمًا﴾
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Whoever sends one blessing upon me, Allah sends ten upon him» [Muslim].

### The trust
- Allah offered the trust to the heavens, earth and mountains; they declined, and **man carried it**.

### Lessons
- **Follow the Prophet ﷺ**, especially in hard times.

## "In the Messenger of Allah You Have an Excellent Example": Quran 33:21 Explained

﴿لَّقَدۡ كَانَ لَكُمۡ فِي رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ أُسۡوَةٌ حَسَنَةࣱ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَ وَذَكَرَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:21]

### The context
- Revealed during the Battle of the Trench (al-Ahzab), when Madinah was besieged. The Prophet ﷺ dug with the Companions, endured hunger, and stayed firm.

### An example in hardship
- The verse first speaks of his example **under pressure**: patience, courage and trust in Allah.

### Who benefits?
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hoping for Allah | Seeking His pleasure |
| Hoping for the Last Day | Working for the Hereafter |
| Remembering Allah much | A heart connected to Him |

### Following him today
| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| Worship | Praying as he prayed |
| Family | His kindness at home |
| Work | Honesty and trustworthiness |
| Trials | Patience and duʿa |

### Example, not imitation of form only
- Following him means his **character and guidance**, not only outward appearance.

### Lessons
- **Study the Seerah** to know who you follow.
- **Ask: how would the Prophet ﷺ act here?**

## "Indeed, the Muslim Men and Muslim Women": The Ten Qualities in Quran 33:35

﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ وَٱلۡمُسۡلِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰدِقَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرَٰتِ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعِينَ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُتَصَدِّقِينَ وَٱلۡمُتَصَدِّقَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰٓئِمِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰٓئِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظِينَ فُرُوجَهُمۡ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظَٰتِ وَٱلذَّـٰكِرِينَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا وَٱلذَّـٰكِرَٰتِ أَعَدَّ ٱللَّهُ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٗ وَأَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:35]

### The occasion
- Scholars mention that Umm Salamah asked the Prophet ﷺ why women were not mentioned in the Quran as men were, and this verse was revealed (reported by Ahmad and al-Nasaʾi in al-Kubra).

### Ten qualities
| # | Quality |
|---|---|
| 1 | Submission (islam) |
| 2 | Faith |
| 3 | Devout obedience |
| 4 | Truthfulness |
| 5 | Patience |
| 6 | Humility |
| 7 | Charity |
| 8 | Fasting |
| 9 | Guarding chastity |
| 10 | Remembering Allah much |

### Equal reward
- Every quality is mentioned for **both** men and women.

### Ending with dhikr
- The list ends with abundant remembrance, which protects all the others.

### Lessons
- **Measure yourself** against these ten.
- **Increase dhikr**, the crown of the qualities.

## "Remember Allah with Much Remembrance": Quran 33:41-43 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ ذِكۡرٗا كَثِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَسَبِّحُوهُ بُكۡرَةٗ وَأَصِيلًا﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُصَلِّي عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتُهُۥ لِيُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِۚ وَكَانَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَحِيمٗا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:41-43]

### "Much" remembrance
- Most acts of worship have a set amount. Dhikr is asked for **abundantly**, without limit.

### Morning and evening
- *Bukrah* and *asil*: the start and end of the day, the times of the morning and evening adhkar.

### Allah's response
| From | What |
|---|---|
| Allah | Sends blessings (praise and mercy) on you |
| The angels | Pray for you |
| Purpose | To bring you out of darkness into light |

### Easy dhikr
- *SubhanAllah, alhamdulillah, la ilaha illa Allah, Allahu akbar*, while walking, driving or waiting.

### Lessons
- **Fill idle moments** with dhikr.
- **Keep the morning and evening adhkar.**

## "Allah and His Angels Send Blessings Upon the Prophet": Quran 33:56 Explained

﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّۚ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ صَلُّواْ عَلَيۡهِ وَسَلِّمُواْ تَسۡلِيمًا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:56]

### Meaning of *salah* here
| From | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Allah | His praise of the Prophet ﷺ among the highest angels, as Abu al-ʿAliyah explained (reported by al-Bukhari) |
| The angels | Their duʿa for him |
| The believers | Asking Allah to bless him |

### The command
- After telling us what Allah and the angels do, the verse commands believers: **send blessings and greet with peace**.

### How to send blessings
- The Companions asked how, and the Prophet ﷺ taught them the Ibrahimi salawat recited in prayer: *Allahumma salli ʿala Muhammad wa ʿala ali Muhammad...* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### The reward
- *Whoever sends one blessing upon me, Allah sends ten blessings upon him* (narrated by Muslim).

### Best times
- On Friday, after the adhan, when his name is mentioned, and in duʿa.

### Lessons
- **Make salawat a daily habit.**
- **Never hear his name** without sending blessings.

## "Fear Allah and Speak Words of Appropriate Justice": Quran 33:70-71 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَقُولُواْ قَوۡلٗا سَدِيدٗا﴾ ﴿يُصۡلِحۡ لَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ ذُنُوبَكُمۡۗ وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَقَدۡ فَازَ فَوۡزًا عَظِيمًا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:70-71]

### What is *sadid* speech?
- *Sadid* means straight and on target, like an arrow that hits its mark: **true, just and appropriate**.

### Two commands, two rewards
| Command | Reward |
|---|---|
| Fear Allah | Allah sets your deeds right |
| Speak rightly | Allah forgives your sins |

### Why speech?
- The tongue is the source of many sins: lying, backbiting, slander. Correcting speech corrects much of life.

### In the khutbat al-hajah
- The Prophet ﷺ taught an opening sermon (*khutbat al-hajah*) that includes these verses, narrated by Abu Dawud and others. Many imams still begin Friday sermons with it.

### The great success
- *Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has achieved a great success.*

### Lessons
- **Speak truthfully** even when it is hard.
- **Fix your words** and Allah fixes your deeds.

## "We Offered the Trust to the Heavens, the Earth and the Mountains": Quran 33:72 Explained

﴿إِنَّا عَرَضۡنَا ٱلۡأَمَانَةَ عَلَى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلۡجِبَالِ فَأَبَيۡنَ أَن يَحۡمِلۡنَهَا وَأَشۡفَقۡنَ مِنۡهَا وَحَمَلَهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ ظَلُومٗا جَهُولٗا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:72]

### What is the Trust?
- Many scholars: the religious obligations of obedience, with reward for fulfilling them and punishment for neglect.

### Declining out of fear
- Their refusal was not disobedience but **fear** of falling short: an offer of choice, not a binding command.

### "Man carried it"
- Humans accepted the Trust despite their weakness.

### *Zaluman jahula*
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Zalum* | Often wronging himself |
| *Jahul* | Often ignorant of consequences |

- A description of human nature, except those Allah guides.

### What follows
- In the next verse Allah punishes the hypocrites and the polytheists and turns in mercy to the believers (33:73): the Trust sorts people.

### Lessons
- **Feel the weight** of your responsibilities.
- **Fulfil trusts** in worship and dealings.
