Surah Al-'Ankabut opens with a question that every believer meets sooner or later: "Do the people think that they will be left to say 'We believe' and they will not be tried?" Faith is not only a statement; it is tested. The surah shows how the prophets were tested, Nuh for 950 years, Ibrahim with fire, Lut with a corrupt people, and it describes those who worship anything other than Allah with an unforgettable image: they are like a spider that builds itself a house, "and indeed, the weakest of houses is the house of the spider". It ends with a promise for everyone who struggles: "And those who strive for Us, We will surely guide them to Our ways."

This guide explains Surah Al-'Ankabut section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-'Ankabut
- **Number of verses**: 69.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 29, in the twentieth and twenty-first juz.
- **Name**: Al-'Ankabut means "the spider", from verse 41.
- **Main themes**: trials of faith, the stories of the prophets, the weakness of false protectors, prayer, migration and striving.

## Verses 1–7: Faith Will Be Tested
﴿الٓمٓ﴾ ﴿أَحَسِبَ ٱلنَّاسُ أَن يُتۡرَكُوٓاْ أَن يَقُولُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا وَهُمۡ لَا يُفۡتَنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَدۡ فَتَنَّا ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۖ فَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ صَدَقُواْ وَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ﴾ (Quran 29:1-3)

**"Do the people think that they will be left to say 'We believe' and they will not be tried? But We have certainly tried those before them, and Allah will surely make evident those who are truthful, and He will surely make evident the liars."** Trials separate true faith from words. In a hadith reported by at-Tirmidhi and graded *hasan sahih*, the Prophet ﷺ was asked who is most severely tested, and he said: "The prophets, then the next best, then the next best. A man is tested according to his religion."

﴿وَمَن جَٰهَدَ فَإِنَّمَا يُجَٰهِدُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَنِيٌّ عَنِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ لَنُكَفِّرَنَّ عَنۡهُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّهُمۡ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:6-7)

**"Whoever strives only strives for himself. Indeed, Allah is Free from need of the worlds."**

## Verses 8–13: Parents and Trials
﴿وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ حُسۡنٗاۖ وَإِن جَٰهَدَاكَ لِتُشۡرِكَ بِي مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمࣱ فَلَا تُطِعۡهُمَآۚ إِلَيَّ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ فَأُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:8)

**"And We have enjoined upon man goodness to parents. But if they endeavour to make you associate with Me that of which you have no knowledge, do not obey them."** In Sahih Muslim, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas said this verse was revealed about him: when he accepted Islam, his mother swore she would not eat or drink until he left his religion. He refused to leave Islam while remaining kind to her.

﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَقُولُ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ فَإِذَآ أُوذِيَ فِي ٱللَّهِ جَعَلَ فِتۡنَةَ ٱلنَّاسِ كَعَذَابِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَلَئِن جَآءَ نَصۡرࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكَ لَيَقُولُنَّ إِنَّا كُنَّا مَعَكُمۡۚ أَوَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَعۡلَمَ بِمَا فِي صُدُورِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 29:10-11)

**"And of the people are some who say: We believe in Allah. But when one of them is harmed for the cause of Allah, he considers the trial of the people as if it were the punishment of Allah."** Some people's faith collapses at the first pressure, while in times of victory they claim: "We were with you."

## Verses 14–15: Nuh's 950 Years
﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ فَلَبِثَ فِيهِمۡ أَلۡفَ سَنَةٍ إِلَّا خَمۡسِينَ عَامٗا فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱلطُّوفَانُ وَهُمۡ ظَٰلِمُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَنجَيۡنَٰهُ وَأَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلسَّفِينَةِ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهَآ ءَايَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 29:14-15)

**"And We certainly sent Nuh to his people, and he remained among them a thousand years minus fifty years."** Then the flood seized them. **"But We saved him and the companions of the ship, and We made it a sign for the worlds."**

## Verses 16–27: Ibrahim
﴿وَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱتَّقُوهُۖ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡثَٰنٗا وَتَخۡلُقُونَ إِفۡكًاۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَا يَمۡلِكُونَ لَكُمۡ رِزۡقٗا فَٱبۡتَغُواْ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرِّزۡقَ وَٱعۡبُدُوهُ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لَهُۥٓۖ إِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:16-17)

**Ibrahim said to his people: "Worship Allah and fear Him."** **"Those you worship besides Allah do not possess for you provision. So seek from Allah provision and worship Him and be grateful to Him."**

﴿فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُواْ ٱقۡتُلُوهُ أَوۡ حَرِّقُوهُ فَأَنجَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ إِنَّمَا ٱتَّخَذۡتُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡثَٰنٗا مَّوَدَّةَ بَيۡنِكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ ثُمَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ يَكۡفُرُ بَعۡضُكُم بِبَعۡضٖ وَيَلۡعَنُ بَعۡضُكُم بَعۡضٗا وَمَأۡوَىٰكُمُ ٱلنَّارُ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن نَّـٰصِرِينَ﴾ ﴿۞فَـَٔامَنَ لَهُۥ لُوطࣱۘ وَقَالَ إِنِّي مُهَاجِرٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّيٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ إِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ وَجَعَلۡنَا فِي ذُرِّيَّتِهِ ٱلنُّبُوَّةَ وَٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ أَجۡرَهُۥ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَإِنَّهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لَمِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ (Quran 29:24-27)

They said: **"Kill him or burn him."** **"But Allah saved him from the fire."** **"And Lut believed him."** Ibrahim said: **"Indeed, I will emigrate to my Lord."** Allah gave him Ishaq and Ya'qub, placed prophethood and scripture among his descendants, **"and We gave him his reward in this world, and indeed, he is in the hereafter among the righteous"**.

## Verses 28–35: Lut
﴿وَلُوطًا إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنَّكُمۡ لَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلۡفَٰحِشَةَ مَا سَبَقَكُم بِهَا مِنۡ أَحَدٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿أَئِنَّكُمۡ لَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلرِّجَالَ وَتَقۡطَعُونَ ٱلسَّبِيلَ وَتَأۡتُونَ فِي نَادِيكُمُ ٱلۡمُنكَرَۖ فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُواْ ٱئۡتِنَا بِعَذَابِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱنصُرۡنِي عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَمَّا جَآءَتۡ رُسُلُنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ بِٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا مُهۡلِكُوٓاْ أَهۡلِ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِۖ إِنَّ أَهۡلَهَا كَانُواْ ظَٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ إِنَّ فِيهَا لُوطٗاۚ قَالُواْ نَحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن فِيهَاۖ لَنُنَجِّيَنَّهُۥ وَأَهۡلَهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱمۡرَأَتَهُۥ كَانَتۡ مِنَ ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَمَّآ أَن جَآءَتۡ رُسُلُنَا لُوطٗا سِيٓءَ بِهِمۡ وَضَاقَ بِهِمۡ ذَرۡعٗاۖ وَقَالُواْ لَا تَخَفۡ وَلَا تَحۡزَنۡ إِنَّا مُنَجُّوكَ وَأَهۡلَكَ إِلَّا ٱمۡرَأَتَكَ كَانَتۡ مِنَ ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا مُنزِلُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِ هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ رِجۡزٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡسُقُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَقَد تَّرَكۡنَا مِنۡهَآ ءَايَةَۢ بَيِّنَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:28-35)

Lut said to his people: **"Indeed, you commit such immorality as no one has preceded you with among the worlds."** They committed open indecency and highway robbery, and did evil in their gatherings. The angels came with good news to Ibrahim and to destroy Lut's town, saving Lut and his family except his wife.

## Verses 36–40: Four Kinds of Destruction
﴿وَإِلَىٰ مَدۡيَنَ أَخَاهُمۡ شُعَيۡبٗا فَقَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱرۡجُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَ وَلَا تَعۡثَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُفۡسِدِينَ﴾ ﴿فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلرَّجۡفَةُ فَأَصۡبَحُواْ فِي دَارِهِمۡ جَٰثِمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَعَادٗا وَثَمُودَاْ وَقَد تَّبَيَّنَ لَكُم مِّن مَّسَٰكِنِهِمۡۖ وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ فَصَدَّهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَكَانُواْ مُسۡتَبۡصِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَقَٰرُونَ وَفِرۡعَوۡنَ وَهَٰمَٰنَۖ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُم مُّوسَىٰ بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ فَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا كَانُواْ سَٰبِقِينَ﴾ ﴿فَكُلًّا أَخَذۡنَا بِذَنۢبِهِۦۖ فَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ حَاصِبٗا وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ أَخَذَتۡهُ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ خَسَفۡنَا بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ أَغۡرَقۡنَاۚ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيَظۡلِمَهُمۡ وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ يَظۡلِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:36-40)

After Shu'ayb, 'Ad, Thamud, Qarun, Pharaoh and Haman: **"So each We seized for his sin. And among them were those upon whom We sent a storm of stones, and among them were those who were seized by the blast, and among them were those whom We caused the earth to swallow, and among them were those whom We drowned. And Allah would not have wronged them, but it was they who were wronging themselves."**

## Verses 41–44: The Spider's House
﴿مَثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡلِيَآءَ كَمَثَلِ ٱلۡعَنكَبُوتِ ٱتَّخَذَتۡ بَيۡتٗاۖ وَإِنَّ أَوۡهَنَ ٱلۡبُيُوتِ لَبَيۡتُ ٱلۡعَنكَبُوتِۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:41)

**"The example of those who take allies other than Allah is like that of the spider who takes a home. And indeed, the weakest of homes is the home of the spider, if they only knew."**

**The meaning**: whoever relies on anything other than Allah, whether idols, people, wealth or power, is like a spider in its web. It looks like a home, but it cannot protect from wind, rain or heat. All support apart from Allah is fragile.

﴿وَتِلۡكَ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلُ نَضۡرِبُهَا لِلنَّاسِۖ وَمَا يَعۡقِلُهَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡعَٰلِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:43)

**"And these examples We present to the people, but none will understand them except those of knowledge."**

## Verses 45–46: Prayer and Dialogue
﴿ٱتۡلُ مَآ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡكَ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۖ إِنَّ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ تَنۡهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِۗ وَلَذِكۡرُ ٱللَّهِ أَكۡبَرُۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ مَا تَصۡنَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:45)

**"Recite what has been revealed to you of the Book and establish prayer. Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing, and the remembrance of Allah is greater."** A prayer offered properly, with presence of heart, changes behaviour. If your prayer does not yet keep you from sin, improve it rather than abandoning it. **"And the remembrance of Allah is greater"**: greater than anything, and the very soul of prayer.

﴿۞وَلَا تُجَٰدِلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِلَّا بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡۖ وَقُولُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِٱلَّذِيٓ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡنَا وَأُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكُمۡ وَإِلَٰهُنَا وَإِلَٰهُكُمۡ وَٰحِدࣱ وَنَحۡنُ لَهُۥ مُسۡلِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:46)

**"And do not argue with the People of the Scripture except in a way that is best, except for those who commit injustice among them, and say: We believe in that which has been revealed to us and revealed to you. And our God and your God is one."** A model of respectful dialogue.

## Verses 47–55: The Book and the Unlettered Prophet
﴿وَمَا كُنتَ تَتۡلُواْ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ مِن كِتَٰبٖ وَلَا تَخُطُّهُۥ بِيَمِينِكَۖ إِذٗا لَّٱرۡتَابَ ٱلۡمُبۡطِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:48)

**"And you did not recite before it any scripture, nor did you inscribe one with your right hand. Otherwise the falsifiers would have had cause for doubt."** The Prophet ﷺ was unlettered, which makes the Quran's origin from Allah clearer.

## Verses 56–64: Allah's Wide Earth and Provision
﴿يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ أَرۡضِي وَٰسِعَةࣱ فَإِيَّـٰيَ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ﴾ ﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۖ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡنَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:56-57)

**"O My servants who have believed, indeed My earth is spacious, so worship only Me."** If you cannot practise your religion in one place, move. **"Every soul will taste death. Then to Us will you be returned."**

﴿وَكَأَيِّن مِّن دَآبَّةٖ لَّا تَحۡمِلُ رِزۡقَهَا ٱللَّهُ يَرۡزُقُهَا وَإِيَّاكُمۡۚ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ (Quran 29:60)

**"And how many a creature carries not its own provision. Allah provides for it and for you."** Birds, insects and animals store nothing, yet they eat every day. Worry about provision should not push you into the forbidden.

﴿وَمَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا لَهۡوࣱ وَلَعِبࣱۚ وَإِنَّ ٱلدَّارَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ لَهِيَ ٱلۡحَيَوَانُۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:64)

**"And this worldly life is not but diversion and amusement. And indeed, the home of the hereafter, that is the real life, if only they knew."** *Al-hayawan*: the fullest, truest life.

## Verses 65–69: Sincerity at Sea and the Promise to Strivers
﴿فَإِذَا رَكِبُواْ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ دَعَوُاْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ فَلَمَّا نَجَّىٰهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡبَرِّ إِذَا هُمۡ يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ (Quran 29:65)

**"When they board a ship, they supplicate Allah, sincere to Him in religion. But when He delivers them to the land, at once they associate others with Him."**

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ جَٰهَدُواْ فِينَا لَنَهۡدِيَنَّهُمۡ سُبُلَنَاۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَمَعَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 29:69)

**"And those who strive for Us, We will surely guide them to Our ways. And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good."** The surah that began with trials ends with a promise: whoever strives sincerely for Allah will be guided, step by step.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Do people think...?"**: a question that removes illusions.
2. **"A thousand years minus fifty"**: the length of patience made concrete.
3. **Four kinds of destruction** in one verse.
4. **The spider's house**: a perfect image of false security.
5. **"The remembrance of Allah is greater"**: short and profound.
6. **"Our ways"**: plural, showing many paths of guidance open to the striver.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Alif Lam Mim*.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *baytan wa inna* (verse 41), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna as-salata* (verse 45), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *al-fahsha'i* (verse 45).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Qasas before it ended with "Everything will be destroyed except His Face", and Al-'Ankabut opens with tests that reveal who truly seeks His Face. Surah Ar-Rum after it opens with a prophecy of victory after defeat, a real-world trial and its outcome.

## Facing Trials With Surah Al-'Ankabut
1. **Expect tests**: they are part of faith.
2. **Stay kind to family** even when they pressure you against your faith.
3. **Do not equate people's harm with Allah's punishment.**
4. **Remember the long patience of the prophets.**
5. **Rely on Allah, not on spider webs.**
6. **Protect yourself with prayer.**
7. **Strive, and expect guidance.**

## Practical Lessons
1. **Prove your faith in action.**
2. **Never obey anyone in shirk**, but keep kindness.
3. **Examine what you rely on.**
4. **Improve your prayer until it changes your conduct.**
5. **Discuss with others in the best manner.**
6. **Trust Allah for provision.**
7. **See the hereafter as the true life.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "Do people think they will be left to say 'We believe'..." mean?
That faith will be tested to show who is truthful.

### What is the parable of the spider's house?
Those who rely on anything other than Allah are like a spider in its fragile web; it cannot protect them.

### How does prayer prevent immorality?
Prayer offered with presence of heart reminds a person of Allah and strengthens him against sin.

### Who was verse 8 revealed about?
Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, whose mother refused to eat until he left Islam, as reported in Sahih Muslim.

### What does "the home of the hereafter is the real life" mean?
That the hereafter is the complete and lasting life, while this world is diversion and amusement.

### What does "those who strive for Us, We will guide them to Our ways" promise?
That Allah guides those who strive sincerely for Him to the paths that lead to Him.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-'Ankabut?
69 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-'Ankabut is honest about the cost of faith: you will be tested. It shows the prophets who passed their tests, the nations who failed, and the fragile webs people hide in. Then it gives you protection and hope: establish prayer, rely on Allah alone, trust Him for your provision, and strive for Him, for He has promised to guide you to His ways.
