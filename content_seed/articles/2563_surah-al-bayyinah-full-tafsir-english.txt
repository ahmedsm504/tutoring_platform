Surah Al-Bayyinah has a story that moves every reader. Allah commanded His Prophet ﷺ to recite it to Ubayy ibn Ka'b, by name, and when Ubayy heard that Allah had mentioned him, he wept. The surah itself sums up the whole religion in one verse: "And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, being sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth, and to establish prayer and give zakah. And that is the correct religion." It divides people into two groups, the worst of creatures and the best of creatures, and ends with the greatest reward anyone could hope for: "Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him."

This guide explains Surah Al-Bayyinah verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Bayyinah
- **Number of verses**: 8.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 98, in the thirtieth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Bayyinah means "the clear evidence", mentioned in its first verse. It is also called *Lam yakun* and *Al-Qayyimah*.
- **Main themes**: the Prophet ﷺ as the clear proof, sincerity as the foundation of religion, and the two final destinations.

## The Story of Ubayy ibn Ka'b
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Anas ibn Malik reported that the Prophet ﷺ said to Ubayy ibn Ka'b: "Allah has commanded me to recite to you: *Lam yakuni alladhina kafaru*." Ubayy said: "He named me?" He said: "Yes." And Ubayy wept.

**Lessons from the story**:
1. **The rank of Ubayy**: the master of the reciters, honoured by Allah by name.
2. **Humility in teaching**: the Prophet ﷺ recited to one of his companions, teaching the Ummah to present recitation to people of mastery.
3. **Tears of joy at being remembered by Allah**.
4. **You can be remembered too**: in the hadith qudsi in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Allah says: "If he remembers Me within himself, I remember him within Myself; and if he remembers Me in a gathering, I remember him in a gathering better than it."

**Who was Ubayy?** An Ansari from Banu an-Najjar, one of the scribes of revelation and among the most knowledgeable of the Companions about the Quran. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Take the Quran from four: 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Salim, Mu'adh and Ubayy ibn Ka'b." And in Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ asked him which verse of the Book of Allah was the greatest; he answered "Allah, there is no god but He, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer", and the Prophet ﷺ struck his chest and said: "May knowledge be pleasant for you, O Abu al-Mundhir."

## Verses 1–3: The Clear Evidence
﴿لَمۡ يَكُنِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ مُنفَكِّينَ حَتَّىٰ تَأۡتِيَهُمُ ٱلۡبَيِّنَةُ﴾ ﴿رَسُولࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ يَتۡلُواْ صُحُفٗا مُّطَهَّرَةٗ﴾ ﴿فِيهَا كُتُبࣱ قَيِّمَةࣱ﴾ (Quran 98:1-3)

**"Those who disbelieved among the People of the Book and the polytheists were not going to depart"** (*munfakkin*) from their disbelief **"until there came to them the clear evidence"**. The disbelievers here are of two kinds: the People of the Book (Jews and Christians) and the polytheists who worshipped idols.

**What is the clear evidence?** **"A Messenger from Allah, reciting purified scriptures, within which are correct writings."** The Prophet ﷺ reciting the Quran, written in pages that are pure from falsehood and distortion, containing upright and just rulings.

**Two descriptions of the Quran**: *suhufan mutahharah*, purified scriptures, and *kutubun qayyimah*, correct, upright writings. Purity in the source and uprightness in the content.

## Verse 4: Division After Knowledge
﴿وَمَا تَفَرَّقَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا جَآءَتۡهُمُ ٱلۡبَيِّنَةُ﴾ (Quran 98:4)

The People of the Book did not divide out of ignorance. They divided **after** the clear evidence came to them: some believed and some rejected. Division after knowledge is worse than division out of ignorance, because it comes from desire, envy and transgression.

**A warning for Muslims**: Allah says in Surah Al 'Imran: "And do not be like those who became divided and differed after the clear proofs had come to them." Having the truth is not enough; we must hold to it together.

## Verse 5: The Whole Religion in One Verse
﴿وَمَآ أُمِرُوٓاْ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ حُنَفَآءَ وَيُقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَۚ وَذَٰلِكَ دِينُ ٱلۡقَيِّمَةِ﴾ (Quran 98:5)

**This verse sums up the religion in four matters**:
1. **"To worship Allah, being sincere to Him in religion"**: *ikhlas*, all worship for Allah alone.
2. **"Inclining to truth"** (*hunafa'*): turning away from shirk towards pure tawhid, as Ibrahim was *hanif*.
3. **"And to establish prayer"**: performing it completely with its conditions and focus.
4. **"And to give zakah"**: paying it to those entitled.

**"And that is the correct religion"** (*din al-qayyimah*): the upright, straight religion.

**Sincerity is the foundation of every deed**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'Umar ibn al-Khattab reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Actions are only by intentions, and every person will have only what he intended." And in Sahih Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah says: "I am the One most free of need of partners. Whoever does a deed in which he associates someone else with Me, I abandon him and his partnership."

**Notice the combination**: worship of the heart (sincerity and *hanifiyyah*), worship of the body (prayer), and worship with wealth (zakah). Religion covers the heart, the body and money.

**And this is the religion of all the prophets**: "they were not commanded" refers to the People of the Book, meaning this is what their own scriptures commanded them. The religion is one.

## Verse 6: The Worst of Creatures
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ فِي نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمۡ شَرُّ ٱلۡبَرِيَّةِ﴾ (Quran 98:6)

The disbelievers who rejected the clear evidence will be **in the Fire of Hell, abiding eternally in it**. **"Those are the worst of creatures"** (*sharr al-bariyyah*), because they recognised the truth and turned away from it. *Al-bariyyah* means creation, from *bara'a*, to create.

## Verses 7–8: The Best of Creatures
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمۡ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡبَرِيَّةِ﴾ ﴿جَزَآؤُهُمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ جَنَّـٰتُ عَدۡنٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۖ رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنۡ خَشِيَ رَبَّهُۥ﴾ (Quran 98:7-8)

**"Those who believe and do righteous deeds, those are the best of creatures."** Two conditions: faith and righteous deeds. Neither is enough without the other.

**Their reward**:
1. **"Gardens of perpetual residence"** ('Adn).
2. **"Beneath which rivers flow."**
3. **"Abiding therein forever."**
4. **"Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him"**: greater than Paradise itself.

**Allah's pleasure is the greatest bliss**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Sa'id al-Khudri reported that the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah will say to the people of Paradise: "O people of Paradise." They will say: "Here we are, our Lord, at Your service." He will say: "Are you pleased?" They will say: "How could we not be pleased when You have given us what You have not given anyone of Your creation?" He will say: "I will give you something better than that." They will say: "O Lord, what could be better than that?" He will say: "I will cause My pleasure to descend upon you, and I will never be displeased with you after it."

**"That is for whoever fears his Lord"**: *khashyah*, reverent fear rooted in knowledge, is the key. Allah says in Surah Fatir: "Only those of His servants who have knowledge truly fear Allah."

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Bayyinah**: the clear evidence.
- **Munfakkin**: departing, giving up.
- **Suhuf**: scriptures, pages.
- **Mutahharah**: purified.
- **Qayyimah**: correct, upright.
- **Mukhlisin**: being sincere.
- **Hunafa'**: inclining away from falsehood to the truth.
- **Al-Bariyyah**: creation, creatures.
- **'Adn**: perpetual residence.
- **Khashiya**: feared with reverence.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Were not going to depart"**: shows how firmly they clung to their disbelief.
2. **"Purified scriptures" and "correct writings"**: purity and uprightness together.
3. **"The worst of creatures" and "the best of creatures"**: a decisive contrast.
4. **"Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him"**: mutual pleasure, the height of happiness.
5. **"That is for whoever fears his Lord"**: a closing line that names the path.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ja'at-hum* (verse 4) and *jaza'uhum* (verse 8).
- **Iqlab**: in *min ba'di* (verse 4), the noon sakinah before the ba' becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *munfakkin* (verse 1), the noon sakinah is hidden before the fa'.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *rasulun mina Allah* (verse 2), the tanween merges into the meem.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Qadr before it described the revelation of the Quran on the Night of Decree, and Al-Bayyinah explains that this Quran is the clear evidence recited by the Messenger in purified pages. Surah Az-Zalzalah after it describes the Day when every person sees his deeds, which is when the best and the worst of creatures will be separated.

## Achieving Sincerity
1. **Check your intention before every deed**: who am I doing this for?
2. **Hide your good deeds** as much as you can.
3. **Do not wait for people's praise.**
4. **Be the same in private and in public.**
5. **Ask Allah for sincerity**: "O Allah, make all my deeds righteous and make them purely for Your face."
6. **Remember that people cannot benefit or harm you** except by Allah's permission.
7. **Review your intention during and after the deed.**

## Faith and Deeds Together
"Those who believe and do righteous deeds" appears dozens of times in the Quran. Faith without deeds is a claim; deeds without faith are dust. A righteous deed has two conditions: sincerity to Allah, and following the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'A'ishah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever introduces into this matter of ours something that is not from it, it is rejected." Verse 5 secures the first condition, and this hadith secures the second.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Make your worship sincere for Allah.**
2. **Establish prayer and pay zakah**: they are the foundation of religion.
3. **Do not divide after knowing the truth.**
4. **Combine faith and righteous deeds.**
5. **Fear your Lord**: reverent fear is the path to His pleasure.
6. **Present your recitation to people of mastery**, as the Prophet ﷺ recited to Ubayy.
7. **Remember Allah and He will remember you.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Bayyinah
- **Day 1**: verses 1–4.
- **Day 2**: verse 5 alone, with reflection.
- **Day 3**: verses 6–8, then revise the whole surah.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them the story of Ubayy ibn Ka'b** and how he wept with joy.
- **Teach them what sincerity means**: doing good for Allah, not to be seen.
- **Explain "the best of creatures"**: believers who do good deeds.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the "clear evidence" in Surah Al-Bayyinah?
The Messenger Muhammad ﷺ reciting the Quran written in purified pages; he is the clear proof.

### What is the story of Ubayy ibn Ka'b and this surah?
Allah commanded the Prophet ﷺ to recite it to Ubayy, and when Ubayy learned that Allah had named him, he wept, as reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does "being sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth" mean?
Worshipping Allah alone, and turning away from shirk to pure tawhid.

### What does "the correct religion" mean?
The upright, straight religion.

### Who are "the best of creatures"?
Those who believe and do righteous deeds.

### What does "Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him" mean?
Allah is pleased with their deeds and honours them, and they are pleased with the bliss He gives them.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Bayyinah?
8 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Bayyinah lays out the path clearly: sincerity to Allah, prayer, zakah, faith, righteous deeds and reverent fear. Whoever walks it is among the best of creatures and receives what every soul longs for: Allah's pleasure. O Allah, make us among those You are pleased with and who are pleased with You.
