**Surah Ar-Rad** (The Thunder, 43 ayat) invites reflection on the signs of creation.

## Signs above and below
- Heavens raised without pillars you can see, the sun and moon running for a term, the earth spread with mountains and rivers.
- Neighbouring plots of land watered with **the same water**, yet producing different fruits.

## Guarded and accountable
- Each person has angels in succession before and behind him, guarding him by Allah's command.

## Change starts within
﴿لَهُۥ مُعَقِّبَٰتࣱ مِّنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ يَحۡفَظُونَهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡۗ وَإِذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ سُوٓءٗا فَلَا مَرَدَّ لَهُۥۚ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَالٍ﴾

## The thunder glorifies Him
- *The thunder glorifies Him with praise, and the angels too, out of awe of Him.*

## Hearts at rest
﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ﴾

## The people of understanding
- They fulfil their covenant, join what Allah ordered to be joined, are patient, pray, spend secretly and openly, and **repel evil with good**.

## Lessons
- **Reform yourself first**: Allah's change follows inner change.
- **Remembrance is the remedy for anxiety.**
