﴿أَفَحَسِبۡتُمۡ أَنَّمَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ عَبَثٗا وَأَنَّكُمۡ إِلَيۡنَا لَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ ﴿فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ﴾ [Al-Muminun 23:115-116]

## *ʿAbath*
- Action without wisdom or goal.

## Two false assumptions
| Assumption | Reality |
|---|---|
| We were created without purpose | We were created to worship |
| There is no return | The return is to Allah |

## "Exalted is Allah"
- Allah is far above aimlessness: the **King** who governs, the **Truth** from whom nothing false comes.

## Its place
- After the scene of the people of the Fire asked *How many years did you remain on earth?*: the world seemed short in their eyes.

## Lessons
- **Ask yourself**: why do I live?
- **Give each day** a goal that pleases Allah.
