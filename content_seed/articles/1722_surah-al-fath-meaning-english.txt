**Surah Al-Fath** (29 ayat) was revealed on the way back from Hudaybiyah. The Prophet ﷺ said: «A surah has been revealed to me tonight that is more beloved to me than everything the sun rises upon» [Al-Bukhari].

﴿إِنَّا فَتَحۡنَا لَكَ فَتۡحٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾ — *Indeed, We have given you a clear victory.*

## A victory that looked like a setback
| What it seemed | What it was |
|---|---|
| Turned back from ʿUmrah | Peace that allowed Islam to spread |
| Terms seemed unfair | Recognition of the Muslims as a power |
| Disappointment | Two years later, Makkah was opened |

## The pledge under the tree
- Allah was **pleased** with the believers when they pledged allegiance under the tree, *and He knew what was in their hearts, so He sent down tranquility upon them*.

## The true dream
- The Prophet ﷺ had seen that they would enter the Sacred Mosque in safety; the surah confirms it would happen, and it did the following year.

## The companions described
﴿مُّحَمَّدࣱ رَّسُولُ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥٓ أَشِدَّآءُ عَلَى ٱلۡكُفَّارِ رُحَمَآءُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۖ﴾
- **Firm** against aggressors, **merciful** among themselves, often bowing and prostrating, with the mark of prostration on their faces.

## Lessons
- **Don't judge events by first appearances.**
- **Tranquility** is a gift Allah places in hearts.
