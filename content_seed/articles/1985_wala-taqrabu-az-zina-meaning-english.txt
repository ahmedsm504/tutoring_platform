## "Do not approach"
- The verse forbids even **coming near**: the look, the private meeting, the suggestive talk and the content that leads to it. This is closing the path before the destination.

## Two descriptions
| Description | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Fahishah* | Extremely shameful, rejected by the fitrah |
| *An evil way* | It destroys families, lineage and hearts |

## Prevention in the Quran
- Lowering the gaze (An-Nur 24:30-31), asking permission before entering homes, and easing marriage (An-Nur 24:32).

## The door of repentance
- Whoever falls and repents, Allah accepts repentance and replaces bad deeds with good for those who repent, believe and do good (Al-Furqan 25:70).

## Lessons
- **Avoid the road**, not just the end.
- **Protect your home** from content that leads there.
