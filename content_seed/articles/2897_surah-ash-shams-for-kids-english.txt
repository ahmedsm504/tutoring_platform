What's the most amazing thing you can see in the sky? Maybe the sun at sunrise, glowing gold and orange? Or the moon following it at night? In Surah Ash-Shams, Allah swears by the sun, the moon, the day, the night, the sky, the earth and the human soul: seven amazing things, in eleven oaths! That's the most oaths in a row anywhere in the Quran. And after all these oaths comes one huge message: "Whoever purifies their soul has succeeded, and whoever corrupts it has failed."

Then the surah tells the story of the people of Thamud, who were given a miraculous she-camel by Allah, and what happened when they chose to harm her. In this guide for parents, we explain Surah Ash-Shams to children in simple English, with fun activities about the sky, practical ways to "purify the soul" through good habits, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ash-Shams

- **Position**: surah 91, in Juz 'Amma.
- **Number of verses**: fifteen.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: ash-Shams means "the sun".

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ told Mu'adh, who made his prayers too long for the people, to recite surahs like "Wash-shamsi wa duhaha", "Sabbihisma Rabbikal-a'la" and "Wal-layli idha yaghsha" (al-Bukhari and Muslim). These are good, medium-length surahs for prayer.

## The Whole Surah

﴿وَٱلشَّمۡسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَنَفۡسٖ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿فَأَلۡهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقۡوَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ بِطَغۡوَىٰهَآ﴾ ﴿إِذِ ٱنۢبَعَثَ أَشۡقَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿فَقَالَ لَهُمۡ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ نَاقَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَسُقۡيَٰهَا﴾ ﴿فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمۡدَمَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّهُم بِذَنۢبِهِمۡ فَسَوَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقۡبَٰهَا﴾ (Quran 91:1-15)

## Verses 1–7: Eleven Oaths

### The sun and its brightness
"By the sun and its morning brightness. The sun gives us light, warmth and energy for plants to grow. Without it, nothing on earth could live."

### The moon when it follows
"By the moon when it follows the sun. After sunset, the moon rises and shines. Its light is the sun's light reflected back to us."

### The day when it reveals
"By the day when it shows everything clearly. In daylight, we can see the world around us."

### The night when it covers
"By the night when it covers everything like a blanket, so we can rest and sleep."

### The sky and how He built it
"By the sky and the One who built it, high and strong, without pillars we can see."

### The earth and how He spread it
"By the earth and the One who spread it out wide, so we can live, walk, farm and build on it."

### The soul and how He shaped it
"By the soul and the One who shaped it perfectly, and inspired it to know right from wrong."

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah swears by all these amazing things to show us how important the next message is. It's like a teacher saying: Listen, listen, listen! Something very important is coming!"

### Activity: the oath chart
Make a big chart with seven pictures: the sun, the moon, a bright day, a starry night, the sky, the earth and a heart (for the soul). Under each, write what it gives us. Hang it in your child's room.

### Activity: sun and moon shadows
On a sunny day, go outside at different times: morning, midday and afternoon. Mark your child's shadow with chalk each time. Notice how it changes. Say: "Allah moves the sun across the sky with perfect timing."

## Verse 8: He Inspired It With Right and Wrong

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah put inside every soul the ability to know right from wrong. That little feeling inside you that says 'this is wrong' when you're about to lie, or 'this is good' when you help someone: that's the inspiration Allah gave your soul."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Righteousness is good character, and sin is what wavers in your heart and you dislike people finding out about it" (Muslim).

### Lesson for kids
"If you're doing something and you hope nobody finds out, that's a sign it might be wrong. Listen to that feeling Allah put in your heart."

## Verses 9–10: Success and Failure

### Explaining it to your child
"Qad aflaha man zakkaha: whoever purifies their soul has succeeded. Wa qad khaba man dassaha: and whoever corrupts it has failed. Purifying your soul is like cleaning a window so the light can shine through: you remove bad habits like lying, jealousy and laziness, and grow good habits like honesty, kindness and prayer."

### Activity: the clean window
Smudge a small mirror with a little butter or soap, then let your child clean it with a cloth until it sparkles. Say: "Bad habits make our hearts cloudy like this smudged mirror. Good deeds and saying sorry to Allah make them shine again."

### Activity: my soul-cleaning plan
Help your child choose:
- **One bad habit to remove** this month: e.g., shouting at siblings, or saying "uff".
- **One good habit to grow**: e.g., saying salam to everyone, or praying on time.
Track it on a simple calendar, with a tick for each day they succeed.

### A du'a from the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ used to pray: "Allahumma ati nafsi taqwaha, wa zakkiha anta khayru man zakkaha, anta waliyyuha wa mawlaha", "O Allah, give my soul its taqwa and purify it, for You are the best to purify it; You are its Guardian and Master" (Muslim). Teach it to your child for their daily du'a.

## Verses 11–15: The Story of Thamud and the She-Camel

### Explaining it to your child
"The people of Thamud lived in a land of rocky mountains and carved beautiful houses into the rocks. Allah sent them Prophet Salih to call them to worship Allah alone. They asked for a miracle, so Allah brought out an amazing she-camel from the rock. Salih told them: This is the she-camel of Allah. Let her drink on her day, and you drink on your day, and don't harm her."

"But the people refused to listen. The worst of them, encouraged by the others, killed the she-camel. Because they rejected Allah's sign and His prophet, Allah destroyed them, and He did not fear the consequences, because Allah answers to no one."

### Lesson for kids: be kind to animals
"The she-camel was a sign from Allah, and harming her was a terrible crime. Islam teaches us to be kind to all animals: give them water, don't hurt them and don't scare them for fun."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ told of a man who found a thirsty dog licking mud, so he filled his shoe with water and gave it a drink. Allah thanked him and forgave him. The companions asked: "Is there a reward for us in animals?" He said: "In every living creature there is a reward" (al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Activity: water for birds
Put out a shallow dish of water for birds in your garden or on your balcony, especially in hot weather. Let your child refill it every day, and tell them about the man and the thirsty dog.

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Shining Mirror"

Ali had a habit he didn't like about himself: whenever he got angry, he shouted at his little brother. Afterwards, he always felt bad, but the next time, it happened again.

One evening, his mother taught him Surah Ash-Shams. When she reached "Qad aflaha man zakkaha", she explained: "Whoever purifies their soul has succeeded." Then she showed him a mirror smudged with fingerprints and asked him to clean it. When it sparkled, she said: "Your heart is like this mirror. Shouting is like a smudge. Every time you say sorry and try again, you clean it."

Ali decided to make a plan. Every day he didn't shout at his brother, he would put a gold star on his calendar. The first week, he got only three stars. "I'm failing," he said sadly. His mother smiled. "Three is better than zero! Remember, purifying the soul takes time."

The second week, Ali got five stars. When he felt angry, he said "A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim" and went to drink water, like his mother had taught him. By the end of the month, he had a full row of gold stars.

His little brother hugged him and said: "Ali, you're the best brother now!" Ali looked at his calendar and recited: "Qad aflaha man zakkaha." He felt his heart was shining like the sun.

Ask your child: "What habit did Ali want to change? How did he do it?"

## What We Learn From Surah Ash-Shams

- **Allah's creation** is full of amazing signs.
- **Allah put inside us** the ability to know right from wrong.
- **Purifying the soul** is the key to success.
- **Bad habits** can be removed, step by step.
- **Good habits** make our hearts shine.
- **Rejecting Allah's signs** leads to failure, as with Thamud.
- **Be kind to animals**; there is reward in every living creature.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### How do I explain "purifying the soul" to a young child?
Use simple images: cleaning a mirror, washing hands, or weeding a garden. Removing bad habits and growing good ones is what purifying the soul means in practice.

### Is the story of Thamud too scary?
Tell it calmly, focusing on the she-camel as a sign from Allah, the importance of listening to good advice, and kindness to animals. Keep the destruction brief.

### Which prayer is this surah good for?
It is a medium-length surah, ideal for older children in Maghrib or 'Isha, as the Prophet ﷺ recommended surahs like it for congregational prayer.


### Can a child really "purify their soul"?
Yes, in small, age-appropriate ways: saying sorry, telling the truth, sharing, praying and controlling anger. Each small step counts, and children love seeing their progress on a chart. Remind them that falling back sometimes is normal; what matters is trying again, and Allah loves those who keep turning back to Him.


### Where did the people of Thamud live?
Scholars mention they lived in al-Hijr, in the north-west of the Arabian Peninsula, where houses carved into rock can still be seen today. Looking at photos together can make the story feel real for children.
