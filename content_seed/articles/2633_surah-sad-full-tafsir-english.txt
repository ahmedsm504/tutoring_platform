Surah Sad was revealed in Makkah at a time of intense opposition. Its opening shows the leaders of Quraysh walking out in protest, astonished that the Prophet ﷺ wanted to make "the gods one God". Then, to strengthen him, the surah tells the stories of prophets who turned to Allah in repentance and patience: Dawud the king who judged between two litigants, Sulayman with his vast kingdom, and Ayyub in his long illness. Each of them is described with the same praise: "an excellent servant; indeed, he was one who repeatedly turned back to Allah." It ends with the oldest story of arrogance: Iblis refusing to prostrate and saying, "I am better than him."

This guide explains Surah Sad section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Sad
- **Number of verses**: 88.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 38, in the twenty-third juz.
- **Name**: from the letter Sad that opens it.
- **Main themes**: tawhid against arrogance, the prophets who turned to Allah, judging with justice, reflecting on the Quran, and the origin of arrogance in Iblis.

## Verses 1–11: The Arrogance of Quraysh
﴿صٓۚ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ذِي ٱلذِّكۡرِ﴾ ﴿بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي عِزَّةٖ وَشِقَاقٖ﴾ (Quran 38:1-2)

**"Sad. By the Quran containing reminder. But those who disbelieve are in pride and dissension."**

﴿وَعَجِبُوٓاْ أَن جَآءَهُم مُّنذِرࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡۖ وَقَالَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا سَٰحِرࣱ كَذَّابٌ﴾ ﴿أَجَعَلَ ٱلۡأٓلِهَةَ إِلَٰهٗا وَٰحِدًاۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَشَيۡءٌ عُجَابࣱ﴾ (Quran 38:4-5)

They wondered that a warner had come from among them, and said: **"Has he made the gods into one God? Indeed, this is a curious thing."** Their leaders went out saying: **"Continue, and be patient over the defence of your gods."**

## Verses 12–16: Nations Before Them
﴿كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ وَعَادࣱ وَفِرۡعَوۡنُ ذُو ٱلۡأَوۡتَادِ﴾ ﴿وَثَمُودُ وَقَوۡمُ لُوطٖ وَأَصۡحَٰبُ لۡـَٔيۡكَةِۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابُ﴾ ﴿إِن كُلٌّ إِلَّا كَذَّبَ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَحَقَّ عِقَابِ﴾ (Quran 38:12-14)

The people of Nuh, 'Ad, Pharaoh, Thamud, the people of Lut and the companions of the thicket all denied their messengers, **"so My penalty was justified"**.

## Verses 17–26: Dawud the Repentant
﴿ٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَٱذۡكُرۡ عَبۡدَنَا دَاوُۥدَ ذَا ٱلۡأَيۡدِۖ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ﴾ (Quran 38:17)

**"Be patient over what they say and remember Our servant Dawud, the possessor of strength. Indeed, he was one who repeatedly turned back to Allah."**

﴿إِنَّا سَخَّرۡنَا ٱلۡجِبَالَ مَعَهُۥ يُسَبِّحۡنَ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ وَٱلۡإِشۡرَاقِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلطَّيۡرَ مَحۡشُورَةٗۖ كُلࣱّ لَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابࣱ﴾ ﴿وَشَدَدۡنَا مُلۡكَهُۥ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَفَصۡلَ ٱلۡخِطَابِ﴾ (Quran 38:18-20)

The mountains and birds glorified Allah with him, **"and We strengthened his kingdom and gave him wisdom and discernment in speech"**.

﴿۞وَهَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ نَبَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَصۡمِ إِذۡ تَسَوَّرُواْ ٱلۡمِحۡرَابَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ دَخَلُواْ عَلَىٰ دَاوُۥدَ فَفَزِعَ مِنۡهُمۡۖ قَالُواْ لَا تَخَفۡۖ خَصۡمَانِ بَغَىٰ بَعۡضُنَا عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تُشۡطِطۡ وَٱهۡدِنَآ إِلَىٰ سَوَآءِ ٱلصِّرَٰطِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَآ أَخِي لَهُۥ تِسۡعࣱ وَتِسۡعُونَ نَعۡجَةٗ وَلِيَ نَعۡجَةࣱ وَٰحِدَةࣱ فَقَالَ أَكۡفِلۡنِيهَا وَعَزَّنِي فِي ٱلۡخِطَابِ﴾ ﴿قَالَ لَقَدۡ ظَلَمَكَ بِسُؤَالِ نَعۡجَتِكَ إِلَىٰ نِعَاجِهِۦۖ وَإِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡخُلَطَآءِ لَيَبۡغِي بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٍ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَقَلِيلࣱ مَّا هُمۡۗ وَظَنَّ دَاوُۥدُ أَنَّمَا فَتَنَّـٰهُ فَٱسۡتَغۡفَرَ رَبَّهُۥ وَخَرَّۤ رَاكِعٗاۤ وَأَنَابَ۩﴾ (Quran 38:21-24)

**The two litigants**: two men climbed over the wall of his place of worship. One said: **"This is my brother; he has ninety-nine ewes, and I have one ewe; and he said: Entrust her to me, and he overpowered me in speech."** Dawud said: **"He has certainly wronged you in demanding your ewe in addition to his ewes."** Then Dawud realised that he was being tested, **"and he asked forgiveness of his Lord and fell down bowing and turned in repentance"**. The Quran does not detail the nature of the test, and the stories found in some books that attack the honour of Dawud are from the isra'iliyyat and must be rejected.

**Prostration**: in Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas said: "Sad is not one of the obligatory prostrations, but I saw the Prophet ﷺ prostrate in it."

﴿يَٰدَاوُۥدُ إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰكَ خَلِيفَةٗ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تَتَّبِعِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ فَيُضِلَّكَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَضِلُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ شَدِيدُۢ بِمَا نَسُواْ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡحِسَابِ﴾ (Quran 38:26)

**"O Dawud, indeed We have made you a successor upon the earth, so judge between the people in truth and do not follow your desire, as it will lead you astray from the way of Allah."** The rule for every judge, leader and parent: truth, not desire.

## Verses 27–29: Not Created in Vain
﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا بَٰطِلٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ ظَنُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۚ فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ (Quran 38:27)

**"And We did not create the heaven and the earth and that between them aimlessly."**

﴿أَمۡ نَجۡعَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ كَٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمۡ نَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ كَٱلۡفُجَّارِ﴾ (Quran 38:28)

**"Or should We treat those who believe and do righteous deeds like corrupters in the land?"**

﴿كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡكَ مُبَٰرَكࣱ لِّيَدَّبَّرُوٓاْ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَلِيَتَذَكَّرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ (Quran 38:29)

**"This is a blessed Book which We have revealed to you, that they might reflect upon its verses and that those of understanding would be reminded."** The purpose of revelation: reflection (*tadabbur*), not mere recitation.

## Verses 30–40: Sulayman and His Kingdom
﴿وَوَهَبۡنَا لِدَاوُۥدَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَۚ نِعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ﴾ (Quran 38:30)

**"And to Dawud We gave Sulayman. An excellent servant; indeed, he was one who repeatedly turned back."**

﴿إِذۡ عُرِضَ عَلَيۡهِ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ ٱلصَّـٰفِنَٰتُ ٱلۡجِيَادُ﴾ ﴿فَقَالَ إِنِّيٓ أَحۡبَبۡتُ حُبَّ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ عَن ذِكۡرِ رَبِّي حَتَّىٰ تَوَارَتۡ بِٱلۡحِجَابِ﴾ ﴿رُدُّوهَا عَلَيَّۖ فَطَفِقَ مَسۡحَۢا بِٱلسُّوقِ وَٱلۡأَعۡنَاقِ﴾ (Quran 38:31-33)

Fine, swift horses were displayed before him in the afternoon. Commentators differ on the details of what followed; the clear lesson is that his love of good things was linked to the remembrance of his Lord, and he turned back to Allah.

﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَهَبۡ لِي مُلۡكٗا لَّا يَنۢبَغِي لِأَحَدٖ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِيٓۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾ (Quran 38:35)

**"He said: My Lord, forgive me and grant me a kingdom such as will not belong to anyone after me. Indeed, You are the Bestower."** He began with forgiveness before asking for the world. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ caught a jinn who tried to disrupt his prayer and wanted to tie it to a pillar of the mosque, but he remembered the du'a of his brother Sulayman and released it.

﴿فَسَخَّرۡنَا لَهُ ٱلرِّيحَ تَجۡرِي بِأَمۡرِهِۦ رُخَآءً حَيۡثُ أَصَابَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلشَّيَٰطِينَ كُلَّ بَنَّآءٖ وَغَوَّاصٖ﴾ ﴿وَءَاخَرِينَ مُقَرَّنِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَصۡفَادِ﴾ ﴿هَٰذَا عَطَآؤُنَا فَٱمۡنُنۡ أَوۡ أَمۡسِكۡ بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ﴾ (Quran 38:36-39)

The wind ran gently at his command, and the devils built and dived for him. **"This is Our gift, so grant or withhold without account."**

## Verses 41–44: Ayyub's Patience
﴿وَٱذۡكُرۡ عَبۡدَنَآ أَيُّوبَ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ بِنُصۡبٖ وَعَذَابٍ﴾ (Quran 38:41)

**"And remember Our servant Ayyub, when he called to his Lord: Indeed, Satan has touched me with hardship and torment."** After long illness and loss, his complaint was only to Allah, with courtesy.

﴿ٱرۡكُضۡ بِرِجۡلِكَۖ هَٰذَا مُغۡتَسَلُۢ بَارِدࣱ وَشَرَابࣱ﴾ (Quran 38:42)

**"Strike the ground with your foot; this is a cool bath and drink."** Healing came from a spring.

﴿وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ أَهۡلَهُۥ وَمِثۡلَهُم مَّعَهُمۡ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنَّا وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ (Quran 38:43)

His family was given back to him and the like of them with them, **"as mercy from Us and a reminder for those of understanding"**.

﴿وَخُذۡ بِيَدِكَ ضِغۡثٗا فَٱضۡرِب بِّهِۦ وَلَا تَحۡنَثۡۗ إِنَّا وَجَدۡنَٰهُ صَابِرٗاۚ نِّعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابࣱ﴾ (Quran 38:44)

**"Indeed, We found him patient, an excellent servant. Indeed, he was one repeatedly turning back."** Patience is what made him excellent.

## Verses 45–64: Other Prophets, Paradise and the Fire
﴿وَٱذۡكُرۡ عِبَٰدَنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ أُوْلِي ٱلۡأَيۡدِي وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّآ أَخۡلَصۡنَٰهُم بِخَالِصَةٖ ذِكۡرَى ٱلدَّارِ﴾ (Quran 38:45-46)

**"And remember Our servants Ibrahim, Ishaq and Ya'qub, those of strength and vision."** **"Indeed, We chose them for an exclusive quality: remembrance of the home of the hereafter."**

﴿هَٰذَا ذِكۡرࣱۚ وَإِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ لَحُسۡنَ مَـَٔابٖ﴾ ﴿جَنَّـٰتِ عَدۡنٖ مُّفَتَّحَةٗ لَّهُمُ ٱلۡأَبۡوَٰبُ﴾ (Quran 38:49-50)

**"This is a reminder. And indeed, for the righteous is a good place of return: gardens of perpetual residence, whose doors will be opened to them."**

## Verses 65–70: The Great News
﴿قُلۡ هُوَ نَبَؤٌاْ عَظِيمٌ﴾ ﴿أَنتُمۡ عَنۡهُ مُعۡرِضُونَ﴾ (Quran 38:67-68)

**"Say: It is great news from which you turn away."**

## Verses 71–85: The Arrogance of Iblis
﴿إِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي خَٰلِقُۢ بَشَرٗا مِّن طِينٖ﴾ ﴿فَإِذَا سَوَّيۡتُهُۥ وَنَفَخۡتُ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِي فَقَعُواْ لَهُۥ سَٰجِدِينَ﴾ (Quran 38:71-72)

**"When your Lord said to the angels: Indeed, I am going to create a human being from clay. So when I have proportioned him and breathed into him of My soul, then fall down to him in prostration."**

﴿قَالَ يَـٰٓإِبۡلِيسُ مَا مَنَعَكَ أَن تَسۡجُدَ لِمَا خَلَقۡتُ بِيَدَيَّۖ أَسۡتَكۡبَرۡتَ أَمۡ كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡعَالِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ أَنَا۠ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡهُ خَلَقۡتَنِي مِن نَّارٖ وَخَلَقۡتَهُۥ مِن طِينٖ﴾ (Quran 38:75-76)

**"O Iblis, what prevented you from prostrating to that which I created with My hands?"** **"He said: I am better than him. You created me from fire and created him from clay."** The first logic of racism and pride.

﴿قَالَ فَبِعِزَّتِكَ لَأُغۡوِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا عِبَادَكَ مِنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ﴾ (Quran 38:82-83)

**"He said: By Your might, I will surely mislead them all, except, among them, Your chosen servants."** The protection from Satan is sincerity.

## Verses 86–88: Not a Pretender
﴿قُلۡ مَآ أَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٖ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُتَكَلِّفِينَ﴾ (Quran 38:86)

**"Say: I do not ask you for the Quran any payment, and I am not of the pretenders."** No reward sought, and no burdening others with what Allah did not command.

﴿وَلَتَعۡلَمُنَّ نَبَأَهُۥ بَعۡدَ حِينِۭ﴾ (Quran 38:88)

**"And you will surely know its information after a time."**

## Word Meanings
- **Dhikr**: reminder, remembrance.
- **'Izzah**: pride.
- **Shiqaq**: dissension.
- **Awwab**: one who repeatedly turns back to Allah.
- **Fasl al-khitab**: discernment in speech.
- **Na'jah**: ewe.
- **Khalifah**: successor.
- **Tadabbur**: reflection.
- **As-Safinat al-jiyad**: fine, swift horses.
- **Rukhaa'**: gently.
- **Al-Mukhlasin**: chosen, sincere servants.
- **Al-Mutakallifin**: pretenders.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Awwab" repeated**: for Dawud, Sulayman and Ayyub.
2. **Ninety-nine and one**: the injustice of greed shown in numbers.
3. **"A blessed Book... that they might reflect"**: the purpose of the Quran.
4. **"I am better than him"**: arrogance in four words.
5. **Forgiveness before kingdom**: the etiquette of Sulayman's du'a.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi mukhaffaf**: in *Sad* (verse 1), the letter name is stretched six counts.
- **Izhar**: in *kitabun anzalnahu* (verse 29), the tanween before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *anzalnahu* (verse 29), the noon sakinah is hidden before the zay.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *khalifatan fi* (verse 26), the tanween is hidden before the fa'.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *naj'alu* (verse 28), the jim sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'a* (verse 27).

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah As-Saffat before it ends with "Exalted is your Lord... and praise to Allah, Lord of the worlds", and Sad opens with the Quran containing reminder. Sad ends with "it is only a reminder to the worlds", and Surah Az-Zumar after it opens with the revelation of the Book from Allah.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Be an awwab**: return to Allah quickly after every slip.
2. **Judge with truth, not desire**, in every role of responsibility.
3. **Reflect on the Quran**, not just recite it.
4. **Ask forgiveness before asking for the world.**
5. **Complain only to Allah**, with courtesy, as Ayyub did.
6. **Beware "I am better than him"** in any form.
7. **Seek sincerity**: it is the shield against Satan.
8. **Do not burden yourself or others** with pretence.

## How to Memorise Surah Sad
- **Week 1**: verses 1–29.
- **Week 2**: verses 30–64.
- **Week 3**: verses 65–88, with full review.

## Teaching Children
- **Act out the case of the ewes** and ask what a fair judge would say.
- **Tell the story of Ayyub** and his patience.
- **Discuss "I am better than him"**: why is it wrong to look down on anyone?

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is there a prostration in Surah Sad?
Yes, at verse 24; Ibn 'Abbas said it is not obligatory, but he saw the Prophet ﷺ prostrate in it, as in al-Bukhari.

### What was the test of Dawud?
The Quran mentions the case of the two litigants and his repentance without detailing more; stories that insult him are isra'iliyyat and rejected.

### What did Sulayman ask for?
Forgiveness, then a kingdom that would not belong to anyone after him.

### What is the lesson of Ayyub?
Patience in long trial, complaining only to Allah, and His reward for the patient.

### Why did Iblis refuse to prostrate?
Out of arrogance, claiming he was better because he was made of fire.

### Who is protected from Satan?
Allah's sincere, chosen servants, as verse 83 says.

### How many verses are in Surah Sad?
88 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Sad sets two attitudes side by side: the prophets who, whatever their kingdom or illness, kept turning back to Allah, and Iblis, who said "I am better" and never turned back. Greatness is not the absence of mistakes; it is quick return. Be patient like Ayyub, just like Dawud, humble like Sulayman, and sincere, for Satan cannot reach the sincere.
