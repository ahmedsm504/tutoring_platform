**Surah Al-Ma'un** (7 ayat) shows that true faith appears in **how you treat people** and **how you pray**.

## Denial in action
﴿أَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿فَذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِي يَدُعُّ ٱلۡيَتِيمَ﴾ ﴿وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ﴾
- **Denying the Recompense** is not only a belief; it shows in pushing away orphans and ignoring the poor.

## Warning to those who pray
﴿فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُصَلِّينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَن صَلَاتِهِمۡ سَاهُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ يُرَآءُونَ﴾ ﴿وَيَمۡنَعُونَ ٱلۡمَاعُونَ﴾
| Fault | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Heedless of their prayer | Delaying it carelessly or neglecting it |
| Showing off | Praying to be seen |
| Withholding *al-māʿūn* | Refusing small help, like lending household items |
- **Note**: it says *ʿan* ṣalātihim (heedless **of** their prayer), not *fī* (in it). Scholars noted that occasional lapses of focus **in** prayer are not meant here.
- **Recitation tip**: do not stop on ﴿فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُصَلِّينَ﴾, but join it with what follows.

## Lessons
- **Prayer and kindness** go together.
- **Small help** matters to Allah.
