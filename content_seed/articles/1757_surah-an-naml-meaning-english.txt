**Surah An-Naml** (The Ant, 93 ayat) is named after a tiny creature whose words the Quran records.

## The ant's warning
- As Sulayman's army approached, an ant told its colony to enter their dwellings so they would not be crushed, adding that the army **would not even notice**.
- Sulayman smiled and asked Allah to help him be grateful.

## The hoopoe and Sheba
- The hoopoe returned with news of a queen ruling Sheba whose people worshipped the sun.
- Sulayman sent a letter beginning with *Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim*.

## The throne brought in a blink
﴿قَالَ ٱلَّذِي عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَنَا۠ ءَاتِيكَ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن يَرۡتَدَّ إِلَيۡكَ طَرۡفُكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهُ مُسۡتَقِرًّا عِندَهُۥ قَالَ هَٰذَا مِن فَضۡلِ رَبِّي لِيَبۡلُوَنِيٓ ءَأَشۡكُرُ أَمۡ أَكۡفُرُۖ وَمَن شَكَرَ فَإِنَّمَا يَشۡكُرُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ رَبِّي غَنِيࣱّ كَرِيمࣱ﴾
- Sulayman's first reaction to a miracle was **gratitude**, not pride.
- The Queen eventually submitted *with Sulayman to Allah, Lord of the worlds*.

## Questions that leave no answer but Allah
﴿أَمَّن يُجِيبُ ٱلۡمُضۡطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكۡشِفُ ٱلسُّوٓءَ وَيَجۡعَلُكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾

## Lessons
- **Care for others**, like the ant.
- **Every blessing is a test** of gratitude.
- **Call on Allah when desperate**: He answers the one in need.
