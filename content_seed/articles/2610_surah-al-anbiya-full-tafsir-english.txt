Surah Al-Anbiya' (The Prophets) opens with an alarm: "The time of their account has approached for the people, while they are in heedlessness turning away." Then it leads the reader through a procession of prophets: Ibrahim breaking the idols and thrown into a fire that Allah made "coolness and safety", Lut, Nuh, Dawud and Sulayman, Ayyub crying out "Harm has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful", Yunus calling from the darkness of the whale, Zakariyya asking for a child in old age, and Maryam. Each one called on Allah, and each was answered. The surah closes with the verse that sums up the Prophet's ﷺ mission: "And We have not sent you except as a mercy to the worlds."

This guide explains Surah Al-Anbiya' section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Anbiya'
- **Number of verses**: 112.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 21, the first surah of the seventeenth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Anbiya' means "the Prophets"; sixteen prophets are mentioned in it.
- **Main themes**: the nearness of the reckoning, tawhid, the unity of the prophets' message, du'a and its answer, and mercy.

## Verses 1–15: Heedlessness and Mockery
﴿ٱقۡتَرَبَ لِلنَّاسِ حِسَابُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ فِي غَفۡلَةٖ مُّعۡرِضُونَ﴾ ﴿مَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن ذِكۡرٖ مِّن رَّبِّهِم مُّحۡدَثٍ إِلَّا ٱسۡتَمَعُوهُ وَهُمۡ يَلۡعَبُونَ﴾ ﴿لَاهِيَةٗ قُلُوبُهُمۡۗ وَأَسَرُّواْ ٱلنَّجۡوَى ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ هَلۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡۖ أَفَتَأۡتُونَ ٱلسِّحۡرَ وَأَنتُمۡ تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:1-3)

**"The time of their account has approached for the people, while they are in heedlessness turning away."** Two problems: heedlessness inside and turning away outside. **"No mention comes to them anew from their Lord except that they listen to it while they are at play, with their hearts distracted."**

﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ فَسۡـَٔلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلذِّكۡرِ إِن كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:7)

**"So ask the people of the message if you do not know."** A general principle: whoever does not know should ask people of knowledge.

﴿لَقَدۡ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ كِتَٰبٗا فِيهِ ذِكۡرُكُمۡۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:10)

**"We have certainly sent down to you a Book in which is your mention."** Your honour and remembrance are in the Quran.

## Verses 16–29: Creation Has Purpose
﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ﴾ ﴿لَوۡ أَرَدۡنَآ أَن نَّتَّخِذَ لَهۡوٗا لَّٱتَّخَذۡنَٰهُ مِن لَّدُنَّآ إِن كُنَّا فَٰعِلِينَ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ نَقۡذِفُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ عَلَى ٱلۡبَٰطِلِ فَيَدۡمَغُهُۥ فَإِذَا هُوَ زَاهِقࣱۚ وَلَكُمُ ٱلۡوَيۡلُ مِمَّا تَصِفُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:16-18)

**"And We did not create the heaven and earth and that between them in play."** **"Rather, We dash the truth upon falsehood, and it destroys it, and thereupon it departs."**

﴿لَوۡ كَانَ فِيهِمَآ ءَالِهَةٌ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ لَفَسَدَتَاۚ فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:22)

**"Had there been within the heavens and earth gods besides Allah, they both would have been ruined."** The order of the universe proves one Lord.

﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ مِن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا نُوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ﴾ (Quran 21:25)

**"And We sent not before you any messenger except that We revealed to him that there is no deity except Me, so worship Me."** The message of every prophet in one sentence.

## Verses 30–35: Signs and Death
﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ كَانَتَا رَتۡقٗا فَفَتَقۡنَٰهُمَاۖ وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمَآءِ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ حَيٍّۚ أَفَلَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:30)

**"Have those who disbelieved not considered that the heavens and the earth were a joined entity, and We separated them, and made from water every living thing? Then will they not believe?"**

﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ وَٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلࣱّ فِي فَلَكٖ يَسۡبَحُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:33)

**"And it is He who created the night and the day and the sun and the moon; all in an orbit are swimming."**

﴿وَمَا جَعَلۡنَا لِبَشَرٖ مِّن قَبۡلِكَ ٱلۡخُلۡدَۖ أَفَإِيْن مِّتَّ فَهُمُ ٱلۡخَٰلِدُونَ﴾ ﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۗ وَنَبۡلُوكُم بِٱلشَّرِّ وَٱلۡخَيۡرِ فِتۡنَةٗۖ وَإِلَيۡنَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:34-35)

**"And We did not grant to any man before you eternity."** **"Every soul will taste death. And We test you with evil and with good as trial."** Good things are tests too.

## Verses 36–50: Man's Haste and the Scales
﴿خُلِقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مِنۡ عَجَلٖۚ سَأُوْرِيكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِي فَلَا تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونِ﴾ (Quran 21:37)

**"Man was created of haste."** We want everything now.

﴿وَنَضَعُ ٱلۡمَوَٰزِينَ ٱلۡقِسۡطَ لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فَلَا تُظۡلَمُ نَفۡسࣱ شَيۡـٔٗاۖ وَإِن كَانَ مِثۡقَالَ حَبَّةٖ مِّنۡ خَرۡدَلٍ أَتَيۡنَا بِهَاۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِنَا حَٰسِبِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:47)

**"And We place the scales of justice for the Day of Resurrection, so no soul will be treated unjustly at all. And if there is even the weight of a mustard seed, We will bring it forth."**

## Verses 51–73: Ibrahim
﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ رُشۡدَهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُ وَكُنَّا بِهِۦ عَٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوۡمِهِۦ مَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلتَّمَاثِيلُ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنتُمۡ لَهَا عَٰكِفُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ وَجَدۡنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا لَهَا عَٰبِدِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ لَقَدۡ كُنتُمۡ أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُمۡ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ ﴿قَالُوٓاْ أَجِئۡتَنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّ أَمۡ أَنتَ مِنَ ٱللَّـٰعِبِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ بَل رَّبُّكُمۡ رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَهُنَّ وَأَنَا۠ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكُم مِّنَ ٱلشَّـٰهِدِينَ﴾ ﴿وَتَٱللَّهِ لَأَكِيدَنَّ أَصۡنَٰمَكُم بَعۡدَ أَن تُوَلُّواْ مُدۡبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَجَعَلَهُمۡ جُذَٰذًا إِلَّا كَبِيرٗا لَّهُمۡ لَعَلَّهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:51-58)

Ibrahim asked his father and people: **"What are these statues to which you are devoted?"** They answered: "We found our fathers worshipping them." He broke the idols except the biggest.

﴿قَالُواْ مَن فَعَلَ هَٰذَا بِـَٔالِهَتِنَآ إِنَّهُۥ لَمِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ سَمِعۡنَا فَتٗى يَذۡكُرُهُمۡ يُقَالُ لَهُۥٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمُ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ فَأۡتُواْ بِهِۦ عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡيُنِ ٱلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَشۡهَدُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُوٓاْ ءَأَنتَ فَعَلۡتَ هَٰذَا بِـَٔالِهَتِنَا يَـٰٓإِبۡرَٰهِيمُ﴾ ﴿قَالَ بَلۡ فَعَلَهُۥ كَبِيرُهُمۡ هَٰذَا فَسۡـَٔلُوهُمۡ إِن كَانُواْ يَنطِقُونَ﴾ ﴿فَرَجَعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّكُمۡ أَنتُمُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ نُكِسُواْ عَلَىٰ رُءُوسِهِمۡ لَقَدۡ عَلِمۡتَ مَا هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ يَنطِقُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ أَفَتَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَنفَعُكُمۡ شَيۡـٔٗا وَلَا يَضُرُّكُمۡ﴾ ﴿أُفّٖ لَّكُمۡ وَلِمَا تَعۡبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:59-67)

They asked who did this. He said: **"Rather, this, the largest of them, did it, so ask them, if they should be able to speak."** They realised: **"You have already known that these do not speak!"** He replied: **"Then do you worship instead of Allah that which does not benefit you at all or harm you?"**

﴿قَالُواْ حَرِّقُوهُ وَٱنصُرُوٓاْ ءَالِهَتَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ فَٰعِلِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡنَا يَٰنَارُ كُونِي بَرۡدٗا وَسَلَٰمًا عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾ ﴿وَأَرَادُواْ بِهِۦ كَيۡدٗا فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡأَخۡسَرِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:68-70)

**"Burn him and support your gods."** **"We said: O fire, be coolness and safety upon Ibrahim."** In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas said: "*Hasbuna Allah wa ni'ma al-wakil* (Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs) was said by Ibrahim when he was thrown into the fire."

﴿وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَلُوطًا إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلَّتِي بَٰرَكۡنَا فِيهَا لِلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ إِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ نَافِلَةٗۖ وَكُلّٗا جَعَلۡنَا صَٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ أَئِمَّةٗ يَهۡدُونَ بِأَمۡرِنَا وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمۡ فِعۡلَ ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَإِقَامَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَإِيتَآءَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةِۖ وَكَانُواْ لَنَا عَٰبِدِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:71-73)

Allah saved him and Lut, gave him Ishaq and Ya'qub, and made them **"leaders guiding by Our command"**.

## Verses 74–91: The Du'as That Were Answered
﴿وَلُوطًا ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ حُكۡمٗا وَعِلۡمٗا وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ ٱلَّتِي كَانَت تَّعۡمَلُ ٱلۡخَبَـٰٓئِثَۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمَ سَوۡءٖ فَٰسِقِينَ﴾ ﴿وَأَدۡخَلۡنَٰهُ فِي رَحۡمَتِنَآۖ إِنَّهُۥ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿وَنُوحًا إِذۡ نَادَىٰ مِن قَبۡلُ فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ فَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡكَرۡبِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَنَصَرۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمَ سَوۡءٖ فَأَغۡرَقۡنَٰهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:74-77)

**Lut** was saved from a town of evil deeds; **Nuh** called and was answered and saved from the great affliction.

﴿وَدَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ إِذۡ يَحۡكُمَانِ فِي ٱلۡحَرۡثِ إِذۡ نَفَشَتۡ فِيهِ غَنَمُ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ وَكُنَّا لِحُكۡمِهِمۡ شَٰهِدِينَ﴾ ﴿فَفَهَّمۡنَٰهَا سُلَيۡمَٰنَۚ وَكُلًّا ءَاتَيۡنَا حُكۡمٗا وَعِلۡمٗاۚ وَسَخَّرۡنَا مَعَ دَاوُۥدَ ٱلۡجِبَالَ يُسَبِّحۡنَ وَٱلطَّيۡرَۚ وَكُنَّا فَٰعِلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَعَلَّمۡنَٰهُ صَنۡعَةَ لَبُوسٖ لَّكُمۡ لِتُحۡصِنَكُم مِّنۢ بَأۡسِكُمۡۖ فَهَلۡ أَنتُمۡ شَٰكِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلِسُلَيۡمَٰنَ ٱلرِّيحَ عَاصِفَةٗ تَجۡرِي بِأَمۡرِهِۦٓ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلَّتِي بَٰرَكۡنَا فِيهَاۚ وَكُنَّا بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ مَن يَغُوصُونَ لَهُۥ وَيَعۡمَلُونَ عَمَلٗا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَۖ وَكُنَّا لَهُمۡ حَٰفِظِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:78-82)

**Dawud and Sulayman** judged a case about sheep that grazed in a field at night. **"We gave understanding of the case to Sulayman, and to each We gave judgement and knowledge."** Both were praised, though Sulayman's judgement was better. The mountains and birds glorified Allah with Dawud; Sulayman commanded the wind.

﴿۞وَأَيُّوبَ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ ٱلضُّرُّ وَأَنتَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ فَكَشَفۡنَا مَا بِهِۦ مِن ضُرّٖۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ أَهۡلَهُۥ وَمِثۡلَهُم مَّعَهُمۡ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَا وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡعَٰبِدِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:83-84)

**Ayyub**: **"And Ayyub, when he called to his Lord: Indeed, harm has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful."** Notice his courtesy: he did not demand; he described his state and Allah's mercy. **"So We responded to him and removed what afflicted him of harm, and We gave him his family and the like thereof with them as mercy from Us and a reminder for the worshippers."**

﴿وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَإِدۡرِيسَ وَذَا ٱلۡكِفۡلِۖ كُلࣱّ مِّنَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَأَدۡخَلۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي رَحۡمَتِنَآۖ إِنَّهُم مِّنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:85-86)

**Isma'il, Idris and Dhul-Kifl**: all among the patient.

﴿وَذَا ٱلنُّونِ إِذ ذَّهَبَ مُغَٰضِبٗا فَظَنَّ أَن لَّن نَّقۡدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ فَنَادَىٰ فِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ أَن لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَمِّۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُـۨجِي ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:87-88)

**Yunus (Dhun-Nun)**: **"He called out within the darknesses: There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers."** Three darknesses: the night, the sea and the whale. **"So We responded to him and saved him from the distress. And thus do We save the believers."** The promise extends to every believer who calls with this du'a. At-Tirmidhi reported that the Prophet ﷺ said no Muslim calls with it for anything except that Allah answers him; al-Albani graded it authentic.

﴿وَزَكَرِيَّآ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥ رَبِّ لَا تَذَرۡنِي فَرۡدٗا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥ يَحۡيَىٰ وَأَصۡلَحۡنَا لَهُۥ زَوۡجَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَيَدۡعُونَنَا رَغَبٗا وَرَهَبٗاۖ وَكَانُواْ لَنَا خَٰشِعِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:89-90)

**Zakariyya**: **"My Lord, do not leave me alone, while You are the best of inheritors."** He was given Yahya. **The secret of their answered prayers**: **"Indeed, they used to hasten to good deeds and supplicate Us in hope and fear, and they were to Us humbly submissive."**

﴿وَٱلَّتِيٓ أَحۡصَنَتۡ فَرۡجَهَا فَنَفَخۡنَا فِيهَا مِن رُّوحِنَا وَجَعَلۡنَٰهَا وَٱبۡنَهَآ ءَايَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:91)

**Maryam**, who guarded her chastity, and her son were made a sign for the worlds.

## Verses 92–112: One Nation and a Mercy to the Worlds
﴿إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أُمَّتُكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ﴾ (Quran 21:92)

**"Indeed, this, your religion, is one religion, and I am your Lord, so worship Me."**

﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فُتِحَتۡ يَأۡجُوجُ وَمَأۡجُوجُ وَهُم مِّن كُلِّ حَدَبٖ يَنسِلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱقۡتَرَبَ ٱلۡوَعۡدُ ٱلۡحَقُّ فَإِذَا هِيَ شَٰخِصَةٌ أَبۡصَٰرُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَا قَدۡ كُنَّا فِي غَفۡلَةٖ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا بَلۡ كُنَّا ظَٰلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:96-97)

**Ya'juj and Ma'juj** are released, and the true promise approaches.

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقَتۡ لَهُم مِّنَّا ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰٓ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ عَنۡهَا مُبۡعَدُونَ﴾ ﴿لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ حَسِيسَهَاۖ وَهُمۡ فِي مَا ٱشۡتَهَتۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ خَٰلِدُونَ﴾ ﴿لَا يَحۡزُنُهُمُ ٱلۡفَزَعُ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرُ وَتَتَلَقَّىٰهُمُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ هَٰذَا يَوۡمُكُمُ ٱلَّذِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:101-103)

**"Indeed, those for whom the best has preceded from Us, they are far removed from it (Hell)."** **"They will not be grieved by the greatest terror."**

﴿يَوۡمَ نَطۡوِي ٱلسَّمَآءَ كَطَيِّ ٱلسِّجِلِّ لِلۡكُتُبِۚ كَمَا بَدَأۡنَآ أَوَّلَ خَلۡقٖ نُّعِيدُهُۥۚ وَعۡدًا عَلَيۡنَآۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا فَٰعِلِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:104)

**"The Day when We will fold the heaven like the folding of a scroll for records. As We began the first creation, We will repeat it."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "You will be gathered barefoot, naked and uncircumcised," and recited this verse.

﴿وَلَقَدۡ كَتَبۡنَا فِي ٱلزَّبُورِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱلذِّكۡرِ أَنَّ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ يَرِثُهَا عِبَادِيَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:105)

**"And We have already written in the scripture after the reminder that the land is inherited by My righteous servants."**

﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:107)

**"And We have not sent you except as a mercy to the worlds."** Mercy to humans, jinn, animals and all creation, through the message he brought.

## Word Meanings
- **Iqtaraba**: has approached.
- **Ghaflah**: heedlessness.
- **Lahiyah**: distracted.
- **Ahl adh-dhikr**: people of the message, people of knowledge.
- **Yadmaghuhu**: destroys it.
- **Ratqan**: joined.
- **Fatqnahuma**: We separated them.
- **Falak**: orbit.
- **'Ajal**: haste.
- **Al-Qist**: justice.
- **Juzadhan**: pieces.
- **Bardan wa salaman**: coolness and safety.
- **Nafashat**: grazed at night.
- **Dhun-Nun**: the man of the whale, Yunus.
- **Raghaban wa rahaban**: in hope and fear.
- **As-Sijill**: scroll.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"The reckoning has approached... while they are heedless"**: urgency against carelessness.
2. **"Ask them, if they can speak"**: Ibrahim's logic exposing idolatry.
3. **"Coolness and safety"**: fire commanded to change its nature.
4. **Ayyub's du'a**: asking by describing need and mercy.
5. **"And thus do We save the believers"**: a personal promise from one prophet's story.
6. **"A mercy to the worlds"**: the whole mission in five words.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *iqtaraba* (verse 1), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly; and in *fa-yadmaghuhu* (verse 18), the dal sakinah.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *bardan wa salaman* (verse 69) and *raghaban wa rahaban* (verse 90), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'a* (verse 16).
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna hadhihi* (verse 92), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Taha before it ended with "Each is waiting, so wait", and Al-Anbiya' opens with "The reckoning has approached". Surah Al-Hajj after it opens with "O mankind, fear your Lord. Indeed, the convulsion of the Hour is a terrible thing."

## The Du'as of the Prophets
1. **Ibrahim**: *Hasbuna Allah wa ni'ma al-wakil.*
2. **Ayyub**: *Anni massaniya ad-durru wa anta arham ar-rahimin.*
3. **Yunus**: *La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min az-zalimin.*
4. **Zakariyya**: *Rabbi la tadharni fardan wa anta khayr al-warithin.*

Memorise them and use them in your own needs.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Wake up from heedlessness.**
2. **Ask people of knowledge.**
3. **See good and bad as tests.**
4. **Do not be hasty.**
5. **Rely on Allah as Ibrahim did.**
6. **Call on Allah with the prophets' du'as.**
7. **Hasten to good deeds**, pray with hope and fear, and be humble.
8. **Be a mercy to those around you.**

- **Week 4**: verses 92–112.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them the story of Ibrahim and the fire.**
- **Teach them the du'as of Ayyub and Yunus.**
- **Explain "a mercy to the worlds"** with stories of the Prophet's ﷺ kindness to animals and people.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How many prophets are mentioned in Surah Al-Anbiya'?
Sixteen, including Musa, Harun, Ibrahim, Lut, Ishaq, Ya'qub, Nuh, Dawud, Sulayman, Ayyub, Isma'il, Idris, Dhul-Kifl, Yunus, Zakariyya and Yahya, as well as Maryam and 'Isa.

### What did Ibrahim say when thrown into the fire?
"Hasbuna Allah wa ni'ma al-wakil", as Ibn 'Abbas reported in Sahih al-Bukhari.

### What is the du'a of Yunus?
"La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min az-zalimin", and Allah promised to save the believers who call with it.

### What does "We made from water every living thing" mean?
Water is the basis of all life Allah created.

### What does "Man was created of haste" mean?
Human beings are naturally impatient and want things quickly.

### What does "a mercy to the worlds" mean?
The Prophet ﷺ and his message are a mercy to all creation.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Anbiya'?
112 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Anbiya' shakes you out of heedlessness and then walks you through the lives of the prophets, each in distress, each calling on Allah, and each answered. Their secret was simple: they hastened to good, called on Allah in hope and fear, and were humble before Him. Learn their du'as, follow their way, and thank Allah for the Prophet ﷺ who was sent as a mercy to the worlds.
