Juz 11 begins with ﴿۞إِنَّمَا ٱلسَّبِيلُ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَـٔۡذِنُونَكَ وَهُمۡ أَغۡنِيَآءُۚ﴾, "The blame is only on those who ask your permission while they are rich." Many memorizers call it **Juz Ya'tadhirun**, after the next verse: ﴿يَعۡتَذِرُونَ إِلَيۡكُمۡ إِذَا رَجَعۡتُمۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡۚ﴾, "They will make excuses to you when you return to them." It runs from **At-Tawbah 9:93 to Hud 11:5**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 201 to 221**.

The juz closes **Surah At-Tawbah** with some of its most beautiful passages: the forgiveness of the three who stayed behind, the bargain between Allah and the believers, and the ending that describes the Prophet's mercy towards his nation. It then contains **all of Surah Yunus**, a Makki surah that addresses mind and heart together, and the first five verses of **Surah Hud**.

## The big idea

If At-Tawbah in Juz 10 exposed the hypocrites, this juz **opens the door of repentance wide**: for those who mixed good deeds with bad, for the three for whom the earth became narrow, and even for the people of Yunus, who believed before punishment fell and so benefited from their faith. Surah Yunus then establishes proof of monotheism and resurrection and shows two destinies: the allies of Allah, who have no fear, and Pharaoh, who believed when belief no longer helped.

The message: **the door back to Allah is open, but it has a time limit.**

## Part one: the close of Surah At-Tawbah (93-129)

### 1. Excuses of the rich and the Bedouins (93-99)
Allah makes clear that the blame lies on the able rich who were content to stay behind with those left at home. Then: "They will make excuses to you when you return to them", swearing so you will be pleased with them. Then the Bedouins: some are the most intense in disbelief and hypocrisy, some regard what they spend as a loss, and some believe in Allah and the Last Day and regard what they spend as a means of nearness to Allah.

**Lesson**: false excuses do not change reality, and the same spending can be worship or a burden depending on the intention.

### 2. The groups within the Muslim community (100-106)
A precise classification of people after Tabuk:
- **The forerunners**: ﴿وَٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلۡأَنصَارِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُم بِإِحۡسَٰنٖ رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُ﴾, "The first forerunners among the Muhajirun and the Ansar and those who followed them in excellence: Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him." A divine testimony for the Companions and those who follow them well.
- **The hardened hypocrites**, around Madinah and within it: "they have persisted in hypocrisy; you do not know them, We know them."
- **The mixed**: ﴿وَءَاخَرُونَ ٱعۡتَرَفُواْ بِذُنُوبِهِمۡ خَلَطُواْ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا وَءَاخَرَ سَيِّئًا عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَتُوبَ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ﴾, "And others who acknowledged their sins; they mixed a good deed with a bad one. Perhaps Allah will turn to them in forgiveness." Then the command: ﴿خُذۡ مِنۡ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ صَدَقَةٗ تُطَهِّرُهُمۡ وَتُزَكِّيهِم بِهَا وَصَلِّ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ﴾, "Take from their wealth a charity to purify them and cause them to grow, and pray for them."
- **Those deferred** to Allah's decision: He will either punish them or forgive them.

In the middle comes a rule for action: ﴿وَقُلِ ٱعۡمَلُواْ فَسَيَرَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَلَكُمۡ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَۖ﴾, "And say: work, for Allah will see your deeds, and so will His Messenger and the believers."

**Reflect**: most people are neither angels nor devils; they mix. The Quran opens the door of hope for them, "perhaps Allah will turn to them", and points them to charity as purification.

### 3. The mosque of harm and the mosque of taqwa (107-110)
The hypocrites built a mosque "for harm, disbelief and division among the believers", and the Prophet ﷺ was forbidden to pray in it: ﴿لَا تَقُمۡ فِيهِ أَبَدٗاۚ لَّمَسۡجِدٌ أُسِّسَ عَلَى ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ مِنۡ أَوَّلِ يَوۡمٍ أَحَقُّ أَن تَقُومَ فِيهِۚ فِيهِ رِجَالࣱ يُحِبُّونَ أَن يَتَطَهَّرُواْۚ﴾, "Never stand in it. A mosque founded on taqwa from the first day is more worthy for you to stand in; in it are men who love to purify themselves." Then the principle of building: is one who founds his building on fear of Allah and His pleasure better, or one who founds it on the edge of a crumbling cliff?

**Lesson**: what counts is not the form of a deed but its foundation and intention. A building can look like a mosque and in reality be a means of harm.

### 4. The profitable bargain (111-112)
﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱشۡتَرَىٰ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلَهُم بِأَنَّ لَهُمُ ٱلۡجَنَّةَۚ﴾, "Allah has purchased from the believers their lives and their wealth in exchange for Paradise", then "so rejoice in the bargain you have made." The buyer is Allah, the price is Paradise, the goods are the self and wealth. Then the qualities of those in this bargain: ﴿ٱلتَّـٰٓئِبُونَ ٱلۡعَٰبِدُونَ ٱلۡحَٰمِدُونَ ٱلسَّـٰٓئِحُونَ ٱلرَّـٰكِعُونَ ٱلسَّـٰجِدُونَ ٱلۡأٓمِرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَٱلنَّاهُونَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ﴾, "those who repent, worship, praise, travel for Allah's sake, bow, prostrate, enjoin good and forbid wrong." Notice the first quality is repentance.

### 5. Seeking forgiveness for idolaters, and Ibrahim's prayer for his father (113-116)
It is not for the Prophet and the believers to seek forgiveness for idolaters once it is clear they are people of Hell. Then the reason Ibrahim asked forgiveness for his father: a promise he had made him; when it became clear his father was an enemy of Allah, he disassociated from him. ﴿إِنَّ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ لَأَوَّـٰهٌ حَلِيمࣱ﴾, "Ibrahim was tender-hearted and forbearing."

### 6. Forgiveness for the three (117-119)
﴿لَّقَد تَّابَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلۡأَنصَارِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُ فِي سَاعَةِ ٱلۡعُسۡرَةِ﴾, "Allah has turned in mercy to the Prophet, the Muhajirun and the Ansar who followed him in the hour of hardship", then ﴿وَعَلَى ٱلثَّلَٰثَةِ ٱلَّذِينَ خُلِّفُواْ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا ضَاقَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ بِمَا رَحُبَتۡ وَضَاقَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَن لَّا مَلۡجَأَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّآ إِلَيۡهِ ثُمَّ تَابَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ لِيَتُوبُوٓاْۚ﴾, "and to the three who were left behind, until the earth, vast as it is, closed in on them, their own souls closed in on them, and they knew there was no refuge from Allah except in Him. Then He turned to them so that they might repent."

The three were Ka'b ibn Malik, Murarah ibn ar-Rabi' and Hilal ibn Umayyah. Their story is in Al-Bukhari and Muslim, told by Ka'b himself: they stayed behind from Tabuk without excuse; when the Prophet ﷺ returned they told him the truth instead of lying as the hypocrites did; people were told to cut off contact with them for fifty nights, until their forgiveness was revealed. Then the conclusion: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَكُونُواْ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ﴾, "O you who believe, fear Allah and be with the truthful."

**The great lesson**: the hypocrites lied and escaped outwardly but were ruined in reality; the three told the truth, suffered fifty nights, and then their forgiveness was revealed as Quran recited forever. Truth is the road to safety even when it hurts.

### 7. Seeking knowledge and going out (120-127)
- No thirst, fatigue or hunger reaches those who strive except that a good deed is recorded for them.
- ﴿۞وَمَا كَانَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ لِيَنفِرُواْ كَآفَّةٗۚ فَلَوۡلَا نَفَرَ مِن كُلِّ فِرۡقَةٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ طَآئِفَةࣱ لِّيَتَفَقَّهُواْ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَلِيُنذِرُواْ قَوۡمَهُمۡ إِذَا رَجَعُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡ﴾, "It is not for the believers to go out all at once. Why should not a group from every section go out to gain understanding of the religion and warn their people when they return?" Studying the religion is also a form of going out in Allah's cause.
- When a surah is revealed, believers increase in faith, while those with disease in their hearts increase in filth.

### 8. The surah's close: the Prophet's mercy (128-129)
﴿لَقَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولࣱ مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ عَزِيزٌ عَلَيۡهِ مَا عَنِتُّمۡ حَرِيصٌ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾, "There has come to you a Messenger from among yourselves. Your suffering is grievous to him; he is anxious over you, and to the believers he is kind and merciful." Five qualities: from among you, your hardship weighs on him, eager for your guidance, kind, merciful. Then: ﴿فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَقُلۡ حَسۡبِيَ ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُۖ وَهُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾, "If they turn away, say: Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but Him. In Him I have put my trust, and He is the Lord of the mighty Throne."

**Reflect**: the surah that began with disavowal and severity ends with kindness and mercy. Severity was for treaty-breakers; mercy is the essence of the message. ## Part two: all of Surah Yunus (1-109)

Surah Yunus is Makki, with 109 verses, opening with the letters ﴿الٓرۚ﴾. It is named after the prophet Yunus although his story takes only one verse, because his people were the only nation that believed as a whole and benefited from their faith before punishment came. Its focus is **revelation, monotheism and resurrection**, answering those amazed that revelation came to a man from among them. For the whole surah see [Surah Yunus Meaning in English](/blog/la-tahzan-inna-allaha-maana-meaning-english/#Surah-Yunus-Meaning-A-Healing-for-Hearts-and-the-Friends-of-).

### 1. Amazement at revelation, and signs in creation (1-10)
﴿أَكَانَ لِلنَّاسِ عَجَبًا أَنۡ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ رَجُلٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ أَنۡ أَنذِرِ ٱلنَّاسَ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ قَدَمَ صِدۡقٍ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡۗ﴾, "Is it amazing to people that We revealed to a man among them: warn mankind and give good news to the believers that they will have a firm standing with their Lord?" Then creation in six days, and: ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ ضِيَآءٗ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ نُورٗا وَقَدَّرَهُۥ مَنَازِلَ لِتَعۡلَمُواْ عَدَدَ ٱلسِّنِينَ وَٱلۡحِسَابَۚ﴾, "It is He who made the sun a radiance and the moon a light, and determined its phases so you may know the number of years and the calculation of time." Note the distinction: the sun is *diya'*, a radiance with heat of its own, and the moon is *nur*, a gentle light.

Then the two destinies, and a beautiful description of the people of Paradise: ﴿دَعۡوَىٰهُمۡ فِيهَا سُبۡحَٰنَكَ ٱللَّهُمَّ وَتَحِيَّتُهُمۡ فِيهَا سَلَٰمࣱۚ وَءَاخِرُ دَعۡوَىٰهُمۡ أَنِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "Their call therein will be: Glory be to You, O Allah; their greeting therein: Peace; and the last of their call: Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds."

### 2. Human nature in hardship and ease (11-23)
﴿وَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ ٱلضُّرُّ دَعَانَا لِجَنۢبِهِۦٓ أَوۡ قَاعِدًا أَوۡ قَآئِمٗا فَلَمَّا كَشَفۡنَا عَنۡهُ ضُرَّهُۥ مَرَّ كَأَن لَّمۡ يَدۡعُنَآ﴾, "When harm touches a person, he calls upon Us lying on his side, sitting or standing; but when We remove his harm, he passes on as though he had never called upon Us." Then the idolaters' demand: bring a different Quran or change it, and the answer: it is not for me to change it of my own accord.

Then the ship scene, one of the Quran's most vivid: ﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا كُنتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ وَجَرَيۡنَ بِهِم بِرِيحٖ طَيِّبَةٖ وَفَرِحُواْ بِهَا جَآءَتۡهَا رِيحٌ عَاصِفࣱ﴾, "until, when you are in ships sailing with a good wind and they rejoice in it, a storm wind comes upon them." They call upon Allah sincerely, and once rescued, they at once transgress on earth. People recognise their Lord in hardship and forget Him in ease.

### 3. The parable of worldly life and the Home of Peace (24-30)
﴿إِنَّمَا مَثَلُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا كَمَآءٍ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَٱخۡتَلَطَ بِهِۦ نَبَاتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ﴾, "The example of worldly life is like rain We send down from the sky, with which the plants of the earth mingle", until, when the earth is adorned and its people think they control it, Allah's command comes and it becomes a harvested field as if it had not flourished the day before. Then the great invitation: ﴿وَٱللَّهُ يَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ دَارِ ٱلسَّلَٰمِ﴾, "And Allah invites to the Home of Peace."

Hizb 22 then begins: ﴿۞لِّلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ وَزِيَادَةࣱۖ﴾, "For those who do good is the best reward and more." In Sahih Muslim, Suhayb reports that the Prophet ﷺ explained the "more" as looking at the noble Face of Allah.

### 4. Questions of monotheism and the challenge (31-56)
﴿قُلۡ مَن يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمَّن يَمۡلِكُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَمَن يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ﴾, "Say: who provides for you from the sky and the earth? Who controls hearing and sight? Who brings the living out of the dead?" They will say: Allah. Then a series of questions: can any of your partners begin creation and then repeat it? Can any of them guide to the truth?

Then the challenge: ﴿أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُۖ قُلۡ فَأۡتُواْ بِسُورَةٖ مِّثۡلِهِۦ﴾, "Or do they say he invented it? Say: then bring a surah like it." And: "Rather, they denied what they could not encompass in knowledge", meaning they rejected it before understanding it.

Then: ﴿قُل لَّآ أَمۡلِكُ لِنَفۡسِي ضَرّٗا وَلَا نَفۡعًا إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۗ لِكُلِّ أُمَّةٍ أَجَلٌۚ﴾, "Say: I have no power to harm or benefit myself except as Allah wills. For every nation is a term."

### 5. Admonition, healing, and the allies of Allah (57-70)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءࣱ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "O mankind, there has come to you an admonition from your Lord, healing for what is in the hearts, and guidance and mercy for the believers", then ﴿قُلۡ بِفَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَبِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَفۡرَحُواْ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ﴾, "Say: in the bounty of Allah and His mercy, in that let them rejoice; it is better than what they accumulate." True joy is in the Quran and faith, not in what people pile up. Read ["In the Bounty and Mercy of Allah, Let Them Rejoice": Quran 10:57-58 Explained](/blog/la-tahzan-inna-allaha-maana-meaning-english/#In-the-Bounty-and-Mercy-of-Allah-Let-Them-Rejoice-Quran-10-5).

Then Allah's watchfulness: ﴿وَمَا تَكُونُ فِي شَأۡنٖ وَمَا تَتۡلُواْ مِنۡهُ مِن قُرۡءَانٖ وَلَا تَعۡمَلُونَ مِنۡ عَمَلٍ إِلَّا كُنَّا عَلَيۡكُمۡ شُهُودًا﴾, "You are not engaged in any matter, nor recite any of the Quran, nor do any deed, except that We are witness over you."

Then the beautiful promise: ﴿أَلَآ إِنَّ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾, "Surely the allies of Allah will have no fear, nor will they grieve." Who are they? ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَكَانُواْ يَتَّقُونَ﴾, "Those who believed and were God-conscious." Being Allah's ally is not about titles or wonders; it is faith and taqwa. Read [The Allies of Allah: 10:62](/blog/la-tahzan-inna-allaha-maana-meaning-english/#The-Friends-of-Allah-Have-No-Fear-Nor-Grief-Quran-10-62-64-E).

### 6. Nuh and Musa (71-93)
- **Nuh** challenges his people: gather your plan and your partners, then carry it out against me without delay. Then his rescue with those in the ark.
- **Musa and Pharaoh**: the magicians throw, and Musa says: ﴿مَا جِئۡتُم بِهِ ٱلسِّحۡرُۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَيُبۡطِلُهُۥٓ﴾, "What you have brought is magic; Allah will expose its falsehood." Only some youths of his people believed in Musa, fearing Pharaoh. He told them: "upon Him rely, if you are Muslims", and they said: ﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تَجۡعَلۡنَا فِتۡنَةࣰ لِّلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾, "Our Lord, do not make us a trial for the wrongdoing people."
- **Prayer at home**: Allah inspired Musa and his brother: make your houses places of prayer and establish prayer.
- **Musa's prayer against Pharaoh**: ﴿رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ ءَاتَيۡتَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَأَهُۥ زِينَةٗ وَأَمۡوَٰلٗا فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا﴾, "Our Lord, You have given Pharaoh and his chiefs splendour and wealth in this life..." and Allah said: your prayer has been answered.
- **The drowning and last-minute faith**: ﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَدۡرَكَهُ ٱلۡغَرَقُ قَالَ ءَامَنتُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَامَنَتۡ بِهِۦ بَنُوٓاْ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ﴾, "until, when drowning overtook him, he said: I believe that there is no god except the one in whom the Children of Israel believe." The reply: ﴿ءَآلۡـَٰٔنَ وَقَدۡ عَصَيۡتَ قَبۡلُ﴾, "Now? When you disobeyed before?", then ﴿فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ نُنَجِّيكَ بِبَدَنِكَ لِتَكُونَ لِمَنۡ خَلۡفَكَ ءَايَةٗۚ﴾, "Today We will save your body so you may be a sign for those after you."

**Lesson**: repentance has a deadline; when death arrives or punishment descends, the door closes.

### 7. The people of Yunus, and freedom of faith (94-109)
﴿فَلَوۡلَا كَانَتۡ قَرۡيَةٌ ءَامَنَتۡ فَنَفَعَهَآ إِيمَٰنُهَآ إِلَّا قَوۡمَ يُونُسَ لَمَّآ ءَامَنُواْ كَشَفۡنَا عَنۡهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡخِزۡيِ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا﴾, "If only there had been a town that believed and benefited from its faith, except the people of Yunus: when they believed, We removed from them the punishment of disgrace in this life." They stand opposite Pharaoh: they believed before punishment fell, and so their faith benefited them.

Then the principle of freedom: ﴿وَلَوۡ شَآءَ رَبُّكَ لَأٓمَنَ مَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كُلُّهُمۡ جَمِيعًاۚ أَفَأَنتَ تُكۡرِهُ ٱلنَّاسَ حَتَّىٰ يَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "Had your Lord willed, all on earth would have believed. Would you then compel people to become believers?" Then: ﴿وَإِن يَمۡسَسۡكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرّٖ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَۖ وَإِن يُرِدۡكَ بِخَيۡرٖ فَلَا رَآدَّ لِفَضۡلِهِۦۚ﴾, "If Allah touches you with harm, none can remove it but Him; and if He intends good for you, none can repel His bounty." The surah ends: follow what is revealed to you and be patient until Allah judges, and He is the best of judges.

## Part three: the opening of Surah Hud (1-5)

Surah Hud is Makki, with 123 verses, beginning ﴿الٓرۚ كِتَٰبٌ أُحۡكِمَتۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ ثُمَّ فُصِّلَتۡ مِن لَّدُنۡ حَكِيمٍ خَبِيرٍ﴾, "Alif Lam Ra. A Book whose verses are perfected and then explained in detail, from One who is Wise and Aware." Then the Prophet's message: worship none but Allah; I am a warner and bearer of good news to you from Him, and ﴿وَأَنِ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ ثُمَّ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ يُمَتِّعۡكُم مَّتَٰعًا حَسَنًا إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى﴾, "and seek forgiveness from your Lord, then repent to Him; He will let you enjoy a good provision until a set term." The juz ends with those who fold up their chests to hide from Allah, while He knows what they conceal and what they reveal. For the whole surah see [Surah Hud Meaning in English](/blog/wa-ma-min-dabbatin-meaning-english/#Surah-Hud-Meaning-Stand-Firm-as-You-Were-Commanded).

**A beautiful link**: the juz that began with the repentance of the three ends with the command to seek forgiveness and repent, with a promise of good provision.

## Tajweed points in Juz 11

### The letters ﴿الٓرۚ﴾
Read *Alif, Laam, Ra*. Laam is stretched six counts (compulsory madd), while Ra takes two counts (natural madd), being one of the letters of *hayy tahur*.

### ﴿ءَآلۡـَٰٔنَ﴾ in Yunus 51 and 91
An interrogative hamzah enters the connecting hamzah of *al-an*. Hafs reads it either by changing the second hamzah into a long alif stretched six counts, or by easing it (*tashil*).

### ﴿أَمَّن لَّا يَهِدِّيٓ﴾ in Yunus 35
Hafs reads it with a fatha on the ya, a kasrah on the ha and a doubled dal: *yahiddi*.

### Ghunnah and ikhfa'
Hold the ghunnah of the doubled meem in ﴿ثُمَّ﴾ and the ikhfa' of noon before fa in ﴿أَنفُسِكُمۡ﴾.

### Compulsory madd in words like ﴿ٱلضَّآلِّينَ﴾
A madd letter followed by a doubled letter in the same word is stretched six counts. For the full system see [Madd Rules in Tajweed](/blog/madd-rules-tajweed-english/).

## How to memorize Juz 11 with understanding

1. **The end of At-Tawbah is groups and a story**: learn the four groups (forerunners, hypocrites, the mixed, the deferred), then the story of the three.
2. **Treat Surah Yunus as one unit** and give its seven sections short names.
3. **Picture the ship scene (22-23)** as a moving scene.
4. **Compare Musa's story in Yunus with Al-A'raf**: here the focus is prayer at home, Musa's prayer, and Pharaoh's faith while drowning.
5. **Start Hud in this juz** and continue it into Juz 12 so the memorization is unbroken.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 11

- **Never excuse yourself with a lie**; truth saves, even if relief is delayed.
- **If you have mixed good and bad, do not despair**: admit it, give charity, and hope for forgiveness.
- **Look at the foundation of your deeds**, not their appearance.
- **Sell yourself to Allah**: the price is Paradise.
- **Study the religion**: it is a form of striving.
- **Remember Allah in ease** as you do in hardship.
- **Do not be deceived by worldly splendour**; it is a field that becomes stubble.
- **Rejoice in the Quran** more than in wealth.
- **Seek Allah's friendship through faith and taqwa.**
- **Repent before time runs out.**
- **Compel no one to believe.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 11 called "Ya'tadhirun"?
Many memorizers name it after its second verse, ﴿يَعۡتَذِرُونَ إِلَيۡكُمۡ إِذَا رَجَعۡتُمۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡۚ﴾; others name it after its first words, ﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلسَّبِيلُ﴾.

### Who were the three who stayed behind?
Ka'b ibn Malik, Murarah ibn ar-Rabi' and Hilal ibn Umayyah; their story is in Al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Where does Juz 11 end?
At Hud 11:5. Juz 12 begins with ﴿۞وَمَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ رِزۡقُهَا﴾, "There is no creature on earth but that its provision is from Allah."

### What is the "more" in "for those who do good is the best reward and more"?
The Prophet ﷺ explained it as looking at the noble Face of Allah, as reported by Suhayb in Sahih Muslim.

### Why is Surah Yunus named after Yunus when his story is so short?
Because his people were the only town that believed before punishment fell and so benefited from their faith, which ties in with the surah's call to believe before it is too late.

## Conclusion

Juz 11 is the juz of hope. It opens the door of repentance to those who mixed good and bad, raises the truthful even after hardship, and presents the profitable bargain with Allah. In Surah Yunus it establishes proof of monotheism and distinguishes faith that benefits before punishment, like that of Yunus's people, from faith that does not help at the moment of drowning, like Pharaoh's.
