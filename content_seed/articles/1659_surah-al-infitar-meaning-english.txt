**Surah Al-Infitar** (19 ayat) is short, but its central question touches every heart.

## The scenes
﴿إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنفَطَرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡكَوَاكِبُ ٱنتَثَرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡقُبُورُ بُعۡثِرَتۡ﴾ ﴿عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسࣱ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ وَأَخَّرَتۡ﴾
- Then **every soul will know** what it sent ahead and what it left behind.

## The question
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّىٰكَ فَعَدَلَكَ﴾ ﴿فِيٓ أَيِّ صُورَةٖ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ﴾
- *O man, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Generous, who created you, proportioned you and balanced you?*
- **The name "Al-Karim"** here is both comfort and reproach: how can you disobey One so generous?

## The recording angels
﴿وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَحَٰفِظِينَ﴾ ﴿كِرَامٗا كَٰتِبِينَ﴾ ﴿يَعۡلَمُونَ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾
| Description | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Guardians | Watching over you |
| Noble | Honoured by Allah |
| Recording | Writing down deeds |

## The two groups
- **The righteous** in bliss, **the wicked** in fire, and on that Day *the command belongs to Allah alone*.

## Lessons
- **Don't let His generosity make you careless**; let it make you grateful.
