Surah Yunus is named after the only prophet whose entire people believed before the punishment fell: "Except the people of Yunus; when they believed, We removed from them the punishment of disgrace." The surah stands in sharp contrast with Pharaoh, who declared belief only when the water closed over him and was told: "Now? And you had disobeyed before." It contains some of the most hopeful verses in the Quran: "For those who have done good is the best reward and more", "Say: In the bounty of Allah and in His mercy, in that let them rejoice", and "Unquestionably, the allies of Allah, there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve." It also describes the greeting and the final du'a of the people of Paradise.

This guide explains Surah Yunus section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Yunus
- **Number of verses**: 109.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 10, in the eleventh juz.
- **Name**: named after the Prophet Yunus ﷺ, whose people are mentioned in verse 98.
- **Main themes**: revelation, signs of creation, human ingratitude, the reward of the good, the friends of Allah, and timely faith.

## Verses 1–10: Revelation, Creation and the People of Paradise
﴿الٓرۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ ﴿أَكَانَ لِلنَّاسِ عَجَبًا أَنۡ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ رَجُلٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ أَنۡ أَنذِرِ ٱلنَّاسَ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ قَدَمَ صِدۡقٍ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡۗ قَالَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَسَٰحِرࣱ مُّبِينٌ﴾ (Quran 10:1-2)

**"Have the people been amazed that We revealed to a man from among them: Warn mankind and give good tidings to those who believe?"**

﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ ضِيَآءٗ وَٱلۡقَمَرَ نُورٗا وَقَدَّرَهُۥ مَنَازِلَ لِتَعۡلَمُواْ عَدَدَ ٱلسِّنِينَ وَٱلۡحِسَابَۚ مَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ ذَٰلِكَ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ يُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ فِي ٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَّقُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:5-6)

**"It is He who made the sun a shining light and the moon a derived light and determined for it phases, that you may know the number of years and account of time."** The sun is *diya'*, a radiance with its own heat and light; the moon is *nur*, a light. In the alternation of night and day are signs **"for a people who fear Allah"**.

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ يَهۡدِيهِمۡ رَبُّهُم بِإِيمَٰنِهِمۡۖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهِمُ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ فِي جَنَّـٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾ ﴿دَعۡوَىٰهُمۡ فِيهَا سُبۡحَٰنَكَ ٱللَّهُمَّ وَتَحِيَّتُهُمۡ فِيهَا سَلَٰمࣱۚ وَءَاخِرُ دَعۡوَىٰهُمۡ أَنِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 10:9-10)

**The people of Paradise**: **"Their call therein will be: Exalted are You, O Allah. And their greeting therein will be: Peace. And the last of their call will be: Praise to Allah, Lord of the worlds."** Glorification, peace and praise: the language of Paradise.

## Verses 11–20: Man in Hardship and Ease
﴿وَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ ٱلضُّرُّ دَعَانَا لِجَنۢبِهِۦٓ أَوۡ قَاعِدًا أَوۡ قَآئِمٗا فَلَمَّا كَشَفۡنَا عَنۡهُ ضُرَّهُۥ مَرَّ كَأَن لَّمۡ يَدۡعُنَآ إِلَىٰ ضُرّٖ مَّسَّهُۥۚ كَذَٰلِكَ زُيِّنَ لِلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:12)

**"And when affliction touches man, he calls upon Us, whether lying on his side or sitting or standing; but when We remove from him his affliction, he continues as if he had never called upon Us."** A sharp description of human ingratitude.

﴿وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَاتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٖ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرۡجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا ٱئۡتِ بِقُرۡءَانٍ غَيۡرِ هَٰذَآ أَوۡ بَدِّلۡهُۚ قُلۡ مَا يَكُونُ لِيٓ أَنۡ أُبَدِّلَهُۥ مِن تِلۡقَآيِٕ نَفۡسِيٓۖ إِنۡ أَتَّبِعُ إِلَّا مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّۖ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ إِنۡ عَصَيۡتُ رَبِّي عَذَابَ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 10:15)

When the Quran was recited, they asked for a different one. The Prophet ﷺ was told to say: **"It is not for me to change it on my own accord. I only follow what is revealed to me."**

## Verses 21–30: The Ship and This World
﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُسَيِّرُكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا كُنتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ وَجَرَيۡنَ بِهِم بِرِيحٖ طَيِّبَةٖ وَفَرِحُواْ بِهَا جَآءَتۡهَا رِيحٌ عَاصِفࣱ وَجَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡجُ مِن كُلِّ مَكَانٖ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ أُحِيطَ بِهِمۡ دَعَوُاْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ لَئِنۡ أَنجَيۡتَنَا مِنۡ هَٰذِهِۦ لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلشَّـٰكِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّآ أَنجَىٰهُمۡ إِذَا هُمۡ يَبۡغُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّۗ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّمَا بَغۡيُكُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمۖ مَّتَٰعَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ ثُمَّ إِلَيۡنَا مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ فَنُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:22-23)

**On a ship in a storm**, people call on Allah sincerely: "If You save us, we will be among the grateful." **"But when He saves them, at once they commit injustice upon the earth without right."**

﴿إِنَّمَا مَثَلُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا كَمَآءٍ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَٱخۡتَلَطَ بِهِۦ نَبَاتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِمَّا يَأۡكُلُ ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمُ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَخَذَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زُخۡرُفَهَا وَٱزَّيَّنَتۡ وَظَنَّ أَهۡلُهَآ أَنَّهُمۡ قَٰدِرُونَ عَلَيۡهَآ أَتَىٰهَآ أَمۡرُنَا لَيۡلًا أَوۡ نَهَارٗا فَجَعَلۡنَٰهَا حَصِيدٗا كَأَن لَّمۡ تَغۡنَ بِٱلۡأَمۡسِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نُفَصِّلُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لِقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:24)

**"The example of worldly life is but like rain which We have sent down from the sky that the plants of the earth absorb... until, when the earth has taken on its adornment and is embellished and its people suppose they have capability over it, there comes to it Our command by night or by day, and We make it as a harvest, as if it had not flourished yesterday."** Everything that looks permanent can vanish overnight.

﴿وَٱللَّهُ يَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ دَارِ ٱلسَّلَٰمِ وَيَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ﴾ ﴿۞لِّلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ وَزِيَادَةࣱۖ وَلَا يَرۡهَقُ وُجُوهَهُمۡ قَتَرࣱ وَلَا ذِلَّةٌۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:25-26)

**"And Allah invites to the Home of Peace."** **"For those who have done good is the best reward and more."** In Sahih Muslim, Suhayb reported that the Prophet ﷺ recited this verse and explained that when the people of Paradise enter it, Allah will remove the veil, **"and they will not have been given anything more beloved to them than looking at their Lord"**. The "more" (*ziyadah*) is seeing the Face of Allah.

## Verses 31–56: Arguments and the Challenge
﴿قُلۡ مَن يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمَّن يَمۡلِكُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَمَن يُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡحَيَّ مِنَ ٱلۡمَيِّتِ وَيُخۡرِجُ ٱلۡمَيِّتَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَيِّ وَمَن يُدَبِّرُ ٱلۡأَمۡرَۚ فَسَيَقُولُونَ ٱللَّهُۚ فَقُلۡ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ﴾ ﴿فَذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ فَمَاذَا بَعۡدَ ٱلۡحَقِّ إِلَّا ٱلضَّلَٰلُۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُصۡرَفُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:31-32)

**"Say: Who provides for you from the heaven and the earth? Who controls hearing and sight? Who brings the living out of the dead? Who arranges every matter? They will say: Allah."** **"For that is Allah, your Lord, the Truth. And what can be beyond truth except error?"**

﴿أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ ٱفۡتَرَىٰهُۖ قُلۡ فَأۡتُواْ بِسُورَةٖ مِّثۡلِهِۦ وَٱدۡعُواْ مَنِ ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 10:38)

**The challenge**: **"Then bring forth a surah like it and call upon whomever you can besides Allah, if you should be truthful."** No one has been able to.

## Verses 57–58: Rejoice in His Bounty and Mercy
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدۡ جَآءَتۡكُم مَّوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَشِفَآءࣱ لِّمَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ بِفَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَبِرَحۡمَتِهِۦ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَفۡرَحُواْ هُوَ خَيۡرࣱ مِّمَّا يَجۡمَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:57-58)

**"O mankind, there has come to you instruction from your Lord and healing for what is in the breasts and guidance and mercy for the believers."** Four descriptions of the Quran: instruction, healing, guidance and mercy.

**"Say: In the bounty of Allah and in His mercy, in that let them rejoice; it is better than what they accumulate."** The real cause for joy is guidance and the Quran, not wealth.

## Verses 61–64: The Friends of Allah
﴿وَمَا تَكُونُ فِي شَأۡنٖ وَمَا تَتۡلُواْ مِنۡهُ مِن قُرۡءَانٖ وَلَا تَعۡمَلُونَ مِنۡ عَمَلٍ إِلَّا كُنَّا عَلَيۡكُمۡ شُهُودًا إِذۡ تُفِيضُونَ فِيهِۚ وَمَا يَعۡزُبُ عَن رَّبِّكَ مِن مِّثۡقَالِ ذَرَّةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَلَآ أَصۡغَرَ مِن ذَٰلِكَ وَلَآ أَكۡبَرَ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٍ﴾ (Quran 10:61)

**"And you are not in any matter or recite any of the Quran and you do not do any deed except that We are witness over you when you are involved in it."** Allah is with you in every moment.

﴿أَلَآ إِنَّ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهِ لَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَكَانُواْ يَتَّقُونَ﴾ ﴿لَهُمُ ٱلۡبُشۡرَىٰ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ لَا تَبۡدِيلَ لِكَلِمَٰتِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ (Quran 10:62-64)

**"Unquestionably, the allies of Allah, there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve. Those who believed and were fearing Allah. For them are good tidings in the worldly life and in the hereafter."**

**Who are the friends of Allah (*awliya'*)?** The verse defines them: **those who believe and fear Allah**. Not people with special lineage or miracles, but every sincere, God-conscious believer. In Sahih al-Bukhari, in a hadith qudsi, Allah says: "Whoever shows enmity to an ally of Mine, I declare war against him. My servant does not draw near to Me with anything more beloved to Me than what I have made obligatory upon him, and My servant continues to draw near to Me with voluntary deeds until I love him."

## Verses 71–74: Nuh
﴿۞وَٱتۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ نَبَأَ نُوحٍ إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِۦ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِن كَانَ كَبُرَ عَلَيۡكُم مَّقَامِي وَتَذۡكِيرِي بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُ فَأَجۡمِعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَكُمۡ وَشُرَكَآءَكُمۡ ثُمَّ لَا يَكُنۡ أَمۡرُكُمۡ عَلَيۡكُمۡ غُمَّةٗ ثُمَّ ٱقۡضُوٓاْ إِلَيَّ وَلَا تُنظِرُونِ﴾ ﴿فَإِن تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ فَمَا سَأَلۡتُكُم مِّنۡ أَجۡرٍۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَأُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَمَن مَّعَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ خَلَـٰٓئِفَ وَأَغۡرَقۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَاۖ فَٱنظُرۡ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُنذَرِينَ﴾ (Quran 10:71-73)

Nuh said to his people: **"If my residence and my reminding of the signs of Allah has become burdensome upon you, then I have relied upon Allah."** He was saved in the ark.

## Verses 75–93: Musa and Pharaoh
﴿وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِن كُنتُمۡ ءَامَنتُم بِٱللَّهِ فَعَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلُوٓاْ إِن كُنتُم مُّسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَقَالُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوَكَّلۡنَا رَبَّنَا لَا تَجۡعَلۡنَا فِتۡنَةٗ لِّلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَنَجِّنَا بِرَحۡمَتِكَ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰ وَأَخِيهِ أَن تَبَوَّءَا لِقَوۡمِكُمَا بِمِصۡرَ بُيُوتٗا وَٱجۡعَلُواْ بُيُوتَكُمۡ قِبۡلَةٗ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 10:84-87)

**Musa said to his people: "If you have believed in Allah, then rely upon Him, if you should be Muslims."** They prayed: **"Upon Allah do we rely. Our Lord, make us not objects of trial for the wrongdoing people, and save us by Your mercy from the disbelieving people."** And they were told: **"Make your houses facing a qiblah and establish prayer."** Homes as places of worship in times of oppression.

﴿۞وَجَٰوَزۡنَا بِبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ فَأَتۡبَعَهُمۡ فِرۡعَوۡنُ وَجُنُودُهُۥ بَغۡيٗا وَعَدۡوًاۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَدۡرَكَهُ ٱلۡغَرَقُ قَالَ ءَامَنتُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَامَنَتۡ بِهِۦ بَنُوٓاْ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ وَأَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿ءَآلۡـَٰٔنَ وَقَدۡ عَصَيۡتَ قَبۡلُ وَكُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ نُنَجِّيكَ بِبَدَنِكَ لِتَكُونَ لِمَنۡ خَلۡفَكَ ءَايَةٗۚ وَإِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِ عَنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا لَغَٰفِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 10:90-92)

**Pharaoh's late belief**: when drowning overtook him, he said: **"I believe that there is no deity except that in whom the Children of Israel believe, and I am of the Muslims."** The answer: **"Now? And you had disobeyed before and were of the corrupters? So today We will save you in body, that you may be to those who succeed you a sign."** Belief at the moment of death is not accepted.

## Verses 98–109: The People of Yunus and the Final Commands
﴿فَلَوۡلَا كَانَتۡ قَرۡيَةٌ ءَامَنَتۡ فَنَفَعَهَآ إِيمَٰنُهَآ إِلَّا قَوۡمَ يُونُسَ لَمَّآ ءَامَنُواْ كَشَفۡنَا عَنۡهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡخِزۡيِ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَمَتَّعۡنَٰهُمۡ إِلَىٰ حِينٖ﴾ (Quran 10:98)

**"Then has there not been a single city that believed so its faith benefited it, except the people of Yunus? When they believed, We removed from them the punishment of disgrace in worldly life."** They believed in time, before the punishment fell, and were saved.

﴿وَلَوۡ شَآءَ رَبُّكَ لَأٓمَنَ مَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كُلُّهُمۡ جَمِيعًاۚ أَفَأَنتَ تُكۡرِهُ ٱلنَّاسَ حَتَّىٰ يَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 10:99)

**"And had your Lord willed, those on earth would have believed, all of them entirely. Then would you compel the people until they become believers?"** Faith cannot be forced.

﴿وَإِن يَمۡسَسۡكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرّٖ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَۖ وَإِن يُرِدۡكَ بِخَيۡرٖ فَلَا رَآدَّ لِفَضۡلِهِۦۚ يُصِيبُ بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 10:107)

**"And if Allah should touch you with adversity, there is no remover of it except Him; and if He intends for you good, then there is no repeller of His bounty."**

﴿وَٱتَّبِعۡ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَٱصۡبِرۡ حَتَّىٰ يَحۡكُمَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 10:109)

**"And follow what is revealed to you and be patient until Allah will judge. And He is the best of judges."**

## Word Meanings
- **Diya'**: radiance.
- **Nur**: light.
- **Manazil**: phases, stations.
- **Da'wahum**: their call.
- **Tahiyyatuhum**: their greeting.
- **Zukhruf**: adornment.
- **Hasid**: harvested.
- **Dar as-Salam**: the Home of Peace.
- **Al-Husna wa ziyadah**: the best and more.
- **Maw'izah**: instruction.
- **Shifa'**: healing.
- **Awliya' Allah**: the allies of Allah.
- **Qiblah**: a direction of prayer.
- **Al-An**: now.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Sun as radiance, moon as light**: precise distinction.
2. **The language of Paradise**: glorification, peace and praise.
3. **"As if it had not flourished yesterday"**: the speed of loss.
4. **"The best and more"**: a promise whose "more" is beyond imagination.
5. **"Now?"**: one word that ends Pharaoh's last hope.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Alif Lam Ra*.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *diya'an* (verse 5) and *awliya'a* (verse 62).
- **Izhar**: in *'ajaban an* (verse 2), the tanween before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *ala inna awliya'a Allah* (verse 62), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *qiblatan* (verse 87), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah At-Tawbah before it ended with "Allah is sufficient for me", and Yunus opens with amazement at revelation and continues with reliance on Allah. Surah Hud after it continues the stories of Nuh and other prophets in greater detail.

## Becoming a Friend of Allah
1. **Believe sincerely.**
2. **Fear Allah** in private and public.
3. **Perform the obligations first.**
4. **Add voluntary deeds** consistently.
5. **Rejoice in guidance**, not only in wealth.
6. **Rely on Allah** in hardship.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Remember Allah in ease, not only in hardship.**
2. **See the world as a passing harvest.**
3. **Do good and hope for "the more".**
4. **Use the Quran as healing.**
5. **Do not delay repentance** like Pharaoh.
6. **Make your home a place of prayer.**
7. **Leave guidance of hearts to Allah**; do not compel.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the "more" in "the best reward and more"?
Seeing the Face of Allah in Paradise, as the Prophet ﷺ explained in Sahih Muslim.

### Who are the friends of Allah?
Those who believe and fear Allah, as verse 63 defines.

### What does "rejoice in the bounty of Allah and His mercy" mean?
The greatest cause of joy is guidance and the Quran, better than any wealth.

### Why was Pharaoh's belief not accepted?
Because he declared it only when drowning, when it was too late.

### What is special about the people of Yunus?
They are the only people mentioned who believed before the punishment fell and were saved.

### What does "would you compel people to believe?" teach?
Faith cannot be forced; guidance is in Allah's hands.

### How many verses are in Surah Yunus?
109 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Yunus invites you to the Home of Peace and promises those who do good the best reward and more. It warns against remembering Allah only in hardship and against delaying faith until it is too late, as Pharaoh did. Rejoice in Allah's bounty and mercy, believe and fear Him, and you will be among His friends who have no fear and no grief.
