Four short ayat that describe Allah more clearly than many books. **Surah Al-Ikhlas** (Sincerity) is the surah of pure belief in One God.

## Ayah 1
﴿قُلۡ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ﴾ — *Say: He is Allah, One.*
- **Aḥad** means One, unique, with no partner and nothing like Him.

## Ayah 2
﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ﴾ — *Allah, As-Samad.*
- **As-Samad**: the Master whom all creation turns to in their needs, while He needs no one.

## Ayah 3
﴿لَمۡ يَلِدۡ وَلَمۡ يُولَدۡ﴾ — *He neither begets nor is begotten.*
- He has no child and no parent.

## Ayah 4
﴿وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدُۢ﴾ — *And there is none comparable to Him.*

## A third of the Quran
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «By the One in whose hand is my soul, it equals a third of the Quran» [Al-Bukhari].
- Scholars explained that the Quran covers **belief, law and stories**, and Al-Ikhlas is pure **belief in Allah**.
- This is about **reward**, not that it replaces reading the whole Quran.

## The companion who loved it
- A companion used to end every recitation in prayer with Al-Ikhlas. When asked, he said: "It is the description of the Most Merciful, and I love to recite it."
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Tell him that Allah loves him» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

## Recitation tips
| Word | Tip |
|---|---|
| ﴿أَحَدٌ﴾ | Clear ḥaa, not haa; qalqalah on daal when stopping |
| ﴿ٱلصَّمَدُ﴾ | Heavy ṣaad |
| ﴿كُفُوًا أَحَدُۢ﴾ | Clear tanween before the hamza |
