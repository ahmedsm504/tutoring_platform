**Surah Al-Ghashiyah** (26 ayat) was recited by the Prophet ﷺ with Al-A'la in the Friday and Eid prayers [Muslim].

﴿هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلۡغَٰشِيَةِ﴾ — *Has there reached you the report of the Overwhelming Event?*

## Two kinds of faces
| Faces | Ayat | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Humbled | ﴿وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٍ خَٰشِعَةٌ﴾ ﴿عَامِلَةࣱ نَّاصِبَةࣱ﴾ | Labouring and exhausted, for nothing |
| Joyful | ﴿وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ نَّاعِمَةࣱ﴾ ﴿لِّسَعۡيِهَا رَاضِيَةࣱ﴾ ﴿فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٖ﴾ | Pleased with their efforts, in a lofty garden |

## Four signs to look at
﴿أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡإِبِلِ كَيۡفَ خُلِقَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ كَيۡفَ رُفِعَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِلَى ٱلۡجِبَالِ كَيۡفَ نُصِبَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَيۡفَ سُطِحَتۡ﴾
- Four things the Arabs saw every day in the desert: **look closer**.

## Your task is to remind
- *So remind; you are only a reminder. You are not a controller over them.*
- **Guidance belongs to Allah**; our role is to convey with wisdom.

## Lessons
- **Effort counts** only when it is for Allah.
- **Everyday sights** are signs.
