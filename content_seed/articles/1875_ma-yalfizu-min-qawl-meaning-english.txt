## "Not a Word Does He Utter Except a Watcher Is Ready": Quran 50:16-18 Explained

﴿وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ وَنَعۡلَمُ مَا تُوَسۡوِسُ بِهِۦ نَفۡسُهُۥۖ وَنَحۡنُ أَقۡرَبُ إِلَيۡهِ مِنۡ حَبۡلِ ٱلۡوَرِيدِ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ يَتَلَقَّى ٱلۡمُتَلَقِّيَانِ عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ قَعِيدࣱ﴾ ﴿مَّا يَلۡفِظُ مِن قَوۡلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيۡهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدࣱ﴾ [Qaf 50:16-18]

### Knowledge of whispers
- Allah knows not just what you say, but what your soul **whispers** to you.

### Nearer than the jugular vein
- Many scholars explain the nearness here as the nearness of His knowledge and His angels, who record everything.

### Two recorders
| Position | Role |
|---|---|
| On the right | Records good deeds |
| On the left | Records bad deeds |

### Every word
- *Ma yalfizu*: *lafz* means to throw out. Every word that leaves your mouth is caught.
- *Raqib*: watching. *ʿAtid*: present and ready.

### A word can change destiny
- The Prophet ﷺ said a servant may say a word pleasing to Allah without thinking much of it, and Allah raises him by degrees; and a word angering Allah that drops him into the Fire (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### Lessons
- **Think before you speak or type.**
- **Fill your record with dhikr.**

## Surah Qaf Meaning: Closer Than the Jugular Vein

**Surah Qaf** (45 ayat) was so often recited by the Prophet ﷺ on the minbar on Fridays that a woman companion said she memorized it from his tongue [Muslim].

### Signs of resurrection
- The deniers asked: *When we die and become dust, will we return?*
- The answer: look at the **sky** with no cracks, the **earth** spread out, the **rain** that revives dead land. *Thus is the coming out.*

### Closer than you think
﴿وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ وَنَعۡلَمُ مَا تُوَسۡوِسُ بِهِۦ نَفۡسُهُۥۖ وَنَحۡنُ أَقۡرَبُ إِلَيۡهِ مِنۡ حَبۡلِ ٱلۡوَرِيدِ﴾
- Many commentators explained this nearness as Allah's **knowledge** and His angels.

### The two recorders
﴿إِذۡ يَتَلَقَّى ٱلۡمُتَلَقِّيَانِ عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ قَعِيدࣱ﴾ ﴿مَّا يَلۡفِظُ مِن قَوۡلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيۡهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدࣱ﴾
- **Every word** is written.

### The journey
| Stage | Ayah meaning |
|---|---|
| Death | *The stupor of death comes in truth* |
| Resurrection | The Trumpet is blown |
| Judgement | *You were heedless of this; We removed your cover* |
| Paradise | *Brought near to the righteous, not far* |

### Who benefits?
﴿إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَذِكۡرَىٰ لِمَن كَانَ لَهُۥ قَلۡبٌ أَوۡ أَلۡقَى ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَهُوَ شَهِيدࣱ﴾

### Lessons
- **Guard your tongue**: every word is recorded.

## Surah Adh-Dhariyat Meaning: I Created Jinn and Mankind Only to Worship Me

**Surah Adh-Dhariyat** (60 ayat) contains the ayah that answers the biggest question of life: **why are we here?**

### The righteous
- They slept little at night, sought forgiveness **before dawn**, and gave a share of their wealth to the one who asks and the deprived.

### Signs everywhere
﴿وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ءَايَٰتࣱ لِّلۡمُوقِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ — *And in the earth are signs for the certain, and in yourselves. Then will you not see?*

### The guests of Ibrahim
﴿هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ضَيۡفِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ ٱلۡمُكۡرَمِينَ﴾
- He hurried to bring them a fat calf; they gave him glad tidings of a knowledgeable son.

### Flee to Allah
- *So flee to Allah. Indeed, I am to you from Him a clear warner.*

### The purpose of creation
﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡتُ ٱلۡجِنَّ وَٱلۡإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُونِ﴾ ﴿مَآ أُرِيدُ مِنۡهُم مِّن رِّزۡقٖ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَن يُطۡعِمُونِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلۡقُوَّةِ ٱلۡمَتِينُ﴾
- **Worship** includes every act done for Allah: prayer, work, kindness.
- **Allah needs nothing** from us; He is the Provider.

### Lessons
- **Make your life worship** with intention.

## "In the Heaven Is Your Provision and What You Are Promised": Quran 51:22-23 Explained

﴿وَفِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ رِزۡقُكُمۡ وَمَا تُوعَدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَوَرَبِّ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِنَّهُۥ لَحَقࣱّ مِّثۡلَ مَآ أَنَّكُمۡ تَنطِقُونَ﴾ [Adh-Dhariyat 51:22-23]

### Provision in the sky
- Many scholars explained it as **rain**, through which crops and fruits grow. Others said provision is decreed and written with Allah. Both are true.

### "And what you are promised"
- Reward and punishment, Paradise and the Fire.

### A great oath
- Allah swears by Himself, *by the Lord of the heaven and earth*, and compares it to your speech: just as you do not doubt that you speak, do not doubt this.

### Calm with effort
| Belief | Practice |
|---|---|
| Provision is decreed | Do not chase it into the unlawful |
| Effort is commanded | Work and strive |
| Allah is the Provider | Ask Him for blessing |

### Lessons
- **Do not fear for provision** to the point of sin.
- **Look up** and remember who provides.

## "So Flee to Allah": Quran 51:50 Explained

﴿فَفِرُّوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّي لَكُم مِّنۡهُ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ [Adh-Dhariyat 51:50]

### The context
- The previous verse: *Of everything We created pairs, that you may remember.* Everything has a counterpart; only Allah is One. So **flee to Him**.

### Fleeing to, not from
- Usually people flee **from** what they fear. Here you flee **to** the One you fear, because only He can protect you from His punishment.

### What we flee from and to
| From | To |
|---|---|
| Sin | Obedience |
| Heedlessness | Remembrance |
| Ignorance | Knowledge |
| Worry | Reliance on Him |
| Shirk | Tawhid |

### Urgency
- *Firaru* implies speed: do not walk slowly toward Allah when danger is near.

### A clear warner
- The Prophet ﷺ is *a clear warner*: his message is plain and sincere.

### Lessons
- **Run to Allah** when life frightens you.
- **Do not delay repentance.**

## Quran 51:56 Explained: The Purpose of Human Life

﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡتُ ٱلۡجِنَّ وَٱلۡإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُونِ﴾ [Adh-Dhariyat 51:56]

### The purpose of life
- The verse answers the biggest question: **why am I here?** To worship Allah.

### What is worship?
- Scholars define *ʿibadah* as everything Allah loves and is pleased with, of words and deeds, outward and inward.
| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Rituals | Prayer, fasting, Hajj |
| Heart | Love, fear, hope, trust |
| Tongue | Dhikr, truthful speech |
| Daily life | Work, family care, kindness, with the right intention |

### Allah does not need it
﴿مَآ أُرِيدُ مِنۡهُم مِّن رِّزۡقٖ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَن يُطۡعِمُونِ﴾
- The next verses state He wants **no provision** from them; He is the Provider. Worship benefits **us**.

### Meaning in every moment
- With the right intention, sleep to wake for Fajr, work to support family, and study to benefit others all become worship.

### Lessons
- **Remember your purpose** each morning.
- **Turn habits into worship** through intention.

## Surah At-Tur Meaning: Were They Created from Nothing?

**Surah At-Tur** (49 ayat) contains a series of questions so powerful that a listener felt his heart almost fly.

### Jubayr ibn Mutʿim
- Before accepting Islam, Jubayr heard the Prophet ﷺ recite At-Tur in the Maghrib prayer. When he reached these ayat, he said: "My heart nearly flew" [Al-Bukhari].
﴿أَمۡ خُلِقُواْ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ شَيۡءٍ أَمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ خَلَقُواْ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۚ بَل لَّا يُوقِنُونَ﴾

### The three options
| Option | Answer |
|---|---|
| Created from nothing? | Impossible |
| Created themselves? | Impossible |
| Then a Creator exists | Allah |

### Families reunited
﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱتَّبَعَتۡهُمۡ ذُرِّيَّتُهُم بِإِيمَٰنٍ أَلۡحَقۡنَا بِهِمۡ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ وَمَآ أَلَتۡنَٰهُم مِّنۡ عَمَلِهِم مِّن شَيۡءٖۚ﴾
- **Believing children** are raised to their parents' level in Paradise, without reducing the parents' reward.

### Patience and glorification
﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ فَإِنَّكَ بِأَعۡيُنِنَاۖ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ﴾

### Lessons
- **Simple logic** leads to faith.
- **Raise your children on faith**: you may be together in Paradise.

## "Be Patient, for You Are Under Our Eyes": Quran 52:48-49 Explained

﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ فَإِنَّكَ بِأَعۡيُنِنَاۖ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ﴾ ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡهُ وَإِدۡبَٰرَ ٱلنُّجُومِ﴾ [At-Tur 52:48-49]

### Patience with His decree
- *Hukm* includes what Allah decrees and what He commands. Be patient with both.

### "Under Our eyes"
- A beautiful comfort: you are seen, protected and cared for by Allah. Ahl al-Sunnah affirm this as Allah describes Himself, without likening.

### Three times of glorification
| Time | Explained as |
|---|---|
| When you rise | From sleep or from a gathering |
| Part of the night | Night prayer |
| At the setting of the stars | The two rakʿahs before Fajr |

- The Prophet ﷺ said the two rakʿahs of Fajr are better than the world and all it contains (narrated by Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Remember you are seen** when life feels lonely.
- **Never miss the Sunnah of Fajr.**

## Surah An-Najm Meaning: He Does Not Speak from Desire

**Surah An-Najm** (62 ayat) was the first surah with a prostration that the Prophet ﷺ recited publicly in Makkah; when he prostrated at its end, those present prostrated too [Al-Bukhari].

﴿وَٱلنَّجۡمِ إِذَا هَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿مَا ضَلَّ صَاحِبُكُمۡ وَمَا غَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحۡيࣱ يُوحَىٰ﴾ ﴿عَلَّمَهُۥ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡقُوَىٰ﴾

### The vision
- The Prophet ﷺ saw Jibril in his true form, and again **at the Lote Tree of the furthest boundary**, near the Garden of Refuge.
- *The sight did not swerve, nor did it transgress.*

### Idols without power
- Al-Lat, al-ʿUzza and Manat: *nothing but names you have named*.

### Personal responsibility
﴿وَأَن لَّيۡسَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّ سَعۡيَهُۥ سَوۡفَ يُرَىٰ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ يُجۡزَىٰهُ ٱلۡجَزَآءَ ٱلۡأَوۡفَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ﴾

### The final command
﴿فَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ۩﴾ — a place of **prostration of recitation**.

### Lessons
- **The Sunnah is revelation** in its meaning.
- **Your effort is seen**, and rewarded in full.

## "Man Will Have Only What He Strives For": Quran 53:39-41 Explained

﴿وَأَن لَّيۡسَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّ سَعۡيَهُۥ سَوۡفَ يُرَىٰ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ يُجۡزَىٰهُ ٱلۡجَزَآءَ ٱلۡأَوۡفَىٰ﴾ [An-Najm 53:39-41]

### Three truths
| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Only what he strives for | Your record is built by your effort |
| His effort will be seen | Nothing is hidden |
| Then fully recompensed | The reward will be complete |

### Context
- These verses are described as being in *the scriptures of Musa and Ibrahim, who fulfilled his duty*: an ancient principle.

### Others' duʿa and charity
- Scholars explain that the verse states the **basic rule**: you are not owed the deeds of others. Yet authentic texts show that a person benefits from the duʿa of believers, charity on his behalf, and a righteous child's duʿa, by Allah's grace.

### Effort, not results
- *Saʿy* is effort. You are judged by what you strive for, not only by outcomes you cannot control.

### Lessons
- **No one carries your load**, so work for yourself.
- **Your effort is seen**, even if people do not notice.

## Surah Al-Qamar Meaning: The Moon Split and the Quran Made Easy

**Surah Al-Qamar** (55 ayat) opens with a miracle witnessed in Makkah.

﴿ٱقۡتَرَبَتِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ وَٱنشَقَّ ٱلۡقَمَرُ﴾ ﴿وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ ءَايَةٗ يُعۡرِضُواْ وَيَقُولُواْ سِحۡرࣱ مُّسۡتَمِرࣱّ﴾
- The splitting of the moon in the time of the Prophet ﷺ is reported in **Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**.

### The refrain
﴿وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرٖ﴾ — *And We have certainly made the Quran easy to remember, so is there anyone who will remember?*
- Repeated **four times**, after the stories of Nuh, ʿAd, Thamud and Lut.
- **An invitation** to every memorizer: the Quran is made easy.

### Five nations
| Nation | Punishment |
|---|---|
| People of Nuh | The flood |
| ʿAd | A furious wind |
| Thamud | A single blast |
| People of Lut | A storm of stones |
| Pharaoh's people | Seized by the Mighty |

### By measure
﴿إِنَّا كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَٰهُ بِقَدَرٖ﴾ — *Indeed, all things We created with predestination.*

### Lessons
- **Signs are many**; the heart must be open to them.

## "We Have Created Everything According to a Measure": Quran 54:49 Explained

﴿إِنَّا كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَٰهُ بِقَدَرٖ﴾ [Al-Qamar 54:49]

### *Qadar*
- Measure: everything is created with a precise amount and wisdom, in its time, form and quantity.

### Four levels of belief in decree
| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Knowledge | Allah knew everything before it happened |
| Writing | He wrote it in the Preserved Tablet |
| Will | Nothing happens except by His will |
| Creation | He creates everything |

### A comprehensive hadith
- *Everything is by decree, even inability and ability* (narrated by Muslim).

### Decree and effort
- Belief in decree does not mean leaving effort; effort is part of decree. *Act, for each is eased to what he was created for* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Its place
- Near the end of Al-Qamar after the destruction of past nations: nothing happens by chance.

### Lessons
- **Be at peace**: what reached you was never going to miss you.
- **Work** and take the means.
