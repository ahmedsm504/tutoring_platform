﴿وَأَنَّ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطِي مُسۡتَقِيمٗا فَٱتَّبِعُوهُۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ ٱلسُّبُلَ فَتَفَرَّقَ بِكُمۡ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ وَصَّىٰكُم بِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ﴾ [Al-Anam 6:153]

## The hadith of the lines
- The Prophet ﷺ drew a line and said: *This is the path of Allah.* Then he drew lines to its right and left and said: *These are paths; on each is a devil calling to it*, then recited this verse (narrated by Ahmad and al-Nasaʾi in al-Kubra).

## Singular and plural
| The path | The ways |
|---|---|
| Singular: truth is one | Plural: falsehood branches |

## "They will divide you"
- Other ways separate you from Allah's path and from each other.

## The last commandment
- This closes the ten commandments, ending with *that you may be mindful*.

## Lessons
- **Hold to the Quran and Sunnah** as one path.
- **Beware** every call that leads away from them.
