﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدَٰفِعُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ خَوَّانٖ كَفُورٍ﴾ [Al-Hajj 22:38]

## *Yudafiʿ*
- The form indicates **continuous and repeated** defence: again and again, in big and small matters.

## Defence by degree
- Scholars note: Allah's defence is according to the strength of faith. The more faith, the greater the protection.

## Forms of defence
| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| Physical | Protection from harm |
| Spiritual | Protection from doubt and misguidance |
| Social | Turning people's plots back on them |

## The opposite group
- *Khawwan*: very treacherous. *Kafur*: very ungrateful. Allah does not love those who betray trusts and deny blessings.

## Context
- The next verse is the first permission for the oppressed believers to defend themselves.

## Lessons
- **Strengthen faith** to strengthen protection.
- **Be faithful and grateful.**
