**Surah An-Naba** (40 ayat) opens Juz ʿAmma. Its name comes from its second ayah: *the great news*, meaning the Resurrection.

﴿عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾ ﴿عَنِ ٱلنَّبَإِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي هُمۡ فِيهِ مُخۡتَلِفُونَ﴾ — *About what are they asking one another? About the great news, over which they disagree.*

## Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | The question about the great news |
| 6–16 | Signs of Allah's power and blessings |
| 17–20 | The Day of Sorting and the Trumpet |
| 21–30 | Hell, waiting for the transgressors |
| 31–36 | Success for the righteous |
| 37–40 | The Day the Spirit and angels stand in rows |

## Signs all around you
﴿أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَلِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِهَٰدٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡجِبَالَ أَوۡتَادٗا﴾ — *Did We not make the earth a resting place, and the mountains as pegs?*
- The surah lists the earth, mountains, pairs, sleep, night and day, the heavens, the sun, and rain.
- **The argument**: the One who made all this can bring you back to life.

## The final scene
﴿ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ مَـَٔابًا﴾ — *That is the True Day; so whoever wills may take a way back to his Lord.*
﴿وَيَقُولُ ٱلۡكَافِرُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَٰبَۢا﴾ — *And the disbeliever will say: I wish I were dust.*

## Lessons
- **Reflect on daily blessings**, such as sleep, as signs.
- **Choose your way back** to Allah now, while there is time.
