> **Note:** If your child is diagnosed with ADHD, or you suspect it, follow-up with a specialist is essential. These are teaching tips that support memorization, not treatment.

## Change your expectations first
- Don't expect a child with ADHD to sit still for 40 minutes. That is not stubbornness; it is how they are wired.
- **Adapt the method to the child**, not the other way round.

## 1. Short, frequent sessions
- Instead of one 30-minute session, try **three 7–10 minute sessions** across the day.

## 2. Memorize with movement
- **Walk** around the room while repeating the ayah.
- **One move per ayah**: a step, a jump or catching a ball after each portion.
- **Hand signs** for meanings, like raising the hand at ﴿ٱلسَّمَآءِ﴾.

## 3. Quick variety
| Minute | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Listen |
| 3–6 | Repeat with movement |
| 6–9 | A quick game: fill in the missing word |
| 9–10 | Celebrate |

## 4. See the progress
- A **wall chart** with a sticker for every ayah.
- **Short goals**: one ayah, not one surah.

## 5. Immediate, small rewards
- Rewards work best **right away** and **often**: a high five, a sticker, a minute of a favourite activity.

## 6. Stay patient
- Some days will be better than others. **Consistency** over weeks matters more than any single session.
