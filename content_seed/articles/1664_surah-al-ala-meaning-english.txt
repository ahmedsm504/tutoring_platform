**Surah Al-A'la** (19 ayat) was loved by the Prophet ﷺ, who recited it with Al-Ghashiyah in the Friday and Eid prayers [Muslim].

﴿سَبِّحِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡأَعۡلَى﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ﴾
- *Glorify the name of your Lord, the Most High, who created and proportioned, who measured and guided.*
- When this was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ instructed that it be said in prostration: *Subḥāna Rabbiyal-Aʿlā*, as reported by Abu Dawud.

## Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Glorifying Allah, the Creator and Guide |
| 6–8 | The promise: you will not forget, and ease |
| 9–13 | Remind; the fearful will take heed, the wretched will avoid it |
| 14–19 | Success through purification and prayer; the Hereafter is better |

## The promise to the Prophet ﷺ
- *We will make you recite, and you will not forget, except what Allah wills.*

## The path to success
﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَذَكَرَ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّهِۦ فَصَلَّىٰ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ تُؤۡثِرُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓ﴾
| Step | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Purify yourself | From sins and bad character |
| Remember Allah | With your tongue and heart |
| Pray | The fruit of remembrance |

## Lessons
- **Better and more lasting**: weigh every choice by this.
