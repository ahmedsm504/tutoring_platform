Surah Ghafir opens with Allah's names of hope: "the Forgiver of sin, Acceptor of repentance, severe in punishment, Owner of abundance". It tells us that the angels who carry the Throne spend their time praying for believers on earth. At its heart is one of the bravest speeches in the Quran: a believing man from Pharaoh's own family, who had hidden his faith, stood up when Pharaoh decided to kill Musa and said: "Do you kill a man because he says, 'My Lord is Allah'?" The surah also contains a verse every Muslim should know by heart: "And your Lord says: Call upon Me; I will respond to you."

This guide explains Surah Ghafir section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ghafir
- **Number of verses**: 85.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 40, in the twenty-fourth juz.
- **Names**: Ghafir ("the Forgiver"), from verse 3, and Al-Mu'min ("the Believer"), after the believing man of Pharaoh's family.
- **The first of the *Hawamim***: the seven surahs beginning with *Ha Mim*.
- **Main themes**: forgiveness, the angels' du'a, the dominion of Allah, the courage of faith, du'a and the signs of creation.

## Verses 1–6: The Forgiver of Sin
﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ﴾ ﴿غَافِرِ ٱلذَّنۢبِ وَقَابِلِ ٱلتَّوۡبِ شَدِيدِ ٱلۡعِقَابِ ذِي ٱلطَّوۡلِۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ إِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 40:1-3)

**Four descriptions**: **"Forgiver of sin, Acceptor of repentance, severe in punishment, Owner of abundance."** Two names of hope, one of warning, and one of generosity. Scholars noted that hope comes first and is greater.

﴿مَا يُجَٰدِلُ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فَلَا يَغۡرُرۡكَ تَقَلُّبُهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ﴾ ﴿كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٖ وَٱلۡأَحۡزَابُ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡۖ وَهَمَّتۡ كُلُّ أُمَّةِۭ بِرَسُولِهِمۡ لِيَأۡخُذُوهُۖ وَجَٰدَلُواْ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ لِيُدۡحِضُواْ بِهِ ٱلۡحَقَّ فَأَخَذۡتُهُمۡۖ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عِقَابِ﴾ ﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ حَقَّتۡ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ (Quran 40:4-6)

**"No one disputes concerning the signs of Allah except those who disbelieve."** Do not be deceived by their movement through the land: nations before them disputed with falsehood and were seized.

## Verses 7–9: The Angels Praying for You
﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَحۡمِلُونَ ٱلۡعَرۡشَ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهُۥ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَيُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦ وَيَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْۖ رَبَّنَا وَسِعۡتَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖ رَّحۡمَةٗ وَعِلۡمٗا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لِلَّذِينَ تَابُواْ وَٱتَّبَعُواْ سَبِيلَكَ وَقِهِمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿رَبَّنَا وَأَدۡخِلۡهُمۡ جَنَّـٰتِ عَدۡنٍ ٱلَّتِي وَعَدتَّهُمۡ وَمَن صَلَحَ مِنۡ ءَابَآئِهِمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِهِمۡۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿وَقِهِمُ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِۚ وَمَن تَقِ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ يَوۡمَئِذٖ فَقَدۡ رَحِمۡتَهُۥۚ وَذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ (Quran 40:7-9)

**"Those who carry the Throne and those around it exalt Allah with praise of their Lord and believe in Him and ask forgiveness for those who have believed."** Their du'a:
- **"Our Lord, You have encompassed all things in mercy and knowledge, so forgive those who have repented and followed Your way and protect them from the punishment of Hellfire."**
- **"Our Lord, and admit them to gardens of perpetual residence which You have promised them, and whoever was righteous among their fathers, their spouses and their offspring."**
- **"And protect them from the evil consequences."**

**Imagine**: the greatest angels, closest to the Throne, are praying for you by name of your faith, for your forgiveness and for your family to be joined with you in Paradise. The conditions: repent and follow His way.

## Verses 10–22: To Whom Belongs the Dominion Today?
﴿رَفِيعُ ٱلدَّرَجَٰتِ ذُو ٱلۡعَرۡشِ يُلۡقِي ٱلرُّوحَ مِنۡ أَمۡرِهِۦ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ لِيُنذِرَ يَوۡمَ ٱلتَّلَاقِ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ هُم بَٰرِزُونَۖ لَا يَخۡفَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ مِنۡهُمۡ شَيۡءࣱۚ لِّمَنِ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ لِلَّهِ ٱلۡوَٰحِدِ ٱلۡقَهَّارِ﴾ ﴿ٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُجۡزَىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسِۭ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡۚ لَا ظُلۡمَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَرِيعُ ٱلۡحِسَابِ﴾ (Quran 40:15-17)

**"The Day they come forth, nothing concerning them will be concealed from Allah. To whom belongs the dominion this Day? To Allah, the One, the Prevailing."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah will grip the earth and fold the heavens in His right hand, then say: "I am the King; where are the kings of the earth?"

**"This Day every soul will be recompensed for what it earned. No injustice today!"**

﴿وَأَنذِرۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓزِفَةِ إِذِ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ لَدَى ٱلۡحَنَاجِرِ كَٰظِمِينَۚ مَا لِلظَّـٰلِمِينَ مِنۡ حَمِيمٖ وَلَا شَفِيعٖ يُطَاعُ﴾ ﴿يَعۡلَمُ خَآئِنَةَ ٱلۡأَعۡيُنِ وَمَا تُخۡفِي ٱلصُّدُورُ﴾ ﴿وَٱللَّهُ يَقۡضِي بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ لَا يَقۡضُونَ بِشَيۡءٍۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 40:18-20)

**"And warn them of the Approaching Day."** **"He knows that which deceives the eyes and what the breasts conceal."** The stolen glance, the hidden intention: all known.

## Verses 23–46: The Believer of Pharaoh's Family
﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مُوسَىٰ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا وَسُلۡطَٰنٖ مُّبِينٍ﴾ ﴿إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَهَٰمَٰنَ وَقَٰرُونَ فَقَالُواْ سَٰحِرࣱ كَذَّابࣱ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّ مِنۡ عِندِنَا قَالُواْ ٱقۡتُلُوٓاْ أَبۡنَآءَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥ وَٱسۡتَحۡيُواْ نِسَآءَهُمۡۚ وَمَا كَيۡدُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلٖ﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ ذَرُونِيٓ أَقۡتُلۡ مُوسَىٰ وَلۡيَدۡعُ رَبَّهُۥٓۖ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ أَن يُبَدِّلَ دِينَكُمۡ أَوۡ أَن يُظۡهِرَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلۡفَسَادَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰٓ إِنِّي عُذۡتُ بِرَبِّي وَرَبِّكُم مِّن كُلِّ مُتَكَبِّرٖ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡحِسَابِ﴾ (Quran 40:23-27)

**Musa came with clear signs** to Pharaoh, Haman and Qarun. They called him "a magician and a liar". Pharaoh said: **"Let me kill Musa and let him call upon his Lord."** Musa said: **"Indeed, I have sought refuge in my Lord and your Lord from every arrogant one who does not believe in the Day of Account."**

﴿وَقَالَ رَجُلࣱ مُّؤۡمِنࣱ مِّنۡ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ يَكۡتُمُ إِيمَٰنَهُۥٓ أَتَقۡتُلُونَ رَجُلًا أَن يَقُولَ رَبِّيَ ٱللَّهُ وَقَدۡ جَآءَكُم بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ وَإِن يَكُ كَٰذِبٗا فَعَلَيۡهِ كَذِبُهُۥۖ وَإِن يَكُ صَادِقٗا يُصِبۡكُم بَعۡضُ ٱلَّذِي يَعِدُكُمۡۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي مَنۡ هُوَ مُسۡرِفࣱ كَذَّابࣱ﴾ ﴿يَٰقَوۡمِ لَكُمُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ ظَٰهِرِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَمَن يَنصُرُنَا مِنۢ بَأۡسِ ٱللَّهِ إِن جَآءَنَاۚ قَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ مَآ أُرِيكُمۡ إِلَّا مَآ أَرَىٰ وَمَآ أَهۡدِيكُمۡ إِلَّا سَبِيلَ ٱلرَّشَادِ﴾ (Quran 40:28-29)

**The believing man spoke**: **"Do you kill a man merely because he says, 'My Lord is Allah', while he has brought you clear proofs from your Lord?"** His argument was wise: if he is lying, his lie is on him; if he is truthful, some of what he promises will strike you.

**Abu Bakr's courage**: in Sahih al-Bukhari, 'Urwah asked 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr about the worst thing the idolaters did to the Prophet ﷺ. He said that 'Uqbah ibn Abi Mu'ayt came while the Prophet ﷺ was praying near the Ka'bah and twisted his garment around his neck, strangling him severely. Abu Bakr came, pushed him away and said: "Do you kill a man because he says 'My Lord is Allah', when he has brought you clear proofs from your Lord?"

﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِيٓ ءَامَنَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُونِ أَهۡدِكُمۡ سَبِيلَ ٱلرَّشَادِ﴾ ﴿يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنَّمَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا مَتَٰعࣱ وَإِنَّ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ هِيَ دَارُ ٱلۡقَرَارِ﴾ ﴿مَنۡ عَمِلَ سَيِّئَةٗ فَلَا يُجۡزَىٰٓ إِلَّا مِثۡلَهَاۖ وَمَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنࣱ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يَدۡخُلُونَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ يُرۡزَقُونَ فِيهَا بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ﴾ ﴿۞وَيَٰقَوۡمِ مَا لِيٓ أَدۡعُوكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلنَّجَوٰةِ وَتَدۡعُونَنِيٓ إِلَى ٱلنَّارِ﴾ ﴿تَدۡعُونَنِي لِأَكۡفُرَ بِٱللَّهِ وَأُشۡرِكَ بِهِۦ مَا لَيۡسَ لِي بِهِۦ عِلۡمࣱ وَأَنَا۠ أَدۡعُوكُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡغَفَّـٰرِ﴾ ﴿لَا جَرَمَ أَنَّمَا تَدۡعُونَنِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ لَيۡسَ لَهُۥ دَعۡوَةࣱ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَلَا فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَأَنَّ مَرَدَّنَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَأَنَّ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ هُمۡ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ ﴿فَسَتَذۡكُرُونَ مَآ أَقُولُ لَكُمۡۚ وَأُفَوِّضُ أَمۡرِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَصِيرُۢ بِٱلۡعِبَادِ﴾ (Quran 40:38-44)

**The believer's sermon**: **"O my people, follow me; I will guide you to the way of right conduct. O my people, this worldly life is only enjoyment, and indeed, the hereafter is the home of permanent settlement."** **"Why is it that I invite you to salvation while you invite me to the Fire?"** Then his final words: **"And you will remember what I tell you, and I entrust my affair to Allah. Indeed, Allah is Seeing of His servants."**

**"Wa ufawwidu amri ila Allah"**: after doing everything he could, he handed the outcome to Allah.

﴿فَوَقَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ سَيِّـَٔاتِ مَا مَكَرُواْۖ وَحَاقَ بِـَٔالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ سُوٓءُ ٱلۡعَذَابِ﴾ ﴿ٱلنَّارُ يُعۡرَضُونَ عَلَيۡهَا غُدُوّٗا وَعَشِيّٗاۚ وَيَوۡمَ تَقُومُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ أَدۡخِلُوٓاْ ءَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ أَشَدَّ ٱلۡعَذَابِ﴾ (Quran 40:45-46)

**"So Allah protected him from the evils they plotted, and the people of Pharaoh were enveloped by the worst of punishment: the Fire, they are exposed to it morning and evening. And the Day the Hour appears: Admit the people of Pharaoh into the severest punishment."** Scholars cite this verse as evidence of the punishment in the grave: exposure to the Fire morning and evening before the Day of Resurrection.

## Verses 47–55: Victory and Patience
﴿إِنَّا لَنَنصُرُ رُسُلَنَا وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَيَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلۡأَشۡهَٰدُ﴾ (Quran 40:51)

**"Indeed, We will support Our messengers and those who believe during the life of this world and on the Day when the witnesses will stand."** Victory may be in this world, in the hereafter, or both.

﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ وَٱلۡإِبۡكَٰرِ﴾ (Quran 40:55)

**"So be patient. Indeed, the promise of Allah is truth. And ask forgiveness for your sin and exalt with praise of your Lord in the evening and the morning."**

## Verses 56–60: Call Upon Me
﴿وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدۡعُونِيٓ أَسۡتَجِبۡ لَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ عَنۡ عِبَادَتِي سَيَدۡخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 40:60)

**"And your Lord says: Call upon Me; I will respond to you. Indeed, those who disdain My worship will enter Hell rendered contemptible."** Abu Dawud and at-Tirmidhi reported that an-Nu'man ibn Bashir said the Prophet ﷺ said: "Du'a is worship," then recited this verse. At-Tirmidhi graded it *hasan sahih*.

**Notice**: refusing to make du'a is called being arrogant towards His worship. Du'a is not only asking; it is admitting your need for Allah.

**Etiquette of du'a**: begin with praise of Allah and blessings on the Prophet ﷺ, be certain of the response, choose the blessed times (the last third of the night, in prostration, between adhan and iqamah, Friday afternoon), and do not be hasty. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Your du'a is answered as long as you are not hasty, saying: I prayed but was not answered."

## Verses 61–68: Signs of Creation
﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلَّيۡلَ لِتَسۡكُنُواْ فِيهِ وَٱلنَّهَارَ مُبۡصِرًاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَذُو فَضۡلٍ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡ خَٰلِقُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُؤۡفَكُونَ﴾ ﴿كَذَٰلِكَ يُؤۡفَكُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَانُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ يَجۡحَدُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ قَرَارٗا وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بِنَآءٗ وَصَوَّرَكُمۡ فَأَحۡسَنَ صُوَرَكُمۡ وَرَزَقَكُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡۖ فَتَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ فَٱدۡعُوهُ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَۗ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿۞قُلۡ إِنِّي نُهِيتُ أَنۡ أَعۡبُدَ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَمَّا جَآءَنِيَ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتُ مِن رَّبِّي وَأُمِرۡتُ أَنۡ أُسۡلِمَ لِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ مِنۡ عَلَقَةٖ ثُمَّ يُخۡرِجُكُمۡ طِفۡلٗا ثُمَّ لِتَبۡلُغُوٓاْ أَشُدَّكُمۡ ثُمَّ لِتَكُونُواْ شُيُوخٗاۚ وَمِنكُم مَّن يُتَوَفَّىٰ مِن قَبۡلُۖ وَلِتَبۡلُغُوٓاْ أَجَلٗا مُّسَمّٗى وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۖ فَإِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمۡرٗا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾ (Quran 40:61-68)

**The night for rest and the day for seeing.** **The earth as a place of settlement and the sky as a ceiling.** **"He formed you and perfected your forms."** **The stages of man**: from dust, then a drop, then a clinging clot, then a child, then full strength, then old age, and some die before. **"It is He who gives life and causes death."**

## Verses 69–85: The End of the Disputers
﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلٗا مِّن قَبۡلِكَ مِنۡهُم مَّن قَصَصۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن لَّمۡ نَقۡصُصۡ عَلَيۡكَۗ وَمَا كَانَ لِرَسُولٍ أَن يَأۡتِيَ بِـَٔايَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ فَإِذَا جَآءَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ قُضِيَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَخَسِرَ هُنَالِكَ ٱلۡمُبۡطِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 40:78)

**"We have already sent messengers before you. Among them are those whose stories We have related to you, and among them are those whose stories We have not related to you."** Not every prophet is named in the Quran.

﴿فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡاْ بَأۡسَنَا قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ وَحۡدَهُۥ وَكَفَرۡنَا بِمَا كُنَّا بِهِۦ مُشۡرِكِينَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمۡ يَكُ يَنفَعُهُمۡ إِيمَٰنُهُمۡ لَمَّا رَأَوۡاْ بَأۡسَنَاۖ سُنَّتَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّتِي قَدۡ خَلَتۡ فِي عِبَادِهِۦۖ وَخَسِرَ هُنَالِكَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 40:84-85)

**Faith too late**: **"When they saw Our punishment, they said: We believe in Allah alone. But never did their faith benefit them once they saw Our punishment."** Repentance is accepted before death arrives, not after the punishment is seen.

## Word Meanings
- **Ghafir**: Forgiver.
- **Qabil at-tawb**: Acceptor of repentance.
- **Dhi at-tawl**: Owner of abundance.
- **Hamalat al-'Arsh**: carriers of the Throne.
- **Yawm at-Talaq**: the Day of Meeting.
- **Al-Azifah**: the Approaching Day.
- **Kha'inat al-a'yun**: deceiving glances.
- **Sarh**: a tower.
- **Ufawwidu**: I entrust.
- **Dar al-qarar**: the home of permanent settlement.
- **Ghuduwwan wa 'ashiyya**: morning and evening.
- **Ad'uni**: call upon Me.
- **Dakhirin**: contemptible.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Hope before warning** in the opening names.
2. **The angels' du'a**: beginning "You have encompassed all things in mercy and knowledge".
3. **"To whom belongs the dominion this Day?"**: a question with only one answer.
4. **The believer's logic**: calm, wise and fearless.
5. **"I entrust my affair to Allah"**: four words of complete trust.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Ha Mim*, the meem stretched six counts.
- **Izhar**: in *rajulan an yaqula* (verse 28), the tanween before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *ad'uni astajib lakum* (verse 60), the ba' sakinah of *astajib* bounces lightly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 13).
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna alladhina* (verse 60), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Az-Zumar before it ended with the groups driven to Paradise and the angels around the Throne, and Ghafir opens with the carriers of the Throne praying for believers. Surah Fussilat after it continues the *Hawamim* and the warnings to those who turn away.

## Lessons From the Believer of Pharaoh's Family
1. **Speak the truth when it matters**, even in a hostile place.
2. **Use wisdom in argument.**
3. **Remind people that this world is temporary.**
4. **Do your part and entrust the outcome to Allah.**
5. **Allah protects those who stand for Him.**

## Practical Lessons
1. **Never despair**: Allah is the Forgiver of sin.
2. **Repent and follow His way** to be included in the angels' du'a.
3. **Guard your glances**: He knows the deceiving eyes.
4. **Say "I entrust my affair to Allah"** in difficulties.
5. **Make du'a constantly**: it is worship.
6. **Do not delay repentance** until it is too late.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is the surah called Ghafir and Al-Mu'min?
Ghafir ("the Forgiver") from verse 3, and Al-Mu'min ("the Believer") after the believing man of Pharaoh's family.

### What do the angels who carry the Throne pray for?
Forgiveness for those who repent and follow Allah's way, protection from Hell, and admission to Paradise with their righteous families.

### Who was the believer of Pharaoh's family?
A man from Pharaoh's household who hid his faith and spoke up to defend Musa.

### Does Surah Ghafir mention the punishment of the grave?
Scholars cite verse 46, about Pharaoh's people being exposed to the Fire morning and evening, as evidence for it.

### What does "Call upon Me; I will respond to you" teach?
That du'a is worship, and Allah promises to respond; the Prophet ﷺ said "Du'a is worship" and recited this verse.

### Is faith accepted after seeing the punishment?
No. Verse 85 says faith did not benefit those who believed only after seeing Allah's punishment.

### How many verses are in Surah Ghafir?
85 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Ghafir opens the door of forgiveness wide, shows the greatest angels praying for you, and tells the story of a believer who spoke the truth in Pharaoh's palace and entrusted his affair to Allah. Then it gives you the simplest and greatest key: call upon your Lord, and He will respond.
