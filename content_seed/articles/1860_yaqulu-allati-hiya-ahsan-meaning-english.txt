﴿وَقُل لِّعِبَادِي يَقُولُواْ ٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ يَنزَغُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ كَانَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ عَدُوّٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:53]

## "My servants"
- Allah calls them **My servants**, a reminder that good speech is part of servitude.

## Not just good, but best
- *That which is best*: when you have a good option and a better one, choose the better.

## Satan's tool
- *Nazgh* means stirring up and provoking. A single harsh word can open a door for Satan between brothers, spouses or friends.

## Practical guidance
| Situation | Best words |
|---|---|
| Disagreement | Calm tone, no insults |
| Advice | Private and gentle |
| Anger | Silence until calm |
| Online | Think before posting |

## A Prophetic reminder
- *Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him say good or remain silent* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Lessons
- **Pause before speaking**: is there a better word?
- **Close Satan's door** with kind speech.
