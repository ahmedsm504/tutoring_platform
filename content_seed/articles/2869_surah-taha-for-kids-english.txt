"Rabbish-rahli sadri, wa yassir li amri, wahlul 'uqdatan min lisani, yafqahu qawli." "My Lord, expand my chest for me, make my task easy for me, and untie the knot from my tongue so they may understand my speech." Many children memorize this du'a and say it before exams or before speaking in front of the class. But many don't know it was the du'a of Prophet Musa, peace be upon him, when Allah commanded him to go to the most powerful king of his time: Pharaoh. This du'a is in Surah Taha, which tells Musa's story in beautiful detail, from the night he saw a fire to his victory over Pharaoh's magicians.

Surah Taha also contains a short, powerful du'a Allah taught the Prophet ﷺ: "Rabbi zidni 'ilma", "My Lord, increase me in knowledge", the du'a of every student. And it has scenes children love: a staff that turns into a snake, a baby in a box floating on a river, and magicians throwing ropes that seemed to move. In this guide, we explain Surah Taha to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Taha

- **Position**: surah 20, in juz 16.
- **Number of verses**: 135.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: it begins with the letters Ta Ha, among the disconnected letters.

### From the seerah
Books of seerah mention that 'Umar ibn al-Khattab accepted Islam after hearing verses from the beginning of Surah Taha in his sister's house. The story is famous, though hadith scholars discuss its chains.

## Lesson 1: The Quran Is Not a Burden

﴿طه﴾ ﴿مَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِتَشۡقَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا تَذۡكِرَةٗ لِّمَن يَخۡشَىٰ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلٗا مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ ٱلۡعُلَى﴾ (Quran 20:1-4)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah tells the Prophet ﷺ: We did not send you the Quran to make you miserable and tired, but as a reminder for those who fear Allah. The Quran brings happiness and peace, not misery. When we read and memorize it, we feel calm and happy."

### Lesson for kids
"If memorizing feels hard, remember: the Quran came to make you happy. Split it into small pieces and enjoy every verse."

## Lesson 2: The Fire in the Night

﴿وَهَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ مُوسَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ رَءَا نَارٗا فَقَالَ لِأَهۡلِهِ ٱمۡكُثُوٓاْ إِنِّيٓ ءَانَسۡتُ نَارٗا لَّعَلِّيٓ ءَاتِيكُم مِّنۡهَا بِقَبَسٍ أَوۡ أَجِدُ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ هُدٗى﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّآ أَتَىٰهَا نُودِيَ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنِّيٓ أَنَا۠ رَبُّكَ فَٱخۡلَعۡ نَعۡلَيۡكَ إِنَّكَ بِٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡمُقَدَّسِ طُوٗى﴾ (Quran 20:9-12)

### Explaining it to your child
"Musa was travelling through the desert with his family on a cold, dark night, and they had lost their way. He saw a fire in the distance and told his family: Wait here, I've seen a fire. Maybe I can bring you a burning torch to warm you, or find someone there to guide us. When he reached it, he heard a mighty call: O Musa, I am your Lord, so take off your sandals. You are in the sacred valley of Tuwa."

### Lesson for kids: respecting sacred places
"Allah told Musa to remove his sandals out of respect for the sacred valley. We take off our shoes when we enter the masjid and walk calmly inside, out of respect for the house of Allah."

## Lesson 3: Musa's Staff

﴿وَمَا تِلۡكَ بِيَمِينِكَ يَٰمُوسَىٰ﴾ ﴿قَالَ هِيَ عَصَايَ أَتَوَكَّؤُاْ عَلَيۡهَا وَأَهُشُّ بِهَا عَلَىٰ غَنَمِي وَلِيَ فِيهَا مَـَٔارِبُ أُخۡرَىٰ﴾ ﴿قَالَ أَلۡقِهَا يَٰمُوسَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَأَلۡقَىٰهَا فَإِذَا هِيَ حَيَّةࣱ تَسۡعَىٰ﴾ ﴿قَالَ خُذۡهَا وَلَا تَخَفۡۖ سَنُعِيدُهَا سِيرَتَهَا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ﴾ (Quran 20:17-21)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah asked Musa: What is that in your right hand, O Musa? Musa said: It is my staff. I lean on it when I'm tired, I knock leaves down with it for my sheep to eat, and I have other uses for it. Allah said: Throw it down. He threw it, and suddenly it was a snake moving quickly! Musa was scared. Allah said: Take it and don't be afraid. We will return it to how it was."

### Activity: "What's in your hand?"
Ask your child while they're holding something: "What is that in your hand?" They answer and list its uses like Musa: "This is my pencil. I write with it, I draw with it, and I do my homework with it." They practise expression and gratitude for blessings.

## Lesson 4: "My Lord, Expand My Chest"

﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشۡرَحۡ لِي صَدۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَيَسِّرۡ لِيٓ أَمۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَٱحۡلُلۡ عُقۡدَةٗ مِّن لِّسَانِي﴾ ﴿يَفۡقَهُواْ قَوۡلِي﴾ (Quran 20:25-28)

### Explaining it to your child
"Musa knew the task was very hard, but he didn't say: I can't. He asked Allah for help: My Lord, give me confidence and calm, make my task easy, and untie the knot in my tongue so I can speak clearly and people understand me."

### Activity: a du'a before the task
Teach your child to say this du'a before exams, before speaking in class, before reciting to their teacher, and before anything they feel nervous about. Write it on a small card for their school bag.

## Lesson 5: Baby Musa in the Chest

﴿إِذۡ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّكَ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَنِ ٱقۡذِفِيهِ فِي ٱلتَّابُوتِ فَٱقۡذِفِيهِ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ فَلۡيُلۡقِهِ ٱلۡيَمُّ بِٱلسَّاحِلِ يَأۡخُذۡهُ عَدُوࣱّ لِّي وَعَدُوࣱّ لَّهُۥۚ وَأَلۡقَيۡتُ عَلَيۡكَ مَحَبَّةٗ مِّنِّي وَلِتُصۡنَعَ عَلَىٰ عَيۡنِيٓ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ تَمۡشِيٓ أُخۡتُكَ فَتَقُولُ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَن يَكۡفُلُهُۥۖ فَرَجَعۡنَٰكَ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّكَ كَيۡ تَقَرَّ عَيۡنُهَا وَلَا تَحۡزَنَۚ وَقَتَلۡتَ نَفۡسٗا فَنَجَّيۡنَٰكَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَمِّ وَفَتَنَّـٰكَ فُتُونٗاۚ فَلَبِثۡتَ سِنِينَ فِيٓ أَهۡلِ مَدۡيَنَ ثُمَّ جِئۡتَ عَلَىٰ قَدَرٖ يَٰمُوسَىٰ﴾ (Quran 20:38-40)

### Explaining it to your child
"Years earlier, when Musa was born, Pharaoh was killing the baby boys of the Children of Israel. Allah inspired Musa's mother: Put him in a chest and place the chest in the river. The river carried him all the way to Pharaoh's own palace! They took him in, and Pharaoh's wife loved him. Baby Musa refused to drink milk from any nurse, so his sister came and said: Shall I show you a family who will look after him? So Allah returned him to his mother, so she would be happy and not grieve. And Allah told Musa: I placed love for you from Me, so that you would be raised under My care."

### Lesson for kids: Allah protects
"Who would imagine that the baby Pharaoh was searching for would be raised in Pharaoh's own palace? Allah protects whoever He wills, in the most amazing ways."

## Lesson 6: Speak Gently

﴿ٱذۡهَبَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَقُولَا لَهُۥ قَوۡلٗا لَّيِّنٗا لَّعَلَّهُۥ يَتَذَكَّرُ أَوۡ يَخۡشَىٰ﴾ ﴿قَالَا رَبَّنَآ إِنَّنَا نَخَافُ أَن يَفۡرُطَ عَلَيۡنَآ أَوۡ أَن يَطۡغَىٰ﴾ ﴿قَالَ لَا تَخَافَآۖ إِنَّنِي مَعَكُمَآ أَسۡمَعُ وَأَرَىٰ﴾ (Quran 20:43-46)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah commanded Musa and his brother Harun to go to Pharaoh, who had become a tyrant and claimed to be a god. Still, Allah said: Speak to him gently, so that he may remember or fear. Musa and Harun were afraid, so Allah said: Don't be afraid. I am with you both, hearing and seeing."

### Lesson for kids: gentle words
"If Allah told Musa to speak gently to Pharaoh, the worst person on earth, how should we speak to our siblings and friends? With kind, calm words, even when they are wrong."

### Activity: loud voice, gentle voice
Act out a scene: your child wants their brother to give back a toy. First loudly: "Give me my toy!" Then gently: "Could you give my toy back, please?" Ask: "Which one makes your brother listen faster?"

## Lesson 7: The Magicians Who Believed

﴿وَأَلۡقِ مَا فِي يَمِينِكَ تَلۡقَفۡ مَا صَنَعُوٓاْۖ إِنَّمَا صَنَعُواْ كَيۡدُ سَٰحِرٖۖ وَلَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلسَّاحِرُ حَيۡثُ أَتَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَأُلۡقِيَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ سُجَّدٗا قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِرَبِّ هَٰرُونَ وَمُوسَىٰ﴾ (Quran 20:69-70)

### Explaining it to your child
"Musa threw his staff and it became a real snake that swallowed all their ropes and sticks! The magicians knew magic very well, so they knew this wasn't magic, it was the truth from Allah. They fell down in prostration and said: We believe in the Lord of Harun and Musa."

﴿قَالُواْ لَن نُّؤۡثِرَكَ عَلَىٰ مَا جَآءَنَا مِنَ ٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنَاۖ فَٱقۡضِ مَآ أَنتَ قَاضٍۖ إِنَّمَا تَقۡضِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ﴾ (Quran 20:72)

### Lesson for kids: standing firm on the truth
"An hour earlier the magicians were on Pharaoh's side. When they recognised the truth, they held on to it and weren't scared by his threats. The truth is worth standing firm for."

## Lesson 8: "My Lord, Increase Me in Knowledge"

﴿فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۗ وَلَا تَعۡجَلۡ بِٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يُقۡضَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَحۡيُهُۥۖ وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدۡنِي عِلۡمٗا﴾ (Quran 20:114)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah told the Prophet ﷺ to pray: My Lord, increase me in knowledge. Knowledge is something we always ask for more of. And Allah didn't tell His Prophet to ask for more of anything else except knowledge!"

### Activity: the study du'a
Make "Rabbi zidni 'ilma" the first thing your child says when they open a book or start studying, and write it beautifully on the cover of their notebook.

## Lesson 9: Tell Your Family to Pray

﴿وَأۡمُرۡ أَهۡلَكَ بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ عَلَيۡهَاۖ لَا نَسۡـَٔلُكَ رِزۡقٗاۖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُكَۗ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰ﴾ (Quran 20:132)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah says: Tell your family to pray, and be patient in it yourself. We don't ask you for provision; We provide for you. And the good end belongs to those who are mindful of Allah. In a Muslim family, everyone reminds each other about prayer: Dad, Mum and even the children."

### Activity: the little prayer caller
Make your child responsible for reminding the family about one prayer every day, such as Asr. They call out in a nice voice: "Prayer time, everyone!" Praise them every time.

## Let's Read Together: The One Who Gave Everything Its Form

﴿قَالَ فَمَن رَّبُّكُمَا يَٰمُوسَىٰ﴾ ﴿قَالَ رَبُّنَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَعۡطَىٰ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلۡقَهُۥ ثُمَّ هَدَىٰ﴾ (Quran 20:49-50)

### Explaining it to your child
"Pharaoh asked: Who is your Lord, O Musa? Musa gave an amazing answer: Our Lord is the One who gave everything its form and then guided it, teaching it how to live. The bird knows how to build its nest, the bee knows its way, and the newborn baby knows how to feed. Allah guided them all."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The First Speech"

Hind was very nervous. Her teacher had asked her to give the morning assembly speech tomorrow, in front of the whole school. She couldn't sleep well and told her mother: "I can't do it. I'll stutter, and everyone will laugh at me."

Her mother sat next to her and said: "Hind, did you know that Prophet Musa found it hard to speak? And Allah sent him to Pharaoh himself! What did he do? He prayed: My Lord, expand my chest, make my task easy, untie the knot from my tongue so they understand my speech. And Allah answered him."

Hind memorized the du'a and practised her speech in front of her mother three times. In the morning, before stepping onto the stage, she closed her eyes and whispered the du'a. Then she gave her speech in a clear voice, and everyone clapped.

Hind came home delighted. "Mum, the du'a really helped!" Her mother said: "Du'a plus practice, that's the secret. Musa made du'a, then he went and did the work."

Ask your child: "What did Hind say before her speech? And what else did she do besides the du'a?"

## What We Learn From Surah Taha

- **The Quran brings happiness**, not misery.
- **Respect sacred places**.
- **Make du'a** before hard tasks.
- **Allah protects** in amazing ways.
- **Speak gently**, even to people who are wrong.
- **Stand firm** on the truth, like the magicians.
- **Always ask for more knowledge**.
- **Remind your family** about prayer.

## Questions to Ask Your Child

1. Why did Musa go towards the fire?
2. What did Allah tell Musa when he arrived?
3. What uses of his staff did Musa mention?
4. What du'a did Musa make before going to Pharaoh?
5. How did Allah tell Musa to speak to Pharaoh?
6. What did the magicians do when they saw the truth?
7. What is the one thing Allah told His Prophet to ask for more of?

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Was Musa's staff magic?
No, it was a miracle from Allah, and the magicians themselves knew the difference, so they believed. Magic is an illusion; a miracle is real, from Allah.

### How do I explain that Pharaoh killed babies without scaring my child?
It's enough to say: "Pharaoh was very cruel and hurt people, and Allah protected Musa when he was little." Focus on Allah's protection and care.

### Can we read the whole of Surah Taha with a child in one sitting?
It's better to split it into scenes: the fire and the valley, then the staff, then the chest on the river, then the magicians. Each scene is enough for one short session.
