[Surah Al-Kahf](/quran/18/) is one of the most loved surahs, recited by many Muslims every Friday. It's a great memorization project once you've finished Juz Amma and Tabarak, and it's easier than it looks because it's **built around stories**, and stories stick.

## Quick facts
- **110 verses**, pages 293–304 in the Madinah Mushaf (about 12 pages).
- A Makkan surah, in juz 15 and 16.

## Its authentic virtue
The Prophet ﷺ said: **"Whoever memorizes ten verses from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjal."** (Muslim). So the first ten verses come first.

## The surah's map
| Section | Verses | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 1–8 | Praise of Allah, the Book, the warning |
| People of the Cave | 9–26 | The young men in the cave |
| Reflection | 27–31 | Staying with the righteous; two outcomes |
| Owner of the two gardens | 32–44 | The arrogant man and his gardens |
| Lessons | 45–59 | The parable of this life, the Day of Judgement, Iblis |
| Musa and Al-Khidr | 60–82 | Musa's journey with the righteous servant |
| Dhul-Qarnayn | 83–98 | His journeys and the barrier |
| Closing | 99–110 | The Hereafter and deeds |

## The 4-week plan (5 days a week)
- **Week 1:** verses 1–26 (opening + People of the Cave), about 5 verses a day. Master the first ten before moving on.
- **Week 2:** verses 27–49, about 4–5 a day.
- **Week 3:** verses 50–82 (Iblis, then Musa and Al-Khidr), 6–7 short verses a day.
- **Week 4:** verses 83–110 (Dhul-Qarnayn and the closing), 5–6 a day.

Use one day each week to revise the whole week, and one to rest or read the full surah from the Mushaf.

## Tips specific to Al-Kahf
1. **Know the story before memorizing it.** Read a short tafsir of the section first.
2. **Watch the similar phrases in the Musa–Khidr story.** Compare verse 72, ﴿قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُلۡ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرًا﴾, with verse 75, ﴿قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكَ إِنَّكَ لَن تَسۡتَطِيعَ مَعِيَ صَبۡرًا﴾, which adds "*laka*". Write them side by side.
3. **The Sakt in verse 1:** in Hafs there's a brief pause without breath on ﴿عِوَجَاۜ﴾ when continuing. Learn it from day one.
4. **Recite your portion on Fridays** to combine revision with the Friday habit.

## After memorizing
- Recite it fully from memory every Friday.
- Pray with parts of it at night.
- Revise one story a day for two weeks after finishing.
