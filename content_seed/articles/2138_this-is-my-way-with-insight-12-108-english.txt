﴿قُلۡ هَٰذِهِۦ سَبِيلِيٓ أَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ عَلَىٰ بَصِيرَةٍ أَنَا۠ وَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَنِيۖ وَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾ [Yusuf 12:108]

## "This is my way"
- My path and method: tawhid and calling to it.

## "To Allah"
- Not to oneself, a group or a party.

## *Basirah*
| Aspect | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Knowledge | Of what you call to |
| Certainty | In its truth |
| Wisdom | In method and timing |

## "And whoever follows me"
- Every follower of the Prophet ﷺ is a caller according to knowledge and ability.

## End of Surah Yusuf
- It closes a surah full of daʿwah: Yusuf called in prison and as a minister.

## Lessons
- **Learn before you call.**
- **Invite** at home and work by example and word.
