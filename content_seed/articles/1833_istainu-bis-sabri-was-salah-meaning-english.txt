﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱسۡتَعِينُواْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:153]

## Two tools
| Tool | Role |
|---|---|
| Patience | Holding the heart steady |
| Prayer | Connecting the heart to Allah |

- Patience is inward strength; prayer is the source that refills it.

## The Prophet's habit
- Hudhayfah reported that when something distressed the Prophet ﷺ, **he would pray** (narrated by Abu Dawud).

## Allah is with the patient
- The verse ends with a promise of Allah's special company: support, help and protection.

## The context
- The verse comes right before the verses of tests (2:155-157): first the tools, then the trials.

## Applying it
- Before a difficult conversation, pray two rakʿahs.
- When anxious, make wudu and pray.
- When waiting for results, be patient and keep praying.

## Lessons
- **Run to prayer**, not away from it, when life is hard.
- **Patience is a skill** that grows with practice.
