## Surah Al-Hajj Meaning: The Call to Pilgrimage and Hearts That See

**Surah Al-Hajj** (The Pilgrimage, 78 ayat) combines warnings of the Hour with the rituals of Hajj.

### The earthquake of the Hour
- It opens: *O mankind, fear your Lord. Indeed the convulsion of the Hour is a tremendous thing*, so terrible that a nursing mother forgets her infant.

### Proof of resurrection
- If you doubt the resurrection, look at your own creation from dust, then a drop, and at the dead earth that **stirs and swells** when rain falls.

### Ibrahim's call
﴿وَأَذِّن فِي ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَجِّ يَأۡتُوكَ رِجَالٗا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٖ يَأۡتِينَ مِن كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٖ﴾
- People still answer this call from every corner of the earth.

### The meaning of sacrifice
- *Their meat will not reach Allah, nor their blood, but what reaches Him is piety from you.*

### Permission to defend
- Fighting was first permitted for those who were wronged and expelled from their homes only for saying "Our Lord is Allah".

### Hearts that see
﴿أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَتَكُونَ لَهُمۡ قُلُوبࣱ يَعۡقِلُونَ بِهَآ أَوۡ ءَاذَانࣱ يَسۡمَعُونَ بِهَاۖ فَإِنَّهَا لَا تَعۡمَى ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَلَٰكِن تَعۡمَى ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلَّتِي فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾

### Lessons
- **Rituals are about the heart**: piety, not the outward act alone.
- **Reflection is sight**: travel and look with an understanding heart.

## "Whoever Honours the Symbols of Allah, It Is from the Piety of Hearts": Quran 22:32 Explained

﴿ذَٰلِكَۖ وَمَن يُعَظِّمۡ شَعَـٰٓئِرَ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّهَا مِن تَقۡوَى ٱلۡقُلُوبِ﴾ [Al-Hajj 22:32]

### What are *shaʿaʾir*?
- Signs and rituals that Allah made marks of His religion.
| Symbol | Example |
|---|---|
| Hajj rites | Safa and Marwah, the sacrificial animals |
| Places | The Kaʿbah, the mosques |
| Acts | Prayer, the adhan, fasting |
| The Quran | Its recitation and the Mushaf |

### Honouring them
- In the context, scholars mention choosing good, healthy sacrificial animals. More broadly: respect, care and doing them well.

### "From the piety of hearts"
- Honour for the symbols **comes from** the heart. Outward carelessness often shows inner weakness.

### Taqwa is in the heart
- The Prophet ﷺ pointed to his chest and said: *Taqwa is here* (narrated by Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Treat the Mushaf and the mosque with respect.**
- **Do acts of worship beautifully.**

## "Allah Defends Those Who Believe": Quran 22:38 Explained

﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدَٰفِعُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ خَوَّانٖ كَفُورٍ﴾ [Al-Hajj 22:38]

### *Yudafiʿ*
- The form indicates **continuous and repeated** defence: again and again, in big and small matters.

### Defence by degree
- Scholars note: Allah's defence is according to the strength of faith. The more faith, the greater the protection.

### Forms of defence
| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| Physical | Protection from harm |
| Spiritual | Protection from doubt and misguidance |
| Social | Turning people's plots back on them |

### The opposite group
- *Khawwan*: very treacherous. *Kafur*: very ungrateful. Allah does not love those who betray trusts and deny blessings.

### Context
- The next verse is the first permission for the oppressed believers to defend themselves.

### Lessons
- **Strengthen faith** to strengthen protection.
- **Be faithful and grateful.**

## "It Is Not the Eyes That Are Blind, But the Hearts": Quran 22:46 Explained

﴿أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَتَكُونَ لَهُمۡ قُلُوبࣱ يَعۡقِلُونَ بِهَآ أَوۡ ءَاذَانࣱ يَسۡمَعُونَ بِهَاۖ فَإِنَّهَا لَا تَعۡمَى ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَلَٰكِن تَعۡمَى ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلَّتِي فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾ [Al-Hajj 22:46]

### Travel with purpose
- Seeing ruins of past nations should lead to **reflection**, not just sightseeing.

### Hearts that reason
- The verse links reasoning to the **heart**: true understanding includes conscience and faith.

### Real blindness
| Blind eyes | Blind heart |
|---|---|
| Cannot see colours | Cannot see truth |
| Not blameworthy | Dangerous |

### Signs of a blind heart
- Feeling nothing at the Quran, repeating sins without regret, and seeing reminders without learning.

### Cure
- Repentance, remembrance, the Quran, and good company.

### Lessons
- **Ask for a seeing heart.**
- **Reflect** on what you see and hear.

## Surah Al-Muminun Meaning: The Qualities of Successful Believers

**Surah Al-Muminun** (The Believers, 118 ayat) opens by announcing success.

### Who succeeds?
﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي صَلَاتِهِمۡ خَٰشِعُونَ﴾
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Khushuʿ | Humble focus in prayer |
| Avoiding idle talk | Time and words are valued |
| Zakah | Purifying wealth and self |
| Chastity | Guarding private parts except in marriage |
| Trust | Keeping trusts and promises |
| Guarding prayers | Praying on time, consistently |

- Their reward: they will inherit **al-Firdaws**, the highest Paradise.

### From a drop to a human being
- The surah describes the stages of creation: a drop, then a clinging clot, then a lump, then bones clothed with flesh, then **another creation**.

### One message, many prophets
- After the stories of Nuh, Hud, Musa and ʿIsa, Allah says this religion of yours is **one religion**, yet people split into sects, each happy with what it has.

### The regret at death
- When death comes, the disbeliever asks to return to do good. The answer: *No! It is only a word he is saying.*

### Lessons
- **Success begins in prayer.**
- **Use life before the wish to return.**

## "Did You Think That We Created You Without Purpose?": Quran 23:115-116 Explained

﴿أَفَحَسِبۡتُمۡ أَنَّمَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ عَبَثٗا وَأَنَّكُمۡ إِلَيۡنَا لَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ ﴿فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ﴾ [Al-Muminun 23:115-116]

### *ʿAbath*
- Action without wisdom or goal.

### Two false assumptions
| Assumption | Reality |
|---|---|
| We were created without purpose | We were created to worship |
| There is no return | The return is to Allah |

### "Exalted is Allah"
- Allah is far above aimlessness: the **King** who governs, the **Truth** from whom nothing false comes.

### Its place
- After the scene of the people of the Fire asked *How many years did you remain on earth?*: the world seemed short in their eyes.

### Lessons
- **Ask yourself**: why do I live?
- **Give each day** a goal that pleases Allah.

## "Let Them Pardon and Overlook": Quran 24:22 Explained

﴿وَلَا يَأۡتَلِ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡفَضۡلِ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلسَّعَةِ أَن يُؤۡتُوٓاْ أُوْلِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينَ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ وَلۡيَعۡفُواْ وَلۡيَصۡفَحُوٓاْۗ أَلَا تُحِبُّونَ أَن يَغۡفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمٌ﴾ [An-Nur 24:22]

### The story
- Abu Bakr used to support his relative Mistah ibn Uthathah, a poor emigrant. Mistah was among those who spoke in the slander against Aisha. Abu Bakr swore to stop supporting him. This verse was revealed, and Abu Bakr said: **"Yes, by Allah, I love that Allah forgives me,"** and resumed his support (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Key phrases
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Let not those of virtue and wealth swear* | Do not vow to stop giving |
| *Relatives, the poor, emigrants* | Those who deserve support |
| *Pardon* | Drop the punishment |
| *Overlook* | Turn the page, without blame |
| *Do you not love that Allah forgives you?* | Forgive others as you hope to be forgiven |

### The highest example
- Abu Bakr's daughter was slandered, yet he forgave. That is the level the verse calls to.

### Lessons
- **Do not cut kindness** because of a wrong done to you.
- **Forgive to be forgiven.**

## "Do Not Enter Houses Other Than Your Own Until You Ask Permission": Quran 24:27 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَدۡخُلُواْ بُيُوتًا غَيۡرَ بُيُوتِكُمۡ حَتَّىٰ تَسۡتَأۡنِسُواْ وَتُسَلِّمُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهَاۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ [An-Nur 24:27]

### *Tastaʾnisu*
- Many scholars: ask permission. The word *uns* hints that permission should come with comfort and welcome.

### Etiquette of permission
| Etiquette | Source |
|---|---|
| Greet and ask | This verse |
| Three times | *Asking permission is three times; if you are permitted, enter, otherwise return* (al-Bukhari and Muslim) |
| Stand to the side | Not facing the doorway |
| Name yourself | Not just "me" |

### If told to return
- *If you are told to go back, then go back; it is purer for you* (24:28), without anger.

### The wisdom
- Protecting privacy and the sanctity of homes.

### Lessons
- **Teach children** to ask permission, even at home at certain times.
- **Respect privacy** in visits and messages.

## "Turn to Allah in Repentance, All of You": Quran 24:31 Explained

﴿وَتُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعًا أَيُّهَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ [An-Nur 24:31]

### After the rulings of modesty
- The verse comes at the end of the commands to lower the gaze and guard modesty. Since everyone may fall short, the call is: **repent, all of you**.

### "All of you, O believers"
- Repentance is not only for sinners in the eyes of people; it is for **every believer**. The Prophet ﷺ said he sought forgiveness and repented more than seventy times a day (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### Conditions of tawbah
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stop | Leave the sin now |
| Regret | Feel genuine remorse |
| Resolve | Intend not to return |
| Restore rights | If others were wronged |

### Success
- *So that you may succeed*: repentance is a path to falah, not a mark of failure.

### Lessons
- **Repent daily**, not only after big sins.
- **Repent together**: families and communities.

## Surah An-Nur Meaning: Light, Modesty and a Protected Society

**Surah An-Nur** (The Light, 64 ayat) is a Madinan surah that builds a society protected by modesty and trust.

### A surah of obligations
- It opens: *A surah which We have sent down and made obligatory*, signalling that its rulings are binding.

### Protecting honour
- Severe penalties for those who accuse chaste women without four witnesses.
- **The incident of the lie (al-Ifk):** a false rumour against Aisha, the Mother of the Believers. Allah revealed her innocence and taught believers to think well of each other and not to spread what they cannot prove.

### Etiquette of the home
| Ruling | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Asking permission | Do not enter others' homes until you seek permission and greet |
| Lowering the gaze | For believing men and women |
| Covering | Guidance on the dress of believing women |
| Children's permission | At three times of privacy during the day |

### The Verse of Light
﴿۞ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِۦ كَمِشۡكَوٰةٖ فِيهَا مِصۡبَاحٌۖ ٱلۡمِصۡبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍۖ ٱلزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوۡكَبࣱ دُرِّيࣱّ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٖ زَيۡتُونَةٖ لَّا شَرۡقِيَّةٖ وَلَا غَرۡبِيَّةٖ يَكَادُ زَيۡتُهَا يُضِيٓءُ وَلَوۡ لَمۡ تَمۡسَسۡهُ نَارࣱۚ نُّورٌ عَلَىٰ نُورٖۚ يَهۡدِي ٱللَّهُ لِنُورِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلَ لِلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾
- Scholars explain it as the light of guidance in the believer's heart: pure, steady, and lit from revelation.

### The promise
- Allah promises those who believe and do good that He will establish them and replace their fear with security, as long as they worship Him alone.

### Lessons
- **Guard tongues**: rumours destroy families and communities.
- **Modesty protects light** in the heart.

## "Allah Is the Light of the Heavens and the Earth": The Verse of Light, Quran 24:35

﴿۞ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِۦ كَمِشۡكَوٰةٖ فِيهَا مِصۡبَاحٌۖ ٱلۡمِصۡبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍۖ ٱلزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوۡكَبࣱ دُرِّيࣱّ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٖ زَيۡتُونَةٖ لَّا شَرۡقِيَّةٖ وَلَا غَرۡبِيَّةٖ يَكَادُ زَيۡتُهَا يُضِيٓءُ وَلَوۡ لَمۡ تَمۡسَسۡهُ نَارࣱۚ نُّورٌ عَلَىٰ نُورٖۚ يَهۡدِي ٱللَّهُ لِنُورِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلَ لِلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ [An-Nur 24:35]

### "The Light of the heavens and the earth"
- Ibn ʿAbbas explained: the **Guide** of those in the heavens and earth. Light is also among Allah's names and attributes, affirmed as befits His majesty.

### The parable in detail
| Element | Explained by many scholars as |
|---|---|
| The niche | The believer's chest |
| The lamp | The light of faith and the Quran |
| The glass | The pure heart of the believer |
| The blessed tree | Blessed revelation |
| Neither east nor west | Sun all day, so its oil is purest |
| Its oil almost glows | Sound fitrah nearly recognises truth on its own |

### "Light upon light"
- The light of the **fitrah** joined with the light of **revelation**.

### Guidance is a gift
- *Allah guides to His light whom He wills*: ask Him for it.

### Lessons
- **Keep your heart clear** like the glass.
- **Pray**: *O Allah, place light in my heart* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).
