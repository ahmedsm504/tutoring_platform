**Surah Al-Hajj** (The Pilgrimage, 78 ayat) combines warnings of the Hour with the rituals of Hajj.

## The earthquake of the Hour
- It opens: *O mankind, fear your Lord. Indeed the convulsion of the Hour is a tremendous thing*, so terrible that a nursing mother forgets her infant.

## Proof of resurrection
- If you doubt the resurrection, look at your own creation from dust, then a drop, and at the dead earth that **stirs and swells** when rain falls.

## Ibrahim's call
﴿وَأَذِّن فِي ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَجِّ يَأۡتُوكَ رِجَالٗا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٖ يَأۡتِينَ مِن كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٖ﴾
- People still answer this call from every corner of the earth.

## The meaning of sacrifice
- *Their meat will not reach Allah, nor their blood, but what reaches Him is piety from you.*

## Permission to defend
- Fighting was first permitted for those who were wronged and expelled from their homes only for saying "Our Lord is Allah".

## Hearts that see
﴿أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَتَكُونَ لَهُمۡ قُلُوبࣱ يَعۡقِلُونَ بِهَآ أَوۡ ءَاذَانࣱ يَسۡمَعُونَ بِهَاۖ فَإِنَّهَا لَا تَعۡمَى ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَلَٰكِن تَعۡمَى ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلَّتِي فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾

## Lessons
- **Rituals are about the heart**: piety, not the outward act alone.
- **Reflection is sight**: travel and look with an understanding heart.
