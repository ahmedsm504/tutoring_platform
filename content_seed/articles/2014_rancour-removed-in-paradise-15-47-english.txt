﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ﴾ ﴿ٱدۡخُلُوهَا بِسَلَٰمٍ ءَامِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَنَزَعۡنَا مَا فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنۡ غِلٍّ إِخۡوَٰنًا عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ﴾ ﴿لَا يَمَسُّهُمۡ فِيهَا نَصَبࣱ وَمَا هُم مِّنۡهَا بِمُخۡرَجِينَ﴾ [Al-Hijr 15:45-48]

## Complete bliss
| Blessing | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gardens and springs | Bliss of the body |
| In peace, secure | Bliss of safety |
| Rancour removed | Bliss of the heart |
| Facing one another | Bliss of companionship |
| No fatigue | Rest without effort |
| Never expelled | Eternity without fear of loss |

## Rancour removed
- Whatever disagreements existed among believers in this world, Allah removes them, and they enter as brothers.

## Facing each other
- No one turns his back on his brother: a sign of love and closeness.

## Work for it now
- A clean heart is a quality of the people of Paradise, as in the duʿa: *and place no rancour in our hearts toward those who believe* (Al-Hashr 59:10).

## Lessons
- **Clean your heart** of resentment today.
- **Reconcile** with your brothers.
