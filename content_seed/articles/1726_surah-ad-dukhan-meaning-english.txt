**Surah Ad-Dukhan** (59 ayat) begins with the night the Quran came down.

﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٍۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُنذِرِينَ﴾
- Most commentators said this **blessed night** is Laylat al-Qadr in Ramadan, *in which every wise matter is made distinct*.
- Read [Surah Al-Qadr Meaning](/blog/surah-al-qadr-meaning-english/).

## The smoke
- *So wait for the Day when the sky will bring a visible smoke covering the people.*
- Ibn Masʿud explained it as the famine that struck Quraysh, when they saw something like smoke from hunger [Al-Bukhari]; other scholars said it is a sign before the Hour.

## Pharaoh's end
- Pharaoh and his people left gardens, springs, crops and noble places.
- *And the heaven and earth did not weep for them.*
- **The meaning**: no good deeds of theirs rose to the sky, and no place of prayer on earth missed them, as some commentators explained.

## Two fates
| The sinful | The righteous |
|---|---|
| The tree of Zaqqum as food | Gardens and springs |
| Boiling water | Silk garments, facing one another |
| | Secure from death after the first death |

## Lessons
- **Would anything weep for you?** Leave good deeds behind.
