If we gathered the du'as of the prophets in one place, we would find many of them in a single surah: Surah Al-Anbiya, "The Prophets". It has Ayyub's du'a when he was ill: "Harm has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful." Yunus's du'a inside the whale: "There is no god but You, glory be to You, I was among the wrongdoers." And Zakariya's du'a: "My Lord, do not leave me alone, and You are the best of inheritors." After each one, Allah says: "So We answered him."

Surah Al-Anbiya mentions sixteen prophets, and it contains one of the stories children love most: young Ibrahim, who broke the idols, left the biggest one, and when they threw him into a huge fire, Allah said: "O fire, be coolness and safety for Ibrahim." It also has verses about the universe: water as the origin of every living thing, and the sun and moon swimming in their orbits. In this guide, we explain Surah Al-Anbiya to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Anbiya

- **Position**: surah 21, at the start of juz 17.
- **Number of verses**: 112.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: al-Anbiya, "The Prophets", because it mentions so many of them: Musa, Harun, Ibrahim, Lut, Ishaq, Ya'qub, Nuh, Dawud, Sulayman, Ayyub, Isma'il, Idris, Dhul-Kifl, Yunus, Zakariya, Yahya and 'Isa, ending with the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

## Lesson 1: Every Living Thing From Water

﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ كَانَتَا رَتۡقٗا فَفَتَقۡنَٰهُمَاۖ وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمَآءِ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ حَيٍّۚ أَفَلَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:30)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah made every living thing from water. People need water, animals need water and plants need water. More than half of your own body is water! No living creature can survive without it."

### Experiment: the plant and the water
Plant two seeds in two cups. Water one every day and leave the other dry. After a week, the first grows and the second doesn't. Then read the verse together.

### Lesson for kids
"Water is a great blessing, so we don't waste it: turn off the tap while brushing your teeth, and don't leave water running for no reason."

## Lesson 2: Mountains and the Protected Sky

﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَوَٰسِيَ أَن تَمِيدَ بِهِمۡ وَجَعَلۡنَا فِيهَا فِجَاجٗا سُبُلٗا لَّعَلَّهُمۡ يَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ سَقۡفٗا مَّحۡفُوظٗاۖ وَهُمۡ عَنۡ ءَايَٰتِهَا مُعۡرِضُونَ﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ وَٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلࣱّ فِي فَلَكٖ يَسۡبَحُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:31-33)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah placed firm mountains on the earth so it would not shake with us, made wide paths between them for us to walk, made the sky a protected roof above us, and created night and day and the sun and moon, each gliding in its orbit, the way fish swim in water."

### Activity: the orbit on a string
Tie a small ball to a string and swing it gently around your head. Say: "This is how the moon goes around the earth in its orbit, and Allah keeps it in place so it never flies off."

## Lesson 3: The Scales on the Day of Judgement

﴿وَنَضَعُ ٱلۡمَوَٰزِينَ ٱلۡقِسۡطَ لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فَلَا تُظۡلَمُ نَفۡسࣱ شَيۡـٔٗاۖ وَإِن كَانَ مِثۡقَالَ حَبَّةٖ مِّنۡ خَرۡدَلٍ أَتَيۡنَا بِهَاۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِنَا حَٰسِبِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:47)

### Explaining it to your child
"On the Day of Judgement, Allah will set up perfectly fair scales, so no one is treated unfairly at all. Even if a deed is as small as a mustard seed, Allah will bring it and count it. So we never think a small good deed is unimportant: a smile, moving something harmful from the road, or a kind word."

### Activity: the mustard seed
If you can, show your child some mustard or sesame seeds and say: "Look how tiny they are! Allah counts even a good deed this small."

## Lesson 4: Ibrahim and the Idols

﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ رُشۡدَهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُ وَكُنَّا بِهِۦ عَٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوۡمِهِۦ مَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلتَّمَاثِيلُ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنتُمۡ لَهَا عَٰكِفُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ وَجَدۡنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا لَهَا عَٰبِدِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ لَقَدۡ كُنتُمۡ أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُمۡ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ ﴿قَالُوٓاْ أَجِئۡتَنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّ أَمۡ أَنتَ مِنَ ٱللَّـٰعِبِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ بَل رَّبُّكُمۡ رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَهُنَّ وَأَنَا۠ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكُم مِّنَ ٱلشَّـٰهِدِينَ﴾ ﴿وَتَٱللَّهِ لَأَكِيدَنَّ أَصۡنَٰمَكُم بَعۡدَ أَن تُوَلُّواْ مُدۡبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَجَعَلَهُمۡ جُذَٰذًا إِلَّا كَبِيرٗا لَّهُمۡ لَعَلَّهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:51-58)

### Explaining it to your child
"Ibrahim was a clever young man whom Allah gave wisdom from a young age. He saw his people worshipping stone statues and asked: What are these statues you are devoted to? They said: We found our fathers worshipping them. He said: You and your fathers are clearly wrong! Then he said: By Allah, I will deal with your idols after you leave. When his people went out to their festival, Ibrahim went in and broke all the idols except the biggest one, so they might come back and ask it."

﴿قَالُوٓاْ ءَأَنتَ فَعَلۡتَ هَٰذَا بِـَٔالِهَتِنَا يَـٰٓإِبۡرَٰهِيمُ﴾ ﴿قَالَ بَلۡ فَعَلَهُۥ كَبِيرُهُمۡ هَٰذَا فَسۡـَٔلُوهُمۡ إِن كَانُواْ يَنطِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:62-63)

### Explaining it to your child
"When the people came back and found their idols broken, they brought Ibrahim and asked: Did you do this to our gods? He answered cleverly: Rather, this biggest one did it. Ask them, if they can speak! They fell silent, because they knew idols can't talk or move. So how could they worship something that can't even help itself?"

### Lesson for kids: think for yourself
"Ibrahim taught his people to think: how can you worship something that can't defend itself? Allah loves us to think and reflect, not just copy others without understanding."

## Lesson 5: "O Fire, Be Cool and Safe"

﴿قَالُواْ حَرِّقُوهُ وَٱنصُرُوٓاْ ءَالِهَتَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ فَٰعِلِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡنَا يَٰنَارُ كُونِي بَرۡدٗا وَسَلَٰمًا عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾ ﴿وَأَرَادُواْ بِهِۦ كَيۡدٗا فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡأَخۡسَرِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:68-70)

### Explaining it to your child
"The people were furious and decided to burn Ibrahim! They gathered a huge pile of wood, lit an enormous fire and threw Ibrahim into it. But Allah said to the fire: O fire, be coolness and safety for Ibrahim. And the fire did not burn him! It became cool and safe. Fire only burns by Allah's permission."

### From the Sunnah
Al-Bukhari reported from Ibn 'Abbas: "Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal-wakil" was said by Ibrahim when he was thrown into the fire.

### Activity: Ibrahim's words
Teach your child to say "Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal-wakil", "Allah is enough for us and He is the best Protector", when they feel scared, and tell them that Ibrahim said it with the fire in front of him, and Allah saved him.

## Lesson 6: Dawud, Sulayman and a Fair Judgement

﴿وَدَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ إِذۡ يَحۡكُمَانِ فِي ٱلۡحَرۡثِ إِذۡ نَفَشَتۡ فِيهِ غَنَمُ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ وَكُنَّا لِحُكۡمِهِمۡ شَٰهِدِينَ﴾ ﴿فَفَهَّمۡنَٰهَا سُلَيۡمَٰنَۚ وَكُلًّا ءَاتَيۡنَا حُكۡمٗا وَعِلۡمٗاۚ وَسَخَّرۡنَا مَعَ دَاوُۥدَ ٱلۡجِبَالَ يُسَبِّحۡنَ وَٱلطَّيۡرَۚ وَكُنَّا فَٰعِلِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:78-79)

### Explaining it to your child
"Two men came to Dawud and his son Sulayman: one man's sheep had wandered at night into the other man's field and eaten his crops. Dawud gave a judgement, and Allah helped Sulayman understand an even finer one: the sheep's owner would look after the field until it grew back as it was, while the field's owner used the sheep's milk and wool in the meantime, and then everything would go back to its owner. Allah gave both of them wisdom and knowledge, and the mountains and birds praised Allah along with Dawud."

### Activity: the little judge
Give your child a simple problem: "Your brother accidentally broke your pencil. What's a fair solution?" Let them suggest something that gives each person their right.

## Lesson 7: The Answered Du'as of the Prophets

### Ayyub's du'a
﴿۞وَأَيُّوبَ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ ٱلضُّرُّ وَأَنتَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ فَكَشَفۡنَا مَا بِهِۦ مِن ضُرّٖۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ أَهۡلَهُۥ وَمِثۡلَهُم مَّعَهُمۡ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَا وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡعَٰبِدِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:83-84)

"Ayyub was ill for many years and lost his wealth and children, and he was patient. Then he called his Lord politely: Harm has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful. He didn't even ask directly; he simply mentioned his weakness and his Lord's mercy. Allah answered him, healed him, and returned his family to him and more."

### Yunus's du'a
﴿وَذَا ٱلنُّونِ إِذ ذَّهَبَ مُغَٰضِبٗا فَظَنَّ أَن لَّن نَّقۡدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ فَنَادَىٰ فِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ أَن لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَمِّۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُـۨجِي ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:87-88)

"Yunus was swallowed by a whale, in layers of darkness: the darkness of night, the darkness of the sea, and the darkness inside the whale. He called out: La ilaha illa anta, subhanaka, inni kuntu minaz-zalimin. Allah answered him and saved him from distress. And Allah said: That is how We save the believers, meaning every believer who calls with this du'a."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The du'a of Dhun-Nun when he called inside the whale: La ilaha illa anta, subhanaka, inni kuntu minaz-zalimin. No Muslim ever calls with it for anything except that Allah answers him" (at-Tirmidhi; graded authentic by al-Albani).

### Zakariya's du'a
﴿وَزَكَرِيَّآ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥ رَبِّ لَا تَذَرۡنِي فَرۡدٗا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥ يَحۡيَىٰ وَأَصۡلَحۡنَا لَهُۥ زَوۡجَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَيَدۡعُونَنَا رَغَبٗا وَرَهَبٗاۖ وَكَانُواْ لَنَا خَٰشِعِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:89-90)

"Zakariya called: My Lord, do not leave me alone without a child, and You are the best of inheritors. Allah answered and gave him Yahya. Then Allah told us the secret: they used to hurry to do good deeds, call on Allah with hope and fear, and they were humble before Him."

### Activity: the prophets' du'a book
Make a small book with one prophet's du'a per page, with a drawing: Ayyub, Yunus and the whale, Zakariya. Your child can choose the right du'a for each situation: Ayyub's when ill, Yunus's when in distress.

## Lesson 8: A Mercy to the Worlds

﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 21:107)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah sent Muhammad ﷺ as a mercy to the worlds: to people, animals and children. He was kind to the young, gentle with animals, and taught us mercy. We follow him by being merciful to everyone around us."

## Let's Read Together: Every Soul Will Taste Death

﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۗ وَنَبۡلُوكُم بِٱلشَّرِّ وَٱلۡخَيۡرِ فِتۡنَةٗۖ وَإِلَيۡنَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 21:35)

### Explaining it to your child
"Every person will die one day and return to Allah. Allah tests us in this life with nice things and hard things: will we be grateful in the nice times? Will we be patient in the hard times?" For older children, this verse can be explained gently, focusing on life as a beautiful test we prepare for with good deeds.

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Hard Exam"

'Abdur-Rahman was scared of his maths exam. He had studied a lot, but the night before, he felt a heavy tightness in his chest. He went to his father and said: "Dad, I feel like I'm in a dark place."

His father smiled. "Do you know who was in the darkest place in the world? Yunus, inside the whale, in the sea, at night. Three darknesses! What did he say?" He taught him: "La ilaha illa anta, subhanaka, inni kuntu minaz-zalimin." Then he said: "And the Prophet ﷺ told us that no Muslim calls with it for anything except that Allah answers him."

'Abdur-Rahman repeated the du'a several times and fell asleep feeling calm. In the morning, he went into the exam and found the questions easier than he had imagined. He came home saying: "Dad, Yunus's du'a calmed my heart, and studying helped me." His father said: "That's it: we study and work, we make du'a, and Allah grants success."

Ask your child: "Which du'a did 'Abdur-Rahman say? Who said it first?"

## What We Learn From Surah Al-Anbiya

- **Water** is the source of life, so we don't waste it.
- **The universe** is perfectly ordered.
- **Small good deeds** are counted.
- **Think**, don't just copy.
- **Allah saves** those who rely on Him.
- **Be fair** when judging between people.
- **The prophets' du'as** are answered.
- **The Prophet ﷺ is a mercy** to the worlds.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### How do I explain Ibrahim saying "the biggest one did it"?
Scholars explain it as a clever argument to make them think, not a lie for personal gain; an authentic hadith calls it one of the statements Ibrahim made for Allah's sake. For children: "He wanted them to realise idols can't talk."

### Should I explain the Day of Judgement verses to my child?
Choose those with mercy and reassurance, and leave the frightening scenes for when they are older.

### How many times should Yunus's du'a be said?
There is no fixed number. It is said in times of distress, and your child can repeat it as much as they like.
