Juz 17 begins with ﴿ٱقۡتَرَبَ لِلنَّاسِ حِسَابُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ فِي غَفۡلَةٖ مُّعۡرِضُونَ﴾, "Mankind's reckoning has drawn near, while they turn away in heedlessness", so it is called **Juz Iqtaraba**. It contains two complete surahs: **Al-Anbiya'** (112 verses) and **Al-Hajj** (78 verses). In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 322 to 341**.

It combines **the stories and prayers of the prophets** in Al-Anbiya' with **scenes of the Hour, the rites of Hajj and the rulings on fighting** in Al-Hajj. 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud said, as reported in Sahih Al-Bukhari: "Bani Isra'il, Al-Kahf, Maryam, Ta-Ha and Al-Anbiya' are among the earliest and finest, and they are among what I learned first."

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **the monotheism every prophet brought, and preparing for the Day of Reckoning**. Al-Anbiya' opens with a warning against heedlessness, presents the prophets one after another, and concludes: ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أُمَّتُكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُونِ﴾, "This community of yours is one community, and I am your Lord, so worship Me." Al-Hajj opens with the quake of the Hour, then builds the rites of monotheism around the House Ibrahim established on ﴿أَن لَّا تُشۡرِكۡ بِي شَيۡـٔٗا﴾, "Do not associate anything with Me", and closes by saying Allah named this community **the Muslims**, on the way of their father Ibrahim.

The message: **the religion of the prophets is one, the reckoning is near, so prepare through worship and by honouring the symbols of Allah.**

## Part one: Surah Al-Anbiya' (1-112)

Al-Anbiya' is Makki, with 112 verses, and eighteen prophets are mentioned in it by name, hence its title "The Prophets". For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Anbiya Meaning in English](/blog/quran-not-sent-for-distress-20-2-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Anbiya-Meaning-The-Prophets-and-a-Mercy-to-the-Worl).

### 1. Heedlessness as the reckoning nears (1-15)
"Mankind's reckoning has drawn near, while they turn away in heedlessness." No fresh reminder comes to them from their Lord except that they listen to it while at play, their hearts distracted. They accused the Prophet ﷺ of being a mere man like them, then shifted in describing the Quran: muddled dreams, no, he invented it, no, he is a poet. The reply: ﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ فَسۡـَٔلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلذِّكۡرِ إِن كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾, "We sent before you only men to whom We revealed, so ask the people of the reminder if you do not know." Then the fate of wrongdoing towns: when they sensed Our punishment, they fled from it.

**Lesson**: the most dangerous state of the heart is to hear the Quran while distracted.

### 2. The universe is purposeful (16-29)
﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ﴾, "We did not create the heaven and earth and all between them in play", ﴿بَلۡ نَقۡذِفُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ عَلَى ٱلۡبَٰطِلِ فَيَدۡمَغُهُۥ فَإِذَا هُوَ زَاهِقࣱۚ﴾, "Rather, We hurl the truth against falsehood and it shatters it, and at once it vanishes." Then the proof from mutual exclusion: ﴿لَوۡ كَانَ فِيهِمَآ ءَالِهَةٌ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ لَفَسَدَتَاۚ فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ﴾, "Had there been in them gods besides Allah, both would have been ruined. Glorified is Allah, Lord of the Throne, above what they describe", ﴿لَا يُسۡـَٔلُ عَمَّا يَفۡعَلُ وَهُمۡ يُسۡـَٔلُونَ﴾, "He is not questioned about what He does, but they will be questioned." Then: every messenger before you received the revelation that there is no god but Me, so worship Me. And the angels are honoured servants who do not speak before Him.

**Reflect**: the order of the universe is among the clearest proofs of its Creator's oneness.

### 3. Signs, death and testing (30-50)
A new quarter begins: ﴿۞وَمَن يَقُلۡ مِنۡهُمۡ إِنِّيٓ إِلَٰهࣱ مِّن دُونِهِۦ فَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِيهِ جَهَنَّمَۚ﴾, "Whoever of them says, I am a god besides Him, We will recompense him with Hell." Then: ﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ كَانَتَا رَتۡقٗا فَفَتَقۡنَٰهُمَاۖ وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمَآءِ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ حَيٍّۚ أَفَلَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾, "Have the disbelievers not seen that the heavens and earth were a joined mass and We split them apart, and made from water every living thing? Will they not believe?" Then the firm mountains, the sky as a protected ceiling, and night, day, sun and moon, each swimming in an orbit.

Then the great truth: ﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۗ وَنَبۡلُوكُم بِٱلشَّرِّ وَٱلۡخَيۡرِ فِتۡنَةٗۖ وَإِلَيۡنَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾, "Every soul will taste death. We test you with evil and good as a trial, and to Us you will be returned." Good is a test just as hardship is. Read [Every Soul Shall Taste Death](/blog/kuntum-khayra-umma-meaning-english/#Every-Soul-Will-Taste-Death-Quran-3-185-Explained).

Then: man was created hasty; and the precise scales: ﴿وَنَضَعُ ٱلۡمَوَٰزِينَ ٱلۡقِسۡطَ لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ فَلَا تُظۡلَمُ نَفۡسࣱ شَيۡـٔٗاۖ وَإِن كَانَ مِثۡقَالَ حَبَّةٖ مِّنۡ خَرۡدَلٍ أَتَيۡنَا بِهَاۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِنَا حَٰسِبِينَ﴾, "We will set up the just scales for the Day of Resurrection, so no soul will be wronged at all; even if it is the weight of a mustard seed, We will bring it forth, and sufficient are We as reckoners." Then mention of Musa, Harun and the Criterion, and this blessed reminder We have sent down.

### 4. Ibrahim and the idols (51-73)
A new quarter: ﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ رُشۡدَهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُ وَكُنَّا بِهِۦ عَٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوۡمِهِۦ مَا هَٰذِهِ ٱلتَّمَاثِيلُ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنتُمۡ لَهَا عَٰكِفُونَ﴾, "We gave Ibrahim his sound judgement before, and We knew him well, when he said to his father and his people: what are these statues to which you are devoted?" They said: we found our fathers worshipping them. He swore: ﴿وَتَٱللَّهِ لَأَكِيدَنَّ أَصۡنَٰمَكُم بَعۡدَ أَن تُوَلُّواْ مُدۡبِرِينَ﴾, "By Allah, I will plan against your idols after you have turned your backs", and broke them into pieces except the largest. When questioned, he replied: ﴿قَالَ بَلۡ فَعَلَهُۥ كَبِيرُهُمۡ هَٰذَا فَسۡـَٔلُوهُمۡ إِن كَانُواْ يَنطِقُونَ﴾, "Rather, this largest one of them did it, so ask them if they can speak." They turned to themselves for a moment, then reverted. He said: do you worship, besides Allah, what neither benefits nor harms you?

They decided: ﴿قَالُواْ حَرِّقُوهُ وَٱنصُرُوٓاْ ءَالِهَتَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ فَٰعِلِينَ﴾, "Burn him and support your gods, if you are to act." Then the divine command: ﴿قُلۡنَا يَٰنَارُ كُونِي بَرۡدٗا وَسَلَٰمًا عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾, "We said: O fire, be coolness and safety for Ibrahim." Then Allah saved him and Lut to the blessed land, and gave him Ishaq and Ya'qub as an extra gift.

**Lesson**: Ibrahim argued with reason until the people admitted the truth within themselves; when they could not answer, they resorted to force. In Al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas reported that Ibrahim's last words when thrown into the fire were: "Allah is sufficient for me, and He is the best Disposer of affairs."

### 5. Lut, Nuh, Dawud and Sulayman (74-82)
- **Lut**: given judgement and knowledge, and saved from the town that practised vile deeds.
- **Nuh**: called out before, and We answered him and saved him and his family from the great distress.
- **Dawud and Sulayman**: ﴿وَدَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ إِذۡ يَحۡكُمَانِ فِي ٱلۡحَرۡثِ إِذۡ نَفَشَتۡ فِيهِ غَنَمُ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ﴾, "And Dawud and Sulayman, when they judged concerning the field into which people's sheep had strayed at night", then ﴿فَفَهَّمۡنَٰهَا سُلَيۡمَٰنَۚ وَكُلًّا ءَاتَيۡنَا حُكۡمٗا وَعِلۡمٗاۚ﴾, "We gave Sulayman understanding of it, and to each We gave judgement and knowledge." Allah praised both, though Sulayman's understanding was finer. Then the mountains and birds glorifying with Dawud, his skill in making armour, the wind subjected to Sulayman, and devils diving for him.

**Lesson**: sincere effort in judgement is rewarded, and differences in understanding do not lower anyone's rank.

### 6. Prayers answered (83-91)
A new quarter opens a procession of supplication:
- **Ayyub**: ﴿۞وَأَيُّوبَ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ ٱلضُّرُّ وَأَنتَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾, "And Ayyub, when he called to his Lord: harm has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful." He did not ask outright; he described his state and praised his Lord. The answer: ﴿فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ فَكَشَفۡنَا مَا بِهِۦ مِن ضُرّٖۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ أَهۡلَهُۥ وَمِثۡلَهُم مَّعَهُمۡ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَا وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡعَٰبِدِينَ﴾, "So We answered him, removed his harm, and gave him his family and as many again with them, as mercy from Us and a reminder for the worshippers."
- **Isma'il, Idris and Dhul-Kifl**: all among the patient.
- **Dhun-Nun (Yunus)**: ﴿وَذَا ٱلنُّونِ إِذ ذَّهَبَ مُغَٰضِبٗا فَظَنَّ أَن لَّن نَّقۡدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ فَنَادَىٰ فِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ أَن لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾, "And the man of the whale, when he went off in anger thinking We would not constrict him, then he called out in the darknesses: there is no god but You, glory be to You, I have been among the wrongdoers." The darknesses: of night, sea and the whale's belly. Then the general promise: "and thus do We save the believers." In Sunan At-Tirmidhi, the Prophet ﷺ said: "The supplication of Dhun-Nun when he called in the whale's belly, 'There is no god but You, glory be to You, I have been among the wrongdoers': no Muslim ever calls with it for anything except that Allah answers him." Read [The Dua of Dhun-Nun](/blog/quran-not-sent-for-distress-20-2-meaning-english/#The-Duʿa-of-Yunus-in-the-Whale-Quran-21-87-88-Explained).
- **Zakariya**: ﴿وَزَكَرِيَّآ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥ رَبِّ لَا تَذَرۡنِي فَرۡدٗا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثِينَ﴾, "And Zakariya, when he called to his Lord: My Lord, do not leave me alone, and You are the best of inheritors." Then the secret of the answer: ﴿إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَيَدۡعُونَنَا رَغَبٗا وَرَهَبٗاۖ وَكَانُواْ لَنَا خَٰشِعِينَ﴾, "They used to hasten to good deeds, call on Us in hope and fear, and were humble before Us."
- **Maryam**: ﴿وَٱلَّتِيٓ أَحۡصَنَتۡ فَرۡجَهَا فَنَفَخۡنَا فِيهَا مِن رُّوحِنَا وَجَعَلۡنَٰهَا وَٱبۡنَهَآ ءَايَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "And she who guarded her chastity, so We breathed into her of Our spirit and made her and her son a sign for the worlds."

**Reflect**: every prophet here called on his Lord in distress, and the answer came with the particle *fa*, signalling speed: "So We answered him." The secret of being answered is in verse 90: hastening to good deeds, calling in hope and fear, and humility.

### 7. One community and the end (92-112)
"This community of yours is one community, and I am your Lord, so worship Me": all the prophets share one religion. Then signs of the Hour: ﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فُتِحَتۡ يَأۡجُوجُ وَمَأۡجُوجُ وَهُم مِّن كُلِّ حَدَبٖ يَنسِلُونَ﴾, "until Ya'juj and Ma'juj are released and descend from every elevation", and the true promise draws near. Then: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقَتۡ لَهُم مِّنَّا ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰٓ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ عَنۡهَا مُبۡعَدُونَ﴾, "Those for whom the best has preceded from Us will be kept far from it", untroubled by the greatest terror.

Then the cosmic scene: ﴿يَوۡمَ نَطۡوِي ٱلسَّمَآءَ كَطَيِّ ٱلسِّجِلِّ لِلۡكُتُبِۚ كَمَا بَدَأۡنَآ أَوَّلَ خَلۡقٖ نُّعِيدُهُۥۚ﴾, "The Day We fold the heaven like a scroll is folded for writings; as We began the first creation, We will repeat it." Then the promise: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ كَتَبۡنَا فِي ٱلزَّبُورِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ ٱلذِّكۡرِ أَنَّ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ يَرِثُهَا عِبَادِيَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحُونَ﴾, "We wrote in the Zabur, after the reminder, that the earth will be inherited by My righteous servants."

Then the verse that sums up the Prophet's mission ﷺ: ﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "We have not sent you except as a mercy to the worlds." Read [A Mercy to the Worlds: 21:107](/blog/quran-not-sent-for-distress-20-2-meaning-english/#We-Sent-You-Only-as-a-Mercy-to-the-Worlds-Quran-21-107-Expla).

## Part two: Surah Al-Hajj (1-78)

Al-Hajj is labelled Madani in the Madinah Mushaf, has 78 verses and two places of prostration. It is named after the Hajj because it contains Ibrahim's call to pilgrimage and its rites. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Hajj Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-hajj-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Hajj-Meaning-The-Call-to-Pilgrimage-and-Hearts-That).

### 1. The quake of the Hour and the stages of creation (1-10)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡۚ إِنَّ زَلۡزَلَةَ ٱلسَّاعَةِ شَيۡءٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾, "O mankind, fear your Lord; the quake of the Hour is a tremendous thing", ﴿يَوۡمَ تَرَوۡنَهَا تَذۡهَلُ كُلُّ مُرۡضِعَةٍ عَمَّآ أَرۡضَعَتۡ وَتَضَعُ كُلُّ ذَاتِ حَمۡلٍ حَمۡلَهَا وَتَرَى ٱلنَّاسَ سُكَٰرَىٰ وَمَا هُم بِسُكَٰرَىٰ وَلَٰكِنَّ عَذَابَ ٱللَّهِ شَدِيدࣱ﴾, "The Day you see it, every nursing mother will forget her nursling, every pregnant woman will deliver her load, and you will see people as if drunk, though they are not drunk; but the punishment of Allah is severe." The nursing mother forgets her infant from the sheer terror.

Then the proof of resurrection from human creation: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِن كُنتُمۡ فِي رَيۡبٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡبَعۡثِ فَإِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ مِنۡ عَلَقَةٖ ثُمَّ مِن مُّضۡغَةٖ مُّخَلَّقَةٖ وَغَيۡرِ مُخَلَّقَةٖ لِّنُبَيِّنَ لَكُمۡۚ﴾, "O mankind, if you are in doubt about the resurrection, We created you from dust, then from a drop, then from a clinging clot, then from a lump of flesh, formed and unformed, to make clear to you." Then the barren earth: when water comes down on it, it stirs and swells. Whoever revives the earth after its death can revive the dead.

### 2. Worship on the edge (11-18)
﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَعۡبُدُ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ حَرۡفٖۖ فَإِنۡ أَصَابَهُۥ خَيۡرٌ ٱطۡمَأَنَّ بِهِۦۖ وَإِنۡ أَصَابَتۡهُ فِتۡنَةٌ ٱنقَلَبَ عَلَىٰ وَجۡهِهِۦ خَسِرَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡخُسۡرَانُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾, "Among people is one who worships Allah on an edge: if good befalls him he is content, and if a trial befalls him he turns on his face, losing this world and the Hereafter. That is the clear loss." *Harf* is the edge: he worships with doubt, like someone standing on the rim of a cliff, steady in ease and falling in hardship.

Then the cosmic prostration: ﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَسۡجُدُۤ لَهُۥۤ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُ وَٱلنُّجُومُ وَٱلۡجِبَالُ وَٱلشَّجَرُ وَٱلدَّوَآبُّ وَكَثِيرࣱ مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِۖ وَكَثِيرٌ حَقَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡعَذَابُۗ﴾, "Do you not see that to Allah prostrates whoever is in the heavens and earth, the sun, moon, stars, mountains, trees, animals and many people, while many have deserved punishment?" This is the first place of prostration. See [Sujud al-Tilawah in English](/blog/ghunnah-english/#Sujud-al-Tilawa-The-15-Places-of-Prostration-in-the-Quran).

**Lesson**: the whole universe prostrates to Allah, so it does not befit man to be the odd one out.

### 3. The two opposing groups (19-24)
A new quarter: ﴿۞هَٰذَانِ خَصۡمَانِ ٱخۡتَصَمُواْ فِي رَبِّهِمۡۖ﴾, "These are two opponents who dispute about their Lord." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Dharr used to swear that it was revealed about those who duelled at Badr: Hamzah, 'Ali and 'Ubaydah ibn al-Harith against 'Utbah and Shaybah sons of Rabi'ah and al-Walid ibn 'Utbah. Then the reward of each side: garments of fire for the disbelievers, and bracelets of gold and pearls and garments of silk for the believers, ﴿وَهُدُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلطَّيِّبِ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ﴾, "and they were guided to good speech."

### 4. The House and the rites (25-37)
﴿وَإِذۡ بَوَّأۡنَا لِإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ مَكَانَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ أَن لَّا تُشۡرِكۡ بِي شَيۡـٔٗا وَطَهِّرۡ بَيۡتِيَ لِلطَّآئِفِينَ وَٱلۡقَآئِمِينَ وَٱلرُّكَّعِ ٱلسُّجُودِ﴾, "And when We showed Ibrahim the site of the House: do not associate anything with Me, and purify My House for those who circle it, stand, bow and prostrate." The House was founded first of all on monotheism. Then the command to call: ﴿وَأَذِّن فِي ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَجِّ يَأۡتُوكَ رِجَالٗا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٖ يَأۡتِينَ مِن كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٖ﴾, "Proclaim the Hajj to people; they will come to you on foot and on every lean camel, from every distant pass." Then the benefits of Hajj, mentioning Allah's name on known days, and circling the Ancient House.

Then honouring the symbols: ﴿ذَٰلِكَۖ وَمَن يُعَظِّمۡ شَعَـٰٓئِرَ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّهَا مِن تَقۡوَى ٱلۡقُلُوبِ﴾, "That is so. And whoever honours the symbols of Allah, it is from the taqwa of hearts." Then the purpose of sacrificial animals: ﴿لَن يَنَالَ ٱللَّهَ لُحُومُهَا وَلَا دِمَآؤُهَا وَلَٰكِن يَنَالُهُ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ مِنكُمۡۚ﴾, "Their meat and blood do not reach Allah; what reaches Him is your taqwa."

**Lesson**: acts of worship are not outward motions but a means to taqwa of the heart, and Allah has no need of meat or blood.

### 5. Permission to fight, and empowerment (38-48)
A new quarter: ﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدَٰفِعُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْۗ﴾, "Allah defends those who believe", then ﴿أُذِنَ لِلَّذِينَ يُقَٰتَلُونَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ ظُلِمُواْۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ نَصۡرِهِمۡ لَقَدِيرٌ﴾, "Permission is given to those who are fought, because they were wronged, and Allah is able to help them." It is reported from Ibn 'Abbas that this was the first verse revealed permitting fighting. The reason is clear: ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ أُخۡرِجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِم بِغَيۡرِ حَقٍّ إِلَّآ أَن يَقُولُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُۗ﴾, "those expelled from their homes without right, only for saying: our Lord is Allah." Then the wisdom: were it not for Allah repelling some people by others, monasteries, churches, synagogues and mosques in which Allah's name is mentioned much would have been demolished.

Then the qualities of those Allah supports: ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ إِن مَّكَّنَّـٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَوُاْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَمَرُواْ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَنَهَوۡاْ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِۗ وَلِلَّهِ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾, "Those who, if We establish them on earth, establish prayer, give zakah, enjoin good and forbid evil; and to Allah belongs the outcome of all matters." Power is not the goal, but a means to establish the religion.

Then blindness of the heart: ﴿فَإِنَّهَا لَا تَعۡمَى ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَلَٰكِن تَعۡمَى ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلَّتِي فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾, "It is not the eyes that are blind, but the hearts within the chests." Then: a day with your Lord is like a thousand years of what you count.

### 6. Protecting revelation, helping the wronged (49-66)
Allah protects His revelation from what Shaytan casts and makes His verses firm. Those who emigrated for Allah's sake and were then killed or died, Allah will surely give them good provision. Then a new quarter: ﴿۞ذَٰلِكَۖ وَمَنۡ عَاقَبَ بِمِثۡلِ مَا عُوقِبَ بِهِۦ ثُمَّ بُغِيَ عَلَيۡهِ لَيَنصُرَنَّهُ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَعَفُوٌّ غَفُورࣱ﴾, "That is so. And whoever retaliates with the like of what he suffered and is then wronged again, Allah will surely help him. Allah is Pardoning, Forgiving." Then: ﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَأَنَّ مَا يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ هُوَ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ﴾, "That is because Allah is the Truth, what they call on besides Him is falsehood, and Allah is the Most High, the Great", followed by signs of rain, greening earth and ships made to sail.

### 7. The parable of the fly and the close (67-78)
For every community We appointed rites they perform. Then the decisive parable: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ضُرِبَ مَثَلࣱ فَٱسۡتَمِعُواْ لَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَن يَخۡلُقُواْ ذُبَابٗا وَلَوِ ٱجۡتَمَعُواْ لَهُۥۖ وَإِن يَسۡلُبۡهُمُ ٱلذُّبَابُ شَيۡـٔٗا لَّا يَسۡتَنقِذُوهُ مِنۡهُۚ ضَعُفَ ٱلطَّالِبُ وَٱلۡمَطۡلُوبُ﴾, "O mankind, a parable is set forth, so listen to it: those you call on besides Allah will never create a fly, even if they all gathered for it; and if the fly snatches anything from them, they cannot recover it. Weak are the seeker and the sought." The so-called gods cannot create the smallest creature, nor even recover what it takes from them.

Then: they did not appraise Allah as He deserves; Allah chooses messengers from angels and from people. Then the close with comprehensive commands: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱرۡكَعُواْ وَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ وَٱفۡعَلُواْ ٱلۡخَيۡرَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾, "O you who believe, bow, prostrate, worship your Lord and do good so that you may succeed", the second place of prostration, then ﴿وَجَٰهِدُواْ فِي ٱللَّهِ حَقَّ جِهَادِهِۦۚ هُوَ ٱجۡتَبَىٰكُمۡ وَمَا جَعَلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ مِنۡ حَرَجٖۚ مِّلَّةَ أَبِيكُمۡ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَۚ هُوَ سَمَّىٰكُمُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ مِن قَبۡلُ وَفِي هَٰذَا﴾, "Strive for Allah as He deserves. He has chosen you and placed no hardship on you in religion, the way of your father Ibrahim. He named you Muslims before and in this", ending with ﴿فَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡمَوۡلَىٰ وَنِعۡمَ ٱلنَّصِيرُ﴾, "an excellent Protector and an excellent Helper."

**Reflect**: the surah began with "O mankind, fear your Lord" and ended with "O you who believe, bow and prostrate": whoever fears the Hour becomes a worshipper.

## Tajweed points in Juz 17

### ﴿إِذ ذَّهَبَ﴾ in Al-Anbiya' 87
The dhal merges into the following dhal: *idgham* of identical letters.

### ﴿نُـۨجِي ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ in Al-Anbiya' 88
Written with one noon in the Mushaf but read in Hafs with two: *nunji*, the second noon sakin and hidden (ikhfa') before the jim.

### ﴿أَنَا۠﴾ in Al-Anbiya' 92
The alif after the noon is written but not pronounced when continuing; it is pronounced only when stopping.

### The two prostrations of Al-Hajj
The first at verse 18, at ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَفۡعَلُ مَا يَشَآءُ﴾; the second at verse 77, at ﴿لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾. For the basics see [Learn Tajweed: A Roadmap](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. "We sent before you"
- Al-Anbiya' 7: ﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا قَبۡلَكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ﴾, without *min*.
- An-Nahl 43: ﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ﴾, with *min*.

### 2. "It is falsehood"
- Al-Hajj 62: ﴿وَأَنَّ مَا يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ هُوَ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُ﴾, with *huwa*.
- Luqman 30: ﴿وَأَنَّ مَا يَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِ ٱلۡبَٰطِلُ﴾, without it.

### 3. The answers to the prophets
- Nuh (76): ﴿فَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡكَرۡبِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾, saved from distress.
- Ayyub (84): ﴿فَكَشَفۡنَا مَا بِهِۦ مِن ضُرّٖۖ﴾, harm removed.
- Yunus (88): ﴿وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَمِّۚ﴾, saved from grief.
- Zakariya (90): ﴿وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥ يَحۡيَىٰ﴾, given a son.
**Key**: the answer matches the need: distress is lifted, harm removed, grief relieved, loneliness answered with a child.

## How to memorize Juz 17 with understanding

1. **Divide Al-Anbiya' into three blocks**: argument with the idolaters (1-50), the prophets' stories (51-91), the close (92-112).
2. **Learn the order of the prophets** in verses 48-91: Musa and Harun, Ibrahim, Lut, Nuh, Dawud and Sulayman, Ayyub, Isma'il, Idris and Dhul-Kifl, Dhun-Nun, Zakariya, Maryam.
3. **Al-Hajj is a surah of calls**: "O mankind" at the start (1, 5) and in the parable of the fly (73), "O you who believe" at the close (77).
4. **Link the Hajj rites in order**: the House, the call, the benefits, circling, the symbols, the sacrifices.
5. **Keep a separate notebook** for similar verses.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 17

- **Beware heedlessness** while listening to the Quran.
- **Reflect on the order of the universe**: it proves His oneness.
- **Know that good is a test** just as hardship is.
- **Prepare for the scales**: not a mustard seed is lost.
- **Argue with reason** and do not fear falsehood.
- **Call on your Lord in distress** with the prayers of the prophets.
- **Hasten to good deeds** and call in hope and fear.
- **Worship Allah with certainty**, not on an edge.
- **Honour Allah's symbols**: it is taqwa of the heart.
- **If you are empowered, establish prayer** and enjoin good.
- **Beware blindness of the heart**: it is worse than blind eyes.
- **Bow, prostrate and do good**, that you may succeed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 17 called "Iqtaraba"?
Because it begins with ﴿ٱقۡتَرَبَ لِلنَّاسِ حِسَابُهُمۡ﴾.

### How many prophets are named in Surah Al-Anbiya'?
Eighteen prophets by name, hence its title.

### How many prostrations are in Juz 17?
Two, both in Surah Al-Hajj: verses 18 and 77.

### What is the supplication of Dhun-Nun?
﴿لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾, "There is no god but You, glory be to You, I have been among the wrongdoers."

### Where does Juz 17 end?
At the end of Al-Hajj. Juz 18 begins with Surah Al-Mu'minun: ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾, "The believers have succeeded."

## Conclusion

Juz 17 wakes you from heedlessness with the nearness of the reckoning, then shows you the procession of prophets on one religion, calling on their Lord in distress and being answered. It then takes you to the House Ibrahim founded on monotheism, teaching that worship is taqwa of the heart rather than bodily motion, and closes with bowing, prostration and doing good.
