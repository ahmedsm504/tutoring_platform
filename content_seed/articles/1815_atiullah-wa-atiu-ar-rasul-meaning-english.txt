﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَمۡرِ مِنكُمۡۖ فَإِن تَنَٰزَعۡتُمۡ فِي شَيۡءٖ فَرُدُّوهُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَٱلرَّسُولِ إِن كُنتُمۡ تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۚ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَحۡسَنُ تَأۡوِيلًا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:59]

## A careful wording
| Phrase | What scholars note |
|---|---|
| *Obey Allah* | Absolute obedience |
| *And obey the Messenger* | The word "obey" is **repeated**: the Sunnah is followed independently |
| *And those in authority among you* | "Obey" is **not repeated**: their obedience follows obedience to Allah and His Messenger |

## The limit
- The Prophet ﷺ said: **"There is no obedience in disobedience to Allah; obedience is only in what is right"** (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Who are "those in authority"?
- Scholars mention rulers and also **scholars**, each in their field.

## When people disagree
- *Refer it to Allah and the Messenger*: to the Quran, and to the Sunnah after his death. This is a condition of faith: *if you believe in Allah and the Last Day*.

## Lessons
- **The Sunnah is a source of guidance** alongside the Quran.
- **Resolve disputes with evidence**, not personal opinion.
