## "We Did Not Send Down the Quran to Make You Distressed": Quran 20:1-3 Explained

﴿طه﴾ ﴿مَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِتَشۡقَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا تَذۡكِرَةٗ لِّمَن يَخۡشَىٰ﴾ [Taha 20:1-3]

### The opening letters
- *Ta Ha* are among the disconnected letters; their exact meaning is known to Allah.

### Not for distress
- Many scholars mention that the Prophet ﷺ prayed at night for so long that his feet swelled. The surah opens by reassuring him: the Quran came for **happiness**, not hardship.

### A reminder
- *Only as a reminder for whoever fears*: those with soft, fearing hearts benefit most.

### Balance
- The Prophet ﷺ also taught: *Take on deeds you can sustain* (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### The Quran and happiness
| The Quran brings | Not |
|---|---|
| Peace | Anxiety |
| Guidance | Confusion |
| Ease | Burden |

### Lessons
- **Approach the Quran with joy**, not pressure.
- **Be consistent** with what you can sustain.

## "My Lord, Expand My Chest": The Duʿa of Musa in Quran 20:25-28 Explained

﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشۡرَحۡ لِي صَدۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَيَسِّرۡ لِيٓ أَمۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَٱحۡلُلۡ عُقۡدَةٗ مِّن لِّسَانِي﴾ ﴿يَفۡقَهُواْ قَوۡلِي﴾ [Taha 20:25-28]

### The moment
- Allah had just commanded Musa: *Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed.* Before going, Musa asked for what he needed.

### Four requests
| Request | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Expand my chest | Calm, courage and patience |
| Ease my task | Make the mission easy |
| Untie the knot from my tongue | Fluent, clear speech |
| That they understand my speech | Being understood, the goal of speaking |

### "From my tongue"
- He asked to untie *a knot*, not every knot: a lesson in asking for what is needed.

### Then Harun
- He continued by asking for his brother Harun as a helper, and Allah said: *You have been granted your request, O Musa* (20:36).

### When to say it
- Before exams, interviews, presentations, difficult conversations, and teaching.

### Lessons
- **Prepare the heart** before the task.
- **Ask Allah for clarity**, not only success.

## Surah Taha Meaning: Musa at the Valley and a Prayer for Ease

**Surah Taha** (135 ayat) reassures the Prophet ﷺ that the Quran was not sent to burden him.

### At the sacred valley
- Musa saw a fire while travelling with his family. At the valley of Tuwa, Allah spoke to him: *I am Allah; there is no god but Me, so worship Me and establish prayer for My remembrance.*

### The prayer of a messenger
﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشۡرَحۡ لِي صَدۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَيَسِّرۡ لِيٓ أَمۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَٱحۡلُلۡ عُقۡدَةٗ مِّن لِّسَانِي﴾ ﴿يَفۡقَهُواْ قَوۡلِي﴾
- He also asked for his brother Harun as a helper.

### Gentle words to a tyrant
- Musa and Harun were told to speak to Pharaoh **gently**, so that perhaps he would remember or fear.

### The magicians
- The magicians saw the truth, fell in prostration, and stood firm against Pharaoh's threats: *Decree whatever you will; you can only decree for this worldly life.*

### The calf
- While Musa was away, the Samiri made a golden calf and the people worshipped it. Musa returned angry and grieved.

### Adam's lesson
- Adam forgot and ate from the tree; then his Lord chose him, accepted his repentance and guided him.
- *Whoever turns away from My remembrance will have a narrow, difficult life.*

### Lessons
- **Ask Allah for ease** before every big task.
- **Gentleness is a method of daʿwah**, even with the arrogant.

## "Speak to Him with Gentle Speech": Quran 20:43-44 Explained

﴿ٱذۡهَبَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَقُولَا لَهُۥ قَوۡلٗا لَّيِّنٗا لَّعَلَّهُۥ يَتَذَكَّرُ أَوۡ يَخۡشَىٰ﴾ [Taha 20:43-44]

### A striking contrast
| Messengers | Addressee |
|---|---|
| Musa and Harun, among the greatest prophets | Pharaoh, who claimed lordship |

- Yet they were told to be **gentle**.

### Gentle speech
- Kind words without harshness, while the truth stays clear. An example: *Would you like to purify yourself?* (An-Naziʿat 79:18).

### "Perhaps he will remember or fear"
- Hope for his guidance, teaching callers never to despair.

### A reported saying
- Some early scholars, when reciting this verse, wept and said: *This is Your gentleness with one who says "I am god", so how is Your gentleness with one who says "You are God"?*

### Lessons
- **If gentleness is for Pharaoh**, how much more for parents and children?
- **Truth does not need harshness** to arrive.

## "I Am Perpetually Forgiving to Whoever Repents": Quran 20:82 Explained

﴿وَإِنِّي لَغَفَّارࣱ لِّمَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا ثُمَّ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ﴾ [Taha 20:82]

### *Ghaffar*
- An intensive form: forgiving **again and again**, however many times the servant returns.

### Four conditions
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Repents | Leaves sin with regret |
| Believes | Correct faith |
| Does good | Righteous action |
| *Then remains guided* | Continues on the path |

### "Then remains guided"
- *Thumma* suggests continuation over time: forgiveness is completed by **steadfastness**, not a one-time burst.

### The context
- The verse comes in the story of the Children of Israel, before the golden calf episode: a reminder that the door was open even for them.

### Lessons
- **Return as often as you fall.**
- **Stay on the path** after repenting.

## "My Lord, Increase Me in Knowledge": Quran 20:114 Explained

﴿فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۗ وَلَا تَعۡجَلۡ بِٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يُقۡضَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَحۡيُهُۥۖ وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدۡنِي عِلۡمٗا﴾ [Taha 20:114]

### Three parts
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Exalted is Allah, the True King* | All authority is His |
| *Do not hurry with the Quran* | Wait until the revelation is complete before repeating it |
| *Say: My Lord, increase me in knowledge* | Ask for more learning |

### Not rushing
- The Prophet ﷺ would repeat the revelation quickly with Jibril out of eagerness. He was told to listen first; Allah would preserve it in his heart (see also Al-Qiyamah 75:16-19).

### The only increase commanded
- Scholars note that Allah did not command His Prophet ﷺ to ask for an increase in anything except knowledge.

### A duʿa for students
- Say it before class, before memorising, and before exams.

### Lessons
- **Listen fully before speaking.**
- **Never feel you have learned enough.**

## "Whoever Turns Away from My Remembrance Will Have a Hard Life": Quran 20:124 Explained

﴿وَمَنۡ أَعۡرَضَ عَن ذِكۡرِي فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ مَعِيشَةٗ ضَنكٗا وَنَحۡشُرُهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ أَعۡمَىٰ﴾ [Taha 20:124]

### The context
- After Adam and his wife were sent down to earth, Allah said that whoever follows His guidance **will not go astray nor suffer**. Then comes the opposite: whoever turns away.

### "My remembrance"
- Scholars explain it as the Quran and the guidance Allah sent, and remembering Him in general.

### A narrow life
- *Danka* means tight and constricted. It is not only about money: a person may have wealth yet feel **inner narrowness, anxiety and emptiness**.
| Outward | Inward |
|---|---|
| Comfort and possessions | Restlessness |
| Many friends | Loneliness |
| Entertainment | Lack of purpose |

### Raised blind
- The next verse: *He will say: My Lord, why did You raise me blind while I could see?* Allah answers: *Our signs came to you and you forgot them; so today you are forgotten.*

### The opposite promise
- Compare An-Nahl 16:97: whoever does good with faith will have **a good life**.

### Lessons
- **Return to the Quran** when life feels tight.
- **Do not judge happiness by appearances.**

## "Do Not Extend Your Eyes Toward What We Gave Others": Quran 20:131 Explained

﴿وَلَا تَمُدَّنَّ عَيۡنَيۡكَ إِلَىٰ مَا مَتَّعۡنَا بِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ زَهۡرَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لِنَفۡتِنَهُمۡ فِيهِۚ وَرِزۡقُ رَبِّكَ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَبۡقَىٰ﴾ [Taha 20:131]

### "Do not extend your eyes"
- Not a passing glance, but **staring with longing** at what others have.

### A flower of this life
- *Zahrah* means a flower: beautiful, but quickly wilting.

### A test for them
- *That We may test them by it*: their wealth is a trial for them, not necessarily a sign of honour.

### Better and lasting
| Their enjoyment | Your Lord's provision |
|---|---|
| Temporary | *Abqa*: more lasting |
| A test | *Khayr*: better |

### Comparison today
- Scrolling through others' highlights can produce the very longing this verse forbids.
- The Prophet ﷺ advised: *Look at those below you, not those above you, so you do not belittle Allah's blessings on you* (narrated by Muslim).

### The next verse
- *And command your family to pray and be patient in it; We do not ask you for provision, We provide for you.*

### Lessons
- **Guard your eyes** from envy.
- **Count your own blessings.**

## "Enjoin Prayer upon Your Family and Be Steadfast in It": Quran 20:132 Explained

﴿وَأۡمُرۡ أَهۡلَكَ بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ عَلَيۡهَاۖ لَا نَسۡـَٔلُكَ رِزۡقٗاۖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُكَۗ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰ﴾ [Taha 20:132]

### *Istabir*
- An intensified form of patience: steady, repeated, enduring. Teaching prayer needs repetition; keeping it needs persistence.

### Prayer and provision
- *We do not ask you for provision; We provide for you.* Do not let earning a living push prayer aside; prayer is a cause of blessing.

### The context
- The previous verse: *Do not extend your eyes toward the enjoyments We gave others* (20:131). The cure for chasing glitter is prayer for yourself and family.

### Practical steps
| Age | Approach |
|---|---|
| Before seven | Pray in front of them, invite gently |
| Seven | Instruct them, as in the hadith narrated by Abu Dawud |
| After | Follow up, encourage, be the example |

### Lessons
- **Start with yourself**: children copy what they see.
- **Be patient** with repetition.

## "We Made Every Living Thing from Water": Quran 21:30 Explained

﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ كَانَتَا رَتۡقٗا فَفَتَقۡنَٰهُمَاۖ وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمَآءِ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ حَيٍّۚ أَفَلَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ [Al-Anbiya 21:30]

### *Ratq* and *fatq*
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Ratq* | Joined together |
| *Fatq* | Split apart |

### The scholars' views
- The heavens and earth were one mass, and Allah separated them.
- Or: the sky gave no rain and the earth no plants, then Allah split the sky with rain and the earth with vegetation.

### Water and life
- *We made from water every living thing*: every living being depends on water to form and survive.

### "Will they not believe?"
- The signs are clear for those who reflect.

### Lessons
- **Do not waste water.**
- **Reflect** on everyday blessings.

## "We Test You with Evil and with Good as a Trial": Quran 21:35 Explained

﴿كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ ذَآئِقَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِۗ وَنَبۡلُوكُم بِٱلشَّرِّ وَٱلۡخَيۡرِ فِتۡنَةٗۖ وَإِلَيۡنَا تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾ [Al-Anbiya 21:35]

### Good is a test too
| Test | Required |
|---|---|
| Hardship: illness, poverty, loss | Patience |
| Ease: health, wealth, success | Gratitude |

### Ibn ʿAbbas
- Reported from him: *We test you with hardship and ease, health and illness, wealth and poverty, the lawful and the unlawful, obedience and sin, guidance and misguidance.*

### Ease can be harder
- Some early believers said: *We were tested with hardship and were patient, and tested with ease and were not.*

### "To Us you will be returned"
- After the test comes the reckoning; death is mentioned first as a reminder.

### Lessons
- **Do not assume** blessing alone means Allah is pleased.
- **Thank in ease**, be patient in hardship.

## The Duʿa of Yunus in the Whale: Quran 21:87-88 Explained

﴿وَذَا ٱلنُّونِ إِذ ذَّهَبَ مُغَٰضِبٗا فَظَنَّ أَن لَّن نَّقۡدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ فَنَادَىٰ فِي ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ أَن لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ [Al-Anbiya 21:87]

### Who is Dhun-Nun?
- *Dhun-Nun* means "the one of the whale", a title of Yunus, peace be upon him.

### The darknesses
- Scholars mention the darkness of the night, of the sea, and of the whale's belly.

### Three parts of the duʿa
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *La ilaha illa anta* | Tawhid: no god but You |
| *Subhanaka* | Glorification: You are free of any injustice |
| *Inni kuntu minaz-zalimin* | Confession: the fault is mine |

### The rescue
﴿فَٱسۡتَجَبۡنَا لَهُۥ وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَمِّۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُـۨجِي ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾
- *And thus We save the believers*: the promise is not only for Yunus.

### The Prophet ﷺ on this duʿa
- He said that no Muslim calls on Allah with the duʿa of Dhun-Nun for anything except that Allah responds to him (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### Lessons
- **In distress, admit your fault** and glorify Allah.
- **No darkness is too deep** for Allah's rescue.

## "We Sent You Only as a Mercy to the Worlds": Quran 21:107 Explained

﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ [Al-Anbiya 21:107]

### A message in one verse
- The sentence uses restriction: *We did not send you except as a mercy*. Mercy is the **purpose** of his mission.

### Mercy to whom?
| Group | Form of mercy |
|---|---|
| Believers | Guidance to Paradise |
| All people | The message of tawhid and justice reached them |
| Children | He shortened prayer when a baby cried |
| Animals | He told of a man forgiven for giving water to a thirsty dog (narrated by al-Bukhari) |
| The earth | He encouraged planting a sapling even if the Hour were about to begin (narrated by Ahmad) |

### Mercy that did not curse
- When asked to curse the polytheists, he ﷺ said: **"I was not sent as one who curses; I was sent as a mercy"** (narrated by Muslim).

### Context in the surah
- The verse comes after the stories of many prophets. He is the final mercy, and his message is for **all the worlds**, not one people.

### Lessons
- **Follow his mercy**: in homes, schools and workplaces.
- **Mercy is strength**, not weakness.

## Surah Al-Anbiya Meaning: The Prophets and a Mercy to the Worlds

**Surah Al-Anbiya** (The Prophets, 112 ayat) mentions many prophets by name.

### The reckoning is near
- It opens: *Mankind's account has drawn near, while they turn away in heedlessness.*

### Ibrahim and the idols
- Ibrahim broke his people's idols and left the largest one. When asked, he told them to ask the idols themselves, exposing that they cannot speak.
- They threw him into the fire, and Allah said: *O fire, be coolness and safety upon Ibrahim.*

### Prayers that were answered
| Prophet | His prayer |
|---|---|
| Ayyub | *Harm has touched me, and You are the most merciful of the merciful* |
| Yunus | *There is no god but You, glory be to You; I was among the wrongdoers* |
| Zakariya | *My Lord, do not leave me alone, and You are the best of inheritors* |

- Each time Allah says: **so We answered him**.

### The final messenger
﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾

### Lessons
- **Call on Allah in your own words** of humility, as the prophets did.
- **The Prophet ﷺ is a mercy**: following him brings mercy to individuals and societies.
