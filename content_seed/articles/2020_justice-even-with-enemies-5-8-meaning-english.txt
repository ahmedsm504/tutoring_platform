﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُواْ قَوَّـٰمِينَ لِلَّهِ شُهَدَآءَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَلَا يَجۡرِمَنَّكُمۡ شَنَـَٔانُ قَوۡمٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَعۡدِلُواْۚ ٱعۡدِلُواْ هُوَ أَقۡرَبُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ [Al-Maidah 5:8]

## The words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *La yajrimannakum* | Let it not drive you |
| *Shanaʾan* | Intense hatred |

## Justice with those you dislike
- Hatred never justifies injustice. Justice is required even with opponents and enemies.

## Two complementary verses
| An-Nisa 4:135 | Al-Maidah 5:8 |
|---|---|
| *Standing firm for justice, witnesses for Allah* | *Standing firm for Allah, witnesses in justice* |
| Even against yourselves and relatives | Even with those you hate |

- Some scholars note: one prevents bias from love, the other prevents bias from hatred.

## Nearest to taqwa
- *Be just; that is nearer to taqwa.*

## Lessons
- **Do not wrong** someone who wronged you.
- **Speak the truth** about opponents as about friends.
