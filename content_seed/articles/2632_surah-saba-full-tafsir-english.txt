Surah Saba is a surah about gratitude: who has it, who loses it, and what happens to each. It shows two families side by side. The family of Dawud was given extraordinary gifts, iron made soft and the wind and jinn made subject, and they were commanded: "Work, family of Dawud, in gratitude." The people of Saba were given two gardens and a good land, and they turned away until a flood swept it all away. Between them stands a striking scene: Sulayman, the mightiest king, dies leaning on his staff, and no one notices until a small creature eats through it. The surah ends with a simple, honest invitation: stand for Allah, alone or in pairs, and think.

This guide explains Surah Saba section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Saba
- **Number of verses**: 54.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 34, in the twenty-second juz.
- **Name**: Saba, the people of Sheba in Yemen, from verses 15–21.
- **Main themes**: Allah's complete knowledge, gratitude and ingratitude, the hereafter, the universal message of the Prophet ﷺ, and reflection.

## Verses 1–2: Praise and Knowledge
﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَهُ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ﴾ ﴿يَعۡلَمُ مَا يَلِجُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهَا وَمَا يَنزِلُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا يَعۡرُجُ فِيهَاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلرَّحِيمُ ٱلۡغَفُورُ﴾ (Quran 34:1-2)

**"All praise is due to Allah, to whom belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, and to Him belongs all praise in the hereafter."** **"He knows what penetrates into the earth and what emerges from it and what descends from the heaven and what ascends therein."** Every seed, every drop and every deed rising up is known to Him.

## Verses 3–9: Not an Atom's Weight
﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَا تَأۡتِينَا ٱلسَّاعَةُۖ قُلۡ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّي لَتَأۡتِيَنَّكُمۡ عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِۖ لَا يَعۡزُبُ عَنۡهُ مِثۡقَالُ ذَرَّةٖ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَلَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَآ أَصۡغَرُ مِن ذَٰلِكَ وَلَآ أَكۡبَرُ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 34:3)

The disbelievers say the Hour will not come. **"Say: Yes, by my Lord, it will surely come to you. Not absent from Him is an atom's weight within the heavens or within the earth or what is smaller than that or greater, except that it is in a clear register."**

﴿أَفَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ إِن نَّشَأۡ نَخۡسِفۡ بِهِمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ أَوۡ نُسۡقِطۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ كِسَفٗا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّكُلِّ عَبۡدٖ مُّنِيبٖ﴾ (Quran 34:9)

**"Then, do they not look at what is before them and what is behind them of the heaven and the earth?"**

## Verses 10–11: Dawud and the Softened Iron
﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ مِنَّا فَضۡلٗاۖ يَٰجِبَالُ أَوِّبِي مَعَهُۥ وَٱلطَّيۡرَۖ وَأَلَنَّا لَهُ ٱلۡحَدِيدَ﴾ ﴿أَنِ ٱعۡمَلۡ سَٰبِغَٰتٖ وَقَدِّرۡ فِي ٱلسَّرۡدِۖ وَٱعۡمَلُواْ صَٰلِحًاۖ إِنِّي بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 34:10-11)

**"And We certainly gave Dawud from Us bounty. O mountains, repeat praises with him, and the birds also. And We made pliable for him iron."** He was taught to make full coats of mail with measured links. **"And work righteousness. Indeed I, of what you do, am Seeing."** In Sahih al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ said: "No one has ever eaten better food than what he eats from the work of his own hands, and the Prophet of Allah Dawud used to eat from the work of his own hands."

## Verses 12–14: Sulayman and His Death
﴿وَلِسُلَيۡمَٰنَ ٱلرِّيحَ غُدُوُّهَا شَهۡرࣱ وَرَوَاحُهَا شَهۡرࣱۖ وَأَسَلۡنَا لَهُۥ عَيۡنَ ٱلۡقِطۡرِۖ وَمِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ مَن يَعۡمَلُ بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِۦۖ وَمَن يَزِغۡ مِنۡهُمۡ عَنۡ أَمۡرِنَا نُذِقۡهُ مِنۡ عَذَابِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ﴾ (Quran 34:12)

**"And to Sulayman the wind, its morning journey a month and its afternoon journey a month."** A spring of molten copper flowed for him, and jinn worked for him by Allah's permission.

﴿يَعۡمَلُونَ لَهُۥ مَا يَشَآءُ مِن مَّحَٰرِيبَ وَتَمَٰثِيلَ وَجِفَانٖ كَٱلۡجَوَابِ وَقُدُورٖ رَّاسِيَٰتٍۚ ٱعۡمَلُوٓاْ ءَالَ دَاوُۥدَ شُكۡرٗاۚ وَقَلِيلࣱ مِّنۡ عِبَادِيَ ٱلشَّكُورُ﴾ (Quran 34:13)

**"Work, O family of Dawud, in gratitude. And few of My servants are grateful."** Gratitude is not only words; it is work. And the grateful are few.

﴿فَلَمَّا قَضَيۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ مَا دَلَّهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَوۡتِهِۦٓ إِلَّا دَآبَّةُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تَأۡكُلُ مِنسَأَتَهُۥۖ فَلَمَّا خَرَّ تَبَيَّنَتِ ٱلۡجِنُّ أَن لَّوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ مَا لَبِثُواْ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡمُهِينِ﴾ (Quran 34:14)

**"And when We decreed for him death, nothing indicated to them his death except a creature of the earth eating his staff. But when he fell, it became clear to the jinn that if they had known the unseen, they would not have remained in humiliating punishment."** The jinn kept working, thinking he was watching them. The lesson: the jinn do not know the unseen, and even the mightiest king is subject to death.

## Verses 15–21: The People of Saba
﴿لَقَدۡ كَانَ لِسَبَإٖ فِي مَسۡكَنِهِمۡ ءَايَةࣱۖ جَنَّتَانِ عَن يَمِينٖ وَشِمَالٖۖ كُلُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِ رَبِّكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لَهُۥۚ بَلۡدَةࣱ طَيِّبَةࣱ وَرَبٌّ غَفُورࣱ﴾ (Quran 34:15)

**"There was for the people of Saba in their dwelling place a sign: two gardens on the right and on the left. Eat from the provision of your Lord and be grateful to Him. A good land and a forgiving Lord."** Everything was given: a good land in this world, and a forgiving Lord for the next.

﴿فَأَعۡرَضُواْ فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَيۡلَ ٱلۡعَرِمِ وَبَدَّلۡنَٰهُم بِجَنَّتَيۡهِمۡ جَنَّتَيۡنِ ذَوَاتَيۡ أُكُلٍ خَمۡطٖ وَأَثۡلٖ وَشَيۡءٖ مِّن سِدۡرٖ قَلِيلٖ﴾ (Quran 34:16)

**"But they turned away, so We sent upon them the flood of the dam, and We replaced their two gardens with gardens of bitter fruit, tamarisks and something of sparse lote trees."** The great dam of Marib broke, and the paradise became a wasteland.

﴿ذَٰلِكَ جَزَيۡنَٰهُم بِمَا كَفَرُواْۖ وَهَلۡ نُجَٰزِيٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡكَفُورَ﴾ (Quran 34:17)

**"And do We thus repay except the ungrateful?"**

﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا بَيۡنَهُمۡ وَبَيۡنَ ٱلۡقُرَى ٱلَّتِي بَٰرَكۡنَا فِيهَا قُرٗى ظَٰهِرَةٗ وَقَدَّرۡنَا فِيهَا ٱلسَّيۡرَۖ سِيرُواْ فِيهَا لَيَالِيَ وَأَيَّامًا ءَامِنِينَ﴾ ﴿فَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَا بَٰعِدۡ بَيۡنَ أَسۡفَارِنَا وَظَلَمُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ أَحَادِيثَ وَمَزَّقۡنَٰهُمۡ كُلَّ مُمَزَّقٍۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّكُلِّ صَبَّارٖ شَكُورٖ﴾ (Quran 34:18-19)

They had safe travel through connected towns, and they said: **"Our Lord, lengthen the distance between our journeys."** They wronged themselves, **"so We made them narrations and dispersed them in total dispersion"**. The scattering of Saba became an Arab proverb.

﴿وَلَقَدۡ صَدَّقَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ إِبۡلِيسُ ظَنَّهُۥ فَٱتَّبَعُوهُ إِلَّا فَرِيقٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 34:20)

**"And Iblis had already confirmed through them his assumption, so they followed him, except a party of believers."**

## Verses 22–27: No Partners, No Intercession Without Permission
﴿قُلِ ٱدۡعُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ زَعَمۡتُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَا يَمۡلِكُونَ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَلَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا لَهُمۡ فِيهِمَا مِن شِرۡكٖ وَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡهُم مِّن ظَهِيرٖ﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَنفَعُ ٱلشَّفَٰعَةُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا لِمَنۡ أَذِنَ لَهُۥۚ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فُزِّعَ عَن قُلُوبِهِمۡ قَالُواْ مَاذَا قَالَ رَبُّكُمۡۖ قَالُواْ ٱلۡحَقَّۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ﴾ (Quran 34:22-23)

**"Say: Invoke those you claim besides Allah. They do not possess an atom's weight in the heavens or on the earth."** **"And intercession does not benefit with Him except for one whom He permits."**

﴿۞قُلۡ مَن يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ قُلِ ٱللَّهُۖ وَإِنَّآ أَوۡ إِيَّاكُمۡ لَعَلَىٰ هُدًى أَوۡ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 34:24)

**"Say: Who provides for you from the heavens and the earth? Say: Allah. And indeed, we or you are either upon guidance or in clear error."** A gentle, fair manner of argument.

## Verses 28–30: A Messenger to All Mankind
﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا كَآفَّةٗ لِّلنَّاسِ بَشِيرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 34:28)

**"And We have not sent you except comprehensively to mankind as a bringer of good tidings and a warner. But most of the people do not know."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ listed among the things given to him and to no prophet before: "Every prophet was sent to his own people specifically, and I was sent to all people."

## Verses 31–42: Wealth Does Not Bring Near
﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا فِي قَرۡيَةٖ مِّن نَّذِيرٍ إِلَّا قَالَ مُتۡرَفُوهَآ إِنَّا بِمَآ أُرۡسِلۡتُم بِهِۦ كَٰفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ نَحۡنُ أَكۡثَرُ أَمۡوَٰلٗا وَأَوۡلَٰدٗا وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُعَذَّبِينَ﴾ (Quran 34:34-35)

Every town that received a warner had its wealthy ones say: **"We are more in wealth and children, and we are not to be punished."**

﴿وَمَآ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُكُم بِٱلَّتِي تُقَرِّبُكُمۡ عِندَنَا زُلۡفَىٰٓ إِلَّا مَنۡ ءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ جَزَآءُ ٱلضِّعۡفِ بِمَا عَمِلُواْ وَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡغُرُفَٰتِ ءَامِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 34:37)

**"And it is not your wealth or your children that bring you nearer to Us in position, but it is those who have believed and done righteousness."**

﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ رَبِّي يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَقۡدِرُ لَهُۥۚ وَمَآ أَنفَقۡتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَهُوَ يُخۡلِفُهُۥۖ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّـٰزِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 34:39)

**"And whatever thing you spend, He will compensate it; and He is the best of providers."** Spending in Allah's way never decreases; He replaces it.

## Verses 43–50: Stand for Allah and Reflect
﴿۞قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَعِظُكُم بِوَٰحِدَةٍۖ أَن تَقُومُواْ لِلَّهِ مَثۡنَىٰ وَفُرَٰدَىٰ ثُمَّ تَتَفَكَّرُواْۚ مَا بِصَاحِبِكُم مِّن جِنَّةٍۚ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا نَذِيرࣱ لَّكُم بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ عَذَابٖ شَدِيدٖ﴾ (Quran 34:46)

**"Say: I only advise you of one thing: that you stand for Allah, in pairs and individually, and then give thought. There is not in your companion any madness."** Away from the crowd and its pressure, alone or with one sincere friend, think honestly about the Prophet ﷺ and his message.

﴿قُلۡ إِن ضَلَلۡتُ فَإِنَّمَآ أَضِلُّ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِيۖ وَإِنِ ٱهۡتَدَيۡتُ فَبِمَا يُوحِيٓ إِلَيَّ رَبِّيٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ سَمِيعࣱ قَرِيبࣱ﴾ (Quran 34:50)

**"Say: If I should err, I would only err against myself. But if I am guided, it is by what my Lord reveals to me."**

## Verses 51–54: Too Late
﴿وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذۡ فَزِعُواْ فَلَا فَوۡتَ وَأُخِذُواْ مِن مَّكَانٖ قَرِيبٖ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ وَأَنَّىٰ لَهُمُ ٱلتَّنَاوُشُ مِن مَّكَانِۭ بَعِيدٖ﴾ (Quran 34:51-52)

**"And if you could see when they are terrified but there is no escape."** **"And they will say: We believe in it. But how for them will be the receiving of faith from a place far away?"**

## Word Meanings
- **Saba'**: Sheba, a people of Yemen.
- **Mithqal dharrah**: an atom's weight.
- **Awwibi**: repeat praises.
- **Sabighat**: full coats of mail.
- **Al-Qitr**: molten copper.
- **Minsa'ah**: staff.
- **Dabbat al-ard**: a creature of the earth.
- **Sayl al-'Arim**: the flood of the dam.
- **Khamt**: bitter fruit.
- **Athl**: tamarisk.
- **Kaffatan**: comprehensively, all.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Two families**: the grateful family of Dawud and the ungrateful people of Saba.
2. **"A good land and a forgiving Lord"**: four words gathering both worlds.
3. **The staff and the creature**: the power of kings undone by a small insect.
4. **"Few of My servants are grateful"**: a call to join the few.
5. **"We or you are upon guidance or error"**: courteous argument.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *min as-sama'i* (verse 2).
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *man ya'malu* (verse 12), the noon merges into the ya'.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *'ayn al-qitri* (verse 12), the ta' (ط) sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *minsa'atahu* (verse 14), the noon sakinah is hidden before the sin.

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Ahzab before it ends with the trust (*amanah*) carried by man, and Saba opens with praise and Allah's complete knowledge. Saba and Surah Fatir after it both open with "All praise is due to Allah".

## Practical Lessons
1. **Remember that Allah knows every atom** of your deeds.
2. **Show gratitude through work**, not only words.
3. **Join the grateful few.**
4. **Earn from your own hands**, as Dawud did.
5. **Do not believe claims that jinn know the unseen.**
6. **Protect your blessings with gratitude**, or lose them like Saba.
7. **Spend generously**: Allah replaces what you spend.
8. **Think honestly**, away from the crowd.

## How to Memorise Surah Saba
- **Week 1**: verses 1–21.
- **Week 2**: verses 22–54, with full review.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell the story of Sulayman's staff** and ask: who knew he had died?
- **Draw the two gardens** of Saba before and after the flood.
- **Make a family gratitude habit**: one thing to thank Allah for at dinner.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who were the people of Saba?
A people of Yemen given two gardens and a good land, who turned away and lost them to the flood of the dam.

### How did the jinn learn of Sulayman's death?
Only when a creature of the earth ate his staff and he fell, proving they did not know the unseen.

### What does "Work, family of Dawud, in gratitude" mean?
That gratitude is shown through action and obedience.

### Was the Prophet ﷺ sent to all people?
Yes, as verse 28 says, and as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Does spending decrease wealth?
Allah says: "Whatever thing you spend, He will compensate it."

### What is the call of verse 46?
To stand for Allah alone or in pairs and reflect honestly about the Prophet ﷺ.

### How many verses are in Surah Saba?
54 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Saba sets before you a choice between two families: the family of Dawud, who used every gift in Allah's service, and the people of Saba, who were given "a good land and a forgiving Lord" and turned away. Blessings stay with gratitude and leave with ingratitude. Join the few who are grateful, and take a quiet moment, alone or with a friend, to stand for Allah and think.
