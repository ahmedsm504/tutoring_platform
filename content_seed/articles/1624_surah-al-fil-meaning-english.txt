**Surah Al-Fil** (The Elephant) tells a story the Arabs knew well, because it happened in the year the Prophet ﷺ was born, known as the **Year of the Elephant**.

## The story in brief
- A ruler from Yemen, named in the history books as **Abrahah**, marched to Makkah with an army and an elephant to destroy the Kaʿbah.
- Allah protected His House.

## Ayah by ayah
| Ayah | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡفِيلِ﴾ | Have you not seen what your Lord did to the people of the elephant? |
| ﴿أَلَمۡ يَجۡعَلۡ كَيۡدَهُمۡ فِي تَضۡلِيلٖ﴾ | He made their plan go astray |
| ﴿وَأَرۡسَلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ طَيۡرًا أَبَابِيلَ﴾ | He sent birds in flocks |
| ﴿تَرۡمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٖ مِّن سِجِّيلٖ﴾ | Striking them with stones of baked clay |
| ﴿فَجَعَلَهُمۡ كَعَصۡفٖ مَّأۡكُولِۭ﴾ | And made them like eaten straw |

## "Have you not seen?"
- The Prophet ﷺ did not see it with his eyes, but it was **so well known** that it was as if everyone had seen it.

## The link with Quraysh
- The next surah begins: ﴿لِإِيلَٰفِ قُرَيۡشٍ﴾. Allah protected the House so Quraysh could live in safety, so **they should worship the Lord of this House**.

## Lessons
- **No power** can defeat Allah's plan.
- **Allah protects** what He wills.
