**Surah Taha** (135 ayat) reassures the Prophet ﷺ that the Quran was not sent to burden him.

## At the sacred valley
- Musa saw a fire while travelling with his family. At the valley of Tuwa, Allah spoke to him: *I am Allah; there is no god but Me, so worship Me and establish prayer for My remembrance.*

## The prayer of a messenger
﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشۡرَحۡ لِي صَدۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَيَسِّرۡ لِيٓ أَمۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَٱحۡلُلۡ عُقۡدَةٗ مِّن لِّسَانِي﴾ ﴿يَفۡقَهُواْ قَوۡلِي﴾
- He also asked for his brother Harun as a helper.

## Gentle words to a tyrant
- Musa and Harun were told to speak to Pharaoh **gently**, so that perhaps he would remember or fear.

## The magicians
- The magicians saw the truth, fell in prostration, and stood firm against Pharaoh's threats: *Decree whatever you will; you can only decree for this worldly life.*

## The calf
- While Musa was away, the Samiri made a golden calf and the people worshipped it. Musa returned angry and grieved.

## Adam's lesson
- Adam forgot and ate from the tree; then his Lord chose him, accepted his repentance and guided him.
- *Whoever turns away from My remembrance will have a narrow, difficult life.*

## Lessons
- **Ask Allah for ease** before every big task.
- **Gentleness is a method of daʿwah**, even with the arrogant.
