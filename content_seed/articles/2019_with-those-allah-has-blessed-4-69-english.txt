﴿وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلرَّسُولَ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَ وَٱلصِّدِّيقِينَ وَٱلشُّهَدَآءِ وَٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَۚ وَحَسُنَ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ رَفِيقٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:69]

## Four ranks
| Rank | Description |
|---|---|
| Prophets | The highest |
| The truthful (*siddiqin*) | Complete in faith and truth |
| Martyrs | Those killed in Allah's cause and those who bear witness to truth |
| The righteous | Those whose deeds are sound |

## Link to Al-Fatihah
- These are the people we ask to follow in every rakʿah: *the path of those You have blessed* (1:7).

## Excellent companions
- *And excellent are they as companions.*

## A person is with whom he loves
- A man asked about the Hour; he had not prepared much, but loved Allah and His Messenger. The Prophet ﷺ said: *You will be with those you love* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Lessons
- **Obedience** is the road to their company.
- **Love the righteous** and follow them.
