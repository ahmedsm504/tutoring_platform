## Surah An-Nahl Meaning: The Bee and Countless Blessings

**Surah An-Nahl** (The Bee, 128 ayat) is often called the surah of blessings.

### Blessings beyond counting
﴿وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾
- Cattle, rain, crops, the sea, stars for navigation, homes, clothing and shade are listed one by one.

### The bee
- Allah **inspired** the bee to build homes in mountains, trees and what people build, and to follow the paths of its Lord.
- From its belly comes a drink of varying colours, **in which there is healing for people**.

### A verse of justice
- *Indeed Allah commands justice, excellence and giving to relatives, and forbids immorality, wrongdoing and oppression.*
- This verse is often recited at the end of Friday sermons.

### The good life
﴿مَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنࣱ فَلَنُحۡيِيَنَّهُۥ حَيَوٰةٗ طَيِّبَةٗۖ وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡرَهُم بِأَحۡسَنِ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾

### How to invite
- *Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction, and argue with them in the best way.*

### Lessons
- **Count your blessings** to grow in gratitude.
- **Men and women are equal** in the reward of good deeds.

## "Ask the People of Knowledge If You Do Not Know": Quran 16:43 Explained

﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ فَسۡـَٔلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلذِّكۡرِ إِن كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:43]

### The context
- The polytheists denied that a messenger could be human. Allah said all previous messengers were men who received revelation.

### Who are *ahl adh-dhikr*?
- In context: the People of the Book who knew earlier messengers were human.
- By the general wording: **people of knowledge** in any field.

### A general rule
| Field | Ask |
|---|---|
| Religion | Trustworthy scholars |
| Tajweed | A qualified, certified teacher |
| Medicine | A specialist doctor |

### Etiquette of asking
- Choose someone trustworthy, ask to learn rather than argue, and remember the Prophet's words: *The cure for ignorance is asking* (narrated by Abu Dawud).

### Lessons
- **Do not be shy** to ask.
- **Do not give religious rulings** without knowledge.

## "Whatever Blessing You Have Is from Allah": Quran 16:53-54 Explained

﴿وَمَا بِكُم مِّن نِّعۡمَةٖ فَمِنَ ٱللَّهِۖ ثُمَّ إِذَا مَسَّكُمُ ٱلضُّرُّ فَإِلَيۡهِ تَجۡـَٔرُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِذَا كَشَفَ ٱلضُّرَّ عَنكُمۡ إِذَا فَرِيقࣱ مِّنكُم بِرَبِّهِمۡ يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:53-54]

### Every blessing
- Health, family, safety, sight, faith: **all** are from Allah, even those that seem earned by effort.

### *Tajʾarun*
- A loud, desperate cry, like an animal in distress. In harm, people call on Allah with full sincerity.

### Then forgetting
| In harm | After relief |
|---|---|
| Sincere duʿa | Some return to shirk or heedlessness |

### The believer's way
- Remember Allah in ease as you do in hardship. The Prophet ﷺ said: *Remember Allah in prosperity and He will remember you in adversity* (narrated by Ahmad).

### Lessons
- **Thank Allah after relief**, not only before.
- **Attribute every blessing** to Him.

## "Allah Commands Justice and Excellence": Quran 16:90 Explained

﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِ وَٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ وَإِيتَآيِٕ ذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَيَنۡهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱلۡبَغۡيِۚ يَعِظُكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:90]

### Three commands
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *ʿAdl* (justice) | Giving everyone their right |
| *Ihsan* (excellence) | Going beyond justice: doing more than what is owed |
| Giving to relatives | Supporting family ties with wealth and care |

### Three prohibitions
| Prohibition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Fahshaʾ* | Shameful immoral acts |
| *Munkar* | Everything known to be evil |
| *Baghy* | Oppression and transgression against others |

### The most comprehensive verse
- Ibn Masʿud is reported to have called it the most comprehensive verse in the Quran for good and evil (reported by al-Bukhari in al-Adab al-Mufrad and others).

### On the minbar
- Many preachers end the Friday sermon with this verse, reminding the community of its core values every week.

### Justice and excellence together
- Justice is the minimum; excellence is the goal. A Muslim does not stop at what is owed.

### Lessons
- **Review your dealings** with these six words.
- **Excellence starts at home** with relatives.

## "We Will Surely Give Them a Good Life": Quran 16:97 Explained

﴿مَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنࣱ فَلَنُحۡيِيَنَّهُۥ حَيَوٰةٗ طَيِّبَةٗۖ وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡرَهُم بِأَحۡسَنِ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:97]

### Two conditions
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Righteous deeds | In line with the Sunnah and sincere |
| *While a believer* | Deeds are accepted only with faith |

### Men and women equally
- *Whether male or female*: an explicit statement that reward is equal for both.

### What is the good life?
- Ibn ʿAbbas and others: lawful, good provision. Others said contentment, happiness and peace, or the sweetness of obedience. The verse gathers them: a life where the **heart is at ease**, even with little money.

### Two rewards
- In this world: a good life. In the Hereafter: reward according to the best of their deeds.

### Contrast
- Compare with Taha 20:124: *whoever turns away from My remembrance will have a hard life.*

### Lessons
- **Happiness is the fruit of faith and deeds**, not possessions.
- **Your deeds count equally**, whoever you are.

## "Invite to the Way of Your Lord with Wisdom": Quran 16:125 Explained

﴿ٱدۡعُ إِلَىٰ سَبِيلِ رَبِّكَ بِٱلۡحِكۡمَةِ وَٱلۡمَوۡعِظَةِ ٱلۡحَسَنَةِۖ وَجَٰدِلۡهُم بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:125]

### The way of your Lord
- You invite to **Allah's way**, not to yourself, your group or your opinion.

### Three methods
| Method | For whom | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Wisdom | The seeker of truth | Clear evidence, right time, right words |
| Good instruction | The heedless | Gentle reminders of reward and warning |
| The best way of discussion | The one who argues | Respectful dialogue with sound proof |

- Scholars note the order: start with the gentlest and most suitable.

### "Good" instruction
- Not every advice is good. It is *good* when it is kind, private when needed, and free of humiliation.

### Guidance is Allah's
- *Your Lord knows best who has strayed and who is guided.* Your job is to convey; results are His.

### Lessons
- **Choose your words** as carefully as your message.
- **Do not argue to win**; argue to clarify.

## "Be Patient, and Your Patience Is Only by Allah": Quran 16:127 Explained

﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ وَمَا صَبۡرُكَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ وَلَا تَحۡزَنۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا تَكُ فِي ضَيۡقٖ مِّمَّا يَمۡكُرُونَ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:127]

### Patience is a gift
- *Your patience is only by Allah*: you cannot endure by yourself alone. Ask Allah for patience as you ask Him for relief.

### Two prohibitions
| Prohibition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Do not grieve over them | Over their turning away from truth |
| Do not be distressed | By what they plot |

### Justice or patience?
- The previous verse: *If you retaliate, retaliate with the like of what you were harmed; but if you are patient, it is better for the patient* (16:126). Justice is permitted; patience is better.

### The final verse
- *Indeed Allah is with those who fear Him and those who do good* (16:128).

### Lessons
- **Pray for patience**: *O Allah, grant me patience.*
- **Do not let plots** squeeze your heart.

## "Allah Is with Those Who Fear Him and Those Who Do Good": Quran 16:128 Explained

﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَواْ وَّٱلَّذِينَ هُم مُّحۡسِنُونَ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:128]

### Two kinds of "with"
| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| General | Allah's knowledge of all creation: *He is with you wherever you are* (Al-Hadid 57:4) |
| Special | Support, help and protection for His friends, as here |

### Taqwa and ihsan
- **Taqwa**: guarding yourself from Allah's punishment by obeying and avoiding sin.
- **Ihsan**: *to worship Allah as if you see Him, and if you do not see Him, He sees you* (narrated by Muslim), and doing good to people.

### The wording
- *Ittaqaw* is a verb; *hum muhsinun* is a nominal sentence. Some scholars note that the nominal form indicates **constancy**: ihsan has become their fixed trait.

### Fruits
- Calm in hardship, success in work, and protection from enemies' plots.

### Lessons
- **Seek Allah's company** through taqwa and ihsan.
- **With Allah on your side**, do not fear schemes.

## "If You Do Good, You Do Good for Yourselves": Quran 17:7 Explained

﴿إِنۡ أَحۡسَنتُمۡ أَحۡسَنتُمۡ لِأَنفُسِكُمۡۖ وَإِنۡ أَسَأۡتُمۡ فَلَهَاۚ﴾ [Al-Isra 17:7]

### The principle
| If you | Result |
|---|---|
| Do good | It benefits **you** |
| Do evil | It harms **you** |

### The context
- The verses describe two periods when the Children of Israel spread corruption and were punished. The principle is placed in the middle as the lesson.

### Allah needs nothing
- Your good does not benefit Allah, and your evil does not harm Him.

### Motivation
- Kindness to others is really an investment in yourself; harm to others hurts you first.

### Lessons
- **Every good deed is a gift to yourself.**
- **Every sin is self-harm.**

## "This Quran Guides to What Is Most Upright": Quran 17:9 Explained

﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ يَهۡدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقۡوَمُ وَيُبَشِّرُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٗا كَبِيرٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:9]

### "Most upright"
- *Aqwam* is a superlative: not just upright, but **the most upright**. And the thing it guides to is left unnamed, *to that which is*, so it covers every path, way and state.

### Where it guides
| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| Belief | Pure tawhid |
| Worship | Balanced, without extremism or neglect |
| Character | Kindness to parents, justice, humility |
| Family | Rights of spouses and children |
| Economy | Honest trade, no riba, charity |

### The good news
- *A great reward* for those who **believe and do good**. Belief and action always come together.

### Context
- The verse comes soon after the Night Journey and the history of the Children of Israel, showing that the final guidance is now in the Quran.

### Lessons
- **Make the Quran your daily guide**, not just a book of blessing.
- **Ask: what does the Quran say about this?**

## Surah Al-Isra Meaning: The Night Journey and the Upright Way

**Surah Al-Isra** (The Night Journey, 111 ayat) begins by glorifying Allah, who took His servant by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa.

### The Quran's guidance
﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ يَهۡدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقۡوَمُ وَيُبَشِّرُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٗا كَبِيرٗا﴾

### Kindness to parents
﴿۞وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنًاۚ إِمَّا يَبۡلُغَنَّ عِندَكَ ٱلۡكِبَرَ أَحَدُهُمَآ أَوۡ كِلَاهُمَا فَلَا تَقُل لَّهُمَآ أُفّٖ وَلَا تَنۡهَرۡهُمَا وَقُل لَّهُمَا قَوۡلٗا كَرِيمٗا﴾
- Not even a sigh of annoyance. Then: lower to them the wing of humility, and pray for mercy for them.

### A code for society
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Give relatives, the poor and travellers their right | Generosity |
| Do not waste | Balance in spending |
| Do not kill your children out of fear of poverty | Allah provides |
| Do not approach zina | Avoid the paths leading to it |
| Do not consume the orphan's wealth | Protect the weak |
| Give full measure | Honest trade |
| Do not follow what you have no knowledge of | Responsibility for hearing, sight and heart |
| Do not walk arrogantly | Humility |

### The night prayer
- The Prophet ﷺ is told to pray at night as an extra act, so that his Lord may raise him to **a praised station**.

### The soul
- When asked about the soul, the answer came: *The soul is of the affair of my Lord, and you have been given only a little knowledge.*

### Lessons
- **The Quran is a complete way**, guiding to what is most upright.
- **Parents' rights come right after Allah's.**

## "Your Lord Has Decreed That You Be Good to Parents": Quran 17:23-24 Explained

﴿۞وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ أَلَّا تَعۡبُدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَٰنًاۚ إِمَّا يَبۡلُغَنَّ عِندَكَ ٱلۡكِبَرَ أَحَدُهُمَآ أَوۡ كِلَاهُمَا فَلَا تَقُل لَّهُمَآ أُفّٖ وَلَا تَنۡهَرۡهُمَا وَقُل لَّهُمَا قَوۡلٗا كَرِيمٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:23]
﴿وَٱخۡفِضۡ لَهُمَا جَنَاحَ ٱلذُّلِّ مِنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَةِ وَقُل رَّبِّ ٱرۡحَمۡهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:24]

### Right after tawhid
- *Qada* means decreed as a firm command. Kindness to parents is placed **immediately after** worshipping Allah alone.

### "With you" in old age
- *ʿIndaka*: when they are with you, in your care. Old age is when patience is tested most.

### Five instructions
| Instruction | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Do not say *uff* | Not even a sound of irritation |
| Do not repel them | No harsh tone or pushing away |
| Speak noble words | Respectful, gentle speech |
| Lower the wing of humility | Tenderness, out of mercy, not fear |
| Pray for them | *My Lord, have mercy on them as they raised me when I was small* |

### "As they raised me"
- The duʿa reminds you of the years they cared for you when you were weak.

### Lessons
- **Watch your tone** with parents, not just your words.
- **Pray for them daily**, alive or deceased.

## "Do Not Approach Unlawful Sexual Relations": Quran 17:32 Explained

﴿وَلَا تَقۡرَبُواْ ٱلزِّنَىٰٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فَٰحِشَةٗ وَسَآءَ سَبِيلٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:32]

### "Do not approach"
- The verse forbids even **coming near**: the look, the private meeting, the suggestive talk and the content that leads to it. This is closing the path before the destination.

### Two descriptions
| Description | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Fahishah* | Extremely shameful, rejected by the fitrah |
| *An evil way* | It destroys families, lineage and hearts |

### Prevention in the Quran
- Lowering the gaze (An-Nur 24:30-31), asking permission before entering homes, and easing marriage (An-Nur 24:32).

### The door of repentance
- Whoever falls and repents, Allah accepts repentance and replaces bad deeds with good for those who repent, believe and do good (Al-Furqan 25:70).

### Lessons
- **Avoid the road**, not just the end.
- **Protect your home** from content that leads there.

## "Do Not Pursue What You Have No Knowledge Of": Quran 17:36 Explained

﴿وَلَا تَقۡفُ مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمٌۚ إِنَّ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡبَصَرَ وَٱلۡفُؤَادَ كُلُّ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ كَانَ عَنۡهُ مَسۡـُٔولٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:36]

### The meaning of *taqfu*
- *Qafa* means to follow behind something. Do not follow, repeat or act upon what you do not truly know.

### What it covers
| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| Speech | Saying "I saw" when you did not |
| Judgement | Accusing someone based on suspicion |
| Religion | Speaking about halal and haram without knowledge |
| News | Sharing unverified messages |

### Three faculties questioned
- **Hearing**: what you listened to and passed on.
- **Sight**: what you looked at and claimed to see.
- **Heart**: what you believed and assumed.

### Social media
- Forwarding a rumour is following what you have no knowledge of. Verify first, as Allah commands in Al-Hujurat 49:6.

### Lessons
- **"I do not know" is a sign of honesty.**
- **Check before you share.**

## "Do Not Walk on the Earth Arrogantly": Quran 17:37 Explained

﴿وَلَا تَمۡشِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَحًاۖ إِنَّكَ لَن تَخۡرِقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَن تَبۡلُغَ ٱلۡجِبَالَ طُولٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:37]

### What is *marah*?
- Walking with pride, swagger and self-admiration.

### A humbling reminder
| You cannot | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tear the earth | No matter how hard you stamp |
| Reach the mountains in height | No matter how tall you stand |

- The verse puts arrogance in perspective: you are small before Allah's creation.

### A similar verse
- Luqman advised his son: *Do not turn your cheek away from people, and do not walk on the earth arrogantly* (Luqman 31:18).

### Arrogance is in the heart
- The Prophet ﷺ said: *Arrogance is rejecting the truth and looking down on people* (narrated by Muslim). Walking is only its outward sign.

### The verses that follow
- *All of that, its evil is hateful to your Lord* (17:38): this closes a list of commands in Al-Isra.

### Lessons
- **Walk with dignity**, not pride.
- **Accept truth** from anyone.

## "There Is Nothing That Does Not Glorify Him with Praise": Quran 17:44 Explained

﴿تُسَبِّحُ لَهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ ٱلسَّبۡعُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَن فِيهِنَّۚ وَإِن مِّن شَيۡءٍ إِلَّا يُسَبِّحُ بِحَمۡدِهِۦ وَلَٰكِن لَّا تَفۡقَهُونَ تَسۡبِيحَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ حَلِيمًا غَفُورٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:44]

### All creation glorifies
- The seven heavens, the earth, those in them, and **everything**.

### Is it real?
- Many scholars: real glorification, which we do not understand.
- The evidence: *but you do not understand their glorification*; if it were only a sign, we would understand it.

### From the Sunnah
- Ibn Masʿud said: *We used to hear the food glorifying Allah while it was being eaten* (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### "Forbearing, Forgiving"
- He does not hasten punishment on the heedless, and forgives those who return.

### Lessons
- **Do not be less** in glorification than stones and trees.
- **Glorify Allah** while walking and looking at creation.

## "Tell My Servants to Say What Is Best": Quran 17:53 Explained

﴿وَقُل لِّعِبَادِي يَقُولُواْ ٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ يَنزَغُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ كَانَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ عَدُوّٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:53]

### "My servants"
- Allah calls them **My servants**, a reminder that good speech is part of servitude.

### Not just good, but best
- *That which is best*: when you have a good option and a better one, choose the better.

### Satan's tool
- *Nazgh* means stirring up and provoking. A single harsh word can open a door for Satan between brothers, spouses or friends.

### Practical guidance
| Situation | Best words |
|---|---|
| Disagreement | Calm tone, no insults |
| Advice | Private and gentle |
| Anger | Silence until calm |
| Online | Think before posting |

### A Prophetic reminder
- *Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him say good or remain silent* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Pause before speaking**: is there a better word?
- **Close Satan's door** with kind speech.

## "We Have Certainly Honoured the Children of Adam": Quran 17:70 Explained

﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ كَرَّمۡنَا بَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ وَحَمَلۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَرَزَقۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَفَضَّلۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٖ مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقۡنَا تَفۡضِيلٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:70]

### Honour for all
- *The children of Adam*: every human, believer or not, has the dignity of creation.

### Forms of honour
| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| Reason | Distinguishing good from evil |
| Form | *In the best stature* (At-Tin 95:4) |
| Speech | *He taught him eloquence* (Ar-Rahman 55:4) |
| Subjugation | What is in the heavens and earth made of service |

### Land and sea
- Animals, ships and all means of transport are His blessings.

### "Over many"
- It says *many*, not *all*: precise wording.

### Preserving dignity
- Full honour is completed by obedience; sin lowers a person.

### Lessons
- **Respect every human**, as Allah honoured him.
- **Do not degrade yourself** through sin.

## "The Recitation of Dawn Is Ever Witnessed": Quran 17:78 Explained

﴿أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِدُلُوكِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ إِلَىٰ غَسَقِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَقُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِۖ إِنَّ قُرۡءَانَ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ كَانَ مَشۡهُودٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:78]

### Five prayers in one verse
| Phrase | Prayers |
|---|---|
| Decline of the sun | Dhuhr and ʿAsr |
| Darkness of the night | Maghrib and ʿIshaʾ |
| Recitation of dawn | Fajr |

### Why "Quran al-Fajr"?
- Because of the long recitation in it: recitation is a major part of Fajr.

### "Ever witnessed"
- *The angels of the night and the angels of the day gather at the Fajr prayer*, then Abu Hurayrah recited this verse (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### The next verse
- *And from the night, pray tahajjud as an extra for you; perhaps your Lord will raise you to a praised station* (17:79).

### Lessons
- **Never miss Fajr**: angels witness it.
- **Lengthen recitation** in Fajr when praying alone.

## "My Lord, Let Me Enter a Sound Entrance and Exit a Sound Exit": Quran 17:80 Explained

﴿وَقُل رَّبِّ أَدۡخِلۡنِي مُدۡخَلَ صِدۡقٖ وَأَخۡرِجۡنِي مُخۡرَجَ صِدۡقٖ وَٱجۡعَل لِّي مِن لَّدُنكَ سُلۡطَٰنٗا نَّصِيرٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:80]

### Three requests
| Request | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A truthful entrance | Begin with sincerity and correctness |
| A truthful exit | End well, with honour |
| Supporting authority from You | Strength and proof to uphold truth |

### The Hijrah
- Many scholars mention that it relates to leaving Makkah and entering Madinah.

### For every new chapter
- A new job, a move, marriage, a school or a project: ask that both the start and the end be **with truth**.

### "From Yourself"
- *Min ladunka*: support directly from Allah, not dependent on people.

### Lessons
- **Say this duʿa** before beginnings and endings.
- **Seek honest paths** in and out.

## "We Send Down of the Quran What Is Healing and Mercy": Quran 17:82 Explained

﴿وَنُنَزِّلُ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَا هُوَ شِفَآءࣱ وَرَحۡمَةࣱ لِّلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَلَا يَزِيدُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا خَسَارٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:82]

### "Of the Quran"
- Scholars explain *min* here as describing the Quran, not dividing it: the whole Quran is healing.

### Healing of what?
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Doubts | Clear proofs remove confusion |
| Desires | Reminders weaken unlawful desires |
| Sadness and anxiety | Comfort and hope |
| Bodily illness | Ruqyah with the Quran, as in the hadith of al-Fatihah used as ruqyah (narrated by al-Bukhari) |

### Mercy
- Guidance, forgiveness and blessing for those who believe and act.

### The same Quran, a different result
- *It increases the wrongdoers only in loss*: when they reject it, the proof against them grows.

### The next verse
- When blessed, man turns away; when harmed, he despairs. The Quran heals this imbalance too.

### Lessons
- **Make Quran part of your healing**, with medical treatment.
- **Approach it with faith**: that is the key to its effect.

## "Everyone Acts According to His Disposition": Quran 17:84 Explained

﴿قُلۡ كُلࣱّ يَعۡمَلُ عَلَىٰ شَاكِلَتِهِۦ فَرَبُّكُمۡ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَنۡ هُوَ أَهۡدَىٰ سَبِيلٗا﴾ [Al-Isra 17:84]

### What is *shakilah*?
| Explanation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Way | Each acts according to his path |
| Intention | According to his intention |
| Nature | According to his character |

### The context
- Just before it: the Quran is healing for believers but increases wrongdoers in loss. The same Quran affects people according to their **shakilah**.

### Judgement belongs to Allah
- *Your Lord knows best who is better guided*: not people's opinions.

### Reform the inside
- *There is a piece of flesh in the body; if it is sound, the whole body is sound... it is the heart* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Watch your intentions.**
- **Leave judgement of others to Allah.**
