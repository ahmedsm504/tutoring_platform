**Surah As-Saffat** (182 ayat) begins with angels standing in rows and ends with peace upon all messengers.

## A conversation in Paradise
- A believer in Paradise remembers a friend who denied resurrection, looks down and sees him in Hell: *By Allah, you almost ruined me.*
- **Lesson**: choose your companions carefully.

## Prophets with a refrain
| Prophet | Greeting |
|---|---|
| Nuh | *Peace upon Nuh among the worlds* |
| Ibrahim | *Peace upon Ibrahim* |
| Musa and Harun | *Peace upon Musa and Harun* |
| Ilyas | *Peace upon Ilyasin* |

## The great sacrifice
﴿فَلَمَّا بَلَغَ مَعَهُ ٱلسَّعۡيَ قَالَ يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰ فِي ٱلۡمَنَامِ أَنِّيٓ أَذۡبَحُكَ فَٱنظُرۡ مَاذَا تَرَىٰۚ﴾
- The son answered: *O my father, do as you are commanded; you will find me, if Allah wills, among the patient.*
- Allah ransomed him with a great sacrifice.

## Yunus
- He was swallowed by the whale, and *had he not been of those who glorify Allah, he would have remained inside it until the Day they are resurrected*.

## The ending
﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ ٱلۡعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَى ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾

## Lessons
- **Glorification** saves in hardship.
- **Obedience** with trust.
