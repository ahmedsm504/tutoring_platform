**Surah Al-Kawthar** is the shortest surah in the Quran, only three ayat, but it carries a great comfort for the Prophet ﷺ and his followers.

## Ayah 1
﴿إِنَّآ أَعۡطَيۡنَٰكَ ٱلۡكَوۡثَرَ﴾ — *Indeed, We have granted you Al-Kawthar.*
- **Al-Kawthar** means abundant good.
- The Prophet ﷺ described it as **a river in Paradise** that his Lord promised him, with much good in it [Muslim].

## Ayah 2
﴿فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنۡحَرۡ﴾ — *So pray to your Lord and sacrifice.*
- **Gratitude in action**: prayer and sacrifice for Allah alone.

## Ayah 3
﴿إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلۡأَبۡتَرُ﴾ — *Indeed, the one who hates you is the one cut off.*
- Some enemies mocked the Prophet ﷺ after his sons died, calling him "cut off".
- Allah answered: **his enemy** is the one cut off, while the Prophet's mention ﷺ fills the world.

## Lessons
| Lesson | Practice |
|---|---|
| Allah's gifts are greater than what people take | Don't despair at mockery |
| Gratitude is worship | Pray and give |
| Truth lasts | Stay firm |

## Recitation tips
- ﴿إِنَّآ﴾: full ghunnah on the doubled noon, then a madd of 4–5 counts.
- ﴿فَصَلِّ﴾: heavy ṣaad.
- ﴿وَٱنۡحَرۡ﴾: clear noon before ḥaa, and the raa is heavy when stopping.
