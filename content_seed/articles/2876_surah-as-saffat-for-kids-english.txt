Why do Muslims sacrifice an animal at Eid al-Adha? Every child asks this when they see the sheep at home before Eid. The answer is in Surah As-Saffat: Prophet Ibrahim saw in a dream that he was sacrificing his son Isma'il, and the dreams of prophets are revelation. He asked his son, and the young, devoted son replied: "O my father, do what you are commanded. You will find me, in sha' Allah, among the patient." When they both submitted to Allah's command, Allah called out: "You have fulfilled the vision," and sent a magnificent ram as a ransom for Isma'il. Ever since, Muslims sacrifice at Eid to remember this great act of obedience.

Surah As-Saffat also tells the story of Prophet Yunus, who was swallowed by a whale and then thrown onto the shore, weak and ill, where Allah grew a gourd plant over him for shade. And it beautifully describes the stars that decorate the sky. In this guide, we explain Surah As-Saffat to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah As-Saffat

- **Position**: surah 37, in juz 23.
- **Number of verses**: 182.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: as-Saffat, "Those Lined in Rows", the angels who stand in neat rows to worship Allah, just as we stand in rows for prayer.

### A repeated greeting
After several prophets' stories, Allah says: "Peace be upon Nuh among the worlds", "Peace be upon Ibrahim", "Peace be upon Musa and Harun", "Peace be upon Ilyas". Allah sends peace upon His prophets and honours them.

## Lesson 1: Straight Rows

﴿وَٱلصَّـٰٓفَّـٰتِ صَفّٗا﴾ (Quran 37:1)

### Explaining it to your child
"The angels stand in neat, straight rows to worship Allah. In prayer, we stand in straight rows like them. The Prophet ﷺ encouraged us to straighten our rows and said: Won't you line up as the angels line up before their Lord? (Muslim)"

### Activity: the straight row
Before a family prayer at home, make your child responsible for straightening the row: shoulder to shoulder, foot to foot. Say: "We're standing like the angels."

## Lesson 2: Stars Adorning the Sky

﴿إِنَّا زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِزِينَةٍ ٱلۡكَوَاكِبِ﴾ ﴿وَحِفۡظٗا مِّن كُلِّ شَيۡطَٰنٖ مَّارِدٖ﴾ (Quran 37:6-7)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah decorated the nearest sky with stars and planets, so we see them sparkling beautifully at night. He also made them a protection against rebellious devils, so they can't eavesdrop on the news of the heavens."

### Activity: star night
On a clear night, away from city lights if possible, lie down with your child and look at the sky. Count stars, find the brightest one, then quietly recite the verse.

## Lesson 3: Nuh and the Best of Answerers

﴿وَلَقَدۡ نَادَىٰنَا نُوحࣱ فَلَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡمُجِيبُونَ﴾ ﴿وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡكَرۡبِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا ذُرِّيَّتَهُۥ هُمُ ٱلۡبَاقِينَ﴾ ﴿وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ نُوحٖ فِي ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:75-79)

### Explaining it to your child
"Nuh called his Lord after being patient with his people for many, many years, and Allah answered him. 'How excellent are the answerers' means Allah is the best One to answer du'a. Allah saved Nuh and his family from the flood, made his descendants the ones who remained, and made people remember him with goodness until today: Peace be upon Nuh among the worlds."

### Lesson for kids
"Allah is the best One to answer. If you want something, ask Allah first."

## Lesson 4: Ibrahim's Sound Heart

﴿۞وَإِنَّ مِن شِيعَتِهِۦ لَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ جَآءَ رَبَّهُۥ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٍ﴾ (Quran 37:83-84)

### Explaining it to your child
"Ibrahim came to his Lord with a sound heart: clean from shirk, jealousy and hatred. A heart that loves Allah and wants good for people."

### Activity: washing the heart
Explain: "We wash our hands when they're dirty, and we can wash our hearts from bad feelings too." Teach your child that when they feel angry with someone, they can say: "O Allah, forgive them," and their heart becomes clean.

## Lesson 5: "My Lord, Grant Me Righteous Children"

﴿وَقَالَ إِنِّي ذَاهِبٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّي سَيَهۡدِينِ﴾ ﴿رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿فَبَشَّرۡنَٰهُ بِغُلَٰمٍ حَلِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 37:99-101)

### Explaining it to your child
"Ibrahim left his land and said: I am going to my Lord, He will guide me. And he prayed: My Lord, grant me a child from the righteous. Allah gave him the good news of a gentle boy, Isma'il. 'Halim' means someone calm and patient, who doesn't get angry quickly and acts wisely."

### Activity: parents' du'a
Tell your child that their parents prayed to Allah for them, and that they are a gift from Allah. Then teach them to pray too: "My Lord, make me one of the righteous."

## Lesson 6: The Great Obedience

﴿فَلَمَّا بَلَغَ مَعَهُ ٱلسَّعۡيَ قَالَ يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰ فِي ٱلۡمَنَامِ أَنِّيٓ أَذۡبَحُكَ فَٱنظُرۡ مَاذَا تَرَىٰۚ قَالَ يَـٰٓأَبَتِ ٱفۡعَلۡ مَا تُؤۡمَرُۖ سَتَجِدُنِيٓ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:102)

### Explaining it to your child
"Isma'il grew up and started walking and working alongside his father. Then Ibrahim saw in a dream that Allah was commanding him to sacrifice his son, and the dreams of prophets are true revelation. He didn't force his son; he asked him gently: O my son, what do you think? Isma'il said: O my father, do what you are commanded. You will find me, in sha' Allah, among the patient."

### Notice the manners of father and son
- **The father consulted his son**: he didn't spring the command on him; he asked his opinion.
- **The son said "Ya abati"**: words full of respect and love.
- **The son said "in sha' Allah"**: he relied on Allah, not on himself.
- **Both obeyed Allah**, even though the command was extremely hard.

## Lesson 7: The Ransom of a Great Sacrifice

﴿فَلَمَّآ أَسۡلَمَا وَتَلَّهُۥ لِلۡجَبِينِ﴾ ﴿وَنَٰدَيۡنَٰهُ أَن يَـٰٓإِبۡرَٰهِيمُ﴾ ﴿قَدۡ صَدَّقۡتَ ٱلرُّءۡيَآۚ إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡبَلَـٰٓؤُاْ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾ ﴿وَفَدَيۡنَٰهُ بِذِبۡحٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 37:103-107)

### Explaining it to your child
"When Ibrahim and Isma'il both submitted to Allah's command and Ibrahim laid his son down, Allah called: O Ibrahim, you have fulfilled the vision! You passed the test! It was a huge test. Then Allah sent a magnificent ram as a ransom for Isma'il, and Ibrahim sacrificed it instead. Isma'il was not harmed at all."

### Lesson for kids: why we sacrifice at Eid
"At Eid al-Adha, we sacrifice an animal to remember the obedience of Ibrahim and Isma'il, to draw closer to Allah, and to share the meat with family and the poor. Eid isn't only about new clothes; it's about remembering obedience."

### Activity: sharing the qurbani
At Eid, let your child help divide the meat into three bags: one for the family, one for relatives and neighbours, and one for the poor. Let them deliver a bag themselves.

## Lesson 8: Yunus and the Gourd Plant

﴿وَإِنَّ يُونُسَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ أَبَقَ إِلَى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ ٱلۡمَشۡحُونِ﴾ ﴿فَسَاهَمَ فَكَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُدۡحَضِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡتَقَمَهُ ٱلۡحُوتُ وَهُوَ مُلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فَلَوۡلَآ أَنَّهُۥ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسَبِّحِينَ﴾ ﴿لَلَبِثَ فِي بَطۡنِهِۦٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾ ﴿۞فَنَبَذۡنَٰهُ بِٱلۡعَرَآءِ وَهُوَ سَقِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ شَجَرَةٗ مِّن يَقۡطِينٖ﴾ ﴿وَأَرۡسَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰ مِاْئَةِ أَلۡفٍ أَوۡ يَزِيدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَـَٔامَنُواْ فَمَتَّعۡنَٰهُمۡ إِلَىٰ حِينٖ﴾ (Quran 37:139-148)

### Explaining it to your child
"Yunus left his town angry before Allah gave him permission, and boarded a crowded ship. Then he was thrown into the sea, and a whale swallowed him. If he had not been one of those who glorify Allah a lot, he would have stayed in the whale's belly until the Day of Resurrection! But he glorified Allah, so the whale threw him out onto a bare shore, weak and ill. Allah grew a gourd plant over him, with big leaves for shade. Then Allah sent him to his people, a hundred thousand or more, and they all believed!"

### Lesson for kids: the power of tasbih
"Yunus was saved because he was among those who glorify Allah a lot, remembering Allah before the hard times came. When we remember Allah in easy days, Allah protects us in hard days."

### Activity: the gourd leaf
Show your child a gourd or pumpkin plant, or a picture of one, and notice how big its leaves are. Then draw Yunus sitting in the shade of the gourd plant. Say: "Allah chose a plant with big leaves to shade him."

## Let's Read Together: Peace Upon Ibrahim

﴿وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾ ﴿كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:108-111)

### Explaining it to your child
"After this great act of obedience, Allah made people remember Ibrahim with goodness until the Day of Judgement: Peace be upon Ibrahim. We mention him in every prayer when we say in the tashahhud: as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim."

## Let's Read Together: The Ending

﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ ٱلۡعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَى ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:180-182)

### Explaining it to your child
"The surah ends by glorifying Allah: Subhana Rabbika Rabbil-'izzah, Glory be to your Lord, the Lord of honour. Then peace upon all the messengers. Then: All praise is for Allah, Lord of the worlds. The surah begins with angels glorifying Allah and ends with glorification and praise."

### Activity: closing the session
After every memorization or revision session, end by reciting these three verses together, so they become the sign that the session is over.

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Eid Sheep"

A few days before Eid al-Adha, Maryam's father brought home a beautiful white sheep and tied it in the garden. Maryam was delighted. She fed it every day and named it "Cotton". She would sit next to it and tell it stories.

On the night before Eid, Maryam learned that Cotton would be the Eid sacrifice, and she cried. Her mother sat with her and asked: "Maryam, do you know why we sacrifice?" She told her the story of Ibrahim and Isma'il: the dream, the question, the beautiful answer, "O my father, do what you are commanded, you will find me, in sha' Allah, among the patient", and the ram Allah sent as a ransom.

Maryam was quiet for a moment, then asked: "Mum, Isma'il was young like me and he was so patient?" Her mother said: "Yes, and he obeyed Allah and his father. We sacrifice to remember that obedience, and to feed poor families who rarely eat meat except at Eid."

In the morning, after the Eid prayer, Maryam helped her mother share the meat. She took a bag herself to a poor neighbour and saw how happy her children were. When she came back, she said: "Mum, now I understand. Cotton made our neighbour's children happy."

Ask your child: "Why was Maryam sad? What did she understand after hearing the story?"

## What We Learn From Surah As-Saffat

- **Straight rows** in prayer, like the angels.
- **The stars** are a decoration from Allah.
- **Allah is the best** to answer du'a.
- **A sound, clean heart**.
- **Pray for righteous children**.
- **Consultation and respect** between parents and children.
- **Obey Allah**, even when it's hard.
- **Tasbih** saves in hard times.
- **The Eid sacrifice** reminds us of obedience and feeds the poor.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### My child got scared by the sacrifice story. How do I reassure them?
Make it clear from the start that Isma'il was not harmed at all, and that Allah wanted to test their love for Him, then sent the ram. With a sensitive child, start with the happy ending.

### Who was the son to be sacrificed: Isma'il or Ishaq?
The majority of scholars hold it was Isma'il, because the good news of Ishaq comes in the surah after the story of the sacrifice.

### Should my child watch the qurbani?
It depends on your child's age and temperament. A sensitive child shouldn't be pushed; helping to share the meat and understanding the meaning is enough.
