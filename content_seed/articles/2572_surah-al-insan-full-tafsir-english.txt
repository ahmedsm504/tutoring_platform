Surah Al-Insan opens with a question that humbles every reader: "Has there come upon man a period of time when he was not a thing even mentioned?" There was a time when you did not exist. Then Allah created you, tested you, and showed you the way, so you could be grateful or ungrateful. The heart of the surah is a portrait of the righteous: they keep their vows, they fear a Day whose evil spreads far, and they feed the poor, the orphan and the captive, saying: "We feed you only for the face of Allah. We wish from you neither reward nor gratitude." Then comes one of the most detailed descriptions of Paradise in the Quran. The Prophet ﷺ recited this surah in Fajr prayer every Friday.

This guide explains Surah Al-Insan section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Insan
- **Number of verses**: 31.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 76, in the twenty-ninth juz.
- **Names**: Al-Insan ("Man") and Ad-Dahr ("Time"), both from the first verse.
- **Recited in Friday Fajr**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah said that the Prophet ﷺ used to recite in Fajr on Friday *Alif Lam Mim Tanzil* (As-Sajdah) and *Hal ata 'ala al-insan*.

## Verses 1–3: Man's Beginning and His Test
﴿هَلۡ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ حِينࣱ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهۡرِ لَمۡ يَكُن شَيۡـٔٗا مَّذۡكُورًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ أَمۡشَاجٖ نَّبۡتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ سَمِيعَۢا بَصِيرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّا هَدَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلسَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرٗا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا﴾ (Quran 76:1-3)

**"Has there come upon man a period of time when he was not a thing even mentioned?"** The question means: yes, certainly. There was a long time when you were nothing.

**"Indeed, We created man from a sperm-drop mixture"** (*amshaj*, mixed from the male and female), **"that We may test him; and We made him hearing and seeing"**. Life is a test, and Allah gave the tools for it: hearing and sight.

**"Indeed, We guided him to the way, be he grateful or be he ungrateful."** Allah showed the path; the choice is ours.

## Verse 4: The End of the Ungrateful
﴿إِنَّآ أَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ سَلَٰسِلَاْ وَأَغۡلَٰلٗا وَسَعِيرًا﴾ (Quran 76:4)

**"Indeed, We have prepared for the disbelievers chains and shackles and a blaze."** Just one verse for the punishment, and then many verses for the reward: the surah is mostly about hope.

## Verses 5–10: The Righteous
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ يَشۡرَبُونَ مِن كَأۡسٖ كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا كَافُورًا﴾ ﴿عَيۡنٗا يَشۡرَبُ بِهَا عِبَادُ ٱللَّهِ يُفَجِّرُونَهَا تَفۡجِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 76:5-6)

**The righteous** (*al-abrar*) **will drink from a cup whose mixture is of camphor**, from **a spring from which the servants of Allah drink, making it gush forth in force** wherever they wish.

﴿يُوفُونَ بِٱلنَّذۡرِ وَيَخَافُونَ يَوۡمٗا كَانَ شَرُّهُۥ مُسۡتَطِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَيُطۡعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسۡكِينٗا وَيَتِيمٗا وَأَسِيرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا نُطۡعِمُكُمۡ لِوَجۡهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمۡ جَزَآءٗ وَلَا شُكُورًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّا نَخَافُ مِن رَّبِّنَا يَوۡمًا عَبُوسٗا قَمۡطَرِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 76:7-10)

**Their qualities**:
1. **"They fulfil their vows"**: they keep their commitments to Allah. In Sahih al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever vows to obey Allah, let him obey Him."
2. **"And fear a Day whose evil will be widespread."**
3. **"And they give food, in spite of love for it"**: although they love it and need it themselves, or out of love for Allah, **"to the needy, the orphan and the captive"**.
4. **Sincerity**: **"We feed you only for the face of Allah. We wish not from you reward or gratitude."** They expect not even a thank-you.
5. **Fear of the Day**: **"Indeed, we fear from our Lord a Day austere and distressful."**

**Notice "the captive"**: at the time, captives were often non-Muslims taken in war. Feeding even them is praised. Kindness to prisoners is part of Islam's ethics.

**About the story of 'Ali and Fatimah**: a popular story says these verses were revealed about 'Ali, Fatimah and their sons giving away their food for three days. Scholars of hadith, including Ibn al-Jawzi, judged that story not authentic. The verses apply to every righteous person with these qualities.

## Verses 11–22: The Reward in Detail
﴿فَوَقَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ شَرَّ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمِ وَلَقَّىٰهُمۡ نَضۡرَةٗ وَسُرُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَجَزَىٰهُم بِمَا صَبَرُواْ جَنَّةٗ وَحَرِيرٗا﴾ ﴿مُّتَّكِـِٔينَ فِيهَا عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِۖ لَا يَرَوۡنَ فِيهَا شَمۡسٗا وَلَا زَمۡهَرِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَدَانِيَةً عَلَيۡهِمۡ ظِلَٰلُهَا وَذُلِّلَتۡ قُطُوفُهَا تَذۡلِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 76:11-14)

**Allah protects them from the evil of that Day** and gives them **radiance and happiness**, **a garden and silk** for their patience. They recline on couches and see **neither burning sun nor freezing cold**. Its shades are close above them, and its fruit **hangs low within reach**.

﴿وَيُطَافُ عَلَيۡهِم بِـَٔانِيَةٖ مِّن فِضَّةٖ وَأَكۡوَابٖ كَانَتۡ قَوَارِيرَا۠﴾ ﴿قَوَارِيرَاْ مِن فِضَّةٖ قَدَّرُوهَا تَقۡدِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَيُسۡقَوۡنَ فِيهَا كَأۡسٗا كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا زَنجَبِيلًا﴾ ﴿عَيۡنٗا فِيهَا تُسَمَّىٰ سَلۡسَبِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 76:15-18)

**Vessels of silver and cups of crystal**, crystal made of silver, measured exactly to their desire. A cup whose mixture is **ginger**, from a spring called **Salsabil**.

﴿۞وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وِلۡدَٰنࣱ مُّخَلَّدُونَ إِذَا رَأَيۡتَهُمۡ حَسِبۡتَهُمۡ لُؤۡلُؤٗا مَّنثُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا رَأَيۡتَ ثَمَّ رَأَيۡتَ نَعِيمٗا وَمُلۡكٗا كَبِيرًا﴾ ﴿عَٰلِيَهُمۡ ثِيَابُ سُندُسٍ خُضۡرࣱ وَإِسۡتَبۡرَقࣱۖ وَحُلُّوٓاْ أَسَاوِرَ مِن فِضَّةٖ وَسَقَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ شَرَابٗا طَهُورًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا كَانَ لَكُمۡ جَزَآءٗ وَكَانَ سَعۡيُكُم مَّشۡكُورًا﴾ (Quran 76:19-22)

**Immortal youths** circulate among them, **like scattered pearls**. **"And when you look there, you will see pleasure and great dominion."** Garments of fine green silk and brocade, bracelets of silver, and **"their Lord will give them a purifying drink"**.

**"Indeed, this is for you a reward, and your effort has been appreciated."** Allah Himself thanks them for their effort, after they did not seek thanks from anyone.

## Verses 23–26: Patience and Remembrance
﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَنزِيلٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تُطِعۡ مِنۡهُمۡ ءَاثِمًا أَوۡ كَفُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱذۡكُرِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ بُكۡرَةٗ وَأَصِيلٗا﴾ ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَٱسۡجُدۡ لَهُۥ وَسَبِّحۡهُ لَيۡلٗا طَوِيلًا﴾ (Quran 76:23-26)

**"Indeed, it is We who have sent down to you the Quran progressively. So be patient for the decision of your Lord, and do not obey from among them a sinner or ungrateful."** Then the daily programme: **remember the name of your Lord morning and afternoon, prostrate to Him during part of the night, and exalt Him a long night.**

## Verses 27–31: Loving the Immediate
﴿إِنَّ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ يُحِبُّونَ ٱلۡعَاجِلَةَ وَيَذَرُونَ وَرَآءَهُمۡ يَوۡمٗا ثَقِيلٗا﴾ ﴿نَّحۡنُ خَلَقۡنَٰهُمۡ وَشَدَدۡنَآ أَسۡرَهُمۡۖ وَإِذَا شِئۡنَا بَدَّلۡنَآ أَمۡثَٰلَهُمۡ تَبۡدِيلًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦ تَذۡكِرَةࣱۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلٗا﴾ ﴿وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا﴾ ﴿يُدۡخِلُ مَن يَشَآءُ فِي رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ وَٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ أَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمَۢا﴾ (Quran 76:27-31)

**"Indeed, these love the immediate and leave behind them a grave Day."** The same diagnosis as in Al-Qiyamah. **"We created them and strengthened their forms"**, and if We willed, We could replace them.

**"Indeed, this is a reminder, so whoever wills may take to his Lord a way. And you do not will except that Allah wills."** Human will is real, but it operates within Allah's will. **"He admits whom He wills into His mercy; but the wrongdoers, He has prepared for them a painful punishment."**

## Word Meanings
- **Ad-Dahr**: time, a long period.
- **Amshaj**: mixed.
- **Nabtalih**: to test him.
- **Kafur**: camphor.
- **Mustatir**: widespread.
- **'Ala hubbihi**: despite love for it.
- **Asir**: captive.
- **Shukur**: gratitude, thanks.
- **'Abus**: austere, frowning.
- **Qamtarir**: distressful, long and hard.
- **Zamharir**: freezing cold.
- **Qawarir**: crystal, glass vessels.
- **Zanjabil**: ginger.
- **Salsabil**: a spring in Paradise.
- **Sundus**: fine silk.
- **Istabraq**: brocade.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The opening question**: a humbling reminder that we were once nothing.
2. **One verse of punishment, many of reward**: hope outweighs fear in this surah.
3. **"Only for the face of Allah"**: sincerity in five words.
4. **"Crystal made of silver"**: an impossible beauty described in words.
5. **"Like scattered pearls"**: a picture of grace and movement.
6. **"Your effort has been appreciated"**: Allah's gratitude to those who sought none.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Stopping on *salasila* and *qawarira***: in the narration of Hafs, *salasila* (verse 4) may be stopped on with or without the alif; the first *qawarira* (verse 15) is stopped on with the alif; the second *qawarira* (verse 16) is stopped on without it. When continuing, the alif is not pronounced in any of them.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *yawman kana* (verse 7), the tanween is hidden before the kaf.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *miskinan wa yatiman wa asira* (verse 8), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *jaza'an* (verse 9).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Qiyamah before it ended with man's creation from a drop and asked whether he would be left neglected, and Al-Insan begins with that creation and explains its purpose: "that We may test him". Surah Al-Mursalat after it focuses on the deniers and their warnings, completing the picture of the two groups.

## Why Friday Fajr?
The Prophet ﷺ recited As-Sajdah and Al-Insan in Fajr on Friday. Scholars explained that both surahs speak about the creation of man, the resurrection, and the gathering of people, which are connected to Friday: Adam was created on a Friday, and the Hour will come on a Friday. Reviving this Sunnah in the mosque, or in your home if you pray Fajr there, connects you to the Prophet's ﷺ weekly rhythm.

## Feeding for the Face of Allah: Practical Ideas
1. **Feed a poor family regularly**, quietly.
2. **Sponsor an orphan's meals.**
3. **Support prisoners' welfare** and families of prisoners.
4. **Give food you like**, not only leftovers.
5. **Do not mention it to anyone**, and do not expect thanks.
6. **Feed people in Ramadan**: in a report graded authentic by at-Tirmidhi, the Prophet ﷺ said whoever gives a fasting person food to break his fast has a reward like his.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Remember that you were once nothing**: it cures arrogance.
2. **Use your hearing and sight in the test of life.**
3. **Keep your promises and vows.**
4. **Feed others sincerely**, expecting neither reward nor thanks.
5. **Fear the Day whose evil spreads far.**
6. **Remember Allah morning and evening, and pray at night.**
7. **Recite Al-Insan in Friday Fajr.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Insan
- **Week 1**: verses 1–10.
- **Week 2**: verses 11–22.
- **Week 3**: verses 23–31.

## Teaching Children
- **Make a "for the face of Allah" project**: they prepare food for someone without telling anyone.
- **Describe Paradise from the surah**: the shade, the fruit, the silver cups and Salsabil.
- **Teach them that we were once nothing**, and Allah gave us hearing and sight.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the names of Surah Al-Insan?
Al-Insan (Man) and Ad-Dahr (Time).

### When did the Prophet ﷺ recite Surah Al-Insan?
In Fajr prayer on Friday, together with As-Sajdah, as reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does "We feed you only for the face of Allah" mean?
The righteous feed others purely for Allah's pleasure, wanting neither reward nor thanks.

### Is the story of 'Ali and Fatimah giving away their food authentic?
Scholars of hadith judged it not authentic; the verses apply to every righteous person with these qualities.

### What is Salsabil?
A spring in Paradise mentioned in verse 18.

### What does "you do not will except that Allah wills" mean?
Human will is real, but it operates within Allah's will.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Insan?
31 verses.

### Who is the "captive" mentioned in verse 8?
A prisoner, who at the time was often a non-Muslim captured in war; feeding him is praised, showing Islam's kindness even to prisoners.

### Why is the punishment mentioned in one verse and the reward in many?
To make hope dominate the surah: Allah's mercy is wider, and the detailed reward motivates people to good.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Insan reminds you that you were once nothing, that you were created to be tested, and that the way has been shown. Its heroes are people who keep their promises and feed others for the face of Allah alone, and their reward is a Paradise described in loving detail, ending with Allah's own words: "your effort has been appreciated." Feed someone this week for His face alone.
