Surah At-Takwir is one of the surahs that let you see the Day of Resurrection as if with your own eyes. In twelve short, rapid verses it shows the universe coming apart: the sun wrapped up and its light extinguished, the stars falling, the mountains moving, the seas set aflame. In the middle of this cosmic upheaval it pauses on one small, unforgettable figure: a baby girl buried alive, asked "for what sin she was killed". Then, after an oath by the stars and the night and the breathing dawn, it describes the angel who brought the Quran and asks every human being a direct question: "So where are you going?"

This guide explains Surah At-Takwir verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah At-Takwir
- **Number of verses**: 29.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 81, in the thirtieth juz (Juz 'Amma).
- **Name**: At-Takwir means "wrapping up", from the first verse.
- **Main themes**: the events of the Hour, accountability, the truth of revelation, and free will under Allah's will.

## The Virtue of At-Takwir
Ibn 'Umar reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever would like to look at the Day of Resurrection as if he sees it with his own eyes, let him recite *Idha ash-shamsu kuwwirat*, *Idha as-sama'u infatarat* and *Idha as-sama'u inshaqqat*." Collected by at-Tirmidhi and Ahmad; al-Albani graded it authentic.

## Verses 1–6: The Universe Unravels
﴿إِذَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ كُوِّرَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:1)

**"When the sun is wrapped up."** Like a turban folded, its light gathered and extinguished. The source of all daylight on earth goes dark.

﴿وَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ ٱنكَدَرَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:2)

**"And when the stars fall, dispersing."** They lose their order and scatter.

﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:3)

**"And when the mountains are removed."** The firmest things on earth move like clouds.

﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡعِشَارُ عُطِّلَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:4)

**"And when full-term she-camels are neglected."** For the Arabs, a camel ten months pregnant was the most precious property. On that Day its owner will abandon it without a glance: wealth loses all value.

﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:5)

**"And when the wild beasts are gathered."** Animals that flee each other will gather in terror.

﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ سُجِّرَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:6)

**"And when the seas are filled with flame."** Water itself will burn.

## Verses 7–14: The Human Scenes
﴿وَإِذَا ٱلنُّفُوسُ زُوِّجَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:7)

**"And when the souls are paired."** Each person is joined with his like: the righteous with the righteous and the wicked with the wicked.

﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡمَوۡءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتۡ﴾ ﴿بِأَيِّ ذَنۢبٖ قُتِلَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:8-9)

**"And when the girl buried alive is asked: For what sin was she killed?"** The Arabs of ignorance buried some baby girls alive out of shame or fear of poverty. On the Day of Judgement the question is directed not to the killer but to the victim, to show the enormity of the crime and to shame the one who did it. She had no sin at all. Islam ended this practice and made raising daughters a path to Paradise: in Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever raises two girls until they reach maturity, he and I will come on the Day of Resurrection like this," and he joined his fingers.

﴿وَإِذَا ٱلصُّحُفُ نُشِرَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:10)

**"And when the pages are made public."** The records of deeds are opened.

﴿وَإِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ كُشِطَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:11)

**"And when the sky is stripped away."** Like a skin peeled off.

﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡجَحِيمُ سُعِّرَتۡ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡجَنَّةُ أُزۡلِفَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:12-13)

**"And when Hellfire is set ablaze, and when Paradise is brought near."**

﴿عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسࣱ مَّآ أَحۡضَرَتۡ﴾ (Quran 81:14)

**"A soul will know what it has brought."** After twelve "when"s comes one answer. Every soul will know exactly what it brought with it. This is the purpose of all the scenes: what have you prepared?

## Verses 15–18: The Oaths
﴿فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلۡخُنَّسِ﴾ ﴿ٱلۡجَوَارِ ٱلۡكُنَّسِ﴾ (Quran 81:15-16)

**"So I swear by the retreating stars, those that run and disappear."** Stars that appear at night and hide by day.

﴿وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا عَسۡعَسَ﴾ (Quran 81:17)

**"And by the night as it closes in."**

﴿وَٱلصُّبۡحِ إِذَا تَنَفَّسَ﴾ (Quran 81:18)

**"And by the dawn when it breathes."** A beautiful image: the morning breathes, light flows in like life returning to the world after night.

## Verses 19–21: The Description of Jibril
﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولٖ كَرِيمٖ﴾ ﴿ذِي قُوَّةٍ عِندَ ذِي ٱلۡعَرۡشِ مَكِينٖ﴾ ﴿مُّطَاعٖ ثَمَّ أَمِينٖ﴾ (Quran 81:19-21)

**"Indeed, it is the word of a noble messenger, possessor of power and with the Owner of the Throne, secure in position, obeyed there and trustworthy."** Five qualities of Jibril: noble, powerful, honoured near Allah, obeyed by the angels, and trustworthy with the revelation. The message cannot be corrupted on its way.

## Verses 22–25: The Prophet ﷺ Is Truthful
﴿وَمَا صَاحِبُكُم بِمَجۡنُونٖ﴾ (Quran 81:22)

**"And your companion is not mad."** "Your companion": you have known him all his life.

﴿وَلَقَدۡ رَءَاهُ بِٱلۡأُفُقِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ﴾ (Quran 81:23)

**"And he has already seen him in the clear horizon."** The Prophet ﷺ saw Jibril in his true form filling the horizon, as reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

﴿وَمَا هُوَ عَلَى ٱلۡغَيۡبِ بِضَنِينٖ﴾ (Quran 81:24)

**"And he is not a withholder of the unseen."** He conveyed all he was given.

﴿وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوۡلِ شَيۡطَٰنٖ رَّجِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 81:25)

**"And it is not the word of a devil, expelled."**

## Verses 26–29: So Where Are You Going?
﴿فَأَيۡنَ تَذۡهَبُونَ﴾ (Quran 81:26)

**"So where are you going?"** After all this evidence, which way are you heading? A question to every reader at every moment.

﴿إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرࣱ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمۡ أَن يَسۡتَقِيمَ﴾ (Quran 81:27-28)

**"It is not except a reminder to the worlds, for whoever wills among you to take a right course."**

﴿وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 81:29)

**"And you do not will except that Allah wills, Lord of the worlds."** Human beings have real will and are responsible for it, and that will operates within the will of Allah. So ask Him for guidance.

## Word Meanings
- **Kuwwirat**: wrapped up, its light extinguished.
- **Inkadarat**: fell, dispersed.
- **Al-'Ishar**: full-term pregnant she-camels.
- **'Uttilat**: neglected.
- **Sujjirat**: filled with flame.
- **Zuwwijat**: paired.
- **Al-Maw'udah**: the girl buried alive.
- **Kushitat**: stripped away.
- **Su''irat**: set ablaze.
- **Al-Khunnas**: the retreating stars.
- **'As'as**: closes in, or departs.
- **Tanaffasa**: breathes.
- **Danin**: a withholder.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Twelve "when"s**, then one answer: a soul will know.
2. **The buried girl among the cosmic scenes**: one injustice as weighty as the collapse of the universe.
3. **The question to the victim**: rebuke by indirect address.
4. **The breathing dawn**: personification of morning.
5. **"So where are you going?"**: three Arabic words that stop the reader.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Lam shamsiyyah**: in *ash-shamsu* (verse 1).
- **Iqlab**: in *bi-ayyi dhanbin* (verse 9), the noon sakinah in *dhanb* before the ba' becomes a hidden mim with ghunnah.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *nafsun ma* (verse 14), the tanween merges into the mim.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *tanaffasa* (verse 18), the noon sakinah is hidden before the fa'.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'u* (verse 11) and *sha'a* (verse 28).

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah 'Abasa before it ends with the faces of the Day, some bright and some covered with dust, and At-Takwir describes that Day in detail. Surah Al-Infitar after it begins with the same structure: "When the sky breaks apart."

## Practical Lessons
1. **Picture the Day** when the sun, stars and seas fail.
2. **Value nothing above your deeds**, as the precious camels will be abandoned.
3. **Honour daughters**, and stand against every injustice to the weak.
4. **Prepare what you will bring**: a soul will know what it has brought.
5. **Trust the Quran**: it came through a trustworthy angel to a truthful Prophet ﷺ.
6. **Ask yourself daily**: "Where am I going?"
7. **Ask Allah for guidance**, since your will is within His will.

## How to Memorise Surah At-Takwir
- **Day 1**: verses 1–14.
- **Day 2**: verses 15–29, then the whole surah with the hadith about the three surahs.

## Teaching Children
- **Count the twelve "when"s** together.
- **Tell them how Islam honoured girls**, using the story of the buried girl gently and age-appropriately.
- **Ask them**: "Where are you going?" and talk about good goals.

## The Structure of the Surah
Surah At-Takwir is built in three movements, and seeing them makes it easier to understand and memorise.

**The first movement (verses 1–14)** is a series of conditions. Six describe the physical universe: the sun, the stars, the mountains, the camels, the wild beasts and the seas. Six describe the human world and the hereafter: the pairing of souls, the buried girl, the open records, the stripped sky, the blazing Fire and the approaching Paradise. Then comes the single answer: "A soul will know what it has brought." The long suspense of twelve conditions makes the answer land with full force.

**The second movement (verses 15–25)** is a set of oaths and their subject. Allah swears by things that appear and disappear, the stars, the night and the dawn, as if to say: just as these come and go by His order, so revelation comes by His order through a trustworthy messenger. The subject of the oath is the truth of the Quran: who brought it (Jibril), who received it (the Prophet ﷺ), and what it is not (the word of a devil).

**The third movement (verses 26–29)** is the call to the reader: where are you going, this is a reminder, and your will is within Allah's will.

## What the Buried Girl Teaches Us Today
The practice of burying daughters ended long ago, but the principle of verse 9 lives on. Every weak person whose rights are taken, whether a child denied care, a woman denied her inheritance, or an unborn life ended without right, will have a question asked on their behalf on the Day of Judgement. The verse also teaches that no social pressure, fear of poverty or concern for reputation justifies wronging the innocent. Surah Al-Isra' says: "And do not kill your children for fear of poverty. We provide for them and for you."

## Reflecting on "Where Are You Going?"
Scholars of the heart suggested a simple daily exercise with verse 26. Before sleeping, ask: where did today take me, closer to Allah or further from Him? What did I add to what my soul will bring? A habit of two minutes each night turns the surah from a description of the future into a guide for the present.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "when the sun is wrapped up" mean?
Its light is gathered and extinguished at the end of the world.

### Who is the buried girl in verse 8?
A baby girl buried alive in the time of ignorance; she will be asked for what sin she was killed.

### Who is the "noble messenger" in verse 19?
The angel Jibril, who brought the revelation.

### Did the Prophet ﷺ see Jibril?
Yes, in his true form on the clear horizon, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the virtue of At-Takwir?
Whoever wants to see the Day of Resurrection as if with his eyes should recite it with Al-Infitar and Al-Inshiqaq, as at-Tirmidhi reported.

### Do we have free will?
Yes, real will for which we are responsible, operating within the will of Allah.

### How many verses are in Surah At-Takwir?
29 verses.

### How is Surah At-Takwir structured?
In three movements: twelve scenes of the Day, oaths affirming the truth of the Quran, and a direct call to the reader.

## Conclusion
Surah At-Takwir switches off the sun and the stars so that you can see clearly what matters: not the camels, not the mountains, but what your soul has brought. It reminds you that no injustice is forgotten, not even that done to a buried baby girl. And it leaves you with one question for every day of your life: "So where are you going?"
