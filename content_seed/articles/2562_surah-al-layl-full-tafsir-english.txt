Surah Al-Layl divides humanity by one simple question: what do you do with what you have? It swears by the night and the day, by male and female, and then says: "Indeed, your efforts are diverse." People run in very different directions. Then it describes two people. One gives, fears Allah and believes in the best reward, and Allah eases his way towards ease. The other is stingy, thinks he needs no one, and denies the best reward, and Allah eases his way towards hardship. The surah ends with a portrait of the most righteous person: he gives his wealth to purify himself, expecting nothing from anyone, seeking only the face of his Lord, "and he will surely be satisfied".

This guide explains Surah Al-Layl verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Layl
- **Number of verses**: 21.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 92, in the thirtieth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Layl means "the night", from the first verse.
- **Recommended by the Prophet ﷺ**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, he told Mu'adh to recite Al-A'la, Ash-Shams and Al-Layl rather than making the prayer too long.
- **Main theme**: giving and stinginess, and how each leads to its own path.

## Verses 1–4: Your Efforts Are Diverse
﴿وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا تَجَلَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ سَعۡيَكُمۡ لَشَتَّىٰ﴾ (Quran 92:1-4)

**The oaths**:
1. **"By the night when it covers"**: when its darkness covers everything.
2. **"And the day when it appears"**: when its light shines.
3. **"And He who created the male and the female."**

**The answer**: **"Indeed, your efforts are diverse"** (*la-shatta*): scattered in different directions. The oaths are all pairs of opposites: night and day, male and female. In the same way, people's efforts are opposites: some work for Paradise and some for the Fire, some for this world only and some for the hereafter.

## Verses 5–7: The Giver
﴿فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أَعۡطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ﴾ (Quran 92:5-7)

**Three qualities**:
1. **"Gives"** (*a'ta*): spends his wealth in the way of Allah.
2. **"And fears Allah"** (*ittaqa*): giving is joined with God-consciousness, so it is pure.
3. **"And believes in the best"** (*al-husna*): believes in Allah's promised reward, or in "la ilaha illa Allah", which is how some early scholars explained it.

**The result**: **"We will ease him towards ease"** (*al-yusra*): Allah makes good deeds easy for him, opens doors of goodness, and makes the path to Paradise smooth.

## Verses 8–10: The Miser
﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۢ بَخِلَ وَٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَكَذَّبَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡعُسۡرَىٰ﴾ (Quran 92:8-10)

**Three opposite qualities**:
1. **"Is stingy"**: withholds what he should give.
2. **"And considers himself free of need"** (*istaghna*): feels he does not need Allah or His reward.
3. **"And denies the best"**: rejects the promised reward.

**The result**: **"We will ease him towards hardship"** (*al-'usra*): the path of evil becomes easy for him, and he slides towards the Fire.

**The perfect symmetry**: each quality of the giver has an opposite in the miser: giving vs. stinginess, fear of Allah vs. self-sufficiency, belief vs. denial, ease vs. hardship.

## "Work, for Everyone Is Eased Towards What He Was Created For"
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'Ali ibn Abi Talib reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "There is none of you except that his seat in the Fire and his seat in Paradise have been written." They said: "O Messenger of Allah, should we not rely on that?" He said: "Work, for everyone is eased towards what he was created for. As for the people of happiness, they are eased towards the deeds of the people of happiness, and as for the people of misery, they are eased towards the deeds of the people of misery." Then he recited: "As for him who gives and fears Allah and believes in the best, We will ease him towards ease..."

**The lesson**: destiny is not an excuse to sit back. Allah knows your end, but you do not. Your job is to act; your deeds are the sign of where you are heading.

## Verses 11–13: Wealth Will Not Help
﴿وَمَا يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُ مَالُهُۥٓ إِذَا تَرَدَّىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا لَلۡهُدَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ لَنَا لَلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰ﴾ (Quran 92:11-13)

**"And what will his wealth avail him when he falls?"** When he falls into the grave or into the Fire, the wealth he hoarded will not help him.

**"Indeed, upon Us is the guidance"**: Allah has made clear the way of truth. **"And indeed, to Us belong the hereafter and the first life"**: both worlds are His, so seek both from Him.

## Verses 14–16: The Blazing Fire
﴿فَأَنذَرۡتُكُمۡ نَارٗا تَلَظَّىٰ﴾ ﴿لَا يَصۡلَىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡأَشۡقَى﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ﴾ (Quran 92:14-16)

**"So I have warned you of a Fire that is blazing. None will burn in it except the most wretched, who denied and turned away."**

## Verses 17–21: The Most Righteous
﴿وَسَيُجَنَّبُهَا ٱلۡأَتۡقَى﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي يُؤۡتِي مَالَهُۥ يَتَزَكَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَمَا لِأَحَدٍ عِندَهُۥ مِن نِّعۡمَةٖ تُجۡزَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱبۡتِغَآءَ وَجۡهِ رَبِّهِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَلَسَوۡفَ يَرۡضَىٰ﴾ (Quran 92:17-21)

**"But the most righteous will be kept away from it"**: and his qualities:
1. **"Who gives his wealth to purify himself"**: his giving is for purification, not reputation.
2. **"And not for anyone who has done him a favour to be repaid"**: he is not paying back a debt of kindness.
3. **"But only seeking the face of his Lord, the Most High"**: pure sincerity.
4. **"And he will surely be satisfied"**: Allah will give him until he is pleased.

**About Abu Bakr**: many commentators, including Ibn Kathir, mentioned that these verses were revealed about Abu Bakr as-Siddiq, who bought and freed believers who were being tortured in Makkah, such as Bilal, expecting nothing in return. The words, however, include everyone who has the same qualities.

## Word Meanings
- **Yaghsha**: covers.
- **Tajalla**: appears, shines.
- **Shatta**: diverse, scattered.
- **A'ta**: gave.
- **Al-Husna**: the best, the promised reward.
- **Al-Yusra**: ease.
- **Istaghna**: considered himself free of need.
- **Al-'Usra**: hardship.
- **Taradda**: fell, perished.
- **Talazza**: blazing.
- **Al-Atqa**: the most righteous.
- **Yatazakka**: purifies himself.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Oaths by opposites**: night and day, male and female, matching "your efforts are diverse".
2. **Perfect symmetry**: three qualities for the giver, three opposite qualities for the miser.
3. **"Ease towards ease" and "ease towards hardship"**: the same verb leading to opposite destinations.
4. **"When he falls"**: a short phrase that captures the end of the miser.
5. **"And he will surely be satisfied"**: an open-ended promise.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Izhar halqi**: in *man a'ta* (verse 5), the noon sakinah before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *naran talazza* (verse 14), the tanween is hidden before the ta'.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *'indahu* (verse 19), the noon sakinah is hidden before the dal.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna 'alayna* (verse 12), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Ash-Shams before it said "He has succeeded who purifies it", and Al-Layl shows a practical way to purify: "who gives his wealth to purify himself". Surah Ad-Duha after it begins with the morning light after the night, and promises the Prophet ﷺ: "And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied", echoing the end of Al-Layl: "and he will surely be satisfied".

## Giving That Opens the Doors of Ease
1. **Give regularly**, even a little: it trains the soul.
2. **Give secretly** when you can: it protects sincerity.
3. **Give to those who cannot repay you.**
4. **Give from what you love**: "You will never attain righteousness until you spend from what you love."
5. **Give while you are healthy and hopeful of wealth**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said the best charity is that given "while you are healthy and greedy, fearing poverty and hoping for wealth".
6. **Expect the ease that Allah promised.**

## When Things Feel Hard
If you find good deeds heavy and bad deeds easy, the surah offers a diagnosis. Look at the three qualities: are you giving? Are you mindful of Allah? Do you believe in His reward? Start with giving, add God-consciousness, renew your faith in the hereafter, and ask Allah to ease you towards ease.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Recognise where your effort is going**: towards which goal?
2. **Be a giver**, not a hoarder.
3. **Never feel independent of Allah.**
4. **Use destiny as motivation**, not an excuse: "Work, for everyone is eased."
5. **Purify your giving from showing off and from expecting repayment.**
6. **Seek only the face of your Lord.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Layl
- **Day 1**: verses 1–10.
- **Day 2**: verses 11–21, then revise the whole surah.

## Teaching Children
- **Explain the two paths** with simple examples: sharing toys and refusing to share.
- **Tell them how Abu Bakr freed Bilal.**
- **Start a family charity box** and let them put in something regularly.

## Stinginess: The Hidden Disease
The surah treats stinginess not as a small flaw but as the start of a road to hardship. Why is it so dangerous? Because stinginess grows out of a belief that wealth protects you, and that belief is exactly the opposite of trust in Allah. In Sahih Muslim, Jabir reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Beware of stinginess, for stinginess destroyed those before you. It drove them to shed one another's blood and to make lawful what was forbidden to them."

**Signs of stinginess to watch for in yourself**:
1. **Delaying obligatory zakah** or calculating it to the lowest possible amount.
2. **Feeling pain when giving**, even small amounts.
3. **Withholding from family**: spending little on your wife and children while able to do more.
4. **Refusing small favours**: lending a tool, sharing food, giving time.
5. **Not saying salam** or replying to it: the Prophet ﷺ called the one who is stingy with salam the stingiest of people, in a report graded authentic by al-Albani.

**The cure**: start giving immediately, even a little; remind yourself that wealth belongs to Allah; and remember that "what will his wealth avail him when he falls?"

## Believing in "the Best"
The third quality of the giver, "believes in the best", is what makes giving possible. A person who truly believes that Allah will replace what he gives, and reward it in the hereafter, finds giving easy. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "There is no day on which the servants wake up except that two angels descend. One of them says: O Allah, give the one who spends a replacement. And the other says: O Allah, give the one who withholds destruction." Faith in this promise is the engine of generosity.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "your efforts are diverse" mean?
People's efforts go in very different directions, some for Paradise and some for the Fire.

### What does "We will ease him towards ease" mean?
Allah makes good deeds and the path to Paradise easy for the one who gives, fears Him and believes in the reward.

### What is the meaning of the hadith "Work, for everyone is eased"?
Destiny is not an excuse to stop acting; everyone is made able to do the deeds that lead to his end, so we must work. It is reported by al-Bukhari and Muslim from 'Ali.

### Who is "the most righteous" in this surah?
The one who gives his wealth to purify himself, seeking only Allah's face. Many commentators said it was revealed about Abu Bakr as-Siddiq.

### What does "when he falls" mean?
When he falls into the grave or into the Fire, his wealth will not help him.

### Why did the Prophet ﷺ mention this surah to Mu'adh?
To recommend short surahs in congregational prayer so as not to burden the people, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Layl?
21 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

### What does "believes in the best" mean?
He believes in Allah's promised reward for good deeds, or, as some early scholars explained, in the testimony "la ilaha illa Allah".

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Layl shows that the road you walk becomes easier the further you go on it. Give, fear Allah and believe in His reward, and He will ease you towards ease. Hoard, feel self-sufficient and deny, and the road to hardship becomes smooth. Give your wealth to purify yourself, seek only the face of your Lord, and He promises: "And he will surely be satisfied."
