Surah Fatir begins with praise of the Originator of the heavens and the earth, who made the angels messengers with wings, two, three and four. It then states a truth that brings deep peace: "Whatever Allah grants to people of mercy, none can withhold it; and whatever He withholds, none can release it after Him." It addresses all of humanity with one of the most important verses about our true condition: "O mankind, you are those in need of Allah, while Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy." It paints the world in colours, white, red and black mountains, varied fruits, peoples and animals, and concludes: "Only those of His servants who have knowledge truly fear Allah." It ends by dividing the inheritors of the Quran into three groups and reminding us that Allah gave us a lifetime long enough to remember.

This guide explains Surah Fatir section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Fatir
- **Number of verses**: 45.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 35, in the twenty-second juz.
- **Names**: Fatir ("the Originator") and Al-Mala'ikah ("the Angels"), both from the first verse.
- **Main themes**: Allah's creation and mercy, human need for Allah, the enmity of Satan, knowledge and fear of Allah, and the inheritors of the Book.

## Verses 1–3: The Originator and His Mercy
﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ فَاطِرِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَاعِلِ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ رُسُلًا أُوْلِيٓ أَجۡنِحَةٖ مَّثۡنَىٰ وَثُلَٰثَ وَرُبَٰعَۚ يَزِيدُ فِي ٱلۡخَلۡقِ مَا يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 35:1)

**"All praise is due to Allah, Originator of the heavens and the earth, who made the angels messengers having wings, two or three or four. He increases in creation what He wills."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud reported that the Prophet ﷺ saw Jibril with six hundred wings.

﴿مَّا يَفۡتَحِ ٱللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ مِن رَّحۡمَةٖ فَلَا مُمۡسِكَ لَهَاۖ وَمَا يُمۡسِكۡ فَلَا مُرۡسِلَ لَهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ (Quran 35:2)

**"Whatever Allah grants to people of mercy, none can withhold it; and whatever He withholds, none can release it thereafter."** This verse frees the heart from fear of people. If Allah opens a door of provision, health or guidance, no one can close it; if He closes it, no one can open it. So ask Him alone.

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡۚ هَلۡ مِنۡ خَٰلِقٍ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِ يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُؤۡفَكُونَ﴾ (Quran 35:3)

**"Is there any creator other than Allah who provides for you from the heaven and earth?"**

## Verses 4–8: Do Not Be Deceived
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّۖ فَلَا تَغُرَّنَّكُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَلَا يَغُرَّنَّكُم بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡغَرُورُ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ لَكُمۡ عَدُوࣱّ فَٱتَّخِذُوهُ عَدُوًّاۚ إِنَّمَا يَدۡعُواْ حِزۡبَهُۥ لِيَكُونُواْ مِنۡ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ﴾ (Quran 35:5-6)

**"O mankind, indeed the promise of Allah is truth, so let not the worldly life delude you, and be not deceived about Allah by the Deceiver."** **"Indeed, Satan is an enemy to you, so take him as an enemy."** Do not befriend him by following his whispers; treat him as the enemy he is. **"He only invites his party to be among the companions of the Blaze."**

﴿أَفَمَن زُيِّنَ لَهُۥ سُوٓءُ عَمَلِهِۦ فَرَءَاهُ حَسَنٗاۖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُضِلُّ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۖ فَلَا تَذۡهَبۡ نَفۡسُكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَسَرَٰتٍۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمُۢ بِمَا يَصۡنَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 35:8)

**"Then is one to whom the evil of his deed has been made attractive so he considers it good"**: one of the most dangerous states is to see your sin as good.

## Verses 9–14: Honour, Good Words and Lifespans
﴿مَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡعِزَّةَ فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ جَمِيعًاۚ إِلَيۡهِ يَصۡعَدُ ٱلۡكَلِمُ ٱلطَّيِّبُ وَٱلۡعَمَلُ ٱلصَّـٰلِحُ يَرۡفَعُهُۥۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَمۡكُرُونَ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ شَدِيدࣱۖ وَمَكۡرُ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُوَ يَبُورُ﴾ (Quran 35:10)

**"Whoever desires honour, then to Allah belongs all honour. To Him ascends good speech, and righteous work raises it."** Good words such as *dhikr*, truthful speech and kind words rise to Allah, and righteous deeds lift them. Words without deeds are weak; deeds give words their weight.

﴿وَٱللَّهُ خَلَقَكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ جَعَلَكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗاۚ وَمَا تَحۡمِلُ مِنۡ أُنثَىٰ وَلَا تَضَعُ إِلَّا بِعِلۡمِهِۦۚ وَمَا يُعَمَّرُ مِن مُّعَمَّرٖ وَلَا يُنقَصُ مِنۡ عُمُرِهِۦٓ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٍۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 35:11)

**"Allah created you from dust, then from a sperm-drop, then made you mates."** **"No aged person is granted additional life, nor is his lifespan lessened, except that it is in a register."** Every lifespan is written.

﴿وَمَا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡبَحۡرَانِ هَٰذَا عَذۡبࣱ فُرَاتࣱ سَآئِغࣱ شَرَابُهُۥ وَهَٰذَا مِلۡحٌ أُجَاجࣱۖ وَمِن كُلّٖ تَأۡكُلُونَ لَحۡمٗا طَرِيّٗا وَتَسۡتَخۡرِجُونَ حِلۡيَةٗ تَلۡبَسُونَهَاۖ وَتَرَى ٱلۡفُلۡكَ فِيهِ مَوَاخِرَ لِتَبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ ﴿يُولِجُ ٱلَّيۡلَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ وَيُولِجُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فِي ٱلَّيۡلِ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ كُلࣱّ يَجۡرِي لِأَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمۡ لَهُ ٱلۡمُلۡكُۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ مَا يَمۡلِكُونَ مِن قِطۡمِيرٍ﴾ ﴿إِن تَدۡعُوهُمۡ لَا يَسۡمَعُواْ دُعَآءَكُمۡ وَلَوۡ سَمِعُواْ مَا ٱسۡتَجَابُواْ لَكُمۡۖ وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ يَكۡفُرُونَ بِشِرۡكِكُمۡۚ وَلَا يُنَبِّئُكَ مِثۡلُ خَبِيرٖ﴾ (Quran 35:12-14)

**The two seas**: one fresh and sweet, one salty and bitter; from each you eat fresh meat and extract ornaments. **The false gods do not own even the membrane of a date seed** (*qitmir*). If you call them, they do not hear; if they heard, they could not respond; and on the Day of Resurrection they will deny your association.

## Verses 15–18: You Are the Needy
﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ أَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ﴾ ﴿إِن يَشَأۡ يُذۡهِبۡكُمۡ وَيَأۡتِ بِخَلۡقٖ جَدِيدٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَا ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ بِعَزِيزٖ﴾ (Quran 35:15-17)

**"O mankind, you are those in need of Allah, while Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy."** Every breath, heartbeat, meal and moment of guidance is a need fulfilled by Allah. Recognising this is the beginning of humility and du'a. In Sahih Muslim, in a hadith qudsi, Allah says: "O My servants, all of you are hungry except whom I feed, so seek food from Me and I will feed you."

**"If He wills, He can do away with you and bring forth a new creation. And that is not difficult for Allah."**

﴿وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةࣱ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۚ وَإِن تَدۡعُ مُثۡقَلَةٌ إِلَىٰ حِمۡلِهَا لَا يُحۡمَلۡ مِنۡهُ شَيۡءࣱ وَلَوۡ كَانَ ذَا قُرۡبَىٰٓۗ إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۚ وَمَن تَزَكَّىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَتَزَكَّىٰ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۚ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 35:18)

**"And no bearer of burdens will bear the burden of another."** Even a relative will not carry your sins.

## Verses 19–26: The Blind and the Seeing
﴿وَمَا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ وَٱلۡبَصِيرُ﴾ ﴿وَلَا ٱلظُّلُمَٰتُ وَلَا ٱلنُّورُ﴾ ﴿وَلَا ٱلظِّلُّ وَلَا ٱلۡحَرُورُ﴾ ﴿وَمَا يَسۡتَوِي ٱلۡأَحۡيَآءُ وَلَا ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰتُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُسۡمِعُ مَن يَشَآءُۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ بِمُسۡمِعٖ مَّن فِي ٱلۡقُبُورِ﴾ (Quran 35:19-22)

**"Not equal are the blind and the seeing, nor the darknesses and the light, nor the shade and the heat. And not equal are the living and the dead."** Faith is sight, light, shade and life; disbelief is blindness, darkness, heat and death.

﴿إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ بَشِيرٗا وَنَذِيرٗاۚ وَإِن مِّنۡ أُمَّةٍ إِلَّا خَلَا فِيهَا نَذِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 35:24)

**"And there was no nation but that there had passed within it a warner."** Allah sent messengers to every people.

## Verses 27–28: Colours and Knowledge
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا بِهِۦ ثَمَرَٰتٖ مُّخۡتَلِفًا أَلۡوَٰنُهَاۚ وَمِنَ ٱلۡجِبَالِ جُدَدُۢ بِيضࣱ وَحُمۡرࣱ مُّخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهَا وَغَرَابِيبُ سُودࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَٱلدَّوَآبِّ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ مُخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥ كَذَٰلِكَۗ إِنَّمَا يَخۡشَى ٱللَّهَ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡعُلَمَـٰٓؤُاْۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ غَفُورٌ﴾ (Quran 35:27-28)

**"Do you not see that Allah sends down rain from the sky, and We produce thereby fruits of varying colours? And in the mountains are tracts, white and red of varying shades, and some extremely black. And among people and moving creatures and grazing livestock are various colours similarly."**

**"Only those of His servants who have knowledge truly fear Allah."** Notice where this sentence comes: after a lesson in nature: rain, fruit, geology, human diversity and animals. The "knowledge" here includes knowing Allah through His creation. The more you understand of creation, the more you stand in awe of the Creator. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "By Allah, I am the most knowledgeable of you about Allah and the one who fears Him most."

## Verses 29–30: The Trade That Never Fails
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَتۡلُونَ كِتَٰبَ ٱللَّهِ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنفَقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ يَرۡجُونَ تِجَٰرَةٗ لَّن تَبُورَ﴾ ﴿لِيُوَفِّيَهُمۡ أُجُورَهُمۡ وَيَزِيدَهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ غَفُورࣱ شَكُورࣱ﴾ (Quran 35:29-30)

**"Indeed, those who recite the Book of Allah and establish prayer and spend from what We have provided them, secretly and publicly, expect a transaction that will never perish."** Three acts: recitation, prayer and spending, and a trade with no loss. **"That He may give them in full their rewards and increase for them of His bounty."**

## Verses 31–35: The Three Inheritors of the Book
﴿ثُمَّ أَوۡرَثۡنَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱصۡطَفَيۡنَا مِنۡ عِبَادِنَاۖ فَمِنۡهُمۡ ظَالِمࣱ لِّنَفۡسِهِۦ وَمِنۡهُم مُّقۡتَصِدࣱ وَمِنۡهُمۡ سَابِقُۢ بِٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَضۡلُ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ﴾ (Quran 35:32)

**"Then We caused to inherit the Book those We have chosen of Our servants; and among them is he who wrongs himself, and among them is he who is moderate, and among them is he who is foremost in good deeds by permission of Allah."**

**The three groups**, all Muslims who inherited the Quran:
1. **The one who wrongs himself**: falls short in obligations or commits sins.
2. **The moderate**: fulfils obligations and avoids prohibitions.
3. **The foremost**: adds voluntary deeds and races in good.

**Notice**: all three are called "chosen" and inheritors, and the verse that follows promises gardens of perpetual residence. This is a great hope for every Muslim, while the wise aim to be among the foremost.

﴿جَنَّـٰتُ عَدۡنٖ يَدۡخُلُونَهَا يُحَلَّوۡنَ فِيهَا مِنۡ أَسَاوِرَ مِن ذَهَبٖ وَلُؤۡلُؤٗاۖ وَلِبَاسُهُمۡ فِيهَا حَرِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَذۡهَبَ عَنَّا ٱلۡحَزَنَۖ إِنَّ رَبَّنَا لَغَفُورࣱ شَكُورٌ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحَلَّنَا دَارَ ٱلۡمُقَامَةِ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ لَا يَمَسُّنَا فِيهَا نَصَبࣱ وَلَا يَمَسُّنَا فِيهَا لُغُوبࣱ﴾ (Quran 35:33-35)

In Paradise they will be adorned with bracelets of gold and pearls, and they will say: **"Praise to Allah, who has removed from us all sorrow."** Sorrow ends forever. **"He who has settled us in the home of duration out of His bounty. There touches us not in it any fatigue, and there touches us not in it weariness."**

## Verses 36–45: Long Enough to Remember
﴿وَهُمۡ يَصۡطَرِخُونَ فِيهَا رَبَّنَآ أَخۡرِجۡنَا نَعۡمَلۡ صَٰلِحًا غَيۡرَ ٱلَّذِي كُنَّا نَعۡمَلُۚ أَوَلَمۡ نُعَمِّرۡكُم مَّا يَتَذَكَّرُ فِيهِ مَن تَذَكَّرَ وَجَآءَكُمُ ٱلنَّذِيرُۖ فَذُوقُواْ فَمَا لِلظَّـٰلِمِينَ مِن نَّصِيرٍ﴾ (Quran 35:37)

The people of the Fire will cry: **"Our Lord, remove us; we will do righteousness other than what we were doing."** The answer: **"Did We not grant you life enough for whoever would remember to remember therein? And the warner had come to you."**

In Sahih al-Bukhari, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah leaves no excuse for a person whose life He has extended until he reaches sixty years."

﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُمۡسِكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ أَن تَزُولَاۚ وَلَئِن زَالَتَآ إِنۡ أَمۡسَكَهُمَا مِنۡ أَحَدٖ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ حَلِيمًا غَفُورٗا﴾ (Quran 35:41)

**"Indeed, Allah holds the heavens and the earth, lest they cease."**

﴿ٱسۡتِكۡبَارٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَكۡرَ ٱلسَّيِّيِٕۚ وَلَا يَحِيقُ ٱلۡمَكۡرُ ٱلسَّيِّئُ إِلَّا بِأَهۡلِهِۦۚ فَهَلۡ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا سُنَّتَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَۚ فَلَن تَجِدَ لِسُنَّتِ ٱللَّهِ تَبۡدِيلٗاۖ وَلَن تَجِدَ لِسُنَّتِ ٱللَّهِ تَحۡوِيلًا﴾ (Quran 35:43)

**"But the evil plot does not encompass except its own people."** Evil plotting eventually traps the plotter.

﴿وَلَوۡ يُؤَاخِذُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلنَّاسَ بِمَا كَسَبُواْ مَا تَرَكَ عَلَىٰ ظَهۡرِهَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ وَلَٰكِن يُؤَخِّرُهُمۡ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗىۖ فَإِذَا جَآءَ أَجَلُهُمۡ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِعِبَادِهِۦ بَصِيرَۢا﴾ (Quran 35:45)

**"And if Allah were to impose blame on the people for what they have earned, He would not leave upon the earth any creature. But He defers them for a specified term."** His patience is mercy, not neglect.

## Word Meanings
- **Fatir**: Originator.
- **Ajnihah**: wings.
- **Mumsik**: withholder.
- **Al-Gharur**: the Deceiver, Satan.
- **Al-'Izzah**: honour.
- **Yas'adu**: ascends.
- **Al-Kalim at-tayyib**: good speech.
- **Mu'ammar**: aged person.
- **Qitmir**: the thin membrane of a date seed.
- **Al-Fuqara'**: the needy.
- **Judad**: tracts, streaks.
- **Gharabib sud**: extremely black.
- **Lan tabur**: will never perish.
- **Muqtasid**: moderate.
- **Sabiq bil-khayrat**: foremost in good deeds.
- **Lughub**: weariness.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Wings "two, three and four"**: variety in angelic creation.
2. **"None can withhold it... none can release it"**: total dependence on Allah.
3. **"You are the needy... Allah is the Free of need"**: a contrast that humbles.
4. **Colours of mountains, fruits and people**, followed by knowledge and fear.
5. **"Removed from us all sorrow"**: the first words of relief in Paradise.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Izhar**: in *rusulan uli* (verse 1), the tanween before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Idgham without ghunnah**: in *min rahmatin* (verse 2), the noon sakinah merges into the ra'; and in *zalimun li-nafsihi* (verse 32), the tanween merges into the lam.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 3).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *muqtasid* (verse 32), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Saba' before it ended with the disbelievers barred from what they desire, and Fatir opens with praise for the Originator and the angels. Surah Ya-Sin after it continues with the messengers sent to a town, connecting to "there was no nation but that a warner passed within it".

## Practical Lessons
1. **Ask Allah alone**: no one can withhold what He gives.
2. **Treat Satan as an enemy.**
3. **Let your deeds lift your words.**
4. **Feel your need for Allah** in every moment.
5. **Study creation to grow in awe of Allah.**
6. **Recite, pray and spend**: the trade that never fails.
7. **Aim to be among the foremost.**
8. **Use your years**: life is long enough to remember.

## How to Memorise Surah Fatir
- **Week 1**: verses 1–14.
- **Week 2**: verses 15–28.
- **Week 3**: verses 29–45.

## Teaching Children
- **Look at fruits and rocks of different colours** and read verses 27–28.
- **Teach them that everything they have comes from Allah** and they need Him always.
- **Explain the three kinds of Quran readers** and encourage them to race in good.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "Fatir" mean?
The Originator, who created the heavens and the earth from nothing.

### What does "Whatever Allah grants of mercy, none can withhold it" mean?
No one can stop what Allah gives or give what He withholds, so the heart should depend on Him alone.

### What does "You are the needy of Allah" mean?
Every human being depends on Allah for everything, while Allah needs nothing.

### Why does the surah say only the knowledgeable truly fear Allah?
Because knowing Allah and His creation leads to awe and reverent fear; the verse comes after describing the variety of creation.

### Who are the three inheritors of the Book?
The one who wrongs himself, the moderate, and the one foremost in good deeds, all chosen by Allah.

### What is the significance of the age of sixty?
The Prophet ﷺ said Allah leaves no excuse for one whose life reaches sixty, as in Sahih al-Bukhari.

### How many verses are in Surah Fatir?
45 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Fatir shows you the Originator in angels' wings, coloured mountains and the two seas, and reminds you of your true condition: you are the needy, and He is the Free of need. Ask Him alone, take Satan as an enemy, let knowledge lead you to awe, and race to be among the foremost inheritors of the Book, before you hear: did We not give you a life long enough to remember?
