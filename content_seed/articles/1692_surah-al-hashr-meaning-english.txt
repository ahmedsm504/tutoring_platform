**Surah Al-Hashr** (24 ayat) moves from history to the heart, ending with some of the most majestic ayat about Allah.

## Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | The expulsion of Banu an-Nadir |
| 6–10 | How wealth is distributed; Muhajirun, Ansar and those after them |
| 11–17 | The hypocrites and their false promises |
| 18–20 | Look at what you have sent ahead |
| 21–24 | The Quran on a mountain, and Allah's names |

## The Ansar
﴿وَيُؤۡثِرُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَلَوۡ كَانَ بِهِمۡ خَصَاصَةࣱۚ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفۡسِهِۦ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾
- *They prefer others over themselves, even when in need.*

## The Quran on a mountain
﴿لَوۡ أَنزَلۡنَا هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ عَلَىٰ جَبَلٖ لَّرَأَيۡتَهُۥ خَٰشِعٗا مُّتَصَدِّعٗا مِّنۡ خَشۡيَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾

## The names of Allah
﴿هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡقُدُّوسُ ٱلسَّلَٰمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُ ٱلۡمُهَيۡمِنُ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡجَبَّارُ ٱلۡمُتَكَبِّرُۚ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾
| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Al-Malik | The King |
| Al-Quddūs | The Most Pure |
| As-Salām | The Source of Peace |
| Al-Muʾmin | The Giver of Security |
| Al-Muhaymin | The Guardian |
| Al-Khāliq, Al-Bāriʾ, Al-Muṣawwir | The Creator, the Maker, the Fashioner |

## Lessons
- **Prepare for tomorrow**: *let every soul look at what it has sent ahead*.
- **Generosity** is the mark of true brotherhood.
