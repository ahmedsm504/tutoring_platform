﴿إِنَّا عَرَضۡنَا ٱلۡأَمَانَةَ عَلَى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلۡجِبَالِ فَأَبَيۡنَ أَن يَحۡمِلۡنَهَا وَأَشۡفَقۡنَ مِنۡهَا وَحَمَلَهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ ظَلُومٗا جَهُولٗا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:72]

## What is the Trust?
- Many scholars: the religious obligations of obedience, with reward for fulfilling them and punishment for neglect.

## Declining out of fear
- Their refusal was not disobedience but **fear** of falling short: an offer of choice, not a binding command.

## "Man carried it"
- Humans accepted the Trust despite their weakness.

## *Zaluman jahula*
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Zalum* | Often wronging himself |
| *Jahul* | Often ignorant of consequences |

- A description of human nature, except those Allah guides.

## What follows
- In the next verse Allah punishes the hypocrites and the polytheists and turns in mercy to the believers (33:73): the Trust sorts people.

## Lessons
- **Feel the weight** of your responsibilities.
- **Fulfil trusts** in worship and dealings.
