Surah Maryam is the only surah of the Quran named after a woman, and Maryam is the only woman mentioned by name in the Quran. It is a surah soaked in mercy: the name *ar-Rahman*, the Most Merciful, recurs throughout it more than in almost any other surah, and its stories are stories of mercy given to prophets and their families in moments of weakness, old age, loneliness and rejection.

It is also a surah with a special place in Islamic history. When the early Muslims emigrated to Abyssinia, Ja'far ibn Abi Talib recited the opening of Surah Maryam before the Negus, who wept until his beard was wet. This account is reported by Ahmad with a chain graded *hasan* by scholars.

This guide explains the surah section by section in English.

## Quick Facts About Surah Maryam
- **Number of verses**: 98.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 19, in the sixteenth juz.
- **Name**: after Maryam, the mother of 'Isa, peace be upon them.
- **Prostration of recitation**: at verse 58.
- **Central theme**: the mercy of Allah to His prophets, and the absolute rejection of attributing a son to Him.

## Verses 1–15: Zakariya's Quiet Prayer and the Birth of Yahya
﴿ذِكۡرُ رَحۡمَتِ رَبِّكَ عَبۡدَهُۥ زَكَرِيَّآ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥ نِدَآءً خَفِيّٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي وَهَنَ ٱلۡعَظۡمُ مِنِّي وَٱشۡتَعَلَ ٱلرَّأۡسُ شَيۡبٗا وَلَمۡ أَكُنۢ بِدُعَآئِكَ رَبِّ شَقِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:2-4)

**Meaning:** A mention of the mercy of your Lord to His servant Zakariya, when he called his Lord in a secret call. He said: My Lord, my bones have weakened and my head is filled with grey, and I have never been disappointed in calling You, my Lord.

Look at how Zakariya prays. He calls *in secret*, a quiet, intimate prayer. He begins by describing his weakness, not his achievements. And he reminds himself of his history with Allah: "I have never been disappointed in calling You." This is the etiquette of du'a: humility, acknowledgement of need, and confidence in Allah's generosity.

He asked for an heir who would carry on the message, though his wife was barren and he was very old. Allah gave him the glad tidings of a son named Yahya, a name given to no one before. When Zakariya asked how, the answer came: "That is easy for Me; I created you before when you were nothing."

﴿يَٰيَحۡيَىٰ خُذِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِقُوَّةٖۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلۡحُكۡمَ صَبِيّٗا﴾ ﴿وَحَنَانٗا مِّن لَّدُنَّا وَزَكَوٰةٗۖ وَكَانَ تَقِيّٗا﴾ ﴿وَبَرَّۢا بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ وَلَمۡ يَكُن جَبَّارًا عَصِيّٗا﴾ ﴿وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡهِ يَوۡمَ وُلِدَ وَيَوۡمَ يَمُوتُ وَيَوۡمَ يُبۡعَثُ حَيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:12-15)

**Meaning:** O Yahya, take the Book with strength. And We gave him wisdom as a child, and tenderness from Us, and purity, and he was mindful of Allah, and dutiful to his parents, and he was not arrogant or disobedient. Peace be upon him the day he was born, the day he dies and the day he is raised alive.

*Take the Book with strength*: with seriousness and commitment. This is an instruction to every young Muslim who carries the Quran.

## Verses 16–40: Maryam and the Birth of 'Isa
﴿وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَرۡيَمَ إِذِ ٱنتَبَذَتۡ مِنۡ أَهۡلِهَا مَكَانٗا شَرۡقِيّٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱتَّخَذَتۡ مِن دُونِهِمۡ حِجَابٗا فَأَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهَا رُوحَنَا فَتَمَثَّلَ لَهَا بَشَرٗا سَوِيّٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَتۡ إِنِّيٓ أَعُوذُ بِٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مِنكَ إِن كُنتَ تَقِيّٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ رَسُولُ رَبِّكِ لِأَهَبَ لَكِ غُلَٰمٗا زَكِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:16-19)

**Meaning:** And mention in the Book Maryam, when she withdrew from her family to a place in the east. She placed a screen between herself and them, and We sent to her Our spirit, and he appeared to her as a well-formed man. She said: I seek refuge in the Most Merciful from you, if you are mindful of Allah. He said: I am only the messenger of your Lord, to give you a pure boy.

The "spirit" here is Jibril. Maryam's first reaction to a stranger is to seek refuge in the Most Merciful, a model of chastity and trust. When told she would have a son, she asked how, since no man had touched her and she was not unchaste. The answer: "It is easy for Me, and so that We make him a sign for people and a mercy from Us."

### The Pains of Childbirth
﴿فَأَجَآءَهَا ٱلۡمَخَاضُ إِلَىٰ جِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ قَالَتۡ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي مِتُّ قَبۡلَ هَٰذَا وَكُنتُ نَسۡيٗا مَّنسِيّٗا﴾ ﴿فَنَادَىٰهَا مِن تَحۡتِهَآ أَلَّا تَحۡزَنِي قَدۡ جَعَلَ رَبُّكِ تَحۡتَكِ سَرِيّٗا﴾ ﴿وَهُزِّيٓ إِلَيۡكِ بِجِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ تُسَٰقِطۡ عَلَيۡكِ رُطَبٗا جَنِيّٗا﴾ ﴿فَكُلِي وَٱشۡرَبِي وَقَرِّي عَيۡنٗاۖ فَإِمَّا تَرَيِنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡبَشَرِ أَحَدٗا فَقُولِيٓ إِنِّي نَذَرۡتُ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ صَوۡمٗا فَلَنۡ أُكَلِّمَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ إِنسِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:23-26)

**Meaning:** The pains of childbirth drove her to the trunk of a palm tree. She said: I wish I had died before this and been forgotten. Then he called her from below her: Do not grieve; your Lord has placed a stream beneath you. Shake the trunk of the palm towards you; fresh ripe dates will fall upon you. So eat and drink and be comforted.

These verses are among the most human in the Quran. A righteous woman, chosen above the women of the worlds, feels pain and fear and says "I wish I had died". The Quran does not hide it. Then comes comfort: water, fresh dates, and an instruction to take a small action, shaking the trunk, even though a tired woman could not truly shake a palm tree. Many scholars draw a lesson from this: Allah wants us to take the means, even if they seem small, and He provides the result.

### The Baby Speaks
When she returned carrying him, her people accused her: "O sister of Harun, your father was not a bad man, nor was your mother unchaste." She pointed to the baby.

In Sahih Muslim, al-Mughirah ibn Shu'bah reported that people asked him how Maryam could be called "sister of Harun" when Musa came long before 'Isa. When he asked the Prophet ﷺ, he said: "They used to name people after their prophets and righteous people before them."

﴿قَالَ إِنِّي عَبۡدُ ٱللَّهِ ءَاتَىٰنِيَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَجَعَلَنِي نَبِيّٗا﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلَنِي مُبَارَكًا أَيۡنَ مَا كُنتُ وَأَوۡصَٰنِي بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱلزَّكَوٰةِ مَا دُمۡتُ حَيّٗا﴾ ﴿وَبَرَّۢا بِوَٰلِدَتِي وَلَمۡ يَجۡعَلۡنِي جَبَّارٗا شَقِيّٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱلسَّلَٰمُ عَلَيَّ يَوۡمَ وُلِدتُّ وَيَوۡمَ أَمُوتُ وَيَوۡمَ أُبۡعَثُ حَيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:30-33)

**Meaning:** He said: I am the servant of Allah. He has given me the Book and made me a prophet. He has made me blessed wherever I am, and enjoined on me prayer and charity as long as I live, and dutifulness to my mother, and He has not made me arrogant or wretched. Peace be upon me the day I was born, the day I die and the day I am raised alive.

The very first words of 'Isa in the Quran are "I am the servant of Allah", a direct answer to every claim of divinity made about him later. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said that none spoke in the cradle except three, the first of them being 'Isa son of Maryam.

﴿ذَٰلِكَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَۖ قَوۡلَ ٱلۡحَقِّ ٱلَّذِي فِيهِ يَمۡتَرُونَ﴾ ﴿مَا كَانَ لِلَّهِ أَن يَتَّخِذَ مِن وَلَدٖۖ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥٓۚ إِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمۡرٗا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾ (Quran 19:34-35)

**Meaning:** That is 'Isa son of Maryam, the word of truth about which they dispute. It is not befitting for Allah to take a son. Glory be to Him. When He decrees a matter, He only says to it: Be, and it is.

## Verses 41–50: Ibrahim's Gentle Call to His Father
﴿إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ يَـٰٓأَبَتِ لِمَ تَعۡبُدُ مَا لَا يَسۡمَعُ وَلَا يُبۡصِرُ وَلَا يُغۡنِي عَنكَ شَيۡـٔٗا﴾ ﴿يَـٰٓأَبَتِ إِنِّي قَدۡ جَآءَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ مَا لَمۡ يَأۡتِكَ فَٱتَّبِعۡنِيٓ أَهۡدِكَ صِرَٰطٗا سَوِيّٗا﴾ ﴿يَـٰٓأَبَتِ لَا تَعۡبُدِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَۖ إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ كَانَ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ عَصِيّٗا﴾ ﴿يَـٰٓأَبَتِ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ أَن يَمَسَّكَ عَذَابࣱ مِّنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ فَتَكُونَ لِلشَّيۡطَٰنِ وَلِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:42-45)

**Meaning:** When he said to his father: O my dear father, why do you worship what does not hear, does not see and cannot help you at all? O my dear father, knowledge has come to me that has not come to you, so follow me and I will guide you to an even path. O my dear father, do not worship Satan; Satan has always been disobedient to the Most Merciful. O my dear father, I fear that a punishment from the Most Merciful will touch you.

Four times Ibrahim says *ya abati*, "O my dear father", a tender form of address. He gives reasons, he speaks humbly ("knowledge has come to me"), he shows care ("I fear for you"). This is how to speak to a parent about faith.

His father replied with harshness and threatened to stone him. Ibrahim answered: "Peace be upon you. I will ask my Lord to forgive you." He left with good manners, and Allah granted him Ishaq and Ya'qub and made them prophets.

## Verses 51–58: Musa, Isma'il and Idris
The surah briefly honours Musa, who was chosen and spoken to by Allah; Harun, given to him as a prophet out of mercy; Isma'il, "true to his promise", who used to command his family to pray and give charity; and Idris, "a man of truth, a prophet", whom Allah raised to a high station.

﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَ مِن ذُرِّيَّةِ ءَادَمَ وَمِمَّنۡ حَمَلۡنَا مَعَ نُوحٖ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّةِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَإِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ وَمِمَّنۡ هَدَيۡنَا وَٱجۡتَبَيۡنَآۚ إِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ خَرُّواْۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ وَبُكِيّٗا۩﴾ (Quran 19:58)

**Meaning:** Those are the ones Allah blessed among the prophets, from the descendants of Adam and of those We carried with Nuh, and of the descendants of Ibrahim and Israel, and from those We guided and chose. When the verses of the Most Merciful were recited to them, they fell down in prostration, weeping.

This is the place of the prostration of recitation. The prophets wept when they heard the words of the Most Merciful; the reader is invited to prostrate with them.

## Verses 59–65: The Generation That Lost the Prayer
﴿۞فَخَلَفَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ خَلۡفٌ أَضَاعُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَٱتَّبَعُواْ ٱلشَّهَوَٰتِۖ فَسَوۡفَ يَلۡقَوۡنَ غَيًّا﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يَدۡخُلُونَ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ وَلَا يُظۡلَمُونَ شَيۡـٔٗا﴾ (Quran 19:59-60)

**Meaning:** Then after them came a generation who neglected prayer and followed desires, so they will meet destruction, except those who repent, believe and do righteous deeds; they will enter Paradise and will not be wronged at all.

After the beautiful scene of weeping prophets comes a sharp contrast: a later generation that *lost* the prayer and chased desires. Notice the order: losing prayer comes first, and following desires comes after it. When the prayer goes, desires take its place. But the door of repentance remains wide open.

## Verses 66–72: Resurrection and the Passing Over Hell
﴿وَإِن مِّنكُمۡ إِلَّا وَارِدُهَاۚ كَانَ عَلَىٰ رَبِّكَ حَتۡمٗا مَّقۡضِيّٗا﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ نُنَجِّي ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَواْ وَّنَذَرُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ فِيهَا جِثِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:71-72)

**Meaning:** There is none of you except that he will pass over it; that is a decree your Lord has made binding. Then We will save those who were mindful of Allah and leave the wrongdoers in it on their knees.

Many scholars explain this "passing" as crossing the bridge (*as-sirat*) over Hell, which every person will cross. The mindful will be saved, each crossing according to his deeds.

## Verses 73–95: Arrogance and the Gravest Claim
The deniers boasted of their gatherings and wealth. The Quran reminds them how many generations before them had better possessions and were destroyed.

﴿وَقَالُواْ ٱتَّخَذَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَلَدٗا﴾ ﴿لَّقَدۡ جِئۡتُمۡ شَيۡـًٔا إِدّٗا﴾ ﴿تَكَادُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ يَتَفَطَّرۡنَ مِنۡهُ وَتَنشَقُّ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَتَخِرُّ ٱلۡجِبَالُ هَدًّا﴾ ﴿أَن دَعَوۡاْ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ وَلَدٗا﴾ ﴿وَمَا يَنۢبَغِي لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ أَن يَتَّخِذَ وَلَدًا﴾ (Quran 19:88-92)

**Meaning:** They say: The Most Merciful has taken a son. You have brought forth something terrible. The heavens almost rupture from it, the earth splits open, and the mountains collapse in ruin, that they attribute a son to the Most Merciful. It is not befitting for the Most Merciful to take a son.

The language here is unmatched in force. The claim of a son for Allah is so grave that the universe almost tears apart in protest. The name "the Most Merciful" is repeated, as if to say: His mercy is a mercy of a Lord over His servants, not of a father over a son.

﴿إِن كُلُّ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّآ ءَاتِي ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ عَبۡدٗا﴾ (Quran 19:93)

**Meaning:** There is no one in the heavens and the earth except that he will come to the Most Merciful as a servant.

## Verses 96–98: The Promise of Love
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ سَيَجۡعَلُ لَهُمُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وُدّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:96)

**Meaning:** Those who believe and do righteous deeds, the Most Merciful will appoint for them love.

This is one of the most beautiful promises in the Quran. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "When Allah loves a servant, He calls Jibril: I love so-and-so, so love him. Jibril loves him, then calls out to the people of heaven: Allah loves so-and-so, so love him. The people of heaven love him, then acceptance is placed for him on earth."

## Key Vocabulary
- **Nida'an khafiyya**: a secret, quiet call.
- **Wahana al-'azm**: the bones have weakened.
- **Bi-quwwah**: with strength and seriousness.
- **Hanan**: tenderness.
- **Ruhana**: Our spirit, Jibril.
- **Jidh' an-nakhlah**: the trunk of the palm.
- **Sariyya**: a stream.
- **Ya abati**: O my dear father.
- **Adaa' as-salah**: they lost or neglected the prayer.
- **Wudda**: love.

## Tajweed Notes for Reciting Maryam
- **Opening letters**: *Kaf Ha Ya 'Ayn Sad* are read with their letter names; *Kaf*, *'Ayn* and *Sad* carry a long madd, and the *nun* at the end of *'Ayn* is hidden before *sad*.
- **Madd 'arid**: most verses end in *-iyya* or *-da*, stopping with a long alif, which gives the surah its soft, flowing sound.
- **Prostration**: stop and prostrate at verse 58 when reciting.

## Practical Lessons from Surah Maryam
1. **Pray quietly and humbly.** Zakariya's secret prayer was answered against all odds.
2. **Take the Quran with strength.** Commit to it seriously, as Yahya was told.
3. **Take the means, however small.** Maryam was told to shake the trunk.
4. **Speak to parents with tenderness.** Learn from Ibrahim's *ya abati*.
5. **Keep your promises.** Isma'il is praised for being true to his word.
6. **Guard the prayer.** Losing it opens the door to following desires.
7. **Seek Allah's love.** Faith and righteous deeds bring love in heaven and on earth.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is the surah named Maryam?
Because it tells the story of Maryam and the birth of 'Isa, and she is the only woman named in the Quran.

### Who was the "spirit" sent to Maryam?
Jibril, who appeared to her in the form of a man.

### Why was Maryam called "sister of Harun"?
Because people used to name others after their prophets and righteous people, as the Prophet ﷺ explained in Sahih Muslim.

### What were 'Isa's first words?
"I am the servant of Allah."

### Where is the prostration in Surah Maryam?
At verse 58.

### What does "the Most Merciful will appoint for them love" mean?
Allah places love for the righteous believers in the hearts of the people of heaven and earth, as explained in the hadith of al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Maryam?
98 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Maryam is a surah of mercy. An old man's quiet prayer is answered, a young woman alone under a palm tree is comforted, a son speaks tenderly to a harsh father, and prophets fall down weeping at the words of the Most Merciful. Against this background the claim that Allah has a son is shown in all its gravity. The surah ends with a promise that every believer can hope for: faith and righteous deeds, and in return, love from the Most Merciful.
