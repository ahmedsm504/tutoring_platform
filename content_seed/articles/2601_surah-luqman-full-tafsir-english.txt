Surah Luqman is known above all for a father's advice to his son. Luqman was a wise man to whom Allah gave wisdom, and the Quran preserves his words as a model for every parent: "O my son, do not associate anything with Allah. Indeed, association is a great injustice." His advice moves from belief, to Allah's complete knowledge, to prayer and calling to good, to patience, and finally to manners: do not turn your cheek away from people in arrogance, walk with moderation, and lower your voice. Around this centrepiece, the surah speaks about the Quran as guidance for those who do good, warns against "idle talk" that leads people away from Allah, shows creation as proof of the Creator, and ends with the five things only Allah knows.

This guide explains Surah Luqman section by section in English, with authentic hadith and a practical parenting guide based on Luqman's advice.

## Quick Facts About Surah Luqman
- **Number of verses**: 34.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 31, in the twenty-first juz.
- **Name**: named after Luqman the wise, mentioned in verses 12–19.
- **Who was Luqman?** A righteous man given wisdom. The majority of scholars say he was a wise man, not a prophet.
- **Main themes**: wisdom, tawhid, kindness to parents, manners, Allah's knowledge and the unseen.

## Verses 1–5: Guidance for Those Who Do Good
﴿الٓمٓ﴾ ﴿تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ ﴿هُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٗ لِّلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ يُوقِنُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ عَلَىٰ هُدٗى مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡۖ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 31:1-5)

**"These are verses of the wise Book, as guidance and mercy for the doers of good."** Who are they? **"Those who establish prayer, give zakah, and are certain of the hereafter."** **"Those are upon guidance from their Lord, and it is those who are the successful."**

## Verses 6–9: Those Who Buy Idle Talk
﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَشۡتَرِي لَهۡوَ ٱلۡحَدِيثِ لِيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖ وَيَتَّخِذَهَا هُزُوًاۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ مُّهِينࣱ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَٰتُنَا وَلَّىٰ مُسۡتَكۡبِرٗا كَأَن لَّمۡ يَسۡمَعۡهَا كَأَنَّ فِيٓ أُذُنَيۡهِ وَقۡرٗاۖ فَبَشِّرۡهُ بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ﴾ (Quran 31:6-7)

**"And of the people is he who buys the amusement of speech to mislead from the way of Allah without knowledge and who takes it in ridicule."** *Lahw al-hadith* is talk and entertainment that distracts from Allah. 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud explained it as singing, swearing by Allah three times, in a report recorded by at-Tabari and others; other scholars included every kind of distracting entertainment used to turn people from the truth. **"And when Our verses are recited to him, he turns away arrogantly as if he had not heard them, as if there was in his ears deafness."**

## Verses 10–11: Creation Without Visible Pillars
﴿خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ بِغَيۡرِ عَمَدٖ تَرَوۡنَهَاۖ وَأَلۡقَىٰ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَوَٰسِيَ أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمۡ وَبَثَّ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ دَآبَّةٖۚ وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجٖ كَرِيمٍ﴾ ﴿هَٰذَا خَلۡقُ ٱللَّهِ فَأَرُونِي مَاذَا خَلَقَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن دُونِهِۦۚ بَلِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 31:10-11)

**"He created the heavens without pillars that you see."** He set firm mountains on the earth so it would not shift with you, spread every kind of creature, and sent rain that brings out every noble pair. **"This is the creation of Allah. So show Me what those other than Him have created."**

## Verse 12: The Wisdom of Luqman
﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا لُقۡمَٰنَ ٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ أَنِ ٱشۡكُرۡ لِلَّهِۚ وَمَن يَشۡكُرۡ فَإِنَّمَا يَشۡكُرُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِيٌّ حَمِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 31:12)

**"And We had certainly given Luqman wisdom: Be grateful to Allah."** The essence of wisdom is gratitude. **"And whoever is grateful is grateful for his own benefit. And whoever denies, then indeed, Allah is Free of need and Praiseworthy."**

## Verses 13–19: Luqman's Advice to His Son
﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ لُقۡمَٰنُ لِٱبۡنِهِۦ وَهُوَ يَعِظُهُۥ يَٰبُنَيَّ لَا تُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّ ٱلشِّرۡكَ لَظُلۡمٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 31:13)

**1. Tawhid**: **"O my son, do not associate anything with Allah. Indeed, association is a great injustice."** Notice the tender address *ya bunayya*, "my dear son". Advice begins with love.

In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud said that when the verse "those who believe and do not mix their belief with injustice" was revealed, the Companions were troubled and said: "Which of us does not wrong himself?" The Prophet ﷺ said: "It is not as you think. It is shirk. Have you not heard what Luqman said to his son: *Indeed, association is a great injustice*?"

﴿وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ حَمَلَتۡهُ أُمُّهُۥ وَهۡنًا عَلَىٰ وَهۡنٖ وَفِصَٰلُهُۥ فِي عَامَيۡنِ أَنِ ٱشۡكُرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيۡكَ إِلَيَّ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ ﴿وَإِن جَٰهَدَاكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن تُشۡرِكَ بِي مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمࣱ فَلَا تُطِعۡهُمَاۖ وَصَاحِبۡهُمَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا مَعۡرُوفٗاۖ وَٱتَّبِعۡ سَبِيلَ مَنۡ أَنَابَ إِلَيَّۚ ثُمَّ إِلَيَّ مَرۡجِعُكُمۡ فَأُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 31:14-15)

**2. Parents** (Allah's words inserted within Luqman's advice): **"And We have enjoined upon man care for his parents. His mother carried him, weakness upon weakness, and his weaning is in two years. Be grateful to Me and to your parents; to Me is the destination."** Gratitude to parents is placed right after gratitude to Allah.

**The limit**: **"But if they endeavour to make you associate with Me that of which you have no knowledge, do not obey them, but accompany them in this world with appropriate kindness."** Do not obey in disobedience to Allah, but never stop being kind.

﴿يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنَّهَآ إِن تَكُ مِثۡقَالَ حَبَّةٖ مِّنۡ خَرۡدَلٖ فَتَكُن فِي صَخۡرَةٍ أَوۡ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ أَوۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَأۡتِ بِهَا ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَطِيفٌ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 31:16)

**3. Allah's complete knowledge**: **"O my son, indeed if wrong should be the weight of a mustard seed and should be within a rock or anywhere in the heavens or in the earth, Allah will bring it forth. Indeed, Allah is Subtle and Acquainted."** Nothing is too small or too hidden.

﴿يَٰبُنَيَّ أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَٱنۡهَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَصَابَكَۖ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾ (Quran 31:17)

**4. Prayer, calling to good and patience**: **"O my son, establish prayer, enjoin what is right, forbid what is wrong, and be patient over what befalls you. Indeed, that is of the matters requiring determination."** Notice the order: first correct yourself with prayer, then correct others, then be patient with what you face as a result.

﴿وَلَا تُصَعِّرۡ خَدَّكَ لِلنَّاسِ وَلَا تَمۡشِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَحًاۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالٖ فَخُورٖ﴾ ﴿وَٱقۡصِدۡ فِي مَشۡيِكَ وَٱغۡضُضۡ مِن صَوۡتِكَۚ إِنَّ أَنكَرَ ٱلۡأَصۡوَٰتِ لَصَوۡتُ ٱلۡحَمِيرِ﴾ (Quran 31:18-19)

**5. Manners**:
- **"Do not turn your cheek away from people"** in arrogance.
- **"Do not walk through the earth exultantly."** **"Indeed, Allah does not like everyone self-deluded and boastful."**
- **"Be moderate in your pace"**: neither arrogant nor lazy.
- **"Lower your voice. Indeed, the most disagreeable of sounds is the voice of donkeys."**

## Verses 20–26: Blessings, the Firmest Handhold and the Creator
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَوۡاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَخَّرَ لَكُم مَّا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَأَسۡبَغَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ نِعَمَهُۥ ظَٰهِرَةٗ وَبَاطِنَةٗۗ وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يُجَٰدِلُ فِي ٱللَّهِ بِغَيۡرِ عِلۡمٖ وَلَا هُدٗى وَلَا كِتَٰبٖ مُّنِيرٖ﴾ (Quran 31:20)

**"Do you not see that Allah has made subject to you whatever is in the heavens and earth and amply bestowed upon you His favours, apparent and hidden?"** Apparent favours: health, wealth, family. Hidden favours: faith, protection from harm you never knew about, sins concealed.

﴿۞وَمَن يُسۡلِمۡ وَجۡهَهُۥٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَهُوَ مُحۡسِنࣱ فَقَدِ ٱسۡتَمۡسَكَ بِٱلۡعُرۡوَةِ ٱلۡوُثۡقَىٰۗ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾ (Quran 31:22)

**"And whoever submits his face to Allah while he is a doer of good, then he has grasped the most trustworthy handhold."** *Al-'urwah al-wuthqa*: a handhold that never breaks.

﴿وَلَئِن سَأَلۡتَهُم مَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ لَيَقُولُنَّ ٱللَّهُۚ قُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 31:25)

**"If you asked them who created the heavens and earth, they would surely say Allah."**

## Verses 27–30: The Words of Allah
﴿وَلَوۡ أَنَّمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِن شَجَرَةٍ أَقۡلَٰمࣱ وَٱلۡبَحۡرُ يَمُدُّهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ سَبۡعَةُ أَبۡحُرٖ مَّا نَفِدَتۡ كَلِمَٰتُ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 31:27)

**"If whatever trees upon the earth were pens and the sea was ink, replenished thereafter by seven more seas, the words of Allah would not be exhausted."** An image of the infinite knowledge and words of Allah.

## Verses 31–34: Do Not Be Deceived, and the Keys of the Unseen
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱلۡفُلۡكَ تَجۡرِي فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِنِعۡمَتِ ٱللَّهِ لِيُرِيَكُم مِّنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّكُلِّ صَبَّارٖ شَكُورٖ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا غَشِيَهُم مَّوۡجࣱ كَٱلظُّلَلِ دَعَوُاْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ فَلَمَّا نَجَّىٰهُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡبَرِّ فَمِنۡهُم مُّقۡتَصِدࣱۚ وَمَا يَجۡحَدُ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآ إِلَّا كُلُّ خَتَّارٖ كَفُورٖ﴾ (Quran 31:31-32)

**The ships sail by Allah's favour.** When waves cover people like canopies, they call on Allah sincerely; when He saves them to land, some of them waver.

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ وَٱخۡشَوۡاْ يَوۡمٗا لَّا يَجۡزِي وَالِدٌ عَن وَلَدِهِۦ وَلَا مَوۡلُودٌ هُوَ جَازٍ عَن وَالِدِهِۦ شَيۡـًٔاۚ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّۖ فَلَا تَغُرَّنَّكُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَلَا يَغُرَّنَّكُم بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡغَرُورُ﴾ (Quran 31:33)

**"O mankind, fear your Lord and fear a Day when no father will avail his son, nor will a son avail his father at all. Indeed, the promise of Allah is truth, so let not the worldly life delude you."**

﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِ وَيُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡغَيۡثَ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِۖ وَمَا تَدۡرِي نَفۡسࣱ مَّاذَا تَكۡسِبُ غَدٗاۖ وَمَا تَدۡرِي نَفۡسُۢ بِأَيِّ أَرۡضٖ تَمُوتُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرُۢ﴾ (Quran 31:34)

**The five keys of the unseen**:
1. **Knowledge of the Hour.**
2. **When the rain will fall.**
3. **What is in the wombs** in its full reality and destiny.
4. **What a soul will earn tomorrow.**
5. **In what land a soul will die.**

In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Umar reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "The keys of the unseen are five; no one knows them except Allah," and he recited this verse.

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Muhsinin**: the doers of good.
- **Lahw al-hadith**: amusement of speech, idle talk.
- **'Amad**: pillars.
- **Al-Hikmah**: wisdom.
- **Ya bunayya**: O my dear son.
- **Wahnan 'ala wahn**: weakness upon weakness.
- **Fisal**: weaning.
- **Khardal**: mustard seed.
- **Tusa''ir khaddak**: turn your cheek away in arrogance.
- **Marahan**: exultantly.
- **Uqsud**: be moderate.
- **Ughdud**: lower.
- **Asbagha**: amply bestowed.
- **Al-'Urwah al-wuthqa**: the most trustworthy handhold.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Ya bunayya"**: a diminutive of tenderness repeated in the advice.
2. **"A great injustice"**: shirk described as wronging the One who deserves all.
3. **"Weakness upon weakness"**: the mother's burden in three words.
4. **"A mustard seed within a rock"**: the smallest thing in the hardest place.
5. **"The voice of donkeys"**: a vivid image to teach a manner.
6. **Pens and seas of ink**: infinity made visible.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Alif Lam Mim*, the lam and meem are stretched six counts.
- **Izhar**: in *la-zulmun 'azim* (verse 13) and *wahnan 'ala wahn* (verse 14), the tanween before the throat letter 'ayn is pronounced clearly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna ash-shirka* (verse 13), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *waqsid fi mashyik* (verse 19), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly; and when stopping on *waqsid*, the dal bounces.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Ar-Rum before it ended with "Indeed, We have presented to the people in this Quran from every kind of example", and Luqman begins with "verses of the wise Book". Surah As-Sajdah after it begins with *Alif Lam Mim* as well, and continues with creation and the unseen.

## A Parenting Guide From Luqman
1. **Speak to your children with tenderness**: "my dear son".
2. **Start with tawhid**: love of Allah and refusal of shirk.
3. **Teach that Allah sees everything**, in a way that builds awareness, not fear.
4. **Teach prayer first**, then service to others.
5. **Prepare them for patience**: life will test them.
6. **Teach humility and manners**: how they walk, talk and treat people.
7. **Live it yourself**: children learn by example.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Be grateful**: it is the core of wisdom.
2. **Avoid idle entertainment** that pulls you from Allah.
3. **Free your worship from every form of shirk.**
4. **Thank your parents** and be kind to them always.
5. **Remember that Allah sees the smallest deed.**
6. **Walk humbly and speak gently.**
7. **Hold the firmest handhold**: submission with excellence.

## How to Memorise Surah Luqman
- **Week 1**: verses 1–11.
- **Week 2**: verses 12–19 (Luqman's advice).
- **Week 3**: verses 20–34.

## Teaching Children
- **Read Luqman's advice together**, one verse a day, and discuss it.
- **Practise lowering the voice** at home.
- **Make a list of hidden blessings** they never thought about.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who was Luqman?
A wise and righteous man to whom Allah gave wisdom; most scholars say he was not a prophet.

### What was Luqman's first advice to his son?
"Do not associate anything with Allah. Indeed, association is a great injustice."

### Why does the surah mention parents within Luqman's advice?
To show that gratitude to parents comes right after gratitude to Allah, while not obeying them in disobedience to Allah.

### What is "idle talk" in verse 6?
Speech and entertainment that distract from Allah and mislead people; Ibn Mas'ud explained it as singing.

### What are the five keys of the unseen?
The Hour, the rain, what is in the wombs, what a soul will earn tomorrow and where it will die, as in Sahih al-Bukhari.

### What does "lower your voice" teach?
Gentleness and humility in speech, as raising the voice without need is disagreeable.

### How many verses are in Surah Luqman?
34 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Luqman gives every parent a curriculum and every child a mirror. Start with tawhid, honour your parents, remember that Allah sees even a mustard seed in a rock, pray, call to good, be patient, and carry yourself with humility. Hold the firmest handhold, and do not let this world deceive you, for only Allah holds the keys of the unseen.
