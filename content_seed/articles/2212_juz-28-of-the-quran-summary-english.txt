Juz 28 begins with ﴿قَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّتِي تُجَٰدِلُكَ فِي زَوۡجِهَا وَتَشۡتَكِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ﴾, "Allah has heard the words of the woman who pleads with you about her husband and complains to Allah", so it is called **Juz Qad Sami'**. It contains nine complete surahs, all **Madani**: Al-Mujadilah, Al-Hashr, Al-Mumtahanah, As-Saff, Al-Jumu'ah, Al-Munafiqun, At-Taghabun, At-Talaq and At-Tahrim. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 542 to 561**.

It is **the juz of the Madinan community**, addressing the family (zihar, divorce and the Prophet's household), relationships (the etiquette of gatherings and private talk), loyalty and disavowal (Al-Mumtahanah), striving and calling (As-Saff), communal worship (Al-Jumu'ah) and the danger of hypocrisy (Al-Munafiqun).

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **building the Muslim community on taqwa and truthfulness**. "O you who believe" is repeated often, the command of taqwa appears in nearly every surah, and "the party of Allah" appears at the end of Al-Mujadilah: ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ حِزۡبُ ٱللَّهِۚ أَلَآ إِنَّ حِزۡبَ ٱللَّهِ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾, "Those are the party of Allah; the party of Allah are the successful." Among its most comprehensive verses: ﴿وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مَخۡرَجٗا﴾ ﴿وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ﴾, "Whoever fears Allah, He will make a way out for him and provide for him from where he does not expect."

The message: **the believing community rests on Allah hearing the complaint of the weak, on the Ansar's selflessness, on words matching deeds, and on taqwa at home, in divorce and in wealth.**

## Surah one: Al-Mujadilah (1-22)

Al-Mujadilah is Madani, with 22 verses; every verse contains the word "Allah". For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Mujadila Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-mujadila-meaning-english/).

### 1. Allah hears the woman who complained (1-6)
﴿قَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّتِي تُجَٰدِلُكَ فِي زَوۡجِهَا وَتَشۡتَكِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَٱللَّهُ يَسۡمَعُ تَحَاوُرَكُمَآۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعُۢ بَصِيرٌ﴾, "Allah has heard the words of the woman who pleads with you about her husband and complains to Allah, and Allah hears your conversation. Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing." It was revealed about Khawlah bint Tha'labah, whose husband had declared zihar against her. 'A'ishah is reported to have said: "Praise be to Allah, whose hearing encompasses all voices", which Al-Bukhari cites without a full chain. Then the ruling on zihar and its expiation: freeing a slave; if unable, fasting two consecutive months; if unable, feeding sixty poor people.

**Lesson**: the complaint of a vulnerable woman in her home was heard by Allah above the seven heavens, and Quran was revealed about it. Never think your worry too small for Allah.

### 2. Allah knows private talk (7-13)
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ مَا يَكُونُ مِن نَّجۡوَىٰ ثَلَٰثَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ رَابِعُهُمۡ وَلَا خَمۡسَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ سَادِسُهُمۡ﴾, "Do you not see that Allah knows what is in the heavens and earth? There is no private conversation of three but He is the fourth, nor of five but He is the sixth." Then the prohibition of private talk about sin and aggression, and the command to confer about righteousness and taqwa.

Then the etiquette of gatherings: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمۡ تَفَسَّحُواْ فِي ٱلۡمَجَٰلِسِ فَٱفۡسَحُواْ يَفۡسَحِ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ﴾, "O you who believe, when told to make room in gatherings, make room; Allah will make room for you", then ﴿يَرۡفَعِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ دَرَجَٰتٖۚ﴾, "Allah raises those of you who believe and those given knowledge by degrees."

**Reflect**: the reward matches the deed: make room for your brother and Allah makes room for you. The raising of the learned comes together with faith.

### 3. The party of Shaytan and the party of Allah (14-22)
A new quarter speaks of the hypocrites who took as allies a people with whom Allah is angry; Shaytan overcame them and made them forget Allah's remembrance; they are the party of Shaytan. The close: ﴿لَّا تَجِدُ قَوۡمٗا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ يُوَآدُّونَ مَنۡ حَآدَّ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَوۡ كَانُوٓاْ ءَابَآءَهُمۡ أَوۡ أَبۡنَآءَهُمۡ أَوۡ إِخۡوَٰنَهُمۡ أَوۡ عَشِيرَتَهُمۡۚ﴾, "You will not find a people who believe in Allah and the Last Day showing affection to those who oppose Allah and His Messenger, even if they were their fathers, sons, brothers or kin." Those are the ones in whose hearts He has inscribed faith: the party of Allah.

## Surah two: Al-Hashr (1-24)

Al-Hashr is Madani, with 24 verses, revealed about the expulsion of Banu An-Nadir from Madinah. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Hashr Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-hashr-meaning-english/).

### 1. The expulsion of Banu An-Nadir (1-10)
﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَخۡرَجَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِن دِيَٰرِهِمۡ لِأَوَّلِ ٱلۡحَشۡرِۚ مَا ظَنَنتُمۡ أَن يَخۡرُجُواْۖ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَنَّهُم مَّانِعَتُهُمۡ حُصُونُهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ﴾, "It is He who expelled the disbelievers among the People of the Book from their homes at the first gathering. You did not think they would leave, and they thought their fortresses would protect them from Allah", but Allah came at them from where they did not expect and cast terror into their hearts. Then the distribution of spoils obtained without fighting: ﴿كَيۡ لَا يَكُونَ دُولَةَۢ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡأَغۡنِيَآءِ مِنكُمۡۚ﴾, "so that it does not circulate only among the rich of you", and what the Messenger gives you, take it, and what he forbids you, refrain.

Then three generations:
1. **The emigrants**: expelled from their homes and wealth, seeking Allah's bounty and pleasure.
2. **The Ansar**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ تَبَوَّءُو ٱلدَّارَ وَٱلۡإِيمَٰنَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ يُحِبُّونَ مَنۡ هَاجَرَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا يَجِدُونَ فِي صُدُورِهِمۡ حَاجَةٗ مِّمَّآ أُوتُواْ وَيُؤۡثِرُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَلَوۡ كَانَ بِهِمۡ خَصَاصَةࣱۚ﴾, "Those who settled in the home and in faith before them love those who emigrated to them, feel no envy in their hearts for what they were given, and prefer them over themselves even when in need." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, an Ansari put out the lamp and fed the Prophet's guest ﷺ his children's supper, and this was revealed.
3. **Those who came after**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَلِإِخۡوَٰنِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ فِي قُلُوبِنَا غِلّٗا لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ﴾, "Those who came after them say: our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith, and put no rancour in our hearts towards those who believe."

**Lesson**: the believer's stance towards those who came before is prayer for them and a heart free of rancour.

### 2. The hypocrites and the parable of Shaytan (11-17)
A new quarter: the hypocrites promised Banu An-Nadir support and then abandoned them, like Shaytan when he tells man to disbelieve, and when he does, says: I am free of you.

### 3. Self-accounting and the greatness of the Quran (18-24)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَلۡتَنظُرۡ نَفۡسࣱ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ لِغَدٖۖ﴾, "O you who believe, fear Allah, and let every soul look to what it has sent ahead for tomorrow", ﴿وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ نَسُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَأَنسَىٰهُمۡ أَنفُسَهُمۡۚ﴾, "and do not be like those who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves." Then: ﴿لَوۡ أَنزَلۡنَا هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ عَلَىٰ جَبَلٖ لَّرَأَيۡتَهُۥ خَٰشِعٗا مُّتَصَدِّعٗا مِّنۡ خَشۡيَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "Had We sent this Quran down upon a mountain, you would have seen it humbled and splitting apart from fear of Allah."

The close with Allah's names:
- ﴿هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِۖ هُوَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾, "He is Allah, besides whom there is no god, Knower of the unseen and the seen; He is the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate."
- ﴿هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡقُدُّوسُ ٱلسَّلَٰمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُ ٱلۡمُهَيۡمِنُ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡجَبَّارُ ٱلۡمُتَكَبِّرُۚ﴾, "He is Allah, besides whom there is no god: the King, the Holy, Peace, the Giver of security, the Overseer, the Almighty, the Compeller, the Supreme."
- ﴿هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُ ٱلۡبَارِئُ ٱلۡمُصَوِّرُۖ لَهُ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ﴾, "He is Allah, the Creator, the Originator, the Fashioner; His are the most beautiful names."

**Lesson**: forgetting Allah leads to forgetting oneself; whoever forgets his Lord loses sight of his own good.

## Surah three: Al-Mumtahanah (1-13)

Al-Mumtahanah is Madani, with 13 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Mumtahana Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-mumtahana-meaning-english/).

### 1. Loyalty to the believers (1-6)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَتَّخِذُواْ عَدُوِّي وَعَدُوَّكُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَ تُلۡقُونَ إِلَيۡهِم بِٱلۡمَوَدَّةِ﴾, "O you who believe, do not take My enemy and your enemy as allies, extending affection to them." In Sahih Al-Bukhari it was revealed about Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah, who wrote to Quraysh informing them of the Prophet's march; the Prophet ﷺ pardoned him because he had fought at Badr. Then the model: ﴿قَدۡ كَانَتۡ لَكُمۡ أُسۡوَةٌ حَسَنَةࣱ فِيٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥٓ﴾, "There has been an excellent example for you in Ibrahim and those with him."

### 2. Kindness and justice to the peaceful (7-9)
A new quarter opens with hope: perhaps Allah will place affection between you and those of them you hold as enemies. Then the rule: ﴿لَّا يَنۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمۡ يُقَٰتِلُوكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَلَمۡ يُخۡرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمۡ أَن تَبَرُّوهُمۡ وَتُقۡسِطُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُقۡسِطِينَ﴾, "Allah does not forbid you from being kind and just to those who have not fought you over religion or expelled you from your homes. Allah loves the just."

**Lesson**: the Shari'ah distinguishes between those at war and those at peace; the peaceful are owed kindness and justice.

### 3. Testing the emigrant women and their pledge (10-13)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا جَآءَكُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ مُهَٰجِرَٰتٖ فَٱمۡتَحِنُوهُنَّۖ﴾, "O you who believe, when believing women come to you as emigrants, test them", hence the surah's name. Then the women's pledge: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ يُبَايِعۡنَكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن لَّا يُشۡرِكۡنَ بِٱللَّهِ شَيۡـٔٗا وَلَا يَسۡرِقۡنَ وَلَا يَزۡنِينَ وَلَا يَقۡتُلۡنَ أَوۡلَٰدَهُنَّ﴾, "O Prophet, when the believing women come to pledge to you that they will not associate anything with Allah, nor steal, nor commit zina, nor kill their children..."

## Surah four: As-Saff (1-14)

As-Saff is Madani, with 14 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah As-Saff Meaning in English](/blog/surah-as-saff-meaning-english/).

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لِمَ تَقُولُونَ مَا لَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ ﴿كَبُرَ مَقۡتًا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَن تَقُولُواْ مَا لَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِهِۦ صَفّٗا كَأَنَّهُم بُنۡيَٰنࣱ مَّرۡصُوصࣱ﴾, "O you who believe, why do you say what you do not do? Most hateful to Allah is that you say what you do not do. Allah loves those who fight in His cause in a row, as though they were a solid structure." Then Musa, whose people harmed him, and 'Isa: ﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ يَٰبَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ إِنِّي رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ إِلَيۡكُم مُّصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيَّ مِنَ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَمُبَشِّرَۢا بِرَسُولٖ يَأۡتِي مِنۢ بَعۡدِي ٱسۡمُهُۥٓ أَحۡمَدُۖ﴾, "When 'Isa son of Maryam said: Children of Israel, I am Allah's messenger to you, confirming the Torah before me and bringing good news of a messenger after me whose name is Ahmad."

Then the profitable trade: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ تِجَٰرَةٖ تُنجِيكُم مِّنۡ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ﴾ ﴿تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَتُجَٰهِدُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِأَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنفُسِكُمۡۚ﴾, "O you who believe, shall I guide you to a trade that will save you from a painful punishment? Believe in Allah and His Messenger and strive in Allah's cause with your wealth and lives." The close: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُوٓاْ أَنصَارَ ٱللَّهِ كَمَا قَالَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ لِلۡحَوَارِيِّـۧنَ مَنۡ أَنصَارِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ﴾, "O you who believe, be supporters of Allah, as 'Isa son of Maryam said to the disciples: who are my supporters for Allah?"

**Lesson**: what Allah hates most is words contradicting deeds; a solid row begins with united hearts.

## Surah five: Al-Jumu'ah (1-11)

Al-Jumu'ah is Madani, with 11 verses. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ used to recite Al-Jumu'ah and Al-Munafiqun in the Friday prayer. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Jumua Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-jumua-meaning-english/).

﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي بَعَثَ فِي ٱلۡأُمِّيِّـۧنَ رَسُولٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَيُزَكِّيهِمۡ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ﴾, "It is He who sent among the unlettered a messenger from themselves, reciting His verses to them, purifying them and teaching them the Book and wisdom." Then the parable of those who do not act on their knowledge: ﴿مَثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ حُمِّلُواْ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَحۡمِلُوهَا كَمَثَلِ ٱلۡحِمَارِ يَحۡمِلُ أَسۡفَارَۢاۚ﴾, "The example of those entrusted with the Torah who then did not carry it is that of a donkey carrying volumes of books."

Then the rulings of Friday: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَوٰةِ مِن يَوۡمِ ٱلۡجُمُعَةِ فَٱسۡعَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَذَرُواْ ٱلۡبَيۡعَۚ﴾ ﴿فَإِذَا قُضِيَتِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةُ فَٱنتَشِرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾, "O you who believe, when the call is made for prayer on Friday, hasten to the remembrance of Allah and leave trade. And when the prayer is finished, disperse in the land, seek Allah's bounty and remember Allah much, that you may succeed." Then: ﴿وَإِذَا رَأَوۡاْ تِجَٰرَةً أَوۡ لَهۡوًا ٱنفَضُّوٓاْ إِلَيۡهَا وَتَرَكُوكَ قَآئِمٗاۚ﴾, "When they saw trade or amusement, they rushed to it and left you standing." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, Jabir reported it was revealed when a caravan arrived while the Prophet ﷺ was delivering the sermon.

**A lesson for carriers of the Quran**: whoever carries the Quran without acting on it resembles the donkey carrying books. Memorizing without acting is a load without benefit.

## Surah six: Al-Munafiqun (1-11)

Al-Munafiqun is Madani, with 11 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Munafiqun Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-munafiqun-meaning-english/).

﴿إِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ قَالُواْ نَشۡهَدُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱللَّهُ يَشۡهَدُ إِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ لَكَٰذِبُونَ﴾, "When the hypocrites come to you, they say: we bear witness that you are the Messenger of Allah. Allah knows you are His Messenger, and Allah bears witness that the hypocrites are liars." Then a new quarter describing them: ﴿۞وَإِذَا رَأَيۡتَهُمۡ تُعۡجِبُكَ أَجۡسَامُهُمۡۖ وَإِن يَقُولُواْ تَسۡمَعۡ لِقَوۡلِهِمۡۖ كَأَنَّهُمۡ خُشُبࣱ مُّسَنَّدَةࣱۖ﴾, "When you see them, their appearance impresses you, and when they speak you listen to them; they are like propped-up planks."

Then their words: ﴿يَقُولُونَ لَئِن رَّجَعۡنَآ إِلَى ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ لَيُخۡرِجَنَّ ٱلۡأَعَزُّ مِنۡهَا ٱلۡأَذَلَّۚ وَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ وَلِرَسُولِهِۦ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "They say: if we return to Madinah, the more honoured will expel the more humble. But honour belongs to Allah, His Messenger and the believers." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Zayd ibn Arqam heard 'Abdullah ibn Ubayy say it, and this was revealed confirming him.

Then the warning to believers: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُلۡهِكُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "O you who believe, let not your wealth and children divert you from the remembrance of Allah", ﴿وَأَنفِقُواْ مِن مَّا رَزَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ أَحَدَكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ فَيَقُولَ رَبِّ لَوۡلَآ أَخَّرۡتَنِيٓ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ قَرِيبٖ فَأَصَّدَّقَ وَأَكُن مِّنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾, "and spend from what We provided you before death comes to one of you and he says: my Lord, if only You would delay me a little so I could give charity and be among the righteous."

**Reflect**: the first thing a dying person wishes for is charity, so do not delay it.

## Surah seven: At-Taghabun (1-18)

At-Taghabun is Madani, with 18 verses; "the Day of mutual loss" is a name of the Day of Resurrection. For the whole surah see [Surah At-Taghabun Meaning in English](/blog/surah-at-taghabun-meaning-english/).

﴿مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِ قَلۡبَهُۥۚ﴾, "No calamity strikes except by Allah's permission, and whoever believes in Allah, He guides his heart." Guidance of the heart in calamity is the fruit of faith. Then: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِكُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدِكُمۡ عَدُوّٗا لَّكُمۡ فَٱحۡذَرُوهُمۡۚ وَإِن تَعۡفُواْ وَتَصۡفَحُواْ وَتَغۡفِرُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمٌ﴾, "O you who believe, among your spouses and children are enemies to you, so beware of them; but if you pardon, overlook and forgive, Allah is Forgiving, Merciful", ﴿إِنَّمَآ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ فِتۡنَةࣱۚ﴾, "Your wealth and children are only a trial", then ﴿فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُمۡ وَٱسۡمَعُواْ وَأَطِيعُواْ وَأَنفِقُواْ خَيۡرٗا لِّأَنفُسِكُمۡۗ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفۡسِهِۦ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾, "So fear Allah as much as you can, listen, obey and spend; it is better for you. Whoever is saved from his own greed, those are the successful."

**Lesson**: beware of love for your family turning you from obedience to Allah, but with pardon and forgiveness, not harshness.

## Surah eight: At-Talaq (1-12)

At-Talaq is Madani, with 12 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah At-Talaq Meaning in English](/blog/surah-at-talaq-meaning-english/).

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِذَا طَلَّقۡتُمُ ٱلنِّسَآءَ فَطَلِّقُوهُنَّ لِعِدَّتِهِنَّ وَأَحۡصُواْ ٱلۡعِدَّةَۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّكُمۡۖ لَا تُخۡرِجُوهُنَّ مِنۢ بُيُوتِهِنَّ﴾, "O Prophet, when you divorce women, divorce them for their waiting period, keep count of it, and fear Allah your Lord. Do not expel them from their houses." Then: ﴿فَإِذَا بَلَغۡنَ أَجَلَهُنَّ فَأَمۡسِكُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفٍ أَوۡ فَارِقُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفٖ﴾, "When they reach their term, keep them honourably or part with them honourably."

Then the comprehensive promise in the middle of the divorce rulings: ﴿وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مَخۡرَجٗا﴾ ﴿وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسۡبُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمۡرِهِۦۚ قَدۡ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدۡرٗا﴾, "Whoever fears Allah, He will make a way out for him and provide for him from where he does not expect. Whoever relies on Allah, He is sufficient for him. Allah accomplishes His purpose; He has set a measure for everything." Then: ﴿لِيُنفِقۡ ذُو سَعَةٖ مِّن سَعَتِهِۦۖ﴾, "Let the man of means spend according to his means", and ﴿سَيَجۡعَلُ ٱللَّهُ بَعۡدَ عُسۡرٖ يُسۡرٗا﴾, "Allah will bring ease after hardship."

**Reflect**: the verse of the way out and provision comes in the context of divorce, because the moments of conflict and separation are when a person most needs taqwa. Read [Quran 65:2-3 Explained: Taqwa, Unexpected Provision and Reliance on Allah](/blog/wa-man-yattaqi-allah-meaning-english/).

## Surah nine: At-Tahrim (1-12)

At-Tahrim is Madani, with 12 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah At-Tahrim Meaning in English](/blog/surah-at-tahrim-meaning-english/).

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ لِمَ تُحَرِّمُ مَآ أَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ لَكَۖ تَبۡتَغِي مَرۡضَاتَ أَزۡوَٰجِكَۚ﴾, "O Prophet, why do you prohibit what Allah has made lawful for you, seeking to please your wives?" In Sahih Al-Bukhari it was revealed about honey, which the Prophet ﷺ had forbidden for himself.

Then the family's responsibility: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ قُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَأَهۡلِيكُمۡ نَارٗا وَقُودُهَا ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلۡحِجَارَةُ﴾, "O you who believe, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones." Read [Quran 66:6 Explained: A Parent's Duty to Guide the Family](/blog/qu-anfusakum-wa-ahlikum-meaning-english/). Then: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوۡبَةٗ نَّصُوحًا﴾, "O you who believe, turn to Allah in sincere repentance."

Then four examples: the wives of Nuh and Lut as an example for the disbelievers, and two for the believers:
- ﴿وَضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱمۡرَأَتَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِذۡ قَالَتۡ رَبِّ ٱبۡنِ لِي عِندَكَ بَيۡتٗا فِي ٱلۡجَنَّةِ﴾, "Allah presents as an example for the believers the wife of Pharaoh, when she said: my Lord, build for me near You a house in Paradise."
- ﴿وَمَرۡيَمَ ٱبۡنَتَ عِمۡرَٰنَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَحۡصَنَتۡ فَرۡجَهَا﴾, "and Maryam daughter of 'Imran, who guarded her chastity", ﴿وَكَانَتۡ مِنَ ٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ﴾, "and she was among the devoutly obedient."

**Lesson**: closeness to a prophet does not help with disbelief, and closeness to a tyrant does not harm with faith. Notice Pharaoh's wife's prayer: she asked for the Neighbour before the house, "near You".

## Tajweed points in Juz 28

### ﴿تَبَوَّءُو﴾ in Al-Hashr 9
The waw after the hamzah is a madd letter, with no alif written after it: give it its natural two counts.

### ﴿ٱلۡأُمِّيِّـۧنَ﴾ in Al-Jumu'ah 2
The small ya is lengthened two counts.

### ﴿فَأَصَّدَّقَ﴾ in Al-Munafiqun 10
The ta has merged into the sad: pronounce *fa-assaddaqa* with doubling.

### ﴿يُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ﴾ in At-Taghabun 11
*Iqlab*: the sakin noon becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah before the ba. For the basics see [Learn Tajweed: A Roadmap](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## How to memorize Juz 28 with understanding

1. **Learn the nine surah names in order**: Al-Mujadilah, Al-Hashr, Al-Mumtahanah, As-Saff, Al-Jumu'ah, Al-Munafiqun, At-Taghabun, At-Talaq, At-Tahrim.
2. **Short, related surahs**: memorize each as a separate unit with its theme.
3. **The Musabbihat in this juz**: Al-Hashr, As-Saff, Al-Jumu'ah and At-Taghabun open with glorification; note *sabbaha* (past) versus *yusabbihu* (present).
4. **Recite Al-Jumu'ah and Al-Munafiqun** on Friday, following the Sunnah.
5. **Memorize the end of Al-Hashr** together with the meanings of the beautiful names.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 28

- **Complain of your worries to Allah**: He hears.
- **Confer in righteousness and taqwa**, not in sin.
- **Make room for your brother**, and Allah makes room for you.
- **Prefer others over yourself**, even in need.
- **Pray for those before you**, and keep your heart free of rancour.
- **Hold yourself to account**: look at what you sent ahead for tomorrow.
- **Be kind and just to the peaceful.**
- **Do not say what you do not do.**
- **Act on what you memorize** of the Quran.
- **Hasten to the Friday prayer** and leave trade.
- **Give charity before death.**
- **Fear Allah as much as you can.**
- **Fear Allah in divorce**, and He will make a way out.
- **Protect yourself and your family** from the Fire.
- **Repent sincerely.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 28 called "Qad Sami'"?
Because it begins with ﴿قَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّتِي تُجَٰدِلُكَ فِي زَوۡجِهَا﴾.

### How many surahs are in Juz Qad Sami'?
Nine, all Madani.

### Is there a prostration in Juz 28?
No, there is no place of prostration in this juz.

### Which two surahs did the Prophet ﷺ recite in the Friday prayer?
Al-Jumu'ah and Al-Munafiqun, as in Sahih Muslim.

### Where does Juz 28 end?
At the end of At-Tahrim. Juz 29 begins with Surah Al-Mulk: ﴿تَبَٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ﴾, "Blessed is He in whose hand is the dominion."

## Conclusion

Juz Qad Sami' draws a community where Allah hears a woman's complaint in her home, where the Ansar prefer their brothers over themselves, where words must match deeds, where Friday is honoured, where hypocrisy is warned against, where Allah is feared even in divorce, and where a man protects himself and his family from the Fire.
