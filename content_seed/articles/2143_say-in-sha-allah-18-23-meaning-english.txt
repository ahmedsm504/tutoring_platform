﴿وَلَا تَقُولَنَّ لِشَاْيۡءٍ إِنِّي فَاعِلࣱ ذَٰلِكَ غَدًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَٱذۡكُر رَّبَّكَ إِذَا نَسِيتَ وَقُلۡ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَهۡدِيَنِ رَبِّي لِأَقۡرَبَ مِنۡ هَٰذَا رَشَدٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:23-24]

## The occasion
- Scholars mention that Quraysh asked the Prophet ﷺ about the people of the cave, Dhul-Qarnayn and the soul. He said *I will tell you tomorrow* without saying *in sha Allah*, and revelation was delayed, then these verses came.

## Etiquette of Allah's will
- Every future plan is tied to Allah's will, because no one owns tomorrow.

## "Remember your Lord when you forget"
- If you forget to say it, say it when you remember. Some said: when you forget something, remember Allah so He reminds you.

## Tajweed note
- *Li-shayʾin* is written with an alif after the shin that is **not pronounced**; it is read *li-shayʾin*, and the Mushaf marks it as silent.

## Lessons
- **Say in sha Allah** sincerely, not as a habit.
- **Do not use it** as an excuse for not keeping promises.
