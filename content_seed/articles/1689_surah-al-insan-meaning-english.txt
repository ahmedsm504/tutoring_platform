**Surah Al-Insan** (31 ayat), also called **Ad-Dahr**, was recited by the Prophet ﷺ with As-Sajdah in the Fajr prayer on Fridays [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].

﴿هَلۡ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ حِينࣱ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهۡرِ لَمۡ يَكُن شَيۡـٔٗا مَّذۡكُورًا﴾ — *Has there come upon man a period of time when he was nothing to be mentioned?*

## The test
- Allah created man, gave him hearing and sight, and showed him the way: **either grateful or ungrateful**.

## The righteous
﴿وَيُطۡعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسۡكِينٗا وَيَتِيمٗا وَأَسِيرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا نُطۡعِمُكُمۡ لِوَجۡهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمۡ جَزَآءٗ وَلَا شُكُورًا﴾
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Fulfil vows | Keep their promises to Allah |
| Fear a Day of widespread evil | Awareness of the Hereafter |
| Feed despite loving the food | Generosity when it is hard |
| For Allah's face | Expecting no reward or thanks |

## Paradise described
- Couches, gardens, no burning sun or bitter cold, silver vessels, a drink mixed with ginger from a spring called *Salsabil*, garments of green silk, *and their Lord will give them a pure drink*.

## Lessons
- **Feed others** with a pure intention.
- **Remember your beginning**: you were once nothing.
