﴿وَلَا تَكۡسِبُ كُلُّ نَفۡسٍ إِلَّا عَلَيۡهَاۚ وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةࣱ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۚ﴾ [Al-Anam 6:164]

## The words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Tazir* | Carries |
| *Waziratun* | A soul carrying |
| *Wizr* | Sin, heavy load |

## Repeated in the Quran
- The principle appears in several surahs, including Al-Anam, Al-Isra, Fatir, Az-Zumar and An-Najm.

## No inherited guilt
- No one is punished for a parent's or ancestor's sin. Every child is born upon the fitrah.

## What about misleaders?
- Al-Ankabut 29:13 says they will carry *their burdens and burdens with their burdens*. This is not a contradiction: they carry the sin of **misleading**, which is their own deed.

## Peace of mind
- You are responsible for your choices, not for others' choices you could not control. Advise, then leave the rest.

## Lessons
- **Focus on your own record.**
- **Never mislead**, for it adds to your burden.
