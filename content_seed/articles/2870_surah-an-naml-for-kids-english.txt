A tiny ant, smaller than a grain of rice, saw a huge army coming. What did she do? She didn't just run away by herself. She called out to her whole colony: "O ants, go into your homes so that Sulayman and his soldiers don't crush you without realising!" Prophet Sulayman heard her, smiled and laughed at what she said, and raised his hands to thank Allah.

Surah An-Naml is the surah of clever animals: the ant who saved her colony, and the hoopoe who discovered a whole kingdom and argued his case in front of the king. It also tells of the Queen of Sheba and her magnificent throne, brought from a faraway land in the blink of an eye, and a palace with a floor made of clear glass. In this guide, we explain Surah An-Naml to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah An-Naml

- **Position**: surah 27, in juz 19 and 20.
- **Number of verses**: 93.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: an-Naml means "The Ants", after the ant who warned her colony. No other surah mentions ants.

## Lesson 1: The Blessing of Knowledge

﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيۡمَٰنَ عِلۡمٗاۖ وَقَالَا ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي فَضَّلَنَا عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٖ مِّنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَوَرِثَ سُلَيۡمَٰنُ دَاوُۥدَۖ وَقَالَ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ عُلِّمۡنَا مَنطِقَ ٱلطَّيۡرِ وَأُوتِينَا مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٍۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡفَضۡلُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾ (Quran 27:15-16)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah gave Dawud and his son Sulayman great knowledge, and they said: Praise be to Allah who favoured us. Allah taught Sulayman the speech of birds, so he understood what sparrows and other birds said! And He gave him a share of everything, so Sulayman said: This is truly a clear favour."

### Lesson for kids
"When Dawud and Sulayman received knowledge, the first thing they said was: Alhamdulillah. When you learn something new or succeed, say Alhamdulillah."

## Lesson 2: The Clever Ant

﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَتَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ وَادِ ٱلنَّمۡلِ قَالَتۡ نَمۡلَةࣱ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّمۡلُ ٱدۡخُلُواْ مَسَٰكِنَكُمۡ لَا يَحۡطِمَنَّكُمۡ سُلَيۡمَٰنُ وَجُنُودُهُۥ وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَتَبَسَّمَ ضَاحِكٗا مِّن قَوۡلِهَا وَقَالَ رَبِّ أَوۡزِعۡنِيٓ أَنۡ أَشۡكُرَ نِعۡمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَيَّ وَأَنۡ أَعۡمَلَ صَٰلِحٗا تَرۡضَىٰهُ وَأَدۡخِلۡنِي بِرَحۡمَتِكَ فِي عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:18-19)

### Explaining it to your child
"Sulayman was marching with his huge army of humans, jinn and birds, and they reached the valley of the ants. An ant said: O ants, go into your homes so Sulayman and his soldiers don't crush you without noticing. Sulayman heard her, smiled, and said: My Lord, inspire me to be grateful for Your favour."

### Notice the ant's character
- **She thought of others**: she didn't only save herself; she warned everyone.
- **She thought well of others**: she said "without noticing", meaning they won't hurt us on purpose, only because they can't see us.
- **She spoke clearly**: she gave a clear instruction and its reason.

### Notice Sulayman's character
"Sulayman was a mighty king, yet he listened to a tiny ant, cared about her words, smiled and wasn't arrogant. Then he thanked Allah for the blessing of understanding animals."

### Activity: ant watching
Watch a line of ants in the garden or kitchen. Notice how they walk in a line and carry food bigger than themselves. Then ask: "What can you learn from the ant?"

## Lesson 3: The Du'a of Gratitude

### Explaining it to your child
"Sulayman said: Rabbi awzi'ni, my Lord, inspire me and help me to be grateful for the favour You gave me and my parents, to do good deeds that please You, and to admit me by Your mercy among Your righteous servants. Look: Sulayman thanked Allah for His blessings on him and on his parents too!"

### Activity: the gratitude card
Write Sulayman's du'a on a beautiful card, and let your child read it on happy days: after a success, on their birthday, or after finishing a surah.

## Lesson 4: The Missing Hoopoe

﴿وَتَفَقَّدَ ٱلطَّيۡرَ فَقَالَ مَالِيَ لَآ أَرَى ٱلۡهُدۡهُدَ أَمۡ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَآئِبِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:20)

### Explaining it to your child
"Sulayman inspected the birds, checking that they were all there, and noticed the hoopoe was missing. He said: Why don't I see the hoopoe? Where has he gone? A good leader cares about every member of the team, even the smallest."

﴿فَمَكَثَ غَيۡرَ بَعِيدٖ فَقَالَ أَحَطتُ بِمَا لَمۡ تُحِطۡ بِهِۦ وَجِئۡتُكَ مِن سَبَإِۭ بِنَبَإٖ يَقِينٍ﴾ ﴿إِنِّي وَجَدتُّ ٱمۡرَأَةٗ تَمۡلِكُهُمۡ وَأُوتِيَتۡ مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَلَهَا عَرۡشٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 27:22-23)

### Explaining it to your child
"The hoopoe came back and said bravely: I have learned something you don't know! I've come from the kingdom of Sheba with certain news. I found a woman ruling them, who has been given everything, and she has a magnificent throne. And I found her and her people bowing to the sun instead of Allah!"

### Lesson for kids: the caring hoopoe
"The hoopoe was a small bird, but he was upset to see people bowing to the sun instead of Allah, and he reported the news truthfully and with evidence. A Muslim loves for all people to worship Allah."

### Activity: the hoopoe reporter
Make your child a "reporter" like the hoopoe: they go into another room, come back, and tell you three things they saw accurately. Then check together if the report was correct.

## Lesson 5: The Throne in the Blink of an Eye

﴿قَالَ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَؤُاْ أَيُّكُمۡ يَأۡتِينِي بِعَرۡشِهَا قَبۡلَ أَن يَأۡتُونِي مُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ عِفۡرِيتࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ أَنَا۠ ءَاتِيكَ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن تَقُومَ مِن مَّقَامِكَۖ وَإِنِّي عَلَيۡهِ لَقَوِيٌّ أَمِينࣱ﴾ ﴿قَالَ ٱلَّذِي عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَنَا۠ ءَاتِيكَ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ أَن يَرۡتَدَّ إِلَيۡكَ طَرۡفُكَۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهُ مُسۡتَقِرًّا عِندَهُۥ قَالَ هَٰذَا مِن فَضۡلِ رَبِّي لِيَبۡلُوَنِيٓ ءَأَشۡكُرُ أَمۡ أَكۡفُرُۖ وَمَن شَكَرَ فَإِنَّمَا يَشۡكُرُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ رَبِّي غَنِيࣱّ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 27:38-40)

### Explaining it to your child
"Sulayman asked those around him: Who can bring me the queen's throne before she arrives? A powerful jinn said: I'll bring it before you get up from your seat. A man with knowledge of the Book said: I'll bring it before you blink! When Sulayman saw the throne right in front of him, he said: This is from the favour of my Lord, to test me whether I will be grateful or ungrateful."

### Lesson for kids
"Sulayman saw an amazing miracle, but he didn't say: I'm so powerful. He said: This is from the favour of my Lord. Every blessing we have is a test: will we thank Allah?"

### Activity: the blink race
Ask your child to blink, then say: "In that short moment, the throne travelled from Yemen to Sulayman's kingdom!" They'll understand the greatness of Allah's power.

## Lesson 6: The Glass Palace

﴿قِيلَ لَهَا ٱدۡخُلِي ٱلصَّرۡحَۖ فَلَمَّا رَأَتۡهُ حَسِبَتۡهُ لُجَّةٗ وَكَشَفَتۡ عَن سَاقَيۡهَاۚ قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ صَرۡحࣱ مُّمَرَّدࣱ مِّن قَوَارِيرَۗ قَالَتۡ رَبِّ إِنِّي ظَلَمۡتُ نَفۡسِي وَأَسۡلَمۡتُ مَعَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:44)

### Explaining it to your child
"The Queen of Sheba came to Sulayman and was told: Enter the palace. When she saw the floor, she thought it was water and lifted her dress so it wouldn't get wet! Sulayman said: It's a palace with a smooth floor of glass with water beneath it. The queen understood that this was no ordinary king, and she submitted with Sulayman to Allah, Lord of the worlds."

## Lesson 7: Who Created All This?

﴿أَمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَأَنزَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا بِهِۦ حَدَآئِقَ ذَاتَ بَهۡجَةٖ مَّا كَانَ لَكُمۡ أَن تُنۢبِتُواْ شَجَرَهَآۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ يَعۡدِلُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمَّن جَعَلَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ قَرَارٗا وَجَعَلَ خِلَٰلَهَآ أَنۡهَٰرٗا وَجَعَلَ لَهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ حَاجِزًاۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ بَلۡ أَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:60-61)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah asks: Who created the heavens and the earth? Who sends down rain to grow beautiful gardens? Could you grow a single tree yourself? Who made the earth stable, with rivers and mountains, and a barrier between the two seas? Is there any god besides Allah? No!"

﴿أَمَّن يُجِيبُ ٱلۡمُضۡطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكۡشِفُ ٱلسُّوٓءَ وَيَجۡعَلُكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:62)

### Explaining it to your child
"Who answers the person in great distress when they call on Him? Only Allah."

## Lesson 8: The Work of Allah Who Perfected Everything

﴿وَتَرَى ٱلۡجِبَالَ تَحۡسَبُهَا جَامِدَةٗ وَهِيَ تَمُرُّ مَرَّ ٱلسَّحَابِۚ صُنۡعَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَتۡقَنَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍۚ إِنَّهُۥ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 27:88)

### Explaining it to your child
"You see the mountains and think they're standing still, but they are moving like clouds. This is the work of Allah who perfected everything. Look at a butterfly's wing, a cat's eye, a leaf on a tree: all perfectly made."

### Activity: doing it well
Ask your child to draw a picture carefully: colouring inside the lines and arranging the details. Then say: "Allah perfected everything, and He loves it when we do our work well."

## Let's Read Together: Who Guides You in the Darkness?

﴿أَمَّن يَهۡدِيكُمۡ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَمَن يُرۡسِلُ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ بُشۡرَۢا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ رَحۡمَتِهِۦٓۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ تَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمَّن يَبۡدَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥ وَمَن يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَءِلَٰهࣱ مَّعَ ٱللَّهِۚ قُلۡ هَاتُواْ بُرۡهَٰنَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 27:63-64)

### Explaining it to your child
"Who guides you in the darkness of land and sea with the stars? Who sends winds bringing good news of rain? Who started creation and will bring it back? Who provides for you from the sky with rain and from the earth with plants? Allah alone."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "Team Ant"

During the holidays, the children of the building were playing in the garden when dark clouds suddenly gathered and raindrops began to fall. Salma, the oldest, remembered the ant from the story her grandmother had told her.

Salma didn't just run inside by herself. She called out clearly: "Everyone, the rain is getting heavier! Each of you grab a toy, and let's run to the building entrance!" So each child carried something: the ball, the little bike, the skipping rope. They all got inside before the rain got heavy, and nobody got soaked.

Grandma looked out of the window, came down and asked: "Who organised all this?" "Salma!" they said. Grandma smiled: "Salma, you're like Sulayman's ant. You didn't just save yourself; you thought of all your friends."

Salma was delighted. "Grandma, teach us Sulayman's du'a when he heard the ant." So Grandma recited: "Rabbi awzi'ni an ashkura ni'matakal-lati an'amta 'alayya wa 'ala walidayya wa an a'mala salihan tardah," and they all repeated it together.

Ask your child: "What did Salma do like the ant? What would you do if you saw danger coming towards your friends?"

## What We Learn From Surah An-Naml

- **Thank Allah** for knowledge and blessings.
- **Think of others**, like the ant.
- **Think well** of people.
- **A leader cares** about every team member.
- **Report news truthfully**, with evidence, like the hoopoe.
- **Every blessing is a test**: will we be grateful?
- **Allah alone** creates and answers.
- **Do everything well**.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Do ants really talk?
Ants have their own ways of communicating, and the Quran tells us an ant spoke and Sulayman understood her by Allah's teaching. This was a miracle given to Sulayman.

### Did the Queen of Sheba become Muslim?
Yes. At the end of the story she said: "I submit with Sulayman to Allah, Lord of the worlds."

### How can I connect a very young child with this surah?
Through acting it out: they play the ant calling their siblings into the room, and a sibling plays Sulayman who smiles and thanks Allah.
