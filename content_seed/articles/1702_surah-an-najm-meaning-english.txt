**Surah An-Najm** (62 ayat) was the first surah with a prostration that the Prophet ﷺ recited publicly in Makkah; when he prostrated at its end, those present prostrated too [Al-Bukhari].

﴿وَٱلنَّجۡمِ إِذَا هَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿مَا ضَلَّ صَاحِبُكُمۡ وَمَا غَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰٓ﴾ ﴿إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحۡيࣱ يُوحَىٰ﴾ ﴿عَلَّمَهُۥ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡقُوَىٰ﴾

## The vision
- The Prophet ﷺ saw Jibril in his true form, and again **at the Lote Tree of the furthest boundary**, near the Garden of Refuge.
- *The sight did not swerve, nor did it transgress.*

## Idols without power
- Al-Lat, al-ʿUzza and Manat: *nothing but names you have named*.

## Personal responsibility
﴿وَأَن لَّيۡسَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّ سَعۡيَهُۥ سَوۡفَ يُرَىٰ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ يُجۡزَىٰهُ ٱلۡجَزَآءَ ٱلۡأَوۡفَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ﴾

## The final command
﴿فَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ۩﴾ — a place of **prostration of recitation**.

## Lessons
- **The Sunnah is revelation** in its meaning.
- **Your effort is seen**, and rewarded in full.
