**Surah Al-Masad** (5 ayat) is the only surah that names an enemy of the Prophet ﷺ: his own uncle, **Abu Lahab**.

## Why it was revealed
- The Prophet ﷺ climbed Mount Safa and called his people to warn them. Abu Lahab said: "May you perish! Is this why you gathered us?" Then this surah was revealed [Al-Bukhari].

﴿تَبَّتۡ يَدَآ أَبِي لَهَبٖ وَتَبَّ﴾ ﴿مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُ مَالُهُۥ وَمَا كَسَبَ﴾ ﴿سَيَصۡلَىٰ نَارٗا ذَاتَ لَهَبٖ﴾ ﴿وَٱمۡرَأَتُهُۥ حَمَّالَةَ ٱلۡحَطَبِ﴾ ﴿فِي جِيدِهَا حَبۡلࣱ مِّن مَّسَدِۭ﴾

## Meaning
| Ayah | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | His efforts are ruined, and he is ruined |
| 2 | His wealth and gains did not help him |
| 3 | He will burn in a blazing fire |
| 4 | His wife, the carrier of firewood |
| 5 | Around her neck a rope of palm fibre |
- **"Carrier of firewood"**: commentators said she spread thorns in the Prophet's path ﷺ, or spread gossip that kindles enmity.

## A prophecy fulfilled
- Abu Lahab lived for years after this surah, and he **never accepted Islam**, even outwardly, confirming the Quran's words.

## Lessons
- **Family ties do not save** without faith.
- **Wealth does not protect** from Allah's decree.
