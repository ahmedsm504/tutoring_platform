﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ ذِكۡرٗا كَثِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَسَبِّحُوهُ بُكۡرَةٗ وَأَصِيلًا﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُصَلِّي عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتُهُۥ لِيُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِۚ وَكَانَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَحِيمٗا﴾ [Al-Ahzab 33:41-43]

## "Much" remembrance
- Most acts of worship have a set amount. Dhikr is asked for **abundantly**, without limit.

## Morning and evening
- *Bukrah* and *asil*: the start and end of the day, the times of the morning and evening adhkar.

## Allah's response
| From | What |
|---|---|
| Allah | Sends blessings (praise and mercy) on you |
| The angels | Pray for you |
| Purpose | To bring you out of darkness into light |

## Easy dhikr
- *SubhanAllah, alhamdulillah, la ilaha illa Allah, Allahu akbar*, while walking, driving or waiting.

## Lessons
- **Fill idle moments** with dhikr.
- **Keep the morning and evening adhkar.**
