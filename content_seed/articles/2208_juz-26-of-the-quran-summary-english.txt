Juz 26 begins with **Surah Al-Ahqaf**, the last of the Hawamim, so it is known as **Juz Al-Ahqaf** or **Juz Ha Mim**. It contains **Al-Ahqaf**, **Muhammad**, **Al-Fath**, **Al-Hujurat** and **Qaf** in full, then the start of **Adh-Dhariyat** up to verse 30. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 502 to 521**.

This juz **moves between Makkan and Madinan themes**: it begins with a Makki surah closing the Hawamim, then three Madani surahs on fighting, victory and building society, then two Makki surahs that return the heart to death, resurrection and provision.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **building the believing community in belief, victory and character**. Al-Ahqaf affirms belief and kindness to parents; Surah Muhammad declares the law of victory: ﴿إِن تَنصُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَنصُرۡكُمۡ وَيُثَبِّتۡ أَقۡدَامَكُمۡ﴾, "If you support Allah, He will support you and plant your feet firmly"; Al-Fath fulfils the promise: ﴿إِنَّا فَتَحۡنَا لَكَ فَتۡحٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾, "We have given you a clear victory"; Al-Hujurat draws the ethics of society; and Qaf reminds of constant watch: ﴿مَّا يَلۡفِظُ مِن قَوۡلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيۡهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدࣱ﴾, "Not a word does he utter but there is a watcher ready beside him."

The message: **support Allah in yourself, your family and your community and He will support you; and watch every word you say.**

## Part one: Surah Al-Ahqaf (1-35)

Al-Ahqaf is Makki, with 35 verses, the last of the Hawamim. *Al-Ahqaf* are long sand dunes where the people of 'Ad lived. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Ahqaf Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-ahqaf-meaning-english/).

### 1. Monotheism and revelation (1-14)
We did not create the heavens, earth and all between them except in truth and for an appointed term. Then: say, have you considered what you call on besides Allah? Show me what they have created of the earth. Then the good news: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ فَلَا خَوۡفٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يَحۡزَنُونَ﴾, "Those who said 'Our Lord is Allah' and then stood firm: no fear will be upon them, nor will they grieve."

### 2. Honouring parents and reaching forty (15-20)
﴿وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ إِحۡسَٰنًاۖ حَمَلَتۡهُ أُمُّهُۥ كُرۡهٗا وَوَضَعَتۡهُ كُرۡهٗاۖ وَحَمۡلُهُۥ وَفِصَٰلُهُۥ ثَلَٰثُونَ شَهۡرًاۚ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا بَلَغَ أَشُدَّهُۥ وَبَلَغَ أَرۡبَعِينَ سَنَةٗ﴾, "We have enjoined on man kindness to his parents. His mother carried him with hardship and delivered him with hardship, and his carrying and weaning take thirty months, until when he reaches maturity and reaches forty years", then his prayer: my Lord, enable me to thank You for the favour You gave me and my parents, to do good that pleases You, and make my offspring righteous for me; I have repented to You and I am among the Muslims.

**Reflect**: forty is the age of maturity, when the believer combines thanks for blessings on himself and his parents with prayer for his children; he stands in the middle of the chain of generations. From this verse together with Luqman's "his weaning is in two years", scholars deduced that the minimum term of pregnancy is six months.

Then the contrast: the disobedient son who said to his parents, "Uff to you both." Read [The Dua of Nuh for Parents: 71:28](/blog/dua-of-nuh-for-parents-71-28-meaning-english/).

### 3. Hud at Al-Ahqaf (21-28)
A new quarter: ﴿۞وَٱذۡكُرۡ أَخَا عَادٍ إِذۡ أَنذَرَ قَوۡمَهُۥ بِٱلۡأَحۡقَافِ﴾, "Remember the brother of 'Ad, when he warned his people at Al-Ahqaf." When they saw the punishment as a cloud approaching their valleys, they said: this cloud will bring us rain. No, it is what you sought to hasten: a wind with a painful punishment, destroying everything by its Lord's command.

**Lesson**: what a person thinks is mercy may be punishment; none feels safe from Allah's plan except the losers.

### 4. The jinn listening to the Quran (29-35)
﴿وَإِذۡ صَرَفۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ نَفَرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ فَلَمَّا حَضَرُوهُ قَالُوٓاْ أَنصِتُواْۖ فَلَمَّا قُضِيَ وَلَّوۡاْ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِم مُّنذِرِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ يَٰقَوۡمَنَآ إِنَّا سَمِعۡنَا كِتَٰبًا أُنزِلَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مُوسَىٰ مُصَدِّقٗا لِّمَا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِلَىٰ طَرِيقٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ﴾, "When We directed to you a group of jinn listening to the Quran, and when they attended it they said 'Be silent'; and when it was finished they went back to their people as warners. They said: our people, we have heard a Book revealed after Musa, confirming what came before it, guiding to the truth and to a straight path."

**The etiquette of listening**: "Be silent". Their first act was silence; then they became callers to their people as soon as the recitation ended. Read [Surah Al-Jinn Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-jinn-meaning-english/).

The close: ﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ كَمَا صَبَرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡعَزۡمِ مِنَ ٱلرُّسُلِ وَلَا تَسۡتَعۡجِل لَّهُمۡۚ﴾, "So be patient as the messengers of firm resolve were patient, and do not seek to hasten it for them."

## Part two: Surah Muhammad (1-38)

Muhammad is Madani, with 38 verses, also called Surah Al-Qital (Fighting). For the whole surah see [Surah Muhammad Meaning in English](/blog/surah-muhammad-meaning-english/).

### 1. The two groups and the rules of fighting (1-9)
Those who disbelieve, He has nullified their deeds; then: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَءَامَنُواْ بِمَا نُزِّلَ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡ كَفَّرَ عَنۡهُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ وَأَصۡلَحَ بَالَهُمۡ﴾, "Those who believe, do good and believe in what was sent down to Muhammad, which is the truth from their Lord, He will remove their misdeeds and set right their state." Then the rules of fighting and captives: either grace or ransom. Then the law: "O you who believe, if you support Allah, He will support you and plant your feet firmly."

**Reflect on "set right their state"**: *bal* is one's inner condition and peace of mind; whoever believes and does good has his inner state set right, which is greater than having his wealth set right.

### 2. The rivers of Paradise and knowledge before action (10-19)
A new quarter reminds of the fates of nations, then describes the four rivers of Paradise: water that never stales, milk whose taste never changes, wine delightful to drinkers, and purified honey. Then the comprehensive command: ﴿فَٱعۡلَمۡ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۗ﴾, "So know that there is no god but Allah, and seek forgiveness for your sin and for the believing men and women." Al-Bukhari titled a chapter in his Sahih "Knowledge comes before speech and action", citing this verse.

### 3. The hypocrites and locked hearts (20-32)
When a decisive surah is revealed mentioning fighting, you see the hypocrites looking at you like someone fainting at death. Then: ﴿فَهَلۡ عَسَيۡتُمۡ إِن تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ أَن تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَتُقَطِّعُوٓاْ أَرۡحَامَكُمۡ﴾, "Would you, if you turned away, spread corruption on earth and sever your ties of kinship?" Then the great question: ﴿أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ أَمۡ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبٍ أَقۡفَالُهَآ﴾, "Do they not reflect on the Quran, or are there locks upon their hearts?"

**Lesson**: a heart that does not reflect on the Quran has a lock on it, and reflection is the key. Read [Do They Not Reflect on the Quran? 47:24](/blog/afala-yatadabbaruna-al-quran-meaning-english/).

### 4. Spending and replacement (33-38)
A new quarter: O you who believe, obey Allah and obey the Messenger and do not invalidate your deeds. The close: ﴿هَـٰٓأَنتُمۡ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ تُدۡعَوۡنَ لِتُنفِقُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَمِنكُم مَّن يَبۡخَلُۖ وَمَن يَبۡخَلۡ فَإِنَّمَا يَبۡخَلُ عَن نَّفۡسِهِۦۚ وَٱللَّهُ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُۚ﴾, "Here you are, called to spend in Allah's cause; some of you withhold, and whoever withholds only withholds from himself. Allah is the Free of need and you are the needy." If you turn away, He will replace you with another people.

**A warning**: the religion will be supported, through you or through others; strive to be among its supporters, not among those replaced.

## Part three: Surah Al-Fath (1-29)

Al-Fath is Madani, with 29 verses, revealed after the Treaty of Hudaybiyah in 6 AH. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Fath Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-fath-meaning-english/).

### 1. The clear victory (1-7)
﴿إِنَّا فَتَحۡنَا لَكَ فَتۡحٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾ ﴿لِّيَغۡفِرَ لَكَ ٱللَّهُ مَا تَقَدَّمَ مِن ذَنۢبِكَ وَمَا تَأَخَّرَ وَيُتِمَّ نِعۡمَتَهُۥ عَلَيۡكَ وَيَهۡدِيَكَ صِرَٰطٗا مُّسۡتَقِيمٗا﴾, "We have given you a clear victory, so Allah may forgive you what preceded of your sin and what is to come, complete His favour upon you and guide you to a straight path." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, 'Umar asked: Messenger of Allah, is it a victory? He said: "Yes." Allah called the treaty a victory, and it opened the door for people to enter Islam.

Then tranquillity: ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ ٱلسَّكِينَةَ فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ لِيَزۡدَادُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنٗا مَّعَ إِيمَٰنِهِمۡۗ﴾, "It is He who sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers so they would increase in faith along with their faith."

**Lesson**: what looks like a concession may in reality be a victory; the measure is what Allah knows, not what we see.

### 2. The pledge and those who stayed behind (8-17)
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُبَايِعُونَكَ إِنَّمَا يُبَايِعُونَ ٱللَّهَ يَدُ ٱللَّهِ فَوۡقَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡۚ فَمَن نَّكَثَ فَإِنَّمَا يَنكُثُ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَنۡ أَوۡفَىٰ بِمَا عَٰهَدَ عَلَيۡهُ ٱللَّهَ فَسَيُؤۡتِيهِ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا﴾, "Those who pledge allegiance to you are pledging allegiance to Allah; the hand of Allah is over their hands. Whoever breaks it only breaks it against himself, and whoever fulfils his covenant with Allah, He will give him a great reward." Then the excuses of the Bedouins who stayed behind: our wealth and families kept us busy.

### 3. The Pledge of Ridwan (18-26)
Hizb 52 begins: ﴿۞لَّقَدۡ رَضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ إِذۡ يُبَايِعُونَكَ تَحۡتَ ٱلشَّجَرَةِ فَعَلِمَ مَا فِي قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَأَنزَلَ ٱلسَّكِينَةَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَأَثَٰبَهُمۡ فَتۡحٗا قَرِيبٗا﴾, "Allah was pleased with the believers when they pledged allegiance to you under the tree. He knew what was in their hearts, sent down tranquillity upon them and rewarded them with a near victory." They numbered around fourteen hundred, as in Al-Bukhari and Muslim.

Then the stance of zealotry: ﴿إِذۡ جَعَلَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ ٱلۡحَمِيَّةَ حَمِيَّةَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِ فَأَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ سَكِينَتَهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِۦ وَعَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَأَلۡزَمَهُمۡ كَلِمَةَ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ﴾, "When the disbelievers put in their hearts zealotry, the zealotry of Ignorance, Allah sent down His tranquillity on His Messenger and the believers and bound them to the word of taqwa."

**The contrast**: the zealotry of Ignorance in the disbelievers' hearts, and Allah's tranquillity in the believers' hearts. Tranquillity is mentioned three times in this surah (4, 18, 26).

### 4. The true vision and the description of the Companions (27-29)
﴿لَّقَدۡ صَدَقَ ٱللَّهُ رَسُولَهُ ٱلرُّءۡيَا بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ لَتَدۡخُلُنَّ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدَ ٱلۡحَرَامَ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ ءَامِنِينَ مُحَلِّقِينَ رُءُوسَكُمۡ وَمُقَصِّرِينَ لَا تَخَافُونَۖ﴾, "Allah has fulfilled His Messenger's vision in truth: you will enter the Sacred Mosque, if Allah wills, in safety, heads shaved or hair shortened, without fear." It was fulfilled in the 'Umrah of fulfilment the following year.

Then the comprehensive verse: ﴿مُّحَمَّدࣱ رَّسُولُ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥٓ أَشِدَّآءُ عَلَى ٱلۡكُفَّارِ رُحَمَآءُ بَيۡنَهُمۡۖ تَرَىٰهُمۡ رُكَّعٗا سُجَّدٗا يَبۡتَغُونَ فَضۡلٗا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِضۡوَٰنٗاۖ﴾, "Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, and those with him are firm against the disbelievers, merciful among themselves. You see them bowing and prostrating, seeking bounty from Allah and His pleasure", then their example in the Torah and Gospel: like a seed that sends out its shoot, strengthens it, thickens and stands on its stem.

**The Companions' traits**: strength against the enemy, mercy among themselves, constant worship, sincerity in seeking, and a visible mark on their faces.

## Part four: Surah Al-Hujurat (1-18)

Al-Hujurat is Madani, with 18 verses, sometimes called the surah of manners. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Hujurat Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-hujurat-meaning-english/).

### 1. Etiquette with Allah and His Messenger (1-5)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُقَدِّمُواْ بَيۡنَ يَدَيِ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ﴾, "O you who believe, do not put yourselves before Allah and His Messenger, and fear Allah." Then: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَرۡفَعُوٓاْ أَصۡوَٰتَكُمۡ فَوۡقَ صَوۡتِ ٱلنَّبِيِّ وَلَا تَجۡهَرُواْ لَهُۥ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ كَجَهۡرِ بَعۡضِكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ أَن تَحۡبَطَ أَعۡمَٰلُكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَشۡعُرُونَ﴾, "O you who believe, do not raise your voices above the Prophet's voice, nor speak loudly to him as you do to one another, lest your deeds be nullified while you do not perceive." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Abu Bakr and 'Umar raised their voices near the Prophet ﷺ and this was revealed; afterwards 'Umar would speak to the Prophet so softly he could barely be heard.

### 2. Verification and reconciliation (6-10)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن جَآءَكُمۡ فَاسِقُۢ بِنَبَإٖ فَتَبَيَّنُوٓاْ أَن تُصِيبُواْ قَوۡمَۢا بِجَهَٰلَةٖ فَتُصۡبِحُواْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلۡتُمۡ نَٰدِمِينَ﴾, "O you who believe, if a defiantly disobedient person brings you news, verify it, lest you harm a people in ignorance and become regretful over what you did." Then: ﴿وَإِن طَآئِفَتَانِ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱقۡتَتَلُواْ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَهُمَاۖ﴾, "If two groups of believers fight, make peace between them", then the rule: ﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ إِخۡوَةࣱ فَأَصۡلِحُواْ بَيۡنَ أَخَوَيۡكُمۡۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ﴾, "The believers are but brothers, so make peace between your brothers and fear Allah, that you may receive mercy."

**A lesson for an age of rumours**: verify before you share; a false report can destroy homes.

### 3. Six prohibitions (11-12)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا يَسۡخَرۡ قَوۡمࣱ مِّن قَوۡمٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونُواْ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ وَلَا نِسَآءࣱ مِّن نِّسَآءٍ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَكُنَّ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُنَّۖ﴾, "O you who believe, let not a people ridicule another people; perhaps they are better than them; nor let women ridicule other women; perhaps they are better than them", and do not insult one another or call each other by offensive nicknames. Then: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱجۡتَنِبُواْ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلظَّنِّ إِنَّ بَعۡضَ ٱلظَّنِّ إِثۡمࣱۖ وَلَا تَجَسَّسُواْ وَلَا يَغۡتَب بَّعۡضُكُم بَعۡضًاۚ أَيُحِبُّ أَحَدُكُمۡ أَن يَأۡكُلَ لَحۡمَ أَخِيهِ مَيۡتٗا﴾, "O you who believe, avoid much assumption; some assumption is sin. Do not spy, and do not backbite one another. Would one of you like to eat the flesh of his dead brother?"

**The six**: ridicule, insult, offensive nicknames, bad assumptions, spying and backbiting. All begin in the heart or on the tongue, and all destroy brotherhood.

### 4. The measure of honour and the reality of faith (13-18)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن ذَكَرٖ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ شُعُوبٗا وَقَبَآئِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ أَكۡرَمَكُمۡ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَتۡقَىٰكُمۡۚ﴾, "O mankind, We created you from a male and a female and made you peoples and tribes so you may know one another. The most noble of you with Allah is the most God-conscious." Lineage is for recognition, not boasting.

Then a new quarter: ﴿۞قَالَتِ ٱلۡأَعۡرَابُ ءَامَنَّاۖ قُل لَّمۡ تُؤۡمِنُواْ وَلَٰكِن قُولُوٓاْ أَسۡلَمۡنَا وَلَمَّا يَدۡخُلِ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنُ فِي قُلُوبِكُمۡۖ﴾, "The Bedouins say: we have believed. Say: you have not believed; rather say 'we have submitted', for faith has not yet entered your hearts." Then the definition of the truthful believers: ﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَرۡتَابُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلصَّـٰدِقُونَ﴾, "The believers are only those who believe in Allah and His Messenger, then do not doubt, and strive with their wealth and lives in Allah's cause. They are the truthful."

## Part five: Surah Qaf (1-45)

Qaf is Makki, with 45 verses. In Sahih Muslim, Umm Hisham bint Harithah said: I learned ﴿قٓۚ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡمَجِيدِ﴾ only from the tongue of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, who recited it every Friday on the pulpit when addressing the people. For the whole surah see [Surah Qaf Meaning in English](/blog/surah-qaf-meaning-english/).

### 1. The deniers' astonishment (1-15)
They marvelled that a warner from among them came to them and said: when we have died and become dust? That is a far-fetched return. The reply: We know what the earth takes away from them, and with Us is a preserving record. Then signs in sky, earth, rain and tall palm trees. Then: were We wearied by the first creation?

### 2. Closeness and watchfulness (16-18)
﴿وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ وَنَعۡلَمُ مَا تُوَسۡوِسُ بِهِۦ نَفۡسُهُۥۖ وَنَحۡنُ أَقۡرَبُ إِلَيۡهِ مِنۡ حَبۡلِ ٱلۡوَرِيدِ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ يَتَلَقَّى ٱلۡمُتَلَقِّيَانِ عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ قَعِيدࣱ﴾ ﴿مَّا يَلۡفِظُ مِن قَوۡلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيۡهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدࣱ﴾, "We created man and know what his soul whispers to him, and We are closer to him than his jugular vein, as the two receivers receive, seated on the right and on the left. Not a word does he utter but there is a watcher ready beside him."

**Lesson**: every word is recorded, so let your tongue speak only what you would love to see in your record.

### 3. From death to reckoning (19-35)
﴿وَجَآءَتۡ سَكۡرَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ ذَٰلِكَ مَا كُنتَ مِنۡهُ تَحِيدُ﴾, "The stupor of death will come in truth: that is what you were trying to avoid." Then the Horn, and every soul comes with a driver and a witness, then: ﴿لَّقَدۡ كُنتَ فِي غَفۡلَةٖ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا فَكَشَفۡنَا عَنكَ غِطَآءَكَ فَبَصَرُكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ حَدِيدࣱ﴾, "You were heedless of this, so We removed your cover, and your sight today is sharp." Then the scene of Hell: ﴿يَوۡمَ نَقُولُ لِجَهَنَّمَ هَلِ ٱمۡتَلَأۡتِ وَتَقُولُ هَلۡ مِن مَّزِيدٖ﴾, "The Day We will say to Hell: are you full? And it will say: are there any more?" A new quarter begins at verse 27. Then Paradise is brought near the God-conscious: this is what you were promised, for everyone who returned to Allah and guarded His limits.

**Reflect on the sequence**: the stupor of death, the Horn, coming with driver and witness, the removal of the cover, then the reckoning: a whole journey in a few verses.

### 4. The reminder and the close (36-45)
﴿إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَذِكۡرَىٰ لِمَن كَانَ لَهُۥ قَلۡبٌ أَوۡ أَلۡقَى ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَهُوَ شَهِيدࣱ﴾, "In that is a reminder for whoever has a heart, or listens attentively while present." Then: ﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ قَبۡلَ طُلُوعِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ وَقَبۡلَ ٱلۡغُرُوبِ﴾, "Be patient with what they say and glorify your Lord with praise before sunrise and before sunset." The close: ﴿نَّحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَقُولُونَۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِجَبَّارٖۖ فَذَكِّرۡ بِٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَن يَخَافُ وَعِيدِ﴾, "We know best what they say, and you are not a tyrant over them. So remind with the Quran whoever fears My warning."

## Part six: the start of Surah Adh-Dhariyat (1-30)

Adh-Dhariyat is Makki, with 60 verses, 30 of which fall in this juz. For the whole surah see [Surah Adh-Dhariyat Meaning in English](/blog/surah-adh-dhariyat-meaning-english/).

### 1. The oath by the scattering winds (1-14)
﴿وَٱلذَّـٰرِيَٰتِ ذَرۡوٗا﴾, "By the winds that scatter", and those that carry a load, those that sail with ease, and those that apportion by command: what you are promised is true, and the Judgement will surely come.

### 2. The traits of the good-doers (15-23)
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ﴾ ﴿ءَاخِذِينَ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَبۡلَ ذَٰلِكَ مُحۡسِنِينَ﴾, "The God-conscious will be among gardens and springs, accepting what their Lord gives them; before that they were doers of good." Their traits: ﴿كَانُواْ قَلِيلٗا مِّنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ مَا يَهۡجَعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَبِٱلۡأَسۡحَارِ هُمۡ يَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَفِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ حَقࣱّ لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَٱلۡمَحۡرُومِ﴾, "They used to sleep little of the night, in the hours before dawn they would seek forgiveness, and in their wealth was a right for the one who asks and the deprived."

Then the signs: ﴿وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ءَايَٰتࣱ لِّلۡمُوقِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَفِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ رِزۡقُكُمۡ وَمَا تُوعَدُونَ﴾, "On earth are signs for the certain, and within yourselves: do you not see? And in the heaven is your provision and what you are promised." Then the oath: by the Lord of the heaven and earth, it is true, just as you speak.

**Reflect**: they prayed through the night and then sought forgiveness before dawn, as if they had fallen short. The true worshipper does not admire his own deeds.

### 3. Ibrahim's guests (24-30)
Has the story of Ibrahim's honoured guests reached you? He brought a fat calf; they gave him good news of a knowledgeable boy; his wife came forward with a cry, struck her face and said: a barren old woman! ﴿قَالُواْ كَذَٰلِكِ قَالَ رَبُّكِۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾, "They said: thus has your Lord said; He is the Wise, the All-Knowing." Juz 27 begins with ﴿۞قَالَ فَمَا خَطۡبُكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ﴾, "He said: then what is your business, O messengers?"

**The etiquette of hospitality**: Ibrahim hurried, brought a fat calf and set it before them. Honouring guests means speed, quality and serving them directly.

## Tajweed points in Juz 26

### ﴿عَلَيۡهُ ٱللَّهَ﴾ in Al-Fath 10
Read in Hafs with a dammah on the ha, *'alayhu-llah*, one of the few places where this ha is read with dammah.

### ﴿قٓۚ﴾ at the start of Qaf
Read *Qaf* with a necessary six-count madd; the fa is sakin.

### ﴿هَـٰٓأَنتُمۡ﴾ in Muhammad 38
The attention particle *ha* is followed by a hamzah, giving a separated madd.

### ﴿بِئۡسَ ٱلِٱسۡمُ﴾ in Al-Hujurat 11
When continuing: *bi'sa-lismu*, with kasrah on the lam. When starting from it, two ways are allowed: *alismu* or *lismu*. For the basics see [Learn Tajweed: A Roadmap](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## How to memorize Juz 26 with understanding

1. **Divide the juz by surah type**: Makki (Al-Ahqaf), Madani (Muhammad, Al-Fath, Al-Hujurat), Makki (Qaf, Adh-Dhariyat).
2. **Al-Hujurat has five "O you who believe" calls**: do not put yourselves before; do not raise your voices; if a sinner brings news; let not a people ridicule; avoid much assumption; then "O mankind".
3. **Al-Fath**: link it to the events of Hudaybiyah in order.
4. **Qaf**: man's journey from creation to Paradise and Hell.
5. **Listen to Qaf with your heart** whenever it is recited in a Friday sermon.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 26

- **Honour your parents** and pray for your children.
- **Be silent for the Quran**, then convey it.
- **Support Allah's cause in yourself**, and He will support you.
- **Learn before you act.**
- **Unlock your heart through reflection.**
- **Do not withhold**: you only withhold from yourself.
- **See events with the eye of faith**: a treaty may be a victory.
- **Be merciful to your brothers** and firm against falsehood.
- **Show respect for the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.**
- **Verify before you share.**
- **Avoid backbiting, ridicule and suspicion.**
- **Know that honour lies in taqwa**, not lineage.
- **Guard your tongue**: every word is written.
- **Seek forgiveness before dawn.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 26 called Juz Al-Ahqaf?
Because it begins with Surah Al-Ahqaf, the last of the Hawamim.

### What is the "clear victory" in Surah Al-Fath?
The Treaty of Hudaybiyah in 6 AH. Allah called it a victory, and when 'Umar asked whether it was a victory, the Prophet ﷺ said "Yes", as in Sahih Al-Bukhari.

### What social prohibitions are in Surah Al-Hujurat?
Ridicule, insult, offensive nicknames, bad assumptions, spying and backbiting.

### Is there a prostration in Juz 26?
No, there is no place of prostration in this juz.

### Where does Juz 26 end?
At Adh-Dhariyat 30. Juz 27 begins with ﴿۞قَالَ فَمَا خَطۡبُكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ﴾.

## Conclusion

Juz 26 builds the believer from within and without: kind to his parents, silent for the Quran, a supporter of Allah's religion, reflective with no lock on his heart, merciful to his brothers, careful to verify news, far from backbiting and ridicule, guarding his tongue because the ready watcher never neglects, and standing at night seeking forgiveness before dawn.
