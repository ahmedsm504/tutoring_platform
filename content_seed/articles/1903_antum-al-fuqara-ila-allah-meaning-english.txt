﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ أَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ﴾ ﴿إِن يَشَأۡ يُذۡهِبۡكُمۡ وَيَأۡتِ بِخَلۡقٖ جَدِيدٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَا ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ بِعَزِيزٖ﴾ [Fatir 35:15-17]

## Total need
- *You are the needy*: every breath, heartbeat, meal and moment of guidance depends on Allah.

## Two names
| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Al-Ghani* | Free of all need |
| *Al-Hamid* | Praiseworthy in all He does |

- Richness alone could be harsh; joined with *al-Hamid*, His richness is generous and praiseworthy.

## Replaceable
- *If He wills, He can remove you and bring a new creation.* Allah does not need us; we need Him.

## Why this frees the heart
- Recognising need for Allah frees you from depending on people. You ask Him first.

## Lessons
- **Start every task** by asking Allah's help.
- **Humility** is the natural result of knowing your need.
