﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحۡيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُۥۖ وَبِذَٰلِكَ أُمِرۡتُ وَأَنَا۠ أَوَّلُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ [Al-Anam 6:162-163]

## Four things dedicated
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Salati* | My prayer |
| *Nusuki* | My sacrifice and acts of devotion |
| *Mahyaya* | Everything I do while alive |
| *Mamati* | The state I die upon |

## Life as worship
- The verse moves from rituals to **all of life**. Work, family and rest become worship when done for Allah and within His limits.

## No partner
- *He has no partner*: not in worship, not in intention. This is sincerity (*ikhlas*).

## In the prayer
- The Prophet ﷺ used these words in one of his opening supplications in prayer (narrated by Muslim from ʿAli).

## "The first of the Muslims"
- The Prophet ﷺ is the first of his nation to submit, the leader in practising what he preached.

## Lessons
- **Renew your intention** in daily actions.
- **Ask for a good ending**: death too is for Allah.
