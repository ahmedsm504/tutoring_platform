Surah Al-Kahf is the surah many Muslims recite every Friday. It is built around four stories, and each one is a trial that every human being faces in some form: the trial of faith in the story of the young men of the cave, the trial of wealth in the story of the owner of the two gardens, the trial of knowledge in the journey of Musa with al-Khidr, and the trial of power in the story of Dhul-Qarnayn. Between the stories stand the keys to passing each trial: good companions, remembering the hereafter, humility and sincerity. The Prophet ﷺ taught that its first verses protect from the greatest trial of all, the Dajjal.

This guide explains Surah Al-Kahf story by story in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Kahf
- **Number of verses**: 110.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 18, beginning in the fifteenth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Kahf means "the cave".
- **Main themes**: the four trials, the reality of this world, sincerity, and preparation for the Dajjal and the hereafter.

## The Virtues of Al-Kahf
1. **Protection from the Dajjal**: in Sahih Muslim, Abu ad-Darda' reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever memorises ten verses from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjal." In another narration in Muslim, "from the end of Al-Kahf".
2. **Tranquillity descends**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, al-Bara' reported that a man was reciting Al-Kahf with his horse tied beside him, and a cloud covered him and came closer. The Prophet ﷺ said: "That was the tranquillity (*sakinah*) which descended because of the Quran."
3. **Light on Friday**: Abu Sa'id al-Khudri reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites Surah Al-Kahf on Friday, a light will shine for him between the two Fridays." Collected by al-Hakim and al-Bayhaqi; al-Albani graded it authentic.

## Verses 1–8: Praise and Warning
﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَلَمۡ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ عِوَجَاۜ﴾ ﴿قَيِّمٗا لِّيُنذِرَ بَأۡسٗا شَدِيدٗا مِّن لَّدُنۡهُ وَيُبَشِّرَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ أَنَّ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرًا حَسَنٗا﴾ (Quran 18:1-2)

**"All praise is due to Allah, who has sent down upon His servant the Book and has not made therein any deviance. Straight."** The surah opens with praise for the greatest blessing: a Book without crookedness.

﴿إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَا مَا عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ زِينَةٗ لَّهَا لِنَبۡلُوَهُمۡ أَيُّهُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلٗا﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّا لَجَٰعِلُونَ مَا عَلَيۡهَا صَعِيدٗا جُرُزًا﴾ (Quran 18:7-8)

**"Indeed, We have made that which is on the earth adornment for it, that We may test them as to which of them is best in deed."** This verse is the key to the whole surah: everything attractive on earth is a test, and it will all become **"barren ground"**.

## Story 1: The Young Men of the Cave (Verses 9–26)
﴿إِذۡ أَوَى ٱلۡفِتۡيَةُ إِلَى ٱلۡكَهۡفِ فَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةٗ وَهَيِّئۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَمۡرِنَا رَشَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:10)

A group of young believers lived among a people who worshipped others besides Allah. They fled with their faith to a cave and prayed: **"Our Lord, grant us from Yourself mercy and prepare for us from our affair right guidance."**

﴿نَّحۡنُ نَقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ نَبَأَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ إِنَّهُمۡ فِتۡيَةٌ ءَامَنُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡ وَزِدۡنَٰهُمۡ هُدٗى﴾ ﴿وَرَبَطۡنَا عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ إِذۡ قَامُواْ فَقَالُواْ رَبُّنَا رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ لَن نَّدۡعُوَاْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ إِلَٰهٗاۖ لَّقَدۡ قُلۡنَآ إِذٗا شَطَطًا﴾ (Quran 18:13-14)

**"Indeed, they were youths who believed in their Lord, and We increased them in guidance. And We made firm their hearts when they stood up and said: Our Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth."**

Allah made them sleep for many years. The sun avoided them as it rose and set; they were turned to the right and left; and their dog stretched its paws at the entrance. Whoever looked at them would have fled in terror.

﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ بَعَثۡنَٰهُمۡ لِيَتَسَآءَلُواْ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ قَالَ قَآئِلࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ كَمۡ لَبِثۡتُمۡۖ قَالُواْ لَبِثۡنَا يَوۡمًا أَوۡ بَعۡضَ يَوۡمٖۚ قَالُواْ رَبُّكُمۡ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا لَبِثۡتُمۡ فَٱبۡعَثُوٓاْ أَحَدَكُم بِوَرِقِكُمۡ هَٰذِهِۦٓ إِلَى ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ فَلۡيَنظُرۡ أَيُّهَآ أَزۡكَىٰ طَعَامٗا فَلۡيَأۡتِكُم بِرِزۡقٖ مِّنۡهُ وَلۡيَتَلَطَّفۡ وَلَا يُشۡعِرَنَّ بِكُمۡ أَحَدًا﴾ (Quran 18:19)

When they woke, they thought they had slept a day or part of a day. They sent one of them with silver coins to buy food, telling him to be gentle and discreet. Their secret was revealed, and people learned that **"the promise of Allah is truth and that of the Hour there is no doubt"**.

﴿سَيَقُولُونَ ثَلَٰثَةࣱ رَّابِعُهُمۡ كَلۡبُهُمۡ وَيَقُولُونَ خَمۡسَةࣱ سَادِسُهُمۡ كَلۡبُهُمۡ رَجۡمَۢا بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۖ وَيَقُولُونَ سَبۡعَةࣱ وَثَامِنُهُمۡ كَلۡبُهُمۡۚ قُل رَّبِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ بِعِدَّتِهِم مَّا يَعۡلَمُهُمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلࣱۗ فَلَا تُمَارِ فِيهِمۡ إِلَّا مِرَآءٗ ظَٰهِرٗا وَلَا تَسۡتَفۡتِ فِيهِم مِّنۡهُمۡ أَحَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:22)

People will argue about their number. **"Say: My Lord is most knowing of their number."** The Quran turns us away from useless details toward the lesson. The names of the young men and of their dog, and the king's identity, come from reports that are not established, and they add nothing to the guidance.

﴿وَلَا تَقُولَنَّ لِشَاْيۡءٍ إِنِّي فَاعِلࣱ ذَٰلِكَ غَدًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَٱذۡكُر رَّبَّكَ إِذَا نَسِيتَ وَقُلۡ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَهۡدِيَنِ رَبِّي لِأَقۡرَبَ مِنۡ هَٰذَا رَشَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:23-24)

**"And never say of anything: Indeed, I will do that tomorrow, except when adding: If Allah wills."**

﴿وَلَبِثُواْ فِي كَهۡفِهِمۡ ثَلَٰثَ مِاْئَةٖ سِنِينَ وَٱزۡدَادُواْ تِسۡعٗا﴾ (Quran 18:25)

**"And they remained in their cave for three hundred years and exceeded by nine."**

**The trial of faith, and its protection**: good companions, fleeing from trial and du'a.

## Between the Stories: Patience With the Righteous
﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ نَفۡسَكَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَدَوٰةِ وَٱلۡعَشِيِّ يُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَهُۥۖ وَلَا تَعۡدُ عَيۡنَاكَ عَنۡهُمۡ تُرِيدُ زِينَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَا تُطِعۡ مَنۡ أَغۡفَلۡنَا قَلۡبَهُۥ عَن ذِكۡرِنَا وَٱتَّبَعَ هَوَىٰهُ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُهُۥ فُرُطٗا﴾ (Quran 18:28)

**"And keep yourself patient with those who call upon their Lord in the morning and the evening, seeking His face. And let not your eyes pass beyond them, desiring adornments of the worldly life."**

﴿وَقُلِ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكُمۡۖ فَمَن شَآءَ فَلۡيُؤۡمِن وَمَن شَآءَ فَلۡيَكۡفُرۡۚ إِنَّآ أَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلظَّـٰلِمِينَ نَارًا أَحَاطَ بِهِمۡ سُرَادِقُهَاۚ وَإِن يَسۡتَغِيثُواْ يُغَاثُواْ بِمَآءٖ كَٱلۡمُهۡلِ يَشۡوِي ٱلۡوُجُوهَۚ بِئۡسَ ٱلشَّرَابُ وَسَآءَتۡ مُرۡتَفَقًا﴾ (Quran 18:29)

**"And say: The truth is from your Lord, so whoever wills, let him believe; and whoever wills, let him disbelieve."**

## Story 2: The Owner of the Two Gardens (Verses 32–44)
﴿۞وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلٗا رَّجُلَيۡنِ جَعَلۡنَا لِأَحَدِهِمَا جَنَّتَيۡنِ مِنۡ أَعۡنَٰبٖ وَحَفَفۡنَٰهُمَا بِنَخۡلٖ وَجَعَلۡنَا بَيۡنَهُمَا زَرۡعٗا﴾ ﴿كِلۡتَا ٱلۡجَنَّتَيۡنِ ءَاتَتۡ أُكُلَهَا وَلَمۡ تَظۡلِم مِّنۡهُ شَيۡـٔٗاۚ وَفَجَّرۡنَا خِلَٰلَهُمَا نَهَرٗا﴾ ﴿وَكَانَ لَهُۥ ثَمَرࣱ فَقَالَ لِصَٰحِبِهِۦ وَهُوَ يُحَاوِرُهُۥٓ أَنَا۠ أَكۡثَرُ مِنكَ مَالٗا وَأَعَزُّ نَفَرٗا﴾ (Quran 18:32-34)

Two men: one was given two gardens of grapevines, surrounded by palms, with crops between and a river flowing through. He said to his companion: **"I am greater than you in wealth and mightier in men."**

﴿وَدَخَلَ جَنَّتَهُۥ وَهُوَ ظَالِمࣱ لِّنَفۡسِهِۦ قَالَ مَآ أَظُنُّ أَن تَبِيدَ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أَبَدٗا﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَظُنُّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ قَآئِمَةٗ وَلَئِن رُّدِدتُّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّي لَأَجِدَنَّ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهَا مُنقَلَبٗا﴾ (Quran 18:35-36)

He entered his garden while wronging himself and said: **"I do not think that this will perish, ever. And I do not think the Hour will occur."**

﴿وَلَوۡلَآ إِذۡ دَخَلۡتَ جَنَّتَكَ قُلۡتَ مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ إِن تَرَنِ أَنَا۠ أَقَلَّ مِنكَ مَالٗا وَوَلَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:39)

His believing companion answered: **"And why did you, when you entered your garden, not say: What Allah willed; there is no power except in Allah?"** (*Ma sha' Allah, la quwwata illa billah*.)

﴿وَأُحِيطَ بِثَمَرِهِۦ فَأَصۡبَحَ يُقَلِّبُ كَفَّيۡهِ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَنفَقَ فِيهَا وَهِيَ خَاوِيَةٌ عَلَىٰ عُرُوشِهَا وَيَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أُشۡرِكۡ بِرَبِّيٓ أَحَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:42)

His fruits were destroyed, and he began **"to turn his hands about in dismay over what he had spent on it"**, saying: **"Oh, I wish I had not associated with my Lord anyone."**

**The trial of wealth, and its protection**: knowing that this world is fleeting and attributing blessings to Allah.

﴿وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا كَمَآءٍ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَٱخۡتَلَطَ بِهِۦ نَبَاتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَأَصۡبَحَ هَشِيمٗا تَذۡرُوهُ ٱلرِّيَٰحُۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مُّقۡتَدِرًا﴾ ﴿ٱلۡمَالُ وَٱلۡبَنُونَ زِينَةُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَٱلۡبَٰقِيَٰتُ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتُ خَيۡرٌ عِندَ رَبِّكَ ثَوَابٗا وَخَيۡرٌ أَمَلٗا﴾ (Quran 18:45-46)

**The parable of this life**: like rain that makes plants grow, then they become dry stalks scattered by the wind. **"Wealth and children are adornment of the worldly life. But the enduring good deeds are better to your Lord for reward and better for hope."**

## Verses 47–59: The Scene of Judgement
﴿وَوُضِعَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ فَتَرَى ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ مُشۡفِقِينَ مِمَّا فِيهِ وَيَقُولُونَ يَٰوَيۡلَتَنَا مَالِ هَٰذَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا يُغَادِرُ صَغِيرَةٗ وَلَا كَبِيرَةً إِلَّآ أَحۡصَىٰهَاۚ وَوَجَدُواْ مَا عَمِلُواْ حَاضِرٗاۗ وَلَا يَظۡلِمُ رَبُّكَ أَحَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:49)

**"And the record will be placed, and you will see the criminals fearful of that within it, and they will say: Oh, woe to us! What is this book that leaves nothing small or great except that it has enumerated it?"**

﴿وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِأٓدَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَفَسَقَ عَنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّهِۦٓۗ أَفَتَتَّخِذُونَهُۥ وَذُرِّيَّتَهُۥٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مِن دُونِي وَهُمۡ لَكُمۡ عَدُوُّۢۚ بِئۡسَ لِلظَّـٰلِمِينَ بَدَلٗا﴾ (Quran 18:50)

**Iblis**: **"He was of the jinn and departed from the command of his Lord. Then will you take him and his descendants as allies other than Me while they are enemies to you?"**

## Story 3: Musa and al-Khidr (Verses 60–82)
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ubayy ibn Ka'b reported from the Prophet ﷺ that Musa stood to give a sermon and was asked who was the most knowledgeable person. He said: "I am." Allah rebuked him for not attributing knowledge to Him, and told him that a servant at the junction of the two seas knew more than him.

﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِفَتَىٰهُ لَآ أَبۡرَحُ حَتَّىٰٓ أَبۡلُغَ مَجۡمَعَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ أَوۡ أَمۡضِيَ حُقُبٗا﴾ (Quran 18:60)

**"I will not cease until I reach the junction of the two seas or continue for a long period."**

﴿فَوَجَدَا عَبۡدٗا مِّنۡ عِبَادِنَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَا وَعَلَّمۡنَٰهُ مِن لَّدُنَّا عِلۡمٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَ لَهُۥ مُوسَىٰ هَلۡ أَتَّبِعُكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن تُعَلِّمَنِ مِمَّا عُلِّمۡتَ رُشۡدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:65-66)

They found **"a servant from among Our servants to whom We had given mercy from Us and had taught him from Us a knowledge"**. Musa said: **"May I follow you on condition that you teach me from what you have been taught of sound judgement?"**

Three events followed:
1. **The boat**: al-Khidr made a hole in the boat of poor men who had carried them without charge.
2. **The boy**: al-Khidr killed a boy.
3. **The wall**: in a town that refused them food, he rebuilt a wall about to collapse without asking for payment.

Musa objected each time, and they parted.

﴿أَمَّا ٱلسَّفِينَةُ فَكَانَتۡ لِمَسَٰكِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ فَأَرَدتُّ أَنۡ أَعِيبَهَا وَكَانَ وَرَآءَهُم مَّلِكࣱ يَأۡخُذُ كُلَّ سَفِينَةٍ غَصۡبٗا﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّا ٱلۡغُلَٰمُ فَكَانَ أَبَوَاهُ مُؤۡمِنَيۡنِ فَخَشِينَآ أَن يُرۡهِقَهُمَا طُغۡيَٰنٗا وَكُفۡرٗا﴾ ﴿فَأَرَدۡنَآ أَن يُبۡدِلَهُمَا رَبُّهُمَا خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُ زَكَوٰةٗ وَأَقۡرَبَ رُحۡمٗا﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّا ٱلۡجِدَارُ فَكَانَ لِغُلَٰمَيۡنِ يَتِيمَيۡنِ فِي ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ وَكَانَ تَحۡتَهُۥ كَنزࣱ لَّهُمَا وَكَانَ أَبُوهُمَا صَٰلِحٗا فَأَرَادَ رَبُّكَ أَن يَبۡلُغَآ أَشُدَّهُمَا وَيَسۡتَخۡرِجَا كَنزَهُمَا رَحۡمَةٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ وَمَا فَعَلۡتُهُۥ عَنۡ أَمۡرِيۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَأۡوِيلُ مَا لَمۡ تَسۡطِع عَّلَيۡهِ صَبۡرٗا﴾ (Quran 18:79-82)

**The explanations**: the boat belonged to poor people, and a king was seizing every sound ship; the damage saved it. The boy would have burdened his believing parents with transgression and disbelief, and Allah would give them one better. The wall covered a treasure belonging to two orphans, **"and their father had been righteous"**. **"And I did it not of my own accord."**

**The trial of knowledge, and its protection**: humility, and knowing that Allah's wisdom is behind what we do not understand.

## Story 4: Dhul-Qarnayn (Verses 83–98)
﴿إِنَّا مَكَّنَّا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ سَبَبٗا﴾ (Quran 18:84)

**"Indeed, We established him upon the earth, and We gave him to everything a way."** A righteous ruler who travelled to the west and to the east. Scholars differ about who he was, and the Quran does not name him; what matters is his conduct.

﴿قَالَ أَمَّا مَن ظَلَمَ فَسَوۡفَ نُعَذِّبُهُۥ ثُمَّ يُرَدُّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ فَيُعَذِّبُهُۥ عَذَابٗا نُّكۡرٗا﴾ ﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۡ ءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا فَلَهُۥ جَزَآءً ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۖ وَسَنَقُولُ لَهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِنَا يُسۡرٗا﴾ (Quran 18:87-88)

He declared: **"As for one who wrongs, we will punish him... But as for one who believes and does righteousness, he will have a reward of Paradise."**

﴿قَالُواْ يَٰذَا ٱلۡقَرۡنَيۡنِ إِنَّ يَأۡجُوجَ وَمَأۡجُوجَ مُفۡسِدُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَهَلۡ نَجۡعَلُ لَكَ خَرۡجًا عَلَىٰٓ أَن تَجۡعَلَ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَهُمۡ سَدّٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَ مَا مَكَّنِّي فِيهِ رَبِّي خَيۡرࣱ فَأَعِينُونِي بِقُوَّةٍ أَجۡعَلۡ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَبَيۡنَهُمۡ رَدۡمًا﴾ (Quran 18:94-95)

A people complained of Gog and Magog (*Ya'juj and Ma'juj*) and offered payment for a barrier. He replied: **"That in which my Lord has established me is better"**, and asked only for their labour.

﴿ءَاتُونِي زُبَرَ ٱلۡحَدِيدِۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا سَاوَىٰ بَيۡنَ ٱلصَّدَفَيۡنِ قَالَ ٱنفُخُواْۖ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَعَلَهُۥ نَارٗا قَالَ ءَاتُونِيٓ أُفۡرِغۡ عَلَيۡهِ قِطۡرٗا﴾ ﴿فَمَا ٱسۡطَٰعُوٓاْ أَن يَظۡهَرُوهُ وَمَا ٱسۡتَطَٰعُواْ لَهُۥ نَقۡبٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَ هَٰذَا رَحۡمَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّيۖ فَإِذَا جَآءَ وَعۡدُ رَبِّي جَعَلَهُۥ دَكَّآءَۖ وَكَانَ وَعۡدُ رَبِّي حَقّٗا﴾ (Quran 18:96-98)

He built a barrier of iron and molten copper, then said: **"This is a mercy from my Lord."**

**The trial of power, and its protection**: humility and attributing success to Allah.

## Verses 99–110: The Greatest Losers and the Conclusion
﴿قُلۡ هَلۡ نُنَبِّئُكُم بِٱلۡأَخۡسَرِينَ أَعۡمَٰلًا﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ضَلَّ سَعۡيُهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَهُمۡ يَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّهُمۡ يُحۡسِنُونَ صُنۡعًا﴾ (Quran 18:103-104)

**"Say: Shall we inform you of the greatest losers as to their deeds? They are those whose effort is lost in worldly life, while they think that they are doing well in work."**

﴿قُل لَّوۡ كَانَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ مِدَادٗا لِّكَلِمَٰتِ رَبِّي لَنَفِدَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ قَبۡلَ أَن تَنفَدَ كَلِمَٰتُ رَبِّي وَلَوۡ جِئۡنَا بِمِثۡلِهِۦ مَدَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:109)

**"Say: If the sea were ink for the words of my Lord, the sea would be exhausted before the words of my Lord were exhausted."**

﴿قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ أَنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱۖ فَمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ لِقَآءَ رَبِّهِۦ فَلۡيَعۡمَلۡ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا وَلَا يُشۡرِكۡ بِعِبَادَةِ رَبِّهِۦٓ أَحَدَۢا﴾ (Quran 18:110)

**"Say: I am only a man like you, to whom has been revealed that your god is one God. So whoever would hope for the meeting with his Lord, let him do righteous work and not associate in the worship of his Lord anyone."** The two conditions of an accepted deed: correctness and sincerity.

## Why Al-Kahf Protects From the Dajjal
The Dajjal will come with all four trials: he will claim divinity (faith), command the sky to rain and the earth to grow (wealth), show wonders (knowledge) and control lands (power). The surah teaches the answer to each one.

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Kahf**: the cave.
- **Ar-Raqim**: the inscription, as many commentators explained.
- **'Iwaj**: crookedness.
- **Fityah**: young men.
- **Ma sha' Allah**: what Allah willed.
- **Majma' al-bahrayn**: the junction of the two seas.
- **Rushd**: right guidance, sound judgement.
- **Dhul-Qarnayn**: the one of the two horns.
- **Ya'juj wa Ma'juj**: Gog and Magog.
- **Radm**: barrier.
- **Al-Baqiyat as-salihat**: the enduring good deeds.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Four stories, four trials**: faith, wealth, knowledge and power.
2. **"If Allah wills"** placed in the middle of the cave story.
3. **Two men, two outcomes**: arrogance and gratitude.
4. **"And I did it not of my own accord"**: the key to al-Khidr's acts.
5. **The sea as ink**: the endlessness of Allah's words.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Sakt**: in the narration of Hafs, a brief pause without breath on *'iwaja* (verse 1) before *qayyiman* (verse 2).
- **Idgham without ghunnah**: in *min ladunhu* (verse 2), the noon sakinah merges into the lam.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *wa lam yaj'al* (verse 1), the jim sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *fantalaqa* (verse 71), the noon sakinah is hidden before the ta'.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ka-ma'in anzalnahu min as-sama'i* (verse 45).

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Isra' before it opens with "Exalted is He", and Al-Kahf opens with "All praise is due to Allah". Al-Kahf ends with the man who is only a human to whom revelation came, and Surah Maryam after it begins with stories of Zakariyya, Yahya, Maryam and 'Isa.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Recite Al-Kahf every Friday.**
2. **Memorise its first ten verses** for protection from the Dajjal.
3. **Choose righteous companions.**
4. **Say "If Allah wills"** for future plans.
5. **Say "Ma sha' Allah, la quwwata illa billah"** when you see a blessing.
6. **Be humble in knowledge.**
7. **Use power to serve**, not to gain.
8. **Make every deed correct and sincere.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Kahf
- **Week 1**: verses 1–26 (start with the first ten).
- **Week 2**: verses 27–59.
- **Week 3**: verses 60–82.
- **Week 4**: verses 83–110, with full review.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell the four stories** as four Friday night stories.
- **Ask after each story**: what was the trial, and how was it passed?
- **Teach them to say "Ma sha' Allah"** when they admire something.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why recite Al-Kahf on Friday?
The Prophet ﷺ said a light shines for the reciter between the two Fridays, as al-Hakim and al-Bayhaqi reported, graded authentic by al-Albani.

### Which verses protect from the Dajjal?
The first ten verses, as in Sahih Muslim; another narration in Muslim mentions the last ten.

### How long did the people of the cave sleep?
Three hundred years and nine more, as in verse 25.

### Who was al-Khidr?
A righteous servant to whom Allah gave mercy and knowledge from Him; the story is detailed in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Who was Dhul-Qarnayn?
A righteous ruler whom Allah established on the earth; the Quran does not give his identity, and scholars differ about it.

### What are the four trials of Al-Kahf?
Faith, wealth, knowledge and power.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Kahf?
110 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Kahf is a weekly lesson for every Friday: whatever trial reaches you, in your faith, your wealth, your knowledge or your power, its answer is in these four stories. Hold to good companions, attribute every blessing to Allah, stay humble in what you know, and use what you are given to serve. And finish as the surah finishes: do righteous work, and associate no one in the worship of your Lord.
