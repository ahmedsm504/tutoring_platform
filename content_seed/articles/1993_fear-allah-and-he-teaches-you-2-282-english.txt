﴿وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۖ وَيُعَلِّمُكُمُ ٱللَّهُۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:282]

## The longest verse
- These words close the **verse of debt**, the longest verse in the Quran, full of detailed rules on writing and witnessing loans.

## Taqwa and knowledge
| View | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Taqwa opens knowledge | The mindful receive understanding beyond books |
| A new sentence | Allah is teaching you these rulings, so fear Him in them |

- A similar promise: *If you fear Allah, He will grant you a criterion* (Al-Anfal 8:29).

## Why in a financial verse?
- Money is where people are tempted to cheat. Knowing the rules is not enough without taqwa to apply them honestly.

## "Allah knows everything"
- No trick in a contract is hidden from Him.

## Lessons
- **Begin learning with taqwa**: sincere intention and leaving sins.
- **Write down debts**, as the Quran teaches.
