Surah Al-Hajj opens with one of the most terrifying scenes in the Quran, the earthquake of the Hour, when a nursing mother will forget her infant and people will appear drunk though they are not. Then it moves to the origin of the human being, the call of Ibrahim to pilgrimage, the rites of Hajj and their inner meaning, permission for the oppressed believers to defend themselves, and a striking challenge: all the false gods together cannot create a fly. It ends by reminding Muslims of their identity: "He named you Muslims before and in this."

This guide explains Surah Al-Hajj section by section in English, with practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Hajj
- **Number of verses**: 78.
- **Type**: Medinan; scholars differ, and many say it contains both Meccan and Medinan verses.
- **Position**: Surah 22, in the seventeenth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Hajj, the pilgrimage, from Ibrahim's call in verse 27.
- **Main themes**: the Hour and resurrection, sincere worship, Hajj and its spirit, defending the oppressed, and the identity of the Muslim nation.

## Verses 1–2: The Earthquake of the Hour
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡۚ إِنَّ زَلۡزَلَةَ ٱلسَّاعَةِ شَيۡءٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ تَرَوۡنَهَا تَذۡهَلُ كُلُّ مُرۡضِعَةٍ عَمَّآ أَرۡضَعَتۡ وَتَضَعُ كُلُّ ذَاتِ حَمۡلٍ حَمۡلَهَا وَتَرَى ٱلنَّاسَ سُكَٰرَىٰ وَمَا هُم بِسُكَٰرَىٰ وَلَٰكِنَّ عَذَابَ ٱللَّهِ شَدِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 22:1-2)

**"O mankind, fear your Lord. Indeed, the convulsion of the Hour is a terrible thing."** On that Day **"every nursing mother will be distracted from that which she was nursing, and every pregnant woman will abort her pregnancy, and you will see the people intoxicated while they are not intoxicated; but the punishment of Allah is severe."** The strongest bond on earth, a mother and her nursing child, is broken by terror.

## Verses 3–7: The Stages of Creation as Proof of Resurrection
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِن كُنتُمۡ فِي رَيۡبٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡبَعۡثِ فَإِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن تُرَابٖ ثُمَّ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ ثُمَّ مِنۡ عَلَقَةٖ ثُمَّ مِن مُّضۡغَةٖ مُّخَلَّقَةٖ وَغَيۡرِ مُخَلَّقَةٖ لِّنُبَيِّنَ لَكُمۡۚ وَنُقِرُّ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِ مَا نَشَآءُ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى ثُمَّ نُخۡرِجُكُمۡ طِفۡلٗا ثُمَّ لِتَبۡلُغُوٓاْ أَشُدَّكُمۡۖ وَمِنكُم مَّن يُتَوَفَّىٰ وَمِنكُم مَّن يُرَدُّ إِلَىٰٓ أَرۡذَلِ ٱلۡعُمُرِ لِكَيۡلَا يَعۡلَمَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ عِلۡمٖ شَيۡـٔٗاۚ وَتَرَى ٱلۡأَرۡضَ هَامِدَةٗ فَإِذَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهَا ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱهۡتَزَّتۡ وَرَبَتۡ وَأَنۢبَتَتۡ مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجِۭ بَهِيجٖ﴾ (Quran 22:5)

**"O people, if you should be in doubt about the Resurrection, then indeed, We created you from dust, then from a sperm-drop, then from a clinging clot, and then from a lump of flesh, formed and unformed."** Then the infant is brought out, grows to full strength, and some die while others are returned to the weakest age. **And the earth**: you see it lifeless, and when rain comes down it stirs and swells and grows every lovely kind. The One who does this gives life to the dead.

## Verses 8–16: Worshipping Allah on an Edge
﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَعۡبُدُ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ حَرۡفٖۖ فَإِنۡ أَصَابَهُۥ خَيۡرٌ ٱطۡمَأَنَّ بِهِۦۖ وَإِنۡ أَصَابَتۡهُ فِتۡنَةٌ ٱنقَلَبَ عَلَىٰ وَجۡهِهِۦ خَسِرَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡخُسۡرَانُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾ (Quran 22:11)

**"And of the people is he who worships Allah on an edge. If he is touched by good, he is reassured by it; and if he is struck by trial, he turns on his face. He has lost this world and the hereafter. That is the manifest loss."** Faith on an edge is faith that depends on comfort. True faith stays firm in hardship as well as ease.

## Verses 17–24: Everything Prostrates
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَسۡجُدُۤ لَهُۥۤ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُ وَٱلنُّجُومُ وَٱلۡجِبَالُ وَٱلشَّجَرُ وَٱلدَّوَآبُّ وَكَثِيرࣱ مِّنَ ٱلنَّاسِۖ وَكَثِيرٌ حَقَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡعَذَابُۗ وَمَن يُهِنِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن مُّكۡرِمٍۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَفۡعَلُ مَا يَشَآءُ۩﴾ (Quran 22:18)

**"Do you not see that to Allah prostrates whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on the earth and the sun, the moon, the stars, the mountains, the trees, the moving creatures and many of the people?"** The whole universe submits; only some humans refuse.

﴿۞هَٰذَانِ خَصۡمَانِ ٱخۡتَصَمُواْ فِي رَبِّهِمۡۖ فَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ قُطِّعَتۡ لَهُمۡ ثِيَابࣱ مِّن نَّارٖ يُصَبُّ مِن فَوۡقِ رُءُوسِهِمُ ٱلۡحَمِيمُ﴾ (Quran 22:19)

**"These are two adversaries who have disputed over their Lord."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Dharr swore that this verse was revealed about those who met in single combat at Badr.

## Verses 25–37: Ibrahim and the Rites of Hajj
﴿وَإِذۡ بَوَّأۡنَا لِإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ مَكَانَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ أَن لَّا تُشۡرِكۡ بِي شَيۡـٔٗا وَطَهِّرۡ بَيۡتِيَ لِلطَّآئِفِينَ وَٱلۡقَآئِمِينَ وَٱلرُّكَّعِ ٱلسُّجُودِ﴾ (Quran 22:26)

**"And when We designated for Ibrahim the site of the House: Do not associate anything with Me and purify My House for those who perform tawaf and those who stand and those who bow and prostrate."**

﴿وَأَذِّن فِي ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَجِّ يَأۡتُوكَ رِجَالٗا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٖ يَأۡتِينَ مِن كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٖ﴾ (Quran 22:27)

**"And proclaim to the people the Hajj; they will come to you on foot and on every lean camel; they will come from every distant pass."** Ibrahim called in an empty valley, and Allah carried his call across the centuries to every continent.

﴿لِّيَشۡهَدُواْ مَنَٰفِعَ لَهُمۡ وَيَذۡكُرُواْ ٱسۡمَ ٱللَّهِ فِيٓ أَيَّامٖ مَّعۡلُومَٰتٍ عَلَىٰ مَا رَزَقَهُم مِّنۢ بَهِيمَةِ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِۖ فَكُلُواْ مِنۡهَا وَأَطۡعِمُواْ ٱلۡبَآئِسَ ٱلۡفَقِيرَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ لۡيَقۡضُواْ تَفَثَهُمۡ وَلۡيُوفُواْ نُذُورَهُمۡ وَلۡيَطَّوَّفُواْ بِٱلۡبَيۡتِ ٱلۡعَتِيقِ﴾ (Quran 22:28-29)

**The purposes of Hajj**: **"That they may witness benefits for themselves and mention the name of Allah on known days"**, eat from the sacrificial animals and feed the poor, complete their rites, fulfil their vows **"and perform tawaf around the Ancient House"**.

﴿ذَٰلِكَۖ وَمَن يُعَظِّمۡ شَعَـٰٓئِرَ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّهَا مِن تَقۡوَى ٱلۡقُلُوبِ﴾ (Quran 22:32)

**"And whoever honours the symbols of Allah, indeed, it is from the piety of hearts."**

﴿لَن يَنَالَ ٱللَّهَ لُحُومُهَا وَلَا دِمَآؤُهَا وَلَٰكِن يَنَالُهُ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ مِنكُمۡۚ كَذَٰلِكَ سَخَّرَهَا لَكُمۡ لِتُكَبِّرُواْ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ مَا هَدَىٰكُمۡۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 22:37)

**"Their meat will not reach Allah, nor will their blood, but what reaches Him is piety from you."** The sacrifice is a means; the goal is the heart's taqwa.

## Verses 38–41: Permission to Fight
﴿أُذِنَ لِلَّذِينَ يُقَٰتَلُونَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ ظُلِمُواْۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ نَصۡرِهِمۡ لَقَدِيرٌ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ أُخۡرِجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِم بِغَيۡرِ حَقٍّ إِلَّآ أَن يَقُولُواْ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُۗ وَلَوۡلَا دَفۡعُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلنَّاسَ بَعۡضَهُم بِبَعۡضٖ لَّهُدِّمَتۡ صَوَٰمِعُ وَبِيَعࣱ وَصَلَوَٰتࣱ وَمَسَٰجِدُ يُذۡكَرُ فِيهَا ٱسۡمُ ٱللَّهِ كَثِيرٗاۗ وَلَيَنصُرَنَّ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَنصُرُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَقَوِيٌّ عَزِيزٌ﴾ (Quran 22:39-40)

**"Permission to fight has been given to those who are being fought, because they were wronged. And indeed, Allah is competent to give them victory. Those who have been evicted from their homes without right, only because they say: Our Lord is Allah."** Many commentators say this was the first verse revealed permitting fighting. The reason: **"And were it not that Allah checks the people, some by means of others, there would have been demolished monasteries, churches, synagogues and mosques in which the name of Allah is much mentioned."**

﴿ٱلَّذِينَ إِن مَّكَّنَّـٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَوُاْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَمَرُواْ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَنَهَوۡاْ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِۗ وَلِلَّهِ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾ (Quran 22:41)

**The description of those given authority**: **"those who, if We give them authority in the land, establish prayer and give zakah and enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong."**

## Verses 42–57: Blind Hearts
﴿أَفَلَمۡ يَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَتَكُونَ لَهُمۡ قُلُوبࣱ يَعۡقِلُونَ بِهَآ أَوۡ ءَاذَانࣱ يَسۡمَعُونَ بِهَاۖ فَإِنَّهَا لَا تَعۡمَى ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَلَٰكِن تَعۡمَى ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلَّتِي فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾ (Quran 22:46)

**"Have they not travelled through the land and have hearts by which to reason and ears by which to hear? For indeed, it is not eyes that are blinded, but blinded are the hearts which are within the breasts."**

﴿وَيَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَكَ بِٱلۡعَذَابِ وَلَن يُخۡلِفَ ٱللَّهُ وَعۡدَهُۥۚ وَإِنَّ يَوۡمًا عِندَ رَبِّكَ كَأَلۡفِ سَنَةٖ مِّمَّا تَعُدُّونَ﴾ (Quran 22:47)

**"And indeed, a day with your Lord is like a thousand years of those which you count."**

## Verses 58–72: Signs of Power
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَتُصۡبِحُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ مُخۡضَرَّةًۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَطِيفٌ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 22:63)

**"Do you not see that Allah has sent down rain from the sky and the earth becomes green?"**

﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَخَّرَ لَكُم مَّا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلۡفُلۡكَ تَجۡرِي فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِأَمۡرِهِۦ وَيُمۡسِكُ ٱلسَّمَآءَ أَن تَقَعَ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِٱلنَّاسِ لَرَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 22:65)

**"He restrains the sky from falling upon the earth, unless by His permission. Indeed Allah, to the people, is Kind and Merciful."**

## Verses 73–74: The Parable of the Fly
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ضُرِبَ مَثَلࣱ فَٱسۡتَمِعُواْ لَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدۡعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَن يَخۡلُقُواْ ذُبَابٗا وَلَوِ ٱجۡتَمَعُواْ لَهُۥۖ وَإِن يَسۡلُبۡهُمُ ٱلذُّبَابُ شَيۡـٔٗا لَّا يَسۡتَنقِذُوهُ مِنۡهُۚ ضَعُفَ ٱلطَّالِبُ وَٱلۡمَطۡلُوبُ﴾ (Quran 22:73)

**"O people, an example is presented, so listen to it. Indeed, those you invoke besides Allah will never create a fly, even if they gathered together for that purpose. And if the fly should steal away from them a thing, they could not recover it from him. Weak are the pursuer and the pursued."** All the idols together cannot create the smallest insect or take back a crumb it steals. **"They have not appraised Allah with true appraisal."**

## Verses 75–78: He Named You Muslims
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱرۡكَعُواْ وَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ وَٱفۡعَلُواْ ٱلۡخَيۡرَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ۩﴾ (Quran 22:77)

**"O you who believe, bow and prostrate and worship your Lord and do good, that you may succeed."**

﴿وَجَٰهِدُواْ فِي ٱللَّهِ حَقَّ جِهَادِهِۦۚ هُوَ ٱجۡتَبَىٰكُمۡ وَمَا جَعَلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ مِنۡ حَرَجٖۚ مِّلَّةَ أَبِيكُمۡ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَۚ هُوَ سَمَّىٰكُمُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ مِن قَبۡلُ وَفِي هَٰذَا لِيَكُونَ ٱلرَّسُولُ شَهِيدًا عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَتَكُونُواْ شُهَدَآءَ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِۚ فَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَٱعۡتَصِمُواْ بِٱللَّهِ هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰكُمۡۖ فَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡمَوۡلَىٰ وَنِعۡمَ ٱلنَّصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 22:78)

**"And strive for Allah with the striving due to Him. He has chosen you and has not placed upon you in the religion any difficulty. It is the religion of your father, Ibrahim. He named you Muslims before and in this."** The religion is easy, the identity is clear, and the nation is a witness over humanity. **"So establish prayer and give zakah and hold fast to Allah. He is your protector; and excellent is the protector, and excellent is the helper."**

**Prostrations of recitation**: scholars agree on the prostration at verse 18, and differ about a second one at verse 77.

## Word Meanings
- **Zalzalah**: convulsion, earthquake.
- **Mudghah**: lump of flesh.
- **'Ala harf**: on an edge.
- **Adhdhin**: proclaim, call.
- **Dhamir**: lean (camel).
- **Fajj 'amiq**: distant pass.
- **Al-Bayt al-'Atiq**: the Ancient House.
- **Sha'a'ir**: symbols, rites.
- **Udhina**: permission was given.
- **Dhubab**: fly.
- **Ijtaba**: chose.
- **Haraj**: difficulty, hardship.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The nursing mother**: terror shown through the strongest human bond.
2. **"Drunk but not drunk"**: a vivid image of confusion.
3. **The fly**: the weakest creature exposes the weakness of idols.
4. **"It is not eyes that are blinded"**: true blindness is of the heart.
5. **"Meat and blood do not reach Allah"**: the spirit behind every rite.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Ikhfa'**: in *yansuruhu* (verse 40), the noon sakinah is hidden before the sad.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *man yansuruhu* (verse 40), the noon of *man* merges into the ya'.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'a* (verse 65).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *ijtabakum* (verse 78), the jim sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Lam shamsiyyah**: in *an-nas* (verse 1).

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Anbiya' before it ends with warnings of the Hour, and Al-Hajj opens with the earthquake of the Hour. Al-Hajj ends with "that you may succeed", and Surah Al-Mu'minun after it opens: "Certainly will the believers have succeeded."

## Practical Lessons
1. **Prepare for the Hour**, whose terror makes a mother forget her child.
2. **Worship Allah firmly**, in ease and in hardship, not on an edge.
3. **Answer Ibrahim's call** and plan for Hajj if you are able.
4. **Honour the symbols of Allah** from a heart of taqwa.
5. **Remember that sincerity reaches Allah**, not outward forms alone.
6. **Stand with the oppressed.**
7. **Travel and reflect** with an open heart.
8. **Be proud of the name Muslim**, which Allah chose for you.

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Hajj
- **Week 1**: verses 1–24.
- **Week 2**: verses 25–48.
- **Week 3**: verses 49–78, with full review.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell the story of Ibrahim's call**, and show a map of where pilgrims come from.
- **Read the parable of the fly** and ask them: could all the people in the world make one fly?
- **Teach them verse 37** before Eid al-Adha.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is it called Surah Al-Hajj?
Because of Ibrahim's call to the pilgrimage and the rites of Hajj in verses 26–37.

### What does "worship Allah on an edge" mean?
Faith that is conditional on comfort and turns away at the first trial.

### What is the parable of the fly?
All false gods together could not create a fly or recover what it took from them.

### What does "their meat will not reach Allah" mean?
That Allah accepts the taqwa and sincerity behind the sacrifice.

### Was this the first verse permitting fighting?
Many commentators say verse 39 was the first revealed about it.

### Who named the Muslims "Muslims"?
Allah, as in verse 78: "He named you Muslims before and in this."

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Hajj?
78 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Hajj moves from the earthquake of the Hour to the stillness of tawaf around the Ancient House. It reminds you that every rite is a path to the heart's taqwa, that faith must stand firm in trial, and that your name, Muslim, was chosen by Allah Himself. Answer the call of Ibrahim, and hold fast to Allah: He is the best protector and the best helper.
