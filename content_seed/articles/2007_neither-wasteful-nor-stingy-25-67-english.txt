﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَنفَقُواْ لَمۡ يُسۡرِفُواْ وَلَمۡ يَقۡتُرُواْ وَكَانَ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ قَوَامٗا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:67]

## The words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Israf* | Going beyond limits, or spending on sin |
| *Qatr* | Tightness and stinginess in what is due |
| *Qawam* | The balanced middle |

## A similar verse
- *Do not chain your hand to your neck, nor extend it completely* (Al-Isra 17:29).

## Applying it
| Area | Balance |
|---|---|
| Home | Needs met without waste on luxuries |
| Children | Reasonable giving and teaching them to save |
| Charity | Regular giving without neglecting family rights |

## Lessons
- **Make a budget** with needs, charity and savings.
- **Moderation** is loved by Allah in money and beyond.
