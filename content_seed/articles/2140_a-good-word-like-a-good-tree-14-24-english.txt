﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا كَلِمَةٗ طَيِّبَةٗ كَشَجَرَةٖ طَيِّبَةٍ أَصۡلُهَا ثَابِتࣱ وَفَرۡعُهَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ﴾ ﴿تُؤۡتِيٓ أُكُلَهَا كُلَّ حِينِۭ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهَاۗ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ لِلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ [Ibrahim 14:24-25]

## The good word
- Ibn ʿAbbas and others: the testimony *la ilaha illa Allah*.

## The palm tree
- *Among trees is a tree whose leaves do not fall, and it is like the Muslim. Tell me what it is.* Ibn ʿUmar thought it was the palm, then the Prophet ﷺ said: *It is the palm tree* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Qualities of the tree
| Quality | In the believer |
|---|---|
| Firm roots | Faith deep in the heart |
| Branches in the sky | Good deeds rising to Allah |
| Fruit in every season | Constant benefit |

## The contrast
- *And the example of a bad word is like a bad tree, uprooted from the surface of the earth, with no stability* (14:26).

## Lessons
- **Strengthen the roots of faith** with knowledge and certainty.
- **Be beneficial** in every season, like the palm.
