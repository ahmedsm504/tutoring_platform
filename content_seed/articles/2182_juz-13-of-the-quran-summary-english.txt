Juz 13 begins with ﴿۞وَمَآ أُبَرِّئُ نَفۡسِيٓۚ إِنَّ ٱلنَّفۡسَ لَأَمَّارَةُۢ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ إِلَّا مَا رَحِمَ رَبِّيٓۚ﴾, "And I do not acquit myself. The soul is ever inclined to evil, except those upon whom my Lord has mercy", so it is called **Juz Wa Ma Ubarri'u**. It runs from **Yusuf 12:53 to the end of Surah Ibrahim (14:52)**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 242 to 261**.

The juz has three stations: **the close of Yusuf's story**, with the reunion of father and brothers and a magnificent pardon; then all of **Surah Ar-Ra'd** with its cosmic signs and parables; then all of **Surah Ibrahim**, with the call of the messengers, gratitude for blessings and the prayer of the father of the prophets.

## The big idea

The juz can be summed up as **the outcome of patience and gratitude**. Yusuf was patient and was given authority, establishment and reunion with his family; Ya'qub showed beautiful patience and Allah restored his sight and his sons. Ar-Ra'd then affirms that truth remains while falsehood is foam that vanishes, and that hearts find rest only in the remembrance of Allah. Ibrahim then states the law of gratitude, ﴿لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ﴾, "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you", and closes with Ibrahim's prayer, which unites gratitude, patience and monotheism.

## Part one: the close of Yusuf's story (53-111)

### 1. From prison to the storehouses (53-57)
It opens with: "And I do not acquit myself; the soul is ever inclined to evil, except those upon whom my Lord has mercy." Commentators hold two views: that these are the words of al-'Aziz's wife completing her confession, or Yusuf's words in humility. The king then says: ﴿ٱئۡتُونِي بِهِۦٓ أَسۡتَخۡلِصۡهُ لِنَفۡسِيۖ﴾, "Bring him to me; I will take him exclusively for myself", and after speaking with him: ﴿إِنَّكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَدَيۡنَا مَكِينٌ أَمِينࣱ﴾, "Today you are established and trusted with us." Yusuf said: ﴿ٱجۡعَلۡنِي عَلَىٰ خَزَآئِنِ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ إِنِّي حَفِيظٌ عَلِيمࣱ﴾, "Put me in charge of the storehouses of the land; I am a knowing guardian."

**A lesson in competence**: Yusuf asked for responsibility because he had both of its qualities: trustworthiness (*hafiz*) and expertise (*'alim*). Positions are sought through competence, not through craving status.

### 2. The brothers arrive (58-68)
The brothers came during the years of famine; Yusuf recognised them, but they did not recognise him. He asked them to bring their brother from their father and hid their payment in their bags. They returned asking their father for their brother, and he said: ﴿هَلۡ ءَامَنُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ إِلَّا كَمَآ أَمِنتُكُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَخِيهِ مِن قَبۡلُ فَٱللَّهُ خَيۡرٌ حَٰفِظٗاۖ وَهُوَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾, "Should I trust you with him as I trusted you with his brother before? But Allah is the best guardian, and He is the most merciful of the merciful." He took a pledge from them and advised: ﴿يَٰبَنِيَّ لَا تَدۡخُلُواْ مِنۢ بَابٖ وَٰحِدٖ وَٱدۡخُلُواْ مِنۡ أَبۡوَٰبٖ مُّتَفَرِّقَةٖۖ﴾, "O my sons, do not enter by one gate, but enter by different gates", then ﴿وَمَآ أُغۡنِي عَنكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن شَيۡءٍۖ﴾, "but I cannot avail you anything against Allah."

**Lesson**: take precautions and rely on Allah. Ya'qub took care, then left the matter to Allah.

### 3. The cup and the brother (69-82)
﴿وَلَمَّا دَخَلُواْ عَلَىٰ يُوسُفَ ءَاوَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِ أَخَاهُۖ قَالَ إِنِّيٓ أَنَا۠ أَخُوكَ فَلَا تَبۡتَئِسۡ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾, "When they entered upon Yusuf, he took his brother to himself and said: I am your brother, so do not grieve over what they used to do." He placed the drinking cup in his brother's bag, and when it was found the brothers said: ﴿إِن يَسۡرِقۡ فَقَدۡ سَرَقَ أَخࣱ لَّهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُۚ﴾, "If he steals, a brother of his stole before." Yusuf kept it to himself and did not reveal it. Then: "Thus did We plan for Yusuf", and ﴿وَفَوۡقَ كُلِّ ذِي عِلۡمٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾, "and above every possessor of knowledge is one more knowing." The eldest stayed in Egypt, ashamed to face their father.

**Lesson**: forbearance in the face of insult; Yusuf heard a wounding accusation and held it in.

### 4. Ya'qub's patience and hope (83-87)
They returned with the news, and Ya'qub said: ﴿بَلۡ سَوَّلَتۡ لَكُمۡ أَنفُسُكُمۡ أَمۡرٗاۖ فَصَبۡرࣱ جَمِيلٌۖ عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَأۡتِيَنِي بِهِمۡ جَمِيعًاۚ﴾, "Rather, your souls have tempted you into something. So patience is most fitting; perhaps Allah will bring them all to me." Then: ﴿وَتَوَلَّىٰ عَنۡهُمۡ وَقَالَ يَـٰٓأَسَفَىٰ عَلَىٰ يُوسُفَ وَٱبۡيَضَّتۡ عَيۡنَاهُ مِنَ ٱلۡحُزۡنِ فَهُوَ كَظِيمࣱ﴾, "He turned away from them and said: Oh, my sorrow for Yusuf. His eyes became white from grief, and he held it in." When they blamed him he said: ﴿إِنَّمَآ أَشۡكُواْ بَثِّي وَحُزۡنِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَأَعۡلَمُ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾, "I only complain of my grief and sorrow to Allah, and I know from Allah what you do not know." Then he sent them searching: ﴿وَلَا تَاْيۡـَٔسُواْ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يَاْيۡـَٔسُ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾, "Do not despair of relief from Allah; none despairs of Allah's relief except the disbelieving people."

**Reflect**: grief does not contradict beautiful patience. Ya'qub grieved until his eyes turned white, but his complaint was to Allah alone and his hope never broke. Read [I Complain of My Grief to Allah: 12:86](/blog/wa-ma-min-dabbatin-meaning-english/#I-Only-Complain-of-My-Grief-and-Sorrow-to-Allah-Quran-12-86-) and [Quran 12:87 Explained: Yaqub's Hope After Years of Loss](/blog/wa-ma-min-dabbatin-meaning-english/#Quran-12-87-Explained-Yaqub-s-Hope-After-Years-of-Loss).

### 5. "I am Yusuf", and the great pardon (88-93)
They returned to Egypt complaining of hardship, and Yusuf asked: do you know what you did to Yusuf and his brother? They said: ﴿أَءِنَّكَ لَأَنتَ يُوسُفُۖ قَالَ أَنَا۠ يُوسُفُ وَهَٰذَآ أَخِيۖ قَدۡ مَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَآۖ إِنَّهُۥ مَن يَتَّقِ وَيَصۡبِرۡ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُضِيعُ أَجۡرَ ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾, "Are you indeed Yusuf? He said: I am Yusuf and this is my brother. Allah has been gracious to us. Whoever is God-conscious and patient, Allah does not let the reward of those who do good be lost." They admitted: ﴿تَٱللَّهِ لَقَدۡ ءَاثَرَكَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَا وَإِن كُنَّا لَخَٰطِـِٔينَ﴾, "By Allah, Allah has preferred you over us, and we were indeed sinners." Then complete pardon: ﴿لَا تَثۡرِيبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ يَغۡفِرُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾, "No blame on you today. Allah will forgive you, and He is the most merciful of the merciful." Read [No Blame on You Today: 12:92](/blog/wa-ma-min-dabbatin-meaning-english/#No-Blame-upon-You-Today-Quran-12-92-Explained).

**The law of the surah**: "whoever is God-conscious and patient": taqwa and patience are the secret of establishment.

### 6. Yusuf's scent and the family reunited (94-101)
﴿وَلَمَّا فَصَلَتِ ٱلۡعِيرُ قَالَ أَبُوهُمۡ إِنِّي لَأَجِدُ رِيحَ يُوسُفَۖ لَوۡلَآ أَن تُفَنِّدُونِ﴾, "When the caravan departed, their father said: I sense the scent of Yusuf, if you will not think me senile." The shirt was cast over his face and he regained his sight. Then the reunion: ﴿وَرَفَعَ أَبَوَيۡهِ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِ وَخَرُّواْ لَهُۥ سُجَّدٗاۖ وَقَالَ يَـٰٓأَبَتِ هَٰذَا تَأۡوِيلُ رُءۡيَٰيَ مِن قَبۡلُ قَدۡ جَعَلَهَا رَبِّي حَقࣰّاۖ﴾, "He raised his parents upon the throne, and they bowed down to him, and he said: O my father, this is the interpretation of my dream of before; my Lord has made it true." Bowing as a greeting was permitted in their law and was abrogated in ours.

**Reflect on his manners**: Yusuf said ﴿وَقَدۡ أَحۡسَنَ بِيٓ إِذۡ أَخۡرَجَنِي مِنَ ٱلسِّجۡنِ﴾, "He was good to me when He brought me out of prison", without mentioning the well so as not to embarrass his brothers, and he attributed what happened to Shaytan: "after Shaytan had sown discord between me and my brothers."

Then Yusuf's prayer at the height of his power: ﴿۞رَبِّ قَدۡ ءَاتَيۡتَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُلۡكِ وَعَلَّمۡتَنِي مِن تَأۡوِيلِ ٱلۡأَحَادِيثِۚ فَاطِرَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَنتَ وَلِيِّۦ فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ تَوَفَّنِي مُسۡلِمٗا وَأَلۡحِقۡنِي بِٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾, "My Lord, You have given me authority and taught me the interpretation of events. Creator of the heavens and earth, You are my protector in this world and the Hereafter. Let me die a Muslim and join me with the righteous." He asked not for more of this world, but for a good end.

### 7. The lessons of the story (102-111)
"That is from the news of the unseen which We reveal to you", then ﴿وَمَآ أَكۡثَرُ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَوۡ حَرَصۡتَ بِمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "And most people, however eager you are, will not believe." Then: ﴿قُلۡ هَٰذِهِۦ سَبِيلِيٓ أَدۡعُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ عَلَىٰ بَصِيرَةٍ أَنَا۠ وَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَنِيۖ﴾, "Say: this is my way; I invite to Allah with insight, I and those who follow me." Then the law of victory: ﴿حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا ٱسۡتَيۡـَٔسَ ٱلرُّسُلُ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ قَدۡ كُذِبُواْ جَآءَهُمۡ نَصۡرُنَا﴾, "until, when the messengers despaired and thought they had been denied, Our help came to them." And the close: ﴿لَقَدۡ كَانَ فِي قَصَصِهِمۡ عِبۡرَةࣱ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِۗ﴾, "In their stories there is a lesson for people of understanding."

**Key lessons of the whole of Surah Yusuf**:
1. Envy begins with imagined favouritism and leads to crime.
2. Trial can be the road to establishment.
3. Chastity and honesty are a young person's capital.
4. Call to Allah wherever you are.
5. Competence is a condition for responsibility.
6. Pardon when you have power.
7. A good end is the believer's goal.

For the whole surah see [Surah Yusuf Meaning in English](/blog/wa-ma-min-dabbatin-meaning-english/#Surah-Yusuf-Meaning-The-Best-of-Stories-and-Never-Losing-Hop).

## Part two: Surah Ar-Ra'd (1-43)

Ar-Ra'd has 43 verses and opens with ﴿الٓمٓرۚ﴾. It is Madani in the classification of the Madinah Mushaf, while many scholars considered it Makki because its themes (monotheism, resurrection, revelation) are those of the Makki surahs. It is named "The Thunder" after ﴿وَيُسَبِّحُ ٱلرَّعۡدُ بِحَمۡدِهِۦ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ مِنۡ خِيفَتِهِۦ﴾, "The thunder glorifies Him with praise, and the angels too, in awe of Him." For the whole surah see [Surah Ar-Ra'd Meaning in English](/blog/la-yughayyiru-ma-biqawm-meaning-english/#Surah-Ar-Rad-Meaning-Thunder-Signs-and-Hearts-at-Rest).

### 1. Signs in creation (1-7)
﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي رَفَعَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ بِغَيۡرِ عَمَدٖ تَرَوۡنَهَاۖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ وَسَخَّرَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ وَٱلۡقَمَرَۖ﴾, "Allah is the one who raised the heavens without pillars that you see, then rose over the Throne and subjected the sun and the moon." Then the spread-out earth, firm mountains, rivers, fruits in pairs, and neighbouring plots watered by the same water yet differing in taste. Variety from a single water proves the will of the Creator.

### 2. Allah's knowledge and protection (8-11)
Allah knows what every female carries and what the wombs lose or exceed; it is the same to Him whether you speak secretly or openly. Then: ﴿لَهُۥ مُعَقِّبَٰتࣱ مِّنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ يَحۡفَظُونَهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ﴾, "For each one are successive angels before and behind him, guarding him by Allah's command", and the law: ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡۗ﴾, "Allah does not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves." Read [Allah Does Not Change a People's Condition: 13:11](/blog/la-yughayyiru-ma-biqawm-meaning-english/#Allah-Does-Not-Change-a-People-Until-They-Change-Themselves-).

### 3. Lightning, thunder and prostration (12-16)
Lightning brings fear and hope, the clouds are heavy, the thunder glorifies Him, and then: ﴿وَلِلَّهِۤ يَسۡجُدُۤ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ طَوۡعٗا وَكَرۡهٗا وَظِلَٰلُهُم بِٱلۡغُدُوِّ وَٱلۡأٓصَالِ﴾, "To Allah prostrate all in the heavens and the earth, willingly or unwillingly, and so do their shadows in the mornings and evenings": a place of prostration. Then the example of those who call on others besides Allah: like one who stretches out his hands to water for it to reach his mouth, and it never reaches it.

### 4. The parable of foam (17)
﴿أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَسَالَتۡ أَوۡدِيَةُۢ بِقَدَرِهَا فَٱحۡتَمَلَ ٱلسَّيۡلُ زَبَدٗا رَّابِيٗاۖ﴾, "He sends down water from the sky, and valleys flow according to their measure, and the torrent carries rising foam", and similarly the foam on metals when smelted. Then: ﴿فَأَمَّا ٱلزَّبَدُ فَيَذۡهَبُ جُفَآءٗۖ وَأَمَّا مَا يَنفَعُ ٱلنَّاسَ فَيَمۡكُثُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ﴾, "As for the foam, it vanishes as scum; but what benefits people remains on the earth."

**Lesson**: falsehood may rise like foam, but it has no value or permanence; truth is like the water that stays and benefits. Each valley takes water according to its size, as hearts take guidance according to their capacity. Read [The Foam Passes Away: 13:17](/blog/la-yughayyiru-ma-biqawm-meaning-english/#The-Foam-Passes-Away-and-What-Benefits-People-Remains-Quran-).

### 5. The qualities of people of understanding (19-24)
Hizb 26 begins: ﴿۞أَفَمَن يَعۡلَمُ أَنَّمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ ٱلۡحَقُّ كَمَنۡ هُوَ أَعۡمَىٰٓۚ﴾, "Is one who knows that what has been revealed to you from your Lord is the truth like one who is blind?" Then the qualities of people of understanding:
1. They fulfil Allah's covenant and do not break the pledge.
2. They join what Allah commanded to be joined.
3. They fear their Lord and dread a bad reckoning.
4. They are patient, seeking their Lord's face.
5. They establish prayer.
6. They spend secretly and openly.
7. They repel evil with good.

Their reward: gardens of Eden which they enter along with the righteous among their fathers, spouses and descendants, while angels enter upon them from every gate: ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكُم بِمَا صَبَرۡتُمۡۚ فَنِعۡمَ عُقۡبَى ٱلدَّارِ﴾, "Peace be upon you for what you patiently endured; how excellent is the final home."

### 6. Hearts at rest (25-31)
In contrast to people of understanding are those who break the covenant and cut what Allah commanded to be joined. Then: ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ﴾, "Those who believe and whose hearts find rest in the remembrance of Allah. Surely, in the remembrance of Allah hearts find rest." The anxiety filling people's lives has its cure in remembering Allah. Read [In the Remembrance of Allah Hearts Find Rest: 13:28](/blog/la-yughayyiru-ma-biqawm-meaning-english/#Verily-in-the-Remembrance-of-Allah-Do-Hearts-Find-Rest-Quran).

### 7. Reassurance, erasing and confirming (32-43)
Comfort for the Prophet ﷺ that messengers before him were mocked, then: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلࣰا مِّن قَبۡلِكَ وَجَعَلۡنَا لَهُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجࣰا وَذُرِّيَّةࣰۚ﴾, "We sent messengers before you and gave them wives and children": the prophets are human. Then ﴿لِكُلِّ أَجَلࣲ كِتَابࣱ﴾, "for every term is a decree", and ﴿يَمۡحُواْ ٱللَّهُ مَا يَشَآءُ وَيُثۡبِتُۖ وَعِندَهُۥٓ أُمُّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ﴾, "Allah erases what He wills and confirms, and with Him is the Mother of the Book." The surah ends with the testimony of Allah and those who have knowledge of the Book. Read [Allah Erases What He Wills: 13:39](/blog/la-yughayyiru-ma-biqawm-meaning-english/#Allah-Erases-What-He-Wills-and-Confirms-Quran-13-39-Explaine).

## Part three: Surah Ibrahim (1-52)

Surah Ibrahim is Makki, with 52 verses, and begins ﴿الٓرۚ كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡكَ لِتُخۡرِجَ ٱلنَّاسَ مِنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِمۡ﴾, "Alif Lam Ra. A Book We have revealed to you so you may bring mankind out of darkness into light by their Lord's permission." Its focus: **the messengers' mission of bringing people from darkness into light, and gratitude for blessings.** For the whole surah see [Surah Ibrahim Meaning in English](/blog/la-yughayyiru-ma-biqawm-meaning-english/#Surah-Ibrahim-Meaning-Gratitude-the-Good-Word-and-Ibrahim-s-).

### 1. Messengers in their people's language (1-8)
﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا بِلِسَانِ قَوۡمِهِۦ لِيُبَيِّنَ لَهُمۡۖ﴾, "We sent no messenger except in the language of his people, to make things clear to them." Then Musa reminds his people of the days of Allah and their rescue from Pharaoh, and the declaration: ﴿وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمۡ لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدࣱ﴾, "And when your Lord proclaimed: if you are grateful, I will surely increase you; but if you deny, My punishment is severe." Read [If You Are Grateful, I Will Increase You: 14:7](/blog/la-yughayyiru-ma-biqawm-meaning-english/#If-You-Are-Grateful-I-Will-Surely-Increase-You-Quran-14-7-Ex).

### 2. The messengers' dialogue with their peoples (9-17)
A comprehensive dialogue between the messengers and the peoples of Nuh, 'Ad, Thamud and those after them. The peoples said: you are only humans like us. The messengers said: we are only humans like you, but Allah confers favour on whom He wills, and ﴿وَمَا لَنَآ أَلَّا نَتَوَكَّلَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ وَقَدۡ هَدَىٰنَا سُبُلَنَاۚ وَلَنَصۡبِرَنَّ عَلَىٰ مَآ ءَاذَيۡتُمُونَاۚ﴾, "Why should we not rely on Allah when He has guided us to our ways? We will surely be patient against your harm." The disbelievers threatened expulsion, and Allah promised the messengers: We will destroy the wrongdoers and settle you in the land after them.

### 3. The parable of ashes and Shaytan's speech (18-23)
The deeds of disbelievers are ﴿كَرَمَادٍ ٱشۡتَدَّتۡ بِهِ ٱلرِّيحُ فِي يَوۡمٍ عَاصِفࣲۖ﴾, "like ashes blown hard by the wind on a stormy day." Then the dialogue of the weak and the arrogant in the Fire, and then Shaytan's speech: ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ لَمَّا قُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَعَدَكُمۡ وَعۡدَ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَوَعَدتُّكُمۡ فَأَخۡلَفۡتُكُمۡۖ﴾, "And Shaytan will say when the matter is decided: Allah promised you the promise of truth, and I promised you and betrayed you", followed by: I had no authority over you except that I invited you and you responded to me, so do not blame me but blame yourselves.

**Lesson**: Shaytan has only invitation and whispering; the decision is the person's own, and no one can shift responsibility for his sins to another.

### 4. The good word and the evil word (24-27)
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا كَلِمَةٗ طَيِّبَةٗ كَشَجَرَةٖ طَيِّبَةٍ أَصۡلُهَا ثَابِتࣱ وَفَرۡعُهَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ﴾, "Do you not see how Allah gives the example of a good word: like a good tree whose root is firm and whose branches are in the sky", yielding its fruit at all times. In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ asked his Companions about a tree that does not shed its leaves and is like the Muslim, then said: "It is the date palm." Then the evil word, like an evil tree uprooted from the earth with no stability. Then: ﴿يُثَبِّتُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ ٱلثَّابِتِ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۖ﴾, "Allah keeps firm those who believe with the firm word, in this life and in the Hereafter." In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, it is linked to the questioning in the grave. Read [A Good Word Is Like a Good Tree: 14:24](/blog/la-yughayyiru-ma-biqawm-meaning-english/#A-Good-Word-Is-Like-a-Good-Tree-Quran-14-24-25-Explained).

### 5. Exchanging blessings and counting them (28-34)
Those who exchanged Allah's favour for disbelief led their people into the home of ruin. Then a command to believers to pray and spend before a Day with no trade. Then a list of blessings: the heavens and earth, water and fruits, ships, rivers, sun and moon, night and day, and: ﴿وَءَاتَىٰكُم مِّن كُلِّ مَا سَأَلۡتُمُوهُۚ وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَظَلُومࣱ كَفَّارࣱ﴾, "He gave you of all you asked of Him. If you counted the blessings of Allah, you could not number them. Indeed mankind is most unjust and ungrateful."

### 6. Ibrahim's prayer (35-41)
This is the heart of the surah named after him:
- ﴿رَبِّ ٱجۡعَلۡ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدَ ءَامِنٗا وَٱجۡنُبۡنِي وَبَنِيَّ أَن نَّعۡبُدَ ٱلۡأَصۡنَامَ﴾, "My Lord, make this city secure and keep me and my sons away from worshipping idols." The leader of the monotheists feared shirk for himself.
- ﴿رَّبَّنَآ إِنِّيٓ أَسۡكَنتُ مِن ذُرِّيَّتِي بِوَادٍ غَيۡرِ ذِي زَرۡعٍ عِندَ بَيۡتِكَ ٱلۡمُحَرَّمِ رَبَّنَا لِيُقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ﴾, "Our Lord, I have settled some of my offspring in a valley without cultivation near Your Sacred House, our Lord, so that they may establish prayer", then he asked that people's hearts incline towards them; Allah answered, and hearts have yearned for Makkah ever since.
- Praise for the gift of Isma'il and Ishaq in old age.
- ﴿رَبِّ ٱجۡعَلۡنِي مُقِيمَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِيۚ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلۡ دُعَآءِ﴾, "My Lord, make me one who establishes prayer, and from my descendants. Our Lord, accept my prayer."
- ﴿رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلۡحِسَابُ﴾, "Our Lord, forgive me, my parents and the believers on the Day the reckoning is established."

**Notice the order**: security, then monotheism, then prayer, then provision, then gratitude, then forgiveness. This prayer is a school in a parent's priorities for their children.

### 7. The Day the earth is changed (42-52)
﴿وَلَا تَحۡسَبَنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَٰفِلًا عَمَّا يَعۡمَلُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَۚ إِنَّمَا يُؤَخِّرُهُمۡ لِيَوۡمٖ تَشۡخَصُ فِيهِ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ﴾, "Never think that Allah is unaware of what the wrongdoers do; He only delays them for a Day when eyes will stare in horror." Then the wrongdoers asking for a delay, then ﴿يَوۡمَ تُبَدَّلُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ غَيۡرَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُۖ﴾, "the Day the earth is changed into another earth, and the heavens too", and the close of the surah and the juz: ﴿هَٰذَا بَلَٰغࣱ لِّلنَّاسِ وَلِيُنذَرُواْ بِهِۦ وَلِيَعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا هُوَ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱ وَلِيَذَّكَّرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾, "This is a message for mankind, that they may be warned by it, know that He is one God, and that people of understanding may take heed."

**The link**: Yusuf ended with "a lesson for people of understanding", Ar-Ra'd described the qualities of people of understanding, and Ibrahim ends with "that people of understanding may take heed". The whole juz addresses living minds.

## Tajweed points in Juz 13

### ﴿الٓمٓرۚ﴾ at the start of Ar-Ra'd
Read *Alif, Laam, Meem, Ra*: Laam and Meem six counts, Ra two counts, and the meem of "Laam" merges into "Meem".

### ﴿تَاْيۡـَٔسُواْ﴾ and ﴿يَاْيۡـَٔسُ﴾
Written with an extra alif after the ta and ya that is not pronounced.

### The sajdah of Ar-Ra'd
At ﴿وَظِلَٰلُهُم بِٱلۡغُدُوِّ وَٱلۡأٓصَالِ﴾ there is the prostration sign ۩.

### The repeated ﴿أَنَا۠﴾ in Yusuf
The alif is pronounced when stopping and dropped when continuing, as in ﴿إِنِّيٓ أَنَا۠ أَخُوكَ﴾, ﴿أَنَا۠ يُوسُفُ﴾ and ﴿أَنَا۠ وَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَنِيۖ﴾.

### Qalqalah in ﴿وَٱبۡيَضَّتۡ﴾
The sakin ba takes a light echo (qalqalah sughra). For the full tajweed path see [A Roadmap for Learning Tajweed](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. "Beautiful patience" twice
- Yusuf 18: ﴿فَصَبۡرࣱ جَمِيلࣱۖ وَٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُسۡتَعَانُ عَلَىٰ مَا تَصِفُونَ﴾, seeking Allah's help at the first shock.
- Yusuf 83: ﴿فَصَبۡرࣱ جَمِيلٌۖ عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَأۡتِيَنِي بِهِمۡ جَمِيعًاۚ﴾, hope after long patience.

### 2. Counting blessings in Ibrahim and An-Nahl
- Ibrahim 34: ﴿وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَظَلُومࣱ كَفَّارࣱ﴾, describing man.
- An-Nahl 18: ﴿وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾, describing Allah.
**Key**: Ibrahim describes the human being, An-Nahl describes Allah; and in Ibrahim the word is written with an open ta (*ni'mat*).

### 3. Ibrahim's prayer in Al-Baqarah and Ibrahim
- Al-Baqarah 126: ﴿رَبِّ ٱجۡعَلۡ هَٰذَا بَلَدًا ءَامِنٗا﴾, "a city" (indefinite), before it became a city.
- Ibrahim 35: ﴿رَبِّ ٱجۡعَلۡ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدَ ءَامِنٗا﴾, "this city" (definite), after it had become one.

### 4. The rescue from Pharaoh in Al-Baqarah and Ibrahim
- Al-Baqarah 49: ﴿يُذَبِّحُونَ أَبۡنَآءَكُمۡ﴾, without *wa*, explaining the "worst torment".
- Ibrahim 6: ﴿وَيُذَبِّحُونَ أَبۡنَآءَكُمۡ﴾, with *wa*, adding to it.

## How to memorize Juz 13 with understanding

1. **Finish Yusuf as one continuous story**; the second half comes easily once you know the first.
2. **Ar-Ra'd is a surah of cosmic pictures**: imagine them as scenes: a sky without pillars, the spread earth, lightning and thunder, a torrent and its foam.
3. **Learn the seven qualities of people of understanding** in Ar-Ra'd by counting.
4. **Ibrahim has three stations**: the messengers, the parables, the prayer.
5. **Memorize Ibrahim's prayer** and make it part of your daily supplications.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 13

- **Seek responsibility if you are qualified** through trustworthiness and skill.
- **Take precautions, then rely on Allah.**
- **Hold in an insult** and do not answer it in kind.
- **Complain to Allah alone** and never despair of His relief.
- **Taqwa and patience** are the road to establishment.
- **Pardon when you have power**, and do not remind the wrongdoer of his wrong.
- **Ask for a good end** even at the height of blessing.
- **Falsehood is foam that vanishes**; do not be deceived by its rise.
- **Join what Allah commanded to be joined.**
- **Repel evil with good.**
- **Treat your anxiety with the remembrance of Allah.**
- **Be grateful for blessings so they increase.**
- **Do not blame Shaytan for your own decisions.**
- **Speak good words**; they are a fruitful tree.
- **Pray for your children's monotheism and prayer** before their provision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 13 called "Wa Ma Ubarri'u"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞وَمَآ أُبَرِّئُ نَفۡسِيٓۚ﴾.

### Is Surah Ar-Ra'd Makki or Madani?
It is Madani in the classification of the Madinah Mushaf, while many scholars considered it Makki because its themes match the Makki surahs.

### Where does Juz 13 end?
At the end of Surah Ibrahim. Juz 14 begins with Surah Al-Hijr: ﴿الٓرۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَقُرۡءَانٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾.

### What is the good tree in Surah Ibrahim?
Allah uses it as a parable for the good word; in Al-Bukhari and Muslim the Prophet ﷺ spoke of a tree like the Muslim whose leaves do not fall: the date palm.

### How many prostrations are in this juz?
One, in Surah Ar-Ra'd, verse 15.

## Conclusion

Juz 13 is the juz of beautiful outcomes: the patience of Yusuf and Ya'qub ends in reunion, establishment and pardon; truth in Ar-Ra'd remains like water while the foam vanishes; and gratitude in Surah Ibrahim brings increase. All of it is addressed to people of understanding, that they reflect, take heed and find rest in the remembrance of Allah.
