**Surah Fatir** (45 ayat) means *The Originator*: the One who brought the heavens and earth into being from nothing.

## Mercy no one can block
- *Whatever mercy Allah opens to people, none can withhold it; and whatever He withholds, none can release it after Him.*

## You need Allah
﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ أَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ﴾

## Colours of creation
| Creation | Colours |
|---|---|
| Fruits | Of varied colours |
| Mountains | White and red streaks, and intensely black |
| People, animals, livestock | Different colours |

## Knowledge and fear
﴿إِنَّمَا يَخۡشَى ٱللَّهَ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡعُلَمَـٰٓؤُاْۗ﴾
- Placed **right after** the colours of creation: knowledge of Allah's signs leads to awe of Him.

## Three kinds of inheritors
- Those given the Book: **some wrong themselves, some are moderate, some race ahead in good deeds**.

## Lessons
- **Study creation** to know the Creator.
- **Race ahead**; don't settle for the minimum.
