﴿قُل لَّن يُصِيبَنَآ إِلَّا مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَنَا هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰنَاۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:51]

## The context
- The hypocrites rejoiced when believers were hurt. Allah taught the believers this reply.

## "For us", not "against us"
- *What Allah has written for us*: scholars note the word **lana** (for us). What Allah decrees for a believer carries good, even when it hurts.

## Three statements
| Statement | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Only what Allah wrote | Nothing happens by chance |
| He is our Protector | We are not alone |
| Upon Allah let believers rely | The right reaction |

## A Prophetic counterpart
- *Know that if the whole nation gathered to benefit you, they could not except with what Allah wrote for you* (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

## Not an excuse for laziness
- Belief in decree comes **with** taking means, as the Prophet ﷺ did in every battle.

## Lessons
- **Peace comes from accepting decree** after effort.
- **Trust that what is written for you is good.**
