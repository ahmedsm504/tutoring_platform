Surah Nuh is a report from a prophet to his Lord after a lifetime of calling people. Nuh ﷺ called his people for nine hundred and fifty years, as Surah Al-'Ankabut tells us, and in this surah he describes every method he used: night and day, in public and in private, with promises and with reasoning. His people stuffed their fingers in their ears and wrapped themselves in their garments so they would not hear. The surah gives us the famous promise attached to seeking forgiveness, reveals how idol worship began, and ends with a du'a that Muslims still make every day.

This guide explains Surah Nuh section by section in English, with authentic hadith and lessons for anyone who calls others to good.

## Quick Facts About Surah Nuh
- **Number of verses**: 28.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 71, in the twenty-ninth juz.
- **Name**: named after the Prophet Nuh ﷺ; the entire surah is about him.
- **Main themes**: the methods and patience of da'wah, the fruits of seeking forgiveness, the signs of creation, and the origin of idolatry.

## Verses 1–4: The Mission
﴿إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ أَنۡ أَنذِرۡ قَوۡمَكَ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنِّي لَكُمۡ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينٌ﴾ ﴿أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱتَّقُوهُ وَأَطِيعُونِ﴾ ﴿يَغۡفِرۡ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمۡ وَيُؤَخِّرۡكُمۡ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمًّىۚ إِنَّ أَجَلَ ٱللَّهِ إِذَا جَآءَ لَا يُؤَخَّرُۚ لَوۡ كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 71:1-4)

**"Indeed, We sent Nuh to his people: Warn your people before there comes to them a painful punishment."** Nuh's message had three parts: **"Worship Allah, fear Him and obey me."** And it carried a promise: **"He will forgive you of your sins and delay you for a specified term."** If they believed, they would live out their appointed time without being destroyed. **"Indeed, the time set by Allah, when it comes, will not be delayed."**

## Verses 5–9: Every Method of Calling
﴿قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي دَعَوۡتُ قَوۡمِي لَيۡلٗا وَنَهَارٗا﴾ ﴿فَلَمۡ يَزِدۡهُمۡ دُعَآءِيٓ إِلَّا فِرَارٗا﴾ ﴿وَإِنِّي كُلَّمَا دَعَوۡتُهُمۡ لِتَغۡفِرَ لَهُمۡ جَعَلُوٓاْ أَصَٰبِعَهُمۡ فِيٓ ءَاذَانِهِمۡ وَٱسۡتَغۡشَوۡاْ ثِيَابَهُمۡ وَأَصَرُّواْ وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ ٱسۡتِكۡبَارٗا﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِنِّي دَعَوۡتُهُمۡ جِهَارٗا﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَنتُ لَهُمۡ وَأَسۡرَرۡتُ لَهُمۡ إِسۡرَارٗا﴾ (Quran 71:5-9)

**Nuh's report**:
- **"I invited my people night and day"**: at every hour.
- **"But my invitation increased them not except in flight."**
- **"Whenever I invited them... they put their fingers in their ears, covered themselves with their garments, persisted and were arrogant with great arrogance."** Four reactions of rejection: refusing to hear, refusing to see, persisting and arrogance.
- **"Then I invited them publicly. Then I announced to them and also confided to them secretly."**

**Lesson for callers**: Nuh used every approach: day and night, public and private, individual and collective. The method should change with the situation, while patience stays constant.

## Verses 10–12: The Fruits of Seeking Forgiveness
﴿فَقُلۡتُ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفَّارٗا﴾ ﴿يُرۡسِلِ ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّدۡرَارٗا﴾ ﴿وَيُمۡدِدۡكُم بِأَمۡوَٰلٖ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ أَنۡهَٰرٗا﴾ (Quran 71:10-12)

**"Ask forgiveness of your Lord. Indeed, He is ever a Perpetual Forgiver."** And the promised results:
1. **"He will send rain from the sky upon you in continuing showers."**
2. **"And give you increase in wealth and children."**
3. **"And provide for you gardens and provide for you rivers."**

**Istighfar opens doors**: these verses show that seeking forgiveness brings blessings in this world as well as the hereafter. Many scholars recommended istighfar for people facing drought, poverty or childlessness, citing these verses.

**How to make istighfar part of life**:
- After every prayer, say *astaghfirullah* three times, as the Prophet ﷺ did according to Sahih Muslim.
- Say *Sayyid al-Istighfar* morning and evening; in Sahih al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ said whoever says it with certainty during the day and dies before evening is among the people of Paradise.
- Make it frequent: in Sahih al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ said: "By Allah, I seek Allah's forgiveness and repent to Him more than seventy times a day."

## Verses 13–20: Signs in Creation
﴿مَّا لَكُمۡ لَا تَرۡجُونَ لِلَّهِ وَقَارٗا﴾ ﴿وَقَدۡ خَلَقَكُمۡ أَطۡوَارًا﴾ ﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَوۡاْ كَيۡفَ خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٖ طِبَاقٗا﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلَ ٱلۡقَمَرَ فِيهِنَّ نُورٗا وَجَعَلَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ سِرَاجٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱللَّهُ أَنۢبَتَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ نَبَاتٗا﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ يُعِيدُكُمۡ فِيهَا وَيُخۡرِجُكُمۡ إِخۡرَاجٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِسَاطٗا﴾ ﴿لِّتَسۡلُكُواْ مِنۡهَا سُبُلٗا فِجَاجٗا﴾ (Quran 71:13-20)

**"What is the matter with you that you do not attribute to Allah due grandeur, while He has created you in stages?"** You were a drop, then a clinging clot, then a lump of flesh, then a complete human. **Stages** in the womb and in life.

**Then the signs above and below**:
- **Seven heavens in layers.**
- **The moon as a light and the sun as a lamp**: the moon reflects, the sun burns.
- **"Allah has caused you to grow from the earth a growth"**: Adam was created from it, and our food comes from it. **"Then He will return you into it and extract you once again."**
- **"Allah has made for you the earth an expanse, that you may follow therein roads of passage."**

## Verses 21–24: How Idol Worship Began
﴿قَالَ نُوحࣱ رَّبِّ إِنَّهُمۡ عَصَوۡنِي وَٱتَّبَعُواْ مَن لَّمۡ يَزِدۡهُ مَالُهُۥ وَوَلَدُهُۥٓ إِلَّا خَسَارٗا﴾ ﴿وَمَكَرُواْ مَكۡرٗا كُبَّارٗا﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ لَا تَذَرُنَّ ءَالِهَتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَذَرُنَّ وَدّٗا وَلَا سُوَاعٗا وَلَا يَغُوثَ وَيَعُوقَ وَنَسۡرٗا﴾ ﴿وَقَدۡ أَضَلُّواْ كَثِيرٗاۖ وَلَا تَزِدِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا ضَلَٰلٗا﴾ (Quran 71:21-24)

**"They have disobeyed me and followed him whose wealth and children will not increase him except in loss."** They followed their rich leaders. **"And they conspired an immense conspiracy."** And they said: **"Never leave your gods, and never leave Wadd, nor Suwa', nor Yaghuth and Ya'uq and Nasr."**

**The origin of these names**: in Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas said these were the names of righteous men from the people of Nuh. When they died, Shaytan inspired their people to set up images in the places where they used to sit, and to name them after them. They did so, and the images were not worshipped. But when those people died and knowledge was forgotten, they were worshipped.

**The lesson**: idolatry did not start with the claim "these are gods". It started with excessive veneration of righteous people, with good intentions, and when knowledge faded it became worship. This is why Islam closes every door that leads to exaggerating the status of the righteous, such as building over graves or making images of them.

## Verses 25–28: The Flood and Nuh's Du'a
﴿مِّمَّا خَطِيٓـَٰٔتِهِمۡ أُغۡرِقُواْ فَأُدۡخِلُواْ نَارٗا فَلَمۡ يَجِدُواْ لَهُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَنصَارٗا﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ نُوحࣱ رَّبِّ لَا تَذَرۡ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ دَيَّارًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّكَ إِن تَذَرۡهُمۡ يُضِلُّواْ عِبَادَكَ وَلَا يَلِدُوٓاْ إِلَّا فَاجِرٗا كَفَّارٗا﴾ ﴿رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيۡتِيَ مُؤۡمِنٗا وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۖ وَلَا تَزِدِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا تَبَارَۢا﴾ (Quran 71:25-28)

**"Because of their sins they were drowned and put into the Fire."** They were drowned in this world and admitted to a Fire straight after.

**Nuh's du'a against the disbelievers**: "My Lord, do not leave upon the earth any of the disbelievers as an inhabitant. If You leave them, they will mislead Your servants and not give birth except to a wicked one and a disbeliever." Nuh said this after Allah told him: "None of your people will believe except those who have already believed" (Surah Hud). It was not impatience; it was based on knowledge from Allah after 950 years.

**Nuh's du'a for the believers**: **"My Lord, forgive me and my parents and whoever enters my house a believer and the believing men and believing women."** A du'a that begins with oneself, then parents, then household, then all believers until the end of time. You are included in the du'a of Nuh ﷺ if you are a believer.

## Word Meanings
- **Nadhir**: warner.
- **Firar**: flight, running away.
- **Istaghshaw thiyabahum**: covered themselves with their garments.
- **Jiharan**: publicly.
- **Israran**: secretly.
- **Ghaffar**: Perpetual Forgiver.
- **Midrar**: abundant, continuous rain.
- **Waqar**: grandeur, reverence.
- **Atwar**: stages.
- **Tibaq**: in layers.
- **Siraj**: lamp.
- **Bisat**: expanse, spread.
- **Kubbar**: immense.
- **Dayyar**: inhabitant.
- **Tabar**: destruction.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The surah as a report**: Nuh speaking to his Lord gives it an intimate tone.
2. **"Night and day... publicly... secretly"**: a full map of da'wah methods.
3. **Fingers in ears and garments over heads**: a vivid image of stubbornness.
4. **The moon a light, the sun a lamp**: precise and different descriptions.
5. **"Grow from the earth a growth"**: man compared to a plant.
6. **The final du'a**: widening in circles from self to all believers.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Izhar**: in *nuhan ila* (verse 1), the tanween before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *laylan wa naharan* (verse 5), the tanween merges into the waw.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *midrara* (verse 11), the dal sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'a* (verse 11).
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *waddan wa la* (verse 23).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Ma'arij before it commanded "beautiful patience", and Surah Nuh shows it lived out over 950 years. Surah Al-Jinn after it offers a striking contrast: Nuh's people did not believe after centuries, while a group of jinn believed the first time they heard the Quran.

## Lessons for Anyone Who Calls Others to Good
1. **Patience is the main tool**: results may take years, or never come.
2. **Vary your methods**: public and private, gentle and direct.
3. **Use hope**: Nuh promised rain, wealth and children.
4. **Use reasoning**: he pointed to the heavens, the earth and their own creation.
5. **Report to Allah**: Nuh's complaint was to his Lord, not to people.
6. **Pray for the believers**: end your efforts with du'a for others.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Seek forgiveness often**: it brings blessings in this world and the next.
2. **Reflect on your own stages of creation.**
3. **Beware of exaggerating the status of righteous people.**
4. **Do not follow people just because they are rich or powerful.**
5. **Make Nuh's du'a for your parents and all believers.**
6. **Be patient in calling your family and friends to good.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How long did Nuh call his people?
Nine hundred and fifty years, as mentioned in Surah Al-'Ankabut.

### What are the benefits of seeking forgiveness in Surah Nuh?
Abundant rain, increase in wealth and children, and gardens and rivers, as promised in verses 10–12.

### Who were Wadd, Suwa', Yaghuth, Ya'uq and Nasr?
Righteous men from the people of Nuh whose images were set up after their death and later worshipped, as Ibn 'Abbas explained in Sahih al-Bukhari.

### Why did Nuh pray against the disbelievers?
Because Allah had told him that none of his people would believe except those who already had, after 950 years of calling.

### What is the du'a of Nuh for his parents?
"My Lord, forgive me and my parents and whoever enters my house a believer and the believing men and believing women."

### What does "created you in stages" mean?
Allah created man in stages in the womb, and through the stages of life.

### How many verses are in Surah Nuh?
28 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

### What does "the moon as a light and the sun as a lamp" mean?
The moon gives a reflected light, while the sun burns and shines like a lamp, a precise description of the difference between them.

## Conclusion
Surah Nuh is a lesson in patience from the most patient of callers. He tried every method for 950 years, offered hope through istighfar, reasoned with his people through creation, and warned them of the road that leads from honouring the righteous to idolatry. Seek forgiveness often, be patient with those you call, and include yourself in Nuh's du'a: "My Lord, forgive me and my parents and the believing men and women."
