**Surah Al-Anfal** (The Spoils, 75 ayat) was revealed about the Battle of Badr.

## Who are the true believers?
﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَجِلَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَإِذَا تُلِيَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ زَادَتۡهُمۡ إِيمَٰنٗا وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ﴾
- Then: they establish prayer and spend from what Allah has given them.

## The Battle of Badr
| Moment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The believers wanted the caravan | Allah wanted the truth established |
| The Prophet ﷺ prayed for help | Allah sent angels in succession |
| Drowsiness came over the believers | A sense of security before battle |
| Rain fell | Purification and firm footing |
| A handful of dust thrown | *You did not throw when you threw, but Allah threw* |

## Unity and steadfastness
- *Obey Allah and His Messenger and do not dispute, lest you lose courage and your strength departs, and be patient.*

## Allah's protection of His Prophet
- The surah recalls the plot in Makkah to imprison, kill or expel the Prophet ﷺ, and Allah's better planning.

## Lessons
- **Victory is from Allah**, not numbers.
- **Disputes destroy strength**; unity preserves it.
