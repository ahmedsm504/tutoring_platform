**Surah Ya-Sin** (83 ayat) is one of the most loved surahs. It focuses on **messengership** and **resurrection**.

﴿يسٓ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾

## Themes
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–12 | The Quran and the Prophet's message |
| 13–32 | The town, the messengers, and the man who came running |
| 33–44 | Signs: the dead earth revived, night and day, sun and moon, ships |
| 45–54 | The Hour and the blowing of the Trumpet |
| 55–68 | The people of Paradise and the people of the Fire |
| 69–83 | The Prophet is not a poet; the proof of resurrection |

## The man who came running
﴿وَجَآءَ مِنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ رَجُلࣱ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ [Ya-Sin: 20]
- He supported the messengers and was killed, yet wished his people knew how Allah honoured him.

## The closing proof
﴿إِنَّمَآ أَمۡرُهُۥٓ إِذَآ أَرَادَ شَيۡـًٔا أَن يَقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ﴾ [Ya-Sin: 82]

## An honest note on virtues
- The hadith **"Everything has a heart, and the heart of the Quran is Ya-Sin"** is graded **weak** by many hadith scholars.
- The hadith **"Recite Ya-Sin over your dying"** is also debated, and many scholars graded it weak.
- Ya-Sin is still a great surah, part of the Quran with all its virtues.
