Surah Al-Baqarah is the longest surah in the Quran: 286 verses, filling more than two and a half juz. A child might think it's a difficult surah far from their world, but it is actually full of stories they love: our father Adam, whom Allah taught the names of everything, so he knew what even the angels did not. A group of people commanded to slaughter a cow who kept asking: What colour? How old? Until they made it hard for themselves. And Ibrahim and his son Isma'il building the Kaaba stone by stone while making du'a.

Surah Al-Baqarah also contains the greatest verse in the Quran: Ayat al-Kursi, which children memorize and recite before sleeping. And at its end are two verses that the Prophet ﷺ said are enough for whoever recites them at night. He also said: "Do not turn your homes into graveyards. Shaytan flees from the house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited" (Muslim). In this guide, we explain Surah Al-Baqarah to children in simple English, choosing its most beautiful lessons, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Baqarah

- **Position**: surah 2, from juz 1 to the middle of juz 3.
- **Number of verses**: 286, the longest surah.
- **Type**: Madinan, among the first revealed in Madinah.
- **Name**: al-Baqarah means "The Cow", after the story of the cow, which appears only in this surah.

### Its virtues
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Recite Surah Al-Baqarah, for taking it is a blessing, leaving it is a regret, and the sorcerers cannot withstand it" (Muslim).

## Lesson 1: Adam and the Names

﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي جَاعِلࣱ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ خَلِيفَةٗۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَتَجۡعَلُ فِيهَا مَن يُفۡسِدُ فِيهَا وَيَسۡفِكُ ٱلدِّمَآءَ وَنَحۡنُ نُسَبِّحُ بِحَمۡدِكَ وَنُقَدِّسُ لَكَۖ قَالَ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:30)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah told the angels: I am placing on earth a khalifah, a human being who will live on it and look after it. The angels asked: Will You place someone who will cause harm, while we praise You? Allah said: I know what you do not know."

﴿وَعَلَّمَ ءَادَمَ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءَ كُلَّهَا ثُمَّ عَرَضَهُمۡ عَلَى ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ فَقَالَ أَنۢبِـُٔونِي بِأَسۡمَآءِ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ لَا عِلۡمَ لَنَآ إِلَّا مَا عَلَّمۡتَنَآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿قَالَ يَـٰٓـَٔادَمُ أَنۢبِئۡهُم بِأَسۡمَآئِهِمۡۖ فَلَمَّآ أَنۢبَأَهُم بِأَسۡمَآئِهِمۡ قَالَ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكُمۡ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ غَيۡبَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَأَعۡلَمُ مَا تُبۡدُونَ وَمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡتُمُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:31-33)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah taught Adam the names of all things: trees, mountains, water and animals. Then He showed these things to the angels and asked: What are their names? The angels said politely: Glory be to You, we only know what You have taught us. Allah told Adam: Tell them their names. And Adam told them every name! The angels understood that Allah had given human beings knowledge."

### Lesson for kids: the value of knowledge
"Allah honoured Adam with knowledge. Every time you learn something new, you become closer to what Allah loves. And look at the angels' manners: we only know what You taught us. If you don't know an answer, say: I don't know. Don't make one up."

### Activity: the names game
Put ten objects from around the house on a tray and have your child name each one. Then say: "Allah taught Adam the names of everything in the world! We learned these names from our families, and all knowledge comes from Allah."

## Lesson 2: The Story of the Cow

﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُكُمۡ أَن تَذۡبَحُواْ بَقَرَةٗۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَتَتَّخِذُنَا هُزُوٗاۖ قَالَ أَعُوذُ بِٱللَّهِ أَنۡ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:67)

### Explaining it to your child
"A man among the Children of Israel was killed, and nobody knew who did it. Allah commanded them, through Musa: Slaughter a cow. It was easy: any cow! But they said: Are you making fun of us? Then they started asking: How old? Middle-aged. What colour? Bright yellow, pleasing to look at. Which one exactly? A cow not used for ploughing or watering, healthy and without any marks."

﴿قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ يَقُولُ إِنَّهَا بَقَرَةࣱ لَّا ذَلُولࣱ تُثِيرُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَا تَسۡقِي ٱلۡحَرۡثَ مُسَلَّمَةࣱ لَّا شِيَةَ فِيهَاۚ قَالُواْ ٱلۡـَٰٔنَ جِئۡتَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ فَذَبَحُوهَا وَمَا كَادُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:71)

### Explaining it to your child
"The more they asked, the harder it got! They found the cow with great difficulty and slaughtered it after nearly not doing it at all. Then Allah told them to strike the dead man with part of it, and Allah brought him back to life, and he told them who killed him."

### Lesson for kids: obey quickly, don't complicate
"If they had slaughtered any cow the first time, it would have been over. Too many unnecessary questions made it hard. If Mum asks you to tidy your room, don't say: Why? When? Which corner first? Just start!"

### Activity: the simple request
Ask your child: "Bring me a glass of water." Then tell them: "If you asked: Which glass? What colour? How full? Cold or warm? The water would take forever! That's what the Children of Israel did."

## Lesson 3: Speak Good to People

﴿وَإِذۡ أَخَذۡنَا مِيثَٰقَ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ لَا تَعۡبُدُونَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَ وَبِٱلۡوَٰلِدَيۡنِ إِحۡسَانٗا وَذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَقُولُواْ لِلنَّاسِ حُسۡنٗا وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ ثُمَّ تَوَلَّيۡتُمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا مِّنكُمۡ وَأَنتُم مُّعۡرِضُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:83)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah took a promise from the Children of Israel, and from us too: worship only Allah, be good to your parents, relatives, orphans and the poor, speak good words to people, establish prayer and give zakah."

### Activity: kind words day
Choose a "kind words day": your child says something nice to everyone they meet: "Thank you", "JazakAllahu khayran", "You're amazing", "Good morning". In the evening they tell you what happened.

## Lesson 4: Ibrahim and Isma'il Build the Kaaba

﴿وَإِذۡ يَرۡفَعُ إِبۡرَٰهِـۧمُ ٱلۡقَوَاعِدَ مِنَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلُ رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلۡ مِنَّآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ ﴿رَبَّنَا وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا مُسۡلِمَيۡنِ لَكَ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِنَآ أُمَّةٗ مُّسۡلِمَةٗ لَّكَ وَأَرِنَا مَنَاسِكَنَا وَتُبۡ عَلَيۡنَآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 2:127-128)

### Explaining it to your child
"Ibrahim and his son Isma'il built the Kaaba in Makkah with their own hands. Isma'il brought the stones and Ibrahim built. As the walls got higher, Ibrahim stood on a stone to reach the top, the Station of Ibrahim that we still see next to the Kaaba. And as they worked, they prayed: Our Lord, accept this from us, You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing. Our Lord, make us Muslims who submit to You, and from our children a Muslim nation."

### Lesson for kids: du'a with good work
"Ibrahim built the greatest house on earth, and still he prayed: Our Lord, accept it from us. He didn't say: Look what I built! He asked Allah to accept it. When you do something good, say: O Allah, accept it from me."

### Activity: build a little Kaaba
Build a small model of the Kaaba with blocks or cardboard boxes. Your child hands you the blocks like Isma'il, and you build. With every block, say together: "Rabbana taqabbal minna, innaka antas-Sami'ul-'Alim."

## Lesson 5: Remember Me, I Will Remember You

﴿فَٱذۡكُرُونِيٓ أَذۡكُرۡكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لِي وَلَا تَكۡفُرُونِ﴾ (Quran 2:152)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah says: Remember Me and I will remember you. Imagine! You remember Allah in your small room, and Allah, the Greatest, remembers you. In a hadith qudsi, Allah says: If he remembers Me in himself, I remember him in Myself, and if he remembers Me in a gathering, I remember him in a better gathering (al-Bukhari and Muslim)."

### Activity: after-prayer dhikr
Teach your child the dhikr after prayer: SubhanAllah 33 times, Alhamdulillah 33 times, Allahu Akbar 33 times, and to complete a hundred: La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamdu wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir (Muslim). They can count on their fingers.

## Lesson 6: Patience and Prayer

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱسۡتَعِينُواْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:153)

### Explaining it to your child
"When you face a problem, sadness or fear, ask Allah for help through two things: patience and prayer. Allah is with those who are patient, meaning He helps them and loves them."

### Activity: two rak'ahs when worried
Teach your child: when they feel sad, scared or stressed, they can make wudu, pray two rak'ahs and ask Allah for what they need. Afterwards, they can tell you how they felt.

## Lesson 7: Fasting

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:183)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah made fasting in Ramadan an obligation, as He did for the nations before us, so that we may have taqwa, becoming closer to Allah and further from wrong. A fasting person stops eating and drinking from Fajr to Maghrib, and also guards their tongue and eyes."

### Activity: little-bird fasting
Young children can try "little-bird fasting": from Fajr until Dhuhr, then eat, then gradually extend it to Asr and then Maghrib. Celebrate every step with them.

## Lesson 8: Allah Is Near

﴿وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌۖ أُجِيبُ دَعۡوَةَ ٱلدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِۖ فَلۡيَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لِي وَلۡيُؤۡمِنُواْ بِي لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡشُدُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:186)

### Explaining it to your child
"If someone asks you: Where is Allah? Is He far? Allah says: I am near. I answer the call of the one who calls on Me. Allah is close to you and hears you even if you whisper. So call on Him any time, anywhere."

## Lesson 9: Ayat al-Kursi

﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُۚ لَا تَأۡخُذُهُۥ سِنَةࣱ وَلَا نَوۡمࣱۚ لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يَشۡفَعُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِهِۦۚ يَعۡلَمُ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيۡءٖ مِّنۡ عِلۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَۚ وَسِعَ كُرۡسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۖ وَلَا يَـُٔودُهُۥ حِفۡظُهُمَاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ (Quran 2:255)

### Explaining it to your child
"This is the greatest verse in the Quran, as the Prophet ﷺ told us (Muslim). It describes Allah's greatness: Allah, there is no god but Him, the Ever-Living who never dies, the Sustainer of everything. He never gets drowsy or sleeps. Everything in the heavens and earth belongs to Him. No one can intercede with Him except by His permission. He knows what was and what will be. His Kursi is wider than the heavens and earth, and guarding them never tires Him. He is the Most High, the Most Great."

### From the Sunnah
Al-Bukhari reported that whoever recites Ayat al-Kursi when going to bed will have a protector from Allah with them, and no shaytan will come near them until morning.

### Activity: the night guard
Make Ayat al-Kursi your child's "night guard": they recite it every night before sleeping. Tell them: "You sleep, but Allah never sleeps, and He protects you all night."

## Lesson 10: One Seed Becomes Seven Hundred

﴿مَّثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ حَبَّةٍ أَنۢبَتَتۡ سَبۡعَ سَنَابِلَ فِي كُلِّ سُنۢبُلَةٖ مِّاْئَةُ حَبَّةٖۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُضَٰعِفُ لِمَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 2:261)

### Explaining it to your child
"Whoever gives charity for Allah is like someone who plants one seed of wheat, and it grows seven ears, with a hundred seeds in each ear! That one seed became seven hundred seeds! And Allah multiplies even more for whoever He wills."

### Activity: counting the ears
Ask your child: "Seven ears, a hundred seeds in each, how many seeds?" Let them work it out: 700! Then say: "If you give one coin in charity, Allah gives you the reward of seven hundred and more!" If you can, bring a real ear of wheat and count the grains together.

## Let's Read Together: The Last Two Verses

﴿ءَامَنَ ٱلرَّسُولُ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِ مِن رَّبِّهِۦ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَۚ كُلٌّ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتِهِۦ وَكُتُبِهِۦ وَرُسُلِهِۦ لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيۡنَ أَحَدٖ مِّن رُّسُلِهِۦۚ وَقَالُواْ سَمِعۡنَا وَأَطَعۡنَاۖ غُفۡرَانَكَ رَبَّنَا وَإِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ ﴿لَا يُكَلِّفُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتۡ وَعَلَيۡهَا مَا ٱكۡتَسَبَتۡۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذۡنَآ إِن نَّسِينَآ أَوۡ أَخۡطَأۡنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحۡمِلۡ عَلَيۡنَآ إِصۡرٗا كَمَا حَمَلۡتَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلۡنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِۦۖ وَٱعۡفُ عَنَّا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَآۚ أَنتَ مَوۡلَىٰنَا فَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:285-286)

### Explaining it to your child
"The last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah: the Messenger and the believers believed in Allah, His angels, His books and His messengers, and said: We hear and we obey. Allah never burdens anyone with more than they can bear. Then a beautiful du'a: Our Lord, don't take us to account if we forget or make a mistake. Our Lord, don't place on us more than we can carry. Pardon us, forgive us, and have mercy on us."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah at night, they will be enough for him" (al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lesson for kids
"Allah never asks you to do what you can't. If fasting is too hard because you're young, Allah understands. And if you forget, say: Rabbana la tu'akhidhna in nasina."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Seven Ears Money Box"

Khalid had saved twenty pounds from his pocket money to buy a new toy. On the way to the shop with his mother, they saw an old man selling tissues on the pavement in the cold, wearing thin clothes.

Khalid stopped and looked at the money in his hand, then at the man. He remembered what he had read with his mother yesterday: the seed that grew seven ears with a hundred seeds in each. He asked: "Mum, if I give him ten pounds, will Allah give me seven hundred?" His mother smiled: "The reward with Allah is bigger than we can count in pounds, but yes, Allah multiplies charity."

Khalid gave the man ten pounds, and the man prayed for him: "May Allah bless you and protect you, son." Khalid felt a big happiness, bigger than a toy could give him.

At home, Khalid made a new money box and wrote on it: "The Seven Ears Box", with a drawing of an ear of wheat. He decided to put part of his pocket money in it every week for charity. A month later, he bought the toy too, with what was left, and said: "Charity didn't make my money less. It made it blessed."

Ask your child: "What did Khalid do with his money? Which verse did he remember?"

## What We Learn From Surah Al-Baqarah

- **Knowledge** is an honour Allah gave Adam.
- **Obey quickly**, without overcomplicating.
- **Speak kind words** to everyone.
- **Ask Allah to accept** every good deed.
- **Remember Allah**, and He remembers you.
- **Patience and prayer** help in hard times.
- **Allah is near** and answers du'a.
- **Ayat al-Kursi** protects us at night.
- **Charity multiplies**.
- **Allah never burdens us** beyond what we can bear.

## Two-Week Memorization Plan

- **Days 1–2**: the first half of Ayat al-Kursi.
- **Days 3–4**: the whole of Ayat al-Kursi.
- **Day 5**: verses 152 and 153.
- **Day 6**: verse 186.
- **Days 7–9**: verse 285.
- **Days 10–12**: verse 286.
- **Days 13–14**: recite everything, use them before sleeping, and celebrate!

## Questions to Ask Your Child

1. What did Allah teach Adam?
2. What did the angels say when Allah asked them about the names?
3. Why did the matter of the cow become hard?
4. Who built the Kaaba, and what did they say?
5. What happens when we remember Allah?
6. Why do we recite Ayat al-Kursi before sleeping?
7. How many seeds grow from the one seed in the charity example?

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Al-Baqarah is very long. Should my child memorize all of it?
Start with selected verses: Ayat al-Kursi, the last two verses, and other short, beloved verses. Memorizing the whole surah can come later, after Juz 'Amma, Tabarak and Qad Sami', with a teacher to keep revision on track.

### Is playing Surah Al-Baqarah at home enough?
The hadith mentions the surah being "recited" in the house. Reciting is best, and listening also brings goodness and blessing. A family could read one page of it together each day.

### How do I explain the murdered man in the story of the cow?
Keep it simple: "A serious problem happened and they didn't know who did wrong, so Allah showed them a miracle that revealed the truth." Focus on the lesson of quick obedience.
