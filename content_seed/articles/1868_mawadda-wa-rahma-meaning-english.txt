﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦٓ أَنۡ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا لِّتَسۡكُنُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَكُم مَّوَدَّةٗ وَرَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ [Ar-Rum 30:21]

## Marriage is a sign
- The verse lists marriage among Allah's signs, alongside the creation of the heavens and the earth.

## Three gifts
| Gift | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *From yourselves* | The same human nature, so they understand each other |
| *Sakan* (tranquility) | The home is a place of rest |
| *Mawaddah and rahmah* | Affection and mercy |

## Affection and mercy
- Some scholars explain: **mawaddah** is love that shows in actions, strongest in youth; **rahmah** is compassion that keeps a marriage going in illness, age and hardship.
- A marriage needs both: love to enjoy, mercy to endure.

## "He placed"
- Love between spouses is **from Allah**, so ask Him for it and protect it from what destroys it.

## Reflect
- *Signs for people who reflect*: two strangers become the closest people to each other.

## Lessons
- **Make your home a place of peace.**
- **Show mercy** when love feels tired.
