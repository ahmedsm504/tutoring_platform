﴿حَٰفِظُواْ عَلَى ٱلصَّلَوَٰتِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِ ٱلۡوُسۡطَىٰ وَقُومُواْ لِلَّهِ قَٰنِتِينَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:238]

## Guarding prayer
- Praying on time, with its conditions and pillars, and with humility.

## The middle prayer
- Mentioned separately for its virtue. It is **ʿAsr** according to the sound view: on the day of the Trench the Prophet ﷺ said, *They kept us from the middle prayer, the ʿAsr prayer* (narrated by Muslim).

## *Qanitin*
- Here it means silence and humility. Zayd ibn Arqam said people used to talk in prayer until this verse came and they were commanded to be silent (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Among the divorce verses
- The verse sits in the middle of rulings on divorce and waiting periods: prayer protects the heart from injustice during family conflict.

## Lessons
- **Guard ʿAsr** especially at work.
- **Turn to prayer** in family crises.
