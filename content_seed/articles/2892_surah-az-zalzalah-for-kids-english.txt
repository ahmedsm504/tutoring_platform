Imagine a tiny speck of dust floating in a sunbeam, so small you can barely see it. Now imagine a good deed as small as that speck: a smile, picking up a piece of litter, saying "thank you", or sharing a crayon. Does it matter? Surah Az-Zalzalah gives an amazing answer: "Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it." Nothing good is ever too small for Allah.

Surah Az-Zalzalah describes the Day of Judgement, when the earth will shake and tell everything that happened on it. Every step, every action, every word: the earth was watching and will tell. This can sound big and serious, but for children, the most important message is wonderful: every little good thing you do is seen and counted. In this guide for parents, we explain Surah Az-Zalzalah to children in simple, gentle English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Az-Zalzalah

- **Position**: surah 99, in Juz 'Amma.
- **Number of verses**: eight.
- **Type**: Madinan according to many scholars.
- **Name**: az-Zalzalah means "the shaking" or "the earthquake". It is also called Surat az-Zilzal.

## The Whole Surah

﴿إِذَا زُلۡزِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زِلۡزَالَهَا﴾ ﴿وَأَخۡرَجَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَثۡقَالَهَا﴾ ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا لَهَا﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَئِذٖ تُحَدِّثُ أَخۡبَارَهَا﴾ ﴿بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوۡحَىٰ لَهَا﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَصۡدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشۡتَاتٗا لِّيُرَوۡاْ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ﴾ ﴿فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيۡرٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾ ﴿وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖ شَرّٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾ (Quran 99:1-8)

## A Gentle Approach for Children

This surah talks about the Day of Judgement, which can feel scary for young or sensitive children. A helpful approach:
- **Start with the ending**: the good news about atoms of good.
- **Keep descriptions calm**: focus on meanings rather than dramatic images.
- **Emphasize Allah's mercy**: He rewards good generously and forgives those who turn back to Him.
- **Answer questions honestly** but simply.

## Verses 1–2: The Earth Shakes and Brings Out Its Burdens

### Explaining it to your child
"On the Day of Judgement, the earth will shake in a way it never has before. Then it will bring out what is inside it: everything buried, and all the people who were buried, coming back to life. It's the beginning of a new day, the Day when everyone meets Allah."

### Lesson for kids
"Allah, who made the earth, can make it do anything. The same earth that holds us up today will one day show everything it carries."

## Verse 3: People Will Ask, "What Is Happening to It?"

### Explaining it to your child
"People will be amazed and ask: What's happening to the earth? It's like when something surprising happens and everyone looks around asking questions."

## Verses 4–5: The Earth Tells Its News

### Explaining it to your child
"On that day, the earth will tell its news, because Allah inspires it to speak. The earth will say what people did on it: this person prayed here, this person helped someone here, this person told the truth here."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ recited this verse and asked: "Do you know what its news is?" They said: "Allah and His Messenger know best." He said: "Its news is that it will testify about every servant, man or woman, about what they did on its back, saying: He did such-and-such on such-and-such a day" (at-Tirmidhi, who graded it hasan sahih).

### Lesson for kids: the earth is a witness
"The floor of your room, the playground at school, the pavement on your street: the earth is everywhere we go. One day it will speak about our good deeds. So let's give it lots of good news to tell!"

### Activity: good news for the earth
Each evening, ask your child: "What good news will the earth tell about you today?" Maybe: "I prayed on this carpet", "I helped a friend in the playground", "I picked up litter in the park". Write a few in a notebook called "The Earth's Good News".

## Verse 6: People Come Out in Groups to Be Shown Their Deeds

### Explaining it to your child
"On that day, people will come out in groups to see their deeds. Everyone will see everything they did, the good and the not-so-good. That's why we try to do lots of good now, and say sorry to Allah for our mistakes."

## Verses 7–8: An Atom's Weight

### Explaining it to your child
"Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of bad will see it. A dharrah is something incredibly tiny: some scholars explained it as a tiny ant or a speck of dust. So even the smallest good deed is counted, and we should be careful even with small mistakes."

### Lesson for kids: no good deed is too small
"Smiling at someone, saying 'Bismillah', helping your sibling tie their shoe, giving your seat to someone: these are 'atoms' of good, and Allah sees every single one!"

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not belittle any good deed, even meeting your brother with a cheerful face" (Muslim). And he said: "Protect yourselves from the Fire, even with half a date in charity" (al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Activity: the atom challenge
Challenge your child to do ten "tiny" good deeds in one day:
1. Smile at someone.
2. Say "thank you" to a parent.
3. Put away one thing without being asked.
4. Give salam to someone.
5. Say "SubhanAllah" three times.
6. Hold the door for someone.
7. Pick up one piece of litter.
8. Share a snack.
9. Say something kind about a friend.
10. Make du'a for someone.
Count them together at bedtime!

### Activity: the speck of dust
On a sunny day, close the curtains so a thin beam of sunlight comes through, and look at the tiny specks of dust floating in it. Say: "Allah counts good deeds as small as these specks. Isn't that amazing?"

## Honesty When No One Is Watching

Because the earth sees everything, this surah helps children understand honesty even when no adult is around.

### Explaining it to your child
"If you're alone and you find money that isn't yours, or you could cheat on a game and nobody would know, remember: the earth is watching, and Allah sees everything. Doing the right thing when no one is looking is one of the most beautiful good deeds."

## The Surah of Balance

The last two verses are often called among the most comprehensive verses in the Quran, because they cover every deed, good and bad, big and small.

### Explaining it to your child
"These two verses are like a perfect scale: nothing is too small to weigh. That's fair and kind: Allah never forgets our good, and He wants us to fix our mistakes."

## Good Deeds That Keep Growing

Some good deeds are like seeds: they keep growing even after we've done them. The Prophet ﷺ said: "When a person dies, their deeds stop except for three: ongoing charity, knowledge that benefits others, or a righteous child who prays for them" (Muslim).

### Explaining it to your child
"Some atoms of good grow into mountains of good! If you teach your little brother a surah, every time he recites it, you get reward too. If you plant a tree, every bird that rests on it and every person who sits in its shade adds to your good deeds."

### Activity: plant a good deed
Plant a small tree or a pot of herbs with your child, and tell them: "Every time someone enjoys this plant, it's good news the earth will tell about you." Then teach a younger sibling or cousin a short du'a or surah, and explain that this is "knowledge that benefits", which keeps adding to their good deeds.

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Lost Coin"

During break time at school, Adam found a shiny two-pound coin on the playground. Nobody was around. He picked it up and thought: "I could buy a snack with this! No one will ever know."

Then he remembered what his teacher had read that morning: Surah Az-Zalzalah. "Yawma'idhin tuhaddithu akhbaraha", on that day, the earth will tell its news. Adam looked down at the playground. "The earth saw me pick this up," he thought.

He walked to the office and gave the coin to the school secretary. "I found this in the playground," he said. Later that afternoon, a little girl from Year 1 came to the office crying because she had lost her money for the school trip. The secretary gave her the coin, and she smiled through her tears.

At home, Adam told his mother. She hugged him and said: "Do you know what you did today? An atom of good, actually much more than an atom! And the earth has good news to tell about you." Adam grinned. "Faman ya'mal mithqala dharratin khayran yarah," he recited. "Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it!"

Ask your child: "What did Adam find? Why did he give it back? How did it help someone?"

## What We Learn From Surah Az-Zalzalah

- **The Day of Judgement** is real, and the earth will testify.
- **The earth tells** what people did on it.
- **Everyone will see** their deeds.
- **An atom's weight of good** is never wasted.
- **Small good deeds** matter a lot.
- **Be honest** even when no one is watching.
- **Allah is perfectly fair** and generous.

## One-Week Plan: Memorize and Practise

- **Day 1**: memorize verses 1–3.
- **Day 2**: memorize verses 4–5 and start "The Earth's Good News" notebook.
- **Day 3**: memorize verse 6.
- **Day 4**: memorize verses 7–8.
- **Day 5**: recite the whole surah and do the atom challenge.
- **Day 6**: look at the specks of dust in a sunbeam.
- **Day 7**: recite Az-Zalzalah in prayer and celebrate!

## Questions to Ask Your Child

1. What will happen to the earth on the Day of Judgement?
2. What will the earth tell?
3. What did the Prophet ﷺ say the earth's news is?
4. What is a "dharrah"?
5. Will a tiny good deed be counted?
6. Name three tiny good deeds you can do today.
7. Why should we be honest even when no one is watching?

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### My child is scared of earthquakes. Should I avoid this surah?
You can teach it with care. Explain that the surah is about the special Day of Judgement, not ordinary earthquakes, and focus on the reassuring message about good deeds. If an earthquake has affected your family, give extra comfort and take it slowly.

### How do I explain the Day of Judgement without frightening my child?
Emphasize Allah's mercy and fairness: He counts every good deed, multiplies rewards, and forgives those who say sorry. Children feel safe when they know Allah is kind and just.

### Does "an atom's weight of bad" mean my child will be punished for small mistakes?
Children are not held accountable before reaching maturity, and Allah forgives those who turn back to Him. Teach your child that saying sorry to Allah and fixing mistakes wipes them away.
