﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ تَبَوَّءُو ٱلدَّارَ وَٱلۡإِيمَٰنَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ يُحِبُّونَ مَنۡ هَاجَرَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا يَجِدُونَ فِي صُدُورِهِمۡ حَاجَةٗ مِّمَّآ أُوتُواْ وَيُؤۡثِرُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَلَوۡ كَانَ بِهِمۡ خَصَاصَةࣱۚ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفۡسِهِۦ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ [Al-Hashr 59:9]

## The Ansar's qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| They love those who emigrated | Genuine welcome |
| No envy for what others received | Clean hearts |
| They prefer others over themselves | Ithar |
| Even when in need | Sacrifice, not surplus |

## The guest story
- A man from the Ansar hosted a guest of the Prophet ﷺ with only enough food for his children. He and his wife put the children to sleep, dimmed the lamp, and pretended to eat so the guest could eat. Allah praised them, and this verse is mentioned with the story (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Protection from greed
- *Whoever is protected from the stinginess of his soul, those are the successful.*

## Lessons
- **Give when it costs you.**
- **Clean your heart** from envy.
