## "Allah Does Not Change a People Until They Change Themselves": Quran 13:11 Explained

﴿لَهُۥ مُعَقِّبَٰتࣱ مِّنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ يَحۡفَظُونَهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡۗ وَإِذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ سُوٓءٗا فَلَا مَرَدَّ لَهُۥۚ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَالٍ﴾ [Ar-Rad 13:11]

### Guarded by angels
- Every person has angels in succession, before and behind, **guarding him by Allah's command** until what is decreed arrives.

### The law of change
- *Allah does not change the condition of a people until they change what is within themselves.*
- Scholars explain it in both directions: a people living in blessing do not lose it until they change through sin, and a people in hardship can move to better when they reform themselves.

### Compare with Al-Anfal 8:53
- A similar verse says Allah would not change a favour He gave a people **until they change what is in themselves**.

### Where change begins
| Level | Example |
|---|---|
| Heart | Sincerity, repentance, trust |
| Habits | Prayer on time, daily Quran |
| Family | Kindness, Islamic upbringing |
| Society | Justice, honesty, cooperation |

### Lessons
- **Do not wait for circumstances to change first.**
- **Small sincere steps** are the start of Allah's change.

## Surah Ar-Rad Meaning: Thunder, Signs and Hearts at Rest

**Surah Ar-Rad** (The Thunder, 43 ayat) invites reflection on the signs of creation.

### Signs above and below
- Heavens raised without pillars you can see, the sun and moon running for a term, the earth spread with mountains and rivers.
- Neighbouring plots of land watered with **the same water**, yet producing different fruits.

### Guarded and accountable
- Each person has angels in succession before and behind him, guarding him by Allah's command.

### Change starts within
﴿لَهُۥ مُعَقِّبَٰتࣱ مِّنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ يَحۡفَظُونَهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡۗ وَإِذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ سُوٓءٗا فَلَا مَرَدَّ لَهُۥۚ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَالٍ﴾

### The thunder glorifies Him
- *The thunder glorifies Him with praise, and the angels too, out of awe of Him.*

### Hearts at rest
﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ﴾

### The people of understanding
- They fulfil their covenant, join what Allah ordered to be joined, are patient, pray, spend secretly and openly, and **repel evil with good**.

### Lessons
- **Reform yourself first**: Allah's change follows inner change.
- **Remembrance is the remedy for anxiety.**

## "The Foam Passes Away, and What Benefits People Remains": Quran 13:17 Explained

﴿أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَسَالَتۡ أَوۡدِيَةُۢ بِقَدَرِهَا فَٱحۡتَمَلَ ٱلسَّيۡلُ زَبَدٗا رَّابِيٗاۖ وَمِمَّا يُوقِدُونَ عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلنَّارِ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ حِلۡيَةٍ أَوۡ مَتَٰعٖ زَبَدࣱ مِّثۡلُهُۥۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَٱلۡبَٰطِلَۚ فَأَمَّا ٱلزَّبَدُ فَيَذۡهَبُ جُفَآءٗۖ وَأَمَّا مَا يَنفَعُ ٱلنَّاسَ فَيَمۡكُثُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ﴾ [Ar-Rad 13:17]

### Two parables
| Parable | Truth | Falsehood |
|---|---|---|
| The flood | Useful water | Foam on the surface |
| Smelted metals | Gold, silver, iron | Dross that floats |

### "Valleys according to their capacity"
- Each valley takes water by its size, and so do **hearts**: they take from revelation according to their readiness.

### "Swelling foam"
- Foam rises high, as falsehood sometimes rises and grows loud.

### "It passes away"
- It is thrown off and disappears without benefit.

### "What benefits people remains"
- Beneficial knowledge, good deeds and truth endure.

### Lessons
- **Do not be fooled** by the rise of falsehood; it is foam.
- **Widen the valley of your heart** for good.

## "Verily, in the Remembrance of Allah Do Hearts Find Rest": Quran 13:28 Explained

﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَطۡمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ أَلَا بِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطۡمَئِنُّ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ﴾ [Ar-Rad 13:28]

### The structure
- *Ala* means "indeed, take note". Then the phrase *bi dhikrillah* comes **first**, a style that indicates restriction: hearts find rest **only** in remembering Allah.

### Why only in Allah?
- The heart was created to know its Creator. Wealth, people and achievements can please it for a while, but they change and end. Allah is the One who never changes.

### What is dhikr?
| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| The Quran | Scholars note the Quran is the greatest dhikr |
| Tasbih and tahmid | *SubhanAllah, alhamdulillah* |
| Duʿa | Talking to Allah about your worries |
| Remembering His names | Ar-Rahman, al-Wakil, al-Latif |
| Remembering Him in decisions | Acting in a way that pleases Him |

### Peace and anxiety
- Dhikr does not mean problems disappear, but the heart **stops shaking** because it knows Who is in control.

### A simple routine
- Morning and evening adhkar, a page of Quran daily, and *hasbunallah wa niʿmal wakil* in moments of fear.

### Lessons
- **Return to dhikr when anxious.**
- **Feed the heart** as you feed the body.

## "Allah Erases What He Wills and Confirms": Quran 13:39 Explained

﴿يَمۡحُواْ ٱللَّهُ مَا يَشَآءُ وَيُثۡبِتُۖ وَعِندَهُۥٓ أُمُّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ﴾ [Ar-Rad 13:39]

### Erasing and confirming
- Scholars give several explanations: erasing rulings and confirming others; erasing sins through repentance; or what is in the records of the angels.

### *Umm al-Kitab*
- The Mother of the Book: explained as the **Preserved Tablet**, which does not change.

### Two levels
| Level | Nature |
|---|---|
| Records with the angels | Can change by Allah's will |
| The Preserved Tablet | Fixed, containing the final outcome |

### Duʿa and good deeds
- The Prophet ﷺ said: *Whoever wishes his provision to be expanded and his life extended, let him keep ties of kinship* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).
- Scholars explain that such causes are themselves part of Allah's decree.

### Lessons
- **Make duʿa** and do good: they are causes Allah set.
- **Trust Allah's knowledge** of the final outcome.

## "If You Are Grateful, I Will Surely Increase You": Quran 14:7 Explained

﴿وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمۡ لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدࣱ﴾ [Ibrahim 14:7]

### A public announcement
- *Taʾadhdhana* means Allah **declared and proclaimed** it, a law, not a possibility.

### The promise
- The verse uses strong emphasis: *I will surely increase you*. Increase in what? It is left open: wealth, health, faith, peace and more.

### The warning
- Notice it does not say "I will punish you", but *My punishment is severe*. Scholars note the gentle phrasing even in warning.

### Three pillars of gratitude
| Pillar | Practice |
|---|---|
| Heart | Recognising that the blessing is from Allah |
| Tongue | Saying *alhamdulillah* and speaking of His favours |
| Limbs | Using the blessing in obedience, not sin |

### Context
- Musa reminded his people of their rescue from Pharaoh and conveyed this law to them.

### Lessons
- **Count three blessings daily.**
- **Use blessings for good**: that is real gratitude.

## Surah Ibrahim Meaning: Gratitude, the Good Word and Ibrahim's Prayers

**Surah Ibrahim** (52 ayat) speaks of bringing people out of darkness into light.

### Messengers in their own language
- Every messenger was sent in the language of his people, **to make the message clear** to them.

### The law of gratitude
﴿وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمۡ لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدࣱ﴾

### The good word and the bad word
| Word | Parable |
|---|---|
| The good word (*la ilaha illa Allah*, as many scholars explain) | A good tree: firm roots, branches in the sky, fruit in every season |
| The bad word | A tree uprooted from the earth, with no stability |

### Satan's speech
- On the Day of Judgment Satan will say: *I had no authority over you except that I invited you and you responded. So do not blame me; blame yourselves.*

### Ibrahim's prayers
- He prayed for Makkah to be a place of security, to be kept away from idol worship, for hearts to incline toward his family settled in a barren valley, and for himself and his descendants to establish prayer.
- *Our Lord, forgive me, my parents and the believers on the Day the account is established.*

### Lessons
- **Gratitude multiplies blessings.**
- **Responsibility is personal**: no one can be blamed for our choices.

## "A Good Word Is Like a Good Tree": Quran 14:24-25 Explained

﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا كَلِمَةٗ طَيِّبَةٗ كَشَجَرَةٖ طَيِّبَةٍ أَصۡلُهَا ثَابِتࣱ وَفَرۡعُهَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ﴾ ﴿تُؤۡتِيٓ أُكُلَهَا كُلَّ حِينِۭ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهَاۗ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ لِلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ [Ibrahim 14:24-25]

### The good word
- Ibn ʿAbbas and others: the testimony *la ilaha illa Allah*.

### The palm tree
- *Among trees is a tree whose leaves do not fall, and it is like the Muslim. Tell me what it is.* Ibn ʿUmar thought it was the palm, then the Prophet ﷺ said: *It is the palm tree* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Qualities of the tree
| Quality | In the believer |
|---|---|
| Firm roots | Faith deep in the heart |
| Branches in the sky | Good deeds rising to Allah |
| Fruit in every season | Constant benefit |

### The contrast
- *And the example of a bad word is like a bad tree, uprooted from the surface of the earth, with no stability* (14:26).

### Lessons
- **Strengthen the roots of faith** with knowledge and certainty.
- **Be beneficial** in every season, like the palm.

## "If You Count Allah's Blessings, You Cannot Enumerate Them": Quran 14:34 and 16:18 Explained

﴿وَءَاتَىٰكُم مِّن كُلِّ مَا سَأَلۡتُمُوهُۚ وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَظَلُومࣱ كَفَّارࣱ﴾ [Ibrahim 14:34]
﴿وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:18]

### One sentence, two endings
| Surah | Ending | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Ibrahim | *Man is unjust and ungrateful* | Human failure to thank |
| An-Nahl | *Allah is Forgiving, Merciful* | Allah's response to that failure |

- Scholars note the beauty: the first ending describes **the receiver**, the second describes **the Giver**.

### "Blessing" in the singular
- In Surah Ibrahim the word is written *niʿmat* with an open taʾ, and in An-Nahl *niʿmah*; in both it is singular, yet you cannot count it: even **one** blessing, like sight, contains countless blessings within it.

### Practising gratitude
- Name three blessings daily, use them in obedience, and say *alhamdulillah* often.

### Lessons
- **Admit you can never thank enough.**
- **Rely on His forgiveness** for your shortfall.

## "We Have Sent Down the Reminder and We Will Preserve It": Quran 15:9 Explained

﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ﴾ [Al-Hijr 15:9]

### Strong emphasis
| Feature | Effect |
|---|---|
| *Inna* (twice) | Certainty |
| *Nahnu* | "We Ourselves" |
| *Lahu* placed before | Preservation **specifically** for it |
| *La-hafizun* | The added "la" for extra emphasis |

### Who preserves?
- Earlier scriptures were entrusted to their people: *of what they were entrusted to preserve of the Book of Allah* (Al-Maidah 5:44). The Quran is preserved **by Allah Himself**.

### How it was preserved
- **In hearts**: millions of memorisers across generations.
- **In writing**: recorded during the Prophet's life, compiled under Abu Bakr, and copies sent out under ʿUthman.
- **In recitation**: chains of transmission (*isnad*) back to the Prophet ﷺ.

### The response of a disbeliever
- The verse answers those who mocked the Prophet ﷺ and called the Quran madness. Their words faded; the Quran remains.

### Lessons
- **Trust the Mushaf you hold.**
- **Be among the preservers**: memorise even a little.

## Surah Al-Hijr Meaning: The Promise to Preserve the Quran

**Surah Al-Hijr** (99 ayat) contains one of the most important promises in the Quran.

### The preservation of the Quran
﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ﴾
- Allah Himself guaranteed its preservation, which is why it remains unchanged in every letter.

### Adam and Iblis
- Iblis refused to prostrate, claiming he was better because he was made from fire.
- He vowed to mislead people, but was told he has **no authority over Allah's sincere servants**.

### Mercy and punishment
﴿۞نَبِّئۡ عِبَادِيٓ أَنِّيٓ أَنَا ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾
- The next verse adds that His punishment is the painful punishment, so hope and fear stay balanced.

### Stories
| Story | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Ibrahim's guests | Good news of a son in old age |
| The people of Lut | Destruction after persistent corruption |
| The people of al-Hijr | Homes carved in mountains could not protect them |

### The Seven Mathani
- *We have given you seven of the oft-repeated verses and the great Quran*: understood as al-Fatihah.

### Worship until certainty
- The surah ends: *And worship your Lord until there comes to you the certainty*, meaning death.

### Lessons
- **Trust the Quran you hold**: it is preserved by Allah.
- **Balance hope and fear.**

## "We Will Remove Whatever Rancour Is in Their Hearts": Quran 15:45-48 Explained

﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ﴾ ﴿ٱدۡخُلُوهَا بِسَلَٰمٍ ءَامِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَنَزَعۡنَا مَا فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنۡ غِلٍّ إِخۡوَٰنًا عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ﴾ ﴿لَا يَمَسُّهُمۡ فِيهَا نَصَبࣱ وَمَا هُم مِّنۡهَا بِمُخۡرَجِينَ﴾ [Al-Hijr 15:45-48]

### Complete bliss
| Blessing | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gardens and springs | Bliss of the body |
| In peace, secure | Bliss of safety |
| Rancour removed | Bliss of the heart |
| Facing one another | Bliss of companionship |
| No fatigue | Rest without effort |
| Never expelled | Eternity without fear of loss |

### Rancour removed
- Whatever disagreements existed among believers in this world, Allah removes them, and they enter as brothers.

### Facing each other
- No one turns his back on his brother: a sign of love and closeness.

### Work for it now
- A clean heart is a quality of the people of Paradise, as in the duʿa: *and place no rancour in our hearts toward those who believe* (Al-Hashr 59:10).

### Lessons
- **Clean your heart** of resentment today.
- **Reconcile** with your brothers.

## "So Overlook with Gracious Forgiveness": Quran 15:85 Explained

﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّۗ وَإِنَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ لَأٓتِيَةࣱۖ فَٱصۡفَحِ ٱلصَّفۡحَ ٱلۡجَمِيلَ﴾ [Al-Hijr 15:85]

### Pardon versus overlooking
| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *ʿAfw* (pardon) | Dropping punishment |
| *Safh* (overlooking) | Dropping punishment and blame, turning the page |

### "Gracious"
- Scholars describe *safh jamil* as overlooking **without reproach** or reminding.

### Why mention truth and the Hour?
- Creation has a purpose and the Hour is coming: Allah will judge. So do not exhaust yourself with revenge.

### Three beautiful acts
| Act | Verse |
|---|---|
| Beautiful patience | Yusuf 12:18 |
| Beautiful overlooking | This verse |
| Beautiful distancing | Al-Muzzammil 73:10 |

### Lessons
- **Forgive without reminding.**
- **Remember the Hereafter** to make forgiving easier.

## "Worship Your Lord Until Certainty Comes to You": Quran 15:97-99 Explained

﴿وَلَقَدۡ نَعۡلَمُ أَنَّكَ يَضِيقُ صَدۡرُكَ بِمَا يَقُولُونَ﴾ ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَكُن مِّنَ ٱلسَّـٰجِدِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱعۡبُدۡ رَبَّكَ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَكَ ٱلۡيَقِينُ﴾ [Al-Hijr 15:97-99]

### Allah knows your pain
- *We know that your chest is tightened by what they say.* Allah acknowledges the Prophet's distress at people's mockery.

### The remedy
| Remedy | Practice |
|---|---|
| Glorify with praise | *SubhanAllahi wa bihamdih* |
| Be among those who prostrate | Prayer and long sujud |
| Worship continuously | Never stop |

### What is *al-yaqin*?
- The Companions and scholars explained it as **death**: worship has no retirement. Al-Bukhari reported this explanation from Salim ibn ʿAbd Allah.

### No end date
- Some claim that after reaching a certain level, worship is no longer required. This verse refutes that: even the Prophet ﷺ worshipped until death.

### Lessons
- **When hurt by words, turn to prayer.**
- **Keep going** until the last breath.
