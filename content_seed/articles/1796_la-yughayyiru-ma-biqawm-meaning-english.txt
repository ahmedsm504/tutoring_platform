﴿لَهُۥ مُعَقِّبَٰتࣱ مِّنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ يَحۡفَظُونَهُۥ مِنۡ أَمۡرِ ٱللَّهِۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡۗ وَإِذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِقَوۡمٖ سُوٓءٗا فَلَا مَرَدَّ لَهُۥۚ وَمَا لَهُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَالٍ﴾ [Ar-Rad 13:11]

## Guarded by angels
- Every person has angels in succession, before and behind, **guarding him by Allah's command** until what is decreed arrives.

## The law of change
- *Allah does not change the condition of a people until they change what is within themselves.*
- Scholars explain it in both directions: a people living in blessing do not lose it until they change through sin, and a people in hardship can move to better when they reform themselves.

## Compare with Al-Anfal 8:53
- A similar verse says Allah would not change a favour He gave a people **until they change what is in themselves**.

## Where change begins
| Level | Example |
|---|---|
| Heart | Sincerity, repentance, trust |
| Habits | Prayer on time, daily Quran |
| Family | Kindness, Islamic upbringing |
| Society | Justice, honesty, cooperation |

## Lessons
- **Do not wait for circumstances to change first.**
- **Small sincere steps** are the start of Allah's change.
