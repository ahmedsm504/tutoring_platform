A **meem sakinah** is a meem with a sukūn (no vowel), like the meem in ﴿عَلَيۡهِمۡ﴾. What you do with it depends on the letter that comes right after it. There are only **three rules**, and they are called *shafawī* (labial) because meem is pronounced with the lips.

## 1. Ikhfa Shafawi (labial hiding)
- **When:** meem sakinah followed by **baa (ب)**.
- **How:** close the lips lightly, without pressing, and hold a **ghunnah of two counts**.
- **Example:** ﴿تَرۡمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٖ مِّن سِجِّيلٖ﴾ [Al-Fil: 4].

## 2. Idgham Shafawi (labial merging)
- **When:** meem sakinah followed by **another meem (م)**.
- **How:** merge the two meems into one stressed meem with a **ghunnah of two counts**.
- **Example:** ﴿وَلَكُم مَّا كَسَبۡتُمۡۖ﴾ [Al-Baqarah: 134].
- It is also called *idgham mithlayn ṣaghīr* (merging two identical letters).

## 3. Izhar Shafawi (labial clarity)
- **When:** meem sakinah followed by **any other letter** (26 letters).
- **How:** pronounce the meem clearly, with no extra nasal sound.
- **Examples:** ﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ﴾, ﴿عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ﴾ [Al-Fatihah: 7].
- **Be extra careful before waw (و) and faa (ف):** these letters are close to meem in the mouth, so students often hide the meem by mistake.

## Summary table
| Next letter | Rule | Ghunnah |
|---|---|---|
| ب | Ikhfa Shafawi | 2 counts |
| م | Idgham Shafawi | 2 counts |
| Any other letter | Izhar Shafawi | None |

## Common mistakes
1. **Pressing the lips hard** in ikhfa shafawi, which turns it into a full meem.
2. **Hiding the meem before waw or faa**, as in ﴿عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا﴾.
3. **Dropping the ghunnah** in ﴿وَلَكُم مَّا﴾.
