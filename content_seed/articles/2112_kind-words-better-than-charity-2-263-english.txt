﴿۞قَوۡلࣱ مَّعۡرُوفࣱ وَمَغۡفِرَةٌ خَيۡرࣱ مِّن صَدَقَةٖ يَتۡبَعُهَآ أَذٗىۗ وَٱللَّهُ غَنِيٌّ حَلِيمࣱ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:263]

## A kind word is charity
| Kind word | Forgiveness |
|---|---|
| A gentle reply and duʿa for the asker | Overlooking his insistence or rudeness |

## Cancelling charity
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُبۡطِلُواْ صَدَقَٰتِكُم بِٱلۡمَنِّ وَٱلۡأَذَىٰ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:264]
| Flaw | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Mann* | Reminding the receiver of your favour |
| *Adha* | Humiliating or exposing him |

## The smooth rock
- A smooth rock with a little soil, hit by heavy rain, left bare. So is the charity of the one who shows off or reminds: **nothing remains**.

## "Self-Sufficient, Forbearing"
- Allah needs no charity mixed with harm, and He does not hasten punishment.

## Lessons
- **Give quietly**; never remind anyone.
- **If you cannot give**, say a kind word.
