Surah Ash-Shu'ara' is built like a series of portraits. One after another, seven prophets come before their peoples with almost the same words: "Indeed, I am to you a trustworthy messenger, so fear Allah and obey me. And I do not ask you for it any payment." One after another, their peoples reject them. And after every story comes the same refrain: "Indeed in that is a sign, but most of them were not to be believers. And indeed, your Lord, He is the Almighty, the Merciful." Almighty, because He destroyed the deniers; Merciful, because He saved the believers and kept sending messengers. The surah opens with the Prophet ﷺ grieving over people who would not believe, and it contains some of the most quoted lines of faith in the Quran, including Musa's "No! Indeed, with me is my Lord; He will guide me", and Ibrahim's description of his Lord.

This guide explains Surah Ash-Shu'ara' section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ash-Shu'ara'
- **Number of verses**: 227.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 26, in the nineteenth juz.
- **Name**: Ash-Shu'ara' means "the poets", from verse 224.
- **Main themes**: comfort for the Prophet ﷺ, the unity of the prophets' message, Allah's might and mercy, and the truth of the Quran.

## Verses 1–9: Do Not Destroy Yourself With Grief
﴿طسٓمٓ﴾ ﴿تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ﴾ ﴿لَعَلَّكَ بَٰخِعࣱ نَّفۡسَكَ أَلَّا يَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِن نَّشَأۡ نُنَزِّلۡ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ ءَايَةٗ فَظَلَّتۡ أَعۡنَٰقُهُمۡ لَهَا خَٰضِعِينَ﴾ (Quran 26:1-4)

**"Ta Sin Mim. These are the verses of the clear Book. Perhaps you would kill yourself with grief that they will not be believers."** The Prophet ﷺ cared so deeply that their rejection hurt him. Allah comforts him: guidance is His.

﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَمۡ أَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجٖ كَرِيمٍ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 26:7-9)

**"Did they not look at the earth, how much We have produced therein from every noble kind?"** Then the first refrain: **"Indeed in that is a sign, but most of them were not to be believers. And indeed, your Lord, He is the Almighty, the Merciful."**

## Verses 10–68: Musa and Pharaoh
﴿وَإِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبُّكَ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنِ ٱئۡتِ ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿قَوۡمَ فِرۡعَوۡنَۚ أَلَا يَتَّقُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ أَن يُكَذِّبُونِ﴾ ﴿وَيَضِيقُ صَدۡرِي وَلَا يَنطَلِقُ لِسَانِي فَأَرۡسِلۡ إِلَىٰ هَٰرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَهُمۡ عَلَيَّ ذَنۢبࣱ فَأَخَافُ أَن يَقۡتُلُونِ﴾ ﴿قَالَ كَلَّاۖ فَٱذۡهَبَا بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآۖ إِنَّا مَعَكُم مُّسۡتَمِعُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأۡتِيَا فِرۡعَوۡنَ فَقُولَآ إِنَّا رَسُولُ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿أَنۡ أَرۡسِلۡ مَعَنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ﴾ (Quran 26:10-17)

Musa was sent to Pharaoh and feared being called a liar and his tongue not being fluent, so he asked that Harun be sent with him. **"Allah said: No. Go both of you with Our signs; indeed, We are with you, listening."**

﴿قَالَ أَلَمۡ نُرَبِّكَ فِينَا وَلِيدٗا وَلَبِثۡتَ فِينَا مِنۡ عُمُرِكَ سِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَفَعَلۡتَ فَعۡلَتَكَ ٱلَّتِي فَعَلۡتَ وَأَنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ فَعَلۡتُهَآ إِذٗا وَأَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ﴾ ﴿فَفَرَرۡتُ مِنكُمۡ لَمَّا خِفۡتُكُمۡ فَوَهَبَ لِي رَبِّي حُكۡمٗا وَجَعَلَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَتِلۡكَ نِعۡمَةࣱ تَمُنُّهَا عَلَيَّ أَنۡ عَبَّدتَّ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ﴾ (Quran 26:18-22)

Pharaoh reminded Musa of his upbringing: **"Did we not raise you among us as a child?"** Musa answered that the "favour" of raising him was rooted in Pharaoh's enslavement of the Children of Israel.

﴿فَأُلۡقِيَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ سَٰجِدِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿رَبِّ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ ءَامَنتُمۡ لَهُۥ قَبۡلَ أَنۡ ءَاذَنَ لَكُمۡۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكَبِيرُكُمُ ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَكُمُ ٱلسِّحۡرَ فَلَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَۚ لَأُقَطِّعَنَّ أَيۡدِيَكُمۡ وَأَرۡجُلَكُم مِّنۡ خِلَٰفٖ وَلَأُصَلِّبَنَّكُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ لَا ضَيۡرَۖ إِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا مُنقَلِبُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا نَطۡمَعُ أَن يَغۡفِرَ لَنَا رَبُّنَا خَطَٰيَٰنَآ أَن كُنَّآ أَوَّلَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 26:46-51)

**The magicians fell in prostration**: **"We believe in the Lord of the worlds, the Lord of Musa and Harun."** Pharaoh threatened to cut off their hands and feet and crucify them. They replied: **"No harm. Indeed, to our Lord we will return."** In one moment they moved from serving Pharaoh to willing martyrdom.

﴿فَلَمَّا تَرَـٰٓءَا ٱلۡجَمۡعَانِ قَالَ أَصۡحَٰبُ مُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّا لَمُدۡرَكُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ كَلَّآۖ إِنَّ مَعِيَ رَبِّي سَيَهۡدِينِ﴾ ﴿فَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنِ ٱضۡرِب بِّعَصَاكَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَۖ فَٱنفَلَقَ فَكَانَ كُلُّ فِرۡقٖ كَٱلطَّوۡدِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَأَزۡلَفۡنَا ثَمَّ ٱلۡأٓخَرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَأَنجَيۡنَا مُوسَىٰ وَمَن مَّعَهُۥٓ أَجۡمَعِينَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ أَغۡرَقۡنَا ٱلۡأٓخَرِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 26:61-68)

**At the sea**: with the sea before them and Pharaoh's army behind, the companions of Musa said: **"Indeed, we are to be overtaken!"** Musa said: **"No! Indeed, with me is my Lord; He will guide me."** Then the sea split, and every part was like a great mountain. Musa and those with him were saved, and the others drowned.

**"Indeed, with me is my Lord"**: the response of certainty when every visible way is closed.

## Verses 69–104: Ibrahim
﴿قَالَ أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡبُدُونَ﴾ ﴿أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُمُ ٱلۡأَقۡدَمُونَ﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّهُمۡ عَدُوࣱّ لِّيٓ إِلَّا رَبَّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَنِي فَهُوَ يَهۡدِينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي هُوَ يُطۡعِمُنِي وَيَسۡقِينِ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا مَرِضۡتُ فَهُوَ يَشۡفِينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِي يُمِيتُنِي ثُمَّ يُحۡيِينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡمَعُ أَن يَغۡفِرَ لِي خَطِيٓـَٔتِي يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ (Quran 26:75-82)

Ibrahim asked his people what they worshipped and declared his disassociation, **except the Lord of the worlds**, whom he described:
- **"Who created me, and He guides me."**
- **"And it is He who feeds me and gives me drink."**
- **"And when I am ill, it is He who cures me."** Notice the courtesy: he attributes illness to himself and healing to Allah.
- **"And who will cause me to die and then bring me to life."**
- **"And who I aspire that He will forgive me my sin on the Day of Recompense."**

﴿رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي حُكۡمٗا وَأَلۡحِقۡنِي بِٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱجۡعَل لِّي لِسَانَ صِدۡقٖ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱجۡعَلۡنِي مِن وَرَثَةِ جَنَّةِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَٱغۡفِرۡ لِأَبِيٓ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَا تُخۡزِنِي يَوۡمَ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ لَا يَنفَعُ مَالࣱ وَلَا بَنُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَنۡ أَتَى ٱللَّهَ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 26:83-89)

**Ibrahim's du'a**: **"My Lord, grant me authority and join me with the righteous. And grant me a mention of honour among later generations. And place me among the inheritors of the Garden of Pleasure."** And: **"Do not disgrace me on the Day they are resurrected, the Day when there will not benefit anyone wealth or children, but only one who comes to Allah with a sound heart."**

**The sound heart** (*qalb salim*): a heart free from shirk, doubt, hatred and attachment to anything that displeases Allah.

## Verses 105–191: Nuh, Hud, Salih, Lut and Shu'ayb
Each prophet began with the same words:
﴿إِنِّي لَكُمۡ رَسُولٌ أَمِينࣱ﴾ ﴿فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرٍۖ إِنۡ أَجۡرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 26:107-109)

- **Nuh**: his people said: "Should we believe you while you are followed by the lowest people?" He said he would not drive away the believers.
- **Hud and 'Ad**: **"Do you construct on every elevation a sign, amusing yourselves, and take for yourselves palaces that you might abide eternally?"** A warning against building for show and imagining permanence.
- **Salih and Thamud**: carving homes in the mountains, the she-camel and its share of water.
- **Lut**: their abandonment of what Allah created for them in their wives.
- **Shu'ayb and the people of the thicket**: **"Give full measure and do not be of those who cause loss. And weigh with an even balance. And do not deprive people of their due."**

﴿۞أَوۡفُواْ ٱلۡكَيۡلَ وَلَا تَكُونُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡمُخۡسِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَزِنُواْ بِٱلۡقِسۡطَاسِ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَلَا تَبۡخَسُواْ ٱلنَّاسَ أَشۡيَآءَهُمۡ وَلَا تَعۡثَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مُفۡسِدِينَ﴾ (Quran 26:181-183)

Every story ends with destruction for the deniers, salvation for the believers, and the refrain.

## Verses 192–212: The Trustworthy Spirit
﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَنزِيلُ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿نَزَلَ بِهِ ٱلرُّوحُ ٱلۡأَمِينُ﴾ ﴿عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِكَ لِتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنذِرِينَ﴾ ﴿بِلِسَانٍ عَرَبِيّٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 26:192-195)

**"And indeed, it is the revelation of the Lord of the worlds. The Trustworthy Spirit has brought it down upon your heart, that you may be of the warners, in a clear Arabic language."** Jibril brought it to the Prophet's ﷺ heart, not only his ears.

﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَفِي زُبُرِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ ﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُمۡ ءَايَةً أَن يَعۡلَمَهُۥ عُلَمَـٰٓؤُاْ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ﴾ (Quran 26:196-197)

It is mentioned in the scriptures of former peoples, and the scholars of the Children of Israel recognised it.

## Verses 213–220: Warn Your Nearest Kin
﴿وَأَنذِرۡ عَشِيرَتَكَ ٱلۡأَقۡرَبِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱخۡفِضۡ جَنَاحَكَ لِمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿فَإِنۡ عَصَوۡكَ فَقُلۡ إِنِّي بَرِيٓءࣱ مِّمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 26:214-216)

**"And warn your closest kindred."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, when this verse was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ climbed as-Safa and called the clans of Quraysh. When they gathered, he said: "If I told you that horsemen were in the valley about to attack you, would you believe me?" They said: "We have never known you to lie." He said: "Then I am a warner to you before a severe punishment." And he said to his own family, including his daughter Fatimah, that he could not avail them anything before Allah.

**"And lower your wing to those who follow you of the believers."** Gentleness and humility with the believers.

﴿وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي يَرَىٰكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ﴾ ﴿وَتَقَلُّبَكَ فِي ٱلسَّـٰجِدِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ (Quran 26:217-220)

**"And rely upon the Almighty, the Merciful, who sees you when you arise, and your movement among those who prostrate."** Allah sees you when you stand alone at night and when you move among the rows of worshippers.

## Verses 221–227: The Poets
﴿هَلۡ أُنَبِّئُكُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَن تَنَزَّلُ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينُ﴾ ﴿تَنَزَّلُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ أَفَّاكٍ أَثِيمٖ﴾ ﴿يُلۡقُونَ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمۡ كَٰذِبُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلشُّعَرَآءُ يَتَّبِعُهُمُ ٱلۡغَاوُۥنَ﴾ ﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّهُمۡ فِي كُلِّ وَادٖ يَهِيمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَأَنَّهُمۡ يَقُولُونَ مَا لَا يَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 26:221-226)

The devils descend upon every sinful liar. **"And the poets, the deviators follow them. Do you not see that in every valley they roam, and that they say what they do not do?"** Poetry that exaggerates, lies and leads astray is condemned.

﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَذَكَرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا وَٱنتَصَرُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا ظُلِمُواْۗ وَسَيَعۡلَمُ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓاْ أَيَّ مُنقَلَبٖ يَنقَلِبُونَ﴾ (Quran 26:227)

**The exception**: **"Except those who believe and do righteous deeds and remember Allah often and defend themselves after they were wronged."** Poetry used for truth is praised. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said to Hassan ibn Thabit: "Answer them, and Jibril is with you."

**"And those who have wronged are going to know to what kind of return they will be returned."**

## Word Meanings
- **Bakhi'**: one who destroys himself with grief.
- **Zawj karim**: noble kind.
- **La dayr**: no harm.
- **Mudrakun**: to be overtaken.
- **Sayahdin**: He will guide me.
- **Lisan sidq**: a mention of honour.
- **Qalb salim**: a sound heart.
- **Ri'**: elevation, high place.
- **Masani'**: palaces, fortresses.
- **Al-Qistas al-mustaqim**: an even balance.
- **Tabkhasu**: deprive.
- **Ar-Ruh al-Amin**: the Trustworthy Spirit, Jibril.
- **'Ashirah**: kindred.
- **Ikhfid janahaka**: lower your wing.
- **Al-Ghawun**: the deviators.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The repeated refrain**: "Almighty, the Merciful" after each story.
2. **The repeated opening of each prophet**: one message across history.
3. **"Indeed, with me is my Lord"**: certainty at the edge of the sea.
4. **"When I am ill, He cures me"**: courtesy towards Allah in speech.
5. **"In every valley they roam"**: an image of aimless poetry.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Ta Sin Mim*, the sin and meem stretched six counts, with the noon of *sin* merged into the meem.
- **Izhar**: in *rasulun amin* (verse 107), the tanween before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna rabbaka* (verse 9), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *al-aqrabin* (verse 214), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ashya'ahum* (verse 183).

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Furqan before it ended with "you have denied, so your denial is going to be adherent", and Ash-Shu'ara' shows nations whose denial clung to them. Surah An-Naml after it continues with Musa, Sulayman, Salih and Lut.

## Lessons for Callers to Allah
1. **Care, but do not destroy yourself with grief.**
2. **The message is one**: tawhid, taqwa and obedience.
3. **Ask for no payment.**
4. **Begin with your family.**
5. **Be gentle with the believers.**
6. **Rely on Allah**, who sees you.
7. **Use words and art for the truth.**

## Practical Lessons
1. **Say "With me is my Lord; He will guide me"** when every door seems closed.
2. **Describe Allah as Ibrahim did**, and attribute good to Him.
3. **Work for a sound heart.**
4. **Do not build for show.**
5. **Give full measure in every transaction.**
6. **Remember that Allah sees you in prayer.**

## How to Memorise Surah Ash-Shu'ara'
- **Week 1**: verses 1–68 (Musa).
- **Week 2**: verses 69–122 (Ibrahim and Nuh).
- **Week 3**: verses 123–191 (Hud to Shu'ayb).
- **Week 4**: verses 192–227.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them the story of Musa at the sea** and teach "Indeed, with me is my Lord".
- **Teach them Ibrahim's description of Allah** in verses 78–82.
- **Let them count the refrains** as you read.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is the surah called Ash-Shu'ara'?
Because verses 224–227 discuss the poets.

### What is the refrain in Surah Ash-Shu'ara'?
"Indeed in that is a sign, but most of them were not to be believers. And indeed, your Lord, He is the Almighty, the Merciful."

### What did Musa say at the sea?
"No! Indeed, with me is my Lord; He will guide me."

### What is a "sound heart"?
A heart free from shirk, doubt and hatred, the only thing that benefits on the Day of Judgement besides Allah's mercy.

### What did the Prophet ﷺ do when told to warn his nearest kin?
He climbed as-Safa, gathered the clans of Quraysh and warned them, as reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Does Islam condemn all poetry?
No. Verse 227 excepts poets who believe, do good, remember Allah and defend the truth.

### How many verses are in Surah Ash-Shu'ara'?
227 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Ash-Shu'ara' shows the same message carried by seven prophets across history, and the same outcome every time: the deniers destroyed and the believers saved, for your Lord is the Almighty, the Merciful. Take Musa's certainty, Ibrahim's sound heart and the Prophet's ﷺ gentleness with the believers, and rely on the One who sees you when you stand alone at night.
