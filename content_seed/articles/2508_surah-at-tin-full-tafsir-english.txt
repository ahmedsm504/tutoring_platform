Surah At-Tin is eight verses that summarise the whole story of the human being. Allah created man in the best form and the most perfect make-up, and then, through his own choices, he either rises or falls to "the lowest of the low". What makes the difference is faith and righteous deeds. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, al-Bara' ibn 'Azib reported that the Prophet ﷺ recited this surah in the 'Isha prayer while travelling, and said: "I have never heard anyone with a more beautiful voice or recitation than him."

This guide explains the surah verse by verse in English and draws out its lessons on human dignity.

## Quick Facts About Surah At-Tin
- **Number of verses**: 8.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 95, in the thirtieth juz.
- **Name**: from its opening oath by *at-tin*, the fig.

## The Full Text of Surah At-Tin
﴿وَٱلتِّينِ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونِ﴾ ﴿وَطُورِ سِينِينَ﴾ ﴿وَهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ ٱلۡأَمِينِ﴾ ﴿لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِيٓ أَحۡسَنِ تَقۡوِيمٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ رَدَدۡنَٰهُ أَسۡفَلَ سَٰفِلِينَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونٖ﴾ ﴿فَمَا يُكَذِّبُكَ بَعۡدُ بِٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَحۡكَمِ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 95:1-8)

**Meaning:** By the fig and the olive, and Mount Sinai, and this secure city, We have certainly created man in the best form. Then We reduced him to the lowest of the low, except those who believe and do righteous deeds; they will have an unending reward. So what makes you deny the Recompense after this? Is Allah not the most just of judges?

## Verses 1–3: The Four Oaths
﴿وَٱلتِّينِ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونِ﴾ ﴿وَطُورِ سِينِينَ﴾ ﴿وَهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ ٱلۡأَمِينِ﴾ (Quran 95:1-3)

Allah swears by four things, and the commentators explain them as follows:
1. **The fig and the olive**: either the well-known fruits, for their blessing and benefit, or the lands where they grow, the region of Syria and Palestine, where 'Isa and many prophets of the Children of Israel were sent.
2. **Mount Sinai** (*Tur Sinin*): the mountain where Allah spoke to Musa.
3. **This secure city**: Mecca, where Muhammad ﷺ was sent and which Allah made a safe sanctuary.

**A subtle point**: Ibn Kathir and others note that these oaths point to the places of three great messages: Jerusalem and its surroundings for 'Isa, Sinai for Musa, and Mecca for Muhammad, peace be upon them all. Swearing by the places of revelation prepares for a statement about the human being whom revelation came to guide.

**If they are the fruits**: they remind us of Allah's blessings in plants. The fig is easy to digest and full of benefits, and the olive is a blessed tree described in Surah An-Nur: "lit from a blessed tree, an olive".

## Verse 4: The Best Form
﴿لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِيٓ أَحۡسَنِ تَقۡوِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 95:4)

This is the answer to the oaths. We created man in the best shape, the most balanced stature and the most complete make-up. *Ahsan taqwim*, the best form, includes:
- **Outward form**: an upright stature, a beautiful face, balanced limbs, and hands that create.
- **Inner form**: a mind that thinks, a heart that loves, a soul that aspires, and a natural disposition that knows its Creator.
- **Abilities**: speech, learning, creativity and discovering the secrets of the universe.

**The effect of this truth**: man is honoured, as Surah Al-Isra' says: "We have certainly honoured the children of Adam." A person should not degrade himself with sin, nor despise himself, nor despise others.

## Verse 5: The Lowest of the Low
﴿ثُمَّ رَدَدۡنَٰهُ أَسۡفَلَ سَٰفِلِينَ﴾ (Quran 95:5)

Two well-known explanations:
1. **Returned to extreme old age**: weakness after strength, as in "and among you is he who is returned to the most decrepit age."
2. **Returned to the Fire**: for whoever disbelieves, falling from the best form to the lowest depths.
The second is preferred by many scholars because of the exception that follows: "except those who believe". Believers are not returned to the Fire, whereas old age affects believers and disbelievers alike.

**The irony**: the being created in the best form can, through disbelief, become lower than animals, as Surah Al-A'raf says: "they are like cattle; rather, they are more astray."

## Verse 6: The Exception
﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونٖ﴾ (Quran 95:6)

**"Except those who believe and do righteous deeds"**: they preserve their dignity and are not reduced to the lowest of the low. **"They will have an unending reward"** (*ajrun ghayru mamnun*): uninterrupted, or not given with reminders of favour. Some commentators add that when a believer grows old and cannot do the deeds he used to do, they continue to be written for him. This is supported by the hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari from Abu Musa: "When a servant falls ill or travels, the like of what he used to do when resident and healthy is written for him."

**Two conditions for salvation**: faith and righteous deeds, always paired in the Quran.

## Verses 7–8: The Most Just of Judges
﴿فَمَا يُكَذِّبُكَ بَعۡدُ بِٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَحۡكَمِ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 95:7-8)

**"So what makes you deny the Recompense after this?"**: what leads you, O man, to deny the Reckoning after all these proofs? *Ad-din* here is the Recompense. **"Is Allah not the most just of judges?"**: a question demanding the answer "yes". He is the most just and wise of judges, and part of His wisdom and justice is that He does not treat the believer and the disbeliever alike. If there were no Recompense, it would contradict His wisdom.

## Word Meanings
- **At-Tin**: the fig, or the land where it grows.
- **Az-Zaytun**: the olive, or the land where it grows.
- **Tur Sinin**: Mount Sinai.
- **Al-Balad al-Amin**: the secure city, Mecca.
- **Taqwim**: form, make-up, balance.
- **Radadnahu**: We returned him.
- **Asfal safilin**: the lowest of the low.
- **Mamnun**: cut off, or given with reminders.
- **Ad-Din**: the Recompense.
- **Ahkam al-hakimin**: the most just and wise of judges.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **A progression in the oaths**: from fruits to a mountain to a city, and from the earliest of the three messages to the latest.
2. **"Best form" and "lowest of the low"**: a complete contrast between the highest and lowest ranks.
3. **"La-qad"**: double emphasis, because a person may forget his own dignity.
4. **Ending with a question**: "Is Allah not the most just of judges?", inviting the heart to answer "Yes".

## Tajweed Notes
- **Lam shamsiyyah** in *wat-tin* and *waz-zaytun*: the lam merges into *ta* and *zay*.
- **Lam qamariyyah** in *al-balad* and *al-amin*.
- **Qalqalah sughra** in *la-qad khalaqna* (*dal* with sukun) and in *khalaqna* (*qaf* with sukun).
- **Izhar** in *ajrun ghayru*: tanween clear before the throat letter *ghayn*.
- **Izhar shafawi** in *fa-lahum ajr*: meem clear before hamza.
- **Madd badal** in *amanu*.
- **Madd 'arid** when stopping on *sinin*, *al-amin* and *al-hakimin*.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Before At-Tin comes Ash-Sharh: "With hardship comes ease". At-Tin shows that from the best form a person may fall, and from weakness he may rise through faith. After it comes Al-'Alaq, which begins "Read in the name of your Lord who created, created man from a clinging clot". At-Tin speaks of man created in the best form; Al-'Alaq speaks of his humble origin and the path to his elevation: reading and knowledge.

## Human Dignity in Islam
"We have certainly created man in the best form" is a foundation for the Islamic concept of human dignity. Among its expressions:
- **The sanctity of life**: "whoever kills a soul unless for a soul or for corruption in the land, it is as if he had killed all mankind" (5:32).
- **The prohibition of mockery and insult**, as in Surah Al-Hujurat.
- **The prohibition of mutilation**, even of enemies in war.
- **Care for the body** through purity, cleanliness and good appearance.
A dignified person preserves his own dignity and the dignity of others, and that is completed only through faith and righteous deeds.

## From the Best Form to the Highest Ranks
Being created in the best form is not the end of the road; it is the beginning. A person can rise through faith and deeds to a station Allah loves, or fall through disbelief and sin to the lowest of the low. Means of rising:
1. **Sincere faith**, the basis of all good.
2. **Righteous deeds**: prayer, fasting, charity and kindness.
3. **Good character**, among the heaviest things on the scale.
4. **Beneficial knowledge**: "Allah raises those who believe among you and those given knowledge by degrees" (58:11).
5. **Good company**: a person follows the religion of his close friend.

## Old Age in the Light of Surah At-Tin
On the view that "the lowest of the low" includes extreme old age, the surah teaches:
1. **Use your youth**: strength does not last.
2. **Be merciful to the elderly**: you will reach that stage if you live long. The Prophet ﷺ used to seek refuge from being returned to the most decrepit age; in Sahih al-Bukhari, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas taught his sons words the Prophet ﷺ used to say after prayer: "O Allah, I seek refuge in You from cowardice, and I seek refuge in You from being returned to the most decrepit age, and I seek refuge in You from the trial of this world, and I seek refuge in You from the punishment of the grave."
3. **Honour parents in old age**: "Whether one or both of them reach old age with you, do not say to them 'uff'" (17:23).
4. **Deeds continue to be written**: when a believer becomes too weak for the worship he used to do, its reward continues by Allah's grace.

## Practical Lessons
1. Thank Allah for your form; do not resent how you look.
2. Preserve your dignity by avoiding sin.
3. Respect every person, created in the best form.
4. Combine faith with deeds.
5. Prepare for old age with good deeds in youth.
6. Trust Allah's justice; He is the most just of judges.
7. Value the blessed lands: Mecca, Jerusalem and Sinai.

## Teaching Children
- Bring figs and olives, taste them together and talk about their blessing.
- Teach them that Allah made them in the best form, so they love themselves and respect others.
- Tell them about Mecca, Sinai and Jerusalem, the lands of the prophets.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is meant by the fig and the olive?
Either the fruits themselves, or the lands where they grow in Syria and Palestine, where 'Isa was sent.

### What is Tur Sinin?
Mount Sinai, where Allah spoke to Musa.

### What is "this secure city"?
Mecca.

### What does "the best form" mean?
The best shape, most balanced stature and most complete make-up, outwardly and inwardly.

### What does "the lowest of the low" mean?
Either extreme old age, or the Fire for the one who disbelieves; the second is preferred by many scholars because of the exception that follows.

### Did the Prophet ﷺ recite At-Tin in prayer?
Yes, in 'Isha while travelling, as reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim from al-Bara'.

### How many verses are in Surah At-Tin?
8 verses.

### Why did Allah swear by the fig and the olive specifically?
Because of their many benefits and blessings, and, on the view that they refer to the lands where they grow, because they come from the blessed lands where many prophets were sent.

### What is the purpose of ending the surah with a question?
So the reader affirms Allah's justice with his heart, and prepares for the Day of Recompense.

## Conclusion
Surah At-Tin tells you: Allah created you in the best form, so do not lower yourself to the lowest of the low. Preserve your dignity through faith, raise your rank through righteous deeds, and trust that Allah is the most just of judges, who will reward every soul for what it earned. When you reach its last verse, answer in your heart: yes, and I bear witness to that.
