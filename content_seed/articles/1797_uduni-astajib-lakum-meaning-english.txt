﴿وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدۡعُونِيٓ أَسۡتَجِبۡ لَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ عَنۡ عِبَادَتِي سَيَدۡخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ﴾ [Ghafir 40:60]

## A command and a promise
- *Call upon Me* is a command; *I will respond to you* is a promise. Allah does not only permit duʿa, He **asks** for it.

## Duʿa is worship
- The verse calls not asking *arrogance toward My worship*. The Prophet ﷺ recited this verse after saying: **"Duʿa is worship"** (narrated by Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi, who graded it sound).

## Why is not asking arrogance?
- Asking is admitting need. The one who never asks Allah behaves as if he does not need Him.

## Etiquette of duʿa
| Etiquette | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Begin with praise | Praise Allah and send blessings on the Prophet ﷺ |
| Be certain | Ask while confident of the answer |
| Be persistent | Do not say "I asked and was not answered" |
| Choose good times | Last third of the night, in prostration, between adhan and iqamah |
| Lawful earnings | Unlawful food blocks the answer |

## Lessons
- **Ask for everything**, big or small.
- **Duʿa itself is rewarded** as worship, even before it is answered.
