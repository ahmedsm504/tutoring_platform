The Quran is the speech of Allah, and reciting it has **etiquettes (ādāb)** that bring more reward and more benefit to the heart.

## 1. Sincere intention
- Recite to seek Allah's pleasure, not to be praised.

## 2. Purity
- Being in a state of wuḍūʾ is best, especially when touching the Mushaf.

## 3. Seeking refuge
﴿فَإِذَا قَرَأۡتَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ فَٱسۡتَعِذۡ بِٱللَّهِ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ ٱلرَّجِيمِ﴾ [An-Nahl: 98].

## 4. The basmalah
- Say *Bismillāh ar-Raḥmān ar-Raḥīm* at the start of every surah except At-Tawbah.

## 5. Tarteel: recite slowly
﴿وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا﴾ [Al-Muzzammil: 4].
- Umm Salamah described the Prophet's recitation ﷺ as clear, letter by letter [Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi].

## 6. Beautify your voice
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Beautify the Quran with your voices» [Abu Dawud].

## 7. Reflect
﴿أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَۚ﴾ [An-Nisa: 82].

## 8. Respond to the ayat
- The Prophet ﷺ, when he passed an ayah of mercy, would ask Allah for it, and at an ayah of punishment would seek refuge [Muslim].

## 9. Don't interrupt with chatter
- Pause if you need to speak, then resume with seeking refuge again.

## 10. Respect the Mushaf
- Keep it in a clean, raised place, and don't place things on top of it.
