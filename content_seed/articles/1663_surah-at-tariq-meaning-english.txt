**Surah At-Tariq** (17 ayat) begins with a star that pierces the darkness and ends with a word that pierces doubt.

## The night-comer
﴿وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ﴾ ﴿ٱلنَّجۡمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ﴾
- **At-Ṭāriq**: what comes at night, explained here as **the piercing star**.

## Every soul has a guardian
﴿إِن كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ لَّمَّا عَلَيۡهَا حَافِظࣱ﴾ — *There is no soul but that it has a guardian over it.*
- Angels who protect and record, by Allah's command.

## How you were created
- *Let man look at what he was created from*: a fluid that gushes forth.
- **The argument**: the One who created you the first time can bring you back.

## The Day secrets are tested
- On that Day, **hidden secrets** will be examined, and man will have no power and no helper.

## A decisive word
﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلࣱ فَصۡلࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمَا هُوَ بِٱلۡهَزۡلِ﴾ — *Indeed, it is a decisive word, and it is not amusement.*

## Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Oath and the guardian over every soul |
| 5–10 | Creation and resurrection |
| 11–17 | The Quran is decisive; the plotters are given respite |

## Lessons
- **Someone is always watching**: live accordingly.
- **Take the Quran seriously.**
