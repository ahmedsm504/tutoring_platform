Surah Al-Jumu'ah is the surah of the weekly gathering. It begins by reminding the believers of the greatest favour they received: a Messenger sent to an unlettered people to recite Allah's verses to them, purify them and teach them the Book and wisdom. It warns against carrying knowledge without acting on it, using the striking image of a donkey loaded with books. Then it gives the clear command every Muslim knows: when the call to Friday prayer is made, hasten to the remembrance of Allah and leave trade. And when the prayer is finished, spread through the land and seek Allah's bounty. The Prophet ﷺ used to recite this surah in the Friday prayer itself.

This guide explains Surah Al-Jumu'ah verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith about Friday and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Jumu'ah
- **Number of verses**: 11.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 62, in the twenty-eighth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Jumu'ah means "Friday" or "the congregation", from verse 9.
- **Recited in Friday prayer**: in Sahih Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas reported that the Prophet ﷺ used to recite Surah Al-Jumu'ah and Surah Al-Munafiqun in the Friday prayer.

## Verse 1: Glorification
﴿يُسَبِّحُ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلۡمَلِكِ ٱلۡقُدُّوسِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ (Quran 62:1)

**"Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth is exalting Allah, the Sovereign, the Pure, the Almighty, the Wise."** Four names that prepare for the theme: the Sovereign who commands, the Pure who purifies, the Almighty who supports, the Wise who legislates.

## Verses 2–4: The Messenger to the Unlettered
﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي بَعَثَ فِي ٱلۡأُمِّيِّـۧنَ رَسُولٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَيُزَكِّيهِمۡ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَإِن كَانُواْ مِن قَبۡلُ لَفِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ ﴿وَءَاخَرِينَ مِنۡهُمۡ لَمَّا يَلۡحَقُواْ بِهِمۡۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ (Quran 62:2-4)

**"It is He who has sent among the unlettered a Messenger from themselves"**: the Arabs, most of whom could not read or write, and who had no recent scripture. **His four tasks**:
1. **"Reciting to them His verses."**
2. **"Purifying them"**: from shirk and bad character.
3. **"Teaching them the Book"**: the Quran.
4. **"And wisdom"**: the Sunnah, as many scholars explained.

**"Although they were before in clear error."** The transformation was astonishing: from tribes at war and idol worship to a nation that carried light to the world.

**"And others among them who have not yet joined them"**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah said: "We were sitting with the Prophet ﷺ when Surah Al-Jumu'ah was revealed, including *and others among them who have not yet joined them*. I said: Who are they, O Messenger of Allah? He did not answer until I asked three times. Salman al-Farisi was among us, and the Messenger of Allah ﷺ put his hand on Salman and said: If faith were at the Pleiades, men from these people would reach it." The message is for all nations and all generations, including you.

**"That is the bounty of Allah, which He gives to whom He wills."**

## Verse 5: The Donkey Carrying Books
﴿مَثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ حُمِّلُواْ ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةَ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَحۡمِلُوهَا كَمَثَلِ ٱلۡحِمَارِ يَحۡمِلُ أَسۡفَارَۢاۚ بِئۡسَ مَثَلُ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 62:5)

**"The example of those who were entrusted with the Torah and then did not carry it is like that of a donkey who carries volumes of books."** The donkey carries heavy books on its back and benefits nothing from them. So too anyone who carries scripture, or memorises it, without understanding and acting on it.

**A warning to Muslims**: the parable was given about those entrusted with the Torah, but its lesson applies to anyone who memorises the Quran or collects knowledge without living by it.

## Verses 6–8: Wish for Death If You Are Truthful
﴿قُلۡ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ هَادُوٓاْ إِن زَعَمۡتُمۡ أَنَّكُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ لِلَّهِ مِن دُونِ ٱلنَّاسِ فَتَمَنَّوُاْ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَا يَتَمَنَّوۡنَهُۥٓ أَبَدَۢا بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمُۢ بِٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ ٱلَّذِي تَفِرُّونَ مِنۡهُ فَإِنَّهُۥ مُلَٰقِيكُمۡۖ ثُمَّ تُرَدُّونَ إِلَىٰ عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 62:6-8)

**"Say: O you who are Jews, if you claim that you are allies of Allah, excluding the people, then wish for death, if you should be truthful."** If you are certain you are Allah's beloved, you would long to meet Him. **"But they will not wish for it, ever, because of what their hands have put forth."** Their deeds made them fear meeting Allah.

**"Indeed, the death from which you flee, indeed, it will meet you."** No one escapes death. **"Then you will be returned to the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, and He will inform you about what you used to do."**

## Verses 9–10: The Command of Friday
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَوٰةِ مِن يَوۡمِ ٱلۡجُمُعَةِ فَٱسۡعَوۡاْ إِلَىٰ ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَذَرُواْ ٱلۡبَيۡعَۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿فَإِذَا قُضِيَتِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةُ فَٱنتَشِرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 62:9-10)

**"O you who have believed, when the call is made for the prayer on the day of Jumu'ah, then proceed to the remembrance of Allah and leave trade."** *Fas'aw* here means go with purpose and attention, not running: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ told people to come to the prayer calmly. **"Leave trade"**: buying and selling after the call for Friday prayer is forbidden for those obliged to attend.

**"And when the prayer has been concluded, disperse within the land and seek from the bounty of Allah, and remember Allah often that you may succeed."** Islam does not tell you to abandon work; it tells you to stop for worship and then return to work, with Allah in your heart.

**The virtues of Friday**:
- In Sahih Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "The best day on which the sun has risen is Friday. On it Adam was created, on it he was admitted into Paradise, and on it he was expelled from it, and the Hour will not take place except on Friday."
- **The hour of response**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ mentioned Friday and said: "In it there is an hour in which no Muslim servant stands in prayer asking Allah for something except that He gives it to him."
- **Ghusl**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "When one of you comes to Friday prayer, let him take a bath."

## Verse 11: The Caravan
﴿وَإِذَا رَأَوۡاْ تِجَٰرَةً أَوۡ لَهۡوًا ٱنفَضُّوٓاْ إِلَيۡهَا وَتَرَكُوكَ قَآئِمٗاۚ قُلۡ مَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهۡوِ وَمِنَ ٱلتِّجَٰرَةِۚ وَٱللَّهُ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّـٰزِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 62:11)

**"But when they saw a transaction or a diversion, they rushed to it and left you standing."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Jabir said: "While we were praying with the Prophet ﷺ, a caravan carrying food arrived. The people turned towards it until only twelve men remained with the Prophet ﷺ, and this verse was revealed." The context is that he was standing delivering the sermon.

**"Say: What is with Allah is better than diversion and than a transaction, and Allah is the best of providers."**

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Malik**: the Sovereign.
- **Al-Quddus**: the Pure, the Holy.
- **Al-Ummiyyin**: the unlettered.
- **Yuzakkihim**: purifies them.
- **Al-Hikmah**: wisdom, the Sunnah.
- **Asfar**: large books, volumes.
- **Hadu**: became Jews.
- **Nudiya**: the call was made.
- **Fas'aw**: proceed, go with purpose.
- **Dharu**: leave.
- **Fantashiru**: disperse.
- **Infaddu**: rushed away, dispersed.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Four names in the opening**: each tied to the surah's themes.
2. **Four tasks of the Messenger**: recitation, purification, teaching the Book and wisdom.
3. **The donkey and the books**: a vivid, unforgettable parable.
4. **"The death from which you flee will meet you"**: running from death only brings you to it.
5. **Prayer then trade**: a balanced picture of worship and work.
6. **"What is with Allah is better"**: a gentle correction.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *rasulan minhum* (verse 2), the tanween merges into the meem.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *yasha'u* (verse 4).
- **Izhar**: in *tijaratan aw* (verse 11), the tanween before the hamzah is pronounced clearly.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *wabtaghu* (verse 10), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah As-Saff before it recorded 'Isa's glad tidings of "a messenger named Ahmad", and Al-Jumu'ah describes that messenger's mission among the unlettered. Surah Al-Munafiqun after it is its partner in the Friday prayer and describes the hypocrites, the opposite of the purified believers.

## Etiquette of Friday
1. **Take a bath** and wear clean clothes.
2. **Use perfume and siwak.**
3. **Go early**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ compared going early to offering a camel, then a cow, then a ram.
4. **Listen to the sermon silently**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, he said that whoever says "be quiet" to his companion during the sermon has engaged in idle talk.
5. **Recite Surah Al-Kahf.**
6. **Send many blessings on the Prophet ﷺ.**
7. **Seek the hour of response**, especially at the end of the afternoon.
8. **Leave trade after the call**, then return to work afterwards.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Thank Allah for the Messenger ﷺ** and the guidance he brought.
2. **Act on what you learn**: do not be like the donkey carrying books.
3. **Prepare for death**: it is coming whatever you do.
4. **Honour Friday**: it is the best day of the week.
5. **Balance worship and work.**
6. **Never prefer trade or entertainment over Allah's remembrance.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Jumu'ah
- **Week 1**: verses 1–5.
- **Week 2**: verses 6–11.

## Teaching Children
- **Make Friday special**: bath, clean clothes, perfume and early to the mosque.
- **Explain the donkey and books parable**: learning without acting.
- **Teach them to stay quiet during the sermon.**

## Work and Worship in Balance
Verses 9 and 10 give a model for the Muslim's week. Islam does not ask people to withdraw from the market, nor does it allow the market to swallow their religion. Friday prayer is a pause: you stop, gather, listen to the reminder and pray. Then you return to earning, and the surah adds a condition: "and remember Allah often". The remembrance continues in the shop, the office and the field. In Sahih al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ said: "No one has ever eaten better food than what he eats from the work of his own hands." Halal work done with Allah in mind is itself worship.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### When did the Prophet ﷺ recite Surah Al-Jumu'ah?
In the Friday prayer, together with Al-Munafiqun, as reported in Sahih Muslim.

### Who are "others among them who have not yet joined them"?
Later generations and nations; the Prophet ﷺ put his hand on Salman al-Farisi and said men from his people would reach faith even if it were at the Pleiades, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does the parable of the donkey carrying books mean?
Whoever carries scripture without understanding and acting on it is like a donkey loaded with books it cannot benefit from.

### What does "proceed to the remembrance of Allah" mean?
Go to the Friday prayer with purpose and attention, calmly, leaving trade.

### Is it allowed to work after Friday prayer?
Yes. The surah says "disperse within the land and seek from the bounty of Allah" after the prayer.

### What happened when the caravan arrived?
People left the Prophet ﷺ during the sermon to go to the caravan until only twelve remained, as Jabir reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Jumu'ah?
11 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

### Is buying and selling forbidden during Friday prayer?
Yes, for those obliged to attend, from the call to prayer during the sermon until the prayer ends, because the verse says "leave trade".

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Jumu'ah reminds us of the greatest favour, a Messenger who recited, purified and taught, and warns us not to carry knowledge like a donkey carries books. It gives every week a sacred pause: when the call comes, leave trade and hasten to the remembrance of Allah; then go back and seek His bounty, remembering Him often. What is with Allah is better than any trade or diversion.
