Surah At-Tahrim lets us look inside the Prophet's ﷺ own home and shows that even the best household had ordinary human moments: jealousy, a shared secret, a promise made to please someone. The surah corrects these moments gently and then widens its view to every Muslim home: "O you who believe, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones." It calls to sincere repentance, describes the light of the believers on the Day of Resurrection, and ends with four women: two who were married to prophets and lost, and two who stood firm, one married to the worst tyrant on earth and one with no husband at all. Faith is a personal choice that no family tie can replace.

This guide explains Surah At-Tahrim verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons for the family.

## Quick Facts About Surah At-Tahrim
- **Number of verses**: 12.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 66, the last surah of the twenty-eighth juz.
- **Name**: At-Tahrim means "the prohibition", from verse 1.
- **Main themes**: oaths and their expiation, family responsibility, sincere repentance, and the independence of faith.

## The Context of Revelation
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'A'ishah said that the Prophet ﷺ used to stay with Zaynab bint Jahsh and drink honey at her house. 'A'ishah and Hafsah agreed that whichever of them he came to would say: "I smell maghafir (a strong-smelling gum) on you; have you eaten maghafir?" He came to one of them and she said it. He said: "No, but I drank honey at Zaynab bint Jahsh's, and I will not do it again." Then the verse was revealed: "O Prophet, why do you prohibit what Allah has made lawful for you?"

## Verses 1–2: Do Not Prohibit What Allah Made Lawful
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ لِمَ تُحَرِّمُ مَآ أَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ لَكَۖ تَبۡتَغِي مَرۡضَاتَ أَزۡوَٰجِكَۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿قَدۡ فَرَضَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ تَحِلَّةَ أَيۡمَٰنِكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ مَوۡلَىٰكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ (Quran 66:1-2)

**"O Prophet, why do you prohibit what Allah has made lawful for you, seeking the approval of your wives?"** A gentle reproach: no one, not even the Prophet ﷺ, makes unlawful what Allah has made lawful. **"And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."**

**"Allah has already ordained for you the dissolution of your oaths"**: whoever swears to give up something lawful may expiate his oath and return to it. The expiation of an oath is explained in Surah Al-Ma'idah: feeding ten poor people, or clothing them, or freeing a slave, and if unable, fasting three days.

## Verses 3–5: The Secret and the Warning
﴿وَإِذۡ أَسَرَّ ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِلَىٰ بَعۡضِ أَزۡوَٰجِهِۦ حَدِيثٗا فَلَمَّا نَبَّأَتۡ بِهِۦ وَأَظۡهَرَهُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِ عَرَّفَ بَعۡضَهُۥ وَأَعۡرَضَ عَنۢ بَعۡضٖۖ فَلَمَّا نَبَّأَهَا بِهِۦ قَالَتۡ مَنۡ أَنۢبَأَكَ هَٰذَاۖ قَالَ نَبَّأَنِيَ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ﴾ ﴿إِن تَتُوبَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ فَقَدۡ صَغَتۡ قُلُوبُكُمَاۖ وَإِن تَظَٰهَرَا عَلَيۡهِ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ مَوۡلَىٰهُ وَجِبۡرِيلُ وَصَٰلِحُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَۖ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ ظَهِيرٌ﴾ ﴿عَسَىٰ رَبُّهُۥٓ إِن طَلَّقَكُنَّ أَن يُبۡدِلَهُۥٓ أَزۡوَٰجًا خَيۡرٗا مِّنكُنَّ مُسۡلِمَٰتٖ مُّؤۡمِنَٰتٖ قَٰنِتَٰتٖ تَـٰٓئِبَٰتٍ عَٰبِدَٰتٖ سَـٰٓئِحَٰتٖ ثَيِّبَٰتٖ وَأَبۡكَارٗا﴾ (Quran 66:3-5)

**The secret**: the Prophet ﷺ confided something to one of his wives, she told another, and Allah informed him. He mentioned part of it to her and passed over part of it, a sign of his generosity in not listing every detail. She asked: **"Who told you this?"** He said: **"I was informed by the Knowing, the Acquainted."**

**"If you two turn in repentance to Allah, your hearts have deviated."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas said he waited a year to ask 'Umar who the two women were, and 'Umar said: "Hafsah and 'A'ishah."

**"But if you cooperate against him, then indeed Allah is his protector, and Jibril and the righteous of the believers, and the angels after that are his assistants."**

**"Perhaps his Lord, if he divorced you, would substitute for him wives better than you"**: Muslim, believing, devoutly obedient, repentant, worshipping, travelling or fasting, previously married and virgins. These qualities form a portrait of the ideal believing wife.

**And the wives chose well**: the Mothers of the Believers repented and remained with the Prophet ﷺ, choosing Allah, His Messenger and the hereafter.

## Verse 6: Protect Yourselves and Your Families
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ قُوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَأَهۡلِيكُمۡ نَارٗا وَقُودُهَا ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلۡحِجَارَةُ عَلَيۡهَا مَلَـٰٓئِكَةٌ غِلَاظࣱ شِدَادࣱ لَّا يَعۡصُونَ ٱللَّهَ مَآ أَمَرَهُمۡ وَيَفۡعَلُونَ مَا يُؤۡمَرُونَ﴾ (Quran 66:6)

**"O you who believe, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones."** This is the core of the surah for every Muslim household. You are responsible not only for yourself but for those in your care.

In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Umar reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Each of you is a shepherd and each of you is responsible for his flock. The man is a shepherd over his family and is responsible for them, and the woman is a shepherd over her husband's house and children and is responsible for them."

**Over it are angels, harsh and severe**, **"who do not disobey Allah in what He commands them, but do what they are commanded"**.

**How to protect your family**:
1. **Teach them tawhid** and love of Allah.
2. **Command them to pray**: in a hadith of Abu Dawud graded hasan, the Prophet ﷺ said to command children to pray at seven.
3. **Be the example** they see.
4. **Teach them the Quran.**
5. **Provide halal income.**
6. **Protect them from harmful influences** with wisdom.
7. **Make du'a for them** constantly.

## Verse 7: No Excuses That Day
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَا تَعۡتَذِرُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ إِنَّمَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 66:7)

**"O you who disbelieve, make no excuses that Day. You will only be recompensed for what you used to do."**

## Verse 8: Sincere Repentance
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ تَوۡبَةٗ نَّصُوحًا عَسَىٰ رَبُّكُمۡ أَن يُكَفِّرَ عَنكُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتِكُمۡ وَيُدۡخِلَكُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ يَوۡمَ لَا يُخۡزِي ٱللَّهُ ٱلنَّبِيَّ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥۖ نُورُهُمۡ يَسۡعَىٰ بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَبِأَيۡمَٰنِهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَآ أَتۡمِمۡ لَنَا نُورَنَا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَآۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 66:8)

**"O you who believe, repent to Allah with sincere repentance"** (*tawbatan nasuha*). Scholars describe its conditions:
1. **Stopping the sin immediately.**
2. **Regretting it.**
3. **Resolving never to return.**
4. **Returning the rights of others**, if the sin involved them.
5. **Doing it before it is too late**: before death or the sun rising from the west.

**The reward**: **"Perhaps your Lord will remove from you your misdeeds and admit you into gardens beneath which rivers flow."**

**The light of the believers**: **"on the Day when Allah will not disgrace the Prophet and those who believed with him. Their light will proceed before them and on their right; they will say: Our Lord, perfect for us our light and forgive us."**

## Verse 9: Strive Against the Disbelievers and Hypocrites
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ جَٰهِدِ ٱلۡكُفَّارَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَٱغۡلُظۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ وَمَأۡوَىٰهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 66:9)

**"O Prophet, strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites and be harsh upon them."** The struggle against disbelievers was with argument and, when required, the sword; against the hypocrites with argument and the application of law.

## Verses 10–12: Four Women
﴿ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱمۡرَأَتَ نُوحٖ وَٱمۡرَأَتَ لُوطٖۖ كَانَتَا تَحۡتَ عَبۡدَيۡنِ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا صَٰلِحَيۡنِ فَخَانَتَاهُمَا فَلَمۡ يُغۡنِيَا عَنۡهُمَا مِنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡـٔٗا وَقِيلَ ٱدۡخُلَا ٱلنَّارَ مَعَ ٱلدَّـٰخِلِينَ﴾ (Quran 66:10)

**The wives of Nuh and Lut**: they were married to two righteous prophets, **"but they betrayed them"**, in religion, not in chastity. Ibn 'Abbas said: "The wife of a prophet never committed fornication." Their betrayal was in disbelief and helping their people against their husbands. **"So they did not avail them from Allah at all."** Being married to a prophet saved neither of them.

﴿وَضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱمۡرَأَتَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِذۡ قَالَتۡ رَبِّ ٱبۡنِ لِي عِندَكَ بَيۡتٗا فِي ٱلۡجَنَّةِ وَنَجِّنِي مِن فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَعَمَلِهِۦ وَنَجِّنِي مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 66:11)

**The wife of Pharaoh**: married to the greatest tyrant on earth, she believed and prayed: **"My Lord, build for me near You a house in Paradise, and save me from Pharaoh and his deeds, and save me from the wrongdoing people."** Notice she asked for **"near You"** before **"a house"**: the neighbour before the house. Being married to Pharaoh did not harm her faith.

﴿وَمَرۡيَمَ ٱبۡنَتَ عِمۡرَٰنَ ٱلَّتِيٓ أَحۡصَنَتۡ فَرۡجَهَا فَنَفَخۡنَا فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِنَا وَصَدَّقَتۡ بِكَلِمَٰتِ رَبِّهَا وَكُتُبِهِۦ وَكَانَتۡ مِنَ ٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ﴾ (Quran 66:12)

**Maryam daughter of 'Imran**: **"who guarded her chastity, so We blew into her through Our angel, and she believed in the words of her Lord and His scriptures and was of the devoutly obedient."**

In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Musa reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Many men reached perfection, but no women reached perfection except Asiyah the wife of Pharaoh and Maryam daughter of 'Imran. And the superiority of 'A'ishah over other women is like the superiority of tharid over other food."

**The lesson of the four women**: faith is personal. A prophet's wife can be lost; a tyrant's wife can be saved; a woman alone can reach perfection.

## Word Meanings
- **Tuharrimu**: prohibit.
- **Mardat**: approval.
- **Tahillah**: dissolution, expiation of oaths.
- **Asarra**: confided a secret.
- **Saghat**: deviated, inclined.
- **Tazahara**: cooperate against.
- **Qanitat**: devoutly obedient.
- **Sa'ihat**: travelling, or fasting.
- **Qu**: protect.
- **Waqud**: fuel.
- **Ghilaz shidad**: harsh and severe.
- **Nasuh**: sincere.
- **Ahsanat**: guarded her chastity.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Why do you prohibit?"**: a gentle question to the beloved Prophet ﷺ.
2. **"He mentioned part of it and passed over part"**: the generosity of not exposing everything.
3. **"Fuel is people and stones"**: a horrifying image.
4. **"Near You, a house in Paradise"**: the neighbour before the house.
5. **Four women, two contrasts**: the closest relationships cannot replace personal faith.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *naran waquduha* (verse 6) and *tawbatan nasuha* (verse 8), the tanween merges into the following letter.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *mala'ikatun* (verse 6).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yubdilahu* (verse 5), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna*, on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah At-Talaq before it regulated family separation, and At-Tahrim addresses family life within the household and responsibility for its members. Surah Al-Mulk after it opens the twenty-ninth juz, turning from the home to the dominion of the whole universe, with "who created death and life to test you".

## Lessons for Every Home
1. **Do not forbid yourself what Allah made lawful** to please people.
2. **Keep family secrets.**
3. **Correct gently**: mention part and pass over part.
4. **You are a shepherd**: protect your family from the Fire.
5. **Repent sincerely** and teach your family to repent.
6. **Do not rely on a pious relative**: each soul answers for itself.
7. **Hold firm even in a difficult home**, like the wife of Pharaoh.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Expiate your oath** if you swore to leave something lawful.
2. **Make your home a place of faith.**
3. **Repent sincerely** today.
4. **Ask Allah to perfect your light.**
5. **Make the du'a of Pharaoh's wife.**
6. **Take Maryam as a model** of chastity and obedience.

## How to Memorise Surah At-Tahrim
- **Week 1**: verses 1–5.
- **Week 2**: verses 6–9.
- **Week 3**: verses 10–12.

## Teaching Children
- **Explain "protect yourselves and your families"**: we help each other do good.
- **Tell them the story of Asiyah**, the believing wife of Pharaoh.
- **Teach them the conditions of sincere repentance.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the context of Surah At-Tahrim?
The Prophet ﷺ declared honey forbidden for himself after some of his wives said they smelled maghafir on him, as reported by 'A'ishah in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the expiation of an oath?
Feeding or clothing ten poor people or freeing a slave; if unable, fasting three days, as in Surah Al-Ma'idah.

### Who were the two wives in verse 4?
'A'ishah and Hafsah, as 'Umar told Ibn 'Abbas in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does "protect yourselves and your families from a Fire" mean?
Every believer must guard himself and his family from the Fire by teaching, guiding and setting an example.

### What is sincere repentance?
Stopping the sin, regretting it, resolving not to return, and returning others' rights.

### How did the wives of Nuh and Lut betray their husbands?
In religion, by disbelieving and helping their people, not in chastity.

### What was the du'a of Pharaoh's wife?
"My Lord, build for me near You a house in Paradise, and save me from Pharaoh and his deeds, and save me from the wrongdoing people."

### How many verses are in Surah At-Tahrim?
12 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah At-Tahrim shows that even the Prophet's ﷺ household had human moments, corrected with gentleness. It calls every believer to protect himself and his family from the Fire, to repent sincerely, and to ask Allah to perfect his light. And through four women it teaches that no relationship can save or ruin a person; faith is each soul's own choice.
