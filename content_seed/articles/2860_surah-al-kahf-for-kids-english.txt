Every Friday, many Muslim families open the mushaf to Surah Al-Kahf. Why this surah in particular? Because the Prophet ﷺ told us that whoever reads it on Friday will have a light shining for them. And because it contains four incredible stories, each of which teaches us how to stay safe from a fitnah, a difficult test: the test of faith, the test of wealth, the test of knowledge, and the test of power.

Young men who ran away to protect their faith, then slept in a cave for 309 years and woke up thinking they had slept a day! A rich man with two magnificent gardens who forgot to say "Masha'Allah". Prophet Musa travelling a long way to learn from a righteous man, and seeing things he could not understand. And a just king who built a huge wall of iron and copper. In this guide for parents, we explain Surah Al-Kahf to children in simple English, with lessons, activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Kahf

- **Position**: surah 18, in juz 15 and 16, right in the middle of the Quran.
- **Number of verses**: 110.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: Al-Kahf means "The Cave".

### The virtues of Surah Al-Kahf
- The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever reads Surah Al-Kahf on Friday, a light will shine for him between the two Fridays" (al-Hakim and al-Bayhaqi; graded authentic by al-Albani).
- And he ﷺ said: "Whoever memorizes ten verses from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjal" (Muslim).

### Activity: Kahf Friday
Make reading Surah Al-Kahf a family Friday habit. Each person reads a part, and little ones read one page or simply listen. Older children can start memorizing the first ten verses.

## Lesson 1: Praise Allah for the Quran

﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَلَمۡ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ عِوَجَاۜ﴾ (Quran 18:1)

### Explaining it to your child
"The surah begins by praising Allah, who sent down the Quran to the Prophet ﷺ as a straight book with no crookedness in it, meaning no mistakes and no contradictions. So we thank Allah for the gift of the Quran."

## Story 1: The Young Men of the Cave

﴿إِذۡ أَوَى ٱلۡفِتۡيَةُ إِلَى ٱلۡكَهۡفِ فَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةٗ وَهَيِّئۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَمۡرِنَا رَشَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:10)

### Explaining it to your child
"There were some young men living in a land ruled by a cruel king who forced people to worship idols. But they believed in Allah alone. So they ran away to protect their faith, went into a cave in a mountain, and made a beautiful du'a: Our Lord, give us mercy from Yourself and guide us to the right way in our situation."

﴿نَّحۡنُ نَقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ نَبَأَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ إِنَّهُمۡ فِتۡيَةٌ ءَامَنُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡ وَزِدۡنَٰهُمۡ هُدٗى﴾ (Quran 18:13)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah says: they were young men who believed in their Lord, and We increased them in guidance. Allah made them sleep in the cave for 309 years! He turned them from side to side so their bodies would not be harmed, and their dog lay stretched out at the entrance. Then Allah woke them, and they thought they had slept a day or part of a day! They sent one of them with old coins to buy food, and that is how the people discovered their story."

### Lesson for kids: young people and faith
"The people of the cave were young, but they stood firm on the truth when everyone around them was against them. So nobody should say: I'm too young. Allah loves young believers, and He increased them in guidance."

### Activity: the du'a of the young men
Teach your child the du'a of the people of the cave: "Rabbana atina min ladunka rahmatan wa hayyi' lana min amrina rashada." They can say it whenever they feel unsure about a decision.

## Lesson 2: Always Say "In Sha' Allah"

﴿وَلَا تَقُولَنَّ لِشَاْيۡءٍ إِنِّي فَاعِلࣱ ذَٰلِكَ غَدًا﴾ ﴿إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ وَٱذۡكُر رَّبَّكَ إِذَا نَسِيتَ وَقُلۡ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَهۡدِيَنِ رَبِّي لِأَقۡرَبَ مِنۡ هَٰذَا رَشَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:23-24)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah teaches us: never say I will do this tomorrow without adding: In sha' Allah, if Allah wills. We do not know what will happen tomorrow, and everything happens by Allah's will. And if you forget to say it, remember Allah as soon as you remember."

### Activity: the In sha' Allah game
At dinner, everyone shares a plan for tomorrow. Whoever forgets to say "In sha' Allah" pays a fun penalty, like saying "SubhanAllah" ten times. Children quickly make the phrase a habit.

## Story 2: The Man With Two Gardens

﴿۞وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلٗا رَّجُلَيۡنِ جَعَلۡنَا لِأَحَدِهِمَا جَنَّتَيۡنِ مِنۡ أَعۡنَٰبٖ وَحَفَفۡنَٰهُمَا بِنَخۡلٖ وَجَعَلۡنَا بَيۡنَهُمَا زَرۡعٗا﴾ (Quran 18:32)

### Explaining it to your child
"There were two men. One was very rich: Allah gave him two gardens full of grapes, surrounded by date palms, with crops between them and a river flowing through the middle. He became arrogant and told his poorer friend: I have more money than you and more people on my side. He walked into his garden and said: I don't think this will ever end!"

﴿وَلَوۡلَآ إِذۡ دَخَلۡتَ جَنَّتَكَ قُلۡتَ مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ إِن تَرَنِ أَنَا۠ أَقَلَّ مِنكَ مَالٗا وَوَلَدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:39)

### Explaining it to your child
"His believing friend said: Why didn't you say, when you entered your garden, Masha'Allah, la quwwata illa billah? Meaning: this is what Allah willed, and there is no power except with Allah. But the rich man would not listen. So Allah destroyed both gardens, and the man was left wringing his hands, sorry for everything he had spent, saying: I wish I had not associated anyone with my Lord."

### Lesson for kids: say Masha'Allah
"Whenever you see something beautiful that you have, or that someone else has, say: Masha'Allah, la quwwata illa billah. It reminds you that the blessing is from Allah, and it protects it."

### Activity: Masha'Allah hunt
Throughout the day, every time your child sees something lovely, like their toys, a fruit tree, or a friend's drawing, they say "Masha'Allah". In the evening they tell you how many times they said it.

## Lesson 3: The Good Deeds That Last

﴿ٱلۡمَالُ وَٱلۡبَنُونَ زِينَةُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَٱلۡبَٰقِيَٰتُ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتُ خَيۡرٌ عِندَ رَبِّكَ ثَوَابٗا وَخَيۡرٌ أَمَلٗا﴾ (Quran 18:46)

### Explaining it to your child
"Money and children are the decoration of this life. They are lovely, but they do not last forever. The good deeds that remain are better with Allah and a better hope. Some scholars explained them as: SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, La ilaha illallah and Allahu Akbar."

### Activity: deeds that last
Give your child a counter or tasbih and have them say "SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, La ilaha illallah, Allahu Akbar" ten times a day. Tell them: "These words stay with you with Allah, longer than any toy."

## Story 3: Musa and Al-Khidr

﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِفَتَىٰهُ لَآ أَبۡرَحُ حَتَّىٰٓ أَبۡلُغَ مَجۡمَعَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ أَوۡ أَمۡضِيَ حُقُبٗا﴾ (Quran 18:60)

### Explaining it to your child
"Prophet Musa was a great prophet, yet he learned that there was a righteous man who had knowledge he did not have. So he told his young helper: I will not stop until I reach the meeting point of the two seas, even if I travel for years! He set out on a journey just to learn."

﴿فَوَجَدَا عَبۡدٗا مِّنۡ عِبَادِنَآ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنۡ عِندِنَا وَعَلَّمۡنَٰهُ مِن لَّدُنَّا عِلۡمٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَ لَهُۥ مُوسَىٰ هَلۡ أَتَّبِعُكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن تُعَلِّمَنِ مِمَّا عُلِّمۡتَ رُشۡدٗا﴾ (Quran 18:65-66)

### Explaining it to your child
"Musa found the righteous servant, whom scholars call Al-Khidr, and asked him politely: May I follow you so you can teach me some of the knowledge Allah has taught you? Al-Khidr said: You will not be able to be patient with me. Musa promised to be patient. On the journey, Al-Khidr did three surprising things: he made a hole in a boat, he fixed a falling wall without asking for payment, and he did a third thing Musa could not understand. Each time, Musa objected: Why did you do that?"

### The secret of the boat and the wall

﴿أَمَّا ٱلسَّفِينَةُ فَكَانَتۡ لِمَسَٰكِينَ يَعۡمَلُونَ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ فَأَرَدتُّ أَنۡ أَعِيبَهَا وَكَانَ وَرَآءَهُم مَّلِكࣱ يَأۡخُذُ كُلَّ سَفِينَةٍ غَصۡبٗا﴾ (Quran 18:79)

"The boat belonged to poor people who worked at sea, and there was a cruel king who seized every good boat. So Al-Khidr damaged it so the king would leave it, and the owners could repair it and keep it."

﴿وَأَمَّا ٱلۡجِدَارُ فَكَانَ لِغُلَٰمَيۡنِ يَتِيمَيۡنِ فِي ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ وَكَانَ تَحۡتَهُۥ كَنزࣱ لَّهُمَا وَكَانَ أَبُوهُمَا صَٰلِحٗا فَأَرَادَ رَبُّكَ أَن يَبۡلُغَآ أَشُدَّهُمَا وَيَسۡتَخۡرِجَا كَنزَهُمَا رَحۡمَةٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ وَمَا فَعَلۡتُهُۥ عَنۡ أَمۡرِيۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَأۡوِيلُ مَا لَمۡ تَسۡطِع عَّلَيۡهِ صَبۡرٗا﴾ (Quran 18:82)

"The wall stood over a treasure that belonged to two young orphans, and their father had been a righteous man. If the wall had fallen, the treasure would have been exposed and taken. So Al-Khidr rebuilt it until the orphans grew up and could take their treasure. Look! The goodness of their father protected his children even after he died."

### Lesson for kids: seeking knowledge
"Musa travelled far to learn, even though he was a prophet. Knowledge is worth the effort. And he was polite with his teacher: May I follow you? So we are polite with our teachers too."

### Lesson for kids: Allah is wise
"Sometimes something happens that we don't understand, and it makes us sad, like the hole in the boat. But Allah knows the wisdom, and there may be good in it that we can't see yet."

## Story 4: Dhul-Qarnayn

﴿إِنَّا مَكَّنَّا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ سَبَبٗا﴾ (Quran 18:84)

### Explaining it to your child
"Dhul-Qarnayn was a righteous and just king. Allah gave him power and a great kingdom, and gave him the means and knowledge to do many things. He travelled to the far west and the far east, punishing wrongdoers and honouring good people. Then he reached a people living between two mountains, who complained about a destructive people called Ya'juj and Ma'juj. They said: We will pay you if you build a barrier between us and them."

﴿قَالَ مَا مَكَّنِّي فِيهِ رَبِّي خَيۡرࣱ فَأَعِينُونِي بِقُوَّةٍ أَجۡعَلۡ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَبَيۡنَهُمۡ رَدۡمًا﴾ (Quran 18:95)

### Explaining it to your child
"Dhul-Qarnayn said: What Allah has given me is better than your money, I don't need it. But help me with your strength and work. So they gathered blocks of iron and stacked them between the two mountains, lit fires until the iron glowed red, then poured melted copper over it. It became a wall so strong that the destroyers could neither climb it nor dig through it. When he finished, he said: This is a mercy from my Lord."

### Lesson for kids: strong and humble
"Dhul-Qarnayn was powerful, yet he didn't take people's money. He taught them to work with him. And when he finished, he didn't say: Look what I built! He said: This is a mercy from my Lord."

### Activity: build the barrier
Build a wall between two books using blocks or clay, then pour coloured water over it like copper. At the end, your child says: "This is a mercy from my Lord."

## Let's Read the Ending Together

﴿قُل لَّوۡ كَانَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ مِدَادٗا لِّكَلِمَٰتِ رَبِّي لَنَفِدَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ قَبۡلَ أَن تَنفَدَ كَلِمَٰتُ رَبِّي وَلَوۡ جِئۡنَا بِمِثۡلِهِۦ مَدَدٗا﴾ ﴿قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ أَنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱۖ فَمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ لِقَآءَ رَبِّهِۦ فَلۡيَعۡمَلۡ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا وَلَا يُشۡرِكۡ بِعِبَادَةِ رَبِّهِۦٓ أَحَدَۢا﴾ (Quran 18:109-110)

### Explaining it to your child
"If the ocean were ink to write the words of Allah, the ocean would run out before Allah's words ran out! Then the last verse: the Prophet ﷺ is a human being like us who receives revelation that our God is One God. So whoever hopes to meet their Lord should do good deeds and not associate anyone in the worship of their Lord."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Friday Light"

Aisha's house was quiet on Friday. Her father was at Jumu'ah and her mother was preparing lunch, so Aisha was watching cartoons. Her grandmother called: "Aisha, come, let's read Surah Al-Kahf."

"Grandma, it's so long!" said Aisha. Her grandmother smiled. "First, I'll tell you its stories, then we'll read." She told her about the young men in the cave, the dog at the entrance and the long sleep. Then the story of the two gardens. Then Musa, Al-Khidr and the boat.

Aisha got excited. "Grandma, I want to read the boat story myself!" She opened the mushaf and read a page with her grandmother, then another. When she reached the secret of the wall and the treasure, she said: "The good father protected his children even after he died!"

Her grandmother said: "And the Prophet ﷺ said that whoever reads Al-Kahf on Friday will have a light until the next Friday." Aisha said: "Then I'll read a part every Friday until I finish it!"

From that day, Aisha looked forward to Fridays to read a new story from Surah Al-Kahf with her grandmother. Two months later, she had read the whole surah and memorized the first ten verses.

Ask your child: "Which story did Aisha like most? Which one do you like?"

## What We Learn From Surah Al-Kahf

- **Stand firm on faith**, like the young men.
- **Say In sha' Allah** about the future.
- **Say Masha'Allah** when you see a blessing.
- **Good deeds last** longer than this world.
- **Seeking knowledge** is worth travelling for.
- **Respect your teachers**.
- **Allah is wise** in everything that happens.
- **Righteous parents** protect their children.
- **Strong people stay humble** and credit Allah.
- **Reading Al-Kahf on Friday** brings light.

## Memorization Plan: The First Ten Verses in Two Weeks

- **Days 1–2**: verses 1–3.
- **Days 3–4**: verses 4–6.
- **Days 5–6**: verses 7–8.
- **Days 7–8**: verses 9–10.
- **Days 9–11**: review all ten.
- **Days 12–13**: verses 23–24.
- **Day 14**: recite everything on Friday and celebrate!

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### When does the Friday reading of Al-Kahf start?
Scholars mention that it starts from sunset on Thursday until sunset on Friday.

### Was Al-Khidr a prophet?
Scholars differ, and many hold that he was a prophet because he said in the story, "I did not do it of my own accord." For children, we can say he was a righteous servant to whom Allah gave special knowledge.

### My child is young. How can they read such a long surah every week?
Listening counts, reading one page counts, and memorizing the first ten verses is a wonderful goal. The aim is for your child to connect with the surah and love it.
