Surah Al-Hadid contains a question that has turned many lives around: "Has the time not come for those who have believed that their hearts should become humbly submissive at the remembrance of Allah?" It was addressed to believers, not to disbelievers, and it is a call to stop living with a hard heart. The surah opens with some of the most profound descriptions of Allah in the Quran: the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden, who is with you wherever you are. It calls the believers to spend, describes the light that will run before them on the Day of Judgement and the wall that will separate them from the hypocrites, gives a sharp description of what this world really is, and mentions iron, sent down with great strength and benefits.

This guide explains Surah Al-Hadid section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Hadid
- **Number of verses**: 29.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 57, the last surah of the twenty-seventh juz.
- **Name**: Al-Hadid means "iron", from verse 25.
- **The first of the *musabbihat***: the surahs beginning with glorification.
- **Main themes**: knowing Allah, spending in His cause, the light of the believers, softening the heart and the reality of this world.

## Verses 1–6: Knowing Allah
﴿سَبَّحَ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُ وَٱلۡأٓخِرُ وَٱلظَّـٰهِرُ وَٱلۡبَاطِنُۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٌ﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ يَعۡلَمُ مَا يَلِجُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهَا وَمَا يَنزِلُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا يَعۡرُجُ فِيهَاۖ وَهُوَ مَعَكُمۡ أَيۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَإِلَى ٱللَّهِ تُرۡجَعُ ٱلۡأُمُورُ﴾ ﴿يُولِجُ ٱلَّيۡلَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ وَيُولِجُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فِي ٱلَّيۡلِۚ وَهُوَ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ﴾ (Quran 57:1-6)

**"Whatever is in the heavens and earth exalts Allah, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise."**

**"He is the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden, and He is Knowing of all things."** In Sahih Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ used to pray before sleep: "O Allah, You are the First, so there is nothing before You; You are the Last, so there is nothing after You; You are the Manifest, so there is nothing above You; You are the Hidden, so there is nothing beyond You. Settle our debts for us and free us from poverty." The Prophet ﷺ explained the four names himself.

**"He created the heavens and earth in six days and then established Himself above the Throne."** He knows what enters the earth and what comes out of it, what descends from the heaven and what ascends to it. **"And He is with you wherever you are."** With you in His knowledge, hearing and sight. Wherever you go, you are not alone and not unseen.

## Verses 7–11: Spend From What You Are Trustees Of
﴿ءَامِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَأَنفِقُواْ مِمَّا جَعَلَكُم مُّسۡتَخۡلَفِينَ فِيهِۖ فَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَأَنفَقُواْ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرࣱ كَبِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمَا لَكُمۡ لَا تُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلرَّسُولُ يَدۡعُوكُمۡ لِتُؤۡمِنُواْ بِرَبِّكُمۡ وَقَدۡ أَخَذَ مِيثَٰقَكُمۡ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُنَزِّلُ عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِۦٓ ءَايَٰتِۭ بَيِّنَٰتٖ لِّيُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُمۡ لَرَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمَا لَكُمۡ أَلَّا تُنفِقُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَلِلَّهِ مِيرَٰثُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ لَا يَسۡتَوِي مِنكُم مَّنۡ أَنفَقَ مِن قَبۡلِ ٱلۡفَتۡحِ وَقَٰتَلَۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَعۡظَمُ دَرَجَةٗ مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنفَقُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدُ وَقَٰتَلُواْۚ وَكُلّٗا وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿مَّن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يُقۡرِضُ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا فَيُضَٰعِفَهُۥ لَهُۥ وَلَهُۥٓ أَجۡرࣱ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 57:7-11)

**"Believe in Allah and His Messenger and spend out of that in which He has made you successors."** The wealth in your hands is not truly yours. You are a trustee (*mustakhlaf*): it was someone else's before you and will be someone else's after you. Spend from it while it is in your trust.

**"Not equal among you are those who spent before the conquest and fought"**: those who gave and struggled when Islam was weak are higher in rank than those who did so after it became strong. **"But to all Allah has promised the best."**

**"Who is it that would loan Allah a goodly loan so He will multiply it for him, and he will have a noble reward?"** Allah calls charity a loan to Himself, though He owns everything.

## Verses 12–15: The Light and the Wall
﴿يَوۡمَ تَرَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ يَسۡعَىٰ نُورُهُم بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَبِأَيۡمَٰنِهِمۖ بُشۡرَىٰكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ جَنَّـٰتࣱ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يَقُولُ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقَٰتُ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱنظُرُونَا نَقۡتَبِسۡ مِن نُّورِكُمۡ قِيلَ ٱرۡجِعُواْ وَرَآءَكُمۡ فَٱلۡتَمِسُواْ نُورٗاۖ فَضُرِبَ بَيۡنَهُم بِسُورٖ لَّهُۥ بَابُۢ بَاطِنُهُۥ فِيهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَةُ وَظَٰهِرُهُۥ مِن قِبَلِهِ ٱلۡعَذَابُ﴾ ﴿يُنَادُونَهُمۡ أَلَمۡ نَكُن مَّعَكُمۡۖ قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ وَلَٰكِنَّكُمۡ فَتَنتُمۡ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَتَرَبَّصۡتُمۡ وَٱرۡتَبۡتُمۡ وَغَرَّتۡكُمُ ٱلۡأَمَانِيُّ حَتَّىٰ جَآءَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ وَغَرَّكُم بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡغَرُورُ﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَا يُؤۡخَذُ مِنكُمۡ فِدۡيَةࣱ وَلَا مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۚ مَأۡوَىٰكُمُ ٱلنَّارُۖ هِيَ مَوۡلَىٰكُمۡۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 57:12-15)

**"On the Day you see the believing men and believing women, their light proceeding before them and on their right."** Their light comes from their faith and deeds.

**The hypocrites ask for light**: **"Wait for us that we may acquire some of your light."** They are told: **"Go back behind you and seek light."** **"And a wall will be placed between them with a door, its interior containing mercy, but on the outside of it is torment."**

The hypocrites call out: **"Were we not with you?"** The answer: **"Yes, but you afflicted yourselves, and awaited misfortune for us, and doubted, and wishful thinking deluded you until the command of Allah came."** Four causes of their ruin: self-temptation, waiting for the believers' failure, doubt, and false hopes.

## Verse 16: Has the Time Not Come?
﴿۞أَلَمۡ يَأۡنِ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَن تَخۡشَعَ قُلُوبُهُمۡ لِذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا نَزَلَ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا يَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَطَالَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡأَمَدُ فَقَسَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡۖ وَكَثِيرࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 57:16)

**"Has the time not come for those who have believed that their hearts should become humbly submissive at the remembrance of Allah and what has come down of the truth?"** In Sahih Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud said: "Between our acceptance of Islam and Allah reproaching us with this verse there were only four years." If the Companions were addressed this way after four years, what about us?

**"And let them not be like those who were given the Scripture before, and a long period passed over them, so their hearts hardened."** Time and familiarity can harden the heart. Hearing the Quran a thousand times without reflection can make it lose its effect.

## Verse 17: Allah Revives the Earth
﴿ٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحۡيِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ مَوۡتِهَاۚ قَدۡ بَيَّنَّا لَكُمُ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 57:17)

**"Know that Allah gives life to the earth after its lifelessness."** Placed directly after the verse about hard hearts, it carries a message of hope: the One who revives dead earth with rain can revive a dead heart with the Quran.

## Verses 18–19: The Charitable and the Truthful
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُصَّدِّقِينَ وَٱلۡمُصَّدِّقَٰتِ وَأَقۡرَضُواْ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنٗا يُضَٰعَفُ لَهُمۡ وَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرࣱ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦٓ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلصِّدِّيقُونَۖ وَٱلشُّهَدَآءُ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرُهُمۡ وَنُورُهُمۡۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ (Quran 57:18-19)

**"Indeed, the men who practise charity and the women who practise charity and have loaned Allah a goodly loan, it will be multiplied for them."** **"And those who have believed in Allah and His messengers, those are the truthful and the martyrs with their Lord."**

## Verse 20: What This World Really Is
﴿ٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لَعِبࣱ وَلَهۡوࣱ وَزِينَةࣱ وَتَفَاخُرُۢ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَتَكَاثُرࣱ فِي ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَوۡلَٰدِۖ كَمَثَلِ غَيۡثٍ أَعۡجَبَ ٱلۡكُفَّارَ نَبَاتُهُۥ ثُمَّ يَهِيجُ فَتَرَىٰهُ مُصۡفَرّٗا ثُمَّ يَكُونُ حُطَٰمٗاۖ وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ عَذَابࣱ شَدِيدࣱ وَمَغۡفِرَةࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِضۡوَٰنࣱۚ وَمَا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَآ إِلَّا مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡغُرُورِ﴾ (Quran 57:20)

**"Know that the life of this world is but amusement and diversion and adornment and boasting to one another and competition in increase of wealth and children."** Five stages that match a human life: play in childhood, diversion in youth, adornment in early adulthood, boasting in maturity, and competing in wealth and children in later life.

**"Like the example of a rain whose resulting plant growth pleases the tillers; then it dries and you see it turned yellow; then it becomes debris."** Green, then yellow, then dust. **"And in the hereafter is severe punishment and forgiveness from Allah and approval. And what is the worldly life except the enjoyment of delusion."**

## Verse 21: Race to Forgiveness
﴿سَابِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَغۡفِرَةٖ مِّن رَّبِّكُمۡ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرۡضُهَا كَعَرۡضِ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكَ فَضۡلُ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ (Quran 57:21)

**"Race to forgiveness from your Lord and a Garden whose width is like the width of the heavens and earth."** After describing the world as a passing game, the surah redirects ambition: compete, but for this.

## Verses 22–24: Every Calamity Is Written
﴿مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٖ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡ إِلَّا فِي كِتَٰبٖ مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن نَّبۡرَأَهَآۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿لِّكَيۡلَا تَأۡسَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَفۡرَحُواْ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالٖ فَخُورٍ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَبۡخَلُونَ وَيَأۡمُرُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ بِٱلۡبُخۡلِۗ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ﴾ (Quran 57:22-24)

**"No disaster strikes upon the earth or among yourselves except that it is in a register before We bring it into being."** **The purpose**: **"In order that you not despair over what has eluded you and not exult over what He has given you."** Knowing that everything is decreed brings balance: no crushing grief at loss and no arrogant joy at gain. **"And Allah does not like everyone self-deluded and boastful."**

## Verse 25: Iron Sent Down
﴿لَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلَنَا بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَأَنزَلۡنَا مَعَهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡمِيزَانَ لِيَقُومَ ٱلنَّاسُ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَأَنزَلۡنَا ٱلۡحَدِيدَ فِيهِ بَأۡسࣱ شَدِيدࣱ وَمَنَٰفِعُ لِلنَّاسِ وَلِيَعۡلَمَ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَنصُرُهُۥ وَرُسُلَهُۥ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَوِيٌّ عَزِيزࣱ﴾ (Quran 57:25)

**"We have already sent Our messengers with clear evidences and sent down with them the Scripture and the balance, that the people may maintain justice. And We sent down iron, wherein is great military might and benefits for the people."** Revelation guides, the balance establishes justice, and iron protects it and builds civilisation. Some contemporary writers have linked "sent down" to iron's origin outside the earth; the verse's main point is that justice needs both guidance and strength.

## Verses 26–29: Nuh, Ibrahim, 'Isa and Monasticism
﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحٗا وَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَجَعَلۡنَا فِي ذُرِّيَّتِهِمَا ٱلنُّبُوَّةَ وَٱلۡكِتَٰبَۖ فَمِنۡهُم مُّهۡتَدٖۖ وَكَثِيرࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ قَفَّيۡنَا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِم بِرُسُلِنَا وَقَفَّيۡنَا بِعِيسَى ٱبۡنِ مَرۡيَمَ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلۡإِنجِيلَۖ وَجَعَلۡنَا فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُ رَأۡفَةٗ وَرَحۡمَةٗۚ وَرَهۡبَانِيَّةً ٱبۡتَدَعُوهَا مَا كَتَبۡنَٰهَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ إِلَّا ٱبۡتِغَآءَ رِضۡوَٰنِ ٱللَّهِ فَمَا رَعَوۡهَا حَقَّ رِعَايَتِهَاۖ فَـَٔاتَيۡنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ أَجۡرَهُمۡۖ وَكَثِيرࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ﴾ ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَءَامِنُواْ بِرَسُولِهِۦ يُؤۡتِكُمۡ كِفۡلَيۡنِ مِن رَّحۡمَتِهِۦ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ نُورٗا تَمۡشُونَ بِهِۦ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿لِّئَلَّا يَعۡلَمَ أَهۡلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ أَلَّا يَقۡدِرُونَ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ مِّن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَأَنَّ ٱلۡفَضۡلَ بِيَدِ ٱللَّهِ يُؤۡتِيهِ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ (Quran 57:26-29)

Allah sent Nuh and Ibrahim and placed prophethood and scripture in their descendants. Then 'Isa, and placed **compassion and mercy** in the hearts of his followers, **"and monasticism, which they innovated; We did not prescribe it for them"**, though they sought Allah's approval, **"but they did not observe it with due observance"**.

**The promise to believers**: **"Fear Allah and believe in His Messenger; He will give you a double portion of His mercy and make for you a light by which you will walk and forgive you."**

## Word Meanings

- **Al-Akhir**: the Last.
- **Az-Zahir**: the Manifest, above whom there is nothing.
- **Al-Batin**: the Hidden, beyond whom there is nothing.
- **Mustakhlafin**: made trustees, successors.
- **Qard hasan**: a goodly loan.
- **Unzuruna**: wait for us.
- **Sur**: wall.
- **Fatantum**: you afflicted yourselves.
- **Ya'ni**: has the time come.
- **Takhsha'**: be humbled.
- **Qasat**: hardened.
- **Lahw**: diversion.
- **Takathur**: competition in increase.
- **Hutam**: debris.
- **Ba's**: might, strength.
- **Rahbaniyyah**: monasticism.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Four names in one verse**: First, Last, Manifest, Hidden, covering time and existence.
2. **"He is with you wherever you are"**: intimacy and watchfulness together.
3. **The wall with mercy inside and torment outside.**
4. **"Has the time not come?"**: a gentle, piercing question.
5. **Dead earth revived after hard hearts**: hope placed beside warning.
6. **The life of this world in five words**, then a plant from green to dust.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 4).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yuqridu* (verse 11), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Izhar**: in *qardan hasanan* (verse 11), the tanween before the throat letter ha' is pronounced clearly.
- **Idgham without ghunnah**: in *bi-surin lahu* (verse 13), the tanween merges into the lam.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna Allaha* (verse 17), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Waqi'ah before it ended with "So exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great", and Al-Hadid begins with everything exalting Allah. Surah Al-Mujadilah after it begins "Allah has heard", continuing the theme of His knowledge and closeness: "He is with you wherever you are".

## Softening a Hard Heart
1. **Recite the Quran with reflection**, not only for quantity.
2. **Remember death and visit graves.**
3. **Weep or try to weep** in prayer and du'a.
4. **Give charity**: it softens the heart.
5. **Show mercy to orphans and the poor.**
6. **Leave sins that harden the heart.**
7. **Sit with people of remembrance.**

## Practical Lessons
1. **Learn Allah's names**, starting with the four in verse 3.
2. **Remember that Allah is with you.**
3. **Treat your wealth as a trust.**
4. **Lend Allah a goodly loan.**
5. **Ask Allah for light on the Day of Judgement.**
6. **Answer the question of verse 16 today.**
7. **See this world for what it is.**
8. **Accept decree with balance**: neither despair nor arrogance.

- **Teach them the bedtime du'a** with the four names.
- **Show them a plant from green to dry** to explain verse 20.
- **Explain that Allah is always with them**, seeing and hearing.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What do "the First, the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden" mean?
The Prophet ﷺ explained them: nothing before Him, nothing after Him, nothing above Him and nothing beyond Him, as in Sahih Muslim.

### What does "He is with you wherever you are" mean?
Allah is with you in His knowledge, hearing and sight wherever you are.

### What is the wall between the believers and the hypocrites?
A wall with a door on the Day of Judgement, with mercy on the believers' side and torment on the other.

### When was "Has the time not come" revealed?
Ibn Mas'ud said it was four years after they accepted Islam, as in Sahih Muslim.

### What does "the life of this world is but amusement and diversion" mean?
It describes the passing stages of worldly life, from play to competition in wealth and children, before it all withers.

### Why is the surah called Al-Hadid?
Because verse 25 mentions iron, sent down with great strength and benefits for people.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Hadid?
29 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Hadid introduces you to your Lord, the First and the Last, who is with you wherever you are; asks you to spend from what He has entrusted to you; and shows you the light you will need on the Day of Judgement. Then it asks the question every believer must answer: has the time not come for your heart to be humbled at the remembrance of Allah? Let today be the answer.
