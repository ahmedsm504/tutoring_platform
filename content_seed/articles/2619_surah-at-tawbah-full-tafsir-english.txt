Surah At-Tawbah is the only surah of the Quran that does not begin with "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful". It opens instead with a declaration of disassociation from those who repeatedly broke their treaties. Much of it was revealed around the expedition of Tabuk, in the intense heat of summer, when the true believers rushed to give and to go out, and the hypocrites invented excuses. Yet the surah is named "Repentance", because through it Allah accepted the repentance of believers who fell short, most famously the three Companions who stayed behind from Tabuk and were left in isolation until "the earth, vast as it is, closed in on them". It also contains the memory of the cave, where the Prophet ﷺ told Abu Bakr: "Do not grieve; indeed, Allah is with us."

This guide explains Surah At-Tawbah section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah At-Tawbah
- **Number of verses**: 129.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 9, in the tenth and eleventh juz.
- **Names**: At-Tawbah ("Repentance") and Bara'ah ("Disassociation").
- **No Bismillah**: scholars gave several explanations, including its close connection to Al-Anfal and its opening with a declaration of disassociation.
- **Main themes**: treaties, sincerity in faith, the expedition of Tabuk, exposing hypocrisy, zakah and repentance.

## Verses 1–16: The Declaration
﴿بَرَآءَةࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦٓ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ عَٰهَدتُّم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾ ﴿فَسِيحُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَرۡبَعَةَ أَشۡهُرٖ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّكُمۡ غَيۡرُ مُعۡجِزِي ٱللَّهِ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡزِي ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 9:1-2)

**"A disassociation from Allah and His Messenger to those with whom you had made a treaty among the polytheists."** Those who had broken treaties were given four months to move freely.

﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ عَٰهَدتُّم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَنقُصُوكُمۡ شَيۡـٔٗا وَلَمۡ يُظَٰهِرُواْ عَلَيۡكُمۡ أَحَدٗا فَأَتِمُّوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡ عَهۡدَهُمۡ إِلَىٰ مُدَّتِهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ (Quran 9:4)

**But those who kept their treaties** and did not support anyone against the Muslims: **"Fulfil for them their treaty until their term."** Faithfulness to agreements is a religious duty.

﴿وَإِنۡ أَحَدࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ ٱسۡتَجَارَكَ فَأَجِرۡهُ حَتَّىٰ يَسۡمَعَ كَلَٰمَ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ أَبۡلِغۡهُ مَأۡمَنَهُۥۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ لَّا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 9:6)

**"And if any one of the polytheists seeks your protection, then grant him protection so that he may hear the words of Allah. Then deliver him to his place of safety."** Even in a time of conflict, the door to hearing the Quran is kept open, with guaranteed safety.

## Verses 17–24: Who Maintains the Mosques
﴿إِنَّمَا يَعۡمُرُ مَسَٰجِدَ ٱللَّهِ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَأَقَامَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَى ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَلَمۡ يَخۡشَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَۖ فَعَسَىٰٓ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَن يَكُونُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ﴾ (Quran 9:18)

**"The mosques of Allah are only to be maintained by those who believe in Allah and the Last Day and establish prayer and give zakah and do not fear except Allah."** Maintaining mosques is both building them and filling them with worship.

﴿قُلۡ إِن كَانَ ءَابَآؤُكُمۡ وَأَبۡنَآؤُكُمۡ وَإِخۡوَٰنُكُمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجُكُمۡ وَعَشِيرَتُكُمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلٌ ٱقۡتَرَفۡتُمُوهَا وَتِجَٰرَةࣱ تَخۡشَوۡنَ كَسَادَهَا وَمَسَٰكِنُ تَرۡضَوۡنَهَآ أَحَبَّ إِلَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَجِهَادٖ فِي سَبِيلِهِۦ فَتَرَبَّصُواْ حَتَّىٰ يَأۡتِيَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَمۡرِهِۦۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 9:24)

**"Say: If your fathers, your sons, your brothers, your wives, your relatives, wealth which you have obtained, commerce wherein you fear decline, and dwellings with which you are pleased are more beloved to you than Allah and His Messenger and striving in His cause, then wait until Allah executes His command."** Eight beloved things, all lawful, placed on one side of the scale. None may outweigh Allah and His Messenger.

## Verses 25–28: Hunayn
﴿لَقَدۡ نَصَرَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ فِي مَوَاطِنَ كَثِيرَةٖ وَيَوۡمَ حُنَيۡنٍ إِذۡ أَعۡجَبَتۡكُمۡ كَثۡرَتُكُمۡ فَلَمۡ تُغۡنِ عَنكُمۡ شَيۡـٔٗا وَضَاقَتۡ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ بِمَا رَحُبَتۡ ثُمَّ وَلَّيۡتُم مُّدۡبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ سَكِينَتَهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِۦ وَعَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَأَنزَلَ جُنُودٗا لَّمۡ تَرَوۡهَا وَعَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۚ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 9:25-26)

**"And on the day of Hunayn, when your great number pleased you, but it did not avail you at all."** At Hunayn, the Muslims were twelve thousand, and some said they would not be defeated because of their numbers. They were ambushed and scattered at first, until Allah sent down His tranquillity and soldiers they did not see. **The lesson**: never rely on numbers or strength.

## Verses 29–37: Hoarding and the Sacred Months
﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ كَثِيرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡأَحۡبَارِ وَٱلرُّهۡبَانِ لَيَأۡكُلُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡبَٰطِلِ وَيَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ يَكۡنِزُونَ ٱلذَّهَبَ وَٱلۡفِضَّةَ وَلَا يُنفِقُونَهَا فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَبَشِّرۡهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٖ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يُحۡمَىٰ عَلَيۡهَا فِي نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ فَتُكۡوَىٰ بِهَا جِبَاهُهُمۡ وَجُنُوبُهُمۡ وَظُهُورُهُمۡۖ هَٰذَا مَا كَنَزۡتُمۡ لِأَنفُسِكُمۡ فَذُوقُواْ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡنِزُونَ﴾ (Quran 9:34-35)

**"Those who hoard gold and silver and spend it not in the way of Allah, give them tidings of a painful punishment."** Wealth on which zakah is not paid will be heated and used to brand their foreheads, sides and backs.

﴿إِنَّ عِدَّةَ ٱلشُّهُورِ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱثۡنَا عَشَرَ شَهۡرٗا فِي كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِ يَوۡمَ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِنۡهَآ أَرۡبَعَةٌ حُرُمࣱۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡقَيِّمُۚ فَلَا تَظۡلِمُواْ فِيهِنَّ أَنفُسَكُمۡۚ وَقَٰتِلُواْ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ كَآفَّةٗ كَمَا يُقَٰتِلُونَكُمۡ كَآفَّةٗۚ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ (Quran 9:36)

**"Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve months... of these, four are sacred."** **"So do not wrong yourselves during them."**

## Verses 38–41: Tabuk and the Cave
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَا لَكُمۡ إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمُ ٱنفِرُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ٱثَّاقَلۡتُمۡ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ أَرَضِيتُم بِٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا مِنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ فَمَا مَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ إِلَّا قَلِيلٌ﴾ (Quran 9:38)

**"O you who believe, what is the matter with you that, when you are told to go forth in the cause of Allah, you adhere heavily to the earth? Are you satisfied with the life of this world rather than the hereafter?"**

﴿إِلَّا تَنصُرُوهُ فَقَدۡ نَصَرَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِذۡ أَخۡرَجَهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ثَانِيَ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ إِذۡ هُمَا فِي ٱلۡغَارِ إِذۡ يَقُولُ لِصَٰحِبِهِۦ لَا تَحۡزَنۡ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَنَاۖ فَأَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ سَكِينَتَهُۥ عَلَيۡهِ وَأَيَّدَهُۥ بِجُنُودٖ لَّمۡ تَرَوۡهَا وَجَعَلَ كَلِمَةَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ ٱلسُّفۡلَىٰۗ وَكَلِمَةُ ٱللَّهِ هِيَ ٱلۡعُلۡيَاۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 9:40)

**"If you do not aid him, Allah has already aided him when those who disbelieved had driven him out, as one of two, when they were in the cave and he said to his companion: Do not grieve; indeed, Allah is with us."**

In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Bakr said: "I said to the Prophet ﷺ while we were in the cave: If one of them looked under his feet, he would see us. He said: What do you think, O Abu Bakr, of two when Allah is their third?"

**The lesson**: Allah's support of His religion does not depend on any one of us. The honour is to be part of it.

﴿ٱنفِرُواْ خِفَافٗا وَثِقَالٗا وَجَٰهِدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِكُمۡ وَأَنفُسِكُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 9:41)

**"Go forth, whether light or heavy, and strive with your wealth and your lives in the cause of Allah."**

## Verses 42–59: The Hypocrites' Excuses
The hypocrites asked permission to stay behind. **"Among them is he who says: Permit me, and do not put me to trial. Unquestionably, into trial they have fallen."** They mocked the believers' charity: some gave much and were accused of showing off, others gave little and were mocked for it.

## Verse 60: The Eight Recipients of Zakah
﴿۞إِنَّمَا ٱلصَّدَقَٰتُ لِلۡفُقَرَآءِ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱلۡعَٰمِلِينَ عَلَيۡهَا وَٱلۡمُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَفِي ٱلرِّقَابِ وَٱلۡغَٰرِمِينَ وَفِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱبۡنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِۖ فَرِيضَةٗ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 9:60)

**"Zakah expenditures are only for the poor and for the needy and for those employed to collect it and for bringing hearts together and for freeing captives and for those in debt and for the cause of Allah and for the stranded traveller, an obligation imposed by Allah."**

## Verses 61–80: Hypocrites and Believers
﴿ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقَٰتُ بَعۡضُهُم مِّنۢ بَعۡضٖۚ يَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَقۡبِضُونَ أَيۡدِيَهُمۡۚ نَسُواْ ٱللَّهَ فَنَسِيَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ هُمُ ٱلۡفَٰسِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 9:67)

**"The hypocrite men and hypocrite women are of one another. They enjoin what is wrong and forbid what is right and close their hands. They have forgotten Allah, so He has forgotten them."**

﴿وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٖۚ يَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَيُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَيُطِيعُونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ سَيَرۡحَمُهُمُ ٱللَّهُۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 9:71)

**"The believing men and believing women are allies of one another. They enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong and establish prayer and give zakah and obey Allah and His Messenger. Those, Allah will have mercy upon them."** A perfect contrast.

## Verses 81–99: Those Who Stayed Behind
﴿وَلَا تُصَلِّ عَلَىٰٓ أَحَدٖ مِّنۡهُم مَّاتَ أَبَدٗا وَلَا تَقُمۡ عَلَىٰ قَبۡرِهِۦٓۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَفَرُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَمَاتُواْ وَهُمۡ فَٰسِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 9:84)

**"And do not pray over any of them who has died, ever, or stand at his grave."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Umar reported that the Prophet ﷺ prayed over 'Abdullah ibn Ubayy, the leader of the hypocrites, out of mercy, and then this verse was revealed.

﴿لَّيۡسَ عَلَى ٱلضُّعَفَآءِ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلۡمَرۡضَىٰ وَلَا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَجِدُونَ مَا يُنفِقُونَ حَرَجٌ إِذَا نَصَحُواْ لِلَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۚ مَا عَلَى ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ مِن سَبِيلٖۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَلَا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا مَآ أَتَوۡكَ لِتَحۡمِلَهُمۡ قُلۡتَ لَآ أَجِدُ مَآ أَحۡمِلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَلَّواْ وَّأَعۡيُنُهُمۡ تَفِيضُ مِنَ ٱلدَّمۡعِ حَزَنًا أَلَّا يَجِدُواْ مَا يُنفِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 9:91-92)

**The sincere who could not go**: there is no blame on the weak, the sick and those who find nothing to spend, **"nor upon those who, when they came to you that you might give them mounts, you said: I can find nothing for you to ride upon. They turned back while their eyes overflowed with tears out of grief that they could not find something to spend."** Their tears counted as their effort.

## Verses 100–110: The Forerunners and Masjid ad-Dirar
﴿وَٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلۡأَنصَارِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُم بِإِحۡسَٰنٖ رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ تَجۡرِي تَحۡتَهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ (Quran 9:100)

**"And the first forerunners among the Muhajirun and the Ansar and those who followed them with good conduct, Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him."** A clear verse on the virtue of the Companions.

﴿وَءَاخَرُونَ ٱعۡتَرَفُواْ بِذُنُوبِهِمۡ خَلَطُواْ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا وَءَاخَرَ سَيِّئًا عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَتُوبَ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمٌ﴾ ﴿خُذۡ مِنۡ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ صَدَقَةٗ تُطَهِّرُهُمۡ وَتُزَكِّيهِم بِهَا وَصَلِّ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ إِنَّ صَلَوٰتَكَ سَكَنࣱ لَّهُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 9:102-103)

**"And others who have acknowledged their sins. They had mixed a righteous deed with another that was bad. Perhaps Allah will turn to them in forgiveness."** **"Take from their wealth a charity by which you purify them and cause them increase."**

﴿وَقُلِ ٱعۡمَلُواْ فَسَيَرَى ٱللَّهُ عَمَلَكُمۡ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَۖ وَسَتُرَدُّونَ إِلَىٰ عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 9:105)

**"And say: Do as you will, for Allah will see your deeds, and so will His Messenger and the believers."**

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مَسۡجِدٗا ضِرَارٗا وَكُفۡرٗا وَتَفۡرِيقَۢا بَيۡنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَإِرۡصَادٗا لِّمَنۡ حَارَبَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُۚ وَلَيَحۡلِفُنَّ إِنۡ أَرَدۡنَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰۖ وَٱللَّهُ يَشۡهَدُ إِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ﴾ ﴿لَا تَقُمۡ فِيهِ أَبَدٗاۚ لَّمَسۡجِدٌ أُسِّسَ عَلَى ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ مِنۡ أَوَّلِ يَوۡمٍ أَحَقُّ أَن تَقُومَ فِيهِۚ فِيهِ رِجَالࣱ يُحِبُّونَ أَن يَتَطَهَّرُواْۚ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُطَّهِّرِينَ﴾ (Quran 9:107-108)

**Masjid ad-Dirar**: a mosque built by hypocrites to cause harm and division. The Prophet ﷺ was told not to pray in it: **"A mosque founded on righteousness from the first day is more worthy for you to stand in."**

## Verses 111–118: The Trade and the Three Who Were Forgiven
﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱشۡتَرَىٰ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَأَمۡوَٰلَهُم بِأَنَّ لَهُمُ ٱلۡجَنَّةَۚ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَقۡتُلُونَ وَيُقۡتَلُونَۖ وَعۡدًا عَلَيۡهِ حَقّٗا فِي ٱلتَّوۡرَىٰةِ وَٱلۡإِنجِيلِ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ وَمَنۡ أَوۡفَىٰ بِعَهۡدِهِۦ مِنَ ٱللَّهِۚ فَٱسۡتَبۡشِرُواْ بِبَيۡعِكُمُ ٱلَّذِي بَايَعۡتُم بِهِۦۚ وَذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ ﴿ٱلتَّـٰٓئِبُونَ ٱلۡعَٰبِدُونَ ٱلۡحَٰمِدُونَ ٱلسَّـٰٓئِحُونَ ٱلرَّـٰكِعُونَ ٱلسَّـٰجِدُونَ ٱلۡأٓمِرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَٱلنَّاهُونَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظُونَ لِحُدُودِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 9:111-112)

**"Indeed, Allah has purchased from the believers their lives and their properties in exchange for that they will have Paradise."** Then their qualities: those who repent, worship, praise, travel (or fast), bow, prostrate, enjoin good, forbid evil and observe Allah's limits.

﴿لَّقَد تَّابَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلۡأَنصَارِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُ فِي سَاعَةِ ٱلۡعُسۡرَةِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا كَادَ يَزِيغُ قُلُوبُ فَرِيقٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ ثُمَّ تَابَ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِهِمۡ رَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَعَلَى ٱلثَّلَٰثَةِ ٱلَّذِينَ خُلِّفُواْ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا ضَاقَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ بِمَا رَحُبَتۡ وَضَاقَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَنفُسُهُمۡ وَظَنُّوٓاْ أَن لَّا مَلۡجَأَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّآ إِلَيۡهِ ثُمَّ تَابَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ لِيَتُوبُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Quran 9:117-118)

**The three who stayed behind**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ka'b ibn Malik told his own story in detail. He had no excuse for missing Tabuk, and when the Prophet ﷺ returned, Ka'b told the truth rather than inventing an excuse. He, Murarah ibn ar-Rabi' and Hilal ibn Umayyah were boycotted for fifty days, **"until the earth, vast as it was, closed in on them and their souls confined them, and they were certain that there is no refuge from Allah except in Him. Then He turned to them so they could repent."**

**The lesson**: truthfulness saved them. Ka'b said: "By Allah, Allah has not blessed me with any blessing after Islam greater than my truthfulness to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ."

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَكُونُواْ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 9:119)

**"O you who believe, fear Allah and be with those who are true."**

## Verses 119–129: Knowledge and the Messenger's Care
﴿۞وَمَا كَانَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ لِيَنفِرُواْ كَآفَّةٗۚ فَلَوۡلَا نَفَرَ مِن كُلِّ فِرۡقَةٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ طَآئِفَةࣱ لِّيَتَفَقَّهُواْ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَلِيُنذِرُواْ قَوۡمَهُمۡ إِذَا رَجَعُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِمۡ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَحۡذَرُونَ﴾ (Quran 9:122)

**"It is not for the believers to go forth all at once. For there should separate from every division of them a group to obtain understanding in the religion and warn their people when they return."** Seeking religious knowledge is also a form of striving.

﴿لَقَدۡ جَآءَكُمۡ رَسُولࣱ مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ عَزِيزٌ عَلَيۡهِ مَا عَنِتُّمۡ حَرِيصٌ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فَإِن تَوَلَّوۡاْ فَقُلۡ حَسۡبِيَ ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُۖ وَهُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ (Quran 9:128-129)

**"There has certainly come to you a Messenger from among yourselves. Grievous to him is what you suffer; he is concerned over you and to the believers is kind and merciful."** The Prophet ﷺ feels your hardship and longs for your good. **"But if they turn away, say: Sufficient for me is Allah; there is no deity except Him. On Him I have relied, and He is the Lord of the Great Throne."**

## Word Meanings
- **Bara'ah**: disassociation.
- **Istajaraka**: seeks your protection.
- **Ma'manahu**: his place of safety.
- **Ya'muru**: maintain.
- **Hunayn**: a valley near Ta'if, site of a battle in 8 AH.
- **Yaknizuna**: hoard.
- **Iththaqaltum**: adhered heavily.
- **Thaniya ithnayn**: one of two.
- **Khifafan wa thiqalan**: light or heavy.
- **Al-Mu'allafah qulubuhum**: those whose hearts are to be brought together.
- **Al-Gharimin**: those in debt.
- **Dirar**: harm.
- **Rahubat**: vast.
- **Ra'uf**: kind.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Opening without Bismillah**: the form matches the stern message.
2. **Eight beloved things on one side of the scale**: a test of love.
3. **"Your great number pleased you"**: the danger of self-reliance.
4. **"Allah is with us"**: four words of total trust in the cave.
5. **"The earth closed in on them"**: the feeling of guilt and isolation.
6. **The final two verses**: the Prophet's ﷺ love and reliance.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *bara'atun* (verse 1).
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna Allaha ma'ana* (verse 40), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *khifafan wa thiqalan* (verse 41) and *rasulun min anfusikum* (verse 128), the tanween merges into the following letter.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Anfal before it dealt with Badr and treaties, and At-Tawbah continues with treaties and later battles; their connection is so close that no Bismillah separates them. Surah Yunus after it returns to Meccan themes of faith and revelation.

## Lessons From the Story of Ka'b ibn Malik
1. **Truthfulness saves**, even when it costs.
2. **Delay can lead to missing great good**: Ka'b kept postponing his preparation.
3. **Accept the consequences of mistakes.**
4. **Hold on to Allah when people turn away.**
5. **Rejoice in repentance**: it is the greatest day.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Keep your agreements.**
2. **Love Allah and His Messenger above all.**
3. **Do not rely on numbers or strength.**
4. **Pay zakah** and know its recipients.
5. **Respond when called to good**, light or heavy.
6. **Be truthful**, as Ka'b was.
7. **Seek knowledge of religion.**
8. **Say "Sufficient for me is Allah"** in every hardship.

## How to Memorise Surah At-Tawbah
- **Weeks 1–2**: verses 1–37.
- **Weeks 3–4**: verses 38–72.
- **Weeks 5–6**: verses 73–110.
- **Week 7**: verses 111–129.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them the story of the cave** and "Allah is with us".
- **Tell them about the Companions who wept** because they could not go.
- **Tell them the story of Ka'b** and the value of honesty.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why does Surah At-Tawbah not begin with Bismillah?
Scholars gave several explanations, including its close connection to Al-Anfal and its opening with a declaration of disassociation.

### What does "Do not grieve; indeed Allah is with us" refer to?
The Prophet's ﷺ words to Abu Bakr in the cave during the Hijrah, as in verse 40 and in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Who are the eight recipients of zakah?
The poor, the needy, zakah collectors, those whose hearts are being reconciled, freeing captives, debtors, the cause of Allah, and the stranded traveller.

### Who were the three who stayed behind?
Ka'b ibn Malik, Murarah ibn ar-Rabi' and Hilal ibn Umayyah, whose repentance was accepted after fifty days, as Ka'b narrated in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What was Masjid ad-Dirar?
A mosque built by hypocrites to cause harm and division, in which the Prophet ﷺ was forbidden to pray.

### What do the last two verses say?
That the Prophet ﷺ feels the believers' hardship and is kind and merciful, and that Allah is sufficient for him.

### How many verses are in Surah At-Tawbah?
129 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah At-Tawbah tests the heart: what do you love more than Allah and His Messenger? Do you rely on numbers or on Him? Will you go forth when called, or invent excuses? Yet it is named Repentance, because its doors stay open to those who are truthful, like Ka'b and his companions. Be with the truthful, and say in every hardship: Sufficient for me is Allah.
