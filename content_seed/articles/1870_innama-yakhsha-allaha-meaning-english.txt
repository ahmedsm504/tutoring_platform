﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَٱلدَّوَآبِّ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ مُخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥ كَذَٰلِكَۗ إِنَّمَا يَخۡشَى ٱللَّهَ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡعُلَمَـٰٓؤُاْۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ غَفُورٌ﴾ [Fatir 35:28]

## The context: colours
- The previous verse mentions rain producing fruits of different colours, and mountains with white, red and black streaks. This verse adds people, animals and livestock.

## Knowledge leads to awe
- After describing this diversity, Allah says: **only those with knowledge truly fear Him**. The more one understands creation and the Creator, the deeper the awe.

## Which knowledge?
| Knowledge | Effect |
|---|---|
| Knowing Allah's names and attributes | Love, fear and hope |
| Knowing His religion | Obedience |
| Knowing His creation | Wonder and humility |

- Ibn Masʿud is reported to have said: knowledge is not abundance of narration, but knowledge is **khashyah** (awe).

## *Khashyah* versus fear
- *Khashyah* is fear based on knowledge and reverence, not panic.

## The ending
- *Indeed Allah is Mighty, Forgiving*: might that inspires awe, forgiveness that inspires hope.

## Lessons
- **Learn to grow closer**, not to boast.
- **Let science increase faith.**
