**Surah An-Naziat** (46 ayat) begins with oaths by angels, then tells a short story with a big lesson: the arrogance of Pharaoh.

## Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Oaths by the angels who take out souls and carry out commands |
| 6–14 | The Day of the quake and the deniers' surprise |
| 15–26 | Musa sent to Pharaoh |
| 27–33 | Creation of the heavens and earth as proof |
| 34–41 | The great overwhelming event and the two fates |
| 42–46 | When is the Hour? Only Allah knows |

## Musa and Pharaoh
- Musa was told to go to Pharaoh and invite him gently to purify himself and know his Lord.
- Pharaoh denied and proclaimed: *"I am your lord, most high."*
- **Lesson**: arrogance ends in punishment, *a lesson for whoever fears*.

## The two fates
﴿فَأَمَّا مَن طَغَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَءَاثَرَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ﴾
﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ وَنَهَى ٱلنَّفۡسَ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ﴾
| Path | Result |
|---|---|
| Transgression and preferring this life | Hellfire |
| Fearing the standing before Allah and restraining desire | Paradise |

## Lessons
- **Desire is not evil**, but it must be restrained.
- **Gentle words**, even with the arrogant, as Musa was commanded.
