Surah Al-Baqarah is the longest surah in the Quran and one of the most comprehensive. It begins by answering the request of Al-Fatihah, "Guide us to the straight path", with: "This is the Book about which there is no doubt, a guidance for those conscious of Allah." Over 286 verses it builds a complete community: its beliefs, its history, its worship, its family law, its economy and its identity as "a middle nation". It contains the greatest verse in the Quran, Ayat al-Kursi, and ends with two verses the Prophet ﷺ said are sufficient for whoever recites them at night. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not make your houses graves. Satan flees from a house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited."

This guide explains Surah Al-Baqarah section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons. Because of its length, it focuses on the main passages and their lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Baqarah
- **Number of verses**: 286.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 2, in the first, second and third juz.
- **Name**: Al-Baqarah means "the cow", from the story in verses 67–73.
- **Contains**: the greatest verse (Ayat al-Kursi) and the longest verse (verse 282, on debts).
- **Main themes**: guidance, the three kinds of people, the story of humanity, the Children of Israel, the new qiblah, worship and law, and charity.

## The Virtues of Al-Baqarah
1. **Satan flees from the house**: Sahih Muslim, as above.
2. **The two bright ones**: in Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Recite Al-Baqarah, for taking it is a blessing, leaving it is a regret, and the sorcerers cannot withstand it."
3. **The greatest verse**: in Sahih Muslim, Ubayy ibn Ka'b identified Ayat al-Kursi as the greatest verse, and the Prophet ﷺ praised his knowledge.
4. **Protection at night**: in Sahih al-Bukhari, in the story of Abu Hurayrah guarding the zakah, he was taught that whoever recites Ayat al-Kursi on going to bed will have a guardian from Allah until morning, and the Prophet ﷺ confirmed it.
5. **The last two verses**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah at night, they will suffice him."

## Verses 1–20: Three Kinds of People
﴿الٓمٓ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيۡبَۛ فِيهِۛ هُدٗى لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَيُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡكَ وَمَآ أُنزِلَ مِن قَبۡلِكَ وَبِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ هُمۡ يُوقِنُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ عَلَىٰ هُدٗى مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡۖ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:1-5)

**The believers**: they believe in the unseen, establish prayer, spend from what Allah gives them, believe in what was revealed to the Prophet ﷺ and before him, and are certain of the hereafter. **"Those are on guidance from their Lord, and it is those who are the successful."**

**The disbelievers** (verses 6–7): whether warned or not, they will not believe; their hearts and hearing are sealed.

**The hypocrites** (verses 8–20): thirteen verses describe them, because they are the most dangerous. They say "We believe" but do not. **"In their hearts is disease."** Two parables: a man who kindled a fire and then lost its light, and people caught in a storm of darkness, thunder and lightning, putting their fingers in their ears.

## Verses 21–29: A Call to All Mankind
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فِرَٰشٗا وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بِنَآءٗ وَأَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَخۡرَجَ بِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ رِزۡقٗا لَّكُمۡۖ فَلَا تَجۡعَلُواْ لِلَّهِ أَندَادٗا وَأَنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:21-22)

**"O mankind, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you, that you may become righteous."** The first command in the Mushaf order is worship, with its reason: the One who created you and provided for you.

﴿وَإِن كُنتُمۡ فِي رَيۡبٖ مِّمَّا نَزَّلۡنَا عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِنَا فَأۡتُواْ بِسُورَةٖ مِّن مِّثۡلِهِۦ وَٱدۡعُواْ شُهَدَآءَكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿فَإِن لَّمۡ تَفۡعَلُواْ وَلَن تَفۡعَلُواْ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِي وَقُودُهَا ٱلنَّاسُ وَٱلۡحِجَارَةُۖ أُعِدَّتۡ لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:23-24)

**The challenge**: **"And if you are in doubt about what We have sent down upon Our servant, then produce a surah the like thereof."**

## Verses 30–39: Adam the Successor
﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي جَاعِلࣱ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ خَلِيفَةٗۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَتَجۡعَلُ فِيهَا مَن يُفۡسِدُ فِيهَا وَيَسۡفِكُ ٱلدِّمَآءَ وَنَحۡنُ نُسَبِّحُ بِحَمۡدِكَ وَنُقَدِّسُ لَكَۖ قَالَ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:30)

**"And when your Lord said to the angels: Indeed, I will make upon the earth a successive authority."** The angels asked about corruption and bloodshed; Allah said: **"Indeed, I know that which you do not know."** He taught Adam the names of all things, and the angels admitted: **"Exalted are You; we have no knowledge except what You have taught us."** Iblis refused to prostrate out of arrogance. Adam slipped, then **"received from his Lord words, and He accepted his repentance"**.

## Verses 40–123: The Children of Israel
This long section reminds the Children of Israel of Allah's favours and their responses: the parting of the sea, the calf, the manna and quails, and their stubbornness.

﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُكُمۡ أَن تَذۡبَحُواْ بَقَرَةٗۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَتَتَّخِذُنَا هُزُوٗاۖ قَالَ أَعُوذُ بِٱللَّهِ أَنۡ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ ٱدۡعُ لَنَا رَبَّكَ يُبَيِّن لَّنَا مَا هِيَۚ قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ يَقُولُ إِنَّهَا بَقَرَةࣱ لَّا فَارِضࣱ وَلَا بِكۡرٌ عَوَانُۢ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَۖ فَٱفۡعَلُواْ مَا تُؤۡمَرُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ ٱدۡعُ لَنَا رَبَّكَ يُبَيِّن لَّنَا مَا لَوۡنُهَاۚ قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ يَقُولُ إِنَّهَا بَقَرَةࣱ صَفۡرَآءُ فَاقِعࣱ لَّوۡنُهَا تَسُرُّ ٱلنَّـٰظِرِينَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ ٱدۡعُ لَنَا رَبَّكَ يُبَيِّن لَّنَا مَا هِيَ إِنَّ ٱلۡبَقَرَ تَشَٰبَهَ عَلَيۡنَا وَإِنَّآ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَمُهۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ إِنَّهُۥ يَقُولُ إِنَّهَا بَقَرَةࣱ لَّا ذَلُولࣱ تُثِيرُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَلَا تَسۡقِي ٱلۡحَرۡثَ مُسَلَّمَةࣱ لَّا شِيَةَ فِيهَاۚ قَالُواْ ٱلۡـَٰٔنَ جِئۡتَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ فَذَبَحُوهَا وَمَا كَادُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:67-71)

**The story of the cow**: Musa told his people that Allah commanded them to slaughter a cow to discover a murderer. Instead of obeying, they asked question after question: what age, what colour, what kind. Each question made it harder. **"So they slaughtered her, but they could hardly do it."** The lesson: obey simply, and do not complicate religion with needless questions.

﴿ثُمَّ قَسَتۡ قُلُوبُكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ فَهِيَ كَٱلۡحِجَارَةِ أَوۡ أَشَدُّ قَسۡوَةٗۚ وَإِنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡحِجَارَةِ لَمَا يَتَفَجَّرُ مِنۡهُ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۚ وَإِنَّ مِنۡهَا لَمَا يَشَّقَّقُ فَيَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهُ ٱلۡمَآءُۚ وَإِنَّ مِنۡهَا لَمَا يَهۡبِطُ مِنۡ خَشۡيَةِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:74)

**"Then your hearts became hardened after that, being like stones or even harder."**

## Verses 124–141: Ibrahim and the Ka'bah
﴿وَإِذۡ يَرۡفَعُ إِبۡرَٰهِـۧمُ ٱلۡقَوَاعِدَ مِنَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ وَإِسۡمَٰعِيلُ رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلۡ مِنَّآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ ﴿رَبَّنَا وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا مُسۡلِمَيۡنِ لَكَ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِنَآ أُمَّةٗ مُّسۡلِمَةٗ لَّكَ وَأَرِنَا مَنَاسِكَنَا وَتُبۡ عَلَيۡنَآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ ﴿رَبَّنَا وَٱبۡعَثۡ فِيهِمۡ رَسُولٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِكَ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَيُزَكِّيهِمۡۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ (Quran 2:127-129)

**"And when Ibrahim was raising the foundations of the House and Isma'il, saying: Our Lord, accept this from us. Indeed, You are the Hearing, the Knowing."** Building the most sacred house, they still asked for acceptance. **"Our Lord, and send among them a messenger from themselves."** The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was the answer to this du'a.

## Verses 142–152: The Qiblah and the Middle Nation
﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ جَعَلۡنَٰكُمۡ أُمَّةٗ وَسَطٗا لِّتَكُونُواْ شُهَدَآءَ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ وَيَكُونَ ٱلرَّسُولُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ شَهِيدٗاۗ وَمَا جَعَلۡنَا ٱلۡقِبۡلَةَ ٱلَّتِي كُنتَ عَلَيۡهَآ إِلَّا لِنَعۡلَمَ مَن يَتَّبِعُ ٱلرَّسُولَ مِمَّن يَنقَلِبُ عَلَىٰ عَقِبَيۡهِۚ وَإِن كَانَتۡ لَكَبِيرَةً إِلَّا عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ هَدَى ٱللَّهُۗ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيُضِيعَ إِيمَٰنَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِٱلنَّاسِ لَرَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 2:143)

**"And thus We have made you a middle nation, that you will be witnesses over the people."** *Ummatan wasatan*: balanced, just and the best, avoiding extremes.

﴿قَدۡ نَرَىٰ تَقَلُّبَ وَجۡهِكَ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِۖ فَلَنُوَلِّيَنَّكَ قِبۡلَةٗ تَرۡضَىٰهَاۚ فَوَلِّ وَجۡهَكَ شَطۡرَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۚ وَحَيۡثُ مَا كُنتُمۡ فَوَلُّواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ شَطۡرَهُۥۗ وَإِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ لَيَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:144)

The qiblah was changed from Jerusalem to the Ka'bah: **"So turn your face toward al-Masjid al-Haram."**

﴿فَٱذۡكُرُونِيٓ أَذۡكُرۡكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لِي وَلَا تَكۡفُرُونِ﴾ (Quran 2:152)

**"So remember Me; I will remember you. And be grateful to Me and do not deny Me."**

## Verses 153–157: Patience and Prayer
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱسۡتَعِينُواْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:153)

**"O you who believe, seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, Allah is with the patient."**

﴿وَلَنَبۡلُوَنَّكُم بِشَيۡءٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡخَوۡفِ وَٱلۡجُوعِ وَنَقۡصٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَنفُسِ وَٱلثَّمَرَٰتِۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَصَٰبَتۡهُم مُّصِيبَةࣱ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّآ إِلَيۡهِ رَٰجِعُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ صَلَوَٰتࣱ مِّن رَّبِّهِمۡ وَرَحۡمَةࣱۖ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُهۡتَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:155-157)

**"And We will surely test you with something of fear and hunger and a loss of wealth and lives and fruits, but give good tidings to the patient, who, when disaster strikes them, say: Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we will return."** **"Those are the ones upon whom are blessings from their Lord and mercy. And it is those who are the rightly guided."**

## Verses 177–203: Righteousness, Fasting and Hajj
﴿۞لَّيۡسَ ٱلۡبِرَّ أَن تُوَلُّواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ قِبَلَ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبِ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلۡبِرَّ مَنۡ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَ وَءَاتَى ٱلۡمَالَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ ذَوِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينَ وَٱبۡنَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَٱلسَّآئِلِينَ وَفِي ٱلرِّقَابِ وَأَقَامَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتَى ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَٱلۡمُوفُونَ بِعَهۡدِهِمۡ إِذَا عَٰهَدُواْۖ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ فِي ٱلۡبَأۡسَآءِ وَٱلضَّرَّآءِ وَحِينَ ٱلۡبَأۡسِۗ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ صَدَقُواْۖ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُتَّقُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:177)

**"Righteousness is not that you turn your faces toward the east or the west, but righteousness is in one who believes in Allah..."** True righteousness combines belief, giving to those in need, prayer, zakah, keeping promises and patience.

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:183)

**"O you who believe, decreed upon you is fasting as it was decreed upon those before you, that you may become righteous."** The goal of fasting is taqwa.

﴿وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌۖ أُجِيبُ دَعۡوَةَ ٱلدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِۖ فَلۡيَسۡتَجِيبُواْ لِي وَلۡيُؤۡمِنُواْ بِي لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡشُدُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:186)

**"And when My servants ask you concerning Me, indeed I am near. I respond to the invocation of the supplicant when he calls upon Me."** Placed among the verses of fasting, as a reminder that the fasting person's du'a is close to acceptance.

﴿وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَقُولُ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٗ وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ حَسَنَةٗ وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ (Quran 2:201)

**"Our Lord, give us in this world that which is good and in the hereafter that which is good and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Anas said this was the du'a the Prophet ﷺ made most often.

## Verses 204–242: Society and Family
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱدۡخُلُواْ فِي ٱلسِّلۡمِ كَآفَّةٗ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوࣱّ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ (Quran 2:208)

**"O you who believe, enter into Islam completely."** Not selectively.

﴿كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡقِتَالُ وَهُوَ كُرۡهࣱ لَّكُمۡۖ وَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تَكۡرَهُواْ شَيۡـٔٗا وَهُوَ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡۖ وَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تُحِبُّواْ شَيۡـٔٗا وَهُوَ شَرࣱّ لَّكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:216)

**"But perhaps you hate a thing and it is good for you; and perhaps you love a thing and it is bad for you. And Allah knows, while you know not."**

**Family law**: divorce may be revoked twice (**"Divorce is twice; then either keep in an acceptable manner or release with good treatment"**), the waiting period, nursing for two full years (verse 233), and repeated reminders: **"and do not forget graciousness between you"**.

## Verses 243–252: Talut and Jalut
﴿فَلَمَّا فَصَلَ طَالُوتُ بِٱلۡجُنُودِ قَالَ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مُبۡتَلِيكُم بِنَهَرٖ فَمَن شَرِبَ مِنۡهُ فَلَيۡسَ مِنِّي وَمَن لَّمۡ يَطۡعَمۡهُ فَإِنَّهُۥ مِنِّيٓ إِلَّا مَنِ ٱغۡتَرَفَ غُرۡفَةَۢ بِيَدِهِۦۚ فَشَرِبُواْ مِنۡهُ إِلَّا قَلِيلٗا مِّنۡهُمۡۚ فَلَمَّا جَاوَزَهُۥ هُوَ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مَعَهُۥ قَالُواْ لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ بِجَالُوتَ وَجُنُودِهِۦۚ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَظُنُّونَ أَنَّهُم مُّلَٰقُواْ ٱللَّهِ كَم مِّن فِئَةٖ قَلِيلَةٍ غَلَبَتۡ فِئَةٗ كَثِيرَةَۢ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:249)

The small army of Talut faced Jalut's mighty forces. Those certain of meeting Allah said: **"How many a small company has overcome a large company by permission of Allah. And Allah is with the patient."** And Dawud killed Jalut.

## Verse 255: Ayat al-Kursi
﴿ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلۡحَيُّ ٱلۡقَيُّومُۚ لَا تَأۡخُذُهُۥ سِنَةࣱ وَلَا نَوۡمࣱۚ لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِي يَشۡفَعُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِهِۦۚ يَعۡلَمُ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيۡءٖ مِّنۡ عِلۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَۚ وَسِعَ كُرۡسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۖ وَلَا يَـُٔودُهُۥ حِفۡظُهُمَاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ (Quran 2:255)

**The greatest verse in the Quran**: it describes Allah as the Ever-Living, the Sustainer; neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him; to Him belongs everything; no one intercedes except with His permission; He knows what is before and behind them; they encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills; His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth; preserving them does not tire Him; and He is the Most High, the Most Great.

**Recite it** after every obligatory prayer and before sleeping.

## Verses 256–260: No Compulsion and Three Stories
﴿لَآ إِكۡرَاهَ فِي ٱلدِّينِۖ قَد تَّبَيَّنَ ٱلرُّشۡدُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَيِّۚ فَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بِٱلطَّـٰغُوتِ وَيُؤۡمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدِ ٱسۡتَمۡسَكَ بِٱلۡعُرۡوَةِ ٱلۡوُثۡقَىٰ لَا ٱنفِصَامَ لَهَاۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 2:256)

**"There shall be no compulsion in religion. The right course has become clear from the wrong."** Whoever disbelieves in false gods and believes in Allah **"has grasped the most trustworthy handhold"**.

Then three stories about life and death: Ibrahim debating the king who claimed to give life and death, the man who passed a ruined town and was made to die for a hundred years and then raised, and Ibrahim asking to see how Allah gives life to the dead, with the four birds.

## Verses 261–274: Charity
﴿مَّثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمۡوَٰلَهُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ حَبَّةٍ أَنۢبَتَتۡ سَبۡعَ سَنَابِلَ فِي كُلِّ سُنۢبُلَةٖ مِّاْئَةُ حَبَّةٖۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُضَٰعِفُ لِمَن يَشَآءُۚ وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 2:261)

**"The example of those who spend their wealth in the way of Allah is like a seed which grows seven spikes; in each spike is a hundred grains. And Allah multiplies for whom He wills."**

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُبۡطِلُواْ صَدَقَٰتِكُم بِٱلۡمَنِّ وَٱلۡأَذَىٰ كَٱلَّذِي يُنفِقُ مَالَهُۥ رِئَآءَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَا يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۖ فَمَثَلُهُۥ كَمَثَلِ صَفۡوَانٍ عَلَيۡهِ تُرَابࣱ فَأَصَابَهُۥ وَابِلࣱ فَتَرَكَهُۥ صَلۡدٗاۖ لَّا يَقۡدِرُونَ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٖ مِّمَّا كَسَبُواْۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:264)

**"O you who believe, do not invalidate your charities with reminders or injury."**

## Verses 275–283: Riba and Debts
﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْ لَا يَقُومُونَ إِلَّا كَمَا يَقُومُ ٱلَّذِي يَتَخَبَّطُهُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ مِنَ ٱلۡمَسِّۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡبَيۡعُ مِثۡلُ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْۗ وَأَحَلَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡبَيۡعَ وَحَرَّمَ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْۚ فَمَن جَآءَهُۥ مَوۡعِظَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ فَٱنتَهَىٰ فَلَهُۥ مَا سَلَفَ وَأَمۡرُهُۥٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَمَنۡ عَادَ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ﴾ ﴿يَمۡحَقُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرِّبَوٰاْ وَيُرۡبِي ٱلصَّدَقَٰتِۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ كَفَّارٍ أَثِيمٍ﴾ (Quran 2:275-276)

**"Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden interest."** **"Allah destroys interest and gives increase for charities."** In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas said the last verse revealed to the Prophet ﷺ was the verse of riba.

﴿وَٱتَّقُواْ يَوۡمٗا تُرۡجَعُونَ فِيهِ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ ثُمَّ تُوَفَّىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ مَّا كَسَبَتۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 2:281)

**"And fear a Day when you will be returned to Allah. Then every soul will be compensated for what it earned."**

**Verse 282**, the longest verse in the Quran, commands writing down debts with witnesses: Islam's care for clear, documented transactions.

## Verses 284–286: The Final Two Verses
﴿ءَامَنَ ٱلرَّسُولُ بِمَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيۡهِ مِن رَّبِّهِۦ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَۚ كُلٌّ ءَامَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتِهِۦ وَكُتُبِهِۦ وَرُسُلِهِۦ لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيۡنَ أَحَدٖ مِّن رُّسُلِهِۦۚ وَقَالُواْ سَمِعۡنَا وَأَطَعۡنَاۖ غُفۡرَانَكَ رَبَّنَا وَإِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ ﴿لَا يُكَلِّفُ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتۡ وَعَلَيۡهَا مَا ٱكۡتَسَبَتۡۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذۡنَآ إِن نَّسِينَآ أَوۡ أَخۡطَأۡنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحۡمِلۡ عَلَيۡنَآ إِصۡرٗا كَمَا حَمَلۡتَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِنَاۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلۡنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِۦۖ وَٱعۡفُ عَنَّا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَآۚ أَنتَ مَوۡلَىٰنَا فَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:285-286)

**"The Messenger has believed in what was revealed to him from his Lord, and so have the believers."** **"Allah does not charge a soul except with that within its capacity."** Then the du'a: **"Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us if we forget or make a mistake... and pardon us, and forgive us, and have mercy upon us. You are our Protector, so give us victory over the disbelieving people."** In Sahih Muslim, after each part of this du'a, Allah said: "I have done so."

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Baqarah**: the cow.
- **Al-Ghayb**: the unseen.
- **Al-Muttaqin**: those conscious of Allah.
- **Khalifah**: successor, vicegerent.
- **Ummatan wasatan**: a middle, balanced nation.
- **Qiblah**: direction of prayer.
- **Kutiba**: decreed.
- **Al-Kursi**: the footstool.
- **Al-'Urwah al-wuthqa**: the most trustworthy handhold.
- **Ar-Riba**: interest, usury.
- **Wus'aha**: its capacity.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **The answer to Al-Fatihah**: "Guide us" answered by "a guidance".
2. **Three groups in the opening**: believers, disbelievers and hypocrites.
3. **The cow's endless questions**: a lesson in simplicity.
4. **"I am near"**: placed among the verses of fasting.
5. **The seed with seven spikes**: charity multiplied.
6. **"I have done so"**: Allah's answer to the final du'a.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Alif Lam Mim*.
- **Idgham without ghunnah**: in *hudan lil-muttaqin* (verse 2), the tanween merges into the lam.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *yunfiqun* (verse 3), the noon sakinah is hidden before the fa'.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ula'ika* (verse 5) and *as-sama'i* (verse 22).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *qiblatan* (verse 144), the ba' sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Al-Fatihah ends with "Guide us to the straight path", and Al-Baqarah answers: "This is the Book... a guidance". Surah Al 'Imran after it is its companion, the second of "the two bright ones".

## Practical Lessons
1. **Recite Al-Baqarah at home regularly.**
2. **Examine yourself against the three groups.**
3. **Obey simply**, without needless questions.
4. **Ask for acceptance** of your deeds, as Ibrahim did.
5. **Be balanced**, as a middle nation.
6. **Seek help through patience and prayer.**
7. **Say "Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un"** in loss.
8. **Recite Ayat al-Kursi after prayers and before sleep.**
9. **Avoid riba completely.**
10. **Recite the last two verses every night.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Baqarah
Memorise it in stages over several months: one page a day with daily review, or by its natural sections: verses 1–20, 21–39, 40–74, 75–123, 124–152, 153–203, 204–252 and 253–286. Start with Ayat al-Kursi and the last two verses.

## Teaching Children
- **Teach them Ayat al-Kursi first.**
- **Tell them the story of the cow** and why too many questions made it hard.
- **Teach them the last two verses** to recite before sleeping.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is it called Surah Al-Baqarah?
Because of the story of the cow in verses 67–73.

### What is the virtue of reciting Al-Baqarah at home?
The Prophet ﷺ said Satan flees from a house in which it is recited, as in Sahih Muslim.

### What is Ayat al-Kursi?
Verse 255, the greatest verse in the Quran, describing Allah's life, knowledge, power and dominion.

### What is the virtue of the last two verses?
Whoever recites them at night, they will suffice him, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does "a middle nation" mean?
A balanced, just nation avoiding extremes and witnessing over humanity.

### What is the longest verse in the Quran?
Verse 282 of Al-Baqarah, about recording debts.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Baqarah?
286 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Baqarah is the answer to the prayer of Al-Fatihah: a complete guidance for a balanced community. It tells you who you are, where you came from and how to live, and it ends by assuring you that Allah does not burden any soul beyond its capacity. Recite it in your home, keep Ayat al-Kursi on your tongue, and end every night with its final du'a, to which Allah replied: I have done so.
