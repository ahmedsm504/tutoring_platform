Surah Al-Munafiqun is a mirror held up to one of the most dangerous diseases of the heart: saying with the tongue what the heart does not believe. It describes the hypocrites of Madinah with a sharpness that makes them unmistakable: they swore oaths to hide behind, they looked impressive and spoke eloquently, yet they were hollow inside like timber propped against a wall. It tells the true story of a young Companion, Zayd ibn Arqam, whose report was denied by a powerful man until Allah confirmed him from above. And it ends with advice for every believer: do not let wealth and children distract you from Allah, and spend before the day you beg for one more moment to give charity.

This guide explains Surah Al-Munafiqun verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons for guarding the heart.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Munafiqun
- **Number of verses**: 11.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 63, in the twenty-eighth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Munafiqun means "the hypocrites".
- **Recited in Friday prayer**: in Sahih Muslim, Ibn 'Abbas reported that the Prophet ﷺ recited Al-Jumu'ah and Al-Munafiqun in the Friday prayer.

## The Story of Zayd ibn Arqam
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Zayd ibn Arqam said: "I was on an expedition and heard 'Abdullah ibn Ubayy saying: *Do not spend on those who are with the Messenger of Allah until they disperse from around him*, and *If we return to Madinah, the more honourable will surely expel the more humble*. I told my uncle, who told the Prophet ﷺ. He sent for 'Abdullah ibn Ubayy and his companions, and they swore they had not said it. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ believed them and did not believe me, and I was struck with a grief like I had never felt. I stayed at home. Then Allah revealed: *When the hypocrites come to you...* The Prophet ﷺ sent for me, recited it to me, and said: Allah has confirmed you, O Zayd."

**And the Prophet's ﷺ restraint**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Jabir reported that 'Umar asked permission to kill the leader of the hypocrites, and the Prophet ﷺ said: "Leave him, so that people do not say that Muhammad kills his companions."

**Lessons**:
1. **Truth is confirmed by Allah**, even when powerful people deny it.
2. **Young people's honesty matters**: Zayd was a young man.
3. **Wisdom in leadership**: the Prophet ﷺ weighed the wider consequences.
4. **Hypocrisy often hides behind oaths.**

## Verses 1–3: False Testimony
﴿إِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ قَالُواْ نَشۡهَدُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُ ٱللَّهِۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱللَّهُ يَشۡهَدُ إِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ لَكَٰذِبُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ أَيۡمَٰنَهُمۡ جُنَّةٗ فَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّهُمۡ سَآءَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمۡ ءَامَنُواْ ثُمَّ كَفَرُواْ فَطُبِعَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يَفۡقَهُونَ﴾ (Quran 63:1-3)

**"When the hypocrites come to you, they say: We testify that you are the Messenger of Allah."** Allah's response is precise: **"And Allah knows that you are His Messenger, and Allah testifies that the hypocrites are liars."** The statement was true; their testimony was a lie, because it did not come from the heart.

**"They have taken their oaths as a cover"** (*junnah*), a shield to protect themselves while they turned people away from Allah's path.

**"That is because they believed, then they disbelieved; so their hearts were sealed over."** Hypocrisy is often a journey: faith, then wavering, then rejection, until the heart is sealed.

## Verse 4: Propped-Up Timber
﴿۞وَإِذَا رَأَيۡتَهُمۡ تُعۡجِبُكَ أَجۡسَامُهُمۡۖ وَإِن يَقُولُواْ تَسۡمَعۡ لِقَوۡلِهِمۡۖ كَأَنَّهُمۡ خُشُبࣱ مُّسَنَّدَةࣱۖ يَحۡسَبُونَ كُلَّ صَيۡحَةٍ عَلَيۡهِمۡۚ هُمُ ٱلۡعَدُوُّ فَٱحۡذَرۡهُمۡۚ قَٰتَلَهُمُ ٱللَّهُۖ أَنَّىٰ يُؤۡفَكُونَ﴾ (Quran 63:4)

**"When you see them, their forms please you, and if they speak, you listen to their speech."** Impressive bodies, polished words. **"They are like pieces of wood propped up"**: large, but dry and lifeless inside, leaning on others, contributing nothing. **"They think that every shout is against them"**: a guilty conscience that fears every sound. **"They are the enemy, so beware of them."**

## Verses 5–6: Turning Their Heads Away
﴿وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ تَعَالَوۡاْ يَسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ لَوَّوۡاْ رُءُوسَهُمۡ وَرَأَيۡتَهُمۡ يَصُدُّونَ وَهُم مُّسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ﴾ ﴿سَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَسۡتَغۡفَرۡتَ لَهُمۡ أَمۡ لَمۡ تَسۡتَغۡفِرۡ لَهُمۡ لَن يَغۡفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَٰسِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 63:5-6)

**"When it is said to them: Come, the Messenger of Allah will ask forgiveness for you, they turn their heads aside"** in arrogance. **"It is all the same for them whether you ask forgiveness for them or do not; never will Allah forgive them."** As long as they persist in hypocrisy, forgiveness will not reach them.

## Verses 7–8: The Two Sayings
﴿هُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ لَا تُنفِقُواْ عَلَىٰ مَنۡ عِندَ رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ حَتَّىٰ يَنفَضُّواْۗ وَلِلَّهِ خَزَآئِنُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ لَا يَفۡقَهُونَ﴾ ﴿يَقُولُونَ لَئِن رَّجَعۡنَآ إِلَى ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ لَيُخۡرِجَنَّ ٱلۡأَعَزُّ مِنۡهَا ٱلۡأَذَلَّۚ وَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ وَلِرَسُولِهِۦ وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 63:7-8)

**First saying**: **"Do not spend on those who are with the Messenger of Allah until they disperse."** They thought they could starve the believers into leaving. **"And to Allah belong the depositories of the heavens and the earth."**

**Second saying**: **"If we return to Madinah, the more honoured will surely expel the more humble."** **"And to Allah belongs honour, and to His Messenger, and to the believers, but the hypocrites do not know."**

**True honour** (*'izzah*): the hypocrites thought honour came from wealth, tribe and power. The verse corrects this: real honour comes from Allah and through obedience to Him.

## Verse 9: Do Not Let Them Distract You
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تُلۡهِكُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَمَن يَفۡعَلۡ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 63:9)

**"O you who believe, let not your wealth and your children divert you from the remembrance of Allah. And whoever does that, those are the losers."** After describing the hypocrites, the surah turns to the believers. Wealth and children are not evil, but when they take the place of remembrance, they become a loss.

## Verses 10–11: Spend Before Death Comes
﴿وَأَنفِقُواْ مِن مَّا رَزَقۡنَٰكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ أَحَدَكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ فَيَقُولَ رَبِّ لَوۡلَآ أَخَّرۡتَنِيٓ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ قَرِيبٖ فَأَصَّدَّقَ وَأَكُن مِّنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَن يُؤَخِّرَ ٱللَّهُ نَفۡسًا إِذَا جَآءَ أَجَلُهَاۚ وَٱللَّهُ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 63:10-11)

**"And spend from what We have provided you before death approaches one of you and he says: My Lord, if only You would delay me for a brief term so I would give charity and be among the righteous."** Notice what the dying person asks for: not more time for pleasure, but time **to give charity**. At that moment he sees its value clearly.

**"But never will Allah delay a soul when its time has come."** The lesson: give now, while you can.

## The Signs of Hypocrisy
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Four qualities, whoever has them is a pure hypocrite, and whoever has one of them has a quality of hypocrisy until he leaves it: when he is entrusted, he betrays; when he speaks, he lies; when he makes a promise, he breaks it; and when he argues, he behaves wickedly."

**Important distinction**: scholars explain that there is hypocrisy of belief, which is disbelief hidden behind a show of Islam, and hypocrisy of action, which is having some of these qualities while still being a believer. The believer fears the second type for himself and works to remove it.

**The Companions feared it**: Ibn Abi Mulaykah said, as mentioned by al-Bukhari: "I met thirty of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ, and every one of them feared hypocrisy for himself."

## Word Meanings

- **Junnah**: shield, cover.
- **Tubi'a**: sealed.
- **Khushub musannadah**: propped-up timber.
- **Sayhah**: shout.
- **Yu'fakun**: deluded, turned away.
- **Lawwaw**: turned aside.
- **Yanfaddu**: disperse.
- **Khaza'in**: treasuries, depositories.
- **Al-A'azz**: the more honourable.
- **Al-Adhall**: the more humble.
- **'Izzah**: honour, might.
- **Tulhikum**: divert you.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Allah testifies that the hypocrites are liars"**: Allah's testimony against theirs.
2. **"Propped-up timber"**: an image of empty grandeur.
3. **"They think every shout is against them"**: the psychology of guilt.
4. **"To Allah belong the depositories"**: a response to economic threats.
5. **"If only You would delay me... so I would give charity"**: the dying man's regret, focused on charity.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ja'aka* (verse 1) and *ja'a* (verse 11).
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *khushubun musannadah* (verse 4), the tanween merges into the meem.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna al-munafiqina* (verse 1), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Jumu'ah before it ended with people leaving the Prophet ﷺ for a caravan, and Al-Munafiqun shows the extreme of preferring the world: hypocrites who threatened to cut off spending. Both were recited together in Friday prayer. Surah At-Taghabun after it continues the warning about wealth and children: "Your wealth and your children are but a trial."

## Protecting Yourself From Hypocrisy
1. **Make your private life better than your public life.**
2. **Keep promises and trusts.**
3. **Speak the truth**, even against yourself.
4. **Control your anger in disputes.**
5. **Pray Fajr and 'Isha in congregation**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said these are the heaviest prayers on the hypocrites.
6. **Remember Allah much**: the hypocrites "do not remember Allah except a little" (Surah An-Nisa').
7. **Fear hypocrisy for yourself**, as the Companions did.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Do not be impressed by appearances and words alone.**
2. **Honour belongs to obedience to Allah**, not money or status.
3. **Do not let wealth and children divert you.**
4. **Give charity now**, not "when I have more".
5. **Prepare for death**: it will not be delayed.
6. **Speak up for the truth** like Zayd, even when young.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them the story of Zayd ibn Arqam**, the young man Allah confirmed.
- **Teach them that honesty in private matters most.**
- **Start a charity jar** and explain the dying man's wish.

## The Dying Man's Request
Verse 10 deserves a pause. Of all the things a dying person could ask for, the verse records this: "If only You would delay me for a brief term so I would give charity." Not more time with family, not one more trip, not finishing a project, but charity. At death, the veil lifts and a person sees what truly counts. Wise people act on that clarity now. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, a man asked the Prophet ﷺ which charity is greatest in reward. He said: "That you give charity while you are healthy and greedy, fearing poverty and hoping to be rich. Do not delay until the soul reaches the throat and you say: to so-and-so this much, and to so-and-so this much, when it already belongs to so-and-so."

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who were the hypocrites in Madinah?
People who showed Islam outwardly while hiding disbelief; their leader was 'Abdullah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul.

### What is the story behind Surah Al-Munafiqun?
Zayd ibn Arqam reported words of 'Abdullah ibn Ubayy, who denied them under oath, and Allah revealed the surah confirming Zayd, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What does "like pieces of wood propped up" mean?
They look large and impressive but are empty and lifeless inside, contributing nothing.

### What does "to Allah belongs honour" mean?
Real honour comes from Allah and obedience to Him, not from wealth or tribe.

### What are the signs of a hypocrite?
Betraying trusts, lying, breaking promises and behaving wickedly in disputes, as in the hadith of 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Why does the dying man ask for time to give charity?
Because at death he sees clearly the value of charity he neglected.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Munafiqun?
11 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

### Is the hypocrisy in this surah the same as having a sign of a hypocrite?
No. The surah describes hypocrisy of belief, which is disbelief; having a quality like lying or breaking promises is hypocrisy of action, a sin a believer must repent from.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Munafiqun exposes those whose words are polished and whose hearts are hollow, and reminds us that honour belongs to Allah, His Messenger and the believers. Then it turns to us: do not let wealth and children distract you, and give now, before you find yourself asking for one more moment that will not be given.
