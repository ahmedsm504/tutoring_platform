Surah Al-Ahzab was revealed around one of the most dangerous moments in the life of the Muslim community: the Battle of the Confederates, or the Trench, when an army of ten thousand surrounded Madinah. "When eyes shifted and hearts reached the throats", the hypocrites said Allah's promise was a delusion, while the believers said: "This is what Allah and His Messenger promised us." In the middle of this crisis comes the verse that defines how Muslims live: "There has certainly been for you in the Messenger of Allah an excellent example." The surah also sets out laws for the family and the Prophet's ﷺ household, honours the Mothers of the Believers, declares the Prophet ﷺ the Seal of the Prophets, commands sending blessings upon him, and ends with the Trust that the heavens, the earth and the mountains refused to carry.

This guide explains Surah Al-Ahzab section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Ahzab
- **Number of verses**: 73.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 33, in the twenty-first and twenty-second juz.
- **Name**: Al-Ahzab means "the Confederates", the allied tribes who besieged Madinah in 5 AH.
- **Main themes**: obedience to Allah, family law, the Battle of the Trench, the Prophet's ﷺ example and household, and the Trust.

## Verses 1–3: Fear Allah and Rely on Him
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ ٱتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ وَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمٗا﴾ ﴿وَٱتَّبِعۡ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡكَ مِن رَّبِّكَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ وَكِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 33:1-3)

**"O Prophet, fear Allah and do not obey the disbelievers and the hypocrites."** **"Follow that which is revealed to you from your Lord."** **"And rely upon Allah. And sufficient is Allah as Disposer of affairs."**

## Verses 4–6: Two Hearts and Adoption
﴿مَّا جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِرَجُلٖ مِّن قَلۡبَيۡنِ فِي جَوۡفِهِۦۚ وَمَا جَعَلَ أَزۡوَٰجَكُمُ ٱلَّـٰٓـِٔي تُظَٰهِرُونَ مِنۡهُنَّ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡۚ وَمَا جَعَلَ أَدۡعِيَآءَكُمۡ أَبۡنَآءَكُمۡۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ قَوۡلُكُم بِأَفۡوَٰهِكُمۡۖ وَٱللَّهُ يَقُولُ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَهُوَ يَهۡدِي ٱلسَّبِيلَ﴾ ﴿ٱدۡعُوهُمۡ لِأٓبَآئِهِمۡ هُوَ أَقۡسَطُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِۚ فَإِن لَّمۡ تَعۡلَمُوٓاْ ءَابَآءَهُمۡ فَإِخۡوَٰنُكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَمَوَٰلِيكُمۡۚ وَلَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحࣱ فِيمَآ أَخۡطَأۡتُم بِهِۦ وَلَٰكِن مَّا تَعَمَّدَتۡ قُلُوبُكُمۡۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمًا﴾ (Quran 33:4-5)

**"Allah has not made for a man two hearts in his interior."** A person cannot hold two contradictory loyalties. The verse also abolishes pre-Islamic customs: a wife does not become a mother by *zihar*, and an adopted son does not become a real son.

**"Call them by the names of their fathers; it is more just in the sight of Allah."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Umar said: "We used to call Zayd ibn Harithah 'Zayd ibn Muhammad' until the Quran was revealed: *Call them by the names of their fathers*." Islam keeps lineage clear while encouraging care for orphans and foster children: **"But if you do not know their fathers, then they are your brothers in religion."**

﴿ٱلنَّبِيُّ أَوۡلَىٰ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ مِنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡۖ وَأَزۡوَٰجُهُۥٓ أُمَّهَٰتُهُمۡۗ وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلَىٰ بِبَعۡضٖ فِي كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ إِلَّآ أَن تَفۡعَلُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَوۡلِيَآئِكُم مَّعۡرُوفٗاۚ كَانَ ذَٰلِكَ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَسۡطُورٗا﴾ (Quran 33:6)

**"The Prophet is more worthy of the believers than themselves, and his wives are their mothers."** The Mothers of the Believers are honoured and could not be married after him.

## Verses 9–27: The Battle of the Trench
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ جَآءَتۡكُمۡ جُنُودࣱ فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ رِيحٗا وَجُنُودٗا لَّمۡ تَرَوۡهَاۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرًا﴾ ﴿إِذۡ جَآءُوكُم مِّن فَوۡقِكُمۡ وَمِنۡ أَسۡفَلَ مِنكُمۡ وَإِذۡ زَاغَتِ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ وَبَلَغَتِ ٱلۡقُلُوبُ ٱلۡحَنَاجِرَ وَتَظُنُّونَ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلظُّنُونَا۠﴾ ﴿هُنَالِكَ ٱبۡتُلِيَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَزُلۡزِلُواْ زِلۡزَالٗا شَدِيدٗا﴾ (Quran 33:9-11)

**"Remember the favour of Allah upon you when armies came to you and We sent upon them a wind and armies you did not see."** **"When they came at you from above you and from below you, and when eyes shifted and hearts reached the throats, and you assumed about Allah various assumptions. There the believers were tested and shaken with a severe shaking."**

﴿وَإِذۡ يَقُولُ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضࣱ مَّا وَعَدَنَا ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ إِلَّا غُرُورٗا﴾ ﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَت طَّآئِفَةࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَـٰٓأَهۡلَ يَثۡرِبَ لَا مُقَامَ لَكُمۡ فَٱرۡجِعُواْۚ وَيَسۡتَـٔۡذِنُ فَرِيقࣱ مِّنۡهُمُ ٱلنَّبِيَّ يَقُولُونَ إِنَّ بُيُوتَنَا عَوۡرَةࣱ وَمَا هِيَ بِعَوۡرَةٍۖ إِن يُرِيدُونَ إِلَّا فِرَارٗا﴾ (Quran 33:12-13)

**The hypocrites**: **"Allah and His Messenger did not promise us except delusion."** Some asked permission to leave, saying: **"Our houses are exposed"**, though they were not; they only wanted to flee.

﴿لَّقَدۡ كَانَ لَكُمۡ فِي رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ أُسۡوَةٌ حَسَنَةࣱ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرۡجُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَ وَذَكَرَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 33:21)

**"There has certainly been for you in the Messenger of Allah an excellent example for whoever has hope in Allah and the Last Day and remembers Allah often."** Revealed during the siege, when the Prophet ﷺ dug the trench with his own hands and endured hunger with his Companions. Scholars say the verse is a general principle: follow him in every aspect of life.

﴿وَلَمَّا رَءَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابَ قَالُواْ هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَنَا ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَصَدَقَ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥۚ وَمَا زَادَهُمۡ إِلَّآ إِيمَٰنٗا وَتَسۡلِيمٗا﴾ ﴿مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رِجَالࣱ صَدَقُواْ مَا عَٰهَدُواْ ٱللَّهَ عَلَيۡهِۖ فَمِنۡهُم مَّن قَضَىٰ نَحۡبَهُۥ وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَنتَظِرُۖ وَمَا بَدَّلُواْ تَبۡدِيلٗا﴾ (Quran 33:22-23)

**The believers**: **"When the believers saw the confederates, they said: This is what Allah and His Messenger had promised us, and Allah and His Messenger spoke the truth. And it increased them only in faith and acceptance."**

**"Among the believers are men true to what they promised Allah."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Anas said this verse was revealed about his uncle Anas ibn an-Nadr, who had missed Badr and promised Allah that if he witnessed another battle, Allah would see what he would do. At Uhud he fought until he was killed, with more than eighty wounds.

﴿وَرَدَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِغَيۡظِهِمۡ لَمۡ يَنَالُواْ خَيۡرٗاۚ وَكَفَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ ٱلۡقِتَالَۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ قَوِيًّا عَزِيزٗا﴾ (Quran 33:25)

**"And Allah repelled those who disbelieved, in their rage, not having obtained any good. And sufficient was Allah for the believers in battle."** A wind and unseen soldiers broke the siege.

## Verses 28–34: The Prophet's ﷺ Wives
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ قُل لِّأَزۡوَٰجِكَ إِن كُنتُنَّ تُرِدۡنَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَزِينَتَهَا فَتَعَالَيۡنَ أُمَتِّعۡكُنَّ وَأُسَرِّحۡكُنَّ سَرَاحٗا جَمِيلٗا﴾ ﴿وَإِن كُنتُنَّ تُرِدۡنَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَٱلدَّارَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَعَدَّ لِلۡمُحۡسِنَٰتِ مِنكُنَّ أَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا﴾ (Quran 33:28-29)

**The choice**: the Prophet's ﷺ wives were offered the choice between the adornment of this world and Allah, His Messenger and the home of the hereafter. They all chose Allah and His Messenger.

﴿يَٰنِسَآءَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ لَسۡتُنَّ كَأَحَدٖ مِّنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ إِنِ ٱتَّقَيۡتُنَّۚ فَلَا تَخۡضَعۡنَ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ فَيَطۡمَعَ ٱلَّذِي فِي قَلۡبِهِۦ مَرَضࣱ وَقُلۡنَ قَوۡلٗا مَّعۡرُوفٗا﴾ ﴿وَقَرۡنَ فِي بُيُوتِكُنَّ وَلَا تَبَرَّجۡنَ تَبَرُّجَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰۖ وَأَقِمۡنَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتِينَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَطِعۡنَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّمَا يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ لِيُذۡهِبَ عَنكُمُ ٱلرِّجۡسَ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡبَيۡتِ وَيُطَهِّرَكُمۡ تَطۡهِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 33:32-33)

**"O wives of the Prophet, you are not like anyone among women."** Guidance on speech, staying in their homes, prayer, zakah and obedience. **"Allah intends only to remove from you the impurity, O people of the household, and to purify you with extensive purification."**

## Verse 35: Ten Qualities of Believing Men and Women
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ وَٱلۡمُسۡلِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰدِقَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرَٰتِ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعِينَ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُتَصَدِّقِينَ وَٱلۡمُتَصَدِّقَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰٓئِمِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰٓئِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظِينَ فُرُوجَهُمۡ وَٱلۡحَٰفِظَٰتِ وَٱلذَّـٰكِرِينَ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا وَٱلذَّـٰكِرَٰتِ أَعَدَّ ٱللَّهُ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةٗ وَأَجۡرًا عَظِيمٗا﴾ (Quran 33:35)

**Ten pairs**, each mentioned for men and women equally: Muslims, believers, the devoutly obedient, the truthful, the patient, the humble, those who give charity, those who fast, those who guard their chastity, and **those who remember Allah often**. **"For them Allah has prepared forgiveness and a great reward."** Men and women stand equal in spiritual reward.

## Verses 36–40: Submission and the Seal of the Prophets
﴿وَمَا كَانَ لِمُؤۡمِنٖ وَلَا مُؤۡمِنَةٍ إِذَا قَضَى ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ أَمۡرًا أَن يَكُونَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡخِيَرَةُ مِنۡ أَمۡرِهِمۡۗ وَمَن يَعۡصِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَقَدۡ ضَلَّ ضَلَٰلٗا مُّبِينٗا﴾ (Quran 33:36)

**"It is not for a believing man or a believing woman, when Allah and His Messenger have decided a matter, that they should thereafter have any choice about their affair."**

﴿وَإِذۡ تَقُولُ لِلَّذِيٓ أَنۡعَمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِ وَأَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيۡهِ أَمۡسِكۡ عَلَيۡكَ زَوۡجَكَ وَٱتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ وَتُخۡفِي فِي نَفۡسِكَ مَا ٱللَّهُ مُبۡدِيهِ وَتَخۡشَى ٱلنَّاسَ وَٱللَّهُ أَحَقُّ أَن تَخۡشَىٰهُۖ فَلَمَّا قَضَىٰ زَيۡدࣱ مِّنۡهَا وَطَرٗا زَوَّجۡنَٰكَهَا لِكَيۡ لَا يَكُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ حَرَجࣱ فِيٓ أَزۡوَٰجِ أَدۡعِيَآئِهِمۡ إِذَا قَضَوۡاْ مِنۡهُنَّ وَطَرٗاۚ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ مَفۡعُولٗا﴾ (Quran 33:37)

**The story of Zayd and Zaynab**: Allah commanded the Prophet ﷺ to marry Zaynab bint Jahsh after her divorce from Zayd, to abolish the custom that forbade marrying an adopted son's former wife. **"And you concealed within yourself that which Allah is to disclose, and you feared the people, while Allah has more right that you fear Him."** 'A'ishah said that if the Prophet ﷺ had concealed anything of the revelation, he would have concealed this verse, showing how faithfully he conveyed everything, as reported in the Sahih collections.

﴿مَّا كَانَ مُحَمَّدٌ أَبَآ أَحَدٖ مِّن رِّجَالِكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن رَّسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَخَاتَمَ ٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٗا﴾ (Quran 33:40)

**"Muhammad is not the father of any one of your men, but he is the Messenger of Allah and the Seal of the Prophets."** There is no prophet after him.

## Verses 41–48: Remember Allah Much
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ ذِكۡرٗا كَثِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَسَبِّحُوهُ بُكۡرَةٗ وَأَصِيلًا﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُصَلِّي عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتُهُۥ لِيُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِۚ وَكَانَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَحِيمٗا﴾ (Quran 33:41-43)

**"O you who believe, remember Allah with much remembrance, and exalt Him morning and afternoon. It is He who confers blessing upon you, and His angels, that He may bring you out from darknesses into the light."**

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ شَٰهِدٗا وَمُبَشِّرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَدَاعِيًا إِلَى ٱللَّهِ بِإِذۡنِهِۦ وَسِرَاجٗا مُّنِيرٗا﴾ (Quran 33:45-46)

**"O Prophet, indeed We have sent you as a witness and a bringer of good tidings and a warner, and one who invites to Allah by His permission, and an illuminating lamp."**

## Verses 53–59: Etiquette, Salawat and Modesty
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَدۡخُلُواْ بُيُوتَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤۡذَنَ لَكُمۡ إِلَىٰ طَعَامٍ غَيۡرَ نَٰظِرِينَ إِنَىٰهُ وَلَٰكِنۡ إِذَا دُعِيتُمۡ فَٱدۡخُلُواْ فَإِذَا طَعِمۡتُمۡ فَٱنتَشِرُواْ وَلَا مُسۡتَـٔۡنِسِينَ لِحَدِيثٍۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكُمۡ كَانَ يُؤۡذِي ٱلنَّبِيَّ فَيَسۡتَحۡيِۦ مِنكُمۡۖ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَسۡتَحۡيِۦ مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ وَإِذَا سَأَلۡتُمُوهُنَّ مَتَٰعٗا فَسۡـَٔلُوهُنَّ مِن وَرَآءِ حِجَابٖۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ أَطۡهَرُ لِقُلُوبِكُمۡ وَقُلُوبِهِنَّۚ وَمَا كَانَ لَكُمۡ أَن تُؤۡذُواْ رَسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَلَآ أَن تَنكِحُوٓاْ أَزۡوَٰجَهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦٓ أَبَدًاۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكُمۡ كَانَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ عَظِيمًا﴾ (Quran 33:53)

**The etiquette of the Prophet's ﷺ house**: do not enter without permission, do not linger after eating, and speak to his wives from behind a screen. In Sahih al-Bukhari, Anas described how this verse was revealed after the wedding feast of Zaynab, when some guests stayed talking.

﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّۚ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ صَلُّواْ عَلَيۡهِ وَسَلِّمُواْ تَسۡلِيمًا﴾ (Quran 33:56)

**"Indeed, Allah confers blessing upon the Prophet, and His angels ask Him to do so. O you who believe, ask Allah to confer blessing upon him and ask Allah to grant him peace."** In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever sends blessings upon me once, Allah will send blessings upon him ten times."

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ قُل لِّأَزۡوَٰجِكَ وَبَنَاتِكَ وَنِسَآءِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يُدۡنِينَ عَلَيۡهِنَّ مِن جَلَٰبِيبِهِنَّۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَدۡنَىٰٓ أَن يُعۡرَفۡنَ فَلَا يُؤۡذَيۡنَۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا﴾ (Quran 33:59)

**"O Prophet, tell your wives and your daughters and the women of the believers to bring down over themselves part of their outer garments. That is more suitable that they will be known and not be harmed."**

## Verses 70–73: Upright Speech and the Trust
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَقُولُواْ قَوۡلٗا سَدِيدٗا﴾ ﴿يُصۡلِحۡ لَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ ذُنُوبَكُمۡۗ وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَقَدۡ فَازَ فَوۡزًا عَظِيمًا﴾ (Quran 33:70-71)

**"O you who believe, fear Allah and speak words of appropriate justice. He will amend for you your deeds and forgive you your sins."** Upright speech (*qawl sadid*) fixes deeds.

﴿إِنَّا عَرَضۡنَا ٱلۡأَمَانَةَ عَلَى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلۡجِبَالِ فَأَبَيۡنَ أَن يَحۡمِلۡنَهَا وَأَشۡفَقۡنَ مِنۡهَا وَحَمَلَهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ ظَلُومٗا جَهُولٗا﴾ ﴿لِّيُعَذِّبَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَٱلۡمُنَٰفِقَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ وَٱلۡمُشۡرِكَٰتِ وَيَتُوبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمَۢا﴾ (Quran 33:72-73)

**"Indeed, We offered the Trust to the heavens and the earth and the mountains, and they declined to bear it and feared it; but man undertook to bear it."** The Trust is the responsibility of obedience with free will. Man accepted it; some fulfil it and some betray it, and the surah ends with Allah's forgiveness and mercy.

## Word Meanings

- **Ad'iya'**: adopted sons.

- **Qawl sadid**: upright speech.
- **Al-Amanah**: the Trust.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Hearts reached the throats"**: fear made physical.
2. **Two reactions to the same army**: "delusion" and "this is what Allah promised".
3. **"An excellent example"**: a whole methodology in two words.
4. **Ten pairs of men and women**: equality in spiritual reward.
5. **"An illuminating lamp"**: the Prophet ﷺ as light.
6. **The Trust refused by mountains**: the weight of human responsibility.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Izhar**: in *uswatun hasanah* (verse 21), the tanween before the throat letter ha' is pronounced clearly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna Allaha* (verse 56), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *mala'ikatahu* (verse 56).
- **Ikhfa'**: in *qawlan sadida* (verse 70), the tanween is hidden before the sin.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *aqsatu* (verse 5), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah As-Sajdah before it ended with "So turn away from them and wait", and Al-Ahzab opens with "fear Allah and do not obey the disbelievers". Surah Saba' after it opens with praise of Allah and continues the theme of His knowledge and the fate of nations.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the Battle of the Confederates?
The siege of Madinah in 5 AH by an alliance of tribes, defended by digging a trench, which ended when Allah sent a wind and unseen soldiers.

### What does "an excellent example" mean?
The Prophet ﷺ is the model to follow in worship, character and every part of life.

### What does the surah say about adoption?
Adopted children must be called by their real fathers' names; they do not become legal sons.

### What does "Seal of the Prophets" mean?
That the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is the last prophet; there is no prophet after him.

### What is the reward for sending blessings on the Prophet ﷺ?
Allah sends blessings ten times on whoever sends one, as in Sahih Muslim.

### What is the Trust in verse 72?
The responsibility of obedience with free will, which the heavens, earth and mountains declined and man accepted.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Ahzab?
73 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Ahzab was revealed when hearts reached the throats, and it shows that faith is proven in crisis. It gives the believers their greatest example in the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, honours his household, declares him the Seal of the Prophets, and commands blessings upon him. Then it reminds you of the Trust you carry: fear Allah, speak uprightly, and He will set your deeds right.
