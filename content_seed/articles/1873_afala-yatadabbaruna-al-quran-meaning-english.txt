## What is tadabbur?
- From *dubur*, the end of something: looking at what the words lead to, their meanings, lessons and application.

## Locks on hearts
- *Or are there locks upon hearts?* The word *qulub* is indefinite, and *locks* are attributed to them: **their own** locks, made by their own sins and heedlessness.

## What blocks reflection
| Lock | Key |
|---|---|
| Persistent sin | Repentance |
| Rushing through recitation | Slower reading |
| Not understanding words | Reading a simple tafsir |
| Distracted heart | Quiet time, phone away |

## A similar verse
- *A blessed Book We revealed to you, that they may reflect upon its verses* (Sad 38:29). Reflection is **the purpose** of revelation.

## Simple steps
1. Read one verse with its translation.
2. Ask: what does Allah want from me here?
3. Write one action and do it today.

## Lessons
- **Quality over quantity** sometimes.
- **Ask Allah to open your heart** to the Quran.
