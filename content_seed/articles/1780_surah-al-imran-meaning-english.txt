**Surah Al Imran** (The Family of ʿImran, 200 ayat) is paired with Al-Baqarah in many hadiths as one of the two radiant surahs.

## Clear and ambiguous verses
- Some verses are clear in meaning, the foundation of the Book; others are open to interpretation. Those firm in knowledge say: *We believe in it; all is from our Lord.*
﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾

## The family of ʿImran
| Person | Story |
|---|---|
| The wife of ʿImran | Dedicated her unborn child to Allah |
| Maryam | Raised by Zakariya, provided for by Allah in her prayer niche |
| Zakariya | Prayed for a child and was given Yahya |
| ʿIsa | His likeness before Allah is like Adam, created from dust |

## Hold together
- *Hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not be divided.*

## Lessons of Uhud
- A group left their position, and the believers suffered. The surah teaches that defeat has causes, that Allah tests to distinguish believers, and that the Prophet ﷺ was **gentle** with them: *Had you been harsh and hard-hearted, they would have dispersed from around you.*

## Reflection on creation
﴿إِنَّ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾

## Lessons
- **Ask Allah for a steady heart.**
- **Learn from setbacks** instead of despairing.
