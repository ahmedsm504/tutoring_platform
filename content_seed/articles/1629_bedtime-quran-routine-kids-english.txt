Bedtime is one of the best moments to connect children with the Quran: they are calm, close to you, and ready to listen.

## The 10-minute routine
| Minutes | Step |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | The three Quls with blowing into the hands and wiping |
| 2–4 | Ayat al-Kursi |
| 4–8 | A short Quran story |
| 8–10 | The sleep du'a and a hug |

## Step 1: The three Quls
- The Prophet ﷺ would cup his hands, blow lightly into them, recite **Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas**, and wipe over as much of his body as he could, three times [Al-Bukhari].
- Do it **with** your child at first.

## Step 2: Ayat al-Kursi
- Whoever recites it before sleep has a guardian from Allah until morning [Al-Bukhari].
- Read [Ayat al-Kursi Meaning](/blog/ayat-al-kursi-meaning-english/).

## Step 3: A short story
- One prophet story each night: Yunus and the whale, Ibrahim and the fire, Musa and the sea.

## Step 4: The sleep du'a
- «Bismika Allāhumma amūtu wa aḥyā» — *In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live* [Al-Bukhari].

## Tips
- **Same time, same place** every night.
- **No screens** for the last half hour.
- **Let them lead** one step when they're ready.
