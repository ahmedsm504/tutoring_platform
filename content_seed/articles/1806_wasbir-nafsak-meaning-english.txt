﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ نَفۡسَكَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَدَوٰةِ وَٱلۡعَشِيِّ يُرِيدُونَ وَجۡهَهُۥۖ وَلَا تَعۡدُ عَيۡنَاكَ عَنۡهُمۡ تُرِيدُ زِينَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ وَلَا تُطِعۡ مَنۡ أَغۡفَلۡنَا قَلۡبَهُۥ عَن ذِكۡرِنَا وَٱتَّبَعَ هَوَىٰهُ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُهُۥ فُرُطٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:28]

## The context
- Some leaders of Quraysh wanted the Prophet ﷺ to send away the poor Companions before they would sit with him. The verse commands the opposite: **stay with them**.

## Patience with good company
- *Wasbir nafsaka* means "hold yourself back with them". Good company sometimes requires patience, because it may not offer status or wealth.

## Who to stay with
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| They call on their Lord | People of duʿa and worship |
| Morning and evening | Constant, not occasional |
| Seeking His Face | Sincere, not showing off |

## Who not to obey
- The one whose heart Allah made heedless of His remembrance, who **follows his desire**, and whose affair is in excess and loss.

## Lessons
- **Choose friends by their hearts**, not their wealth.
- **Do not let glitter pull your eyes** away from people of good.
