﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾ [Al Imran 3:8]

## The context
- The previous verse speaks of clear and ambiguous verses: those with deviation in their hearts follow the ambiguous to cause confusion, while **those firm in knowledge** say: *We believe in it, all is from our Lord.* Then they make this duʿa.

## Even the guided ask
- The duʿa is said by people already guided. Guidance is not a one-time event; it must be **kept**.

## Two requests
| Request | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Do not let our hearts deviate | Keep us firm after You guided us |
| Grant us mercy from Yourself | Mercy that protects and strengthens |

- It ends with *al-Wahhab*, the One who gives freely without return.

## The Prophet's similar duʿa
- He ﷺ often said: **"O Turner of hearts, keep my heart firm on Your religion"** (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

## Causes of a steady heart
- Regular Quran, good company, avoiding doubtful content, and constant duʿa.

## Lessons
- **Never feel safe from deviation**; ask Allah daily.
- **Knowledge brings humility**, not pride.
