## "Our Lord, Let Not Our Hearts Deviate": Quran 3:8 Explained

﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾ [Al Imran 3:8]

### The context
- The previous verse speaks of clear and ambiguous verses: those with deviation in their hearts follow the ambiguous to cause confusion, while **those firm in knowledge** say: *We believe in it, all is from our Lord.* Then they make this duʿa.

### Even the guided ask
- The duʿa is said by people already guided. Guidance is not a one-time event; it must be **kept**.

### Two requests
| Request | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Do not let our hearts deviate | Keep us firm after You guided us |
| Grant us mercy from Yourself | Mercy that protects and strengthens |

- It ends with *al-Wahhab*, the One who gives freely without return.

### The Prophet's similar duʿa
- He ﷺ often said: **"O Turner of hearts, keep my heart firm on Your religion"** (narrated by al-Tirmidhi).

### Causes of a steady heart
- Regular Quran, good company, avoiding doubtful content, and constant duʿa.

### Lessons
- **Never feel safe from deviation**; ask Allah daily.
- **Knowledge brings humility**, not pride.

## Surah Al Imran Meaning: Firmness of Heart and Lessons from Uhud

**Surah Al Imran** (The Family of ʿImran, 200 ayat) is paired with Al-Baqarah in many hadiths as one of the two radiant surahs.

### Clear and ambiguous verses
- Some verses are clear in meaning, the foundation of the Book; others are open to interpretation. Those firm in knowledge say: *We believe in it; all is from our Lord.*
﴿رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ﴾

### The family of ʿImran
| Person | Story |
|---|---|
| The wife of ʿImran | Dedicated her unborn child to Allah |
| Maryam | Raised by Zakariya, provided for by Allah in her prayer niche |
| Zakariya | Prayed for a child and was given Yahya |
| ʿIsa | His likeness before Allah is like Adam, created from dust |

### Hold together
- *Hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not be divided.*

### Lessons of Uhud
- A group left their position, and the believers suffered. The surah teaches that defeat has causes, that Allah tests to distinguish believers, and that the Prophet ﷺ was **gentle** with them: *Had you been harsh and hard-hearted, they would have dispersed from around you.*

### Reflection on creation
﴿إِنَّ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱخۡتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾

### Lessons
- **Ask Allah for a steady heart.**
- **Learn from setbacks** instead of despairing.

## "Those Who Seek Forgiveness Before Dawn": Quran 3:17 Explained

﴿ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ وَٱلۡمُنفِقِينَ وَٱلۡمُسۡتَغۡفِرِينَ بِٱلۡأَسۡحَارِ﴾ [Al Imran 3:17]

### Five qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The patient | In obedience, away from sin, in trials |
| The truthful | In words, deeds and intention |
| The devout | Constant in humble obedience |
| The spenders | In good causes |
| Seekers of forgiveness before dawn | At the end of the night |

### What is *sahar*?
- The last part of the night before Fajr.

### Allah's descent
- *Our Lord descends every night to the lowest heaven when the last third of the night remains, saying: Who calls on Me that I may answer? Who asks Me that I may give? Who seeks My forgiveness that I may forgive?* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Why end with forgiveness?
- After all these deeds they still seek forgiveness, never proud of their worship.

### Lessons
- **Take a few minutes** before Fajr for istighfar.
- **Seek forgiveness after obedience**, too.

## "Say: O Allah, Owner of Sovereignty": Quran 3:26 Explained

﴿قُلِ ٱللَّهُمَّ مَٰلِكَ ٱلۡمُلۡكِ تُؤۡتِي ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتَنزِعُ ٱلۡمُلۡكَ مِمَّن تَشَآءُ وَتُعِزُّ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتُذِلُّ مَن تَشَآءُۖ بِيَدِكَ ٱلۡخَيۡرُۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ [Al Imran 3:26]

### Meaning
*Say: O Allah, Owner of sovereignty, You give sovereignty to whom You will and take it from whom You will. You honour whom You will and humble whom You will. In Your hand is all good. Indeed You are over all things capable.*

### Four actions, one Owner
| Action | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Giving power | No ruler holds power by his own strength |
| Removing power | No kingdom lasts except by His will |
| Honouring | True honour comes from obedience to Him |
| Humbling | Arrogance ends in humiliation |

### "In Your hand is all good"
- The verse mentions only **good** being in His hand, though He is in control of everything. Scholars explain this as a lesson in etiquette: evil is not attributed to Allah in speech, and His decrees always carry wisdom.

### The next verse
- Night enters into day and day into night, the living from the dead and the dead from the living: the same Power that rotates kingdoms rotates the hours.

### Lessons
- **Do not envy the powerful**; their power is a loan.
- **Seek honour from Allah**, not from people.

## "If You Love Allah, Then Follow Me": Quran 3:31 Explained

﴿قُلۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ تُحِبُّونَ ٱللَّهَ فَٱتَّبِعُونِي يُحۡبِبۡكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ ذُنُوبَكُمۡۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ [Al Imran 3:31]

### The verse of testing
- Some scholars of the early generations called it *ayat al-mihnah*, the verse of testing: anyone can claim to love Allah; the proof is **following the Messenger**.

### A chain of results
| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| You love Allah | A claim |
| You follow the Prophet ﷺ | The proof |
| Allah loves you | The greater reward |
| He forgives your sins | An added gift |

### Being loved is greater
- Scholars note: the important thing is not that you love, but that **you are loved** by Allah.

### Following in practice
- Learning the Sunnah, praying as he prayed, his character with people, and his duʿas.

### Love without innovation
- Following means doing what he did in the way he did it, not inventing new acts of worship.

### Lessons
- **Show love through action.**
- **Learn one Sunnah each week** and practise it.

## "You Will Not Attain Righteousness Until You Spend What You Love": Quran 3:92 Explained

﴿لَن تَنَالُواْ ٱلۡبِرَّ حَتَّىٰ تُنفِقُواْ مِمَّا تُحِبُّونَۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِن شَيۡءٖ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِهِۦ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ [Al Imran 3:92]

### Meaning
*You will never attain righteousness until you spend from what you love. And whatever you spend, Allah knows it.*

### Abu Talhah's response
- When this verse was revealed, Abu Talhah went to the Prophet ﷺ and gave away **Bayruha**, his most beloved garden near the mosque. The Prophet ﷺ praised it as profitable wealth and advised him to give it to his relatives (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### What counts as "what you love"?
| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| Wealth | Giving from the best, not the leftovers |
| Time | Visiting the sick when you want to rest |
| Effort | Teaching someone what you worked hard to learn |
| Comfort | Waking for Fajr or night prayer |

### "Allah knows it"
- No gift is too small to be seen. Even a small sincere amount is recorded.

### Connection with Al-Baqarah 2:177
- There, righteousness includes *giving wealth despite loving it*. Both verses agree: **love tests sincerity**.

### Lessons
- **Give your best** to Allah.
- **Start small but sincere.**

## "Fear Allah as He Should Be Feared, and Do Not Die Except as Muslims": Quran 3:102 Explained

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ حَقَّ تُقَاتِهِۦ وَلَا تَمُوتُنَّ إِلَّا وَأَنتُم مُّسۡلِمُونَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:102]

### True taqwa
- Reported from Ibn Masʿud: *That He is obeyed and not disobeyed, remembered and not forgotten, thanked and not denied.*

### With At-Taghabun 64:16
- *Fear Allah as much as you are able.* Many scholars said 64:16 explains this verse: true taqwa is giving your full capacity.

### "Do not die except as Muslims"
- No one controls the time of death, so the meaning is: **stay on Islam always**, so death finds you upon it.

### Causes of a good ending
| Cause | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Steadfastness | One dies on what he lived on |
| Duʿa | *Take me in death as a Muslim and join me with the righteous* (Yusuf 12:101) |
| A sound heart | Free of rancour and hypocrisy |

### Lessons
- **Strive in taqwa** to your capacity.
- **Ask for a good ending** in every prostration.

## "Hold Firmly to the Rope of Allah All Together": Quran 3:103 Explained

﴿وَٱعۡتَصِمُواْ بِحَبۡلِ ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعٗا وَلَا تَفَرَّقُواْۚ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡ إِذۡ كُنتُمۡ أَعۡدَآءٗ فَأَلَّفَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِكُمۡ فَأَصۡبَحۡتُم بِنِعۡمَتِهِۦٓ إِخۡوَٰنٗا وَكُنتُمۡ عَلَىٰ شَفَا حُفۡرَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ فَأَنقَذَكُم مِّنۡهَاۗ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:103]

### The rope of Allah
- Ibn Masʿud and others explained it as **the Quran**. Others said the religion of Allah and His covenant; the meanings meet.

### Two commands
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hold firmly, all together | Collective attachment to the Quran |
| Do not be divided | Avoid splits that weaken the community |

### A blessing to remember
- The Aws and Khazraj of Madinah had long wars before Islam. Islam made them **brothers**. The verse asks them to remember this blessing.

### On the edge of the Fire
- *You were on the brink of a pit of Fire, and He saved you from it.* Disbelief and enmity were leading them there.

### Unity and differences
- Unity does not mean everyone agrees on every detail, but that differences do not become hostility and division.

### Lessons
- **Make the Quran the centre** of family and community.
- **Remember brotherhood** before arguing.

## "Let There Be from You a Nation Inviting to Good": Quran 3:104 Explained

﴿وَلۡتَكُن مِّنكُمۡ أُمَّةࣱ يَدۡعُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡخَيۡرِ وَيَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِۚ وَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:104]

### "From you"
- Many scholars: a group dedicated to this, a communal obligation.
- Others: be all of you a nation calling to good, each according to ability.

### Three duties
| Duty | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Inviting to good | Islam and every good |
| Enjoining right | What the Shariʿah knows as good |
| Forbidding wrong | What the Shariʿah knows as evil |

### Levels of changing evil
- *Whoever sees an evil, let him change it with his hand; if he cannot, then with his tongue; if he cannot, then with his heart, and that is the weakest faith* (narrated by Muslim).

### Qualities of the caller
- Knowledge, gentleness and patience.

### Lessons
- **Start with your family**, gently.
- **Be part of the nation of good** in your field.

## "On the Day Some Faces Turn White and Some Turn Dark": Quran 3:106-107 Explained

﴿يَوۡمَ تَبۡيَضُّ وُجُوهࣱ وَتَسۡوَدُّ وُجُوهࣱۚ فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡوَدَّتۡ وُجُوهُهُمۡ أَكَفَرۡتُم بَعۡدَ إِيمَٰنِكُمۡ فَذُوقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:106]

### The context
- The previous verse: *Do not be like those who divided and differed after clear proofs came to them* (3:105). The faces show the result.

### Two kinds of faces
| Face | Meaning |
|---|---|
| White | Light, joy and honour for the faithful |
| Dark | Grief and disgrace for those who disbelieved after faith |

### Ending with mercy
﴿وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ٱبۡيَضَّتۡ وُجُوهُهُمۡ فَفِي رَحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ﴾
- The verse opens with white faces, details the dark ones, then **ends with mercy**.

### Lessons
- **Stay firm** on the truth.
- **Avoid division** that weakens faith.
