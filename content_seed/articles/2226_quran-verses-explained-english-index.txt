This page is a **complete index** of our English articles that explain the meaning of Quranic verses and surahs, arranged in **Mushaf order** from Al-Fatiha to An-Nas. If you want the meaning of a particular verse, find its surah below and open the article.

Each article focuses on one verse, a short passage, or a whole short surah. It quotes the verse in the Uthmani script exactly as in the Madinah Mushaf, gives a clear English rendering of its meaning, explains key words, and draws out lessons for daily life.

## Our method and sources
- **The text first**: every verse is quoted in Arabic script and checked letter by letter against the Mushaf text.
- **Reliable tafsir**: explanations draw on established works such as the tafsirs of At-Tabari, Ibn Kathir and As-Sa'di, without unreliable Israelite narratives or fringe opinions.
- **Hadith with references**: every hadith is cited with its source, and we use only authentic or sound reports.
- **Meaning, not word-for-word translation**: English renderings convey the meaning; they are not a substitute for the Arabic Quran.
- **Practical lessons**: every article ends with what the verse asks of us today.

## How to use this index
### For daily reflection
Pick one verse from your daily reading, find its explanation here, and read it before or after your recitation. You will find the verse becomes close to your heart and you pause at it whenever you pass it.

### For memorization
If you are memorizing a surah, read the explanations of its verses first. Understanding ties words to meaning, which makes memorization easier and forgetting less likely. See our [step-by-step memorization method](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

### For family study
Hold a short weekly family circle: one verse, recited by one of the children, then its explanation read from here, then a question: what does this teach us, and how will we act on it this week?

### For new Muslims and learners
If you are new to the Quran, start with the short surahs of Juz 'Amma near the end of this list, then move to famous verses such as Ayat al-Kursi in Surah Al-Baqarah.
