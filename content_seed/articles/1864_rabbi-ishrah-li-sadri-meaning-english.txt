﴿قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشۡرَحۡ لِي صَدۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَيَسِّرۡ لِيٓ أَمۡرِي﴾ ﴿وَٱحۡلُلۡ عُقۡدَةٗ مِّن لِّسَانِي﴾ ﴿يَفۡقَهُواْ قَوۡلِي﴾ [Taha 20:25-28]

## The moment
- Allah had just commanded Musa: *Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed.* Before going, Musa asked for what he needed.

## Four requests
| Request | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Expand my chest | Calm, courage and patience |
| Ease my task | Make the mission easy |
| Untie the knot from my tongue | Fluent, clear speech |
| That they understand my speech | Being understood, the goal of speaking |

## "From my tongue"
- He asked to untie *a knot*, not every knot: a lesson in asking for what is needed.

## Then Harun
- He continued by asking for his brother Harun as a helper, and Allah said: *You have been granted your request, O Musa* (20:36).

## When to say it
- Before exams, interviews, presentations, difficult conversations, and teaching.

## Lessons
- **Prepare the heart** before the task.
- **Ask Allah for clarity**, not only success.
