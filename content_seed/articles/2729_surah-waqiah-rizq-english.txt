In many homes there is a beautiful habit: reciting Surah Al-Waqi'ah every night after 'Isha', based on a well-known hadith: "Whoever recites Surah Al-Waqi'ah every night will never be afflicted by poverty." Some call it "the surah of wealth", and recommend it to anyone struggling financially.

Reciting Al-Waqi'ah is a good deed without doubt; it is part of the Quran and full of profound meanings. But did this hadith come authentically from the Prophet ﷺ? Is provision tied to this surah specifically? And what are the established causes of provision? This guide answers these questions fairly.

## The Hadith and Its Sources
It is narrated from 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud that he said: I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say: "Whoever recites Surah Al-Waqi'ah every night will never be afflicted by poverty." And Ibn Mas'ud used to instruct his daughters to recite it every night. It is reported by al-Harith ibn Abi Usamah, Abu Ya'la, al-Bayhaqi in *Shu'ab al-Iman*, Ibn as-Sunni and others.

The narration includes a story: 'Uthman ibn 'Affan visited Ibn Mas'ud during his final illness and offered him a stipend, which he declined. 'Uthman said: "It will be for your daughters after you." Ibn Mas'ud replied: "Do you fear poverty for my daughters? I have told my daughters to recite Surah Al-Waqi'ah every night," and he mentioned the hadith.

## Its Grading
**Most hadith scholars graded it weak**, including:
- **Imam Ahmad**, according to what is reported from him.
- **Abu Hatim ar-Razi** and his son, who called it *munkar*.
- **Ad-Daraqutni**, who noted the inconsistency in its chains.
- **Al-Bayhaqi**, who indicated its weakness.
- **Ibn al-Jawzi**, who listed it in *al-'Ilal al-Mutanahiyah*.
- **Al-Albani**, in his series of weak hadith.

The reasons: **unknown narrators** such as Abu Tibah and Abu Shuja', and **inconsistency in the chain**, which is narrated in different ways, none of them established.

## Can It Be Acted On?
Scholars hold two views on acting on weak hadith in virtuous deeds:
- **Those who permit it** with conditions (the weakness is not severe, it falls under an established general principle, and one does not believe it to be authentic) may see reciting Al-Waqi'ah nightly as a good deed under the general virtue of reciting the Quran.
- **Those who forbid it** entirely do not attribute this specific virtue to Al-Waqi'ah.

In practice:
1. **Reciting Al-Waqi'ah every night is fine**: it is Quran, and every letter brings ten good deeds.
2. **Do not claim with certainty** that it prevents poverty, since the hadith is not established.
3. **Do not attribute the hadith to the Prophet ﷺ as certain**; mention its weakness when you quote it.
4. **Do not neglect the established causes of provision** in reliance on a specific surah.

## The Meanings of Surah Al-Waqi'ah
Al-Waqi'ah is Makkan, with 96 verses. Its main theme is **the Day of Judgement and the three groups of people on it**:
1. **The terror of the Hour**: "When the Inevitable occurs, there is no denying its occurrence."
2. **The three groups**: the foremost who are brought near, the people of the right, and the people of the left.
3. **The bliss of the foremost and the people of the right**, and the punishment of the people of the left.
4. **Proofs of resurrection**: the creation of man, crops, water and fire.
5. **The greatness of the Quran**: "I swear by the positions of the stars... it is a noble Quran."
6. **The scene of death**: "Why then, when the soul reaches the throat..."

﴿إِذَا وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ﴾ ﴿لَيۡسَ لِوَقۡعَتِهَا كَاذِبَةٌ﴾ ﴿خَافِضَةࣱ رَّافِعَةٌ﴾ ﴿إِذَا رُجَّتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ رَجّٗا﴾ ﴿وَبُسَّتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ بَسّٗا﴾ ﴿فَكَانَتۡ هَبَآءٗ مُّنۢبَثّٗا﴾ ﴿وَكُنتُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا ثَلَٰثَةٗ﴾ ﴿فَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ﴾ ﴿وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ ٱلسَّـٰبِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 56:1-10)

Some scholars suggested that the link between Al-Waqi'ah and freedom from poverty lies in its reminder of the Hereafter and its making the world small in the eyes: whoever lives by its meanings becomes rich in heart. That meaning is true in itself, even though the hadith is weak.

### Verses about provision in Al-Waqi'ah
The surah mentions the foundations of human provision as proofs of resurrection:

﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَحۡرُثُونَ﴾ ﴿ءَأَنتُمۡ تَزۡرَعُونَهُۥٓ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلزَّـٰرِعُونَ﴾ (Quran 56:63-64)

﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱلَّذِي تَشۡرَبُونَ﴾ ﴿ءَأَنتُمۡ أَنزَلۡتُمُوهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُزۡنِ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُنزِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 56:68-69)

Crops, water and fire are among Allah's greatest provisions, and the surah reminds us that it is Allah who makes crops grow and sends down water.

## The Established Causes of Provision
The Quran and Sunnah contain established causes of provision that deserve far more attention than a weak hadith:

### 1. Seeking forgiveness
﴿فَقُلۡتُ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفَّارٗا﴾ ﴿يُرۡسِلِ ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّدۡرَارٗا﴾ ﴿وَيُمۡدِدۡكُم بِأَمۡوَٰلٖ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ أَنۡهَٰرٗا﴾ (Quran 71:10-12)

Allah links seeking forgiveness to rain, wealth and children.

### 2. Taqwa and reliance on Allah
﴿فَإِذَا بَلَغۡنَ أَجَلَهُنَّ فَأَمۡسِكُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفٍ أَوۡ فَارِقُوهُنَّ بِمَعۡرُوفٖ وَأَشۡهِدُواْ ذَوَيۡ عَدۡلٖ مِّنكُمۡ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلشَّهَٰدَةَ لِلَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ يُوعَظُ بِهِۦ مَن كَانَ يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِۚ وَمَن يَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مَخۡرَجٗا﴾ ﴿وَيَرۡزُقۡهُ مِنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَحۡتَسِبُۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسۡبُهُۥٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمۡرِهِۦۚ قَدۡ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدۡرٗا﴾ (Quran 65:2-3)

### 3. Keeping family ties
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever would like his provision to be increased and his life to be extended, let him keep the ties of kinship."

### 4. Gratitude
﴿وَإِذۡ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمۡ لَئِن شَكَرۡتُمۡ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمۡۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 14:7)

### 5. Spending in Allah's way
﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ رَبِّي يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَقۡدِرُ لَهُۥۚ وَمَآ أَنفَقۡتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَهُوَ يُخۡلِفُهُۥۖ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّـٰزِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 34:39)

And in Sahih Muslim: "Charity does not decrease wealth."

### 6. True reliance with effort
At-Tirmidhi reported, and graded authentic, that the Prophet ﷺ said: "If you relied on Allah as He should be relied upon, He would provide for you as He provides for the birds: they go out hungry in the morning and return full in the evening." The birds go out; they do not sit in their nests.

### 7. Following Hajj with 'Umrah
At-Tirmidhi and an-Nasa'i reported from Ibn Mas'ud that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Follow Hajj with 'Umrah, for they remove poverty and sins as the bellows removes impurity from iron." At-Tirmidhi graded it authentic.

### 8. Du'a
Authentic supplications include "Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an, wa rizqan tayyiban, wa 'amalan mutaqabbala" (O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, good provision and accepted deeds) after Fajr, reported by Ibn Majah and graded authentic by al-Albani; and "Allahummakfini bi halalika 'an haramik, wa aghnini bi fadlika 'amman siwak" (O Allah, suffice me with what You have made lawful instead of what You have forbidden, and make me independent by Your bounty of all besides You), reported by at-Tirmidhi, who graded it hasan.

### 9. Effort and work
﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ ذَلُولٗا فَٱمۡشُواْ فِي مَنَاكِبِهَا وَكُلُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِهِۦۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ ٱلنُّشُورُ﴾ (Quran 67:15)

Provision does not come from recitation alone, but from striving in the earth while relying on Allah.

## What Blocks Provision
- **Sins**: "Whatever misfortune befalls you is because of what your hands have earned."
- **Laziness and abandoning effort**.
- **Interest (riba)**: "Allah destroys interest and gives increase for charities."
- **Cheating and lying in trade**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, when buyer and seller lie and conceal, the blessing of their sale is erased.

## How to Recite Al-Waqi'ah Beneficially
If you love reciting Al-Waqi'ah every night, that is good. Make your recitation:
- **An act of drawing near to Allah**, not a "money charm".
- **Reflective**: which of the three groups do I want to be among?
- **Joined with action**: work, seeking forgiveness, family ties and charity.
- **Free of superstition**: missing it one night does not bring poverty.

## Provision Is More Than Money
Provision (*rizq*) in the Quran and Sunnah is much wider than money: health, safety, knowledge, a righteous spouse and children, peace of heart, guidance and faith. At-Tirmidhi reported, and graded hasan, that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever wakes up secure in his home, healthy in his body, with his food for the day, it is as if the world has been gathered for him." And in al-Bukhari and Muslim: "Richness is not having many possessions; richness is the richness of the soul."

## When Provision Is Delayed
A Muslim may take every means and still find provision delayed or tight. Remember:
- **Provision is apportioned** by Allah's wisdom: "Allah extends provision for whom He wills and restricts it."
- **Trials are Allah's way** with His servants, and patience brings reward.

﴿وَلَنَبۡلُوَنَّكُم بِشَيۡءٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡخَوۡفِ وَٱلۡجُوعِ وَنَقۡصٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡوَٰلِ وَٱلۡأَنفُسِ وَٱلثَّمَرَٰتِۗ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 2:155)

- **Delay can be good**: Allah may protect you from a harm you do not know.
- **Keep striving** and do not despair.
- **Examine yourself**: is there anything unlawful in your earnings, any injustice or unpaid right?

## Lawful Earnings First
There is no point seeking blessing in provision while earning unlawfully. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ mentioned a man on a long journey, dishevelled and dusty, raising his hands to the sky, "O Lord, O Lord," while his food, drink and clothing were unlawful, and he was nourished by the unlawful, "so how can he be answered?" The first step towards blessed provision is lawful earning: no bribery, cheating, interest or taking others' rights.

## Contentment and Planning
Among the causes of feeling at ease is contentment with what Allah has given and wise management of spending. Many people with small incomes live comfortably because they manage well and are content, and many with large incomes live in hardship because they overspend and compare themselves with others. Allah describes the servants of the Most Merciful:

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَنفَقُواْ لَمۡ يُسۡرِفُواْ وَلَمۡ يَقۡتُرُواْ وَكَانَ بَيۡنَ ذَٰلِكَ قَوَامٗا﴾ (Quran 25:67)

Moderate spending, avoiding unnecessary debt and saving even a little are practical means of stability, and part of taking the means that Islam encourages.

## A Plan for Someone in Financial Difficulty
1. Guard the five prayers on time.
2. Seek forgiveness daily, at least a hundred times.
3. Keep family ties, even with a call or short visit.
4. Give charity regularly, even a little.
5. Work hard and improve your skills.
6. Avoid unlawful earnings however tempting.
7. Make du'a in prostration and at times of acceptance.
8. Recite the Quran daily, including Al-Waqi'ah if you wish, to draw near to Allah.
9. Be grateful for what you have; gratitude brings increase.
10. Be content with Allah's decree while striving.

If financial stress leads to severe anxiety or affects your daily life, there is no harm in seeking help from a professional, or from relatives and charities when needed; that too is taking the means.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is the hadith about reciting Al-Waqi'ah every night authentic?
Most hadith scholars, including Ahmad, Abu Hatim, ad-Daraqutni and al-Albani, graded it weak.

### Should I recite Al-Waqi'ah every night?
There is no harm; it is Quran recitation. But do not claim with certainty that it prevents poverty.

### What are the strongest causes of provision in the Quran?
Seeking forgiveness, taqwa, gratitude, charity, and effort combined with reliance on Allah.

### Do family ties increase provision?
Yes, as in the hadith in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Do Hajj and 'Umrah remove poverty?
Yes, according to the hadith of Ibn Mas'ud reported by at-Tirmidhi and an-Nasa'i.

### What is Surah Al-Waqi'ah about?
The Day of Judgement, the three groups of people, and proofs of resurrection.

### What should I do if my provision is delayed despite my efforts?
Be patient, examine your earnings, keep striving and making du'a, and remember that provision is apportioned with wisdom.

### Is financial planning part of the Islamic means?
Yes. Moderate spending and wise management are qualities of the servants of the Most Merciful.

## Conclusion
The hadith "Whoever recites Surah Al-Waqi'ah every night will never be afflicted by poverty" is weak according to most hadith scholars, so its virtue cannot be claimed with certainty, although reciting Al-Waqi'ah is a good deed in itself. The established causes of provision are greater and more reliable: seeking forgiveness, taqwa, reliance with effort, family ties, gratitude, charity, following Hajj with 'Umrah, and du'a. Recite Al-Waqi'ah if you wish, reflecting on the Hereafter, and seek your provision through the means Allah has prescribed.
