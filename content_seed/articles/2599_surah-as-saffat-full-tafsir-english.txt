Surah As-Saffat opens with angels standing in ranks and closes with "Peace be upon the messengers, and praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds." Between them is a gallery of prophets, each story ending with a greeting of peace: "Peace upon Nuh among the worlds", "Peace upon Ibrahim", "Peace upon Musa and Harun", "Peace upon Ilyas". At its centre is one of the most moving scenes of obedience in human history: Ibrahim telling his young son, "I have seen in a dream that I am sacrificing you, so see what you think", and the son replying, "O my father, do as you are commanded; you will find me, if Allah wills, of the patient." The surah also contains the story of Yunus in the whale, saved because he was "of those who glorify Allah".

This guide explains Surah As-Saffat section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah As-Saffat
- **Number of verses**: 182.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 37, in the twenty-third juz.
- **Name**: As-Saffat means "those lined up in rows", the angels.
- **Recited by the Prophet ﷺ in prayer**: an-Nasa'i reported that Ibn 'Umar said the Prophet ﷺ commanded them to keep the prayer light, and he would lead them reciting As-Saffat; al-Albani graded it authentic.
- **Main themes**: tawhid, resurrection, the fate of the sincere and the deniers, and the stories of the prophets.

## Verses 1–10: The Ranks and the Stars
﴿وَٱلصَّـٰٓفَّـٰتِ صَفّٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلزَّـٰجِرَٰتِ زَجۡرٗا﴾ ﴿فَٱلتَّـٰلِيَٰتِ ذِكۡرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ إِلَٰهَكُمۡ لَوَٰحِدࣱ﴾ ﴿رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا وَرَبُّ ٱلۡمَشَٰرِقِ﴾ (Quran 37:1-5)

Allah swears by **the angels lined up in rows**, **those who drive** the clouds or drive away evil, and **those who recite a reminder**: **"Indeed, your God is One, Lord of the heavens and the earth and what is between them, and Lord of the sunrises."**

**Straight rows**: in Sahih Muslim, Jabir ibn Samurah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Will you not line up as the angels line up before their Lord?" They asked how. He said: "They complete the front rows and stand close together in the row." The rows of prayer imitate the rows of angels.

﴿إِنَّا زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِزِينَةٍ ٱلۡكَوَاكِبِ﴾ ﴿وَحِفۡظٗا مِّن كُلِّ شَيۡطَٰنٖ مَّارِدٖ﴾ ﴿لَّا يَسَّمَّعُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡمَلَإِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ وَيُقۡذَفُونَ مِن كُلِّ جَانِبٖ﴾ ﴿دُحُورٗاۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ وَاصِبٌ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَنۡ خَطِفَ ٱلۡخَطۡفَةَ فَأَتۡبَعَهُۥ شِهَابࣱ ثَاقِبࣱ﴾ (Quran 37:6-10)

**"We have adorned the nearest heaven with an adornment of stars, and as protection against every rebellious devil."** The devils who try to eavesdrop on the heavenly assembly are pelted, and a piercing flame pursues any who snatch a word.

## Verses 11–39: Resurrection and Mutual Blame
﴿فَٱسۡتَفۡتِهِمۡ أَهُمۡ أَشَدُّ خَلۡقًا أَم مَّنۡ خَلَقۡنَآۚ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُم مِّن طِينٖ لَّازِبِۭ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ عَجِبۡتَ وَيَسۡخَرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ لَا يَذۡكُرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا رَأَوۡاْ ءَايَةٗ يَسۡتَسۡخِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُوٓاْ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرࣱ مُّبِينٌ﴾ ﴿أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابٗا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ﴾ ﴿أَوَءَابَآؤُنَا ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ﴾ ﴿قُلۡ نَعَمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ دَٰخِرُونَ﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّمَا هِيَ زَجۡرَةࣱ وَٰحِدَةࣱ فَإِذَا هُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَقَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَا هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ﴾ (Quran 37:11-21)

**"Ask them: Are they a stronger creation or those others We have created?"** They mocked resurrection: "When we are dust and bones, will we be resurrected?" **"Say: Yes, and you will be humbled."** It takes only **"one shout"**. They will say: **"O woe to us! This is the Day of Recompense."** **"This is the Day of Judgement which you used to deny."**

﴿۞ٱحۡشُرُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ وَأَزۡوَٰجَهُمۡ وَمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡبُدُونَ﴾ ﴿مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَٱهۡدُوهُمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَقِفُوهُمۡۖ إِنَّهُم مَّسۡـُٔولُونَ﴾ ﴿مَا لَكُمۡ لَا تَنَاصَرُونَ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ هُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ مُسۡتَسۡلِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 37:22-26)

**"Gather those who committed wrong, their kinds, and what they used to worship other than Allah, and guide them to the path of Hellfire. And stop them; indeed, they are to be questioned."** Everyone will be asked.

﴿وَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّكُمۡ كُنتُمۡ تَأۡتُونَنَا عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ بَل لَّمۡ تَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَمَا كَانَ لَنَا عَلَيۡكُم مِّن سُلۡطَٰنِۭۖ بَلۡ كُنتُمۡ قَوۡمٗا طَٰغِينَ﴾ ﴿فَحَقَّ عَلَيۡنَا قَوۡلُ رَبِّنَآۖ إِنَّا لَذَآئِقُونَ﴾ ﴿فَأَغۡوَيۡنَٰكُمۡ إِنَّا كُنَّا غَٰوِينَ﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ مُشۡتَرِكُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَفۡعَلُ بِٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:27-34)

**Mutual blame**: followers turn to their leaders: "You used to come at us from the right", meaning with apparent religion and power. The leaders reply: "Rather, you were not believers. We had no authority over you." Both share the punishment.

## Verses 40–61: The Sincere Servants
﴿إِلَّا عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ رِزۡقࣱ مَّعۡلُومࣱ﴾ ﴿فَوَٰكِهُ وَهُم مُّكۡرَمُونَ﴾ ﴿فِي جَنَّـٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾ ﴿عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ﴾ ﴿يُطَافُ عَلَيۡهِم بِكَأۡسٖ مِّن مَّعِينِۭ﴾ ﴿بَيۡضَآءَ لَذَّةٖ لِّلشَّـٰرِبِينَ﴾ ﴿لَا فِيهَا غَوۡلࣱ وَلَا هُمۡ عَنۡهَا يُنزَفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَعِندَهُمۡ قَٰصِرَٰتُ ٱلطَّرۡفِ عِينࣱ﴾ ﴿كَأَنَّهُنَّ بَيۡضࣱ مَّكۡنُونࣱ﴾ (Quran 37:40-49)

**"But not the chosen servants of Allah."** Their reward: **a known provision, fruits, honour, gardens of pleasure, couches facing each other, a cup from a flowing spring, white and delicious, with no headache in it and no intoxication.**

﴿فَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٖ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ قَآئِلࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ إِنِّي كَانَ لِي قَرِينࣱ﴾ ﴿يَقُولُ أَءِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُصَدِّقِينَ﴾ ﴿أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابٗا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَدِينُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ هَلۡ أَنتُم مُّطَّلِعُونَ﴾ ﴿فَٱطَّلَعَ فَرَءَاهُ فِي سَوَآءِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿قَالَ تَٱللَّهِ إِن كِدتَّ لَتُرۡدِينِ﴾ ﴿وَلَوۡلَا نِعۡمَةُ رَبِّي لَكُنتُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُحۡضَرِينَ﴾ ﴿أَفَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَيِّتِينَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَوۡتَتَنَا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُعَذَّبِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ ﴿لِمِثۡلِ هَٰذَا فَلۡيَعۡمَلِ ٱلۡعَٰمِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 37:50-61)

**The believer and his former friend**: one of the people of Paradise says: **"I had a companion who used to say: Are you among those who believe that when we have died and become dust and bones we will be recompensed?"** He looks down and sees him **"in the midst of the Hellfire"**, and says: **"By Allah, you almost ruined me. If not for the favour of my Lord, I would have been among those brought in."**

**The lesson about friends**: one bad companion can almost ruin your hereafter. **"For the like of this let the workers work."**

## Verses 62–74: The Tree of Zaqqum
﴿أَذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرࣱ نُّزُلًا أَمۡ شَجَرَةُ ٱلزَّقُّومِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰهَا فِتۡنَةٗ لِّلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهَا شَجَرَةࣱ تَخۡرُجُ فِيٓ أَصۡلِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿طَلۡعُهَا كَأَنَّهُۥ رُءُوسُ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّهُمۡ لَأٓكِلُونَ مِنۡهَا فَمَالِـُٔونَ مِنۡهَا ٱلۡبُطُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِنَّ لَهُمۡ عَلَيۡهَا لَشَوۡبٗا مِّنۡ حَمِيمٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِنَّ مَرۡجِعَهُمۡ لَإِلَى ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ (Quran 37:62-68)

**"Is that a better accommodation or the tree of Zaqqum?"** **"It is a tree issuing from the bottom of the Hellfire, its emerging fruit as if it was heads of the devils."** They eat from it and fill their bellies, then drink scalding water. **"They found their fathers astray, so they hastened in their footsteps."**

## Verses 75–82: Nuh
﴿وَلَقَدۡ نَادَىٰنَا نُوحࣱ فَلَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡمُجِيبُونَ﴾ ﴿وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلۡكَرۡبِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا ذُرِّيَّتَهُۥ هُمُ ٱلۡبَاقِينَ﴾ ﴿وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ نُوحٖ فِي ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ أَغۡرَقۡنَا ٱلۡأٓخَرِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:75-82)

**"Nuh had certainly called Us, and excellent are We as responders."** Allah saved him and his family from the great affliction, **made his descendants those remaining**, and left for him praise among later generations: **"Peace upon Nuh among the worlds. Indeed, We thus reward the doers of good."**

## Verses 83–113: Ibrahim
﴿۞وَإِنَّ مِن شِيعَتِهِۦ لَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ جَآءَ رَبَّهُۥ بِقَلۡبٖ سَلِيمٍ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوۡمِهِۦ مَاذَا تَعۡبُدُونَ﴾ ﴿أَئِفۡكًا ءَالِهَةٗ دُونَ ٱللَّهِ تُرِيدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَمَا ظَنُّكُم بِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿فَنَظَرَ نَظۡرَةٗ فِي ٱلنُّجُومِ﴾ ﴿فَقَالَ إِنِّي سَقِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فَتَوَلَّوۡاْ عَنۡهُ مُدۡبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَرَاغَ إِلَىٰٓ ءَالِهَتِهِمۡ فَقَالَ أَلَا تَأۡكُلُونَ﴾ ﴿مَا لَكُمۡ لَا تَنطِقُونَ﴾ ﴿فَرَاغَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ضَرۡبَۢا بِٱلۡيَمِينِ﴾ ﴿فَأَقۡبَلُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِ يَزِفُّونَ﴾ ﴿قَالَ أَتَعۡبُدُونَ مَا تَنۡحِتُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱللَّهُ خَلَقَكُمۡ وَمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿قَالُواْ ٱبۡنُواْ لَهُۥ بُنۡيَٰنٗا فَأَلۡقُوهُ فِي ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿فَأَرَادُواْ بِهِۦ كَيۡدٗا فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡأَسۡفَلِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:83-98)

**"And indeed, among his kind was Ibrahim, when he came to his Lord with a sound heart."** *Qalb salim*: a heart free of shirk, doubt and malice. Ibrahim broke the idols, and his people built a structure, lit a fire and threw him in, **"but We made them the most debased"**.

﴿وَقَالَ إِنِّي ذَاهِبٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّي سَيَهۡدِينِ﴾ ﴿رَبِّ هَبۡ لِي مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿فَبَشَّرۡنَٰهُ بِغُلَٰمٍ حَلِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 37:99-101)

**"I will go to my Lord; He will guide me."** He prayed: **"My Lord, grant me a child from among the righteous."** **"So We gave him good tidings of a forbearing boy."**

﴿فَلَمَّا بَلَغَ مَعَهُ ٱلسَّعۡيَ قَالَ يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰ فِي ٱلۡمَنَامِ أَنِّيٓ أَذۡبَحُكَ فَٱنظُرۡ مَاذَا تَرَىٰۚ قَالَ يَـٰٓأَبَتِ ٱفۡعَلۡ مَا تُؤۡمَرُۖ سَتَجِدُنِيٓ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فَلَمَّآ أَسۡلَمَا وَتَلَّهُۥ لِلۡجَبِينِ﴾ ﴿وَنَٰدَيۡنَٰهُ أَن يَـٰٓإِبۡرَٰهِيمُ﴾ ﴿قَدۡ صَدَّقۡتَ ٱلرُّءۡيَآۚ إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡبَلَـٰٓؤُاْ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾ ﴿وَفَدَيۡنَٰهُ بِذِبۡحٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾ (Quran 37:102-107)

**The sacrifice**: when the boy was old enough to work with him, Ibrahim said: **"O my son, indeed I have seen in a dream that I am sacrificing you, so see what you think."** The prophets' dreams are revelation. Ibrahim consulted his son, and the son answered: **"O my father, do as you are commanded. You will find me, if Allah wills, of the patient."**

**"And when they had both submitted and he put him down upon his forehead"**: both father and son submitted. **"We called to him: O Ibrahim, you have fulfilled the vision. Indeed, We thus reward the doers of good. Indeed, this was the clear trial. And We ransomed him with a great sacrifice."** The majority of scholars hold that the son was Isma'il, because the good tidings of Ishaq come afterwards in verse 112. Muslims commemorate this every year with the sacrifice of Eid al-Adha.

﴿وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ﴾ ﴿كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَبَشَّرۡنَٰهُ بِإِسۡحَٰقَ نَبِيّٗا مِّنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ﴾ ﴿وَبَٰرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ وَعَلَىٰٓ إِسۡحَٰقَۚ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِهِمَا مُحۡسِنࣱ وَظَالِمࣱ لِّنَفۡسِهِۦ مُبِينࣱ﴾ (Quran 37:108-113)

**"Peace upon Ibrahim."** **"And We gave him good tidings of Ishaq, a prophet from among the righteous."**

## Verses 114–132: Musa, Harun and Ilyas
﴿وَلَقَدۡ مَنَنَّا عَلَىٰ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَنَجَّيۡنَٰهُمَا وَقَوۡمَهُمَا مِنَ ٱلۡكَرۡبِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَنَصَرۡنَٰهُمۡ فَكَانُواْ هُمُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبِينَ﴾ ﴿وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَبِينَ﴾ ﴿وَهَدَيۡنَٰهُمَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ﴾ ﴿وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمَا فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُمَا مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:114-122)

Musa and Harun were saved from the great affliction and given the clear Scripture: **"Peace upon Musa and Harun."**

﴿وَإِنَّ إِلۡيَاسَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ قَالَ لِقَوۡمِهِۦٓ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ﴾ ﴿أَتَدۡعُونَ بَعۡلٗا وَتَذَرُونَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡخَٰلِقِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱللَّهَ رَبَّكُمۡ وَرَبَّ ءَابَآئِكُمُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ ﴿فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَإِنَّهُمۡ لَمُحۡضَرُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ﴾ ﴿وَتَرَكۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ﴾ ﴿سَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰٓ إِلۡ يَاسِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:123-132)

**Ilyas** said to his people: **"Do you call upon Ba'l and leave the best of creators, Allah, your Lord and the Lord of your first forefathers?"** **"Peace upon Ilyasin."**

## Verses 133–148: Lut and Yunus
﴿وَإِنَّ لُوطٗا لَّمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ نَجَّيۡنَٰهُ وَأَهۡلَهُۥٓ أَجۡمَعِينَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا عَجُوزٗا فِي ٱلۡغَٰبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ دَمَّرۡنَا ٱلۡأٓخَرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّكُمۡ لَتَمُرُّونَ عَلَيۡهِم مُّصۡبِحِينَ﴾ ﴿وَبِٱلَّيۡلِۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ (Quran 37:133-138)

**Lut** and his family were saved, except an old woman among those who remained behind, and the others were destroyed. **"And indeed, you pass by them in the morning and at night. Then will you not use reason?"**

﴿وَإِنَّ يُونُسَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿إِذۡ أَبَقَ إِلَى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ ٱلۡمَشۡحُونِ﴾ ﴿فَسَاهَمَ فَكَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُدۡحَضِينَ﴾ ﴿فَٱلۡتَقَمَهُ ٱلۡحُوتُ وَهُوَ مُلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فَلَوۡلَآ أَنَّهُۥ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُسَبِّحِينَ﴾ ﴿لَلَبِثَ فِي بَطۡنِهِۦٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ﴾ ﴿۞فَنَبَذۡنَٰهُ بِٱلۡعَرَآءِ وَهُوَ سَقِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ شَجَرَةٗ مِّن يَقۡطِينٖ﴾ ﴿وَأَرۡسَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰ مِاْئَةِ أَلۡفٍ أَوۡ يَزِيدُونَ﴾ ﴿فَـَٔامَنُواْ فَمَتَّعۡنَٰهُمۡ إِلَىٰ حِينٖ﴾ (Quran 37:139-148)

**Yunus** fled to the laden ship. **Lots were drawn, and he was among the losers**, so he was thrown into the sea. **"The fish swallowed him while he was blameworthy."** **"And had he not been of those who exalt Allah, he would have remained inside its belly until the Day they are resurrected."** His glorification, *"There is no god but You; exalted are You; I was among the wrongdoers"*, saved him. He was thrown onto the open shore while ill, and Allah caused a gourd plant to grow over him. Then he was sent to a hundred thousand or more, **"and they believed, so We gave them enjoyment for a time"**.

**A lesson for every distress**: at-Tirmidhi reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "The du'a of Dhun-Nun (Yunus) when he called while in the belly of the whale: *La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min az-zalimin*; no Muslim ever calls with it for anything except that Allah answers him." Al-Albani graded it authentic.

## Verses 149–182: The Angels and the Final Victory
﴿فَٱسۡتَفۡتِهِمۡ أَلِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡبَنَاتُ وَلَهُمُ ٱلۡبَنُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ إِنَٰثٗا وَهُمۡ شَٰهِدُونَ﴾ ﴿أَلَآ إِنَّهُم مِّنۡ إِفۡكِهِمۡ لَيَقُولُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَدَ ٱللَّهُ وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ﴾ ﴿أَصۡطَفَى ٱلۡبَنَاتِ عَلَى ٱلۡبَنِينَ﴾ ﴿مَا لَكُمۡ كَيۡفَ تَحۡكُمُونَ﴾ ﴿أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿أَمۡ لَكُمۡ سُلۡطَٰنࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ ﴿فَأۡتُواْ بِكِتَٰبِكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ﴾ ﴿وَجَعَلُواْ بَيۡنَهُۥ وَبَيۡنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ نَسَبٗاۚ وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمَتِ ٱلۡجِنَّةُ إِنَّهُمۡ لَمُحۡضَرُونَ﴾ ﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّكُمۡ وَمَا تَعۡبُدُونَ﴾ ﴿مَآ أَنتُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ بِفَٰتِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا مَنۡ هُوَ صَالِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿وَمَامِنَّآ إِلَّا لَهُۥ مَقَامࣱ مَّعۡلُومࣱ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّا لَنَحۡنُ ٱلصَّآفُّونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّا لَنَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُسَبِّحُونَ﴾ (Quran 37:149-166)

The idolaters claimed angels were Allah's daughters. **"Exalted is Allah above what they describe."** The angels themselves say: **"Indeed, we are those who line up in rows, and indeed, we are those who exalt Allah."**

﴿وَلَقَدۡ سَبَقَتۡ كَلِمَتُنَا لِعِبَادِنَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُمۡ لَهُمُ ٱلۡمَنصُورُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّ جُندَنَا لَهُمُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبُونَ﴾ (Quran 37:171-173)

**"And Our word has already preceded for Our servants, the messengers: indeed, they would be those given victory, and indeed, Our soldiers will be those who overcome."**

﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ ٱلۡعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَى ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 37:180-182)

**The closing**: **"Exalted is your Lord, the Lord of might, above what they describe. And peace upon the messengers. And praise to Allah, Lord of the worlds."** Glorification, peace and praise: a perfect ending.

## Word Meanings
- **As-Saffat**: those lined up in rows.
- **Az-Zajirat**: those who drive.
- **Marid**: rebellious.
- **Shihab thaqib**: piercing flame.
- **Zajrah**: a single shout.
- **Ghawl**: headache, harm.
- **Qarin**: companion.
- **Sawa' al-jahim**: the midst of Hellfire.
- **Az-Zaqqum**: a tree in Hell.
- **Qalb salim**: a sound heart.
- **Ghulam halim**: a forbearing boy.
- **Tallahu lil-jabin**: put him down on his forehead.
- **Dhibh 'azim**: a great sacrifice.
- **Al-Mudhadin**: the losers in the lot.
- **Yaqtin**: a gourd plant.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Peace upon..."** after each prophet: a refrain of honour.
2. **"You almost ruined me"**: a whole friendship summed up.
3. **"Heads of the devils"**: an image of horror for an unseen thing.
4. **"See what you think"**: a father consulting his son on the hardest command.
5. **"They had both submitted"**: shared obedience in two words.
6. **The ending**: glorification, peace and praise.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna ilahakum la-wahid* (verse 4), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'a* (verse 6).
- **Izhar**: in *bi-dhibhin 'azim* (verse 107) and *salamun 'ala* (verse 79), the tanween before the throat letter 'ayn is pronounced clearly.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yaqtin* (verse 146), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Ya-Sin before it ended with resurrection and "His command is only when He intends a thing that He says to it, Be, and it is", and As-Saffat continues with "it will be only one shout". Surah Sad after it continues the stories of the prophets with Dawud, Sulayman and Ayyub.

## Lessons From the Story of the Sacrifice
1. **Obedience comes before understanding.**
2. **Consult even when the command is clear**, as Ibrahim did with his son.
3. **Raise children who love Allah more than themselves.**
4. **Trials are proportionate to faith**: "this was the clear trial".
5. **Allah never wastes sincere obedience**: the son was ransomed.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Straighten your rows in prayer** like the angels.
2. **Choose friends carefully**: one could ruin you.
3. **Come to Allah with a sound heart.**
4. **Teach your children obedience to Allah through love.**
5. **Glorify Allah often**: it saved Yunus.
6. **Say the du'a of Yunus** in every distress.
7. **End your gatherings with glorification and praise.**

## How to Memorise Surah As-Saffat
- **Week 1**: verses 1–74.
- **Week 2**: verses 75–113.
- **Week 3**: verses 114–148.
- **Week 4**: verses 149–182.

## Teaching Children
- **Tell them the story of Ibrahim and Isma'il** and connect it to Eid al-Adha.
- **Tell them the story of Yunus in the whale** and teach his du'a.
- **Practise straight rows** in family prayer.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who are "those lined up in rows"?
The angels, standing in ranks in worship.

### Which son did Ibrahim prepare to sacrifice?
The majority of scholars hold it was Isma'il, because the good tidings of Ishaq come after the story.

### What was the "great sacrifice"?
A ram sent by Allah as ransom for Ibrahim's son, commemorated in Eid al-Adha.

### Why was Yunus saved from the whale?
Because he was among those who glorified Allah, as verse 143 says.

### What is the du'a of Yunus?
"La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min az-zalimin"; at-Tirmidhi reported that no Muslim calls with it except that Allah answers, and al-Albani graded it authentic.

### What does "a sound heart" mean?
A heart free from shirk, doubt and malice.

### How many verses are in Surah As-Saffat?
182 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah As-Saffat moves from the angels in their rows to a parade of prophets, each honoured with "Peace upon him". It shows a father and son who both submitted, a prophet saved from the belly of a whale by glorification, and a believer who almost lost Paradise because of a friend. Come to Allah with a sound heart, glorify Him in every distress, and end with what the surah ends with: peace upon the messengers, and praise to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
