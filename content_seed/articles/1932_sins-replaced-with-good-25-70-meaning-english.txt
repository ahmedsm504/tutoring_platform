﴿إِلَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ عَمَلٗا صَٰلِحٗا فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يُبَدِّلُ ٱللَّهُ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمۡ حَسَنَٰتٖۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورٗا رَّحِيمٗا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:70]

## The context
- The previous verses mention shirk, murder and zina and their punishment. Then comes **the exception**.

## Three conditions
| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Repentance | Turning back sincerely |
| Faith | Correct belief |
| Righteous deeds | Proof of change |

## How are sins replaced?
- Scholars give two explanations:
  1. Allah replaces sinful **habits** with good ones in this life.
  2. On the Day of Judgment, the sins themselves are turned into good deeds, as in a hadith narrated by Muslim.

## Generosity beyond forgiveness
- Forgiveness wipes the record clean; replacement **fills** it with good.

## Lessons
- **No past is too dark** for repentance.
- **Follow repentance with action.**
