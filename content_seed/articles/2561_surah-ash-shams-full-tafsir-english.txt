Surah Ash-Shams contains the longest chain of consecutive oaths in the Quran: eleven of them. Allah swears by the sun and its morning light, the moon, the day, the night, the sky, the earth and the human soul, and all of it leads to one conclusion: "He has succeeded who purifies it, and he has failed who buries it." Your success or failure is tied to what you do with your own soul. Then, as an example of failure, the surah tells the story of Thamud, who killed the she-camel of Allah and were destroyed.

This guide explains Surah Ash-Shams verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and a practical programme for purifying the soul.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ash-Shams
- **Number of verses**: 15.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 91, in the thirtieth juz.
- **Name**: Ash-Shams means "the sun", from the first verse.
- **Recommended by the Prophet ﷺ**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, when Mu'adh made the prayer too long for his people, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Are you causing trouble, O Mu'adh? Why did you not pray with *Glorify the name of your Lord, the Most High*, *By the sun and its brightness* and *By the night when it covers*? For behind you pray the old, the weak and those with needs."

## Verses 1–7: Eleven Oaths
﴿وَٱلشَّمۡسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَنَفۡسٖ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا﴾ (Quran 91:1-7)

**The oaths in order**:
1. **The sun**: the greatest source of light and heat.
2. **Its morning brightness** (*duha*): its light when it rises high in the morning.
3. **The moon when it follows it**: rising after the sun sets, or taking its light from it.
4. **The day when it displays it**: revealing the sun's brightness.
5. **The night when it covers it**: hiding its light.
6. **The sky.**
7. **And He who built it**: Allah, or its well-built structure.
8. **The earth.**
9. **And He who spread it**.
10. **A soul.**
11. **And He who proportioned it**.

**Notice the pairs**: sun and moon, day and night, sky and earth. Then the series ends with the soul, which also contains a pair: wickedness and righteousness. The whole universe of opposites prepares you to understand the opposites inside yourself.

**"A soul" without "the"**: the word *nafs* is indefinite, to show its greatness and to include every soul.

## Verse 8: Inspired With Wickedness and Righteousness
﴿فَأَلۡهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقۡوَىٰهَا﴾ (Quran 91:8)

**"And inspired it with its wickedness and its righteousness."** Allah placed in every soul the knowledge of right and wrong and the ability to choose between them. Every person knows in his nature that honesty is good and lying is ugly, that justice is right and oppression is wrong. Then the messengers came to explain this in detail.

**Destiny and choice together**: in Sahih Muslim, 'Imran ibn Husayn reported that two men asked the Prophet ﷺ whether what people do was already decreed. He said: "Yes, it has been decreed for them and has come to pass in them, and the confirmation of that is in the Book of Allah: *And by a soul and He who proportioned it, and inspired it with its wickedness and its righteousness*." Allah knows and decrees, and man chooses and is responsible.

## Verses 9–10: The Verdict
﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا﴾ (Quran 91:9-10)

After eleven oaths comes the answer that deserves all this emphasis:
- **"He has succeeded who purifies it"** (*zakkaha*): purification has two sides, cleansing the soul of vices and growing it with virtues.
- **"And he has failed who buries it"** (*dassaha*): the word means to hide or bury, as if the sinner pushes his soul down into the dirt so its dignity can no longer be seen.

**The Prophet's ﷺ du'a**: in Sahih Muslim, Zayd ibn Arqam reported that the Prophet ﷺ used to say: *"Allahumma ati nafsi taqwaha, wa zakkiha anta khayru man zakkaha, anta waliyyuha wa mawlaha."* "O Allah, give my soul its piety and purify it, for You are the best of those who purify it. You are its Guardian and its Master." Notice: the Prophet ﷺ asked Allah to purify his soul. Purification is the servant's effort and Allah's gift together.

## Verses 11–15: Thamud and the She-Camel
﴿كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ بِطَغۡوَىٰهَآ﴾ ﴿إِذِ ٱنۢبَعَثَ أَشۡقَىٰهَا﴾ ﴿فَقَالَ لَهُمۡ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ نَاقَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَسُقۡيَٰهَا﴾ ﴿فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمۡدَمَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّهُم بِذَنۢبِهِمۡ فَسَوَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقۡبَٰهَا﴾ (Quran 91:11-15)

**"Thamud denied in its transgression"**: their arrogance made them reject their prophet Salih ﷺ.

**"When the most wretched of them rose up"**: the most wretched man of the tribe hurried to kill the she-camel. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'Abdullah ibn Zam'ah reported that he heard the Prophet ﷺ give a sermon in which he mentioned the she-camel and the one who hamstrung it, and he said: "*When the most wretched of them rose up*: a strong, wicked man, powerful among his people, rose up for it."

**"The Messenger of Allah said to them: The she-camel of Allah, and her drink"**: Salih warned them not to harm the camel and not to deny her the water on her appointed day.

**"But they denied him and hamstrung her"**: the act was done by one man, yet the verse says "they". Why? Because the people approved of it and supported him. **"So their Lord crushed them for their sin and levelled them"**, and **"He does not fear its consequence"**.

## Why Did the Verse Say "They" Hamstrung Her?
This is one of the strongest lessons in the surah: **the one who approves of a sin shares in it**. Only one man struck the camel, but the whole people were satisfied, so the punishment included them all. It warns us against approving of wrongdoing or staying silent about it when we are able to object.

## Word Meanings
- **Duha**: the brightness of the morning sun.
- **Talaha**: followed it.
- **Jallaha**: displayed it.
- **Yaghshaha**: covers it.
- **Tahaha**: spread it out.
- **Sawwaha**: proportioned it.
- **Alhamaha**: inspired it.
- **Fujur**: wickedness.
- **Taqwa**: righteousness, God-consciousness.
- **Zakkaha**: purified it.
- **Dassaha**: buried it, corrupted it.
- **Taghwa**: transgression.
- **Damdama**: crushed, brought total destruction.
- **'Uqbaha**: its consequence.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Eleven oaths**: the longest chain in the Quran, showing how serious purification is.
2. **Every verse ends in "-ha"**: a single rhyme that gives the surah its gentle, flowing rhythm.
3. **Opposites everywhere**: sun and moon, day and night, sky and earth, wickedness and righteousness, success and failure.
4. **"Buries it"**: a vivid image of the soul pushed into the dirt.
5. **"Crushed them"**: *damdama* sounds like the crushing it describes.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Plain fath in the verse endings**: endings such as *duhaha* and *talaha* are read with fath without imala in the narration of Hafs.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *wa nafsin wa ma* (verse 7), the tanween merges into the waw with nasalisation.
- **Iqlab**: in *inba'atha* (verse 12), the noon sakinah before the ba' becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *as-sama'i* (verse 5).
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *inba'atha* (verse 12), the ba' sakinah, and in *'uqbaha* (verse 15), the qaf sakinah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Balad before it ended with the companions of the right and the companions of the left, and Ash-Shams explains the cause: purifying the soul or burying it. Surah Al-Layl after it describes the two groups in more detail: "As for him who gives and fears Allah... and as for him who is stingy and considers himself free of need."

## Purification Between Effort and Allah's Help
Allah says "He has succeeded who purifies it", crediting the servant, and the Prophet ﷺ prayed "and purify it, for You are the best of those who purify it", asking Allah. There is no contradiction. The servant is commanded to strive, and success comes from Allah. In Surah An-Nur: "And if not for the favour of Allah upon you and His mercy, not one of you would ever have been pure, but Allah purifies whom He wills." So work hard, do not rely on your work, keep making du'a, and never praise yourself: "So do not claim yourselves to be pure; He is most knowing of who fears Him."

## A Weekly Programme for Purifying the Soul
- **Day 1, belief**: review your tawhid and reflect on Ayat al-Kursi.
- **Day 2, prayer**: pray at least one prayer with complete focus.
- **Day 3, the tongue**: guard it from backbiting and lies all day.
- **Day 4, wealth**: give charity, even a little.
- **Day 5, the heart**: let go of a grudge and forgive someone.
- **Day 6, relationships**: call a relative and serve your parents.
- **Day 7, accounting**: review the week, thank Allah and seek forgiveness.

Then repeat. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "The most beloved deeds to Allah are those done regularly, even if small."

## Signs of Burying the Soul
1. **Persisting in sin** without repentance.
2. **Approving of wrongdoing** and staying silent about it.
3. **Transgression and arrogance**, as with Thamud.
4. **Belittling what Allah has made sacred**.
5. **Neglecting prayer and remembrance**.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Make the du'a "Allahumma ati nafsi taqwaha" daily.**
2. **Have a plan for purifying yourself**: it does not happen by accident.
3. **Do not approve of sin**: the one who approves is a partner.
4. **Beware of arrogance**: it destroyed Thamud.
5. **Respect what Allah has made sacred.**
6. **Reflect on the sun, the moon, the day and the night.**
7. **Keep the prayer light for others**: recite short surahs like this one.

## How to Memorise Surah Ash-Shams
- **Day 1**: verses 1–7 (the oaths).
- **Day 2**: verses 8–10.
- **Day 3**: verses 11–15, then revise the whole surah.

## Teaching Children
- **Go out at sunset** and read the first verses as the sun goes down and the moon appears.
- **Tell them the story of the she-camel** simply.
- **Teach them the du'a** "Allahumma ati nafsi taqwaha".
- **Explain purification** as cleaning the heart from bad habits, like washing hands before eating.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How many oaths are in Surah Ash-Shams?
Eleven, the longest chain of consecutive oaths in the Quran.

### What is the answer to the oaths?
"He has succeeded who purifies it, and he has failed who buries it."

### What does "inspired it with its wickedness and its righteousness" mean?
Allah made every soul know the way of good and evil and gave it the ability to choose.

### What does "dassaha" mean?
To bury or hide the soul in sins, corrupting it and lowering its dignity.

### Who killed the she-camel?
A man from Thamud whom the Prophet ﷺ described as strong, wicked and powerful among his people, as in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### Why does the verse say "they hamstrung her" when one man did it?
Because the people approved of his act, and the one who approves of a sin shares in it.

### What du'a is connected to Surah Ash-Shams?
"Allahumma ati nafsi taqwaha, wa zakkiha anta khayru man zakkaha, anta waliyyuha wa mawlaha", reported by Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Ash-Shams?
15 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

### What does "the moon when it follows it" mean?
When the moon follows the sun, rising after it sets, or taking its light from it.

### What does "He does not fear its consequence" mean?
Allah does not fear any consequence of destroying them, because He is the Sovereign whom no one can question or hold to account.

## Conclusion
Surah Ash-Shams makes the whole universe a witness to one fact: your destiny is tied to your own soul. Purify it and you succeed; bury it and you fail. The sun rises every morning to remind you, and the night covers every evening to remind you. Use each day to purify yourself, and ask your Lord: give my soul its piety and purify it, for You are the best of those who purify it.
