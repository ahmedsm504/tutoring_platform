﴿قَالَ لَا تَثۡرِيبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ يَغۡفِرُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ وَهُوَ أَرۡحَمُ ٱلرَّـٰحِمِينَ﴾ [Yusuf 12:92]

## What is *tathrib*?
- Reproach and blame, repeating someone's mistake to them.

## Complete forgiveness
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *No blame upon you* | I will not remind you |
| *Today* | At the moment of my power |
| *May Allah forgive you* | A duʿa for them |
| *He is the Most Merciful* | Hope in Allah's mercy |

## Forgiveness with power
- The brothers stood guilty before the most powerful man in Egypt. He could have punished; he forgave.

## At the conquest of Makkah
- It is reported in the Seerah that when the Prophet ﷺ entered Makkah victorious, he told Quraysh he would say to them what Yusuf said to his brothers: *No blame upon you today.*

## Lessons
- **Forgive without reminding.**
- **Use power to forgive**, not to avenge.
