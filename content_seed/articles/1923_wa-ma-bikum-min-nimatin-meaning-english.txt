﴿وَمَا بِكُم مِّن نِّعۡمَةٖ فَمِنَ ٱللَّهِۖ ثُمَّ إِذَا مَسَّكُمُ ٱلضُّرُّ فَإِلَيۡهِ تَجۡـَٔرُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِذَا كَشَفَ ٱلضُّرَّ عَنكُمۡ إِذَا فَرِيقࣱ مِّنكُم بِرَبِّهِمۡ يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ [An-Nahl 16:53-54]

## Every blessing
- Health, family, safety, sight, faith: **all** are from Allah, even those that seem earned by effort.

## *Tajʾarun*
- A loud, desperate cry, like an animal in distress. In harm, people call on Allah with full sincerity.

## Then forgetting
| In harm | After relief |
|---|---|
| Sincere duʿa | Some return to shirk or heedlessness |

## The believer's way
- Remember Allah in ease as you do in hardship. The Prophet ﷺ said: *Remember Allah in prosperity and He will remember you in adversity* (narrated by Ahmad).

## Lessons
- **Thank Allah after relief**, not only before.
- **Attribute every blessing** to Him.
