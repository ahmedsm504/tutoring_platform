**Surah Abasa** (42 ayat) begins with a scene that teaches a timeless lesson: **every sincere seeker matters**.

﴿عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَن جَآءَهُ ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ﴾ — *He frowned and turned away, because the blind man came to him.*

## The story
- The Prophet ﷺ was busy inviting leaders of Quraysh, hoping they would accept Islam.
- ʿAbdullah ibn Umm Maktum, a blind companion, came asking to be taught, as reported in At-Tirmidhi.
- Allah gently corrected: ﴿وَمَا يُدۡرِيكَ لَعَلَّهُۥ يَزَّكَّىٰٓ﴾ — *And what would make you know? Perhaps he would be purified.*

## Structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–16 | The blind man and the honoured pages of the Quran |
| 17–23 | Man's ingratitude |
| 24–32 | Look at your food: rain, crops, fruits |
| 33–42 | The Day of the deafening blast |

## Look at your food
﴿فَلۡيَنظُرِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِلَىٰ طَعَامِهِۦٓ﴾ — *Let man look at his food.*
- Water poured down, the earth split open, grain, grapes, olives, palms, gardens and fruits.

## The Day of fleeing
﴿يَوۡمَ يَفِرُّ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مِنۡ أَخِيهِ﴾ ﴿وَأُمِّهِۦ وَأَبِيهِ﴾ ﴿وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَبَنِيهِ﴾ ﴿لِكُلِّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ شَأۡنࣱ يُغۡنِيهِ﴾

## Lessons
- **Value people by their sincerity**, not their status.
- **Gratitude** for every meal.
