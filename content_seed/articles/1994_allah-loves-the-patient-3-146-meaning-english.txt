﴿وَكَأَيِّن مِّن نَّبِيّٖ قَٰتَلَ مَعَهُۥ رِبِّيُّونَ كَثِيرࣱ فَمَا وَهَنُواْ لِمَآ أَصَابَهُمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَمَا ضَعُفُواْ وَمَا ٱسۡتَكَانُواْۗ وَٱللَّهُ يُحِبُّ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ [Al Imran 3:146]

## The context
- At Uhud a rumour spread that the Prophet ﷺ had been killed, and some lost heart. Allah reminded them of the followers of earlier prophets.

## *Ribbiyyun*
- Explained as large groups, or devoted scholars of the Lord.

## Three things they did not do
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Wahanu* | Lose heart |
| *Daʿufu* | Weaken in body and resolve |
| *Istakanu* | Submit to the enemy |

## Their duʿa
- *Our Lord, forgive us our sins and our excesses, make our feet firm, and grant us victory* (3:147). They began with **forgiveness**, knowing sins are a cause of defeat.

## Lessons
- **Firmness is tied to Allah**, not to any person.
- **Start duʿa in hardship** with istighfar.
