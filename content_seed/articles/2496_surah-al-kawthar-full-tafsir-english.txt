Surah Al-Kawthar is the shortest surah in the Quran: three verses and ten words. Yet it carries enormous good news for the Prophet ﷺ and his Ummah: a river in Paradise called al-Kawthar, and a Pond on the Day of Resurrection from which the believers will drink, never to be thirsty again. It then commands gratitude through prayer and sacrifice for Allah alone, and closes by declaring that whoever hates the Prophet ﷺ is the one truly cut off.

This guide explains the surah in English and gathers the authentic narrations about al-Kawthar and the Pond.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Kawthar
- **Number of verses**: 3, the shortest surah in the Quran.
- **Type**: Meccan in the standard mushaf; the hadith of Anas in Sahih Muslim suggests it was revealed in Madinah, and scholars have discussed the point.
- **Position**: Surah 108, in the thirtieth juz.
- **Name**: from *al-Kawthar*, the abundant good.

## The Full Text of Surah Al-Kawthar
﴿إِنَّآ أَعۡطَيۡنَٰكَ ٱلۡكَوۡثَرَ﴾ ﴿فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنۡحَرۡ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلۡأَبۡتَرُ﴾ (Quran 108:1-3)

**Meaning:** Indeed, We have given you al-Kawthar. So pray to your Lord and sacrifice. Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off.

## The Story of Its Revelation
In Sahih Muslim, Anas ibn Malik reported: "While the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was among us one day, he dozed briefly, then raised his head smiling. We said: What makes you smile, O Messenger of Allah? He said: A surah has just been revealed to me. Then he recited: *In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Indeed, We have given you al-Kawthar. So pray to your Lord and sacrifice. Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off.* Then he said: Do you know what al-Kawthar is? We said: Allah and His Messenger know best. He said: It is a river that my Lord, Mighty and Majestic, has promised me. On it is abundant good. It is a Pond to which my Ummah will come on the Day of Resurrection. Its vessels are as numerous as the stars. A servant among them will be pulled away, and I will say: My Lord, he is from my Ummah. And it will be said: You do not know what he introduced after you."

**Lessons from the hadith:**
1. The Prophet ﷺ smiled with joy at this surah.
2. He explained al-Kawthar himself.
3. Some will be driven away from the Pond because of what they introduced into the religion after him.

## Verse 1: Indeed We Have Given You al-Kawthar
﴿إِنَّآ أَعۡطَيۡنَٰكَ ٱلۡكَوۡثَرَ﴾ (Quran 108:1)

**"Indeed, We"**: the plural of majesty emphasises the greatness of the Giver, and a gift reflects the greatness of the one who gives it.

**"Have given you"**: in the past tense, as if already accomplished, because Allah's promise is certain.

**"Al-Kawthar"**: from *kathrah*, abundance. The word's form indicates something extremely abundant.

### What Is al-Kawthar?
1. **A river in Paradise.** Besides the hadith above, in Sahih al-Bukhari Anas reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "When I was taken up to the heavens, I came to a river whose banks were domes of hollow pearls. I said: What is this, O Jibril? He said: This is al-Kawthar."
2. **Abundant good.** In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas said that al-Kawthar is "the abundant good that Allah gave him". Abu Bishr told Sa'id ibn Jubayr that people say it is a river in Paradise, and Sa'id replied: "The river in Paradise is part of the good that Allah gave him."

Both views agree: al-Kawthar is abundant good, and among its greatest forms is the river in Paradise. The good given to the Prophet ﷺ includes prophethood, the Quran, intercession, the praised station, countless followers, the raising of his mention, the Pond and the river.

## Verse 2: So Pray to Your Lord and Sacrifice
﴿فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنۡحَرۡ﴾ (Quran 108:2)

**"So pray"**: the *fa* links the command to the gift. Because of this great blessing, pray, as an act of thanks.

**"To your Lord"**: sincerely and to Him alone, unlike the idolaters who prayed to idols.

**"And sacrifice"** (*wanhar*): slaughter your sacrifice for Allah alone and in His name, unlike those who slaughtered in the names of idols.

**Why prayer and sacrifice?** Prayer is the greatest physical act of worship, and sacrifice is among the greatest financial acts of worship. Surah Al-An'am brings them together:

﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحۡيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 6:162)

**Meaning:** Say: My prayer, my sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds.

Some scholars also understand the verse as the Eid prayer followed by the sacrifice. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, al-Bara' reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "The first thing we begin with on this day of ours is to pray, then we return and sacrifice. Whoever does that has followed our Sunnah."

## Verse 3: Your Enemy Is the One Cut Off
﴿إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلۡأَبۡتَرُ﴾ (Quran 108:3)

**"Shani'aka"**: the one who hates you. **"Al-abtar"**: the one cut off from every good, or whose memory ends when he dies.

The commentators report that some of the idolaters, such as al-'As ibn Wa'il, mocked the Prophet ﷺ after his sons died in infancy, calling him *abtar*, meaning a man without male descendants whose name would end with him. Allah answered: it is your enemy who is cut off.

**The promise came true.** The Prophet's name is called out in the adhan five times a day in every corner of the earth; billions send blessings on him; his followers are in every land and generation. His enemies are remembered, if at all, only with blame.

## The Description of the Pond in Authentic Hadith
In al-Bukhari and Muslim, 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr reported that the Prophet ﷺ said:
- "My Pond is a month's journey across."
- "Its water is whiter than milk, and its scent is better than musk."
- "Its cups are like the stars of the sky."
- "Whoever drinks from it will never be thirsty again."

In Sahih Muslim, Thawban reported that two channels pour into it from Paradise.

**Who will be turned away?** As in the hadith of Anas, those who introduced changes into the religion after the Prophet ﷺ. This is a serious warning against innovation in religion.

## Word Meanings
- **A'taynaka**: We have given you.
- **Al-Kawthar**: abundant good, including the river in Paradise.
- **Fa-salli**: so pray.
- **Li-rabbika**: to your Lord alone.
- **Wanhar**: and sacrifice (originally for camels).
- **Shani'aka**: your hater.
- **Al-Abtar**: cut off from all good and remembrance.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Inna" with the plural of majesty**: emphasising the greatness of the gift.
2. **"Al-Kawthar"**: an intensive form meaning extreme abundance.
3. **The "fa" in fa-salli**: gratitude follows the gift.
4. **"To your Lord" placed first**: sincerity.
5. **"Huwa al-abtar"**: the pronoun gives exclusivity: *he* is the one cut off, not you.
6. **Three balanced verses**: a gift, a response of gratitude, and divine protection.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Ghunnah** on the doubled nun in *inna* (verses 1 and 3).
- **Natural madd** in *a'taynaka*.
- **Izhar** in *wanhar*: the nun sakinah is clear before the throat letter *ha*.
- **Madd muttasil** in *shani'aka*: alif followed by hamza in one word, four or five counts.
- **Qalqalah sughra** in *al-abtar*: the *ba* with sukun.
- **Heavy ra**: when stopping on *al-kawthar*, *wanhar* and *al-abtar*, the ra is pronounced heavy because the letter before it has a fatha or a sukun preceded by a fatha.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Al-Kawthar is the opposite of the surah before it, Al-Ma'un. Al-Ma'un condemns those heedless of their prayer; Al-Kawthar commands sincere prayer. Al-Ma'un condemns showing off; Al-Kawthar says "to your Lord". Al-Ma'un condemns withholding small kindnesses; Al-Kawthar commands sacrifice, which feeds people. After Al-Kawthar comes Al-Kafirun: having been told to pray and sacrifice for Allah alone, the Prophet ﷺ declares: "I do not worship what you worship."

## Gratitude Through Worship
The surah sets a rule for dealing with blessings: a gift from Allah, then thanks through worship. Allah did not say after "We have given you al-Kawthar": so rejoice, or so enjoy. He said: pray and sacrifice. Every Muslim can apply this:
- When Allah gives you wealth, give its zakat and some charity.
- When He gives you a child, thank Him with good upbringing.
- When He gives you knowledge, act on it and teach it.
- When He gives you health, use it in worship.

The Prophet ﷺ prayed at night until his feet swelled, and when asked why, he said: "Should I not be a grateful servant?" (al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## Sending Blessings on the Prophet ﷺ: A Path to the Pond
In Sahih Muslim, 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever sends one blessing upon me, Allah sends ten upon him." The best wording is the Ibrahimic salawat taught in al-Bukhari and Muslim from Ka'b ibn 'Ujrah.

## Practical Lessons
1. Thank Allah for blessings through worship.
2. Make your prayer and sacrifice sincerely for Allah.
3. Love the Prophet ﷺ: his enemy is the one cut off.
4. Follow the Sunnah and avoid innovation, so you are not turned away from the Pond.
5. Do not be shaken by those who insult the Prophet ﷺ; Allah defends him.
6. Offer the udhiyah if you are able.
7. Send blessings on the Prophet ﷺ often.

## Teaching Children
- Describe the river: whiter than milk, sweeter than honey, with cups like stars.
- Tell them that those who love and follow the Prophet ﷺ will drink from it.
- Share the story of the Prophet ﷺ smiling when the surah was revealed.

## "The One Cut Off" in Our Times
Everyone who hates the Prophet ﷺ, mocks him or mocks his Sunnah has a share of the description *al-abtar*: cut off from goodness and blessing. History bears witness that those who fought and insulted him are remembered only with blame, while his name rises from every minaret. This should reassure a Muslim who sees the Prophet ﷺ insulted today. Allah has taken it upon Himself to defend him, and the believer's role is to support him by following his Sunnah, spreading his biography and responding with wisdom and evidence rather than with anger alone.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is al-Kawthar?
A river in Paradise promised to the Prophet ﷺ, and more broadly the abundant good Allah gave him, as reported in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the shortest surah of the Quran?
Surah Al-Kawthar, with three verses.

### What does "pray to your Lord and sacrifice" mean?
Pray sincerely to Allah and sacrifice for Him alone; some scholars apply it to the Eid prayer and the udhiyah.

### What does "your enemy is the one cut off" mean?
Whoever hates the Prophet ﷺ is the one cut off from all good and remembrance.

### What is the description of the Pond?
A month's journey across, whiter than milk, more fragrant than musk, with cups like the stars; whoever drinks from it will never be thirsty.

### Who will be turned away from the Pond?
Those who introduced changes into the religion after the Prophet ﷺ, as in Sahih Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Kawthar?
3 verses.

### Is al-Kawthar the same as the Pond?
Al-Kawthar is a river in Paradise, and the Pond is on the plains of the Day of Resurrection, fed from it. That is why the hadith says "it is a Pond to which my Ummah will come": the two are connected.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Kawthar tells you that when Allah gives abundantly, the right response is sincere prayer and generous giving. And it reminds you that whoever loves the Prophet ﷺ and follows his Sunnah can hope to drink from his Pond a drink after which there is no thirst. O Allah, let us drink from the Pond of Your Prophet ﷺ a pleasant drink after which we never thirst again.
