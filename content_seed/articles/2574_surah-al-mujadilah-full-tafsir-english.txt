Surah Al-Mujadilah opens with one of the most comforting sentences a person in pain could hear: "Certainly has Allah heard the speech of the one who argues with you concerning her husband and directs her complaint to Allah. And Allah hears your dialogue." A woman, wronged by her husband's words, came to the Prophet ﷺ in a small room and pleaded her case, and Allah answered her from above the seven heavens with a law that protects every woman after her. The surah then teaches that Allah is present in every private conversation, gives the etiquette of gatherings, raises the people of faith and knowledge, and ends by describing the true party of Allah.

This guide explains Surah Al-Mujadilah section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Mujadilah
- **Number of verses**: 22.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 58, the first surah of the twenty-eighth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Mujadilah means "the woman who argues" or "pleads".
- **A unique feature**: the name of Allah appears in every one of its verses.
- **Main themes**: Allah's hearing and knowledge, family law, the etiquette of private talk and gatherings, and loyalty to Allah.

## The Story of Khawlah bint Tha'labah
The woman was Khawlah bint Tha'labah. Her husband, Aws ibn as-Samit, said to her in anger: "You are to me like my mother's back", a pre-Islamic formula called *zihar* that was considered permanent divorce. She came to the Prophet ﷺ to complain, pleading for herself and her children.

'A'ishah said: "Praise be to Allah, whose hearing encompasses all voices. The woman who argued came to the Prophet ﷺ and spoke to him while I was in a corner of the house, and I could not hear what she said. Then Allah revealed: *Certainly has Allah heard the speech of the one who argues with you concerning her husband*." Al-Bukhari mentioned it without a full chain, and an-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah reported it with an authentic chain.

**Lessons from the story**:
1. **Allah hears every complaint**, even when the person next to you cannot.
2. **A woman's voice was honoured** with revelation.
3. **Pain should be taken to Allah**: "and directs her complaint to Allah".
4. **Islam abolished an unjust custom** and replaced it with a merciful law.

## Verses 1–4: The Ruling of Zihar
﴿قَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّتِي تُجَٰدِلُكَ فِي زَوۡجِهَا وَتَشۡتَكِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَٱللَّهُ يَسۡمَعُ تَحَاوُرَكُمَآۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعُۢ بَصِيرٌ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُظَٰهِرُونَ مِنكُم مِّن نِّسَآئِهِم مَّا هُنَّ أُمَّهَٰتِهِمۡۖ إِنۡ أُمَّهَٰتُهُمۡ إِلَّا ٱلَّـٰٓـِٔي وَلَدۡنَهُمۡۚ وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَيَقُولُونَ مُنكَرٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡقَوۡلِ وَزُورٗاۚ وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَعَفُوٌّ غَفُورࣱ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يُظَٰهِرُونَ مِن نِّسَآئِهِمۡ ثُمَّ يَعُودُونَ لِمَا قَالُواْ فَتَحۡرِيرُ رَقَبَةٖ مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَتَمَآسَّاۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ تُوعَظُونَ بِهِۦۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿فَمَن لَّمۡ يَجِدۡ فَصِيَامُ شَهۡرَيۡنِ مُتَتَابِعَيۡنِ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يَتَمَآسَّاۖ فَمَن لَّمۡ يَسۡتَطِعۡ فَإِطۡعَامُ سِتِّينَ مِسۡكِينٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِتُؤۡمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦۚ وَتِلۡكَ حُدُودُ ٱللَّهِۗ وَلِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 58:1-4)

**Zihar is rejected**: **"Their mothers are none except those who gave birth to them. And indeed, they are saying an objectionable statement and a falsehood."** A wife does not become a mother by words.

**The expiation** for whoever says it and then wants to return to his wife, before they touch each other:
1. **Freeing a slave.**
2. **If he cannot, fasting two consecutive months.**
3. **If he cannot, feeding sixty poor people.**

**"That is so you will believe in Allah and His Messenger. And those are the limits of Allah."** The expiation is heavy so that people take their words seriously.

## Verses 5–7: Allah Is With Every Secret Meeting
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُحَآدُّونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ كُبِتُواْ كَمَا كُبِتَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۚ وَقَدۡ أَنزَلۡنَآ ءَايَٰتِۭ بَيِّنَٰتٖۚ وَلِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ عَذَابࣱ مُّهِينࣱ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يَبۡعَثُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ جَمِيعٗا فَيُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا عَمِلُوٓاْۚ أَحۡصَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ وَنَسُوهُۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ﴾ ﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ مَا يَكُونُ مِن نَّجۡوَىٰ ثَلَٰثَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ رَابِعُهُمۡ وَلَا خَمۡسَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ سَادِسُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَدۡنَىٰ مِن ذَٰلِكَ وَلَآ أَكۡثَرَ إِلَّا هُوَ مَعَهُمۡ أَيۡنَ مَا كَانُواْۖ ثُمَّ يُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا عَمِلُواْ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 58:5-7)

**"There is no private conversation of three but that He is the fourth of them, nor of five but that He is the sixth of them, nor of fewer than that or more except that He is with them wherever they are."** Allah's knowledge encompasses every whisper. **"Then He will inform them of what they did on the Day of Resurrection."**

## Verses 8–10: Secret Talk for Harm
﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ نُهُواْ عَنِ ٱلنَّجۡوَىٰ ثُمَّ يَعُودُونَ لِمَا نُهُواْ عَنۡهُ وَيَتَنَٰجَوۡنَ بِٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِ وَمَعۡصِيَتِ ٱلرَّسُولِۖ وَإِذَا جَآءُوكَ حَيَّوۡكَ بِمَا لَمۡ يُحَيِّكَ بِهِ ٱللَّهُ وَيَقُولُونَ فِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَوۡلَا يُعَذِّبُنَا ٱللَّهُ بِمَا نَقُولُۚ حَسۡبُهُمۡ جَهَنَّمُ يَصۡلَوۡنَهَاۖ فَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا تَنَٰجَيۡتُمۡ فَلَا تَتَنَٰجَوۡاْ بِٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِ وَمَعۡصِيَتِ ٱلرَّسُولِ وَتَنَٰجَوۡاْ بِٱلۡبِرِّ وَٱلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلنَّجۡوَىٰ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ لِيَحۡزُنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَيۡسَ بِضَآرِّهِمۡ شَيۡـًٔا إِلَّا بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلۡيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 58:8-10)

The hypocrites used to whisper together in sin and aggression and disobedience to the Messenger, and when they came to him they greeted him with a distorted greeting. Allah's rule for believers: **"When you converse privately, do not converse about sin and aggression and disobedience to the Messenger, but converse about righteousness and piety."**

**"Private conversation is only from Satan, that he may grieve those who have believed."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "If you are three, two should not converse privately apart from the third until you mix with other people, because that would grieve him."

## Verse 11: Make Room and Rise
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمۡ تَفَسَّحُواْ فِي ٱلۡمَجَٰلِسِ فَٱفۡسَحُواْ يَفۡسَحِ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ وَإِذَا قِيلَ ٱنشُزُواْ فَٱنشُزُواْ يَرۡفَعِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنكُمۡ وَٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ دَرَجَٰتٖۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 58:11)

**"When you are told: Make room in gatherings, then make room; Allah will make room for you."** The reward matches the deed: whoever makes room for others, Allah makes room for him in this world, in the grave and in Paradise. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Umar reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: "No one should make another man get up from his seat and then sit in it; rather, make room and spread out."

**"And when you are told: Arise, then arise."** If asked to leave, do so without resentment.

**"Allah will raise those who have believed among you and those who were given knowledge, by degrees."** Faith raises, and knowledge with faith raises higher. In Sahih Muslim, 'Umar said that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah raises some people by this Book and lowers others by it."

## Verses 12–13: Charity Before Private Counsel
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا نَٰجَيۡتُمُ ٱلرَّسُولَ فَقَدِّمُواْ بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ نَجۡوَىٰكُمۡ صَدَقَةٗۚ ذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكُمۡ وَأَطۡهَرُۚ فَإِن لَّمۡ تَجِدُواْ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمٌ﴾ ﴿ءَأَشۡفَقۡتُمۡ أَن تُقَدِّمُواْ بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ نَجۡوَىٰكُمۡ صَدَقَٰتٖۚ فَإِذۡ لَمۡ تَفۡعَلُواْ وَتَابَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥۚ وَٱللَّهُ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 58:12-13)

The believers were commanded to give charity before speaking privately with the Prophet ﷺ, to lighten the burden on him and to distinguish sincere people. Then the ruling was lightened: **"Have you feared to present before your consultation charities? Then when you do not and Allah has forgiven you, then establish prayer and give zakah and obey Allah and His Messenger."**

## Verses 14–21: The Party of Satan
﴿۞أَلَمۡ تَرَ إِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ تَوَلَّوۡاْ قَوۡمًا غَضِبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡهِم مَّا هُم مِّنكُمۡ وَلَا مِنۡهُمۡ وَيَحۡلِفُونَ عَلَى ٱلۡكَذِبِ وَهُمۡ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿أَعَدَّ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٗا شَدِيدًاۖ إِنَّهُمۡ سَآءَ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱتَّخَذُوٓاْ أَيۡمَٰنَهُمۡ جُنَّةٗ فَصَدُّواْ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَلَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ مُّهِينࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّن تُغۡنِيَ عَنۡهُمۡ أَمۡوَٰلُهُمۡ وَلَآ أَوۡلَٰدُهُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيۡـًٔاۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلنَّارِۖ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يَبۡعَثُهُمُ ٱللَّهُ جَمِيعٗا فَيَحۡلِفُونَ لَهُۥ كَمَا يَحۡلِفُونَ لَكُمۡ وَيَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّهُمۡ عَلَىٰ شَيۡءٍۚ أَلَآ إِنَّهُمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡكَٰذِبُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱسۡتَحۡوَذَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ فَأَنسَىٰهُمۡ ذِكۡرَ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ حِزۡبُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ أَلَآ إِنَّ حِزۡبَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُحَآدُّونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ فِي ٱلۡأَذَلِّينَ﴾ ﴿كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَأَغۡلِبَنَّ أَنَا۠ وَرُسُلِيٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَوِيٌّ عَزِيزࣱ﴾ (Quran 58:14-21)

The hypocrites took as allies people with whom Allah was angry, and swore false oaths. **"They took their oaths as a cover."** Their wealth and children will not help them. **"Satan has overcome them and made them forget the remembrance of Allah. Those are the party of Satan. Unquestionably, the party of Satan, they will be the losers."**

**"Allah has written: I will surely overcome, I and My messengers."** Victory belongs to Allah and His messengers.

## Verse 22: The Party of Allah
﴿لَّا تَجِدُ قَوۡمٗا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡأٓخِرِ يُوَآدُّونَ مَنۡ حَآدَّ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَوۡ كَانُوٓاْ ءَابَآءَهُمۡ أَوۡ أَبۡنَآءَهُمۡ أَوۡ إِخۡوَٰنَهُمۡ أَوۡ عَشِيرَتَهُمۡۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ كَتَبَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ ٱلۡإِيمَٰنَ وَأَيَّدَهُم بِرُوحٖ مِّنۡهُۖ وَيُدۡخِلُهُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٖ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَاۚ رَضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ حِزۡبُ ٱللَّهِۚ أَلَآ إِنَّ حِزۡبَ ٱللَّهِ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ (Quran 58:22)

**"You will not find a people who believe in Allah and the Last Day having affection for those who oppose Allah and His Messenger, even if they were their fathers or their sons or their brothers or their kindred."** The loyalty of the heart belongs to Allah first.

**Important clarification**: this verse is about loving and supporting those who fight Allah and His Messenger and their cause. It does not forbid kindness and justice towards non-Muslim relatives who are not hostile: Allah says in Surah Al-Mumtahanah: "Allah does not forbid you from those who do not fight you because of religion... from being righteous towards them and acting justly towards them." And in Surah Luqman about non-Muslim parents: "accompany them in this world with appropriate kindness".

**Their reward**: **"Those, He has decreed within their hearts faith and supported them with a spirit from Him."** Gardens, Allah pleased with them and they with Him. **"Those are the party of Allah. Unquestionably, the party of Allah, they are the successful."**

## Word Meanings
- **Tujadiluka**: argues with you, pleads.
- **Tashtaki**: complains.
- **Tahawur**: dialogue, conversation.
- **Yuzahirun**: declare zihar.
- **Munkar**: objectionable.
- **Zur**: falsehood.
- **Najwa**: private conversation, secret counsel.
- **Tafassahu**: make room.
- **Unshuzu**: arise.
- **Junnah**: cover, shield.
- **Istahwadha**: overcame, took control.
- **Yuhadduna**: oppose.
- **Hizb**: party.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Allah has heard"**: an opening of comfort to every wronged person.
2. **Allah's name in every verse**: a surah filled with His presence.
3. **"The fourth of three... the sixth of five"**: precise numbers that show nothing escapes Him.
4. **"Make room... Allah will make room for you"**: reward matching the deed.
5. **The party of Satan and the party of Allah**: a clear contrast to end the surah.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *qad sami'a* (verse 1), the dal sakinah bounces lightly and is not merged into the sin in the narration of Hafs.
- **Iqlab**: in *sami'un basir* (verse 1), the tanween before the ba' becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna Allaha* (verse 1), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *alla'i* (verse 2), the hamzah follows the long vowel in the same word.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Hadid before it ended with Allah's bounty being in His hand, given to whom He wills, and Al-Mujadilah shows His bounty to a single wronged woman. Surah Al-Hashr after it begins with the expulsion of Banu an-Nadir, the victory promised in "I will surely overcome, I and My messengers".

## Etiquette of Gatherings From This Surah
1. **Make room** for newcomers.
2. **Do not whisper** with one person while a third is excluded.
3. **Keep private talk for good**: righteousness and piety.
4. **Give up your seat** if asked, without resentment.
5. **Honour people of knowledge.**
6. **Remember that Allah is the fourth of every three.**

## Practical Lessons
1. **Take your complaint to Allah**: He hears.
2. **Guard your words in marriage**: careless words have consequences.
3. **Fulfil expiations** when required.
4. **Make room for others** and Allah will make room for you.
5. **Seek knowledge with faith**: it raises you by degrees.
6. **Let your deepest loyalty be to Allah**, while being just and kind to all.

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Mujadilah
- **Week 1**: verses 1–6.
- **Week 2**: verses 7–11.
- **Week 3**: verses 12–17.
- **Week 4**: verses 18–22.

- **Tell them the story of Khawlah** and how Allah heard her.
- **Practise making room** when guests arrive.
- **Teach them not to whisper** in front of a friend who is left out.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who was the woman who argued in Surah Al-Mujadilah?
Khawlah bint Tha'labah, whose husband Aws ibn as-Samit declared zihar against her.

### What is zihar?
Saying to one's wife "You are to me like my mother's back", a pre-Islamic form of divorce that Islam rejected and made subject to expiation.

### What is the expiation for zihar?
Freeing a slave; if unable, fasting two consecutive months; if unable, feeding sixty poor people.

### What does "He is the fourth of them" mean?
Allah is with every group of three in His knowledge, hearing and seeing, wherever they are.

### What does "Allah will raise those who believe and those given knowledge" mean?
Allah raises believers in rank, and raises those who combine faith with knowledge even higher.

### Who are the party of Allah?
Those whose deepest loyalty is to Allah and His Messenger, in whose hearts Allah has decreed faith.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Mujadilah?
22 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Mujadilah begins with a woman whose quiet complaint was heard above the seven heavens, and ends with the party of Allah, who will succeed. Between them it teaches that Allah is present in every private conversation, that making room for others brings room from Allah, and that faith joined with knowledge raises a person by degrees. Whatever your complaint, take it to the One who heard Khawlah.
