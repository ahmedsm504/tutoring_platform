Surah Al-Mutaffifin opens with a warning about something that looks small: cheating in weights and measures. A little less on the scale, a little extra taken when buying. But the surah connects this everyday dishonesty to the greatest reality of all: "Do they not think that they will be resurrected, for a tremendous Day, the Day when mankind will stand before the Lord of the worlds?" Then it opens two books: the record of the wicked in Sijjin and the record of the righteous in 'Illiyyin, and it describes the rust that slowly covers the heart until it can no longer see the truth.

This guide explains Surah Al-Mutaffifin verse by verse in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons for business and daily life.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Mutaffifin
- **Number of verses**: 36.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 83, in the thirtieth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Mutaffifin means "those who give short measure".
- **Main themes**: honesty in dealings, the two records, the rust on the heart, the bliss of the righteous and the mockery of the believers by the wicked.

## The Context of Revelation
An-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah reported from Ibn 'Abbas that when the Prophet ﷺ came to Madinah, its people were among the worst in giving measure, so Allah revealed "Woe to those who give short measure", and after that they improved their measuring. Al-Albani graded it *hasan*. A single surah changed the market practice of a whole city.

## Verses 1–6: Woe to the Cheaters
﴿وَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُطَفِّفِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ٱكۡتَالُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ يَسۡتَوۡفُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا كَالُوهُمۡ أَو وَّزَنُوهُمۡ يُخۡسِرُونَ﴾ ﴿أَلَا يَظُنُّ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَنَّهُم مَّبۡعُوثُونَ﴾ ﴿لِيَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٖ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلنَّاسُ لِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 83:1-6)

**"Woe to those who give short measure"**: *tatfif* means giving less than what is due. The surah defines them precisely: **when they take a measure from people, they take it in full**, but **when they measure or weigh for others, they give less**. They have two standards: one for themselves and one for others.

**The root of the problem**: "Do they not think that they will be resurrected?" The cheater cheats because he does not really believe he will stand before Allah. If he pictured the Day when **"mankind will stand before the Lord of the worlds"**, he would never touch the scale.

**How long that standing is**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn 'Umar reported that the Prophet ﷺ said about "the Day mankind will stand before the Lord of the worlds": "One of them will stand in his sweat up to the middle of his ears."

**Tatfif is wider than scales**: scholars point out that it covers any situation where you demand your full rights and give others less than theirs: an employee who demands his full salary but does not do his full work, an employer who demands full work but delays wages, a person who wants to be treated fairly but is unfair to others. All of it is a kind of short measure.

## Verses 7–17: The Record of the Wicked
﴿كَلَّآ إِنَّ كِتَٰبَ ٱلۡفُجَّارِ لَفِي سِجِّينٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا سِجِّينࣱ﴾ ﴿كِتَٰبࣱ مَّرۡقُومࣱ﴾ (Quran 83:7-9)

**"No! Indeed, the record of the wicked is in Sijjin"**: Sijjin is described as **"a written register"** (*kitabun marqum*). Commentators explain it as a place of confinement in the lowest depths, where the records of the wicked are kept, a register that is sealed and complete, with nothing added or removed.

﴿وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُكَذِّبُونَ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿وَمَا يُكَذِّبُ بِهِۦٓ إِلَّا كُلُّ مُعۡتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ﴾ ﴿إِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَٰتُنَا قَالَ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ﴾ (Quran 83:10-13)

**Who are the deniers?** Those who deny the Day of Recompense, and nobody denies it except **"every sinful transgressor"**. When the verses are recited to him, he says: **"Legends of the former peoples."**

﴿كَلَّاۖ بَلۡۜ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾ ﴿كَلَّآ إِنَّهُمۡ عَن رَّبِّهِمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لَّمَحۡجُوبُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ إِنَّهُمۡ لَصَالُواْ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ يُقَالُ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ﴾ (Quran 83:14-17)

**"No! Rather, the rust has covered their hearts because of what they used to earn."** This is one of the most important verses about the heart. *Ran* is rust or a layer of stain that builds up. At-Tirmidhi reported from Abu Hurayrah that the Prophet ﷺ said: "When the servant commits a sin, a black spot appears on his heart. If he gives it up, seeks forgiveness and repents, his heart is polished. But if he returns to it, the spot increases until it covers his heart. That is the *ran* that Allah mentioned: No! Rather, the rust has covered their hearts because of what they used to earn." At-Tirmidhi graded it *hasan sahih*.

**The lesson**: sins are not only individual acts; they leave marks that accumulate. A heart covered in rust stops being moved by the Quran and starts calling it "legends". The cure is quick repentance before the layers build up.

**"No! Indeed, from their Lord, that Day, they will be veiled."** Many scholars, including Imam ash-Shafi'i, took from this verse that the believers will see their Lord, because if the disbelievers are veiled from Him as a punishment, the believers are not. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, Jarir ibn 'Abdullah reported that the Prophet ﷺ looked at the full moon and said: "You will see your Lord as you see this moon, without crowding to see it."

## Verses 18–28: The Record of the Righteous
﴿كَلَّآ إِنَّ كِتَٰبَ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارِ لَفِي عِلِّيِّينَ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا عِلِّيُّونَ﴾ ﴿كِتَٰبࣱ مَّرۡقُومࣱ﴾ ﴿يَشۡهَدُهُ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ﴾ (Quran 83:18-21)

**"No! Indeed, the record of the righteous is in 'Illiyyin"**: a place of honour in the highest heights, also **"a written register"**, and **"witnessed by those brought near"**, the angels closest to Allah.

﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٍ﴾ ﴿عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ يَنظُرُونَ﴾ ﴿تَعۡرِفُ فِي وُجُوهِهِمۡ نَضۡرَةَ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾ ﴿يُسۡقَوۡنَ مِن رَّحِيقٖ مَّخۡتُومٍ﴾ ﴿خِتَٰمُهُۥ مِسۡكࣱۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَتَنَافَسِ ٱلۡمُتَنَٰفِسُونَ﴾ ﴿وَمِزَاجُهُۥ مِن تَسۡنِيمٍ﴾ ﴿عَيۡنٗا يَشۡرَبُ بِهَا ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ﴾ (Quran 83:22-28)

**The bliss of the righteous**:
- **On couches, looking**: at the delights Allah has given them, and at their Lord.
- **"You will recognise in their faces the radiance of bliss."**
- **"They will be given to drink from a sealed nectar, whose seal is musk."** A pure drink, sealed so no hand has touched it, and when it is finished, the last of it leaves the scent of musk.
- **"So for this let the competitors compete."** This is where competition belongs. People compete over money, positions and possessions; the Quran redirects all that energy towards Paradise.
- **Its mixture is from Tasnim**: a spring from which **"those brought near"** drink pure, while the righteous have it mixed into their drink.

## Verses 29–36: The Mockers
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أَجۡرَمُواْ كَانُواْ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ يَضۡحَكُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا مَرُّواْ بِهِمۡ يَتَغَامَزُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا ٱنقَلَبُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِمُ ٱنقَلَبُواْ فَكِهِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا رَأَوۡهُمۡ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِ لَضَآلُّونَ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أُرۡسِلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَٰفِظِينَ﴾ (Quran 83:29-33)

**How the wicked treated the believers in this world**:
1. **They laughed at them.**
2. **They winked at each other when passing them.**
3. **They returned home delighted with their mockery.**
4. **They said: "These people are truly astray."**

**"But they had not been sent as guardians over them"**: nobody appointed them to judge the believers.

﴿فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡكُفَّارِ يَضۡحَكُونَ﴾ ﴿عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ يَنظُرُونَ﴾ ﴿هَلۡ ثُوِّبَ ٱلۡكُفَّارُ مَا كَانُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 83:34-36)

**The tables turn**: on that Day, **the believers will laugh at the disbelievers**, from their couches, looking. **"Have the disbelievers been repaid for what they used to do?"** The answer is yes. Whoever mocks the people of faith in this world will see the situation reversed.

## Word Meanings
- **Al-Mutaffifin**: those who give short measure.
- **Iktalu**: when they take a measure for themselves.
- **Yukhsirun**: they cause loss, they give less.
- **Sijjin**: the place where the records of the wicked are kept, in the lowest depths.
- **Marqum**: written, inscribed, sealed.
- **Asatir al-awwalin**: legends of the former peoples.
- **Ran**: rust, the stain covering the heart.
- **'Illiyyin**: the place of the records of the righteous, in the highest heights.
- **Rahiq**: pure nectar, the finest drink.
- **Makhtum**: sealed.
- **Tasnim**: a spring in Paradise, the highest of its drinks.
- **Yataghamazun**: they wink at one another mockingly.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"Woe" at the very start**: an immediate, shocking warning.
2. **The double standard**: "when they take... when they give", exposing the cheater in two short verses.
3. **Sijjin and 'Illiyyin**: a contrast between the lowest and the highest, each "a written register".
4. **"Rust has covered their hearts"**: a powerful image of sin's effect.
5. **"Whose seal is musk"**: a detail that appeals to every sense.
6. **"Let the competitors compete"**: redirecting human ambition.
7. **The mockers mocked**: a perfect reversal at the end.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Saktah in "kalla bal rana"** (verse 14): in the recitation of Hafs there is a brief pause without breath on *bal* before *rana*, so that the lam is not merged into the ra'.
- **Idgham without ghunnah**: in *waylun lil-mutaffifin* (verse 1), the tanween merges into the lam.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *ula'ika* (verse 4).
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna kitaba* (verse 7), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Infitar before it ended with the recording angels and the Day of Recompense, and Al-Mutaffifin gives a concrete example of what they record: cheating in measure. Surah Al-Inshiqaq after it continues with the receiving of records: "As for the one given his record in his right hand..." which completes the picture of Sijjin and 'Illiyyin.

## Honesty in Business: Lessons from Al-Mutaffifin
1. **Give full measure and weight**: exactly as you would want to receive.
2. **Describe your goods honestly**: hiding defects is a form of short measure. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever cheats us is not of us."
3. **Pay wages on time**.
4. **Do the full work you are paid for**.
5. **Avoid one standard for yourself and another for others**.
6. **Remember the standing before the Lord of the worlds** whenever money is involved.

## Polishing the Heart
The image of rust is one of the most practical lessons in the surah. Here is how to keep the heart polished:
1. **Repent quickly**: do not let sins pile up.
2. **Seek forgiveness often**: the Prophet ﷺ sought forgiveness more than seventy times a day, as in Sahih al-Bukhari.
3. **Recite the Quran with reflection**: if it stops moving you, that is a warning sign.
4. **Do good deeds after bad ones**: they wipe them out.
5. **Avoid insisting on small sins**: small stains add up.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Be fair in every transaction**, large or small.
2. **Picture the Day of standing** when tempted to cheat.
3. **Repent before the rust settles**.
4. **Compete for Paradise**, not for the leftovers of this world.
5. **Do not mock religious people**: the tables will turn.
6. **Be patient if you are mocked for your faith**: the last laugh belongs to the believers.

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Mutaffifin
- **Day 1**: verses 1–9.
- **Day 2**: verses 10–17.
- **Day 3**: verses 18–28.
- **Day 4**: verses 29–36, then revise the whole surah.

## Teaching Children
- **Use a kitchen scale** to show what giving full measure means.
- **Explain the rust on the heart** with a mirror that gets dusty and is cleaned by saying "astaghfirullah".
- **Teach them to compete in good deeds**: who prays on time, who helps first.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does Al-Mutaffifin mean?
Those who give short measure: they take full measure for themselves but give less to others.

### What are Sijjin and 'Illiyyin?
Sijjin is where the records of the wicked are kept, in the lowest depths, and 'Illiyyin is where the records of the righteous are kept, in the highest heights.

### What is the "rust" on the heart?
The stain that builds up on the heart from repeated sins, as explained in the hadith of Abu Hurayrah reported by at-Tirmidhi.

### Does this surah indicate that believers will see Allah?
Many scholars took that from verse 15: if the disbelievers are veiled from their Lord, the believers will see Him, as confirmed in the hadith of Jarir in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the "sealed nectar"?
A pure drink of Paradise, sealed so nobody has touched it, whose last trace is the scent of musk.

### What is Tasnim?
A spring in Paradise from which those brought near to Allah drink pure.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Mutaffifin?
36 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Mutaffifin begins with a small coin on a scale and ends with the sealed nectar of Paradise. In between it shows how every unfair transaction, every repeated sin and every mocking smile is written in a register that will be opened. Give full measure, polish your heart with repentance, and let your competition be for 'Illiyyin.
