Juz 25 begins with ﴿۞إِلَيۡهِ يُرَدُّ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِۚ﴾, "To Him is referred knowledge of the Hour", so it is called **Juz Ilayhi Yuradd**. It runs from **Fussilat 41:47 to the end of Al-Jathiyah (45:37)**, containing four complete Hawamim: **Ash-Shura**, **Az-Zukhruf**, **Ad-Dukhan** and **Al-Jathiyah**. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 482 to 501**.

This is **the juz of the Hawamim**: Makki surahs with short verses about revelation and the Quran, Allah's oneness, the fate of the deniers and the Day of Reckoning.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **the revelation brought by all the prophets, and the vindication of the Quran against the deniers' doubts**. In Ash-Shura: ﴿شَرَعَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلدِّينِ مَا وَصَّىٰ بِهِۦ نُوحٗا﴾, "He has ordained for you of religion what He enjoined upon Nuh", closing with ﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ رُوحٗا مِّنۡ أَمۡرِنَاۚ﴾, "And thus We have revealed to you a spirit of Our command." In Az-Zukhruf: ﴿إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰهُ قُرۡءَٰنًا عَرَبِيّٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾, "We have made it an Arabic Quran so you may understand." In Ad-Dukhan: ﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٍۚ﴾, "We sent it down on a blessed night." In Al-Jathiyah: ﴿هَٰذَا كِتَٰبُنَا يَنطِقُ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ﴾, "This is Our record, speaking about you in truth."

The message: **the Quran is spirit and light. Whoever turns from it, Allah appoints a devil for him; whoever follows it is guided to a straight path.**

## Part one: the close of Surah Fussilat (47-54)

﴿۞إِلَيۡهِ يُرَدُّ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِۚ وَمَا تَخۡرُجُ مِن ثَمَرَٰتٖ مِّنۡ أَكۡمَامِهَا وَمَا تَحۡمِلُ مِنۡ أُنثَىٰ وَلَا تَضَعُ إِلَّا بِعِلۡمِهِۦۚ﴾, "To Him is referred knowledge of the Hour. No fruit emerges from its sheath, and no female conceives or gives birth, except with His knowledge." Then man's fickleness: he never tires of praying for good, but if evil touches him he despairs. Then the great promise: ﴿سَنُرِيهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا فِي ٱلۡأٓفَاقِ وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ يَتَبَيَّنَ لَهُمۡ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّۗ أَوَلَمۡ يَكۡفِ بِرَبِّكَ أَنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ﴾ ﴿أَلَآ إِنَّهُمۡ فِي مِرۡيَةٖ مِّن لِّقَآءِ رَبِّهِمۡۗ أَلَآ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مُّحِيطُۢ﴾, "We will show them Our signs in the horizons and within themselves until it becomes clear to them that it is the truth. Is it not enough that your Lord is Witness over all things? They are in doubt about meeting their Lord; He encompasses all things."

**Lesson**: the more a person learns about the universe and himself, the more evidence of the Quran's truth appears.

## Part two: Surah Ash-Shura (1-53)

Ash-Shura is Makki, with 53 verses, opening with letters across two verses: ﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿عٓسٓقٓ﴾. It is named for ﴿وَأَمۡرُهُمۡ شُورَىٰ بَيۡنَهُمۡ﴾, "and their affairs are by consultation among them." For the whole surah see [Surah Ash-Shura Meaning in English](/blog/surah-ash-shura-meaning-english/).

### 1. Revelation and Allah's greatness (1-12)
Thus Allah, the Almighty, the Wise, reveals to you and to those before you. Then: ﴿تَكَادُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ يَتَفَطَّرۡنَ مِن فَوۡقِهِنَّۚ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَيَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ لِمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ﴾, "The heavens almost break apart from above them, and the angels glorify their Lord with praise and seek forgiveness for those on earth." Then the reference point for disagreement: ﴿وَمَا ٱخۡتَلَفۡتُمۡ فِيهِ مِن شَيۡءٖ فَحُكۡمُهُۥٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبِّي عَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡتُ وَإِلَيۡهِ أُنِيبُ﴾, "Whatever you differ about, its judgement is with Allah. That is Allah, my Lord; in Him I trust and to Him I turn."

Then the great rule in knowing Allah: ﴿فَاطِرُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا وَمِنَ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ أَزۡوَٰجٗا يَذۡرَؤُكُمۡ فِيهِۚ لَيۡسَ كَمِثۡلِهِۦ شَيۡءࣱۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ﴾, "Originator of the heavens and earth. He made for you mates from yourselves and mates among the cattle, multiplying you thereby. There is nothing like Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing."

**Reflect**: the verse combines negation, "nothing is like Him", with affirmation, "He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing". We affirm for Allah what He affirmed for Himself without likening Him to creation. Read [Nothing Is Like Him: 42:11](/blog/laysa-kamithlihi-shay-meaning-english/).

### 2. The one religion (13-26)
A new quarter: ﴿۞شَرَعَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلدِّينِ مَا وَصَّىٰ بِهِۦ نُوحٗا وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ وَمَا وَصَّيۡنَا بِهِۦٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ وَعِيسَىٰٓۖ أَنۡ أَقِيمُواْ ٱلدِّينَ وَلَا تَتَفَرَّقُواْ فِيهِۚ﴾, "He has ordained for you of religion what He enjoined upon Nuh, what We revealed to you, and what We enjoined upon Ibrahim, Musa and 'Isa: establish the religion and do not be divided in it." The five messengers of firm resolve, united by one charge: establish the religion and do not divide.

Then: ﴿ٱللَّهُ لَطِيفُۢ بِعِبَادِهِۦ يَرۡزُقُ مَن يَشَآءُۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡقَوِيُّ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ﴾, "Allah is subtle with His servants; He provides for whom He wills, and He is the Strong, the Almighty." Then: ﴿قُل لَّآ أَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ أَجۡرًا إِلَّا ٱلۡمَوَدَّةَ فِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰۗ﴾, "Say: I ask no payment of you for it, except love for kinship." Then: ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يَقۡبَلُ ٱلتَّوۡبَةَ عَنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَنِ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾, "He is the One who accepts repentance from His servants, pardons evil deeds and knows what you do."

### 3. The wisdom of provision and calamities (27-35)
A new quarter: ﴿۞وَلَوۡ بَسَطَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِعِبَادِهِۦ لَبَغَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَٰكِن يُنَزِّلُ بِقَدَرٖ مَّا يَشَآءُۚ﴾, "Had Allah extended provision to His servants without limit, they would have transgressed on earth; but He sends down in measure what He wills." Then the cause of calamities: ﴿وَمَآ أَصَٰبَكُم مِّن مُّصِيبَةٖ فَبِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَن كَثِيرٖ﴾, "Whatever calamity befalls you is because of what your hands have earned, and He pardons much." Read [Whatever Calamity Befalls You: 42:30](/blog/wa-ma-asabakum-min-musiba-meaning-english/).

**Lesson**: measured provision is a mercy; were it extended without limit, people would transgress. A calamity expiates some sins, and Allah pardons most.

### 4. The traits of believers (36-43)
﴿فَمَآ أُوتِيتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَمَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَبۡقَىٰ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ﴾, "Whatever you are given is the enjoyment of worldly life, but what is with Allah is better and more lasting for those who believe and rely on their Lord." Then their traits:
1. **Faith and reliance on Allah**.
2. **Avoiding major sins, and forbearance**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَجۡتَنِبُونَ كَبَـٰٓئِرَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ وَإِذَا مَا غَضِبُواْ هُمۡ يَغۡفِرُونَ﴾, "who avoid major sins and indecencies, and when angry, they forgive."
3. **Responding, prayer, consultation and spending**: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَجَابُواْ لِرَبِّهِمۡ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَمۡرُهُمۡ شُورَىٰ بَيۡنَهُمۡ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾, "who respond to their Lord, establish prayer, whose affairs are by consultation, and who spend from what We provided them."
4. **Defending against oppression**, while preferring pardon: ﴿وَجَزَـٰٓؤُاْ سَيِّئَةٖ سَيِّئَةࣱ مِّثۡلُهَاۖ فَمَنۡ عَفَا وَأَصۡلَحَ فَأَجۡرُهُۥ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "The recompense of an evil is an evil like it, but whoever pardons and makes reconciliation, his reward is with Allah."
5. **Patience and forgiveness**: ﴿وَلَمَن صَبَرَ وَغَفَرَ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ لَمِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾, "Whoever is patient and forgives, that is of the matters requiring resolve."

**Reflect**: consultation is placed between prayer and spending, showing its rank in the believers' lives.

### 5. The gift of children and the modes of revelation (44-53)
﴿لِّلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ يَهَبُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ إِنَٰثٗا وَيَهَبُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ ٱلذُّكُورَ﴾ ﴿أَوۡ يُزَوِّجُهُمۡ ذُكۡرَانٗا وَإِنَٰثٗاۖ وَيَجۡعَلُ مَن يَشَآءُ عَقِيمًاۚ﴾, "To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and earth. He creates what He wills: He gives daughters to whom He wills and sons to whom He wills, or combines them, males and females, and makes barren whom He wills." Daughters are mentioned first, and all of it is a gift.

Then a new quarter on the modes of revelation: ﴿۞وَمَا كَانَ لِبَشَرٍ أَن يُكَلِّمَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا وَحۡيًا أَوۡ مِن وَرَآيِٕ حِجَابٍ أَوۡ يُرۡسِلَ رَسُولٗا فَيُوحِيَ بِإِذۡنِهِۦ مَا يَشَآءُۚ﴾, "It is not for any human that Allah should speak to him except by inspiration, from behind a veil, or by sending a messenger who reveals by His permission what He wills." The close: ﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ رُوحٗا مِّنۡ أَمۡرِنَاۚ مَا كُنتَ تَدۡرِي مَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ وَلَا ٱلۡإِيمَٰنُ وَلَٰكِن جَعَلۡنَٰهُ نُورٗا نَّهۡدِي بِهِۦ مَن نَّشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَاۚ﴾, "Thus We revealed to you a spirit of Our command. You did not know what the Book or faith was, but We made it a light by which We guide whom We will of Our servants."

**Reflect**: Allah called the Quran a "spirit" because hearts live by it, and a "light" because it guides.

## Part three: Surah Az-Zukhruf (1-89)

Az-Zukhruf is Makki, with 89 verses; *zukhruf* means gold and ornament. For the whole surah see [Surah Az-Zukhruf Meaning in English](/blog/surah-az-zukhruf-meaning-english/).

### 1. The Arabic Quran and the blessing of riding (1-14)
"We have made it an Arabic Quran so you may understand", ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ فِيٓ أُمِّ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَدَيۡنَا لَعَلِيٌّ حَكِيمٌ﴾, "and it is, in the Mother of the Book with Us, exalted and full of wisdom." Then Allah's favours in the earth, water and cattle, and the supplication for riding: ﴿لِتَسۡتَوُۥاْ عَلَىٰ ظُهُورِهِۦ ثُمَّ تَذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَةَ رَبِّكُمۡ إِذَا ٱسۡتَوَيۡتُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَتَقُولُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُۥ مُقۡرِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ﴾, "so you may settle on their backs, then remember your Lord's favour when you are settled, and say: glory be to the One who subjected this to us, for we could not have done it ourselves; and to our Lord we will return." In Sahih Muslim, when the Prophet ﷺ mounted his camel to set out on a journey, he said "Allahu akbar" three times, then this supplication.

**Lesson**: every means of transport, from animals to cars and planes, is a blessing that calls for glorification and the reminder of our return to Allah. See [The Quran While Travelling](/blog/quran-while-traveling-english/).

### 2. Imitating forefathers, and Ibrahim (15-28)
The claim of daughters for Allah, then the affluent's perpetual argument: ﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ مَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ فِي قَرۡيَةٖ مِّن نَّذِيرٍ إِلَّا قَالَ مُتۡرَفُوهَآ إِنَّا وَجَدۡنَآ ءَابَآءَنَا عَلَىٰٓ أُمَّةٖ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰٓ ءَاثَٰرِهِم مُّقۡتَدُونَ﴾, "Likewise We sent no warner before you to any town but its affluent said: we found our fathers on a way, and we are following in their footsteps." Hizb 50 begins at verse 24. Then Ibrahim, who disowned what they worshipped except the One who created him, and made it a lasting word among his descendants.

### 3. The trial of wealth and status (29-45)
They said: why was this Quran not sent down to a great man from the two towns? The reply: ﴿أَهُمۡ يَقۡسِمُونَ رَحۡمَتَ رَبِّكَۚ نَحۡنُ قَسَمۡنَا بَيۡنَهُم مَّعِيشَتَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَرَفَعۡنَا بَعۡضَهُمۡ فَوۡقَ بَعۡضٖ دَرَجَٰتٖ لِّيَتَّخِذَ بَعۡضُهُم بَعۡضٗا سُخۡرِيّٗاۗ﴾, "Do they apportion your Lord's mercy? It is We who apportioned their livelihood in worldly life and raised some above others in degrees, so some may employ others." Then: were it not that people would become one community of disbelief, We would have given those who disbelieve in the Most Merciful roofs of silver; this world is that insignificant to Allah.

Then the grave rule: ﴿وَمَن يَعۡشُ عَن ذِكۡرِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ نُقَيِّضۡ لَهُۥ شَيۡطَٰنٗا فَهُوَ لَهُۥ قَرِينࣱ﴾, "Whoever turns blindly from the remembrance of the Most Merciful, We appoint for him a devil who becomes his companion."

**Lesson**: one who turns from the Quran does not remain empty; a devil fills the space and stays with him.

### 4. Pharaoh and the glitter of kingship (46-56)
﴿وَنَادَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنُ فِي قَوۡمِهِۦ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ أَلَيۡسَ لِي مُلۡكُ مِصۡرَ وَهَٰذِهِ ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِيٓۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ﴾, "Pharaoh called out to his people: my people, is not the kingdom of Egypt mine, and these rivers flowing beneath me? Do you not see?" He then belittled Musa: am I not better than this contemptible man who can barely speak clearly? Then: ﴿فَٱسۡتَخَفَّ قَوۡمَهُۥ فَأَطَاعُوهُۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمٗا فَٰسِقِينَ﴾, "So he made fools of his people, and they obeyed him. They were a defiantly disobedient people."

**Reflect**: a tyrant makes light of people's minds and succeeds only with a corrupt people. Tyranny is a partnership between an oppressor and those willing to accept him.

### 5. 'Isa and close friends (57-73)
A new quarter: ﴿۞وَلَمَّا ضُرِبَ ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَ مَثَلًا إِذَا قَوۡمُكَ مِنۡهُ يَصِدُّونَ﴾, "When the son of Maryam was presented as an example, your people raised an outcry." He was only a servant We favoured. Then: ﴿وَإِنَّهُۥ لَعِلۡمࣱ لِّلسَّاعَةِ فَلَا تَمۡتَرُنَّ بِهَا وَٱتَّبِعُونِۚ﴾, "He is a sign of the Hour, so do not doubt it, and follow me." 'Isa's descent is among the signs of the Hour in the Sunnah.

Then a truth about friendship: ﴿ٱلۡأَخِلَّآءُ يَوۡمَئِذِۭ بَعۡضُهُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ عَدُوٌّ إِلَّا ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾, "Close friends that Day will be enemies to one another, except the God-conscious." Then the bliss of Paradise: ﴿يُطَافُ عَلَيۡهِم بِصِحَافٖ مِّن ذَهَبٖ وَأَكۡوَابٖۖ وَفِيهَا مَا تَشۡتَهِيهِ ٱلۡأَنفُسُ وَتَلَذُّ ٱلۡأَعۡيُنُۖ﴾, "Platters of gold and cups will be passed among them, with whatever souls desire and eyes delight in."

**Lesson**: friendship not built on taqwa turns into enmity on the Day of Resurrection.

### 6. The people of the Fire and the close (74-89)
The criminals will abide in the punishment of Hell, and they will call out: O Malik, let your Lord finish us. He will say: you will remain. Then: ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ إِلَٰهࣱ وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِلَٰهࣱۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾, "He is the One who is God in the heaven and God on earth, the Wise, the All-Knowing": the One worshipped in both. The close: so turn aside from them and say "peace"; they will come to know.

## Part four: Surah Ad-Dukhan (1-59)

Ad-Dukhan is Makki, with 59 verses. For the whole surah see [Surah Ad-Dukhan Meaning in English](/blog/surah-ad-dukhan-meaning-english/).

### 1. The blessed night (1-8)
﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٍۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُنذِرِينَ﴾ ﴿فِيهَا يُفۡرَقُ كُلُّ أَمۡرٍ حَكِيمٍ﴾, "We sent it down on a blessed night; We are always warning. On it every wise matter is made distinct." It is Laylat al-Qadr, as Surah Al-Qadr makes clear: ﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةِ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ﴾, "We sent it down on the Night of Decree." Read [Surah Al-Qadr Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-qadr-meaning-english/).

### 2. The smoke and the great seizure (9-16)
Rather, they are in doubt, playing. ﴿فَٱرۡتَقِبۡ يَوۡمَ تَأۡتِي ٱلسَّمَآءُ بِدُخَانٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾, "So wait for the Day the sky brings a visible smoke", covering the people: this is a painful punishment. In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, Ibn Mas'ud related it to the famine that struck Quraysh after the Prophet ﷺ prayed against them, when a man would see something like smoke between the sky and the earth from exhaustion. Then: the Day We strike with the greatest seizure, We will take retribution.

### 3. Pharaoh and what they left behind (17-33)
A new quarter with Pharaoh's story: We tested before them the people of Pharaoh. Musa was told to travel by night with Allah's servants and leave the sea parted. Then what they left: ﴿كَمۡ تَرَكُواْ مِن جَنَّـٰتٖ وَعُيُونٖ﴾ ﴿وَزُرُوعٖ وَمَقَامٖ كَرِيمٖ﴾, "How many gardens and springs they left behind, crops and noble places", and comforts they enjoyed; and neither heaven nor earth wept for them.

**Lesson**: tyrants leave their palaces and gardens in a moment and no one weeps for them.

### 4. Resurrection and recompense (34-59)
The idolaters' denial: there is only our first death. The reply: ﴿وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ﴾, "We did not create the heavens and earth and all between them in play." Then: ﴿إِنَّ شَجَرَتَ ٱلزَّقُّومِ﴾ ﴿طَعَامُ ٱلۡأَثِيمِ﴾, "The tree of Zaqqum is the food of the sinner", like molten metal boiling in the bellies. The contrast: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي مَقَامٍ أَمِينٖ﴾, "The God-conscious will be in a secure place", in gardens and springs. The close: ﴿فَإِنَّمَا يَسَّرۡنَٰهُ بِلِسَانِكَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ﴾, "We have made it easy in your tongue so they may remember"; so wait, they too are waiting.

## Part five: Surah Al-Jathiyah (1-37)

Al-Jathiyah is Makki, with 37 verses, named for ﴿وَتَرَىٰ كُلَّ أُمَّةٖ جَاثِيَةٗۚ﴾, "You will see every nation kneeling." For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Jathiya Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-jathiya-meaning-english/).

### 1. Subjection and signs (1-15)
Signs in the heavens and earth, in the creation of man and animals, and in the alternation of night and day. Then a new quarter on subjection, including: ﴿وَسَخَّرَ لَكُم مَّا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا مِّنۡهُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾, "He has subjected to you whatever is in the heavens and earth, all from Him. In that are signs for people who reflect."

### 2. The law and following desire (16-26)
﴿ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَٰكَ عَلَىٰ شَرِيعَةٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَمۡرِ فَٱتَّبِعۡهَا وَلَا تَتَّبِعۡ أَهۡوَآءَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾, "Then We put you on a clear way of the matter, so follow it and do not follow the desires of those who do not know." Then: ﴿أَمۡ حَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱجۡتَرَحُواْ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ أَن نَّجۡعَلَهُمۡ كَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ سَوَآءٗ مَّحۡيَاهُمۡ وَمَمَاتُهُمۡۚ سَآءَ مَا يَحۡكُمُونَ﴾, "Do those who commit evils think We will make them like those who believe and do good, equal in life and death? Evil is their judgement."

Then the frightening picture: ﴿أَفَرَءَيۡتَ مَنِ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَٰهَهُۥ هَوَىٰهُ وَأَضَلَّهُ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٖ وَخَتَمَ عَلَىٰ سَمۡعِهِۦ وَقَلۡبِهِۦ وَجَعَلَ عَلَىٰ بَصَرِهِۦ غِشَٰوَةٗ﴾, "Have you seen one who takes his desire as his god, whom Allah has let go astray despite knowledge, sealing his hearing and heart and putting a cover over his sight?" Then the materialists' claim: ﴿وَقَالُواْ مَا هِيَ إِلَّا حَيَاتُنَا ٱلدُّنۡيَا نَمُوتُ وَنَحۡيَا وَمَا يُهۡلِكُنَآ إِلَّا ٱلدَّهۡرُۚ وَمَا لَهُم بِذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عِلۡمٍۖ إِنۡ هُمۡ إِلَّا يَظُنُّونَ﴾, "They say: there is only our worldly life; we die and live, and nothing destroys us but time. They have no knowledge of that; they only assume."

**Lesson**: "astray despite knowledge": knowledge alone does not save if desire prevails.

### 3. The kneeling nations and the close (27-37)
﴿وَتَرَىٰ كُلَّ أُمَّةٖ جَاثِيَةٗۚ كُلُّ أُمَّةٖ تُدۡعَىٰٓ إِلَىٰ كِتَٰبِهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ ﴿هَٰذَا كِتَٰبُنَا يَنطِقُ عَلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا نَسۡتَنسِخُ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾, "You will see every nation kneeling, every nation called to its record: today you will be recompensed for what you used to do. This is Our record, speaking about you in truth; We used to have recorded what you did." The close with praise and majesty: ﴿فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ رَبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَرَبِّ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَهُ ٱلۡكِبۡرِيَآءُ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾, "So praise be to Allah, Lord of the heavens, Lord of the earth, Lord of the worlds. His is the majesty in the heavens and earth, and He is the Almighty, the Wise."

## Tajweed points in Juz 25

### ﴿عٓسٓقٓ﴾ at the start of Ash-Shura
Read *'Ayn, Sin, Qaf*. 'Ayn may take four or six counts; Sin and Qaf take a necessary six-count madd. The noon of "'Ayn" is hidden before the sin, and the noon of "Sin" before the qaf.

### ﴿وَرَآيِٕ﴾ in Ash-Shura 51
A kasrah hamzah on a seat after an alif, with a connected madd.

### ﴿لِتَسۡتَوُۥاْ﴾ in Az-Zukhruf 13
The small waw is lengthened two counts, and the alif after it is not pronounced.

### ﴿حمٓ﴾ at the start of the four surahs
Ha two counts, Mim a necessary six-count madd. For the basics see [Learn Tajweed: A Roadmap](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## How to memorize Juz 25 with understanding

1. **The Hawamim share an atmosphere**: build a table of their openings and endings.
2. **Ash-Shura**: revelation, the one religion, provision, believers' traits, modes of revelation.
3. **Az-Zukhruf**: the Quran, riding, imitation, wealth, Pharaoh, 'Isa, close friends.
4. **Ad-Dukhan has short verses**: the blessed night, the smoke, Pharaoh, Zaqqum, the God-conscious.
5. **Al-Jathiyah**: subjection, the law, desire, the kneeling nations, praise.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 25

- **Reflect on Allah's signs** in the horizons and in yourself.
- **Affirm for Allah what He affirmed for Himself**, without likening.
- **Do not divide in religion**: the prophets' charge is one.
- **Accept your measured provision**: it comes with wisdom.
- **Examine yourself when calamity strikes.**
- **Forgive when you are angry.**
- **Consult others in your affairs.**
- **Say the travel supplication** on every journey.
- **Do not turn from the remembrance**, lest a devil be appointed for you.
- **Do not let a tyrant make light of your mind.**
- **Choose your close friends from the God-conscious.**
- **Beware taking your desire as a god.**
- **Remember the Day nations kneel** and your record is read.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 25 called "Ilayhi Yuradd"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞إِلَيۡهِ يُرَدُّ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِۚ﴾.

### What is the blessed night in Surah Ad-Dukhan?
Laylat al-Qadr, as indicated by Surah Al-Qadr: ﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةِ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ﴾.

### Is there a prostration in Juz 25?
No, there is no place of prostration in this juz.

### What is the riding supplication in Az-Zukhruf?
﴿سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُۥ مُقۡرِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَإِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ﴾.

### Where does Juz 25 end?
At the end of Al-Jathiyah. Juz 26 begins with Surah Al-Ahqaf.

## Conclusion

Juz 25 introduces you to revelation as spirit and light, to the one religion enjoined on the messengers of firm resolve, and to believers who forgive when angry and consult one another. It warns you against turning from the remembrance of the Most Merciful, against friendships without taqwa and against desire taken as a god, then stands you on the Day nations kneel while Our record speaks in truth.
