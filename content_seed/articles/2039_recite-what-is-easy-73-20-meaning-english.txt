﴿فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ﴾ [Al-Muzzammil 73:20]

## The easing
- The beginning of the surah commanded long night prayer. Its end brought ease: *He knew you would not be able to keep count of it, so He turned to you in mercy.*

## Three excuses
| Excuse | In the verse |
|---|---|
| Illness | *Some of you will be ill* |
| Earning a living | *Others travel the land seeking Allah's bounty* |
| Striving | *Others fight in Allah's cause* |

- Note: earning a lawful living is placed **beside** striving in Allah's cause.

## Repeated twice
- *Recite what is easy* appears twice in the verse: never cut off from the Quran, even with a little.

## The ending
- Prayer, zakah, a goodly loan to Allah, and seeking forgiveness.

## Lessons
- **Keep a fixed portion**, even one page.
- **Small and lasting** beats big and broken.
