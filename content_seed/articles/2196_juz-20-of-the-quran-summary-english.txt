Juz 20 begins with ﴿۞فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوٓاْ أَخۡرِجُوٓاْ ءَالَ لُوطٖ مِّن قَرۡيَتِكُمۡۖ﴾, "The only answer of his people was to say: expel the family of Lut from your town", and is popularly known as **Juz Amman Khalaq** because its first pages contain ﴿أَمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ﴾, "Or who created the heavens and the earth?" It runs from **An-Naml 27:56 to Al-'Ankabut 29:45**, with all of **Surah Al-Qasas** (88 verses) in between. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 382 to 401**.

This is the juz of **oppression and empowerment, trial and steadfastness**. It opens with questions of monotheism, shows in Al-Qasas how Allah provides for His oppressed servants from where they never expected, and establishes in Al-'Ankabut that faith must be tested.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **Allah favouring the oppressed and destroying the arrogant, and the testing of faith**. The opening of Al-Qasas announces the rule: ﴿وَنُرِيدُ أَن نَّمُنَّ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتُضۡعِفُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَنَجۡعَلَهُمۡ أَئِمَّةٗ وَنَجۡعَلَهُمُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثِينَ﴾, "We wished to favour those oppressed in the land, make them leaders and make them the inheritors." Then it shows two tyrants: Pharaoh through power and Qarun through wealth, both destroyed. Al-'Ankabut opens with ﴿أَحَسِبَ ٱلنَّاسُ أَن يُتۡرَكُوٓاْ أَن يَقُولُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا وَهُمۡ لَا يُفۡتَنُونَ﴾, "Do people think they will be left to say 'we believe' without being tested?", and within this juz ends by likening every power other than Allah to a spider's house.

The message: **do not be deceived by the oppressor's strength, do not despair of your weakness, and stand firm when tested.**

## Part one: the close of Surah An-Naml (56-93)

### 1. The end of Lut's people (56-58)
"The only answer of his people was to say: expel the family of Lut from your town; they are people who keep themselves pure." Purity became a charge for which its people were expelled. Allah saved him and his family except his wife, and rained a rain upon them.

### 2. Five questions: is there a god with Allah? (59-66)
﴿قُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَىٰ عِبَادِهِ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱصۡطَفَىٰٓۗ ءَآللَّهُ خَيۡرٌ أَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾, "Say: praise be to Allah, and peace on His servants whom He has chosen. Is Allah better, or what they associate with Him?" Then five questions, each closing with "Is there a god with Allah?":
1. **Creation and growth**: ﴿أَمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَأَنزَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا بِهِۦ حَدَآئِقَ ذَاتَ بَهۡجَةٖ مَّا كَانَ لَكُمۡ أَن تُنۢبِتُواْ شَجَرَهَآۗ﴾, "Or who created the heavens and earth and sent down water from the sky, with which We grew gardens of beauty whose trees you could not have grown?"
2. **A stable earth**: ﴿أَمَّن جَعَلَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ قَرَارٗا وَجَعَلَ خِلَٰلَهَآ أَنۡهَٰرٗا وَجَعَلَ لَهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَجَعَلَ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَيۡنِ حَاجِزًاۗ﴾, "Or who made the earth a firm place, placed rivers within it, set firm mountains on it and put a barrier between the two seas?"
3. **Answering the distressed**: ﴿أَمَّن يُجِيبُ ٱلۡمُضۡطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكۡشِفُ ٱلسُّوٓءَ وَيَجۡعَلُكُمۡ خُلَفَآءَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ﴾, "Or who answers the distressed when he calls on Him, removes evil and makes you successors on earth?"
4. **Guidance in darkness**: ﴿أَمَّن يَهۡدِيكُمۡ فِي ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ وَمَن يُرۡسِلُ ٱلرِّيَٰحَ بُشۡرَۢا بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ رَحۡمَتِهِۦٓۗ﴾, "Or who guides you in the darkness of land and sea, and sends the winds as good news before His mercy?"
5. **Beginning, repeating and provision**: ﴿أَمَّن يَبۡدَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَلۡقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥ وَمَن يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ﴾, "Or who begins creation and then repeats it, and provides for you from the sky and earth?"

Then: ﴿قُل لَّا يَعۡلَمُ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُۚ﴾, "Say: no one in the heavens and earth knows the unseen except Allah."

**Reflect**: even an idolater in distress at sea calls on Allah alone, so answering the distressed is a proof every person knows from within. Read [Who Answers the Distressed: 27:62](/blog/wa-tawakkal-ala-al-hayy-meaning-english/#Who-Answers-the-Distressed-When-He-Calls-Quran-27-62-Explain).

### 3. Signs and scenes of the Hour (67-93)
﴿إِنَّكَ لَا تُسۡمِعُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَلَا تُسۡمِعُ ٱلصُّمَّ ٱلدُّعَآءَ إِذَا وَلَّوۡاْ مُدۡبِرِينَ﴾, "You cannot make the dead hear, nor make the deaf hear the call when they turn their backs." Then a new quarter: ﴿۞وَإِذَا وَقَعَ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ أَخۡرَجۡنَا لَهُمۡ دَآبَّةٗ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تُكَلِّمُهُمۡ أَنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ كَانُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا لَا يُوقِنُونَ﴾, "When the word befalls them, We will bring out for them a beast from the earth that speaks to them, because people had no certainty in Our signs." The emergence of the Beast is among the major signs of the Hour established in the Sunnah.

Then the Horn, and ﴿وَتَرَى ٱلۡجِبَالَ تَحۡسَبُهَا جَامِدَةٗ وَهِيَ تَمُرُّ مَرَّ ٱلسَّحَابِۚ صُنۡعَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَتۡقَنَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍۚ﴾, "You see the mountains, thinking them firmly fixed, while they pass like clouds: the work of Allah who perfected everything", then ﴿مَن جَآءَ بِٱلۡحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُۥ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡهَا وَهُم مِّن فَزَعٖ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ءَامِنُونَ﴾, "Whoever comes with a good deed will have better than it, and they will be safe from the terror of that Day." The close: I have been commanded to worship the Lord of this city who made it sacred, and to recite the Quran, ﴿وَقُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ سَيُرِيكُمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ فَتَعۡرِفُونَهَاۚ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ﴾, "and say: praise be to Allah; He will show you His signs and you will recognise them, and your Lord is not unaware of what you do."

## Part two: Surah Al-Qasas (1-88)

Al-Qasas is Makki, with 88 verses, opening with ﴿طسٓمٓ﴾ ﴿تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ﴾, "Ta Sin Mim. These are the verses of the clear Book." It is the fullest account of Musa's early life: birth, nursing, the killing of the Egyptian, flight to Madyan, marriage and return as a prophet. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Qasas Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-qasas-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Qasas-Meaning-The-Full-Story-of-Musa-and-the-Fall-o).

### 1. Pharaoh and the oppressed (1-6)
﴿نَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡكَ مِن نَّبَإِ مُوسَىٰ وَفِرۡعَوۡنَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ لِقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ فِرۡعَوۡنَ عَلَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَجَعَلَ أَهۡلَهَا شِيَعٗا يَسۡتَضۡعِفُ طَآئِفَةٗ مِّنۡهُمۡ يُذَبِّحُ أَبۡنَآءَهُمۡ وَيَسۡتَحۡيِۦ نِسَآءَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ﴾, "We recite to you some of the news of Musa and Pharaoh in truth, for people who believe. Pharaoh exalted himself in the land and divided its people into factions, oppressing one group, slaughtering their sons and sparing their women. He was among the corrupters." Then the opposing divine will: "We wished to favour those oppressed in the land, make them leaders and make them the inheritors."

**Reflect**: Pharaoh wills and Allah wills, and only Allah's will is carried out. Pharaoh split people into "factions": division is every tyrant's weapon.

### 2. The mother of Musa (7-13)
﴿وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنۡ أَرۡضِعِيهِۖ فَإِذَا خِفۡتِ عَلَيۡهِ فَأَلۡقِيهِ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ وَلَا تَخَافِي وَلَا تَحۡزَنِيٓۖ إِنَّا رَآدُّوهُ إِلَيۡكِ وَجَاعِلُوهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾, "We inspired the mother of Musa: nurse him, and when you fear for him, cast him into the river, and do not fear or grieve; We will return him to you and make him one of the messengers." A mother casts her child into the river to save him: two commands, two prohibitions and two glad tidings in one verse.

Pharaoh's household picked him up, and his wife said: ﴿قُرَّتُ عَيۡنٖ لِّي وَلَكَۖ لَا تَقۡتُلُوهُ عَسَىٰٓ أَن يَنفَعَنَآ أَوۡ نَتَّخِذَهُۥ وَلَدٗا وَهُمۡ لَا يَشۡعُرُونَ﴾, "A comfort of the eye for me and you. Do not kill him; perhaps he will benefit us or we will adopt him as a son", while they did not perceive. Then the mother's state: ﴿وَأَصۡبَحَ فُؤَادُ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰ فَٰرِغًاۖ إِن كَادَتۡ لَتُبۡدِي بِهِۦ لَوۡلَآ أَن رَّبَطۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَلۡبِهَا لِتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "The heart of Musa's mother became empty; she nearly revealed him, had We not strengthened her heart so she would be among the believers." Then a new quarter: ﴿۞وَحَرَّمۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَرَاضِعَ مِن قَبۡلُ﴾, "We had prevented him from all wet nurses before", and his sister pointed them to a family who would care for him; then ﴿فَرَدَدۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّهِۦ كَيۡ تَقَرَّ عَيۡنُهَا وَلَا تَحۡزَنَ وَلِتَعۡلَمَ أَنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّ﴾, "So We returned him to his mother, that she might be comforted and not grieve, and know that Allah's promise is true."

**Lesson**: Allah raised Musa in his enemy's house and returned him to his mother, who was even paid to nurse him. Never despair of what seems impossible.

### 3. Youth, a mistake and repentance (14-21)
﴿وَلَمَّا بَلَغَ أَشُدَّهُۥ وَٱسۡتَوَىٰٓ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ حُكۡمٗا وَعِلۡمٗاۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ﴾, "When he reached full strength and maturity, We gave him judgement and knowledge; thus do We reward those who do good." He entered the city and found two men fighting; the Israelite sought his help against the Egyptian, and Musa struck him and killed him without intending to. He said: ﴿هَٰذَا مِنۡ عَمَلِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۖ﴾, "This is from the work of Shaytan", then ﴿قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي ظَلَمۡتُ نَفۡسِي فَٱغۡفِرۡ لِي فَغَفَرَ لَهُۥٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾, "My Lord, I have wronged myself, so forgive me. So He forgave him; He is the Forgiving, the Merciful."

Then came the well-wisher: ﴿وَجَآءَ رَجُلࣱ مِّنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّ ٱلۡمَلَأَ يَأۡتَمِرُونَ بِكَ لِيَقۡتُلُوكَ فَٱخۡرُجۡ إِنِّي لَكَ مِنَ ٱلنَّـٰصِحِينَ﴾, "A man came running from the far end of the city: O Musa, the chiefs are conspiring to kill you, so leave; I am your sincere adviser." He left ﴿خَآئِفٗا يَتَرَقَّبُۖ قَالَ رَبِّ نَجِّنِي مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾, "fearful and watchful, saying: my Lord, save me from the wrongdoing people."

### 4. Musa in Madyan (22-28)
﴿وَلَمَّا تَوَجَّهَ تِلۡقَآءَ مَدۡيَنَ قَالَ عَسَىٰ رَبِّيٓ أَن يَهۡدِيَنِي سَوَآءَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ﴾, "When he turned towards Madyan, he said: perhaps my Lord will guide me to the right way." At the water of Madyan he found people watering their flocks and two women holding back. He asked what was the matter; they said: we cannot water until the shepherds leave, and our father is a very old man. He watered for them, then:

﴿فَسَقَىٰ لَهُمَا ثُمَّ تَوَلَّىٰٓ إِلَى ٱلظِّلِّ فَقَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَآ أَنزَلۡتَ إِلَيَّ مِنۡ خَيۡرٖ فَقِيرࣱ﴾, "So he watered for them, then turned to the shade and said: my Lord, I am in need of whatever good You send down to me."

**The prayer of a stranger**: alone, hungry, hunted, with no money or shelter, he did not complain to people; he showed his need to Allah. Relief came quickly: ﴿فَجَآءَتۡهُ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا تَمۡشِي عَلَى ٱسۡتِحۡيَآءٖ﴾, "Then one of them came to him, walking shyly." He told her father his story, who said: do not fear, you have escaped the wrongdoing people. Read [Musa's Dua in Madyan: 28:24](/blog/surah-al-qasas-meaning-english/#My-Lord-I-Am-in-Need-of-Whatever-Good-You-Send-Me-The-Duʿa-o).

Then the standard for choosing: ﴿قَالَتۡ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا يَـٰٓأَبَتِ ٱسۡتَـٔۡجِرۡهُۖ إِنَّ خَيۡرَ مَنِ ٱسۡتَـٔۡجَرۡتَ ٱلۡقَوِيُّ ٱلۡأَمِينُ﴾, "One of them said: father, hire him; the best one you can hire is the strong and trustworthy." Strength alone is not enough, and trustworthiness alone is not enough. Then he offered marriage on condition of eight years' work, ten if Musa chose. In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas said Musa completed the longer and better of the two terms.

### 5. Prophethood and confrontation (29-50)
A new quarter: ﴿۞فَلَمَّا قَضَىٰ مُوسَى ٱلۡأَجَلَ وَسَارَ بِأَهۡلِهِۦٓ ءَانَسَ مِن جَانِبِ ٱلطُّورِ نَارٗاۖ﴾, "When Musa completed the term and travelled with his family, he perceived a fire on the side of the mount." Then the call: ﴿فَلَمَّآ أَتَىٰهَا نُودِيَ مِن شَٰطِيِٕ ٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡأَيۡمَنِ فِي ٱلۡبُقۡعَةِ ٱلۡمُبَٰرَكَةِ مِنَ ٱلشَّجَرَةِ أَن يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنِّيٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "When he came to it, he was called from the right side of the valley, in the blessed spot, from the tree: O Musa, I am Allah, Lord of the worlds." Then the staff and the hand, and the request for his brother: ﴿وَأَخِي هَٰرُونُ هُوَ أَفۡصَحُ مِنِّي لِسَانٗا فَأَرۡسِلۡهُ مَعِيَ رِدۡءٗا يُصَدِّقُنِيٓۖ﴾, "My brother Harun is more eloquent than me, so send him with me as a helper who will confirm me."

Then Pharaoh's arrogance: ﴿وَقَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمَلَأُ مَا عَلِمۡتُ لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرِي﴾, "Pharaoh said: O chiefs, I know of no god for you other than me", and he ordered Haman to build a tower; then We seized him and his soldiers and cast them into the sea.

**Reflect on the link**: the quality Musa was known for in Madyan, "strong and trustworthy", is the one with which he carried the message. Allah prepared him during his years of shepherding.

### 6. The word conveyed and guidance (51-75)
Hizb 40 begins: ﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ وَصَّلۡنَا لَهُمُ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ﴾, "We have conveyed the word to them in succession, so they may remember." Then praise of the believers among the People of the Book, who are given their reward twice for their patience, repel evil with good, and turn away from idle talk.

Then the decisive verse: ﴿إِنَّكَ لَا تَهۡدِي مَنۡ أَحۡبَبۡتَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ﴾, "You cannot guide whom you love, but Allah guides whom He wills, and He knows best who are guided." In Sahih Muslim it was revealed about Abu Talib, the Prophet's uncle ﷺ, who died outside Islam despite the Prophet's eagerness for his guidance.

**Lesson**: your task is to call and to pray; guidance is in Allah's hand alone, so do not burden yourself with what you do not control.

Then: say, have you considered, if Allah made the night continuous until the Day of Resurrection, what god other than Allah could bring you light? Out of His mercy He made the night and day so you may rest and seek His bounty.

### 7. Qarun: the trial of wealth (76-84)
A new quarter: ﴿۞إِنَّ قَٰرُونَ كَانَ مِن قَوۡمِ مُوسَىٰ فَبَغَىٰ عَلَيۡهِمۡۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ مِنَ ٱلۡكُنُوزِ مَآ إِنَّ مَفَاتِحَهُۥ لَتَنُوٓأُ بِٱلۡعُصۡبَةِ أُوْلِي ٱلۡقُوَّةِ﴾, "Qarun was from the people of Musa, but he oppressed them. We gave him treasures whose keys would burden a band of strong men." Then his people's advice in five points:
1. ﴿لَا تَفۡرَحۡۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡفَرِحِينَ﴾, "Do not exult; Allah does not love the exultant", meaning arrogant glee.
2. ﴿وَٱبۡتَغِ فِيمَآ ءَاتَىٰكَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلدَّارَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۖ﴾, "Seek the home of the Hereafter through what Allah has given you."
3. ﴿وَلَا تَنسَ نَصِيبَكَ مِنَ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۖ﴾, "Do not forget your share of this world."
4. ﴿وَأَحۡسِن كَمَآ أَحۡسَنَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَيۡكَۖ﴾, "Do good as Allah has done good to you."
5. ﴿وَلَا تَبۡغِ ٱلۡفَسَادَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ﴾, "Do not seek corruption in the land."

He replied with the word of arrogance: ﴿قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أُوتِيتُهُۥ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٍ عِندِيٓۚ﴾, "I was only given it because of knowledge I have." Then he went out in his finery, and people split: ﴿قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُرِيدُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا يَٰلَيۡتَ لَنَا مِثۡلَ مَآ أُوتِيَ قَٰرُونُ إِنَّهُۥ لَذُو حَظٍّ عَظِيمٖ﴾, "Those who desired worldly life said: if only we had what Qarun has been given; he is truly fortunate", ﴿وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡعِلۡمَ وَيۡلَكُمۡ ثَوَابُ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرࣱ لِّمَنۡ ءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗاۚ﴾, "but those given knowledge said: woe to you, Allah's reward is better for whoever believes and does good." Then the end: ﴿فَخَسَفۡنَا بِهِۦ وَبِدَارِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ﴾, "So We caused the earth to swallow him and his home", and those who had wished for his place said: had Allah not been gracious to us, He would have made it swallow us.

Then the rule: ﴿تِلۡكَ ٱلدَّارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ نَجۡعَلُهَا لِلَّذِينَ لَا يُرِيدُونَ عُلُوّٗا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَا فَسَادٗاۚ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾, "That home of the Hereafter We assign to those who desire neither exaltation in the land nor corruption; and the best outcome is for the God-conscious."

**The contrast**: Pharaoh transgressed through power and drowned in water; Qarun transgressed through wealth and was swallowed by the earth.

### 8. The close: a promise of return (85-88)
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِي فَرَضَ عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لَرَآدُّكَ إِلَىٰ مَعَادٖۚ﴾, "The One who made the Quran binding on you will return you to a place of return." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas said: to Makkah. Just as Allah returned Musa to his mother, He would return His Prophet ﷺ to his city. The close: ﴿وَلَا تَدۡعُ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَۘ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ كُلُّ شَيۡءٍ هَالِكٌ إِلَّا وَجۡهَهُۥۚ لَهُ ٱلۡحُكۡمُ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾, "Do not call on another god with Allah; there is no god but Him. Everything will perish except His Face. His is the judgement, and to Him you will be returned."

## Part three: the start of Surah Al-'Ankabut (1-45)

Al-'Ankabut is Makki, with 69 verses, 45 of which fall in this juz. For the whole surah see [Surah Al-Ankabut Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-qasas-meaning-english/#Surah-Al-Ankabut-Meaning-Faith-Is-Tested-and-the-Spider-s-Fr).

### 1. The law of trial (1-13)
﴿الٓمٓ﴾ "Do people think they will be left to say 'we believe' without being tested?" ﴿وَلَقَدۡ فَتَنَّا ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡۖ فَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ صَدَقُواْ وَلَيَعۡلَمَنَّ ٱلۡكَٰذِبِينَ﴾, "We tested those before them, and Allah will surely make evident those who are truthful and those who are liars." Then: ﴿وَمَن جَٰهَدَ فَإِنَّمَا يُجَٰهِدُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَنِيٌّ عَنِ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾, "Whoever strives only strives for himself; Allah is free of need of the worlds."

Then the test of one's relationship with parents: ﴿وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ حُسۡنٗاۖ وَإِن جَٰهَدَاكَ لِتُشۡرِكَ بِي مَا لَيۡسَ لَكَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمࣱ فَلَا تُطِعۡهُمَآۚ﴾, "We have enjoined on man goodness to his parents, but if they strive to make you associate with Me what you have no knowledge of, do not obey them." Kindness is required, but there is no obedience in shirk.

Then the weak in faith: ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَقُولُ ءَامَنَّا بِٱللَّهِ فَإِذَآ أُوذِيَ فِي ٱللَّهِ جَعَلَ فِتۡنَةَ ٱلنَّاسِ كَعَذَابِ ٱللَّهِۖ﴾, "Among people is one who says 'we believe in Allah', but when harmed for Allah's sake, treats people's persecution like Allah's punishment."

### 2. Stories of the steadfast (14-40)
- **Nuh**: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ فَلَبِثَ فِيهِمۡ أَلۡفَ سَنَةٍ إِلَّا خَمۡسِينَ عَامٗا فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱلطُّوفَانُ وَهُمۡ ظَٰلِمُونَ﴾, "We sent Nuh to his people, and he remained among them a thousand years less fifty, then the flood seized them while they were wrongdoers." Nine hundred and fifty years of calling!
- **Ibrahim** called his people to worship Allah; they said kill him or burn him, and Allah saved him from the fire. Then a new quarter: ﴿۞فَـَٔامَنَ لَهُۥ لُوطࣱۘ وَقَالَ إِنِّي مُهَاجِرٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّيٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾, "Lut believed in him, and he said: I am emigrating to my Lord; He is the Almighty, the Wise."
- **Lut** condemned indecency and highway robbery; the angels came with good news for Ibrahim and destruction for Lut's people.
- **Shu'ayb** in Madyan, **'Ad and Thamud**, and **Qarun, Pharaoh and Haman**.

Then a summary of the punishments: ﴿فَكُلًّا أَخَذۡنَا بِذَنۢبِهِۦۖ فَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ حَاصِبٗا وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ أَخَذَتۡهُ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ خَسَفۡنَا بِهِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ أَغۡرَقۡنَاۚ﴾, "We seized each for its sin: on some We sent a storm of stones, some were seized by the blast, some We caused the earth to swallow, and some We drowned."

**Reflect**: four punishments through four elements of nature: the stone-carrying wind for Lut's people, the blast for Thamud and Madyan, the swallowing earth for Qarun, and drowning for Nuh's people and Pharaoh.

### 3. The spider's house (41-45)
﴿مَثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوۡلِيَآءَ كَمَثَلِ ٱلۡعَنكَبُوتِ ٱتَّخَذَتۡ بَيۡتٗاۖ وَإِنَّ أَوۡهَنَ ٱلۡبُيُوتِ لَبَيۡتُ ٱلۡعَنكَبُوتِۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾, "The example of those who take protectors besides Allah is that of the spider who takes a house; and the weakest of houses is the spider's house, if only they knew." Every protector besides Allah, however strong it looks, is flimsier than a spider's thread.

Then the last verse of the juz: ﴿ٱتۡلُ مَآ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡكَ مِنَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَۖ إِنَّ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ تَنۡهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِۗ وَلَذِكۡرُ ٱللَّهِ أَكۡبَرُۗ﴾, "Recite what has been revealed to you of the Book and establish prayer. Prayer forbids indecency and wrongdoing, and the remembrance of Allah is greater." The remedy for trials: reciting the Quran and establishing prayer.

## Tajweed points in Juz 20

### ﴿ءَآللَّهُ خَيۡرٌ﴾ in An-Naml 59
An interrogative hamzah enters on the connecting hamzah of the divine name. The connecting hamzah is changed into an alif and lengthened six counts (a necessary madd); easing it (*tas-hil*) is also permitted, but the lengthened alif is more common.

### ﴿شَٰطِيِٕ﴾ in Al-Qasas 30
A hamzah with kasrah on a seat after a small sakin ya, pronounced *shati'i*.

### ﴿وَيۡكَأَنَّ﴾ in Al-Qasas 82
Written joined in the Mushaf and read as written.

### ﴿يَسۡتَحۡيِۦ﴾ in Al-Qasas 4
The small ya is lengthened two counts when continuing.

### ﴿طسٓمٓ﴾ at the start of Al-Qasas
As in Ash-Shu'ara': Ta two counts, Sin and Mim six counts, with the noon of Sin merged into the meem. See [Learn Tajweed: A Roadmap](/blog/learn-tajweed-roadmap-english/).

## How to memorize Juz 20 with understanding

1. **Learn the five "Or who" questions** by topic: creation, the earth, the distressed, darkness, beginning.
2. **Al-Qasas is chronological**: birth, river, palace, nursing, youth, killing, flight, Madyan, marriage, fire, message, Pharaoh. Memorize it like a film in sequence.
3. **Compare Musa in Ta-Ha, An-Naml and Al-Qasas** in a table: what each passage alone contains.
4. **Qarun's five pieces of advice**: do not exult, seek the Hereafter, do not forget your share, do good, do not spread corruption.
5. **Al-'Ankabut**: order the stories: Nuh, Ibrahim, Lut, Shu'ayb, 'Ad and Thamud, Qarun, Pharaoh and Haman.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 20

- **Call on Allah in distress**: He alone answers.
- **Do not despair when oppressed**: Allah wills to favour you.
- **Trust Allah's promise**, even when the path looks impossible.
- **When you err, seek forgiveness at once**, as Musa did.
- **Show your need to Allah**, not to people.
- **Choose the strong and trustworthy** at work and in marriage.
- **Call those you love**, and leave guidance to Allah.
- **Do not credit blessings to your own knowledge**, as Qarun did.
- **Beware desiring exaltation and corruption**: the Hereafter is for those who give them up.
- **Prepare for trials**: they are an unchanging law.
- **Be kind to your parents** without obeying them in sin.
- **Establish prayer**: it forbids indecency and wrongdoing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 20 called "Amman Khalaq"?
Because its first pages contain ﴿أَمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ﴾; its first verse is ﴿۞فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ﴾.

### How long did Nuh remain among his people?
A thousand years less fifty, as in Al-'Ankabut 14.

### About whom was "You cannot guide whom you love" revealed?
About Abu Talib, the Prophet's uncle ﷺ, as in Sahih Muslim.

### Is there a prostration in Juz 20?
No, there is no place of prostration in this juz.

### Where does Juz 20 end?
At Al-'Ankabut 45. Juz 21 begins with ﴿۞وَلَا تُجَٰدِلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِلَّا بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ﴾, "Do not argue with the People of the Book except in the best manner."

## Conclusion

Juz 20 teaches that Allah's will is above every will: a mother casts her child into the sea and he returns to her; a hunted stranger shelters in the shade and relief comes; two tyrants of power and wealth end in drowning and in the earth swallowing them. It then reminds you that faith must be tested, that every power besides Allah is a spider's house, and that prayer and recitation are your provision on the way.
