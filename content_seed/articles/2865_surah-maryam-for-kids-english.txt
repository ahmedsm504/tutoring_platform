Surah Maryam is the only surah in the Quran named after a woman: Maryam, the mother of Prophet Isa (Jesus), peace be upon them. Allah honoured her above the women of the worlds. In this surah, she is alone under a palm tree, tired and worried, and a voice tells her: "Do not grieve, your Lord has placed a stream beneath you. Shake the trunk of the palm tree towards you, and fresh ripe dates will fall on you." Then, when people blame her, she points to her newborn baby, and baby Isa speaks from the cradle!

The surah also tells us about Zakariya, an elderly prophet who called his Lord in a quiet, secret du'a, and Allah gave him a son called Yahya. And it shows Ibrahim talking to his father with the gentlest words: "Ya abati", "O my dear father", four times. In this guide, we explain Surah Maryam to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Maryam

- **Position**: surah 19, in juz 16.
- **Number of verses**: 98.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **A special moment in history**: when the early Muslims migrated to Abyssinia, Ja'far ibn Abi Talib recited the beginning of Surah Maryam to the Christian king An-Najashi, who wept until his beard was wet, as mentioned in the books of seerah.

## Lesson 1: The Mercy of Allah to Zakariya

﴿ذِكۡرُ رَحۡمَتِ رَبِّكَ عَبۡدَهُۥ زَكَرِيَّآ﴾ (Quran 19:2)

### Explaining it to your child
"This is a reminder of the mercy of your Lord to His servant Zakariya. Zakariya was a prophet who had grown old and had no children. Allah tells us his story so we learn that His mercy is always near."

## Lesson 2: The Quiet Du'a

﴿قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي وَهَنَ ٱلۡعَظۡمُ مِنِّي وَٱشۡتَعَلَ ٱلرَّأۡسُ شَيۡبٗا وَلَمۡ أَكُنۢ بِدُعَآئِكَ رَبِّ شَقِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:4)

### Explaining it to your child
"Zakariya called his Lord quietly, in a gentle voice that nobody else heard. He said: My Lord, my bones have grown weak and my hair has turned white, but I have never been disappointed when calling You, my Lord. Notice his manners: he described how weak he was, and he remembered that Allah had always answered him before."

### Lesson for kids: a whispered du'a
"Allah hears even your whisper. You don't need to shout. When you make du'a in sujud, you can speak quietly, and Allah hears every word."

### Activity: the whisper du'a
Teach your child to make one quiet du'a each night before sleeping, with their own words. Tell them: "Zakariya whispered, and Allah answered."

### The gift of Yahya
"Allah answered Zakariya and gave him the good news of a son named Yahya, a name nobody had ever been given before. Yahya grew up kind to his parents, gentle, and never arrogant or disobedient."

## Lesson 3: Maryam Goes to a Quiet Place

﴿وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَرۡيَمَ إِذِ ٱنتَبَذَتۡ مِنۡ أَهۡلِهَا مَكَانٗا شَرۡقِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:16)

### Explaining it to your child
"Maryam went away from her family to a quiet place in the east to worship Allah. There, Allah sent the angel Jibril to her in the form of a man, and he gave her good news: Allah would give her a pure son, without a father. It was a miracle from Allah, who only has to say 'Be' and it is."

## Lesson 4: The Palm Tree

﴿فَأَجَآءَهَا ٱلۡمَخَاضُ إِلَىٰ جِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ قَالَتۡ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي مِتُّ قَبۡلَ هَٰذَا وَكُنتُ نَسۡيٗا مَّنسِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:23)

### Explaining it to your child
"When the time came for the baby to be born, Maryam felt pain and went to the trunk of a palm tree. She was worried about what people would say, and she said: I wish I had died before this and been forgotten."

﴿فَنَادَىٰهَا مِن تَحۡتِهَآ أَلَّا تَحۡزَنِي قَدۡ جَعَلَ رَبُّكِ تَحۡتَكِ سَرِيّٗا﴾ ﴿وَهُزِّيٓ إِلَيۡكِ بِجِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ تُسَٰقِطۡ عَلَيۡكِ رُطَبٗا جَنِيّٗا﴾ ﴿فَكُلِي وَٱشۡرَبِي وَقَرِّي عَيۡنٗاۖ فَإِمَّا تَرَيِنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡبَشَرِ أَحَدٗا فَقُولِيٓ إِنِّي نَذَرۡتُ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ صَوۡمٗا فَلَنۡ أُكَلِّمَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ إِنسِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:24-26)

### Explaining it to your child
"Then a voice called her from below: Don't be sad! Your Lord has placed a small stream beneath you. Shake the trunk of the palm tree, and fresh, ripe dates will fall on you. Eat, drink and be happy. And if you see anyone, tell them: I have promised the Most Merciful a fast of silence, so I will not speak to anyone today."

### Lesson for kids: Allah takes care of us
"Maryam was alone and worried, but Allah gave her water to drink, dates to eat, and calm for her heart. When you feel worried, remember that Allah is with you and takes care of you."

### Lesson for kids: dates are a blessed food
"Scholars mention that Allah chose fresh dates for Maryam, and dates are a wonderful, healthy food. The Prophet ﷺ used to break his fast with fresh dates."

### Activity: the date tasting
Bring a few kinds of dates to try together. While eating, tell your child the story of Maryam and the palm tree, and ask: "Which one do you think Maryam ate? Fresh ones!"

## Lesson 5: Baby Isa Speaks

﴿فَأَشَارَتۡ إِلَيۡهِۖ قَالُواْ كَيۡفَ نُكَلِّمُ مَن كَانَ فِي ٱلۡمَهۡدِ صَبِيّٗا﴾ ﴿قَالَ إِنِّي عَبۡدُ ٱللَّهِ ءَاتَىٰنِيَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَجَعَلَنِي نَبِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:29-30)

### Explaining it to your child
"Maryam came back to her people carrying her baby. They were shocked and started blaming her. She did not say a word; she simply pointed to the baby. They said: How can we talk to a baby in a cradle? Then baby Isa spoke! He said: I am the servant of Allah. He has given me the Book and made me a prophet."

﴿وَٱلسَّلَٰمُ عَلَيَّ يَوۡمَ وُلِدتُّ وَيَوۡمَ أَمُوتُ وَيَوۡمَ أُبۡعَثُ حَيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:33)

### Explaining it to your child
"Baby Isa continued: Peace is on me the day I was born, the day I die, and the day I am raised back to life. What a miracle! A newborn baby speaking clearly to defend his mother."

### Lesson for kids: Isa is a servant of Allah
"The very first thing Isa said was: I am the servant of Allah. Muslims love and respect Prophet Isa, and believe he was a great prophet and a servant of Allah, not Allah's son."

### Lesson for kids: defending our mothers
"Baby Isa defended his mother from people's unfair words. We also defend our parents and never let anyone speak badly of them."

## Lesson 6: Ibrahim and His Father

﴿وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ صِدِّيقٗا نَّبِيًّا﴾ ﴿إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ يَـٰٓأَبَتِ لِمَ تَعۡبُدُ مَا لَا يَسۡمَعُ وَلَا يُبۡصِرُ وَلَا يُغۡنِي عَنكَ شَيۡـٔٗا﴾ (Quran 19:41-42)

### Explaining it to your child
"Ibrahim was truthful and a prophet. His father worshipped idols. Ibrahim wanted to guide him, so he spoke to him with the gentlest words: Ya abati, O my dear father, why do you worship something that cannot hear, cannot see and cannot help you at all?"

### Notice Ibrahim's manners
"Ibrahim said 'Ya abati', O my dear father, four times! He never said: You're wrong! He never shouted. Even though his father was wrong, Ibrahim spoke with love and respect."

﴿قَالَ سَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡكَۖ سَأَسۡتَغۡفِرُ لَكَ رَبِّيٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ بِي حَفِيّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:47)

### Explaining it to your child
"Ibrahim's father got angry and threatened him. But Ibrahim answered: Salamun 'alayk, peace be on you. I will ask my Lord to forgive you. He has always been kind to me."

### Lesson for kids: gentle with parents
"If you disagree with your parents, speak politely and calmly. Ibrahim's father was very wrong, and Ibrahim still spoke gently. How much more gently should we speak to our parents, who love us?"

### Activity: "Ya abati" role-play
Role-play with your child: they want to ask their dad for something, or remind him about prayer. First, they try with a demanding voice, then with "Dad, my dear...". Which feels better?

## Let's Read Together: Allah Puts Love in Hearts

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ سَيَجۡعَلُ لَهُمُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وُدّٗا﴾ (Quran 19:96)

### Explaining it to your child
"Those who believe and do good deeds, the Most Merciful will put love for them in people's hearts. If you are kind, honest and helpful, Allah makes people love you."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said that when Allah loves a servant, He calls Jibril and says: I love so-and-so, so love him. Then Jibril loves him and calls out to the people of the heavens, and then acceptance is placed for him on earth (al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## The Name of Allah "Ar-Rahman" in Surah Maryam

"Allah's name Ar-Rahman, the Most Merciful, appears many times in Surah Maryam, more than in almost any other surah. It's as if the whole surah is wrapped in mercy: mercy for Zakariya, mercy for Maryam, mercy for Isa, and mercy for Ibrahim."

### Activity: count Ar-Rahman
As you read or listen to the surah, ask your child to make a mark each time they hear "Ar-Rahman". They will be surprised how often it appears!

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Gentle Words"

Salma noticed that her grandfather stopped praying Fajr at the masjid because his knees hurt, and sometimes he forgot to pray at home. She wanted to remind him but felt shy. "What if he gets upset?" she thought.

That evening, her mother read Surah Maryam with her and explained how Ibrahim spoke to his father: "Ya abati", O my dear father, four times, gently, even though his father was very wrong.

The next morning, Salma made her grandfather a cup of tea, sat next to him and said: "Grandpa, my dear, I love praying next to you. Would you pray Fajr with me tomorrow? I'll bring your chair." Her grandfather smiled, his eyes shining. "Of course, my sweet girl."

The next day, Salma woke him gently, brought his chair to the prayer mat, and they prayed together. Her grandfather told her mother later: "Salma reminded me in the kindest way. I can't say no to such kind words."

Ask your child: "How did Salma remind her grandfather? Who did she learn this from?"

## What We Learn From Surah Maryam

- **Allah's mercy** is always near.
- **A quiet du'a** is heard by Allah.
- **Allah takes care** of us when we are worried.
- **Dates** are a blessed, healthy food.
- **Isa is a servant of Allah** and a great prophet.
- **We defend our parents**.
- **We speak gently** to our parents, even when we disagree.
- **Allah puts love** for believers in people's hearts.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### How do I explain that Isa had no father?
Simply: "Allah is able to do anything. He created Adam without a father or mother, and He created Isa without a father, as a sign of His power."

### How do Muslims see Isa compared to Christians?
Muslims love and honour Isa as one of the greatest prophets and believe he was a servant and messenger of Allah, not Allah or His son. This can be taught respectfully, without mocking anyone's beliefs.

### Is the surah suitable for young children?
Yes. The palm tree, the stream, the dates and the speaking baby are vivid scenes that children love and easily remember.
