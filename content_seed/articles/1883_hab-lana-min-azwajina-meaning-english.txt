﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعۡيُنٖ وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا﴾ [Al-Furqan 25:74]

## Among the servants of the Most Merciful
- This duʿa is one of the final qualities of *ʿIbad ar-Rahman* in Surah Al-Furqan.

## "Comfort of the eyes"
- *Qurrat aʿyun* describes an eye that is calm and cool with joy. Scholars explain that what truly comforts a believer's eye is seeing his family **obeying Allah**.

## Two requests
| Request | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Spouses and children as comfort | A righteous, peaceful family |
| *Make us leaders for the pious* | Models others follow in good |

## "Grant us" (*hab*)
- *Hab* means give as a gift. A righteous family is a **gift from Allah**, so ask for it constantly.

## Ambition in good
- Asking to be a leader of the pious shows that seeking a high rank in religion is praiseworthy.

## Lessons
- **Make this a daily duʿa** for your family.
- **Be the example** you want your children to follow.
