Three short ayat that summarize the path to success.

﴿وَٱلۡعَصۡرِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَفِي خُسۡرٍ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ﴾

## The oath by time
- **Al-ʿAṣr**: time, or the late afternoon, as commentators explained.
- Allah swears by time to show its **value**: it is your life.

## Everyone is in loss…
- ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَفِي خُسۡرٍ﴾: every human, with emphasis.

## …except those with four qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Faith | Belief in Allah and what He revealed |
| Good deeds | Acting on that belief |
| Advising to truth | Encouraging one another to what is right |
| Advising to patience | Supporting one another through difficulty |
- The first two **fix yourself**; the last two **fix your community**.

## Ash-Shafi'i's comment
- It is reported that Imam ash-Shafi'i said: if people reflected on this surah alone, it would be enough for them.

## Lessons
- **Audit your day**: how much went to the four qualities?
- **Find good company** that reminds you of truth and patience.
