Surah Ash-Shura contains the verse that sums up how Muslims understand Allah: "There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing." It also gives its name to a principle that has shaped Muslim communities for centuries: *shura*, mutual consultation. The surah declares that all the prophets brought one religion, explains that Allah measures out provision because unlimited wealth would make people transgress, teaches that many calamities come from what our own hands earn while Allah pardons much, and draws a portrait of the believers: they avoid major sins, forgive when angry, establish prayer, consult one another and spend from what Allah gives them.

This guide explains Surah Ash-Shura section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Ash-Shura
- **Number of verses**: 53.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 42, in the twenty-fifth juz.
- **Name**: Ash-Shura means "consultation", from verse 38.
- **Opens with five letters**: *Ha Mim 'Ayn Sin Qaf*.
- **Main themes**: Allah's uniqueness, the unity of revelation, provision, calamities, the qualities of believers and the ways of revelation.

## Verses 1–6: The Majesty of Allah
﴿حمٓ﴾ ﴿عٓسٓقٓ﴾ ﴿كَذَٰلِكَ يُوحِيٓ إِلَيۡكَ وَإِلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِكَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾ ﴿لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَلِيُّ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾ ﴿تَكَادُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ يَتَفَطَّرۡنَ مِن فَوۡقِهِنَّۚ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَيَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ لِمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَلَآ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَ ٱللَّهُ حَفِيظٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِوَكِيلٖ﴾ (Quran 42:1-6)

**"Thus has He revealed to you and to those before you, Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Wise."** **"The heavens almost break from above them"** out of awe of Allah, and the angels exalt Him and ask forgiveness for those on earth.

## Verses 7–12: There Is Nothing Like Him
﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ قُرۡءَانًا عَرَبِيّٗا لِّتُنذِرَ أُمَّ ٱلۡقُرَىٰ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهَا وَتُنذِرَ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡجَمۡعِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِۚ فَرِيقࣱ فِي ٱلۡجَنَّةِ وَفَرِيقࣱ فِي ٱلسَّعِيرِ﴾ (Quran 42:7)

The Quran was revealed to warn the Mother of Cities (Makkah) and those around it, and to warn of the Day of Assembly: **"A party will be in Paradise and a party in the Blaze."**

﴿فَاطِرُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا وَمِنَ ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ أَزۡوَٰجٗا يَذۡرَؤُكُمۡ فِيهِۚ لَيۡسَ كَمِثۡلِهِۦ شَيۡءࣱۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡبَصِيرُ﴾ ﴿لَهُۥ مَقَالِيدُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَقۡدِرُۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 42:11-12)

**"There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing."** This verse is a foundation of belief about Allah. The first half negates any likeness to creation; the second affirms His attributes. So we affirm what Allah affirmed for Himself, such as hearing and seeing, without likening Him to creation and without denying the attributes. **"To Him belong the keys of the heavens and the earth. He extends provision for whom He wills and restricts it."**

## Verses 13–15: One Religion for All Prophets
﴿۞شَرَعَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلدِّينِ مَا وَصَّىٰ بِهِۦ نُوحٗا وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ وَمَا وَصَّيۡنَا بِهِۦٓ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ وَعِيسَىٰٓۖ أَنۡ أَقِيمُواْ ٱلدِّينَ وَلَا تَتَفَرَّقُواْ فِيهِۚ كَبُرَ عَلَى ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ مَا تَدۡعُوهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِۚ ٱللَّهُ يَجۡتَبِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِيٓ إِلَيۡهِ مَن يُنِيبُ﴾ (Quran 42:13)

**"He has ordained for you of religion what He enjoined upon Nuh, and that which We have revealed to you, and what We enjoined upon Ibrahim, Musa and 'Isa: to establish the religion and not be divided therein."** Five great messengers, one religion: tawhid and obedience. The command is twofold: establish it, and do not divide over it.

﴿فَلِذَٰلِكَ فَٱدۡعُۖ وَٱسۡتَقِمۡ كَمَآ أُمِرۡتَۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعۡ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡۖ وَقُلۡ ءَامَنتُ بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ مِن كِتَٰبٖۖ وَأُمِرۡتُ لِأَعۡدِلَ بَيۡنَكُمُۖ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّنَا وَرَبُّكُمۡۖ لَنَآ أَعۡمَٰلُنَا وَلَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلُكُمۡۖ لَا حُجَّةَ بَيۡنَنَا وَبَيۡنَكُمُۖ ٱللَّهُ يَجۡمَعُ بَيۡنَنَاۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 42:15)

**"So to that invite, and remain on a right course as you are commanded, and do not follow their inclinations."** And: **"Allah is our Lord and your Lord. For us are our deeds, and for you your deeds."**

## Verses 16–20: Allah Is Gentle With His Servants
﴿ٱللَّهُ لَطِيفُۢ بِعِبَادِهِۦ يَرۡزُقُ مَن يَشَآءُۖ وَهُوَ ٱلۡقَوِيُّ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ﴾ ﴿مَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ حَرۡثَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ نَزِدۡ لَهُۥ فِي حَرۡثِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ حَرۡثَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا نُؤۡتِهِۦ مِنۡهَا وَمَا لَهُۥ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ مِن نَّصِيبٍ﴾ (Quran 42:19-20)

**"Allah is Subtle (gentle) with His servants; He gives provision to whom He wills."** *Al-Latif* is the One who knows the finest details and brings good to His servants in ways they do not perceive.

**"Whoever desires the harvest of the hereafter, We increase for him his harvest. And whoever desires the harvest of this world, We give him thereof, but there is not for him in the hereafter any share."**

## Verses 21–26: Love of Kin and Acceptance of Repentance
﴿ذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِي يُبَشِّرُ ٱللَّهُ عِبَادَهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِۗ قُل لَّآ أَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ أَجۡرًا إِلَّا ٱلۡمَوَدَّةَ فِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰۗ وَمَن يَقۡتَرِفۡ حَسَنَةٗ نَّزِدۡ لَهُۥ فِيهَا حُسۡنًاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ شَكُورٌ﴾ (Quran 42:23)

**"Say: I do not ask you for this message any payment except good will through kinship."** In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas explained it: the Prophet ﷺ had ties of kinship with every clan of Quraysh, so he asked them at least to respect those ties and not harm him. **"And whoever commits a good deed, We will increase for him good therein."**

﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يَقۡبَلُ ٱلتَّوۡبَةَ عَنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَنِ ٱلسَّيِّـَٔاتِ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَيَسۡتَجِيبُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَيَزِيدُهُم مِّن فَضۡلِهِۦۚ وَٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ شَدِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 42:25-26)

**"And it is He who accepts repentance from His servants and pardons misdeeds, and He knows what you do."** And **"He answers those who have believed and done righteous deeds and increases for them from His bounty."**

## Verses 27–35: Provision, Rain and Calamities
﴿۞وَلَوۡ بَسَطَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِعِبَادِهِۦ لَبَغَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَٰكِن يُنَزِّلُ بِقَدَرٖ مَّا يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِعِبَادِهِۦ خَبِيرُۢ بَصِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 42:27)

**"If Allah had extended excessively provision for His servants, they would have committed tyranny throughout the earth. But He sends it down in an amount which He wills."** Unlimited wealth would corrupt people. The amount you have may be exactly what protects your religion.

﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡغَيۡثَ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ مَا قَنَطُواْ وَيَنشُرُ رَحۡمَتَهُۥۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡوَلِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ﴾ (Quran 42:28)

**"And it is He who sends down the rain after they had despaired and spreads His mercy."**

﴿وَمَآ أَصَٰبَكُم مِّن مُّصِيبَةٖ فَبِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَن كَثِيرٖ﴾ (Quran 42:30)

**"And whatever strikes you of disaster, it is for what your hands have earned; but He pardons much."** Many calamities are consequences of sins, and they are also an expiation. And Allah pardons far more than He holds us to account for. In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said that no fatigue, illness, worry, grief, harm or sorrow afflicts a Muslim, even a thorn that pricks him, except that Allah expiates some of his sins by it.

﴿وَمِنۡ ءَايَٰتِهِ ٱلۡجَوَارِ فِي ٱلۡبَحۡرِ كَٱلۡأَعۡلَٰمِ﴾ ﴿إِن يَشَأۡ يُسۡكِنِ ٱلرِّيحَ فَيَظۡلَلۡنَ رَوَاكِدَ عَلَىٰ ظَهۡرِهِۦٓۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّكُلِّ صَبَّارٖ شَكُورٍ﴾ ﴿أَوۡ يُوبِقۡهُنَّ بِمَا كَسَبُواْ وَيَعۡفُ عَن كَثِيرٖ﴾ ﴿وَيَعۡلَمَ ٱلَّذِينَ يُجَٰدِلُونَ فِيٓ ءَايَٰتِنَا مَا لَهُم مِّن مَّحِيصٖ﴾ (Quran 42:32-35)

**"And of His signs are the ships in the sea, like mountains."** If He willed, He could still the wind and they would stand motionless. **"Indeed in that are signs for everyone patient and grateful."**

## Verses 36–43: The Qualities of the Believers
﴿فَمَآ أُوتِيتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَمَتَٰعُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَبۡقَىٰ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يَجۡتَنِبُونَ كَبَـٰٓئِرَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ وَإِذَا مَا غَضِبُواْ هُمۡ يَغۡفِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱسۡتَجَابُواْ لِرَبِّهِمۡ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَمۡرُهُمۡ شُورَىٰ بَيۡنَهُمۡ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَصَابَهُمُ ٱلۡبَغۡيُ هُمۡ يَنتَصِرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَجَزَـٰٓؤُاْ سَيِّئَةٖ سَيِّئَةࣱ مِّثۡلُهَاۖ فَمَنۡ عَفَا وَأَصۡلَحَ فَأَجۡرُهُۥ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ﴾ ﴿وَلَمَنِ ٱنتَصَرَ بَعۡدَ ظُلۡمِهِۦ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ مَا عَلَيۡهِم مِّن سَبِيلٍ﴾ ﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلسَّبِيلُ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يَظۡلِمُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ وَيَبۡغُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بِغَيۡرِ ٱلۡحَقِّۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَلَمَن صَبَرَ وَغَفَرَ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ لَمِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾ (Quran 42:36-43)

**"Whatever thing you have been given is but enjoyment of the worldly life. But what is with Allah is better and more lasting for those who believe."** Their qualities:
1. **"Upon their Lord they rely."**
2. **"They avoid the major sins and immoralities."**
3. **"And when they are angry, they forgive."** In al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "The strong man is not the one who wrestles others down; the strong man is the one who controls himself when angry."
4. **"They have responded to their Lord and established prayer."**
5. **"Their affair is determined by consultation among themselves."**
6. **"They spend from what We have provided them."**
7. **"When tyranny strikes them, they defend themselves."**

**Justice and forgiveness**: **"The retribution for an evil act is an evil one like it, but whoever pardons and makes reconciliation, his reward is with Allah."** Retribution is permitted; forgiveness is better. **"And whoever is patient and forgives, indeed, that is of the matters requiring determination."**

**Shura**: consultation is placed between prayer and spending, showing its importance. The Prophet ﷺ, despite receiving revelation, consulted his Companions often, such as before Badr and Uhud and at the Trench.

## Verses 44–48: The Wrongdoers' Regret
﴿وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن وَلِيّٖ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦۗ وَتَرَى ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ لَمَّا رَأَوُاْ ٱلۡعَذَابَ يَقُولُونَ هَلۡ إِلَىٰ مَرَدّٖ مِّن سَبِيلٖ﴾ ﴿وَتَرَىٰهُمۡ يُعۡرَضُونَ عَلَيۡهَا خَٰشِعِينَ مِنَ ٱلذُّلِّ يَنظُرُونَ مِن طَرۡفٍ خَفِيّٖۗ وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِنَّ ٱلۡخَٰسِرِينَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ وَأَهۡلِيهِمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِۗ أَلَآ إِنَّ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ فِي عَذَابٖ مُّقِيمٖ﴾ ﴿وَمَا كَانَ لَهُم مِّنۡ أَوۡلِيَآءَ يَنصُرُونَهُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِۗ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن سَبِيلٍ﴾ ﴿ٱسۡتَجِيبُواْ لِرَبِّكُم مِّن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَ يَوۡمࣱ لَّا مَرَدَّ لَهُۥ مِنَ ٱللَّهِۚ مَا لَكُم مِّن مَّلۡجَإٖ يَوۡمَئِذٖ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن نَّكِيرٖ﴾ ﴿فَإِنۡ أَعۡرَضُواْ فَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَفِيظًاۖ إِنۡ عَلَيۡكَ إِلَّا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُۗ وَإِنَّآ إِذَآ أَذَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِنَّا رَحۡمَةٗ فَرِحَ بِهَاۖ وَإِن تُصِبۡهُمۡ سَيِّئَةُۢ بِمَا قَدَّمَتۡ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ فَإِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ كَفُورࣱ﴾ (Quran 42:44-48)

The wrongdoers will see the punishment and ask: **"Is there for return any way?"** **"Respond to your Lord before a Day comes from Allah of which there is no repelling."**

## Verses 49–50: Sons, Daughters, or Neither
﴿لِّلَّهِ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ يَهَبُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ إِنَٰثٗا وَيَهَبُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ ٱلذُّكُورَ﴾ ﴿أَوۡ يُزَوِّجُهُمۡ ذُكۡرَانٗا وَإِنَٰثٗاۖ وَيَجۡعَلُ مَن يَشَآءُ عَقِيمًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمࣱ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 42:49-50)

**"He gives to whom He wills females, and He gives to whom He wills males. Or He makes them both males and females, and He renders whom He wills barren."** Children are a gift; their absence is a decree. Notice that females are mentioned first, honouring daughters in a society that disliked them.

## Verses 51–53: How Allah Speaks to Humans
﴿۞وَمَا كَانَ لِبَشَرٍ أَن يُكَلِّمَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا وَحۡيًا أَوۡ مِن وَرَآيِٕ حِجَابٍ أَوۡ يُرۡسِلَ رَسُولٗا فَيُوحِيَ بِإِذۡنِهِۦ مَا يَشَآءُۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيٌّ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ رُوحٗا مِّنۡ أَمۡرِنَاۚ مَا كُنتَ تَدۡرِي مَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ وَلَا ٱلۡإِيمَٰنُ وَلَٰكِن جَعَلۡنَٰهُ نُورٗا نَّهۡدِي بِهِۦ مَن نَّشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِنَاۚ وَإِنَّكَ لَتَهۡدِيٓ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ﴾ ﴿صِرَٰطِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۗ أَلَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ تَصِيرُ ٱلۡأُمُورُ﴾ (Quran 42:51-53)

**Three ways**:
1. **By inspiration** (*wahy*): placing it in the heart or in a dream.
2. **From behind a partition**: as He spoke to Musa.
3. **By sending a messenger**: an angel, Jibril, who reveals by His permission.

**"And thus We have revealed to you a spirit of Our command."** The Quran is called *ruh*, a spirit, because it gives life to hearts. **"And indeed, you guide to a straight path."**

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"There is nothing like unto Him"**: perfect negation followed by affirmation.
2. **"The heavens almost break"**: awe expressed through the universe.
3. **"He sends it down in an amount which He wills"**: wisdom in limits.
4. **"He pardons much"**: mercy placed beside accountability.
5. **"A spirit of Our command"**: the Quran as life for hearts.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd lazim harfi**: in *Ha Mim 'Ayn Sin Qaf*, the meem, sin and qaf are stretched six counts, and the 'ayn four or six.
- **Ikhfa'**: in *'Ayn Sin*, the noon at the end of *'ayn* is hidden before the sin.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *jaza'u sayyi'atin* (verse 40).
- **Ikhfa'**: in *sayyi'atun sayyi'atun* (verse 40), the tanween is hidden before the sin.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yaqbalu* (verse 25), the qaf sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Fussilat before it ended with "We will show them Our signs in the horizons", and Ash-Shura continues with signs and the majesty of revelation. Surah Az-Zukhruf after it begins with the Quran in the Mother of the Book, continuing the theme of "a spirit of Our command".

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does "There is nothing like unto Him" mean?
Allah is unlike any creation in His essence and attributes, while He is the Hearing and the Seeing.

### What is shura?
Mutual consultation, praised in verse 38 as a quality of the believers.

### Why does Allah not give unlimited provision?
Because, as verse 27 says, people would transgress on earth if provision were extended excessively.

### Are calamities caused by sins?
Verse 30 says many are from what our hands earned, while Allah pardons much; calamities also expiate sins.

### What are the three ways Allah speaks to humans?
Inspiration, from behind a partition, or by sending a messenger, as in verse 51.

### What does "good will through kinship" mean?
Ibn 'Abbas explained that the Prophet ﷺ asked Quraysh to respect the kinship between him and them, as in Sahih al-Bukhari.

### How many verses are in Surah Ash-Shura?
53 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Ash-Shura begins with the majesty of a Lord like whom there is nothing, and ends with the Quran as a spirit that brings hearts to life. In between it shows the believers: relying on their Lord, forgiving when angry, praying, consulting one another and spending. Be among them, and remember that what is with Allah is better and more lasting.
