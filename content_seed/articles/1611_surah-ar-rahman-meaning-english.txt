**Surah Ar-Rahman** (78 ayat) opens with one of Allah's names and then lists His gifts, again and again asking humans and jinn a single question.

## The refrain
﴿فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكُمَا تُكَذِّبَانِ﴾ — *So which of the favours of your Lord will you both deny?*
- Repeated **31 times**.
- **"You both"**: humans and jinn.

## The structure
| Ayat | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | The Most Merciful taught the Quran, created man and taught him speech |
| 5–13 | The sun and moon, the stars and trees, the balance, the earth and its fruits |
| 14–25 | Creation of man and jinn, the two easts and two wests, the two seas, ships |
| 26–30 | Everything perishes except the Face of your Lord |
| 31–45 | The Day of Judgement and the fate of the criminals |
| 46–61 | Two gardens for those who feared standing before their Lord |
| 62–78 | Two more gardens, and the blessing of His name |

## Key ayat
﴿كُلُّ مَنۡ عَلَيۡهَا فَانٖ﴾ ﴿وَيَبۡقَىٰ وَجۡهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلۡجَلَٰلِ وَٱلۡإِكۡرَامِ﴾ [Ar-Rahman: 26–27]
﴿هَلۡ جَزَآءُ ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ إِلَّا ٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنُ﴾ [Ar-Rahman: 60]

## The jinn's answer
- The Prophet ﷺ recited this surah to his companions and said the jinn had answered better: each time he recited the refrain they said, "None of Your favours, our Lord, do we deny; to You belongs all praise" [At-Tirmidhi].

## Lessons
- **Count your blessings** daily.
- **Answer the refrain** in your heart when you hear it.
