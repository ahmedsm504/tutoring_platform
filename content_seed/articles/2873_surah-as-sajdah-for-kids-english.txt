Every Friday morning, the Prophet ﷺ used to recite two surahs in the Fajr prayer: in the first rak'ah, "Alif Lam Mim Tanzil", Surah As-Sajdah, and in the second, "Hal ata 'alal-insan", Surah Al-Insan. In the middle of Surah As-Sajdah there is a verse of prostration, so the reciter and listeners prostrate. That is why children who pray Fajr in the masjid on Fridays know that surprising moment when the imam suddenly goes into sujud in the middle of the rak'ah.

Surah As-Sajdah is a short surah of thirty verses, but it is full of meaning: Allah perfected everything He created, made the first human from clay, and gave us hearing, sight and hearts. It beautifully describes believers who leave their beds at night to call on their Lord, and their reward is something hidden that no one knows. In this guide, we explain Surah As-Sajdah to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah As-Sajdah

- **Position**: surah 32, in juz 21.
- **Number of verses**: 30.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Other name**: "Alif Lam Mim Tanzil", as the companions used to call it.
- **Name**: as-Sajdah, "The Prostration", because verse 15 contains a prostration of recitation.

### From the Sunnah
Al-Bukhari and Muslim reported from Abu Hurayrah that the Prophet ﷺ used to recite "Alif Lam Mim Tanzil" (As-Sajdah) and "Hal ata 'alal-insan" in Fajr on Fridays.

### Another virtue
It is reported that the Prophet ﷺ would not sleep until he had recited "Alif Lam Mim Tanzil" and "Tabarakal-ladhi biyadihil-mulk" (at-Tirmidhi; graded authentic by al-Albani).

## Lesson 1: The Quran Without Doubt

﴿تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ لَا رَيۡبَ فِيهِ مِن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ (Quran 32:2)

### Explaining it to your child
"The Quran was sent down from Allah, the Lord of the worlds, and there is no doubt in it at all. Every word is true. That's why we trust everything in it, learn it and act on it."

## Lesson 2: Created in Six Days

﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٖ ثُمَّ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِۖ مَا لَكُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَلِيّٖ وَلَا شَفِيعٍۚ أَفَلَا تَتَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:4)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah created the heavens, the earth and everything between them in six days. He could create them in an instant, but He taught us patience and order. Then He rose over the Throne in a way that suits His majesty. We have no protector or intercessor besides Him. Won't we remember?"

### Lesson for kids: patience and order
"Allah can do anything in an instant, yet He created the universe in six days. We learn to do our work in order, calmly, step by step, without rushing."

### Activity: a six-day project
Plan a small project with your child over six days: collecting materials, drawing, colouring, cutting, sticking, and presenting. They learn that good work takes its time.

## Lesson 3: He Perfected Everything He Created

﴿ٱلَّذِيٓ أَحۡسَنَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقَهُۥۖ وَبَدَأَ خَلۡقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ مِن طِينٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ جَعَلَ نَسۡلَهُۥ مِن سُلَٰلَةٖ مِّن مَّآءٖ مَّهِينٖ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ سَوَّىٰهُ وَنَفَخَ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِهِۦۖ وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةَۚ قَلِيلٗا مَّا تَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:7-9)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah perfected everything He created: flowers are beautiful, cats are beautiful, the sky is beautiful. He began the creation of man from clay, meaning Adam. Then He made his children grow in their mothers' wombs. Then He completed the human being, breathed the soul into him, and gave us hearing, sight and hearts. And still, few people are grateful!"

### Activity: the three blessings
- **Hearing**: close your eyes and count how many sounds you can hear.
- **Sight**: look out of the window and count five colours.
- **The heart and mind**: think of something beautiful you love.
Then say: "Allah gave us all of these. How do we thank Him? We use our ears for good things, our eyes for what is halal, and our hearts to love Allah."

### Lesson for kids: don't criticise how you look
"Teach your child not to criticise their own appearance or anyone else's, because Allah perfected everything He created. If they say 'my nose is too big' or 'my hair is too curly', gently remind them of the verse."

## Lesson 4: The Prostration of Recitation

﴿إِنَّمَا يُؤۡمِنُ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ بِهَا خَرُّواْۤ سُجَّدٗاۤ وَسَبَّحُواْ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ۩﴾ (Quran 32:15)

### Explaining it to your child
"True believers, when they hear Allah's verses, fall down in prostration, glorify their Lord with praise, and are not arrogant. This is the verse of sajdah: when we recite or hear it, we make one prostration."

### How to do sajdat at-tilawah
1. When you recite or hear the verse, prostrate once.
2. In the prostration, say "Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la". There is also a special du'a: "Sajada wajhi lilladhi khalaqahu wa sawwarahu wa shaqqa sam'ahu wa basarahu bi hawlihi wa quwwatih" (Abu Dawud and at-Tirmidhi, who graded it authentic).
3. Then come up and continue reciting.

### Activity: practise the sajdah
Read verses 13–15 with your child, and when you reach the end of verse 15, prostrate together. Then tell them: "On Friday at Fajr, the imam will make an extra sujud in the first rak'ah, and you'll prostrate with him."

## Lesson 5: Praying at Night

﴿تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلۡمَضَاجِعِ يَدۡعُونَ رَبَّهُمۡ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعٗا وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:16)

### Explaining it to your child
"The believers leave their warm beds at night to pray and call on their Lord, fearing His punishment and hoping for His mercy, and they give from what Allah has provided them."

### Activity: two rak'ahs before bed
For children, night prayer can start small: two rak'ahs before sleeping, or joining you once in the holidays ten minutes before Fajr. Tell them the verse and say: "Now you are like the believers Allah described."

## Lesson 6: The Comfort of the Eyes

﴿فَلَا تَعۡلَمُ نَفۡسࣱ مَّآ أُخۡفِيَ لَهُم مِّن قُرَّةِ أَعۡيُنٖ جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:17)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah has prepared a hidden reward for the believers who pray at night, something nobody knows! Something that makes the eyes happy and the heart joyful. Like a wrapped present: you don't know what's inside, but you know it's the best gift ever."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah said: I have prepared for My righteous servants what no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no heart has ever imagined." Then he recited this verse (al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Activity: the wrapped present
Wrap a small gift for your child and tell them they can open it after praying two rak'ahs before bed for three nights. When they open it, say: "The gifts of Jannah are far better, and nobody knows what they are."

## Lesson 7: Leaders Who Were Patient

﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنۡهُمۡ أَئِمَّةٗ يَهۡدُونَ بِأَمۡرِنَا لَمَّا صَبَرُواْۖ وَكَانُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا يُوقِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:24)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah made leaders from the Children of Israel who guided people to good. Why? Because they were patient and certain about Allah's verses. A real leader needs patience and certainty."

### Lesson for kids
"If you want to be a leader in your class or team, learn patience: patience with studying, patience with your friends, and trust that Allah is with you."

## Lesson 8: Water Brings Land to Life

﴿أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّا نَسُوقُ ٱلۡمَآءَ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ٱلۡجُرُزِ فَنُخۡرِجُ بِهِۦ زَرۡعٗا تَأۡكُلُ مِنۡهُ أَنۡعَٰمُهُمۡ وَأَنفُسُهُمۡۚ أَفَلَا يُبۡصِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 32:27)

### Explaining it to your child
"Barren land is dry land with no plants. Allah drives water to it, and crops grow, which animals and people eat. Don't they see? Look and reflect!"

### Activity: dry soil
Put dry soil in a pot, plant seeds, and water it every day. After a few days you'll see green shoots. Say: "This is how Allah brings land to life with water."

## The "Sajdah and Mulk" Bedtime Programme

Since the Prophet ﷺ would not sleep until he recited As-Sajdah and Tabarak, you could build a four-week programme with your child:

1. **Week 1**: your child listens to Surah As-Sajdah from a calm reciter in bed, without memorizing.
2. **Week 2**: read verses 1–9 together and explain the blessings of hearing and sight.
3. **Week 3**: memorize verses 15–17 and practise the prostration.
4. **Week 4**: add listening to Surah Al-Mulk, and write in their notebook: "I follow the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ before sleeping."

### Tip for parents
Don't let the programme delay bedtime or make your child dislike Quran time. Listening alone is beautiful worship, and memorizing will come with time and repetition.

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "The Surprise Sajdah"

For the first time, Anas went with his father to Fajr prayer on a Friday. It was cold, the masjid was quiet, and the imam was reciting beautifully. Suddenly, in the middle of the first rak'ah, the imam said "Allahu Akbar" and went down into sujud! Anas was confused. He looked around and saw everyone prostrating, so he quickly prostrated too.

After the prayer, Anas asked: "Dad, why did the imam prostrate before ruku'? Did he make a mistake?" His father laughed. "No, my son. The imam recited Surah As-Sajdah, which the Prophet ﷺ used to recite at Fajr on Fridays. It has a verse of prostration, so when he reached it, he prostrated."

"Which verse?" asked Anas. His father recited verse 15 and explained it: believers who fall into prostration when they hear Allah's verses and are not arrogant. Then he said: "You're one of them now. You prostrated when you heard Allah's verses."

Anas was thrilled and decided to memorize Surah As-Sajdah. Every Friday after that, he waited for that moment and prostrated with the imam, smiling.

Ask your child: "Why did the imam prostrate in the middle of the rak'ah? On which day does the imam recite Surah As-Sajdah at Fajr?"

## What We Learn From Surah As-Sajdah

- **The Quran is true**, without doubt.
- **Patience and order** in our work.
- **Allah perfected** everything He created.
- **Thank Allah** for hearing, sight and our hearts.
- **Prostrate** when we hear the verse of sajdah.
- **Pray at night**, even two rak'ahs.
- **Jannah** holds what we cannot imagine.
- **Patience and certainty** make true leaders.

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Is the prostration of recitation obligatory?
According to the majority of scholars, it is a confirmed sunnah, not obligatory. It is recommended for both the reciter and the listener.

### Does it require wudu?
Scholars differ, and it is more cautious to prostrate with wudu. For children, it's enough to teach them to prostrate in prayer and when reciting with wudu.

### My child is startled by the sajdah at Fajr. What should I do?
Explain beforehand: "The imam will recite a surah with a sajdah, so when he prostrates, you prostrate too." Practise at home first.

### Why are As-Sajdah and Al-Insan recited on Fridays?
Scholars mention that both surahs remind us of the beginning of human creation and its end, in Paradise or Hell, and Friday is the day Adam was created and the day the Hour will take place.
