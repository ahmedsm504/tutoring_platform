﴿وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتُ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٖۚ يَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَيَنۡهَوۡنَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَيُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَيُطِيعُونَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ سَيَرۡحَمُهُمُ ٱللَّهُۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ [At-Tawbah 9:71]

## A contrast
| Hypocrites (9:67) | Believers (9:71) |
|---|---|
| *Some of them are of others* | *Some of them are allies of others* |
| Enjoin evil | Enjoin good |
| Close their hands | Give zakah |

## *Wilayah*
- Love, support and cooperation among believing men and women.

## Five qualities
- Enjoining good, forbidding evil, establishing prayer, giving zakah, obeying Allah and His Messenger.

## Women as partners
- Believing women are named alongside men in loyalty and in enjoining good: partners in reform.

## "Allah will have mercy on them"
- The *sa-* prefix confirms a promised future.

## Lessons
- **Support your brothers and sisters** in good.
- **Measure your community** by these qualities.
