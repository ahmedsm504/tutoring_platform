**Surah Al-Mujadilah** (22 ayat) begins Juz 28 with a woman's complaint that Allah answered from above the seven heavens.

﴿قَدۡ سَمِعَ ٱللَّهُ قَوۡلَ ٱلَّتِي تُجَٰدِلُكَ فِي زَوۡجِهَا وَتَشۡتَكِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَٱللَّهُ يَسۡمَعُ تَحَاوُرَكُمَآۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعُۢ بَصِيرٌ﴾

## The story
- **Khawlah bint Thaʿlabah**'s husband told her "you are to me like my mother's back", a pre-Islamic form of divorce called *ẓihār*.
- She came to the Prophet ﷺ and pleaded. ʿAishah said: "Blessed is the One whose hearing encompasses all voices", as she had been in the room yet could not hear all of it.

## The ruling on zihar
| Expiation, in order | |
|---|---|
| 1 | Freeing a slave |
| 2 | Fasting two consecutive months |
| 3 | Feeding sixty poor people |

## Etiquette in gatherings
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمۡ تَفَسَّحُواْ فِي ٱلۡمَجَٰلِسِ فَٱفۡسَحُواْ يَفۡسَحِ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمۡۖ﴾
- **Make room**, and Allah will make room for you.
- *Allah raises those who believe among you and those given knowledge by degrees.*
- **Private talk**: not in sin and aggression, but in righteousness and piety.

## Lessons
- **Take your complaints to Allah**: He hears.
- **Knowledge raises** its people.
