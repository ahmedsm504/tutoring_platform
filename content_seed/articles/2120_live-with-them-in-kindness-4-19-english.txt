﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا يَحِلُّ لَكُمۡ أَن تَرِثُواْ ٱلنِّسَآءَ كَرۡهٗاۖ وَلَا تَعۡضُلُوهُنَّ لِتَذۡهَبُواْ بِبَعۡضِ مَآ ءَاتَيۡتُمُوهُنَّ إِلَّآ أَن يَأۡتِينَ بِفَٰحِشَةٖ مُّبَيِّنَةٖۚ وَعَاشِرُوهُنَّ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِۚ فَإِن كَرِهۡتُمُوهُنَّ فَعَسَىٰٓ أَن تَكۡرَهُواْ شَيۡـٔٗا وَيَجۡعَلَ ٱللَّهُ فِيهِ خَيۡرٗا كَثِيرٗا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:19]

## What it abolished
| Pre-Islamic practice | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Inheriting a widow as property | Forbidden (narrated by al-Bukhari from Ibn ʿAbbas) |
| Pressuring a wife to give up her dowry | Forbidden |

## Kindness (*maʿruf*)
- What the Shariʿah and decent custom recognise as good: kind words, provision, fairness and good character.

## If you dislike something
- *Perhaps you dislike a thing and Allah places much good in it*: a righteous child, or affection later.
- *A believing man should not hate a believing woman; if he dislikes one of her traits, he will be pleased with another* (narrated by Muslim).

## Lessons
- **Look at the good**, not only the flaws.
- **Kindness** is the standard at home.
