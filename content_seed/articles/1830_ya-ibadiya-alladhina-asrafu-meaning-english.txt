﴿۞قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ [Az-Zumar 39:53]

## "O My servants"
- Allah addresses those who have **sinned excessively** and still calls them *My servants*. Sin did not cancel the relationship.

## The core message
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Who have transgressed against themselves* | Sin harms the sinner first |
| *Do not despair* | Despair is forbidden |
| *Allah forgives all sins* | No sin is too big for repentance |
| *He is the Forgiving, the Merciful* | Two names that confirm the promise |

## The condition
- The next verse: *And turn to your Lord and submit to Him before the punishment comes.* Forgiveness of all sins is for those who **repent**. Scholars note that this verse is about the repentant, while An-Nisa 4:48 is about the one who dies upon shirk without repentance.

## Most hopeful
- Many scholars describe this as one of the most hopeful verses in the Quran.

## Lessons
- **Never let a sin keep you from Allah.**
- **Repent today**, before the chance ends.
