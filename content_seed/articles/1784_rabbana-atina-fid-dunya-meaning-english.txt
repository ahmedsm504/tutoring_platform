﴿وَمِنۡهُم مَّن يَقُولُ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةٗ وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ حَسَنَةٗ وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:201]

## Meaning
*And among them are those who say: Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.*

## The context
- The verse comes in the passage on Hajj. Allah describes two types of people: those who ask only for this world, and those who ask for **both**.

## The Prophet's most frequent duʿa
- Anas ibn Malik reported that the most frequent duʿa of the Prophet ﷺ was this one (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

## What is "good"?
| In this world | In the Hereafter |
|---|---|
| Health and safety | Forgiveness |
| Lawful provision | Easy reckoning |
| A righteous spouse and family | Paradise |
| Beneficial knowledge | Salvation from the Fire |

- Scholars say the word *hasanah* is left open so it includes **every good**.

## Balance
- Islam does not ask you to abandon this world, nor to make it your only goal. This duʿa holds both together.

## Lessons
- **Make it a daily duʿa**, especially between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone in tawaf, where it is commonly recited.
- **Ask for the world without forgetting the Hereafter.**
