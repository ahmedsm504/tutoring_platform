﴿أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَسَالَتۡ أَوۡدِيَةُۢ بِقَدَرِهَا فَٱحۡتَمَلَ ٱلسَّيۡلُ زَبَدٗا رَّابِيٗاۖ وَمِمَّا يُوقِدُونَ عَلَيۡهِ فِي ٱلنَّارِ ٱبۡتِغَآءَ حِلۡيَةٍ أَوۡ مَتَٰعٖ زَبَدࣱ مِّثۡلُهُۥۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَٱلۡبَٰطِلَۚ فَأَمَّا ٱلزَّبَدُ فَيَذۡهَبُ جُفَآءٗۖ وَأَمَّا مَا يَنفَعُ ٱلنَّاسَ فَيَمۡكُثُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَالَ﴾ [Ar-Rad 13:17]

## Two parables
| Parable | Truth | Falsehood |
|---|---|---|
| The flood | Useful water | Foam on the surface |
| Smelted metals | Gold, silver, iron | Dross that floats |

## "Valleys according to their capacity"
- Each valley takes water by its size, and so do **hearts**: they take from revelation according to their readiness.

## "Swelling foam"
- Foam rises high, as falsehood sometimes rises and grows loud.

## "It passes away"
- It is thrown off and disappears without benefit.

## "What benefits people remains"
- Beneficial knowledge, good deeds and truth endure.

## Lessons
- **Do not be fooled** by the rise of falsehood; it is foam.
- **Widen the valley of your heart** for good.
