**Surah Al-Hijr** (99 ayat) contains one of the most important promises in the Quran.

## The preservation of the Quran
﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ﴾
- Allah Himself guaranteed its preservation, which is why it remains unchanged in every letter.

## Adam and Iblis
- Iblis refused to prostrate, claiming he was better because he was made from fire.
- He vowed to mislead people, but was told he has **no authority over Allah's sincere servants**.

## Mercy and punishment
﴿۞نَبِّئۡ عِبَادِيٓ أَنِّيٓ أَنَا ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾
- The next verse adds that His punishment is the painful punishment, so hope and fear stay balanced.

## Stories
| Story | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Ibrahim's guests | Good news of a son in old age |
| The people of Lut | Destruction after persistent corruption |
| The people of al-Hijr | Homes carved in mountains could not protect them |

## The Seven Mathani
- *We have given you seven of the oft-repeated verses and the great Quran*: understood as al-Fatihah.

## Worship until certainty
- The surah ends: *And worship your Lord until there comes to you the certainty*, meaning death.

## Lessons
- **Trust the Quran you hold**: it is preserved by Allah.
- **Balance hope and fear.**
