Juz 22 begins with ﴿۞وَمَن يَقۡنُتۡ مِنكُنَّ لِلَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَتَعۡمَلۡ صَٰلِحٗا نُّؤۡتِهَآ أَجۡرَهَا مَرَّتَيۡنِ﴾, "Whoever of you is devoutly obedient to Allah and His Messenger and does good, We will give her reward twice", so it is called **Juz Wa Man Yaqnut**. It runs from **Al-Ahzab 33:31 to Ya-Sin 36:27**, with **Saba'** and **Fatir** complete in between. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 422 to 441**.

This is the juz of **gratitude and our need of Allah**. It begins with the upbringing of the Prophet's household and his rank ﷺ, then shows in Saba' two models: the grateful family of Dawud, and the people of Sheba who turned away and lost their gardens. Fatir declares: ﴿أَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ﴾, "You are the ones in need of Allah", and Ya-Sin opens with the story of a man so sincere in his call that he entered Paradise.

## The big idea

The juz revolves around **gratitude to Allah and our need of Him**. In Saba': ﴿ٱعۡمَلُوٓاْ ءَالَ دَاوُۥدَ شُكۡرٗاۚ وَقَلِيلࣱ مِّنۡ عِبَادِيَ ٱلشَّكُورُ﴾, "Work, family of Dawud, in gratitude; and few of My servants are grateful", while the people of Sheba were told ﴿كُلُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِ رَبِّكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لَهُۥۚ﴾, "eat from your Lord's provision and be grateful to Him", and turned away. In Fatir: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡۚ﴾, "O mankind, remember Allah's favour upon you", then ﴿أَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ﴾, "You are the ones in need of Allah, and Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy." Both surahs open with ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ﴾, and praise is the head of gratitude.

The message: **everything you have is from Allah, so thank Him with deeds, and know you need Him at every moment.**

## Part one: the rest of Surah Al-Ahzab (31-73)

### 1. The etiquette of the Mothers of the Believers (31-34)
"Whoever of you is devoutly obedient to Allah and His Messenger and does good, We will give her reward twice and have prepared for her a noble provision." Then: ﴿يَٰنِسَآءَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ لَسۡتُنَّ كَأَحَدٖ مِّنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ إِنِ ٱتَّقَيۡتُنَّۚ فَلَا تَخۡضَعۡنَ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ فَيَطۡمَعَ ٱلَّذِي فِي قَلۡبِهِۦ مَرَضࣱ وَقُلۡنَ قَوۡلٗا مَّعۡرُوفٗا﴾, "Wives of the Prophet, you are not like any other women if you fear Allah. So do not be soft in speech, lest one with disease in his heart should covet, but speak appropriate words." Then: ﴿وَقَرۡنَ فِي بُيُوتِكُنَّ وَلَا تَبَرَّجۡنَ تَبَرُّجَ ٱلۡجَٰهِلِيَّةِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰۖ وَأَقِمۡنَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتِينَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَطِعۡنَ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓۚ﴾, "Stay in your houses and do not display yourselves as in the former Ignorance; establish prayer, give zakah and obey Allah and His Messenger", and remember what is recited in your homes of Allah's verses and wisdom.

**Lesson**: the higher the rank, the greater the responsibility: the reward is doubled and so is the penalty.

### 2. Equality in reward (35)
﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ وَٱلۡمُسۡلِمَٰتِ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتِينَ وَٱلۡقَٰنِتَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰدِقَٰتِ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ وَٱلصَّـٰبِرَٰتِ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعِينَ وَٱلۡخَٰشِعَٰتِ﴾, "The Muslim men and women, the believing men and women, the devout men and women, the truthful men and women, the patient men and women, the humble men and women", and those who give charity, fast, guard their chastity and remember Allah often: Allah has prepared for them forgiveness and a great reward.

**Ten traits**: Islam, faith, devotion, truthfulness, patience, humility, charity, fasting, chastity and frequent remembrance, shared equally by men and women.

### 3. Submitting to Allah's decision, and the story of Zayd (36-40)
﴿وَمَا كَانَ لِمُؤۡمِنٖ وَلَا مُؤۡمِنَةٍ إِذَا قَضَى ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥٓ أَمۡرًا أَن يَكُونَ لَهُمُ ٱلۡخِيَرَةُ مِنۡ أَمۡرِهِمۡۗ﴾, "It is not for a believing man or woman, when Allah and His Messenger have decided a matter, to have any choice about their affair." Then the story of Zayd ibn Harithah, the only Companion named in the Quran: ﴿فَلَمَّا قَضَىٰ زَيۡدࣱ مِّنۡهَا وَطَرٗا زَوَّجۡنَٰكَهَا﴾, "When Zayd had ended his relationship with her, We married her to you", abolishing in practice the custom that forbade marrying the ex-wife of an adopted son. Then: ﴿مَّا كَانَ مُحَمَّدٌ أَبَآ أَحَدٖ مِّن رِّجَالِكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن رَّسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَخَاتَمَ ٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَۗ﴾, "Muhammad is not the father of any of your men, but he is the Messenger of Allah and the Seal of the Prophets."

**Lesson**: the finality of prophethood with Muhammad ﷺ is a definitive text; there is no prophet after him.

### 4. Abundant remembrance and the Prophet's qualities (41-50)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ ذِكۡرٗا كَثِيرٗا﴾ ﴿وَسَبِّحُوهُ بُكۡرَةٗ وَأَصِيلًا﴾ ﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُصَلِّي عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتُهُۥ لِيُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنَ ٱلظُّلُمَٰتِ إِلَى ٱلنُّورِۚ وَكَانَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ رَحِيمٗا﴾, "O you who believe, remember Allah with much remembrance, and glorify Him morning and evening. It is He who sends blessings on you, as do His angels, to bring you out of darkness into light; and He is ever Merciful to the believers." Then the Prophet's five qualities ﷺ: a witness, a bringer of good news, a warner, one who calls to Allah by His permission, and an illuminating lamp.

**Reflect**: Allah commanded nothing to be done "much" as He did remembrance, because it costs no effort or money.

### 5. Rulings for the Prophet's household and blessings on him (51-58)
A new quarter: ﴿۞تُرۡجِي مَن تَشَآءُ مِنۡهُنَّ وَتُـٔۡوِيٓ إِلَيۡكَ مَن تَشَآءُۖ﴾, "You may defer whom you will of them, or take to yourself whom you will." Then the verse of the screen and the etiquette of entering the Prophet's homes: do not enter unless given permission, and when you ask them for something, ask from behind a screen; that is purer for your hearts and theirs.

Then the great verse: ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِيِّۚ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ صَلُّواْ عَلَيۡهِ وَسَلِّمُواْ تَسۡلِيمًا﴾, "Allah and His angels send blessings on the Prophet. O you who believe, send blessings on him and greet him with peace." In Sahih Muslim the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever sends one blessing on me, Allah sends ten on him." Read [Salawat on the Prophet: 33:56](/blog/salawat-on-the-prophet-33-56-meaning-english/).

**Reflect**: Allah began with His own blessings and those of His angels before commanding the believers, honouring the Prophet ﷺ and encouraging us to follow suit.

### 6. Outer garments and the rumour-mongers (59-68)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ قُل لِّأَزۡوَٰجِكَ وَبَنَاتِكَ وَنِسَآءِ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ يُدۡنِينَ عَلَيۡهِنَّ مِن جَلَٰبِيبِهِنَّۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَدۡنَىٰٓ أَن يُعۡرَفۡنَ فَلَا يُؤۡذَيۡنَۗ﴾, "O Prophet, tell your wives, your daughters and the believing women to draw their outer garments over themselves; that is more suitable so they will be recognised and not harmed." Then a new quarter: ﴿۞لَّئِن لَّمۡ يَنتَهِ ٱلۡمُنَٰفِقُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضࣱ وَٱلۡمُرۡجِفُونَ فِي ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ لَنُغۡرِيَنَّكَ بِهِمۡ﴾, "If the hypocrites, those with disease in their hearts and the rumour-mongers in Madinah do not stop, We will surely set you over them." Then people's questions about the Hour, and a scene of the Fire: if only we had obeyed Allah and obeyed the Messenger.

### 7. Words of justice and the Trust (69-73)
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَقُولُواْ قَوۡلٗا سَدِيدٗا﴾ ﴿يُصۡلِحۡ لَكُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلَكُمۡ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡ ذُنُوبَكُمۡۗ وَمَن يُطِعِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَقَدۡ فَازَ فَوۡزًا عَظِيمًا﴾, "O you who believe, fear Allah and speak words of justice. He will amend your deeds for you and forgive your sins. Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has attained a great success." Upright speech is a cause of upright deeds. Read [Speak Words of Justice: 33:70](/blog/qulu-qawlan-sadida-meaning-english/).

Then the close of the surah: ﴿إِنَّا عَرَضۡنَا ٱلۡأَمَانَةَ عَلَى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱلۡجِبَالِ فَأَبَيۡنَ أَن يَحۡمِلۡنَهَا وَأَشۡفَقۡنَ مِنۡهَا وَحَمَلَهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ ظَلُومٗا جَهُولٗا﴾, "We offered the Trust to the heavens, the earth and the mountains, but they declined to bear it and feared it; and man bore it. He was unjust and ignorant." The Trust is the whole of religious obligation, which man accepted and so became accountable. Read [We Offered the Trust: 33:72](/blog/we-offered-the-trust-33-72-meaning-english/).

## Part two: Surah Saba' (1-54)

Saba' is Makki, with 54 verses, opening with ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَهُ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ﴾, "Praise be to Allah, to whom belongs whatever is in the heavens and earth, and His is the praise in the Hereafter." For the whole surah see [Surah Saba Meaning in English](/blog/surah-saba-meaning-english/).

### 1. Allah's knowledge and the resurrection (1-9)
He knows what enters the earth and what comes out of it, what descends from the sky and what ascends into it. Then the disbelievers deny the Hour, and the reply: yes, by my Lord, it will come to you; not an atom's weight escapes Him.

### 2. The grateful family of Dawud (10-14)
A new quarter: ﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ مِنَّا فَضۡلٗاۖ يَٰجِبَالُ أَوِّبِي مَعَهُۥ وَٱلطَّيۡرَۖ وَأَلَنَّا لَهُ ٱلۡحَدِيدَ﴾, "We gave Dawud a favour from Us: O mountains, echo glorification with him, and you birds; and We made iron soft for him", commanding him to make coats of mail with balanced links. Then Sulayman and the wind, the spring of molten copper, and the jinn working for him: ﴿يَعۡمَلُونَ لَهُۥ مَا يَشَآءُ مِن مَّحَٰرِيبَ وَتَمَٰثِيلَ وَجِفَانٖ كَٱلۡجَوَابِ وَقُدُورٖ رَّاسِيَٰتٍۚ ٱعۡمَلُوٓاْ ءَالَ دَاوُۥدَ شُكۡرٗاۚ وَقَلِيلࣱ مِّنۡ عِبَادِيَ ٱلشَّكُورُ﴾, "They made for him whatever he wished: sanctuaries, statues, basins like reservoirs and fixed cauldrons. Work, family of Dawud, in gratitude; and few of My servants are grateful."

Then Sulayman's death: ﴿فَلَمَّا قَضَيۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ مَا دَلَّهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَوۡتِهِۦٓ إِلَّا دَآبَّةُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تَأۡكُلُ مِنسَأَتَهُۥۖ﴾, "When We decreed death for him, nothing showed them his death except a creature of the earth eating his staff", revealing that the jinn do not know the unseen.

**Lesson**: gratitude is action, not words alone: "work in gratitude". The grateful are few, so be among the few.

### 3. Sheba and the flood of 'Arim (15-21)
﴿لَقَدۡ كَانَ لِسَبَإٖ فِي مَسۡكَنِهِمۡ ءَايَةࣱۖ جَنَّتَانِ عَن يَمِينٖ وَشِمَالٖۖ كُلُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِ رَبِّكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لَهُۥۚ بَلۡدَةࣱ طَيِّبَةࣱ وَرَبٌّ غَفُورࣱ﴾, "There was for Sheba in their dwelling a sign: two gardens, on the right and the left. Eat from your Lord's provision and be grateful to Him: a good land and a forgiving Lord." Then: ﴿فَأَعۡرَضُواْ فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَيۡلَ ٱلۡعَرِمِ وَبَدَّلۡنَٰهُم بِجَنَّتَيۡهِمۡ جَنَّتَيۡنِ ذَوَاتَيۡ أُكُلٍ خَمۡطٖ وَأَثۡلٖ وَشَيۡءٖ مِّن سِدۡرٖ قَلِيلٖ﴾, "But they turned away, so We sent upon them the flood of the dam, and replaced their two gardens with gardens of bitter fruit, tamarisks and a few lote trees." Then the visible towns and safe travel, and they said: our Lord, lengthen the distance between our journeys; so We made them tales and scattered them completely. Then: ﴿وَلَقَدۡ صَدَّقَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ إِبۡلِيسُ ظَنَّهُۥ فَٱتَّبَعُوهُ إِلَّا فَرِيقٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾, "Iblis found his assumption about them true, and they followed him, except a group of believers."

**The contrast**: Dawud's family was grateful and Allah gave them more; the people of Sheba turned away and lost what they had.

### 4. Dialogue with the idolaters (22-45)
Hizb 44 begins: ﴿۞قُلۡ مَن يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ قُلِ ٱللَّهُۖ وَإِنَّآ أَوۡ إِيَّاكُمۡ لَعَلَىٰ هُدًى أَوۡ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾, "Say: who provides for you from the heavens and earth? Say: Allah. And indeed, either we or you are on guidance or in clear error." This is among the fairest styles of dialogue: it does not say "you are in error" but leaves the verdict to reason. Then the universality of the message: ﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰكَ إِلَّا كَآفَّةٗ لِّلنَّاسِ بَشِيرٗا وَنَذِيرٗا وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَ ٱلنَّاسِ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾, "We have sent you only to all mankind, as a bringer of good news and a warner, but most people do not know."

Then the dialogue in the Fire between the weak and the arrogant, each blaming the other. Then the affluent: we have more wealth and children and will not be punished; the reply: it is not your wealth or children that bring you near to Us, but whoever believes and does good.

### 5. A single counsel (46-54)
A new quarter: ﴿۞قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَعِظُكُم بِوَٰحِدَةٍۖ أَن تَقُومُواْ لِلَّهِ مَثۡنَىٰ وَفُرَٰدَىٰ ثُمَّ تَتَفَكَّرُواْۚ مَا بِصَاحِبِكُم مِّن جِنَّةٍۚ﴾, "Say: I counsel you with one thing: stand for Allah in pairs or individually, then reflect. There is no madness in your companion." A call to free reflection away from the pressure of the crowd. The close: a barrier will be placed between them and what they desire.

## Part three: Surah Fatir (1-45)

Fatir is Makki, with 45 verses, also called Surah Al-Mala'ikah (The Angels). It opens: ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ فَاطِرِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَاعِلِ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ رُسُلًا أُوْلِيٓ أَجۡنِحَةٖ مَّثۡنَىٰ وَثُلَٰثَ وَرُبَٰعَۚ﴾, "Praise be to Allah, Originator of the heavens and earth, who made the angels messengers with wings, two, three and four." For the whole surah see [Surah Fatir Meaning in English](/blog/surah-fatir-meaning-english/).

### 1. The keys of mercy (1-8)
﴿مَّا يَفۡتَحِ ٱللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ مِن رَّحۡمَةٖ فَلَا مُمۡسِكَ لَهَاۖ وَمَا يُمۡسِكۡ فَلَا مُرۡسِلَ لَهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ﴾, "Whatever mercy Allah opens for people, none can withhold it; and whatever He withholds, none can release it after Him. He is the Almighty, the Wise." Then: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱذۡكُرُواْ نِعۡمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيۡكُمۡۚ هَلۡ مِنۡ خَٰلِقٍ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِ يَرۡزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ﴾, "O mankind, remember Allah's favour upon you. Is there any creator other than Allah who provides for you from the sky and earth?" Then two warnings: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّ وَعۡدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقࣱّۖ فَلَا تَغُرَّنَّكُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَلَا يَغُرَّنَّكُم بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡغَرُورُ﴾, "O mankind, Allah's promise is true, so let not worldly life delude you, and let not the Deceiver delude you about Allah", ﴿إِنَّ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَ لَكُمۡ عَدُوࣱّ فَٱتَّخِذُوهُ عَدُوًّاۚ﴾, "Shaytan is your enemy, so take him as an enemy."

Then the danger of evil made to look good: ﴿أَفَمَن زُيِّنَ لَهُۥ سُوٓءُ عَمَلِهِۦ فَرَءَاهُ حَسَنٗاۖ﴾, "Is one whose evil deed has been made attractive to him, so he sees it as good...?"

**Lesson**: if Allah opens a door for you, none can close it; if He closes it, none can open it. So ask Allah alone.

### 2. Honour and good words (9-14)
﴿مَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡعِزَّةَ فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡعِزَّةُ جَمِيعًاۚ إِلَيۡهِ يَصۡعَدُ ٱلۡكَلِمُ ٱلطَّيِّبُ وَٱلۡعَمَلُ ٱلصَّـٰلِحُ يَرۡفَعُهُۥۚ﴾, "Whoever desires honour, all honour belongs to Allah. To Him ascends good speech, and righteous deeds raise it." Honour is sought from Allah through good words and righteous deeds. Then the verse of the two seas: one fresh, sweet and pleasant to drink, the other salty and bitter, and from each you eat tender meat.

### 3. Our need of Allah (15-26)
A new quarter opens with the comprehensive verse: ﴿۞يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ أَنتُمُ ٱلۡفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ وَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلۡغَنِيُّ ٱلۡحَمِيدُ﴾, "O mankind, you are the ones in need of Allah, and Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy." Read [You Are the Ones in Need of Allah: 35:15](/blog/antum-al-fuqara-ila-allah-meaning-english/). Then individual responsibility: ﴿وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةࣱ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰۚ وَإِن تَدۡعُ مُثۡقَلَةٌ إِلَىٰ حِمۡلِهَا لَا يُحۡمَلۡ مِنۡهُ شَيۡءࣱ وَلَوۡ كَانَ ذَا قُرۡبَىٰٓۗ﴾, "No bearer of burdens bears another's burden; and if a heavily laden soul calls for help with its load, none of it will be carried, even by a relative." Then: the blind and the seeing are not equal, nor darkness and light, nor shade and heat, nor the living and the dead.

### 4. The fear of the knowledgeable and the heirs of the Book (27-35)
The colours of fruits and mountains, streaks white and red of varying hues and jet black, then: ﴿وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَٱلدَّوَآبِّ وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ مُخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥ كَذَٰلِكَۗ إِنَّمَا يَخۡشَى ٱللَّهَ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِ ٱلۡعُلَمَـٰٓؤُاْۗ﴾, "And among people, creatures and cattle are various colours likewise. Only those of His servants who have knowledge truly fear Allah." True knowledge produces awe, and it comes here in the context of reflecting on creation.

Then a trade that never fails: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَتۡلُونَ كِتَٰبَ ٱللَّهِ وَأَقَامُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنفَقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ سِرّٗا وَعَلَانِيَةٗ يَرۡجُونَ تِجَٰرَةٗ لَّن تَبُورَ﴾, "Those who recite the Book of Allah, establish prayer and spend from what We provided them, secretly and openly, hope for a trade that will never perish."

Then the categories of the heirs of the Book: ﴿ثُمَّ أَوۡرَثۡنَا ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱصۡطَفَيۡنَا مِنۡ عِبَادِنَاۖ فَمِنۡهُمۡ ظَالِمࣱ لِّنَفۡسِهِۦ وَمِنۡهُم مُّقۡتَصِدࣱ وَمِنۡهُمۡ سَابِقُۢ بِٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ بِإِذۡنِ ٱللَّهِۚ﴾, "Then We gave the Book as inheritance to those We chose of Our servants: among them is one who wrongs himself, one who is moderate, and one foremost in good deeds by Allah's permission":
1. **One who wrongs himself**: falling short in some obligations.
2. **The moderate**: fulfilling obligations and avoiding prohibitions.
3. **The foremost in good**: hastening to voluntary deeds along with the obligations.

All of them are chosen heirs of the Book; then they enter the Gardens of 'Adn and say: ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَذۡهَبَ عَنَّا ٱلۡحَزَنَۖ إِنَّ رَبَّنَا لَغَفُورࣱ شَكُورٌ﴾, "Praise be to Allah, who has removed grief from us. Our Lord is Forgiving, Appreciative."

**Reflect, people of the Quran**: whoever carries the Quran is among the chosen heirs of the Book, so strive to be among the foremost.

### 5. Holding the heavens and the close (36-45)
A scene of the people of the Fire crying out: our Lord, take us out so we may do good. Then a new quarter: ﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُمۡسِكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ أَن تَزُولَاۚ﴾, "Allah holds the heavens and earth, lest they cease." Then the rule: ﴿وَلَا يَحِيقُ ٱلۡمَكۡرُ ٱلسَّيِّئُ إِلَّا بِأَهۡلِهِۦۚ﴾, "Evil plotting encompasses only its own people." The close: if Allah were to punish people for what they earned, He would not leave a single creature on the earth's surface.

## Part four: the start of Surah Ya-Sin (1-27)

Ya-Sin is Makki, with 83 verses, 27 of which fall in this juz. For the whole surah see [Surah Yasin Meaning in English](/blog/surah-yasin-meaning-english/).

### 1. The oath by the Quran and the warning (1-12)
﴿يسٓ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ﴾ ﴿تَنزِيلَ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾ ﴿لِتُنذِرَ قَوۡمٗا مَّآ أُنذِرَ ءَابَآؤُهُمۡ فَهُمۡ غَٰفِلُونَ﴾, "Ya Sin. By the wise Quran, you are among the messengers, on a straight path, a revelation from the Almighty, the Merciful, so you may warn a people whose forefathers were not warned, so they are heedless." Then an image of those who turn away: ﴿وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنۢ بَيۡنِ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ سَدّٗا وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ سَدّٗا فَأَغۡشَيۡنَٰهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُبۡصِرُونَ﴾, "We have put a barrier before them and a barrier behind them and covered them, so they do not see." Then who benefits from the warning: ﴿إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَخَشِيَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۖ﴾, "You only warn one who follows the reminder and fears the Most Merciful unseen."

Then: ﴿إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَنَكۡتُبُ مَا قَدَّمُواْ وَءَاثَٰرَهُمۡۚ﴾, "We give life to the dead and record what they sent ahead and what they left behind." What you did is written, and so are the traces you leave after you, good or bad.

**Lesson**: leave a good trace that will be written for you after death: knowledge you taught, or a child you raised on the Quran.

### 2. The people of the town and the man of Ya-Sin (13-27)
﴿وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلًا أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ إِذۡ جَآءَهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ﴾, "Set forth for them the example of the people of the town, when the messengers came to it." Two were sent and denied, then strengthened with a third, and the people said: we see an evil omen in you. Then the man came: ﴿وَجَآءَ مِنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ رَجُلࣱ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱتَّبِعُواْ مَن لَّا يَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ أَجۡرٗا وَهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَمَالِيَ لَآ أَعۡبُدُ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنِي وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ﴾, "A man came running from the farthest part of the city and said: my people, follow the messengers; follow those who ask no payment of you and are rightly guided. Why should I not worship the One who created me, and to whom you will be returned?" Then he declared: ﴿إِنِّيٓ ءَامَنتُ بِرَبِّكُمۡ فَٱسۡمَعُونِ﴾, "I have believed in your Lord, so listen to me." They killed him, and the reward was: ﴿قِيلَ ٱدۡخُلِ ٱلۡجَنَّةَۖ قَالَ يَٰلَيۡتَ قَوۡمِي يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿بِمَا غَفَرَ لِي رَبِّي وَجَعَلَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُكۡرَمِينَ﴾, "It was said: enter Paradise. He said: I wish my people knew how my Lord has forgiven me and made me among the honoured."

**Reflect**: he advised his people in life and after death; he held no grudge though they killed him, wishing only that they knew so they would believe.

## Tajweed points in Juz 22

### ﴿يسٓ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ﴾ at the start of Ya-Sin
Read *Ya, Sin* with a six-count necessary madd on Sin. In Hafs via Ash-Shatibiyyah the noon is pronounced clearly (izhar) before the waw, an exception to the general rule of merging.

### ﴿ٱلۡعُلَمَـٰٓؤُاْ﴾ in Fatir 28
The hamzah is written on a waw followed by an unpronounced alif; the preceding vowel takes a connected madd.

### ﴿ٱلسَّيِّيِٕۚ﴾ in Fatir 43
A kasrah hamzah on a seat: pronounce it clearly; one may stop on it with sukun.

### ﴿مِنسَأَتَهُۥ﴾ in Saba' 14
Read with a fathah hamzah: it means his staff.

### ﴿ٱلرَّسُولَا۠﴾ in Al-Ahzab 66
The final alif is written but not read when continuing; it is pronounced when stopping, as with ﴿ٱلسَّبِيلَا۠﴾ in verse 67. For more see [How to Memorize Surah Yasin](/blog/memorize-surah-yasin-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. Surahs opening with praise
Five surahs begin with ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ﴾: Al-Fatihah, Al-An'am, Al-Kahf, Saba' and Fatir. Two of them come consecutively in this juz.

### 2. The running man
- Ya-Sin 20: ﴿وَجَآءَ مِنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ رَجُلࣱ يَسۡعَىٰ﴾, the place first.
- Al-Qasas 20: ﴿وَجَآءَ رَجُلࣱ مِّنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ يَسۡعَىٰ﴾, "man" first.

### 3. The two seas
- Al-Furqan 53: ﴿هَٰذَا عَذۡبࣱ فُرَاتࣱ وَهَٰذَا مِلۡحٌ أُجَاجࣱ﴾.
- Fatir 12: "this one fresh and sweet, pleasant to drink, and that one salty and bitter", adding "pleasant to drink".

### 4. "No bearer of burdens bears another's burden"
It recurs in Al-An'am, Al-Isra', Fatir, Az-Zumar and An-Najm; Fatir alone adds ﴿وَإِن تَدۡعُ مُثۡقَلَةٌ إِلَىٰ حِمۡلِهَا﴾.

## How to memorize Juz 22 with understanding

1. **Learn the ten traits in Al-Ahzab 35** in pairs: Muslim men and women, believing men and women, and so on.
2. **Order the calls in Al-Ahzab**: wives of the Prophet; O believers, remember; O Prophet, We have sent you; O believers, do not enter; O Prophet, tell your wives; O believers, fear Allah.
3. **Saba' has two contrasting stories**: Dawud's family gives thanks; the people of Sheba turn away.
4. **Fatir has three "O mankind" calls** near its start (3, 5, 15): use them as markers.
5. **Learn the start of Ya-Sin well**, as it is recited often.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 22

- **Submit to the command of Allah and His Messenger** without hesitation.
- **Remember Allah often**: it is the deed commanded "much".
- **Send blessings on the Prophet ﷺ** and receive ten from Allah.
- **Speak words of justice**, and Allah will amend your deeds.
- **Fulfil the Trust**: you took it on by choice.
- **Give thanks through deeds**: the grateful are few.
- **Beware turning from blessings**: they can be replaced.
- **Reflect on your own**, away from crowd pressure.
- **Seek mercy from Allah alone.**
- **Seek honour from Allah** through good words and righteous deeds.
- **Remember your need of Allah** at every moment.
- **Be among the foremost in good** as you carry the Quran.
- **Leave a good trace** that is written for you after death.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 22 called "Wa Man Yaqnut"?
Because it begins with ﴿۞وَمَن يَقۡنُتۡ مِنكُنَّ لِلَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ﴾.

### Who is the only Companion named in the Quran?
Zayd ibn Harithah, may Allah be pleased with him, in Al-Ahzab 37.

### What are the categories of the heirs of the Book in Fatir?
One who wrongs himself, the moderate, and the foremost in good deeds.

### Is there a prostration in Juz 22?
No, there is no place of prostration in this juz.

### Where does Juz 22 end?
At Ya-Sin 27. Juz 23 begins with ﴿۞وَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ مِن جُندٖ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ﴾, "And We did not send down upon his people after him any army from the sky."

## Conclusion

Juz 22 teaches you to praise and thank Allah, to remember Him often and send blessings on His Prophet, and to carry the Trust faithfully. In Saba' it shows a family that gave thanks and was given more, and a people who turned away and lost what they had; in Fatir it reminds you that you are in need of Allah and that the carriers of the Quran are chosen heirs; and it ends with a running man who advised his people in life and after death.
