﴿ٱللَّهُ نَزَّلَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡحَدِيثِ كِتَٰبٗا مُّتَشَٰبِهٗا مَّثَانِيَ تَقۡشَعِرُّ مِنۡهُ جُلُودُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُمۡ ثُمَّ تَلِينُ جُلُودُهُمۡ وَقُلُوبُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِي بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡ هَادٍ﴾ [Az-Zumar 39:23]

## The best statement
- The Quran is the best speech in wording, meaning, news and rulings.

## *Mutashabih*
- Its parts resemble one another in beauty, truth and precision: no contradiction.

## *Mathani*
- Stories, warnings and rulings are repeated, and recitation never tires.

## Its effect
| Verses | Effect |
|---|---|
| Of warning | Skins shiver in awe |
| Of mercy | Skins and hearts soften |

## Guidance from Allah
- Benefiting from the Quran is Allah's guidance: ask Him for it.

## Lessons
- **Read verses of mercy and warning** with reflection.
- **If your heart does not move**, tend it with dhikr and duʿa.
