Surah Al-Ma'arij gives one of the most honest descriptions of human nature in the Quran: "Indeed, man was created anxious: when evil touches him, impatient, and when good touches him, withholding." Most of us recognise ourselves in that sentence. We panic in hardship and become tight-fisted in ease. But the surah does not stop there. It immediately names the exception: "Except the worshippers", and lists eight qualities that cure this anxiety. Before that, it describes a Day whose length is fifty thousand years, when a criminal would hand over his own children to escape punishment.

This guide explains Surah Al-Ma'arij section by section in English, with authentic hadith and a practical programme built on the qualities of the worshippers.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Ma'arij
- **Number of verses**: 44.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Position**: Surah 70, in the twenty-ninth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Ma'arij means "the ascending stairways", from verse 3: "from Allah, Owner of the ways of ascent".
- **Main themes**: the reality of the punishment, the length and terror of the Day, the anxious nature of man and the qualities of the worshippers.

## Verses 1–7: A Questioner Asked
﴿سَأَلَ سَآئِلُۢ بِعَذَابٖ وَاقِعٖ﴾ ﴿لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ لَيۡسَ لَهُۥ دَافِعࣱ﴾ ﴿مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ ذِي ٱلۡمَعَارِجِ﴾ ﴿تَعۡرُجُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ إِلَيۡهِ فِي يَوۡمٖ كَانَ مِقۡدَارُهُۥ خَمۡسِينَ أَلۡفَ سَنَةٖ﴾ ﴿فَٱصۡبِرۡ صَبۡرٗا جَمِيلًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُمۡ يَرَوۡنَهُۥ بَعِيدٗا﴾ ﴿وَنَرَىٰهُ قَرِيبٗا﴾ (Quran 70:1-7)

**"A questioner asked for a punishment bound to happen"**: commentators mention that some of the idolaters mockingly demanded the punishment they were warned of. The answer: it is coming, **"to the disbelievers; of it there is no preventer"**.

**"From Allah, Owner of the ways of ascent"**: the angels and the Spirit (Jibril) ascend to Him **"in a Day the extent of which is fifty thousand years"**. Commentators explained this as the Day of Resurrection. In Sahih Muslim, Abu Hurayrah reported that the Prophet ﷺ described the punishment of the one who did not pay the zakah of his gold and silver, and said it would continue "on a Day whose length is fifty thousand years, until judgement is passed between the servants". For the believer, however, that Day will be made light by Allah's mercy.

**"So be patient with a beautiful patience"** (*sabran jamila*): patience without complaint to people, without panic and without bitterness. **"Indeed, they see it as distant, but We see it as near."**

## Verses 8–18: The Terror of That Day
﴿يَوۡمَ تَكُونُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ كَٱلۡمُهۡلِ﴾ ﴿وَتَكُونُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَٱلۡعِهۡنِ﴾ ﴿وَلَا يَسۡـَٔلُ حَمِيمٌ حَمِيمٗا﴾ ﴿يُبَصَّرُونَهُمۡۚ يَوَدُّ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُ لَوۡ يَفۡتَدِي مِنۡ عَذَابِ يَوۡمِئِذِۭ بِبَنِيهِ﴾ ﴿وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَأَخِيهِ﴾ ﴿وَفَصِيلَتِهِ ٱلَّتِي تُـٔۡوِيهِ﴾ ﴿وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا ثُمَّ يُنجِيهِ﴾ (Quran 70:8-14)

**The scenes**: **the sky will be like molten metal**, **the mountains like wool**, puffed and scattered. **"And no friend will ask about a friend"**, though they will see each other. **The criminal will wish he could ransom himself with his children, his wife, his brother, his clan that sheltered him, and everyone on earth**, if only it could save him.

**Notice the order**: he begins with his children, the dearest to him, then his wife, then his brother, then his tribe, then all of humanity. The love that filled his life is now offered up for his own escape.

﴿كَلَّآۖ إِنَّهَا لَظَىٰ﴾ ﴿نَزَّاعَةٗ لِّلشَّوَىٰ﴾ ﴿تَدۡعُواْ مَنۡ أَدۡبَرَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَجَمَعَ فَأَوۡعَىٰٓ﴾ (Quran 70:15-18)

**"No! Indeed, it is Laza"**, a blazing Fire, **"removing the skin of the head"**. **"It invites he who turned his back and went away, and collected wealth and hoarded."** The Fire calls by name the one who turned away from the truth and hoarded money.

## Verses 19–21: Man Created Anxious
﴿۞إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ خُلِقَ هَلُوعًا﴾ ﴿إِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ جَزُوعٗا﴾ ﴿وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلۡخَيۡرُ مَنُوعًا﴾ (Quran 70:19-21)

**"Halu'"**: extremely anxious and restless. Allah then explains it Himself:
- **"When evil touches him, impatient"** (*jazu'a*): he panics, complains and despairs.
- **"When good touches him, withholding"** (*manu'a*): he becomes stingy and refuses to share.

This is the default human condition without faith. Fear of loss controls him in both states: in hardship he fears losing more, and in ease he fears losing what he has.

## Verses 22–35: The Worshippers
﴿إِلَّا ٱلۡمُصَلِّينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ دَآئِمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ فِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ حَقࣱّ مَّعۡلُومࣱ﴾ ﴿لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَٱلۡمَحۡرُومِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ يُصَدِّقُونَ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم مِّنۡ عَذَابِ رَبِّهِم مُّشۡفِقُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ عَذَابَ رَبِّهِمۡ غَيۡرُ مَأۡمُونٖ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِفُرُوجِهِمۡ حَٰفِظُونَ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا عَلَىٰٓ أَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ أَوۡ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُمۡ فَإِنَّهُمۡ غَيۡرُ مَلُومِينَ﴾ ﴿فَمَنِ ٱبۡتَغَىٰ وَرَآءَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡعَادُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِأَمَٰنَٰتِهِمۡ وَعَهۡدِهِمۡ رَٰعُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِشَهَٰدَٰتِهِمۡ قَآئِمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ فِي جَنَّـٰتٖ مُّكۡرَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 70:22-35)

**"Except the worshippers"** (*al-musallin*), those whose prayer has shaped them. **Their eight qualities**:
1. **"Those who are constant in their prayer"**: they never abandon it. In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ said: "The most beloved deeds to Allah are those done regularly, even if small."
2. **"And those within whose wealth is a known right for the one who asks and the deprived"**: a fixed share, zakah and regular charity, for those who ask and for those too proud to ask.
3. **"And those who believe in the Day of Recompense."**
4. **"And those who are fearful of the punishment of their Lord"**, because **"the punishment of their Lord is not that from which one is safe"**.
5. **"And those who guard their private parts"**, except with their spouses.
6. **"And those who are to their trusts and promises attentive."**
7. **"And those who are in their testimonies upright"**: they give true testimony and do not conceal it.
8. **"And those who carefully maintain their prayer"**: its times, its conditions and its focus.

**"They will be in gardens, honoured."**

**Notice the structure**: the list begins with prayer and ends with prayer. Constancy at the start, careful maintenance at the end. Prayer is the frame that holds all the other qualities together. This is why "the worshippers" are exempted from anxiety: the prayer, done properly, cures the panic in hardship and the stinginess in ease.

## Verses 36–44: Why Do They Rush?
﴿فَمَالِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ قِبَلَكَ مُهۡطِعِينَ﴾ ﴿عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ عِزِينَ﴾ ﴿أَيَطۡمَعُ كُلُّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ أَن يُدۡخَلَ جَنَّةَ نَعِيمٖ﴾ ﴿كَلَّآۖ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُم مِّمَّا يَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِرَبِّ ٱلۡمَشَٰرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغَٰرِبِ إِنَّا لَقَٰدِرُونَ﴾ ﴿عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّبَدِّلَ خَيۡرٗا مِّنۡهُمۡ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَسۡبُوقِينَ﴾ (Quran 70:36-41)

The disbelievers came rushing towards the Prophet ﷺ in groups from right and left, mocking. **"Does every person among them aspire to enter a garden of pleasure? No!"** They were **created from what they know**, a humble origin. Then Allah swears **"by the Lord of the points of sunrise and sunset"** that He is able to replace them with better people.

﴿فَذَرۡهُمۡ يَخُوضُواْ وَيَلۡعَبُواْ حَتَّىٰ يُلَٰقُواْ يَوۡمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ﴾ ﴿يَوۡمَ يَخۡرُجُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ سِرَاعٗا كَأَنَّهُمۡ إِلَىٰ نُصُبٖ يُوفِضُونَ﴾ ﴿خَٰشِعَةً أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ تَرۡهَقُهُمۡ ذِلَّةࣱۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمُ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يُوعَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 70:42-44)

**"So leave them to converse vainly and amuse themselves until they meet their Day."** On that Day **"they will emerge from the graves rapidly as if they were, towards an erected idol, hastening"**, rushing just as they once rushed to their idols, **"their eyes humbled, humiliation covering them. That is the Day which they had been promised."**

## Word Meanings
- **Ma'arij**: ways of ascent, stairways.
- **Ar-Ruh**: the Spirit, Jibril.
- **Sabr jamil**: beautiful patience.
- **Al-Muhl**: molten metal, or dregs of oil.
- **Al-'Ihn**: wool.
- **Hamim**: close friend.
- **Fasilah**: clan, close relatives.
- **Laza**: a blazing Fire, a name of Hell.
- **Ash-Shawa**: the skin of the head, or the limbs.
- **Aw'a**: hoarded.
- **Halu'**: anxious, restless.
- **Jazu'**: impatient, panicking.
- **Manu'**: withholding, stingy.
- **Al-Mahrum**: the deprived who does not ask.
- **Al-Ajdath**: the graves.
- **Yufidun**: hastening.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **"They see it as distant, but We see it as near"**: two perspectives in one verse.
2. **The ransom list**: children first, then outward to all mankind, showing how far self-interest will go.
3. **"It invites"**: the Fire personified, calling its people.
4. **"Halu'" explained by two verses**: the Quran defines its own word.
5. **The list framed by prayer**: beginning and ending with it.
6. **Rushing to the graves as they rushed to idols**: punishment mirroring their life.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Iqlab**: in *sa'ilun bi-'adhab* (verse 1), the tanween before the ba' becomes a hidden meem with ghunnah.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *sa'il* (verse 1) and *as-sama'u* (verse 8).
- **Ikhfa'**: in *sabran jamila* (verse 5), the tanween is hidden before the jim.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *tad'u* (verse 17), the dal sakinah bounces lightly.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna al-insana* (verse 19), on the noon with shaddah.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Haqqah before it described the Day and the two records, and Al-Ma'arij continues with its length, its terror and the people who will be honoured in it. Surah Nuh after it shows a model of "beautiful patience": a prophet who called his people for 950 years.

## Curing Anxiety With the Qualities of the Worshippers
The surah offers a complete programme against the anxious nature:
1. **Constant prayer**: five daily prayers that never slip, anchoring your day.
2. **A fixed share for others**: decide a regular amount of charity, so giving does not depend on your mood.
3. **Belief in the Day of Recompense**: it puts every loss and gain in perspective.
4. **Healthy fear of Allah**: it removes fear of everything else.
5. **Chastity**: guarding the heart from scattered desires.
6. **Keeping trusts and promises**: a clear conscience brings calm.
7. **Honest testimony**: no hidden lies weighing on you.
8. **Careful prayer**: on time, with focus.

Each quality treats a cause of restlessness. Together they turn an anxious person into a calm, generous one.

## Beautiful Patience
*Sabr jamil* is patience without complaining to people. Ya'qub ﷺ said it twice in Surah Yusuf, and he also said: "I only complain of my suffering and my grief to Allah." So beautiful patience does not mean hiding pain from Allah; it means taking it to Him, not spreading it before people, and not losing hope.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Recognise the anxious nature in yourself**, in both hardship and ease.
2. **Be constant in prayer**, even when life is busy.
3. **Set a known share of your wealth for the poor.**
4. **Guard trusts and keep promises.**
5. **Give honest testimony**, even against yourself.
6. **Be patient with a beautiful patience.**
7. **Remember the Day of fifty thousand years.**

## How to Memorise Surah Al-Ma'arij

## Teaching Children
- **Make a chart of the eight qualities** and tick off one each day.
- **Teach them beautiful patience**: not crying and complaining to everyone, but making du'a.
- **Let them decide a small "known share"** of their pocket money for charity.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does al-Ma'arij mean?
The ways of ascent or stairways by which the angels ascend to Allah.

### What is the Day of fifty thousand years?
The Day of Resurrection, whose length is fifty thousand years, as also mentioned in the hadith of Abu Hurayrah in Sahih Muslim.

### What does "man was created anxious" mean?
Man by nature is restless: he panics in hardship and becomes stingy in ease.

### Who are the worshippers exempted in this surah?
Those who are constant in prayer, give a known share of their wealth, believe in the Day of Recompense, fear Allah's punishment, guard their chastity, keep trusts and promises, give honest testimony and carefully maintain their prayer.

### What is "beautiful patience"?
Patience without complaining to people, while turning to Allah alone.

### What is the difference between "the one who asks" and "the deprived"?
The one who asks requests help openly, while the deprived is needy but does not ask.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Ma'arij?
44 verses, and it is a Meccan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Ma'arij holds up a mirror: we are anxious by nature, panicking in hardship and holding tight in ease. Then it shows the way out: become one of "the worshippers", whose constant, careful prayer reshapes the whole life around it. Be patient with a beautiful patience, for the Day that seems far is near.
