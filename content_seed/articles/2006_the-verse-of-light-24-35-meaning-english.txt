﴿۞ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِۦ كَمِشۡكَوٰةٖ فِيهَا مِصۡبَاحٌۖ ٱلۡمِصۡبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍۖ ٱلزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوۡكَبࣱ دُرِّيࣱّ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٖ مُّبَٰرَكَةٖ زَيۡتُونَةٖ لَّا شَرۡقِيَّةٖ وَلَا غَرۡبِيَّةٖ يَكَادُ زَيۡتُهَا يُضِيٓءُ وَلَوۡ لَمۡ تَمۡسَسۡهُ نَارࣱۚ نُّورٌ عَلَىٰ نُورٖۚ يَهۡدِي ٱللَّهُ لِنُورِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَيَضۡرِبُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡأَمۡثَٰلَ لِلنَّاسِۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ [An-Nur 24:35]

## "The Light of the heavens and the earth"
- Ibn ʿAbbas explained: the **Guide** of those in the heavens and earth. Light is also among Allah's names and attributes, affirmed as befits His majesty.

## The parable in detail
| Element | Explained by many scholars as |
|---|---|
| The niche | The believer's chest |
| The lamp | The light of faith and the Quran |
| The glass | The pure heart of the believer |
| The blessed tree | Blessed revelation |
| Neither east nor west | Sun all day, so its oil is purest |
| Its oil almost glows | Sound fitrah nearly recognises truth on its own |

## "Light upon light"
- The light of the **fitrah** joined with the light of **revelation**.

## Guidance is a gift
- *Allah guides to His light whom He wills*: ask Him for it.

## Lessons
- **Keep your heart clear** like the glass.
- **Pray**: *O Allah, place light in my heart* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).
