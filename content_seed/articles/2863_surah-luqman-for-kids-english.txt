"Ya bunayya", "O my dear son". These are gentle, loving words a father says to his child. Luqman the Wise began every piece of advice to his son this way: "O my son, do not associate anything with Allah", "O my son, establish prayer", "O my son, even if it is the weight of a mustard seed". He did not shout or give harsh orders. He advised with love and gentleness. That is why his advice became one of the most famous pieces of guidance in the Quran, passed from parents to children for generations.

Surah Luqman is a complete school of good character: belief in one God, kindness to parents, awareness that Allah sees everything, prayer, patience, humility, and manners in walking and speaking. It also shows Allah's greatness: the sky raised without pillars we can see, and Allah's words that would never run out even if every tree on earth became a pen. In this guide, we explain Surah Luqman to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Luqman

- **Position**: surah 31, in juz 21.
- **Number of verses**: 34.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Who was Luqman?** A wise and righteous man to whom Allah gave wisdom. Most scholars hold that he was not a prophet but a righteous servant, and the surah is named after him to honour his wisdom.

## Lesson 1: Wisdom Begins With Gratitude

﴿وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا لُقۡمَٰنَ ٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ أَنِ ٱشۡكُرۡ لِلَّهِۚ وَمَن يَشۡكُرۡ فَإِنَّمَا يَشۡكُرُ لِنَفۡسِهِۦۖ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِيٌّ حَمِيدࣱ﴾ (Quran 31:12)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah gave Luqman hikmah, wisdom. Wisdom means understanding things well and putting everything in its right place. And the first lesson of wisdom is: be grateful to Allah. Whoever is grateful benefits themselves, because Allah does not need our thanks, but He loves those who are grateful and gives them more."

### Activity: the gratitude jar
Place a jar at home. Every day, your child writes one blessing on a small paper and drops it in. At the end of the month, open the jar and read all the blessings together.

## Lesson 2: The First Advice: Do Not Associate Partners With Allah

﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ لُقۡمَٰنُ لِٱبۡنِهِۦ وَهُوَ يَعِظُهُۥ يَٰبُنَيَّ لَا تُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّ ٱلشِّرۡكَ لَظُلۡمٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 31:13)

### Explaining it to your child
"The first thing Luqman told his son: do not worship anyone along with Allah. Allah alone created you and provides for you, so you worship Him alone. Shirk is a terrible wrong, because it gives Allah's right to someone else."

### Notice Luqman's style
- **He started with the most important thing**: tawhid.
- **He said "O my son"**: words of love and tenderness.
- **He gave the reason**: he didn't just command, he explained why.

## Lesson 3: Kindness to Parents

﴿وَوَصَّيۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ حَمَلَتۡهُ أُمُّهُۥ وَهۡنًا عَلَىٰ وَهۡنٖ وَفِصَٰلُهُۥ فِي عَامَيۡنِ أَنِ ٱشۡكُرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيۡكَ إِلَيَّ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ﴾ (Quran 31:14)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah told us to be good to our parents, and He mentioned the mother specially: your mother carried you for nine months, getting more and more tired, then she nursed you for two years. So be grateful to Allah, and be grateful to your parents. Look how Allah put thanking Him and thanking your parents in the same sentence!"

### Activity: baby photos
Show your child photos of when they were a baby and tell them how their mother stayed up at night with them, fed them and carried them. Then ask them to make a thank-you card or drawing for their mother.

## Lesson 4: The Mustard Seed

﴿يَٰبُنَيَّ إِنَّهَآ إِن تَكُ مِثۡقَالَ حَبَّةٖ مِّنۡ خَرۡدَلٖ فَتَكُن فِي صَخۡرَةٍ أَوۡ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ أَوۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَأۡتِ بِهَا ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَطِيفٌ خَبِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 31:16)

### Explaining it to your child
"Luqman said: O my son, even if a deed is as tiny as a mustard seed, hidden inside a rock, or in the heavens, or deep in the earth, Allah will bring it out. Allah is Al-Latif, who knows the tiniest hidden things, and Al-Khabir, who is aware of everything."

### Activity: the hidden seed
Hide one small lentil somewhere in a room and ask your child to find it. They'll find it hard! Then say: "We can't find one seed in one room, yet Allah knows every seed on the whole earth, and every deed we do, however small."

### Lesson for kids: Allah sees you
"Allah sees you all the time: in your room alone, at school and in the street. If you do something good and nobody notices, Allah saw it. And if you are about to do something wrong, remember that Allah sees you."

## Lesson 5: Prayer, Good and Patience

﴿يَٰبُنَيَّ أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأۡمُرۡ بِٱلۡمَعۡرُوفِ وَٱنۡهَ عَنِ ٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَآ أَصَابَكَۖ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنۡ عَزۡمِ ٱلۡأُمُورِ﴾ (Quran 31:17)

### Explaining it to your child
"Luqman said: O my son, establish prayer on time, encourage people to do good, gently stop them from doing wrong, and be patient with whatever happens to you. These are important matters that need determination."

### Notice the beautiful order
"First: fix yourself with prayer. Second: invite others to good. Third: be patient, because people who invite to good sometimes hear things they don't like."

### Activity: the advice board
Make a board with a box for each of Luqman's pieces of advice, with a little drawing. Each day your child picks one piece of advice to practise and puts a star on it.

## Lesson 6: Humility

﴿وَلَا تُصَعِّرۡ خَدَّكَ لِلنَّاسِ وَلَا تَمۡشِ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مَرَحًاۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخۡتَالٖ فَخُورٖ﴾ (Quran 31:18)

### Explaining it to your child
"Luqman said: O my son, don't turn your face away from people out of pride, as if you're too important to look at them when they speak. And don't walk around showing off. Allah does not love anyone arrogant and boastful."

### Activity: the smiling face
Act out two situations: a friend speaks and the other turns his face away, and a friend speaks and the other looks at him and smiles. Ask your child: "Which one do people love? Which one does Allah love?"

## Lesson 7: How to Walk and Talk

﴿وَٱقۡصِدۡ فِي مَشۡيِكَ وَٱغۡضُضۡ مِن صَوۡتِكَۚ إِنَّ أَنكَرَ ٱلۡأَصۡوَٰتِ لَصَوۡتُ ٱلۡحَمِيرِ﴾ (Quran 31:19)

### Explaining it to your child
"Luqman said: O my son, walk at a moderate pace, not rushing wildly and not dragging lazily, and lower your voice when you talk. The ugliest of sounds is the braying of a donkey, because it is loud and annoying for no reason."

### Activity: the voice meter
Draw a meter from 1 to 5: 1 is a whisper, 2 is a calm voice, 3 is normal, 4 is loud, and 5 is shouting. Agree with your child to speak at level 2 or 3 at home. If their voice rises, smile and point to the meter.

## Lesson 8: The Sky Without Pillars

﴿خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ بِغَيۡرِ عَمَدٖ تَرَوۡنَهَاۖ وَأَلۡقَىٰ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَوَٰسِيَ أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمۡ وَبَثَّ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ دَآبَّةٖۚ وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجٖ كَرِيمٍ﴾ ﴿هَٰذَا خَلۡقُ ٱللَّهِ فَأَرُونِي مَاذَا خَلَقَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن دُونِهِۦۚ بَلِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ﴾ (Quran 31:10-11)

### Explaining it to your child
"Look at the sky: it has no pillars holding it up, yet it is raised above us! Allah placed mountains on the earth to keep it steady, spread every kind of animal across it, and sent rain to grow beautiful plants. This is Allah's creation, so show me what anyone else has created!"

### Activity: the tent and the sky
Build a small tent at home with a blanket and chairs, then remove the chairs. The blanket falls! Then say: "A tent needs poles, but the huge sky has no pillars we can see. Allah holds it up."

## Lesson 9: The Words of Allah Never Run Out

﴿وَلَوۡ أَنَّمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِن شَجَرَةٍ أَقۡلَٰمࣱ وَٱلۡبَحۡرُ يَمُدُّهُۥ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ سَبۡعَةُ أَبۡحُرٖ مَّا نَفِدَتۡ كَلِمَٰتُ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 31:27)

### Explaining it to your child
"If every tree on earth became a pen, and the whole ocean became ink, with seven more oceans added to it, and we used them to write Allah's words, the pens and ink would run out, but Allah's words would not!"

### Activity: how many trees?
Ask your child: "How many pencils can be made from one tree? How many trees are there in the world?" Then say: "All those pencils still would not be enough to write the words of Allah!"

## Let's Read Together: The Keys of the Unseen

﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلسَّاعَةِ وَيُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡغَيۡثَ وَيَعۡلَمُ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِۖ وَمَا تَدۡرِي نَفۡسࣱ مَّاذَا تَكۡسِبُ غَدٗاۖ وَمَا تَدۡرِي نَفۡسُۢ بِأَيِّ أَرۡضٖ تَمُوتُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرُۢ﴾ (Quran 31:34)

### Explaining it to your child
"There are five things only Allah knows: when the Hour will come, exactly when rain will fall, every detail of what is in the womb, what we will do tomorrow, and where each person will die. So we make plans and work hard, and we say: In sha' Allah."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "Dad's Letters"

Ziyad's father travelled often for work, and Ziyad always missed him. On one trip, Ziyad found an envelope under his pillow with seven folded notes inside. On the envelope was written: "One note for each day. From your loving Dad."

He opened the first note: "O my son, Allah always sees you, so worship Him alone." On day two: "O my son, your mother works so hard for you. Help her today." So Ziyad tidied the kitchen with his mum. On day three: "O my son, pray on time." So he prayed Asr as soon as he heard the adhan.

On day four: "O my son, don't be proud with your friends, and smile at them." Day five: "O my son, lower your voice. The best words are the calmest." Day six: "O my son, if you do even a seed's weight of good, Allah knows it." Day seven: "O my son, I'm coming home today, and I want you to tell me what you did with the notes!"

When his father returned, Ziyad ran to him and told him everything. His father said: "Do you know where I got these notes from? From Luqman's advice to his son in the Quran!" They opened the mushaf together and read Surah Luqman, and Ziyad was delighted to recognise every piece of advice.

Ask your child: "Which note did you like best? If you wrote a note to your younger sibling, what would it say?"

## What We Learn From Surah Luqman

- **Wisdom** starts with thanking Allah.
- **Tawhid** is the first and most important advice.
- **Honour your parents**, especially your mother.
- **Allah knows everything**, even a mustard seed.
- **Prayer, good and patience** go together.
- **Be humble** and don't show off.
- **Walk moderately** and lower your voice.
- **Allah's greatness** is seen in the sky and in His words.
- **Advise with love**: "O my son".

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### Was Luqman a prophet?
Most scholars hold that he was a righteous, wise man, not a prophet. Allah knows best.

### How can I advise my child like Luqman?
Start with a loving word like "my dear", give the reason, choose a calm moment, and don't pile up many instructions at once.

### My child is loud by nature. What should I do?
Don't punish them. Train gradually: agree on a gentle signal when their voice rises, praise them whenever they speak calmly, and model a calm voice at home. The verse teaches manners; it is not meant to embarrass them in front of others.

### What age suits Luqman's advice?
From about four, children can learn "O my son" and the advice through stories; from about seven, they can memorize the verses and understand their meanings.
