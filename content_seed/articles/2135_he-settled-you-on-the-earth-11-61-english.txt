﴿۞وَإِلَىٰ ثَمُودَ أَخَاهُمۡ صَٰلِحٗاۚ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥۖ هُوَ أَنشَأَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَٱسۡتَعۡمَرَكُمۡ فِيهَا فَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُوهُ ثُمَّ تُوبُوٓاْ إِلَيۡهِۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي قَرِيبࣱ مُّجِيبࣱ﴾ [Hud 11:61]

## From the earth
- Humanity's origin is from the earth: Adam from dust, and food from its plants.

## *Istaʿmarakum*
- He made you its builders: you build, farm and live in it. Building the earth is a human mission, within obedience to Allah.

## Forgiveness then repentance
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Istighfar* | Asking that sins be erased |
| *Tawbah* | Turning back and leaving sin |

## "Near and Responsive"
- Near by His knowledge and mercy, responsive to those who call.

## Lessons
- **Build your world** with useful work and taqwa.
- **Allah is near**: do not hesitate to repent.
