**Surah Al-Qari'ah** (11 ayat) is a short warning about the Day the scales decide everything.

﴿ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ﴾ ﴿مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ﴾
- **Al-Qāriʿah**: the one that **strikes** hearts with terror, one of the names of the Day of Resurrection.

## Two images
﴿يَوۡمَ يَكُونُ ٱلنَّاسُ كَٱلۡفَرَاشِ ٱلۡمَبۡثُوثِ﴾ ﴿وَتَكُونُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَٱلۡعِهۡنِ ٱلۡمَنفُوشِ﴾
| Image | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Scattered moths | Confused, rushing in every direction |
| Fluffed wool | Mountains losing their solidity |

## The scales
﴿فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ﴾ ﴿فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةٖ رَّاضِيَةٖ﴾
﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَفَّتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ﴾ ﴿فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةࣱ﴾ ﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا هِيَهۡ﴾ ﴿نَارٌ حَامِيَةُۢ﴾
- **"His mother is Hawiyah"**: the abyss will embrace him as a mother holds her child, a powerful image of no escape.

## What makes the scale heavy?
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «Nothing is heavier in the scale than good character» [Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi].
- And: «Two words light on the tongue, heavy in the scale: *Subḥānallāhi wa biḥamdih, Subḥānallāhil-ʿAẓīm*» [Al-Bukhari and Muslim].
