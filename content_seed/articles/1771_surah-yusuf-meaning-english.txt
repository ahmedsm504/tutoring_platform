**Surah Yusuf** (111 ayat) tells one complete story from beginning to end.

## The best of stories
﴿نَحۡنُ نَقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡقَصَصِ بِمَآ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ هَٰذَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ وَإِن كُنتَ مِن قَبۡلِهِۦ لَمِنَ ٱلۡغَٰفِلِينَ﴾

## The story in scenes
| Scene | Event |
|---|---|
| The dream | Eleven stars, the sun and the moon prostrating |
| The well | The brothers' envy and their lie to their father |
| The house of al-ʿAziz | Temptation refused: *I seek refuge in Allah* |
| Prison | Inviting fellow prisoners to tawhid and interpreting dreams |
| The king's dream | Seven fat cows and seven lean ones, and a plan for the famine |
| The brothers' return | Recognising them, keeping his brother, and the shirt |
| Reunion | The family prostrates, and the first dream comes true |

## Yaqub's hope
﴿يَٰبَنِيَّ ٱذۡهَبُواْ فَتَحَسَّسُواْ مِن يُوسُفَ وَأَخِيهِ وَلَا تَاْيۡـَٔسُواْ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يَاْيۡـَٔسُ مِن رَّوۡحِ ٱللَّهِ إِلَّا ٱلۡقَوۡمُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ﴾
- He described his response to grief as *beautiful patience*, and said: *I only complain of my grief and sorrow to Allah.*

## Forgiveness
- Yusuf told his brothers: *No blame upon you today. Allah will forgive you.*

## Lessons
- **Hard chapters are part of a bigger plan.**
- **Chastity and integrity** are worth prison.
- **Forgive when you have power.**
