Juz 30 begins with ﴿عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾ ﴿عَنِ ٱلنَّبَإِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾, "About what are they asking one another? About the great news", so it is called **Juz 'Amma**. It is the last juz of the Quran and contains **thirty-seven surahs**, from **An-Naba'** to **An-Nas**, more than any other juz. In the Madinah Mushaf it fills **pages 582 to 604**.

Juz 'Amma is **the first part most Muslims memorize** and the most often recited in prayer. Its surahs are short, with harmonious endings, and most are Makki, building belief: faith in Allah, the Last Day, the message, and purifying the soul. This guide reads the juz thematically by grouping its surahs.

## The big idea

Juz 'Amma revolves around **certainty of the Last Day and purifying the soul in preparation for it**. It opens with a question about "the great news", strikes the heart with scenes of the Resurrection in An-Naba', An-Nazi'at, At-Takwir, Al-Infitar and Al-Inshiqaq, then ties success to purifying the soul: ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ﴾, "Successful is he who purifies himself", and ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا﴾, "Successful is he who purifies it." It reassures the Prophet ﷺ and the believers in Ad-Duha and Ash-Sharh, and closes with the foundations of belief in Al-Ikhlas and the two Mu'awwidhat.

The message: **the Resurrection is great news beyond doubt, salvation lies in purifying the soul, and every atom of deeds will be seen.**

## Group one: scenes of the Resurrection (An-Naba' to Al-Inshiqaq)

### 1. An-Naba' (40 verses)
﴿عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾ ﴿عَنِ ٱلنَّبَإِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾. Then nine favours: the earth as a resting place, mountains as pegs, creation in pairs, sleep as rest, night as a covering, day for livelihood, seven strong heavens, a blazing lamp, and rain from the clouds. Then: the Day of Decision is an appointed time. The close: ﴿إِنَّآ أَنذَرۡنَٰكُمۡ عَذَابٗا قَرِيبٗا يَوۡمَ يَنظُرُ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مَا قَدَّمَتۡ يَدَاهُ وَيَقُولُ ٱلۡكَافِرُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَٰبَۢا﴾, "We have warned you of a near punishment, the Day a man will see what his hands have sent ahead, and the disbeliever will say: I wish I were dust." Read [Surah An-Naba Meaning in English](/blog/surah-an-naba-meaning-english/).

### 2. An-Nazi'at (46 verses)
An oath by the angels who extract souls, then the quaking blast and its follower, then the story of Musa and Pharaoh, who said "I am your lord, most high", then the Great Overwhelming Event. Then the decisive division: ﴿وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ وَنَهَى ٱلنَّفۡسَ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ﴾, "As for one who feared standing before his Lord and restrained the soul from desire, Paradise will be his refuge."

### 3. 'Abasa (42 verses)
﴿عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ﴾ ﴿أَن جَآءَهُ ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ﴾, "He frowned and turned away because the blind man came to him": 'Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum, who came seeking knowledge while the Prophet ﷺ was busy inviting the leaders of Quraysh. Then the wonders of man's food, then the Deafening Blast: ﴿يَوۡمَ يَفِرُّ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مِنۡ أَخِيهِ﴾ ﴿وَأُمِّهِۦ وَأَبِيهِ﴾ ﴿وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَبَنِيهِ﴾ ﴿لِكُلِّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ شَأۡنࣱ يُغۡنِيهِ﴾, "the Day a man will flee from his brother, his mother and father, his wife and children; every one of them that Day will have enough to occupy him."

**Lesson**: a person's worth lies in turning towards the truth, not in social standing. Read [Surah Abasa Meaning in English](/blog/surah-abasa-meaning-english/).

### 4. At-Takwir (29 verses)
﴿إِذَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ كُوِّرَتۡ﴾, "When the sun is wrapped up", then twelve cosmic scenes, including ﴿وَإِذَا ٱلۡمَوۡءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتۡ﴾ ﴿بِأَيِّ ذَنۢبٖ قُتِلَتۡ﴾, "and when the girl buried alive is asked for what sin she was killed." Then every soul will know what it has brought. Then the oath: it is the word of a noble messenger. Read [Surah At-Takwir Meaning in English](/blog/surah-at-takwir-meaning-english/).

**Reflect**: the buried girl is asked, not her killer, as a rebuke to him; Allah takes the side of the weak who were wronged.

### 5. Al-Infitar (19 verses)
When the sky breaks apart and the stars scatter. Then the gentle reproach: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ﴾, "O man, what has deceived you about your Lord, the Most Generous?" Then the noble recorders, then: the righteous will be in bliss and the wicked in Hellfire.

### 6. Al-Mutaffifin (36 verses)
﴿وَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُطَفِّفِينَ﴾, "Woe to those who give short measure", who take full measure from people but give less when measuring or weighing for them. Then the cause of hardened hearts: ﴿كَلَّاۖ بَلۡۜ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ﴾, "No! Rather, what they used to earn has covered their hearts with rust." Then the righteous in 'Illiyyin: ﴿خِتَٰمُهُۥ مِسۡكࣱۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَتَنَافَسِ ٱلۡمُتَنَٰفِسُونَ﴾, "its seal is musk; so for this let the competitors compete."

**Lesson**: short-changing covers every right that is withheld, and sins pile up until they rust the heart. Read [Surah Al-Mutaffifin Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-mutaffifin-meaning-english/).

### 7. Al-Inshiqaq (25 verses)
When the sky splits open, obeying its Lord as it must. Then: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدۡحٗا فَمُلَٰقِيهِ﴾, "O man, you are toiling towards your Lord with great effort, and you will meet Him." Then the record in the right hand and behind the back. Then: ﴿وَإِذَا قُرِئَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ لَا يَسۡجُدُونَۤ﴾, "and when the Quran is recited to them, they do not prostrate": the first prostration of the juz. In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, Abu Hurayrah prostrated here and said: had I not seen the Prophet ﷺ prostrate, I would not have prostrated.

## Group two: faith and trial (Al-Buruj to Al-Fajr)

### 8. Al-Buruj (22 verses)
The story of the people of the trench, who burned the believers in their pit: ﴿وَمَا نَقَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ﴾, "and they resented them only because they believed in Allah, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy." Then: ﴿إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ يُبۡدِئُ وَيُعِيدُ﴾ ﴿وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلۡوَدُودُ﴾, "He begins creation and repeats it, and He is the Forgiving, the Loving."

### 9. At-Tariq (17 verses)
By the sky and the night-comer, the piercing star, then: ﴿إِن كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ لَّمَّا عَلَيۡهَا حَافِظࣱ﴾, "there is no soul but has a guardian over it." Then man created from a gushing fluid, and the Day secrets are put on trial.

### 10. Al-A'la (19 verses)
Hizb 60 begins here: ﴿سَبِّحِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡأَعۡلَى﴾, "Glorify the name of your Lord, the Most High", who created and proportioned, and who decreed and guided. Then the promise of preservation: We will make you recite, and you will not forget. Then: ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ﴾ ﴿وَذَكَرَ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّهِۦ فَصَلَّىٰ﴾ ﴿بَلۡ تُؤۡثِرُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓ﴾, "Successful is he who purifies himself, remembers the name of his Lord and prays. But you prefer worldly life, while the Hereafter is better and more lasting." In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ recited it in the two 'Eids and Friday along with Al-Ghashiyah. Read [Surah Al-Ala Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-ala-meaning-english/).

**Good news for the memorizer**: "We will make you recite, and you will not forget" was a promise to the Prophet ﷺ, and the carrier of the Quran shares in its blessing when he revises and is sincere.

### 11. Al-Ghashiyah (26 verses)
Has the story of the Overwhelming Event reached you? Some faces that Day will be humbled, others full of joy. Then the call to reflect: ﴿أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡإِبِلِ كَيۡفَ خُلِقَتۡ﴾, "Do they not look at the camels, how they were created?", and the sky, how it was raised, the mountains, how they were set, and the earth, how it was spread.

### 12. Al-Fajr (30 verses)
An oath by the dawn and ten nights, then 'Ad, Thamud and Pharaoh. Then man's false measure: ﴿فَأَمَّا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِذَا مَا ٱبۡتَلَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَأَكۡرَمَهُۥ وَنَعَّمَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّيٓ أَكۡرَمَنِ﴾, "As for man, when his Lord tests him by honouring and favouring him, he says: my Lord has honoured me", and when He restricts his provision, he says: my Lord has humiliated me. No! The beautiful close: ﴿يَـٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفۡسُ ٱلۡمُطۡمَئِنَّةُ﴾ ﴿ٱرۡجِعِيٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةٗ مَّرۡضِيَّةٗ﴾ ﴿فَٱدۡخُلِي فِي عِبَٰدِي﴾ ﴿وَٱدۡخُلِي جَنَّتِي﴾, "O soul at peace, return to your Lord, well-pleased and pleasing; enter among My servants, and enter My Paradise." Read [Surah Al-Fajr Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-fajr-meaning-english/).

**Lesson**: abundance and scarcity are both tests, not signs of honour or humiliation.

## Group three: the soul and its two paths (Al-Balad, Ash-Shams, Al-Layl)

### 13. Al-Balad (20 verses)
We created man in hardship. Then: ﴿وَهَدَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلنَّجۡدَيۡنِ﴾, "and We showed him the two ways", then the steep path: freeing a slave, or feeding on a day of hunger an orphan relative or a poor person in misery.

### 14. Ash-Shams (15 verses)
Eleven consecutive oaths, the longest in the Quran, answered by: ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا﴾ ﴿وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا﴾, "Successful is he who purifies it, and ruined is he who corrupts it." Then Thamud and the she-camel. Read [Surah Ash-Shams Meaning in English](/blog/surah-ash-shams-meaning-english/).

### 15. Al-Layl (21 verses)
Your efforts are diverse. Then the two paths: ﴿فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أَعۡطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ﴾ ﴿فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ﴾, "As for one who gives, fears Allah and affirms the best reward, We will ease him towards ease", while one who is stingy and self-sufficient will be eased towards hardship. Then the most God-conscious, who gives his wealth to purify himself.

**The link**: Ash-Shams, Al-Layl and Al-Balad all speak of two paths: purification or corruption, giving or stinginess, climbing the steep path or avoiding it.

## Group four: Allah's favours on His Prophet (Ad-Duha to Al-Qadr)

### 16. Ad-Duha (11 verses)
﴿مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ﴾ ﴿وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ﴾, "Your Lord has not abandoned you, nor is He displeased. And your Lord will give you, and you will be satisfied." Then three favours recalled: orphanhood and shelter, being lost and guided, poverty and enrichment; and three matching commands: do not oppress the orphan, do not repel the one who asks, ﴿وَأَمَّا بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثۡ﴾, "and proclaim the favour of your Lord." Read [Surah Ad-Duha Meaning in English](/blog/surah-ad-duha-meaning-english/).

### 17. Ash-Sharh (8 verses)
﴿أَلَمۡ نَشۡرَحۡ لَكَ صَدۡرَكَ﴾, "Did We not expand your chest for you?", relieve you of your burden, and raise your mention? Then the repeated rule: ﴿فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرًا﴾ ﴿إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرٗا﴾, "With hardship comes ease. Indeed, with hardship comes ease."

**Reflect**: "the hardship" is definite, so it is one; "ease" is indefinite, so it is many. One hardship will not overcome two eases. Read [Surah Ash-Sharh Meaning in English](/blog/surah-ash-sharh-meaning-english/).

### 18. At-Tin (8 verses)
An oath by the fig and the olive, Mount Sinai and this secure city, then: ﴿لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِيٓ أَحۡسَنِ تَقۡوِيمٖ﴾, "We created man in the best form", then reduced him to the lowest of the low, except those who believe.

### 19. Al-'Alaq (19 verses)
The first revelation, as in Sahih Al-Bukhari: ﴿ٱقۡرَأۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ﴾ ﴿خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِنۡ عَلَقٍ﴾ ﴿ٱقۡرَأۡ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلۡأَكۡرَمُ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِٱلۡقَلَمِ﴾ ﴿عَلَّمَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمۡ يَعۡلَمۡ﴾, "Read in the name of your Lord who created, created man from a clinging clot. Read, and your Lord is the Most Generous, who taught by the pen, taught man what he did not know." Then transgression through self-sufficiency, and the man who forbade a servant from praying. The close and the second prostration: ﴿كَلَّا لَا تُطِعۡهُ وَٱسۡجُدۡۤ وَٱقۡتَرِب﴾, "No! Do not obey him; prostrate and draw near." Read [Surah Al-Alaq Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-alaq-meaning-english/).

**Lesson**: the first word revealed was "Read" and the last word of the surah is "draw near": reading is the path to nearness to Allah.

### 20. Al-Qadr (5 verses)
We sent it down on the Night of Decree, ﴿لَيۡلَةُ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡ أَلۡفِ شَهۡرٖ﴾, "The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months"; the angels and the Spirit descend in it; peace it is until the rise of dawn. Read [Surah Al-Qadr Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-qadr-meaning-english/).

## Group five: deeds and reckoning (Al-Bayyinah to At-Takathur)

### 21. Al-Bayyinah (8 verses)
﴿وَمَآ أُمِرُوٓاْ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ حُنَفَآءَ وَيُقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤۡتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَۚ﴾, "They were commanded only to worship Allah, sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth, to establish prayer and give zakah." Then the best and worst of creatures. In Al-Bukhari and Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ told Ubayy ibn Ka'b: "Allah has commanded me to recite to you 'Lam yakun alladhina kafaru'", and Ubayy wept.

### 22. Az-Zalzalah (8 verses)
When the earth is shaken with its quake, brings out its burdens and reports its news. Then the precise balance: ﴿فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيۡرٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾ ﴿وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖ شَرّٗا يَرَهُۥ﴾, "Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it." Read [Surah Az-Zalzala Meaning in English](/blog/surah-az-zalzala-meaning-english/).

### 23. Al-'Adiyat (11 verses)
An oath by the charging horses, then: ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودࣱ﴾, "Man is ungrateful to his Lord", and intense in his love of wealth. A new quarter begins at verse 9: does he not know, when what is in the graves is scattered?

### 24. Al-Qari'ah (11 verses)
The Day people will be like scattered moths and mountains like carded wool. Then whoever's scales are heavy will be in a pleasant life, and whoever's scales are light, ﴿فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةࣱ﴾, "his refuge will be an abyss."

### 25. At-Takathur (8 verses)
﴿أَلۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ﴾, "Competition in increase diverted you" until you visited the graves; then: ﴿ثُمَّ لَتُسۡـَٔلُنَّ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ﴾, "then you will surely be asked that Day about pleasure." Read [Surah At-Takathur Meaning in English](/blog/surah-at-takathur-meaning-english/).

## Group six: short comprehensive surahs (Al-'Asr to An-Nas)

### 26. Al-'Asr (3 verses)
﴿وَٱلۡعَصۡرِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَفِي خُسۡرٍ﴾ ﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ﴾, "By time, man is in loss, except those who believe, do good, and urge one another to truth and to patience." Four conditions for escaping loss: faith, deeds, urging truth and urging patience. Read [Surah Al-Asr Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-asr-meaning-english/).

### 27-30. Al-Humazah, Al-Fil, Quraysh and Al-Ma'un
- **Al-Humazah**: ﴿وَيۡلࣱ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٖ لُّمَزَةٍ﴾, "Woe to every scorner and mocker", who gathers wealth and counts it, thinking it will make him immortal.
- **Al-Fil**: ﴿أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡفِيلِ﴾, "Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the companions of the elephant?", then the birds in flocks.
- **Quraysh**: ﴿فَلۡيَعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَيۡتِ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٖ وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنۡ خَوۡفِۭ﴾, "Let them worship the Lord of this House, who fed them against hunger and secured them against fear."
- **Al-Ma'un**: the one who repels the orphan and does not encourage feeding the poor; then ﴿فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُصَلِّينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَن صَلَاتِهِمۡ سَاهُونَ﴾, "woe to those who pray, who are heedless of their prayer."

Read [Surah Al-Fil Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-fil-meaning-english/), [Surah Quraysh Meaning in English](/blog/surah-quraysh-meaning-english/) and [Surah Al-Maun Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-maun-meaning-english/).

### 31-34. Al-Kawthar, Al-Kafirun, An-Nasr and Al-Masad
- **Al-Kawthar**: ﴿إِنَّآ أَعۡطَيۡنَٰكَ ٱلۡكَوۡثَرَ﴾, "We have given you Al-Kawthar", the shortest surah in the Quran. In Sahih Muslim, Al-Kawthar is a river Allah promised His Prophet ﷺ in Paradise.
- **Al-Kafirun**: complete disavowal of shirk: ﴿لَكُمۡ دِينُكُمۡ وَلِيَ دِينِ﴾, "For you is your religion, and for me is mine."
- **An-Nasr**: ﴿فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ تَوَّابَۢا﴾, "Glorify your Lord with praise and seek His forgiveness; He is ever accepting of repentance." In Sahih Al-Bukhari, Ibn 'Abbas understood it as a sign that the Prophet's death ﷺ was near, and 'Umar agreed.
- **Al-Masad**: perish the hands of Abu Lahab: a prophecy that he would die in disbelief, which came true.

Read [Surah Al-Kawthar Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-kawthar-meaning-english/) and [Surah An-Nasr Meaning in English](/blog/surah-an-nasr-meaning-english/).

### 35. Al-Ikhlas (4 verses)
﴿قُلۡ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ﴾ ﴿ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ﴾ ﴿لَمۡ يَلِدۡ وَلَمۡ يُولَدۡ﴾ ﴿وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدُۢ﴾, "Say: He is Allah, One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, and there is none comparable to Him." In Sahih Al-Bukhari the Prophet ﷺ said of it: "By the One in whose hand is my soul, it equals a third of the Quran." Read [Surah Al-Ikhlas Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-ikhlas-meaning-english/).

### 36-37. The two Mu'awwidhat
- **Al-Falaq**: ﴿قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلۡفَلَقِ﴾, "Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak", from the evil of what He created, of darkness when it settles, of those who blow on knots, and of an envier when he envies.
- **An-Nas**: ﴿قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ﴾, "Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind", the King of mankind, the God of mankind, from the evil of the retreating whisperer.

**Reflect**: in Al-Falaq refuge is sought by one attribute from four external evils; in An-Nas by three attributes from one internal evil, whispering, because corruption of one's religion is the greater danger. The Prophet ﷺ used to recite Al-Ikhlas and the Mu'awwidhatayn, blow into his palms and wipe over himself before sleeping, as in Sahih Al-Bukhari. Read [Surah Al-Falaq Meaning in English](/blog/surah-al-falaq-meaning-english/) and [Surah An-Nas Meaning in English](/blog/surah-an-nas-meaning-english/).

## Tajweed points in Juz 30

### The pause in ﴿بَلۡۜ رَانَ﴾, Al-Mutaffifin 14
One of the four obligatory *saktah* places in Hafs: a brief pause on the lam without breath. Without the pause, the lam would merge into the ra.

### ﴿ٱلۡمَوۡءُۥدَةُ﴾ in At-Takwir 8
A sakin *leen* waw followed by a hamzah, allowing *madd al-leen*, then a small madd waw.

### ﴿كُفُوًا﴾ in Al-Ikhlas 4
Read in Hafs with the hamzah changed to waw and a dammah on the fa: *kufuwan*.

### The two prostrations
The first at Al-Inshiqaq 21, at ﴿لَا يَسۡجُدُونَۤ﴾; the second at the end of Al-'Alaq, at ﴿وَٱسۡجُدۡۤ وَٱقۡتَرِب﴾. See [Sujud al-Tilawah in English](/blog/sujud-al-tilawa-english/).

### Qalqalah in Juz 'Amma
Qalqalah is frequent at verse endings, such as ﴿ٱلۡفَلَقِ﴾, ﴿وَقَبَ﴾ and ﴿حَسَدَ﴾; make it strong when stopping. For word meanings see [Juz Amma Word Meanings](/blog/juz-amma-word-meanings-english/).

## Similar verses to watch

### 1. "Successful is he"
- Al-A'la 14: ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ﴾, "who purifies himself".
- Ash-Shams 9: ﴿قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا﴾, "who purifies it".

### 2. "And what will make you know"
﴿وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ﴾ recurs throughout the juz, in Al-Infitar, Al-Mutaffifin, At-Tariq, Al-Balad, Al-Qadr, Al-Qari'ah and Al-Humazah, with a different continuation each time.

### 3. "When the sky"
- Al-Infitar 1: when the sky breaks apart.
- Al-Inshiqaq 1: when the sky splits open.
- At-Takwir 11: when the sky is stripped away.

### 4. The people of the right under different names
- Al-Inshiqaq 7: "as for one given his record in his right hand".
- Al-Balad 18: "those are the companions of the right".

## How to memorize Juz 30 with understanding

1. **Start from the end**: An-Nas, then Al-Falaq, then Al-Ikhlas, working back to An-Naba'; the short surahs are easier.
2. **Memorize by the thematic groups** above, not by order alone.
3. **Recite what you memorize in prayer**: Juz 'Amma is what is most recited there.
4. **Learn word meanings** before memorizing.
5. **Watch the verse endings**: many surahs keep a single rhyme.

For technique see [How to Memorize the Quran Step by Step](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran-step-by-step/).

## Practical lessons from Juz 30

- **Be certain of the great news**: it is coming.
- **Restrain your soul from desire**, and Paradise will be your refuge.
- **Honour the seeker of knowledge**, however humble.
- **Do not short-change** in measure, weight or any right.
- **Beware the rust** of piled-up sins.
- **Purify yourself**: successful is he who does.
- **Reflect on camels, sky and mountains.**
- **Do not take abundance or scarcity** as the measure of Allah's pleasure.
- **Give and fear Allah**, and He will ease you towards ease.
- **Speak of Allah's favour.**
- **Trust that with hardship comes ease.**
- **Read in your Lord's name**, prostrate and draw near.
- **Count your deeds**: every atom will be seen.
- **Urge one another to truth and patience.**
- **Be sincere to Allah**: Al-Ikhlas equals a third of the Quran.
- **Protect yourself with the Mu'awwidhatayn** every day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Juz 30 called Juz 'Amma?
Because it begins with ﴿عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ﴾.

### How many surahs are in Juz 'Amma?
Thirty-seven, from An-Naba' to An-Nas.

### How many prostrations are in Juz 'Amma?
Two: Al-Inshiqaq 21 and the end of Al-'Alaq (19).

### What was the first revelation of the Quran?
The opening of Al-'Alaq: ﴿ٱقۡرَأۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ﴾, as in Sahih Al-Bukhari.

### Which surah equals a third of the Quran?
Al-Ikhlas, as in Sahih Al-Bukhari.

## Conclusion

Juz 'Amma is both the seal and the gateway of the Quran: with it the reciter completes his reading, and from it the child begins his memorization. It strikes your heart with the great news, calls you to purify your soul, reassures you that with hardship comes ease, reminds you that every atom of deeds will be seen, and ends with pure monotheism and refuge in Allah from every evil.
