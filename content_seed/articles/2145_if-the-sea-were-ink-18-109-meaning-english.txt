﴿قُل لَّوۡ كَانَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ مِدَادٗا لِّكَلِمَٰتِ رَبِّي لَنَفِدَ ٱلۡبَحۡرُ قَبۡلَ أَن تَنفَدَ كَلِمَٰتُ رَبِّي وَلَوۡ جِئۡنَا بِمِثۡلِهِۦ مَدَدٗا﴾ [Al-Kahf 18:109]

## The words
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Midad* | Ink used for writing |
| *Words of my Lord* | His speech, knowledge and wisdom, without end |

## The image
- The whole sea as ink, with another like it added, would run out before Allah's words do.

## Luqman 31:27
- *If all the trees on earth were pens and the sea, with seven more seas added, the words of Allah would not be exhausted.*

## Its place in Al-Kahf
- Just before the final verse of a surah that speaks of knowledge in the story of Musa and al-Khidr: human knowledge is a drop before Allah's.

## Lessons
- **Stay humble** however much you learn.
- **Glorify Allah**, whose words never end.
