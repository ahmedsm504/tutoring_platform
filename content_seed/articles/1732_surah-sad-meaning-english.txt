**Surah Sad** (88 ayat) gathers stories of prophets who were tested with **power, wealth and illness**.

## A blessed Book
﴿كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡكَ مُبَٰرَكࣱ لِّيَدَّبَّرُوٓاْ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَلِيَتَذَكَّرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ [Sad: 29]

## Dawud
﴿يَٰدَاوُۥدُ إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰكَ خَلِيفَةٗ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تَتَّبِعِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ﴾
- **Justice** and avoiding desire: the duty of anyone in authority.
- At ayah 24 there is a **prostration**; the Prophet ﷺ prostrated there, as Ibn ʿAbbas reported [Al-Bukhari].

## Prophets and their tests
| Prophet | Test | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| Dawud | Judgement and power | Repentance |
| Sulayman | A vast kingdom | *An excellent servant, always turning back* |
| Ayyub | Illness and loss | *We found him patient* |

## Iblis's pride
- Iblis refused to prostrate to Adam, saying: *I am better than him; You created me from fire and him from clay.*
- **The first sin** was arrogance.

## Lessons
- **Power is a test**; judge with truth.
- **Pride** destroys.
