## Surah Sad Meaning: Dawud, Sulayman and Judging with Truth

**Surah Sad** (88 ayat) gathers stories of prophets who were tested with **power, wealth and illness**.

### A blessed Book
﴿كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡكَ مُبَٰرَكࣱ لِّيَدَّبَّرُوٓاْ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَلِيَتَذَكَّرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ [Sad: 29]

### Dawud
﴿يَٰدَاوُۥدُ إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰكَ خَلِيفَةٗ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تَتَّبِعِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ﴾
- **Justice** and avoiding desire: the duty of anyone in authority.
- At ayah 24 there is a **prostration**; the Prophet ﷺ prostrated there, as Ibn ʿAbbas reported [Al-Bukhari].

### Prophets and their tests
| Prophet | Test | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| Dawud | Judgement and power | Repentance |
| Sulayman | A vast kingdom | *An excellent servant, always turning back* |
| Ayyub | Illness and loss | *We found him patient* |

### Iblis's pride
- Iblis refused to prostrate to Adam, saying: *I am better than him; You created me from fire and him from clay.*
- **The first sin** was arrogance.

### Lessons
- **Power is a test**; judge with truth.
- **Pride** destroys.

## Surah Az-Zumar Meaning: Do Not Despair of Allah's Mercy

**Surah Az-Zumar** (75 ayat) is named after the **groups** (zumar) driven on the Day of Judgement, and holds one of the greatest ayat of hope.

### Sincerity
- *So worship Allah, being sincere to Him in religion. Unquestionably, for Allah is the pure religion.*

### The night worshipper
﴿أَمَّنۡ هُوَ قَٰنِتٌ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيۡلِ سَاجِدٗا وَقَآئِمٗا يَحۡذَرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَيَرۡجُواْ رَحۡمَةَ رَبِّهِۦۗ﴾
- *Say: are those who know equal to those who do not know?*
- **Balance**: fear of the Hereafter, hope for mercy.

### Never despair
﴿۞قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًاۚ﴾
- Allah calls even sinners **"My servants"**.
- Followed immediately by: *and turn back to your Lord and submit to Him*.

### The groups
| Group | Their arrival |
|---|---|
| The disbelievers | Driven to Hell in groups; its gates opened |
| The righteous | Driven to Paradise in groups; its keepers greet them with peace |

### Lessons
- **No sin is too big** for repentance.
- **Knowledge with worship** raises a person.

## "Are Those Who Know Equal to Those Who Do Not Know?": Quran 39:9 Explained

﴿أَمَّنۡ هُوَ قَٰنِتٌ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيۡلِ سَاجِدٗا وَقَآئِمٗا يَحۡذَرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَيَرۡجُواْ رَحۡمَةَ رَبِّهِۦۗ قُلۡ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡلَمُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَۗ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ﴾ [Az-Zumar 39:9]

### The devout one
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Qanit* | Devoutly obedient |
| Hours of the night | In night prayer |
| Prostrating and standing | In prayer |
| Fears the Hereafter | Fear |
| Hopes for his Lord's mercy | Hope |

### True knowledge
- The question about knowledge comes **after** describing the worshipper: true knowledge leads to worship and awe.

### Fear and hope
- The two wings of the believer, joined in one sentence.

### People of understanding
- *Only people of understanding take heed.*

### Lessons
- **Let knowledge lead to worship.**
- **Balance fear and hope.**

## "The Patient Will Be Given Their Reward Without Measure": Quran 39:10 Explained

﴿قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱتَّقُواْ رَبَّكُمۡۚ لِلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةࣱۗ وَأَرۡضُ ٱللَّهِ وَٰسِعَةٌۗ إِنَّمَا يُوَفَّى ٱلصَّـٰبِرُونَ أَجۡرَهُم بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابٖ﴾ [Az-Zumar 39:10]

### Four parts
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Fear your Lord* | The foundation |
| *Good in this world for those who do good* | Excellence brings good even now |
| *Allah's earth is spacious* | If you cannot practise your faith in one place, move |
| *The patient receive their reward without measure* | A reward beyond counting |

### Without measure
- Most deeds are rewarded from ten to seven hundred times. Patience is singled out: its reward is **poured out** without calculation.

### Why patience?
- It includes all of religion: patience in obedience, patience away from sin, and patience in trials.

### The spacious earth
- Revealed in Makkah when believers were persecuted, it hinted at migration. For us it means: do not stay where your faith is lost if you can move.

### Lessons
- **Every moment of patience counts.**
- **Do not trap yourself** where you cannot obey Allah.

## "Is One Whose Chest Allah Has Opened to Islam...": Quran 39:22 Explained

﴿أَفَمَن شَرَحَ ٱللَّهُ صَدۡرَهُۥ لِلۡإِسۡلَٰمِ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ نُورٖ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦۚ فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡقَٰسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مِّن ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ﴾ [Az-Zumar 39:22]

### An opened chest
- Widened to accept truth, glad with obedience, at peace with faith.

### "On light from his Lord"
- He walks through life with the insight of revelation.

### Signs
| Sign | Example |
|---|---|
| Loving obedience | Finding rest in prayer |
| Accepting truth | Returning when shown a mistake |
| Preparing for the Hereafter | Not drowning in this world |

### Hardened hearts
- Hearts that do not soften with remembrance, but harden further.

### The next verse
- *Allah has sent down the best statement* (39:23): the Quran opens chests and softens hearts.

### Lessons
- **Pray**: *My Lord, expand my chest.*
- **Treat hardness** with dhikr and Quran.

## "Allah Has Sent Down the Best Statement": Quran 39:23 Explained

﴿ٱللَّهُ نَزَّلَ أَحۡسَنَ ٱلۡحَدِيثِ كِتَٰبٗا مُّتَشَٰبِهٗا مَّثَانِيَ تَقۡشَعِرُّ مِنۡهُ جُلُودُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُمۡ ثُمَّ تَلِينُ جُلُودُهُمۡ وَقُلُوبُهُمۡ إِلَىٰ ذِكۡرِ ٱللَّهِۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُدَى ٱللَّهِ يَهۡدِي بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَن يُضۡلِلِ ٱللَّهُ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِنۡ هَادٍ﴾ [Az-Zumar 39:23]

### The best statement
- The Quran is the best speech in wording, meaning, news and rulings.

### *Mutashabih*
- Its parts resemble one another in beauty, truth and precision: no contradiction.

### *Mathani*
- Stories, warnings and rulings are repeated, and recitation never tires.

### Its effect
| Verses | Effect |
|---|---|
| Of warning | Skins shiver in awe |
| Of mercy | Skins and hearts soften |

### Guidance from Allah
- Benefiting from the Quran is Allah's guidance: ask Him for it.

### Lessons
- **Read verses of mercy and warning** with reflection.
- **If your heart does not move**, tend it with dhikr and duʿa.

## Quran 39:53 Explained: Allah Forgives All Sins for the Repentant

﴿۞قُلۡ يَٰعِبَادِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسۡرَفُواْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمۡ لَا تَقۡنَطُواْ مِن رَّحۡمَةِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغۡفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًاۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ﴾ [Az-Zumar 39:53]

### "O My servants"
- Allah addresses those who have **sinned excessively** and still calls them *My servants*. Sin did not cancel the relationship.

### The core message
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Who have transgressed against themselves* | Sin harms the sinner first |
| *Do not despair* | Despair is forbidden |
| *Allah forgives all sins* | No sin is too big for repentance |
| *He is the Forgiving, the Merciful* | Two names that confirm the promise |

### The condition
- The next verse: *And turn to your Lord and submit to Him before the punishment comes.* Forgiveness of all sins is for those who **repent**. Scholars note that this verse is about the repentant, while An-Nisa 4:48 is about the one who dies upon shirk without repentance.

### Most hopeful
- Many scholars describe this as one of the most hopeful verses in the Quran.

### Lessons
- **Never let a sin keep you from Allah.**
- **Repent today**, before the chance ends.

## "Those Who Carry the Throne Ask Forgiveness for the Believers": Quran 40:7 Explained

﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يَحۡمِلُونَ ٱلۡعَرۡشَ وَمَنۡ حَوۡلَهُۥ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَيُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦ وَيَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْۖ رَبَّنَا وَسِعۡتَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٖ رَّحۡمَةٗ وَعِلۡمٗا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لِلَّذِينَ تَابُواْ وَٱتَّبَعُواْ سَبِيلَكَ وَقِهِمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ﴾ [Ghafir 40:7]

### The greatest angels
- The bearers of the Throne and those around it: among the closest angels to Allah.

### What they do
| Act | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Glorify with praise | Constant worship |
| Believe in Him | Faith, the bond with believers |
| Seek forgiveness for believers | Pray for us |

### Their duʿa
- *Our Lord, You encompass all things in mercy and knowledge.* They begin with praise before asking.
- *Forgive those who repented and followed Your way, and protect them from Hellfire.*

### The next verses
- They also ask for Paradise for the believers and their righteous parents, spouses and children (40:8).

### Who benefits?
- **Those who repent and follow Allah's way**: a condition and an invitation.

### Lessons
- **Imagine being prayed for** by the bearers of the Throne.
- **Repent and follow** to be included.

## Surah Ghafir Meaning: The Believer from Pharaoh's Family

**Surah Ghafir** (85 ayat) opens with Allah's names of mercy and power, and tells the story of a hidden believer who spoke up.

### Who is Allah?
- *The Forgiver of sin, the Accepter of repentance, severe in punishment, owner of abundance.*
- **Mercy first**, then a warning, then generosity.

### Angels praying for you
- Those who carry the Throne and those around it glorify their Lord and **ask forgiveness for the believers**.
- Read [Rabbana Du'as in the Quran](/blog/quran-revision-games-kids-english/#Rabbana-Du-as-in-the-Quran-10-Supplications-Beginning-with-O).

### The believer from Pharaoh's family
﴿وَقَالَ رَجُلࣱ مُّؤۡمِنࣱ مِّنۡ ءَالِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ يَكۡتُمُ إِيمَٰنَهُۥٓ أَتَقۡتُلُونَ رَجُلًا أَن يَقُولَ رَبِّيَ ٱللَّهُ﴾
| His argument | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Will you kill a man for saying "my Lord is Allah"? | Courage in truth |
| If he lies, it's on him; if truthful, you'll be struck | Wisdom in persuasion |
| Remember the nations before you | History as warning |
| *I entrust my affair to Allah* | Tawakkul |

### Call upon Me
﴿وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدۡعُونِيٓ أَسۡتَجِبۡ لَكُمۡۚ﴾
- **Du'a is worship**: the surah calls refusing it arrogance.

### Lessons
- **Speak the truth wisely**, even when alone.

## "I Entrust My Affair to Allah": Quran 40:44 Explained

﴿فَسَتَذۡكُرُونَ مَآ أَقُولُ لَكُمۡۚ وَأُفَوِّضُ أَمۡرِيٓ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَصِيرُۢ بِٱلۡعِبَادِ﴾ [Ghafir 40:44]

### Who said it?
- A believing man from the family of Pharaoh who had concealed his faith. When Pharaoh planned to kill Musa, he spoke up with wisdom, warning his people of the fate of earlier nations.

### Three statements
| Statement | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *You will remember what I say* | The truth will become clear, even if later |
| *I entrust my affair to Allah* | I have done my part; the result is His |
| *Allah sees His servants* | Nothing is hidden from Him |

### *Tafwid*
- Entrusting means handing over the outcome to Allah **after** doing what you must. It is a form of tawakkul.

### The result
- The next verse: *So Allah protected him from the evils they plotted, and the people of Pharaoh were enveloped by the worst punishment.*

### Courage with wisdom
- He did not attack; he reasoned, reminded and advised. Then he trusted Allah.

### Lessons
- **Say the truth wisely**, then let go of fear.
- **Allah protects those who trust Him.**

## "Call Upon Me, I Will Respond to You": Quran 40:60 Explained

﴿وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدۡعُونِيٓ أَسۡتَجِبۡ لَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ عَنۡ عِبَادَتِي سَيَدۡخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ﴾ [Ghafir 40:60]

### A command and a promise
- *Call upon Me* is a command; *I will respond to you* is a promise. Allah does not only permit duʿa, He **asks** for it.

### Duʿa is worship
- The verse calls not asking *arrogance toward My worship*. The Prophet ﷺ recited this verse after saying: **"Duʿa is worship"** (narrated by Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi, who graded it sound).

### Why is not asking arrogance?
- Asking is admitting need. The one who never asks Allah behaves as if he does not need Him.

### Etiquette of duʿa
| Etiquette | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Begin with praise | Praise Allah and send blessings on the Prophet ﷺ |
| Be certain | Ask while confident of the answer |
| Be persistent | Do not say "I asked and was not answered" |
| Choose good times | Last third of the night, in prostration, between adhan and iqamah |
| Lawful earnings | Unlawful food blocks the answer |

### Lessons
- **Ask for everything**, big or small.
- **Duʿa itself is rewarded** as worship, even before it is answered.
