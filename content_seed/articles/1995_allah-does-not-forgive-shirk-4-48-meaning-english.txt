﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَغۡفِرُ أَن يُشۡرَكَ بِهِۦ وَيَغۡفِرُ مَا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَن يُشۡرِكۡ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدِ ٱفۡتَرَىٰٓ إِثۡمًا عَظِيمًا﴾ [An-Nisa 4:48]

## The meaning
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Does not forgive shirk | For the one who dies upon it |
| Forgives what is less | Other sins are under His will |
| A great sin invented | Shirk is the greatest lie against Allah |

## With Az-Zumar 39:53
- *Allah forgives all sins* (39:53) is about **those who repent**, even from shirk. Al-Nisa 4:48 is about the one who **dies** without repentance.

## Hope for the believer
- A believer in tawhid who dies with sins unrepented is under Allah's will: He may forgive or punish, but he will not remain in the Fire forever.

## Why is shirk the gravest?
- It gives Allah's exclusive right to others and equates creation with the Creator.

## Lessons
- **Guard tawhid** above everything.
- **Repent now**: repentance erases even shirk.
