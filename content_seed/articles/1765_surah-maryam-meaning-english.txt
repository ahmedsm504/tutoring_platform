**Surah Maryam** (98 ayat) is the only surah named after a woman, and the word **mercy** (*rahmah*) and the name **ar-Rahman** recur throughout it.

## Zakariya's quiet prayer
- An old man with a barren wife called his Lord **in a hidden voice**, and Allah gave him Yahya, a name given to no one before.

## Maryam and the birth of ʿIsa
﴿وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَرۡيَمَ إِذِ ٱنتَبَذَتۡ مِنۡ أَهۡلِهَا مَكَانٗا شَرۡقِيّٗا﴾
- An angel came to her with the news of a pure son. She asked how, since no man had touched her, and was told it is easy for Allah.
- When she returned with the baby, ʿIsa spoke from the cradle: *I am the servant of Allah.*

## Ibrahim and his father
- Ibrahim called his father four times with "*Ya abati*" (O my dear father), with gentle reasoning against idols. Even when threatened, he replied: *Peace be upon you; I will ask forgiveness for you from my Lord.*

## The line of prophets
- Musa, Ismaʿil and Idris are mentioned with their qualities: sincerity, keeping promises, and truthfulness.

## Love placed by Allah
﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ سَيَجۡعَلُ لَهُمُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وُدّٗا﴾

## Lessons
- **Pray privately**: Allah hears a hidden call.
- **Speak gently even when you disagree**, as Ibrahim did with his father.
