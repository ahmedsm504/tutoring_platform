A beautiful town with a huge garden on its right, full of trees and fruit, and another garden just like it on its left. The air is fresh, the water plentiful and the food abundant. And Allah told its people: "Eat from the provision of your Lord and be grateful to Him: a good land and a forgiving Lord." This was the town of Saba, Sheba, in Yemen. But its people turned away from thanking Allah. What happened? A great flood broke their dam and swept their gardens away, leaving only bitter fruit and thorny bushes.

Surah Saba is the surah of gratitude. It tells us about Prophet Dawud, for whom Allah made iron soft, and to whose family Allah said: "Work, O family of Dawud, in gratitude." It tells us about Sulayman, who had the wind at his command, and the amazing story of his death while leaning on his staff. And it contrasts them with the people of Saba, who lost their blessings when they stopped being grateful. In this guide, we explain Surah Saba to children in simple English, with activities, a story and a memorization plan.

## Quick Facts About Surah Saba

- **Position**: surah 34, in juz 22.
- **Number of verses**: 54.
- **Type**: Meccan.
- **Name**: after the people of Saba (Sheba) in Yemen, the same kingdom whose queen appears in Surah An-Naml.

### A beautiful opening
Surah Saba is one of five surahs that begin with "Alhamdulillah": Al-Fatiha, Al-An'am, Al-Kahf, Saba and Fatir.

## Lesson 1: Allah Knows Everything

﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَهُ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ فِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ﴾ ﴿يَعۡلَمُ مَا يَلِجُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا يَخۡرُجُ مِنۡهَا وَمَا يَنزِلُ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا يَعۡرُجُ فِيهَاۚ وَهُوَ ٱلرَّحِيمُ ٱلۡغَفُورُ﴾ (Quran 34:1-2)

### Explaining it to your child
"All praise is for Allah, who owns everything in the heavens and the earth. Allah knows everything that goes into the earth: raindrops, seeds and tree roots. He knows everything that comes out of it: plants, spring water and ants from their nests. He knows what comes down from the sky, like rain and angels, and what goes up into it, like birds, du'as and good deeds."

### Activity: in and out
In the garden, ask your child to find three things that go into the earth, like a seed, water and roots, and three that come out of it, like a plant, a worm and ants. Then say: "Allah knows all of this, everywhere in the world, at the same time!"

## Lesson 2: Dawud and the Glorifying Mountains

﴿۞وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا دَاوُۥدَ مِنَّا فَضۡلٗاۖ يَٰجِبَالُ أَوِّبِي مَعَهُۥ وَٱلطَّيۡرَۖ وَأَلَنَّا لَهُ ٱلۡحَدِيدَ﴾ ﴿أَنِ ٱعۡمَلۡ سَٰبِغَٰتٖ وَقَدِّرۡ فِي ٱلسَّرۡدِۖ وَٱعۡمَلُواْ صَٰلِحًاۖ إِنِّي بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 34:10-11)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah gave Dawud a great gift: he had a beautiful voice, and when he glorified Allah, the mountains and birds glorified Allah with him! And Allah gave him another miracle: He made iron soft in his hands, like dough. Dawud made coats of armour from it to protect soldiers. Allah told him: Make full coats of armour and fit the rings together evenly, and do good deeds."

### From the Sunnah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "No one has ever eaten food better than what he earned with his own hands, and the Prophet of Allah Dawud used to eat from what he earned with his own hands" (al-Bukhari).

### Lesson for kids: working with your hands
"Dawud was a prophet and a king, and still he worked with his hands making armour. Working with your hands is honourable, and doing it well is important."

### Activity: the chain of rings
Make a paper chain with your child, with every ring the same size and neatly joined. Say: "This is how Dawud made armour from rings of iron, carefully and evenly."

## Lesson 3: Sulayman and the Wind

﴿وَلِسُلَيۡمَٰنَ ٱلرِّيحَ غُدُوُّهَا شَهۡرࣱ وَرَوَاحُهَا شَهۡرࣱۖ وَأَسَلۡنَا لَهُۥ عَيۡنَ ٱلۡقِطۡرِۖ وَمِنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ مَن يَعۡمَلُ بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡهِ بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِۦۖ وَمَن يَزِغۡ مِنۡهُمۡ عَنۡ أَمۡرِنَا نُذِقۡهُ مِنۡ عَذَابِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ﴾ ﴿يَعۡمَلُونَ لَهُۥ مَا يَشَآءُ مِن مَّحَٰرِيبَ وَتَمَٰثِيلَ وَجِفَانٖ كَٱلۡجَوَابِ وَقُدُورٖ رَّاسِيَٰتٍۚ ٱعۡمَلُوٓاْ ءَالَ دَاوُۥدَ شُكۡرٗاۚ وَقَلِيلࣱ مِّنۡ عِبَادِيَ ٱلشَّكُورُ﴾ (Quran 34:12-13)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah gave Sulayman, son of Dawud, the wind to carry him: in the morning it travelled a month's journey, and in the evening another month's journey! Allah made melted copper flow for him like water, and made jinn work for him: building places of worship and palaces, making huge fixed cooking pots and bowls as big as pools. And Allah said: Work, O family of Dawud, in gratitude. And few of My servants are truly grateful."

### Lesson for kids: gratitude through actions
"Allah didn't say: say thank you. He said: work in gratitude. We thank Allah with our actions: thanking Him for our eyes by looking at good things, for our hands by helping people, and for our money by giving charity."

### Activity: thanking with my body
Ask your child about each part of their body: "How can you thank Allah for your eyes? Your ears? Your hands? Your feet? Your tongue?" Let them suggest an action for each.

## Lesson 4: The Staff and the Termite

﴿فَلَمَّا قَضَيۡنَا عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ مَا دَلَّهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَوۡتِهِۦٓ إِلَّا دَآبَّةُ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تَأۡكُلُ مِنسَأَتَهُۥۖ فَلَمَّا خَرَّ تَبَيَّنَتِ ٱلۡجِنُّ أَن لَّوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ ٱلۡغَيۡبَ مَا لَبِثُواْ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡمُهِينِ﴾ (Quran 34:14)

### Explaining it to your child
"Sulayman died while standing, leaning on his staff, with the jinn working in front of him. They thought he was alive and watching them! They kept working for a long time. Then a tiny creature, a termite that eats wood, ate Sulayman's staff from the inside until it broke and Sulayman fell. The jinn realised he had died long before, and understood that they didn't know the unseen. If they had known it, they wouldn't have kept slaving away!"

### Lesson for kids: only Allah knows the unseen
"The jinn don't know the unseen, and nobody knows it except Allah. So we don't believe anyone who says they can tell the future."

### Activity: the eaten wood
If you can, show your child a picture of wood eaten by termites from the inside. Say: "A tiny insect uncovered a secret that the powerful jinn didn't know!"

## Lesson 5: The Gardens of Saba

﴿لَقَدۡ كَانَ لِسَبَإٖ فِي مَسۡكَنِهِمۡ ءَايَةࣱۖ جَنَّتَانِ عَن يَمِينٖ وَشِمَالٖۖ كُلُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِ رَبِّكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لَهُۥۚ بَلۡدَةࣱ طَيِّبَةࣱ وَرَبٌّ غَفُورࣱ﴾ (Quran 34:15)

### Explaining it to your child
"The people of Saba had an amazing sign: two huge gardens on the right and left, full of fruit, and a great dam that stored water. Allah told them: Eat from your Lord's provision and thank Him. Your land is good, and your Lord is forgiving."

## Lesson 6: The Flood of the Dam

﴿فَأَعۡرَضُواْ فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَيۡلَ ٱلۡعَرِمِ وَبَدَّلۡنَٰهُم بِجَنَّتَيۡهِمۡ جَنَّتَيۡنِ ذَوَاتَيۡ أُكُلٍ خَمۡطٖ وَأَثۡلٖ وَشَيۡءٖ مِّن سِدۡرٖ قَلِيلٖ﴾ ﴿ذَٰلِكَ جَزَيۡنَٰهُم بِمَا كَفَرُواْۖ وَهَلۡ نُجَٰزِيٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡكَفُورَ﴾ (Quran 34:16-17)

### Explaining it to your child
"But the people of Saba turned away from thanking Allah and didn't worship Him. So Allah sent a great flood that broke the dam and swept over the gardens. The two gardens turned into trees with bitter fruit, thorny bushes and a few lote trees. This is what happens to people who are ungrateful for blessings."

### Lesson for kids: blessings grow with gratitude
"Compare Dawud and Sulayman with the people of Saba: Dawud and Sulayman were grateful, so Allah gave them more. The people of Saba weren't grateful, so they lost their blessings. If you want your blessings to last, thank Allah."

### Activity: the dam experiment
In a sandpit or garden, build a small dam of mud with water behind it and a little "garden" of sticks in front. Then open a gap in the dam and watch the water sweep everything away. Say: "That's what happened to the people of Saba when they stopped being grateful."

## Lesson 7: Charity Is Replaced

﴿قُلۡ إِنَّ رَبِّي يَبۡسُطُ ٱلرِّزۡقَ لِمَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦ وَيَقۡدِرُ لَهُۥۚ وَمَآ أَنفَقۡتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَهُوَ يُخۡلِفُهُۥۖ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّـٰزِقِينَ﴾ (Quran 34:39)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah gives plenty to whom He wills and less to whom He wills. And whatever you spend for good, Allah replaces it with something better. He is the best Provider. Charity never makes money less."

### Activity: the charity box
Give your child two money boxes: one for themselves, and one for charity. Whenever they get pocket money, they put a small part into the charity box. At the end of the month, they give it to someone in need themselves.

## Let's Read Together: Calm Thinking

﴿۞قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَعِظُكُم بِوَٰحِدَةٍۖ أَن تَقُومُواْ لِلَّهِ مَثۡنَىٰ وَفُرَٰدَىٰ ثُمَّ تَتَفَكَّرُواْۚ مَا بِصَاحِبِكُم مِّن جِنَّةٍۚ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا نَذِيرࣱ لَّكُم بَيۡنَ يَدَيۡ عَذَابٖ شَدِيدٖ﴾ (Quran 34:46)

### Explaining it to your child
"Allah teaches us a beautiful way to think: stand for Allah, in pairs or alone, and then think calmly. Quiet, honest thinking, away from noise, leads to the truth."

## A Story to Tell Your Child: "Grandma's Garden"

Layla's grandmother had a small, beautiful rooftop garden: pots of tomatoes, mint, peppers and colourful flowers. She watered them every morning and said as she picked: "Alhamdulillah, O Allah, bless it."

One summer, Grandma travelled for a month and asked Layla to water the garden. In the first week, Layla watered it every day. But then she got busy with games and her tablet and forgot. Whenever she remembered, she said: "Tomorrow."

When Grandma came home and went up to the roof, she found the plants wilted, the leaves yellow and the tomatoes dry. Layla was very sad. "Sorry, Grandma, I forgot."

Grandma hugged her and said: "Layla, do you know the people of Saba? They had two huge gardens, and Allah told them: eat and be grateful. But they turned away and weren't grateful, and their gardens were lost. Blessings are like plants: if we look after them and thank Allah, they grow. If we neglect them, they wilt."

Layla decided to start again. She planted new seeds with her grandmother and watered them every day, saying "Alhamdulillah". A month later, the garden was more beautiful than before.

Ask your child: "Why did the garden wilt? What blessing do you have that you want to thank Allah for and look after?"

## What We Learn From Surah Saba

- **Alhamdulillah** to the One who knows everything.
- **Working with our hands** is honourable.
- **Do things carefully** and well.
- **Thank Allah through actions**, not just words.
- **Only Allah** knows the unseen.
- **Blessings last** with gratitude and disappear with ingratitude.
- **Charity** is replaced by Allah.
- **Calm thinking** leads to the truth.

1. What did the mountains and birds do with Dawud?
2. What did Allah make soft for Dawud, and what did he make with it?
3. What did Allah give Sulayman control over?

## Frequently Asked Questions From Parents

### What was the "creature of the earth" in Sulayman's story?
Commentators say it was a termite, a small insect that eats wood, which ate Sulayman's staff until he fell.

### Where was the kingdom of Saba?
In Yemen. They had a famous dam, the Ma'rib Dam, and commentators mention that the flood of al-'Arim broke it.

### How can I teach my child gratitude through actions?
Link every blessing to an action: food with saying Alhamdulillah and not wasting, clothes with looking after them, toys with sharing, and health with helping others.
