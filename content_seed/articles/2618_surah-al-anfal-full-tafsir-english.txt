Surah Al-Anfal was revealed after the Battle of Badr, the first great battle of Islam, in which about 313 poorly equipped believers defeated an army three times their size. But the surah does not celebrate the victory as a human achievement. It opens by taking the question of spoils away from the fighters and handing it to Allah and His Messenger, and then defines who the true believers are. It reminds the Companions that they wanted the caravan while Allah wanted the battle, that angels were sent in answer to their du'a, and that "you did not throw when you threw, but it was Allah who threw". It then gives lasting lessons: respond to what gives you life, beware of trials, do not dispute and lose courage, prepare what strength you can, and remember that only Allah can unite hearts.

This guide explains Surah Al-Anfal section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah Al-Anfal
- **Number of verses**: 75.
- **Type**: Medinan.
- **Position**: Surah 8, in the ninth and tenth juz.
- **Name**: Al-Anfal means "the spoils of war".
- **Revealed**: after the Battle of Badr in 2 AH.
- **Main themes**: the qualities of believers, the lessons of Badr, obedience and steadfastness, the causes of victory and defeat, and brotherhood.

## Verse 1: The Spoils Belong to Allah
﴿يَسۡـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلۡأَنفَالِۖ قُلِ ٱلۡأَنفَالُ لِلَّهِ وَٱلرَّسُولِۖ فَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَأَصۡلِحُواْ ذَاتَ بَيۡنِكُمۡۖ وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥٓ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ (Quran 8:1)

**"They ask you about the bounties of war. Say: The bounties of war are for Allah and the Messenger. So fear Allah and amend that which is between you."** After the battle, some disagreement arose about the spoils. The first lesson: fix your relationships before you think about gains.

## Verses 2–4: The Qualities of True Believers
﴿إِنَّمَا ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَجِلَتۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ وَإِذَا تُلِيَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ زَادَتۡهُمۡ إِيمَٰنٗا وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾ ﴿أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ حَقّٗاۚ لَّهُمۡ دَرَجَٰتٌ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ وَمَغۡفِرَةࣱ وَرِزۡقࣱ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 8:2-4)

**"The believers are only those who, when Allah is mentioned, their hearts become fearful, and when His verses are recited to them, it increases them in faith; and upon their Lord they rely."** **"Those who establish prayer and spend from what We have provided them."** **"Those are the believers, truly."**

**Five qualities**: hearts that tremble at Allah's mention, faith that grows with the Quran, reliance on Allah, prayer and spending. Notice that faith increases; it can also decrease.

## Verses 5–14: Badr and the Angels
﴿كَمَآ أَخۡرَجَكَ رَبُّكَ مِنۢ بَيۡتِكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِنَّ فَرِيقٗا مِّنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ لَكَٰرِهُونَ﴾ ﴿يُجَٰدِلُونَكَ فِي ٱلۡحَقِّ بَعۡدَ مَا تَبَيَّنَ كَأَنَّمَا يُسَاقُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡمَوۡتِ وَهُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِذۡ يَعِدُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ إِحۡدَى ٱلطَّآئِفَتَيۡنِ أَنَّهَا لَكُمۡ وَتَوَدُّونَ أَنَّ غَيۡرَ ذَاتِ ٱلشَّوۡكَةِ تَكُونُ لَكُمۡ وَيُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ أَن يُحِقَّ ٱلۡحَقَّ بِكَلِمَٰتِهِۦ وَيَقۡطَعَ دَابِرَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ﴾ ﴿لِيُحِقَّ ٱلۡحَقَّ وَيُبۡطِلَ ٱلۡبَٰطِلَ وَلَوۡ كَرِهَ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ﴾ (Quran 8:5-8)

The Prophet ﷺ had set out to intercept a trade caravan. **"And you wished that the unarmed one would be yours. But Allah intended to establish the truth by His words."** The believers wanted an easy gain; Allah wanted a decisive day.

﴿إِذۡ تَسۡتَغِيثُونَ رَبَّكُمۡ فَٱسۡتَجَابَ لَكُمۡ أَنِّي مُمِدُّكُم بِأَلۡفٖ مِّنَ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ مُرۡدِفِينَ﴾ ﴿وَمَا جَعَلَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بُشۡرَىٰ وَلِتَطۡمَئِنَّ بِهِۦ قُلُوبُكُمۡۚ وَمَا ٱلنَّصۡرُ إِلَّا مِنۡ عِندِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ﴾ (Quran 8:9-10)

**"When you asked help of your Lord, and He answered you: Indeed, I will reinforce you with a thousand from the angels, following one another."** In Sahih Muslim, 'Umar described the Prophet ﷺ at Badr facing the qiblah, raising his hands and crying out: "O Allah, fulfil for me what You promised me. O Allah, if this small band of Muslims is destroyed, You will not be worshipped on the earth," until his cloak fell from his shoulders. **"And there is no victory except from Allah."**

﴿إِذۡ يُغَشِّيكُمُ ٱلنُّعَاسَ أَمَنَةٗ مِّنۡهُ وَيُنَزِّلُ عَلَيۡكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗ لِّيُطَهِّرَكُم بِهِۦ وَيُذۡهِبَ عَنكُمۡ رِجۡزَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ وَلِيَرۡبِطَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِكُمۡ وَيُثَبِّتَ بِهِ ٱلۡأَقۡدَامَ﴾ (Quran 8:11)

**Sleep and rain**: **"He overwhelmed you with drowsiness, giving security from Him, and sent down upon you from the sky rain by which to purify you... and make firm thereby your feet."** Calm hearts and firm ground before the battle.

## Verses 15–19: You Did Not Throw
﴿فَلَمۡ تَقۡتُلُوهُمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ قَتَلَهُمۡۚ وَمَا رَمَيۡتَ إِذۡ رَمَيۡتَ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ رَمَىٰ وَلِيُبۡلِيَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ مِنۡهُ بَلَآءً حَسَنًاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 8:17)

**"And you did not kill them, but it was Allah who killed them. And you threw not when you threw, but it was Allah who threw."** The Prophet ﷺ threw a handful of dust towards the enemy, and Allah made it reach their eyes. The believer does the action; Allah gives the result. This removes both arrogance and despair.

## Verses 20–29: Respond to What Gives You Life
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱسۡتَجِيبُواْ لِلَّهِ وَلِلرَّسُولِ إِذَا دَعَاكُمۡ لِمَا يُحۡيِيكُمۡۖ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَحُولُ بَيۡنَ ٱلۡمَرۡءِ وَقَلۡبِهِۦ وَأَنَّهُۥٓ إِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ﴾ (Quran 8:24)

**"O you who believe, respond to Allah and to the Messenger when he calls you to that which gives you life."** Obedience brings true life to the heart. **"And know that Allah intervenes between a man and his heart."** Do not delay responding; hearts can change.

﴿وَٱتَّقُواْ فِتۡنَةٗ لَّا تُصِيبَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ مِنكُمۡ خَآصَّةٗۖ وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ﴾ (Quran 8:25)

**"And fear a trial which will not strike those who have wronged among you exclusively."** When evil spreads and no one stops it, its harm reaches everyone.

﴿وَٱذۡكُرُوٓاْ إِذۡ أَنتُمۡ قَلِيلࣱ مُّسۡتَضۡعَفُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ تَخَافُونَ أَن يَتَخَطَّفَكُمُ ٱلنَّاسُ فَـَٔاوَىٰكُمۡ وَأَيَّدَكُم بِنَصۡرِهِۦ وَرَزَقَكُم مِّنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 8:26)

**"And remember when you were few and oppressed in the land, fearing that people might abduct you, but He sheltered you, supported you with His victory, and provided you with good things."**

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ لَا تَخُونُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلرَّسُولَ وَتَخُونُوٓاْ أَمَٰنَٰتِكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَآ أَمۡوَٰلُكُمۡ وَأَوۡلَٰدُكُمۡ فِتۡنَةࣱ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ عِندَهُۥٓ أَجۡرٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 8:27-28)

**"Do not betray Allah and the Messenger or betray your trusts while you know."** **"And know that your properties and your children are but a trial."**

﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِن تَتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَ يَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ فُرۡقَانٗا وَيُكَفِّرۡ عَنكُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتِكُمۡ وَيَغۡفِرۡ لَكُمۡۗ وَٱللَّهُ ذُو ٱلۡفَضۡلِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ﴾ (Quran 8:29)

**"O you who believe, if you fear Allah, He will grant you a criterion"** (*furqan*): an inner light that distinguishes truth from falsehood, **"and will remove from you your misdeeds and forgive you"**.

## Verses 30–40: The Plot Against the Prophet ﷺ
﴿وَإِذۡ يَمۡكُرُ بِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِيُثۡبِتُوكَ أَوۡ يَقۡتُلُوكَ أَوۡ يُخۡرِجُوكَۚ وَيَمۡكُرُونَ وَيَمۡكُرُ ٱللَّهُۖ وَٱللَّهُ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡمَٰكِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 8:30)

**"And when those who disbelieved plotted against you to restrain you or kill you or expel you. But they plan, and Allah plans. And Allah is the best of planners."** This refers to the plot before the Hijrah.

﴿وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيُعَذِّبَهُمۡ وَأَنتَ فِيهِمۡۚ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ مُعَذِّبَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ يَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ﴾ (Quran 8:33)

**"But Allah would not punish them while you are among them, and Allah would not punish them while they seek forgiveness."** Two safeguards: the presence of the Prophet ﷺ, and *istighfar*, which remains with us.

## Verses 41–44: The One-Fifth
﴿۞وَٱعۡلَمُوٓاْ أَنَّمَا غَنِمۡتُم مِّن شَيۡءٖ فَأَنَّ لِلَّهِ خُمُسَهُۥ وَلِلرَّسُولِ وَلِذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَٱلۡيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلۡمَسَٰكِينِ وَٱبۡنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ إِن كُنتُمۡ ءَامَنتُم بِٱللَّهِ وَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ عَبۡدِنَا يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفُرۡقَانِ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡتَقَى ٱلۡجَمۡعَانِۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ قَدِيرٌ﴾ (Quran 8:41)

**One-fifth of the spoils** is for Allah and the Messenger, his near relatives, orphans, the needy and the traveller. Badr is called **"the Day of the Criterion, the day when the two armies met"**.

## Verses 45–49: Rules of Steadfastness
﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا لَقِيتُمۡ فِئَةٗ فَٱثۡبُتُواْ وَٱذۡكُرُواْ ٱللَّهَ كَثِيرٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تُفۡلِحُونَ﴾ ﴿وَأَطِيعُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ وَلَا تَنَٰزَعُواْ فَتَفۡشَلُواْ وَتَذۡهَبَ رِيحُكُمۡۖ وَٱصۡبِرُوٓاْۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 8:45-46)

**"O you who believe, when you encounter a company, stand firm and remember Allah much that you may be successful. And obey Allah and His Messenger, and do not dispute and thus lose courage and your strength would depart; and be patient. Indeed, Allah is with the patient."**

**Five rules of victory**: firmness, remembrance, obedience, unity and patience. Disputes destroy courage and strength, whether in armies, organisations or families.

﴿وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّذِينَ خَرَجُواْ مِن دِيَٰرِهِم بَطَرٗا وَرِئَآءَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَيَصُدُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا يَعۡمَلُونَ مُحِيطࣱ﴾ (Quran 8:47)

**"And do not be like those who came forth from their homes insolently and to be seen by people."** The army of Quraysh marched out with music and pride, and was defeated.

﴿وَإِذۡ زَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ وَقَالَ لَا غَالِبَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَإِنِّي جَارࣱ لَّكُمۡۖ فَلَمَّا تَرَآءَتِ ٱلۡفِئَتَانِ نَكَصَ عَلَىٰ عَقِبَيۡهِ وَقَالَ إِنِّي بَرِيٓءࣱ مِّنكُمۡ إِنِّيٓ أَرَىٰ مَا لَا تَرَوۡنَ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ ٱللَّهَۚ وَٱللَّهُ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡعِقَابِ﴾ (Quran 8:48)

**Satan** made their deeds attractive and said: "No one can overcome you today, and indeed I am your protector." But when the two armies saw each other, he turned on his heels and said: **"Indeed, I am disassociated from you. Indeed, I see what you do not see."**

## Verses 50–64: Changing Blessings and Preparing Strength
﴿ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَمۡ يَكُ مُغَيِّرٗا نِّعۡمَةً أَنۡعَمَهَا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُواْ مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمۡ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 8:53)

**"That is because Allah would not change a favour which He had bestowed upon a people until they change what is within themselves."**

﴿وَأَعِدُّواْ لَهُم مَّا ٱسۡتَطَعۡتُم مِّن قُوَّةٖ وَمِن رِّبَاطِ ٱلۡخَيۡلِ تُرۡهِبُونَ بِهِۦ عَدُوَّ ٱللَّهِ وَعَدُوَّكُمۡ وَءَاخَرِينَ مِن دُونِهِمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ يَعۡلَمُهُمۡۚ وَمَا تُنفِقُواْ مِن شَيۡءٖ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ يُوَفَّ إِلَيۡكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ لَا تُظۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 8:60)

**"And prepare against them whatever you are able of power."** In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ recited this verse and said: "Indeed, power is in shooting." Preparation in every field is part of faith.

﴿۞وَإِن جَنَحُواْ لِلسَّلۡمِ فَٱجۡنَحۡ لَهَا وَتَوَكَّلۡ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ﴾ (Quran 8:61)

**"And if they incline to peace, then incline to it and rely upon Allah."**

﴿وَإِن يُرِيدُوٓاْ أَن يَخۡدَعُوكَ فَإِنَّ حَسۡبَكَ ٱللَّهُۚ هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَيَّدَكَ بِنَصۡرِهِۦ وَبِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿وَأَلَّفَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِهِمۡۚ لَوۡ أَنفَقۡتَ مَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعٗا مَّآ أَلَّفۡتَ بَيۡنَ قُلُوبِهِمۡ وَلَٰكِنَّ ٱللَّهَ أَلَّفَ بَيۡنَهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 8:62-63)

**"And brought together their hearts. If you had spent all that is in the earth, you could not have brought their hearts together; but Allah brought them together."** The Aws and Khazraj, who had fought for generations, became brothers. Unity of hearts is a gift no money can buy.

## Verses 65–75: Captives and Brotherhood
﴿مَا كَانَ لِنَبِيٍّ أَن يَكُونَ لَهُۥٓ أَسۡرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ يُثۡخِنَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ تُرِيدُونَ عَرَضَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا وَٱللَّهُ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿لَّوۡلَا كِتَٰبࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ سَبَقَ لَمَسَّكُمۡ فِيمَآ أَخَذۡتُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿فَكُلُواْ مِمَّا غَنِمۡتُمۡ حَلَٰلٗا طَيِّبٗاۚ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 8:67-69)

The captives of Badr were ransomed. Allah gently corrected the decision, then permitted the ransom: **"So consume what you have taken of war booty as lawful and good."**

﴿إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَهَاجَرُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ بِأَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ وَأَنفُسِهِمۡ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاوَواْ وَّنَصَرُوٓاْ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٖۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَلَمۡ يُهَاجِرُواْ مَا لَكُم مِّن وَلَٰيَتِهِم مِّن شَيۡءٍ حَتَّىٰ يُهَاجِرُواْۚ وَإِنِ ٱسۡتَنصَرُوكُمۡ فِي ٱلدِّينِ فَعَلَيۡكُمُ ٱلنَّصۡرُ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِۭ بَيۡنَكُمۡ وَبَيۡنَهُم مِّيثَٰقࣱۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ بَصِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلِيَآءُ بَعۡضٍۚ إِلَّا تَفۡعَلُوهُ تَكُن فِتۡنَةࣱ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَفَسَادࣱ كَبِيرࣱ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَهَاجَرُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَاوَواْ وَّنَصَرُوٓاْ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ حَقّٗاۚ لَّهُم مَّغۡفِرَةࣱ وَرِزۡقࣱ كَرِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنۢ بَعۡدُ وَهَاجَرُواْ وَجَٰهَدُواْ مَعَكُمۡ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ مِنكُمۡۚ وَأُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَرۡحَامِ بَعۡضُهُمۡ أَوۡلَىٰ بِبَعۡضٖ فِي كِتَٰبِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمُۢ﴾ (Quran 8:72-75)

**The Muhajirun and the Ansar**: those who emigrated and those who gave shelter and support are **"allies of one another"**. **"They are the believers, truly. For them is forgiveness and noble provision."**

## Word Meanings

- **Dhat baynikum**: the relations between you.
- **Wajilat**: became fearful.

- **Jan'ahu lis-salm**: incline to peace.
- **Allafa**: brought together.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Opening with spoils, then turning to hearts**: priorities corrected.
2. **"You wished for the unarmed one, but Allah intended..."**: human wishes and divine wisdom.
3. **"You did not throw when you threw"**: action and result distinguished.
4. **"That which gives you life"**: obedience as life.
5. **"Lose courage and your strength would depart"**: the psychology of disunity.
6. **"If you had spent all that is in the earth"**: the value of united hearts.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Madd muttasil**: in *mina al-mala'ikah* (verse 9).
- **Ikhfa'**: in *fi'atan fa-thbutu* (verse 45), the tanween is hidden before the fa'.
- **Ghunnah**: in *inna Allaha* (verse 46), on the noon with shaddah.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *yaj'al* (verse 29) and *anfaqta* (verse 63), the jim and the qaf sakinah bounce lightly.

## Connection with the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-A'raf before it ended with the angels who do not disdain worship, and Al-Anfal shows angels sent to support the believers. Surah At-Tawbah after it continues the themes of war and treaties, and is so closely connected that it has no Bismillah between them.

## Lessons From Badr for Every Struggle
1. **Victory is from Allah**: prepare, but rely on Him.
2. **Du'a is a weapon**: the Prophet ﷺ prayed with all his heart.
3. **Unity is strength**: disputes bring defeat.
4. **Remembrance brings firmness.**
5. **Arrogance brings loss.**
6. **Small numbers with faith can overcome large numbers.**

## Practical Lessons
1. **Fix relationships before gains.**
2. **Let the Quran increase your faith.**
3. **Respond quickly to Allah's commands.**
4. **Seek forgiveness**: it protects from punishment.
5. **Fear Allah to gain a criterion.**
6. **Avoid disputes.**
7. **Prepare strength in your field.**
8. **Pray for united hearts.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does al-Anfal mean?
The spoils of war, which the surah assigns to Allah and His Messenger.

### What are the qualities of true believers in verses 2–4?
Hearts that tremble at Allah's mention, faith that increases with the Quran, reliance on Allah, prayer and spending.

### How many angels were sent at Badr?
The surah mentions a thousand angels following one another.

### What does "you did not throw when you threw" mean?
The Prophet ﷺ threw the dust, but Allah made it reach the enemy; the result belongs to Allah.

### What does "do not dispute and lose courage" teach?
Disunity destroys strength and courage, while unity, remembrance and patience bring victory.

### What did the Prophet ﷺ say about "prepare whatever power you can"?
"Indeed, power is in shooting", as reported in Sahih Muslim.

### How many verses are in Surah Al-Anfal?
75 verses, and it is a Medinan surah.

## Conclusion
Surah Al-Anfal turns a battlefield into a classroom. It shows that true believers are defined by trembling hearts, growing faith and reliance on Allah; that victory comes from Allah through du'a, firmness and unity; and that disputes drain strength. Respond to what gives you life, seek forgiveness, prepare what you can, and ask Allah to unite hearts, for no amount of wealth can do it.
