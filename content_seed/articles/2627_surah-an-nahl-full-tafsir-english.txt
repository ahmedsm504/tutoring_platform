Surah An-Nahl is often called "the surah of blessings" (*surat an-ni'am*), because it lists more of Allah's gifts than almost any other surah: cattle for warmth and food, horses and mules for riding, rain and crops, the sea with its fish and pearls, the stars for guidance, milk pure between dung and blood, and honey from the bee in which there is healing. After every list comes the same question in different words: will you not be grateful? It also contains one of the most comprehensive verses in the Quran, the verse of justice and excellence, and the golden method of da'wah: "Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction."

This guide explains Surah An-Nahl section by section in English, with authentic hadith and practical lessons.

## Quick Facts About Surah An-Nahl
- **Number of verses**: 128.
- **Type**: Meccan; most scholars hold it is Meccan, with some saying its final verses were revealed in Madinah.
- **Position**: Surah 16, in the fourteenth juz.
- **Name**: An-Nahl means "the bee", from verses 68–69.
- **Other name**: Surat an-Ni'am, the surah of blessings.
- **Main themes**: tawhid through blessings, gratitude, the bee as a sign, justice, the good life, and patient da'wah.

## Verses 1–2: The Command of Allah Is Coming
﴿أَتَىٰٓ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ فَلَا تَسۡتَعۡجِلُوهُۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ ﴿يُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةَ بِٱلرُّوحِ مِنۡ أَمۡرِهِۦ عَلَىٰ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنۡ عِبَادِهِۦٓ أَنۡ أَنذِرُوٓاْ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱتَّقُونِ﴾ (Quran 16:1-2)

**"The command of Allah is coming, so be not impatient for it."** The disbelievers mockingly asked for the punishment to come quickly. The past tense *ata* ("has come") is used for something certain to come, as though it has already happened. Then Allah declares that He sends the angels with revelation to whom He wills: **"Warn that there is no deity except Me; so fear Me."**

## Verses 3–8: Created With Truth, and Cattle
﴿خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ تَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ﴾ ﴿خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ فَإِذَا هُوَ خَصِيمࣱ مُّبِينࣱ﴾ (Quran 16:3-4)

**"He created the heavens and the earth in truth."** Then: **"He created man from a sperm-drop; then at once he is a clear adversary."** The creature made from a drop turns around and argues against his Creator.

﴿وَٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمَ خَلَقَهَاۖ لَكُمۡ فِيهَا دِفۡءࣱ وَمَنَٰفِعُ وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ﴾ ﴿وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا جَمَالٌ حِينَ تُرِيحُونَ وَحِينَ تَسۡرَحُونَ﴾ ﴿وَتَحۡمِلُ أَثۡقَالَكُمۡ إِلَىٰ بَلَدٖ لَّمۡ تَكُونُواْ بَٰلِغِيهِ إِلَّا بِشِقِّ ٱلۡأَنفُسِۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكُمۡ لَرَءُوفࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿وَٱلۡخَيۡلَ وَٱلۡبِغَالَ وَٱلۡحَمِيرَ لِتَرۡكَبُوهَا وَزِينَةٗۚ وَيَخۡلُقُ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:5-8)

**The blessing of cattle**: warmth from their wool and hair, food, and **"beauty when you bring them in and when you send them out"**. They carry loads to lands you could not reach without great hardship. And horses, mules and donkeys for riding and adornment, **"and He creates that which you do not know"**: a door left open for every means of transport Allah would later give mankind.

## Verses 9–18: Rain, Stars, Sea, and Countless Blessings
﴿هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءٗۖ لَّكُم مِّنۡهُ شَرَابࣱ وَمِنۡهُ شَجَرࣱ فِيهِ تُسِيمُونَ﴾ ﴿يُنۢبِتُ لَكُم بِهِ ٱلزَّرۡعَ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونَ وَٱلنَّخِيلَ وَٱلۡأَعۡنَٰبَ وَمِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:10-11)

**Rain**: from it you drink, and from it grow plants on which you graze your cattle, crops, olives, palms, grapes and every fruit. **"Indeed in that is a sign for a people who give thought."**

﴿وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي سَخَّرَ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ لِتَأۡكُلُواْ مِنۡهُ لَحۡمٗا طَرِيّٗا وَتَسۡتَخۡرِجُواْ مِنۡهُ حِلۡيَةٗ تَلۡبَسُونَهَاۖ وَتَرَى ٱلۡفُلۡكَ مَوَاخِرَ فِيهِ وَلِتَبۡتَغُواْ مِن فَضۡلِهِۦ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:14)

**The sea**: **"It is He who subjected the sea for you to eat from it tender meat and to extract from it ornaments which you wear. And you see the ships plowing through it."**

﴿وَأَلۡقَىٰ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ رَوَٰسِيَ أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمۡ وَأَنۡهَٰرٗا وَسُبُلٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ ﴿وَعَلَٰمَٰتٖۚ وَبِٱلنَّجۡمِ هُمۡ يَهۡتَدُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:15-16)

**Mountains** to stabilise the earth, **rivers and roads**, **landmarks**, **"and by the stars they are guided"**.

﴿أَفَمَن يَخۡلُقُ كَمَن لَّا يَخۡلُقُۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ ﴿وَإِن تَعُدُّواْ نِعۡمَةَ ٱللَّهِ لَا تُحۡصُوهَآۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَغَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 16:17-18)

**"Then is He who creates like one who does not create? So will you not be reminded? And if you should count the favours of Allah, you could not enumerate them. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."** The verse ends with forgiveness, because no one can thank Allah as He deserves; His mercy covers our shortcoming in gratitude.

## Verses 19–34: Arrogance and Its End
﴿إِلَٰهُكُمۡ إِلَٰهࣱ وَٰحِدࣱۚ فَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مُّنكِرَةࣱ وَهُم مُّسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ﴾ ﴿لَا جَرَمَ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعۡلَمُ مَا يُسِرُّونَ وَمَا يُعۡلِنُونَۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلۡمُسۡتَكۡبِرِينَ﴾ (Quran 16:22-23)

**"Your god is one God. But those who do not believe in the hereafter, their hearts are disapproving, and they are arrogant."** **"Indeed, He does not like the arrogant."**

﴿۞وَقِيلَ لِلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَوۡاْ مَاذَآ أَنزَلَ رَبُّكُمۡۚ قَالُواْ خَيۡرٗاۗ لِّلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ فِي هَٰذِهِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةࣱۚ وَلَدَارُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ خَيۡرࣱۚ وَلَنِعۡمَ دَارُ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ (Quran 16:30)

When the righteous are asked what their Lord sent down, they say: **"Good."** **"For those who do good in this world is good; and the home of the hereafter is better."**

## Verses 35–50: Every Nation Had a Messenger
﴿وَلَقَدۡ بَعَثۡنَا فِي كُلِّ أُمَّةٖ رَّسُولًا أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱجۡتَنِبُواْ ٱلطَّـٰغُوتَۖ فَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ هَدَى ٱللَّهُ وَمِنۡهُم مَّنۡ حَقَّتۡ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلضَّلَٰلَةُۚ فَسِيرُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱنظُرُواْ كَيۡفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ (Quran 16:36)

**"And We certainly sent into every nation a messenger, saying: Worship Allah and avoid false deities."** This is the one message of all prophets.

﴿وَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ إِلَّا رِجَالٗا نُّوحِيٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡۖ فَسۡـَٔلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلذِّكۡرِ إِن كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ ﴿بِٱلۡبَيِّنَٰتِ وَٱلزُّبُرِۗ وَأَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلذِّكۡرَ لِتُبَيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ مَا نُزِّلَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:43-44)

**"So ask the people of the message if you do not know."** And to the Prophet ﷺ: **"And We revealed to you the message that you may make clear to the people what was sent down to them."** The Sunnah is the explanation of the Quran.

﴿وَلِلَّهِۤ يَسۡجُدُۤ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِن دَآبَّةٖ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ وَهُمۡ لَا يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ﴾ ﴿يَخَافُونَ رَبَّهُم مِّن فَوۡقِهِمۡ وَيَفۡعَلُونَ مَا يُؤۡمَرُونَ۩﴾ (Quran 16:49-50)

Everything in the heavens and earth prostrates to Allah, and the angels **"fear their Lord above them, and they do what they are commanded"**.

## Verses 51–65: The Injustice of Burying Daughters
﴿وَإِذَا بُشِّرَ أَحَدُهُم بِٱلۡأُنثَىٰ ظَلَّ وَجۡهُهُۥ مُسۡوَدّٗا وَهُوَ كَظِيمࣱ﴾ ﴿يَتَوَٰرَىٰ مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ مِن سُوٓءِ مَا بُشِّرَ بِهِۦٓۚ أَيُمۡسِكُهُۥ عَلَىٰ هُونٍ أَمۡ يَدُسُّهُۥ فِي ٱلتُّرَابِۗ أَلَا سَآءَ مَا يَحۡكُمُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:58-59)

**"And when one of them is informed of the birth of a female, his face becomes dark, and he suppresses grief."** He hides from people and wonders whether to keep her in humiliation or bury her in the dust. **"Unquestionably, evil is what they decide."** Islam ended this crime and made the raising of daughters a path to Paradise.

## Verses 66–69: Milk and the Bee
﴿وَإِنَّ لَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ لَعِبۡرَةٗۖ نُّسۡقِيكُم مِّمَّا فِي بُطُونِهِۦ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ فَرۡثٖ وَدَمٖ لَّبَنًا خَالِصٗا سَآئِغٗا لِّلشَّـٰرِبِينَ﴾ (Quran 16:66)

**"And indeed, for you in grazing livestock is a lesson. We give you drink from what is in their bellies, between excretion and blood, pure milk, palatable to drinkers."** Pure white milk is produced from between waste and blood, and neither stains it.

﴿وَأَوۡحَىٰ رَبُّكَ إِلَى ٱلنَّحۡلِ أَنِ ٱتَّخِذِي مِنَ ٱلۡجِبَالِ بُيُوتٗا وَمِنَ ٱلشَّجَرِ وَمِمَّا يَعۡرِشُونَ﴾ ﴿ثُمَّ كُلِي مِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ فَٱسۡلُكِي سُبُلَ رَبِّكِ ذُلُلٗاۚ يَخۡرُجُ مِنۢ بُطُونِهَا شَرَابࣱ مُّخۡتَلِفٌ أَلۡوَٰنُهُۥ فِيهِ شِفَآءࣱ لِّلنَّاسِۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةٗ لِّقَوۡمٖ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:68-69)

**"And your Lord inspired the bee: Take for yourself among the mountains houses, and among the trees and in that which they construct. Then eat from all the fruits and follow the ways of your Lord laid down for you."** **"There emerges from their bellies a drink, varying in colours, in which there is healing for people. Indeed in that is a sign for a people who give thought."**

**The inspiration of the bee**: Allah gave the bee an instinct to build, gather and produce, following "the ways of your Lord" without error. **Healing**: in al-Bukhari and Muslim, a man came to the Prophet ﷺ saying his brother had a stomach complaint. He told him to give him honey. The man returned twice saying it had not helped, and the Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has spoken the truth and your brother's stomach has lied." He gave him honey again, and he recovered.

## Verses 70–83: Life Stages and Signs
﴿وَٱللَّهُ خَلَقَكُمۡ ثُمَّ يَتَوَفَّىٰكُمۡۚ وَمِنكُم مَّن يُرَدُّ إِلَىٰٓ أَرۡذَلِ ٱلۡعُمُرِ لِكَيۡ لَا يَعۡلَمَ بَعۡدَ عِلۡمٖ شَيۡـًٔاۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمࣱ قَدِيرࣱ﴾ (Quran 16:70)

**"And Allah created you; then He will take you in death. And among you is he who is reversed to the most decrepit age so that he will not know, after having had knowledge, a thing."**

﴿وَٱللَّهُ أَخۡرَجَكُم مِّنۢ بُطُونِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ شَيۡـٔٗا وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:78)

**"And Allah has extracted you from the wombs of your mothers not knowing a thing, and He made for you hearing and vision and hearts that perhaps you would be grateful."**

﴿أَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَى ٱلطَّيۡرِ مُسَخَّرَٰتٖ فِي جَوِّ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَا يُمۡسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتٖ لِّقَوۡمٖ يُؤۡمِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:79)

**"Do they not see the birds controlled in the atmosphere of the sky? None holds them up except Allah."**

## Verses 84–89: The Witness of Every Nation
﴿وَيَوۡمَ نَبۡعَثُ فِي كُلِّ أُمَّةٖ شَهِيدًا عَلَيۡهِم مِّنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡۖ وَجِئۡنَا بِكَ شَهِيدًا عَلَىٰ هَـٰٓؤُلَآءِۚ وَنَزَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ تِبۡيَٰنٗا لِّكُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَهُدٗى وَرَحۡمَةٗ وَبُشۡرَىٰ لِلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ﴾ (Quran 16:89)

**"And We have sent down to you the Book as clarification for all things and as guidance and mercy and good tidings for the Muslims."**

## Verse 90: The Most Comprehensive Verse
﴿۞إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأۡمُرُ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِ وَٱلۡإِحۡسَٰنِ وَإِيتَآيِٕ ذِي ٱلۡقُرۡبَىٰ وَيَنۡهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلۡفَحۡشَآءِ وَٱلۡمُنكَرِ وَٱلۡبَغۡيِۚ يَعِظُكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:90)

**"Indeed, Allah orders justice and good conduct and giving to relatives and forbids immorality and bad conduct and oppression. He admonishes you that perhaps you will be reminded."**

**Three commands**: justice (*'adl*), excellence (*ihsan*) and giving to relatives. **Three prohibitions**: immorality (*fahsha'*), wrongdoing (*munkar*) and oppression (*baghy*). Many scholars called it among the most comprehensive verses in the Quran, and it is commonly recited at the end of the Friday sermon.

## Verses 91–96: Keeping Promises
﴿وَلَا تَكُونُواْ كَٱلَّتِي نَقَضَتۡ غَزۡلَهَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ قُوَّةٍ أَنكَٰثٗا تَتَّخِذُونَ أَيۡمَٰنَكُمۡ دَخَلَۢا بَيۡنَكُمۡ أَن تَكُونَ أُمَّةٌ هِيَ أَرۡبَىٰ مِنۡ أُمَّةٍۚ إِنَّمَا يَبۡلُوكُمُ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦۚ وَلَيُبَيِّنَنَّ لَكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ مَا كُنتُمۡ فِيهِ تَخۡتَلِفُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:92)

**"And do not be like she who untwisted her spun thread after it was strong."** Breaking a promise is like undoing your own work.

﴿مَا عِندَكُمۡ يَنفَدُ وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ بَاقٖۗ وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ صَبَرُوٓاْ أَجۡرَهُم بِأَحۡسَنِ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:96)

**"Whatever you have will end, but what Allah has is lasting."**

## Verse 97: The Good Life
﴿مَنۡ عَمِلَ صَٰلِحٗا مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوۡ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنࣱ فَلَنُحۡيِيَنَّهُۥ حَيَوٰةٗ طَيِّبَةٗۖ وَلَنَجۡزِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡرَهُم بِأَحۡسَنِ مَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:97)

**"Whoever does righteousness, whether male or female, while he is a believer, We will surely cause him to live a good life, and We will surely give them their reward according to the best of what they used to do."** The good life (*hayatan tayyibah*) is contentment, peace of heart and sweetness of faith in this world, before the reward of the next.

## Verses 98–105: Seeking Refuge Before Reciting
﴿فَإِذَا قَرَأۡتَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ فَٱسۡتَعِذۡ بِٱللَّهِ مِنَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِ ٱلرَّجِيمِ﴾ ﴿إِنَّهُۥ لَيۡسَ لَهُۥ سُلۡطَٰنٌ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:98-99)

**"So when you recite the Quran, seek refuge in Allah from Satan, the expelled."** Satan has no authority over those who believe and rely on their Lord.

## Verses 106–119: Under Compulsion, and Repentance
﴿مَن كَفَرَ بِٱللَّهِ مِنۢ بَعۡدِ إِيمَٰنِهِۦٓ إِلَّا مَنۡ أُكۡرِهَ وَقَلۡبُهُۥ مُطۡمَئِنُّۢ بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ وَلَٰكِن مَّن شَرَحَ بِٱلۡكُفۡرِ صَدۡرٗا فَعَلَيۡهِمۡ غَضَبࣱ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمࣱ﴾ (Quran 16:106)

Whoever disbelieves after belief faces Allah's anger, **"except for one who is forced while his heart is secure in faith"**. Commentators connect this with 'Ammar ibn Yasir, who was tortured.

﴿وَضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا قَرۡيَةٗ كَانَتۡ ءَامِنَةٗ مُّطۡمَئِنَّةٗ يَأۡتِيهَا رِزۡقُهَا رَغَدٗا مِّن كُلِّ مَكَانٖ فَكَفَرَتۡ بِأَنۡعُمِ ٱللَّهِ فَأَذَٰقَهَا ٱللَّهُ لِبَاسَ ٱلۡجُوعِ وَٱلۡخَوۡفِ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَصۡنَعُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:112)

**The parable of a town**: secure and provided for, it denied Allah's favours, **"so Allah made it taste the garment of hunger and fear"**.

## Verses 120–128: Ibrahim and the Method of Da'wah
﴿إِنَّ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ كَانَ أُمَّةٗ قَانِتٗا لِّلَّهِ حَنِيفٗا وَلَمۡ يَكُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُشۡرِكِينَ﴾ (Quran 16:120)

**"Indeed, Ibrahim was a nation, devoutly obedient to Allah, inclining toward truth, and he was not of those who associate others with Allah."** One man is described as a whole nation (*ummah*), because he combined all good.

﴿ٱدۡعُ إِلَىٰ سَبِيلِ رَبِّكَ بِٱلۡحِكۡمَةِ وَٱلۡمَوۡعِظَةِ ٱلۡحَسَنَةِۖ وَجَٰدِلۡهُم بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ﴾ (Quran 16:125)

**"Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction, and argue with them in a way that is best."** Three levels: wisdom for those who seek truth, good instruction for those who need reminding, and the best argument for those who dispute.

﴿وَإِنۡ عَاقَبۡتُمۡ فَعَاقِبُواْ بِمِثۡلِ مَا عُوقِبۡتُم بِهِۦۖ وَلَئِن صَبَرۡتُمۡ لَهُوَ خَيۡرࣱ لِّلصَّـٰبِرِينَ﴾ ﴿وَٱصۡبِرۡ وَمَا صَبۡرُكَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِۚ وَلَا تَحۡزَنۡ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا تَكُ فِي ضَيۡقٖ مِّمَّا يَمۡكُرُونَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّقَواْ وَّٱلَّذِينَ هُم مُّحۡسِنُونَ﴾ (Quran 16:126-128)

**"And if you punish, punish with an equivalent of that with which you were harmed. But if you are patient, it is better for those who are patient."** **"Indeed, Allah is with those who fear Him and those who are doers of good."**

## Word Meanings
- **An-Nahl**: the bee.
- **Ni'mah**: blessing, favour.
- **Khasim mubin**: a clear adversary.
- **Ad-Dif'**: warmth.
- **Al-Fulk**: ships.
- **Awha**: inspired.
- **Shifa'**: healing.
- **Al-'Adl**: justice.
- **Al-Ihsan**: excellence, good conduct.
- **Al-Baghy**: oppression.
- **Hayatan tayyibah**: a good life.
- **Ummah**: nation, or one who combines all good.

## Rhetorical Beauty
1. **Lists of blessings**: each ending with a call to reflect or give thanks.
2. **"Between excretion and blood, pure milk"**: purity from within impurity.
3. **The feminine bee**: the verbs for the bee are feminine, matching the worker bees that build and gather.
4. **Three commands and three prohibitions** in verse 90.
5. **Forgiveness after countless blessings**: verse 18.

## Tajweed Notes
- **Ikhfa'**: in *wa anzalna* (verse 44), the noon sakinah is hidden before the zay.
- **Idgham with ghunnah**: in *man yasha'* (verse 93), the noon sakinah merges into the ya' with nasalisation.
- **Madd muttasil**: in *min as-sama'i ma'an* (verse 10).
- **Lam shamsiyyah**: in *ilan-nahl* (verse 68), the lam is not pronounced and the noon is doubled.
- **Qalqalah sughra**: in *wajtanibu* (verse 36), the jim sakinah bounces lightly.

## Connection With the Surrounding Surahs
Surah Al-Hijr before it ends with "And worship your Lord until there comes to you the certainty", and An-Nahl opens with "The command of Allah is coming". An-Nahl ends with patience and da'wah, and Surah Al-Isra' after it opens with the Night Journey, an honour given to the patient Prophet ﷺ.

## Practical Lessons
1. **Count your blessings daily**, and thank Allah for them.
2. **Be grateful even when you cannot count**: admit your shortcoming and ask forgiveness.
3. **Honour daughters**, as Islam erased the shame of the ignorant.
4. **Use honey** as the Sunnah recommends, trusting Allah for healing.
5. **Live by verse 90**: justice, excellence and generosity to relatives.
6. **Keep your promises.**
7. **Seek refuge from Satan before reciting.**
8. **Call to Allah with wisdom**, kindness and the best manners.

- **Teach verse 90** as the family's rule of good manners.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why is it called Surah An-Nahl?
Because of the bee mentioned in verses 68–69, whose honey contains healing.

### What is "the surah of blessings"?
Another name for An-Nahl, because of its many lists of Allah's favours.

### Is there healing in honey?
Yes, Allah says "in which there is healing for people", and the hadith of the man with a stomach complaint is in al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### What is the most comprehensive verse in An-Nahl?
Verse 90: "Indeed, Allah orders justice and good conduct and giving to relatives..."

### What is the good life in verse 97?
Contentment, peace and the sweetness of faith in this world for the believer who does good.

### What is the method of da'wah in An-Nahl?
Wisdom, good instruction and arguing in the best way, from verse 125.

### How many verses are in Surah An-Nahl?
128 verses.

## Conclusion
Surah An-Nahl opens your eyes to blessings you pass by every day: a glass of milk, a spoon of honey, a drop of rain, a star at night. Each is a message from the One who gives. The surah asks for one response: gratitude, shown in justice, excellence, kept promises and gentle calling to Allah. Count what you can, admit what you cannot, and remember that He is Forgiving and Merciful.
