﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ كُلُواْ مِمَّا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ حَلَٰلٗا طَيِّبٗا وَلَا تَتَّبِعُواْ خُطُوَٰتِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنِۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوࣱّ مُّبِينٌ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:168]

## To all people
- *O mankind*: food is a blessing for every human.

## Halal and tayyib
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Halal* | Permitted in itself and in how it was earned |
| *Tayyib* | Wholesome, pleasant and not harmful |

## The default is permissibility
- *Of what is on earth*: food is permitted unless Allah forbade it.

## Lawful food and duʿa
- The Prophet ﷺ mentioned a man on a long journey, dishevelled, raising his hands: *O Lord, O Lord*, while his food, drink and clothing were unlawful. *How can he be answered?* (narrated by Muslim).

## Satan's footsteps
- Forbidding what Allah permitted without evidence, permitting what He forbade, or unlawful earning.

## Lessons
- **Seek the lawful** in income and food.
- **Do not forbid** what Allah permitted without knowledge.
