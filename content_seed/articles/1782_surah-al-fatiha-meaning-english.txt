## Surah Al-Fatiha Meaning: The Opening Prayer Recited in Every Rakah

**Surah Al-Fatiha** (The Opening, 7 ayat) is recited in every unit of every prayer. The Prophet ﷺ called it the greatest surah in the Quran and *the seven oft-repeated verses* (narrated by al-Bukhari).

### Verse by verse
| Ayah | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾ | In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Especially Merciful |
| ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ | All praise belongs to Allah, Lord of all worlds |
| ﴿ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾ | Mercy that covers all creation, and mercy reserved for believers |
| ﴿مَٰلِكِ يَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ | Master of the Day of Recompense |
| ﴿إِيَّاكَ نَعۡبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسۡتَعِينُ﴾ | You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help |
| ﴿ٱهۡدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ﴾ | Guide us to the straight path |
| ﴿صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ غَيۡرِ ٱلۡمَغۡضُوبِ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ﴾ | The path of those You blessed, not of those who earned anger nor of those astray |

### A conversation with Allah
- In a Hadith Qudsi narrated by Muslim, Allah says He has divided the prayer between Himself and His servant. When the servant says *al-hamdu lillah*, Allah says: *My servant has praised Me.* When he reaches *ihdina*, Allah says: *This is for My servant, and My servant will have what he asked.*

### Its structure
1. **Praise and glorification** (verses 1 to 4).
2. **The covenant**: worship and reliance on Allah alone (verse 5).
3. **The request**: guidance (verses 6 and 7).

### Lessons
- **Praise before asking**: it teaches the etiquette of duʿa.
- **The greatest request is guidance**, repeated at least seventeen times a day in the obligatory prayers.

## The Disconnected Letters (Huruf Muqatta'at) at the Start of Surahs

**29 surahs** begin with letters read one by one, like ﴿الٓمٓ﴾ at the start of Al-Baqarah. They are called **al-ḥurūf al-muqaṭṭaʿah**, the disconnected letters.

### Their forms
| Number of letters | Example |
|---|---|
| One | ﴿صٓۚ﴾, ﴿قٓۚ﴾, ﴿نٓۚ﴾ |
| Two | ﴿حمٓ﴾, ﴿يسٓ﴾ |
| Three | ﴿الٓمٓ﴾, ﴿طسٓمٓ﴾ |
| Four | ﴿الٓمٓرۚ﴾ |
| Five | ﴿كٓهيعٓصٓ﴾ |

### The 14 letters
- Only **14 letters** of the alphabet appear in them, about half of the 28.
- Some scholars gathered them in the phrase: **نَصٌّ حَكِيمٌ قَاطِعٌ لَهُ سِرٌّ**.

### How to recite them
- You read each **letter by its name**: *alif lām mīm*.
- Letters whose names are spelled with three letters and a madd letter in the middle, like *lām* (ل ا م) and *mīm* (م ي م), take a **long madd of six counts**. They are gathered in **نَقَصَ عَسَلُكُمۡ**.
- *ʿAyn* (ع) may be stretched **four or six counts**.
- Letters with two-letter names, gathered in **حَيٌّ طَهُرَ**, like *ḥā* and *yā*, take **two counts**.
- *Alif* has no madd.

### What do they mean?
- The most cautious view: **Allah knows best** what He meant by them.
- Many scholars noted a **challenge**: the Quran is made of the same letters the Arabs used, yet they could not produce anything like it.
- **Evidence for this view:** most of these surahs mention the Book right after the letters, as in ﴿ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيۡبَۛ فِيهِۛ﴾ [Al-Baqarah: 2].

### A reward for every letter
- The Prophet ﷺ said: «I do not say *Alif Lām Mīm* is one letter, but *alif* is a letter, *lām* is a letter and *mīm* is a letter» [At-Tirmidhi].

## "This Is the Book About Which There Is No Doubt": Quran 2:2-3 Explained

﴿ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيۡبَۛ فِيهِۛ هُدٗى لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ﴾ ﴿ٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَيُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقۡنَٰهُمۡ يُنفِقُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:2-3]

### Why "that"?
- *Dhalika* points to something distant, though the Quran is in our hands. Scholars explain: it shows its **high rank**.

### No doubt
- A complete denial: no doubt it is from Allah, in its news or in the justice of its rulings.

### The embracing stop
- The three dots over *rayba* and *fihi* mean: stop on **one** of them, not both.
| Stop on | Reading |
|---|---|
| *la rayba* | No doubt. In it is guidance... |
| *fihi* | No doubt in it. Guidance for the pious |

### Guidance for the pious
- The Quran guides all people by showing the way, but those who truly benefit are the pious.

### The first qualities
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Belief in the unseen | Allah, angels, the Last Day, what senses cannot reach |
| Establishing prayer | Complete and on time |
| Spending | *From what We provided*: part of what Allah gave |

### Lessons
- **Read with certainty** that it is the truth.
- **Be among the pious** to benefit from its guidance.

## "I Will Place a Successor on Earth": Quran 2:30 Explained

﴿وَإِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي جَاعِلࣱ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ خَلِيفَةٗۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَتَجۡعَلُ فِيهَا مَن يُفۡسِدُ فِيهَا وَيَسۡفِكُ ٱلدِّمَآءَ وَنَحۡنُ نُسَبِّحُ بِحَمۡدِكَ وَنُقَدِّسُ لَكَۖ قَالَ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَمُ مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:30]

### The meaning of *khalifah*
- Scholars explain it as **generations succeeding one another** on earth, entrusted to settle it and live by Allah's law.

### The angels' question
- Their question was **not objection**, but asking about the wisdom. They knew what corruption could come.

### The answer
- *I know what you do not know*: among humans would be prophets, scholars, martyrs and the righteous.

### Human responsibility
| Responsibility | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Worship | The purpose of creation |
| Building the earth | Lawful work and development |
| Justice | Ruling and dealing fairly |

### The next verses
- Allah taught Adam the names and showed his honour through knowledge.

### Lessons
- **You were created for a mission.**
- **Trust Allah's wisdom** in what you do not understand.

## "Do You Command People to Righteousness and Forget Yourselves?": Quran 2:44 Explained

﴿۞أَتَأۡمُرُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ بِٱلۡبِرِّ وَتَنسَوۡنَ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ تَتۡلُونَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:44]

### The context
- Addressed to the Children of Israel, whose scholars told people to do good but did not do it themselves. The lesson applies to **everyone** who teaches and preaches.

### "Forget yourselves"
- Not literal forgetting, but **neglecting** as if forgotten.

### "Do you not reason?"
- Reason demands starting with oneself.

### Should the one who falls short stop advising?
- Scholars say no: two duties remain, to act and to advise. Failing one does not cancel the other.

### A stern warning
- A man will be thrown into the Fire, his intestines spilling out. People will ask: *Did you not command us to good and forbid evil?* He will say: *I commanded good and did not do it, and forbade evil and did it* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lessons
- **Start with yourself.**
- **Parents and teachers** are models before they are speakers.

## "And Speak Good to People": Quran 2:83 Explained

﴿وَقُولُواْ لِلنَّاسِ حُسۡنٗا﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:83]

### The covenant
- The verse mentions a covenant taken from the Children of Israel with eight items:
| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Worship only Allah | Tawhid |
| Kindness to parents | Right after tawhid |
| Relatives | Keeping ties |
| Orphans | Care for the vulnerable |
| The poor | Support |
| **Speak good to people** | Gentle words for everyone |
| Prayer | Connection to Allah |
| Zakah | Purifying wealth |

### "To people"
- The verse says **people**, not only believers. Good speech is owed to everyone: Muslim and non-Muslim, friend and stranger.

### What is *husn*?
- Scholars explain it as truthful, gentle words, enjoining good and forbidding evil kindly, and avoiding harshness and insults.

### A lesson for us
- The covenant was broken by many: *then you turned away, except a few*. The Quran tells us so we do not repeat it.

### Lessons
- **Your words are part of your religion.**
- **Be kind in speech**, even in disagreement.

## "Wherever You Turn, There Is the Face of Allah": Quran 2:115 Explained

﴿وَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقُ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبُۚ فَأَيۡنَمَا تُوَلُّواْ فَثَمَّ وَجۡهُ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمࣱ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:115]

### East and west
- All directions belong to Allah; no direction limits His greatness.

### Occasions mentioned
| View | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Voluntary prayer while riding | The Prophet ﷺ prayed on his mount wherever it faced while travelling; Ibn ʿUmar mentioned this verse (narrated by Muslim) |
| The one who errs in qiblah | After genuine effort in darkness or travel |
| Before the qiblah change | A preparation for turning to the Kaʿbah |

### "There is the Face of Allah"
- Scholars explained: there is the qiblah Allah permitted, or Allah sees you there, while affirming the Face of Allah as befits His majesty.

### "All-Encompassing, All-Knowing"
- Vast in generosity and ease, knowing your intentions.

### Lessons
- **Face the qiblah** in obligatory prayers; the concession is for voluntary prayer on a mount.
- **Allah sees you** wherever you are.

## "They Recite It with Its True Recitation": Quran 2:121 Explained

﴿ٱلَّذِينَ ءَاتَيۡنَٰهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ يَتۡلُونَهُۥ حَقَّ تِلَاوَتِهِۦٓ أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦۗ وَمَن يَكۡفُرۡ بِهِۦ فَأُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰسِرُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:121]

### Two meanings of *tilawah*
- Reading, and **following**: in Arabic, *talahu* also means he followed it.

### The true recitation
| Aspect | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Correct reading | As it was revealed |
| Understanding | Reflecting on meanings |
| Action | Making lawful its lawful and unlawful its unlawful |

- Ibn Masʿud is reported to have explained it as making lawful what it makes lawful, forbidding what it forbids, reciting it as Allah revealed it, and not distorting its words.

### The sign of faith
- *Those believe in it*: true recitation is a mark of real faith.

### Lessons
- **Correct your recitation** with a teacher.
- **Read to act**, not only to finish.

## "We Have Seen the Turning of Your Face Toward the Heaven": Quran 2:144 Explained

﴿قَدۡ نَرَىٰ تَقَلُّبَ وَجۡهِكَ فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِۖ فَلَنُوَلِّيَنَّكَ قِبۡلَةٗ تَرۡضَىٰهَاۚ فَوَلِّ وَجۡهَكَ شَطۡرَ ٱلۡمَسۡجِدِ ٱلۡحَرَامِۚ وَحَيۡثُ مَا كُنتُمۡ فَوَلُّواْ وُجُوهَكُمۡ شَطۡرَهُۥۗ وَإِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ لَيَعۡلَمُونَ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمۡۗ وَمَا ٱللَّهُ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا يَعۡمَلُونَ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:144]

### The story
- After the Hijrah, the Prophet ﷺ prayed toward Jerusalem for sixteen or seventeen months and wished to face the Kaʿbah (narrated by al-Bukhari from al-Baraʾ).

### Waiting with manners
- He looked up toward the sky awaiting revelation, without asking aloud.

### "A qiblah that will please you"
- An honour: Allah gave him what he loved.

### *Shatr*
- Direction. Whoever is far faces the direction of the Kaʿbah; whoever sees it faces it directly.

### A test of obedience
- The change revealed who follows the Messenger and who turns back (2:143).

### Lessons
- **Allah sees your need** before you speak.
- **Obeying when commands change** is a sign of sincerity.

## "Remember Me, and I Will Remember You": Quran 2:152 Explained

﴿فَٱذۡكُرُونِيٓ أَذۡكُرۡكُمۡ وَٱشۡكُرُواْ لِي وَلَا تَكۡفُرُونِ﴾ [Al-Baqarah 2:152]

### An incredible exchange
- You remember Allah, and **Allah remembers you**. The servant's small act is answered by the Lord of the worlds.

### The Hadith Qudsi
- Allah says: *If he remembers Me within himself, I remember him within Myself. If he remembers Me in a gathering, I remember him in a better gathering* (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Gratitude and its opposite
| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Be grateful to Me | Recognise and use blessings in obedience |
| Do not be ungrateful | Do not deny or misuse them |

### The context
- The verse comes after the change of the qiblah and the mention of sending a Messenger who recites, purifies and teaches. Remembrance and gratitude are the response to such blessings.

### Ways to remember Allah
- Reciting the Quran, morning and evening adhkar, duʿa, and remembering Him at the moment of temptation.

### Lessons
- **Remember Allah in private**: He remembers you in private.
- **Gratitude completes remembrance.**
