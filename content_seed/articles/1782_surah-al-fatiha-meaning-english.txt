**Surah Al-Fatiha** (The Opening, 7 ayat) is recited in every unit of every prayer. The Prophet ﷺ called it the greatest surah in the Quran and *the seven oft-repeated verses* (narrated by al-Bukhari).

## Verse by verse
| Ayah | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ﴿بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾ | In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Especially Merciful |
| ﴿ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ﴾ | All praise belongs to Allah, Lord of all worlds |
| ﴿ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ﴾ | Mercy that covers all creation, and mercy reserved for believers |
| ﴿مَٰلِكِ يَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ﴾ | Master of the Day of Recompense |
| ﴿إِيَّاكَ نَعۡبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسۡتَعِينُ﴾ | You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help |
| ﴿ٱهۡدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ﴾ | Guide us to the straight path |
| ﴿صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ غَيۡرِ ٱلۡمَغۡضُوبِ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ﴾ | The path of those You blessed, not of those who earned anger nor of those astray |

## A conversation with Allah
- In a Hadith Qudsi narrated by Muslim, Allah says He has divided the prayer between Himself and His servant. When the servant says *al-hamdu lillah*, Allah says: *My servant has praised Me.* When he reaches *ihdina*, Allah says: *This is for My servant, and My servant will have what he asked.*

## Its structure
1. **Praise and glorification** (verses 1 to 4).
2. **The covenant**: worship and reliance on Allah alone (verse 5).
3. **The request**: guidance (verses 6 and 7).

## Lessons
- **Praise before asking**: it teaches the etiquette of duʿa.
- **The greatest request is guidance**, repeated at least seventeen times a day in the obligatory prayers.
